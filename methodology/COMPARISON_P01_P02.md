# La Longue Marche, Pro pilot packages P01–P02: comparison with the scans and the witnesses

Scope: scans 1–20 of *cote 140-2* (archive title, cover, the handwritten table of contents, a page of numbers, and
manuscript pages 1–8: §§ 1–4 and the start of § 5 up to Déf. 5.3). Pro's answers: `answers/LM_P01_transcription.tex`
with `LM_P01_reading_apparatus.md`, and `answers/LM_P02_transcription.tex` with `LM_P02_notes.md`.

## Summary

- **Main text (scans 6–20, about 1,600 words):** Pro made about 22 reading errors (1.4 % of words, about
  1.5 per scan page). 16 of them carry no doubt mark. The table of contents (scans 3–4, about 420 words) was
  worse: 14 errors (3.3 %), 9 of them unmarked. These two pages were also the lowest-resolution images Pro received.
- **Witnesses on the same main text:** the CSG draft (2026-02) has about 2 errors, Carmona's earlier edition
  ("galoisI") about 9, and Carmona–Künzer about 17 (the SWH copy has the same text). Several of the Carmona–Künzer
  errors change the mathematics, for example "≤" for "≥" throughout Lemma 5.2 d).
- **Pro's errors read fluently.** Nothing is invented wholesale, but several misreadings put a plausible
  mathematical word in place of the ink: "fonctorielle" for *tangentielle*, "homotopique" for *heuristique*,
  "propres" for *quasi-*. Pro also drops the condition "S ≠ ∅" in Lemma 5.2 f). Such errors cannot be found by
  reading Pro's text alone.
- **Doubt marks.** About 80 % of Pro's `\unsure` readings are right, so the marks are informative. But only about
  30 % of its actual errors are marked, and only 2 of the 12 consequential ones.
- **What Pro adds that the witnesses lack.** It keeps deletions, insertions, abbreviations and Grothendieck's own
  forms ("E₀", "Π₀", "anabélique", "à isom. unique près"). Several times it is right where all three witnesses
  normalise or misread.
- **Verdict:** this route can carry the manuscript as a **first, blind diplomatic pass**, but not alone. Every
  package needs a second pass that checks the readings against the image. Where a witness exists, that pass should
  collate against the witness. The input images also need to be better: native resolution, landscape spreads
  split, dense pages tiled.

## Method

- **Witnesses** (text layers, with rendered pages where a glyph mattered):
  - **CSG**: `CSG_G103d_draft_2026-02.pdf`.
  - **CK**: `Carmona_Kuenzer_207page_historical_witness.pdf`. `SWH_agrothendieck_galois_historical_witness.pdf`
    has the same text and the same errors, so it is counted once.
  - **IUT**: `IUT_galoisI_historical_witness.pdf`, Carmona's earlier edition "from the typescript".
  - **Circle**: `Grothendieck_Circle_tableLM.pdf`. `Grothendieck_Circle_section_49.pdf` does not cover these pages.
- **How independent the witnesses are.** CK, IUT and CSG all come from Carmona's group, so they are not
  independent of each other. All three normalise: they expand abbreviations, regularise punctuation and drop
  deletions and insertions. Normalisation is not counted as a disagreement.
- **The tables of contents are not transcriptions of scans 3–4.** CK, IUT and CSG print editorial tables built
  from the headings in the body. Circle prints a later table by the author, with different wording in places: its
  § 17 title, for instance, does not match scan 3. So scans 3–4 were read against the image, with Circle used
  only as a hint.
- **Adjudication images.** Every disputed reading was settled on the embedded JPEG extracted from the archive PDF
  at native resolution, cropped and enlarged, with margins rotated. Normal pages are about 1,680 × 2,360 px.
  Scan 3 is only 1,197 × 1,684 px. Scan 4 is a 2,372 × 1,699 px landscape spread, which the pilot rendering
  squeezed to 1,786 px wide (75 % of native).
- **Full checks against the image:** scans 3, 4 and 6 were checked top to bottom. Scans 8–20 got a full-page
  overview plus enlarged crops at every disagreement. Scans 7 and 15 were checked at the disagreements.
- **What counts as an error:** one error is a wrong, missing or added word or symbol group. "Probable" means the
  image favours the other reading but does not settle it. "Unresolved" means both readings are defensible; these
  are not counted.

## Estimated error rates

Errors are counted per reading. Pro's probable errors are included: 6 in total. Witness normalisation is not
counted, and the witnesses' tables of contents are not scored, because they are not transcriptions of scans 3–4.

| Source | Table of contents (scans 3–4, ≈ 420 words) | Main text (scans 6–20, ≈ 1,600 words) | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro, all errors | 14 (3.3 %) | 22 (1.4 %) | 36 (≈ 1.8 %) |
| Pro, unmarked errors only | 9 (2.1 %) | 16 (1.0 %) | 25 (≈ 1.2 %) |
| CK (= SWH) | — | 17 (≈ 1.1 %) | — |
| IUT ("galoisI") | — | 9 (≈ 0.6 %) | — |
| CSG draft 2026-02 | — | 2 (≈ 0.1 %) | — |

- **Pro against the witnesses.** Pro's error rate is close to the typeset Carmona–Künzer edition and well above the
  CSG draft. But CSG is a normalised reading edition, not a diplomatic one: it silently drops every deletion and
  insertion that Pro records.
- **Collation would catch nearly everything.** In this sample, every one of Pro's 16 unmarked main-text errors is
  contradicted by at least one witness. Conversely, every witness error listed above is contradicted by Pro.

## Invented text

- **Nothing invented wholesale.** Pro adds no sentences, formulas or notes that are absent from the page, and drops
  no whole line.
- **Fluent substitutions.** Several readings put a smooth phrase where the ink says something else: "dans le cas
  considéré", "définition du cas", "fonctorielle", "homotopique", "propres", "; topos profinis", "Conclusions".
  "considéré" bears no resemblance to the ink (*anabéliens*), so it is the closest thing to invented text. The
  others are guided by expectation.
- **"m̄" not read.** Pro renders *même* (written *m̄*) as a mathematical sign on scan 10, although it reads the
  same abbreviation correctly on scan 14.
- **Archive numbering.** P02 transcribes the archive's lower-left page numbers 10–19 as `\margin{}` entries, while
  P01 leaves them out. These numbers are not authorial, and the two packages handle them inconsistently.

## Calibration of the doubt marks

- **Marked readings.** I checked about 89 of them across the two apparatuses. About 59 are right as first reading
  and 2 are right only in the alternative; about 10 are wrong, 2 are honest illegible marks, and about 16 cannot
  be settled. So roughly 80 % of resolvable marked readings are right. Many marks therefore sit on readings that
  were fine (letterforms Pro was unsure of).
- **Unmarked text.** About 1.2 % of unmarked words are wrong. 25 of the 36 errors carry no mark (69 %), including
  10 of the 12 consequential ones.
- **The pattern.** Pro doubts letters but not words: when it "recognises" a familiar term (fonctorielle,
  homotopique, propres, pointé), it writes it with confidence. The marks are a useful priority list for a checker,
  but they are no substitute for checking.

## Verdict

**Yes as a first pass, no as the edition.** On ordinary manuscript pages, a single Pro pass gives a usable
diplomatic draft: about 1.5 errors per page, nearly three quarters of them unmarked. It preserves the revision layer that the
existing editions discard, and it independently corrects several errors shared by the witnesses.

**Uncorrected, it is not acceptable.** Its unmarked errors are fluent and sometimes change the mathematics
(tangentielle/fonctorielle, quasi-/propres, S ≠ ∅).

**Expected volume.** For 1,588 scans, expect roughly 2,000–2,500 errors in a single pass, 1,500 or more of them
unmarked.
The route can carry the manuscript only with a systematic second pass that checks readings against the image. The
dense pages also need better input, as the table of contents showed.

## What would make it better

1. **Resolution.**
   - Feed the embedded scan JPEG at native size, not a re-render of the PDF page.
   - Split landscape spreads (scan 4) into two halves at full resolution.
   - Tile dense or low-resolution pages (scans 3–4) into half-page crops enlarged about 2×.
   - Add rotated crops for vertical margin notes (scans 6, 12, 20).
   - Save as PNG or JPEG quality ≥ 95, and add an unsharpened copy next to the enhanced one.
2. **Pages per package.** Ten ordinary pages per package is fine: P02 shows no sign of fatigue. Pages like the
   table of contents, formula-dense pages and heavily corrected pages should go 3–5 per package, as tiles.
3. **A second pass, mandatory.**
   - *Where a witness exists* (CSG/CK cover Part I): collate Pro against CSG word by word, list the
     disagreements, and settle each one on an image crop. The arbiter can be Claude, or Pro in a fresh chat given
     the crop and both readings unlabelled. Here this would catch all of Pro's main-text errors found and all the
     witness errors.
   - *Where no witness exists:* make a second independent Pro pass with different tiling, diff the two, and
     settle the differences on the image. Add a verification prompt that gives the image and the transcription
     and asks for every word that does not match.
4. **Do not give Pro the witness in the blind pass.** It would anchor on the witness's normalisations and errors
   ("anabélien", ≤/≥, the lim arrows), and lose the independence that made Pro useful here. Use witnesses only
   in the settling step.
5. **Prompt changes.**
   - Require `\unsure` on any word identified from context rather than letter by letter.
   - Warn explicitly against replacing a word with a more familiar mathematical term.
   - Give a fixed rule for the archive's lower-left numbering.
   - Treat *m̄* and similar marks as abbreviations.
   - Ask for inclusion signs and vertical relations in displayed formulas.
