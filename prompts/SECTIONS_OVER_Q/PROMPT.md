## Conventions

`U = P¹_Q ∖ {0, 1, ∞}` with coordinate `t`; `E_U = π_1(U)`, `Π` its geometric subgroup; a *section* is a continuous section of `E_U → G_Q`, taken up to conjugation by `Π`. It is *cuspidal* if its image lies in the decomposition group of a cusp. `𝓔 → U` is the Legendre family `y² = x(x − 1)(x − t)`; `ρ_{s,ℓ}` and `ρ̄_{s,ℓ}` are the pull-backs along `s` of its `ℓ`-adic and mod-`ℓ` Tate modules, and `ρ_Leg : E_U → GL_2(Ẑ)` its monodromy. `κ` is the Kummer map; `A_s = s^*κ(t)` and `B_s = s^*κ(1 − t)` lie in `Q̂^× = lim_n Q^×/n`. The local section `s_p` of `s` at a prime `p` is the section of a point `x_p ∈ U(Q_p)` ("ordinary") or lies in the packet of one of the cusps `0, 1, ∞` (its "label"). A *Selmer* section is one whose local sections are of these two kinds at every finite place, with a component at the real place. A *ghost* is an object that passes every test at one prime, at one nilpotent level or at one finite level; a *ghost solution of the unit equation* is a pair `(A, B)` in `Q̂^×` with a point `x_p` at every `p` such that `A_p = κ(x_p)` and `B_p = κ(1 − x_p)`. The *Fermat successor* is a separate prompt of the same series, on the Fermat curves and their twists; two of its items are named where this prompt leaves a computation to it.

**The shape of the problem.**
- **L1** (manuscript reader; from memory; open the paper first). Stix, *On the birational section conjecture with local conditions* (Invent. Math. 2015), as recalled: (i) a birationally liftable section has an adelic point that is integral outside a set of places of density 0, or is cuspidal; (ii) over totally real and imaginary quadratic fields, *birationally adelic* non-cuspidal sections come from rational points, where "adelic" asks integrality at almost all places on every open subcurve. Hypothesis (ii) assumes away the places of density 0, which are what is open. So conjecture b) does not by itself give Conjecture A; they are two statements. What b) does give: a birational lift makes the local points unconditional (the birational `p`-adic section theorem of Koenigsmann and Pop replaces the local theorem for that step), and it gives the classes of L2. - **L2** (manuscript reader; statement (G), the ghost at every puncture; hunch: true, and the arithmetic core of the main question).
