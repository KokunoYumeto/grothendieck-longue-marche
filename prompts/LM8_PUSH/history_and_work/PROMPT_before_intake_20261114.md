## Setting and notation (used in every Part)

- For a profinite group `G` and a prime `ℓ`, `G^(ℓ)` is its maximal pro-`ℓ` quotient. `Ẑ(1)`, `χ`, `χ_ℓ` are the Tate module of the roots of unity and the cyclotomic characters.
- **The CM curve (Parts A, G).** `K = Q(i)`, `E : y² = x³ − x`, `U = E ∖ {O}`. `π = π₁(U_K̄, v⃗)` is the full profinite geometric fundamental group (free profinite of rank two), `v⃗` the tangential base point of the parameter `x/y` at `O`; `Π` is the arithmetic fundamental group of `U` over `K`; `I ⊂ π` is the inertia group at `O` given by `v⃗`, with generator `c`; `P_ℓ = π^(ℓ)`, `P = P₅`; `Ω = ⋂_ℓ ker(π → P_ℓ)` (LM8 G.20 writes `R`). `Δ = Gal(K̄/K^ab)`; since `K` has class number one, `K^ab = K(E_tors)`. A section of the packet at `O` is a section `s_b` of `Π → G_K` whose image normalizes `I` (LM8 G.5). For an open subgroup `H ⊂ π`: `K_H` is a finite extension of `K` with `s_b(G_{K_H})` normalizing `H`, `Δ_H = Δ ∩ G_{K_H}`, `Y_H → U` the covering, `Ȳ_H` its smooth compactification, `S_H = Ȳ_H ∖ Y_H`, `J_H` the Jacobian of `Ȳ_H`.
- **The tripod (Parts B, C, D).** `F` is the free profinite group on `x, y`, with `z = (xy)⁻¹`. `𝒫` is the group of pure peripheral outer automorphisms of `F`: the classes of the automorphisms `φ_{u,f} : x ↦ x^u, y ↦ f⁻¹y^u f` (`u ∈ Ẑ^×`, `f ∈ F′`) that send `z` to a conjugate of `z^u` (LM8 A.13, A.19: this normal form is unique and is a group section); `u` is the multiplier, `𝒫₁` the subgroup `u = 1`. `𝔖₃ ⊂ Out(F)` is the geometric symmetric group. `GT̂₀` is the centralizer of `𝔖₃` in `𝒫`, the pairs `(λ, f)` with the 2-cycle and 3-cycle relations (Harbater–Schneps); `GT̂ ⊂ GT̂₀` adds the pentagon.
- **The metabelian quotient (Part B).** `M = F/[F′, F′]‾`, `A = M^ab ≅ Ẑ²` with basis `X, Y` (the images of `x, y`), `Z = (XY)⁻¹`, `Λ = Ẑ[[A]]`; `M′ = Λ·c₀` is free of rank one on `c₀ = [x, y]` (Chen–Deligne, Corollary 4.1.5, as used in LM8 A.22). An element of `𝒫₁` acts on `M′` by multiplication by a unit of `Λ`, its beta function.
- **The data of §47 (Part C)**, in the notation of LM8 B.25 and LM2 B.m1. `B = ⟨s, r | s² = r³ = 1⟩` and `B̂` its profinite completion; `p : B̂ → 𝔖₃` sends `s` to a transposition and `r` to a 3-cycle; `K₂ = ker p` is free profinite on `(rs)²` and `(sr)²`, and `ι : F → K₂` is the isomorphism `x ↦ (rs)², y ↦ (sr)²`; `φ : F → B̂` is the surjection `x ↦ s, y ↦ e := sr, z ↦ r⁻¹`; `N = ker(pφ)` is free profinite of rank seven (the sphere minus eight points), `d = φ|N : N → K₂` fills five of the eight punctures.
