## Setting and notation

- The tripod is `P¹_Q − {0, 1, ∞}` with coordinate `t`. For `n ≥ 2`, `X_n` is the plus Fermat curve `X^n + Y^n + Z^n = 0` over `Q`, `U_n = X_n − {XYZ = 0}`, and `U_n → tripod`, `t = −X^n/Z^n`, is Galois with group `μ_n²`; `N_n = π_1(U_{n, Q̄})` is the kernel of `π_1(tripod_{Q̄}) → (Z/n)²` (LM6 E.1). For odd `n` the rational cusps of `U_n` are `[0:1:−1]`, `[1:0:−1]`, `[1:−1:0]` (LM6 E.4).
- `C_n` is the classical curve `x^n + y^n = z^n` minus `{xyz = 0}`. For odd `n` it is isomorphic to `U_n` over `Q`; for even `n` it is not (the plus curve has no real point).
- A section of a curve `U/Q` is a continuous splitting of `π_1(U) → G_Q`, taken up to conjugation by `π_1(U_{Q̄})`. A section is *cuspidal* when it lies in a decomposition group of a rational cusp; the cuspidal sections at one cusp form its *packet*, a torsor under `H¹(Q, Ẑ(1))`. A section is *Selmer* in the sense of LM6 (the definition used in LM6 E.6–E.9; task A.1 fixes it): its localization at every place is the section of a local point or of a local cusp.
- *The local theorem* is Theorem 1.1 of the attached preprint of 6 October 2026 (the `p`-adic section conjecture); LM6 calls it Tool (1). It is unrefereed.
- For a profinite group `G`, `G^{(k)}` is the `k`-th closed derived subgroup (`G^{(1)} = G′`), `γ_k G` the lower central series, and `R_nil(G)` the intersection of the kernels of all continuous homomorphisms from `G` to finite nilpotent groups (LM7_PUSH D.50).
- In Part C the letters are those of LM7 Part D and of LM7_PUSH: `B = C_2 * C_3 = ⟨s, r⟩` is the modular group (not the tripod), `F ⊂ B` the free subgroup used there, `Q_576 = SL_2(Z/12)/±1`, of order 576, and `N = ker(B → Q_576)`, free of rank 97 (LM7 D.25; LM7 writes `Q` for `Q_576`). `GT̂₀` is the gentle Grothendieck–Teichmüller group (the hexagon-type relations without the pentagon), `GT̂ ⊂ GT̂₀` the full one, `λ` the multiplier, `ℋ = ker λ`, and `Δ : GT̂₀ → F₂` the CM parity of LM7_PUSH E.65. Take every definition from those returns; this list only fixes letters.
