# Archive typescripts transcribed over the API: state on 13 November 2026

This note is added to the folder on 13 November 2026. The folder itself went online on 12 November 2026 (commit
`9eb13d1`); `README.md` is the account written then. Nothing in the folder was changed or removed.

## What the folder is

- **Diplomatic transcriptions of typescripts in Grothendieck's Montpellier archive**, made from the scans, one page
  at a time, by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort high over the Anthropic Message Batches API on 12 November 2026:
  - *Pursuing Stacks* (cote 134): 680 pages, in `pursuing_stacks/`;
  - *Esquisse d'un programme* (cote 119, copy 1): 58 pages, in `esquisse/`;
  - "Nouveau EGA V" (cote 25) and EGA V, Blass's copy (cote 24): 3 pages each, in `ega_v_cote25/` and
    `ega_v_cote24_blass/`.
- **Each page was compared with an existing edition.** The model received the page images together with the matching
  passage of the edition as a draft. It transcribed the page, then listed every place where the draft differs from
  the page.
- **The difference tables**: `pursuing_stacks/DIFFERENCES.tsv` (12,140 rows) and `esquisse/DIFFERENCES.tsv` (327
  rows); `PS_ARXIV_DIFF.tsv` gives the *Pursuing Stacks* differences by file, line, section and paragraph of the
  arXiv source. `*/NOTES.tsv` lists the pages the model marked for a look at the scan.

## What was checked

- **Only the 28 pilot pages were checked by eye** (11 of *Pursuing Stacks*, 11 of the *Esquisse*, 6 of EGA V). The
  pilot's tables are in `pilot/`.
- **The transcriptions are not independent of the editions.** The model saw the edition's passage while it read the
  page. The pilot shows two places where it followed the edition against the ink.
- Every other page, and every row of the difference tables outside the pilot, is the model's reading and nobody has
  checked it.

## The scans

The page images are not copied here. The archive publishes the scans, one PDF per cote:

- *Pursuing Stacks*: `https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/134-1.pdf` to `134-8.pdf`;
- *Esquisse d'un programme*: `https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/119.pdf`;
- EGA V: `https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/24.pdf` and `https://grothendieck.umontpellier.fr/25.pdf`.

## The editions used as drafts

- *Pursuing Stacks*: the edition by Mateo Carmona with the collaboration of Ulrik Buchholtz, an extended version of
  the 2015 edition by "the scrivener"; arXiv:2111.01000, version 2, LaTeX source, dedicated to the public domain
  under CC0 1.0 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.01000v2).
- *Esquisse d'un programme*: the French text published by the Grothendieck Circle,
  https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/EsquisseFr.pdf.
