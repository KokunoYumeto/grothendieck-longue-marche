Witness coverage: none. These scans are §48.XI, 9°–10° (ms. pp. 539–541) and §48.XII "Rétrospective" (ms. pp.
542–548). Searches of the CSG draft, the IUT text and the Carmona–Kuenzer text for distinctive words of the package
("Rétrospective", "comme c'est beau", "poignet", "cafouill", "motivique", "tilp", "corps de classes",

at its p. 191. The Grothendieck Circle table gives "XII. Rétrospective … 542", which agrees with the upper number and the
heading of scan 479, and it dates the cross-references of these pages (§42.IV p. 335, §44 p. 356, §45 p. 384, §46.III
p. 396, §47 p. 415, §48.III–V pp. 443–454). Every reading below was settled on the ink alone, with enlarged and colour
crops in `second_pass/work_LM_C3-473-491`. The first-pass transcription of scans 447–472 was read only to identify the
equations and pages that these scans cite (p. 526' = scan 448, p. 535' = scan 466, p. 536' = scan 468, p. 538' = scan 471).
