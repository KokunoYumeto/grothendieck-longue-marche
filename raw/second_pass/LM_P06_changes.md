# LM_P06 (scans 51–60): second-pass changes

Second pass by Claude Opus 5.5 over `answers/fixed/LM_P06_transcription.tex`. Both halves of every scan were read
against the first pass, word by word and sign by sign; 25 enlarged crops (some rotated) are in
`second_pass/work_LM_P06`. The passage was collated with the CSG draft (lines ≈1203–1435), the IUT witness
(≈1290–1584), Carmona–Kuenzer (≈925–1035; it lacks cases I–II) and the author's table in the Circle file (line 22:
"8. Réflexion taxonomique . . . 25"). The ink decided every difference. Line numbers (L) are those of
`LM_P06_checked.tex`. Inside the table a `|` of `\unsure{a|b}` is written `\|`.

Readings that a witness contests but the ink confirms are listed under the witnesses' errors. Readings confirmed on
enlargement without a change include "se fait donc" (scan 55), "qu'on donne" (scan 56), the cancelled square
(scan 56), "ss-extension" (scan 57) and `\inserted{fini}` before "étale" (scan 59).
