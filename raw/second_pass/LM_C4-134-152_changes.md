# LM_C4-134-152 (scans 134–152): second-pass changes

I checked the first pass line by line against both halves of every scan (`second_pass\img_140-4\scan134…152_a/_b`).
Enlarged and rotated crops are in `second_pass\work_LM_C4-134-152` (files `v2_*.png`; the `s1xx_*.png` files there
are from an earlier, unfinished attempt and were not used).

Witness coverage: none. The CSG draft, the IUT text and the Carmona–Kuenzer text have no text of § 51, 13°–14°. I
searched for « triomphale », « contre toute attente », « jacobienne g », « Legendre », « pas trop grosse »,
« fibre type », « axiomati », « Brauer », « Fermat », « Hochschild » and « scindage »; every hit is in another
section. The author's table (`Grothendieck_Circle_tableLM.txt`, l. 257–265) gives only section titles and pages
(11° p. 695, 12° p. 700, 13° p. 707, 14° p. 720), which agree with the references on scans 134, 135, 139 and 146.
The ink alone decides.

the subscript of μ in 𝔖⁺/μ is the same small 𝔖 (its row 8); the category is 𝒳' (its scan 133).
