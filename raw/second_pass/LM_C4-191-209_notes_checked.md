## My own notes

- **M1** (scans 193–194; my own idea: counterexample to the expectation as stated). Without a hyperbolicity hypothesis
  the expectation of scans 193–194 is false. Over C, P^1 with one stacky point of order n ≥ 2 (teardrop), or with two of
  different orders m ≠ n (spindle), is a smooth, proper, separated, 0-connected multiplicity of dimension 1 whose π_1 is
  Z/gcd(m,n) (trivial for the teardrop), so no finite étale cover by a scheme exists (Thurston's bad orbifolds; algebraic
  treatment in Behrend–Noohi, « Uniformization of Deligne–Mumford curves », 2006). For hyperbolic orbifold curves the
  cover exists (Fuchsian groups have torsion-free subgroups of finite index), and these are all the cases with anabelian
  loop groups.
- **M2** (scans 199–200; my own idea: proof). (21)–(22) hold for every field K and every homomorphism u, continuous or
  not, which explains the struck « bon » and « de car. 0 » (row 16). χ(N(L)) is open in Ẑ*, so it contains 1 + NẐ and
  hence an integer m ≥ 2; for g ∈ N(L) with χ(g) = m and any λ ∈ Ẑ, gℓ^λg⁻¹ = (ℓ^λ)^m is an ordinary power, so
  u(ℓ^λ) is conjugate to its m-th power. Its eigenvalues (in a faithful representation of dimension d) are then permuted
  by x ↦ x^m, so they are roots of unity of order dividing e = ∏_{k≤d}(m^k − 1), and u(eL) consists of unipotent
  elements, with eL open in L.
- **M3** (scan 196; my note, implication not pursued). The 2-categorical difficulty disappears for multiplicities with
  trivial generic stabiliser: between two morphisms of such objects there is at most one 2-morphism, so the fibred
  2-category is equivalent to a category fibred in groupoids (as Abramovich and Vistoli, 2002, show for twisted stable
  maps). It is genuine when the generic stabiliser is non-trivial, e.g. when the finite group of (9) acts on U
  non-faithfully, giving a gerbe over an orbifold curve.
- **M4** (scans 205–206; my note, implication not pursued). The refusal to require 𝒢 ≅ (𝒢_0)^ and 𝒯 ≅ (𝒯_0)^ leaves
  room for congruence kernels. In the ambient example (7) of scan 191, 𝓜 ⊂ Aut_L(Π̂), the closure of the discrete
  Teichmüller group is its congruence completion, and the kernel of the profinite completion of 𝒯_0 onto 𝒯 is the
  congruence kernel of the mapping class group, known to be trivial only in low genus (g ≤ 2) and open in general. With
  𝓜 the fundamental group of the universal curve over Q (case b)), 𝒢 is the full profinite completion.
- **M5** (scans 206–207; my note, consistency of the corrected reading). « Groupe de Teichmüller "direct" » is the
  author's term from scan 187: L-automorphisms acting directly, i.e. trivially on the orientation module T. Hypothesis
  (33), χ trivial on 𝒢 so that Π = Π^+ and 𝒢 = 𝒢^+, says exactly that 𝒯_0 = 𝒢_0/Π_0 acts directly. The corrected word
  (row 25) therefore fits the mathematics, and « absolu » did not.
- **M6** (scan 207; my note, implication not pursued). With « convenable » instead of « universelle » (row 29), case b)
  covers subgroups of finite index of the Teichmüller group (insertion on scan 206), i.e. modular multiplicities with
  level structure. Their geometric components may be defined only over an extension of Q (for full level n, over
  Q(μ_n)), which bears on the identification of Γ with Γ_{Q̄/Q} in the same sentence.
