## Witnesses' own errors noticed

- Grothendieck Circle table, §46.III (p. 396): "Calculs dans GL(2, Zˆ) et dans GL(2, Zˆ)"; the ink (scan 194) has
  "Gl(2,Ẑ)′ et dans Gl(2,Ẑ)": the prime of the first group is lost (and Gl normalised), which makes the title repeat
  itself.
- Grothendieck Circle table, §46.IV (p. 402): "Les trois cas fondamentaux et les relèvements K1/2, K2, K−1; …"; the ink
  (scan 206) has "Les trois cas particuliers fondamentaux." A later rewording, not an error, but "particuliers" is gone.
- Grothendieck Circle table, §48.II (p. 434, outside this package): "L'équation ζ = η ℓ1(μ − 1)" writes ζ for the
  letter that its §46.VI and these scans write ξ (defined in (41)); probably a misreading of his ξ.
