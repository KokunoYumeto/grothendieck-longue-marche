  αρ(α)⁻¹ = βσ(β)⁻¹ε₁(μ−1): the "c" is spurious and ε is lost (probably extraction), and α⁻¹ is placed inside the
  parenthesis. The heading on scan 285 itself reads only "III) Étude de l'équation (Suite)": the table's title is a later
  wording, not an error.
- Grothendieck Circle table, §48.IV (p. 446): "Un épinglage canonique associé à ρ, σ"; the ink (scan 290) has "Un
  épinglage canonique". A later expansion, not an error.
