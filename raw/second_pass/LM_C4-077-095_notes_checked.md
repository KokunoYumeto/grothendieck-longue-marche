## First-pass notes, scans 77–81

1. **Scan 77, implication not pursued — keep.** The author checks that N̲_ρ → 𝕆₁₂* is epi by giving, for each local section μ̲, the element v = σ^{ν₃}δ^{−ν″}u_μ̲ ∈ ΓN̲_ρ with χ̲(v) = μ̲ ((154)). This is a local lift, not a homomorphic section; the page does not check how these lifts multiply.

2. **Scan 78, claim without proof — correct.** The ink reads « nul au dessus de Spec ℤ ∖ {3}, mais de dim 1 en dim 3 », a slip for « en car. 3 ». If ρ_M̲ acts on the rank-2 module M̲ with characteristic polynomial X²+X+1, then det(1−ρ_M̲) = 3. Hence 1−ρ_M̲ is injective where 3 is invertible, and its kernel is a line over 𝔽₃. That argument justifies the claim, but it is not on the page.

## Claude's notes (new)

C1. **Scans 77, 82, 94 — implication not pursued.** (154) gives the lift v = σ^{ν₃}δ^{−ν″}u_μ̲, and the repeated (212) gives the fibre of N̲_ρ^? over μ̲ as {ω^i v | i ∈ Γ{0,1}_{S₀}}. The ambiguity ω^i is therefore exactly the kernel μ_ρ = {1, ω}_{S₀} of (172). The page does not state this check of the bi-section claim, which is immediate from the two formulas.

C2. **Scan 78 (158) — Claude's observation.** The condition ρ_M̲x = x is equivalent to (1−ρ_M̲)x = 0, but the ink's sign after « (1 » is a hooked stroke, not a clean minus. If it is a plus, it is a slip; nothing later depends on it, since (159) writes (1−ρ_M̲).

C3. **Scan 81 — Claude's observation.** The Proposition's criterion in terms of μ̲(x) agrees with (154), where χ̲(v) = μ̲. For x ∈ ΓG̲^♮_ρ,σ, μ̲(x) is χ̲(x), so the change of letter does not change the statement.

C4. **Scans 78–79 — question.** The kernel Z̲_ρ^0 = Z̲_ρ ∩ M̲ of (158) has a fibre of dimension 1 at 3. The improved N̲_ρ is the schematic closure of the part over S₀ − {3}. The page leaves open whether this closure is flat over ℤ₍₃₎ and what its fibre at 3 is: it states only the exact sequence (159) with kernel L̲_ρ.

C5. **Scans 84 and 89 — Claude's observation.** The signs joining 𝔾_a with SZ̲_σ^♮ (scan 84) and, probably, M̲(½)/μ_ρ with 𝕆₁₂*·𝔾_a ((204), scan 89) are vertical ≃, not equalities. The identification with 𝔾_a depends on the chosen generator λ₀+λ₁ of the σ-fixed line (178), so it is canonical only up to sign. The action through χ in (181) is the scalar action on that line.

C6. **Scan 89 (204) — question.** The graph λ ↦ (λ, λ(λ₁+λ₂)) puts the M̲-coordinate on the line spanned by λ₁+λ₂. But (178) gives the σ-fixed line SZ̲_σ^♮ = {a(λ₀+λ₁)}, and M̲(½) is cut out by U = b with b ∈ N̲_σ^♮. Unless the coordinates of (204) are permuted relative to (178), one expects λ₀+λ₁. The ink reads λ₁+λ₂ in both places on the page; check against the definitions of λ₀, λ₁, λ₂ and of σ_M̲ in (150)–(151).

C7. **Scan 91 — question.** The group in « d'indice 2 dans Γ̲′_ρ,σ » carries a prime but no tilde. The second (209) defines Γ′~ = Ker(Γ̲~ → μ) through ε₂, so index 2 would be expected in Γ̲~_ρ,σ. Either the prime marks a different group or the tilde was left out.

C8. **Scans 92 and 95 — implication pursued later.** On scan 92 the author proposes to replace the ~ of Γ_ℚ~ by « ét » (« étendu ») and writes M = N_{1,1}~. In (217) on scan 95 he already writes M = N_{1,1}^{ét}: the proposed notation is adopted three pages later.

C9. **Scan 95 (216) — Claude's observation.** Z_ρ,σ = Ẑ*·L̂₀ with L̂₀ ≃ Ẑ·λ₀ uses the line of λ₀, the completed L₀^M̲ = Ker(Z̲_ρ,σ → 𝕆₁₂*) of scan 85. SZ̲_σ^♮ ≃ 𝔾_a uses the line of λ₀+λ₁. These are two different 𝔾_a-factors, which the page does not relate.
