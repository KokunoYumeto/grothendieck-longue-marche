- **B1 — scan 2, implication (an author's slip in the c₀ line).** With the page's sign −, the bottom-right determinant is det a̲ = (μ/δ_α)²(d² − cdw/v − c²v₁/v). - **B2 — scan 6, claim checked (why σ̃ and σ̃^* agree).** σ̃² = ω̃₀ is central, so conjugation by σ̃ and by σ̃⁻¹ induce the same automorphism of Π̃ₙᴰ. This is the content of "σ̃(ρ̃₀) = σ̃*(ρ̃₀) = ρ̃σ̃⁻¹ρ̃σ̃". Whether the heavy superscript is ∗ or −1 therefore does not affect any formula where it marks an action. It matters only inside the product ρ̃₀⁻¹σ̃(ρ̃₁) = ρ̃⁻¹σ̃ρ̃⁻¹σ̃⁻¹σ̃⁻¹ρ̃σ̃ρ̃, where a plain σ̃ is needed.

- **B4 — scans 8 and 16, correction of the stated reason for non-splitting.** In D′₃ = ⟨ρ,σ | ρ³ = σ², σ⁴ = 1, σρσ⁻¹ = ρ⁻¹⟩, the dicyclic group of order 12, the lifts of ρ_{D₃} are ρ (order 6) and ρω = ρ⁴ (order 3). - **B5 — scan 10, the guess E ≃ Sℓ(2,Z/6Z)/H cannot hold (claim refuted).** E maps onto Z/12Z. This follows from the cartesian square and from the page's map ρ ↦ 10, σ ↦ 3, since 3 + 10 ≡ 1 mod 12. This fits the author's "à déterminer" and the later switch (scan 15) to the comparison of orders 72 and 6·24.
