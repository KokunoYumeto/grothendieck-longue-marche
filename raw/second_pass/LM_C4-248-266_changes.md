# LM_C4-248-266 (scans 248–266): second-pass changes

Checked against the archive images (`second_pass/img_140-4/scan248…266`, both halves of every scan; enlarged and
rotated crops in `second_pass/work_LM_C4-248-266`, files `m248.png` … `m266.png`, `m_misc.png`, `m_T.png`).
No witness covers these scans (ms. pp. 772–780, § 53, 5): the CSG draft, the IUT text and the Carmona–Kuenzer text
stop long before § 53 (searched for "surfaces ponctuées", "demi-tangente", "épaississement", "germe d'arc",
"germes d'arcs", "Revuni", "groupoïde enveloppant", "module d'orientation", "cônes fermés", "germe résiduel",
"multigaloisien", "Digression"). Only the author's table (Grothendieck Circle) lists § 53, 5) "Digression sur les
revêtements des germes d'espaces, et relations entre germes topologiques et germes différentiables des surfaces"
at p. 768, which matches the content (B) surfaces ponctuées, C) germes d'arcs, D) structure différentielle,
E) axiomatisation). The ink alone decides.

## Places still unresolved

- 248: the struck word at the top (`Bref.`?); the struck layer in "le germe D*ₓ ~~…~~ admet une "base"" (a struck
  "est" and a second struck word, possibly "constant"); the inserted word read "provenant" (a short word with a
  bracket-like initial; "induits" or another participle possible).
- 248: "de \unsure{groupoïde|groupe} fondamental T̄ₓ": the ink has the same "groupoïde" as the line above (diaeresis,
  ending); the sense requires "groupe". Kept as in the first pass.
- 249: the digit before "-connexe" (row 1): 1 written over 0 is the preferred reading, not certain.
- 250: the quoted phrase `"chem. de \unsure{domin.}"` (third word may be "chemins"); "de D*~ₓ/U \unsure{à|:} Ũ/U";
  in the NB the connective read "alors" (ink also fits "donc"), and the short line under "Ũ = Revuni(U, z)" ("i.e.
  que …"?) is not read.
- 251: (63) subscript of π (z | s); "quand on \unsure{change|compose} (62)": a straight stroke runs through the
  middle of the word and may be a deletion, which would leave the clause without its verb; under π₁(U) in (65)
  the vertical sign is drawn differently from the ≀ under π₁(D*ₓ) (kept as ≃); a vertical stroke stands in the
  right margin of (65).
- 251: margin C) "(\unsure{jouant} le rôle \deleted{origine} de "pts base")": the words are compressed.
- 252: "germe d'arc de courbe \deleted{de} X sortant de x": the struck "de" leaves no preposition before X.
- 254–255, 262: the category label `\unsure{Germarc|Courarc}` (initial C- or G-shaped; on scan 262 written
  "germesdiff").
- 256: "c'est un ensemble ordonné, \unsure{et on|donc}"; the word carries a crossbar that may be a strike.
- 259: "Soit \unsure{C𝒜ₓ|C ∈ 𝒞𝒜ₓ} l'un de ces cônes": the ink has "l'un" and a single bold C before 𝒜ₓ, while the
  next lines use 𝒞𝒜ₓ for the set of cones; the heavy sign before "C strictement" (kept as deleted ∈).
- 260: `\unsure{simétriques|symétriques}`; the separator "V∩(C'∖{0}) \unsure{, où|:} V".
- 262: in (93) the second argument of Revuni(𝕊ₓ, ·) is a short dash (kept as ·); "la construction de \unsure{ce|---} Θ".
- 263: "Soit maintenant [u] \unsure{un} un": two short groups follow the insertion mark; the word over the struck
  "difféomorphisme" ends "-morphisme" (automorphisme or homéomorphisme).
- 264: margin heading "\unsure{la structure|structure}"; the struck symbols under Ep(A), Ep(C).
- 265: Ep versus E_p throughout; the scribble over the parenthesis in Γ(Ep(Ã), …); the connector in (99) (a dash,
  no arrowhead); the label of the left arrow of (102) (ρ | γ; the glyph is r-like).
- 266: "\unsure{constant}" (a stroke through the word may be a deletion); "T̄ₓ-\unsure{torseurs|foncteurs}".
- Passim: the bar over T in T̄ₓ (orientation module) is kept as in the first pass. Crops (`m_T.png`) show a long
  stroke over T and its subscript, much longer than the crossbar of the T of "Tang"; it may be an overline or only
  the author's long crossbar. To be settled once for the manuscript.
