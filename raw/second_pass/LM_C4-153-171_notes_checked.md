## My own notes (Claude, second pass)

- **M1**: scans 160–161; consequence not pursued (own, checked). The Kummer parameter in the Fermat case is −1/2
  (scan 161: « 1 ↦ −1/2 »). Here √(−1/2) = i/√2 lies in Q(μ₈). (−1/2)^{1/4} is not in Q^ab: since (1+i)⁴ = −4, that
  would force 2^{1/4} into Q^ab. For odd ℓ, (−1/2)^{1/ℓ} is not in Q^ab. So the image of Γ_Q⁺ in Ẑ(1) is 2Z₂ ×
  ∏_{ℓ odd} Z_ℓ, and the image of Γ_Q under (329) = (219) = (326) in Ẑ*·Ẑ has index exactly 2: the hom is not
  surjective, which answers the question « voir s'il est surjectif » attached to (219). Confidence: high for the field
  theory; medium for identifying (329) with this Kummer action, which is the author's own expectation on scan 160.
- **M2**: scan 168; answer to question a) (own, checked). For every prime p, √p lies in Q(μ_{4p}) (Gauss sums; √2 in
  Q(μ₈)), so ζ_{p,2}(Γ_Q⁺) ⊂ 2Z₂. Since p^{1/4} ∉ Q^ab the image is exactly 2Z₂, and since p^{1/ℓ} ∉ Q^ab ζ_{p,ℓ} is
  surjective for every odd ℓ. So ζ_p is never surjective; its image is open, of index 2. Confidence: high.
- **M3**: scan 168; answer to question b) (own, checked). For distinct p₁ … p_n the joint map has image
  (2Z₂)ⁿ × ∏_{ℓ odd} Z_ℓⁿ, open of index 2ⁿ: so « linéairement indépendants » holds. Kummer theory over Q^ab reduces
  this to showing that no ∏ p_i^{a_i} with some a_i ≢ 0 (mod ℓ), resp. some a_i odd, has an ℓ-th, resp. 4th, root in
  Q^ab. That follows from Schinzel's theorem (x^m − c has abelian splitting field over Q iff c² = b^m, b ∈ Q).
  Confidence: high for the computation.
- **M4**: scan 167; answer to the question (own, checked). Take S₁ = A¹ over Z[1/p]: it is normal, 0-connected, of
  finite type over Z and of dimension 2, with A = H⁰(S₁, G_m) = {±1} × p^Z, S = A¹_Q and Π₁ = Γ_Q. The image of
  Π₁⁺ in Hom(A, T) ≃ Ẑ is then the index-2 subgroup of M2, so the hom is not necessarily surjective. G_m(S)/A =
  Q*/⟨−1, p⟩ is torsion-free, so the criterion « G_m(S)/Im A sans torsion » is not sufficient: the condition has to
  be imposed over K(μ∞), not over K.
