## Witnesses' own errors noticed

No text witness reaches these pages, so none can be collated here. Two remarks on what the witnesses do contain:

- Grothendieck Circle table (§49.III): the entry "III. Passages de M̃0,3 à Ñ1,1 et de GL(2,Z)^/{±1} à GL(2,Z)^" has no page number in this copy; the ink starts section III on p. 565 (scan 524). The table writes GL where the ink has Gl (normalisation, not an error).
- Carmona–Kuenzer: the §49 heading reads "Les groupes ρ,σ généraux" (and "49. Les groupes ρ,σ généraux . . . 204" in its contents): the M of M_ρ,σ is lost; the Circle table has "Les groupes Mρ,σ généraux".
