# LM_C3-682-696: second-pass changes

Scans 682–696 (upper manuscript pagination 644–650, lower foliation 681–695): §49 VI, "8°) Retour sur le cas universel" and "9°) Digression de formalisme des groupes".

Witness coverage: none. The four witnesses were searched (distinctive words: "cas universel", "formalisme des groupes", "somme amalgamée", "tout petit", "ci-devant", "valeur commune", "reconstituer", Norm/Centr, ρ,σ). The CSG draft and the IUT text end hundreds of manuscript pages earlier (their last items are (80) on edition pp. 300 and 317). The Carmona–Kuenzer text stops at the heading of §49, and the Circle table gives only "VI. Les groupes Mρ,σ généraux: récapitulation et raffinement de la construction précédente" (628) and §50 (650), with no entry for 8° or 9°. Every reading below was therefore settled on the ink alone, from the two halves of each scan and enlarged crops in `work_LM_C3-682-696`.

## Places still unresolved

1. 682 and throughout: the recurring angular superscript (kept as `\unsure{\natural|\sharp}`) and the alphabet of the curled group letter (kept as `\unsure{\mathcal G}`). The glyphs are consistent across the package, but their identity is not settled by these pages.
2. 682, NB margin: the relation in "M♮ ⊃ μ" may be ∋.
3. 682, (107) d): the ink reads "u stabilise M♮" (an M-shaped letter). The definition is then circular, and (109) d) has "stabilise 𝒢₀♮", so 𝒢♮ may have been meant. The reading is kept.
4. 683, (108): the mark under Z_{ρ0,σ0} (cancelled ♮/♯ sign?).
5. 683: the sign between π and Π₀ ("π := Π₀").
6. 683, (109): the subscript of 𝒢♮ and of Π₀ (τ̄?), and the sign after u in d) ("u ε/∈ stabilise").
7. 683: the insertion read "qu'on exige" ("quitte à" is not excluded), the cancelled "Bref", "s/ss groupe", and the first sign of the insertion "= via M̃^!_{0,3}[0]".
8. 684: the superscripts in "(110*)", in the label "(111^?)" (which resembles the superscript of M[0] in the same display), M[0]^{!|♮}, and the ⊕-like superscript of L₀ in (111) and in "L₀♮ ⊂ L₀^…" (kept as 𝒢).
9. 685: the left vertical label "1" in (112), and the μ in "Z_{ρ0,σ0}/L₀^μ". The ink looks like μ, while (113) has L₀♮ at the same place.
10. 685: a short group "cnt" at the start of the line "Z_{ρ0,σ0} ∩ 𝒢₀⁺ …" is not transcribed. It may be a stray abbreviation or the end of a divided "ét-ant".
11. 686: "donc|donne", "≃|=" and "≡|=" in the two N♮ definitions, "≠|=" in (118) (the sense and (116) give =), and the cancelled target in (119).
12. 687: the last factor of (121) (G or M), the clipped "et" of the margin, and the superscript "?|!".
13. 688: N_ρ or N_{ρ0} in (122), μ_{τ1|τ′} in (123), and the margin word "indépendant|indépendamment".
14. 690: the index of u₀ in "u₀ ∈ M♮", "tour|tours de Fermat", and "générales".
15. 691, (3) a): "induit par celle de G|𝒢". The ink shows a G-like letter, but the sense requires N (the action Θ restricted to SN). Also unresolved are the cancelled "Il est / Je vois" before "clair".
16. 692: "(Ch direct)", and "l'image de (4)". The ink resembles a 4; the sense requires the hom (5), and the label (5) itself is written over an earlier numeral.
17. 695: "via|ici", and the function of the x-marks.
18. 696: the retraced reference "(11)".

## Witnesses' own errors noticed

None can be collated: no witness transcribes scans 682–696. The Circle table lists no subsection of §49 VI. Its page figures (VI at 628, §50 at 650) fit the upper pagination of these scans, on which the 9° digression still occupies pp. 648–650.
