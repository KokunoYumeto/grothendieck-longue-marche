## Readings settled across the block

- **"standart".** As on scans 003–017, every occurrence ends in his crossed *t*, distinct from the looped *d* inside the
  word. The first pass wrote "standard" without doubt on 018 and `\unsure{standard|standart}` elsewhere; all are now
  "standart" (rows 1, 3, 5, 9, 19, 26, 31, 32).
- **"=" for "même".** In "il en soit de = des" (020), "Il en est donc de = de" (022), "(= sur une base 𝒮 connexe" (023) and
  "En = temps" (031) the sign is two horizontal strokes, the upper one at most slightly arched; there is no *m* and no bar
  over a letter. The first pass offered `\unsure{=|\overline m}`; the ink is "=" (rows 6, 10, 13, 37). The sense is "même".
- **Upright M and script 𝓜.** On 022, 025, 026, 029 and 031 the moduli letters are often written as an upright M, elsewhere
  as a curly script 𝓜. Both forms occur for the same object in one sentence (026: upright M̂_{g,ν} in (26), script 𝓜̂_{g,ν}
  in the next line), so the difference is handwriting, not notation. The first pass's `\mathcal M` throughout is kept.
