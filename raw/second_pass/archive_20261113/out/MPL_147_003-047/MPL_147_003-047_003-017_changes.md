## A recurring reading: "standart"

The first pass wrote "standard" without doubt on the contents page (scan 003) and `\unsure{standard|standart}` at every later
occurrence. In the enlargements (s003_c1, s004_c1, s008_c2, s_multi2, s017_c2) the word ends in a crossed vertical, i.e. his *t*:
the *d* inside the same word is drawn as a loop with a left-curving ascender, and the final letter has a different form and a
crossbar. Every occurrence on scans 003–017 was checked in the half images; all show the same ending. The spelling is therefore
"standart" throughout (rows 1, 2, 11, 12, 16, 18–20, 25–27, 34, 35, 37, 40, 41, 44, 48–50). On scan 017 the word is struck and
rewritten above with the same spelling (rows 48–49); the reason for the rewriting is not visible.
