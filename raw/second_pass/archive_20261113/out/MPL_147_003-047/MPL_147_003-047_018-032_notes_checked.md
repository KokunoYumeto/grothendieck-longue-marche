**C01 — scan 028 (with 018, 027), unpursued implication.** With the corrected 30), the chain is consistent: ν̂_α = ν_α + μ̃_α
("poids total" on 018 = "poids marqué" + "ordre" on 027), Σ_α ν̂_α = ν + 2s₁, and h₁ = s₁ − s₀ + 1 for the connected G assumed
on 020, so μ(G) = 3(g−1) + ν − s₁ with g = Σg_α + h₁, and the codimension is s₁ (29). He does not draw the consequence for
divisors: the codimension-one strata are exactly the one-edge MD-graphs, namely one vertex of genus g−1 with a loop and the ν
marks (stable when 2g + ν ≥ 3 and g ≥ 1), or two vertices of types (g₁,ν₁), (g₂,ν₂) joined by one edge, g₁ + g₂ = g,
ν₁ + ν₂ = ν, 2g_i + ν_i ≥ 2. With the unnumbered (étale) marking of 024 the second kind is indexed by unordered pairs
{(g₁,ν₁),(g₂,ν₂)}. Confidence high: direct from (29) and the stability of each vertex, 2g_i + (ν_i + 1) ≥ 3.

l'adhérence de 𝓜_[G]": the map M̂_[G] → 𝓜̂_{g,ν} is proper and quasi-finite, hence finite, and unramified (the "imm. locale"
of the margin on 030). Over the open 𝓜_[G] it is the immersion (25), and the preimage of 𝓜_[G] is 𝓜_[G], because points with
new nodes go to strata with more edges. 𝓜_G is dense fibre by fibre in M̂_G = ∏𝓜̂_{g_α,Î_α} (each factor by the theorem on

normalization of the closure. His "explicitation" holds up to normalization, which N049–N050 show cannot be dropped.
Confidence high for the argument (a finite birational morphism from a normal source is the normalization of its image);
medium for writing it out for algebraic stacks.
