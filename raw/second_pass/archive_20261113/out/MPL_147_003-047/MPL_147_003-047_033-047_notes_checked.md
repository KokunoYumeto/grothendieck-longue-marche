## Second-pass notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

**S1 — scan 034, item 5°), with 040 c) and 047. Kind: unpursued implication (reading of the definition).** In 5°) the condition that G(α) be an MD-graph is written only for vertices "de genre 0" ("i.e. tel que γ_α(β)=0, on ait card I_{α,β} + card Ã_{α,β} ≥ 3"). The Mumford-Deligne condition also asks each genus-1 vertex to carry at least one special point, and it counts the half-edges internal to G(α). The later formulation covers both: 040 c) speaks of "les conditions de rigidité relatives aux sommets β tels que g'_β ∈ {0,1}", and 047 writes 2g'_β + (card I'_β + card B̃'_β) + card C̃'_β ≥ 3. A reader should take 040 and 047 as the definition and 034 as a shortened statement. Confidence high: direct comparison of the three passages.

**S3 — scan 040 (margin and struck block) and scan 047. Kind: claim stated without proof, proved later.** The margin of 040 says that the rigidity condition on the fibre maquette G̲'(α) is "aut. assur." and "équiv à : 2g'_β + card I'_β + card B̃'_β ... poids ≥ 3". The struck block below first called the fibre condition "a priori ... une condition plus faible (puisque G̲' est un MD-graphe)". 047 settles it: since Ã' = B̃' ∐ C̃', the condition at β in the fibre is the MD condition on G̲' at β, term by term. The same identity gives the converse, so the two conditions are equivalent vertex by vertex, not only one implied by the other. Confidence high: the equivalence is the displayed identity on 047.
