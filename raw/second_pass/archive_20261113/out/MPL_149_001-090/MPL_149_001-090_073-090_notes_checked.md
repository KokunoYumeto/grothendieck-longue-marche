## Notes added in the second pass (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

**C1 — scans 079, 080, 085, 090 — implication not pursued.** With the corrected reading on 079, the cuspidal splittings
have a name in the manuscript: "les sections de 2ième espèce de l'extension 1−π−E_U−E_S−1 associées à i=i_0", each
defining the π-conjugacy class of the inertia subgroup L_{i0}. On 080 they come from the normalizer extension (13),
whose class c_i (14) is asserted on 081 to be the image of the conormal class γ_i. The question on 085, how "une section
d'extensions du type (1)" (from a rational point) "peut se spécialiser en une section du type (2)", is the subject of
086–090. The manuscript stops at 090 with the decomposition group N_{s′}≃E_K̃ placed inside N(L_i) with index μ. It does
not draw the evident consequence: when μ=1 (f and g_{i0} meet transversally at s), the image of E_K̃ is the whole of
N(L_i). In general it is a subgroup of index μ, so the specialization lands among the second-kind data only up to that
index. This uses only the index statement on 090 and its target as written (E_{X_s}; see D57).

**C2 — scan 078 — reading question.** "car ν est la longueur du schéma artinien local" has ν in the ink, the same glyph as
in "ν=1" and in the margin. The length was named μ on 077, and ν is card I (076). The slip is kept diplomatically. Anyone
using 078 must read this ν as μ and not as the number of boundary sections.

**C3 — scan 076 — reading question.** With the section letter read as g (changes file), the inserted hypothesis "Si g est
le genre relatif, ν=card I, on suppose (g,ν)≠(0,1)" uses g for the genus on the same line as the sections g_i. The
insertion was written later than the sections, so the clash is the author's. The first pass's q reading had hidden it.

**C4 — scans 077–078, 082 — reading question.** The ideal letter 𝒥 (𝒥_{g_{i0}}, 𝒥_f; J_i/J_i² on 082) is the letterform
of the generalized Jacobians 𝒥¹_{X/S} and 𝒥⁰_{U/S} on 076–077. In 082, "base de J_i/J_i²" is a trivialization of the
conormal line of the section g_i, and its class is γ_i ∈ Pic(S) (081). The obstruction "γ_i=0" and the
G_m(S)-indeterminacy of the base on 082 are two statements about the same line bundle.

**C5 — scan 086 — reading question.** "avec une g_i=g_{i0} section de T sur I" has a capital I in the ink (top and bottom
bars), not S. Since T≃I_S (076), "section de T sur S" is meant. The slip is kept as written.
