occurrences of the same letter on a page, the number of occurrences is given.

Two readings recur across the block and were decided by comparing letterforms on the same pages:

- **Section letter g, not q.** The section letters (`\unsure{q|g}`, `\unsure{g|q}` in the first pass) have a closed bowl
  and a descender that turns or loops to the left. His q (in "que", "qu'il", "quand", on the same pages) has a straight
  descender with no hook. Clearest on 077 (crop `c077`), then 076, 078, 079, 081, 082 and 086 (crop `c086`).
- **Ideal letter 𝒥, not ℐ.** The ideal letter on 077, 078 and 082 has a top bar and a stem that runs below the line
  and curls left. That is the same letterform as the Jacobian 𝒥 on 076–077. His I ("card I", "i∈I", "I_x") is a
  straight stem with a top and a bottom bar.
