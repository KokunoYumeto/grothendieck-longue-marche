## Own notes (second pass, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

**O037.1 — scan 037 — reading question.** The base field is "K alg. clos" in the lemma, but the points are written with
a lowercase k: "u ∈ J°_Y(k)", "J°_Y(k)", "(G_m^I/G_m)(k)", "λ_i ∈ k^*", and "à k par les λ_i" on 038. The two
letters are transcribed as written; whether they name one field is not decided by these pages.

**O038.1 — scans 037–038 — unproved claim.** The cases are split as follows. On 037 the vanishing of the image of u
in the abelian part is obtained "au moins si g≠1". On 038 the struck qualifier "(pour un genre g≠1)" is replaced by
"(pour un g≥1)", attached to the step "x = y". The positive-genus argument needs both conditions, so it covers g ≥ 2.
g = 0 is treated separately on 038, and g = 1 is left to "l'argument homologique" (margin of 037; "ces arguments ne
marchent que pour g≠1", 038). This agrees with B037.3, whose counterexample has genus 1.

**O038.2 — scan 038 — own idea.** The margin "Cependant, on ne sait pas que l'image de u dans J°_{Ŷ∖J} est nulle!"
can be made concrete. For g = 0 and |J| = 2, J°_{Ŷ∖J} ≅ G_m^J/G_m ≅ G_m, so the vanishing needed is that of one
cross-ratio-type coordinate of u ∈ G_m^I/G_m. Nothing on 037–038 forces that coordinate to be 1, and the inserted
vanishing in J°_Ŷ says nothing in genus 0, where J°_Ŷ = 0. Confidence: high. The generalized Jacobian of P^1 with a reduced modulus of two
points is G_m, and the coordinate is a projection of the torus G_m^I/G_m.

**O038.3 — scans 038–039 — reading question.** The second pass reads "Considérons les deux ~~applications~~ morphismes
U →^i Y, \unsure{inclusion}, et j: U − Y induit par τ_u" (first pass: "projections/applications … don-"). With this
reading i is named as the inclusion, which the comparison H_1(i) = H_1(j) on 039 uses.

**O045.1 — scan 045 — reading question.** See B045.4. The open circles elsewhere on the page and the filled blot on
the domain of the composite are different marks. A cancelled circle is the most economical reading, but it is not
proved by the ink.

**O046.1 — scans 042, 046 — reading question.** "dont on \unsure{décrète} d'avance" on 046 repeats the wording of 042,
"dont on décrète d'avance que l'image soit ouverte". In both places the restriction on the homomorphisms (open image
on 042; compatibility with the model filtrations on 046) is imposed as part of the definition of the morphisms
considered, not derived. This supports B042.2 and B046.2.

**O048.1 — scans 048, 050 — reading question.** The Conjecture fondamentale assumes "V séparés réguliers". The same
hypotheses return on 050 for the pro-scheme V ("un schéma lim de schémas séparés réguliers de t.f. sur Q"). The first
pass's "V supposé" carried no hypothesis.

**O049.1 — scan 049 — reading question.** The bracketed insertion "(\unsure{complètes} pour U)" lies between "hom.
compatibles aux filtrations modéliques," and "Plus généralement, prenant des schémas,". If it belongs to the line
above, the condition is on the filtrations ("filtrations modéliques complètes pour U"). If it belongs to the line
below, it qualifies the pro-schemes. The ink shows no caret that decides between them.

**O050.1 — scans 049–050 — unpursued implication.** Both pro-objects have open immersions as transition morphisms:
"des immersions ouvertes affines" for U on 049, and "imm. ouvertes affines" for V on 050 (the insertion stands before
"affines"). His example fits this: "V = les spectres d'anneaux locaux réguliers, ess. de type fini sur Q" (and the
struck "Spec(O_{X,x})" for U). Spec O_{X,x} is the limit of the affine open neighbourhoods of x, with open immersions as
transition maps. Confidence: high, because this is the standard limit description of a local scheme.

**O052.1 — scan 052 — own idea.** With the reading "la structure à lacets", the test reads: "tout autom ext. de E_K
… qui respecte la structure à lacets et qui commute à l'action de Γ, provient d'un autom de X". If E_K is the absolute
Galois group of the function field K of X over a number field k, the relative birational theorem cited by the first
pass in B044.1 (Mochizuki 1999, Theorem B) would give the "fait" for outer automorphisms over Γ without the lacet
hypothesis. k-automorphisms of K are the automorphisms of the normalization of X, which are those of X when X is
smooth. Confidence: medium. The theorem's exact hypotheses (a regular function field over a sub-p-adic field,
conjugation by the geometric subgroup) were not re-checked here, and the manuscript does not say that X is smooth.

**O052.2 — scan 052 — reading question.** In the parenthesis on 1-fidélité a small "ég" (or "f g") over an open
bracket stands between "soient" and "isomorphes". It is now transcribed `\unsure{ég|$fg$}`. It may be an abandoned
"égaux"; the sentence ends "sont égaux".

**O053.1 — scans 053–054 — unproved claim.** The section construction at a point at infinity needs i to be rational
over K ("au moins si i est /K i.e. \unsure{fixé} par E_{K̄/K}"). The proposition on 054 does not repeat this
condition; it speaks of "deux indices i = i_1 et i = i_2 distincts". As written, the comparison of packets
presupposes that both points are K-rational, or that the construction has been made over a finite extension (as 053
allows for sections over open subgroups E′_K).
