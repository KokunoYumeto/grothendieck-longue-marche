## Second-pass notes (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max)

**O1, scan 072, own idea.** "Si X de genre relatif ≥ 1, on sait (th. de Weil) que la section se prolonge en une
section de X" covers positive genus only. Scan 070 also allows fibres "à π₁ non nul", hence genus 0 with T of degree at
least 2. In genus 0 the step fails as stated: a rational section of P¹_S need not extend across codimension 2 (the map
(x:y) on A²∖{0} has no extension to A²). The case closes another way. With T decomposed and containing two sections,
say 0 and ∞, a section of U_{S′} = P¹_{S′} − T over S′ is a unit u on S′ (and 1−u is a unit if 1 ∈ T). On a normal S,
units extend across codimension ≥ 2 (Hartogs), so the section extends to U_S directly, without the divisor D.
Confidence high: both the counterexample and the extension of units are standard. The first pass's notes on the next
scan ("elle se prolonge … genus-zero extension argument") suggest he takes this case up on scan 073.

**O2, scans 064 and 070, reading question.** If the square on scan 064 is labelled (5), "reprenons le diagramme 5" on
scan 070 points to it: the fibre-product square with the dashed lift φ: E_K → E_{U_S}. Scan 070's criterion ("ssi le
relèvement E_K → E_{U_S} s'annule sur le noyau de E_K → E_S") is then the descent statement of scan 064 ("ceux qui sont
triviaux sur le noyau de E_K → E_S correspondent exactement aux sections …") applied to a geometric rational section.
With the label (4′), the reference on scan 070 would have no target on these pages.

**O3, scan 059, reading question.** The settled "compatible avec son action sur Ũ" (l. 8) is circular as written. The
sentence before says that E_U "opère sur Spec K̄ de façon naturelle", and B′ on scan 063 has "compatible avec son
action sur K̄". The condition he means is semilinearity over K̄. Reading: Ũ, probably a slip for K̄; the transcription
keeps Ũ.

**O4, scan 070, unpursued implication.** "à fibres successives anabéliennes (s'il le faut) ou du moins à π₁ non nul"
admits fibres of types (0,2) and (1,0), where the point/section correspondence of Conjecture A fails (C054.4 for G_m).
The regularity criterion of scans 070–072 therefore does not rest on the anabelian conjectures: it compares a rational
section with the splitting it defines. Confidence medium, because "s'il le faut" leaves open where he will need the
anabelian hypothesis.
