### S021.1 — Scan 021, "ce qui prouvera déjà alors, que 𝔷 est d'ordre 1 ou 2" — unpursued implication

This step is the Artin–Schreier theorem, which G does not name. Since 𝔷 ∩ π = (1) by Théorème 1, 𝔷 maps injectively to
the Galois quotient; a finite subgroup H of an absolute Galois group is the absolute Galois group of its fixed field,
so |H| ≤ 2, and |H| = 2 only when that fixed field is real closed, the generator then being a complex conjugation:
exactly "engendrée par un τ de conjugaison complexe". The whole content of the remark is therefore the finiteness of
the image, and after it the injectivity of Γ → Autext(π) that G says he can prove. Own idea; confidence high, because
Artin–Schreier is a standard theorem and the reduction uses only Théorème 1 and the injectivity of 𝔷 into the quotient.
