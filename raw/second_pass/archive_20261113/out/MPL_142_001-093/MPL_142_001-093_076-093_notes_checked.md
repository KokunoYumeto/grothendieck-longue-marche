## My notes (S2)

- **S2.1** (076, own idea; confidence medium). The presentation on 076 lists generators l^α_{y,x} (x,y ∈ T_{αK}) and the relations l^β_{y,x} = φ_{βα}(l^α_{y,x}), but not the composition relations l^α_{z,y} l^α_{y,x} = l^α_{z,x} inside one piece, which hold because each T_α is 1-connected. Without them the presented groupoid is free (on the generators modulo those identifications) and is not equivalent to Π_1(X_K); with them, and with an interior cover, the statement is the groupoid van Kampen theorem (R. Brown 1967; Brown–Razak Salleh 1984 for many base points), which gives an isomorphism, not only the surjection on Π_1 that he sets out to check. Reason: standard; the condition that every overlap component contains a chosen rational point (N076.1, N077.3) is still needed.
- **S2.2** (077, 086, 090, reading question; confidence medium). The quantifier sign read ∀ by the first pass is, on 077, two t's with a joined crossbar and a hooked foot: his abbreviation "tt" (tout). The same sign stands on 086 and, less clearly, on 090. The other ∀ of this package (090 "pour ∀ rev. étale", 093 "∀x′ ∈ E_0", and those in scans 001–075) should be compared with it. The meaning does not change; the transcription convention does.
- **S2.3** (089, reading question; confidence low). In "J'avais|J'ai pas observé, car le saturé I′ de I dans C n'est pas tjrs contenu dans C_K", the second word is blotted and the third may be "mal" rather than "pas". "J'avais mal observé" would make the self-correction of 088 explicit: there the saturation I′ was tacitly taken inside C_K, and N089.3 gives a cover where it is not.

- **S2.6** (090, unpursued implication, own explanation; confidence high). Why the weakened condition uses a power Θ^n. If Θ comes from u ∈ Γ and C → U_{0,3} is an étale cover defined over K, then u permutes the finite fibre f⁻¹(I_0). Some power u^n (n ≥ 1, the order of that permutation) fixes it pointwise, so u^n lifts to an automorphism of Π_1(C, f⁻¹(I_0)) that is the identity on objects and compatible with Θ^n. The condition "il existe une puissance Θ^n … satisfaisant aux conditions précédentes, pour I = f⁻¹(I_0)" is therefore necessary for every cover, with n depending on the cover, as N090.3 notes. It is the object-level form of 089–090's passage to an open subgroup Γ′. Reason: elementary; the fibre is finite and Γ acts on it through a finite quotient.
