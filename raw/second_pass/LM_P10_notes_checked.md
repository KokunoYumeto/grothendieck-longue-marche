## Second-pass notes (own)

**S01 — scans 93, 96, 97, 100 — implication not pursued (own).**
χ: Γ→ℤ* = Aut(T) for T=ℤ is the discrete counterpart of the cyclotomic character of the arithmetic case (LM_P09,
scan 89: "caractère cyclotomique χ"). The compatibility in b) of scan 100 says sgn∘ρ = χ|Σ, where ρ: Σ→Teichmüller
étendu de π and sgn is its sign character (whether an outer automorphism respects the lacet structure with sign +1 or
−1); so the datum of b) is a homomorphism of groups over {±1}. The sign is defined through κ ↦ κ(−·) when I≠∅ and
through κ ↦ −κ on H²(π,ℤ) when I=∅ (scan 93 and its margin).

**S03 — scans 98–99 — own idea (proof sketch).**
The inclusion inserted on scan 99 follows from "(s′ unique !)" on scan 98. If n∈Norm_ℰ(L_s), the relation of scan 98
for g=n gives L_s = int(n)L_s = int(α)L_{s′}, so L_{s′} is conjugate to L_s; since the relation determines s′ (the
peripheral subgroups of distinct points are not conjugate, which is the content of "s′ unique"), s′=s and p(n) fixes
s, i.e. the image of Norm_ℰ(L_s) lies in Σ_s. With ℰ_i⊂Norm_ℰ(L_s), Image(ℰ_i)=Σ_s and Norm_ℰ(L_s)∩π = L_s = ℰ_i∩π,
the lemma of R20 gives ℰ_i = Norm_ℰ(L_s).
