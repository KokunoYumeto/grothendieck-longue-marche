## Second-pass notes (own)

**S1 — scan 216 — own idea (medium).** The reading "des monomorphismes universels" fits (54): a monomorphism of group schemes G_{a,ℤ} ≅ \underline{T₀} ↪ \underline{Π} remains a monomorphism, hence a closed immersion, on each fibre, so Lie(k_i′) is injective modulo every p when \underline{Π} is smooth. The image of a generator of T₀ is then a primitive vector of the lattice Lie(\underline{Π}), and the generation hypothesis of B4 concerns primitive vectors; this ties it to the smoothness of the closure in (53).

**S2 — scan 211 — own idea (high, given the readings).** If M is compact and A_ℚ denotes the finite adèles, A4 holds: the compact image of M in GL(V ⊗ A_f), for a faithful \underline{M}_ℚ ⊂ GL(V), fixes a ℤ̂-lattice Λ ⊗ ℤ̂ with Λ ⊂ V, and the schematic closure \underline{M} of \underline{M}_ℚ in GL(Λ) is affine, flat and of finite type with \underline{M}(ℤ̂) = \underline{M}_ℚ(A_f) ∩ GL(Λ ⊗ ℤ̂) ⊇ image of M. Smoothness, asked for on scan 213, and the extension (48) of χ do not follow.

**S3 — scan 218 — own idea (medium).** Δ reads naturally as "diagonal": for a finite constant group γ, \underline{γ}(ℤ̂) = ∏_p γ, and \underline{Π}^Δ(ℤ̂) is the preimage of the diagonal γ_Π. It is a closed subgroup of the profinite group ∏_p \underline{Π}(ℤ_p) containing \underline{Π}(ℤ), since Spec ℤ is connected. This explains the "car" of (62) and why only the first two factorisations are automatic: M is not obtained from a group of integral points.

**S4 — scans 221, 226 — implication not pursued.** The margin of scan 221 assumes the multiplier character 𝒢₀ → ℤ* = {±1}, given by the action of 𝒢₀ on the L-group Π₀, trivial ("soit trivial"); that excludes orientation-reversing elements such as those of GL(2,ℤ) outside SL(2,ℤ). §53 removes the restriction: the NB of scan 226 lets G act on T₀^I through the orientation character, and 2°) starts with GL(2,ℝ)^{±1}.

**S5 — scans 225–228 — own idea (medium).** For X = ℂ, S = {0} and G = SL(2,ℝ), the group G̃ of (4) is the universal covering of SL(2,ℝ), realised by lifts of the action on the circle of directions; the preimage of SL(2,ℤ) is the central extension of SL(2,ℤ) by ℤ isomorphic to the braid group B₃, the mapping class group fixing a tangent direction at the puncture of a once-punctured torus. With GL(2,ℝ)^{±1}, elements of determinant −1 act on the kernel ℤ by −1, as in the NB of scan 226. This fits the table's §53 title (special Teichmüller group ST₁,₁ and GL(2,ℤ)) and the raised wave in the heading of 2°) read as a tilde.

**S6 — scan 226 — own idea (check).** The prose says T₀ "opère en fait sur 𝕊" (no tilde in the ink, checked at 2.5×), while (3) reads 𝕊̃/T₀ ≃ 𝕊: T₀ acts on 𝕊̃. A slip of the pen, kept as written.
