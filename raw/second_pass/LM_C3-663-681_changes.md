# LM_C3-663-681: second-pass changes (scans 663–681)

**Witness coverage:** none of the witnesses has the text of these pages. They are manuscript pp. 634–643 (upper
numbers on scans 663, 665, …, 681), which the Circle table places in §49 VI, "Les groupes M_{ρ,σ} généraux:
récapitulation et raffinement de la construction précédente" (pp. 628–650). Searches of the CSG draft, the IUT text
and the Carmona–Kuenzer text for "fourre", "mal de mer", "exorcis", "alambi", "entortill", "rectifieront",
"environnants", "merveilleux diagramme", "dégel", "découpage", "erreurs d'orientation", "Gρ,σ", "SL(2, Ẑ)", "Lρ", "Nρ"
found nothing from these pages. "trop gros" hits CSG l. 5227 (M_g^♮, another passage); "ρ,σ" hits only the §49
running heads at the end of Carmona–Kuenzer (l. 80, l. 7216), which carry no text. Every reading below was settled
on the ink alone. The Circle table supports two readings indirectly: §48 XI is "Récapitulation sur M_{ρ,σ}" (p. 508),
the reference on scan 672, and §48 III is "L'équation αρ(α⁻¹) = βσ(β)⁻¹ … et le groupe G_{ρ,σ}" (p. 443), the
relation that (96) on scan 677 restates.

Context (not authority): the second pass of LM_C3-606-624 reads ε₀ = (1 −1; 0 1), L₀^𝒢 = ε₀^Ẑ and the groups
M^![0], M^!_{0,3} with an exclamation superscript; the second passes of LM_C3-568-586 and LM_C3-606-624 write the
looped ambient group 𝒢 as `\mathcal G` and keep the zigzag superscript as `\unsure{\natural|\sharp}`.

Enlarged and rotated crops are in `second_pass/work_LM_C3-663-681` (`c663_1.png` … `c681_1.png`, `c_eps.png`,
