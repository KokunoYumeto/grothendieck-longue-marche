## Notes added in the second pass (own)

**S01 — scan 116 — own idea (verification), high.** The isomorphism M ≃ K of (254) holds over ℤ. In (253), K is
{(α₀,α₁,β) : 2α₁ − α₀ − 3β = 0} ≅ ℤ² via (α₁,β). The map (255), λ = c₀λ₀ + c₁λ₁ ↦ (α₁,β) = (2c₁ − c₀, c₁ − c₀),
has determinant (−1)(1) − (2)(−1) = 1. So the uniqueness « conjugués par une unique section de M̲ » holds over every
base S, including residue characteristics 2 and 3.

**S02 — scans 119–120 — implication not pursued (own), medium.** (276) states u′_μ̲(σ′) = σ′ and (274) gives σ′ = σ,
while (277) states u′_μ̲(σ) = ω^{ν₂}σ. The two agree only because u_{ρ′,σ′} takes values in G̲_{ρ,σ}/μ_G for
μ̲ ∈ 𝕆₁₂*, and in G̲_{ρ,σ} only for μ̲ ∈ 𝕆₁₂*'' (275), where ν₂ = 0. So (276) is to be read modulo μ_G, as the
parenthesis « (= σ dans G̲_{ρ,σ}/μ_G …) » of (277) says. The factor ω^{ν₂} is the obstruction of R04 seen on a single
element.

**S03 — scan 132 — claim without proof (own), medium.** The inserted « M^Γ = Cent(M̃) » holds for M ∩ Cent(M̃), the
elements of M central in M̃. It equals the whole centre only if no element outside M is central. An element g over γ
that is central centralises M, so γ acts trivially on M. This holds in the faithful quotient M̃̃ of (283)–(284), but
for M̃ itself it needs Γ → Aut(M) injective, which the general setting does not assume.

**S04 — scan 130 — claim without proof (own), high.** In « un quotient M de Π, par un sous-groupe distingué dans 𝓜,
ou ce qui revient au m̄ un quotient de Π_ab stable par l'action de Γ », the equivalence presupposes that M is
commutative. A quotient of Π by a subgroup normal in 𝓜 factors through Π_ab only if it is abelian. The text adds
« où M est comm. » after (287), so the hypothesis is intended but stated after its use.

**S05 — scan 125 — claim without proof (own, from the author's margin), medium.** The margin « les scindages de
Aff(E) déterminés par les pts de E — dépt de l'origine … » states the standard fact behind « Aff(E) scindé ». A point
x ∈ E gives the splitting Aut(M) → Aff(E), φ ↦ (the affine map fixing x with linear part φ). Replacing x by x + u
conjugates this splitting by the translation u. This is the map E → Scind of (285)/(290), and its fibres are the
M^Γ-orbits of (286)/(291). The margin reading is partly uncertain (« dépt », « intérieurs »).

**S06 — scan 127 — implication not pursued (own), high.** In (282) the left vertical arrow is the identity of M̂, so
M̃ is the pull-back of G_{ρ,σ̃} along χ × id_𝔖₃: Γ → Ẑ*×𝔖₃ (the canonical map to the fibre product is a morphism
of extensions of Γ by M̂, hence an isomorphism). Every splitting of G_{ρ,σ̃} therefore pulls back to a splitting of
M̃ over Γ. A unique M̂-conjugacy class downstairs gives a canonical class upstairs, which need not be the only one,
since H¹(Γ,M̂) is large (margin).

**S07 — scans 118 and 132 — own idea (two author's slips, kept as written).** In the profinite Corollary of scan 118
the bijection is stated for « scindages de G″_{ρ,σ} sur Ẑ*×H⁺ », but G″_{ρ,σ} is an extension of Ẑ*″×H⁺, as the
previous sentence says. On scan 132, « l'extension (287) de Γ par M̃ » should be « M̃ de Γ par M »: (287) is the
extension M̃ of Γ by M. Both stay as written in the transcription.

**S08 — scan 133 — implication not pursued (own), high.** If M is finite of order prime to |Γ̃|, then
H¹(Γ̃,M) = 0 automatically, and (292) reduces to M^Γ = 0. The rigidity of the Corollary therefore covers all
coprime cases without invariants. The profinite case of interest (M̂, with Γ̃ the image of Γ_ℚ × 𝔖₃) is not
coprime. The margin of scan 127 (« H¹(Γ,M̂) est un groupe fort gros ! ») concerns Γ itself; by inflation–restriction,
H¹(Γ̃,M̂) is only the part of it inflated from Γ̃. These pages do not settle whether (292) holds for Γ̃ there.
