# LM_C3-435-453 — second-pass changes (scans 435–453)

**Witness coverage.** These scans hold ms. pp. 434–452, upper numbers 520–529. The Grothendieck Circle table places
them in §48 ("Construction d'un schéma en groupes"), part XI "Récapitulation sur M_ρ,σ" (p. 508; XII "Rétrospective"
starts at p. 542). The sub-headings 7° (scan 448) and 8° (scan 453) belong to that part. Searches of the CSG draft,
the IUT text and the Carmona–Kuenzer text for distinctive words of the package found nothing: "délectable",
"regrettablement", "cocartésiennes", "isogénie", "timidement", "si content", "Faisons une", "produit triple",
"prismatiques", "clivages", "multiplicatif", "points entiers". CSG and IUT end before §48. Carmona–Kuenzer has §48
only in its table of contents ("48. Construction d'un schéma en groupes … 203"). The only hits ("peu nous chaut",
"diagonalement", "décidément") come from other sections. Every reading below was therefore settled on the ink alone,
using enlarged and rotated crops in `second_pass/work_LM_C3-435-453` (c435a–c453c).
