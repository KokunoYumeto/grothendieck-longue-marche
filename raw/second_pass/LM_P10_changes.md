## The author's slips, kept as written

- 91: g names both the generator of T and the genus ("g∈T", "g∈ℕ").
- 91: "(auquel cas …" is opened and never closed.
- 93: "d'une (X,S) T-orientée" (feminine for the pair).
- 98: the second display has κ_i on the right where the display it rewrites has κ_{s'} (notes S02).
- 98: the exception list for "le centre de π est réduit à 1" names only the couronne; the torus (g=1, I=∅, π=ℤ²), which scan 91 admits, has centre π (notes R15).
- 99: the revision leaves "n'est ⊂ Σ_s": the "n'" of "n'est autre que" is not struck.
- 100: the parenthesis "(autom. extérieurs de π, … modulo signe" is not closed.
