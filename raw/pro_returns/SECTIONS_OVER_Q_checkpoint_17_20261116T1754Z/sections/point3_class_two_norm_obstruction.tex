\subsection{The quadratic norm Selmer group and an order-thirty-two obstruction}

\begin{statement}{A.209}{Proved}
{The complete projected Selmer image and failure of local matching at class two}
Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 C:y^2=x^5-54x,\quad J=\operatorname{Jac}(C),\qquad
 K=\Q(\theta),\quad \theta^4=54,\\
 L=\Q(r),\quad r^2=6,\quad\theta^2=3r,\qquad
 \pi_2=2+r,\quad\pi_3=3+r,\quad\epsilon=5+2r.
\end{gathered}
\tag{A.209.1}
\]
Let \(v\) be the geometric cyclic-eight deck generator of
the actual cover in (A.207.8), and put \(\delta=v-1\)
on \(J[2]\).  There is an actual Galois-equivariant
quotient and its continuous cohomology map
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \overline M:=J[2]/\delta^2J[2]
      &\simeq\operatorname{Res}_{L/\Q}\mu_2,\\
 H^1(\Q,J[2])\longrightarrow H^1(\Q,\overline M)
      &\simeq L^\times/L^{\times2},
 & [a]&\longmapsto[N_{K/L}a].
\end{aligned}
\tag{A.209.2}
\]
Here the first cohomology group is identified with
\(K^\times/K^{\times2}\) by A.186's literal branch
pairing.  At every place use the same maps after base change,
and define
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \mathcal L_w=\operatorname{im}\bigl(
 J(\Q_w)/2J(\Q_w)\longrightarrow H^1(\Q_w,\overline M)
 \bigr),\\
 \mathcal S=\{c\in H^1(\Q,\overline M):
                   \operatorname{res}_w c\in\mathcal L_w
                   \text{ for every place }w\}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{A.209.3}
\]
The entire global group in (A.209.3) is
\[
 \mathcal S=\langle[-\pi_2],[\pi_3]\rangle
       \simeq(\Z/2)^2.
\tag{A.209.4}
\]
It is the image of the actual rational divisor classes
\(D=[(3,9)-\infty]\) and \(T=[(0,0)-\infty]\).
In particular, projection of the two-Selmer group gives
all of (A.209.3); the proof below independently bounds
every global class in the smaller coefficient module.

Let \(\overline E\) be the arithmetic quotient obtained
from A.207's order-128 quotient by its third lower-central
subgroup.  Its geometric group and the actual quotient map are
\[
 \overline Q=\overline M\rtimes C_8,
 \qquad |\overline Q|=32,
 \qquad\operatorname{cl}(\overline Q)=2,
 \qquad E_2\longrightarrow\overline E.
\tag{A.209.5}
\]
The everywhere locally point-or-cusp-matching section locus
of \(\overline E\) with eighth-power class
\(s^*\kappa_8(t(1-t)^3)=[-24]\) and ordinary local
centre in \(828+1129\Z_{1129}\) is empty.
Consequently the ordinary infinite-support pair constructed
in A.207 has a continuous global class-four lift and has
no everywhere locally point-or-cusp-matching class-two lift.
Both assertions concern the same completed abelian pair.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual coefficient quotient and its norm map.}
Over the splitting field order the four roots cyclically as
\(\theta_j=i^j\theta\), and set
\(T_j=[(\theta_j,0)-\infty]\), \(e_j=T+T_j\).
The branch Weil pairing of A.186 gives
\(e_2(e_j,T_k)=(-1)^{\delta_{jk}}\).
Thus the four square-root cocycles of \(x-\theta_j\)
are exactly the coordinates in this basis of \(J[2]\).
The deck transformation fixes \(T\) and cycles the
\(e_j\).  In characteristic two,
\[
 \delta^2J[2]=\langle e_0+e_2,e_1+e_3\rangle,
 \qquad
 (a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)\longmapsto(a_0+a_2,a_1+a_3)
\tag{A.209.6}
\]
is the quotient map.  Its two coordinates are indexed
by the two values of \(\theta^2\), namely \(3r\)
and \(-3r\).  Every arithmetic permutation of the
four roots permutes these two pairs as its action on
\(L\).  This proves the first isomorphism of (A.209.2).
Multiplication of the two paired square-root cocycles
is the actual quadratic field norm.  Kummer theory and
continuous Shapiro therefore give the second map of
(A.209.2), with no further scalar quotient.
The point and rational branch formulas are consequently
\[
 \overline\kappa([P-\infty])=[x(P)^2-3r],
 \qquad
 \overline\kappa(D)=[9-3r],\qquad
 \overline\kappa(T)=[-3r].
\tag{A.209.7}
\]

\emph{All possible global squareclasses.}
The proper curve \(C\) has good reduction away from
two and three: the only prime divisors of the discriminant
of \(x(x^4-54)\) are two and three.  At any other finite
prime, multiplication by two on its abelian scheme is
finite etale.  Pulling it back by an integral Jacobian
point gives an unramified Kummer torsor.  Properness
extends every local Jacobian point integrally, so its
class, and then its image in (A.209.2), is unramified.
Thus every member of \(\mathcal S\), regarded as an
element of \(L^\times/L^{\times2}\), has even valuation
at every prime of \(L\) away from two and three.
At these odd residue characteristics this is precisely
the unramified quadratic Kummer condition: after removing
an even uniformizer power, a unit gives the etale quadratic
algebra over the valuation ring.

Here is a full arithmetic calculation of that global
squareclass group.  We have \(\mathcal O_L=\Z[r]\)
and discriminant \(24\).  The Minkowski bound
\cite[Theorem 4.3, printed p.~70]{AgentMilneANT} is
\(\sqrt{24}/2<3\).  Every ideal class therefore has
an integral representative of norm one or two.
The unique prime over two is \((\pi_2)\), since
\(N_{L/\Q}(\pi_2)=-2\).  Hence \(\operatorname{Cl}(L)=0\).
Likewise the unique prime over three is \((\pi_3)\),
with norm three.  The exact identities are
\[
 \pi_2^2=2\epsilon,\qquad
 \pi_3^2=3\epsilon,\qquad N_{L/\Q}(\epsilon)=1.
\tag{A.209.8}
\]

For completeness \(\mathcal O_L^\times=
\langle-1,\epsilon\rangle\).
An integral unit \(a+br\) of norm minus one would
satisfy \(a^2\equiv-1\pmod3\), which is impossible.
For a positive norm-one unit \(u>1\), the identity
\(b=(u-u^{-1})/(2\sqrt6)\) shows that a unit strictly
between one and \(\epsilon\) has \(0<b<2\).
The remaining integer \(b=1\) would require \(a^2=7\).
Multiplying any positive unit by an appropriate power
of \(\epsilon\) reduces it to this interval or to one.
Changing sign and taking inverses covers every unit.
This proves the claimed unit group directly.

The class-number calculation permits removal of every
even valuation outside these two primes by an actual
global square.  Every candidate in \(\mathcal S\)
therefore has the unique squareclass expression
\[
                  (-1)^a\epsilon^b\pi_2^c\pi_3^d,
                 \qquad a,b,c,d\in\F_2.
\tag{A.209.9}
\]
Independence follows first from the two prime valuations
and then from the displayed full unit group.  This
accounts for all sixteen global candidates.

\emph{The exact real and three-adic tests.}
At the real embedding \(r\mapsto-\sqrt6\), the
quadratic algebra \(K/L\), defined by \(\theta^2=3r\),
is \(\C/\R\).  Its norm on nonzero elements is
positive.  Every class in the projected real Kummer
image thus has positive sign in this component.
The four signs in (A.209.9), at this embedding, are
respectively \(-,+,-,+\).  Consequently
\[
                              a=c.
\tag{A.209.10}
\]

At three, \(X^4-54\) is irreducible over \(\Q_3\).
Indeed all its roots have valuation \(3/4\), so the
constant term of any monic local factor of degree \(j\)
has valuation \(3j/4\); its integrality forces
\(4\mid j\).  By (A.209.2)'s original four-coordinate
permutation module, \(J(\Q_3)[2]\) has order two.
There is an open formal pro-three subgroup of the compact
group \(J(\Q_3)\) on which multiplication by two is
an isomorphism.  On the finite quotient the kernel and
cokernel of this endomorphism have equal order.
Its two-torsion lifts uniquely through the formal group,
so
\[
                         |J(\Q_3)/2J(\Q_3)|=2.
\tag{A.209.11}
\]
The local field \(L_3=\Q_3(r)\) is ramified quadratic,
with \(v_{L_3}(3)=2\), \(v_{L_3}(r)=1\).
Thus \(-3r\) has odd valuation three and is a nonzero
projected class of \(T\).  It generates the entire
local image in (A.209.11).

The exact global identities
\[
              9-3r=9/\pi_3,\qquad
              \frac{-3r}{9-3r}=-\pi_2
\tag{A.209.12}
\]
also specify that local generator.
The unit \(-\pi_2\) has residue one in \(\F_3\),
and is a square by the ordinary simple-root Hensel map.
The unit \(\epsilon\) has nonsquare residue two,
whereas \(\pi_3\) has valuation one.
These last two classes form the full local squareclass
basis, since squaring is an isomorphism on principal
three-units.  The required local image is therefore
exactly \(\langle[\pi_3]\rangle\).
Combining (A.209.9)--(A.209.10), its unramified coordinate
is precisely \(b\), which must vanish.  The four global
survivors are
\[
                         (-\pi_2)^a\pi_3^d.
\tag{A.209.13}
\]
Conversely (A.209.7) and (A.209.12) show that \(D\)
and \(T\) generate every survivor.  Their actual global
Kummer classes satisfy every local condition by construction.
Thus all places, including two, are accounted for, and
(A.209.4) follows in both directions.

\emph{The actual class-two quotient and the selected cover.}
A.207 identifies the order-128 geometric group with
\(J[2]\rtimes C_8\) and its third lower-central
subgroup with \(\delta^2J[2]\).
This subgroup is characteristic in that geometric group,
so its inverse image is normal in the full arithmetic
tripod group.  The quotient is the actual
\(\overline M\rtimes C_8\) of (A.209.5).
The deck generator swaps the two coordinates of
\(\overline M\); its commutator subgroup is
\(\langle(1,1)\rangle\), nonzero and central.
This proves its exact order and class, and proves
\(\Gamma_3\Pi\) lies in the arithmetic quotient kernel.
The resulting map from the full profinite class-two
extension is the stated \(E_2\to\overline E\).

For the class \(s^*\kappa_8(t(1-t)^3)=[-24]\),
the actual cover \(z^8=-t(1-t)^3/24\) has a rational
fixed sheet after pullback by \(s\).
Selecting that sheet gives a section of its arithmetic
cover subgroup.  Compactification and the rational
origin \(\infty\), followed by (A.209.2), give
one global class in \(H^1(\Q,\overline M)\).
At each place the actual geometric conjugator aligning
with a full local point or cusp section transports this
same sheet.  An ordinary sheet is a rational cover point;
a cuspidal sheet compactifies to its rational boundary
point.  Their proper Abel--Jacobi classes project into
\(\mathcal L_w\), including at the real place.
Thus the resulting global class belongs to the entire
group \(\mathcal S\) just computed.

At \(p=1129\) take the two embeddings of \(L\)
modulo \(p\) to be \(r=838,291\).
They come from the cyclic roots \(16,430,1113,699\)
of \(X^4-54\).  The two global generators have the
following literal square witnesses in the two factors:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &r=838&r=291\\\hline
 -\pi_2&289=17^2&836=426^2\\
 \pi_3&841=29^2&294=221^2
\end{array}\quad\pmod {1129}.
\tag{A.209.14}
\]
All four global classes therefore localize to the zero
squareclass vector \((0,0)\) here.

For \(t\equiv828\pmod p\), the eight roots of the
cover equation are the simple residues
\(35,44,235,511,618,894,1085,1094\).
The actual inverse formulas of (A.207.8) give
\(x\equiv28,941,1101,188\), each with two sheets.
For every one of these sheets the quadratic norm in
(A.209.7) has values
\[
 (x^2-3r)_{r=838,291}=
       (528,1040)\quad\hbox{or}\quad(89,601),
\tag{A.209.15}
\]
and all four entries are nonsquares in \(\F_{1129}\).
Their local Kummer vector is therefore \((1,1)\).
Every entry is a unit, so these squareclasses persist
through the whole stated residue ball.
Simple-root Hensel lifting retains all eight sheets;
there are no further local roots of the degree-eight
equation.  Equations (A.209.14)--(A.209.15) exclude
every possible transported sheet from \(\mathcal S\).
This proves the empty locus in the statement.

Finally use the same completed pair as A.207.
Its two individual eighth-power classes give the
required combined class, and its ordinary centre at
1129 belongs to the excluded ball.  A locally matching
full class-two section with this abelian pair must have
that same ordinary centre there: full local Kummer
injectivity fixes both coordinates.  The three actual
cusp maps in (A.205.9) would instead force \(x=0\),
\(x=1\), or \((1-x)/x=-1\), respectively, and
none holds for this field point.
The map (A.209.5) would consequently give a section
in the empty finite locus.  Hence its locally matching
class-two fibre is empty, while its actual global
class-four lift is already supplied by A.207.

\end{proof}
