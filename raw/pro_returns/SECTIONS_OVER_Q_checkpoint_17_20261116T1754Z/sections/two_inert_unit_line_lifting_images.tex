\subsection{The exact lifting images on the completed unit line}

\begin{statement}{A.200}{Proved}
{The local and finite image is \(3\Z_3\), and the continuous class-three image is \(9\Z_3\)}
Retain \(k,A,U,S,\mathcal Q,\mathcal Q_c\) and their actual
arithmetic actions from A.196.  Put
\[
 \mathcal H=\mathcal Q/\Gamma_3\mathcal Q,\qquad
 B_z=[U]^z\in\widehat{k^\times}_3\quad(z\in\Z_3).
\tag{A.200.1}
\]
The exponent is scalar multiplication in the multiplicative
completion, and the first abelian coordinate is always
\(\kappa_3(A)\).  With these fixed coordinate maps the exact
lifting images are
\[
\begin{array}{l|c}
 \text{required lift of }(\kappa_3(A),\kappa_3(B_z))
       &\text{set of }z\\ \hline
 \text{a global continuous section with geometric group }\mathcal H
       &3\Z_3\\
 \text{a continuous }\mathcal Q\text{-section at every place of }k
       &3\Z_3\\
 \text{a global }\mathcal Q_c\text{-section, for any fixed }c\ge1
       &3\Z_3\\
 \text{a global continuous }\mathcal Q\text{-section}
       &9\Z_3
\end{array}
\tag{A.200.2}
\]
In the last row the section can be chosen with its geometric
cocycle factoring through \(G_{k,S}\).  All rows include
\(z=0\) with its explicit section \(x^{\kappa_3(A)}\).

The continuous class-three obstruction of \(U\) itself has
exact order nine:
\[
 \xi=\delta\kappa_3(U)\in H^2_{\rm cts}(G_k,D_2),
 \qquad \operatorname{ord}(\xi)=9,\qquad
 3\xi=\mathfrak o_2.
\tag{A.200.3}
\]
On the interval \(z\in3\Z_3\setminus9\Z_3\), every absolute
finite quotient in A.200.2 lifts, every local continuous
quotient lifts, and no continuous global \(\mathcal Q\)-lift
exists.  For every finite \(S'\supset S\), the geometric
cocycles of those finite lifts eventually fail to factor
through \(G_{k,S'}\).  At \(S'=S\) the first failing
coefficient modulus is nine.

The same unit line passes every individual completed
root-torsor norm test of A.181 exactly for \(z\in3\Z_3\).
The actual map from coherent completed norm families to
this line has image contained in \(9\Z_3\), by its quotient
map to \(\mathcal Q\).
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The literal unit line and coefficient maps.}
The element \(U=\alpha^2-2\) satisfies
\(U^3+6U^2+12U-1=0\); hence it is a global unit of norm
one.  In the specified real embedding, \(2<\alpha^2<3\),
so \(0<U<1\) and it has infinite order.  Its completed
unit line is the actual continuous image of \(\Z_3\)
under \(z\mapsto[U]^z\).  Equivalently, its class at
exponent \(3^c\) is represented by \(U^{z_c}\), where
\(z_c\) is any integer representative of \(z\bmod3^c\).
The pro-three unit basis in A.165, or the displayed
nonzero cubic residue character below, makes this line
injective.

Choose the compatible integral cocycles
\(a=\kappa_3(A)\) and \(u=\kappa_3(U)\), factoring through
\(\Gamma=G_{k,S}\).  The cocycle for \(B_z\) is exactly
\(zu\).  The A.196 coefficient sequence gives a
\(\Z_3\)-linear connecting homomorphism
\[
\begin{gathered}
 0\longrightarrow D_2\xrightarrow{T}V_A/T^3V_A
      \xrightarrow{\epsilon}\Z_3(1)\longrightarrow0,\\
 \xi_S=\delta[u],\qquad
       \delta[zu]=z\xi_S.
\end{gathered}
\tag{A.200.4}
\]
All absolute and local classes in the proof are the
images of these particular cocycles under restriction,
inflation, or coefficient reduction.

\emph{The supported Tate exponents and exact order nine.}
We specify the exponent argument for both weights.
Over \(F=k(\zeta_3)\), A.138 gives zero three-primary
class group and one prime above three.  On
\(X_0=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_F[1/3]\), the Kummer
and Brauer sequences give
\(H^2_{\acute et}(X_0,\mu_3)=0\).  For \(j=2,3\), the
chosen \(\zeta_3\) identifies the sheaf \(\Z/3(j)\)
with \(\mu_3\).  The successive coefficient sequences
therefore give \(H^2_{\acute et}(X_0,\Z/3^c(j))=0\)
for every \(c\).  Finite first cohomology removes the
derived inverse-limit term, so the corresponding
integral second cohomology is zero.

The degree-two group-to-scheme injection and actual
localization maps proved in A.183 and A.196 now give
\[
 H^2(G_{F,S_F},\Z_3(j))
   \lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
 \bigoplus_{v\mid2\cdot11\cdot59}
        \Z_3/(|\mathbf F_v|^{\,j-1}-1)\Z_3,
             \qquad j=2,3.
\tag{A.200.5}
\]
There are three primes at each of the three rational
primes, with residue sizes \(4,121,3481\), respectively.
For every one of these sizes \(Q\),
\(v_3(Q-1)=v_3(Q^2-1)=1\).  Thus both groups in
A.200.5 are killed by three.  Restriction to \(F\)
followed by corestriction is multiplication by two,
which is invertible on the three-primary coefficient
groups.  It follows that
\[
        3H^2(\Gamma,\Z_3(2))
        =3H^2(\Gamma,\Z_3(3))=0.
\tag{A.200.6}
\]

The actual normalized coefficient sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\Z_3(3)\xrightarrow{T}D_2
       \longrightarrow\Z_3(2)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{A.200.7}
\]
For any class \(e\in H^2(\Gamma,D_2)\), its bottom
image is killed by three.  Exactness puts \(3e\) in
the image of \(H^2(\Gamma,\Z_3(3))\), which is also
killed by three.  Therefore \(9e=0\).

In particular \(9\xi_S=0\), whereas
\(3\xi_S=\mathfrak o_{S,2}\) is nonzero by A.195.
Its order is exactly nine.  The integral inflation
\(H^2(\Gamma,D_2)\to H^2_{\rm cts}(G_k,D_2)\)
is injective by A.183: an equivariant homomorphism
on the kernel vanishes on every outside-\(S\) inertia
group because the two torsion-free Frobenius weights
on \(D_2(-1)\) are \(Q,Q^2\), neither equal to one.
Those inertias normally generate the kernel, so a
cobounding cochain descends.  Thus \(\xi\) has the
same exact order nine, proving A.200.3.

By the exact group/cochain comparison in A.196,
the global continuous \(\mathcal Q\)-lifting condition
is \(z\xi=0\).  An element of order nine has
annihilator exactly \(9\Z_3\); this proves the last
row of A.200.2.  The same order calculation for
\(\xi_S\) supplies the lift already over \(\Gamma\).

\emph{The first weight and local necessity.}
The actual bottom image of \(\xi_S\) in A.200.7 is
\[
             a\cup u\in H^2(\Gamma,\Z_3(2)).
\tag{A.200.8}
\]
At either quadratic prime of \(k\) above eleven or
fifty-nine, its reduction modulo three is nonzero.
Indeed the local field contains \(\mu_3\), the
valuation of \(A\) is eight, and the actual cubic
residue character of \(U\), in the A.159 convention,
is two.  The tame cup is the product \(8\cdot2=1\)
in \(\F_3\), up to the fixed nonzero choice of local
invariant generator.  Equivalently, the unit \(U\)
has noncube residue and is not a norm from the
totally ramified extension obtained by adjoining
\(A^{1/3}\).  This is the actual Kummer/cyclic-algebra
map, also used in A.195.

Consequently A.200.8 is nonzero both globally and
locally, and A.200.6 makes its exact global order
three.  The class-two obstruction for \(B_z\) is
\(z(a\cup u)\), so its global vanishing locus is
precisely \(3\Z_3\).  This proves the first row.
For \(z\notin3\Z_3\), its finite local reduction
already excludes a class-two lift at the quadratic
prime above eleven.  Every \(\mathcal Q\)-lift there,
and every global \(\mathcal Q_c\)-lift for \(c\ge1\),
would project to this class-two lift modulo three.
Thus all the remaining local and finite lifting
sets in A.200.2 are contained in \(3\Z_3\).

\emph{Actual scalar maps give every sufficiency.}
For every \(m\in\Z_3\), there is an actual continuous
arithmetic-equivariant group endomorphism
\[
 \varphi_m:\mathcal Q\longrightarrow\mathcal Q,\qquad
                (v,a_0)\longmapsto(mv,a_0).
\tag{A.200.9}
\]
It fixes \(x\) and sends \(y\) to \(y^m\).
The semidirect product formula gives
\(m(v+X^{a_0}v')=mv+X^{a_0}(mv')\), proving the
homomorphism identity.  Scalar multiplication
commutes with the arithmetic action of A.196.5.
The same maps descend to every \(\mathcal Q_c\),
with \(m\) reduced modulo \(3^c\), and exist on the
full one-variable group \(P\) of A.181.
Their abelian maps are
\((a,b)\mapsto(a,mb)\).

For \(z=3m\), apply \(\varphi_m\) to the actual
A.181 local \(P\)-sections for \(U^3\), followed by
the quotient to \(\mathcal Q\).  This proves local
sufficiency at every place, including the
archimedean places.  Apply the finite versions to
the A.196 global \(\mathcal Q_c\)-sections for
\(U^3\).  This proves global sufficiency at every
finite coefficient level, for the same \(z\).
It also explains all comparisons without changing
the fixed first coordinate.

For \(z=9m\), choose the supported continuous
\(\mathcal Q\)-section furnished by
\(9\xi_S=0\), and apply \(\varphi_m\) to it.
This realizes the last row by an actual section.
At \(m=0\) the formula is the literal section
\(g\mapsto(x^{a(g)},g)\).  Thus no zero parameter
is omitted from any row.

\emph{The finite support and norm-tower comparisons.}
For \(z=3m\) with \(m\in\Z_3^\times\), the supported
modulo-nine obstruction is
\[
             z\xi_S\bmod9
                =m(\mathfrak o_{S,2}\bmod9)\ne0.
\tag{A.200.10}
\]
The inequality follows from A.195 and the invertibility
of multiplication by \(m\).  Its modulo-three target
is zero, and the explicit \(x^{a_1}\) section gives
the first supported level.  Thus the first failing
supported modulus is exactly nine.
For \(z\notin3\Z_3\), the local cup excludes level
modulo three already.  For \(z\in9\Z_3\), the supported
continuous section supplies all supported finite
levels.  These three cases exhaust \(\Z_3\).

For any fixed finite \(S'\supset S\), the normalized
finite cocycle solution sets over \(G_{k,S'}\) are
finite, with the literal quotient transition maps
of A.196.  Their nonemptiness at unbounded levels
would give a compatible system and then a continuous
global \(\mathcal Q\)-section.  For
\(z\in3\Z_3\setminus9\Z_3\) this contradicts the
last row of A.200.2.  This proves the asserted
escaping-ramification statement through the actual
arithmetic pullback A.196.17.

Finally A.181 gives, for each \(n\ge2\), an actual
element of \(K_n^\times\) of norm \(U^3\) to \(k\).
Raising its completed Kummer class to \(m\in\Z_3\)
gives a completed norm preimage of \(B_{3m}\).
For \(z\notin3\Z_3\), the same first cubic local
norm test above excludes a preimage already in
\(K_2\); every higher norm factors through \(K_2\).
Hence the intersection of all individual norm
images on the unit line is exactly \(3\Z_3\).
A coherent family maps, by the actual inverse
Shapiro map of A.181, to a section of \(P\).
Its quotient to \(\mathcal Q\) places its parameter
in \(9\Z_3\), as asserted.

endomorphisms, Tate residue exponents, and

with retained output and data.  The proof of the
exact cohomology orders uses A.195 and the displayed
supported-to-absolute maps.
\end{proof}
