\section*{Conventions and status}
We write $U=\Pj^1_\Q\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}$ and
$1\to\Pi\to E_U\to G_\Q\to1$ for its arithmetic fundamental group
sequence. Sections are continuous and are identified only by conjugation
under $\Pi$. For a field $F$ the exponent completion is
$\widehat{F^\times}=\varprojlim_{n\geq1}F^\times/(F^\times)^n$,
with transition maps for divisibility of exponents.
