## Solvable embedding and auxiliary cyclic ramification

Root searched the web for “solvable embedding problems prescribed local solutions cyclic decomposition groups Neukirch Shafarevich”, “Galois modules approximation ramification split primes cyclic decomposition solvable embedding problems”, and “Solving embedding problems with bounded ramification pdf”.

Schmidt–Wingberg, *Šafarevič's Theorem on Solvable Groups as Galois Groups*, primary arXiv text https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9809211, opened 16 November 2026. Theorem13, printed pages13–19, starts with an actual class over the splitting field, unramified at the old places and zero at the base ramification, coefficient, and infinite places. Its corestriction is matched there and the output is cyclic at newly ramified places. Step4, printed page30, cancels an unramified component using this theorem. The required input class is an additional condition. Theorem14 solves split nilpotent embedding problems; the Introduction explains why its shrinking step does not retain arbitrary prescribed local classes. No unrestricted cyclic-repair theorem is inferred. The explicit A119 obstruction confirms why that stronger inference fails here.

Jarden–Ramiharimanana, *Solving embedding problems with bounded ramification*, author's primary text https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~jarden/Articles/paper111.pdf, Theorem A on printed pages3–4, opened 16 November 2026. Its solvable-kernel theorem retains prescribed local solutions and bounds additional ramified primes, under the displayed coprimality condition between the kernel order and the roots of unity in the given splitting field. In the current three-primary problem that field contains µ3, so this hypothesis fails. The result has not been invoked outside its hypotheses. Official author-institution metadata at https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/solving-embedding-problems-with-bounded-ramification/ confirms PLMS117(1),149–191 (2018), DOI10.1112/plms.12126.

König's publisher-hosted *On unramified solvable extensions of small number fields*, Bull. Aust. Math. Soc.103 (2021),428–437, DOI10.1017/S0004972720001136, was also opened. Its Theorem3.1 restates the cyclic-decomposition realization consequence of Shafarevich; it is not an arbitrary prescribed-local-class statement. No result in the active manuscript is derived solely from this overview.
