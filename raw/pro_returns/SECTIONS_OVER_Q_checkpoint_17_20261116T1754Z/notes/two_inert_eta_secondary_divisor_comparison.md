# Exact eta and secondary-character divisor maps after A206

Reviewer/author: local_preprint, with independent checks and the tau-cancellation supplied by birational_main. Date: 2026-11-16.

This note records proved morphisms and the remaining unevaluated comparison. No equality between eta and psi_2 is asserted.

## 1. The eta target has a canonical ideal-class realization

Retain M/E2 with cyclic generator h from A206. Every actual E2-unit a is an actual field norm by A203. Choose w in M^times with N_h(w)=a. The residue-weighted ideal norm of div(w) is zero. On each h-orbit of primes the coefficient sum is zero, so there is an actual integral divisor J with

    div(w)=(h-1)J.

Then [J] is h-invariant. Changing w by a norm-one element changes it by h(z)/z, by Hilbert90, and changes J by a principal divisor and an h-invariant divisor. The three-primary classes of h-invariant divisors are generated by the lower extended classes and the unique ramified three-prime. A206 kills the former and identifies the latter with c3.

It follows that the literal map

    a -> [J]_3 modulo <c3>

is well-defined on O_E2^times/N_h(O_M^times). It is onto A^h/<c3>: for an invariant class choose J and a principal generator of (h-1)J; its norm is a unit. It is injective by an actual principalization argument. If its value is zero, choose an h-invariant divisor I with [J-I]_3=0. An exponent e prime to three makes e(J-I)=div(z). The element w^e/(h(z)/z) is a global unit with norm a^e. Combining this with the scalar norm a^3 using a Bezout identity gives an actual unit norm equal to a.

Thus there is an exact isomorphism

    O_E2^times/N_h(O_M^times) -> A^h/<c3>.

Composing with the actual ideal norm N_h is injective, because its entire kernel is <c3>. The map is tau-equivariant. A203's unit quotient is entirely tau-minus and has dimension two, while A173 gives dim A2^- =2. Therefore

    Theta_h: O_E2^times/N_h(O_M^times) -> A2^-
    Theta_h(a)=N_h([J]_3)

is a canonical isomorphism, with the displayed h-1 convention fixing its sign. The same map applies to the completed quotient because it is the same finite exponent-three quotient. Composing with A203 eta gives an actual onto map R1 -> A2^-.

## 2. The cyclic-nine Hilbert90 divisor is actually principal

Put Omega=K3(zeta9), with gamma in Gal(Omega/F') of order nine, gamma(rho3)=zeta9*rho3. Then gamma|M=sigma, and s=gamma^3 generates Gal(Omega/M). This gamma is not the A199 automorphism t->zeta9*t. Extend h by fixing rho3; then h gamma h^-1=gamma^4. Complex conjugation tau fixes the pure roots and satisfies tau gamma tau=gamma^-1 and tau h tau=h.

Take an actual normalized relative unit v= sigma^2(a)/sigma(a), with a a product of the certified tau-anti units r2,r3,r4 (each has N_sigma(a)=1), and an actual pure unit b in K3 with N_s(b)=v. These are the known actual one-step unit lifts used to define psi_2 on the explicit relative-unit basis. Since N_gamma(b)=1, choose an actual w in Omega^times with

    b=gamma(w)/w.

Set m=N_s(w). Then sigma(m)/m=v, hence

    m=f sigma(a),   f in F'^times.

The divisor D=div_Omega(w) is gamma-invariant because b is a global unit. At every ramified prime of Omega/M, namely those above3,11,59, the residue degree is one and M/F' is also ramified cubic. The equality N_s(D)=div_M(f) forces every corresponding coefficient of D to be divisible by three. At the other primes Omega/M is unramified. Therefore D is the literal extension of an actual divisor J on M:

    D=j_s(J),   3J=div_M(f).

The divisor J is sigma-invariant. The strong sigma-ambiguous divisor classes are generated by classes extended from F' and the ramified-prime classes. The base has trivial three-class group and the changed-q prime classes of M vanish by A206. Hence [J] lies in <c3>.

There is an exact tau cancellation. The element

    a0=gamma(w) tau(w)

is gamma-fixed, using tau(b)=b, so a0 lies in F'. Its s-norm satisfies

    a0^3=sigma(m) tau(m)=f tau(f),

because a is anti. Taking divisors and using 3J=div(f) gives

    J+tau(J)=div_M(a0).

Thus [J] is tau-minus. The line <c3> is tau-plus, because the prime above three is unique. Since [J] is killed by three, these two identities force [J]=0 in the full ordinary ideal class group. No prime-to-three power or completed principalization is required.

Choose an actual x in M^times with div_M(x)=J. Then

    u=w/x in O_Omega^times,
    b=(gamma(u)/u) (sigma(x)/x),

and sigma(x)/x is an actual M-unit, because J is sigma-invariant. This equality preserves the original target v exactly.

## 3. The actual tame secondary character in this representation

At either changed q, Omega/F' is totally ramified of degree nine, so gamma acts trivially on the residue of the global unit u. Hence gamma(u)/u has residue one. The residue of b is exactly that of sigma(x)/x.

For the chosen prime over q with alpha reducing to r*zeta3, use the literal uniformizer pi_M=t^2/q in M/F'. It has valuation one and sigma(pi_M)=zeta3^2*pi_M. Thus

    residue(sigma(x)/x)=zeta3^(2 v_Rq(J)).

Normalizing the cubic residue character by omega_q=lambda_q(alpha), as in the earlier tame-character certificates, gives

    psi_2(v)_q = 2 v_Rq(J) modulo3.

The character is evaluated in the residue field of the pure quadratic q-branch, F_(q^2). Its embedding into F_(q^6) is retained; applying the cubic character of the larger field would erase this information and is not the asserted map.

Changing the Hilbert90 primitive by F'^times changes these q-valuations by multiples of three. Changing the principal generator x by a unit does not alter the ramified residue. Independence from the chosen pure unit preimage b is the actual one-step norm-kernel character theorem A184.

## 4. The retained h-alignment equation

The condition h(b)=b gives the exact invariant

    K=P4(gamma)h(w)/w in F'^times,
    P4(gamma)=1+gamma+gamma^2+gamma^3

in multiplicative group-ring notation. The chosen certified units have N_sigma(a)=1, so taking N_s gives

    K^3=(h(f))^4/f.

Consequently r=K/h(f) satisfies r^3=h(f)/f and

    (h-1)J=div_M(r).

For the actual principal generator x, the quotient h(x)/(r x) is therefore a global M-unit. These equations retain the arithmetic h-alignment after principalization.
