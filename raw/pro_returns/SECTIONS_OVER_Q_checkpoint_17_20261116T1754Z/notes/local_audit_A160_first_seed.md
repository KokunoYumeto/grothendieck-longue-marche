## Complete first pro-three S-unit fibre

integers is Z[alpha]. The discriminant is -243: only three can divide
the index, and locally alpha is a uniformizer of the totally ramified

ideal-class bound 8 sqrt(3)/pi < 4.42.

The two primes over two are principal, generated by
g_2=alpha-1 and 2/g_2; the unique prime over three is generated by alpha.

representative and the class group is trivial.

* g_59=2+2alpha-alpha^2, with norm 59, and 59/g_59, with norm 59^2.
