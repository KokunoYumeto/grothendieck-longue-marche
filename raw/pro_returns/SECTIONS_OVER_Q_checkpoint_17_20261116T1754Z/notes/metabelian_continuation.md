# Metabelian continuation and exact fixed-depth limitation

## 7. Exact scope of the fixed-support denominator criterion

At a fixed prime q and a coefficient prime ell different from q, write d_a for the finite order of q in (Z/ell^a)^×. Every positive multiple i of d_a satisfies ell^a | q^i−1. Consequently v_ell(product_(i=2)^(R−1)(q^i−1)) tends to infinity with R. A fixed nonzero ordinary integer valuation E therefore cannot satisfy the sufficient product-divisibility criterion at that same q for every depth. This is a proved limitation of that particular sufficient criterion. The actual strict residues can in principle vanish through other cancellations, so this does not prove that a fixed ordinary ghost lacks an infinite sequence of lifts.

### Exact remaining finite-precision recursion issue

One may preserve a chosen finite local precision of a completed norm representative by selecting each future A-support prime q with a prescribed exact v3(q−1) and requiring the appropriate finite radical classes to split there.
