## Exact logarithmic lattice comparison

Let F=Q(alpha,zeta_3), F'=F(zeta_9), M=E_2(zeta_9). The four cubic intermediate fields of M/F are F' and the three conjugates of E_2. The field F is totally imaginary, with r_2(F)=3; a CM hypothesis is neither used nor needed. Its complex conjugation is noncentral in Gal(F/Q)=S_3.

Use the standard Euclidean logarithmic spaces, with the doubled complex logarithms used for the ordinary regulator. The M-space decomposes orthogonally as V_F plus four spaces W_i of dimension six. The shared space V_F has dimension two.

The actual common lattice is Lambda_i intersect V_F = Lambda_F. Indeed a unit u with logarithm in V_F has sigma(u)/u of absolute value one in every embedding; it is a root of unity by Kronecker. In each E_2 conjugate, that ratio lies in mu_3, so u^3 is an F-unit. The nontrivial Kummer restriction kernel is generated by d^2 alpha (or its conjugate), whose changed-prime valuation is two modulo three, and it cannot contain an F-unit class. Thus u has the logarithm of an F-unit. For F'/F, norm-one of the root-of-unity ratio forces the ratio into mu_3. Dividing u by a suitable power of zeta_9 makes it an F-unit.

For the sum map from the four intermediate logarithmic spaces to the M-space, the kernel lattice is Lambda_F tensor the A_3 root lattice. Its covolume is 4 times covol(Lambda_F)^3, because dim(V_F)=2 and det(A_3)=4. The determinant of the sum map on its orthogonal complement is also 4. These factors cancel exactly. Each L_i-to-M logarithmic embedding scales lengths by sqrt(3) in dimension eight, while F-to-M scales lengths by three in dimension two. Consequently

    covol(B_log) = 3^10 product_i covol(Lambda_Li) / covol(Lambda_F)^3.

Since covol(Lambda_K)=sqrt(r_2(K)) R_K, the remaining square-root factor is

    sqrt(9)^4 / (sqrt(27) sqrt(3)^3) = 3.

Therefore

    product_i R_Li / (R_M R_F^3) = [U_M:B] / 3^11.

The product subgroup contains the roots of unity of M because F' contains zeta_9. Thus its logarithmic index has the same three-part 3^6 as the exact A199 completed-unit index.
