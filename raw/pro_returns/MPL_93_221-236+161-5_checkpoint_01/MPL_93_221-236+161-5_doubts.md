# Legibility and limitations

The transcription is being made visually, without OCR, in one reading stream. The first sheet has highly compressed cursive, overwriting, and ambiguous abbreviations. In particular, readings under cancellations and very short connective words require caution. Every doubtful formula symbol is represented by a proposed reading with `\unsure`, not omitted. No context contents have been consulted.
