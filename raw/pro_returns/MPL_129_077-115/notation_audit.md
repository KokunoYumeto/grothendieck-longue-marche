# Cross-package notation audit (before context)

The second visual reading covered every scan. The following distinctions were compared across the page files and against the images; no context source was used.

| Family | Scans checked | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D-star, group dual stars, primes and double primes | 082, 093–096, 106–108, 114–115 | Stars remain stars; group primes remain primes; uncertain overwritten superscripts remain locally marked. No secure exclamation-mark exponent was identified in this package. The separate exclamation-like mark on 114 remains doubtful. |
| Twists and alphabet shapes | 080–082, 092–094, 107–108, 110, 113–115 | Capital Φ, small φ/varphi, sigma twists, script categories and fraktur formal schemes are not globally normalized. The sigma of082 and phi-like twist of094 differ visibly. |
| Typography in typescripts | 080–081, 099, 101, 103, 105, 109, 111 | Literal underlined and double-underlined letters retained; reused sheets' scalar conventions differ from the Witt-scheme notation. |
| L, ell, n and nu | 092–096 | Cotangent capital L, co-Lie ell, roman n and Greek nu separated; underlines and dual signs preserved. |
| Torsion and divided-power parentheses | 084–091, 095–096, 113–115 | G(n), G[n], left torsion subscripts and parenthesized divided powers are distinct; anomalous mixtures retained. |
| Closure, underline and direct limits | 080–081, 090, 098–108, 113 | Closure bars not converted to underlines; undecorated lim remains undecorated; arrow under W retained where visible. |
| f-iterates, indices, kernel/image and inequalities | 098, 100, 102, 104, 107–108 | j, i, n−j and n−i read locally; contradictory degree ranges and the quotient-height wording are retained. |
| Plain strokes and arrows | 080–082, 087–096, 098–108, 110, 113–115 | No blanket conversion of strokes into arrows. Scan 106 curled vertical strokes are geometric curves; scan 115 assignment head remains unsure. |
| Quotation marks and abbreviations | all text-bearing scans | Double quotes use two backticks/two apostrophes. Abbreviations remain unexpanded; proposed expansions are confined to doubts.md. |
| Archive and printed pagination | all 37 | Archive foliation is commented only. Reused typescript page numbers are ordinary face text. No secure authorial handwritten sheet number requiring mspage was identified. |

This is a transcription consistency check, not a mathematical verification. Genuine differences in the ink remain differences in the transcription.
