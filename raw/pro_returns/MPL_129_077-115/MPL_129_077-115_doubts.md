# Legibility and limits

The blue typescripts are generally clear, although missing type, overtyping, handwritten insertions, and a few symbol forms still require care. The rapid handwritten sheets are substantially less secure. In particular, the lower part of scan 100, the compact derived-category notes in scans 092–095, the revisions in scans 104 and 106–110, and the clipped lower writing of scan 113 contain low-confidence readings; the local doubt marks are part of the deliverable, not silent editorial repairs.

Whole scans and their supplied halves are read visually in one stream. Enlargements and rotations are used only for inspection; no OCR is used. Faint mirrored show-through on reused backs is distinguished from the face text and is transcribed from the corresponding clearly printed face when available. Scan 112 has no discernible author text, only archive foliation; it is retained as a page. Ink offsets, paper defects, and stains are not treated as words.
