Research review: possible exact scalar kernel after the twenty-chief layer
16 November 2026. Central-series reduction is checked; the final rank128
count/attainment is a separate step under investigation.

Let M be any finite actual central refinement of the new residual Q over
the original C. Its central kernel Z is C-trivial. As before, original
coefficient acyclicity/relative perfectness gives M_ab=A_n. Since Q has
class3, M has class at most4. Use the three Frobenius weight families at
each physical order-seven weight, after splitting the odd action.

The only possible invariant gamma4 pairing involves U_(3lambda) and
A_(4lambda). Write U=[T_(2lambda),a_lambda]. The actual bracket
[T_(2lambda),d_(4lambda)] is zero by physical/Frobenius support. Its lift
is central of nontrivial weight6lambda, hence zero. The actual bracket
[a_lambda,d_(4lambda)] lies in the ordinary central Z-block. Any lift of
that block lies in Z2(M), and Z2(M) commutes with M'. Jacobi in the
associated graded modulo gamma5=1 therefore kills [U,d]. This proves
gamma4(M)=1, including all three Frobenius rotations.

Modulo gamma3, the invariant part of the derived group is a quotient of
the same exterior-square coinvariants that already give the complete
old center M_n. It also surjects that old center. The A65 averaging/
exterior-square argument therefore kills the scalar kernel modulo
gamma3. Hence every new scalar direction belongs to gamma3(M).

Potential T_(2lambda)-A_(-2lambda) scalar triples must also be removed:
T is generated by two inputs at physical lambda, whereas -2lambda
belongs to the opposite physical point. The alternate ordinary brackets
in Jacobi vanish by that physical support, and their putative central
lifts have nontrivial weights. Thus these scalar triples are zero.
An old H-trivial central input pairs trivially by odd averaging. A
binary-s input paired with an ordinary bracket also vanishes by Jacobi,
since ordinary cups use only free cusp rows.

The remaining possible scalar triples use three free cusp inputs at
the same physical point, one each in the lambda,2lambda,4lambda families.
This supports a128-dimensional bound after the precise H-coinvariant and
Frobenius-descent calculation, but that numerical conclusion is not
proved by the weight list alone. In particular arithmetic weight sums
cannot replace the actual physical-coordinate support of the cup map.
