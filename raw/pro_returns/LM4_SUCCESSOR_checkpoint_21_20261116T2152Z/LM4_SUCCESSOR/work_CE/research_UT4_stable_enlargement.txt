The four ordered triples i<j<k of the four cusp cocycles suggest a
class-three UT4 extension with cubic coefficient S=V^(3), and local
cocycle a_i*t_jk+t_ij*a_k.  Here V^(3) means literal cubic monomial

Stability gate.
After gauging away the affine inner terms in the action, a degree-two
coordinate t_ij changes by an actual V^(2)-cocycle eta_ij.  The induced
change of the triple defining system is, in cohomology,

               [eta_ij] cup [e_k] + [e_i] cup [eta_jk],

with coordinate multiplication V^(2) tensor V -> V^(3).  Changes by
inner coboundaries do not enlarge this indeterminacy.  The complete
H1 space has eight classes.  A general stable upper bound is therefore
the32 ordinary classes e_r^[2] cup e_i, r=1..8 and i=1..4 cusp.
The four cusp classes are fixed and the entire eight-class space is
stable, so this span is stable under both known coefficient actions.

For the stored repaired A64 tuple the bound is smaller: every first-eight
coefficient vector in PCOR and CCOR is supported only on the four cusp
classes.  The full16 ordinary cusp-cup span is therefore sufficient.
The four positive and four canonical-c defining-system defect vectors
have exact coefficient rank7 overF8 in that16-space.  All its generators
are fixed in cohomology.  The small exact certificate is
code/check_UT4_stable_cup_span.py/.out and UT4_stable_cup_span.json.
This records a defining-system calculation, not an actual UT4 action
or a solved next negative norm.  A is retaining the full16-space to
avoid an unnecessary basis/minimality dependence.

Why the sixteen ordinary classes are genuinely new.
They can be tested through their actual residual commutator maps.
On inputs at a single monomial weight lambda, the new cup commutator
has output at lambda and is a combination of a_r^2*b_i+b_r^2*a_i.
For different rows, the functions a^2*b and a*b^2 are independent
overF8; for a single row, a^2*b+a*b^2 is nonzero.  Thus the16
coefficient classes have independent alternating residual maps.

They cannot be killed by the derived group already retained in A63.
An H-map from its old globally trivial center to S is zero.  Every
H-map V^(2)->V^(3) is d times partner-coordinate squaring.  Indeed an
additive polynomial on a weight coordinate has exponents1,2,4, and
odd-weight covariance requires2*2^j*mu=3lambda; the three disjoint
Frobenius weight orbits leave only j=1 and mu=-lambda.  Consequently
such a correction sends the old same-weight T bracket to the opposite
weight.  It cannot cancel a new ordinary commutator supported at the
original weight.  Restricting a hypothetical splitting to the residual
would require exactly such cancellation, so no nonzero coefficient
combination splits.  The same argument works for the larger32-span.

The actual source extension exists for each ordinary class: the cusp
factor e_i has value0 at s, so its source s-square is zero; the order3
source lift is chosen by coprime conjugation.  The commutator argument
shows the new simple residual image is nonzero, hence full.  Joint
independence gives the full retained sum by the semisimple module
quotient test.  Stability can then use the same injective transgression
as A63, since the residual abelianization remains the original A_n.

Independent explicit scalar-cocycle check.
For the local64-element group G={(a,z)}, law(a,z)(b,w)=(a+b,z+w+a²b),
A's formula is kappa_q=Tr((q+q²)a⁴w+q a⁶b+q² a⁴b³).  Its cocycle
identity checks directly: the polynomial part has coboundary
Tr((q+q²)a⁴b²c), because Tr(q a²b⁴c)=Tr(q²a⁴bc²); this cancels the
coboundary of the a⁴w term.  All terms have scalar weight7 and hence
are C7-invariant.  For q=1 the formula is the coboundary of Tr(a⁷),
by the two length-three Frobenius orbits of the six intermediate
binomial terms.  The triple commutator is
Tr((q+q²)c⁴(a²b+ab²)), so q moduloF2 gives two independent attained
central directions.  The independent exact checker
code/verify_cubic_scalar_cocycles.py/.out verifies every64³ cocycle
identity for all eight q, all seven scalar actions, the q=1 coboundary,
and attainment of both q=2,4 scalar coordinates by triple commutators.
This verifies the explicit lower bound; A's separate normalized-bar
calculation supplies the two-dimensional upper bound.

Actual lift of a degree-two shear.
Let F_L fix the a and ordinary z coordinates, send
 t_ij -> t_ij+sum_r L_ij,r a_r²,
and send each UT4 coordinate to
 u_ijk+sum_r[L_ij,r z_rk+L_jk,r(z_ri+a_i a_r²)].
The identities delta z_rk=a_r² b_k and
 delta(z_ri+a_i a_r²)=a_i b_r²
prove that this is an actual H-equivariant automorphism of the full
20-chief group; all terms have cubic monomial weight.  Its square is
one since a,z are fixed.  Consequently the actual A64 degree-two
corrections can be lifted without choosing an ambiguous Massey system.
