The actual first mixed-neck pair: radial vanishing arcs and a two-relator
block. CE research checkpoint21. This is NOT the whole shared edge group.

1. Exact marked horizontal paths

Put c_j=cot(j*pi/5), lambda_j=1-cos(2*j*pi/5),
f_j(Q)=lambda_j*(Q-i*c_j), and

  y0=(c1+c2)/2, delta=(c1-c2)/2>0,
  Q_*=i*y0, Q(x)=x+i*y0.

This boundary point belongs to the first mixed q arc, between zeta
and zeta^2, since c2<y0<c1. The selected upper Z punctures are
B1=1/f1, A2=f2, A3=f3, A4=f4 and the upper retained branch root.
For every x with ReQ=x and this fixed imaginary part, A2,A3,A4
have positive imaginary part and B1 also does. Their F partners
lie below the real axis. The branch roots solve Z+1/Z=2Q; since
ImQ=y0>0 neither root is real, and exactly one is upper.

The selected collision B1=A2 occurs at x=+x_c in the inner region
and x=-x_c in the outer region, where

  x_c^2=1/(lambda1*lambda2)-delta^2=(3+sqrt(5))/10>0.

The exact identities lambda1*lambda2=5/4 and
delta^2=(1-1/sqrt(5))/2 prove the equality. Every other mixed
collision has imaginary Q coordinate (c_i+c_j)/2, including i=j;
none equals y0 except the same unordered pair(1,2). Same-sign and
branch collisions are on the removed boundary ReQ=0. Thus the
open horizontal segments from Q_* to these two nodes meet no
other critical value. They are actual based vanishing paths.

2. Actual radial arcs, including the retained branch

Along these paths,

  A2=lambda2*(x+i*delta),
  B1=(x+i*delta)/(lambda1*(x^2+delta^2)).

For |x|<x_c they are two distinct positive multiples of the same
vector x+i*delta. The straight radial segment joining them shrinks
at the node. For j=3,4 the oriented cross product of that vector
with A_j is lambda_j*x*(c2-c_j), which has the sign of x. Thus
neither other A puncture crosses this radial segment.

The upper branch root cannot lie anywhere on its positive ray for
x!=0. If Z=k*(x+i*delta) with k>0, the real and imaginary parts
of Z+1/Z=2Q would give

  k+1/(k*(x^2+delta^2))=2,
  k-1/(k*(x^2+delta^2))=2*y0/delta.

Their difference requires a positive number to equal1-y0/delta,
which is negative. Near x=0 the branch lies on the same side of
the radial segment as A3,A4. For example its derivative at Q_*
is 1+y0/sqrt(1+y0^2), while its initial height divided by delta
is strictly larger. Continuity and the proved ray exclusion keep
it on that side throughout. The negative-x picture is reflection
in the imaginary axis, with all initial punctures fixed.

At x=0 the exact ordered upper heights are

  A2, A4, A3, branch_upper, B1.

The independent rational interval construction in
code/check_first_mixed_neck_arc.py places them respectively in
(95,96)/100, (153,155)/100, (212,214)/100, (216,217)/100,
and(275,276)/100. Every radical bound is obtained by rational
bisection with its squared inequalities checked. The input,
genuine output/error and exact interval records are retained as

  work_CE/code/check_first_mixed_neck_arc.py/.out/.err
  work_CE/next_symmetric_nodes/first_mixed_neck_arc.json.

Therefore the positive-x vanishing arc approaches the outer
right-hand arc joining the lowest and highest punctures, with
the middle three on its other side. The negative-x arc approaches
the reflected left-hand arc. No omitted-puncture threading is
assumed away: the literal upper-half-plane separation and the
displayed radial arcs supply it. This repairs the invalid generic
disk-transport inference preserved in mixed_neck_marking_limitations_21h.txt.

3. One simultaneous based planar free basis

Choose a common basepoint just below the upper end puncture b,
and common short local paths to b and to a point just above the
lower end puncture a. Let gamma_R and gamma_L run from the first
point to the second to the right and left of all three intervening
punctures. Their endpoints avoid a,b. Their closed difference
gamma_L*gamma_R^-1 is counterclockwise about precisely the middle
three punctures, excluding both endpoints.

Choose the standard disjoint interior meridian arcs so that its
positive boundary word is

  w=c1*c2*c3.

Here c1,c2,c3 name the three middle meridians in their boundary
order, not the cotangents from sections1--2. Put a equal to the
lower meridian reached along gamma_R, and b equal to the fixed
upper meridian. These five meridians form a geometric free basis
of the actual five-puncture disk. The left-path lower meridian is

  a_L=w*a*w^-1.

With the same chosen local commutator convention [x,y]=xyx^-1y^-1,
the two actual node relations can consequently be oriented as

  r_R=[a,b], r_L=[w*a*w^-1,b].

Conjugating the second defining relation by w^-1 gives the exact
equivalent pair [a,b], [a,w^-1*b*w]. Reversing a node-cell orientation
only inverts its relator and does not change its normal closure.
The branch meridian is one of the three middle factors and is
retained throughout. This is a new explicitly specified geometric
free basis; no uncomputed conversion to the old F21 array is claimed.
An independent planar-cut check has been requested.

4. Exact algebra of this two-relator block only

The product w=c1*c2*c3 is primitive in their rank-three free group:
(w,c2,c3) is a basis, with inverse c1=w*c3^-1*c2^-1. Thus the
quotient by precisely these two node relations is

  <a,b,w | [a,b], [a,w^-1*b*w]> * F(c2,c3).

Introducing d=w^-1*b*w identifies the first factor as the genuine
HNN extension of Z(a) times F(b,d) which identifies <b> with <d>
by the stable letter w. The cyclic associated subgroups are
injective. Under the homomorphism to Z sending w to1 and a,b to0,
the kernel has the Reidemeister--Schreier presentation

  < a_n,b_n (n in Z) | [a_n,b_n], [a_n,b_(n-1)] for all n >,
  a_n=w^n*a*w^-n, b_n=w^n*b*w^-n.

Its commutation graph is a bi-infinite bipartite path. The two
extra free generators contribute freely all their w translates.
This describes the two-relator block, not the quotient by the
other physical nodes, the other base handles, or the whole normal
relation family. In particular it does not prove that the actual
shared edge group has this presentation or that its map into either
bad regional vertex is injective. Those are the remaining gates.
