  inner bad support: winding_0 = winding_1;

It cannot exchange either square-root lift of that puncture.

Thus arbitrary lift transversals can be obtained by unrestricted

retained puncture1 is essential to the obstruction. This is not
a proof that every conceivable common disk or abstract subgroup

A tempting candidate is a quotient of the original F5 resembling

nodes forcing the distinguished puncture to commute with the other
three. This is only a candidate. One must identify the full based
vanishing meridians and show that the requisite three conjugated
peripherals have the needed simultaneous free-basis marking.
The physical incidence graph does not give that marking.

In particular, simply transporting the initial five-puncture disk
along a q path to a selected-selected collision does NOT show that
the actual vanishing arc lies inside that disk. The boundary may
thread between the two approaching punctures. An internal arc can
wind around omitted punctures, and its isotopy class need not agree
with the short local vanishing arc. The disk then need not extend
across the node in the required marked way. Therefore no nontrivial
element of the initial F5 kernel has yet been certified by that
argument alone.
