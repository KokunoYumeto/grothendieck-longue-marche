# Root review: C.44 and its relative pronilpotent strengthening

Date: 16 November 2026. The full original formal proof of C.44 was read and accepted. Its extension to every prime and the relative pronilpotent quotient is also accepted, subject to the explicit product comparison described below.

For a normalized regular pants involution sigma on F=Fhat(x,z), C.43 gives a unique g with sigma(g)g^-1=z. Put K=the closed normal closure of z and A=F/K=Zhat. The exact compact Alexander module is K/Phi_2(K)=F2[[A]][z]. The proof checks this with the actual cyclic-cover Schreier bases and their homology transition maps; extension of every continuous finite character from K to an open neighborhood supplies the required comparison.

Multiplication by T-1 is a topological isomorphism onto the augmentation ideal: the finite cyclic kernels are the constant vectors, and the transition from 2m to m kills them. The divided power D_a is consequently defined for every a in Zhat. The equation theta D_a=1 makes a odd and excludes divisibility by every odd prime by evaluation at its primitive roots in characteristic two. Thus a is a full profinite unit, not merely a dyadic unit.

The identical cyclic-cover argument works over Fp for each prime p, using m to pm to kill the free x-coordinate. Since multiplication by a permutes all exponents in A, the conjugates g^t z g^-t span Fp[[A]]. Pro-p Frattini therefore gives generation of F/O^p(K), even though its pro-p kernel need not have finite rank.

In F/R_nil(K), the kernel is the product of the distinct-prime pro-p factors. The closed subgroup generated by g,z maps onto A; its intersection with the kernel projects onto every Sylow factor by the same conjugates. A closed subgroup of a pronilpotent group is the product of its Sylow intersections, so this intersection is the whole kernel. The resulting endomorphism is surjective and hence an automorphism by finite generation and Hopficity. The generator equations give the asserted conjugacy.

This conjugacy belongs to Aut(F/R_nil(K)). It is not asserted to preserve the original three peripheral classes, and it does not prove that g,z generate the full F. Those restrictions remain material.
