## A complete one-direction intersection

Use the source-contained Nielsen basis of tangent_nielsen_marking.py:
U1=old x3^-1*x2^-1*x1*x2*x3, with U2=x2,U4=x4,U6=x6 and the
three retained old letters x3,x5,x7. Make the additional actual free-basis
change

 U=<U1,U2,U4,U6>, D=BC.

This preserves the killed free factor U and the retained filling. In this
basis the actual source monodromy alpha1 around q1 satisfies literally

 alpha1(D)=D, alpha1(U)=D^-1 U D.

The second equality is equality of subgroups, not merely normal closures.
After removing the displayed common conjugator, the four images form an
automorphism of U. The exact inverse expressions are stored and checked
by free substitution in tangent_block_transport.py/.out and
 tangent_infinity_action.json.

The actual source image I contains U. Conversely all four original source
meridians belong to <U,alpha1(U)>; this is also checked with explicit inverse
words. Hence I is precisely the subgroup generated by the translates of U
under the six actual base automorphisms (and their inverses). Its actual
compact version is the closure of that same discrete image.

For every integer n, alpha1^n(U)=D^-n U D^n. It follows that

 J=normalclosure_{U*<D>}(U) is contained in I.

Since D=BC is primitive in the retained free factor, V=U*<D> is an actual
rank-five free factor of F7. The filling F7->F3 restricts on V to the free
retraction V-><D>. Since I is contained in its ambient kernel R,

 I intersect V = J = ker(V-><D>).

The same equality holds in the actual full completed ambient group:

 closure(I) intersect V-hat = ker(V-hat -> <D>-hat).

Indeed the free-factor inclusion gives the full induced topology on V.
The closure of J in V-hat is exactly that displayed kernel, by the free
retraction and its quotient presentation. It is contained in closure(I),
while the opposite containment follows from closure(I) being inside the
ambient filling kernel. No full completion of abstract infinitely generated
J or R is substituted. This proves one complete retained direction on an
actual free factor; it does NOT prove that D normalizes all of I.

## Exact ambient inversion exchanges the two signs

There is an actual involution of the unordered surface X:

 sigma(q,T)=(1/q,T/q).

It preserves q!=0, q notin mu5, and T^2!=q; it sends the tangent line
indexed by u to the line indexed by u^-1. It therefore preserves the set of
four U lines and the retained line u1. Its action on the two source curves is

 S_plus: T=1  ->  S_minus: T=q.

In the original normalized positive-marginal coordinates
q=s^2, T=s(1+p)/(1-p), the literal map is

 (s,p) -> (1/s,p).

Before normalizing zeta, it is (s,p)->(zeta^2/s,p), with label u sent to
zeta^2/u. Since a(s)=(1-s)/(1+s) satisfies a(1/s)=-a(s), the positive section
p=a(s) maps to the negative section p=-a(s') at s'=1/s. This is an equality
of the actual rational maps, not an inferred symmetry of I_plus.

At the common q basepoint1/100, the positive and negative fiber basepoints
are T_plus=1 and T_minus=1/100. Sigma sends the positive basepoint to
(q,T)=(100,100) on S_minus. Specify the negative-source transport by the
q path

 100 -> 100+100i -> 100i -> 1/100, with T=q.

The negative source holes are0, mu5 and q=u^2/(2u-1), u!=1. The latter are
reciprocals of the positive source holes indexed by u^-1. The displayed
path avoids them (the exact rational-deformation distances are audited
separately). It supplies a literal based isomorphism from the positive
source and its kernel to the negative source and its kernel.

To view both marginal groups in the same actual fiber, use the fiber path

 T: 1 -> 2+2i -> 1/100+2i -> 1/100, at q=1/100.

Both roots have modulus1/10 and all five tangent points have modulus at
most101/200, so the first two segments lie outside them; the last vertical
segment has real part1/100, distinct from every fiber puncture. This is a
specified transport from the positive to the negative fiber basepoint.
Transporting the negative marginal back along this path gives a second
actual subgroup of the same F7. Sigma and these paths identify it with the
positive subgroup by an ambient automorphism, but do not assert equality
of the two subgroups. Their interaction belongs to the original joint-source
problem. In particular the inversion is not an additional positive-source
normalizer.

## Based infinity comparison: remaining algebraic gate

In the retained quotient the freshly computed infinity action is exactly

 A -> C^-1 B C,
 B -> A C A C^-1 A^-1,
 C -> A C A^-1.

It equals beta0 followed by beta1 in the convention beta0(beta1(x)).
This is checked on the three retained free generators. Its U-conjugator
indices are not one common word: the pair U1,U4 uses D^-1 B D, while
U2,U6 uses A. Thus the known D-strip does not directly remove all the
corrections from the infinity transport. A finite automorphism-word
identity preserving the entire orbit subgroup has not been found.
A whole-block image or a single peripheral membership statement cannot
be substituted for such an identity.

A potentially sufficient constructive route remains the free Schreier
basis R=F(U_{g,j}:g in F(A,B,C), j in{1,2,4,6}). Each actual base action
sends each such generator to a conjugate of another one, with explicitly
computable R-word corrections. A well-founded triangular choice of
source orbit words, one for every Schreier generator, would prove I=R
by induction if every conjugating word uses only earlier Schreier
letters. The present certificate records those corrections, not just
the index permutation. No such globally well-founded order has been
proved; the current supports involve both sides of candidate steps.
