# The unordered tangent-parabola bundle: an exact smaller marginal model

Research after B79 integration; no source-image equality is claimed.

## Actual twofold quotient and original coordinates

Let Y be the B78 normalized ordered surface. Its swap(v,w) acts freely,
since v=w is deleted. Put q=vw and T=(v+w)/2. The quotient is exactly

 X = {(q,T) in C^2: q notin {0} union mu5,
       T^2 != q, q-2uT+u^2 !=0 for every u in mu5}.

The inverse unordered pair consists of T plus/minus sqrt(T^2-q).
Thus Y->X is the unramified double cover of the retained discriminant
T^2-q. The five nonvertical lines q=2uT-u^2 are tangent to q=T^2 at
(T,q)=(u,u^2). Four lines (u!=1) are U; the u1 line is retained.

The original positive marginal has the second unramified double cover
s^2=q. Its coordinate change is T=s(1+p)/(1-p). The seven deleted fiber
points become

  T=+s,-s, (s^2+u^2)/(2u), u in mu5.

The old source p=(1-s)/(1+s) becomes exactly T=1. Both double-cover
characters extend after U filling: they are the winding characters of
q and T^2-q respectively, whose divisors remain retained. Hence the
actual ambient U-kernels belong to both covering subgroups and are
identified by the covering embeddings. The same holds on the source.
Passing to full completions uses only finite-index induced topology:
the actual compact kernels identify as well. No abstract completion
of an infinitely generated kernel is substituted.

## A genuine bundle, with no exceptional fibers left in the base

Projection q has base B=C\({0} union mu5) and fiber the plane minus
seven points

  +sqrt(q), -sqrt(q), T_u(q)=(q+u^2)/(2u).

The two square roots meet only at q0. Distinct T_u,T_v meet precisely
at q=uv in mu5. Finally

 T_u(q)^2-q = (q-u^2)^2/(4u^2),

so a line point meets a parabola root only at q=u^2 in mu5. Thus no
collision remains in B. Local analytic root choices and disjoint-disk
isotopies give actual bundle trivializations. A continuous far section
is T=|q|+2. In particular the group is F7 semidirect F6, with a finite
presentation of13 generators and42 action relations after a specified
basis is chosen. This does not assert that the source is that far section.

After U filling the fiber has precisely the three retained points
+sqrt(q),-sqrt(q),(q+1)/2. The actual source T=1 is a global section of
this filled bundle: its possible collisions require q1, already deleted.
Before filling, its four additional holes are

  q_u=2u-u^2, u in mu5\{1}.

They are distinct because their pair difference is (u-v)(2-u-v).
Their absolute values equal |2-u|>1, so none is an old base puncture.
The actual source is therefore F10, filling onto F6, and its kernel
maps into R=ker(F7->F3). This is exactly the previous marginal question,
with four source meridians and six old base actions instead of eight
and eleven. Its completed source image remains the actual compact
image of the kernel in F10-hat.

The source double covers have characters q and1-q. Each is unramified
on this source and extends after its four U holes are filled. They
recover the old rank19 sources, their rank11 filled sources and their
same actual kernels. The common fourfold cover has source rank37;
these ranks independently check the cover bookkeeping.

## Exact local monodromy, before choosing a common based system

At q0 the two retained parabola roots exchange in a positive half twist;
the other five points lie near the distinct u/2. At q=u^2, the line T_u
and the root near u have difference of order two in q-u^2, hence give
a positive two-strand fourth power. The remaining four line points
form two colliding pairs with products u^2. Their centers are
u*cos(2pi/5) and u*cos(4pi/5); both collisions are transverse, giving
two disjoint two-strand full twists. The remaining parabola root is
near -u. Thus the total local exponent is8, on three disjoint disks.
These are local forms only. Conjugation by the actual source-based
whiskers remains essential before deriving source-kernel identities.

The automorphism (q,T)->(u^2 q,uT) permutes the five tangent lines,
preserves the ambient bundle and acts on the local collision pattern.
It moves the chosen source T1 to Tu, so it is not silently treated
as an automorphism of the original source subgroup.

## Scope

This reduction is a smaller exact presentation/geometry for the same
problem. It does not prove source-kernel normality, subgroup injection,
induced topology of an infinite-index source image, or K=1. In
particular the twofold source enlargements cannot be used as unexplained
additional normalizers: their cover maps and their basepoint transports
must be retained.
