# Manuscript-explicit questions, admitted inputs, and revised proof steps

## Scope and interpretation

This coverage register searches **all 36 frozen cote 12 page fragments** and checks the mathematical contexts of the hits; it does not reopen or modify any frozen reading. Locations refer to the retained scan number and, where given, the line number in `pages/MPL_12_099-149_12_scanNNN.tex`, not an inferred manuscript line. The search included literal question marks and `vérifi`, `admi`, `admet`, `sembl`, `probab`, `conject`, `facile`, `évident`, `sans doute`, `peut`, `pourra`, `devrait`, `je`, `on`, `dévissage`, `trivial`, `prouve`, `démontr`, `vrai`, `suppos`, `Deligne`, `Lefschetz`, and `résol`, followed by manual exclusion of lexical false positives such as *admissible*.

**A** below contains genuine manuscript questions, reservations, or requests to verify a mathematical step. **B** records explicitly admitted or invoked mathematical inputs, with admission distinguished from citation of a theorem. **C** is a separate coverage register for abridged proof transitions and unfinished mathematical tasks: words such as “trivial” and “évident” are **not** misreported as admissions of doubt. **D** records substantive visible revisions and cancellations; a deletion proves that wording was withdrawn, not that the author established its negation. A transcriptional `\unsure{...}` is not itself a manuscript question, and editorial uncertainty alone never qualifies a passage for A.

Research-note IDs refer to the existing working reports: `Wnn` in `research_weights.md`, `Mnn` in `research_context_motives.md`, and `LNnn`/`SMnn` in `research_linear_nonlinear.md`; those reports supply the context searches, source comparisons, and results-register connections. Quotations retain the selected source's wording and doubt macros; Markdown table pipe escaping has no significance for the transcription.
