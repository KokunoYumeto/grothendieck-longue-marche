## Limits and attribution

The primary reader's web screenshot requests for the separate Grothendieck *Standard conjectures* PDF returned image placeholders rather than inspectable image pixels; no visual check of those screenshots is claimed. The printed lettering used in M17 was independently verified in Kleiman's primary text, and the Grothendieck paper remains the historical companion reference supplied by the research contribution. Durfee (1983) remains an explicitly uninspected full-text lead; the Durfee–Saito (1990) result is the separately checked theorem.

The Legendre local/global counterexamples and the double-blowup calculation are deductions made in this research phase, not examples claimed to have been quoted from Katz or Riou. The finite-flat-group counterexamples are published Vasiu–Zink results; the all-prime comparison with scans 140–142 is our explicit deduction. The normalization and dimension-type tests already appear in Hua and are credited to that account.
