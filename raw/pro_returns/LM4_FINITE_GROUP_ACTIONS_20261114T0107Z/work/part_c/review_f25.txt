1. Actual images and normal generation
The surjective continuous map D^+ -> R has compact domain and Hausdorff
target. Images of the relevant closed normal subgroup are closed and
normal; the two inclusions in F.25.5 follow from the image of conjugates
and from inverse images of closed normal subgroups. Thus M normally
generates N. Applying the scalar map to the finite abelian target gives
chi(K)=chi(R)=C. When C=1 the entire N is an abelian translation group,
so M=N immediately. This proves that case without any extra scalar-field
assumption.

2. The actual translation intersection
The set U is an additive subgroup of a characteristic-p field, hence is
an F_p-vector subspace. For each c in C, the proved equality chi(K)=C
provides an actual element (b,c) of M. Its conjugation on translations
is exactly multiplication by c. Therefore U is stable under F_p[C].
That ring is a finite integral domain contained in k. Multiplication by
each nonzero element is injective and hence surjective, producing its
inverse. Thus F_p[C]=F_p(C)=k1, and U is k1-linear. This reasoning also
includes C=1, where k1=F_p.

3. The scalar lift and constructed complement
For C nontrivial the proof establishes cyclicity of C directly from
commuting prime-power components and the polynomial root bound in a
field. The chosen scalar generator c has an actual lift m=(b,c) in M.
Its explicit fixed point is z=b/(1-c). Conjugation by tau_z(w)=w+z
takes m to (0,c) and fixes every translation. This constructs the
complement inside M_z itself. No splitting of an auxiliary extension
is presumed. An element of scalar c^j multiplied by (0,c^{-j}) on the
right has translation coordinate b in U. Conversely the actual U
translations and powers of (0,c) give every pair in U semidirect C.
Both directions of the exact equality M_z=U semidirect C are proved.

4. Ambient scalar linearity and exact coefficient maps
Under the stated core hypothesis U is a k0-subspace. The finite field k
is finite-dimensional over k0; extending a basis of U gives the displayed
annihilator identity U=intersection ker(ell), with ell:k -> k0 k0-linear.
The character chi takes values in k0^*, exactly by definition of k0.
Therefore ell(d_z) satisfies the crossed-homomorphism equation with that
same character; this is precisely where ambient scalar linearity is used.
The coefficient maps and all target fields are finite and discrete, so
continuity is retained. The actual decomposition of M_z shows d_z(K) is
contained in U, making every ell(d_z) vanish on K. Since chi(R)=C is
nontrivial, F.24 applies over the finite field k0 and forces each of these
cocycles to vanish on all V. The annihilator identity consequently gives
d_z(V) subset U. For elements of R the scalar lies in C, so N_z subset
U semidirect C=M_z. The original K subset R gives the reverse inclusion,
and the explicit inverse affine conjugation transports equality back.

The equality k1=k0 supplies the core hypothesis by the proved k1-linearity
of U. Together with the separately proved C=1 case this gives F.25.3.
For the prime field every generated scalar subfield is F_p, so the
argument applies for every prime, including2 and3, with no exception.

For k1 properly contained in k0, the proof retains only the k1-linearity

commutes with the full ambient scalar action. The required k0-linearity
is explicitly named in F.25.10. Thus the outstanding class and the full
marginal/root-adjoining equalities remain accurately delimited.
