C.90 research and proof record

Result proved
For the actual C.81 data with compact quotient N=O/Q, every positive based
homotopy group of the original boundary-fixed centralizer vanishes. This
includes the original compactification, all individual Q labels, maps and
inverses, and nonorientable quotients. No extension of a coordinate change
or of q to the original circle is used.

Exact proof route
The one-orbit evaluation bundle from C.81 identifies the positive homotopy
groups with those of the subgroup fixing one specified x over p. A based
sphere in that subgroup descends to the pointed quotient identity component.
The quotient is closed and aspherical because its universal cover is O.
Yagasaki's Lemma2.1(i), with fixed set {p}, gives a contractible quotient
identity component. Its full exception list excludes this pair: sphere and
projective plane have nonzero pi2, while the torus and Klein-bottle exceptions
have empty fixed set. The exact item used is (i), not (ii).

Normalize a chosen contraction using J_r(b)=C_{1-r}(b)C_{1-r}(1)^{-1}.
Lift J_r(b_xi) through the original covering from identity. Independently
lifting the inverse family and using uniqueness proves the inverse equations.
The same uniqueness retains every Q label. The track of the fixed lift x
stays in its discrete fiber; it is constant. This proves that the final lift
equals the original a_xi, since both lifts fix x. The sphere basepoint stays
identity. Finally C.88's exact compact-family extension criterion applies
with K=N and empty tail. The resulting original closed-disk family provides
the required filling, and the C.81 based homotopy carries it back to the
arbitrary original sphere.

Scope
The theorem asserts vanishing of based homotopy groups in all positive
dimensions. Only the specified based sphere's descent is placed in the
pointed identity component. The proof does not identify all components of
the original centralizer with that component and does not construct a single
continuous contraction of the entire original centralizer. For noncompact N,
the tail condition of C.88 still has to be proved for any proposed filling.

Further source probe toward noncompact quotients
The original publisher PDF of Yagasaki, Groups of uniform homeomorphisms
of covering spaces, J. Math. Soc. Japan66(4)(2014),1227-1248,
DOI10.2969/jmsj/06641227, was reopened. Theorem1.1 and Corollary1.1 on
printed1228 explicitly require a compact quotient manifold. The existing
bibliography entry C_YagasakiUniform2014 already records this restriction.
No extra consequence for the noncompact original quotient is used here.
URL https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmath/66/4/66_1227/_pdf

Also opened: Yagasaki, Local and end deformation theorems for uniform
embeddings, arXiv:1301.3265v3 (2014), subsequently Topology Appl.239(2018),
191-225, DOI10.1016/j.topol.2018.02.023. The introduction, Theorem1.3,
Definition5.1 and the stated scope of Theorem5.1 were inspected. Its end
theorem uses finitely many isolated proper product ends with property(ED).
Definition5.1 requires a proper metric on each product end. C.88 provides
the specific bounded d_Q metric; no suitable proper-metric replacement
with the required displacement and equivariance comparisons has been proved.
These hypotheses remain to be checked before that end deformation is used.
This paper is a recorded search lead only, not an input to C.90.
