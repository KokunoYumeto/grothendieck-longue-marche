1. The local group and its support condition.

Properness of C_O on O makes the orbit quotient Hausdorff.
The image of the fixed compact D is compact and closed, so
its inverse image C_O D is closed in O. Fixing its complement
pointwise is a closed subgroup condition in the actual local
homeomorphism group. Commutation with the full local centralizer
and each pointwise quotient equation are also closed conditions.
They are preserved under composition and inversion.

For every homeomorphism, its fixed-point set is exactly that
of its inverse; hence Mov(a^{-1})=Mov(a), with no replacement
or enlargement of the prescribed saturation. Composing each
quotient equation with a^{-1} proves the inverse equations.

2. Independence of the operator and exact algebraic transport.

Each H translate of O is an entire open G orbit. Two such
translates are either equal or disjoint. In the equality case,
the restriction of h_2^{-1}h_1 belongs to the actual image of
the operator stabilizer in C_O. Commutation with the full
local centralizer therefore makes the two transported maps
identical. An operator fixing O pointwise contributes no
additional ambiguity.

Each transported map preserves its particular open orbit;
the rule on the complement is the identity. Thus these rules
give an algebraic bijection, with the stated algebraic inverse.
They respect composition on every piece. The union of pieces
is H-invariant, and conjugation by any original operator carries
each rule to the correspondingly labelled rule, proving exact
H commutation before any continuity conclusion.

3. The compact saturation supplies the required convergent point.

For a compact parameter family, a sequence of moving points
approaching the complement can be written
u_n=h_n q_n y_n with y_n in the one fixed compact set D.
After a subsequence, y_n converges to y in D, which is still
inside O. Openness of the transitive L orbit map gives
l_n -> 1 with l_n y=y_n; this is a sequential lifting
consequence of openness and requires no continuous local
section.

Every q_n commutes with L restricted to O by C.55. The
operators h_n commute globally with L by closure of the
original commutation relation. Thus m_n=h_n q_n is an
actual continuous L-equivariant map O -> plane. In particular
m_n(y)=l_n^{-1}u_n -> z. No q_n is extended to the plane
or replaced by a global approximation.

The points m_n(y) are fixed by L_y, so their limit is too.
C.42's evaluation homeomorphism gives convergence of these
actual equivariant maps uniformly on compact subsets to
Phi_z^y. Its image is the actual orbit Gz. That orbit
cannot be open: an open G orbit about z would contain all
late u_n while being disjoint from their union U.

4. Exact source basepoint change and the frontier value.

Choose l with l x=y. The stabilizers satisfy L_y=l J l^{-1}.
For every l' in L, both maps
Phi_z^y and Phi_{l^{-1}z}^x take l'y=l'lx to l'z.
Thus they are exactly the same map on O. The point
l^{-1}z is fixed by J. For a nontrivial limit orbit, C.42
and C.53 retain its intrinsic type and the same actual
quotient map. Hence the original pointwise quotient
condition applies. For a point orbit the limit map is
constant and needs no extra equation.

The image of the compact set K x D under local evaluation
(lambda,y) -> a_lambda(y) is compact in O, and
a_{lambda_n}(y_n) -> a_lambda(y). Uniform convergence of
m_n on this compact set therefore gives
E_D(a_{lambda_n})(u_n) -> Phi_z^y(a_lambda(y))=z.
Commutation of q_n with a_{lambda_n} is exactly what puts
the displayed image in this form.

Terms fixed by their transported maps already converge
to z. Any subsequence of moving terms has the same
compact-point argument and a further subsequence with
that limit. This proves the conclusion for the whole
sequence. Sequences in the complement are fixed from
the outset. Joint continuity on the entire plane follows.

5. Inverses and the actual topological embedding.

The local inverse family is continuous in the specified
group topology and has the identical moving-point
saturation. The same compact-parameter proof applies to
it, including its own compact image of K x D. Therefore
the algebraic inverse is a jointly continuous family
of inverse homeomorphisms.

For a convergent sequence of local maps and its limit,
the parameter set consisting of that sequence and limit
is compact metrizable. The preceding argument gives
convergence of the global maps and their inverses in
the actual compact-open group topology. Both groups
are metrizable, so this proves continuity of E_D.
Restriction to O is continuous on the subgroup preserving
O, and it recovers the original map exactly. This proves
the asserted continuous inverse onto the image.

Only D was compact in this proof. No compactness of
O/C_O, finite number of H translates, or properness of
the original global H action is used.

6. The two-partition identity path component.

A based path in the full pointwise quotient kernel has
a uniquely based source-cover lift. The equality
q_i F_i tilde(a_s)=q_i F_i holds for each chosen target
cover. For a line target it is equality of the real
coordinates. For a circle target the two coordinates
differ by a deck integer, continuous on the connected
parameter/source product and zero at the initial time.
That integer is therefore zero.

Both lifted quotient coordinates remain unchanged.
C.45 makes their pair injective, so the lifted isotopy,
and then the downstairs path, is the identity. This
argument proves the stated identity-path-component
claim, even without the additional compact-support or
centralizer conditions. It makes no assertion about
other components with possible discrete deck increments.

7. Relative scope.

An open representative outside the invariant closed
region X_flat has all its H translates outside that
region, so the extended map fixes X_flat pointwise.
The final limitation is also exact: transitivity makes
the union of the original generating loop's moving sets
the whole open orbit. One fixed compact quotient support
for that entire loop would therefore make the orbit
quotient compact. The proof supplies localized factors
and their extensions; it does not supply their required
fragmentation or convergence for the full C.12 problem.

No numerical certificate is needed for these topological
and group-map statements.
