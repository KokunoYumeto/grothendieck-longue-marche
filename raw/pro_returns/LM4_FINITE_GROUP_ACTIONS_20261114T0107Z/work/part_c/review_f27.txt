8. THE INVARIANT-KERNEL ARGUMENT AND THE PRECISE TRACE KERNEL

The kernel of summed H1 restriction is invariant under all eleven
actual source operators. A nonzero such kernel contains an invariant
subspace T of least positive dimension. Each coordinate projection

Its image has no proper nonzero invariant subspace: the inverse
image of one would contradict minimality of T. Essentiality in Q_j
puts L_j in that image, so the image equals L_j. All coordinates
of T consequently lie in their L_j. But the actual evaluation
Vandermonde map is injective on the entire direct sum of those
lines. This contradicts T being in the summed restriction kernel.
The argument requires finite-dimensional minimality, not complete
reducibility.

Zero summed restriction at the cocycle level now makes each d_j
principal. The sum of their restrictions is
(chi|K-1) sum_j b_j. Nontrivial C makes this zero exactly when
sum_j b_j=0. Each principal parameter is unique because chi_j
is nontrivial. Hence the kernel dimension is m-1 and the rank
is exactly 7m-(m-1)=6m+1.

A trace-zero restricted cocycle produces the tuple
(d^(q1^j))_j with zero summed restriction. Its zeroth member is
therefore principal, d=b(chi-1). Since chi(K) is nontrivial and
lies in k1, trace vanishing gives Tr(b)=0. Conversely these
principal cocycles have zero trace restriction. This proves
exactly the F26 trace-kernel equality for the present locus.
