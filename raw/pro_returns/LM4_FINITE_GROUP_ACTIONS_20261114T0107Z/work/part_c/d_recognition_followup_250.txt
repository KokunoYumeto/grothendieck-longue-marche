A. Exact correspondence between the two D75 reflection labels
-------------------------------------------------------------
Retain the original F, c, v, the unique g with g v g^{-1}=c v, and
  a_(mu,0)=c^((mu-1)/2) g,
  a_(mu,1)=c^((mu+1)/2) g^{-1}
from D75. For i=0,1 define the actual compact solution space
  T_i = {(mu,k) in Zhat^times times F:
          k (c^(-mu) a_(mu,i)) k^{-1} = a_(mu,i)}.
The profinite exponent maps, group operations, and equality relation make
these closed subspaces of the indicated compact product.

There are explicit mutually inverse continuous maps
  Phi: T_1 -> T_0,
       (mu,k) -> (-mu, c^(-(mu+1)/2) k g),
  Psi: T_0 -> T_1,
       (nu,l) -> (-nu, c^((1-nu)/2) l g^{-1}).
All half-exponents are the same full Zhat half-exponents as in D75.
In particular the original c is retained in both source and target equations.

Proof of the maps. Put d=(mu+1)/2 and b=a_(-mu,0)=c^(-d)g. Then
  a_(mu,1)=c^d b^{-1} c^{-d}.
Conjugating the T_1 equation by c^{-d}, and setting k'=c^{-d}kc^d,
gives
  k' c^{-mu} b^{-1} k'^{-1}=b^{-1}.
Taking inverses gives k' b c^mu k'^{-1}=b. Set l=k'b=c^{-d}kg.
The literal product calculation
  l(c^mu b)l^{-1}
     =k'b(c^mu b)b^{-1}k'^{-1}
     =k'b c^mu k'^{-1}
     =b
is exactly the T_0 equation with multiplier nu=-mu.
All operations reverse: from this T_0 equation, put k'=l b^{-1},
take inverses and conjugate by c^d. The resulting k is
  c^d k' c^{-d}=c^d l g^{-1}.
This is exactly Psi, and substitution in the two formulas proves both
composites are the identity. Their continuity follows from the actual
continuous group and exponent operations.

Thus both labelled solution spaces are related by these explicit maps.
The D75 existence question is precisely nonemptiness of T_0 (and equally
of T_1). This calculation establishes no nonemptiness assertion. It also
does not alter the two model maps or the full outside-centralizer/root
constructions already given in D75.23--26.
