Exact proof route to audit
--------------------------
1. Strengthen D75.14's transpose-transgression argument to a perfect
localized absolute-relative cup pairing for M and M^dual. The target trace
is the quotient edge map onto the surviving surface orientation row.
For trivial coefficients that row survives because the absolute
transgression is zero by the original section, and the relative one is
its transpose. On the two surviving graded pieces, the pairings are
coker(d) times ker(d') and ker(d) times coker(d'), both perfect.

2. Brown restriction has the original cusp factor and other normalizer
factor. Let e be the inverse of the idempotent (0,1). D75.19 gives exactly
zero restriction of e to BOTH actual cusp occurrences. Apply e to the
localized long exact sequence: j:eR->eA is an actual isomorphism. Its
inverse is any relative lift followed by multiplication by e; the
ambiguity is killed because the connecting image comes from the cusp.
The perfect absolute-relative pairing then gives a perfect Frobenius
pairing on the other normalizer factor, using its actual cup product.

3. For ANY finite continuous E-set X, let M=F2[X]. In B times T, the
literal T-fixed basis elements form Q_X=F2[X^T]. The complementary
T-two-point orbits contribute no cohomology above degree one. The exact
localized other factor is
  U_other(M)=H0(B,Q_X) direct-sum H1(B,Q_X),
via q*u^n + inf(z)*u^{n-1}. Its restriction to T is exactly (q,z)->q,
with image Q_X^B. The H1 summand has zero product with itself because
cd_2(B)<=1, so the perfect pairing gives dim H1<=dim H0.

4. Decompose X^T into actual B-orbits. Shapiro gives one H1(B_i,F2)
summand for each open stabilizer B_i. Every B_i maps under chi onto a
finite-index d_i Zhat, giving the explicit nonzero character chi/d_i mod2.
Thus each summand has dimension>=1, while dim H0 is the number of orbits.
Equality is forced. Every open B0 occurs: choose normal open N in E,
N<=F and N intersect B<=B0; use X=E/(B0*T*N) and its identity coset.
This proves the assertion for all actual open B0.

5. Choose a Sylow pro-2 subgroup P containing <a^{e2}>. Two independent
characters of P would extend to P*N0 for a suitable normal open N0 in B,
contradicting the open-subgroup assertion. All finite 2-group quotients
of P are therefore cyclic, so P is procyclic. Its map onto Z2 is an
isomorphism and P=<a^{e2}>. The kernel of chi_2 meets every conjugate
Sylow subgroup trivially, hence is pro-odd; K is its actual subgroup.

Finite coefficients and the exact remaining map
-----------------------------------------------
For every finite F2[B]-module Q, averaging over K's finite odd image
makes K-invariants exact. The actual inflation comparison is
  Hn(Zhat,Q^K) -> Hn(B,Q),
an isomorphism, and restriction to A=<a> is induced by Q^K inclusion Q.
For Q_X=F2[X^T], Q_X^K is the actual permutation module on K-orbits,
embedded by orbit sums; all orbit lengths are odd. The original pairing
restricts to the ordinary orbit-sum basis pairing.

The equality B=A is equivalent to surjectivity for every finite E-set X
of the actual coefficient map
  Q_X^B -> Q_X^A.
Every nonidentity k in K has an explicit detecting coefficient: choose
normal open N<=F in E with k outside A*T*N and let X=E/(A*T*N). Its
identity-coset vector is A-fixed and T-fixed but moved by k. Hence it
belongs to Q_X^A and not to Q_X^B. This is the surviving restriction map;
the cusp restriction is zero on the other factor and does not force it
onto.
