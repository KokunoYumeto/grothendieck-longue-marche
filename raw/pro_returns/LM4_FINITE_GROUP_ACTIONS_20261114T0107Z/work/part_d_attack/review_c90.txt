1. Quotient and exceptional pairs
--------------------------------
The hypotheses retained from C.81 make O -> N a covering of a connected
surface without boundary. O is the actual open disk and is simply
connected and contractible. Covering invariance of higher homotopy gives
pi_2(N)=0. This excludes S^2 and RP^2 by their actual universal covers.
The compact quotient triangulation is finite; subdividing the simplex
containing p, or refining its coordinate neighborhood, makes p a vertex.
Thus (N,{p}) is an actual compact PL pair of the form required by Yagasaki.

Lemma 2.1(i), page 2, lists the full compact exceptions. The closed
exceptions are the indicated sphere pairs, unpointed torus, unpointed
Klein bottle, and the indicated projective-plane pairs. The sphere and
projective plane have just been excluded. The specified point excludes
the empty-fixed-set torus and Klein-bottle pairs. The four exceptions
having boundary cannot be this N. The proof on page 3 explicitly applies
the cited compact results to the full homeomorphism group in its
compact-open topology. Hence H_p is contractible, including the two
aspherical exceptional unpointed surfaces once this point is fixed.
