Explicit comparison of the two coproducts
----------------------------------------
Take the actual groups
  P_all=C2(a)*C2(b) in the full profinite category,
  P_2=C2(a2)*_pro2 C2(b2) in the pro-2 category.
There are explicit mutually inverse maps identifying
  P_all = Zhat(t) semidirect_-1 C2(a), t=ba,
  P_2   = Z2(t2) semidirect_-1 C2(a2), t2=b2 a2.
For the first equality, send a to (0,1), b to (1,1), and in the
reverse direction send (lambda,epsilon) to (ba)^lambda a^epsilon.
The latter map is a homomorphism because a(ba)a=(ba)^(-1).
Both composites fix the displayed generators. The same two formulas
over Z2 give the second equality by the pro-2 coproduct universal
property. All maps are continuous.

The factor-preserving comparison map is the surjection
  kappa:P_all -> P_2,
  kappa(a)=a2, kappa(b)=b2,
  kappa(lambda,epsilon)=(lambda_2,epsilon).
Its exact kernel, with the displayed embeddings, is
  ker(kappa)=(product over odd primes l of Z_l) x {0}.        (W1)
For example the 3-primary idempotent e_3 in Zhat gives a nonidentity
kernel element t^e_3.

An executed finite witness is the full continuous quotient
  P_all -> S3, a->(12), b->(23).
The product t=ba maps to (132), of order three; the two factor images
generate all six elements of S3. Hence t^e_3 has that same nonidentity
image. This quotient does not factor through kappa, since a continuous
finite image of P_2 is a 2-group. This proves the nontrivial comparison
kernel by an explicit actual homomorphism as well as by (W1).

The relative projective-pair property holds for this actual P_2
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Here is a direct finite lifting argument in Wilkes Definitions 6.1--6.2,
with no use of his Theorem 6.9. Let pi:B->A be a finite surjection,
phi:P_2->A a continuous homomorphism, and sigma_i:C2(i)->B the two
specified local lifts. Replace A by phi(P_2), a 2-group, and B by its
inverse image. Put K=ker(pi). A 2-Sylow subgroup S of B maps onto A,
so B=SK. Each sigma_i(C2(i)) is a 2-subgroup of B. Sylow conjugacy
puts it into S by some element of B; writing that element as s k
with s in S and k in K shows that a K-conjugation already puts it
into S. Thus choose k_i in K with
  sigma'_i=k_i sigma_i k_i^(-1):C2(i)->S.
The actual pro-2 coproduct universal property gives gamma:P_2->S
extending these two homomorphisms. Composing with S->B supplies
  pi gamma=phi,
  gamma|C2(i)=k_i sigma_i k_i^(-1).
The first equality holds on both factors, which topologically generate
P_2. These are the exact relative lifting equalities; the inverse k_i
are Wilkes's displayed local comparison conjugators. Thus this pair
satisfies the projective profinite-pair property of his part (II).

Interpreting part (III) literally as a full coproduct, even with an
additional free pro-2 factor, would give a full continuous quotient
onto S3: use (12),(23) on the two C2 factors and kill that extra
factor. The actual P_2 has no such quotient. The comparison (W1)
and the preceding lifting proof therefore exhibit the precise failure
of that literal full-coproduct reading. No replacement source wording
or conjecture about the author's intention is used.

Application scope for the D74 map
---------------------------------
Proposition 6.8's actual proof and its pro-p coproduct remain usable in
their stated pro-p setting. They do not identify the actual full P3
or P4 in the pants construction with any pro-2 completion. The frozen
note obtains its full section i using its separately proved Haran
lifting argument. The remaining full image of rho i is not supplied
by the phrase in Theorem 6.9(III).
