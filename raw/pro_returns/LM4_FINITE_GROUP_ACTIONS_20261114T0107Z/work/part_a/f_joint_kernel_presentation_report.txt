The compact generator topology is retained
------------------------------------------
For a profinite space T, F_c(T) is constructed from the abstract free
group on its underlying set using only finite quotient maps for which
T->finite group is continuous. Its inverse limit has the universal
property for continuous maps from T to profinite groups.

For a finitely generated free profinite Q and a finite set I, left
translation on Q times I gives a jointly continuous action on F_c(QxI).
Every continuous finite-valued map on QxI factors through Q/N times I
for some open normal N. Its translates have finite orbit; intersecting
the finitely many translated quotient kernels gives invariant open
subgroups refining every quotient. These prove the required joint
continuity, not merely pointwise continuity of automorphisms.
