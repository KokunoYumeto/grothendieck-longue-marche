1. The literal local exchange and the word convention
----------------------------------------------------
Use the oriented projected plane w=P*z, P=1+371*i/1000. It is an
orientation-preserving real-linear coordinate, with inverse w/P. After
straightening a configuration with distinct projected real coordinates,
consecutive points are placed on the real axis in increasing order.
Let c be their midpoint and a>0 their half separation. With
  x(t)=2t-1,   y(t)=1-x(t)^2=4t(1-t),
the explicit positive exchange is
  w_left(t)=c+a*(x(t)+i*y(t)),
  w_right(t)=c-a*(x(t)+i*y(t)),       0<=t<=1.
It exchanges the left and right points, the left point passing above.
Its relative projected coordinate is a*(4t-2), so there is exactly one
crossing, at t=1/2, of height difference 2a>0. Thus the geometry producer
assigns this path the letter +sigma_j, by its literal height rule.

The pair never collides: writing x=2t-1,
  |x+i(1-x^2)|^2=x^4-x^2+1=(x^2-1/2)^2+3/4.
For |x|<=1 this norm is at most 1. The derivative of its argument is
  -2(1+x^2)/(x^4-x^2+1)<0.
The left-point argument moves from pi to zero, so the exchange is
clockwise. A disk slightly larger than the interval between these
consecutive points avoids every other real-axis point. The pair motion
extends there by a radial angular isotopy and an interior radial scaling,
equal to the identity near that disk's boundary. This gives an actual
supported isotopy. The reverse exchange is sigma_j^{-1}.

The same definition works after the chart is carried back by its inverse.
A generic path decomposes into these exchanges and intervals inside a
fixed projection-order chamber. Such a chamber is convex: it is defined
by strict linear inequalities on the real coordinates, with imaginary
coordinates unrestricted. Its comparison paths are therefore unique up
to endpoint-preserving homotopy. Adjacent inverse crossings cancel, and
the local three-point exchange gives the usual Artin braid relation.
These actual generators identify the configuration braid group with its
Artin presentation. Both directions send the named generator to the
named path/presentation generator. The local polynomial exchange fixes
which of the two inverse geometric exchanges is called sigma_j.

All written path products are read from left to right in time. On the
abstract free group with ordered basis X_1,...,X_n define
  phi_j(X_j)=X_j*X_(j+1)*X_j^{-1},
  phi_j(X_(j+1))=X_j,
and fix the other basis elements. Define phi_{b1...br} as
phi_b1 composed with ... composed with phi_br, exactly as the producer
does. The substitutions and their inverse substitutions satisfy the
Artin presentation relations, so they give a homomorphism from the
specified geometrically generated braid group into Aut(F_n). This
statement defines its convention on the geometric generators. The actual
fiber coordinate, and the section action in that coordinate, are derived
below; no conventional drawing of the abstract X_i is required.

2. The actual point-pushing basis and the two inverse coordinate maps
------------------------------------------------------------------
Place n old points q_1<...<q_n and one moving point q_(n+1)>q_n on the
real line. Forgetting the last point has actual fiber the plane punctured
at q_1,...,q_n, based at q_(n+1). The Fadell--Neuwirth injection identifies
its fundamental group with the last-strand kernel. Write
  beta_i=sigma_n ... sigma_(i+1) sigma_i^2
                         sigma_(i+1)^{-1} ... sigma_n^{-1}.

lasso around q_i. The final inverse word returns that lower whisker.
Deleting the last strand contracts the remaining old motion inside the
old projection-order chambers. Holding those old points fixed in that
contraction identifies beta_i with the literal lower-whiskered clockwise
lasso in the fiber. The lower whiskers form the usual embedded tree from
the rightmost basepoint to the n punctures. Cutting along that tree gives
a simply connected region, so these n lassos are an actual free basis.
Their clockwise orientation is part of this statement.

For a pure (n+1)-strand braid b, the substitution sends
  phi_b(d)=W_b*d*W_b^{-1},      d=X_(n+1).
Any two such W_b differ on the right by a power of d, whose image under
the filling d=1 is trivial. Thus w_b=fill_d(W_b) is well-defined. Put
u_b=w_b^{-1}, and let alpha_b be the filled substitution on F_n.
Substitution, with the displayed composition convention, gives exactly
  w_bc=alpha_b(w_c)*w_b,
  u_bc=u_b*alpha_b(u_c).
For the section using only the first n strands, u=1. Filling d makes the
substitution of every kernel beta_i the identity. Its u-value is
  tau_i(X)=X_n^{-1} ... X_(i+1)^{-1} X_i^{-1} X_(i+1) ... X_n.
The substitution tau:X_i->tau_i(X) is its own inverse, as follows by
substitution from i=n down to i=1 and free cancellation.

Consequently the actual fiber-coordinate isomorphism and its inverse are
  u: pi_1(C\{q_1,...,q_n},q_(n+1)) -> F(X_1,...,X_n),
                         beta_i -> tau_i(X),
  u^{-1}: F(X_1,...,X_n) -> pi_1(C\{q_1,...,q_n},q_(n+1)),
                         X_i -> tau_i(beta).
Both composites are the identity on the respective named free bases.
In particular each X_i corresponds to a specified conjugate of the
inverse clockwise lasso beta_i, so it is an actual based puncture meridian
with that precise word as its whisker convention.

The cocycle now proves the actual action. For an old-strand section

For any pure b, the actual conjugation on the kernel becomes
  v -> u_b*alpha_b(v)*u_b^{-1}.
This proves the coordinate action used in F.20/F.22 and in the finite
