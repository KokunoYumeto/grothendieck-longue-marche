  k1=F_p(C)=F_p(c),

6. SUMMED RESTRICTION, TRACE AND AFFINE IMAGE

The map direct_sum_j Q_j -> H1_cont(K,k0_chi|K) given by summed
restriction commutes with all actual source operators. A nonzero
invariant kernel would contain a nonzero invariant subspace of
smallest dimension. Every nonzero projection of that subspace to
Q_j is injective and has no proper nonzero invariant subspace.
Essentiality forces its image to be L_j. Thus the minimal subspace
lies in the sum of the essential lines, where the actual evaluation
Vandermonde map is injective. This is a contradiction.

At the cocycle level, zero summed restriction therefore makes every
cocycle principal, d_j=b_j*(chi_j-1). Nontrivial C makes their sum
vanish on K exactly when sum_j b_j=0. The kernel has dimension
m-1 on 7m coordinates, so the exact rank is 6m+1.

A cocycle with trace-zero restriction gives the tuple (d^(q1^j))_j
and hence is principal. Its parameter has trace zero because C is
nontrivial. Conversely those principal cocycles have zero trace.
This establishes precisely the trace-kernel equality of F.26 for
the present locus. The F.26 reduction sends every proper original

the same scalar field and retains the original scalar character.
The proved equality excludes it, even when the original coefficient
field is larger. The exact F.23 sign isomorphism transports the
negative image equality to the stated positive coordinates.
