The first expanded-free-word marginal extractor was interrupted after
processing source indices 0--3. Expanding a source automorphism and then
substituting its expanded inverse produced unnecessarily large intermediate
words. No contrary mathematical result was found; no completed marginal
output was written by that run. Its producer and partial stdout are retained.

The compact marginal braid stage retains the same exact braid words and
uses the explicit Artin/conjugator recurrence. This recurrence can be
evaluated in a finite group without expanding the free words, and its
identity with the actual point-pushing coordinate follows by induction
from the Artin substitutions and the u=w^-1 formula proved in F20/F22.
