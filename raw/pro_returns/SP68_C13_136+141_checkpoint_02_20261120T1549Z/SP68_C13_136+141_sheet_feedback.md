# Error-sheet feedback

All ten error-sheet categories appear in their supplied order, including zero counts. Counts are substantive collation places: “against C” counts places that change witness C; “all witness differences” also includes places differing only from H. A place differing from both witnesses is counted once in the latter column. Certainty-only decisions are separately enumerated in decisions.tsv and the doubts file’s certainty/decision record, and do not increase these counts. Overlapping phenomena are assigned the principal kind for counting.
