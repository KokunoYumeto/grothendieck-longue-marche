f(x)=x+1/x for nonzero x.
x=1, f(x)=2, 1-1/x^2=0
x=-1, f(x)=-2, 1-1/x^2=0
Formal differentiation gives f'(x)=1-1/x^2. In characteristic zero the two zeros x=1,-1 give values2,-2.
Equivalently f(x)-2=(x-1)^2/x and f(x)+2=(x+1)^2/x.
This establishes two branch values for this rational map in characteristic zero only. It is not a proof of the manuscript moduli/stack identification, of integral fibers, or of characteristic2 behavior.
The manuscript final section is T=2 without a minus; preserve it.
