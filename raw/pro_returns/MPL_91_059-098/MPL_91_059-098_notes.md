### N077-02 — The product argument tests subsets in one fibre

**Source:** 91.pdf, scan 077, lines 14–27. **Kind:** own idea / proof qualification. **Confidence:** high; the distinction is visible in the fibres of a product over \(S\). **Quote:** “l'hyp. signifie que” followed by \(\bigcup_i\mathfrak X_i^n=\mathfrak X^n\), products over \(S\). **Context:** H-W; Hua VI, “Ce que la restriction de Weil préserve”, and VII, “Recollement”; no matching caveat was found.
