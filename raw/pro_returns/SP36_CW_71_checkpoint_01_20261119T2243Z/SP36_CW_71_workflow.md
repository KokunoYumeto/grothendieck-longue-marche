# SP36_CW_71 — Reading workflow

## Scope and independence

The supplied outer ZIP was extracted once. Each scan is taken in archive order. Three new agents, R1/R2/R3, receive a fresh context containing the brief and access only to that scan’s packet, images and current notation table. They are instructed not to read each other’s returns. The main reader retains their texts/reports and inspects local crops for adjudication. A reader finishes after one page. No OCR or external research is permitted or used.
