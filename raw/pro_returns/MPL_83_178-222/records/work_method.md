# Method and source boundary

One reader (the primary assistant) reads the full scan and both supplied halves in ascending scan order. No OCR, handwriting recognition service, published transcription, or context text is used to generate readings. Additional rotated/cropped views are inspection aids only and are excluded from the final deliverable.

The context archive's central-directory filenames and byte sizes were listed during the initial attachment inventory, concurrently with the first reading of the instructions. No context member's contents were read at that stage. Context contents remain closed until all page readings, the second audit, the notation review, and the freeze hashes are complete.

Page-level records preserve the inventory, doubts, notation observations, and research leads available before context. The final doubts table is generated from the frozen page files and these records. Uncertain words remain marked in the transcription even when a longer passage seems mathematically plausible.
