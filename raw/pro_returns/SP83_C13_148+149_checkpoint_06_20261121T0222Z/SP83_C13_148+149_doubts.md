# Legibility and witnesses

These are visual model readings of the supplied raster scans, not a human verification. Every leaf received a full first reading against both drafts, followed by main-reader review of disagreements and open places on enlarged crops. Independent readings, token comparisons and the main reader's crop adjudications are preserved page by page in `second/`; `progress.log` records their completion. No OCR, external source research or TeX compilation was used.

Most ordinary prose and large, uncrossed formula letters are legible. Confidence is lower where a pen return merges with an abbreviation point or final letter, where a correction covers an earlier character, or where oblique marginal writing crosses the main baseline. The marginal material on 148:053, 061, 062 and 064 needs particular caution. Scan 067 contains pale and overwritten mathematical layers whose partial survival does not support a complete confident recovery. Some of the printed zero pattern near the upper edge of 068, several enclosed labels on 069, the unidentified cancelled and isolated marks on 071, the coined caption and small isolated mark on 072, and the six overworked superscripts on 149:004 remain less secure than the surrounding text. The actual unresolved locations and alternatives are listed above.

The coloured drawings are explicit TikZ approximations, supported by the retained source crops and geometry documentation. Their connectivity, labels, relative positions, layer distinctions and visible interruptions were checked; the drawn curves are not claimed to be a photographic facsimile. Pale show-through was excluded from the transcription, including the background beneath 071. Where the author or another hand wrote a small folio mark, its writer was not inferred merely from its position or ink colour.

C generally supplies more diplomatic structure and drawing detail than H, but this did not make its reading authoritative. C also contains substantial layout and graph errors, including the extra red contour on 072 and the treatment of 071's show-through as a recto figure. H supplies useful alternative words and readings but often replaces a drawing by prose, expands a shorthand, changes punctuation or loses a revision layer. Both drafts agree at some wrong places: the boundary labels on 067 and the initial accents in réconstitue / réconstituer on 149:003–004 are concrete examples. Agreement was therefore checked against the image throughout. The discrepancy totals count the documented places in this run; they are not comparable error rates for the two models or an estimate of remaining error.

Uncertainty is retained in place rather than hidden by grammatical repair or mathematical expectation. A clear source slip remains in the transcription and is discussed in the notes. A computational or proof check is identified only where it actually took place in `CHECK_EVENTS.tsv`; reading a source assertion did not by itself verify it.
