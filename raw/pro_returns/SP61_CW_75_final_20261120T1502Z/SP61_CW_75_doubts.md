## Certainty-only decisions and audit qualifications

### Scan 016 — certainty-only decisions

The cap on P remains unresolved; T is excluded by the visible loop. C’s bottom verb, terminal clause, corollary sign, two foot deletions, and obscured marginal cancellation remain doubtful. ### Scan 017 — notation and certainty decisions

The repeated category subscript on the three Isom expressions is C. The second reading changes the first pass’s overconfident calligraphic choice to `\unsure{C|\mathcal C}` at the first occurrence, plain C thereafter. Its first form curls inward like e; subsequent forms are open. The first Isom result has a detached bar, so it is `\overline P`; attached broad-cap P forms retain the page’s initial doubt. The left superscript is P, not T. C’s muni is resolved without a final s; raised str is clear; `(ug)(x)` has x; the middle F-to-P connector has a reduced but visible head and source bar; the final cancelled `(ug` is legible. The eight remaining doubt loci are retained in remaining_doubts.tsv. These certainty-only decisions do not create change rows.
