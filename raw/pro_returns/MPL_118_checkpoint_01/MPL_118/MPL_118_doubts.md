| Prime | `'` | A short raised stroke; distinguish from the circle and from an exclamation mark at every occurrence. |

| Double quotation marks | two backticks / two apostrophes | Typeset as LaTeX opening and closing double quotation marks; guillemets only if actually drawn. |
| Underlining | `\uline{}`, `\uuline{}` | Double strokes are distinguished from a neighbouring diagram edge. |
