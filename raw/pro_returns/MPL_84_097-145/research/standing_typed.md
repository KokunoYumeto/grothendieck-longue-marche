### E.3. Qualifications and withdrawals in the reused typed leaves

These are the author's limits on predictions, interpretations and recollections, rather than unfinished algebraic proofs. The linked D items contain their individual context comparisons and searches. A doubtful letter or clipped ending inside `\unsure` is editorial uncertainty and does not itself count as one of the author's reservations.

The absence of an authorial question mark on another claim does not certify it: the itemized notes separately record unproved assertions. Conversely, the quoted “OK” in a mathematical check records the author's local conclusion, and the standing mathematical tables preserve where that conclusion is narrower than a proposed general claim.
