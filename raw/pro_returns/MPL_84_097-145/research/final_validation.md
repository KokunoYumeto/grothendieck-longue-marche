## Transcription preservation and mechanical verification

The root transcriber performed the original image reading, second visual audit and notation comparison without OCR or delegated handwriting work. The final mechanical TeX structure check examined all 48 fragments and found zero structural issues. All 49 frozen SHA-256 hashes—the individual pages and combined text—were unchanged when the final research validator ran; the archive command verifies and logs this again immediately before packaging.

A full TeX compilation was attempted but blocked by missing formats/packages in the available installation. No successful compilation or rendered-page verification is claimed. The editorial macros in the requested fragment must be defined by the receiving document.

The context ZIP's directory metadata was listed before the transcription freeze. This procedural deviation is disclosed in `progress.log`, the doubts file and the package README. No context contents were extracted or read at that stage; the contents were opened only after the 49 transcription files were frozen, and those files have not changed.
