\statement{B.84}{Proved}{Whole filling kernels for all five corrected positive source lifts}
Use the actual surfaces, source, partial kernels \(J_c,I\), and common
w-marking of B.83. All five positive old source lifts can be corrected
by specified actual source words so that the valid stage argument
controls a whole filling kernel on four deleted Nielsen generators.
The resulting quotients are \(\mathbb Z\times F_5\) for the constant
filling and \(\mathbb Z^2\) for the both-family filling, also for actual
compact images. The old zero operator and arbitrary retained source
operators remain outside this statement.

\begin{proof}
\emph{The exact source corrections.}\par
Write the actual B.83 fiber basis as
\[
 z,c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4,p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4,a,d,b,
 \qquad T_0=badb.
 \tag{B.84.1}
\]
Let \(g_i\), \(1\leq i\leq5\), be the actual source lift of the
positive old lasso at \(w=\zeta^{i-1}\), and let \(\gamma_i\) be its
conjugation action. Thus \(g_1,g_2,g_5\) are precisely those of B.83;
the five base images are a free-basis subset of the actual \(F_6\).
Put \(P_j=p_j^-\), the other actual product source lasso over the
jth nonidentity product. Both product lassos are retained separately.

Set
\[
 \begin{split}
 C_3&=c_4^{-1}c_3^{-1}c_4,\qquad
 k=b^{-1}c_4b=\gamma_2^{-1}(c_4),\\
 v_3&=P_1^{-1}k^{-1}P_1k,\\
 c_2^*&=c_4^{-1}c_3^{-1}z^{-1}T_0c_2^{-1}T_0^{-1}zc_3c_4.
 \end{split}
 \tag{B.84.2}
\]
The equality defining k is literal in the actual action table. Hence
\(v_3\in J_c\). The fourth deleted generator has the actual source
witness
\[
 c_2^*=(zc_3c_4)^{-1}
       \gamma_1^{-1}(p_2^{-1}c_2^{-1}p_2)(zc_3c_4),
 \tag{B.84.3}
\]
so \(c_2^*\in J_c\). The exact inverse w1 action proves this identity.
Replacing \(c_2\) by \(c_2^*\) is a Nielsen change: its conjugating
word contains no \(c_2\), and only that generator is inverted.
Both inverse substitutions on all twelve generators are checked.

For the fourth correction write
\(A=\gamma_4(P_3)\), \(B=\gamma_4(P_2)\), and
\(C=\gamma_4(P_1)\), and define the actual source word
\[
 \begin{split}
 v_4={}&A P_3^{-1}P_2^{-1}k^{-1}P_2kP_2P_3A^{-1}\\
       &\mathord{}\cdot B^{-1}CBAP_3^{-1}P_2^{-1}P_1^{-1}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{B.84.4}
\]
This expression was obtained by one targeted membership calculation in
the subgroup generated by the thirteen actual source fiber words and
their first old-action images. Its sixteen factors are independently
substituted and freely reduced in the actual generic fiber.
In particular \(v_4\in H\), the actual full source image. We do not
claim \(v_4\in J_c\).

Its source word does lie in the both-family kernel. That filling kills
\(c_4\) and \(g_4\), so k becomes1 and \(A,B,C\) become
\(P_3,P_2,P_1\); (B.84.4) then freely reduces to1. Thus \(v_4\in I\).
The actual based node restrictions are
\[
 \gamma_3|_{\langle b,C_3\rangle}
     =\operatorname{Inn}(v_3C_3b),\qquad
 \gamma_4|_{\langle b,c_2^*\rangle}
     =\operatorname{Inn}(v_4c_2^*b).
 \tag{B.84.5}
\]
These are identities for the actual source operators, including their
common transports; no local marking is substituted for a based source
lift.

Define
\[
 h_1=g_1,\quad h_2=g_2,\quad h_3=v_3^{-1}g_3,\quad
 h_4=v_4^{-1}g_4,\quad h_5=g_5.
 \tag{B.84.6}
\]
All five are actual source words. The four with \(i\ne1\) belong to I:
the corresponding \(g_i\) are moving-family source meridians, and
their corrections were just shown to lie in I. Their w-base projections
are unchanged, since \(v_3,v_4\) are fiber words.

\emph{The selected whole-kernel statement.}\par\nopagebreak
Put
\[
 \begin{split}
 H^\sharp&=\langle c_1,c_2^*,c_3,c_4,h_1,\ldots,h_5\rangle,
       \\
 T^\sharp&=\langle H^\sharp,b\rangle,\\
 J^\sharp&=\langle\!\langle c_1,c_2^*,c_3,c_4
                         \rangle\!\rangle_{H^\sharp},\\
 I^\sharp&=\langle\!\langle c_1,c_2^*,c_3,c_4,h_2,h_3,h_4,h_5
                         \rangle\!\rangle_{H^\sharp}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{B.84.7}
\]
Let \(R_c\) and R be the actual ambient constant and both-family
filling kernels. We will prove
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T^\sharp\cap R_c=T^\sharp\cap J_c&=J^\sharp,
       &T^\sharp/J^\sharp&\simeq\mathbb Z\times F_5,\\
 T^\sharp\cap R=T^\sharp\cap I&=I^\sharp,
       &T^\sharp/I^\sharp&\simeq\mathbb Z^2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{B.84.8}
\]

Write \(\eta_i=\operatorname{Inn}(h_i)\). Besides the restrictions
for \(\eta_2,\eta_5\) in B.83, we now have
\(\eta_3=\operatorname{Inn}(C_3b)\) on \(\langle b,C_3\rangle\)
and \(\eta_4=\operatorname{Inn}(c_2^*b)\) on
\(\langle b,c_2^*\rangle\). Let \(K_0\) be the image of
\[
 \ker\bigl(F(b,c_1,c_2^*,c_3,c_4)\longrightarrow\langle b\rangle\bigr).
 \tag{B.84.9}
\]
The signed b-conjugators all lie in \(K_0\). In order \(i=1,\ldots,5\),
the forward conjugators are
\[
 1,\quad c_4^{-1},\quad C_3,\quad c_2^*,\quad bc_1^{-1}b^{-1},
\]
and the inverse conjugators are
\[
 1,\quad b^{-1}c_4b,\quad b^{-1}C_3^{-1}b,\quad
 b^{-1}(c_2^*)^{-1}b,\quad c_1.
 \tag{B.84.10}
\]
Every entry is checked literally, together with all120 corrected
whole-basis action/inverse identities.

First \(K_0\subseteq J^\sharp\). The B.83 node arguments supply the
entire b-strips of \(c_1,c_4\). The \(\eta_3\) node supplies the
entire b-strip of \(C_3\); combining it with the \(c_4\) strip gives
the entire \(c_3\) strip. The \(\eta_4\) node supplies the entire
\(c_2^*\) strip. These four families generate the full kernel in
(B.84.9), including mixed products. Only their images in the actual
surface group are used.

Apply the valid B.83 stage induction to \(K_0\) and the five
\(\eta_i^{\pm1}\). Every correcting element belongs to \(K_0\)
itself. If a stage is b-normal, the middle conjugation in
\(b\eta(w)b^{-1}=\eta(kb(k^{-1}wk)b^{-1}k^{-1})\)
therefore remains in that stage. The next stage is b-normal.
Their union is exactly \(J^\sharp\): it contains the four seeds,
is invariant under the five source lifts, and conversely \(K_0\)
was generated by their seed orbits. Thus \(J^\sharp\) is normal in
\(T^\sharp\).

Modulo \(J^\sharp\), b commutes with the five \(h_i\). This gives
a surjection \(\mathbb Z\times F_5\to T^\sharp/J^\sharp\).
The actual character \(\chi_b\) of B.83---minus conventional winding
of \((vw-1)/(w-1)^2\)---kills \(H^\sharp\) and sends b to1.
The actual w-base projection detects the five independent positive
basis elements. These maps provide a left inverse, and factor through
the constant filling. The first line of (B.84.8) follows.

The image of \(H^\sharp\) in that product is exactly
\(\{0\}\times F_5\). The normal closure of \(h_2,h_3,h_4,h_5\)
in this factor is normal in the entire product, and its quotient is
\(\langle h_1\rangle\). Both \(\chi_b\) and winding of \(w-1\)
factor through the actual both-family filling and detect the resulting
\(\mathbb Z^2\). This proves the second line of (B.84.8).
In particular \(H^\sharp=\ker(\chi_b|_{T^\sharp})\) is itself
b-normal; this assertion concerns only the specified selected subgroup.

\emph{Actual compact images and scope.}\par\nopagebreak
Use the abstract free group \(F_9\) on the nine selected source
\emph{words}, and its free retraction killing the four seeds.
Its compact image is the actual closure of \(H^\sharp\), and the
image of its compact kernel is the closure of \(J^\sharp\).
The actual \(F_5\) base detector makes the surviving quotient exact.
For the both-family statement use the analogous retraction retaining
only \(h_1\). We do not assert that these nine words are a subset of
an \(F_{19}\) free basis, or that \(H^\sharp\) has full induced
topology there.

The universal pro-direct-product presentations give surjections from
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\times\widehat F_5\) and
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\,2}\) to the actual closed quotients.
The completed actual detectors are their left inverses; the positive
base subgroup is an actual free factor of \(F_6\). The detectors
also factor through the actual completed fillings. Thus every equality
in (B.84.8) holds for the specified closed images. No abstract completion
of \(T^\sharp\), or of an infinitely generated kernel, is substituted
for its ambient closure.

\end{proof}
