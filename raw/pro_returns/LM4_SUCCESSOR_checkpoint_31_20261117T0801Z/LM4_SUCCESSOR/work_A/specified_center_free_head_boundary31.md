# A specified center-free sphere head and its invariant-free lifts

Cycle31 research. This preserves the read-only argument from the checkpoint30 capture. It is a uniform statement about one **specified actual head map**, not a classification of arbitrary nonabelian heads, extensions, or canonical Grothendieck--Teichmüller data.

## 1. Actual marking and hypotheses

Use the exact pure generators

    (a,b,c,d,e,f)=(A12,A13,A23,A14,A24,A34),
    P4=F(d,e,f) semidirect P3,
    Z=abc,  P3=F(a,c) times <Z>.

The inverse Artin actions and full-twist identity are those proved and primarily checked in `actual_P4_affine_character_pentagon_boundary30.md`, `P4_affine_marking_provenance30.md`, and `P4_affine_primary_action_binding30.md`. In particular

    Ad(Z^-1)|F(d,e,f)=Ad(def),
    a^-1 d a=Ad(de)d,  a^-1 e a=Ad(d)e,  [a,f]=1,
    [c,d]=1,  c^-1 e c=Ad(ef)e,  c^-1 f c=Ad(e)f.

The original action convention was freshly read in Cohen (2010), Example1.1, printed465--466, and the exact pentagon word in Furusho (2010), printed547, Equation(4). No theorem from their cohomological or prounipotent conclusions is used.

Let H be a finite center-free group generated by two specified elements D0,F0. Fix the actual sphere-head homomorphism

    a -> F0, c -> D0, d -> D0, f -> F0,
    b -> (D0 F0)^-1, e -> (F0 D0)^-1.                  (1)

This is the fifth marked free-two quotient of the preceding proof. Let V be a finite abelian H-module, in additive notation, and assume V^H=0. No assumption on the characteristic or semisimplicity of V is required. Put E=V semidirect H. Consider ANY homomorphism phi:P4->E whose projection to H is exactly (1). Equivalently one may start with a continuous map from the full profinite completion, since E is finite.

## 2. Entire lifting assertion

Write A,B,C,D,E0,F for the six images, keeping E0 distinct from the target group E, and put P=D E0 F. The lifted fiber K=<D,E0,F> maps onto H. If g centralizes K, its H-image centralizes H and hence is1. Thus g belongs to V, and centralizing K says precisely that every element of H fixes g. Consequently

    C_E(K)=V^H=0.                                      (2)

The full-twist action says that Z and P^-1 have the same conjugation action on K. Their quotient belongs to (2), so

    phi(Z)=P^-1.                                       (3)

The specified head of P is D0(F0 D0)^-1 F0=1, so P lies in V. Centrality of Z in the actual P3 base implies that A and C commute with P. Their heads F0,D0 generate H; because conjugation on the abelian normal subgroup V is the specified H-action, P belongs to V^H. Therefore P=1 and phi(Z)=1.

It remains to recover literal generator equalities, rather than equality modulo a centralizer. From P=1 one has E0=(F D)^-1. Substituting this into the COMPLETE a-row of the actual inverse action shows that conjugation by A^-1 on K is conjugation by F^-1. For example DE0=F^-1, while DE0D^-1=F^-1 E0F, and F itself is fixed. Equation(2) therefore gives A=F. The complete c-row similarly gives C=D. Finally B=A^-1 phi(Z) C^-1=(D F)^-1. Thus

    A=F, C=D, B=(D F)^-1, E0=(F D)^-1.                 (4)

Every lift factors through the SAME specified marked free-two quotient. This uses the full group relations and their actual order; it is not a tangent-space dimension calculation.

## 3. The entire pentagon and an all-odd example

Let u belong to the commutator subgroup of the full free profinite group and satisfy the exact word identity u(Y,X)=u(X,Y)^-1. The actual pentagon is

    u(a,ce)u(bc,f)=u(c,f)u(ab,ef)u(a,c).

Using(4), the left pairs become (F,F^-1),(F^-1,F), and the middle right pair becomes (D^-1,D^-1). Their u-values are1. The remaining equation is exactly

    1=u(D,F)u(F,D),

which holds by the given full duality. Thus these lifted-head pentagon evaluations supply no further constraint, even though H itself can be nonabelian.

For example let n>1 be odd, H=D_(2n) with two generating reflections D0,F0 and rotation R=D0F0. Over a finite field of characteristic two containing a primitive nth root zeta, its usual two-dimensional representation has rotation diag(zeta,zeta^-1) and reflection exchanging the two coordinates. Since zeta differs from1, the rotation already has zero invariant subspace. The module satisfies V^H=0, and H is center-free. The theorem applies simultaneously to every odd n; this is not a list of computed heads. It concerns this specified sphere-head map, not every map from P4 to D_(2n).

## 4. Actual canonical scope and the next nonlinear location

An actual full-GT canonical u is one of the words satisfying full duality, so the conclusion applies to it. The theorem does not realize arbitrary module data as canonical, force the actual tuple into such a module, or compare a_reg with d_alpha.

The proof exposes the information a successful lift must retain. For a general extension, failure of the reduction can reside in the centralizer of the lifted fiber, or in a nonidentity boundary P=DEF fixed by the lifted a,c. The hypotheses above kill both. The actual relative pro-two kernel over a sphere head retains P and its nonlinear conjugation, so it is a genuinely different possible source of information. No fixed-boundary theorem, characteristic-kernel comparison, or relative marked R2 input is supplied by this note. In particular the cubic relation for the prospective positive modular map is not assumed.
