# Actual third-cusp half-rotations and two normalized full-word tests

Research, checkpoint27; separate from the frozen V4/Fox note. This gives an exact reformulation and normalization of the existing full third-cusp gate. It does not prove that either test holds, and does not claim the canonical Fox element is a unit. The actual GT0 swap duality and the regular tau centralizer are essential; the statement is not transferred to arbitrary P-involutions.

## 1. The actual square root in the swap extension

Retain the actual F=hat F(x,y), alpha,u,w,v,tau of `actual_GT0_V4_Fox_defect27.md`, and put

G_v=F semidirect<v>,
e=(xu)v,
z=(xy)^-1.

The exact identities v(x)=w, v(u)=u^-1 give

e²=xu v(xu)=xu(wu^-1)=xy=z^-1.

Also v(z)=(xu)^-1 z(xu), so e centralizes z. Under the actual isomorphism

G_v -> F semidirect<iota>, identity on F, v maps to u^-1 iota,

the element e maps to x iota. Since iota swaps x and y, the target has the explicit full profinite presentation C2(iota) amalg Zhat(x). Replacing x by x iota also gives the free factors C2(iota), Zhat(e). This is a presentation of the actual ambient group, not a claim that the marked subgroup generated by x and v is the whole group.

## 2. Exact full centralizer and cancellation of the square

The primitive cusp xy is a free procyclic factor in F, since (xy,y) is a full free basis. Thus C_F(xy)=closure<xy> is Zhat, by the already used full free-product centralizer theorem. The odd element e centralizes xy. Every element of the full centralizer in G_v either lies in that even subgroup or differs from e by an element of it. Consequently

C_(G_v)(xy)=closure<xy> union closure<xy>e=closure<e>.

This last procyclic group is a copy of Zhat: if e^a=1 for a in Zhat, then (e²)^a=1 and the primitive xy subgroup forces a=0. In particular multiplication by2 in its exponent group is injective. For every unit lambda in Zhat the same proof gives

C_(G_v)((xy)^lambda)=closure<e>.

Define the actual canonical half-rotation

d_lambda=x^lambda v.

Then

d_lambda²=x^lambda w^lambda=x^lambda u^-1 y^lambda u,
tau(d_lambda)=d_lambda^-1.

The second equality is literal, because tau(x)=w^-1 and tau(v)=v.

For any specified t in F, the full third-cusp identity

d_lambda²=t e^(2lambda) t^-1

holds if and only if

d_lambda=t e^lambda t^-1.

Only the reverse implication needs proof. The element t^-1 d_lambda t centralizes its square e^(2lambda), so it lies in the full group closure<e>. Write it e^a. Equality of squares gives 2a=2lambda in Zhat, hence a=lambda. The conjugator is unchanged throughout this argument.

Conjugacy in G_v and conjugacy in F are also equivalent for these two elements. An odd conjugator taking e^lambda to d_lambda can be multiplied on the right by e^-1, which centralizes e^lambda and changes the parity. Thus the original full third-cusp condition is exactly

x^lambda u^-1 iota is conjugate to (x iota)^lambda

inside the actual full free profinite product C2(iota) amalg Zhat(x). This is not a finite or pro-p conjugacy test. By A45 it suffices to use the uniquely determined positive choice lambda=d_alpha; the opposite global sign gives the equivalent original condition.

## 3. Normalize the actual cusp reflection using the one-section theorem

Because tau is the actual regular negative pants involution, there is an actual h in F with

tau(z)=h^-1 z^-1 h.

Set r=h tau in F semidirect<tau>. It reverses z. Its square lies in C_F(z)=closure<z>. Since r commutes with its own square and inverts this procyclic group, that square is both fixed and inverted; torsion-freeness of Zhat implies r²=1.

The actual one-section theorem and C_F(tau)=1 give a unique q in F satisfying

r=q tau q^-1.

This q is attached to the chosen genuine cusp reflection r; it is not asserted to lie in H or to be obtained from an unproved positive conjugator.

In the total actual F semidirect V4, conjugation by r preserves G_v. The element r e r^-1 centralizes e² and has square e^-2. The centralizer and square-cancellation argument of Section2 therefore gives r e r^-1=e^-1. Hence

b=q^-1 e q

has v-parity and is literally inverted by tau:

tau(b)=b^-1.

Its full centralizer in G_v is closure<b>, a torsion-free procyclic group. Both b and d_lambda are now normalized with respect to the same actual involution tau.

## 4. There are only two normalized representatives

Suppose d_lambda=g b^lambda g^-1 for some g in F. Applying tau and using literal reversal of both sides gives

g^-1 tau(g) in C_(G_v)(b^lambda)=closure<b>.

Write g^-1 tau(g)=b^s. The left side is in F, whereas b has the nontrivial v-parity. Thus s belongs to2 Zhat. Put a=s/2. The exact calculation

tau(g b^a)=g b^s b^-a=g b^a

shows that g b^a centralizes tau in G_v. But an element of G_v has the form fv^epsilon, and v commutes with tau. Since C_F(tau)=1,

C_(G_v)(tau)=<v>.

It follows that g b^a is either1 or v. Conjugating b^lambda, the right factor b^-a has no effect. Therefore the original third-cusp condition is equivalent to one of the following two literal full-group equalities:

(1) d_lambda=b^lambda;
(2) d_lambda=v b^lambda v.

The converse is explicit. In case(1), take g=1. In case(2), take g=v b^-1, which lies in F and has the same conjugation action on b as v. Returning to e, actual F-conjugators for the original square equation are respectively

t=q^-1,
t=v b^-1 q^-1.

The two tests are mutually exclusive. If both held, v would centralize b^lambda, placing the nontrivial order-two v in the torsion-free procyclic group closure<b>, a contradiction.

Changing the initially chosen actual cusp reflection changes the normalizing q, but not the validity of the equivalence. This statement gives a pair of complete full-word tests relative to that chosen reflection. It does not assert that all unnormalized conjugators form a two-element set; their procyclic centralizer factors remain present.

## 5. Relation to the Fox unit and exact remaining gate

The canonical substitution psi has image H=closure<x,w>. Unit powering does not change H. If either normalized test of Section4 holds for lambda=d_alpha, then the original three-cusp condition holds. The already proved full finite-cover peripheral-generation theorem C29 makes the substitution x->x^lambda, y->w^lambda an automorphism. Hence H=F, and the full Fox element B=1+(y-1)u_y is a unit at every coefficient prime.

No step above proves that either normalized test holds. The one-section theorem supplies the unique q for an actual cusp reflection, but it does not identify the normalized half-rotation b with either canonical word x^lambda v or its v-conjugate. That is a simultaneous marked comparison. In particular it cannot be inferred merely because the associated alpha-reflections have one of the allowed ambient conjugacy classes.

This isolates the remaining assertion without dropping any topology: compare two actual full-group words after a uniquely normalized actual tau reflection. The complete V4/Fox induced-defect theorem stays at its frozen99a0b836 source hash. No new target trial, general recognition theorem, full unit conclusion, or all-P extension is made here.
