Use the actual modular delta from checkpoint28, so x=delta^-2. Put

    sigma_x=j_delta alpha.

    G_ab=(-2t,a),  a=a_reg=1-4t in Zhat^times.          (1)

Let K be the closed normal closure of x in F and A=F/K=Zhat, generated by the image T of y. Its exact full-head mod-two Alexander module is

    M=K/closure(K^2[K,K])=Lambda[x],
    Lambda=F2[[A]].                                    (2)

This is the same complete cyclic-cover calculation proved in C44, with the two basis labels exchanged. In particular it retains all finite cyclic heads, including odd orders, and is not the maximal pro-two group algebra. Both alpha and j_delta preserve K and invert A. On M, alpha sends [x] to [-x]=[x] in characteristic two, and j_delta sends [x] to itself. Thus both have the exact semilinear action

For example conjugation of x by an arbitrary g acts on M through the image of g in A. The extra conjugator f in alpha(y) belongs to K and has no further action on the abelian module. This verifies (3) from the actual generator maps; it is not an assumed linearization of an unrelated involution.

Since u and f lie in F', they belong to K. Write U=[u] and F_f=[f] in the coefficient identification (2). The canonical equality u=f alpha(u) gives

In particular bar(F_f)=F_f. The augmentation of U is the ordinary x-exponent of u modulo two, hence is zero. There is consequently a unique V in Lambda such that

## 2. The exact full cyclic identity for a_reg

The first equality uses characteristic two; the coefficient T is the conjugation by y in the last factor. The order of the original noncommutative word has therefore been retained before passing to M.
