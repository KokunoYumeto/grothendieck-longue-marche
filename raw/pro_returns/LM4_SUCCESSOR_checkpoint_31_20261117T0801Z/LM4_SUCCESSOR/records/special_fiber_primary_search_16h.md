# Special source fiber: primary search and exact boundary

We searched for relative or nongeneric Zariski--Lefschetz comparisons
for fundamental groups of complements. The search was motivated by the
actual special source fiber, not by a claim of priority. Generic-pencil
presentation theorems alone do not supply its required kernel comparison.

Primary source opened and read: Tibăr, M. (2002). *Connectivity via
nongeneric pencils* (arXiv:math/0202123v1).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0202123
We inspected Theorem1.1 and the proof of Proposition3.5 and Remark3.6
(preprint pages1--2,7--8). Verified for this reading; no entry was added
to the already frozen formal bibliography. The theorem treats a generic
member of a nongeneric pencil. Proposition3.5 permits one exceptional
fiber but concludes connectivity with a tube around it. Remark3.6 needs
the inclusion of the fiber in that tube to be a homotopy equivalence
before the tube can be replaced by the fiber. This condition has not been
proved for the actual source here. No application of that theorem to the
actual source or its filling kernel is asserted.

The attempted arXiv HTML and PDF retrieval of Eyral--Petrov1609.01427
failed. Its abstract and publisher/search text were found, but we do not
claim to have opened its full proof or to use it as an authority.

Our actual algebraic observation is independent of that literature:
eta+1 = w(v+w-2)/(w-1)^2. The special fiber in the open surface is the
source v+w=2, but its boundary degeneration also involves the removed
component w=0. This component contains the added v=u punctures before
filling. The earlier exact degeneration note shows an associated root
escaping toward w=0. Thus a tube/source replacement cannot be assumed.
A possible next route would compute the relative van Kampen contribution
of these boundary pieces before and after filling, retaining the actual
based maps. This is a proposed calculation, not a proof of equality.
