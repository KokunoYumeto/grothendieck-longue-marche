## Central-extension lemma

For the pullback P=C# times_C Pi, the kernel R0 of P→C# is the canonical
copy of R. Its conjugation action is that of Pi. Thus R=[R,Pi] implies
R0=[R0,P], and the map P^ab→(C#)^ab is an isomorphism. The displayed
central presentation of P is valid: quotienting by A gives the free
profinite product C2 coproduct C3, and the comparison map is the identity
on both A and that quotient.

To verify A injects into P^ab, one embeds A in a divisible torsion abelian
group, chooses the prescribed half and third roots, and uses the finite
subgroup generated by A and those roots. This gives an actual finite
abelian target of the presentation that is injective on A. It is not an
assumption that A is a direct summand of the abelianization.

The full positive lift h0 gives an automorphism of P by conjugating the
two lifted finite-order generators and fixing A. The difference on R0
is a continuous Pi-invariant homomorphism R→A. Relative perfectness
makes it zero, so the automorphism descends. It acts identically on P^ab.
Since h0 fixes tau literally, the discrepancy on ST lies in A; the
just-proved injection then makes that discrepancy one. This verifies
the literal cusp condition, as well as the prescribed C-frames.
