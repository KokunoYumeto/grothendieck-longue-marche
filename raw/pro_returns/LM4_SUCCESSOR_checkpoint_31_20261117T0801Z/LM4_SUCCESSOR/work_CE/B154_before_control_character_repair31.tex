\subsection*{A finite permutation extension and the completed fixed section}
\statement{B.154}{Proved}{The actual mixed fixed source has full induced topology and a marked completed section}
\label{stmt:B154}
In the actual one-neck annular model, the subgroup
$J_a=\Gamma_a^\delta\cong\mathbb Z^2*\mathbb Z$ of B.149 carries
its full induced profinite topology in $\Gamma_a$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B154-fixed}
 (\widehat{\Gamma_a})^\delta
 =\widehat J_a
 \cong(\widehat{\mathbb Z}_A\times\widehat{\mathbb Z}_m)
       \amalg\widehat{\mathbb Z}_v.
\end{equation}
The actual marked section extends to an injective continuous section
\[
 \widehat\sigma:\widehat J_a\longrightarrow
       (\widehat{\Gamma_0})^\delta
\]
of the completed fixed projection. The actual subgroup
$\langle A_{\rm src},m,v\rangle$ therefore has full induced topology
in $\Gamma_0$ as well.
Writing closures in $\widehat{\Gamma_0}$, one moreover has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B154-kernels}
\begin{gathered}
 \overline{I_{
m src}}
   =\overline{H_{
m src}}\cap\overline{K_{
m rem}},\\
 (\widehat{\Gamma_0})^\delta
   =(\overline{K_{
m rem}})^\delta
       \rtimes\widehat\sigma(\widehat J_a),\qquad
 \overline{H_{
m src}}
   =\overline{I_{
m src}}\rtimes\widehat\sigma(\widehat J_a).
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Thus the local completed fixed-source equality is precisely
$(\overline{K_{
m rem}})^\delta=\overline{I_{
m src}}$;
that remaining equality is not concluded here.
\begin{proof}
\emph{An actual quotient retaining the ordinary coarse factor.}
In B.139 put $D=Q_f\rtimes S$, $D_0=T_0\rtimes S$.
The actual even section $H$ lies in $D_0$, and
$\Gamma_a=A(\mathcal G)\rtimes D_0$ has vertices $dH$, $d\in D$.
Their orientation is well-defined. Every graph edge has one even and
one odd endpoint, and $D_0$ preserves each parity. Deleting all odd
graph generators is therefore an actual quotient
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B154-quotient}
 \Gamma_a\longrightarrow Q_*:=F(D_0/H)\rtimes D_0
   =\langle D_0,a\mid[a,h]=1\ (h\in H)\rangle.
\end{equation}
The inverse coset presentation sends the letter indexed $dH$ to
$dad^{-1}$. The actual norm $A=a_Ha_{\tau H}$ maps to $a_H=a$.
Its source conjugates map to the distinct free letters indexed by
$S/(S\cap H)=S/\langle m\rangle$. Thus this quotient restricts
injectively to
\[
 J_a=\langle a,S\rangle
    =S*_{\langle m\rangle}(\langle m\rangle\times\mathbb Z_a).
\]
All of $T_0$ is retained in \eqref{eq:B154-quotient}.

\emph{An arbitrary finite source target.}
Let $\psi:J_a\to A_0$ be any homomorphism to a finite group and put
$K_S=\ker(\psi|_S)$. By B.153 the compact sections in
$\widehat D_0$ satisfy
$\widehat H\cap\widehat S=\overline{\langle m\rangle}$.
The compact set
\[
 X=\widehat S\setminus
       (\overline{\langle m\rangle}\,\widehat K_S)
\]
is disjoint from $\widehat H$. Compactness and the full induced
source topology supply an open normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq
\widehat D_0$ such that
\[
 \widehat HN\cap X=\varnothing,
 \qquad N\cap\widehat S\le\widehat K_S.
\]
Let $\overline D=\widehat D_0/N$, with actual images
$\overline S,\overline H$. Then $\psi|_S$ factors through
$\beta:\overline S\to A_0$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B154-intersection}
 \beta(\overline H\cap\overline S)subseteq\psi(\langle m\rangle).
\end{equation}
Indeed every source lift of that intersection lies in $\widehat HN$
and hence in $\overline{\langle m\rangle}\widehat K_S$.
The element $a_0=\psi(A)$ commutes with the right side of
\eqref{eq:B154-intersection}.

Form the finite induced set
\[
 \Omega=\overline D\times_{\overline S}A_0,
 \qquad(ds,t)\sim(d,\beta(s)t).
\]
The group $\overline D$ acts on the left. Its identity fiber
$\Omega_0=\{[1,t]:t\in A_0\}$ identifies with $A_0$, and
$\overline S$ acts there by left multiplication through $\beta$.
Define a permutation $P_a$ on the invariant subset
$\overline H\Omega_0$ by
\[
 P_a([h,t])=[h,a_0t],\qquad h\in\overline H,
\]
and make it the identity on its complement. If two representatives
are equal, their first-coordinate discrepancy belongs to
$\overline H\cap\overline S$; its $\beta$ image commutes with $a_0$
by \eqref{eq:B154-intersection}. Thus $P_a$ is well-defined, its
inverse uses $a_0^{-1}$, and it commutes with the entire left
$\overline H$ action. Consequently
\[
 D_0\longrightarrow\overline D\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega),
 \qquad a\longmapsto P_a
\]
satisfies every relation in \eqref{eq:B154-quotient} and gives a finite
quotient representation of the actual $\Gamma_a$.

The subgroup generated by $\overline S,P_a$ preserves $\Omega_0$.
Its restriction there is exactly the left regular action through
$\psi$. Hence its ambient finite kernel restricts to a subgroup of
$\ker\psi$. This proves full induced topology of $J_a$, since $\psi$
was arbitrary. Evaluation is used only on this invariant fiber;
no homomorphism $\Gamma_a\to A_0$ extending $\psi$ is asserted.

\emph{The section and actual closed kernel.}
B.139 identifies the compact fixed group with the actual closure of
$J_a$. Its full topology just proved gives \eqref{eq:B154-fixed}.
Completing B.149's marked $\sigma$ gives a continuous map whose
composite with $\widehat p_a$ is the embedded $\widehat J_a$.
It is therefore injective and a genuine section of the fixed
projection; its image is pointwise fixed and belongs to
$\overline{H_{
m src}}$.

Right exactness gives
$\ker\widehat p_a=\overline{K_{
m rem}}$.
To prove the first equality in \eqref{eq:B154-kernels}, take a net
$h_i\in H_{
m src}$ converging to an element of its right side.
Then $p_a(h_i)\in J_a$ converges to $1$ in
$\widehat{\Gamma_a}$, and hence in $\widehat J_a$ by the induced
topology. The elements
\[
 h_i\sigma(p_a(h_i))^{-1}\in I_{
m src}
\]
converge to the same limit. The reverse inclusion is immediate.
Splitting the actual fixed projection and its source restriction now
gives both semidirect products in \eqref{eq:B154-kernels}.
\end{proof}
