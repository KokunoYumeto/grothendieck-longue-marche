1. The pre-filling subgroup is an actual retract

Let
  B=<a,tau,m> <= G_inner.
Killing C,f,b,d,e in the full inner presentation leaves precisely
  B = (Z(a)*C2(tau)) x Z(m).
Every minus node becomes trivial: the only potentially surviving diagonal
is [a,CaC], which becomes [a,a]. Every generic equation is respected and
m commutes with a,tau. Thus the assignment is a genuine retraction, with
the evident generator section. This proves the displayed subgroup
presentation and its full induced profinite topology, without a subgroup
recognition argument.

The original orientation restricted to B sends tau to1 and a,m to0.
Its ordinary subgroup is therefore
  B0 = F(a,a*) x Z(m).
This is the actual Schreier basis of the free-product factor. B0 has its
full induced topology too, by finite index in the actual retract B.
The retraction from all G_inner need not preserve its ORIGINAL orientation;
no such assertion is needed here.

2. Its final image is also an actual retract

B130's actual plus-node argument gives w=1 after filling. Therefore the
map of B to E_actual factors through
  Q = B/normal_B(w)
    = (Z²(a,a*) semidirect_swap C2(tau)) x Z(m).
The ordinary subgroup Q0 is Z³(a,a*,m). The complete character/section
proof in whole_node_m_star_presentation27.txt constructs the actual

homomorphism to E_actual=Gamma0 semidirect<tau> by sending tau to tau.
It is a retraction E_actual->Q: its composite with the map just defined
is the identity on a,tau,m. Thus Q embeds as exactly the actual image of
B, and both Q and Q0 have their full induced topologies in the indicated
actual ambient groups.

Consequently the exact discrete subgroup kernels are
  ker(B->E_actual)=normal_B([a,a*]),
  ker(B0->Gamma0)=normal_B0([a,a*]).
The second equality can be read directly from the presentation
F(a,a*) x Z(m) -> Z³. Equivalently the normal relation is invariant under
tau, which interchanges the two a-generators and inverts the commutator.
No larger normal-closure intersection in G_inner is determined by this.

3. Actual full-completion comparison

The two genuine retractions identify Bhat with its actual closure in
G_innerhat and Qhat with its actual closure in E_actualhat. The completed
map Bhat->E_actualhat factors through Bhat->Qhat followed by an injection.
For any group quotient by a specified normal subgroup, the full completion
of the quotient is the quotient of the original completion by the closed
normal closure of that subgroup: finite quotients on the two sides are
exactly the same. Applied here this gives
  ker(Bhat->E_actualhat)=closednormal_Bhat([a,a*]).
The ordinary version is identical:
  ker(B0hat->Gamma0hat)=closednormal_B0hat([a,a*]),
with B0hat=Fhat(a,a*) x Zhat(m) and Q0hat=Zhat³. These assertions concern
actual full closures supplied by retractions and finite index. They do
not identify the completion of an unspecified infinite kernel.
