Classical three-trace rigidity applied to full peripheral source maps
16November2026. Primary reference search pending; no novelty claim.

Let q2:Fhat2->SL2(k) have absolutely irreducible image, wherek is any
finite field. LetPhi be a full peripheral automorphism with common
multiplier+1 or-1. Then there isB inGL2(k) such that
 q2 Phi=Ad(B) q2.
In particular ker(q2) isPhi-stable as a CONCLUSION. For any epimorphism
q:Fhat2->PSL2(k), choose arbitrary determinant-one liftsX,Y of the two
free generator images. Their image is absolutely irreducible. Thus
ker(q) isPhi-stable and the induced automorphism is projective linear.
No stability hypothesis, no semilinear automorphism hypothesis, and no
classification ofAut(PSL2(k)) is required for this stronger formulation.

Proof. PutX=q2(x),Y=q2(y),X'=q2Phi(x),Y'=q2Phi(y). The peripheral
conditions imply
 trX'=trX, trY'=trY, tr(X'Y')=tr(XY),
because conjugation preserves trace and inversion inSL2 does as well.
Phi is onto, soX',Y' generate the same absolutely irreducible image.
Seta=trX,b=trY,c=trXY. Cayley-Hamilton yields
 X^2=aX-I, Y^2=bY-I, YX=-XY+bX+aY+(c-ab)I.
ThusI,X,Y,XY span the generated matrix algebra. Absolute irreducibility
makes itM2(k), so they form a basis, as doI,X',Y',X'Y'. Their multiplication
tables depend only ona,b,c. SendingX toX',Y toY' therefore defines a
k-algebra automorphism ofM2(k), and the matrix-unit construction shows
that it is conjugation by a matrixB inGL2(k). The equality of the two
homomorphisms follows on free generators. This proof works also in
characteristic2; all displayed signs have their field meaning.

For a projectively onto pair, absolute irreducibility follows because
PSL2(k) has no common invariant line over an algebraic closure (k has
at least4 elements in the simple-group application). There is no need
to assert that the chosenSL2 lifts themselves generate allSL2(k).

Scope: multiplier is literally+1 or-1 inZhat, not merely congruent to
one of these at selected primes. More generally the same conclusion
holds whenever the three evaluated traces agree, but no such equality
is asserted for arbitrary multipliers. This result concerns each actual
finiteSL2/PSL2 quotient and its kernel. It does not identify the action
on the full free profinite group, settle higher-rank simple quotients,
or prove an equivariant central-lifting theorem.

Verified primary literature context: McCullough,D., & Wanderley,M.(2013),
Nielsen equivalence of generating pairs ofSL(2,q), Glasgow Mathematical
Journal55,481-509, DOI10.1017/S0017089512000675, Theorem8.2(pp490-491),
records Macbeath's trace-triple theorem over finite fields. The precise
GL2(k) descent used above is supplied by the matrix-algebra proof, so
no mistaken claim of unique SL2(k)-conjugacy in odd characteristic is
made. Goldman's author manuscript arXiv:math/0402103v2 supplies the
classical Fricke-Vogt context overC. The original Macbeath1969 chapter
was located but not accessible; it was not claimed to have been read.
