Put b_s=h_s^-1. Then b_(st)=b_s alpha_s(b_t). Thus s->(b_s,alpha_s) is a homomorphism into the ordinary holomorph Q_f semidirect Aut(Q_f). The actual a-stabilizer is H={b_s s:s in S}=<m,fv>, and H->S is an isomorphism.

2. Finite cocycle models refining an arbitrary actual finite target

Then Q->D0 is an actual finite quotient, rho|Q factors through it, and the cocycle descends. Its subgroup

Define a finite graph Gamma0 with vertices D0/H0 and edges all D0-translates of {H0,CH0} and {H0,tau H0}. The seed edges are not loops: C,tau have trivial S0 projection and nontrivial chi, whereas H0 intersects F0 trivially. Let

This proves finite-target factorization. The same construction allows a refinement D1->D0: choose N1 contained in N0, then choose L1 contained in L0 and the finite action/cocycle kernel for F1=Q_f/N1. The graph maps Gamma1->Gamma0 and group maps G1->G0 are then literal and compatible with E_a.

Fix N0 as above. There is a characteristic finite-index N1 contained in N0 such that
    image(C_(Q_f/N1)(tau) -> Q_f/N0) is contained in <tau>.
Here is the full compactness argument, rather than a finite observation. Characteristic subgroups are a cofinal directed family of finite kernels. Their finite centralizers form an inverse system, and its inverse limit is exactly C_(widehat Q_f)(tau). At the prescribed finite level F0, the images of finer centralizers are decreasing subsets of a finite set. Their intersection equals the image of that inverse limit. This follows by the finite-intersection property on the compact sets of possible compatible lifts; surjectivity of individual centralizer transition maps is not needed. The intersection is <tau> by the factor-centralizer theorem. A decreasing directed family of finite subsets attains its intersection after a finite refinement.
