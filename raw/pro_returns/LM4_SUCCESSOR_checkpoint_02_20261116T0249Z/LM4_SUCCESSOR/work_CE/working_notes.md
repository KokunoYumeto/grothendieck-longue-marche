### The naive finite-order scalar power automorphism fails the cusp test
Choose a unit u of order3 in Zhat× whose7-coordinate reduces to2 and whose
other coordinates may be1. The actual automorphism x↦x^u,y↦y^u has order3,
with inverse obtained from u^−1. It is NOT a torus loop automorphism.
The standalone check_scalar_power_candidate.py realizes the free class-four
exponent-seven quotient in the truncated associative algebra on X,Y, with
x=exp(X), y=exp(Y). Every effective conjugator of c^4, c=[x,y], has logarithm
aX+bY+d[X,Y] modulo terms of degree≥3, which centralize c at this degree.
All343 choices give49 distinct conjugates. Matching through degree3 forces
a=b=4; the degree4 residual is
4(ad_X²[X,Y]+ad_Y²[X,Y]) ≠0.
Thus [x²,y²] is not conjugate to [x,y]^4 in this actual finite quotient.
The general formal coefficient is u²(u²−1)/24 when the characteristic is
not2 or3. This rules out that explicit tempting construction. It does not
exclude a cusp-preserving IA correction: the latter can change the higher
terms, and a local pro-q correction does not produce a full finite-order lift.
