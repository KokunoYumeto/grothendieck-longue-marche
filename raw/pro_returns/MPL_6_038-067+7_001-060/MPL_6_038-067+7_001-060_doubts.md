## Legibility and confidence

All 85 supplied pages, including the covers, reused typescript and cancelled material, were read manually from the whole-page image and its two supplied halves. Every page then received a second visual comparison. No OCR or outside text was used to supply readings.

The reused typescript is substantially clearer than the fast manuscript. The largest remaining uncertainty lies in short cursive words, overwritten symbols, the attachment of crowded additions, and strongly cancelled text. The least secure passages include 6/041, the revisions on 6/043 and 049–050, the small Tate annotation on 6/054, and the clipped final expression on 6/061. In cote 7 the small introduction on 021, the cancellations and margin on 023, the oblique Brauer note on 032, the clipped annotations on 036, the pencil calculation on 048, and the revisions on 053, 055, 057–058 and 060 deserve the most caution.

`high` means that the reported visible feature is clear, even when its interpretation is unresolved; `medium` marks a plausible local reading with remaining graphic doubt; `low` marks an insecure reconstruction or physically obscured words. These labels do not certify mathematical correctness. In particular, an unmarked phrase is still an editorial reading and the audit cannot guarantee that every error has been eliminated.
