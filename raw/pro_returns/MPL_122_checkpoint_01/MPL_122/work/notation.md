## Package notation table

This table is maintained from the first sheet, from visual readings only. The final cross-page comparison precedes the context opening.

| Manuscript sign | LaTeX | Reading convention / source |
|---|---|---|
| Small circle above P | `\mathring{P}` | Interior; scan 003. |
| Boundary sign | `\partial P` | Not a lower-case delta; scan 003. |
| Exclamation beneath cohomology H | `H_!^i` | Compact-support notation as drawn; scan 003. |
| Underlined cohomology H | `\underline{H}` | Preserve the underline; scan 003. |
| Small vee over or after a letter/expression | `F^\vee`, `(\cdot)^\vee` | Dual; scan 003. |
| Tilde over boundary expression | `\widetilde{\partial P}` | Collar notation; scan 003. |
| Plain stroke between displayed expressions | `\mathbin{-}` or `\arrow[r,dash]` | Do not supply an absent arrowhead; scan 003. |
| Handwritten double quotation marks | Two backticks and two apostrophes | Use guillemets only if visibly drawn. |
| Recurrent uncertainty | `\unsure{...}` | Marks the visible reading, never a silent mathematical correction. |
