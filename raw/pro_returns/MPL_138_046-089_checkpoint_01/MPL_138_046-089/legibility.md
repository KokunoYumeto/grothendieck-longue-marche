## Legibility and limits

The notes are written rapidly, with compressed word endings, very faint strokes, overwritten symbols, and corrections that can obscure the earlier state. The transcription records proposed readings with `\unsure` and reserves `\illegible` for words that remain physically unreadable after enlarged visual inspection. No OCR is used. A doubt mark is not a claim that the surrounding material has been omitted.

The uncertainty table is generated from the actual doubt macros, so it includes small mathematical signs as well as words. The most difficult locations are recorded individually in that table. Blank areas and later archive foliation are distinguished from authorial content. This file will be updated after the full second reading.
