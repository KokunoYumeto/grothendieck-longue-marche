## Abbreviations encountered

| Written form | Possible expansion (editorial, not substituted in the transcription) |
|---|---|
| prop. | proposition |
| C.Q.F.D. | ce qu'il fallait démontrer |
| n° | numéro |

## Legibility and scope

This register is maintained during direct visual reading, without OCR. The typed reused sheets and handwritten notes are treated equally as source text. Any marginal foliation whose authorship cannot be established is transcribed as a margin, without attributing it to Grothendieck.

Additional forms: `tt` = tout; `pt` = point; `dim` = dimension; `EGA` = Éléments de géométrie algébrique; `conn. géom.` (uncertain reading) = connexité géométrique.
