\subsection{Regulator routes and the independent geometric resolution}

The following source checks delimit alternative regulator
routes. The arithmetic comparison and integral refinement are
resolved independently in B.20 and B.37--B.40. These source
limitations are retained to make clear which literature statements
are used and which are not needed for that resolution.

Theorem 1.1.5 of \citet{BKings2001} excludes both the prime three
and primes dividing the CM conductor.  Its polylogarithm
specialization construction does not remove those restrictions from
the arithmetic lattice comparison.  The all-prime characterwise
Iwasawa theorem of \citet[Theorem 5.2]{BJohnsonLeungKings2011}
is a distinct statement.  Their equivariant theorem, Theorem 5.7,
retains Conjecture 5.5 as a hypothesis; Corollary 5.12 proves that
hypothesis for split primes not dividing six.  None of these
statements identifies, without an additional comparison, the actual
Ross-symbol Frobenius value defining our intrinsic extension.

For the good-prime syntomic route,
\citet[Theorem 4.11]{RootAsakura2023} gives the Ross-symbol formula
for a Fermat curve of bidegree $(N,M)$ only when
$p>\max(N,M)$.  In particular it does not apply at the coefficient
prime three to the cubic.  Its Conjecture 4.17 also distinguishes
the general nonvanishing expectation from the proved formula.
The conductor-$27$ comparison in the introduction of
\citet{BNemoto2025} is an equality of real regulators; the motivic
equalities established in its Propositions 4.3 and 4.10 concern
conductors $36$ and $64$.  We therefore do not transfer a real
normalization to an unevaluated $3$-adic class.

Two further sources were located but could not be invoked.
The publisher's two-page preview of \citet{RootBesser2021}
defines a Vologodsky triple-index expression and states that its
main theorem proves the proposed regulator formula under an
additional condition on the differential.  That condition lies
beyond the accessible preview; the complete theorem was not read.
The later Hesse-cubic preprint \citep{RootNemoto2026} was found in
arXiv search metadata and the author's institutional publication
list, but attempts to open its theorem text failed.  We make no
assertion about its applicability at three.  Both entries are
marked unverified in the bibliography, with the access limit
specified.  The exact attempted routes and URLs are retained in
\path{work/root_regulator_followup.md}.

A Vologodsky computation would still need a checked theorem,
a suitable differential and potentially semistable model, a
certified evaluation, and comparison with the actual integral
\'{e}tale extension.  B.32 proves a separate obstruction to the
direct inherited Jacobi-sum route: the relevant extension is
killed by that quotient. B.35 proves actual integral nonvanishing
at every good prime, including nonvanishing modulo inner classes
at two. B.36 determines the exact local order at two as $3^{m_2}=27$
by comparing the actual tangential paths with the framed generalized
Jacobian and solving the resulting integral congruences. This exact
local order alone does not evaluate the rational Ross coefficient.
B.37 now supplies the additional arithmetic argument: every one
of the $27$ local candidates fails a necessary condition for the
localization of a rationally zero global outer class. A functional
with five nonzero Hall entries gives the uniform value $6$ modulo
nine. Rational averaging over the $54$ global automorphisms places
this detected class in precisely the Ross summand of B.20, proving
that the specified arithmetic Ross edge is nonzero. Thus the
rational coefficient is $-1$ relative to that edge, or $-1/6$
relative to the tame multiple. B.38 reduces the homogeneous
integral ambiguity under the stated global and local identities
to a single group of order three. Its explicit four-bracket
generator is detected by an intrinsic local coefficient quotient
of order $81$, and B.39 realizes a corresponding class-two
finite quotient. B.40 computes the actual geometric extension
using the multiplication-three elliptic cover, selecting the
unique local vector $b+6h_9$. The detector values are $57$
modulo $81$ and $19$ modulo $27$ on the integral commutator
module. This identifies the integral refinement over the
specified rational Ross class. The prescribed inherited
quotient kills that commutator module and cannot perform
the separation. No numerical three-adic regulator is inferred.
