# Full pro-three cusp colours at two rational primes

Date: 2026-11-16. Status: B.10(a) remains open in its unrestricted form. The final B.42 proof is in `deliverable/sections/B_inert_colours.tex`; it includes the strengthened exact factor27 bound, its compact sharpness, and the split-prime necessary formula. The earlier elementary proof below is retained as an independent check. No new single-prime section classification or finite-quotient counterexample is substituted for the full question.

## 1. Actual groups and conventions

Let `E: X^3+Y^3+Z^3=0`, let `D` be its nine coordinate cusps, and put `U=E-D`. Use the rational unit tangent at `O=C_0=[1:-1:0]`, with parameter `Z/X`. Let `N=pi_1(U_Qbar)^(3)` be the full rank-ten free pro-three group, and let `T=T_3E` be its compact abelian quotient. The arithmetic actions are the actual based actions. Set `K=Q(zeta_3)`, `lambda=1-zeta_3`, and `P=G_{K,{lambda}}^(3)`. B.28/B.29 prove that P is free on the actual conjugate Frobenii `tau,bar(tau)=c tau c` at seven; c is actual complex conjugation. The geometric cocycle and its coefficient action factor through `Gamma=P semidirect C_2`. An actual section retains its identity arithmetic coordinate on `G_Q`.

Use B.24/B.28's compact lattice basis:

\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
C=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
T_\tau=I+3R,\quad T_{\bar\tau}=I+3R^2.
\]

The chosen seven-adic prime has `zeta_3 -> 2` modulo seven and primary CM scalar `1+3 zeta_3`. Its conjugate has `zeta_3 -> 4`. Their cubic Kummer rows on `(zeta_3,lambda)` are respectively `(2,0)` and `(2,2)`, with the identification `1 -> zeta_3` in the coefficient group. These choices are consistent because `1+3 zeta_3=zeta_3(2-zeta_3)`.

Let `R_0` be the rational three-torsion point corresponding to `e_1/3` in `T tensor Q_3/T`. Thus the three rational cusp centres are `O,O+R_0,O+2R_0`. One may identify `R_0=A_0` with the compatible cusp coordinates of B.24, but this geometric label is not needed for the equality theorem.

## 2. Proved cross-prime rigidity theorem

**Theorem.** Let s be any actual full pro-three section of `N semidirect G_Q -> G_Q`, and let alpha be its compact T-valued cocycle. Normalize alpha by an integral compact coboundary so that `alpha(c)=0`, and put

\[
a=\alpha(\tau)=(a_1,a_2)^t,\qquad
k(s)=a_1+a_2\pmod3.
\]

Then k(s) is independent of the remaining compact conjugacy. For every rational prime `q != 3` with `q=2 or 5 mod 9`,

\[
\operatorname{loc}_q\alpha=\kappa_q(k(s)R_0)
\quad\text{in } H^1(G_{\mathbf Q_q},T).
\]

In particular, whenever s is locally cuspidal at such q, its cusp centre is `O+k(s)R_0`. Consequently any two ramified primes in these two congruence classes have the same cusp colour, using the local ramification-implies-cuspidality statement at the point where that implication is needed. The compact localization assertion itself does not assume local cuspidality.

The same compact argument applies to any quotient retaining T, but identification of a ramified quotient's colour separately requires the corresponding local cusp-recognition result. This theorem does not assert a new local recognition theorem.

### Proof: every compact cocycle is unramified outside three

At a good prime q the coefficient action on T is unramified. Wild inertia is pro-q and therefore has zero continuous homomorphism to the pro-three additive lattice T. The remaining inertia map factors through the three-primary tame quotient `Z_3(1)`. If v is its value on a positive generator, the local arithmetic relation forces

\[
F_q v=qv.
\]

Every complex absolute value of an eigenvalue of compact Frobenius is `sqrt(q)`, so `F_q-q` is rationally invertible. Since T is torsion-free, v=0. This proves unramifiedness of the *whole compact cocycle* at every q other than three, without any ramification support assumption on s. Killing the closed normal subgroup generated by these inertias gives its factorization through `G_{Q,{3,infinity}}`.

Over K the actual T action is pro-three (it is trivial modulo three), and the action/cocycle graph therefore factors through P. Complex conjugation supplies the genuine quotient `Gamma=P semidirect C_2`. Since two is invertible in `Z_3`, compact conjugacy normalizes alpha(c) to zero. The free basis then gives `alpha(bar(tau))=Ca`. Remaining coboundaries have conjugator in `T^c=Z_3 e_1` and change a by a multiple of `3R e_1=3e_2`; hence k(s) is well-defined.

### Proof: the inert Frobenius word is used only at its justified Frattini level

Fix an inert rational prime q, so its K-residue field is `F_{q^2}`. Let `f_q` denote an actual rational arithmetic Frobenius. The square `f_q^2` is the K-Frobenius. Set

\[
e_q=(q^2-1)/3\pmod3.
\]

The cubic residue character of zeta_3 is e_q. For lambda, use

\[
\lambda^2=-3\zeta_3.
\]

Every element of `F_q^*` is a cube when `q=2 mod3`. Therefore the cubic symbol of -3 is trivial, and twice the lambda exponent equals the zeta_3 exponent. Thus the row on `(zeta_3,lambda)` is `(e_q,2e_q)`. Comparing this row with the two actual seven-adic rows gives

\[
f_q^2=e_q\tau+e_q\bar\tau
\quad\text{in }P/\Phi(P),
\]

where the right side is written additively. This is only a Frattini identity; no equality of full Frobenius words is asserted.

Because the coefficient action over K is trivial on `T/3`, the reduction of alpha is a homomorphism `P -> T/3`. Hence

\[
\overline\alpha(f_q^2)=e_q(a+Ca)
=-e_q(a_1+a_2)e_1.
\tag{1}
\]

This equation is unchanged by the choice of prolongation, since conjugation by P has trivial action on its Frattini quotient and on `T/3`.

### Proof: exact local Kummer comparison

For `q=2,5 mod9`, one has `v_3(q+1)=1`, and hence `e_q != 0`. On the plane cubic over `F_q`, cubing is a bijection. Its affine chart has q points and there is one point at infinity, so `#E(F_q)=q+1`. Good reduction identifies the three-adic completion of `E(Q_q)` with `E(F_q)[3^infinity]`, which has order three. Its three elements are exactly the rational cusp differences. Equivalently,

\[
H^1_{\rm unr}(G_{\mathbf Q_q},T)
=T/(F_q-1)T\simeq\mathbf Z/3.
\]

The same conclusion follows from `F_q^2=-qI`, `det(F_q-1)=q+1`, and `F_q mod3=C`: the Smith factors of `F_q-1` have valuations 0 and 1. Therefore reduction modulo three detects the entire unramified local class. Restriction to the unramified quadratic extension `K_q/Q_q` is injective, since restriction followed by corestriction is multiplication by two.

The global Kummer cocycle of `R_0=e_1/3 mod T` is literally

\[
\kappa(R_0)(g)=(g-1)e_1/3.
\]

It vanishes at c and takes value `R e_1=e_2` at tau. Applying (1) to this cocycle gives `-e_q e_1`. Thus (1) shows that alpha and `k(s) kappa(R_0)` have the same restricted local class. Injectivity of restriction proves the asserted equality over Q_q.

If the local section is at cusp P, its compact class is `kappa(P-O)`. At an inert q the only Q_q-rational cusps are the three rational cusps (the other cusp coordinates require zeta_3). Their three-primary local Kummer images are distinct by good reduction and the order-three calculation. It follows that `P=O+k(s)R_0`, proving the colour assertion. This completes the proof.

The following exact affine calculation extends the inert range. An arbitrary two-prime full-section counterexample is still not constructed.

## 2A. Stronger exact inert bound: a factor of twenty-seven

Define the global compact class beta by the actual free arithmetic basis:

\[
\beta(\tau)=a=(1,2)^t,\quad
\beta(\bar\tau)=Ca=-a,\quad \beta(c)=0.
\]

The normalized compact cohomology calculation gives

\[
H^1(G_{\mathbf Q},T)=T/(3\mathbf Z_3e_2)
=\mathbf Z_3\beta\oplus(\mathbf Z/3)\kappa(R_0).
\]

Thus every compact class has the unique form

\[
\alpha=b\beta+k\kappa(R_0),\qquad
b=a_1\in\mathbf Z_3,\quad k=a_2-2a_1\pmod3.
\tag{2}
\]

Here a on the right denotes the original class's tau value; the fixed vector defining beta is `(1,2)`. This distinction will be kept explicit in a typeset statement.

**Stronger theorem.** At every inert rational prime q,

\[
\operatorname{loc}_q\beta\in27H^1_{\rm unr}(G_{\mathbf Q_q},T).
\tag{3}
\]

The local group is cyclic of order `3^e`, where `e=v_3(q+1)`. Consequently, if `e <= 3+v_3(b)`, the local compact class of (2) is precisely `kappa(kR_0)`. At any locally cuspidal such prime the centre is `O+kR_0`. In particular, for every full section, the same rational colour occurs at all ramified inert primes with `q not=-1 mod81`.

If b=0, the compact class is globally the rational torsion Kummer class `kappa(kR_0)`. Then every locally cuspidal good prime, split or inert, has centre `O+kR_0`, by local Kummer injectivity on the nine three-torsion cusp differences. This does not assert global cuspidality of the full section.

### Proof of the exact affine image

Use the semidirect-product convention `(z,M)(w,L)=(z+Mw,ML)`. The actual action/cocycle graph of beta has compact image H generated topologically by

\[
A=(a,T_\tau),\qquad B=(-a,T_{\bar\tau}),\qquad c=(0,C).
\]

Write `H_K` for its pro-three subgroup, generated by A and B. Since `T_tau T_bartau=7I` and `T_tau+T_bartau=-I`, direct multiplication gives

\[
[A,B]=(3a,I).
\]

Conjugation by A multiplies this translation by `T_tau`, so the normal closure contains both `3a` and `9Ra`. Put

\[
L=3\mathbf Z_3a+9\mathbf Z_3Ra
 =3\mathbf Z_3a\oplus27\mathbf Z_3e_1.
\tag{4}
\]

This lattice is stable under `T_tau`, `T_bartau`, their inverses, and C. Hence it is exactly the closed normal subgroup generated by the commutator, provided no further coefficient relation contributes translations. To verify this last point, the CM logarithms of `1+3 zeta_3` and `1+3 zeta_3^2`, divided by three, form a `Z_3` basis of `O_K tensor Z_3`: modulo three they are zeta_3 and zeta_3^2, which are independent. Thus the coefficient image is `1+3 O_{K,3}`, freely parametrized by the two exponents in `Z_3^2`. The quotient `H_K/L` is abelian and generated by the images of A and B. The resulting map `Z_3^2 -> H_K/L -> 1+3 O_{K,3}` is an isomorphism on its composite; the first map is onto. Therefore the second map is an isomorphism too. This proves that L is the **entire** translation kernel.

In particular,

\[
L^c=27\mathbf Z_3e_1.
\tag{5}
\]

The translation `-3a/2` belongs to L. Multiplying it by AB gives the actual element

\[
D_0=(-3a/2,I)AB=(9e_1/2,7I)\in H_K^c.
\]

Since seven topologically generates `1+3 Z_3`, every scalar `t in 1+3 Z_3` has a unique form `7^u`. By continuous powering,

\[
D_0^u=(3(t-1)e_1/4,tI).
\tag{6}
\]

Every c-fixed element of H_K with coefficient tI differs from (6) by a translation in (5). Thus its translation is

\[
3(t-1)e_1/4+27ze_1,\qquad z\in\mathbf Z_3.
\tag{7}
\]

### Proof at an inert prime, including the conjugation step

Let g be the actual affine image of a rational Frobenius at q. It lies in the nontrivial c-coset. Its closed procyclic subgroup has a unique order-two component d. Write `d=h c` with `h in H_K`. Since d has order two, `c(h)=h^{-1}`. The unique square root `u=h^{1/2}` lies in H_K and satisfies `u c u^{-1}=d`. Thus conjugation by u makes the order-two component literally c; no hypothetical splitting of a local absolute Galois group is used.

After this conjugation g has the form `h'c`, with `h' in H_K^c`. A coefficient matrix commuting with C inside the CM algebra is scalar. Its scalar is the unique `t in 1+3 Z_3` satisfying `t^2=-q`, since inert compact Frobenius has square `-qI`. We can therefore apply (7). On `T^c=Z_3e_1`, the local coboundary operator is multiplication by `t-1`; on the negative eigenspace it is multiplication by `-t-1`, a unit. The first term in (7) is the integral local coboundary `(t-1)(3e_1/4)`. The remaining class is represented by `27ze_1`, proving (3). Also

\[
v_3(t-1)=v_3(q+1)=e,
\]

because `q+1=1-t^2` and `t+1` is a three-adic unit.

For precision about the conjugation: an affine conjugator `(v,J) in H_K` replaces `(z,M)` by `(J^{-1}(z+(M-1)v),J^{-1}MJ)`. It induces an isomorphism on local integral cohomology, preserving divisibility by 27. Moreover `J=I mod3`, so it fixes the rational torsion point `e_1/3 mod T`. The comparison with `kappa(R_0)` is therefore preserved, including its orientation. Combining (3) with (2) proves the stronger colour bound.

## 2B. Why the compact method cannot force unrestricted colour equality

The exponent 27 in (3) is sharp as a compact necessary condition. Set `t=7^27`, so `v_3(t-1)=4`. For each `r in {0,1,2}`, the element

\[
g_r=\bigl(r(t-1)e_1/3,I\bigr)D_0^{27}c\in H
\tag{8}
\]

exists because `(t-1)/3 in 27 Z_3=L^c`. Its coefficient is `F=tC`, and its translation differs from `r kappa(R_0)(F)=r(t-1)e_1/3` by the integral local coboundary `(F-1)(3e_1/4)`.

Modulo `3^5=243`, the three explicitly specified affine elements are

\[
F=\begin{pmatrix}163&80\\0&80\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z_r=(54r,0)^t,
\quad\det F=161\pmod{243}.
\tag{9}
\]

Take the actual finite Galois quotient supplied by the action/cocycle graph of beta modulo 243, and the conjugacy class of (9). Chebotarev supplies a positive-density set of rational primes for each r with Frobenius in that class. Such primes are inert, satisfy `q=161 mod243` and `v_3(q+1)=4`, and have

\[
\operatorname{loc}_q\beta=r\kappa_q(R_0).
\tag{10}
\]

Here the finite congruence really determines the asserted **integral** compact class: `F-1` has Smith valuations 0 and 4, so `3^4T subset (F-1)T`. Perturbing F by `3^5` changes `(F-1)e_1/3` by `3^4T`, while the cocycle value changes by `3^5T`. Therefore (8)'s equality modulo local coboundaries persists for every affine pair in the indicated congruence class. Rational R_0 is fixed under all Galois conjugation, so the condition also holds on the entire conjugacy class.

Beta itself is the compact image of actual full N-sections unramified outside three: choose any `n in N` mapping to `(1,2)` in T, give the free generator tau the graph value n and its conjugate the c-conjugate value, and set the cocycle at c to one. The actual pro-three action and the free universal property make this a continuous full graph map, exactly as in B.29 with no away-three local factors.

Thus a compact class arising from an actual full section can have all three different rational cusp Kummer labels at different primes. Equations (8)–(10) supply exact finite Galois conjugacy-class data and a positive-density existence theorem; they do not provide a numerical list of primes, do not claim that the corresponding unramified full local sections are cuspidal, and do not introduce ramification there. This is **not** a refutation of B.10(a). It proves that a global argument using only the compact Kummer labels cannot establish the unrestricted colour claim. The nonlinear local cusp-normalizer constraints remain essential.

## 3. Exact bottleneck in a two-prime global construction

Let `S={lambda} union T_away` with `r=|T_away|`. Class number one and Kummer theory give

\[
\dim H^1(G_{K,S}^{(3)},\mathbf F_3)=r+2.
\]

Each away local maximal pro-three group has H1 dimension two. Thus the free product of all r such local groups has dimension 2r. The dimensions agree only when r=2. Two split rational primes have r=4, so the local product has dimension eight while the global group has dimension six.

More precisely, where the two global unit Frobenius rows span, restriction in H1 to all away local groups is injective and has cokernel dimension `r-2`: valuations give r independent inertia coordinates, and the two unit directions give the remaining two coordinates. At a central lifting stage with trivial F3 coefficients over K, restriction in H2 to all away places is injective by the same Kummer/Brauer argument as B.29: Picard is zero, and the missing lambda invariant is determined by reciprocity. Consequently local liftability removes the global central obstruction, but it does **not** make the chosen local lifts match. Their differences must satisfy the `r-2` H1 compatibility conditions. This is the exact gap in an induction based only on local solvability.

For two inert rational primes the two unit rows are proportional `(e,2e)`, so even the H1 isomorphism needed for B.29 fails. For a split pair plus one inert prime, r=3 and there is one matching defect when the unit rows span. The full Frobenius words in a Koch-style presentation cannot be replaced by their Frattini images when solving those nonlinear matching equations.

## 4. Primary arithmetic free-product theorem opened and its limit

I independently opened Alexander Schmidt's author PDF, *On pro-p fundamental groups of marked arithmetic curves*, dated 16 January 2009, at

<https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~schmidt/papers/marked.pdf>.

The author bibliography lists the German publication in *Journal fuer die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, 640 (2010), 203–235. Theorem 1.1(iii), author PDF p.2, identifies a free product of local groups **inside a kernel over** `k_{S0}^{S union T}(p)`, indexed by primes of that intermediate field. It does not state that the selected local groups are a free factor of `G_{S union S0}^T(k)(p)`, and it supplies no retraction with which arbitrary prescribed local geometric graph maps could be extended. Lemma 2.2, p.4, gives the degree-two injection. This source is useful for arithmetic group structure but does not solve the matching issue above.

Other exploratory routes (finite split embedding problems, enlarged base fields, or finite Chebotarev conditions) were not promoted to constructions: one must preserve actual full local cusp liftability at every newly ramified prime and prove a compatible inverse limit. A finite-level Frobenius condition cannot guarantee the exact infinite-level cusp-normalizer condition at a fixed rational prime.
