## Supports containing two

Put c³=2 and F0=K(c). The point `S2=(6ca²,0)` is two-torsion, and direct substitution gives

\[
g_d(S_2)=\frac{\zeta^2c-1}{c-1}=h,
\]

the exact A.69 witness. Moreover `F0(E_d[2])=F0(a)`: the coordinate6ca² generates a over F0, since c belongs to F0 and a² generates K(a). The equality a∈K(c) occurs precisely when the Kummer class of d lies in the line generated by2. For rational d, descent through the degree-two extension K/Q shows that these are exactly the rational cubeclasses1,2,4. Indeed a rational element which is a cube in K is a cube in Q, by taking the norm and using that two is coprime to three.

Those three cubic twists have the already certified rank zero, so they cannot satisfy the hypothesis of an infinite-order rational R. For every twist under consideration the residual two-torsion image over F0 is therefore C3. The joint proper division tower includes L through its two-torsion field, and the C3 element kills the entire two-primary translation kernel in every quadratic character, exactly as in A.69. Other primary translation kernels are odd and are killed automatically.

The witness h is nonsquare in F0 by the explicit prime31 calculation. Its three c-conjugates have product one. Hence F0(√h)/K is not Galois and cannot lie in the abelian CM torsion tower. This proves the required noncontainment before any base extension that would remove the residual C3 action. The subsequent prescribed-primary Chebotarev argument is therefore correctly ordered.

## Supports not containing two but containing p≥5

The untwisting scalar t=b²/a satisfies t³=9/d. On short Weierstrass models its map is `(X,Y)->(t²X,t³Y)`.
