## 1. Data and the smaller coefficient quotient

Write (N=\pi_1(U_{\overline{\mathbf F}_2})^{(3)}), with the fixed good tangential base point used in B.35–B.38. It is free pro-three of rank ten. Put (V=N^{\mathrm{ab}}), (A=\operatorname{Lie}_3V), (M=A(-1)), and (H_0=\mathbf Z_3u+\mathbf Z_3v\subset V). Let

\[
\mathcal J=[H_0,\Lambda^2V](-1),\qquad w=[u,v].
\]

The B.38 coefficient row \(\lambda_{81}:M\to\mathbf Z/81\) has trivial local arithmetic action. Here and below the same underlying Hall-coordinate row is used on the untwisted lattice; its equivariance there is \(\lambda_{81}(F_2a)=2\lambda_{81}(a)\).

Direct exact multiplication of the saved row proves two further facts:

\[
\lambda_{81}(\operatorname{ad}_wV)=0,
\qquad
\lambda_{81}(\mathcal J)=3\mathbf Z/81.
\]

The second equality includes a unit after division by three; it is not just a divisibility bound. Consequently there is a primitive, well-defined local coefficient quotient

\[
\lambda_{\mathcal J}:\mathcal J\longrightarrow\mathbf Z/27,
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathcal J}(j)=\lambda_{81}(j)/3.
\]

Division denotes the canonical isomorphism (3\mathbf Z/81\simeq\mathbf Z/27). It is not division of an arbitrary residue modulo 81. The map is (G_{\mathbf Q_2})-equivariant for the twisted coefficient module. The B.38 oriented remaining class has value (54/3=18\pmod{27}), so this quotient detects the remaining line by the subgroup generated by nine. B.36 also implies that the actual value is one modulo three, hence has order 27.

This does not alter B.38's statement that reduction of the **whole** coefficient lattice (M) modulo 27 kills the residual local difference. It uses the additional, literal membership of the cocycle in the primitive submodule \(\mathcal J\).

## 2. A surjective pair of alternating forms

Let (R=\mathbf Z/27). Define

\[
\phi:\Lambda^2(V/27V)\longrightarrow R^2,
\qquad
\phi(b)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\lambda_{81}([u,b])/3\\
\lambda_{81}([v,b])/3
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1}
\]

This is well defined because each bracket belongs to \(\mathcal J\). The exact (2\times45) matrix is saved in `c_residual_class_two_detector.json` and its NPZ. A two-column minor is a unit modulo three, so \(\phi\) is onto (R^2), not merely onto modulo three. Its kernel is a direct summand of rank 43 over (R). The same computation gives \(\phi(w)=0\).

Let (F\) be the B.35 degree-one matrix of arithmetic Frobenius two, and (M_2\) its compact matrix on the ordered pair (u,v):

\[
F(u,v)=(u,v)M_2,\qquad\det M_2=2.
\]

Equivariance of \(\lambda_{81}\) gives the exact identity

\[
\phi\circ\Lambda^2F=C\phi,
\qquad
C=2M_2^{-t}
 =\begin{pmatrix}26&6\\4&1\end{pmatrix}\pmod{27}.
\tag{2}
\]

For example, evaluating \(\phi(\Lambda^2Fb)\) on (Fh), (h\in H_0), gives (2\phi(b)(h)); this is precisely the contragredient formula in (2). The script verifies all 90 scalar entries of (2), using the actual quaternion-compatible (F), including the integral gluing block. Thus \(\ker\phi\) is stable under actual local Frobenius. Good reduction with the fixed unit tangent makes inertia act trivially here.

## 3. The finite group is an actual quotient of (N)

There is no use of a class-three Lazard correspondence. Only the class-two quotient is needed, where (1/2\in\mathbf Z_3). The free class-two pro-three group has BCH coordinates

\[
N/\gamma_3N\simeq V\oplus\Lambda^2V,
\qquad
(x,z)(x',z')=(x+x',z+z'+\tfrac12x\wedge x').
\]

This can be checked from Hall collection and the central commutator formula. A choice of free generators gives the displayed splitting. The central subgroup \(\Lambda^2V\), its quotient below, and the resulting group quotient do not require a splitting.

Taking 27th powers gives \((x,z)^{27}=(27x,27z)\), so quotienting by the closed subgroup generated by these powers gives the same BCH group over (R). Quotient its central degree-two subgroup further by \(\ker\phi\). Equivalently, if (\widetilde K\) is the preimage of \(\ker\phi\) in the commutator subgroup of (N/\gamma_3N\), put

\[
G=
\bigl(N/\gamma_3N\bigr)
\big/
\bigl( (N/\gamma_3N)^{27}\,\widetilde K\bigr).
\tag{3}
\]

The normality is immediate because \(\widetilde K\) is central modulo the already imposed subgroup. Equation (2) and the characteristic nature of the lower-central and power subgroups prove that (3) is an actual local arithmetic-stable quotient of (N). In coordinates it is

\[
G=(V/27V)\oplus R^2,
\qquad
(x,z)(x',z')=
(x+x',z+z'+14\phi(x\wedge x')).
\tag{4}
\]

The map from (N) is onto: its abelianization is onto (V/27V), and its commutators generate (R^2) because \(\phi\) is onto. Thus

\[
|G|=27^{12}=3^{36},\qquad
G^{\mathrm{ab}}\simeq(\mathbf Z/27)^{10},\qquad
G'\simeq(\mathbf Z/27)^2.
\]

The exponent is 27 and the nilpotency class is exactly two. Associativity of (4) follows from bilinearity of \(\phi\); its commutator is \((0,\phi(x\wedge x'))\). These are identities, not conclusions from the supplemental numerical samples in the script.

This quotient is intrinsic to the cubic group with the selected local coefficient map. It is not asserted to factor through the inherited tripod metabelian quotient that kills \(\mathcal J\).

## 4. The precise Frobenius statistic

The actual Frobenius automorphism of (4) necessarily has the form

\[
\mathscr F(x,z)=(Fx,Cz+B x),
\qquad B\in\operatorname{Mat}_{2\times10}(R).
\tag{5}
\]

Indeed (2) makes the graded linear map an automorphism, and every additional degree-two term is additive, hence (R)-linear. Conversely every (B) in (5) defines an automorphism with these graded actions. The matrix (B) is the projection by \(\phi\) of the actual integral quadratic action term on the free group. It is the arithmetic datum not yet computed.

Write

\[
M_2=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},
\quad
B(u)=\binom{p_u}{q_u},
\quad
B(v)=\binom{p_v}{q_v}.
\]

Use an integral pure-bracket lift of (w), as justified in B.38. Its class-three action variation depends only on the quadratic action term, not on any cubic term on generators. The formula is

\[
\eta(F_2)=\tfrac12\bigl([B_{F_2}u,Fv]+[Fu,B_{F_2}v]\bigr),
\]

where (B_{F_2}:V\to\Lambda^2V) is the unprojected quadratic term. Applying \(\lambda_{\mathcal J}\) gives

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathcal J}(\eta(F_2))
 =\tfrac12(-b p_u-d q_u+a p_v+c q_v)\pmod{27}.}
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the sought value can be read from the **class-two** Frobenius automorphism (5), even though the original action extension occurs in class three. The scalar is an explicit primitive linear functional of the twenty entries of (B). In the fixed ten-generator coordinates, its two rows are

```
[ 6,15,13, 3, 2,26,15, 4, 1,17 ]
[15,18, 3, 7,14, 6,17, 1,20, 4 ]   modulo 27.
```

The assertion \(\phi(w)=0\) does not make (6) vanish: it says that two particular lifts commute in the quotient group, while (6) measures the central correction in the arithmetic action on those lifts.

## 5. Independence of quadratic coordinates and of the Frobenius lift

A change of BCH splitting (z'=z+Ex) replaces (B) by

\[
B'=B+EF-CE.
\]

Let (E_H=E(u,v)), and let
\(J_0=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
Formula (6), applied to the (2\times2) matrix (B_H=B(u,v)), is

\[
P(B_H)=2^{-1}\operatorname{tr}\bigl((M_2J_0)^tB_H\bigr).
\]

The identities (M_2J_0M_2^t=2J_0) and (C=2M_2^{-t}) give

\[
P(E_HM_2)=\operatorname{tr}(J_0^tE_H)
 =P(CE_H).
\]

Hence (6) annihilates every quadratic coordinate change. The script checks the same equality on all twenty basis changes.

Replacing the chosen arithmetic Frobenius lift by its product with a geometric element of abelian image (t) adds

\[
x\longmapsto\phi(t\wedge Fx)
\]

to (B). By the Jacobi identity, its contribution to (6) is \(\lambda_{\mathcal J}([t,w])\), which is zero because \(\lambda_{81}\) kills every \(\operatorname{ad}_w(t)\). The script checks all ten basis inner changes as well. Therefore the statistic depends only on the actual **outer** Frobenius action on the explicitly defined group (G). A separate choice of an arithmetic lift or of quadratic generator corrections is unnecessary for this statistic.
