# Exact projective Legendre level-three comparison

Let F=pi1(P1_{Qbar}-{0,1,infinity}), freely generated by two peripheral loops A,B, and let H be the Fermat cubic subgroup ker(F -> (Z/3)^2), sending A,B to the standard basis. A cubic twist changes arithmetic descent but not this geometric subgroup.

The projective mod-three Legendre representation sends these loops, up to the usual simultaneous basis/conjugation conventions, to the images of

 A=[[1,2],[0,1]], B=[[1,0],[-2,1]] in PSL2(F3)=A4.

The three peripheral images A,B,(AB)^(-1) all have order3 and the same class in the quotient A4/V4=C3. Since H contains F', its image contains A4'=V4; since its abelian exponents are multiples of3, its image in C3 vanishes. Thus its image is exactly V4.

The nine geometric cusp inertia groups of the cubic are generated by conjugates in F of A^3,B^3,(AB)^3, hence the projective mod-three representation kills all of them. It therefore factors through pi1 of the compact cubic, as an actual arithmetic representation: quotienting by the normal closure of all geometric cusp inertia is exactly compactification. This assertion is unaffected by the determinant/cyclotomic arithmetic action (over Q the projective arithmetic image naturally lies in PGL2(F3)=S4), since the geometric kernel is Galois stable.

Consequently the joint arithmetic image of the full compact group and this finite projective level-three quotient is the graph of a homomorphism out of pi1(E), hence is isomorphic to pi1(E). Every proper Kummer section a*kappa(R) therefore lifts to this bare joint quotient, and is locally geometric there at every finite place: the compact local centre [a]R is an actual local E-point, represented by an interior point when outside D or by any tangential cusp section when in D. Thus this finite congruence quotient cannot detect any rank-one ghost.

Geometrically the pullback of the A4 cover has three connected components, each a V4 cover of degree4 of the cubic. Each extends etale over the compactification. A connected degree4 etale V4 cover of an elliptic curve over an algebraically closed field is the multiplication-by-two cover up to isomorphism and translation: its subgroup in the compact geometric fundamental group is the kernel of reduction modulo2. This identifies the retained information as compact two-division.

The conclusion is deliberately about the finite projective Legendre mod-three monodromy quotient (X(6) over the lambda-line) and the joint quotient retaining the full compact group. It does NOT retain the full fundamental group of the punctured pullback cover, nor show that every compact section lifts to that larger group. The boundary-unit mod-two quotient used in A.60 and A.65 is not contained in this finite level-three quotient, because its cusp monodromy is nonzero while the latter's cusp monodromy is zero.
