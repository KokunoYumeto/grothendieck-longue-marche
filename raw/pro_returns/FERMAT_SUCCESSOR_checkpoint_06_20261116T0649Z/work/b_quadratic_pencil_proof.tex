\subsection{The full quadratic maps of the two flex pencils}

\begin{statement}{B.44}{The two class-two pullback maps in the actual geometric marking}{Proved}
Use the ten elliptic Schreier generators, their positive peripheral
marking, and the integral matrix $P$ from B.40.  Since three is odd,
class-two logarithms identify the group with its integral BCH Lie
algebra.  Use the pure linear lifts of the original $V$ basis obtained
by the matrix $P$ in that Lie algebra.  This fixes the geometric
class-two coordinate system; it is not an assertion about arbitrary
choices of lifts of a homology basis.

For the actual two maps of B.43 there are target peripheral markings,
unique up to target inner conjugation, in which the full maps are
\[
 (v,z)\longmapsto
 \bigl(P_i v,\ q_i v+(\Lambda^2P_i)z\bigr),                \tag{B.44.1}
\]
where $P_i$ are the integral matrices (B.43.15), the target degree-two
basis is the intrinsic commutator $c=[y,x]$, and
\[
 \begin{split}
 q_1&=(3/2,0,3,0,-1,0,-1,0,0,-1),\\
 q_2&=(0,1/2,0,0,0,0,1/2,-1/2,0,0).
 \end{split}                                                \tag{B.44.2}
\]
All coefficients belong to $\mathbb Z_3$.  Thus the full quadratic
pullback comparison needed in the two B.43 probes is explicit.
The nonabelian actual cusp origins themselves are supplied by the
compatible radical fibres of B.43; a linear action coordinate is
still a difference from a specified reference origin, not a raw
finite central value.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{The quotient geometry fixes the maps.}
The first map is the quotient by the elliptic involution
$h(Q)=2A_0-Q$, followed by forgetting two puncture images.
Its permutation of the nine cusps is
\[
 (O,B_0),\quad(A_1,A_2),\quad(B_1,C_2),\quad(B_2,C_1),
 \quad A_0\text{ fixed}.
\]
The quotient has five punctures.  The images of $O,B_0$ and
of $B_2,C_1$ are filled in to obtain the tripod.
There are three further interior fixed points of the involution.
Their order-two stabilizers in the orbifold fundamental group
are killed on passage to the pro-three tripod group.  Thus there
is an order-two based lift of $h$ for which the actual map satisfies
$\phi_1h=\phi_1$.  All order-two complements in this pro-three
extension are conjugate by $N$.  Precomposing a group map by an
inner source automorphism is postcomposing it by an inner target
automorphism.  Hence any explicitly constructed order-two lift
in this same geometric extension may be used.

For the second map, $\sigma:[X:Y:Z]\mapsto[X:Y:\zeta Z]$
acts freely on $U_3$, since all three fixed points $C_0,C_1,C_2$
have been removed.  The quotient is a five-punctured sphere.
The points corresponding to $C_0,C_2$ are then filled in.
The positive one-third tangent path at $C_0$ projects to a full
loop around its interior image in the tripod, hence to a null
loop.  In this marking the actual map therefore satisfies
$\phi_2\sigma=\phi_2$.

The based representatives used here really are the ones in the
elliptic Schreier calculation.  In the discrete once-punctured
torus group the CM word and the actual positive tangent lift have
the same homology matrix and preserve the positive peripheral
word $\delta$.  Nielsen's theorem makes their difference inner.
Preserving $\delta$ restricts that difference to conjugation by
$\delta^a$, since a nontrivial peripheral word has cyclic
centralizer.  Their cubes are both conjugation by $\delta$,
so $3a=0$ and $a=0$.  The same argument, using the square, pins
the positive half-tangent involution word.  This strengthens the
outer comparison precisely where the present map calculation
requires a based representative.

\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{The exact order-two normalization.}
Write $F=\langle a,b\rangle$ for the discrete parent group and
$\delta=aba^{-1}b^{-1}$.  Its boundary-fixed involution is
\[
 \iota(a)=aba^{-1}b^{-1}a^{-1},\qquad
 \iota(b)=ab^{-1}a^{-1}.
\]
Put $t=a^2$ and $h=\operatorname{Int}(t)\iota$.
Then exact free-word substitution gives
\[
 h^2=\operatorname{Int}(k),\quad h(k)=k,
 \qquad k=t\iota(t)\delta\in N.                            \tag{B.44.3}
\]
These are chronological words.  Under actual fibre monodromy,
the square is $\operatorname{Int}(M_k^{-1})$, as in B.40.
Consequently
\[
 h_0=\operatorname{Int}(M_k^{1/2})h                         \tag{B.44.4}
\]
has order two.  The square root exists uniquely in the pro-three
group; its class-two calculation divides only by two.  The word
$t$ has deck coordinate $(2,0)$, so $h_0$ is a lift of the
required involution $Q\mapsto2A_0-Q$.

\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{The finite linear calculation.}
Let $C,B$ be respectively the linear and quadratic parts of either
actual source automorphism in these coordinates.  For a target
map with given linear part $p=P_i$, let $q$ be its unknown
quadratic row.  Its invariance under the source automorphism is
exactly
\[
              q(C-I)=-(\Lambda^2p)B.                       \tag{B.44.5}
\]
For a peripheral word whose class-two logarithm is $(e_P,z_P)$,
filling its image imposes
$q e_P=-(\Lambda^2p)z_P$.
For the first map impose this at $B_0,B_2$, and normalize the
images at $A_1,B_1$ to $x,y$ by setting the same central
expressions to zero.  For the second, fill $O,C_2$ and normalize
$A_0,B_0$ to $x,y$.

The target normalization is legitimate integrally.  An
automorphism of a free class-two pro-three group on two generators
which is the identity on abelianization has
$x\mapsto xc^a$, $y\mapsto yc^b$; every such pair $(a,b)$ is
realized by conjugation by an element of its abelianization.
Thus its entire IA group is inner, and the two central
normalizations can be imposed simultaneously.

The program starts again from the exact Schreier words, not
from the desired rows.  It reverses the words for actual
monodromy, so every quadratic word logarithm is the negative
of the chronological one.  It computes the order-two correction
in (B.44.4), verifies $C^2=I$ and
$BC+(\Lambda^2C)B=0$, and solves (B.44.5) with the four
additional peripheral conditions.  Each coefficient matrix
has rank ten and its unique solution is exactly (B.44.2).
This proves existence and uniqueness of the displayed maps
among the actual quotient maps, rather than inferring a
geometric map from a numerical rank alone.

For additional checking, the source peripheral images are
the following class-two logarithms; the last coordinate is
in the intrinsic Hall generator $c$.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 P&\phi_1(\log I_P)&\phi_2(\log I_P)\\\hline
 A_0&(-2,-2,3)&(1,0,0)\\
 A_1&(1,0,0)&(1,0,-1)\\
 A_2&(1,0,-1)&(1,0,0)\\
 B_0&(0,0,0)&(0,1,0)\\
 B_1&(0,1,0)&(0,1,0)\\
 B_2&(0,0,0)&(0,1,-1)\\
 C_1&(0,0,0)&(-3,-3,-3/2)\\
 C_2&(0,1,0)&(0,0,0)\\
 O&(0,0,0)&(0,0,0)
\end{array}                                                  \tag{B.44.6}
\]
In particular the two pole inertias are conjugates of the
second and third powers of the positive infinity inertia,
with the ramification indices already proved in B.43.

\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{What the new comparison supplies.}
For any class-two input given in these geometric coordinates,
equation (B.44.1) computes its two actual Heisenberg images.
In particular it computes the image of a specified reference
origin, so the missing quadratic-map part of the B.43 affine
comparison has been removed.  The target inner identification
does not change the nonabelian section class, nor the central
difference of two origins with the same abelian origin.
The separate remaining work is to choose and subtract a
compatible global reference, extract the actual linear
arithmetic-action components, and insert them into the full
integral local-obstruction and convergence law.  This theorem
does not claim that those remaining steps in B.5 are automatic.
\end{proof}
