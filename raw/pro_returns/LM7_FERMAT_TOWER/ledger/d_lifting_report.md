# Additional profinite lifting attempt

We opened Bleher–Chinburg–Lubotzky, arXiv:2004.08860v3, at Lemma2.3, Corollary2.4, Theorem2.1 and their proofs. This supplies surjectivity of unrestricted automorphism lifting for every closed normal quotient of a finitely generated free profinite group. D.24 applies it to the full metabelian quotient and proves precisely what its lift leaves uncontrolled.

An explicit genuine automorphism in the kernel of the action on the entire metabelian quotient fails peripheral preservation. It is obtained by lifting the identity on the metabelian quotient together with an outer automorphism of A5. The quotient pair is onto by the proved absence of a common quotient. The finite certificate enumerates A5, its conjugacy classes and normal closures, and all3600 multiplicativity checks.

This route supplies actual unrestricted profinite lifts, beyond the pro-l lifts in D.12. It does not supply lifts in the specified peripheral subgroup or its S3 centralizer. The exact remaining step is to impose those conjugacy conditions while retaining generation and compatibility through every finite quotient, as in D.22. D.24 neither proves nor refutes the existence of such a different lift.
