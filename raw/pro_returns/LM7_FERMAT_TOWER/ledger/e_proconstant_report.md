# Arithmetic proconstant relative completion

E.30 is in `sections/E_proconstant.tex`; its statement ledger is `e_proconstant_results.json`.

The finite-stage kernel of the arithmetic orbifold group over S3×Gal(Q(μ_m)/Q) is the tangential semidirect product Fhat2⋊G_{Q(μ_m)}. Every unipotent representation of this kernel kills Fhat2. Continuously over Q_l, a suitable arithmetic element acts by χ_l≠1 on geometric abelianization but by a unipotent adjoint operator on the target, forcing the geometric image to vanish. Abstractly over Q, Nikolov–Segal2007 Theorem1.4 identifies Fhat2's abstract abelianization with Zhat²; choose χ−1 equal to a nonzero integer times a profinite unit. Its induced adjoint-minus-identity is both surjective and nilpotent, with the same conclusion.

Hence the exact relative completion is S3 times the arithmetic relative completion of G_Q. The section specifies its finite stages, kernel maps, completed inverse limit and universal property. Every finite-dimensional algebraic representation of a proconstant base factors through a finite quotient, so this inverse system is precisely the completion over S3×Zhat*. It kills all the Fermat geometric homology.

The statement also constructs the retaining alternative: the negatively weighted completion over algebraic G_m is F^un⋊A_l, with its geometric kernel injected. The proof gives its universal property and proves that no algebraic map from the proconstant cyclotomic base implements the infinite ℓ-adic character.

Primary-source verification: Nikolov–Segal, *On finitely generated profinite groups I*, Annals165(2007), Theorem1.4, was opened in the original publisher PDF, https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v165-n1-p05.pdf . Its citation key `dNikolovSegal2007` is in `d_refs.bib`. The weighted-completion reference is the already opened `E_HainMatsumoto`, sections2–4. All remaining conclusions are proved in E.30 from these definitions and the actual cyclotomic action.

The full structure of each ordinary Galois unipotent kernel is not claimed computed. Its exact completed inverse-system description is part of the proved result; replacing it by a free group of an asserted rank would require additional verified arithmetic cohomology input.
