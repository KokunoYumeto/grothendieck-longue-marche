Reviewed work_A/A51_filiform_scalar_working.md and check_filiform7_scalar.py/.out. The scoped strict second scalar layer and subsequent stabilization pass. No norm obstruction or full involution statement is supported or claimed.

## Actual quotient and the strict layer

The semidirect head law is consistent with BDB^{-1}=3D. The explicit representation formula follows by inducing the character ell from the abelian normal subgroup of the7-group; the seven character translates are distinct. The central character forces every F2-linear intertwiner to be F8-linear, and the different positive/negative Frobenius orbits separate V and its dual. The additional r0 Frobenius action reduces the endomorphism field to F2. Twisting by s gives the distinct second J-summand, so the two trace forms and their s-swap action give exactly the stated odd/full wedge coinvariant dimensions2/1.

The normalized cocycle dimensions are correct:21+28−42=7; after setting the s value to zero the coboundary rank is21. Every nonzero class gives a full affine image because a proper surjective subgroup would either have zero module kernel and be a complement, or contain the irreducible kernel. H1(H,V)=0 follows from the normal odd subgroup and its zero invariants. Thus the nonzero-class argument really proves H1(C,V)=0 for all classes, not just the selected cocycle, and likewise for the dual. The paired image is full by distinctness of the two irreducible modules.

The displayed two-center group law is associative, since each bilinear cross term is a2-cocycle; its H action is honest because s exchanges the two pairings. Correcting the r lift by its central cube works since r acts trivially on the center and has order3. The order2/3 generated image surjects Vbar:H. Its intersection with P surjects Vbar, and independent commutators generate both central coordinates, so it is the full P:H. The same argument gives image(R)=P and image(W)=Z. Therefore [W,Pi] maps onto the diagonal central C2 and its next commutator maps to1. This alone already proves strict W2<W1, without any stabilization hypothesis.

## Upper bounds and the third step

The finite Pontryagin-duality step remains valid at the second and third stages. One detail worth making explicit is that the potential kernel in the R-preimage abelianization has a finite filtration by trivial C-modules: at the third stage the old center is the length-two unipotent F2[C2] module, whose composition factors are still trivial. Any nonzero such finite module, or its dual, has nonzero invariants. Relative perfectness and H1(C,Vbar^vee)=0 force those invariants to vanish, hence force the whole abelian kernel to vanish. This avoids assuming that the old center itself is a trivial H-module.

After obtaining R-preimage abelianization Vbar, the central commutator pairing at stage2 factors through the old trivial center tensor Vbar and vanishes. At stage3 its source is instead Z tensor Vbar; the odd normal N acts trivially on Z and has zero coinvariants on Vbar, so that pairing still vanishes. This use of N is justified through the finite H action on both source factors; a splitting of H inside C is unnecessary.

Only after this vanishing is the new center identified with the R-preimage derived subgroup, hence with an elementary-abelian quotient of wedge2(Vbar). Its action then factors through H. The odd J acts trivially on both old and new central subquotients; Maschke then makes its action on the extension trivial. The dimension bound2 is attained by the old two-center quotient, so the third central layer vanishes. The argument must, as the draft states, be applied to all finite quotients retaining G2 before passing to the inverse limit. This supplies the necessary global step.

## Limits

The head need not be c-stable, and no compatible negative action or nonzero norm class is constructed. The assertion is an actual failure of immediate stabilization for this specific initial congruence-pronilpotent base, with W1/W2=C2 and W3=W2. It does not show that an indefinitely chosen characteristic tower fails to stabilize or that the full involution problem has a negative answer.
