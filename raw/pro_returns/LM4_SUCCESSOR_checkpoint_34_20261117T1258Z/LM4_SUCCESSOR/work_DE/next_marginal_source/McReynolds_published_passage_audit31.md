The published Lemma 5.5 still starts with a fixed prime ell without a coprimality condition and concludes that its quotient S_ell/C is centerless. This construction cannot work for every such prime: take a noncyclic finite p-group P and ell=p. Both successive regular-module semidirect products, and their subgroups R_p and S_p, are finite p-groups. The displayed quotient S_p/C still maps onto nontrivial P, so it is a nontrivial finite p-group and has nontrivial center. This is a precise obstruction to the printed unrestricted construction. It does not refute the centerless-refinement theorem or the congruence subgroup theorem. The independent coprime relation-module construction in the main audit supplies the refinement actually needed.

The published Lemma 5.4 still displays an embedding of the quotient by the joint peripheral normal closure into the product of the coordinate peripheral quotients. In general, coordinate normal closures need not have the joint intersection property required for such an embedding. This audit does not assert a counterexample to that particular geometric product; it simply does not import the displayed embedding without an additional proof. The one-target finite construction and the direct compact-normalization argument in the main audit avoid this step.
