# The center-line basis change cannot fix the actual family basis

Cycle29 research. Proposed exact obstruction to one specified compatible extension. This is neither a failure of an arbitrary family-basis change nor a post-filling collapse assertion.

Use the frozen center-line proof and its notation

    T_h=F(h,a,d,e,V), Q_0=<h^2,a,d,e,V>,
    W=a h V h^{-1}a^{-1}h(de d^{-1})h^{-1}d^{-1},
    r=W h W^{-1}, Y_0=X_{WQ_0}, Y_1=X_{WhQ_0},
    Z_0=k, Z_1=X_{rQ_0}, q=Y_1^{-1}r.

In the actual graph Sigma for N_reg, let lambda_0 be the deleted family vertex alpha_1=bar a_1. By B141's complete link table, it is adjacent to the center k_0=Z_0. Its stabilizer for the T_h graph action is

    Q_alpha=h<a,B,d,e>h^{-1},   B=h V h^{-1}.

There is no hidden h-period in this stabilizer: h shifts the actual family index, so a stabilizer has height zero; the displayed retained link is the complete stabilizer from the graph-kernel coset construction. The center stabilizer is Q_0. The pair (k_0,alpha_1) is one actual edge, so Y_0 is adjacent to alpha_1 exactly when

    W in Q_alpha Q_0.                                     (1)

Here the equivalence is the explicit coset-edge rule: WQ_0 and Q_alpha have a common representative.

Condition (1) is false, with an elementary exact detector. Work in the index-two subgroup of T_h with even h exponent. Its free Schreier basis is

    H=h^2,
    a_0=a, a_1=hah^{-1},
    d_0=d, d_1=hdh^{-1},
    e_0=e, e_1=heh^{-1},
    V_0=V, V_1=hVh^{-1}.

Let chi be the exponent of the basis letter V_1. Both Q_0 and Q_alpha lie in this index-two subgroup. The first is generated by H,a_0,d_0,e_0,V_0. The second is generated by

    a_1, H V_0 H^{-1}, d_1,e_1.

Thus chi vanishes on both subgroups and on their product. But the complete marked word is

    W=a_0 V_1 a_0^{-1}d_1e_1d_1^{-1}d_0^{-1},

and chi(W)=1. This proves nonadjacency of Y_0 and lambda_0, without a word-depth or coset census.

Apply the graph automorphism r and put lambda=r(lambda_0). Then lambda is adjacent to Z_1=r(Z_0), and is not adjacent to Y_1=r(Y_0). The two centers Z_1 and Y_1 are distinct and nonadjacent. Their full three-vertex induced graph consequently gives the actual coordinate retraction

    N_reg -> <Y_1>*<Z_1,lambda | [Z_1,lambda]>=Z*Z^2.       (2)

The free-center basis automorphism of the center-line proof sends

    Z_1 -> u_1=qkq^{-1}=Y_1^{-1}Z_1Y_1.

Suppose this prescribed center basis change extended to a homomorphism of the actual N_reg while fixing every family vertex. The actual defining relation [Z_1,lambda]=1 would then have to map to

    [Y_1^{-1}Z_1Y_1,lambda]=1.                            (3)

Under (2), the word in (3) has eight nontrivial alternating syllables in Z*Z^2, hence is nontrivial by free-product normal form. This is a contradiction.

Therefore the actual center-line basis change has NO extension to an endomorphism of N_reg fixing the actual family basis. It can still be used as the valid free-generator/Tietze change in the complete presentation, but every family-edge relation must be rewritten. In particular one cannot retain the old graph edges after changing only the centers, nor replace the carried leaf families by unchanged coordinate vertices.

The obstruction says exactly which next compatibility problem remains: any successful larger basis comparison must also change family whiskers while respecting all original graph relations and both initial mirror families. No arbitrary such comparison is excluded here, and no statement about compact extension or ambient filling survival follows.
