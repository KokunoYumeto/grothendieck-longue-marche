No general fixed-lifting theorem is invoked. For g in Gamma0^delta choose h in Hsrc with pi(h)=pi(g); then h^-1 g lies in Krem^delta. Consequently

This is a subgroup-intersection criterion, not a replacement of Hsrc by its normal closure. The sequence is not asserted to split: the actual source-to-quotient map may have a kernel.

This only proves failure of that literal pre-node section/action in that marking.
