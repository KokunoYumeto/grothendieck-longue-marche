\subsection*{The full ordinary stabilizer and its marked section}
\statement{B.149}{Proved}{The actual one-family stabilizer is cyclic and its fixed projection has a marked ordinary section}
\label{stmt:B149}
In the exact marking of B.148,
\[
 H\cap S=\langle m\rangle.
\]
Consequently the actual one-family fixed group and its marked lift are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B149-section}
\begin{gathered}
 J_a=\Gamma_a^\delta
   =\langle A,m,v\mid[A,m]=1\rangle
   \cong(\mathbb Z_A\times\mathbb Z_m)*\mathbb Z_v,\\
 \sigma:J_a\longrightarrow\Gamma_0^\delta,
 \qquad(A,m,v)\longmapsto(A_{\rm src},m,v),\qquad
 A_{\rm src}=s_1s_4^{-1}s_1^{-1},\\
 p_a\sigma=\operatorname{id}_{J_a}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Here $p_a$ is the actual one-family filling in the one-neck annular
model; the section is a section of its fixed subgroup restriction.
If $K_{\rm rem}=\ker p_a$ and $H_{\rm src}$ is the full six-source
image, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B149-split}
\begin{aligned}
 \Gamma_0^\delta&=K_{\rm rem}^\delta\rtimes\sigma(J_a),\\
 H_{\rm src}&=(H_{\rm src}\cap K_{\rm rem})\rtimes\sigma(J_a).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\begin{proof}
Retain the actual words $f=w^{-1}\bar r^{-1}$ and $g=CfC=r^{-1}w$.
The quotient of $T_0$ by the normal closure of
$r^2,\bar r^2,f^2,g^2$ is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B149-pillow}
 P=\langle z,w,r\mid[z,w],\ r^2=1,\ rzr=z^{-1},\ rwr=w^{-1}\rangle
   =\mathbb Z^2(z,w)\rtimes C_2(r),\qquad z=\bar r r.
\end{equation}
Indeed $g^2=1$ makes $r$ invert $w$, $f^2=1$ makes $\bar r$ invert
$w$, and $\bar r=zr$ then gives $[z,w]=1$ and $rzr=z^{-1}$.
Conversely these formulas make all four original squares trivial,
with inverse substitutions on $r,\bar r,w$. Thus the translation
lattice in \eqref{eq:B149-pillow} is genuinely free abelian.

In the unique normal form $(n,\varepsilon)=z^{n_1}w^{n_2}r^\varepsilon$,
the actual source maps are affine:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B149-matrices}
\begin{gathered}
 (n,\varepsilon)\longmapsto(A_sn+\varepsilon b_s,\varepsilon),\\
 M=A_m=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad b_m=0,
 \qquad U=A_u=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\4&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
 b_u=\binom{0}{-2}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
These are automorphisms, with inverse $(A^{-1},-A^{-1}b)$, so the
quotient is invariant under both signed source generators. In
particular $m(w)=wz^{-1}$, while $u(r)=w^{-2}r$ and $u(z)=zw^4$.
For $q=(1/2,0)^t$ the two generator formulas give
$b_s=(I-A_s)q$. The affine composition rule
$b_{st}=A_sb_t+b_s$ proves this identity for every signed source
word. If $s$ fixes the actual $r$, its quotient correction is zero,
and therefore $A_s(1,0)^t=(1,0)^t$.

We verify whole-source faithfulness. Conjugating $M,U$ by
$\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ gives
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
On $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb R)$ put
$X=\{|x|>1\}\cup\{\infty\}$ and $Y=\{|x|<1\}$.
For every nonzero integer $n$,
\[
 A^n(Y)\subset X,\qquad B^n(X)\subset Y,
 \quad A^n(x)=x-2n,\quad B^n(x)=\frac{x}{2nx+1}.
\]
The second strict inequality follows from
$|2nx+1|\ge2|n||x|-1>|x|$; the image of infinity is $1/(2n)$.
A nontrivial reduced word is conjugate to a nonzero pure power or
a mixed cyclically reduced word. In the mixed case conjugate by a
power of its last generator chosen not to cancel the last syllable.
The resulting word starts and ends in that generator, and sends
$X$ into $Y$ or $Y$ into $X$. It cannot act trivially. Thus the
representation of $F(m,u)$ is faithful even projectively; no
nontrivial word maps to $I$ or $-I$.

An integral determinant-one matrix fixing $(1,0)^t$ is
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&k\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)=M^{-k}$.
Faithfulness therefore gives $s=m^{-k}$. Conversely $m$ fixes the
actual $r$, so B.148 proves $H\cap S=\langle m\rangle$.

B.144's entire-stabilizer presentation now gives the first line of
\eqref{eq:B149-section}, with both inverse presentation maps retained.
B.130 proves that the actual fixed word $A_{\rm src}$ projects to $A$
and satisfies $[A_{\rm src},m]=1$. Hence its displayed assignments
respect every relation and give $p_a\sigma=1$. This proves injectivity
of the marked section and both decompositions \eqref{eq:B149-split}.
No equality of the remaining fixed kernel with its source part is
implied.
\end{proof}
