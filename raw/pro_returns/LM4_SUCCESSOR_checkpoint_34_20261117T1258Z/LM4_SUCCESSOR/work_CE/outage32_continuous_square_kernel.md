# Continuous peripheral-square kernel

THEOREM. Let F=Fhat(x,y), epsilon:F->Zhat with x->0,y->1, K=ker(epsilon). Let N_x^square be the closed normal closure in F of the ONE word x², and D_x=closure(K²[K,K]). All groups below are FULL profinite, not merely pro-two.

D_x is characteristic in K, hence normal in F, and contains x²; therefore N_x^square <= D_x <= K. The profinite universal property identifies L=F/N_x^square with the full profinite coproduct C2(a)*Zhat_y. Its map to Zhat has the actual section generated by y. If P is its kernel, K maps ONTO P because the two height maps agree. Its kernel is exactly N_x^square, giving the actual topological isomorphism K/N_x^square=P by compactness. No completion of an infinite-index discrete kernel is substituted.

CONTINUOUS COPRODUCT. For n>=1 let U_n be the full profinite coproduct of n copies C2(a_j), j in Z/n. For n dividing m, fold a_j to a_(j mod n). These are compatible continuous surjections. Put U=lim_n U_n. For t in Zhat let a_t=(a_(t mod n))_n. This is a continuous family of involutions that topologically generates U: it generates each coordinate, and a closed subgroup surjecting onto every coordinate in a directed inverse limit is all U.

If Q is finite and t->b_t in Q is continuous with b_t²=1, the assignment factors through some Z/n. The finite-family coproduct property gives a unique continuous U_n->Q and then U->Q taking a_t to b_t. For general profinite Q apply this to each finite quotient; uniqueness gives compatibility. Conversely every continuous U->Q yields such a continuous family. Thus U has the continuous involution-family universal property, not unrestricted assignments on discrete labels.

Translation by lambda in Zhat sends a_t to a_(lambda+t). At each level it is the cyclic Z/n action on U_n. These actions commute with folding and are jointly continuous coordinatewise. Form U semidirect Zhat. Send a to a_0 and y to the standard generator to obtain L->U semidirect Zhat. Conversely the actual continuous family t->y^t a y^(-t) in L gives U->L. It is equivariant and extends to the semidirect product. The composites fix all topological generators. Thus
L=U semidirect Zhat, P=U,
with a_t literally y^t a y^(-t).

ABELIANIZATION. The image of D_x under K->P is Phi_2(P): squares and commutators map onto the corresponding generators and compact images of closed subgroups are closed. P is generated by involutions, so P_ab has exponent two. Consequently
D_x/N_x^square=closure([P,P]), K/D_x=P_ab.
Put M=lim_n F2[Z/n], with basis vectors folded by residue. Its continuous generating family t->T^t has the universal property for continuous families into elementary abelian profinite groups, proved by the same finite-target argument. Therefore
P_ab=M=F2[[Zhat_y]],
taking the ACTUAL conjugate y^t a y^(-t) to T^t. This does not interchange abelianization with an inverse limit without proof.

COFINAL FINITE AMBIENT TARGETS. Let phi:U->Q be continuous and finite. The family phi(a_t) factors through some Z/n, giving phi_n:U_n->Q. Intersect the n cyclic translates of ker(phi_n) to get an open normal cyclically invariant subgroup B of U_n. Then (U_n/B) semidirect C_n is an ACTUAL finite L-target whose restriction kernel on U is contained in ker(phi). This is kernel refinement, not extension to the same codomain, and says nothing about noncontinuous abstract finite-index subgroups of U.

FINITE-LABEL RETRACTS. For distinct t_1,...,t_r in Zhat choose n separating their residues. Let V be the full profinite coproduct C2(b_1)*...*C2(b_r). Assign b_j to the residue class of t_j and 1 to other classes, defining a continuous involution family. It gives U->V; the map V->U sending b_j to a_(t_j) has identity composite. Thus the actual closed subgroup generated by these finitely many labels is an embedded retract isomorphic to V.

STRICT INDIVIDUAL INCLUSION. U surjects to U_2, which is nonabelian: send its two involutions to (0,1),(1,1) in Zhat semidirect C2 with inversion action. Their commutator is translation by -2, nonzero. Hence closure([P,P]) is nontrivial and N_x^square < D_x. This is a universal-map argument, not a finite-target search.

DISCRETE INTEGER ORBIT. The discrete free product R_Z=*_(i in Z) C2(a_i) embeds in U. Any nontrivial word uses finitely many labels; the finite-label retract reduces to residual finiteness of a finite free product of finite groups. Such a free product has a free finite-index subgroup (the kernel of its map to the product of the factors); free residual finiteness and finite-index cores prove the assertion.

R_Z nevertheless does NOT have full induced topology from L. Let psi:R_Z->C2 send only a_0 nontrivially. In every finite L-target the image of y has order n>=1, so a_n and a_0 have the same image. The element a_0 a_n is killed there but not by psi. No finite ambient target refines ker(psi). This single-integer-label assignment is not continuous on Zhat.

The integer labels are dense in the compact label space, so R_Z is dense in U. Its full abstract completion therefore surjects onto U. That map is noninjective: an isomorphism would make psi continuous on U, and the proved finite ambient refinement would contradict the preceding paragraph. This reinforces the distinction between U and the unrestricted discrete-index completion.
