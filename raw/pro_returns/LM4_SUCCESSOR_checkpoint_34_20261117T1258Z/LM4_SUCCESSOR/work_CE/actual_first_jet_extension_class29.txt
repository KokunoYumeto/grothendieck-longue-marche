Its coordinates are (epsilon,D_f,D_g,D_x), equivalently the coefficients

3. The extension class has infinite order

Extension (1), with the displayed Q0-module action on V, defines its
ordinary abelian extension class c in H^2(Q0,V). Its lifts over the free
T and free S may be chosen arbitrarily. A change of such lifts is exactly
the unrestricted derivation/source-correction change used in the eight
equations of the companion proof. Thus the weighted functional there
annihilates every possible lift coboundary and evaluates the actual
defect to a nonzero value of magnitude2 (the sign depends only on whether
the right-side convention is -d or d).

More concretely, the n-fold Baer sum has the same module action and
correction table multiplied by n: choose the original free lifts in
each factor and add their kernel coordinates in the Baer quotient.
A split n-fold sum would satisfy the same fourteen-variable equations
with both nonzero right sides multiplied by n. Their combination would
give 0=2n. For every nonzero integer n this is impossible. Hence c has
infinite order. This argument does not require an unproved presentation
complex or a choice of cellular sign conventions for H^2.

the explicit finite quotient

       E_p -> (P_p/U) semidirect Sbar

It is not necessary for the first finite quotient of P_p itself to be
