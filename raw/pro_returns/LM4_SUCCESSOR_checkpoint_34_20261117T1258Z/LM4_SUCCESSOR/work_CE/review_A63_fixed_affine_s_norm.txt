Exhaustiveness for the fixed affine base.
For the same old positive affine action and the same old affine s-frame,
the difference between two new actions is a T-valued cocycle.  The
literal cusp and the s-class requirement kill its two restriction
classes.  The actual joint restriction H1(Pi,V^(2))->k^4+k^4 is an
isomorphism, so the difference is an inner automorphism Inner(a).
Literal cusp preservation forces a in T^tau.  The canonical negative
coefficient action is the identity on T^tau, hence b(a)=a.  With
b'=Inner(a)b and beta'=a beta one has exactly

        beta'^{-1} b'(beta'^{-1})=beta^{-1} b(beta^{-1}).

Any other frame over that same affine frame differs by the already

This includes the newer central-half choices allowed in A61/A62.

Boundary of the conclusion.
The preceding argument does not keep track of every different old
affine action or old affine frame.  Those changes can alter the cup
obstruction.  Root and A have now constructed a controlled alternative
A56 affine tuple with all four cusp-frame offsets t_i=0.  That tuple
is being checked separately in the six-cup quotient.  Thus the result
above is a genuine obstruction to retaining the specified affine datum,
not a claim that the next quotient has no involutory framed action.
No scalar-refinement or full R2 realization is inferred merely from
the finite action computation.
