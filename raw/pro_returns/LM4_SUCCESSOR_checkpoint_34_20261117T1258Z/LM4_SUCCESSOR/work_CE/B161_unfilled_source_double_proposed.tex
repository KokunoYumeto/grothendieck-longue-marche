\subsection*{The actual unfilled source double and its complete defect kernel}
\statement{B.161}{Proved}{The unfilled source double has an exact free coset kernel, and its intersections with both actual defect fillings are determined}
\label{stmt:B161}
Let $G_{\rm gen}=F_{11}\rtimes S$, $S=F(m,v)$, be the actual
generic ordinary group of B.123, with $\delta=\operatorname{Inn}(\tau)$.
Use the \emph{filled} composite of B.139
\[
 G_{\rm gen}\longrightarrow\Gamma_0=G_{\rm gen}/N
       \longrightarrow\Gamma_a,
 \qquad q_a:G_{\rm gen}\longrightarrow\Gamma_a.
\]
Put $J=\langle A,m,v\mid[A,m]=1\rangle\cong\mathbb Z^2*\mathbb Z$,
$K_a=\ker q_a$, and $P=q_a^{-1}(J)$. The actual generic section
$\lambda:J\to P$ of B.159 sends $A$ to $A_0=a\tau a\tau$ and fixes
$m,v$. Write
\[
 D_0=\langle\lambda(J),\delta\lambda(J)\rangle,\qquad
 D_+=\langle D_0,\tau\rangle.
\]
Then these are actual embedded groups with presentations
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B161-double}
 D_0=J*_{S}J,\qquad
 D_+=J*_{S}(S\times C_2(\tau)).
\end{equation}
The two copies in $D_0$ are interchanged by $\delta$. Its fold kernel
$R=\ker(D_0\to J)$ is free on the entire family
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B161-basis}
 X_{jS}=\delta\lambda(j)\lambda(j)^{-1},
 \qquad jS\in J/S,\quad jS\ne S.
\end{equation}
Set $X_S=1$. The complete formulas are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B161-action}
 \begin{gathered}
 D_0=R\rtimes\lambda(J),\qquad
 \lambda(g)X_{hS}\lambda(g)^{-1}=X_{gS}^{-1}X_{ghS},\\
 \delta(X_{jS})=X_{jS}^{-1}.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
For $c_0=X_{AS}=[a^*,a]$, $a^*=\tau a\tau$, and
$N_\lambda=\langle\!\langle c_0\rangle\!\rangle_P$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B161-kernels}
 \begin{gathered}
 R=\langle\!\langle c_0\rangle\!\rangle_{D_0}
   =\langle\!\langle c_0\rangle\!\rangle_{D_+},\qquad
 N_\lambda=\langle\!\langle R\rangle\!\rangle_{K_a},\\
 N_\lambda\cap D_+=N\cap D_+=K_a\cap D_0=R.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
These statements retain all source words; they assert no free-product
factor position for $R$ in the entire generic group.
\begin{proof}
\emph{A legitimate pre-node detector.}
B.139.1--B.139.2 first delete the other three families and their mates,
before imposing the surviving nodes. Their normal closure is invariant
under both signed source generators. The resulting actual generic quotient is
\[
 E_{\rm pre}=(\mathbb Z(a)*Q_f)\rtimes S,
 \quad Q_f=C_2(C)*\mathbb Z(f)*C_2(\tau),\quad Q_c=Q_f\rtimes S.
\]
In this quotient the complete actions are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 &a&C&f&\tau\\\hline
 m&a&f^{-1}Cf&f^{-1}CfCf&\tau\\
 v&f^{-1}af&f^{-1}Cf&\tau f\tau&\tau.
\end{array}
\]
Their displayed inverses in B.139 preserve $Q_f$ as well. Thus the
pre-node free kernel has simultaneous basis $a_q$, indexed by $Q_c/H$,
where
\[
 H=\{h_s^{-1}s:s\in S\}=\langle m,fv\rangle,
 \quad h_{st}=\alpha_s(h_t)h_s,
 \quad h_m=1,\quad h_v=f^{-1}.
\]
Equivalently $E_{\rm pre}=Q_c*_{H}(H\times\mathbb Z_a)$.
The actual stabilizer calculation B.149 gives $H\cap S=\langle m\rangle$.
The original orientation makes $H,S$ even and $\tau$ odd. Consequently
the pairs
\[
 \{sH,\tau sH\}\qquad(s\langle m\rangle\in S/\langle m\rangle)
\]
are disjoint: equality of two first endpoints is exactly equality of
the indicated source cosets, and no first endpoint is a second endpoint.

For two distinct free letters $x,y$, the elements $xy,yx$ freely
generate a free group of rank two. Indeed $z=xy,y$ is a free basis,
and $z,yzy^{-1}$ are distinct members of the Schreier basis
$\{y^nzy^{-n}:n\in\mathbb Z\}$ of the kernel of the $y$-exponent.
Applying this simultaneously to the disjoint pairs proves independence
of all norm letters and all their reversed mates. Source conjugation
permutes the pairs by its complete left action. The two copies of
$J=S*_{\langle m\rangle}(\langle m\rangle\times\mathbb Z)$ therefore
have precisely their common subgroup $S$ as amalgamating subgroup.
The source relations already hold in the original generic group.
The quotient detector is injective on the group with that presentation,
so it proves the asserted embeddings there, not merely in a quotient.
Adjoining $\tau$, which swaps the copies and centralizes $S$, gives
both presentations in \eqref{eq:B161-double}.

\emph{Both maps for the complete free kernel.}
Let $F_X$ be the abstract free group on the nonbase cosets $jS$.
Define on its generators
$\theta_g(X_{hS})=X_{gS}^{-1}X_{ghS}$. This is independent of the
representative $h$. Direct substitution gives
$\theta_g\theta_h=\theta_{gh}$ and inverse $\theta_{g^{-1}}$.
In $F_X\rtimes_\theta J$ the two maps
\[
 j\longmapsto j,\qquad j\longmapsto X_{jS}j
\]
are homomorphisms and agree on $S$, because
$X_{ghS}=X_{gS}\theta_g(X_{hS})$. They give a homomorphism from
the double. Conversely its actual cocycle $\delta\lambda(j)\lambda(j)^{-1}$
is constant on $jS$ and satisfies the same identity, giving a reverse
homomorphism. Both composites fix every generator. This proves the
free basis and semidirect presentation. Swapping the two copies sends
each $X_{jS}$ to its inverse, proving the final formula.

\emph{Exact restricted kernels.}
Identifying $A$ in the two copies identifies the entire copies, since
both are generated by $A,S$. Hence the normal closure of $c_0$ in
$D_0$ is exactly its fold kernel. It is invariant under $\delta$,
so the same holds in $D_+$. The filled diagram gives $N\le K_a$;
the actual node relation gives $c_0\in N$. Therefore
$R\le N_\lambda\le N\le K_a$ and
$K_a\cap D_0=R$, proving all intersections (an element of $N$ is even).
Finally $P=K_a\rtimes\lambda(J)$, and $R$ is stable under $\lambda(J)$
by \eqref{eq:B161-action}. Its normal closure in $K_a$ is consequently
normal in $P$. The two inclusions now give the claimed formula for
$N_\lambda$.
\end{proof}
