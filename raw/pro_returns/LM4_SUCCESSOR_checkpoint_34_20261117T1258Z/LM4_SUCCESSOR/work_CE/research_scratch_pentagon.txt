Primary Furusho2010, Pentagon and hexagon equations, Annals171(1),545-556, DOI10.4007/annals.2010.171.545. Cor12 and13 concern pro-l and pronilpotent shadows; the displayed lambda can lie in a quadratic extension and need not be a Z_l unit. Question14 explicitly asks, for full profinite f satisfying pentagon and a given lambda in Zhat with lambda²=1+24c2(f), whether the3-cycle follows. Pentagon already implies2-cycle there. This is exactly the boundary relevant to the canonical fixed associator for a GT involution.

Primary verification of later status: Furusho2017 Galois action on knots I, publisher EMS PDF https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/36830?nt=1, Remark1.7(2), still explicitly lists the full profinite implication as unknown. Search both routine and stronger engines, including recent-window searches for Question14/profinite/pentagon/hexagon, found no proof of the full statement. New October2026 cyclic-reflection paper arXiv2610.17482 is expressly graded GRT1 and cannot be transferred. No current theorem of nonexistence or impossibility inferred from the search.

Mathematical remaining point: even if the R1 unique coboundary h automatically satisfies the genuine pentagon by A36 fixed-arrow argument, one still needs an integral unit multiplier mu, the full3-cycle, and invertibility of the resulting full free-group endomorphism. Pro-p conjugacy does not constrain the particular full h sufficiently: fixed subgroups of complex conjugation in free pro-p groups can be nontrivial for odd p, so local conjugators need not equal the image of the unique FULL h. No false local-to-global uniqueness argument.
