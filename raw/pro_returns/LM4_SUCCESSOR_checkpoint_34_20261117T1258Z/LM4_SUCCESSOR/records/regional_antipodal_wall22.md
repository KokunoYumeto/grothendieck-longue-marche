# A precise obstruction to the tempting clustering deformation

Research observation, not a formal group comparison. Put
u=exp(2ia), r=exp(2ib), with a,b in (0,pi). Then

    |u+r-ur|^2-1 = 8 sin(a) sin(b) cos(a-b).

Indeed u+r-ur=1-(1-u)(1-r), while
(1-u)(1-r)=-4 sin(a)sin(b) exp(i(a+b)). Expanding the
squared modulus and using
2 sin(a)sin(b)+cos(a+b)=cos(a-b) gives the formula.
Consequently the two reciprocal-conjugate physical collision roots
exchange their inner/outer roles exactly at an antipodal pair.

For the four fifth roots indexed 1,2,3,4, the pair(1,4) is the only
unordered distinct pair whose angular separation in (0,2pi) exceeds pi.
Its half-angle difference is 3pi/5; every other difference is pi/5
or 2pi/5. A deformation clustering all four roots in one small arc
must cross an antipodal wall for this pair. At that wall the relevant
collision leaves the open regional base. Such a deformation is not
automatically an isotopy of the actual regional divisor complement.

This identifies a concrete issue in the suggested product presentation
((F4 x F4) semidirect_swap C2)*Z. It neither proves nor refutes that
presentation, and it does not determine a transported commutator word.
The actual branch puncture and both simultaneous(1,4)/(2,3) nodes must
remain in the complete based calculation.
