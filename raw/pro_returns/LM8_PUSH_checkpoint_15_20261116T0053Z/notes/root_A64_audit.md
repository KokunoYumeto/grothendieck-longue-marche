The comparison is the actual open immersion on the same proper source
curve, with the composed map to the punctured elliptic curve specified
separately. It does not claim a refinement of the original covering map.
Prime-to-residue geometric monodromy gives the tame good model after
finite extension. Both ordinary and supersingular torsion clusters are
counted, including residue characteristic two. At a ramified cusp the
count is one plus the ramification index times the number of additional
base points, so there is always a marked companion. The inherited
normalizer argument uses the identity on proper homology and the proved
zero proper-homology image of cusp inertia. No lifting from a quotient is
substituted for this actual map.

For inert Heisenberg covers and pure powers of the Heisenberg prime,
the main component is radicial over the elliptic curve, the positive
genus tails have nonzero constant images, and every marked point maps
to the origin. The compact-type tree therefore gives the required
connected rational marked paths. Together with A.63 and the tame case,
this covers every odd Heisenberg prime and every enlargement factor.
