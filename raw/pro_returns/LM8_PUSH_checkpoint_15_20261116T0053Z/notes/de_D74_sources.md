## Precision checks incorporated in the candidate

The ordinary maximal-ideal associated graded ring over Z5 need not be commutative: the commutator has a leading term 5u. The candidate instead assigns weight two to 5 and weight one to u,v,gamma-1. Then all commutators raise weight strictly, and its ordered expansion gives a commutative polynomial associated graded ring. This valuation, extended to Ore fractions, proves that a nonidentity tame automorphism is outer.

The coefficient ring R is used for an auxiliary actual continuous cocycle problem. Its division-ring rank is not transferred to Lambda by tensoring; Lambda would be a torsion R-module. Instead a right Ore denominator produces integral operator-valued cycles, and evaluation on actual tame-fixed vectors produces integral continuous Alexander cocycles. A nonzero residual operator detects every actual coboundary. This is the step that avoids unbounded-denominator failures in rational inverse limits.

The invariant Ore denominator is not obtained by assuming a Reynolds average is nonzero. The intersection of the four conjugate right ideals is nonzero by Ore, and independence of automorphisms modulo inner automorphisms produces a nonzero average of a suitable right multiple. That independence argument is reproduced.

Faithfulness and infinite rank are proved on the actual completed Lambda action. Integral finite-order linearized coordinates supply fixed vectors e U^n with n=2 modulo 4. Lowest augmentation degree reduces the completed operator to convergent scalar evaluations f(6^n-1). The elementary Strassmann division proof is included; it handles all infinitely many completed coefficients.

The compact product multiplicity in the final answer is countably infinite. This terminology is kept separate from the algebraic Z5-rank of a countable product. D.35 and D.47 supply torsion-freeness, compactness, the upper bound on product multiplicity, and equality with unrestricted first cohomology.
