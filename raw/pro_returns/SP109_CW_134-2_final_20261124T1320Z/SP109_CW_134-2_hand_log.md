# Hand log

Habits below are local observations, not permission to normalize fresh occurrences. Each entry states its local scope; doubtful cases remain local.

## Local hand and notation synthesis

Each entry gives the pages on which a form was observed, whether it can safely be generalized, and the precise witness places it affected. **Variable** means that the observation is a comparison to make, not a substitution rule. A resolution cited below rests on a named recorded audit; a still-doubtful reading is not silently resolved by this synthesis. Original witness readings are distinguished from doubts introduced during the first pass.

No convention from the earlier-cote seed table is treated as established solely because it occurred in the seed. The observations here are on scans 153–169. References to the mathematical identity of a field, coefficient or fundamental group explain a local allograph decision; they do not claim a proof of the manuscript's mathematical assertions.

### Exact locator key

`Cnnn:Lx` means original witness C, file line x in `codex/<folder>/pages/<folder>_134-2_scannnn.tex`; the folder is `H134-02` for scan 153, `H134-03` for 154–161, and `H134-04` for 162–169. `H08:Lx` and `H09:Lx` mean actual file lines in `hua/transcripts/134-2/batch-08.fr.tex` and `batch-09.fr.tex`. H's page marker is scan minus one. A cited audit box is documented in the corresponding page record and `crops/second/`; a named first-pass crop is in `crops/`. These original-file locators remain fixed when the consolidated page is edited.

### Limits and witness-layer controls

A real separator, rule or diagram is retained as graphic matter; a word, letter, ordinary sign or inserted text is never substituted by a drawing. H's editorial note may describe a mark absent from its body, or acknowledge uncertainty without an in-text doubt macro. Such cases are reported by their exact layers: C155:L7–9 / H08:L182 body and L183 note; C161:L5 / H08:L242 body and L243 note; C168:L7–11 / H09:L142 note between L141 and L144; C169:L7–8 / H09:L153–154 body and L161 note; C160:L2 / H08:L227 body and L233 uncertainty note.

These distinctions prevent three false conclusions: an editorial acknowledgement is not a body transcription, a local handwriting comparison is not automatically a witness error, and a provisional doubt introduced during this run is not evidence that the original C had that doubt. The detailed page observations below preserve the remaining local qualifications; completed audit decisions control any superseded first-pass statement.
