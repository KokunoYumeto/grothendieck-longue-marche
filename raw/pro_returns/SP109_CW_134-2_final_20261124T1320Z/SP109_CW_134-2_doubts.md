## Legibility and comparison of the witnesses

Most complete baseline words can be read, but the hand often compresses several low letters into one horizontal return. The difficult decisions concern endings, small accents and stops, the attachment of additions, overlapping corrections, and the endpoints of connectors. A readable sentence is therefore not evidence that every letter or mark in it is secure. The saved records distinguish complete readings, local alternatives, and physical losses; they do not turn the sentence's expected wording into missing ink.
