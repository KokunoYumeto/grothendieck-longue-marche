### What local comparisons settle

The completed audits identify the single-shoulder field glyph as k by its clearer local forms and the explicitly named field: scans 162–167 provide examples. On 167 the opening C doubt is resolved; the later two k forms were already k in both witnesses. The tall pi on 159 and 162 likewise belongs to the same fundamental-group notation, with a curved right foot as a local comparison. Barred m on 159 and 163 is resolved from the reduced m in *mod*, while the shorthand itself remains unexpanded.
