## Legibility and limitations

The weakest passages are the compressed agenda on 170 (especially item 1 and the bottom addition), the internal cancellations on 179, the initial adjective and a parenthesis on 183, the short qualification before the order-two insertion on 185, the cancelled opening and lower side note on 191, and the cancelled/bracketed lower passage and rotated side additions on 193. Several of these have tentative readings rather than blank placeholders; they should not be quoted as securely deciphered French. The recurrent xi/zeta-like sign, the doubled small-loop superscript, and a few small indices also remain doubtful.

The typescripts are substantially clearer, but their handwritten symbols, overwritten letters, underbars, and anomalous typed forms need caution. The audit retained literal forms such as “de dans”, “gens donnés”, “pouah dominant”, “paraboréliques”, “ça conflicte”, and the ungrammatical English rather than repairing them. Page 181's fourteen tabular rows were checked cell by cell. The lower pencil numbers are transcribed without attributing them to Grothendieck, and the typed page numbers and headers are recorded separately.

All eighteen whole-page images and all thirty-six supplied halves were personally inspected in order without OCR. Every page received a second visual audit, using additional crops or rotation where necessary. Reverse-side show-through was distinguished from writing on the scanned face. No physically unreadable word was replaced with an illegible token: all difficult passages received tentative readings with explicit uncertainty. This does not imply that their words have been securely recovered. Confidence labels are conservative: “medium” means a plausible but not secure visual reading; “low” denotes serious ambiguity, including the particularly compressed single-word readings listed above.

“Upper / middle / lower” in the index is a rough location within the transcription, not a reconstruction of manuscript line numbering. The exact fragment line and the quoted reading allow the passage to be found; a marginal item can appear later in the fragment than its vertical location in the image.
