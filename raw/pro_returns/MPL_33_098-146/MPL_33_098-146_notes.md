### N10. A point already exhibits infinite Tor

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 108, 112. **Approx. source line(s):** 108:10; 112:15–17. **Kind:** own idea.

### N16. An explicit characteristic-p failure in degree one

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 118. **Approx. source line(s):** 6–9. **Kind:** own idea.

For algebraically closed $k$ of characteristic $p$, Artin–Schreier gives $H^1_{\mathrm{ét}}(\mathbb A_k^2,\mathbb F_p)=k[x,y]/\{h^p-h\}$ (The Stacks Project Authors, n.d., [Tag 0A3J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A3J)). This checks the manuscript's failure claim and is consistent with the doubtful addition “même pour $H^1$,” but it gives no permission to remove that paleographic doubt from the frozen text.

**Confidence:** high — the Artin–Schreier quotient and the mixed-monomial obstruction are explicit.

### N25. A perfect complex with nonprojective cohomology

**File:** cote 33. **Scan(s):** 126–132. **Approx. source line(s):** 126:6–8; 128:4; 130:2–6; 132:5–10. **Kind:** own idea.
