**Issue:** The summary of the second regime omits two hypotheses that are explicitly present on scan 006 and in the cited [Stacks 0EYU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0EYU).

**Suggested replacement for the first sentence:** “The fundamental theorem says that proper direct images of torsion sheaves ‘commute à tt changement de base,’ whereas the following NB drops properness for a smooth base change, keeping the original morphism quasi-compact and quasi-separated and the coefficients prime to residual characteristics.”

This is a scope clarification, not a transcription correction.

### N17 — Give the full scope of the constructibility comparison

**Issue:** [Stacks 0GL0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GL0) also requires separatedness and a Noetherian coefficient ring, and distinguishes bounded-below torsion complexes from the unbounded case for a torsion ring; the present sentence calls its comparison precise while naming only some of these conditions.

**Suggested replacement for the final sentence:** “[Stacks 0GL0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0GL0) gives a modern comparison for separated maps of finite presentation between quasi-compact, quasi-separated schemes: with Noetherian coefficients, it preserves constructibility for bounded-below complexes with torsion cohomology, and also for unbounded constructible complexes when the coefficient ring is torsion.”

The fixed ring Z/n used elsewhere in these pages satisfies the relevant coefficient conditions, but that should be stated rather than leaving the modern comparison apparently unrestricted.

### N18 — Preserve uncertainty in the alleged authorial question marker

**Suggested opening:** “The doubtful heading ‘[Question] de la généralisation’ continues with ‘à des f de type fini au lieu de f propre’ and proposes reducing to an open immersion.”

### N41 — Make the unbounded torsion-coefficient restriction explicit

**Issue:** [Stacks 0G2A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G2A) proves its unbounded statement when the coefficient ring is torsion; for a general coefficient ring its domain is the bounded-below torsion-cohomology category. The lemma also assumes a separated finite-type map between quasi-compact, quasi-separated schemes.

**Suggested replacement for the second sentence:** “The justification still appeals to finite cohomological dimension for the proper compactification; [Stacks 095U](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/095U) supplies the proper fibre-dimension bound, and [0G2A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G2A) gives the corresponding unbounded boundedness statement for a separated finite-type map of quasi-compact, quasi-separated schemes over a torsion coefficient ring, such as the fixed Z/n here.”

This keeps the comparison within the manuscript's coefficient setting and does not imply an unbounded theorem over arbitrary rings.
