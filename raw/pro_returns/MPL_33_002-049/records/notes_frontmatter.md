The README places the cote in the SGA 4/5/7, étale-cohomology, fundamental-group and monodromy area, and asks for the standing inventories plus the **S19** comparison against print and the Stacks project. The field/Galois example on **002** is foundational, but this package contains no substantial treatment of vanishing cycles or a developed SGA 7 monodromy argument; it supplies no specific advance on the open arithmetic/anabelian entries LM7 E.23, LM4 D.12 or LM4 F.7.

The geometric counit and top-degree trace of **020–036** must first be distinguished from those endomorphism traces, then compared through the duality formalism. This is a structural comparison, not a dating argument, and it does not close the later finite-level-to-adic compatibility task.
