R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(F^\cdot,\unsure{G|C}^\cdot)

\[
\boxed{R\operatorname{Hom}(F^\cdot,\unsure{G|C}^\cdot)=R\Gamma_X R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(F^\cdot,\unsure{G|C}^\cdot)}

\left\{\begin{aligned}
