## N153.1 — Serre's frame torsor supplies the quotient and its étaleness

- **File/scans:** cote 29, scans 153–154 and 156.
- **Kind:** own idea.
- **Confidence/reason:** high, by étale-local splitting and descent.
- **Context:** `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “La proposition 3.2” and “La démonstration de Serre” (their 152–155 = our 153–156); no matching settled RESULTS_STATE entry.

## N157.1 — The marked inertia argument needs separatedness

- **File/scans:** cote 29, scans 157 and 155; finite case on scans 153–154.

## N162.1 — Finite index, categorical cokernel, and a component hypothesis

**Scan:** 162. **Quote:** “propre dominant”; “a un conoyau fini”. **Kind:** own idea. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, modernised reading of cote 29, “La normalité n'est pas une commodité”, “L'énoncé”, their p.161; no exact matching RESULTS_STATE theorem (LM1 E.11 concerns a different group-scheme problem).

## N162.3 — Normal target alone does not give universal openness

**Scan:** 162. **Quote:** “universellement ouvert [p. ex. $Y$ normale]”. **Kind:** own idea. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, modernised reading of cote 29, “La normalité n'est pas une commodité”, their pp.161–162; the bracket is not discussed there; no matching RESULTS_STATE theorem.

**Confidence/reason:** High: the explicit blow-up chart image D(xy) union the origin is checked directly; the refutation is conditional on the bracket having no extra implicit hypothesis.

If the bracket claims that the previously stated proper dominant morphism is universally open merely because $Y$ is normal, an additional hypothesis is missing. ## N183.5 — The same functor has no alternative topological representing space

**Witness:** cote 29, scan183, “Ces conditions sont néc et suffisantes”. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, by the naturality and coordinate argument, independently checked in this run. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “Quand un foncteur est pro-représentable”, their p.182; relation: strengthens N183.1's contradiction of the asserted sufficiency, while leaving abstract strict pro-representability untouched.

If the functor F of N183.1 were naturally C(L,−) for some other space L, the elements represented by identities of ℕ^n would give compatible continuous maps q_n:L→ℕ^n. Each q_n must be surjective, since factoring through a proper image A would put that identity element in F(A), contrary to the surjective truncation maps. On the clopen piece q_1^{-1}(m), define h_L to be the parity of coordinate m+1 of q_{m+2}; these definitions make h_L:L→{0,1} continuous. Naturality would make h_L factor through some q_n, but surjectivity of q_{m+2} for m≥n supplies two points with equal n-prefix and different selected parity, a contradiction, so no such topological representing space exists.

## N187.2 — The global normalisation arrow is false even after its correction

- **File/scan:** cote 29, scan 187, final paragraph.
- **Kind:** own idea.
- **Confidence/reason:** high, from the open-immersion example satisfying every recovered hypothesis.
- **Context:** `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “Une proposition sur les morphismes de type fini” (their 186 = our 187), differing from its unqualified “on conclut par la normalisation”; no matching settled RESULTS_STATE entry.

## N187.3 — A repair of the proposition via the open normalisation model

- **File/scan:** cote 29, scan 187.
- **Kind:** own idea.
- **Confidence/reason:** high under the recovered locally quasi-finite interpretation; the proof is written out and its standard inputs are identified.
- **Context:** `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “Une proposition sur les morphismes de type fini” (their 186 = our 187); `notes/RESULTS_STATE.md`, LM1 E.11 and LM1 E.2 checked for scope and not used.

To prove “il suffit de voir que f est quasi. cpt”, work over an affine Nagata base and on one reduced irreducible component, let \(Y_0\) be its integral image closure, and form \(X^\nu\) and the normalization \(T\) of \(Y_0\) in \(k(X)\). Both normalisations are finite over their respective schemes, and the separated locally quasi-finite birational map \(X^\nu\to T\) to the resulting normal finite \(Y_0\)-scheme is an open immersion, proved locally by Zariski's main theorem. Since \(T\) is noetherian, \(X^\nu\) is quasi-compact; its finite surjection onto \(X\) gives quasi-compactness of \(X\), and the finite collection of components completes the proof. This repairs the theorem without the false reverse arrow in N187.2, using [Stacks, 033R](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/033R), [035A](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035A), [035S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035S), and [02LR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LR), and has no substantive connection with LM1 E.11 or E.2.
