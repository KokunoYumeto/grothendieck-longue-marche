## N179.2 — Finite tests do not imply finite topological generation

**Witness:** cote 29, scan 179, “les πᵢ sont de type fini”; the tested stronger equivalence is in the context, not on this scan. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, from the explicit profinite product and its finite quotients. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “Le pont entre H¹ et π1”, their pp.46–57 = archive scans47–58, and “La proposition”, their p.178; relation: contradicts a general equivalence in the modern context, without refuting the separate generator construction on this scan.

Let $p_n$ be distinct primes and $A=\prod_{n\geq1}(C_{p_n})^n$: its quotients $(C_{p_n})^n$ require unboundedly many generators, so A is not topologically finitely generated. For each fixed finite group G, every continuous map $A\to G$ kills the factors with $p_n\nmid|G|$, and continuity plus density of finite-support elements makes it factor through the remaining finite product, so there are only finitely many such maps. Taking $G=S_m$ also gives finitely many open subgroups of each index m, disproving Hua's unrestricted converse from finite tests to finite generation. The refined assertion is one-way—finite topological generation implies finite tests—and this counterexample does not affect geometric finite-generation theorems with additional hypotheses or the separate explicit presentation proposed on scan179.

## N186.3 — Encoding, representing and effecting descent are separate operations

**Witness:** cote 29, scans 179–186, “données de descente effectives”; “Van Kampen”. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “La traduction simpliciale” and “Le van Kampen”, their pp.180–185; “π1 et Picτ birationnels : la première page”, their pp.59–60 = archive scans60–61; relation: adds an internal cross-reference and separates three claims.

Hua explicitly connects the later cocycle formalism with an earlier use of van Kampen in the normalization discussion. Encoding descent by cocycles, representing the pointed cocycle functor by a group or progroup, and proving that its data produce geometric objects are three different tasks. Scans179–180 themselves separate the last two, which explains why the modern fpqc answer in N180.2 should be added to the programme rather than presumed from the existence of the cocycle presentation.

## N183.5 — The same functor has no alternative topological representing space

**Witness:** cote 29, scan183, “Ces conditions sont néc et suffisantes”. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, by the naturality and coordinate argument, independently checked in this run. **Context:** Hua, `hua_project/29.modern.tex`, “Quand un foncteur est pro-représentable”, their p.182; relation: strengthens N183.1's contradiction of the asserted sufficiency, while leaving abstract strict pro-representability untouched.

If the functor F of N183.1 were naturally C(L,−) for some other space L, the elements represented by identities of ℕ^n would give compatible continuous maps q_n:L→ℕ^n. Each q_n must be surjective, since factoring through a proper image A would put that identity element in F(A), contrary to the surjective truncation maps. On the clopen piece q_1^{-1}(m), define h_L to be the parity of coordinate m+1 of q_{m+2}; these definitions make h_L:L→{0,1} continuous. Naturality would make h_L factor through some q_n, but surjectivity of q_{m+2} for m≥n supplies two points with equal n-prefix and different selected parity, a contradiction, so no such topological representing space exists.
