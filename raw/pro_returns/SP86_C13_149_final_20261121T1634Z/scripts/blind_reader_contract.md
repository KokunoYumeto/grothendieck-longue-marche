# Independent image-only reading

Read only the one page assigned by the main reader, its original scan image, and its `second/<page>_boxes.tsv`. You may use this contract and the transcription conventions below. Do not open the page transcription, the first-reading TSV, either witness, page packets, notation tables, reading reports, reviews, changes, notes, or comparison files. Do not inspect another page. You have not previously read the assigned page.

For each supplied box, crop the original image once at the listed coordinates. Enlarge with LANCZOS to about 2500–3200 pixels across, use autocontrast and mild sharpening where useful, and rotate the crop upright where the handwriting slopes. Save it as `crops/<page>_<box>_second.png`. Visually read every box yourself. Do not use OCR or any text-recognition software. Read the complete central word, expression, revision, or note enclosed by the box; do not complete clipped neighboring fragments. Mention a scope ambiguity in the metadata comments. Do not use mathematical expectations to supply ink.

Write `second/<page>_second.tsv`, with exactly the columns `box` and `reading`, and one row per input box in its order. Use a TSV writer with proper quoting for readings containing tabs or newlines. This file must exist before any comparison is performed. Do not run a comparison yourself; the main reader will do that after your return. Once you have returned it, leave it unchanged.

Use the diplomatic conventions: `\deleted{…}`, `\inserted{…}`, `\margin{…}`, `\uline{…}`, `\uuline{…}`, `\unsure{reading}`, `\unsure{reading|alternative}`, and `\illegible{n}`. Preserve abbreviations, punctuation, quoted forms, source spelling, exact mathematical letters and alphabets, indices, accents, arrows, bars, hats, and tildes. Use standard LaTeX math commands. Double quotation ticks are two backticks and two apostrophes. A handwritten page number at the head may use `\mspage{n}`; a separate foliation in the margin may use `\margin{n}`. A source formula label uses `\tag{n}`. Diagrams use `\diagram{…}` with tikz-cd; genuine drawings use `\figure{…}` with TikZ. Do not draw words or formula letters instead of reading them.

Record unresolved ink locally, including doubtful letters under cancellation. Give both the extent and state of a revision. Read the full formula around an uncertain symbol, and both ends of each connector. Do not expand abbreviations in the TSV.

Also write `second/<page>_reader.json` with `page`, `reader`, `completed_utc` (the actual UTC time after the TSV has been written), and `boxes` (one object per box containing its id, original coordinates, crop path, rotation, and any comments on the ink or scope). Record that every listed crop was visually read and that only the permitted inputs were opened. The main reader checks independence, compares the written readings by script, and decides every resulting difference on a crop. Return file paths and the box count, then wait for your next single-page assignment.
