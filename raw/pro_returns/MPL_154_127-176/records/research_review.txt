VERDICT

Three concrete amendments are warranted. I05 and G01 have already been
reported to root and acknowledged for amendment. The additional substantive
source-bound issue is I01's unestablished dimensional interpretation of K_0.
No mathematical error was found in the conditional calculations of M05,
I02–I04, I06, or the G04 face-poset example. X01–X03 pass the mathematical
scope review.

1. I05 — State integrality of n as well as the multiplicities.

The reduction modulo two requires n to be an integer. Integer multiplicities
and a 0/1 epsilon make the left side an integer, but that only forces 2n to
be an integer; it need not be even. Algebraically, one epsilon=1, all nu=0,
and n=1/2 satisfy the displayed equality for a face but violate the claimed
parity conclusion.

Recommended change: begin, "If n and the multiplicities are integers..."
and keep exactly the incidence indexing intended in (h). The latter point
matters because the source still has an uncertain subset/member symbol and
an uncertain barred F; a parity consequence does not resolve those readings.
This is a small but real gap in the stated conditional hypothesis, not a
counterexample to the intended manuscript configuration.

2. G01 — Do not add a cardinality to the incident faces.

Frozen scan 151:77 says "les faces incidentes" and "leur circuit des arêtes";
it does not say "the two faces." The explicit two and inserted (?) qualify
the vertices later in that same sentence. Replace "the two faces incident
to a semistable edge" by "the incident faces and their edge circuits."

This is material to the surrounding discussion: two local sides of an edge
need not belong to two distinct global faces in a nonregular cell structure.
G04 correctly preserves precisely that distinction.

3. I01 — K_0 is not defined as a zero-dimensional locus in this selection.

The title "retains lower-dimensional data" and especially the body's
"zero-dimensional and attachment data" assign geometric content not
established by the cited passage. A search of all 50 frozen page files
finds K_0 only at scan 160:9 and 160:68: the proposed union with L, and a
point t on the boundary of F which lies on K_0. Neither occurrence defines
K_0 as the zero-skeleton, a zero-dimensional stratum, or a lower-dimensional
subcomplex.

Recommended title: "The cancelled exceptional-facet correction retains an
extra locus." In the body, use "the extra locus and its attachment data"
or simply "additional incidence data" instead of "zero-dimensional and
attachment data." If an independently located definition of K_0 is later
supplied, the stronger interpretation can be cited there; otherwise retain
the note's own acknowledgment that the notation is not fully defined.

MATHEMATICAL CHECKS THAT PASS

M05: The conditional identity is correct:
C(C(n,2),2) - n C(n-1,2) = 3 C(n,4).
For pairs of edges in a complete graph, the subtracted term counts exactly
the pairs sharing one vertex. Using n-1 rather than n-2 in the uncertain
factor changes lower-order terms but retains leading coefficient 1/8.
The note does not use this to decide the ink or assert the counted objects.

I02: At an embedded four-valent vertex, the cyclic order determines the
opposite pairing; without that order there are three pairings. The
construction is explicitly conditional and allows valence-one boundary
vertices as endpoints. No regularity or global surface reconstruction is
silently inferred.

I03: For the proposed side double cover over the normalized curve, a
section exists precisely when the componentwise monodromy is trivial.
A connected two-sided component has two continuous choices. The note
correctly treats the identification of this cover and the reading of
"section"/"rev." as uncertain, and allows (b) to be extra input rather
than a consequence of four-valence.

I04: For a finite graph with even degrees and no unmatched ends, pairing
half-edges gives closed traversal components, but degree data alone do not
determine a geometric branch pairing. The note makes this distinction
without asserting that the manuscript has forgotten an already fixed
immersion convention.

I06: Under its stated closed-surface and distinct-face-incidence assumptions,
summing (h) gives
  2nF = 4 sum_s(2 nu_s + epsilon_s) + 2 sum_a nu_a,
and therefore
  nF = 4 sum_s nu_s + 2 sum_s epsilon_s + sum_a nu_a.
For the further finite cellular four-valent assumption, 2E=4V and
V-E+F=chi(X), so F=V+chi(X). The final formula follows. The hypotheses
explicitly avoid silently counting a repeated corner as several distinct
face memberships, and the boundary mismatch is acknowledged.

G04: The regular-CW reconstruction comparison is correctly qualified.
The circle with one vertex and one open edge has the two-element face
poset v<e, whose order complex is an interval. The example therefore
really distinguishes the original nonregular circle from its bare
incidence poset, without refuting the separate minimum-vertex realization
claims in the supplied register.

EXTENSION-SPACE CANDIDATES X01–X03

Pass. I independently opened the author-hosted Sturmfels–Ziegler primary
preprint:
https://www.mi.fu-berlin.de/math/groups/discgeom/ziegler/Preprintfiles/020PREPRINT.pdf

The consulted text defines extensions excluding loops and coloops
(pp.2,7), states the strongly-euclidean sphere result and its application
to rank at most three (Theorem 1.2, pp.2–3), distinguishes extensions of
one fixed realization from all realizable extensions (Proposition 2.1,
p.3), and gives the rank-three failure of the sphere property for the
latter (Theorem 1.1/Proposition 2.2, pp.2,4).

X01 correctly restricts its conclusion to the order complex's homotopy
type. Its interval-attached-to-a-sphere example is valid. X02 preserves
the fixed-realization/all-realizable/all-combinatorial distinction.
X03 correctly leaves the orientation, projective, parallel-extension,
and contact-orbit conventions unproved. These are useful boundaries,
not identifications of the manuscript's surface with a known extension
space. No correction is needed to the three candidate bodies.

The supplementary sign-reversal/free-action observation is also valid:
a nonzero localization cannot be comparable with its negative, hence
no finite chain can be invariant under this involution. This check does
not give a manuscript orientation cover or a projective-plane conclusion.
