## Legibility and witness comparison

The covers and most short reference lists are comparatively clear, while the density and condition of the individual loci vary sharply. Scan59 is the least secure continuous passage: its lower edge is torn, several formula components are damaged or crossed, and different cursive labels can share the same reduced shape. The crossed opening references on83 and the cancelled cover line on84 remain substantially obscured. Other vulnerable places include overwritten digits on60 and80, the alphabet of the tensor expression on61, the final subscript of the cotangent expression on78, the bibliographic cluster and cancelled words on79, the short prefix before donne on82, and the compressed writing on100. The faint line on95 is short enough to inspect throughout, but the original detail remains limited.

Every retained doubt expresses a limitation of the visible ink, including alternatives in deletion state or alphabet where appropriate. Enlargement makes the supplied pixels easier to inspect; it does not recover letters hidden by cancellation or missing paper. These are visual readings of the supplied raster scans, without inspection of physical originals or an independent human review. The blind second readings are by different assigned model readers; their recorded disagreements, including purely typographical ones, remain available in `second/` and the changes log.

C is often more complete about letterhead, physical rules and doubtful local states. It nevertheless contains substantive readings requiring correction, including an agreed-looking word that the ink supports as conventions on62, abbreviated and punctuated forms, and uncertain or overinterpreted layers. H sometimes supports a useful alternative, such as ou non on95, and sometimes leaves readable words or labels unresolved. Its omission of letterhead and many upper rules accounts for a substantial part of the collation, so the number of H rows is not a lexical error rate and is not directly comparable with a word-accuracy score for C. H’s appended cover heading in the scan83 file is a boundary issue, documented separately from a failure to read its letters.

Agreement between C and H was never used as independent evidence of the ink. No cited exposition, theorem, bibliographic title or numbering sequence was consulted to fill a gap. Confidence attaches to each recorded locus, not uniformly to a whole page. Ordinary spacing, line wrapping and equivalent macro nesting are distinguished from changes to words, signs, state or placement during adjudication.
