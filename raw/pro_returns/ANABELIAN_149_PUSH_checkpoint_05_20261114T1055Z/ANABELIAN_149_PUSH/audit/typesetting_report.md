# Typesetting and visual verification

The checkpoint manuscript was compiled with LuaHBTeX/LuaLaTeX and BibTeX, with four passes in the latest build. The final stable log is `build/lualatex-final.log`; it contains no unresolved references, citations, rerun warnings or overfull boxes. The 203-page PDF has SHA-256 `a9c801f60ae2806857c30c653df4a5c255d3a58268e776c0546f30c8214b1272`.

Across the pre-repair builds, two distinct overflowing boxes were observed: a bibliography URL exceeded the margin by 51.91634 pt (then on physical page124), and a code filename exceeded it by 2.44035 pt (then on physical page46). Loading xurl and using a breakable path for the filename removed both. Before/after counts: 2 distinct boxes above2pt before correction; 0 overfull boxes of any size after correction; largest final overflow0pt. An equation tag inside a split environment and an undefined script-font command were also corrected before the successful build. The earlier 73-page baseline had no overfull box and two hyperref warnings; neither warning remains.

The automated audit rendered 73 pages distributed across all Parts, the new proofs, the statement register, and the bibliography. All 73 rendered pages were visually inspected in nine contact sheets. Physical pages 46 and 124 and the relocated problematic bibliography entry on page 198 were additionally inspected individually at full resolution. The sample includes A.29d–A.29g, B.11f/B.12, C.7a–C.7c, D.15–D.22, and F.32–F.41. No clipping, overlap, illegible equation, orphan heading, or unusable URL layout was observed. The complete reviewed-page list and PDF hash are in `audit/typesetting_review_203pages.json`; rendered pages are under `audit/pdf_pages/`. Rendering alone is not counted as visual review. Earlier review records are retained as historical evidence and do not certify a later modified PDF.

This verification applies to the stated PDF hash. Subsequent mathematical additions require a new compile, overflow audit, and visual review before final delivery.
