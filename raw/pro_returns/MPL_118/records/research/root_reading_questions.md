## Post-freeze reading questions and comparison with Hua

The comparison source throughout this section is **Hua, modernised reading of cote 118**, supplied as `context/hua_project/118.modern.tex`; the subsection title in each entry is its locator. Its README describes an AI-produced modern interpretation, not a diplomatic transcription. These differences were found after the freeze, and none has changed a page file. Hua's substantive contributions are credited in C04–C05, C09–C10 and M07, M10, M13, M16, M27–M28 as well as here.

### MPL118-R01 — The first subtitle

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 002, `\unsure{Fibre-by-fibre}`. **Hua locator:** “Le fil du dossier, et les conventions” and the initial title summary. **Context link:** `context/README.md`, the dossier title and dating.

The frozen cover reading is “Fibre-by-fibre properties”, with explicit doubt, while Hua renders a fibration-like subtitle. The competing interpretation is recorded as a lead for a future independent image reading, not a reason to replace the frozen letters. No substantive theorem is inferred from either subtitle alone.

### MPL118-R02 — The reused notice is from 1983, not 1985

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 018, “Montpellier, le 28 avril 1983” and “13 MAI 1983”. **Hua locator:** “Le fil du dossier, et les conventions”, which calls the notice one of 29 April 1985. **Context link:** `context/README.md`; [MPL-CATALOG].

The typed dates in the frozen transcription differ from Hua's metadata on both day and year. They date the reused notice and scheduled defence, not automatically the mathematical writing on neighboring sheets. The full notice is included in the diplomatic transcription, whereas Hua explicitly omits this nonmathematical page.

### MPL118-R03 — Normalized letters in the factorization diagrams

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scans 011–014, `\unsure{\eta_f|H_f}`, `f^\sharp`, and the doubtful flat/b exponents. **Hua locator:** “Les deux factorisations par les catégories comma” and “Cylindres et cônes”. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XIIc-a.tex`, §4, ms. 209–218; DOSSIER RESIDUE C.1.

Hua's normalized μ and natural-style notation make the constructions easier to compare but do not determine which handwritten letter or exponent is on these sheets. ### MPL118-R04 — Repeated class letters and the anomalous WCF/WSE forms

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scans 003, 005 and 009, the C/F alternatives, explicitly doubtful WCF and unmarked anomalous WSE. **Hua locator:** “Les treillis (pages 2 et 4)” and “Les caractérisations (page 8)”. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XI-XIIa.tex`, XII §III, ms. 26–52; DOSSIER BISITE A.15.

The modern reading systematically chooses labels according to the dual lattice, while the frozen version marks places where individual strokes do not decide them. Those normalizations are possible readings, not independent ink evidence. A diagrammatic identity must therefore not be treated as fully established simply because a normalized version makes its letters consistent.

### MPL118-R05 — “Strong equivalences” versus “cocartesian squares”

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 009, “base change by f preserves … équiv” with doubtful “strong”. **Hua locator:** “Les caractérisations (page 8)”, footnote after the proper/smooth discussion. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XI-XIIa.tex`, XII §III, ms. 31–36; DOSSIER BISITE B.1/F.2.

Hua quotes the question as preservation of cocartesian squares, whereas the frozen reading is preservation of strong equivalences. These are different mathematical assertions, so the discrepancy cannot be treated as harmless modernization. The two question-marked arrows and the uncertain adjective remain in the transcription; the later base-change theory does not decide the reading.

### MPL118-R06 — The opening of the localizer programme

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 015, `Après \unsure{l'axiome} d'asphéricité`. **Hua locator:** “Le programme (pages 14 et 15)”. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XVI.tex`, §1, ms. 1–3; RESULTS_STATE GALOIS B.1/B.3.

Hua's “Après l'histoire” differs from the doubtful frozen “l'axiome”. Hua expands the role of Loc 3 as relative Theorem A, while expressly observing that the axiom is not written out on this page. Both the verbal discrepancy and the added axiom content remain research annotations.

### MPL118-R07 — The programme's square says “cart”

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 015, the square labelled `\mathrm{cart}`. **Hua locator:** “Le programme (pages 14 et 15)”, footnote saying it is read cocartesian. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XIIb-b.tex`, ms. 147–149; RESULTS_STATE 118-pushout-along-cofibrations-in-cat.

The frozen label is cart, and the later theorem on 021 also explicitly says cartesian. Hua's pushout-oriented reinterpretation adds a different property rather than reading the written abbreviation. C02 shows that the comparison theorem itself needs no ordinary-pushout hypothesis, which removes a mathematical reason for overriding the literal label.

### MPL118-R08 — The inverse image in the gluing homotopy

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 017, `h^{-1}(Y)` in the lower display. **Hua locator:** “Le sorite des recollements (page 16)”, footnote reporting Y′ on the page. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XIIb-a.tex`, §V.1.1, ms. 59–61; DOSSIER RESIDUE I.5.

The frozen personal reading already has Y in this position, while Hua reports correcting Y′ to Y. That claimed slip is therefore not corroborated by this transcription. The interval symbol elsewhere in the same formulas remains explicitly uncertain and has not been regularized from his Δ¹ notation.

### MPL118-R09 — Coaspherical and aspherical are not interchangeable

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 020, “coasphérique”. **Hua locator:** “Le théorème”, where the target is called aspherical. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XI-XIIa.tex`, XII §III, ms. 31–36; DOSSIER BISITE B.1.

The frozen adjective has the co- prefix, which is significant for the comma variance. The opfibration discussion naturally invites this distinction, but that mathematical fit is not substituted for the already frozen reading. Any future comparison should state which under- or over-categories define its asphericity.

### MPL118-R10 — The allegedly missing cartesian hypothesis is present

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 021, “un diagramme cartésien, avec i′,f des cofibrations”. **Hua locator:** “Le théorème”, footnote asserting that cartesianness is not stated and must be added. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-pushout-along-cofibrations-in-cat; `context/companion/chunks/DER_XIIb-b.tex`, ms. 147–149.

The literal hypothesis is present in the frozen text, so Hua's footnote about adding it is not supported by this reading. Conversely, cocartesianness is not written in this theorem's hypothesis, although Hua's modern statement includes it. C02 checks the comparison under the actually stated cartesian and opfibration hypotheses.

### MPL118-R11 — Isomorphic to the point, equivalent to it, or W-aspherical?

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 021, “isom. à la catégorie ponctuelle” and the separate W-aspherical margin. **Hua locator:** “Le théorème”. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-pushout-along-cofibrations-in-cat; `context/companion/chunks/DER_XVI.tex`, §1, ms. 3–8.

Hua's theorem uses equivalence to the point, while the main manuscript line uses isomorphism and the margin proposes the broader W-aspherical condition. These are three distinct textual levels even though the stronger mathematical argument may cover them. The independent application check in C02 validates the marginal weakening under relative-localizer hypotheses and leaves all three written formulations separate.

### MPL118-R12 — The cancelled proof is still transcribed

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 021, deleted “Il suffit de voir que …” and the unfinished bottom “Lemme”. **Hua locator:** “Le théorème”, footnote describing the cancelled passage as too fragmentary. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XVI.tex`, §1, ms. 3–8; RESULTS_STATE 118-pushout-along-cofibrations-in-cat.

### MPL118-R13 — The infinite fence is a mathematical correction, not a silent reading

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 029, “copies de X_n” after X_n=Δ₀. **Hua locator:** “Les sommes dans Hot (pages 26 à 30)”, footnote replacing those copies by elementary intervals. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XIII-a.tex`, ms. 55–56; DOSSIER PATHS D.16.

The frozen text retains X_n, the unfinished parenthesis and the drawn zigzag, so a reader can distinguish the ink from the natural repair. The same paragraph's “Je soupçonne” differs from Hua's quoted “Je suppose”, without changing the underlying expectation.

### MPL118-R14 — The retraction identity on 031

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 031 margin, `r_ji_j=\mathrm{id}_{\overline{Y'_j}}`. **Hua locator:** coproduct section, footnote on the common-target factorization. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XVI.tex`, §2, ms. 9–10; DOSSIER PATHS D.17 and RESIDUE B.12.

Hua reverses the retraction identity when completing the proposed cylinder factorization. The frozen composition is type-correct for the drawn arrows; Hua’s alternative identity comes from his proposed cylinder interpretation, not from a typing necessity. That correction would also leave the asserted strict factorization and simultaneous weak amalgam to be justified, so it does not by itself close the common-target gap.

### MPL118-R15 — A product inserted into the Hom target

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 032, the target `\unsure{Y_j|Y}` in the left Hom of (*). **Hua locator:** “Les produits finis (pages 31 à 36)”. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-product-condition-iv.

This discrepancy is recorded as a proposed mathematical repair, not resolved by the surrounding corollary.

### MPL118-R16 — Hot_A and Hot(W) appear at different stages

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 033, condition (iv) has a doubtful subscript A; scan 035 explicitly has Hot(W). **Hua locator:** “Le lemme des pages 32 à 35”. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-product-condition-iv and 118-groupoid-products-not-totally-aspherical.

The modern reading distinguishes the two functors consistently, while the manuscript's early list still appears to repeat Hot_A. The later clarification is evidence about the argument's development, not permission to replace the first occurrence. This matters because product preservation in the localization of presheaves and in the global category of homotopy types are different conditions.

### MPL118-R17 — The sum paragraph concerns (ii), not (iv)

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 036, “La condition (ii) est visiblement stable par sommes”. **Hua locator:** product lemma, paragraph and Lean footnote on stability under sums. **Context link:** `context/companion/chunks/DER_XVI.tex`, §2, ms. 9–10; DOSSIER RESIDUE B.11; C11.

Hua attacks the assertion for (iv), but the frozen page repeatedly has (ii), the product stability of W_A. His cross-component representable-product argument therefore does not refute the literal W∞ assertion. C11 gives a separate qualification for arbitrary W, using an example already supplied in the context; it does not rehabilitate the incorrect (iv) quotation.

### MPL118-R18 — The answer “Non” is an interpretation of the groupoid example

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scan 036, the connectedness assertion followed by “NB Prenons p.ex. … un groupoïde”. **Hua locator:** product lemma, the inserted “Non” before the groupoid explanation. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-groupoid-products-not-totally-aspherical; [CIS-CORRECTION].

### MPL118-R20 — “Saturated” and the abandoned first proof

**Kind:** reading question. **118:** scans 016, 040 and 041, the added “forte”, the live saturation theorem, the deleted first proof and its telescope replacement. **Hua locator:** “Le programme (pages 14 et 15)” and “La saturation (pages 39 et 40)”. **Context link:** RESULTS_STATE 118-strong-saturation; [CIS-2006], Proposition 4.2.4.

The intended strong-saturation conclusion is supported by the criterion that an invertible image in Hot should lie in W, although 040's theorem itself simply says “saturé”. Hua's modern wording is therefore an interpretation of that criterion, not a missing adjective to be inserted into the line. The final proof still has the dependencies recorded in C09, and the cancelled 040 proof remains cancelled.
