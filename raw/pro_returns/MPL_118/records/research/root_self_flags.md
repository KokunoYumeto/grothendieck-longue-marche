## Standing question 1 — Steps the manuscript itself leaves open

This register includes questions later answered on the same sheets, cancelled attempts, explicit qualifications and postponed verifications. A question in a doubtful reading remains a reading question; it is not promoted to a confident conjecture. “Resolved here” refers to the research argument named, never to an alteration of the manuscript. The source locations in each entry identify the relevant contextual search result, including cases where the later material is only analogous.
