## Where this comes from

Grothendieck, *La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois* (1981), §51, "Exemple: la tour de Fermat", cote 140-4. In §51.15 (scans 156–170) he maps the arithmetic Galois group to an extension of `Ẑ*` by `Ẑ` and asks for the image of the Kummer maps. ## Setting and notation (used in every Part)

- `E = X₃⁺ : x³+y³+z³ = 0` over `Q`; `D` its nine coordinate cusps; `U = E ∖ D`. Rational cusps `O = [1:−1:0]`, `P = [0:−1:1]`, `Q_c = [−1:0:1]` (LM7 writes `Q`); the other six lie over `K = Q(ζ)`, `ζ²+ζ+1 = 0`. `E(Q) = {O, P, Q_c}` and `U(Q)` is empty (LM7 E.24).
- `N`: the maximal pro-3 quotient of the geometric fundamental group of `U`, free pro-3 of rank 10, with the tangential base point at `O` (parameter `s = z/x`, tangent one), as in LM7 E.36. `V = N^ab = T₃J_{E,D}` (rank 10), `T = T₃E`, `W = ker(V → T) = Z₃(1)[D]/Z₃(1)δ` (rank 8, `δ` the sum of the cusps), `e_d ∈ W` the inertia vector of the cusp `d`, `I = ker(N → T)`.
- `N^cc = N/[N,I]` (LM7 E.36); `𝒦 = ker(Λ²V → Λ²T) = [N,I]/γ₃N`, a free `Z₃`-module of rank 44, an extension of `Λ²W` (rank 28) by `T ⊗ W` (rank 16); `1 → 𝒦 → N/γ₃N → N^cc → 1` is central (E36.2).
- For a quotient `N̄` of `N` by a `G_Q`-stable closed normal subgroup, `Sec(N̄)` is the set of continuous sections of `N̄ ⋊ G_Q → G_Q` modulo conjugation by `N̄`; it is the continuous non-abelian `H¹(G_Q, N̄)`. `Sec_L(N̄)` is the same over `G_L` for a number field `L`.
- `ℛ_I(Z₃)`: families `(z_i)_{i∈I}` of 3-adic integers with `z_i ∈ 3ⁿZ₃` outside a finite set, for every `n`. `ℛ = ℛ_{P*}(Z₃)`, `P*` the primes `q ≠ 3`; `d : ℛ → ℛ`, `d(z)_q = (q−1)z_q`.
- `F̂₂` the free profinite group on `x, y`, with `z = (xy)⁻¹`; `Out^♯(F̂₂)` the outer automorphisms sending each of `x, y, z` to a conjugate of a power of one of them with a common exponent; `GT̂₀` the centralizer of the geometric `𝔖₃` in it, that is the pairs `(λ, f)` with the 2-cycle and 3-cycle relations and no pentagon (Harbater–Schneps, Main Theorem; LM7 A.17); `GT̂ ⊂ GT̂₀` with the pentagon.
