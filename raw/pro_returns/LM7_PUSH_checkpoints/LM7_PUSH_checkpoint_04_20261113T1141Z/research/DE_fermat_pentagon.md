# Exact group pentagon for the intrinsic Fermat calculation

## Actual braid convention and comparison map

Let the free group of the fourth-strand fibre be generated by X,Y,Z.
The two Artin automorphisms used in the computation are

    r1: X↦Y, Y↦Y^-1 X Y, Z↦Z,
    r2: X↦X, Y↦Z, Z↦Z^-1 Y Z.

Composition is left action: (ab)(w)=a(b(w)). Set A12=r1²,
A23=r2², A13=r2 r1² r2^-1, and A14=Inn(X), A24=Inn(Y),
A34=Inn(Z), with Inn(h)(w)=hwh^-1. The exact Fadell--Neuwirth
comparison is PB4=F3⋊PB3, using its fourth-strand fibre and the
standard inclusion of PB3. The action of its first two pure
generators has inverses

    A12^-1: (X,Y,Z)↦(X Y X Y^-1 X^-1, X Y X^-1, Z),
    A23^-1: (X,Y,Z)↦(X, Y Z Y Z^-1 Y^-1, Y Z Y^-1).

These are precisely the literal inverse Artin words used and
audited in D.7, not their infinitesimal replacements. The primary
pure-Artin presentation used there is Cohen--Falk--Randell,
equation (2.1). The free-word audit in
`code/de_fermat_pentagon_magnus.py` verifies the Artin braid
relation, every inverse, the four pure triple relations, both
noncrossing disjoint relations, and

    A12 A13 A23 = Inn((XYZ)^-1),
    (A12 A13 A23) A14 A24 A34 = Id.

Thus the actual center of PB4 is killed by this action. The five
ordered pairs used by the program are exactly

    (A12, A23 A24), (A13 A23, A34), (A23, A34),
    (A12 A13, A24 A34), (A12, A23).

Their product has signs +,+,-,-,- in the pentagon equation;
the actual residual is F0 F1 F4^-1 F3^-1 F2^-1.

For every input word in F2', forgetting the fourth strand sends
this residual to the identity in PB3: A14,A24,A34 become 1,
every f(u,1) is 1, and the two f(A12,A23) factors cancel.
Consequently the actual group residual belongs to the free
fourth-strand fibre F3. Its displayed action is its inner action.
No general equality of the Andreadakis and lower central
filtrations is required for this particular residual.

## Denominator-free Magnus evaluation

Work over F3 in the associative tensor algebra on three variables,
truncated after degree 8. A free-group word is evaluated by
X_i↦1+X_i and X_i^-1↦1-X_i+X_i²-⋯, with all products truncated.
The actual pure Artin maps therefore give explicit algebra
automorphisms ΦA,ΦB. Their differences ΔA=ΦA-Id and ΔB=ΦB-Id
raise augmentation degree by at least 1. For the degree≤7 source
Magnus series f=1+Σ_w c_w w(x-1,y-1), its action on a target
generator is exactly

    f(A,B)(X_i)=X_i+Σ_w c_w Δ_{w1}⋯Δ_{wk}(X_i).

Source terms of degree 8 or higher produce target degree 9 or higher
and vanish in this algebra. This is a homomorphism from the
completed group algebra into its continuous linear endomorphisms;
the generator images prove the equality. There are no logarithms,
factorials, or division by 3 in this evaluation.

The program evaluates the five factors and their genuine source
inverses, then composes their algebra automorphisms. Fifteen
nontrivial factor evaluations were independently checked by
direct composition of the literal free-word automorphisms.

The degree shift is necessary. For a fibre word with first
nonzero Magnus term h_d, its inner action on X_i has first term
[h_d,X_i] in degree d+1. A positive-degree homogeneous tensor
that commutes with all three generators is zero: commuting with
X forces it to be a polynomial in X, and then commuting with Y
forces it to be scalar. Hence the three generator images through
degree 8 detect this actual fibre word through group Magnus
degree 7. In characteristic3 these are the augmentation, or
Zassenhaus, degrees.
