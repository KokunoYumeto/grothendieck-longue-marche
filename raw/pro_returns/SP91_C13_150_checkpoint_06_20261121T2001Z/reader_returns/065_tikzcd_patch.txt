------------------------------------------

   positions and 2 detached-point centres is an accepted coordinate offset
   from scan065I. Its physical position therefore adds (0,-1.84 cm).
   Together with the 7 node centres, all 49 point correspondences passed.

   = (1-t)P0+tP1+v, with v=(0,-1.84 cm).
   For each of the five cubic components, substitution of its four translated

   Expansion was checked as the coefficient vector (1,0,0,0), so each of

   The connecting straight components share their corresponding translated
