## Scope

The packet is dominated by typescripts. Formula blanks, overprints, pale pencil, source typing errors, and editorially supplied symbols require separate attention. Further recurring observations are in the hand log.

## Recurring observations for another run

- **Blank formula spaces and independently added pen signs need separate treatment.** Scans 94, 97 and 98 leave relations unfilled; scan 96 has visible blue arrows and a blue delta, and scan 93 has handwritten relation signs. The same expected expression cannot determine which state appears on a particular leaf.
- **Same-cell overtyping can conceal two different questions.** A glyph may be identifiable while the order of the two impressions remains unsettled (87, 89, 92, 96, 97, 98). Record both layers; retain order uncertainty where no stroke establishes it. Doubling alone does not establish a mathematical alphabet.
- **A cancellation's alternative may be a different state, not just a different letter.** The mark after valables on scan 98 can represent a cancelled comma or a live x. Putting all candidates inside a deletion macro would prejudge the state.
- **Underlining needs an extent and a stroke count.** Scan 96 distinguishes underlines under O and X; scan 98 underlines only the first i of utilisant. The close impressions below O on scan 98 require a stated uncertainty about double versus repeated single underlining.
- **A marginal leader's end is evidence separate from its words.** On scan 91 the insertion belongs below 10.15. Its irregular enclosure has an actual gap and a return stroke, and its words are transcribed as text outside the drawing.

These are refinements of the supplied error-sheet categories rather than claims of a new general handwriting rule. Their scopes and exceptions are in the hand log.
