## Consequential corrections and preserved source problems

- Scan 91: the handwritten addition reads **Morphismes** followed by **affines|finis**; the second word remains unresolved, with **affines** preferred. Its leader securely attaches after 10.15, before APPENDICE, without a supplied item number.
- Scan 93: the first coefficient-index condition is **0 ≤ i ≤ n**, correcting C's strict upper inequality. The handwritten relation strokes are present.
- Scan 93: several source expressions remain anomalous, including `(X_i)_{1≥0}`, `B[T]=A[T,(X_i)]`, and the plain `T` in the expression involving `F_o` and `X_o`. Their mathematical appearance does not authorize replacement letters or alphabets.
- Scans 94, 97 and 98: unfilled mathematical symbols stay blank. In particular, scan 94 has no visible base symbol before the superscript 1 and subscript X/Y; scan 97 leaves several relations and sequence arrows uninked; scan 98 leaves the homomorphism and implication arrows blank. H supplies some of these symbols.
- Scans 97 and 98: a plain `M_i` or `M` is retained where H supplies a tilde; handwritten tildes on other occurrences are recorded individually. The two letter states are not made uniform by mathematical expectation.
- Scan 98: the middle cell contains superposed **s/i** between two clear **s** cells. **sis** is the preferred surface reading, with reverse impression order retained as uncertain; H reads doubtful **ssi**. The transcription does not turn the source into an equivalence abbreviation.
- The check of scan 93 refutes the stated B-linear independence but separately verifies the needed minimal-generator conclusion in the polynomial example over a field. The check of scan 97 verifies the local-generation lemma. These are research notes with check events, not alterations of the source.
