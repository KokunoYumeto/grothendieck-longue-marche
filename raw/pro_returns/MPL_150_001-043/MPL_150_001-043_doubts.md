# Legibility and limits

All 41 supplied pages were read manually in sequence from the whole image and both overlapping halves, then compared with the transcription in a separately logged second reading. No OCR was used, and no page was delegated. Enlarged and rotated crops were used for dense or oblique writing; the supplied blank omissions, scans 006 and 011, have no page files.

The ordinary large handwriting is generally readable, but this is not a certified error-free edition. The least reliable readings are the heavily cancelled pink sheets (003–005), the preliminary tube notation on 007, the cancelled draft 021, the diagonal notes on 023 and 027–028, the overlapping addition on 031, and the compressed marginal mathematics on 039–042. Scan 031 has an especially uncertain annotation crossing the main text: the proposed words and formula are marked, and approximately three intervening words remain physically unreadable. Five illegibility markers occur in total; their word counts are estimates, not measured omissions.

Margin notes have been placed near the text they concern; their physical location is recorded in the inventory, whose detailed deletion/insertion rows also give LaTeX line locators. Nesting records a cancelled passage containing its own revisions. Typographic normalization is limited to LaTeX encoding and drawing coordinates: apparent mathematical mistakes, unfinished phrases, author spellings, unheaded strokes, and uncertain letters are retained. The cancelled accounting sheet (009), the cover (001), and the tube drawing sheet (010) are included; faint reverse-side show-through has not been promoted to foreground text.

The package-wide audit compared quotation marks, primes versus possible exclamation marks, stars and raised circles, the bar/dot/interior marks on T, calligraphic versus ordinary E and T, arrowheads and hooks, and equals-sign shorthand in prose. No secure occurrence of an exclamation-mark exponent was identified. The end of scan 043 is genuinely incomplete at “— plus”; the continuation is outside the supplied images.

The delivered transcription is a fragment and intentionally has no preamble or macro definitions. Structural checks cover balanced braces, matching environments, page coverage, and inventory/doubt-marker coverage; an attempted local typesetting check could not run because the available TeX executable lacks its LaTeX format and package tree. No compiled output is claimed. The research-context ZIP remained unopened until the transcription files were frozen and hashed.
