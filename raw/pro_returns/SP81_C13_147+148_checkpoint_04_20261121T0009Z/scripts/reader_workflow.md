# Single-page reader assignment
Read ONLY the assigned page's packet, images, rules, and supplied current notation. Do not open other pages or the root's finished pages. No OCR or text-recognition software, external research, TeX compilation, or assumptions from expected mathematics. Look at all images with view_image. You are the first reader; root personally adjudicates your return.

1. Read reading_rules.md in this folder in full and your page packet. The full source image and the two full-resolution overlapping halves have already been prepared; see the three once each. Read the halves word by word, line by line, against C and H, including every mathematical sign and agreed word. Inspect every deleted/inserted span separately for exact extent and attachment.
2. Create one tight crop per unresolved locus, every C/H disagreement in words, and every C mark you resolve. One crop can contain a coherent phrase/formula needed to decide its details. Never use another crop for a locus already decided during this reading. Use ../scripts/crop.py PAGE NAME X0 Y0 X1 Y1 [ANGLE], then view the saved PNG. Full formula for a doubtful sign. Use original pixel coordinates, not half coordinates. Maintain every actual doubt; nothing inferred gets written as observed.
3. Produce the complete provisional page at work/PAGE.tex. Use the provided preamble's macros only plus standard LaTeX, TikZ and tikz-cd. Header: \\cotefile{COTE} and \\scanpage{N}; preserve manuscript page numbers. All handwriting, diagrams and drawings included; no merely described diagrams. Preserve every abbreviation and unusual spelling.
4. Produce work/PAGE_report.json with the schema below. JSON is intermediate evidence; root produces all required Markdown/TSV/CSV deliverables. Every substantive difference against either witness needs a change; macro syntax and editorial markers alone do not. One change per physical locus; adjacent diff fragments may be one locus with complete respective readings. Specify differing_from_C and differing_from_H truthfully; H absent means false, never an omission row. Added doubts with no reading change go in doubts. C resolved marks also have a decisions entry and crop, even when same word reading.
5. Prepare complete second-reading boxes in the report. Cover EVERY insertion, margin note, struck passage, whole formula with a source/current doubt, handwritten number (including page label and formula numbers/coefficients/indices), every C/H word disagreement, and departures from C at < high confidence. A box may encompass one coherent passage that contains several triggers. Each box must have a full reading of all substantive ink in it, using source conventions, not a summary. Root will use it for blind re-reading after every first read finishes. Boxes must be tight and avoid cutting glyphs. If the box includes context, record the context in reading. Do not create second-reading crops yet.
6. Tell root you have finished, where files are, major uncertainties, counts. Do not start another page until assigned one. Keep evidence concise but precise.
