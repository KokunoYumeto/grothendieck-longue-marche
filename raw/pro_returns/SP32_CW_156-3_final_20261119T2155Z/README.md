# SP32_CW_156-3

Second-pass diplomatic transcription and research notes for Alexander Grothendieck's Montpellier cote 156-3, scans **026–041**, manuscript folios **25–40**.

## Reading the deliverable

- `SP32_CW_156-3_transcription.tex` is the consolidated LaTeX fragment, with the sixteen individual fragments in `pages/`. `PREAMBLE.tex` supplies the conventions requested in the task.
- `SP32_CW_156-3_changes.md`, `SP32_CW_156-3_collation.tsv`, `SP32_CW_156-3_inventory.csv`, and `SP32_CW_156-3_doubts.md` are the diplomatic apparatus. The changes count localized editorial places, not word-error rates.
- `SP32_CW_156-3_notes.md` is the complete research catalogue, with page-specific notes, contextual comparisons, both standing questions and the exhaustive check of witness C's notes. Detailed proofs and finite certificates are in `research/`.
- `CHECK_EVENTS.tsv` distinguishes mathematical checking from background use. It does not count transcription reading as proof.
- `progress.log`, `records/`, `evidence/`, and `scripts/` preserve the audit trail, targeted image enlargements, frozen hashes, and reproducible processing/checking code.

The primary reader personally performed both image readings of every page. Research assistance began only after the transcription freeze; no research worker inspected the manuscript images. The sixteen page files and combined fragment were frozen before context was opened and remain unchanged. Mathematical objections and suggested repairs are recorded only in the research notes.

No OCR or external literature retrieval was used. LaTeX was checked lexically and structurally, without compilation. Original attached scans, witnesses and contextual packets are deliberately excluded; they are needed separately if replaying a script that reads the source directory.

Scheduled checkpoint archives have distinct names and are never nested in this archive. Their identity records, where available before packaging, are retained under `records/`. The current files supersede their earlier versions without discarding the documentation of the work.
