## Protocol and scope

Latest attached instructions: PROMPT(20261124-182809).md. Thirty selected scans, not the complete cote. Source archive unpacked once. No OCR and no TeX compilation. Blind readers receive only one scan image, its coordinate boxes, and general transcription conventions; no witness text or first readings. This run distinguishes textual checking from mathematical proof checking. Source files are excluded from returned archives; completed edited transcriptions are deliverables even when a page needs no textual change.

Earlier SP59 and SP60 notation and hand records were consulted as hypotheses. Their confidence and mathematical explanations do not replace inspection of the ink. Roman foliation, archive numerals, layers, and page joins are recorded during the audit and reconciled before its last page is finished.
