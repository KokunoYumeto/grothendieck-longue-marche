**Place/kind:** 138, scan 103, lines 3–6; unproved claim.

### BC03 — The Galois action itself is a stated conclusion

### BC04 — Relabelling and arithmetic are commuting actions

**Place/kind:** 138, scan 103, line 6; unproved claim.
**Source:** “compatible (i.e. commutant) avec les opérations de γ”.

### BC09 — Each fixed finite type sees only a finite quotient

**Place/kind:** 138, scans 99 and103; own idea.
**Source:** “C₀ fini”; “Γ … opère”.
**Note:** A fixed finite passport gives finitely many permutation representations, so its Galois action factors through a finite symmetric group. Since Γ_Q has cyclotomic quotients of arbitrarily large order, no one such action is faithful. The faithful-action question concerns the intersection of kernels over an entire family, which is not computed on these pages.
**Confidence/reason:** high; finiteness follows by enumerating tuples in a finite symmetric group, and the kernel observation is elementary.
**Context/status:** Esquisse scans 119/15–18 contrasts finite orbits with the global outer action; bears on EXIT_TOWER E.24 and LM1 D.7, both open.

### BC12 — Naturality on maps is not full tower compatibility

**Place/kind:** 138, scans 122–124; own idea.
**Source:** “préfaisceau”; “sections”.
**Note:** A natural operation on maps gives compatibility with the covering morphisms in its stated category. **Confidence/reason:** high for the distinction; the two categories have different objects and morphisms.

### OP12 — A finite face-wise version can be proved

**Place/kind:** 138, scans 123–125; own idea.
**Source:** “comp. conn. ≃ disques”.
**Note:** A finite connected graph on the sphere whose complementary discs each contain at most one branch value pulls back to a cellular graph under every finite cover branched in {0,1,∞}. Each lifted face is a cover of a disc with at most one branch point and is itself a disc; in the folded-edge category, a face containing two branch values is excluded by a double-cover annulus counterexample. This is a bounded theorem supporting a possible intended criterion, not a claim to have settled every reading or every infinite case.
**Confidence/reason:** high under the explicitly stated finite-cover hypotheses; proofP1 gives both directions and the category restriction.
**Context/status:** Refines 138-operations-subset-criterion and Hua §15; no supplied entry already proves this corrected version.
