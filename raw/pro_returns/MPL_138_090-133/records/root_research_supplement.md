### SR06 — Passing to inertia orbits forgets ramification multiplicities

**Place/kind:** 138, scan 94, lines 30–39; unpursued implication.
**Source:** “oubli de π”; “E_i/I_i = … E_i”.
**Note:** The diagram distinguishes transport to the local fibre functor from forgetting the group action and taking inertia orbits. An orbit set alone does not retain ramification lengths: on four letters, cycle types(2,2) and(3,1) both have two orbits. Thus a reconstruction using this quotient must retain the preceding action or additional multiplicity data, rather than treating the orbit set as an equivalent invariant.
**Context/status:** LM_140-2_text.tex, scans 71–72, describes peripheral outer maps and the punctured-disc-to-disc quotient; ER09–ER13 give the torsor comparison; MPL_147_003-047 N075 is a related first-pass lead about retaining more than isolated group data.

### SR07 — The catalogue uses its own p/q notation for the degree sum

**Place/kind:** 138, scan 114, lines 47–49; unproved claim checked by double counting.
**Source:** “n=Σ_s q_s=Σ_f p_f”.
**Note:** Each dart belongs to one vertex rotation and one face rotation, so either sum equals the dart degree. This is compatible with the common-degree condition of scan 99, but the letter roles differ from the precarte notation on scan 98 and have not been normalized in the source. It is a degree identity, not a proof that the surrounding drawings exhaust all maps of that degree.
**Context/status:** Hua §13.I, common degreeδ, and §14, low-degree catalogue; no directly settling RESULTS_STATE entry.

### SR09 — The theta-graph notation change need not change its abstract group

**Place/kind:** 138, scan 119, line 15, first drawing; withdrawn in the same text / reading question.
**Source:** “Z₂.S₃” beside deleted “D₃×Z₂”.
**Note:** The dot does not specify a direct product, semidirect product or nonsplit extension, and the deletion alone does not prove the earlier abstract group was false. Under the convention D₃≅S₃, the direct product is itself one extension described by the dotted notation. The exact intended extension and the geometrically defined symmetry action should be checked separately before assigning a group structure from the label.
**Context/status:** Hua §14's general automorphism-group interpretation supplies the comparison but does not settle this notation; MM13 distinguishes the relevant symmetry notion; no direct RESULTS_STATE entry.

### SR11 — A complete comparison of the low-degree catalogue remains a separate project

**Place/kind:** 138, scans 114–120; own idea.
**Source:** “Cartes sphériques en bas degrés” on scan 113.
**Note:** A full enumeration should compare permutation classes, orientation reversal, graph duality, and the live versus cancelled drawings separately. The current checks settle two specified passports and several diagnostic groups, not a claimed bijection with every drawn case. Recording that boundary avoids treating a systematic-looking working catalogue as a proved exhaustive classification.
**Confidence/reason:** high for the need to separate these equivalences; the repeated passport and chiral examples already show why a single count is insufficient.
**Context/status:** Hua §14 explicitly treats the catalogue as a working list; MM13–MM17 provide the checked subset; no supplied RESULTS_STATE result settles completeness.

### SR12 — The classification theorem is an invoked input, with a finite-case meaning

**Place/kind:** 138, scan 129, lines 17–23; unproved claim using background classification.
**Source:** “Par le th. de classification”; “un unique isomorphisme de cartes, isotope à l'identité”.
**Note:** In the finite setting the relevant input is the classification of covers of the punctured sphere by monodromy, with the local fillings and cartographic incidence data retained. An isomorphism over the fixed complex sphere is holomorphic off the branch fibres and extends across them by the local power-map model; uniqueness in the identity component also uses the absence of a finite-order kernel, as in OP22. The source does not identify the theorem's full hypotheses here, and this explanation supplies no infinite-cover classification without extra assumptions.
**Context/status:** Esquisse §3, scans 119/13–14, explicitly states the finite cartographic/cover dictionary; Hua §15 invokes the same reduction; LM4 C.12 remains a different open comparison. The general classification is carried as background, not re-proved in this package.
