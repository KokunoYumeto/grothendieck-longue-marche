## Candidate observations for integration

### MS01 — The new pages assume the category rather than constructing it

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **New evidence:** cote 15, scan 219, lines 8–10, “sous-catégorie … de t.f. de … M(X)”; cote 18, scan 011, lines 5–8, the three Isom functors. **Context search:** `anneau.*Lefschetzien|category of motives|motif`; C 16/018, combined lines 694–724; C 16/049, line 1829. **Relation:** adds realization and fibre-functor structure, without adding a construction from the rings. **Ledger:** WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan]; MPL_14_087-132 M01 and MPL_14_002-044 M01 [first-pass leads].

The expressions “sous-catégorie … de t.f.” and the three comparison torsors use an already-defined category, so this excerpt does not identify its morphisms with cote 16's lefschetzien rings. The missing construction would have to specify cross-variety correspondences, their equivalence relation, tensor composition, idempotent completion and Tate twists; the standard pure-motive definition illustrates precisely these additional data (Jannsen, 1992, pp. 447–448). This adds the concrete realization-side evidence to the existing ledger leads without promoting their reconstruction to an established manuscript result.

### MS02 — The actual elliptic SL₂ is not the Lefschetz SL₂

**Kind:** own idea. **New evidence:** cote 18, scan 012, lines 13–22, within the deleted draft; quote “courbe elliptique sans mult. complexe”; the formula at line 22 is G^{u|n}=Sl(2)⊂Gl(2|7). **Context search:** `operator|opérateur|graduation|semi-simple`; C 16/007–018, especially 16/014–018; C 16/048–049. **Relation:** differs from the Lefschetz-operator setting. **Ledger:** WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan]; MPL_14_087-132 M04 [first-pass own-idea lead, not reviewed]. **Confidence:** high for the mathematical distinction, not for unresolved superscripts in the manuscript.

The displayed SL₂ in the cancelled elliptic-period draft must be distinguished from the Lefschetz action: on H¹ of a smooth elliptic curve, L:H¹→H³ and its lowering operator both vanish, while the written period group acts on a two-dimensional realization. Thus the page does not rescue the proposed general subgroup identification already questioned by ledger M04; it adds a different concrete test from within this package. The reason for high confidence is the degree range of curve cohomology, which is independent of the uncertain name of G's superscript and of whether the cancelled conjecture is retained.

### MS03 — A grading, a Hodge cocharacter and a Lefschetz grading cannot be interchanged

**Kind:** reading question / unpursued implication. **New evidence:** cote 18, scan 011, lines 25–29 and 38; quotes “hom central (Tate)” and “associé à … filtration”. **Context search:** `graduation|pi_i|Lefschetzien`; C 16/003–004, 16/015–018, especially 16/017's rejected uniqueness from L alone. **Relation:** adds a parabolic-and-Levi realization of filtration data, differing from a single operator-ring grading. **Ledger:** WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan]; MPL_14_002-044 B04 and MPL_14_087-132 M04 [first-pass leads].

The page's j belongs to B′/U′ and its lift has Levi centralizer, whereas a grading of the entire pure Tannakian category gives a central cocharacter of its tensor-automorphism group (Deligne & Milne, 1982/2012, Definition 5.1). The equation ε∘j=id is therefore not enough to identify j with the usual weight map, whose composite with the character of a Tate object has weight ±2 in cohomological conventions. Cote 16's ring grading, a Lefschetz Cartan operator and this Hodge-filtration splitting should remain separately named unless a further manuscript comparison supplies the normalization.

### MS06 — The displayed p-adic extension is not the missing arithmetic motivic sequence

**Kind:** reading question / unpursued implication. **New evidence:** cote 18, scan 006, lines 2–14 and 41–45; quote “une … ext. de E de π par G′(C)”; cote 15, scan 215, lines 18–21, “avec la comp. conn.” **Context search:** `Galois|extension|torseur|fondamental`; C's extension matches concern Picard groups, universal vector extensions or reused typed material; no arithmetic G_mot/Q sequence; README's planning paragraph explicitly asks for the extension by Γ_Q. **Relation:** adds a different local extension calculation, not the planned global exact-sequence witness. **Ledger:** WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan]; MPL_7_061-072+8_001-078 Scan 061 (§66) [first-pass neutralization lead].

The extension on scan 006 has kernel G′(C) and quotient π in a p-adic comparison calculation, so it cannot be relabelled as 1→G_mot(Qbar)→G_mot(Q)→underline Gal(Qbar/Q)→1. The latter is the modern arithmetic sequence for compatible rational Nori fibre functors, with exactness as affine group schemes, established by Galois descent (Kahn, 2025, Theorem 5.10 and §10). The fragment over R on cote 15/215 is evidence of attention to components and real conjugation, but supplies no proof or written statement of the Q-sequence.

### MS07 — Modern reconstruction supplies groups without supplying all algebraic correspondences

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **New evidence:** cote 15, scan 213, lines 18–27, “G(X,ξ)=underline Aut(ξ)”; cote 18, scan 011, lines 5–8, comparison Isom functors. **Context search:** `semi-simple|algebraic isomorph|catégor`; C 16/048–049; CN S15 (numerical motives and the semisimplicity programme). **Relation:** adds explicit fibre-functor groups and torsors to the contextual operator programme. **Ledger:** MPL_14_087-132 M23 and MPL_14_002-044 M28a [first-pass leads]; WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan]; S15 is named in README, not supplied as a separate register row.

For k⊂C, rational Nori motives provide the neutral Tannakian category and its fibre-functor group, and Choudhury and Gallauer Alves de Souza (2017, Theorem 9.1) identify that group with Ayoub's classical motivic Galois group. The pure subcategory is semisimple and agrees with André's motivated category, as in the existing ledger leads (Arapura, 2013, Theorems 6.3.3 and 6.4.1). The new formulas can be compared with these theorems, but identifying every inverse Lefschetz morphism or projector thereby obtained with an algebraic cycle is an additional standard-conjecture assertion, not a consequence of having constructed the group.

The manuscript expressly assigns conjectural input to the Hodge realization's full faithfulness, and neither the Nori construction nor pure semisimplicity removes that input. Arapura (2013, §7.1) separately formulates Nori's Hodge full-faithfulness conjecture and proves the stated abelian-type case, making the distinction between construction and fullness precise. ### MS09 — Rational modern theorems do not justify the integral or crystalline range automatically

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **New evidence:** cote 15, scan 213, lines 5–14, “nb fini”; scan 223, lines 6–17, “défini sur Z”; scan 211, lines 8–10 and 33–36, “W(h)” and “(F,V)”. **Context search:** `sur.*Z|anneau|p-alg|Cartier`; C 16/014–018 and C 54/005–007. **Relation:** adds an integral and crystalline programme distinct from the rational characteristic-zero construction. **Ledger:** WG2 §2.3 #3 and WG2 S28 (§1.7) [unchecked plans about integral failures]; MPL_7_061-072+8_001-078 Scan 061 (§66) [first-pass lead].

The Nori/Ayoub comparison itself permits principal-ideal-domain coefficients, including Z, but it does not prove finiteness of the manuscript's integral fibre-functor forms or classify its crystalline points with F,V. Cote 16's integral Lefschetz warnings and cote 54's p-primary extensions are relevant checks on the additional claims, not proofs of them. A completion would need the manuscript's integral fibre-functor category to be specified, appropriate representability and arithmetic finiteness arguments, and a separately specified positive-characteristic realization theory.
