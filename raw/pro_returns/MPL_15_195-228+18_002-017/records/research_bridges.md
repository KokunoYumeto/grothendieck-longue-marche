## 5. Limits and integration cautions

The supplied context's own README, lines 34–37, labels its transcription and research notes as unreviewed claims. Exact context cote/scan references above therefore identify comparison witnesses, not independently re-read manuscript evidence; only their text was searched. Searches included the roots `anabél`, `anabel`, `cyclotom`, `normalis`, `normaliz`, `semi-simple`, `semisimp`, `Tannak`, `foncteur fibre`, and the relevant polarization/Hodge terms; no direct anabelian Hom or cyclotomic-normalizer theorem was located in the supplied earlier manuscript transcription. Absence here is not a claim about unsupplied cotes or the full archive.

The current package includes reused typed Bourbaki Lie-theory material. Occurrences of centres, semisimplicity, weights, or normalizers in that material must not be silently reassigned to the handwritten motivic programme. The strongest direct bridge evidence in the handwritten pages is the conditional realization discussion (15/213–215), the polarized-moduli proposal (15/217), and the reconstruction/image questions (15/225–227).

No catalogue-wide priority claim, six-operations construction, or general theorem about a motivic fundamental group is established here. The marginal “à vérifier” annotations, uncertain `anabélien` reading, incomplete questions, and withdrawn separation claim remain part of the evidence. The distinct-place cohomology-ring question at 15/223 and the Borel–Harish-Chandra finiteness passage at 15/213 are left to the parent's separate research notes; neither is used to prove the four comparisons above.
