## Primary bibliography and checked versions

This bibliography covers the works cited in the observation register and its introductory comparison tables. Published bibliographic details are distinguished from the particular author or preprint version used to check a numbered statement. The project’s citation keys **Grothendieck (1969a)** and **Grothendieck (1969b)** denote the standard-conjectures paper and the Hodge-conjecture paper, respectively. References to **Deligne and Milne (1982)** or **(1982/2012)** use the corrected 2012 version identified below.

**Arapura, D. (2013).** An abelian category of motivic sheaves. *Advances in Mathematics, 233*, 135–195. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2012.10.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2012.10.004).  
Checked version: [arXiv:0801.0261v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.0261v4), revised October 9, 2012; Theorems 6.3.3 and 6.4.1, and §7.1. The cited theorem numbering refers to that author version.

**Bakker, B., & Tsimerman, J. (2025).** Functional transcendence of periods and the geometric André–Grothendieck period conjecture. *Forum of Mathematics, Sigma, 13*, Article e97, 1–24. [https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2025.10036](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2025.10036).  
Checked version: [publisher’s article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-sigma/article/functional-transcendence-of-periods-and-the-geometric-andregrothendieck-period-conjecture/6B3E39BB9BC68DC2BC6E793D15B0735B), especially Conjecture 1.2 and the distinction between arithmetic and geometric period formulations.

**Borel, A., & Harish-Chandra. (1962).** Arithmetic subgroups of algebraic groups. *Annals of Mathematics, Second Series, 75*(3), 485–535. [https://doi.org/10.2307/1970210](https://doi.org/10.2307/1970210).  
Checked version: [university-hosted copy of the published article](https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~wienhard/retreat10/references/borel_harishchandra_2.pdf); the arithmetic-orbit finiteness results in §6 are distinguished from the finite-volume results in §§7–9.

**Bost, J.-B., & Charles, F. (2016).** Some remarks concerning the Grothendieck period conjecture. *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 714*, 175–208. [https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2014-0025](https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2014-0025).  
Checked version: [arXiv:1307.1045v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.1045v2), revised April 9, 2014; §§2.1–2.2, including the period-locus and period-torsor distinctions. This is the revision that corrects and substantially revises §2 of the first preprint.

**Charles, F. (2009).** Conjugate varieties with distinct real cohomology algebras. *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 630*, 125–139. [https://doi.org/10.1515/CRELLE.2009.036](https://doi.org/10.1515/CRELLE.2009.036).  
Checked version: [author-hosted proof PDF](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~charles/crll5855.pdf), introduction and Theorems 1–2. The proof file carries a 2008 production header and placeholder journal metadata; the published reference is the 2009 article specified above.

**Choudhury, U., & Gallauer Alves de Souza, M. (2017).** An isomorphism of motivic Galois groups. *Advances in Mathematics, 313*, 470–536. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2017.04.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2017.04.006).  
Checked version: [arXiv:1410.6104v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.6104v3), revised May 18, 2017 and identified in the arXiv record as the published version; §2 and Theorem 9.1.

**Colmez, P., & Fontaine, J.-M. (2000).** Construction des représentations p-adiques semi-stables. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 140*(1), 1–43. [https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220000042](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220000042).  
Checked version: [author-hosted PDF](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~pierre.colmez/CF.pdf), Theorem A and §4.

**Deligne, P. (1971).** Théorie de Hodge. II. *Publications mathématiques de l’IHÉS, 40*, 5–57. [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684692](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684692).  
Checked version: [published article in Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1971__40__5_0.pdf), §§2.1.13–2.1.14 on Tate twists and comparison lattices, Corollary 4.1.2, and §§4.1.3.1–4.1.3.2 on fixed parts and families.

**Deligne, P. (1982).** Hodge cycles on abelian varieties (notes by J. S. Milne). In P. Deligne, J. S. Milne, A. Ogus, & K.-Y. Shih, *Hodge cycles, motives, and Shimura varieties* (Lecture Notes in Mathematics, Vol. 900, pp. 9–100). Springer.  
Checked version: [Milne’s revised and updated PDF](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Documents/Deligne82.pdf), whose title page records October 1, 2018 as its latest update; §1, including Tate objects and comparison factors. The original chapter’s pagination and the updated PDF’s pagination differ.

**Deligne, P., & Milne, J. S. (1982).** Tannakian categories. In P. Deligne, J. S. Milne, A. Ogus, & K.-Y. Shih, *Hodge cycles, motives, and Shimura varieties* (Lecture Notes in Mathematics, Vol. 900, pp. 101–228). Springer.  
Checked version: [corrected author PDF dated August 15, 2012](https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/tc.pdf). The numbered statements retain their original numbering; the relevant checks are §§2–3, Theorems 2.11 and 3.2, Proposition 2.23, Theorem 4.17, and Definition 5.1. Citations designated “1982/2012” refer to this corrected text, including its editorial footnotes.

**Glöckner, H. (2016).** *Lectures on Lie groups over local fields* (arXiv:0804.2234v5) [Preprint]. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2234v5).  
Checked version: **v5**, revised December 28, 2016; [version-specific HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/0804.2234v5), Example 1.9 and Proposition 2.3. The latter is the closed-subgroup theorem used in P33 after establishing that the continuous image is compact and closed in the stated finite-dimensional target. Earlier versions have different proposition numbering.

**Grothendieck, A. (1966).** On the de Rham cohomology of algebraic varieties. *Publications mathématiques de l’IHÉS, 29*, 95–103. [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684807](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684807).  
Checked version: [published article in Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_1966__29__95_0.pdf), especially p. 102, note 10.

**Grothendieck, A. (1969a).** Standard conjectures on algebraic cycles. In *Algebraic geometry: International Colloquium, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Bombay, 1968* (pp. 193–199). Oxford University Press.  
Checked version: [Grothendieck Circle copy of the published article](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/StandardConjs.pdf), especially pp. 195–197. The printed A–D terminology is kept distinct from the provisional dictionary for the manuscript’s lettering.

**Grothendieck, A. (1969b).** Hodge’s general conjecture is false for trivial reasons. *Topology, 8*(3), 299–303. [https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(69)90016-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(69)90016-0).  
Checked version: [Grothendieck Circle copy](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/HodgeConj.pdf), §1; [publisher’s article record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0040938369900160).

**Jannsen, U. (1992).** Motives, numerical equivalence, and semi-simplicity. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 107*, 447–452. [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01231898](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01231898).  
Checked version: [institutional repository copy of the article](https://epub.uni-regensburg.de/26642/1/jannsen10.pdf), pp. 447–450, especially Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.

**Kahn, B. (2025).** Galois descent for motivic theories. *Theory and Applications of Categories, 44*(20), 588–616. [Publisher’s article record](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/44/20/44-20abs.html).  
Checked numbered statements: [arXiv:2312.01825v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.01825v4), dated May 29, 2025; Theorem 5.10 and §10. The publisher records a July 7, 2025 revision; unchanged numbering or pagination in that later publisher version is not asserted here.

**Katz, N. M. (1976).** p-adic interpolation of real analytic Eisenstein series. *Annals of Mathematics, Second Series, 104*(3), 459–571. [Publisher’s article record](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1976/104-3/p06).  
Checked version: [author-hosted copy of the published article](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/padicinterp.pdf), p. 501, Lemma 4.0.7 and Question 4.0.8.

**Kleiman, S. L. (1994).** The standard conjectures. In U. Jannsen, S. L. Kleiman, & J.-P. Serre (Eds.), *Motives* (Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics, Vol. 55, Part 1, pp. 3–20). American Mathematical Society. [https://doi.org/10.1090/pspum/055.1/1265519](https://doi.org/10.1090/pspum/055.1/1265519).  
Checked version: [university-hosted copy of the published chapter](https://download.uni-mainz.de/mathematik/Algebraische%20Geometrie/Lehre/WS23.Padische1994.Kleiman.Standard.Conjectures.pdf), §5 and Proposition 5-1, for the printed numerical/homological equivalence formulation used in the terminology table.

**Kreutz, T., Shen, M., & Vial, C. (2026).** *De Rham–Betti classes with coefficients* (arXiv:2206.08618v3) [Preprint]. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08618v3).  
Checked version: **v3**, [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08618v3) and [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.08618v3), especially Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 5.13(ii). The version identifier is essential: earlier versions circulated under a different title and with different numbering. No journal publication is claimed.

**Milne, J. S. (2005).** Introduction to Shimura varieties. In *Harmonic analysis, the trace formula, and Shimura varieties* (Clay Mathematics Proceedings, Vol. 4, pp. 265–378). American Mathematical Society.  
Checked version: [author revision dated September 16, 2017](https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/svi.pdf), Proposition 6.5, Theorem 6.8, and Corollary 6.9, on pp. 72–73 of the revised PDF. Its header states that numbering is unchanged except for displays. Citations designated “2005/2017” refer to this checked revision.

**Mochizuki, S. (1999).** The local pro-p anabelian geometry of curves. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 138*(2), 319–423. [https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220050381](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220050381).  
Checked version: [author-hosted PDF](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/The%20Local%20Pro-p%20Anabelian%20Geometry%20of%20Curves.pdf), Theorem A; the author’s [June 2019 comments](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/The%20Local%20Pro-p%20Anabelian%20Geometry%20of%20Curves%20(comments).pdf) were also checked.

**Mochizuki, S. (2004).** The absolute anabelian geometry of hyperbolic curves. In K. Hashimoto, K. Miyake, & H. Nakamura (Eds.), *Galois theory and modular forms* (Developments in Mathematics, Vol. 11, pp. 77–122). Kluwer Academic Publishers.  
Checked version: [author-hosted PDF](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Absolute%20Anabelian%20Geometry.pdf), Lemma 1.3.1 and Corollary 1.3.2, with the author’s [June 2016 comments](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Absolute%20Anabelian%20Geometry%20(comments).pdf). The 2004 bibliographic year follows the author’s publication list and the volume’s copyright year; the [publisher](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4613-0249-0) separately lists a November 2003 hardcover release.

**Oda, T. (1969).** The first de Rham cohomology group and Dieudonné modules. *Annales scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure, Fourth Series, 2*(1), 63–135. [https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1175](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1175).  
Checked version: the supplied published paper and its [Numdam counterpart](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1969_4_2_1_63_0.pdf), Corollary 5.11 and the Hodge-sequence compatibility on p. 131.

**Pál, A. (2011).** The real section conjecture and Smith’s fixed-point theorem for pro-spaces. *Journal of the London Mathematical Society, 83*(2), 353–367. [https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdq075](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdq075).  
Checked version: [arXiv:0905.1205v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.1205v2), revised September 17, 2009; Theorem 1.3 and its projective-variety hypotheses.

**Stix, J. (2012).** On cuspidal sections of algebraic fundamental groups. In H. Nakamura, F. Pop, L. Schneps, & A. Tamagawa (Eds.), *Galois–Teichmüller theory and arithmetic geometry* (Advanced Studies in Pure Mathematics, Vol. 63, pp. 519–563). Mathematical Society of Japan.  
Checked version: [author-hosted PDF](https://www.math.uni-frankfurt.de/~stix/research/preprints/STIXcuspsec20120802homepage.pdf), Definitions 26 and 29 and Theorem 30. These are the locators used for the weights/normalizers comparison.

**Tate, J. (1966).** Endomorphisms of abelian varieties over finite fields. *Inventiones Mathematicae, 2*, 134–144. [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01404549](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01404549).  
Checked version: [university-hosted copy of the published article](https://pazuki.perso.math.cnrs.fr/index_fichiers/Tate66.pdf), the main theorem and initial notation on pp. 134–135.
