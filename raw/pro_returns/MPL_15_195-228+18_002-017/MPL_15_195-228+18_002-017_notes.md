### A13 — A gerbe of points and individual path torsors

### X02 — The integral Hodge structure separates points in the test family

**Kind:** own idea  
**Witness:** `15/225:L3,16`  
**Brief quotation:** foncteur dans les structures de Hodge ?; d’invariant l’indéterminée \(j\)  
**Context search:** `Hodge | elliptique | VA | Z-modules` → Adds an explicit integral test to CT54/003,005 and CT44/008’s abelian realization framework; no chosen-point injectivity argument of this form was located in the supplied context  
**Results ledger:** MPL_14_002-044 M24a [first-pass lead]; WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan; this is a conditional test, not a status change]

For an elliptic curve, recover its analytic torus as \(E(\mathbf C)\simeq(F^1H^1(E,\mathbf C))^\vee/\iota H_1(E,\mathbf Z)\), where \(\iota(\gamma)(\omega)=\int_\gamma\omega\); the integral Hodge structure determines this construction (Milne, 2005/2017, Theorem 6.8). An integral Hodge isomorphism therefore induces a holomorphic group isomorphism of the elliptic curves, so for the family X01 it implies \(1728t_1=1728t_2\) and hence \(t_1=t_2\). Confidence: high, because the period embedding of the actual lattice, together with the exact j-calculation, gives the implication without an additional chosen polarization. Replacing the integral lattice by rational cohomology retains only the isogeny class, so this proof does not establish equality of j in that variant (Milne, 2005/2017, Corollary 6.9).

### X03 — A conditional separation of chosen complex embeddings

**Kind:** own idea  
**Witness:** `15/225:L3,9,16;15/227:L3`  
**Brief quotation:** Attention, il faut parler des pl. complexes  
**Context search:** `Hodge | plongement | place | elliptique` → Adds an objectwise integral criterion to CT54/003,005 and the abelian comparisons in CT44/008–009; the supplied materials do not state this chosen-embedding separation argument  
**Results ledger:** MPL_14_002-044 M24a and §3 [first-pass leads]; WG2 §2.4 #3 [unchecked plan; restricted injectivity evidence only]

Let \(\sigma,\tau:k\hookrightarrow\mathbf C\) be chosen field embeddings, and suppose their Betti–Hodge realizations are isomorphic on an integral category containing \(h^1(E_a)\) for every \(a\in k\setminus\{0,1\}\), with the X01 family defined over k. Applying X02 objectwise gives \(1728\sigma(a)=1728\tau(a)\) for every such a, hence \(\sigma=\tau\), since embeddings also fix 0 and 1. Confidence: high for this conditional implication, because it uses only the reconstructed elliptic curves and the explicit invariant; even objectwise integral Hodge isomorphisms suffice. The hypotheses require preservation of the integral lattice and ordinary Hodge type over the identity of \(\mathbf C\); they do not settle the rational-functor variant, the essential image, or a formulation that identifies conjugate Hodge structures.
