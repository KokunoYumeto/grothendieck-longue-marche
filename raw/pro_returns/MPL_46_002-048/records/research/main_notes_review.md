## 6. Avoid making connectedness logically necessary in N15

**N15, opening sentence:** replace “the connectedness needed for the preceding Lang surjectivity assertion” by **“the connectedness hypothesis under which the preceding Lang theorem gives surjectivity.”** Connectedness is the theorem's sufficient hypothesis, not necessary for every individual Lang map: over F₂, the disconnected smooth group μ₃ has f(g) = g² and f(g)g⁻¹ = g, hence a surjective Lang map. The note can retain its point that the corollary inherits the theorem's hypotheses without adding this example.
