## Individual notes proposed for the main research apparatus

### N01 — Programme contact is present, but no requested second text is identified

**File/scan:** 46:003, page-file lines 20, 28, 31, 33; compare 46:020 and 46:041–047. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §1.1 #3.

The Jacobian heading on the same sheet remains uncertain in the frozen transcription, and its nearby cycle-theory question marks do not state Theorems 1–7 or complete cote 47's kernel comparison. ### N02 — The Fourier proof obligation has an exact local endpoint

**File/scan:** context 47:007–008, lines 2346 and 2380–2393. **Kind:** self-flagged. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4.

The supplied first-pass transcription says “Nous allons ici admettre le lemme 2” and then stops at the unit-section case with “à faire une vérification de compatibilité.....”. The context notes already isolate this at 47:8:1 and F7–F8: equality of the candidate fibre cohomology groups does not verify the particular comparison morphism. Nothing in cote 46 identifies a manuscript in which that remaining computation was written out; the present packet therefore restates and precisely locates the open documentary request.

### N03 — A theorem in the literature is different evidence from a second manuscript

**File/scan:** context 47:005–008; compare the absence of a corresponding text in 46:003 and 46:020–048. **Kind:** reading question. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4.

Mukai (1981, Theorem 2.2, p. 156) proves field-case inversion through the composition kernel, citing Mumford §13 for the Poincaré direct image. Laumon (1996, §1.1 and Lemma 1.2.5, p. 5) states the relative canonical direct-image isomorphism with its determinant line, but his proof explicitly says “Nous rappellerons seulement la construction du morphisme canonique”; it does not print the remaining compatibility calculation. This refines context notes F3–F8: their literature leads support the mathematical statement, while neither source is a second Grothendieck manuscript witness of its completed check.

### N04 — The public 12 August transcription derives from the same cote

**File/scan:** context 44:008–009, lines 2966–3001. **Kind:** reading question. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4.

The CSG/Grothendieck Institute edition of the 12 August 1964 Serre letter states on its provenance page that it derives from the Montpellier scan of cote 44 (Grothendieck, 1964/2024, PDF p. 2). It is another editorial transcription of the same archival source, so it cannot by itself count as an independent witness or an earlier draft. This confirms the provenance of the parallel material discussed in context notes 44:8:1 and D08–D13 without validating either transcription's uncertain readings.

### N05 — The supplied letter gives parallel formulations for Theorems 1–4

**File/scan:** context 44:002 and 44:008–009; especially lines 2793–2803, 2974–2995. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §2.3 #12.

The dated letter gives the Betti bound and complementary duality, sketches the quotient by the kernel of a correspondence, and asks “A prouver que cette dernière est positive !”. These are substantial parallels to Th. 1–4, already recognized in the first-pass notes D08–D13, but the letter also says the equivalence relation is not yet well identified and stresses the lack of proofs. The numbered programme's date is not written in that transcription, so calling the letter an earlier version of the complete sequence would exceed the evidence.

### N06 — A distinct 16 August 1964 letter documents the unresolved height connection

**File/scan:** compare context 44:002 (Th. 2–4), 44:004 (Th. 5), 44:008–009; current-package contact 46:003. **Kind:** self-flagged, in external correspondence. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §2.3 #12; WG H9.

In the letter dated 16 August 1964 reproduced by Serre (1991, Appendix 1, pp. 342–344, especially p. 343), Grothendieck reports the intermediate-Jacobian construction and half-Betti bound but says “je n'ai pas encore compris le lien” between Hodge positivity and the Néron–Tate form. He describes the surface case as motivating a hoped-for broader reduction. This is an independent dated formulation of the research problem behind Th. 4–5, adding documentary evidence to context note D12 rather than proving the programme's formula or establishing that the letter precedes its undated wording.

### N07 — A second recipient confirms the 1964 construction programme

**File/scan:** compare context 44:002, 44:008–009; current-package contact 46:003. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §2.3 #12.

Grothendieck's letter to Mumford of 31 August 1964 independently reports the construction, its Betti bound and complementary duality, while distinguishing these varieties from the full classical intermediate Jacobians (letter 24, p. 89). It adds a dated parallel to context notes D08–D11, not a proof or a complete earlier seven-item draft.

### N08 — Later correspondence changes how the positivity programme should be read

**File/scan:** compare context 44:004 (Th. 5), 44:006 (Th. 7), 44:008–009; current-package contact 46:003. **Kind:** reading question, with external later reassessment. **Bears on:** WG H9; WG2 §2.3 #12; WG2 §2.4 #4.

On 9 August 1968 Grothendieck links the intended Hodge–height comparison to the tempting numerical-versus-τ equivalence assertion (letter 43, p. 143). On 4 September he says Griffiths's result convinced him that “my feelings on the relations between Hodge’s index theorem and the Néron-Tate form were erroneous” and accepts a distinction between τ and homological equivalence (letter 44, p. 145). This adds direct authorial reassessment to context notes D12–D14 and prevents WG H9 from being treated as settled; it neither identifies which repaired version of Th. 5 might hold nor constitutes a withdrawal within the supplied manuscript itself.

### N09 — A securely earlier divisor statement, already present as a context lead

**File/scan:** context 44:006, Th. 7; compare the divisor/Picard subject of 46:041–047. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §2.3 #12.

Grothendieck's May 1962 Bourbaki exposé 236, §4, p. 242, identifies torsion and numerical equivalence for divisors on a smooth projective variety, crediting Matsusaka (Grothendieck, 1962). This is a dated, codimension-one precursor, already cited in context note D14 and now checked in the primary publication. It supplies no earlier proof of the unrestricted cycle assertion on cote 44 scan 6; the distinction is material to the later reassessment in N08.

### N10 — Separate chronology, mathematical completion and witness independence

**File/scan:** 46:003; context 47:008 and 44:002,004,006,008–009. **Kind:** own idea. **Bears on:** WG2 §2.4 #4.

A useful follow-up register should keep three independent fields: whether a text is physically independent of the Montpellier witness, whether its date is independently established, and whether it actually carries the missing proof or merely asserts its conclusion. The first-pass RESULTS_STATE lead MPL_136_095-141 1.5 and the remaining, unsupplied cote-46 leaves are places to check, not positive identifications. **Confidence: high** in this documentary distinction, because the CSG edition, the dated letters and the later Fourier theorem exhibit three different evidence types; **confidence: low** that the unsupplied leads will contain the requested witness.
