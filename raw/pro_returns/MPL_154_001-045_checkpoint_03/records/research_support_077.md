They add substantial construction data to the open item **follow-ups @944**. They do not finish its surface, Euler-characteristic, deformation-generation, or move-relations proofs.

It differs from the much coarser facet-set isotopy criterion reported in R20–R22 for 088. The conditional torus example in R22 does not refute the fixed-pseudoline cyclic-order statement.
3. **The strongest reported conflict is very specific:** current 044(c) characterizes exceptional edges of K by absence of vertices of L; R53 reports 118:50 saying that criterion is no longer correct and such edges can contain several vertices of L. This is a formulation-level conflict to record. The report concerns a cancelled draft, so it is not authority to repair current 044 or to declare a final theorem refuted.

R12–R19 then report a dihedral-surjectivity argument with qualifications. None of those steps can be supplied by the others without identifying the exact surface and attaching relations.
5. **The justified classification remains the combinatorial topology of pseudoline relative positions, with the Esquisse modular-surface comparison as a contextual lead.** The current pages do not establish tame-topology foundations, the 1986 geometry of forms, or any equivalence with an exit-category construction. All research-state statuses remain unchanged.

## 9. Follow-up audit: the two-element generization complement

**A01 — Conditional cardinality incompatibility at a supercritical target.** **Kind:** own idea / scope diagnosis. **Confidence:** high for the conditional cardinality calculation; medium for identifying an omitted ordinary-arrow restriction as the intended repair. **Exact frozen anchors:** 012:29,31; 014:33,48; 015:25,27,29; 016:12,14,16; 017:4. Further scope comparisons: 020's ordinary-target qualification; 026:7 and 028:10 defer the supercritical case.

Current 016:16 identifies Gen₀(D) with the inverse image of D∩S under a two-sheeted cover. It explicitly distinguishes the cover of sides for a nonsupercritical D from the inverse-image cover in X-tilde for a supercritical D. These different cover conventions do not change the cardinality of a two-element fibre. Current 015:27 names ν as the number of vertices on Δ, and 015:25,29 corrects the immediate-generization count to **2ν**, excluding the stable generizations that factor through under-stable ones. Thus there is no need to use the earlier uncorrected 4ν expression.

For a corridor specialization D→Δ of the kind introduced in 014:33, D is under-stable and D∩S={a}; this is explicitly an immediate-specialization discussion, and 014:48 admits corridors alongside specialization triangles. Consequently the definitions in 012:31 and 016:16 give

\[
|\operatorname{Gen}_0(D)|=2,
\qquad
|\operatorname{Gen}_0(\Delta)|=2\nu.
\]

**If** the “Toute flèche” statement continued from 016:16 to 017:4 applies to that corridor arrow, and **if** its stated injection exists, its complement therefore has cardinality

\[
2\nu-2,
\]

which equals two only when ν=2. For a permitted target with ν≥3, this is incompatible with the literal universal complement-two assertion. This calculation challenges **the claimed complement size or its scope**, not injectivity itself: a two-element set can still inject into a 2ν-element set.

The most natural conditional diagnosis is that the complement-two rule was carried over from an **ordinary arrow adding one new vertex of S**, as expressed by 012:29's D′∩S=(D∩S)∪{s}. Under that ordinary-arrow count, the two-sheeted-cover description changes the cardinality by two. The source later distinguishes an ordinary nonsupercritical target in 020 and explicitly defers the supercritical comparison in 026 and 028, which makes this diagnosis plausible. Nevertheless, 016:14 explicitly includes supercritical vertices in Γ, and 016:16 explicitly gives their cover convention immediately before “Toute flèche”; no written ordinary-arrow restriction is present at the assertion itself. The diagnosis must therefore remain an interpretation to test, not an inserted correction or a claim that the author stated the restriction.

**Limits.** This audit does not independently construct and classify a geometric corridor example with ν≥3, nor prove the canonical injection for the exceptional case. It checks the incompatibility **conditional on admitting such an arrow under the stated global conventions**. It also does not treat all generizations as immediate, replace the full target by an unspecified local subset, or silently identify the two cover constructions. Those changes would be new hypotheses requiring text or proof. No RESULTS_STATE theorem is refuted or proved; the observation adds a sharply localized exceptional-case test to **follow-ups @944 [Open]**, alongside the exceptional-transport concerns in **N77 R15, 086:61–64**, **R31, 093:70–76**, and **R41, 105:122–124**.
