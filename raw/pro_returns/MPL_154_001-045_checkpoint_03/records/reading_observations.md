These are working observations, not context-informed research notes.

## Scan 001

## Scan 002

Dated 18.1.84; own page 1. Boxed heading says 5 variants; initial heading tentatively Quatre possibilités. Variant 1 gives surface X_0 with valence 4n supersingular vertices. Variant 2 admits supersingular relative positions as vertices in the starting graph and discusses choices of specialization arcs; numerical correction explicitly restricts 2(n−1) to the stable case and gives 2n_i generally. NB and placement of several late insertions need priority audit. Four manuscript drawings, including two coloured ovals and a local fan illustration.

## Scan 003

It changes the placement of the generization arc. The final stable-face count is retained as read, with a doubt, even though the displayed totals invite a mathematical correction. Contains one coloured oval illustration.

## Scan 004

## Scan 012

## Scan 013

## Scan 014

## Scan 015

Author says corridors and ordinary immediate specialization/generization data fail to form a natural polygon structure. Tentatively switches to starting from Δ and generizations. Counts two stable per side and two sous-stable per vertex, then margin corrects: stable are not immediate and factor through sous-stable. 4ν count carries footnote 2ν when restricted to immediate generizations. Orientation dependence of comparing the lifted topological polygon with the previous vis polygon is emphasized; state of pas is uncertain, so avoid treating this clause as an unqualified contradiction.

## Scan 016

Returns to sous-stable D and open lifted arcs, arguing at least four total incident specialization/generization objects. States a global graph result including supercritical positions: degree≥3, ≥4 when not stable, natural polygon structure, orientations canonically identified with orientations of D, and canonical orientation-set isomorphism along specialization arrows. NB identifies Gen_0 with inverse images of D∩S but changes meaning of the covering for supercritical D; continuation next page. This repairs the corridor/polygon concern voiced on15, but proof details and functorial compatibility deserve separate notes.

## Scan 017

## Scan 023

## Scan 035

## Scan 036

The claims are not proved here, and the rewritten conditions deserve separate research items, not mathematical correction of the transcription.

## Scan 037

If there are two exceptional specialization arcs, L is characterized as a corridor bisector; exactly two generalization arcs then complete a four-sided polygon. General transition arcs only meet in exceptional-specialization/generalization adjacency. Defines ordinary inertia arcs, then improves definition through transition vertices and characteristic polygon. Without exceptional arcs, characteristic arcs alternate inertia/transition; two inertia arcs are never adjacent. Adjacent non-inertia arcs must be exceptional-specialization/generalization. All assertions to be inventoried as research notes.

## Scan 038

With shortened characteristic arcs he claims universal disjointness/alternation. The generalization move crosses the strands; the structural polygon is claimed unchanged up to canonical combinatorial isomorphism, but the selected arc becomes a specialization arc. Geometric antibalayage description and gluing ΔL to ΔL′ along corresponding arcs by affine isomorphism begin. Canceled middle drawing retained.
