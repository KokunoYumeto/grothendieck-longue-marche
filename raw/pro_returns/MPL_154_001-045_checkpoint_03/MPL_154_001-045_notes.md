### C02 — A connection with tame topology is useful but bounded

**Source:** 154, scans 016–017, 025, 031–033, 044–045. **Kind:** own idea.

## 2. The five variants and their unresolved comparisons

### V01 — Exceptional valences and stable-case counts

**Source:** 154, scan 002:8–11, 22–23. **Kind:** unproved claim.

The first variant has exceptional vertices “d'ordre $4n$”, whereas the second replaces them by exceptional faces and aims at ordinary valence four. The count $2(n-1)$ is explicitly restricted to the stable case and replaced by $2n_i$ in general, so any global cell count must retain the number of vertices on each original pseudoline rather than substitute $n-1$ indiscriminately. A proof needs the local cyclic incidence, including the intervening stable bigon faces and the no-free-end assertion at the original exceptional vertex.

**Context:** H, pp. 1–2 / scans 002–003: **restates these variants**; N77 R57–R58, scan 120:69–87: **adds local input but does not establish its global counts**. **Register:** `follow-ups @944` [Open], which also cites scan 120: method and necessary hypothesis control for the counting problem.
