The homotopy applications impose further connectivity conditions; no fixed integral coefficient ring for the entire filtered product is asserted. |
| **Cenkl (1989), §5, Theorem 2 and corollary, pp. 31–33; L14** | **Direct:** publisher PDF. It forces each coefficient square to be divisible by two and excludes \(\mathbb Z\). This is a functorial obstruction independent of a divided-power structure, not a claim about every isolated commutative algebra. |
