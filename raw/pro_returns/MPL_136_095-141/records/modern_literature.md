# Primary-source access and verification record

**Accessed:** 15 November 2026, after the transcription freeze. No scans or transcription files were consulted or changed. This record identifies sources and the scope directly checked; substantive comparisons are in research notes L06–L11, and the conditional diagnostics are in O01–O12 and pd_diagnostics.md. The checks cover definitions, theorem statements, relevant remarks, and publication metadata, not independent verification of every proof.

## L06 — Mandell: integral cochains

**APA reference:** Mandell, M. A. (2006). Cochains and homotopy type. *Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS, 103*, 213–246. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10240-006-0037-6

**Access:** [Primary journal PDF](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_2006__103__213_0.pdf); [journal metadata](https://www.numdam.org/item/?id=PMIHES_2006__103__213_0).

**Checked locators:** Main Theorem, p. 213; Theorem 0.1, p. 214; Theorem 0.2 and non-fullness discussion, pp. 215–216.

**Verified scope and limitation:** Integral \(E_\infty\) cochains classify weak-equivalence classes of finite-type nilpotent spaces; the functor on their homotopy categories is faithful, not full. Theorem 0.1 allows arbitrary source \(X\) and finite-type nilpotent target \(Y\); its cohomological compatibility additionally assumes finite-type \(X\). Neither cohomology rings nor unspecified PD cochain algebras are the theorem's input structures.

## L07 — Mandell: p-adic cochains

**APA reference:** Mandell, M. A. (2001). \(E_\infty\) algebras and \(p\)-adic homotopy theory. *Topology, 40*(1), 43–94. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0040-9383(99)00053-1

**Access:** [Author's archival PDF](https://mmandell.pages.iu.edu/papers/einffinal.pdf); [publisher metadata](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040938399000531); [author's version notice](https://mmandell.pages.iu.edu/papers/).

**Checked locators:** Main Theorem, author-version p. 1; introductory qualifications, pp. 1–2. These are author-version page numbers, not journal pagination.

**Verified scope and limitation:** Cochains over \(\overline{\mathbf F}_p\) give a contravariant fully faithful functor on the homotopy category of connected \(p\)-complete nilpotent spaces of finite \(p\)-type. This is the p-adic statement; \(\mathbf F_p\) cannot replace its algebraic closure in this full-embedding formulation. The author-hosted version was preferred following the author's copyediting notice.

## L08 — Ekedahl: numerical models

**APA reference:** Ekedahl, T. (2002). On minimal models in integral homotopy theory. *Homology, Homotopy and Applications, 4*(2), 191–218. https://doi.org/10.4310/HHA.2002.v4.n2.a9

**Access:** [Author's arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0107004); [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0107004); [journal mirror](https://ftp2.de.freebsd.org/pub/EMIS/journals/HHA/volumes/2002/n2a8/v4n2a8.pdf).

**Checked locators:** Introduction, p. 1; Lemma 2.8 and Theorem 2.9, p. 6; nonuniqueness remark, p. 7, in arXiv v2. Journal metadata was verified from the indexed primary mirror; its subsequent direct fetch returned HTTP 502.

**Verified scope and limitation:** Connected nilpotent simplicial sets with degreewise finitely generated homology admit special numerical models. Numerical maps are rational polynomials integral-valued on integral inputs. Numerical cochains compute cohomology; Lemma 2.8 compares based maps from a numerical source to a special numerical target modulo homotopy. Minimal models need not be isomorphic; integer-valued does not mean integer-coefficient.

## L09 — Toën: integral affinization

**APA reference:** Toën, B. (2020). Le problème de la schématisation de Grothendieck revisité. *Épijournal de Géométrie Algébrique, 4*, Article 14. https://doi.org/10.46298/epiga.2020.volume4.6060

**Access:** [Primary journal PDF](https://epiga.episciences.org/6822/pdf); [publisher record](https://epiga.episciences.org/6822). Journal publication: 5 October 2020; the 2019 arXiv submission does not change the publication year.

**Checked locators:** Theorem 0.1, pp. 2–3; Definition 2.1 and Proposition 2.2, pp. 5–6; Corollary 5.1, p. 14; Corollaries 5.4–5.5, pp. 16–17.

**Verified scope and limitation:** For simply connected finite-type \(X\), integral affinization has homotopy sheaves \(\pi_i(X)\otimes\mathbb H\); its integral-point unit has a functorial retraction. \(\mathbb H\)'s coordinate ring consists of integer-valued polynomials. Affinization is faithful and detects equivalence classes. This does not assert full faithfulness or equality with all integral points: Corollary 5.1 gives additional homotopy. The target is fpqc affine stacks.

## L10 — Horel: binomial homotopy theory

**APA reference:** Horel, G. (2024). Binomial rings and homotopy theory. *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik, 2024*(813), 283–305. https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2024-0039

**Access:** [Author's PDF](https://geoffroy.horel.org/binomial.pdf); [publisher record](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/crelle-2024-0039/html). The journal year is 2024, not the first arXiv year, 2022.

**Checked locators:** Definition 1.1 and Remark 1.3, author-PDF p. 3; Theorem 3.7, p. 7; Remark 3.11, p. 8; Theorems 5.4 and 6.1, p. 13.

**Verified scope and limitation:** The contravariant cochain functor to cosimplicial binomial rings, localized at quasi-isomorphisms, is fully faithful on connected nilpotent finite-type spaces; without nilpotence, connected finite-type spaces yield Bousfield–Kan \(\mathbf Z\)-completion. Binomial rings are torsion-free, with uniquely determined binomial operations; identity Frobenius lifts give their \(\lambda\)-ring description. The ordinary forgetful functor is fully faithful, but its derived counterpart is not. This is not a PD-ideal theorem.

## L11 — Richter and Sagave: diagrammatic strict commutativity

**APA reference:** Richter, B., & Sagave, S. (2020). A strictly commutative model for the cochain algebra of a space. *Compositio Mathematica, 156*(8), 1718–1743. https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X20007319

**Access:** [Primary article and publisher metadata](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/compositio-mathematica/article/strictly-commutative-model-for-the-cochain-algebra-of-a-space/4076D51EAAD865E63F13B718B6026074).

**Checked locators:** Theorems 1.1–1.2; §1.4; Definition 2.8; Theorem 6.2.

**Verified scope and limitation:** Commutative monoids in \(\mathcal I\)-chain complexes with Day convolution model singular cochains through their \(E_\infty\) homotopy colimits, over any commutative base ring. Theorem 1.2 detects weak-equivalence classes of finite-type nilpotent spaces integrally. Strict commutativity belongs to this diagram monoidal category, not ordinary CDGAs; the integral cochain functor still fails to be full.

### Stacks Project: divided-power identities

**APA reference:** The Stacks Project Authors. (2026). *Divided power algebra* (§23.6). *The Stacks Project*. https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09PF

**Access and locators:** [Definition 23.6.1, tag 09PG](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09PG); [Definition 23.6.5, tag 09PJ](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09PJ); [section warning, tag 09PF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09PF). Version consulted 15 November 2026.
