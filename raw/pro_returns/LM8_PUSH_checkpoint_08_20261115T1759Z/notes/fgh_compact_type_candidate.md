# Candidate extension after A.40: compact-type reduction

Superseded research note. The complete proof passed independent root audit and is now registered as A.41 in sections/A_compact_type_fixed.tex. DE separately audited the tensor-intersection and diagonal-conjugacy lemmas. The historical argument below records the development; the registered section is authoritative.

## Proposed criterion

Let X be an affine hyperbolic curve over a number field, with at least two cusps. At a finite place of residue characteristic p, suppose its proper curve has potentially compact-type stable reduction. Suppose the selected cusp P and another cusp Q lie, in the pointed stable model, on a path containing only genus-zero vertices. Then for every ell != p, every cusp packet has fixed subgroup exactly selected inertia under the closed derived Galois subgroup (and hence under the permitted open Delta subgroups in the CM setting).

The condition includes two sections colliding at a point of an unpointed positive-genus component: separation introduces a rational marked tree. It also includes two distinct marks on the same rational component or connected rational subtree. It excludes a path through a positive-genus component.

## 1. The unbounded algebra extends componentwise

Write V=direct sum V_i for the proper homology of the positive-genus components of the compact-type special fibre, with orthogonal symplectic classes Omega_i and Omega=sum Omega_i. Use all cusp letters except Q, each of weight two; handles have weight one. Root the graph at P.

For an edge e let i_e,Q_e,j_e indicate whether genus component i, eliminated cusp Q, and retained cusp j lie downstream. Its boundary is delta_e=sum_i i_e Omega_i+sum_j j_e t_j before eliminating Q. The genuine first weighted derivation, including arbitrary packet lambda ad(t_P), has

    D(v_i)=[W_i,v_i],       D(t_j)=[W_j,t_j],
    W_i=sum_e length(e) i_e delta_e +lambda t_P,
    W_j=sum_e length(e) j_e delta_e +lambda t_P.

The coefficient of Omega_i in W_i is

    r_i=sum_e length(e) i_e(1-Q_e).

This is the distance from the P,Q,i branching point to component i. The assumed genus-free P--Q path makes every r_i strictly positive.

Take B=k<all retained t_j, all Omega_i>. The separate handle-core bases of A.27/A.39 yield a first-core prefix for each core of component type i, with operator K_i=D_B+R_(W_i). Count Omega_i. Its top part is D_(c_i)+r_i R_(Omega_i), where D_(c_i)(Omega_i)=0 and every other B-letter u satisfies D_(c_i)(u)=c_u[Omega_i,u]. The coefficient c_u is just that of Omega_i in the corresponding W_u. Thus exactly the slot-polynomial argument of A.40 applies: on a fixed word of all non-Omega_i letters the polynomial multiplier has coefficients telescoping to r_i. It is injective. The other terms preserve the Omega_i count. Therefore no first core survives, giving

    ker D contained in L(T,U),
    T=span(retained cusp letters), U=span(Omega_i).

This is an all-degree argument, including arbitrary lambda. It is stronger and simpler than separately requiring a generic packet scalar or an explicit CM matrix.

## 2. Exact tensor intersection

The global Galois-stable leading Lie space W of the actual derived-fixed unipotent closure is contained in L(T,U), and hence in every L(T,gU). Here T is globally Tate and the proper quadratic subspace is stable in the weighted graded free Lie algebra.

The free associative algebra generated by T and V tensor V embeds into T(V direct sum T), with V of weight one and T of weight two: a maximal handle string of even length has its unique consecutive pairing, and cusp letters are separately recognizable. Consequently in weight 2m,

    intersection_g (T direct sum gU)^(tensor m)
       = (T direct sum intersection_g gU)^(tensor m).

The tensor-intersection identity is elementary over a field and extends to the infinite family because the intersection of these finite-dimensional subspaces equals a finite subintersection. Intersecting with primitives gives

    intersection_g L(T,gU)=L(T,U_*),  U_*=intersection_g gU.

No freeness of arbitrary surface quotients, no root closure of pro-ell images, and no fixed-point/quotient commutation is being assumed.

## 3. A diagonal symplectic lemma makes U_* Tate

Let G be the Zariski closure of the global action on V; after a finite number-field extension assume G connected. The ambient polarization gives a map from bivectors beta to endomorphisms T_beta using the inverse total symplectic bivector Omega. It sends U to the diagonal algebra of scalar multiples of the mutually orthogonal block projectors V→V_i. For g in G,

    T_(g beta)=chi(g) g T_beta g^(-1).

Thus U_* corresponds, after removing chi, to a conjugation-stable linear subspace of this fixed diagonal algebra. For any element of that subspace, its G-conjugacy orbit remains in the diagonal algebra and preserves its multiset of eigenvalues. There are only finitely many diagonal block-scalar elements with that multiset: each of finitely many fixed blocks receives one of finitely many eigenvalues. The orbit of a connected algebraic group is connected, so this finite orbit is a single point. Therefore G acts on U_* by chi.

This argument uses only the symplectic decomposition, not a CM decomposition or Zariski density theorem. It also works for nonreductive connected G.

It follows that every generator of L(T,U_*) has weight two and character chi. The actual fixed closure satisfies A.38, which proves the proposed criterion.

## Audit questions to resolve before promotion

1. Confirm that the independent handle-core normal form used for several components has exactly the same first-core-prefix behavior as in A.27; inserting boundary letters cannot remove an existing first core.
2. State the compact-type local H1 decomposition and global weighted action precisely: the Omega_i are local symplectic block bivectors, not assumed global endomorphisms. The intersection construction, rather than an arithmetic CM identification, is what makes U_* global.
3. Ensure the graph condition is stated on the pointed stable model after finite extension, and that all finite edge lengths used for r_i are positive.
4. Keep the passage to connected global monodromy compatible with enlarging the field and replacing the derived subgroup by the smaller one. The conclusion for a larger specified Delta group follows by containment and its known trivial action on selected inertia.

If valid, this extends A.40's good-reduction criterion to a large compact-type family relevant to Task A.5. It does not address covers with positive graph Betti number or cases where every path from the selected cusp to another cusp passes through positive genus.
