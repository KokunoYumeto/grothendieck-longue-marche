## Analytic integrals and Picard–Fuchs equations

### P01 — The characteristic-zero qualification is genuinely left open

**Location:** 93/221, circled marginal question and factorisation diagram. **Kind:** self-flagged. **Words:** “Spécial à car. 0 ?” (first word doubtful).

The question qualifies the proposed canonical factorisation of an operator “à coeff cts” through closed one-forms, so it should not be silently dropped from a modern statement. ### P02 — Closedness of the logarithmic differential needs the group hypothesis

**Location:** 93/221, last diagram \(g^{-1}dg\to Z^1C\). **Kind:** reading question. **Words:** “\(g^{-1}dg\)”.

For a general noncommutative group, the Maurer–Cartan equation contains the bracket term, so a logarithmic differential is not automatically an ordinary closed Lie-algebra-valued one-form. The preceding argument must therefore supply commutativity or another condition that kills that term before this displayed target is interpreted literally. ### D02 — “À coeff. cts” has a local definition here

**Location:** 93/231, final NB. **Kind:** reading question. **Words:** “rel. à la stratification”.

### D03 — The formal Poincaré hypothesis cannot be discarded in characteristic p

**Location:** 93/231, “Supposons” paragraph and characteristic-zero example; compare 221. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the elementary obstruction.

Over a field of characteristic p, the closed formal one-form \(x^{p-1}dx\) is not the derivative of a formal power series, because differentiation gives zero as the coefficient of \(x^{p-1}dx\). Thus the displayed formal Poincaré hypothesis has real content, and a positive-characteristic replacement must account for Cartier classes instead of copying the characteristic-zero exact sequence. Context comparison: this supports the circled question on 221 and Hua, modernised reading of cote 93, XIII and XX, scans 168–187 and 231–233; it connects to MPL_93_002-046 E26 in notes/RESULTS_STATE.md without re-proving that earlier note's Hasse–Witt interpretation.

### D04 — A conditional answer to “injectif ?”

**Location:** 93/232, circled question after the corollary. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, conditional on the factorisation and quotient asserted on 231–232.

If \(D=\Delta d_E^0\) and \(\Delta\) is changed by \(B d_E^1\), then its restriction to \(\ker d_E^1\) is unchanged, so the proposed map is well defined under the asserted quotient description. If that restriction vanishes, \(D=0\) because \(\operatorname{im}d_E^0\subseteq\ker d_E^1\), which proves the questioned injectivity under precisely those assumptions. Context comparison: this answers the local question retained by Hua, modernised reading of cote 93, XX, scan 232, but does not prove the formal exactness assumptions in D01 or remove the characteristic qualification of D03.
