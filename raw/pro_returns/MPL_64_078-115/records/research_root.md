## R05: why the two pinnings serve different purposes

Scan084 explicitly says that Legendre pinning alone is not rigid. It identifies the remaining automorphisms with even permutations of J fixing t. Scan085 adds a full ordering of the three nonzero points of E[2]. Over a base where 2 is invertible, a level-2 automorphism is locally +1 or -1 (Conrad, *Level structures*, Proposition2.1, https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/248BPage/handouts/level.pdf). Its action on the tangent line at the identity is the same sign. An actual isomorphism of that line with the specified twisted line therefore kills the remaining -1. This checks rigidity, not the complete descent/reconstruction assertion of scan083. The manuscript uses a relative tangent-line isomorphism, not a globally chosen nonzero tangent vector on every unpinned family.

## R06--R08: the finite central extension and its character

After choosing i, put G={(g,a) in S3×Z/4:sgn(g)=(-1)^a}. Let r=((123),0), s=((12),1). Then r^3=s^4=1 and srs^-1=r^-1; these relations give all twelve elements r^a s^b. The kernel of G→S3 is {1,s²}; every lift of a transposition has order4, so this central extension by C2 does not split. In the abelianization the relation r=r^-1 and r³=1 forces r=1, while the second projection gives a surjection to C4; hence G_ab=C4 and [G,G]=<r>. This is a direct proof of the insertion in scan092(54), independent of the modernized reading.

The exact enumeration in check_moduli.py confirms order12, centre order2, derived subgroup order3, and element-order counts 1:1,2:1,3:2,4:6,6:2. Scan092's line action has multiplier lambda on O(1), lambda² on its tensor square, and lambda²sgn(g) on the tangent line. Restriction to lambda²=sgn(g) makes the last factor 1. This explains why the central sign is invisible on the parameter line but remains visible on the elliptic tangent line. It does not compute an outer automorphism of a profinite fundamental group. The supplied LM_140-2_notes.md, second-pass S09 for scan529, already claims the same abstract dicyclic quotient for the unique normal complement to the central sign in Gamma(2); that complement and its monodromy are not re-derived here. The added evidence is the manuscript's explicit pinning interpretation and its line character, not a new abstract finite group.

Hua, *modernised reading of cote64*, section “L'épinglage de Legendre sur une base ; le schéma de modules,” independently names the same abstract group dicyclic and records its abelianization. This run **restates and checks** that finite assertion; it adds the non-splitting witness and relates it to the displayed line action. The words “groupe modulaire” here refer to this finite group attached to the pinning, not by themselves to SL2(Z), its congruence completion, or GT. ## R09--R10: exceptional stabilizers and characteristic3

Choose a standard Legendre cross-ratio lambda on the four-branch-point line; this is a coordinate convention, not an identification of the manuscript's t with lambda. The S3 orbit is lambda,1-lambda,1/lambda,1/(1-lambda),lambda/(lambda-1),(lambda-1)/lambda. The standard model y²=x(x-1)(x-lambda) has c4=16(lambda²-lambda+1), discriminant16lambda²(1-lambda)², and therefore j=256(lambda²-lambda+1)³/[lambda²(1-lambda)²]. Substitution of all six transformations preserves this j. The exact calculation is in moduli_check_results.json.

A generator of A3 acts by lambda→1/(1-lambda); its fixed equation is lambda²-lambda+1=0. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from2,3 this gives the two equianharmonic parameters j=0, and the remaining Legendre-pinned stabilizer is C3, as scans084–085 anticipate with “exceptionnelle hexagonale.” The supplied LM_140-3_notes.md, second-pass S4 for scan009, already gives this j-map and its exceptional parameters in characteristic zero; our computation is a check and our added point is its application to these pinnings and its characteristic3 limit. The three order2 fixed parameters are -1,2,1/2, as follows either from the transformations or the factorization

    j-1728 = 64(lambda-2)²(lambda+1)²(2lambda-1)² / [lambda²(lambda-1)²].

For the unpinned curve the finite extension G gives the familiar stabilizer orders2 generically,4 at j=1728,6 at j=0 in characteristic different from2,3. The classical geometric automorphism comparison is also in Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, IV, Proposition7.13 (https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf); that source is used only for this standard comparison, not for any new archive conclusion.

The dossier works over Z[1/2], so characteristic3 must not be discarded. There lambda²-lambda+1=(lambda+1)², and all three harmonic parameters coalesce at lambda=-1. This is a nonreduced fixed subscheme of the parameter line, while the constant order3 permutation group itself is still etale. The full S3 stabilizes that geometric parameter, and its inverse image in G is the group of order12; the Legendre-only stabilizer remains its order3 even-permutation subgroup. For an independent geometric model over an algebraic closure of F3, y²=x³-x admits (x,y)→(x+a,y) for a in F3 and (x,y)→(-x,iy), i²=-1, giving C3 semidirect C4; the usual origin-preserving Weierstrass changes require u⁴=1 and a³-a=0, giving exactly twelve automorphisms. This refinement comes from the displayed finite moduli problem, not from a new p-curvature calculation or from a complete deformation comparison with the unavailable cote93 notes.

## R11--R14: reconstruction, linear equations and the CM distinction

Scan094 classifies square roots T of the quotient tangent line relative to a fixed T0 by mu2-torsors. The torsor assertion itself follows by taking Isom(T0,T) compatible with the squares: the stabilizer is mu2, and a local square-compatible isomorphism gives the contracted-product inverse. The further identification of such roots with elliptic curves is the manuscript's Kummer-reconstruction assertion, not newly proved here. Scan095's universal E_alpha is relative to fixed J,t,alpha; it is not a compactified modular family at a cusp.

Scan100's two linear equations sum psi_i=0 and sum c_i psi_i=0 constrain psi to a one-dimensional line. Multiplication of a nonzero solution by any scalar preserves these equations. The quadratic compatibility (73) can therefore not be recovered from the linear equations alone: it is a separate scale condition. This verifies that “Il reste ensuite à exprimer (73)” is a substantive unfinished calculation. The inverse scaling law in delta and the factor i under orientation reversal do transport one quadratic equation to its companions, but do not supply its first value.

Choosing i in scans090–096 trivializes a cyclotomic/orientation twist for every point of the parameter line. It does not impose the exceptional condition j=1728. Thus the occurrence of Q(i) bears on the notation and framing in LM7_PUSH E.3 and LM6 A.22, but does not supply an element of their CM Galois coset or settle either open question. Those register entries are used only as background claims, not checked proofs.
