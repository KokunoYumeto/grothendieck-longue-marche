## All-characteristic cyclic and abelian extension

A.21 now includes characteristic two. Its target x²+x=z^e is treated by a complete direct Cartier calculation, including the absence of geometric 2-primary Jacobian torsion. The chosen nonzero odd-torsion inversion fibres are unramified even when inversion is wild elsewhere. A.23 proves the all-degree cyclic theorem by separate outer degree and inner full-index-branch inductions; A.24 uses character quotients and retains stabilizers at ramified fibres. A.25 proves the double-cover branch-pair induction over an elliptic base, with auxiliary genus-two covers supplied by divisibility of Pic^0. A.26 gives the S3 quotient criterion; its explicit genus-six branch tuple is realized using the same opened SGA1 XIII2.12 prime-to-p presentation, not an unverified existence assumption. Its Kummer norm, even-valuation involution check, unramified quadratic twisting via E[4]→E[2] and E[8]→E[2], the bielliptic reflection quotient in degree eight, branch-count induction, and normalization assertions are proved in the manuscript and independently reviewed by root. A.29 now subsumes this positive conclusion using an independent proof; none of these broader theorems is attributed to an external source.

A.29 removes the elliptic-cover exponent restriction completely in odd characteristic and treats supersingular elliptic quotients in characteristic two. Its divisor identity q_i=binom(i,2), exact periodic second differences, local Kummer ramification, and character/inertia recovery are proved directly. The auxiliary separation inputs are precisely A.19 and the Cartier/logarithmic-differential argument of A.21; the characteristic-two supersingular normal form is justified in the proof by the explicit Weierstrass inversion formula. This result makes no assertion for ordinary elliptic quotients in characteristic two or general nonabelian covers. A.25 and A.27 remain as separate earlier proofs.
