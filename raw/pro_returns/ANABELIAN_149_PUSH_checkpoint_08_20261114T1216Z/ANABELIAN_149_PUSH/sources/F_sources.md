## Literature-status search

Searches through the web tool on 14 November 2026 included the exact CHW title and combinations of “étale reconstruction”, “cyclotomic”, “Qab”, “TKND”, “Tsujimura”, and “pinned”; both available search systems were used. The CHW author text and the RIMS institutional preprint were opened, not only their search snippets.

Hoshi, Mochizuki, and Tsujimura (2025), *Combinatorial construction of the absolute Galois group of the field of rational numbers*, was also opened at https://www.ms.u-tokyo.ac.jp/journal/32-1-1.pdf and in the author's 14 March 2025 version (`tmp/pdf_text/HMT2025.txt`). The introduction, Definitions 6.1 and 6.6, and Theorem 6.8 concern TKND-AVKF fields, the AA property, commensurators inside GT, and semi-absolute anabelian geometry of configuration spaces. They do not themselves state full reconstruction of arbitrary finite-type schemes from Hom-sets of small étale topoi. They therefore cannot simply be cited as LM5-F.47 or F.50.

The updated Carlson–Stix v2 was checked after following Stix's current publication list. Its newest relative result still assumes sub-p-adic function fields. Neither Qab nor Qsolv embeds in a finitely generated extension of Qp: the algebraic closure of Qp in such an extension is finite over Qp, while these two fields contain every p-power root of unity. The new paper therefore does not settle the intended F2 or supply the full cyclotomic-field theorem F.7. Its bibliography records CHW as published in *Selecta Mathematica* 31 (2025), no. 4, article 65, under the title *A generalization of Voevodsky's reconstruction theorem of schemes from their étale topoi*. The numbered CHW statements in this report refer consistently to the supplied 2024 source archive.

No opened primary source explicitly stated the full F.47 or F.50 in that form. This does not establish novelty or absence from the literature; that bibliographic priority question is left undetermined. The repaired theorem in F.7 is supported by its proof, not by a claim of prior publication.
