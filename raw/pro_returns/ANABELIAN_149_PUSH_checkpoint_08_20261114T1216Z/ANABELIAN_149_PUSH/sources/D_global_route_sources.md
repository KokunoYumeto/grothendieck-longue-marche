# D.20--D.21 and the exact global gap: sources opened

1. Milne, J. S. (2021). *Elliptic curves* (2nd ed.). World Scientific.
   Author's primary PDF https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf,
   opened 2026-11-14, Chapter II, Proposition 5.4 and Corollary 5.7,
   printed p.66. The finite certificate uses only good-reduction
   injectivity on prime-to-residue-characteristic torsion.
2. Betts, L. A., & Stix, J. (2025). Galois sections and p-adic period
   mappings. *Annals of Mathematics, 201*(1), 79--166.
   https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2025.201.1.2 . Publisher record opened;
   authors' primary 29 April 2022 preprint also opened at
   https://www.math.uni-frankfurt.de/~stix/research/preprints/BETTS_STIX-GaloisSectionsPadicPeriods20220429.pdf,
   Definition 1.1 and Theorem A, pp.1--2. The hypothesis is no CM
   subfield. Its finite-local-image conclusion does not assert finite
   support over the base number field. The all-place Selmer condition
   includes real places, which require the real section theorem rather
   than the p-adic D.19.
3. Stix, J. (2015). Birational sections with local conditions.
   Supplied primary TeX opened at
   `background/literature/stix_birational_local_conditions/src_arXiv_1203.3236/STIX-birSCoverQthmAB-arXivv3.tex`:
   section `Finite support`, `thm:stoll`, `cor:stollforsections`,
   `cor:nonconstantunit`; Theorem 2 / `thmB` and section 4.1 distinguish
   density-one integral places from the cofinite adelic condition.

The two counterexamples, the quartic maps, and the V4 idempotent proof
are derived in the manuscript. Exact finite counts are retained in
`audit/D_Fermat_quartic_certificate.json`, generated by
`code/D_Fermat_quartic_certificate.py`. Root independently reviewed
both constructions and the isogeny decomposition before promotion.

## D.22 positive global class

4. Mochizuki, S. (2003). Topics surrounding the anabelian geometry of
   hyperbolic curves. In *Galois groups and fundamental groups*, MSRI
   Publications 41, 119--166. Author's primary September 2002 PDF,
   https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Topics%20Surrounding%20the%20Anabelian%20Geometry%20of%20Hyperbolic%20Curves.pdf,
   opened Section3.5, Corollary3.13 and proof, p26. It gives the full
   profinite real section theorem, including nonproper hyperbolic curves.
5. Stoll's supplied primary TeX, labels `BM0`, `Dom`, `CorMor`,
   `ModCurves`, opened in full: Theorem8.2, Proposition8.5, Theorem8.6,
   Corollary8.8. The positive-genus modular hypothesis is explicit.
   All three pages of the author's 29 March 2017 errata were reopened:
   https://mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/papers/Errata-FiniteDescent-ANT.pdf .
   Section3 repairs the real-place step in Proposition8.5 by using
   Theorem8.2 only at a density-one set of finite places; the statement
   of Proposition8.5 survives. Section1 omits complex factors.
6. Harari--Stix supplied primary TeX,
   `background/literature/harari_stix/src_arXiv_1005.1302/main.tex`,
   Theorem2.1 (`numbers`) and Remark2.2(1), opened in full. The section
   induces compatibility for every finite group-scheme torsor, giving
   the full finite-descent condition needed for Stoll's theorem.

The resulting positive class uses the checked p-adic D.19 and affine
D.6, plus the independently opened real theorem. It does not impose
Betts--Stix's no-CM restriction and does not claim all Fermat or modular
twists satisfy the stated target hypothesis.
