### R27 and the two definitions

CHW Definition 2.19 says that pinned morphisms preserve closed points. Its Notation 2.20 defines the corresponding full subgroupoid of relative morphisms. Specifying the commutation isomorphism over BG_k is already part of being a relative geometric morphism; it does not impose this condition. LM5-F.5 uses the correct definition. Consequently the error is in the prompt's R27 summary, not a discovered error in the intended statement of LM5-F.50.

The explicit splitting G_k -> G_{k(t)} produces a relative geometric morphism Spec(k)_et -> (P1_k)_et with generic image.
