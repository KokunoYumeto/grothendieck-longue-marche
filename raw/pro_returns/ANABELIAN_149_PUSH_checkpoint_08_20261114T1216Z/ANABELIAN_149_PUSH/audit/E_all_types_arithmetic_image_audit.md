# All-types arithmetic-image audit

**Superseded historical audit.** On 14 November 2026, E.13–E.14 completed the formerly missing comparison and framed arithmetic-image characterization. The former E.U2 obligation is removed from the current numbered register. The sections below preserve the earlier searches and failures at the time they were written. Their statements that E2 remains unresolved no longer describe the current return. In addition, AA means **algebraically ample**; the earlier expansion “arithmetically ample” was incorrect. See `E_arithmetic_selection_report.md` and the three complete reviews for the accepted proof.

This audit concerns E2, which remains unresolved. It records a more precise obstruction than the absence of a genus-zero recognition theorem. No claim of priority is made.

## Opened explicit all-types constructions

Nakamura and Schneps (2000), *On a subgroup of the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group acting on the tower of profinite Teichmüller modular groups*, Inventiones Mathematicae 141, 503–560, was opened in the authors' 48-page version (`newGT.pdf`, version 3.14). Theorems1.2–1.4 construct the subgroup Γ, its quilt representations, and compatibility with pants-subsurface inclusions. Sections10–11 give boundary-filling comparisons and explicit Dehn-twist formulas. The introduction identifies the restriction to G_Q with the arithmetic action for compatible rational tangential choices, referring to the earlier arithmetic comparison. These results provide an explicit family of compatible actions; they do not by themselves identify every abstract compatible system with one arising from this family.

Ichikawa (2006), *Teichmüller groupoids and number theory*, RIMS Kôkyûroku1521,1–7, was opened in full. Its three numbered theorems concern the extended Hatcher complex, integral unit-tangent base points, and Galois actions on the elementary moves. The 2002 author review, *Teichmüller groupoids*, RIMS Kôkyûroku1267,48–61, was also opened. Its Section4 describes the A-move by the genus-zero associator and the S-move using the moduli problem with a first-order tangent structure. The latter is not silently replaced by the unextended M_1,1 group. The 2002 article explicitly reviews the then-preprint without all proofs. The full 2003 journal article was located but could not be opened; its stronger assertions are not treated as independently checked here.

Lochak, Nakamura and Schneps (2026), arXiv:2602.12462v1, was opened. Its explicit GT actions on the unpunctured and once-punctured profinite mapping-class groups do not supply the missing all-puncture assertion. A later modular-operad preprint, Basualdo Bonatto and Robertson, arXiv:2606.01466v2, was located; only its abstract and version information were accessible. Its full proof was not opened and is not used. In particular the version-two qualification restricting the stated étale comparison to genera zero and one is retained.

## Exact coupling problem

The opened Hoshi–Mochizuki–Tsujimura Theorem5.17(ii) recognizes the GT-conjugacy class of G_Q through maximal closed arithmetically ample subgroups, with the COF, RGC, ArBC and configuration-space conditions present. Thus E2 is not left open merely because a genus-zero arithmetic subgroup has no criterion.

To turn that theorem into the requested characterization of the given all-types category, one still needs a proved comparison retaining all markings, all covering arrows and all puncture-forgetting operations. In particular, conjugating a genus-zero arithmetic subgroup by an element of GT does not establish that the conjugator extends to the specified all-types category. Nor does the inclusion G_Q⊂Γ prove that every relevant conjugate has a compatible lift. The constructions above do not, without a further argument, establish faithfulness and the required image statement for the restriction from the full category's compatible outer-automorphism systems to the genus-zero group.

We therefore retain E.U2. We have not replaced this missing comparison by a definition referring to the reconstructed field, by an unproved normality assertion for Γ in GT, or by the weaker fact that a family of actions is explicitly constructible. In the Hoshi–Mochizuki–Tsujimura source, the notation C_GT used in the relevant commensurator theorem denotes a commensurator; it is not a centralizer.

## Further special-locus search

Schneps (2006), *Automorphisms of curves and their role in Grothendieck–Teichmüller theory*, Math. Nachrichten279,656–671, was opened in the author's published PDF at https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/automorphisms.pdf. Definition1.2 and Theorem3.1 describe two special-locus relations in genus zero; Corollary3.3 contains the Galois group of the extension generated by roots of unity and all roots of2 and3. Theorem3.4 derives five further relations, including the relation that permits the stated all-genus action. The paper explicitly restricts to cyclotomic component one and the two vanishing Kummer characters. It supplies another explicit sufficient family of compatible actions, without the missing faithfulness/image comparison for every covering arrow and puncture filling. Thus it does not remove the coupling problem above. The search terms included “profinite Teichmuller tower automorphisms covering morphisms Belyi Galois image injective restriction genus zero” and “Grothendieck Teichmuller group all genera action restriction genus zero faithful quilts covers Ichikawa”.

Attempts to open the full October2026 modular-operad revision through arXiv and the MPI repository did not return the full text. Its previously recorded abstract-only status remains unchanged; the older abstract's broader phrasing is not substituted for the revised qualification.

## Subsequent full-text check, 14 November 2026

The earlier access failure was overcome by downloading arXiv:2606.01466v2 directly. Basualdo Bonatto–Robertson's Theorem3.18 and Corollaries4.3–4.4 give determination by genus at most one for their modular operad. Theorem4.9 constructs the Nakamura–Schneps action. Proposition6.14 and Corollary6.23 compare the associated homotopy1-types; Proposition6.22 retains the genus-one étale comparison. These results concern gluing and contraction. They do not identify the requested category with all finite-cover arrows and puncture fillings. Proposition6.9 assumes a homotopy to the identity, so it cannot be used as injectivity of restriction to genus zero. The earlier abstract-only bibliography status is superseded; E.U2 remains unresolved.

A separate attempted reduction through the universal curve would need to distinguish the profinite mapping class group from its image in Out of the profinite surface group. Boggi (2026), Section1.1, formula(1), identifies injectivity of that map as the congruence subgroup problem. TheoremA and CorollaryB address symmetric centralizers under a quotient-genus hypothesis; they do not supply that injectivity in every genus. Thus this proposed route has an additional exact comparison to prove, rather than a licence to replace the two groups.
