## 1. The selected subgroup and framing

The group calculation is exact. Write B for the full profinite GT, G for its standard arithmetic image, and c=(-1,1). The original Lochak–Schneps author PDF was reopened at Proposition 4(ii) and its complete proof on printed p.581. Commuting with c forces inversion invariance of f, Proposition 4(i) makes f trivial, and the hexagon then gives lambda=±1. Thus C_B(c)=<c>. This is the full profinite group, not a pro-ell or unipotent substitute.

If H=bGb^{-1} contains c, then b^{-1}cb is a nonidentity involution of G. Artin–Schreier and the unique ordering of Q imply b^{-1}cb=g c g^{-1} for some g in G. Hence bg centralizes c and belongs to G, so b belongs to G and H=G. HMT Definition 5.12 and Theorem 5.17(ii), read in their full form including the second-configuration condition, identify the maximal closed AA subgroups with exactly these conjugates. Thus c selects the actual standard arithmetic subgroup. No conjugator has to lift to an all-types automorphism.

The marking qualification is necessary and present. The standard real/tangential marking specifies c. Changing that GT framing transports c and the selected subgroup together. The criterion does not pretend that an unframed conjugacy class has a canonical representative. The complete genus-zero system condition is also essential: equality only at one free group would not supply E.13's stated input.

## 2. Braid automorphisms really descend to all sphere quotients

The exact full profinite braid formula in E.14.5 agrees with Lochak–Schneps (1994), equation(5), and Proposition10. Symmetry of f turns their f(sigma_i²,y_i) into f(y_i,sigma_i²)^{-1}. Their multiplication convention is equivalent to E.14.3 by conjugating the evaluation of the second word by f; there is no inversion or opposite-action mismatch.

Here is an explicit all-n descent argument. Let omega_n=y_1...y_n be the full twist. The profinite centre is the procyclic group generated by omega_n for n≥3. One can obtain this directly from the pure-braid decomposition Khat_n≅Khat(0,n+1)×<omega_n>, the centre-free pure genus-zero groups proved in E.13, and the centre-free permutation group S_n. The n=2 case is immediate from the displayed formula. The automorphism multiplies the abelianization Bhat_n^ab=Zhat by lambda, while omega_n has image n(n−1). Consequently phi_n(omega_n)=omega_n^lambda: multiplication by the nonzero integer n(n−1) is injective on Zhat.

Compatibility with the standard Bhat_(n−1) inclusion gives phi_n(omega_(n−1))=omega_(n−1)^lambda. As y_n=omega_n omega_(n−1)^{-1} and these factors commute, phi_n(y_n)=y_n^lambda. The closed normal subgroup generated by y_n and omega_n is therefore preserved; the inverse GT element gives equality rather than mere inclusion. The formula descends to the entire sphere mapping-class quotient Bhat_n/<<y_n,omega_n>>. This uses no congruence subgroup assertion.

## 3. Labels, colours, relabelling and deletion

The permutation quotient is fixed pointwise. The conjugating word in the displayed formula lies in the pure braid subgroup, and lambda is odd because it belongs to Zhat^×. Therefore the image of every sigma_i in S_n remains the same transposition. Every inverse image of a colour-preserving permutation subgroup is preserved, including the fully labelled subgroup. Finite-index completion agrees with its closure, so this gives the required full profinite coloured groups, not just their dense discrete subgroups.

For deletion of the last distinguished puncture, the kernel on pure braids is normally generated by the x_(i,n). Every x_(i,j) is carried to a pure conjugate of x_(i,j)^lambda. Indeed, write x_(i,j)=a^{-1}x_(1,2)a. The conjugator a^{-1}phi(a) is pure because phi fixes the permutation quotient. Thus the deletion kernel is preserved. The induced automorphism on the remaining strands agrees with the lower-level formula by the standard inclusion compatibility. This also handles the corresponding coloured subgroups on which the deleted puncture is distinguished. Other specified deletions follow by relabelling; forgetting labels is simply inclusion of the appropriate permutation inverse-image subgroup.

Relabelling need only commute in the outer sense stated in E.14. If beta implements a relabelling, then phi(beta)beta^{-1} is pure. The discrepancy between applying phi before or after that relabelling is therefore an inner automorphism of the target coloured subgroup. This gives the required outer compatibility without falsely claiming literal commutation for a fixed nonpure braid representative.

## 4. Exact arithmetic compatibility: an opened all-n primary formula

A short primary backstop is Hiroaki Nakamura, *On arithmetic monodromy representations of Eisenstein type in fundamental groups of once punctured elliptic curves*, RIMS preprint1691, **February2010**, §5.4, printed p.28 (PDF page29), equation(5.4.2). The complete passage and its surrounding construction were opened at <https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/preprint/file/RIMS1691.pdf>.

It constructs the Ihara–Matsumoto standard tangential basepoint b_n represented by the ordered roots (0,t^(n−1),...,t²,t), identifies the geometric fundamental group of the monic-discriminant complement with Bhat_n, and gives an arithmetic splitting G_Q⋉Bhat_n. Equation(5.4.2) states the Drinfeld action for every n in terms of the standard (chi(sigma),f_sigma). This is an actual full profinite arithmetic action, not an inference from a Lie algebra or a pro-ell result.

Nakamura uses omega_i in the first input of f for i≥3, whereas E.14.5 uses y_i. These give exactly the same formula. We have omega_i=omega_(i−1)y_i; omega_(i−1) commutes with y_i and with sigma_i. A derived profinite word f ignores a central factor in either input, because the corresponding exponent sum is zero. The identical central-factor observation is explicitly explained in Lochak–Schneps (1994), §3, printed p.13. Thus no change of GT coordinates is required.

The configuration-to-sphere-moduli map is defined over Q, so quotienting the arithmetic braid action gives the arithmetic genus-zero outer action. The ordered and coloured configuration spaces are its finite étale variants defined by the same permutation subgroups; hence the restrictions agree on every labelled/coloured genus-zero group. This supplies the exact necessity step in E.14. An alternative complete proof through Harbater–Schneps deletion rigidity, independently being checked by degeneration, may be retained instead or alongside this formula.

## 5. Applying E.13 and scope

For an arbitrary outer family, condition(i) really requires a coherent type-preserving autoequivalence of the completed labelled/coloured cover-and-filling category, preserving the stated distinguished structure. This is stronger than asserting unrelated group automorphisms at each type, and is the actual hypothesis needed by E.13.

Once condition(ii) supplies equality with the complete genus-zero system of b in G_c, the subgroup selection gives a genuine sigma in G_Q. Its arithmetic coherent autoequivalence exists by the Q-defined algebraic presentation in E.8. The composite with the proposed coherent autoequivalence has trivial genus-zero outer system, so E.13 gives trivial outer action in all types. The conclusion is about the supplied outer-action family. It does not identify all coherent autoequivalences with those outer actions, and the text does not make that stronger claim.

Uniqueness is correct: an arithmetic element acting inner on the labelled (0,4) group fixes every connected finite tripod cover up to isomorphism. Belyi presentations of all algebraic elliptic curves then force all algebraic j-invariants to be fixed, hence the Galois element is the identity.

The criterion is a mathematical group-theoretic characterization, with explicit framing and infinite conditions. It is not an algorithmic decidability assertion, a proof GT=G_Q, or a claim that every arbitrary genus-zero system is in the standard GT image. With these scopes, no remaining gap was found.

## Uniqueness scope correction after the parent's final check

The initially specified family a may contain only the unlabelled full mapping-class groups. Therefore the last paragraph of Section5 should not assume that equality of two such families already gives equality on the labelled (0,4) group. The parent's repair is valid and is the precise argument to use. Suppose an arithmetic element acts inner on the full profinite (0,5) mapping-class group. Its restriction to the pure group has outer class equal to the permutation pi of that inner conjugator. The arithmetic outer action on the pure group commutes with the S5 relabelling action, since relabelling is defined over Q. The S5 image in Out of the pure group is faithful by E.13's rational-homology calculation. Hence pi is central in S5 and must be the identity. The full-group conjugator is consequently pure, so the arithmetic outer action on the pure group is inner. Forgetting one labelled puncture gives trivial arithmetic outer action on the labelled (0,4) group, and the Belyi argument then makes the Galois element trivial. This proves uniqueness for the actually supplied unlabelled family without assuming extra labelled data in that family; the coherent extension is used for the comparison criterion itself.
