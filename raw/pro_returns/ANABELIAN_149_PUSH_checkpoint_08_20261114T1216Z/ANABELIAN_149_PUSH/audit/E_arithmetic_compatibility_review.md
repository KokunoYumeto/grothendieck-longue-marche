## Derivation for all labels and colours

Natural arithmetic actions preserve each boundary collision inertia by the cyclotomic character and commute with all Q-defined label permutations. They therefore belong to the same quasi-special symmetric groups as the standard formula action. The two actions agree at n=4 by the normalized arithmetic embedding. Successive injective deletion maps force equality at every pure level.

For n≥5, the centralizer of the pure group inside the full sphere mapping-class completion is trivial. Its image in S_n acts trivially on pure rational first homology, which forces that permutation to be trivial by E.13; its remaining pure part is central and hence trivial. If two full-group automorphisms agree outerly on the pure group, adjust by a pure inner automorphism to make them agree exactly there. For every full-group element h their discrepancy h^-1 alpha(h) centralizes the pure group, so vanishes. This proves equality on the full group and every colour subgroup at once, because the inner adjustment lies in every colour subgroup.

The arithmetic representatives may be based at an ordered rational configuration, so all colour subgroups are preserved. For n=3 or4, add distinguished points to reach n≥5 and descend through a surjective forgetting map. This avoids an incorrect centrelessness claim in the exceptional four-punctured full group.

The standard deletion compatibility itself is checked on the last-strand stabilizer in the Artin group: the kernel is normally generated by the pure meridians involving that strand; quasi-speciality preserves it, and the remaining braid generators have the lower-rank formula. Sphere quotient and relabelling then give all specified deletions.

## Uniqueness repair for a family of unlabelled groups

One cannot simply read a labelled n=4 action from equality of unlabelled outer families. The revised proof uses the full n=5 group. An inner arithmetic action on that group restricts on the pure subgroup to the outer action of a permutation pi∈S5. Arithmetic commutation with S5 and its faithful outer action imply pi∈Z(S5)=1. The arithmetic pure action is therefore trivial, hence so is its deletion to n=4, where Belyi faithfulness applies. This proves uniqueness for the literal unlabelled input family as well.
