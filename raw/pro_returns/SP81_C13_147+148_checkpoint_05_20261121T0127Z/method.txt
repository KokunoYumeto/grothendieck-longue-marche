SP81_C13_147+148 — reading and evidence conventions

The page fragments follow the supplied scan order. The combined transcription is the exact ordered join of those fragments and has no preamble. Use the PREAMBLE.tex supplied with the source witnesses.

Witness C and witness H are draft readings. Every accepted source reading was checked against the supplied images. No OCR, external mathematical research, or TeX compilation is used in this run.

In the collation table, their_page is the numerical H page: the supplied task specifies our_scan = their_page + 1. The H filename is hua_<cote>_scan<NNN>.tex in the supplied texts folder. A missing H page has no collation rows.

Change rows identify physical loci. A locus can differ from both witnesses. Differences of reading, source punctuation, abbreviation, correction state, placement, and depicted geometry are recorded; macro syntax alone is excluded. Adding or removing a doubt wrapper without a change of reading is recorded in the doubts/decisions evidence and does not by itself count as a changed C reading.

Crops are enlarged from recorded boxes in original-image pixels. The original scans, supplied witnesses, and reproducible full-resolution halves are excluded from the deliverable. Saved crop files remain included. The source date and manuscript/archival numerals are transcribed without converting them to modern numbering.

The neutral second-reading box lists and first readings are written before the blind reading. The blind reader receives the source image, box list, and generic transcription conventions. Token comparisons and the main reader's crop adjudications are retained in second/. A token difference may concern layout syntax or uncertainty notation; its adjudication explains whether the page changed.

The notes attribute statements to the manuscript. CHECK_EVENTS.tsv records only mathematical checking actually performed; reading or paraphrasing a claim does not certify it.

The progress log records page completion, blind audits, periodic checkpoint saves, and the final frozen hashes. Scripts document the mechanical assembly and static completeness checks; they do not select manuscript readings.

First-reading TSVs were hashed at the start of the blind phase and remain historical records. Each comparison also records the exact hashes of its saved first and second TSVs. Filesystem modification times are retained as observed; subagent filesystem timestamps can differ from the root UTC clock. Operational order is established by saving the second TSV before the root reads and compares it, not by reconciling those clocks.
