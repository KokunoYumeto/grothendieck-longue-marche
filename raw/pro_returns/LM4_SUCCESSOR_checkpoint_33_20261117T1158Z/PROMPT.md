## Where this comes from

In §§12–31 Grothendieck studies a finite group acting on a surface, or exteriorly on its fundamental group with its loop subgroups, discrete or profinite, and asks which profinite actions come from discrete ones (scan 353; scans 391–394). LM4 answered most of it: for finite groups and discrete or pro-`p` groups his expectations hold after one correction each; for the full profinite completion, lifts and finite subgroups behave (LM4 D.2, D.3) and recognition fails (LM4 D.29, D.30). After twenty hours three rows were open and stayed open for the last eighty rows: C.12, D.12, F.7. This prompt asks for the open rows with those tools.

## Setting and notation (used in every Part)

- `F` is the free profinite group on `x, y`, with `z = (xy)⁻¹`; `F^(p)` its maximal pro-`p` quotient. `𝒫` (LM4's `P`) is the group of outer automorphisms of `F` that send each of `x, y, z` to a conjugate of a power; the three exponents agree and give the multiplier `λ ∈ Ẑ^×`. `GT̂₀ ⊂ 𝒫` is the centralizer of the geometric `𝔖₃` (Harbater–Schneps), with elements `(λ, f)`, `f ∈ F′`; `GT̂ ⊂ GT̂₀` is cut out by the pentagon. `ℋ` is the multiplier-one subgroup of `GT̂₀`. `c = (−1, 1)` is complex conjugation.
- `R_nil(G)` is the intersection of the kernels of the continuous homomorphisms of `G` to finite nilpotent groups; `O^p(U)` is the kernel of `U → U^(p)`. For an open characteristic `U ⊂ F`, the level `U` is the group `Out^♯(F/R_nil(U))` of outer classes that send the three boundary elements to conjugates of powers; `F/R_nil(U)` is the fibre product over `F/U` of the groups `F/O^p(U)`.
- `B = C₂ * C₃ = ⟨s, r⟩ = PSL₂(Z)`, `τ = sr`, `B̂` its completion; `F` is identified with the completion of the level-two subgroup as in LM7_PUSH. For `Φ ∈ ℋ` normalized by `Φ(τ) = τ`, the frames are the unique `β_Φ, A_Φ ∈ F` with `Φ(s) = β_Φ s β_Φ⁻¹`, `Φ(r) = A_Φ r A_Φ⁻¹`. `Δ : GT̂₀ → F₂` is the character of LM7_PUSH E.65, read from the classes of the two frames modulo 12.
- `K = Γ(5)/±1 ⊂ B`, free of rank eleven, the group of the sphere punctured at the twelve vertices of the icosahedron, with `B/K = A₅`. `Γ = Γ_{0,[12]}` is the mapping class group of the sphere with twelve unordered punctures, `Γ̂` its profinite completion. `𝒦 = {Φ ∈ ℋ : Φ restricted to K̂ lies in Γ̂}`.
