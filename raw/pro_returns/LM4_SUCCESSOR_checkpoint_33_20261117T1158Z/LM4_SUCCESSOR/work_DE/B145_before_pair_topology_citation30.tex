\subsection*{The actual center line and its whole cyclic square filling}

\statement{B.145}{Proved}{The actual center kernel admits an exact cyclic-preimage retraction and two-generator square normalization, with an explicit ambient discontinuity}
\label{stmt:B145}
Use the actual regional neck retraction $r_{\rm reg}:R^0\to K$
and the two-region group $V=R_L*_KR_R$ of B.141. Put
$V_4=f^{-1}Bf$ and $h=fk$. There is an explicit auxiliary free
section $T_h$ and an entire free coset basis for the center kernel.
For the actual marked element $q=\eta(afa^{-1})^{-1}$, the subgroup
$r_{\rm reg}^{-1}(\langle q\rangle)$ retracts, ordinarily, onto the
actual free pair $\langle k,q\rangle$. The specified retraction
is not continuous for the topology induced by the full regional
completion.

The entire cyclic orbit of the first mixed square has the exact
normal form in \eqref{eq:B145-square-normal}. Its explicit two-sided
recursion also eliminates the corresponding center symbols in the
complete ordinary presentation, provided every remaining graph edge,
every $K$-translate and both mirror families are carried along.
No ambient filling-survival or compact filling assertion follows.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.145.1. The complete center coset basis.}
The actual B.117 color retraction onto $L=\langle K,k\rangle$
fixes $K$ and $k$. Its exact presentation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-L}
 L=\langle h,k,a,d,e,V_4\mid
 [h^2,k],[k,a],[k,d],[k,e],[k,V_4]\rangle.
\end{equation}
Indeed $f=hk^{-1}$ and
$B=fV_4f^{-1}=hV_4h^{-1}$, using $[k,V_4]=1$; these substitutions
are inverse to the B.117 presentation. Define
\[
 T_h=F(h,a,d,e,V_4),\qquad Q_0=\langle h^2,a,d,e,V_4\rangle\leq T_h.
\]
There is an exact isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-coset-basis}
 L=F(X_{gQ_0}:gQ_0\in T_h/Q_0)\rtimes T_h,
 \qquad k=X_{Q_0},\quad tX_{gQ_0}t^{-1}=X_{tgQ_0}.
\end{equation}
For one map send $X_{gQ_0}$ to $gkg^{-1}$; changing a representative
by $Q_0$ has no effect because all its generators commute with $k$.
For the inverse send $k$ to the root basis letter and $T_h$ to its
permutation section. Both maps respect every displayed relation
and are inverse on all generators. Thus this is an entire free
basis, not a list of linearly independent sample columns.

The map $N_{\rm reg}\to F(T_h/Q_0)$ is the actual coordinate
retraction deleting all expanded families in B.141. It is equivariant
under the actual $K$ action because it extends to the color
retraction on $R^0$. The auxiliary section is identified abstractly
with $K$ by $h\mapsto f$, $V_4\mapsto f^{-1}Bf$, fixing $a,d,e$;
$h$ and $f$ are not equated in the actual region.

\emph{B.145.2. The exact zero-correction conjugator.}
Put $D=ded^{-1}$ and use the literal B.141 reflection words
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-W}
\begin{split}
 w&=afV_4f^{-1}a^{-1}fDf^{-1}d^{-1},\\
 \ell&=afa^{-1},\qquad \eta(\ell)=wf^{-1}w^{-1},\\
 W&=ahV_4h^{-1}a^{-1}hDh^{-1}d^{-1}.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Both $V_4$ and $D$ commute with $k$. Substituting $f=hk^{-1}$
therefore proves the actual equality $w=W\in T_h$, without a
suppressed center correction. Consequently
\[
 q=\eta(\ell)^{-1}=WfW^{-1},\qquad r=WhW^{-1}\in T_h.
\]
Set $Y_0=X_{WQ_0}$ and $Y_1=X_{WhQ_0}$. In
\eqref{eq:B145-coset-basis} the exact equations are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-q}
 q=Y_1^{-1}r,\qquad r(Y_0)=Y_1,\qquad r(Y_1)=Y_0.
\end{equation}
The last equation uses $h^2\in Q_0$; the two letters are distinct
because $h\notin Q_0$.

\emph{B.145.3. All pivots and both complementary correction letters.}
For every integer $n$ put $Z_n=X_{r^nQ_0}$. These are distinct
basis letters, and none equals $Y_0$ or $Y_1$. Here is a uniform
coset proof. The folded core for $Q_0$ has two vertices on its
$h$-cycle, with $a,d,e,V_4$ loops only at the root. Its completion
to the Schreier cover attaches trees at the missing edges. The
reduced word $W$ begins $ahV_4$, leaving the core at the $V_4$
edge from the nonroot vertex, and ends $d^{-1}$. For $n\ne0$,
$Wh^nW^{-1}$ is reduced at both displayed junctions, so its
initial exit cannot return along a reduced path in the attached
tree. Hence $r^n\notin Q_0$. The same argument gives
$Wh^s\notin Q_0$ for every integer $s$. An equality
$r^nQ_0=Wh^jQ_0$, $j=0,1$, would give
$Wh^{j-n}\in Q_0$, again impossible. This proves the assertions
for all integers.

Write $q^n=\nu_n r^n$. For $n>0$, $\nu_n$ is the alternating
$n$-letter word $Y_1^{-1}Y_0^{-1}Y_1^{-1}\cdots$; for $n<0$
it is the alternating $(-n)$-letter word $Y_0Y_1Y_0\cdots$;
$\nu_0=1$. Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-u}
 u_n=q^nkq^{-n}=\nu_nZ_n\nu_n^{-1}\qquad(n\in\mathbb Z).
\end{equation}
Conjugate each pivot $Z_n$ by its $\nu_n$, fixing every other
center basis letter. This is an automorphism of the free center
kernel: its inverse conjugates each pivot by $\nu_n^{-1}$ because
both correction letters are fixed. Every image is a finite word,
and both composites fix every generator, so infinite rank causes
no limiting issue. In particular $F(u_n:n\in\mathbb Z)$ is an
actual free factor of the center kernel.

\emph{B.145.4. The ordinary retraction and its precise failure of continuity.}
Define on the center free basis
\[
 \pi(Z_n)=u_n,\qquad
 \pi(X_z)=1\quad\text{if }z\notin\{r^nQ_0:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]
It fixes every $u_n$ and is $q$-equivariant. Indeed $q$ acts by
$X_z\mapsto Y_1^{-1}X_{rz}Y_1$; the pivot orbit and its complement
are $r$-invariant, $\pi(Y_1)=1$, and $qu_nq^{-1}=u_{n+1}$.
Composing with the actual family deletion, which fixes $q\in K$,
gives a $q$-equivariant map on $N_{\rm reg}$. Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-retract}
 r_{\rm reg}^{-1}(\langle q\rangle)
       =N_{\rm reg}\rtimes\langle q\rangle
       \longrightarrow\langle k,q\rangle
\end{equation}
is an ordinary retraction fixing $k,q$. Its target is free on that
pair: its kernel over $\langle q\rangle$ has the free shift basis
$u_n$, equivalently the standard free-product presentation.
No extension over the rest of $K$ is asserted.

This specified map is not continuous for the induced full regional
topology. Let $g_m=ha^{m!}h^{-1}$, $m\geq1$. For any nonzero
integer $j$ and any integer $n$, the element
$r^{-n}ha^jh^{-1}$ is outside $Q_0$. For $n=0$ the core exit
occurs at $a$ from its nonroot vertex; otherwise the reduced initial
$W$ gives the same exit as above. Hence all
$X_{g_mQ_0}=g_mkg_m^{-1}$ are complementary letters killed by
$\pi$. But $g_m\to1$ in the full profinite topology and therefore
$X_{g_mQ_0}\to k$. The element $k$ is nontrivial in the regional
completion, detected by the actual base-winding character. A
continuous extension would send this sequence to both $1$ and
$k$, a contradiction. This excludes this projection only. It does
not contradict the separately established full topology of the
actual pair, and it does not exclude every possible continuous
retraction.

\emph{B.145.5. The entire cyclic square orbit.}
In $\ker(V\to K)=N_L*N_R$, let $\alpha=\operatorname{Ad}(q)$.
It preserves both factors. Denote the marked map of B.141 by
$\mathfrak d$, where
$\mathfrak d(u)=p(aC(a^{-1}\eta(u)a)Ca^{-1})$.
Write the actual element
$\gamma=\mathfrak d(f)=c_0q$ with $c_0\in N_R$, and put
$c_n=\alpha^n(c_0)$. The correction $c_0$ is not a cone involution.
The preceding retraction on the left and the identity on the right
show that
\[
 \mathscr D=(F(u_n:n\in\mathbb Z)*N_R)\rtimes\langle q\rangle
\]
is an actual ordinary subgroup and retract of
$(N_L*N_R)\rtimes\langle q\rangle$, before filling.
The complete $q^{\mathbb Z}$ orbit of the mixed square is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-square-recurrence}
 u_n=(c_nu_{n+1}c_{n+1})u_{n+2}
                  (c_nu_{n+1}c_{n+1})^{-1}
                  \qquad(n\in\mathbb Z).
\end{equation}
Its kernel quotient is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-square-normal}
 \frac{F(u_n:n\in\mathbb Z)*N_R}
 {\langle\!\langle\eqref{eq:B145-square-recurrence}
                  \rangle\!\rangle}
       \simeq N_R*F(u_0,u_1).
\end{equation}
For a direct inverse, start with $u_0,u_1$ and recursively define
\[
 u_{n+2}=c_{n+1}^{-1}u_{n+1}^{-1}c_n^{-1}u_n
                      c_nu_{n+1}c_{n+1}\quad(n\geq0).
\]
Use \eqref{eq:B145-square-recurrence} itself successively for all
negative $n$. These finite recursions define a map killing every
relation. The reverse map includes $N_R,u_0,u_1$, and the two
composites fix every generator by the same recursions.
The induced shift is the explicit automorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B145-beta}
\begin{aligned}
 \beta|_{N_R}&=\alpha,& \beta(u_0)&=u_1,\\
 \beta(u_1)&=c_1^{-1}u_1^{-1}c_0^{-1}u_0c_0u_1c_1,\\
 \beta^{-1}|_{N_R}&=\alpha^{-1},&\beta^{-1}(u_1)&=u_0,\\
 \beta^{-1}(u_0)&=(c_{-1}u_0c_0)u_1(c_{-1}u_0c_0)^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Direct substitution checks both inverses. Thus the square-only
cyclic quotient is the displayed free-product group semidirect
$\mathbb Z$. This does not include other retained translates or
either complete leaf family.

\emph{B.145.6. Exact use inside the complete presentation.}
In the full B.141 presentation substitute
$Z_n=\nu_n^{-1}u_n\nu_n$, keeping every other center and every
family generator. Rewrite every original graph relator by this
substitution; no unchanged graph-edge assertion is made. Retain
all actual $K$-translates of all five first-family relations and
all five $\theta$-relations. The subset
\eqref{eq:B145-square-recurrence} is among them, so the explicit
two-sided recursions eliminate every $u_n$, $n\ne0,1$, while
substituting its word into every other relation. Both mirror
families, all leaf types, every other retained translate and every
regional graph edge remain. The inverse-presentation argument
above justifies this countable elimination without a stabilization
assumption. It supplies no ambient normal-closure restriction,
$\kappa$-kernel symmetry or fixed-source equality.

\end{proof}
