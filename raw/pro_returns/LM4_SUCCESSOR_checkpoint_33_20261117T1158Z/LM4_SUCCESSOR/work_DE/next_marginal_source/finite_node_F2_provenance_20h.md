# Actual node-face involution in the finite model

This supplements B104's geometric construction; it does not choose an arbitrary lift of a one-chain matrix. Let r_nu be one of the 128 actual transported node relators in the even-base model, with its actual orientation, and let mu be its physical F-mate in the same sheet.

## 1. Why the physical mate acts on one node cell

The involution fixes q. It therefore carries the chosen critical disk and its base whisker to that same disk and base whisker. Over the regular whisker the two fiber transports are compared in the actual punctured-sphere bundle over an interval. This comparison occurs before the critical disk is attached and inside the regular bundle, so no other node disk is crossed. The corresponding local normal-crossing block has the symmetric torus model used in B102. Its two coordinates are exchanged, which reverses its oriented two-cell. Transporting this actual local map gives a single physical-mate cell, with the based conjugator determined by the regular-fiber comparison. This is the geometric reason for monomiality; it is not inferred from equality of one-chain boundaries.

The even old-base sheet has its actual returned source section, and the same argument applies to the opposite sheet. When the formal cell orientation is not A-first, the two stored orientation signs must be retained. Thus the literal identity is F(r_nu)=g_nu r_mu^(epsilon_nu) g_nu^-1, where epsilon_nu is the actual face orientation sign, and its cellular map is epsilon_nu times the G image of g_nu times the mate cell.

## 2. Why the coefficient is unambiguous in G

Each r_nu is an automorphic conjugate of the local free-basis commutator [x_i,x_j]. This is not a proper power: its cyclically reduced four-letter word is not periodic, and automorphisms and conjugations preserve the property of being a proper power. The centralizer in the free generic-fiber group is therefore generated by r_nu.

Consequently any two literal conjugators giving the same oriented physical-mate word identity differ by a power of r_mu. Every node relator maps to the identity in G. The two conjugators thus have exactly the same G coefficient. An exact free-word conjugacy solver recovers the geometric coefficient once the physical mate and orientation are fixed; it is not free to choose a different two-cell map.

Applying F twice gives a conjugator centralizing r_nu. The same argument shows its G image is1. Hence the signed semilinear two-cell action squares literally to the identity. Its compatibility with the full21 Fox boundary is the conjugate-relator Fox identity and is independently testable on every coefficient. Together with B104's 231 genuine graph-cylinder face maps, this supplies all359 actual two-cell maps.

## 3. Scope

This proof uses the actual same-q regular-whisker comparison and the actual local torus symmetry. A mere equation F(d c)=d(F c) without this geometric input would permit adding a syzygy and would not determine the action on H2. No such arbitrary lifting argument is used. The complete matrix and its involution still do not give a relation-kernel basis, a value of kappa, or a fixed-lifting theorem.
