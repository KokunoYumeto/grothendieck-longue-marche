# Exact boundary of the filling-only route

The checked13-critical-value partial group (special node, four b triples,
eight retained branch half twists) is an HNN extension of H=F19 between
two rank17 subgroups. Their common rank9 part is generated by c and the
four normalized peripheral pairs. The eight further edge generators are
the transported retained half-twist domain/range words. The edge subgroups
are distinct: their exact folded graphs have13/39 vertices, and each
misses x3*x9*x3^-1. Adding the two zero generators does not identify them.
The preliminary attempt to infer equality from rank17 was rejected; all
eight half twists were recomputed with their local prefixes absorbed.

Every one of the eight U/zero node relators reduces into H. For each of
the eight other triples, its actual local circle is a positive standard
three-point full twist in the recorded local marking. Let m1,m2,m3 be
the three transported meridians and P=m1*m2*m3. Any two of the three
commutators[P,mj] normally generate the local variation relations.
All24 of these local commutators cyclically Britton-reduce into H.
Thus choosing two per triple gives16 H relators, in addition to8 node
relators. Some of the full20-generator variation equations retain b
after linear reduction; those do not contradict this smaller normal
generating set. Every cyclic rotation and pinch is explicitly recorded.

Consequently the group obtained by imposing the29 untransformed filled
monodromies is exactly the QUOTIENT of the partial HNN extension by these

embed: kernel compatibility after quotienting has not been established.
Adding the two actual retained-base action families gives the total
presentation with22 generators and81 listed relations. The Euler count
1-22+81=60 matches the original free7-by-free11 bundle, but this numerical
agreement is only a consistency check.

This boundary is sharp. The single prescribed tuple

    b=(34), x9=(12), x7=(13), all other special generators1

{1,2,3} and b(x7*x9*x7^-1)b^-1 maps to(24). Thus those relations alone
cannot prove even finite-quotient source-kernel normality. The tuple
does not extend to the actual total group: base1 and its inverse violate
the translated special node and fourth b triple at depth1. Base2 fixes
the tuple. The translated-node value is a3-cycle, whose normal closure
in S4 is A4, so any compatible factor of this specific finite head

being inferred or pursued from this prescribed test.
