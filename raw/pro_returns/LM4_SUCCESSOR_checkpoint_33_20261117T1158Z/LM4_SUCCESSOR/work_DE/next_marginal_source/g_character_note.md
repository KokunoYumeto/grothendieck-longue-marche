# The extra character and its actual special fiber

The regular map on the normalized ordered surface

    g=(v-1)(w-1)/(vw-1)=z/(z+t)

takes values in C*. On the generic t-fiber it sends the two merging
meridians a,b to +1,-1 respectively and every other fiber meridian to0.
The special source t=0 lies in g=1. This proves that the missing retained
direction persists in abelianization; it does not prove that the whole
source image equals ker(g_*).

Indeed, solving the g-fiber gives

    w=(g+1-v)/(1+(g-1)v).

A generic compactified v-fiber has22 punctures, hence free rank21:
v=0,mu5,infinity; w=0 and the four nontrivial mu5 values; the w-pole;
the second diagonal point; and the eight points vw=q for q!=1.
The q=1 equation is always(v-1)^2=0 and contributes no additional point.
The second diagonal point is -(g+1)/(g-1), while the w-pole is
-1/(g-1). At g=1 these two punctures coalesce with v=infinity.
The actual special source therefore has20 punctures and free rank19.
This is another retained-only special degeneration, now at infinity.
Shimada's generic-fiber theorem does not imply source surjectivity at g=1.

The map is a submersion on the open surface: its partial derivatives are
(w-1)^2/(vw-1)^2 and(v-1)^2/(vw-1)^2, both nonzero there. Nonproper
fiber degeneration, rather than a critical point in the open surface,
is the issue. Root and DE independently derived this same obstruction.
This note is a structural observation only; no new formal statement or
completed-kernel equality is inferred.
