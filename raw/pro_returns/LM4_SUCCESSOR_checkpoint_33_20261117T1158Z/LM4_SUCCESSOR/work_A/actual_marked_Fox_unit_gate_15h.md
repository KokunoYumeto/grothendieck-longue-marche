# The actual marked generation gate as one Fox unit

Research note, 2026-11-16, after A82–A84. This is an exact reduction for the actual canonical word. It is not a new generation theorem or a claim of a counterexample to the main problem.

Let F be the full free profinite group on x,y, let alpha be the actual pure negative involution, and write

    alpha(x)=x^(-1), alpha(y)=f^(-1)y^(-1)f,
    alpha(f)=f^(-1), u=f alpha(u).

The last equation is the actual R1 canonical-word equation. Put w=u^(-1)yu and let psi send x to x and y to w. Then alpha psi=psi c, where c inverts x,y.

Fix a prime p and work in Lambda=Z_p[[F]]. The continuous left Fox derivatives are specified by

    g-1 = g_x (x-1) + g_y (y-1),
    (gh)_i = g_i + g h_i.

These formulas use the actual full-group augmentation ideal, free on x-1,y-1; they are not maximal-pro-p derivatives. Put

    A=1+(y-1)f_y,
    B=1+(y-1)u_y,
    D=u^(-1)B.

## 1. Exact single-element criterion

The derivative of w is

    w_i = u^(-1)(delta_(i,y)+(y-1)u_i).

The left ideal generated by x-1 and w-1 has, relative to the free augmentation basis, a triangular generator matrix with diagonal entries 1 and D. Its cokernel inside I_F is the reduced coset module

    ker(Z_p[[F/H]] -> Z_p),  H=closure<x,w>.

Consequently the following are equivalent:

1. psi is onto F (and hence is an automorphism by finite generation/Hopfianity);
2. H=F;
3. B, or equivalently D, is a unit of Lambda.

For the matrix assertion, choose row coordinates so a left-linear endomorphism acts by right multiplication of its coefficient matrix. Surjectivity gives a left inverse for the lower diagonal entry. Every finite ring quotient of Lambda is finite and therefore directly finite; taking the inverse-limit inverse gives a genuine two-sided unit. Conversely a unit D makes the triangular map invertible. The reduced coset module vanishes only for H=F, since a proper closed subgroup is contained in a proper open subgroup and has a nonzero reduced finite coset module.

This shows that even one coefficient prime detects full group generation when the entire completed group ring is retained. Restricting the head category changes the criterion and is not justified by local generation alone.

## 2. The exact involution equation for this element

Since alpha is an automorphism and fixes x up to inversion, its Fox matrix is triangular with y-diagonal

    alpha(y)_y = -f^(-1)y^(-1)A.

Thus A is a unit in the same full completed group ring. Differentiate the actual equality alpha(w)=w^(-1) in the y-coordinate:

    alpha(D) [-f^(-1)y^(-1)A] = -w^(-1)D.

The canonical equation gives alpha(u)=f^(-1)u, hence alpha(D)=u^(-1)f alpha(B). After cancellation the result is

    B = (yf) alpha(B) (yf)^(-1) A.                 (1)

Let theta=Inn(yf) alpha on Lambda. Since (yf) alpha(yf)=1, theta is an involution. Therefore

    B=theta(B)A,     theta(A)A=1.                (2)

The second equality also follows from the y-diagonal of alpha squared. It is an identity of actual continuous ring automorphisms and actual canonical-word derivatives. It does not say B theta(B)=1. In particular it is not an invertibility proof for B.

Even an independently supplied unit solution B0 to the same equation would only make B B0^(-1) theta-fixed. Such a fixed element with augmentation one need not be a unit over a full completed group ring. This elementary warning already occurs for inversion on F=Zhat over F2: 1+x+x^(-1) has augmentation one and is invariant, but is singular on the nontrivial C3 block. This example is outside the actual rank-two canonical-word claim and is not a proposed counterexample to it.

The exact finite-ring certificate is `code/check_Fox_fixed_nonunit.py`, with genuine executed output `code/check_Fox_fixed_nonunit.out`. In F2[C3] it verifies inversion invariance, augmentation one, and the explicit nonzero annihilator `(1+x+x^2)(1+x)=0`; it also checks that this element is idempotent. This is one ring calculation, not a finite-group census.

## 3. What the current actual data do and do not supply

A82 supplies a free target for the marked incidence map. A84 supplies the correct orientation-twisted projective duality. Neither statement supplies a unit factorization of the particular element B in (1). Local pro-p and the established relative finite-head results show that particular specializations of B are units. The remaining full assertion is invertibility in all finite heads simultaneously.

The actual pants boundary form cannot be applied to psi as a map of the specified pairs until its third peripheral product has been identified with the actual third cusp. That is the original missing marking condition, not a consequence of equation (1). No third-cusp relation is introduced in this reduction.

## 4. Bounded recognition literature check

The primary March2026 preprint by A. Jaikin-Zapirain, H. Souza and P. Zalesskii, Profinite detection of free products and free factors, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.16674, was opened directly. Theorem1.3 assumes an abstract finitely generated virtually free group G and an embedding of its full profinite completion into a free profinite product. Its conclusion is a Kurosh decomposition of that abstract G. The completed-group-algebra flatness in Proposition2.15 also starts with this abstract virtually free G. These hypotheses do not recognize the actual E merely from its free open profinite subgroup, involution centralizers, or A82. The source therefore supplies no missing implication here.
