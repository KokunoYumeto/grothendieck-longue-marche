# Boundary of extending the regional d-source detector

Research only, cycle 26. This preserves the bounded follow-up to
`further_source_norm_obstructions26.md`; it is not a new whole-annular
noncommutation statement.

The three-vertex coordinate quotient used there is a homomorphism from
the embedded graph group N of the plus regional vertex. It does not
extend with its same kernel to the whole regional semidirect product.
Indeed it kills b_0 but retains C b_0 C=bar b_0, and it kills d_1 while
retaining d_0, although h shifts d_0 to d_1. The kernel is therefore not
invariant under either the required cone action or the height action.
One cannot apply that quotient to the other vertex and the cross relation
without supplying additional maps.

There is a legitimate invariant enlargement: retain all b_n,d_n and their
barred mates for every integer n, and kill a_n,e_n,k,bar k. This is a full
induced subgraph, stable under C and h. Its edges are exactly the b and d
diagonals and the pairs (b_n,d_(n+1)) with equal bar status. Coordinate
retraction therefore extends to the full semidirect product

    A(Gamma_bd) semidirect (C2_C times Z_h).

It preserves the previous three-vertex certificate, since the latter is
a further graph-group coordinate quotient. This enlargement is not yet
a homomorphism of the actual whole annular group. The actual inner
vertex must be mapped compatibly on the entire common neck; both inner
mapping-torus relations, all inner nodal relations, and both selected-cone
cross relations still have to be satisfied. No such extension has been
constructed in this bounded attempt.

Killing a and e in the actual common-neck free group does preserve the
normal closure of those two generators under eta. The resulting literal
reflection is

    eta(B)=B^-1,
    eta(d)=B d^-1 B^-1,
    eta(f)=B d^-1 f^-1 d B^-1.

These formulas suggest a smaller remaining presentation, but do not
identify its group or show that the d-source commutator survives. No
normal-form or injectivity assertion for that smaller quotient is made.
The independent whole-J class-two calculation owned by DE is a different
approach and is not assumed here.
