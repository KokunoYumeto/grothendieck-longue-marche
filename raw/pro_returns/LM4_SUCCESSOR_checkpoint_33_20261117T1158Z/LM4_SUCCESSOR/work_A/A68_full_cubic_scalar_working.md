## The 128-dimensional upper bound

All scalar third commutators are generated by triples in A_n/2. A first bracket in old M_n contributes zero. A first bracket in T has weight2lambda from two equal-family weight-lambda inputs. Its only possible scalar third partner has weight -2lambda. That partner is at the opposite physical weight; both alternative brackets in Jacobi project to zero in Q_n, and their lifts are globally central. Hence the T contribution vanishes.

A first ordinary Z bracket is between free-row inputs at weights lambda and2lambda at the same physical weight. Its scalar third partner must have weight4lambda. If this third input belongs to a binary s-row, the two alternative brackets vanish, so Jacobi again gives zero. Therefore only three free-row inputs in the three distinct Frobenius families lambda,2lambda,4lambda contribute.

For a fixed physical weight, there are three cyclic bracket tensors on k^4 tensor k^4 tensor k^4. Jacobi supplies one relation for each of the 4^3 choices.
