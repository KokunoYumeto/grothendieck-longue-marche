It is finite by an elementary transfer argument. Let Z=N/[N,Pi],

generated by Schreier's argument. The continuous transfer G→Z

using an unproved finite presentation or a convenient smaller cover.

The existing A56 full ordinary lift realizes these central data.
The claim is that NO such full lift can also have an involutory
negative companion while retaining this old finite affine s-frame.
The multiplier is globally negative for the companion, not mixed.

## 5. Why a full negative involution would have norm one

Suppose a full positive peripheral h, fixingtau literally and retaining
the stated finite action/frame, had b=hc with b²=1. Let beta be its
full s-conjugator whose prescribed finite projection is retained.
Then beta^-1 b(beta^-1) centralizes s. In the full free profinite
productPi=C2 amalg C3,

 C_Pi(s)=<s>.

Its S3 image is1: b acts identically onS3 and beta's prescribed image
is1 (even the alternative s-image would square to1). But s has a
nontrivial S3 image. Therefore beta^-1 b(beta^-1)=1 inPi.
Its image in the actual six-cup quotient would be a norm-one frame,
contradicting section4.

Thus actual finite source-central negative involution data with BOTH
local normalizer norms and an ordinary full framed lift need not have
an order-preserving full framed lift, even at global multiplier−1.
The counterexample keeps a prescribed finite affine frame. It does not
exclude a full involution with the same broad parity after reselecting
that datum, and it does not refute ordinary R2 or unframed universal
conjugacy.
