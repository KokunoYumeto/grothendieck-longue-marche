\subsection*{A literal pre-node section and its exact involution defect}
\statement{B.159}{Proved}{The marked fixed-source section already exists generically, with one exact crossed defect and a minimal invariant filling}
\label{stmt:B159}
Let $G_{\mathrm{gen}}=F_{11}\rtimes S$, $S=F(m,v)$, and let
$q_a:G_{\mathrm{gen}}\to\Gamma_a$ be the composite through the FULL
actual nodal quotient. Set
\[
 J_a=\langle A,m,v\mid[A,m]=1\rangle,\qquad
 K_a=\ker q_a,\qquad P=q_a^{-1}(J_a).
\]
In the actual generic marking put $a^*=\delta(a)$ and $A_0=aa^*$.
There is a literal section
\[
 \lambda:J_a\hookrightarrow P,\qquad
 \lambda(A)=A_0,\quad\lambda(m)=m,\quad\lambda(v)=v,
 \qquad P=K_a\rtimes\lambda(J_a).
\]
Its involution defect
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B159-crossed}
 \kappa(j)=\delta(\lambda(j))\lambda(j)^{-1}
\end{equation}
is an ordered crossed map, with
\[
 \kappa(A)=c_0=[a^*,a]\ne1,\qquad
 \kappa(m)=\kappa(v)=1,\qquad
 \kappa^{-1}(1)=S.
\]
Let $N_\lambda=\langle\!\langle c_0\rangle\!\rangle_P$.
It is the smallest $P$-normal subgroup in $K_a$ for which this section
becomes pointwise fixed, is $\delta$-stable, and is contained in the
complete actual node kernel $N$. All statements have the actual-closure
counterparts described below, without completing an infinite-index
kernel abstractly.
\begin{proof}
The original generic $m$ operator fixes both $a$ and $a^*$ literally.
Hence $[m,A_0]=1$ before node filling, so the defining presentation
of $J_a$ gives $\lambda$. In the actual one-family quotient the
image of $A_0$ is its marked norm $A$; the source letters are unchanged.
Thus $q_a\lambda$ is the identity on $J_a$, proving injectivity and
the displayed semidirect decomposition.

The quotient $J_a$ is pointwise fixed, so \eqref{eq:B159-crossed}
has values in $K_a$. Direct multiplication gives the EXACT order
\[
 \kappa(ij)=\kappa(i)\lambda(i)\kappa(j)\lambda(i)^{-1}.
\]
The involution gives $\delta(\kappa(j))=\kappa(j)^{-1}$.
For $A$, its value is
$a^*a(a a^*)^{-1}=[a^*,a]$, nontrivial in the literal free fiber.
For $m,v$ it is $1$, since the generic involution fixes $S$.
The actual generic fixed subgroup is $G_{\mathrm{gen}}^\delta=S$:
the extended free fiber is
$C_2(\tau)*C_2(c)*F_5$, whose selected-cone centralizer is
$\langle\tau\rangle$, and $S$ fixes that cone. Therefore
$\kappa(j)=1$ exactly when $\lambda(j)\in S$. Applying $q_a$
then identifies this with $j\in S$. The crossed map is not replaced
by a group homomorphism, and its zero fiber is not declared normal.

Since $\delta(c_0)=c_0^{-1}$ and $\delta(P)=P$, the normal subgroup
$N_\lambda$ is $\delta$-stable. Killing $c_0$ makes the images of
$A_0,m,v$ fixed and hence kills every $\kappa(j)$, either by their
generators or by the crossed identity. Conversely any $P$-normal
subgroup for which this marked section is fixed must contain $c_0$.
This proves minimality. Also $c_0\in K_a$, so the section remains
injective in $P/N_\lambda$ by its projection to $J_a$.

The actual whole-node relation proved in B.130/B.135 gives
$[a,\tau a\tau]=1$ in $\Gamma_0$ and identifies the actual marked
source $A_{\mathrm{src}}$ with $a\tau a\tau$. Consequently
$c_0\in N$, $N_\lambda\le N$, and the image of $\lambda$ after
the complete filling is exactly the actual $\sigma$ of B.149.
The rest of $N$ is retained as a further normal subgroup. Nothing
here constructs a section onto the entire one-family group.

For the compact assertions take closures only inside
$\widehat G_{\mathrm{gen}}$. The continuous map
$\widehat\lambda:\widehat J_a\to\widehat G_{\mathrm{gen}}$ is
injective: its composite with $\widehat q_a$ is the actual embedded
$\widehat J_a$ of B.154. This proves full induced topology of
$\lambda(J_a)$, and gives
\[
 \overline P=\overline{K_a}\rtimes\widehat\lambda(\widehat J_a).
\]
Indeed the product is compact and contains $P$ densely, and its
intersection is trivial by the projection. The continuous formula
for $\widehat\kappa$ is still \eqref{eq:B159-crossed}.
The full profinite free-product centralizer gives
$(\widehat G_{\mathrm{gen}})^\delta=\widehat S$, so the same
projection argument gives $\widehat\kappa^{-1}(1)=\widehat S$.

Let $\mathcal N_\lambda$ be the closed normal closure of $c_0$
in $\overline P$. It is exactly the ambient closure of $N_\lambda$:
ordinary conjugates are dense in their compact conjugate family,
and closure of the ordinary normal subgroup is normal in
$\overline P$. It lies in $\overline{K_a}$ and in the actual closed
node kernel, is $\delta$-stable, and is the smallest closed normal
subgroup making $\widehat\lambda(\widehat J_a)$ fixed.
The quotient section is still injective by $\widehat q_a$.
No equality between the abstract completion of $K_a$ or
$N_\lambda$ and its ambient closure is asserted.
\end{proof}
