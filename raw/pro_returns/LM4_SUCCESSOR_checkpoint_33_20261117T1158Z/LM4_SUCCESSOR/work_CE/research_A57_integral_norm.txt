Setup and notation

O=GR(4,3). Let L=O^36 be the genuine Teichmuller monomial lift of V, and

identity on H. The standard c satisfies c(s)=s, c(r)=s r^-1 s, c(tau)=tau^-1.
Its actual monomial intertwiner C on L has C^2=1 and

on C_new. A57's joint quotient is the actual fiber product which lifts the

1. Integral invariant and coinvariant modules, without a mod2 shortcut.
The s action is a monomial permutation with four fixed lines and sixteen
exchanged pairs. Fixed-line scalars are1 because they are Teichmuller
seventh roots of order dividing2. Rescaling each exchanged pair makes
it an ordinary permutation pair. Thus L^s is free O^20 and its reduction
surjects V^s.

The tau matrix is monomial with Teichmuller seventh-root entries. On a
permutation cycle, if the product of those entries is not1, then one
minus that product is a unit in O and the cycle contributes no invariants
or coinvariants. If the product is1, rescale the cycle by Teichmuller units
so that tau is an ordinary cycle permutation. That cycle contributes one
free O line to both invariants and coinvariants. There are four admissible
cycles, by the already verified mod2 cusp dimension. Hence
    L^tau = O^4,    L/(tau-1)L = O^4.

The actual c normalizes tau by inversion. Its mod2 action on cusp
cohomology is identity, as verified in the A56 coefficient calculation.
It follows that it fixes each admissible tau orbit. In the rescaled cycle
basis f_j, the relation C tau=tau^-1 C gives C f_j=d f_(a-j), where d is
a Teichmuller seventh root. The action on both the invariant sum and the
coinvariant generator is multiplication by this same d. Mod2 identity
forces d=1 because reduction is injective on Teichmuller seventh roots.
Consequently C is literally identity on both L^tau and L/(tau-1)L.
This uses the actual monomial form; it does not infer integral identity
from a general mod2 identity.

2. The integral cusp kernel is free and restriction is an isomorphism.
Let K_L=ker[H1(Pi,L)->H1(<s>,L)]. A class in this kernel can be represented
by a cocycle d with d(s)=0. Its r-value lies in ker(1+r+r^2), a free O^24
summand because3 is a unit. The remaining coboundaries come from L^s,
free O^20, by a ->(1-r)a. This map is injective modulo2: its kernel would
be V^s intersect V^r=V^H=0. Thus it has a unit20 by20 minor, its image is
a direct summand, and its cokernel K_L is free O^4.

Reduction gives the analogous four-dimensional kernel K_k over k.
Cusp restriction reduces to the isomorphism K_k -> H1(<tau>,V)=k^4 from
A56's s/cusp boundary isomorphism. Its integral source and target are both
free O^4. Nakayama, or the determinant of a representing matrix, gives
    K_L -> L/(tau-1)L
as an isomorphism.

3. Cohomology action of h and c.
Since rho h=rho and h(tau)=tau, the positive full map h acts identically
on K_L, by this cusp-restriction isomorphism. It preserves the zero
s-restriction because it preserves the conjugacy class of s.
The coefficient-twisted c action on cusp cohomology is minus C^-1,
because c(tau)=tau^-1. Section1 makes it minus identity. Therefore c acts
as minus identity on K_L after the C coefficient identification.

For a cocycle d in K_L there are unique vectors w,v in L such that
    d(hg)=d(g)+(1-rho(g))w,
    d(cg)=-C d(g)+(1-rho(cg))v.
Uniqueness follows from L^Pi=0, already implied by the nontrivial odd
weight action. Evaluating the first equation at tau gives w in L^tau,
so Cw=w. Applying the second equation twice and using c^2=1 gives
    (1-rho(g))(v-Cv)=0 for every g,
hence v=Cv.

4. The negative square vanishes in the actual lifted cocycle quotient.
Put b=h c and q=v+w. Since h is identity on H,
    d(bg)=-C d(g)+(1-rho(bg))q.
Applying this formula twice gives
    d(b^2 g)=d(g)+(1-rho(g))(q-Cq)=d(g),
since Cv=v and Cw=w. This is an equality of actual source cocycle values,
not just of cohomology classes. The b-square is already identity on
C_new. Applying the calculation to all four chosen cusp-kernel cocycles
therefore proves that b acts as an involution on their actual joint fiber
product with C_new, namely A57's Bockstein quotient.

5. Independence of the chosen lifts and literal coboundary corrections.
Choose representatives e_i on C_new for a basis of the mod2 cusp kernel.
The integral free-kernel cocycles can be chosen to reduce to those classes.
Adjust a lifted coboundary if necessary to make the reductions literally
e_i on Pi. Such an adjustment vanishes on R_new=ker(Pi->C_new), since
rho(R_new)=1. If desired normalize e_i(s)=0 first; invariant reduction
L^s->V^s is onto, so this can be done while retaining d_i(s)=0.

If d_i and d_i' are two integral cocycle lifts of the same literal e_i,
then d_i-d_i'=2t for a V-valued cocycle t. Every H1(Pi,V) class inflates
from C_new. Both an inflated cocycle and a coboundary vanish on R_new,
so t|R_new=0. Thus the two lifted cocycles agree on R_new. Their actual
residual maps, and hence the kernel of the joint quotient, are identical.
This proves that Section4 concerns the precise A57 quotient, rather than
a different convenient quotient selected by integral normalization.

6. Scope.

and their negative companion has square1 there. The proof gives no full
negative involution. It does not yet realize chosen new equivariant s/r
frames by a full R2 lift, and it does not settle the norm on A59's extra
central refinement. A separate proposed s-frame lift uses invariants of
