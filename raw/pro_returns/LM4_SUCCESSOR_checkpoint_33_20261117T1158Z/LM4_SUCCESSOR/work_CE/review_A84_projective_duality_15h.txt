The finite-field descent is explicit and valid: extension to a finite splitting field yields an isomorphism; restriction is an isomorphism of d copies; finite-length Krull-Schmidt cancels d. Both the contragredient dual and orientation twist remain projective. A83's actual G0 equality therefore gives the required finite module isomorphism without a prime-to-order assumption.

The anti-involution formula defines the correct left-module projective dual. A finite idempotent matrix gives canonical base change through every finite group-ring quotient; this does not require quotient flatness. The coefficient-of-identity pairing and its displayed inverse identify that finite projective dual with ordinary contragredient duality. The formula is left-linear and the anti-involution conversion produces exactly the contragredient action.

For each fixed p^a and finite head, projectivity lifts the mod-p isomorphism. Cokernel and split kernel vanish by finite Nakayama. The finite sets of isomorphisms have the common-refinement finite intersection property, so compactness gives an actual compatible inverse-limit isomorphism. This avoids taking inverse limits of ordinary linear duals with reversed transition arrows. The dyadic assertion follows from the previously proved actual freeness.

For modular projective covers, the primitive-idempotent pairing identifies P(S)^* with P(S^*). The unique simple head follows because iota(e) acts nontrivially on T precisely when e acts nontrivially on T^*. The regular multiplicity dim_End(S) S and both multiplicity equations are correct. These identify self-dual projective-cover summands, not composition-factor counts. Nonselfdual imbalances and incidence surjectivity remain open.
