Independent diagonal scalar-shear cochain review, 16 November 2026
Verdict: PASS, including opposite-weight scaling and the diagonal case.

Use one orientation(l,r) of every opposite-weight pair. For a diagonal
ordinary coordinate z_ii, the shear is
 z_ii,l -> z_ii,l + L a_j,r^4
and similarly with l,r reversed. The local scalar cocycle is
 pi sum_l [z_ii,l b_i,l^4 + a_i,l^2 w_ii,l^4],
where pi:k->k/F2. Its change under the shear is
 pi[L u^4 + L^4 v^2],
 u=sum_pairs(a_j,r b_i,l+a_j,l b_i,r),
 v=sum_pairs(a_i,l b_j,r+a_i,r b_j,l).
The second exponent is exactly2 because16 is congruent2 modulo7.
Frobenius is additive, so powers may be moved outside the finite sums.

For i!=j put
 k_ij=sum_pairs(a_i,l b_j,r+a_j,l b_i,r),
 q=sum_pairs a_i,l a_j,r, p=sum_pairs a_j,l a_i,r.
The old normalized free center satisfies delta m_ij=k_ij. Direct
polarization gives u=k_ij+delta q and v=k_ij+delta p. Therefore
 psi=pi[L(m_ij+q)^4+L^4(m_ij+p)^2]
has the required coboundary. For j=i, u=v=delta q and the correct
formula is psi=pi[Lq^4+L^4q^2], with no m term.

All opposite-weight scalar products are1, so q,p,m and psi respect the
monomial scaling. Reversing the orientation sends q to p, p to q, and
m to m+q+p. Hence m+q and m+p are individually unchanged. In the
diagonal case q itself is unchanged. Thus these formulas are invariant
under the full oriented-pair transport, not just the odd diagonal group.
There is no missing factor2: q sums18 pairs, whereas its coboundary
supplies both orientations in the sum over36 physical weights.

This proves a scalar action-lift cochain. It does not by itself settle
the source normalization, action square, or literal frame norms; those
require the separate actual group/source checks.
