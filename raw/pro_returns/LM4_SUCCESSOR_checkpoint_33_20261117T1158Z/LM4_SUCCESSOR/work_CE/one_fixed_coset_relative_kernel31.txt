The actual single-fixed-coset presentation for the remaining kernel

Cycle31 research, after B153/B154. This records an exact ordinary
Schreier/Kurosh reduction and its ambient-closure counterpart. It is not
a proof of remaining fixed generation. All node relations and all source
and retained-deck translates are retained.

1. Actual groups and maps

Let Ggen=F11 semidirect S be the actual pre-node ordinary group of B123,
S=F(m,v). Its involution delta fixes S pointwise. Let R be the complete
forty ordinary node seeds, with both region and both covering sheets as
in B123, and let

    N=normal_closure_Ggen(R),  Gamma0=Ggen/N.

Equivalently N is the whole F11-normal closure of every signed S-translate
of all forty seeds. No finite orbit truncation is made. The actual
one-family quotient is p_a:Gamma0 -> Gamma_a. Write q_a for its composite
from Ggen. B149 and B153/B154 give

    J_a=Gamma_a^delta=<A,m,v | [A,m]=1> = Z^2 * Z,
    (hatGamma_a)^delta=hatJ_a,

with full induced topology of the displayed subgroup. The actual marked
section sigma sends A to A_src and fixes m,v. Put

    U=ker(J_a -> S)=F(A_s : s in S/<m>),
    W_a=ker(Gamma_a -> S),
    P=q_a^-1(J_a), Q=P intersect F11.

Thus P=Q semidirect S, N is contained in Q, and the actual quotient is

    P/N = p_a^-1(J_a) = K_rem semidirect sigma(J_a).

The first equality follows directly from the definition of P; the second
uses the proved actual section, not a section of the full filling onto
Gamma_a. In particular the already proved whole-filling nonsplitting is
not contradicted.

2. The only fixed coset

Delta acts on the actual left coset space Gamma_a/J_a. Suppose gJ_a is
fixed. Then delta(g)=g h for some h in J_a. Since delta fixes h, applying
delta twice gives h^2=1. The group Z^2*Z is torsion-free, so h=1 and
g belongs to Gamma_a^delta=J_a. Consequently the identity coset is the
unique fixed coset.

The same proof applies to hatGamma_a/hatJ_a. The group hatJ_a is the free
profinite product of the torsion-free factors hatZ^2 and hatZ; the finite
order theorem for full profinite free products places every torsion
element in a conjugate factor. Thus hatJ_a is torsion-free. Its actual
ambient embedding and the complete fixed-group identification are B154
and B139. Hence the identity is also the unique compact fixed coset.
This statement does not assert a continuous transversal.

3. The ordinary lifted fiber has exactly one cone factor

Extend the pre-node fiber by its actual involution. In the original
ordinary-cover marking,

    E=F11 semidirect <tau>
     =<tau | tau^2=1> * <c | c^2=1> * F5.

This is the actual quotient-fiber orbifold presentation, with c the
other physical cone and tau the selected cone. Let E_a=W_a semidirect
<tau>. The actual quotient E -> E_a is onto. Put

    J_f=U times <tau>,   Q_plus=preimage_E(J_f).

Then Q_plus intersect F11=Q and Q_plus=Q semidirect <tau>. The centralizer
of tau in E_a is exactly J_f: this is the intersection with the fiber of
the already proved centralizer J_a times <tau> in Gamma_a semidirect
<tau>. The only element of order two in J_f is tau, since U is free and
tau acts trivially on U.

Apply the ordinary Kurosh subgroup theorem to Q_plus inside the literal
free product E. A tau-factor indexed by a representative g can occur
only when the image of g tau g^-1 belongs to J_f. That image is tau, so
the image of g centralizes tau in E_a and belongs to J_f. Therefore g is
already in Q_plus. Exactly one tau-factor occurs, represented by tau
itself.

No c-factor occurs. The actual coarse quotient E_a -> Q_f, which deletes
the remaining one-family graph letters, kills U and sends J_f to <tau>.
It sends c to the other cone C (after the actual local a-conjugation has
been killed). In Q_f=C2(C)*C2(tau)*Z(f), C is not conjugate to tau and is
nontrivial. Thus no image of a conjugate of c can belong to J_f.
Intersections with conjugates of the F5 factor are free, and can be
absorbed into the free factor in Kurosh's decomposition. Hence

    Q_plus = <tau | tau^2=1> * F(Z)

for a possibly infinite discrete free basis Z. Multiplying each free
basis element by tau when necessary changes that free basis so that
every z has ordinary orientation zero. This is an invertible free-product
basis change fixing tau. The ordinary index-two Schreier basis is then

    Q=F(z, delta(z) : z in Z),

with delta literally exchanging the two halves. In particular Q^delta=1.
The basis is not claimed canonical or finite, and its abstract completion
is not identified with its ambient closure.

4. The complete actual node presentation in this free paired group

Choose a transversal T for Gamma_a/J_a as follows: retain 1, and for each
two-element delta orbit choose a representative g and its exact mate
delta(g). Lift the first representative to F11 and lift the mate by
applying delta to that lift. Such fiber lifts exist because S is already
in J_a. This is a discrete choice, not a continuous section. It gives
an exactly delta-paired transversal for Ggen/P.

For each t in T and each seed rho in R retain the literal word

    t^-1 rho t in Q.                                    (1)

Also retain its exact delta-image if it is not literally already present.
Adding these images does not change N, since the complete actual node
closure is delta-stable. Both original covering sheets remain present;
this instruction does not replace one sheet by a peripheral conjugacy
class. Then

    N=normal_closure_P({ t^-1 rho t : t in T, rho in R }
                      together with their delta-images). (2)

Indeed any g in Ggen has form t p with p in P, and
g^-1 rho g=p^-1(t^-1 rho t)p. Conversely every displayed generator lies
in N, so the two normal closures agree. Since P=Q semidirect S, (2) is
exactly the Q-normal closure of every signed S-translate of (1) and its
delta-image. Thus it retains all original source translates, every
retained-deck conjugator through T, and all nonlinear connectors. It is
an actual complete presentation of Q/N in the free paired basis of §3.
Nothing in the argument turns those relators into coordinate graph
commutators or proves that the free basis is simultaneously peripheral.

5. Exact fixed/source comparison in the quotient

The four actual contact words A_src,B_src,D_src,E_src all lift to Q: the
first projects to A in U and the other three project to 1; all four have
trivial S-coordinate. Their whole signed S-orbits generate

    H_f=H_src intersect ker(Gamma0 -> S)
       =I_src semidirect sigma(U).

The equality follows from the actual marked source presentation in
B150 and the injective section of J_a. On the other hand

    Q/N=K_rem semidirect sigma(U),
    (Q/N)^delta=K_rem^delta semidirect sigma(U).

Therefore the remaining ordinary fixed-generation question is exactly

    (Q/N)^delta = H_f,

with Q the free paired group and N the complete closure (2). Equivalently
it is K_rem^delta=I_src. The paired free group itself had no nontrivial
fixed elements; all these contact norms are created by the actual node
relations. Unique fixed cosets alone do not control how many fixed words
that filling creates. In particular they do not justify deleting any
translate in (2), a fixed-cell lifting assertion, or a free-source claim.

6. The actual ambient compact counterpart

Work only with actual closures in hatGgen=hatF11 semidirect hatS. The
full topology of the finitely generated generic fiber follows from its
cofinal characteristic finite quotients. The same argument applies to
the finitely generated quotient fibers W and W_a. The ordinary U closure
is taken inside the proved full-topology hatJ_a, and its intersection
with the fiber is the kernel of hatJ_a -> hatS.

Let mathcalQ denote the inverse image of barU under
hatF11 -> hatW_a. Then mathcalQ is the actual closure of Q: the kernel
of that completed quotient is the closure of its discrete kernel, lies
in barQ, and the compact image of barQ is exactly barU. Thus there is no
claim that mathcalQ is the abstract completion of the infinite Q.

Let mathcalN be the actual closed normal node kernel in hatF11 from
B123. The image mathcalQ/mathcalN is the corresponding actual subgroup
of hatW. In the pre-node ambient completion, the free-product torsion
centralizer theorem gives C_hatE(tau)=<tau>. Hence

    (mathcalQ)^delta=1.

This does not use a profinite Kurosh decomposition of an arbitrary
infinite-index closed subgroup. Completing the actual marked section
and using B154's genuine closed-kernel identity gives

    mathcalQ/mathcalN = barK_rem semidirect hatsigma(barU),
    (mathcalQ/mathcalN)^delta
        =barK_rem^delta semidirect hatsigma(barU),
    barH_f=barI_src semidirect hatsigma(barU).

Here barH_f is the actual closed subgroup generated by all signed source
orbits of the four contacts. Its equality with the displayed semidirect
product follows from the same net correction used in B154, now with
the continuous projection H_src -> S and its literal S section.
Consequently the actual completed remaining question is precisely

    (mathcalQ/mathcalN)^delta=barH_f.

The right side uses actual ambient closures, not an abstract completion
of the infinite four-orbit free group or of its relation kernel.

7. Scope and next gate

The new reduction provides a single fixed coset, a single cone factor
in the discrete lifted fiber, an exact free paired presentation, and
the full node/source/deck relation scheme in it. A uniform argument for
the fixed subgroup of that entire filled paired presentation is still
needed. The original full-base B75 characteristic-neighborhood criterion
also needs its actual comparison with all other necks. Neither that
global comparison nor the local remaining generation is proved here.

No producer, finite target search, truncated orbit, or private rendering
was used. The actual inputs are the complete B123 node scheme,
B139/B149 one-family fixed subgroup, B150 source presentation, and the
proved B153/B154 full topology and compact section. The ordinary Kurosh
and full profinite free-product torsion-centralizer results are used in
their respective stated categories only.
