Independent review of root's one-retained-tangency counterexample
2026-11-16.  Result: PASS.

Retain the parabola y=x^2 and the line at infinity.  Delete additionally the
tangent line y=2x-1.  The source is y=0.  Its retained bad intersection is only
(0,0), with tangency order two.  The source infinity point [1:0:0] lies on
neither the parabola (whose infinity point is [0:1:0]) nor the deleted tangent
line (whose infinity point is [1:2:0]).  Thus infinity is a transverse
intersection with the retained infinity component alone.  The deleted line
meets the source transversely at x=1/2.

Set u=x-1 and v=y-2x+1.  Then the ambient complement is
Y={(u,v):v != 0 and v != u^2}.  The map
  u=(alpha^2+beta^2)/2,
  v=(alpha^2-beta^2)^2/4
from W={alpha*beta*(alpha-beta)*(alpha+beta) != 0} is an actual connected
finite etale cover of degree eight.  Its Jacobian is
-2*alpha*beta*(alpha^2-beta^2), nonzero on W.  The two values alpha^2,beta^2
are u plus/minus sqrt(v), both nonzero since u^2-v != 0; each has two square
roots.  This proves the degree and exhausts all fibers.  The signed coordinate
permutations form a free deck action of D8, and W is a connected complex line
arrangement complement.  Consequently the actual fundamental group maps onto
D8; no abstract finite head is substituted for a source quotient.

On the source v+2u+1=0, the pulled-back equation is
  ((alpha+beta)^2+2)*((alpha-beta)^2+2)=0.
Write c=i*sqrt(2), choose alpha-beta=c, and parameterize beta=t, alpha=t+c.
The actual source map is x=t(t+c).  Its domain is the line with the three
points 0,-c,-c/2 removed, and its target is C minus {0,1/2}.  It is connected
of degree two.  The source retained meridian at x=0 has two unramified
preimages and hence trivial deck image.  The deleted meridian at x=1/2 has
one double preimage and acts by t -> -c-t, i.e. (alpha,beta) -> (-beta,-alpha).
The stabilizer of this source component is exactly {I,-swap}.

The source deletion kernel therefore has image C2=<-swap>.  The ambient
deletion kernel is the normal closure of the deleted meridian (by transverse
disk perturbation), and its D8 image is
  < -swap, swap >={I,-I,swap,-swap}=V4.
Conjugation by diag(-1,1) takes -swap to swap.  Thus the actual ambient
deletion kernel has a finite image strictly larger than the source kernel.
The same finite quotient separates the actual compact images in full
profinite completions.

This refutes the proposed general relative statement even with one retained
bad source point, one retained tangency, transverse deleted intersections,
and a generic source infinity point.  It says nothing negative about the
specific fifth-root marginal source: that source additionally has F21's
internal normal-generation property, which this V4/C2 example does not.
