1. THE ACTUAL p-CENTRAL QUOTIENT

Let Pi=C2 amalg C3, L=ker(Pi->Q), N=Phi_p(L), p not dividing 2|Q|,
M=L/N, and G=Pi/N. Factor faithfulness implies p>=5. The actual relation
sequence gives M_Q=0. If A is the p-primary central kernel above G and
Ptilde its inverse image of M, then A'=ker(Ptilde_ab->M) is a trivial
Q-module. Exact coinvariants for finite abelian p-groups give
Ptilde_ab,Q=A', because M_Q=0. A nonzero A' has a nonzero Cp-character;
its pullback along the surjective actual L->Ptilde contradicts M_Q=0.
Therefore Ptilde_ab=M and A=Ptilde'.

The commutator pairing factors through Lambda^2_Fp(M) and is Q-invariant,
so A is a quotient of W=(Lambda^2 M)_Q and is elementary abelian. The
pth-power map from M to A is well defined and additive: central lift
choices have exponent p, and the class-two cross term has coefficient
binom(p,2), divisible by p. It is a Q-map to a trivial module, hence zero.
Thus Ptilde has exponent p. No assertion about a priori freeness of this
subgroup is needed.

The converse target is P(M)=M x W, with BCH cocycle (a wedge b)/2.
After a coprime splitting of the actual G, the actual factor coordinates
s=(a_s,s0), r=(a_r,r0) have lifts
 S=(a_s,0,s0), R=(a_r,-(a_r wedge r0 a_r)_Q/6,r0).
Direct multiplication gives S^2=1 and
 (a_r,z,r0)^3=(0,3z+(a_r wedge r0 a_r)_Q/2,1).
The actual source map therefore exists. Its image surjects G; its L-image
surjects M, and commutators of these actual lifts attain every exterior
generator of W. Consequently the source image is the whole target.
The upper bound and this attained target identify the marked actual
p-central quotient. This is stronger than an abstract possible extension.

2. CUSP COORDINATES, BOUNDARY CORRECTION, AND COMPACT RESIDUE

Transport the entire marked source map by an inner change so that
tau=(a0,z,t), t a0=a0. This transports all central coordinates too;
it is not an unsupported replacement of just the affine cusp coordinate.
For m=ord(t), tau^m=(m a0,m z,1). The Q-span of a0 is the actual boundary
module P, since m is invertible in Fp.

Let B be a b0-semilinear involution with Ba0=-a0, and let B_W be its
exterior action. The homogeneous action sends the cusp to the required
inverse up to the central defect (1+B_W)z. For v in M^t with Bv=-v,
compose with Inn((v,0)). Direct BCH multiplication changes that defect by
 -(v wedge a0)_Q = (a0 wedge v)_Q.
Its square remains identity, since (v,0)(Bv,0)=1, including the central
BCH term. The affine cusp remains literally inverted.

The map M^t -> (P wedge M)_Q, v |-> (a0 wedge v)_Q, is onto. Every
Q-translate in the first slot can be moved to the second slot in
coinvariants; averaging the second slot over <t> does not change its
image. The map intertwines -B on its source with B_W on the target.
Since 2 is invertible, any B_W-fixed target vector has a preimage with
Bv=-v. Thus every boundary/mixed central residue is removable by an
actual involutory inner change of the affine action.

After quotienting P, the remaining module is the actual compact-surface
homology H and the remaining center is (Lambda^2 H)_Q. In an oriented
surface presentation, each of the |Q|/m cusp generators is conjugate to
tau^m. It contributes m z after killing P; conjugating it does not change
that central value. The handle commutators contribute the actual
symplectic bivector Omega. The exact relation is
 |Q| z + Omega = 0.
A uniform reversal of boundary orientation changes both signs and leaves
the criterion unchanged. Averaging identifies invariants and coinvariants,
so (1+B_W)z=0 in the compact quotient is precisely
 (Lambda^2 B)Omega=-Omega.
Thus anti-symplecticity is the exact remaining compact criterion.

For a fixed G-action, the action on W is forced by commutators. Two lifts
with that action differ by Hom(G,W), which is zero because G_ab is a
quotient of C6 and p>=5. The preceding boundary changes deliberately alter
the G-action; they are not claimed to be invisible scalar adjustments.

3. ANTI-SYMPLECTIC SEMILINEAR INVOLUTION

Over the algebraic numbers the compact Q-module has class
 2[1]+[Q]-[Q/<s0>]-[Q/<r0>]-[Q/<t>].
Its trivial multiplicity is zero, and for nontrivial simple S its
multiplicity is dim S-dim S^s-dim S^r-dim S^t. Duality and b0-twisting
commute; their orbits give the four exhaustive cases in the working proof.

For a four-constituent orbit, dual partners can be assigned negative
adjoints and return maps inverses. If b0 fixes a nonselfdual dual pair,
normalize an intertwiner A to A^2=1 and use -(A^-1)^* on the dual. These
maps are involutory, semilinear, and anti-symplectic.

If S^b=S^* but S is not selfdual, the twisted form F satisfies
 F(qx,b0(q)y)=F(x,y).
Its transpose is plus or minus F. In the alternating case the parity
argument uses the actual norm-one frames and is valid. In detail,
 F_s(x,y)=F(x,beta0 y), F_r(x,y)=F(x,J0 y)
are alternating because b0(beta0)=beta0^-1 and b0(J0)=J0^-1.
The first makes s0 an isometry. The second makes r0 self-adjoint:
 F_r(r0 x,y)=F(x,b0(r0)^-1 J0 y)=F(x,J0 r0 y).
Similarly t is self-adjoint for F because b0(t)=t^-1. In characteristic
zero these finite-order operators are semisimple. The eigenvalue-one
spaces are nondegenerate for the indicated alternating forms. Their
dimensions and dim S are even, so the multiplicity is even. Tensoring
with an alternating multiplicity form makes the total twisted form
symmetric. For its matrix A, the exchange (x,phi)->(A^-1 phi,A x) is the
required involution on X plus X^*.

If S is selfdual and b0-stable, choose an involutory intertwiner A. Its
multiplier on the simple invariant form is nu=+1 or -1. On the actual
symplectic isotypic block S tensor U, use A tensor J. If nu=-1, J=1.
If nu=+1, choose an involutory anti-isometry of the multiplicity form.
An alternating form always admits one. For a symmetric multiplicity
form its dimension must be even; here the simple form is alternating,
and the same fixed-space multiplicity formula proves exactly that
parity. An even-dimensional symmetric form over the algebraic closure
is hyperbolic and admits the required involution.

If b0 exchanges two selfdual constituents, their types and multiplicities
coincide. A twisted anti-isometry between the two blocks exists, and its
inverse supplies the return map. A square root of -1 converts a chosen
isometry to an anti-isometry when needed.

These constructions require only finitely many algebraic entries and
square roots. Combine the result on H with the prescribed signed action
on the boundary module using a Q-linear splitting. There is no claim
that an arbitrary preselected semilinear involution is anti-symplectic.

4. REDUCTION AT FRESH PRIMES

5. OTHER CENTRAL PRIMES AND THE ORDINARY FULL LIFT

For ell different from p, put T_ell=Q_ell/Z_ell with trivial action.
The finite p-group L/N=M has vanishing positive cohomology on T_ell.
Inflation-restriction gives the actual restriction isomorphism
 Hom(L,T_ell) -> Hom(N,T_ell)^M.
Taking Pi-invariants gives the claimed isomorphism of invariant
characters. Pontryagin duality identifies the ell-primary parts of
N/[N,Pi] and L/[L,Pi] under the actual inclusion N->L. It is not merely
an equality of their orders. The old central group has no p-part because
a nonzero p-part would yield a nonzero invariant mod-p character of L,
contrary to M_Q=0.

The full new central quotient is therefore the actual fiber product of
E_p and the old actual Q-central quotient over Q. The source attains
this fiber product: over Q its two kernel images are respectively a
p-group and a prime-to-p central group, and a subdirect product of
groups of coprime order is the full product. Equivalently, the actual
central-kernel identifications show that the joint source map is an
isomorphism onto that fiber product.

Pair the old involutory negative action with the newly corrected action
on E_p. Their Q-actions agree. Prescribed lower norm-one beta0/J0 frames
lift in the p-group normalizer preimages by coprime complement conjugacy;

The A80 detector then rules out a full negative involution inducing that
