Independent research-only review, 16 November 2026
Source: work_DE/next_common_involution/faithful_quotient_note.md, section 5.
Verdict: PASS, conditional only on the already accepted prime-power
centralizer/topology comparisons explicitly cited by that note.

The quotient orbifold for the order-five action is the same before and
after its two fixed points are made ordinary marked points upstairs:
two ordinary orbit marks and two branch points. A centralizing oriented
map fixes the latter individually, since their local rotation residues
are +1 and -1 modulo five. Equivariant isotopy/quotient comparison thus
gives inverse marking and forgetting maps on the centralizers. The deck
C5 meets the pure mapping-class subgroup trivially in either marking,
so the restricted pure centralizers are the same free rank-six group.
The full induced topology on each centralizer, proved in the accepted
C5 comparison, upgrades this particular forgetting isomorphism to the
actual embedded completions. Discrete injectivity alone would not do so.

The common involution still acts freely on the remaining ten labels.
Its quotient has five labelled ordinary marks and the unordered branch
pair; the simultaneous sheet-flip ambiguity gives index 2^(5-1)=16.
The accepted C2 comparison then gives the faithful completed seven-point
map on the cyclic source. This is not an original rigid forgetting:
its two branch marks were unmarked upstairs. The tetrahedral source maps
to the same target, but no injectivity on that restriction, nor equality
of the original intersection with the unrestricted image intersection,
is established. The note states both limitations correctly.
