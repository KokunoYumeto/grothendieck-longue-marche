\subsection*{The compact stabilizer from a corrected inner action}
\statement{B.153}{Proved}{The two actual completed sections intersect in the procyclic closure of the marked inner source}
\label{stmt:B153}
Use B.148's actual coarse group $E^0=T_0\rtimes S$, with
$T_0=F(r,\bar r,w)$, $S=F(m,u)$, and graph section
$H=\{h_s^{-1}s:s\in S\}$. Both section completions embed in the
actual full completion of $E^0$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B153-conclusion}
 \widehat H\cap\widehat S
 =\{s\in\widehat S:\alpha_s(r)=r\}
 =\overline{\langle m\rangle}
 =\widehat{\mathbb Z}(m).
\end{equation}
The intersection is taken in $\widehat{E^0}$; no completion of an
abstract subgroup intersection is presumed.
\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual completed sections.}
Every finite quotient of the finite-rank free fiber $T_0$ is refined by
a characteristic finite-index subgroup. Its finite automorphism action
extends it to a finite quotient of the split group. Thus $T_0$ has full
induced topology, and
$\widehat{E^0}=\widehat T_0\rtimes\widehat S$.
The source $S$ and the graph section $H$ each project isomorphically to
$S$, so both have full topology as well. The actual ordered cocycle
extends continuously, and
\[
 L_c:=\widehat H\cap\widehat S
     =\{s\in\widehat S:h_s=1\}.
\]
Put $b=\bar r$ and let $\delta$ be conjugation by the actual $\tau$:
it exchanges $r,b$ and inverts $w$. Every $\alpha_s$ commutes with
$\delta$. Define $q_s=\delta(h_s)$. B.148's exact ordered identity gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B153-correction}
\begin{gathered}
 q_{st}=\alpha_s(q_t)q_s,\qquad
 \alpha_s(b)=q_sbq_s^{-1},\\
 \widetilde\beta_s=\operatorname{Inn}(q_s^{-1})\alpha_s,
 \qquad\widetilde\beta_s\widetilde\beta_t
       =\widetilde\beta_{st}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Indeed the composite inner head is
$q_s^{-1}\alpha_s(q_t^{-1})=q_{st}^{-1}$.
This is a genuine continuous source action fixing $b$, without any
choice of peripheral representatives.

\emph{An actual free-two quotient and its complete even-source image.}
Kill $b$ to obtain $R=F(r,w)$, or its actual full completion.
The corrected action descends to a continuous action $\beta$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B153-beta}
 \beta_m(r)=r,\quad\beta_m(w)=rw^{-1},\qquad
 \beta_u=\operatorname{Inn}(w^{-2}).
\end{equation}
For the last formula, $q_u=\delta(w^{-1}r)=wb$; hence the corrected
$r$ image is $b^{-1}w^{-2}rw^2b$ and its $w$ image is $b^{-1}wb$.
The additional power of $w^{-1}$ from the correction is retained.

The index-two subgroup
$S_2=\ker(\operatorname{exp}_m\bmod2)$ has the entire Schreier basis
\[
 x=m^2,\qquad y=u,\qquad z=mum^{-1}.
\]
Their $\beta$ actions are inner, with respective heads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B153-heads}
 \theta(x)=r,\qquad\theta(y)=w^{-2},\qquad
 \theta(z)=wr^{-1}wr^{-1}.
\end{equation}
Here $\beta_m^2=\operatorname{Inn}(r)$, and conjugating
$\operatorname{Inn}(w^{-2})$ by $\beta_m$ gives
$\operatorname{Inn}((rw^{-1})^{-2})$.
These heads are a free basis of
$R_2=\ker(\operatorname{exp}_w:R\to C_2)$.
In its usual Schreier basis $X=r$, $T=w^2$, $Z=wrw^{-1}$ they are
\[
 X,\qquad Y=T^{-1},\qquad C=Z^{-1}TX^{-1},
 \qquad T=Y^{-1},\quad Z=(CXY)^{-1}.
\]
The last two formulas give the inverse free substitutions. Thus
$\theta:S_2\to R_2$ is an actual isomorphism in the displayed bases.
Both subgroups have finite index in their respective free groups;
therefore their completions are actual open subgroups. Completing the
isomorphism and using density gives
\[
 \beta_s=\operatorname{Inn}(\widehat\theta(s)),
 \qquad s\in\widehat S_2.
\]
No infinite-rank kernel completion occurs in this comparison.

\emph{The entire compact return subgroup.}
For $s\in L_c$, one has $h_s=q_s=1$. Thus
$\widetilde\beta_s=\alpha_s$ and its induced $\beta_s$ fixes $r$.
If $s$ is even, the preceding identity gives
\[
 \widehat\theta(s)\in C_{\widehat R}(r)
       =\widehat{\mathbb Z}(r).
\]
The equality is the centralizer of the primitive procyclic free factor
in $\widehat R=\widehat{\mathbb Z}(r)\amalg\widehat{\mathbb Z}(w)$.
In the explicit basis \eqref{eq:B153-heads}, its preimage is exactly
$\widehat{\mathbb Z}(m^2)$. If $s$ has odd $m$ parity, multiply by
$m^{-1}$, which belongs to both actual sections, and apply the even
case. Conversely every profinite power of $m$ belongs to both closed
sections. This proves $L_c=\widehat{\mathbb Z}(m)$.

Finally B.148's continuous identities
$\alpha_s(r)=h_srh_s^{-1}$ and $\chi_x(h_s)=0$, with $\chi_x(r)=1$,
show that an arbitrary compact source fixing $r$ has
$h_s\in C_{\widehat T_0}(r)=\widehat{\mathbb Z}(r)$ and therefore
$h_s=1$. This proves the remaining equality in
\eqref{eq:B153-conclusion}.
\end{proof}
