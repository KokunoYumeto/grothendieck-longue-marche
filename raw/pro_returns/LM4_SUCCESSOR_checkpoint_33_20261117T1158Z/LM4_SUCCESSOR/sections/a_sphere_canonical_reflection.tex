\statement{A.97}{Proved}{The original involution and its full sphere-kernel correction}
Let \(\alpha\) be an actual multiplier-minus-one involution in full
\(\widehat{GT}\), with its original based four-strand action. Use
A.96 and put \(R=PA,S=CP,L=CPA\). Then \(\alpha\) preserves
\(\widehat N\), induces a negative peripheral involution
\(\alpha_0\) on \(\widehat B\), and acts as minus identity on
\((\widehat N)_{\rm ab}\). There is a unique full transporter
\(h_0\in\widehat N\) with zero L-coordinate in the basis
\((L,A,C)\) and
\[
 \alpha(L)=h_0^{-1}L^{-1}h_0.
 \tag{A.97.1}
\]
It satisfies
\[
\begin{gathered}
 h_0\alpha(h_0)=1,\qquad \beta=\operatorname{Inn}(h_0)\alpha,
 \qquad \beta^2=1,\\
 \beta|_{\widehat B}=\alpha_0,\qquad
 \alpha(b)=h_0^{-1}\alpha_0(b)h_0\quad(b\in\widehat B).
\end{gathered}
\tag{A.97.2}
\]
Both involutions act on every actual relative pro-two quotient over
the specified odd-dihedral base head, with this same correction.

\begin{proof}
The original identities are
\[
 P^{-1}=abc,\qquad R=ade,\qquad S=cef,
 \qquad L^{-1}=fbd=f(bdf)f^{-1}.
 \tag{A.97.3}
\]
For R use \([a,P]=[a,f]=1\); for L use \([c,d]=1\) and
\(b^{-1}=cPa\). Thus \((S,P^{-1},R,L^{-1})\) has product one
and comprises the four labelled triple-full-twist classes. Both
\((P,R,S)\) and \((L,A,C)\) are free bases of N. The action table
in A.96 preserves their N-conjugacy classes and fixes L literally.

The actual operadic cabling theorem A.36/C.28 sends each labelled
pair or triple full twist to a conjugate of its inverse. It also
inverts \(\Delta_4^2\), so descends to \(\widehat G\).
The closed normal closure of P,R,S is \(\widehat N\), because
killing them kills A,C and leaves the actual B section. Applying
the involution in both directions proves invariance of this kernel.
On the quotient, d,f and their product are inverted up to conjugacy:
the third class is the image of b, namely \((df)^{-1}\). Thus
\(\alpha_0\) has the asserted peripheral type and acts as minus
identity on the abelianization of B.

Every ambient conjugator for a class in (A.97.3) can be replaced by
an N-conjugator. Write the conjugator as nb and use the explicitly
verified B-preservation of that N-conjugacy class. Hence alpha
inverts the free basis \((P,R,S)\) up to N-conjugacy and acts as
minus identity on \((\widehat N)_{\rm ab}\). In particular a
transporter in (A.97.1) exists.

Since B fixes the primitive element L literally, the primitive
free-factor centralizer theorem gives
\[
 C_{\widehat G}(L)=\widehat{\mathbb Z}(L)\times\widehat B.
 \tag{A.97.4}
\]
Two N-transporters differ on the left by a unique power of L;
their L-coordinates therefore give the unique normalization h0.
The square of beta is inner with head \(h_0\alpha(h_0)\).
This head lies in N and centralizes L, hence is a power of L.
Direct substitution shows that beta fixes the head, while beta
inverts L. The head equals its inverse, and torsion-freeness of
\(\widehat{\mathbb Z}(L)\) gives \(h_0\alpha(h_0)=1\).

Beta preserves (A.97.4) and induces alpha0 on its B quotient, so
\(\beta(b)=L^{\chi(b)}\alpha_0(b)\) for a continuous homomorphism
\(\chi:\widehat B\to\widehat{\mathbb Z}\). Applying beta twice
gives \(\chi(\alpha_0(b))=\chi(b)\). The action on the
abelianization gives the opposite sign, hence \(2\chi=0\).
Cancellation of two proves chi zero and all of (A.97.2).

For odd n, let \(H_n\) be the kernel of the specified map
\(\widehat B\to D_{2n}\) sending d,f to the two reflections.
Its closed normal defining relators are \(d^2,f^2,(df)^n\).
The exact peripheral property makes this kernel alpha0-invariant.
Thus \(K_n=\widehat N\rtimes H_n\) is beta-invariant and
alpha-invariant, since their difference is the inner twist by h0.
Its characteristic pro-two residual is invariant too. A.96 gives
the embedded \(N^{(2)}\) in \(\widehat G/O^2(K_n)\), and the
same full formulas descend. In particular
\(\beta_N\theta_b\beta_N^{-1}=\theta_{\alpha_0(b)}\) for the
two actual partial-inner actions of A.96.

The twist is not asserted to be a conjugacy of involutions: that
would require h0 to be an alpha-coboundary. The same full word is
retained on both base generators. No four-strand action is supplied
for a general \(\widehat{GT}_0\) element, and no quotient fixed
element is assumed to lift to a full fixed element.
\end{proof}
