\subsection*{The surviving outer source factor and the full source abelianization}

\statement{B.124}{Proved}{The actual outer five-source factor is a retract after all final relations, and the complete actual source has free abelianization of rank six}
\label{stmt:B124}
Let $\Gamma^0$ be the actual ordinary annular one-neck group of
B.119--B.123, after all regional node, cross and prefix relations.
Use its six based positive source loops
\[
 (s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4,v,m)
\]
from B.123, with $s_i$ the four contact loops, $v$ the outer annular
source loop and $m$ the inner source loop. Let
\[
 L_+=\langle s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4,v\rangle,\qquad
 H_{\mathrm{src}}=\langle L_+,m\rangle\leq\Gamma^0.
\]
Then the displayed five generators freely generate $L_+\cong F_5$,
and there is an actual retraction
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-source-retract}
 \rho_+:\Gamma^0\longrightarrow L_+,
 \qquad \rho_+(s_i)=s_i,
 \quad\rho_+(v)=v,\quad\rho_+(m)=1.
\end{equation}
In particular $L_+$ has its full induced profinite topology in
$\Gamma^0$ and in the actual orbifold group $\Gamma$ containing
$\Gamma^0$ with index two.

Let $\iota:F_6\to\Gamma^0$ be the actual source map and let
$p_{\mathrm{out}}:F_6\to F_5$ kill $m$, while
$p_{\mathrm{base}}:F_6\to F(v,m)$ kills the four contacts. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-kernel-bounds}
 \ker\iota\subseteq\ker p_{\mathrm{out}}\cap\ker p_{\mathrm{base}},
 \qquad
 \ker\widehat\iota\subseteq
 \ker\widehat p_{\mathrm{out}}\cap\ker\widehat p_{\mathrm{base}}.
\end{equation}
The second statement uses the actual map into
$\widehat{\Gamma^0}$ and the full completions of the source free
factors. If $\overline H_{\mathrm{src}}$ denotes the actual compact
source image in $\widehat{\Gamma^0}$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-source-abelianization}
 H_{\mathrm{src}}^{\mathrm{ab}}\cong\mathbb Z^6,
 \qquad
 (\overline H_{\mathrm{src}})^{\mathrm{ab}}_{\mathrm{cont}}
       \cong\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\,6},
\end{equation}
with the indicated six source generators as basis. The continuous
abelianization means quotient by the closed commutator subgroup.
These statements do not assert injection of the entire $F_6$ or
identify the actual compact source image with its abstract completion.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.124.1. The actual detector and the complete source connector.}
Use the literal common neck
\[
 K=F(a,B,d,e,f),\qquad B=CbC,
\]
and the actual reflection automorphism $\eta$ of B.114 and B.121.
The final-neck retraction constructed in B.121 restricts to a map
$\mathcal R:\Gamma^0\to K$. Its values on the original regional
ordinary words are the actual B.112 detector $r$, and its values
on an entire reflected word are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-reflected-detector}
 \mathcal R(w)=r(w),\qquad
 \mathcal R(jwj)=\eta(r(w)).
\end{equation}
This is a property of the explicit final quotient map: the target
is the neck semidirect product $K\rtimes_\eta\langle j\rangle$.
It does not require that a regional group survive injectively
in the final group. The same construction has $r(k)=1$, so the
inner actual source loop $m=k$ is killed.

For clarity, retain the raw regional source marking before the
whole selected-gap conjugation. Put $\bar U=CUC$,
$\Delta_U=UCUC$, and
\[
 E=fde d^{-1}f^{-1},\qquad
 T_0=CbCbCECb^{-1}Cb^{-1}.
\]
The four local contact words and the annular source lift are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-raw-local-source}
 \begin{gathered}
 \ell_1=CbCb,\qquad \ell_2=T_0^2,\\
 \ell_3=Cb[d,\bar d]b^{-1}C\Delta_d,\qquad
 \ell_4=Cb[a,\bar a]b^{-1}C\Delta_a,\qquad \ell_5=h.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
These are the actual positive source words from B.112, not arbitrary
conjugate meridians. The common selected-gap word is $a$. Thus the
five actual outer source words are the images of
$j(a\ell_i a^{-1})j$. In particular the reflection in
\eqref{eq:B124-reflected-detector} applies to the entire connector.

The exact B.112 detector sends the raw tuple
\eqref{eq:B124-raw-local-source} to
\[
 \bigl(B,\ (Bfd)e(Bfd)^{-1},\ d,\ a,\ f\bigr).
\]
This calculation is literal under that detector: the conjugated
commutators in $\ell_3,\ell_4$ vanish because their barred cores
are killed, and the two factors of $T_0$ retain their displayed
order. Consequently, before reflection the whole gap-conjugated
images are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-five-basis}
 \begin{aligned}
 u_1&=aBa^{-1},&
 u_2&=(aBfd)e(aBfd)^{-1},\\
 u_3&=ada^{-1},&u_4&=a,&u_5&=afa^{-1}.
 \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The actual outer detector images are $\eta(u_1),\ldots,\eta(u_5)$.

\emph{B.124.2. An inverse free basis, hence an actual retraction.}
The words in \eqref{eq:B124-five-basis} have the explicit inverse
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-basis-inverse}
 \begin{aligned}
 a&=u_4,& B&=u_4^{-1}u_1u_4,\\
 d&=u_4^{-1}u_3u_4,& f&=u_4^{-1}u_5u_4,\\
 e&=u_4^{-1}u_3^{-1}u_5^{-1}u_1^{-1}
       u_2u_1u_5u_3u_4.
 \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Direct substitution in both directions proves a free-basis
isomorphism. Since $\eta$ is the actual involutory automorphism
of K, the reflected tuple is also a free basis, with inverse
obtained by applying \eqref{eq:B124-basis-inverse} to the literal
$\eta$ images of the old generators.

Let $F_5^{\mathrm{src}}$ be the abstract free group on the five outer
source loops. Its actual map into $\Gamma^0$, followed by
$\mathcal R$, is the just-proved basis isomorphism onto K.
Therefore its actual map is injective. Composing $\mathcal R$
with the inverse basis isomorphism $K\to L_+$ gives
\eqref{eq:B124-source-retract}. In particular the argument proves
survival after the final relations; it does not infer survival
from the earlier regional source embedding alone.

\emph{B.124.3. Finite targets and the two exact kernel bounds.}
Every finite target of $L_+$ extends to $\Gamma^0$ by composing
with $\rho_+$. Hence $L_+$ has its full induced profinite topology.
To obtain the same conclusion in $\Gamma$, take the core of a
finite-index kernel in the index-two subgroup $\Gamma^0$.
The resulting finite-index subgroup of $\Gamma$ restricts to a
subgroup of the prescribed kernel in $L_+$. This proves the full
induced topology in both ambient groups and the actual embedded
copy of $\widehat F_5$.

The composite $\rho_+\iota$ is exactly the source free retraction
killing $m$ and preserving the other five generators. Independently,
the actual base projection of B.119--B.123 kills the four contacts
and carries $v,m$ to the specified free basis of $F_2$. Its
restriction to the source is $p_{\mathrm{base}}$. These two actual
factorizations give the first containment in
\eqref{eq:B124-kernel-bounds}. Extending the maps continuously and
using the actual embedded completed free targets gives the second.
No assertion about the size of the intersection of these two
kernels is needed.

\emph{B.124.4. Exact discrete and continuous abelianizations.}
Compose $\rho_+$ with abelianization in the five indicated source
coordinates, and let $\lambda:\Gamma^0\to\mathbb Z$ be the actual
base character satisfying
\[
 \lambda(m)=1,\qquad \lambda(v)=\lambda(s_i)=0.
\]
The combined homomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B124-six-character-detector}
 (\rho_+^{\mathrm{ab}},\lambda):\Gamma^0
       \longrightarrow\mathbb Z^5\times\mathbb Z
\end{equation}
sends the six actual source generators, in their displayed order,
to the standard basis. Since they generate $H_{\mathrm{src}}$,
they induce a surjection $\mathbb Z^6\to H_{\mathrm{src}}^{\mathrm{ab}}$.
The detector in \eqref{eq:B124-six-character-detector} is an inverse
on those generators. Thus the surjection is an isomorphism.

The same proof works directly for the actual compact source image.
It is topologically generated by the six images of the free
profinite source generators, so they give a continuous surjection
\[
 \widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\,6}\longrightarrow
       (\overline H_{\mathrm{src}})^{\mathrm{ab}}_{\mathrm{cont}}.
\]
The continuous extension of
\eqref{eq:B124-six-character-detector} is its inverse. This proves
\eqref{eq:B124-source-abelianization} without identifying
$\overline H_{\mathrm{src}}$ with $\widehat H_{\mathrm{src}}$.
The retraction used here is not asserted to commute with the
original selected-cone involution, so it gives no fixed-source
equality by itself.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact marked verification.}
The independent word certificate retains the complete gap-conjugated
five-tuple, the actual reflection, both inverse-basis substitutions
and the reflection square. All twenty basis compositions and five
reflection-square identities pass. The proof above supplies the
ambient quotient, topology and abelianization consequences; they
are not inferred from a rank calculation.
