## Legibility and comparison of witnesses

Much of the running prose is readable, although low letter joins can collapse several syllables into one horizontal stroke. Tall ascenders, descenders, detached dots, word spacing and insertion leaders often distinguish the competing readings. Every supplied leaf has now been read against both witnesses and independently reread at its difficult places; all 328 token-different blind boxes received explicit crop decisions. Residual doubt is a completed editorial decision, not an unexamined gap.

The least secure passages remain dense cancellations and overwriting, tiny nested indices, compressed marginal words, faint accents and ambiguous terminal hooks. The final text marks those uncertainties locally. For example, the cancelled group name beneath E on 092 retains a tentative Gal reading, and the NB clause on 102 retains several possible cancelled forms. The opening class symbol on 082, map kappa/k on 088/090, the xi-like raised form on 098, and the homology subscript 1/n on 102 retain separate doubts. A clear neighboring formula does not supply hidden letters. Sources: the named pages' changes and doubt records;092 B021;098 B005;102 B009/B011.
