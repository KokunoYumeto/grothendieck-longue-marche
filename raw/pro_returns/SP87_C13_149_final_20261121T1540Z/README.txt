SP87 / cote 149 — diplomatic transcription and audit

The authoritative consolidated text is in pages/ and in
SP87_C13_149_transcription.tex. It is a LaTeX fragment, without a preamble.
Use the PREAMBLE.tex supplied with the source archive. No TeX compilation
was performed during this work.

The changes, collation, decisions, inventory, doubts, notes, check events,
hand log, feedback and progress log document the editorial decisions.
Crops are evidence images enlarged from the supplied scans; every recorded
box uses the original scan pixel coordinates. A doubt is a transcription
decision and is not a gap to be silently filled from mathematical context.

Readers' intermediate drafts and reports are retained as work documentation.
They are superseded by the files in pages/ and the reviewed reports. The
second/ directory records the first and blind second readings separately;
the first readings are preserved even where the audit changes the final text.

Recovery from a checkpoint: extract this archive as SP87_C13_149_work in the
workspace, restore the original supplied ZIP separately as source/, and
consult progress.log plus review/reader_assignments.tsv and the reviewed
reports before resuming. prepare_page.py can recreate the full-resolution
halves and the assigned-page witness packets from the source archive.
The source attachments, original scans, original witness texts, temporary
halves, generated witness packets (input.json), bytecode and earlier ZIPs
are intentionally excluded. All retained crops and work documentation are
included. A checkpoint is cumulative; it does not depend on an older ZIP.

Scripts contain the original workspace location as ROOT. If restoring into
a different workspace, update ROOT in the scripts before running them.

Final coverage: all 23 supplied leaves 079–098 and 100–102. Scan099 was not
supplied. All 23 blind audits are complete. Each second reader differed from
the first reader and saved the independent reading before comparison. The
466 anonymous boxes yielded 328 token-different boxes, all explicitly
adjudicated; 122 boxes changed a reading or its doubt state. These are box
counts, not token error rates or a comparative accuracy percentage.

The canonical reviewed reports feed all generated tables. Earlier drafts,
proposals and static-audit snapshots remain as historical documentation;
final reconciliation is recorded in review/final_completion_report.md.
The 24 transcription hashes are in review/frozen_hashes.tsv and progress.log.
