# Sheet feedback — completed adjudications within scans 079–095

This proposed replacement is based on the canonical reviewed records and completed second-reading adjudications for **079–095**. It describes final decisions within that scope. First-pass observations that were later superseded are identified as historical. It makes no manuscript-wide accuracy ranking or final numerical claim.

## Refinements supported by the completed records

### The written equals shorthand can have a hooked lower entry

The early distinction between a small barred m on 083/086 and a plain equals sign on other leaves is superseded. The completed decisions on 083 and 086 identify two strokes, a hooked lower entry and no independent m humps; 091 also retains the written equals shorthand. The sign remains literal in the transcription rather than being expanded to *même*. The visual decision is local to the recorded occurrences; semantic equivalence is not a reason to erase a differently written letter form elsewhere. Sources: `second/149_scan083_adjudications.tsv`, B009; `second/149_scan086_adjudications.tsv`, B009; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B002.

### Small field K must be compared at the relevant scale

Final adjudication selects K in the specified nested field indices on 086, 088, 090 and 093 and in both N-identifications on 091. The recorded distinction is a sharp junction, short rising branch and short returning foot, compared with the same-page larger K and the documented controls. This supersedes the first consolidation’s eta preferences and the later K/U alternatives on 091. The opening rounded U on 091 remains U. The separate Greek-kappa/Latin-k map labels on 088 and 090 remain doubtful; they are not covered by the field-K decision. Sources: `second/149_scan086_adjudications.tsv`, B008; `second/149_scan088_adjudications.tsv`, B008/B010; `second/149_scan090_adjudications.tsv`, B008/B021; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B002/B004/B012; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B007.

### Connector decisions depend on the individual endpoint

The first-pass arrow interpretation is not uniformly confirmed. On 088, completed review changes the upper middle link and the first three lower-row links to headless shafts. On 080, the first link of (13) and second link of (16) retain arrow/shaft uncertainty, while the first link of (17) prefers a shaft. On 089, the pi-to-E link in (27) also becomes shaft-preferred. Conversely, the small angled heads and downward maps of 087 are retained. The exact-sequence setting does not supply a missing arrowhead. Vertical equality, tilted isomorphism and an arrow carrying a wave remain separate written constructions. Sources: `second/149_scan080_adjudications.tsv`, B005/B009/B010; `second/149_scan087_adjudications.tsv`, B016; `second/149_scan088_adjudications.tsv`, B002; `second/149_scan089_adjudications.tsv`, B002/B014.

### Abbreviations and punctuation can be lost through fluent expansion

The final 091 reading is **tr.-scend/tr.-scendance**, not a certain expanded *transcendance*: the initial point and dash are explicit, while the final return does not settle the full ending. On 095 the raised parenthesis retains a p-with-raised-r short form, with *par* as an alternative. On 094, **pt.** remains singular as written after **des**. Literal colons replace earlier à readings after **cet hom** on 090, after **pt** and **relatif** on 094, and after **justement** on 095. These are source forms, including awkward syntax; they should not be smoothed into a grammatical paraphrase. Sources: `second/149_scan090_adjudications.tsv`, B003; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B007; `second/149_scan094_adjudications.tsv`, B009/B011/B014; `second/149_scan095_adjudications.tsv`, B003/B013.

### A tentative earlier layer can become recoverable without becoming certain

The final target in (29) on 092 now has tentative cancelled **Gal** beneath a clear replacement E, sharing k(s). The historical first-stage statement that the term could not be divided into layers is no longer the final decision. Similarly, the small cancelled sublabel on 091 is **de/d**, replacing an editorial question-mark placeholder. The letters remain doubtful. On 087, the detached last marginal line also retains uncertainty about its cancellation state, rather than inheriting the clear cancellation of the two lines above. Sources: `second/149_scan092_adjudications.tsv`, B021; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B006; `second/149_scan087_adjudications.tsv`, B003.

### Insertion position and cancellation scope are independent of wording

The replacement after cancelled **voit que** on 094 is raised and must be marked as an insertion even though its ending remains **regarder./regardons**. On 088, **le groupe de décomposition** belongs before E_tildeK after **Ainsi**. On 095, **de deuxième type** attaches before **d’extensions**. Correct words in the wrong layer or attached to the wrong phrase are still transcription errors. Sources: `second/149_scan094_adjudications.tsv`, B004; `second/149_scan088_adjudications.tsv`, B016; `review/149_scan095_reviewed.json`, insertion inventory and hand observation for **de deuxième type**.

### Continuous pen strokes can imitate emphasis or deletion

The recorded stroke-continuity checks distinguish the g return in **prolonge** on 083 and **généralisant** on 092 from an independent underline. The local f crossbar in **de façon** on 094 does not cancel the phrase. These are useful controls for apparent rules crossing nearby writing, but they do not justify removing a separately evidenced cancellation elsewhere. Sources: `review/149_scan083_reviewed.json`, hand observation on **prolonge**; `review/149_scan092_reviewed.json`, hand observation on **généralisant**; `review/149_scan094_reviewed.json`, hand observation on **de façon** and adjudication B014.

### Small points, reference digits and accents require explicit dispositions

On 095, the point beside nested B and the two points before the second N are retained under local alternatives; the first-pass explanation that these marks were not promoted to text is superseded. On 089, one overwritten reference has underlying **6/5** and live **5/6**; the expected reference number does not settle the layers. On 085, the display label remains **(22)/(21)**. The spelling **complete** on 085, **tantot** on 093 and **brulant** on 095 is preserved without supplied accents, while the circumflex in **tantôt/tantot** on 094 remains doubtful. Sources: `second/149_scan095_adjudications.tsv`, B007/B019 and canonical accent observation; `second/149_scan089_adjudications.tsv`, B006; `second/149_scan085_adjudications.tsv`, B003/B012; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B020; `second/149_scan094_adjudications.tsv`, B006.

## Witness errors and first-consolidation errors

These categories should remain distinct. C preserves some literal forms or layers that H expands or omits; H is supported at other locations, including **ni** on 091 and **inverse** on 093. Both witnesses and the first consolidation expanded the abbreviated form on 091 to *transcendance*. Some important corrections, such as the withdrawn eta preference and overread arrowheads, are corrections to the first consolidation rather than a new blanket judgment against one witness. Sources: `review/149_scan091_reviewed.json`, hand observations on **ni** and **tr.-scend**; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B017; the field-K and connector decisions cited above.

Author pagination and later foliation must also remain distinct where the records establish the hand and position, as for 44/88 on 089, 45/90 on 091 and 47/94 on 095. That local evidence should not be turned into an automatic attribution for every numeral elsewhere. Sources: the respective adjudications B001/B019, B001/B020 and B001/B031.
