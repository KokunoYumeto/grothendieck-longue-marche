- **Small K versus eta, scans 090, 093, 096 and 102.** The saved 090 `d_insertion` and `final_scindage`, 093 `composite`, and 096 `first_group` and `rational_point` crops were compared after 102's `point_U`, `rat_K`, `c_formula`, and `d_formula` had been inspected.
