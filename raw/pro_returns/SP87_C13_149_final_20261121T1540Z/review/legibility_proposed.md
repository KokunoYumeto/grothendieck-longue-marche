# Legibility assessment — completed adjudications within scans 079–095

This proposed assessment summarizes the canonical reviewed records and completed second-reading adjudications for **079–095**. It is an assessment of those records, with no new source-image reading or mathematical verification. First-pass method accounts remain historical; final readings and remaining doubts are taken from the completed adjudications and current page text. No manuscript-wide totals or accuracy percentages are asserted.

## What is recoverable

Much of the running prose is recoverable despite compressed low letter sequences. Tall stems, detached dots, short terminal returns and the placement of additions often distinguish otherwise plausible words. The completed record confirms, for example, **ni** at the opening of 091, the short form **l’ens** on 091–092, and **inverse** on 093. These decisions are grounded in the recorded strokes rather than a fluent reconstruction of the sentence. Sources: `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B002/B018; `second/149_scan092_adjudications.tsv`, B003/B016; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B017.

Cross-leaf handwriting comparison also resolves some small field indices that remained uncertain on first pass. The specified K forms on 086/088/090/091/093 are now settled in the canonical page text. This does not make all small symbols secure: the local kappa/Latin-k map labels on 088/090, the first retraced gamma/xi occurrence on 082, and the crowded pi/T target on 093 retain their separate doubts. Sources: `second/149_scan090_adjudications.tsv`, B008/B021; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B004/B012; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B007; `review/149_scan082_reviewed.json`, current unresolved class-sign entry; `second/149_scan088_adjudications.tsv`, B014; `second/149_scan090_adjudications.tsv`, B004; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B008.

## Where uncertainty remains

Dense cancellation is a major limit. Readable surviving letters can justify a tentative word while still failing to expose its whole interior. The added doubts over cancelled **groupe** and **schéma** on 081 and the cancelled label and G_m on 082 are examples of reduced certainty after independent review. Conversely, review can recover a candidate from a formerly undifferentiated patch: cancelled **de/d** on 091 and tentative **Gal** beneath replacement E on 092 are now represented explicitly, without being treated as certain. Sources: `second/149_scan081_adjudications.tsv`, B002/B014; `second/149_scan082_adjudications.tsv`, B015/B016/B021; `second/149_scan091_adjudications.tsv`, B006; `second/149_scan092_adjudications.tsv`, B021.

Uncertainty can concern a letter, an accent, a terminal inflection, punctuation, or the state and attachment of ink. Those cases should remain distinguishable. Examples include **lacets/lacet** on 088; **peut/par** and **s’identifier/s’identifie** on 089; **tantôt/tantot** on 094; and the altered closing-parenthesis form, **de/donc/du** and following two points on 095. The doubtful cancellation of the final marginal line on 087 is a state question, not merely a disputed word. Sources: `second/149_scan088_adjudications.tsv`, B015; `second/149_scan089_adjudications.tsv`, B016/B017; `second/149_scan094_adjudications.tsv`, B006; `second/149_scan095_adjudications.tsv`, B018/B019; `second/149_scan087_adjudications.tsv`, B003.

Enlargement improves inspection of existing pixels but cannot restore ink hidden by an opaque strike or absent beyond the paper edge. A crop boundary must also be distinguished from a source boundary. On 095 the anonymous crop clipped the end of **regarder**; the recorded additional root crop supplied the missing viewing area, and the word was retained. On 094 the expanded second box still did not settle the faint circumflex. These are different reasons for caution and should not be conflated. Sources: `second/149_scan095_adjudications.tsv`, B003; `second/149_scan094_adjudications.tsv`, B006.

## Comparing the witnesses

The records support local comparisons, not an overall accuracy percentage. C preserves some literal short forms, shafts and correction layers that H expands, regularizes or omits. H is supported at other disputed words, including **ni** and **inverse**. Agreement also fails locally: the abbreviated **tr.-scend/tr.-scendance** on 091 was expanded in both witnesses and the first consolidation. The completed audit therefore checks agreement as well as disagreement and can revise the consolidation independently of which witness it originally followed. Sources: `review/149_scan086_reviewed.json`, hand observations on **tt**, the written equals form and overwritten subscript; `review/149_scan091_reviewed.json`, hand observations on **ni** and **tr.-scend**; `second/149_scan093_adjudications.tsv`, B017.

A logged place can cover several words or symbols; one place may differ from both witnesses; a certainty-only decision is different from a changed preferred reading; and macro or font conventions can encode the same source symbol. Counts of places therefore are not token error rates. The operative prompt and `scripts/aggregate.py` maintain separate records for these purposes. Final numerical summaries should be generated only from the approved records for the completed run.

## Authorial doubt, editorial doubt and mathematical scope

The author’s own qualifications remain part of the text. The parenthetical **le diable sait** on 081 concerns conormal versus normal; the caution **il faudrait quand même les/le vérifier !** on 079 is likewise authorial, with an editorial doubt only over the article. Such passages should not be described as editorial uncertainty about the surrounding mathematics. Sources: `review/149_scan081_reviewed.json`, notes and final page; `second/149_scan079_adjudications.tsv`, B015.

The source can also be legible while mathematically awkward. Literal punctuation, mismatched local forms and unexpanded abbreviations remain in the page text. Recorded conditional mathematical checks are listed separately in `check_events`; neither the transcription process nor this documentation audit certifies every asserted equivalence, exact sequence or geometric specialization. Earlier statements that a first reader made no check are historical and must not imply that the canonical check list remains empty after a later root check. Sources: `review/149_scan082_reviewed.json` and `review/149_scan085_reviewed.json`, the appended checks and current `check_events`; operative prompt, CHECK_EVENTS requirements.
