### 079-N1 — centre-free pullback construction
For a group P with Z(P)=1, the map P to Inn(P) is an isomorphism. Pulling Aut(P) → Out(P) back along a homomorphism B → Out(P) gives an extension 1 → P → B×_{Out(P)}Aut(P) → B → 1. Surjectivity follows from the definition of Out(P) as the quotient. This verifies the algebraic construction invoked in the live margin, conditional on the asserted outer action; it does not establish the geometric fibration hypotheses. The margin visibly writes pi_1(S) → pi, although the main-text boundary in a homotopy exact sequence is pi_2(S) → pi_1(fibre); retain the written 1.

### 080-N1 — quotient versus torsion in Kummer
In the long exact cohomology sequence for multiplication by n on G_m (where this Kummer sequence is valid), the cokernel of multiplication by n on Pic(S) injects into H²(S,mu_n); its quotient is the kernel of multiplication by n on H²(S,G_m). This distinguishes Pic(S)/nPic(S) from n-torsion, agreeing with the explicit definition below (17). It is a notation consistency check, not a proof of inverse-limit exactness or of (15).

### 081-N1 — conditional injection into completion
If A is a finitely generated abelian group then A → lim_n A/nA is injective: an element in its kernel lies in nA for every n; its free part vanishes by choosing n larger than the absolute value of any nonzero coordinate, and its torsion part vanishes by taking n divisible by the torsion exponent. This checks the group-theoretic implication used in (18), conditional on the page's finite-generation assertion; finite generation of Pic(S) and its geometric hypotheses were not independently proved.
