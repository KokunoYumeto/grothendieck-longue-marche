## 142_scan013

Manuscript page 6, pencil 12. The author returns to the isotopy category of anabelian oriented surfaces and calls its completion “compactifiée profinie”. He describes Hom spaces as locally compact, countable at infinity, totally disconnected and metrizable, with “toutes les qualités !” and adds “Et pas de pts isolés, par surcroît…”. This last blanket assertion needs a low-complexity exception: for the thrice-punctured sphere, self-coverings have degree one by Euler characteristic, and the isotopy classes of self-homeomorphisms form a finite group (trivial with individually fixed punctures, or S_3 if they may permute), so its completed self-Hom is finite and has isolated points. This is a mathematical note, not an alteration of the source. The second paragraph proposes a purely algebraic-geometric description independent of the complex topology. The first two field letters are plain C; the final one has the doubled stroke of blackboard C. Their common meaning does not justify normalizing their ink.
