## Cross-page notation review

Recurring forms were compared across the supplied sheets, using the whole-page readings, the original-resolution halves, and the saved crops cited in the local rows. The following conventions consolidate those observations; they do not erase clear local slips or ambiguous overwritten states.

The explicit Sigma/E slips in complements on015,017,018 and021 remain. |

Actual indices are not corrected to fit the preferred sign. |

The final self-indexed alpha on025 remains alpha despite the mathematical expectation of i. |

Differences of implementation that draw the same sign—such as ne/neq or the two witnesses' macro names for a visibly identical disjoint join—are not counted as reading changes. Differences in visible sign, placement, revision state, or primary candidate are recorded. At unresolved places, consistency means retaining the same stated alternatives and scope, not claiming a stroke is present because a neighbouring formula needs it.

The blind audits rechecked the same recurring forms independently across all fifteen pages. They preserved the circle/zero doubts and local alphabet differences, recovered or qualified small revision traces, and did not use a shared witness reading to erase a doubtful mark. The lower Sigma on018 is explicitly plain/barred uncertain after comparison with ordinary detached-cap Sigma forms on019–020; no definite omitted closure bar is counted there. The possible earlier Z beneath Y on022 remains an alternative rather than a recovered certain letter.

# Legibility and witnesses

The principal body writing is readable in its full-resolution context, but the author frequently reduces word endings to a short horizontal return and reuses strokes when revising a letter. Confidence is lowest in cancelled words, overlaid capitals and indices, edge-clipped parentheses, tiny raised circles, and darkened plus/minus signs. Slanted notes become substantially clearer when rotated, but black cancellation swaths still conceal some letters. The drawing sheet and bibliographical cover require different checks: individual strokes and tiny labels on the former, dates, repetitions and overwritten numbers on the latter.

C is generally more faithful to revision states and literal irregularities in these pages. It is nevertheless a draft: the changes tables distinguish newly recovered ink, changed primary readings, and actual resolutions of its doubts. H often supplies fluent wording or expanded abbreviations, loses an underlying letter, or treats editorial descriptions as a substitute for a placed annotation. It also supplies useful alternatives, including the clipped parenthesis after K on scan022. Agreement between the witnesses has never been treated as independent evidence for a reading.

The primary notation is kept consistent while local limitations are stated: capital Pi names partitions, lowercase pi with subscript zero names component sets, hats and horizontal closure bars remain distinct, and a short transverse on a union is recorded geometrically where its precise intention is not legible. A clear written Sigma in a complement remains Sigma even when the argument requires E. Unbalanced punctuation, repeated labels and unrepaired agreement are retained.

All fifteen pages have complete first readings and independently committed blind readings: 342 prescribed regions, 324 token-different boxes personally adjudicated by the main reader, and 27 amended boxes. Different amounts of crop context, macro spelling and independently drawn TikZ coordinates account for many differences; those are not correction counts. The final doubt table retains 184 unresolved entries, including low-confidence cancellation and overwrite readings. Visual inspection and structural validation were completed without OCR, tracing, external research or TeX compilation. The lack of compilation means the archive makes no claim of a rendered-layout check; the transcription source, explicit uncertainties and saved facsimile crops are the reviewable evidence.
