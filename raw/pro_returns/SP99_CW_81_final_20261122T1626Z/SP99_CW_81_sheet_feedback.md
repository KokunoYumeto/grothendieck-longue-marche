## Recurring issues beyond the sheet

No additional recurring error class outside the ten categories of the supplied sheet was established. The following local refinements make those categories more specific for this piece; they should remain checks against the ink rather than automatic replacement rules.

- **A high transverse on a large union is not a projecting basal bar.** The middle union on scan017, the middle representation on scan021, the last union on scan023 and the main union on scan025 have additional high strokes. The first union on scan023 instead has an unresolved internal diagonal. They must not all be converted to a standard disjoint-join glyph on the authority of a witness's explanatory note. Ordinary round unions and visibly footed disjoint joins also occur.
- **Overwriting is local to a letter or index.** The Sigma/E revisions on scans015–018 and020, the Pi/U and I/K revisions on020, and the V/E revision on021 leave earlier strokes inside the live glyph. Conversely, retracing of E_beta on023 does not by itself certify an earlier Sigma. Preserve only the old forms that have separable evidence, with doubts when necessary.
- **Crossing strokes need an identified origin.** On017 and023, a descender from an inserted or cancelled q can reach the following line. On022, the boundary of a cancelled proof passes below small live additions. On024, the underline below un is not a cancellation. These cases caused added words, altered deletion states, or misplaced insertions in H.
- **The grammatical noun implied by a formula can mislead the prose reading.** On024, H supplies feminine une/contenue/telle where the ink has masculine un/contenu/tel. On018 and022, other compressed endings remain genuinely unresolved. Grammar is not sufficient to decide a terminal stroke.
- **An abbreviation's point and a word's accent are separate local observations.** The first ens on024 has no point; def on024 is unaccented; the final déf on026 has a detached accent; mut with raised t and a point on025 is not an expanded word. The unusual determinés spelling on025 retains its distinct final acute and lacks the first acute.
- **A complete formula box must include displaced accents, bars and replacements.** The last closure bar on024 sits unusually high above the last baseline, and the changes around the inserted formulas on026 occupy more than one baseline. A narrow crop of only the letter bodies would lose evidence.
- **Shared primary readings can still need revision or explicit doubt.** The local de before the edge-word on024 and sait/voit on020 illustrate medium-confidence alternatives to a shared C/H reading. Their uncertainty and confidence levels matter; the count of changed places does not turn them into certain corrections.
- **An unfinished expression must stay unfinished.** On025, H's closing set brace is not on the scan. The visible self-indexed inclusion also remains as written. A simple subscript repair would still yield an overbroad characterization, as the counterexample in note81-025-M3 shows. Neither syntactic completion nor expected mathematics supplies ink.

The category totals above count places changed against at least one witness, not all inspected occurrences of that kind. A retained doubt, an added uncertainty with an unchanged primary, or a shared correctly transcribed sign may have an evidence row but contributes no correction count. A resolved C doubt does count, as required by the task. In particular, circle/zero alternatives are kept explicit; a primary circle reading against H's zero is counted consistently as a notation difference without claiming the tiny round stroke resolves its interpretation.

The blind pass also exposed the risk of counting a plausible new interpretation as a resolved correction. The possible lower closure bar on018 and possible Z-to-Y sequence on022 remain explicit alternatives. New uncertainties with unchanged primary readings do not inflate correction totals.
