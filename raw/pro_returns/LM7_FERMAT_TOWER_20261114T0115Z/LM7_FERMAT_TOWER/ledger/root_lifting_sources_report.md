Drinfeld, *Leningrad Mathematical Journal*2 (1991), Section4, constructs the arrows from the full profinite group to its pro-l and rational pro-unipotent versions. Propositions5.5--5.9 concern the rational pro-unipotent associator space and its graded comparison; Proposition5.8 proves successive lifting there. The final remark in Section6 compares the degree-seven graded Lie spaces. We checked these statements in the original English translation. The required comparison in D.13 goes in the reverse direction from the constructed profinite-to-pro-l arrow, and a peripheral full profinite lift is still required. No reverse lifting result is inferred from those passages.
