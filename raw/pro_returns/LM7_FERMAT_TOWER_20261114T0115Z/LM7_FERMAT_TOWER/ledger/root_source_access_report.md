## A checked lifting-source route

The published original of Herfort and Zalesskii, *Virtually free pro-p
groups*, Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS 118 (2013), 193–211,
DOI 10.1007/s10240-013-0051-4, was opened at
https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-013-0051-4.pdf.
Section 6, especially Theorems 6.2 and 6.3, was read for the residual
order-two deck action. Its conjugator is an unrestricted free-group
automorphism; the proof supplies no preservation of the prescribed cusp
classes or simultaneous odd deck action. That precise additional condition
therefore remains in the present lifting calculation.

The author's publication page https://mat.unb.br/~pz/publication.html
identifies corrections to Lemmas 2.6 and 3.8. Both linked files were opened:
the corrected 2017 version
https://mat.unb.br/~pz/arquivos/IHEScorr%20lemma2.613.pdf
and the July 2018 corrigendum/addendum
https://mat.unb.br/~pz/arquivos/permext5.pdf.
The latter replaces the relevant centralizer statement and revises the
permutation-extension proof. No claim of simultaneous peripheral
conjugacy is extracted from these corrections.

The primary paper by Porto and Zalesskii, *Cyclic extensions of free
pro-p groups and p-adic modules*, Mathematical Research Letters 20
(2013), 537–545, was also opened through the publisher. Its module
decompositions and cyclic-extension results do not themselves supply
the specified simultaneous conjugator. This route is retained as a
checked source attempt, not as a completed reverse-lifting argument.
