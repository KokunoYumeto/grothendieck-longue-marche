# Focused E.90 global path and Kummer audit

Scope: E90.6--E90.11 and the proof E90.27--E90.30 in
`research_e90/sections/E_relative_multiples.tex`. This is a focused proof
audit, not a claim to have independently rebuilt the entire E.90 certificate.

**Result: PASS.**

The rational involution `[x:y:z] -> [x:z:y]` carries the two specified
tangents exactly. When an element of the kernel of the actual arithmetic
quotient acts trivially at both endpoints, its difference on any geometric
path centralizes the free pro-three group. The Magnus argument makes that
centre trivial, so the entire path torsor factors through the same quotient.
The displayed path cocycle and the cusp power cocycle obey the literal
semidirect cocycle identity, and a Tate coboundary is the stated geometric
conjugation.

The actual map `-y/x` has value 1 at the source cusp, order 1 at the target
cusp, and leading coefficient -1 in its specified parameter. The compatible
odd-power roots of -1 are rational. Consequently the induced path cocycle
is a coboundary and the map supplies the asserted left inverse on Kummer
classes, globally and locally.

Neither Q nor Q3 has nontrivial three-power roots of unity. The finite-level
boundary coefficients in the inverse-limit argument are therefore unique;
representative alignment and injectivity are exact. Unique factorization
gives the restricted exponent module with discrete finite-exponent
quotients. The local decomposition, the integral logarithms and the exact
identity `log(-2)/log(4)=1/2` give both directions of the localization formula.
All infinite sums converge in the stated uniform power topology. The
triangular change of Kummer basis retains the actual two evaluation
constants and yields the claimed local section literally.
