\subsection{Joining the elliptic comparison to the evaluated Frobenius quotient}

\begin{statement}{E.53}{Proved}{An exact common quotient and a class-three refinement with kernel of rank~329}
Use the actual based maps of E.50 and E.54 over
$K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$. To distinguish their targets, put
\[
 D=N/\gamma_4N,\qquad
 B=N/(F_{\rm ell}''\gamma_4N),\qquad
 C=F_h/\gamma_4F_h .
 \tag{E53.1}
\]
Here $F_{\rm ell}$ is the group of the once-punctured elliptic
curve reached by $[3]$, and $F_h$ is the free rank-two group
of the thrice-punctured line reached by $h$.
The maps $D\twoheadrightarrow B$ and
$D\twoheadrightarrow C$ have their actual tangential
origins and arithmetic actions from those two results.
Let $I=\ker(N\to T_3E)$ be the full peripheral kernel,
let $K_0=I'\gamma_4N/\gamma_4N$, and let
$K_h=\ker(D\to C)$.

Write $x,y,c,d,e$ for the Hall generators of $C$ in
(E54.3). The image of $K_0$ in $C$ is the exact closed
normal subgroup
\[
        P'=\langle c^3,d^3,e\rangle
              \simeq\mathbb Z_3^3.
 \tag{E53.2}
\]
Consequently the maximal common quotient of $B$ and $C$
induced by their maps from $D$ is
\[
 Q=D/(K_0K_h)
      =C/\langle c^3,d^3,e\rangle .
 \tag{E53.3}
\]
In particular every element of $Q$ has unique coordinates
$x^a y^b c^{\bar n}d^{\bar r}$ with
$a,b\in\mathbb Z_3$ and $\bar n,\bar r\in\mathbb Z/3$.
These are group coordinates with the quotient of the
Hall law (E54.18).

There is an exact based arithmetic sequence
\[
 1\longrightarrow J
 \longrightarrow D
 \longrightarrow B\times_Q C
 \longrightarrow1,\qquad
       J=K_0\cap K_h\simeq\mathbb Z_3^{329}.
 \tag{E53.4}
\]
The fiber product uses the actual two maps to (E53.3);
the map on the right is surjective.
All the maps and kernels in (E53.2)--(E53.4) are
$G_K$-equivariant.

Put $V=N^{\rm ab}$, $T=\ker(V\to T_3E)$, and
$W=F_h^{\rm ab}=\mathbb Z_3(1)^2$.
Write $f:V\to W$ for the map induced by $h$.
Then $f$ is onto and its cusp image is exactly
\[
                f(T)=3\mathbb Z_3x+\mathbb Z_3y.
 \tag{E53.5}
\]
The induced lower central filtration on $J$ is given
by the integral kernels
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{gr}_1J&=0,\\
 \operatorname{gr}_2J&=
       \ker\bigl(\Lambda^2T\longrightarrow\Lambda^2W\bigr),\\
 \operatorname{gr}_3J&=
       \ker\bigl([T,\operatorname{Lie}_2(V)]
                         \longrightarrow\operatorname{Lie}_3(W)\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E53.6}
\]
Their ranks are $0,27,302$. The quotient in (E53.4)
has torsion-free graded lattices of ranks
\[
                    10,\quad18,\quad28.
 \tag{E53.7}
\]
The map on the degree-two part of $K_0$ has image
$3\mathbb Z_3c$; the map on its degree-three part
has image $3\mathbb Z_3d+\mathbb Z_3e$. These precise
integral image lattices are retained in (E53.3).
In chosen integral coordinates the projection
$B\times_Q C\to B$ has kernel (E53.2);
its three coordinates have degrees two, three, three.

The actual based action on the common quotient $Q$
factors through the cyclotomic character itself.
Put $r=(\chi-1)/3$ for $\chi\in1+3\mathbb Z_3$.
The action is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 x&\longmapsto x^\chi,&
 y&\longmapsto y^\chi c^r d^{r(r-1)/2},\\
 c&\longmapsto c d^r,&
 d&\longmapsto d,
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E53.8}
\]
where the exponents of $c,d$ are taken modulo three.
At the actual Frobenius of E.54, these images are
$x^4,y^4c,cd,d$, respectively.
This supplies the literal compatibility map between
the elliptic quotient and the newly evaluated
class-three Frobenius quotient.

The integral certificate
\texttt{a\_combined\_quotient.py} records the actual
cusp matrix, a unimodular cusp basis change,
the two graded kernel bases, and integer right
inverses for the quotient maps of ranks 18 and 28.
It also verifies the group identities generating
(E53.2), 375 tensor substitutions, 270 normality
identities and 72 common-action identities.
The manuscript passage is \S51.16,
folios~733v--736v, scans~172--178, equations~(371)--(379).
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The cusp lattice and its actual subgroup image.}
Use the eight positive peripheral classes away from
$O$, ordered as $A_0,A_1,A_2,B_0,B_1,B_2,C_1,C_2$.
They are a basis of the primitive cusp lattice $T$.
The valuation of $h$ gives the first coordinate of
$f$, and that of $1-h$ gives the second. Thus
(E31.5) gives the literal matrix
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 0&0&0&0&0&0&0&-3\\
 0&0&0&1&1&1&0&-3
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{E53.9}
\]
For example the new cusp basis
\[
 -C_2-3B_0,\ B_0,\ A_0,\ A_1,\ A_2,\
 B_1-B_0,\ B_2-B_0,\ C_1
 \tag{E53.10}
\]
is unimodular, and its image is $3x,y,0,\ldots,0$.
This proves (E53.5) with its factor three.

Let $P$ be the image of $I$ in $C$.
The map $h_*:N\to F_h$ is surjective by E.54,
so $h_*(N')=F_h'$. As $N'\subset I$, the group
$P$ contains $\gamma_2C$.
Its image in $C^{\rm ab}$ is exactly (E53.5).
It follows that
\[
       P=\{w\in C:\ w^{\rm ab}\in
                    3\mathbb Z_3x+\mathbb Z_3y\}
         =\langle x^3,y,c,d,e\rangle .
 \tag{E53.11}
\]
This is equality of closed subgroups.
Derived subgroups commute with a continuous
surjection of compact groups, so the image
of $K_0$ is $P'$.
The Hall law gives the actual identities
\[
 [x^3,y]=c^3d^3,\qquad
 [x^3,c]=d^3,\qquad [y,c]=e.
 \tag{E53.12}
\]
The elements $c^3,d^3,e$ commute and are
independent in the integral Hall coordinates.
Their subgroup is normal in $C$:
conjugation by $x$ changes $c^3$ by $d^3$,
conjugation by $y$ changes it by $e^3$,
and $d,e$ are central.
Equations (E53.12) put this subgroup in $P'$;
the generators in (E53.11) commute modulo it.
This proves the exact equality (E53.2), including
closedness, normality and its underlying free
three-adic group.

\emph{The common quotient and the integral kernel.}
The product $K_0K_h$ of the two compact normal
subgroups is closed. Taking its quotient gives
(E53.3), with the displayed Hall coordinates.
The image of $D$ in $B\times C$ lies in the
fiber product over $Q$.
Given a compatible pair, lift its $B$ coordinate
to $D$. The discrepancy in $C$ belongs to the
image of $K_0$ by (E53.3); lift that discrepancy
to $K_0$ and correct the chosen element of $D$.
This proves surjectivity to the actual fiber
product and the kernel formula in (E53.4).
Any common quotient obtained from the two
maps out of $D$ kills both $K_0$ and $K_h$,
hence factors through their product quotient.
This proves the stated maximal common quotient.

E.50 proves that $K_0$ is actually abelian
$\mathbb Z_3^{332}$ and that its induced
graded pieces are the saturated lattices
$\Lambda^2T$ and $[T,\operatorname{Lie}_2(V)]$.
The map $K_0\to P'$ is onto a free group
$\mathbb Z_3^3$ by (E53.12). Its kernel is
therefore an actual free abelian group of
rank~329. The strict filtered assertion
requires the following additional check.
The degree-three map has image
\[
 [\,3\mathbb Z_3x+\mathbb Z_3y,\,
                        \operatorname{Lie}_2(W)\,]
           =3\mathbb Z_3d+\mathbb Z_3e .
 \tag{E53.13}
\]
This is also $P'\cap\gamma_3C$.
There are actual central preimages:
choose $a,b\in I$ with images $x^3,y$ and
$w\in N'$ with image $c$.
Then $[a,w]$ and $[b,w]$ belong to
$I'\cap\gamma_3N$ and map to $d^3,e$.
Such choices exist by (E53.11) and the
surjectivity on derived subgroups.
Consequently a degree-two kernel class
in $K_0$ can be corrected in its degree-three
coordinates to have zero image in $C$.
This proves exactness on both graded pieces
in (E53.6). The respective image ranks are
one and two, giving 27 and 302.
Both kernels are saturated in the respective
free integral lattices: their quotients
are the free image lattices $3\mathbb Z_3c$
and $3\mathbb Z_3d+\mathbb Z_3e$.
Together with the saturation proved in
E.50, this proves torsion-freeness and
the ranks in (E53.7).

For completeness, these saturation arguments
have an explicit integer coordinate certificate.
Choose a splitting $V=L\oplus T$ and then
adjust the two handle lifts by elements of
$T$. Since $f$ is onto and (E53.5) has
index three in $W$, a unit change of
handle basis and the adjustments in $T$
give the following linear map in a
basis $u,v,t_1,\ldots,t_8$:
\[
 f(u)=x,\qquad f(v)=0,\qquad
 f(t_1)=3x,\qquad f(t_2)=y,\qquad
 f(t_i)=0\quad(i\ge3).
 \tag{E53.14}
\]
Here one first chooses a handle with unit
$x$ coefficient, scales it to coefficient
one, and subtracts its $y$ coefficient
using $t_2$. Subtracting a multiple of
this handle from the other kills its
$x$ coefficient; another $t_2$ adjustment
kills its $y$ coefficient.
The resulting handle images under $p$
still form an integral basis.
This is a chosen coordinate splitting;
the maps and kernel in (E53.4) remain
the previously specified equivariant ones.

For the Hall basis
$[[v_i,v_j],v_k]$ with $i>j$ and $k\ge j$,
write $H_i=f(v_i)$ and
$\Delta_{ij}=\det(H_i,H_j)$.
The actual induced graded maps are
\[
 [v_i,v_j]\longmapsto\Delta_{ij}c,\qquad
 [[v_i,v_j],v_k]\longmapsto
       -\Delta_{ij}(H_{k,x}d+H_{k,y}e).
 \tag{E53.15}
\]
On the 28 cusp-cusp rows, division of the
image coordinate by three leaves a row
with one entry $-1$ and all other entries
zero. On the 304 degree-three kernel rows,
use coordinates relative to $3d,e$.
The two rows $(i,j,k)=(3,0,2),(3,0,3)$
map to the two unit vectors.
Subtracting their multiples from the
other 302 rows gives an integral kernel
basis and an integral direct-sum inverse.
Adding the 17 and 26 retained rows of
E.50 gives integer quotient maps with
integer right inverses of ranks 18 and 28.
The saved certificate checks both products
of each claimed inverse and all brackets
of a degree-two kernel vector with a
weight-one generator. Thus it verifies the
integral normal subgroup calculation.

\emph{The common based arithmetic action.}
The equalities of closed subgroups and all
quotient maps above start with the two
based arithmetic maps of E.50 and E.54.
They therefore give exactly the asserted
$G_K$ actions and equivariance.
In $Q$, (E53.12) shows that $x^3$ is central.
The action $\alpha_{\chi,k}$ of E.54
therefore depends on $k$ only modulo three.
Its genuine image satisfies
$k\equiv(\chi-1)/3\pmod3$.
Substituting this congruence into the
literal Hall images (E54.5), and using
$c^3=d^3=e=1$, gives (E53.8).
Equivalently, the homomorphism
$\chi\mapsto\alpha_{\chi,(\chi-1)/3}$
gives this action, since
$(\chi\mu-1)/3=(\chi-1)/3+
\chi(\mu-1)/3$.
This proves the factorization and the
stated actual Frobenius images.
\end{proof}
