\subsection{The entire global intrinsic abelian localization image}

\begin{statement}{E.92}{Proved}{Exact global abelian localization, its finite carry and every fibre}
Retain the actual cubic group, source marking and
integral lattices of E.37, E.74 and E.77. Put
\[
 W=T_3T_D,\qquad V=T_3J_{E,D},\qquad E_3=T_3E,
 \qquad0\longrightarrow W\longrightarrow V
                       \xrightarrow{\pi_E}E_3\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{E92.1}
\]
Here $W$ is the span of the eight positive peripheral
vectors, and $\pi_E$ is the literal first-two-coordinate
map in the basis of E.37. Its rational torus is
$T_D=\mathbb G_m^2\times(\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb Q}\mathbb G_m)^3$,
where $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$. The two rational factors
are $A_0,B_0$, and the three ordered pairs are
$(A_1,A_2),(B_1,B_2),(C_1,C_2)$.

Localization of the full global intrinsic abelian
cohomology has the exact sequence
\[
\begin{gathered}
0\longrightarrow\mathscr L
 \longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q,V)
 \xrightarrow{\operatorname{loc}_3}\mathcal I
 \longrightarrow0,\\
\mathcal I=
 \{[z]\in H^1(\mathbb Q_3,V):
      \text{the relative value in E.77 belongs to }W\},\\
\mathcal I\simeq\mathbb Z_3^{14}\oplus C_3^3,\\
\mathscr L\simeq
 \mathscr R_{\mathbb Q}(\mathcal P_{\mathbb Q}\setminus\{2,3\})^2
 \times
 \mathscr R_K(\mathcal P_K\setminus\{\mathfrak l,\mathfrak u,\mathfrak t\})^3.
\end{gathered}
 \tag{E92.2}
\]
The notation $\mathscr R_F$ means restricted
$\mathbb Z_3$ exponent sequences on the displayed
prime ideals, with the inverse-limit topology of
discrete finite-exponent direct sums. The three
omitted prime ideals of $K$ have the actual generators
\[
 \lambda=1-\zeta_3,\qquad u_2=1+\lambda^2,
 \qquad2,\qquad
 \mathfrak l=(\lambda),\quad\mathfrak u=(u_2),\quad
 \mathfrak t=(2).
 \tag{E92.3}
\]
Their norms are $3,13,4$. The sequence (E92.2)
has an explicit continuous $\mathbb Z_3$-linear
section described below. Every localization fibre
is exactly the displayed $\mathscr L$, with its
full forward and inverse coordinates.

In E.87's actual local coordinates the relative
value is
\[
 A=\theta(3f_1+f_2)+\sum_{d\ne O}a_d e_d.
 \tag{E92.4}
\]
Thus the complete global image is the single exact
condition $\theta=0$. This is an abelian localization
criterion, including both image inclusions. In
particular every global intrinsic section at any
geometric level projects to a local abelian input
with $\theta=0$. The local branch with nonzero
relative compact handle in E.87 has no global
abelian preimage. Its exclusion uses the actual
compact filling and localization maps in (E92.1).

All toric localization maps and their inverses are
concrete. The two rational factors use (E90.7)--(E90.9).
For a quadratic factor put $k=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_3)$.
Choose one generator $\pi_{\mathfrak p}$ of every
prime ideal of $K$, using the three literal choices
(E92.3). There are exact topological identifications
\[
 \widehat{K^\times}_3=C_3\oplus\mathscr R_K(\mathcal P_K),
 \qquad
 \widehat{k^\times}_3
  =\lambda^{\mathbb Z_3}\times\mu_3
                      \times u_2^{\mathbb Z_3}\times2^{\mathbb Z_3}.
 \tag{E92.5}
\]
The $C_3$ coordinate is the exponent of the actual
root $\zeta_3$. For $\mathfrak p\ne\mathfrak l$, write
the unique literal local decomposition
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \pi_{\mathfrak p}
   =\omega_{\mathfrak p}\zeta_3^{e_{\mathfrak p}}v_{\mathfrak p},\\
 \quad\omega_{\mathfrak p}\in\{1,-1\},\quad
 e_{\mathfrak p}\in\{0,1,2\},\quad v_{\mathfrak p}\in1+3\mathcal O_k,
 \\
 \log v_{\mathfrak p}
  =a_{\mathfrak p}\log u_2+b_{\mathfrak p}\log(-2).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E92.6}
\]
The coefficients $a_{\mathfrak p},b_{\mathfrak p}$ belong
to $\mathbb Z_3$. The actual completed localization is
\[
 (\epsilon,(t_{\mathfrak p}))\longmapsto
 \left(t_{\mathfrak l},\
  \epsilon+\sum_{\mathfrak p\ne\mathfrak l}e_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p}\bmod3,\
  \sum_{\mathfrak p\ne\mathfrak l}a_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p},\
  \sum_{\mathfrak p\ne\mathfrak l}b_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p}\right).
 \tag{E92.7}
\]
The finite torsion sum and both convergent logarithmic
sums are retained. Over a local point $(v,e,a,b)$,
choose arbitrary restricted exponents on all primes
except the three pivots. The complete inverse fibre is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 t_{\mathfrak l}&=v,\\
 t_{\mathfrak u}&=a-\sum{}'a_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p},\qquad
 t_{\mathfrak t}=b-\sum{}'b_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p},\\
 \epsilon&=e-\sum{}'e_{\mathfrak p}t_{\mathfrak p}\pmod3.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{E92.8}
\]
The primes on the sums exclude all three pivots.
The section takes the literal global completed
element $\lambda^v\zeta_3^e u_2^a2^b$.
In particular the finite $\mu_3$ coordinate is
mapped identically, and no torsion remains in
the kernel of (E92.7).

The finite elliptic carry is also explicit.
Use E.37's representatives of a global class
\[
 (b,\epsilon,\beta),\qquad
 b\in\mathbb Z_3,\quad\epsilon\in\{0,1,2\},\quad
 \beta\in H^1(\mathbb Q,W),
 \tag{E92.9}
\]
where the elliptic lift has value $bf_1+\epsilon f_2$
on $\tau$, its conjugate on $\tau'$, and zero on $c$.
Define the following actual toric Kummer tuple:
\[
       c_T=(3,9,\lambda^2,\lambda,\zeta_3^{-1}\lambda^3)
                 \in\widehat{\mathbb Q^\times}_3^2
                         \times\widehat{K^\times}_3^3.
 \tag{E92.10}
\]
If $\epsilon+\epsilon'=\epsilon''+3k$ with
$\epsilon''\in\{0,1,2\}$, the exact addition law is
\[
 (b,\epsilon,\beta)+(b',\epsilon',\beta')
        =(b+b',\epsilon'',\beta\beta'c_T^k).
 \tag{E92.11}
\]
This formula holds for the global set and its
local image, with the actual localization of $c_T$.
To give additive coordinates, let
$a=v_3(\beta_{A_0})$ and set
\[
       w=3a+\epsilon,\qquad\gamma=\beta c_T^{-a}.
 \tag{E92.12}
\]
Then $v_3(\gamma_{A_0})=0$. The map
$(b,\epsilon,\beta)\mapsto(b,w,\gamma)$ and the inverse
\[
 \epsilon=w\bmod3\text{ in }\{0,1,2\},\qquad
 a=(w-\epsilon)/3,\qquad\beta=\gamma c_T^a
 \tag{E92.13}
\]
are mutually inverse topological group coordinates.
Localization is the identity on $(b,w)$ and is
the literal toric localization on $\gamma$.
For the $A_0$ factor its local section is $4^z$;
for the $B_0$ factor it is $3^z4^{z'}$; for each
quadratic factor it is the section following
(E92.8). These give the promised actual linear
section in (E92.2), including every finite carry.

All maps in the statement are maps on the complete
global abelian group. They do not assert a class-two
lift for all of its points. The remaining intrinsic
wild class-two calculation is thereby reduced to
the eight toric relative directions in (E92.4),
with the actual global ramified relations of E.73
still retained. The manuscript anchor is
\S51.16, folios733v--736v, scans172--178,
formulas(371)--(379), Part E(c),(d), follow-up(4).
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The exact global and local compact comparisons.}
The sequence (E92.1) is the actual Tate-module
sequence of the generalized Jacobian in E.37.
Its inclusion uses the eight peripheral columns;
its projection selects $f_1,f_2$. The retained
matrices intertwine both maps. In particular
$T$ acts on $W$ by $7I$, and $J$ by minus the
permutation of the three indicated pairs, with
$-1$ on $A_0,B_0$.

We first check the full scope of the global
proper elliptic assertion. At every finite
prime $p\ne3$, $E$ has good reduction and
$E_3$ is unramified. The restriction of a
continuous $E_3$-valued cocycle to inertia
is a homomorphism. Its wild prime-to-three
part has zero image, and its tame pro-three
part is determined by a vector satisfying
$Fv=pv$. The good-reduction eigenvalues have
complex magnitude $\sqrt p$, so this vector
is zero. This argument applies integrally:
$F-p$ is invertible over $\mathbb Q_3$ and
the lattice is torsion free. The cocycle
itself is therefore unramified away from three.

Over $K$, both its arithmetic action and its
cocycle form a homomorphism into a pro-three
semidirect group: $E[3]$ is $K$-rational.
Consequently this homomorphism factors through
the actual maximal pro-three group $P$ used
in E.34. The order-two descent gives precisely
$\Gamma$. Thus E37.22 computes the entire
global group, in its inverse-limit cohomology
topology:
\[
 H^1(\mathbb Q,E_3)=H^1(\Gamma,E_3)
      =\mathbb Z_3t_1\oplus C_3t_2.
 \tag{E92.14}
\]
The local map onto $\Gamma$ is surjective
by E.74. Hence inflation of (E92.14) to the
local group is injective, and in the actual
E.77 coordinates its image is exactly zero
relative value. Indeed the two relative
generators normally generate the kernel
of the marked quotient. The proper elliptic
local coordinates are explicitly
\[
     U_E=(b,s),\qquad A_E=\theta(3,1),\qquad
     (b,s,\theta)\sim(b,s+3z,\theta).
 \tag{E92.15}
\]
Thus the image of (E92.14) is precisely
$\theta=0$, with coordinates $(b,s\bmod3)$.

There are actual short exact cohomology sequences
\[
\begin{aligned}
0&\longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q,W)
 \longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q,V)
 \longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q,E_3)\longrightarrow0,\\
0&\longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q_3,W)
 \longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q_3,V)
 \longrightarrow H^1(\mathbb Q_3,E_3)\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{E92.16}
\]
Both left injections follow from $H^0(E_3)=0$:
the actual matrix $A_E(\tau)-I$ has determinant
nine. Globally, every class (E92.14) has the
specified $V$ lift $bf_1+\epsilon f_2$ on the
free source $P$, proving the last surjection.
Locally, E.37 gives an actual map $j:E_3\to V$
with $\pi_Ej=3$. Hence the connecting homomorphism
is killed by three. Its target is torsion free:
Shapiro and local duality give
\[
       H^2(\mathbb Q_3,W)
       =H^2(\mathbb Q_3,\mathbb Z_3(1))^2
          \oplus H^2(k,\mathbb Z_3(1))^3
       =\mathbb Z_3^5.
 \tag{E92.17}
\]
This is the finite-to-integral duality proved
in E.74; alternatively the local Brauer invariant
and the finite Kummer sequence give each copy
of $\mathbb Z_3$ under its actual inverse transition.
Thus the local connecting map is zero. Restriction
commutes with every arrow in (E92.16).

The toric restriction map is onto by the explicit
field constructions below. Therefore any local
$V$-class whose compact image has $\theta=0$
is global: lift that compact class by (E92.14),
subtract its actual global $V$ lift locally,
and lift the unique remaining toric class.
Conversely every global class has compact
image in (E92.14). This proves both image
inclusions in (E92.2). If a global $V$-class
restricts to zero, its compact image is zero
by injectivity of (E92.14). Both injections
in (E92.16) then identify the complete
localization kernel with the toric kernel.
This proves the required kernel comparison,
before any coordinates or ranks are imposed.

\emph{Actual toric Shapiro maps.}
On a quadratic pair write its two cocharacters
in the order stated after (E92.1). For the
global group use the actual complex conjugation
$c$, and for the local calculation use E.74's
actual relative group and its chosen $c$.
On the index-two subgroup the pair has scalar
action $\chi$, while $c$ acts by minus swap.
The normalized Shapiro inverse of a Tate
cocycle $b$ on that subgroup is
\[
       z(h)=\bigl(b(h),-b(chc)\bigr),\qquad z(c)=0.
 \tag{E92.18}
\]
Its first coordinate on the subgroup is the
inverse map. The cocycle and conjugation
identities follow by substitution; a boundary
with coefficient $a$ is carried to the boundary
with coefficient $(a,-a)$. Averaging over the
order-two element proves that these formulas
give the full cohomology isomorphism. E.74's
actual maximal-pro-three comparison identifies
the local computation with absolute local
cohomology, so no order-two element of an
absolute nonreal local Galois group is assumed.
These are the exact maps used in (E92.16).

\emph{The quadratic field, units and root carries.}
The Eisenstein integer ring $\mathbb Z[\zeta_3]$
is Euclidean for its norm. For completeness,
write any complex quotient as $a+b\zeta_3$
and round both real coefficients to integers.
The remainder has $|a|,|b|\le1/2$, whence
$a^2-ab+b^2\le3/4<1$. Division with strictly
smaller norm follows. Thus every prime ideal
has a generator and the units are the six
solutions of norm one, $\mu_6$. This proves
the global multiplicative decomposition used
in (E92.5). The norm of $u_2=1-3\zeta_3$
is thirteen, and $X^2+X+1$ is irreducible
modulo two; these prove all three prime
assertions in (E92.3).

In the local ring use the actual basis $(1,\lambda)$.
The identities
\[
 \lambda^2=3\lambda-3=-3\zeta_3,
 \quad \overline\lambda=3-\lambda,
 \quad N(a+b\lambda)=a^2+3ab+3b^2
 \tag{E92.19}
\]
fix all signs and products. For a unit
$a+b\lambda$, first choose $\omega\in\{1,-1\}$
so that $\omega a\equiv1\pmod3$.
Then put $e=-\omega b\bmod3$ in $\{0,1,2\}$.
Multiplication by $\zeta_3^{-e}$ gives a unit
congruent to one modulo $3\mathcal O_k$.
The choices are unique, since
$\mathcal O_k/(\lambda^2)=\mathbb F_3[\lambda]/(\lambda^2)$
and $\zeta_3^e\equiv1-e\lambda$ there.
This proves the literal decomposition (E92.6).

Logarithm and exponential converge and are inverse
on $1+3\mathcal O_k$ and $3\mathcal O_k$.
The termwise valuations prove convergence;
formal composition and multiplication identities
then hold by their convergent substitutions.
In the basis $(1,\lambda)$, the two columns are
\[
 \left[\frac{\log u_2}{3}\ \frac{\log(-2)}3\right]
       \equiv\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\pmod3.
 \tag{E92.20}
\]
Its determinant is a unit. The matrix geometric
series, or the adjugate divided by its unit
determinant, gives its actual inverse over
$\mathbb Z_3$. Thus $u_2,-2$ are a basis
of the whole principal unit group. The factor
$-1$ is removed by odd-power completion,
whereas $\mu_3$ survives. This proves the
local identification (E92.5), with all
torsion and all logarithmic coefficients.

Kummer theory gives the corresponding cohomology
maps at each finite power by the exact sequence
and Hilbert 90, as in
\cite[II, Proposition1.22; III, \S4, Step2]{E_MilneCFT}.
Here the groups of invariant roots can contain
$\mu_3$. The continuous comparison nevertheless
has a direct proof: recursively align compatible
finite cocycles by lifting their boundary
coefficients. For injectivity, the sets of
boundary coefficients of a zero class are
nonempty finite sets; their compatible inverse
limit is nonempty by the finite intersection
property. This proves the inverse-limit
isomorphism, including the finite $\mu_3$.
The same finite-level kernels prove its
topological assertion.

Every coefficient in (E92.6) is integral.
For a restricted exponent sequence the torsion
sum has finite support modulo three, and both
logarithmic sums converge uniformly modulo
every power of three. The three pivot images
are literally
\[
 \lambda\mapsto(1,0,0,0),\quad
 u_2\mapsto(0,0,1,0),\quad
 2\mapsto(0,0,0,1),\quad
 \zeta_3\mapsto(0,1,0,0).
 \tag{E92.21}
\]
Substitution now proves (E92.7)--(E92.8) in
both directions, with every finite torsion
carry. Their continuity is reduction modulo
the same fixed power, using uniform tails.
The rational maps of E.90 have the same
property. This computes the whole toric
kernel in (E92.2).

\emph{The literal elliptic carry and additive coordinates.}
In the retained integral basis put
\[
 h_+=\tfrac12(I+J_1)f_1
   =f_1+\tfrac12(A_1+A_2+B_1+B_2+C_1+C_2).
 \tag{E92.22}
\]
It is fixed by $J_1$. The exact matrices give
\[
 (T_1-I)h_+=3f_2+d_T,
 \qquad
 d_T=(-1,-3,1,-2,0,-1,-1,-2)\in W.
 \tag{E92.23}
\]
This is an actual global and local coboundary
in the normalized coordinates. Thus
$3[f_2]=-[d_T]$ in both cohomology groups.

We verify the precise Kummer tuple for this
carry. On the marked unramified generators
the actual classes have values
\[
 \kappa_3:(1,-1),\qquad
 \kappa_{\zeta_3}:(2,2),\qquad
 \kappa_\lambda:(3/2,1/2).
 \tag{E92.24}
\]
The first is the fixed E.49/E.78 normalization;
the second follows from the coherent roots
of $\zeta_3$ and $\chi(\tau)=\chi(\tau')=7$;
the third follows from $\lambda^2=-3\zeta_3$
with the coherent rational roots of $-1$.
These are cohomology identities with their
indicated compatible representatives.
Applying (E92.18) to (E92.10) gives the
eight-coordinate value
\[
 v(c_T)=(1,3,-1,2,3/2,-1/2,5/2,1/2).
 \tag{E92.25}
\]
The difference $-d_T-v(c_T)$ is exactly
$(I-7I)q$, where
\[
       q=(0,0,0,0,1/4,-1/4,1/4,-1/4),
                         \qquad J_Wq=q.
 \tag{E92.26}
\]
Thus the two normalized cocycles differ by
the displayed actual fixed coboundary.
This proves $3[f_2]=[c_T]$, with the
toric/elliptic basis map and all signs
fixed. It proves (E92.11) and hence both
additive-coordinate directions
(E92.12)--(E92.13).

For precision, the actual extraction of the
toric remainder is also explicit. Normalize
a cocycle to vanish on $c$. Its compact
projection factors through $\Gamma$ globally;
for a local point in the stated image its
relative compact value is zero and the
same factorization holds. Write its
horizontal value as $U$. Put
\[
 b=U_{f_1},\quad \epsilon=U_{f_2}\bmod3,\quad
 k=(U_{f_2}-\epsilon)/3,\qquad
 U_T=U-k(T_1-I)h_+-bf_1-\epsilon f_2.
 \tag{E92.27}
\]
The first two entries of $U_T$ are zero.
Apply the actual boundary $kh_+$ to the
whole cocycle, and then subtract the
specified global elliptic lift. The result
is the unique toric cohomology class in
(E92.16); (E92.18) and the Kummer inverse
give its five field classes. Conversely
these steps are reversible. They include
the boundary on all generators, not just
its value at $\tau$.

The topology is equally explicit. Evaluation
on $c,\tau$, the finite residue extraction,
and division by three on its stated image
are continuous. The toric Kummer maps have
the topology already proved. Therefore
(E92.9) and (E92.12) are continuous bijections
with the displayed continuous inverses,
even though their global restricted factors
are not compact. In the additive coordinates,
the local image is
\[
 \mathbb Z_3^2\times\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_3^2
                   \times(C_3\times\mathbb Z_3^3)^3
                =\mathbb Z_3^{14}\times C_3^3.
 \tag{E92.28}
\]
The first two factors are $(b,w)$; the next
two are the rational torus factors, the
first with valuation at three zero.
The actual field sections described after
(E92.13) preserve this condition. They give
a continuous linear section of localization.
The two rational and three quadratic kernel
formulas give every fibre, including its
inverse, in (E92.2).

One may also read this image directly in
E.74's Smith coordinates. The horizontal
group is $\mathbb Z_3^6\oplus C_3^3$, and
the actual relative kernel has the basis
$(3f_1+f_2,e_d:d\ne O)$. Setting its first
coordinate to zero leaves eight free
coordinates. The three global $\mu_3$
generators map to the three intrinsic
torsion coordinates by the invertible
matrix and inverse
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}-1&0&0\\-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}2&0&0\\2&1&0\\2&1&1\end{pmatrix}\pmod3.
 \tag{E92.29}
\]
These are obtained by applying the retained
Smith map to $2(A_1-A_2)$, $2(B_1-B_2)$,
$2(C_1-C_2)$. Their triples are literal
fixed coboundaries, so the calculation
identifies the actual torsion classes.
It agrees with (E92.28) and proves the
same image using the original local
source marking.

The certificate retains both actual coefficient
maps, the marked relative basis, all carry
vectors and coboundaries, and the torsion
matrix/inverse. It verifies $243$ complete
elliptic/toric inverse calculations and $729$
finite-carry identities. In the actual ring
(E92.19) it checks $67$ prime-ideal generators,
$462$ prime--precision logarithmic identities,
and $567$ full localization inverses with
their $567$ finite $\mu_3$ carries. All
computation uses the stated integral ring,
actual input matrices and recorded hashes.
\end{proof}
