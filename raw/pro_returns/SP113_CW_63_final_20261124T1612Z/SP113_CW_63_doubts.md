## Legibility and witness comparison

The English manuscript has mostly legible running text, with weaker ink in fine indices, equality bars, punctuation and crowded or cancelled passages. The typescript has worn impressions and occasional handwritten amendments. Remaining doubts are retained at their places in the text. Witness C supplied a useful draft but was checked against the images throughout; witness H supplies no source transcription on these sixteen scans. Confidence refers to the visible reading, not to mathematical correctness.
