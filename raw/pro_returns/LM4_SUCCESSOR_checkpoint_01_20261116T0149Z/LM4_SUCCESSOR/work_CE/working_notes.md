# Parts C and E.1: active proof audit

## Findings

1. LM6 C.15 is an exact elementary nonabelian-cohomology calculation. Its action is a twisted conjugation action of the quotient centralizer; it is not a based action in general.
2. LM6 C.16 and C.17 use Boggi--Zalesskii only at the cyclic group generated by one involution. Their applications do not use the nonsolvable finite-subgroup intersection step.
3. The scalar cubic torus problem survives the quartic obstruction: after the Frobenius cover and filling, the 3-adic module has a cyclotomic rank-two summand and regular summands. Fixed vectors of a regular summand have pairing divisible by 3. Therefore the mod-3 isotropy consequence does not contradict that module.
4. A nontrivial abstract pro-odd kernel model exists even inside a free profinite group of rank two. Let L be free pro-3 on x,y, Z_(3')=product_{p!=3} Z_p, and let z act by x->x,y->y^-1 through mod 2. Then B=L semidirect Z_(3') is generated by a=xz and y, B_ab=Zhat, every open subgroup B0 has H1(B0,F2) dimension one, and chi:B->Zhat splits via a. Its kernel is the nontrivial free pro-3 kernel of L->Z3. Sylow 3 is L and all other Sylows are Zp, so B is projective and is a retract of Fhat2. This model does not satisfy, or purport to satisfy, the additional torus peripheral-duality conditions.

## Current obstructions

- C.1: no proof that the actual torus fixed-group kernel is finitely generated or that it is trivial; the abstract structural assertions alone cannot establish triviality.
- C.2: the original commutator transporter is unique, but existence of a conjugator between c^-mu a and a is additional.
- C.3: no full profinite lift of scalar cubic local data; degree-three cup obstruction does not exclude it.
- E.1: K_c^tau and nonabelian H1(C2,K_c) for genus three remain uncomputed. Knowing the quotient centralizer and a complement does not determine the fixed subgroup in K_c.
