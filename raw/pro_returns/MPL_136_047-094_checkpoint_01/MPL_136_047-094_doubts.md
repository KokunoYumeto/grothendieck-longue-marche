# Notation table and doubts

## Recurring notation (maintained by the single primary reader)

| Manuscript form | LaTeX | Reading policy |
|---|---|---|
| Star over C, H | `C^*`, `H^*` | Preserve the drawn star; do not replace by a bullet. |
| Star below X, C, Z | `X_*`, `C_*`, `Z_*` | Separate an asterisk from a numerical index; mark faint instances. |
| Hatted simplex category | `\widehat{\Delta}` | Preserve the hat where visible. |
| Small circle as opposite-category superscript | `^{\circ}` | Do not silently convert to `op`. |
| Half sign before simplicial | `$\frac12$` | Preserve the author's abbreviated half sign. |
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes | Guillemets only when visibly drawn. |
| Exclamation-mark exponent | `^!` | Never conflate with a prime. |
| Prime | `'` | Never conflate with an exclamation mark. |
