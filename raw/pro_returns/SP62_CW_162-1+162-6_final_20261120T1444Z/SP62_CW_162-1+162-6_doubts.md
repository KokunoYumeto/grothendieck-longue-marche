## Legibility and comparison of witnesses

The large loan-list entries and the main pencil formulas are mostly legible. Reliability is lowest in the overwritten Picard lines on 162-1/005, the locally crossed lower Deligne entries on 162-1/006, the microfiche qualification on 162-1/007, the completely overstruck word beside Illusie on 162-1/008, and the faint right-hand term and bottom jottings on 162-6/006. Three physically unreadable word-sized regions remain as illegible{1}; none is used in place of a mathematical symbol or diagram object. A doubtful punctuation mark may be an incidental speck, and this is stated rather than resolved from syntactic expectation.

C is substantially closer to the visible writing on this selection. Most final differences against C are concentrated in a few words/endings, punctuation, one name’s preferred outline, local cancellation or placement, the product’s field-letter forms, and the left diagonal label. Many other C doubts could be reduced or confirmed without changing its first reading; those are not counted as substantive changes. H supplies an independent list of places to inspect but often expands abbreviations, substitutes familiar names or titles, describes drawings instead of setting them, or omits difficult lower entries. Its agreement with C was never accepted without looking at the scan. The counts describe localized decisions in this package, not statistical error rates for either transcription as a whole.

Every page was read by the same reader in the specified order, using the whole page, both supplied halves, and one crop per remaining open place. After all eleven first readings, all eleven pages were read again against their images in order. The second reading changed the preferred collaborator outline to Bon|Bou|Bass on 162-1/007 and made the x/u doubt explicit at both positions of the conjugation equation on 162-6/003. No OCR, recognition library, external bibliography, web research, or TeX compilation was used. A separate worker handled mechanical validation code only; it made no manuscript reading decisions.

Cross-page consistency was checked for the Picard/SGA ordinal forms, repeated surnames, field-letter doubling, h(p,q) and h_p signs, v/nu, x/u and overbars, I’s raised oval, norm bars, quotation marks and cancellation conventions. A clearly different local spelling or graphic alphabet was retained: Brieskorin is not silently made Brieskorn; plain R in S(R) is not silently doubled. Caps and feet on Roman references are retained graphically. A different macro or font representation of those same numeral strokes is not by itself counted as a witness error; an actual numeral change, underline omission or mistaken cancellation is counted.
