# Legibility and limits

All 43 retained scans were personally read as whole pages and in their supplied halves, then read visually a second time against the page fragments. No OCR was used. The transcription has no `\illegible` word placeholders: damaged words and mathematical symbols received attempted readings with explicit doubt; this does not make those attempts secure.

The weakest handwritten passages are the cancelled algebra on 144/173,175,177,183; the small ranks and alphabet forms on 144/180; the interlinear words and group subscripts on 148/002–004,007; the compressed coloured marginal notes on 148/018–019; and the overwritten counts and labels on 148/023–028. Some large group capitals remain uncertain between Γ and a script T, and the round ornamental S-like capital remains doubt-marked. The numerical alternatives 8/9 on 148/018, 17/14 on 148/024 and 82/32 on 148/027 preserve the visible reading before any arithmetical expectation.

The mirrored computer impressions on 148/020–021 are especially faint beneath later drawings. Every doubtful row has been retained, including repeated characters, but individual counts and covered character shapes are low-confidence readings. Printed notices are generally more legible; signatures, damaged photocopy letters, clipped right edges on 148/023 and a few punctuation marks remain uncertain.

The complex surface sheets have been reconstructed in TikZ, including faint construction lines, superposed contours, arrow directions and attempted labels. Thick ink obscures some local joins, and these drawings should be treated as traced approximations, especially those bearing the requested crop-candidate comment. Handwritten double quotation marks use TeX backticks/apostrophes; raised exclamation marks, primes, bars, arrows, deletion strokes and insertion attachments were reviewed across the package. Context files were still unopened during all of these reading decisions.
