# Main research checkpoints

## Verified corrections under development

- The ordinary exit-category criterion is (i) aspherical strata and (ii) full local meridian lattice injectivity into the connected component of U. The proof reduces all relative lattices to the open one via the exact sequence for the normal torus bundle over the target stratum; no assertion that an image is automatically a direct summand is needed.
- Codimension-one conditions do NOT suffice, even with (i): blow up p=(0,1) in the x=0 branch of the axes in C², then delete a point r of the exceptional curve different from its intersection q with the strict transform. U=(C*)²; A°=C\{0,1}, B°=C*, E°=C*. At q, the two meridians both map to e_x; every adjacent meridian is nontrivial/infinite order.
- Aspherical elementary pieces already permit reconstruction of X by the homotopy colimit of BΠ1 pieces. Extra injectivity is needed to replace the exit infinity-category by its ordinary homotopy category. Thus the Remords is an obstruction to the latter 1-topos shape, not to derived reconstruction from the complete coherent groupoid diagram.
- For P¹ with one puncture, Map(p,u)=S¹, hExit=[1], q:S²→* has fibre S². For P^n with a hyperplane, hExit=[1], fibre P^n; the crossing total space is S^(2n−1), mapping fibre S¹. For the product of two (P¹,∞) pairs, hExit=[1]², fibre S²×S², and only (ii) fails.
- General q:BExit→BhExit should be 2-connected, with first relative module from degree-one homology of mapping spaces modulo composition relations, calculated in the universal cover of BhExit. Need give full bar proof. First discrepancy need not occur in π2: (Q^r×C,Q^r×0), Q^r={Σz_i²=1}≃S^r, gives first discrepancy π_r for every r≥2.
