## Leads (by Claude Fable 5.2; unverified; prove, correct or refute each)

- **Lead 1 (what his three structures are).** Let `X^log → X` be the real oriented blow-up of `X` along `Θ` (the Kato–Nakayama space), a manifold with corners with interior `X^*`. Then `P_d^*`, divided by the positive reals in each factor, is the open face of `X^log` over `D_d^*`, a bundle of tori `(S¹)^d`; `Π̃_{d_0,…,d_r}` is the fundamental groupoid of the space of chains of exit paths from a stratum of codimension `d_0` through strata of codimensions `d_1, …, d_r` into the open stratum; his generization morphism is induced by the inclusion of the open face over `D^*_{d_0}` in the closed face over `D_{d_1}`; and his gluing theorem (scan 150) says that the boundary of `X^log` is the homotopy colimit of its closed faces over the flags. His "déploiement" (scan 114) is the category `𝒟` of flags, not `X^log` (this corrects an earlier hunch of ours, H4 (i)).
- **Lead 2 (the criterion).** The exit-path ∞-category of `(X, Θ)` is equivalent to an ordinary category if and only if (i) every stratum is aspherical and (ii) for every point `x` the lattice `Z^{I(x)}` of meridians of the branches through `x` injects into `π_1(X^*)`. Condition (ii) is his 1°) of 149, scan 130. For strata `S ⊂ S̄′` of codimensions `d > d′` the condition "the lattice `Z^{d−d′}` injects into `π_1(S′)`" follows from (ii), because the image of `Z^d` in the fundamental group of the link of `S′` contains the fibre lattice `Z^{d′}` as a direct summand. Exactly under (i) and (ii) the Remarque of scan 179 holds as written.
- **Lead 3 (blow-ups).** The criterion is unchanged by blowing up the closure of a stratum of codimension ≥ 2 (the new meridian is the sum of the old ones; the lattices change by unimodular matrices). It is destroyed by blowing up a point, or any smooth centre that is not a closure of a stratum, inside `Θ`: on one branch the exceptional divisor acquires a stratum with contractible fibres over the centre, in which a meridian dies (for a surface: a copy of `(S², point)`); at a point of a curve of double points in a threefold the open exceptional stratum is `C × C^*` and a lattice of rank 2 maps to `Z`. His margin of scan 130 is the first case.
- **Lead 4 (minimal models).** Minimality does not give injectivity (`C² ⊂ P²`), and minimal normal crossings completions of a surface are not unique (`C^* × C^*`). Guess: any two completions of `U` that satisfy the criterion are joined by blow-ups and blow-downs along closures of strata. When a boundary component has finite local monodromy the criterion fails on every smooth model and holds on a stack (a root stack along the boundary); the Deligne–Mumford compactifications are of this kind.
- **Lead 5 (the maquette).** The maquette is the dual complex of the divisor, the classifying space of the category `π_0(𝒟)` of components of his `𝒟`. The nerve lemma gives the homotopy type of `D`, not of `U_∞`, when the closed `D_J` are contractible; `U_∞` maps to it, and on rational cohomology this map is the inclusion of the weight-zero part. Its independence of the compactification is a theorem of Thuillier (2007), its relation to weights a theorem of Payne (2013); for the stable moduli it is the moduli space of tropical curves (Chan–Galatius–Payne 2021). These citations are from memory: open the sources.
- **Lead 6 (the levels).** Corollaire 2 is false when three branches meet: for `X = C³` with the three coordinate planes, `π_1(X^{*∞}) = Z³`, while the colimit of `Π̃_1 ⟵ Π̃_{2,1} ⟶ Π̃_2` (three, six and three copies of `Z³`, joined by isomorphisms in a hexagon) has fundamental group `Z⁴`. The face over `D_d` has codimension `d − 1` in the boundary of `X^log`, so the `k`-type of `X^{*∞}` needs the flags in codimensions `≤ k + 2`, and `C^{k+2}` shows this is sharp. His struck first draft of the corollary has "(1 ≤ d_0 ≤ 3)". A second truncation, by dimension (our hunch H3): for `M̄_{0,n}`, of dimension `d = n − 3`, let `P_k` be the poset of multicurves with at least `d − k` components; `P_k` is `(k − 1)`-connected, because the curve complex of a sphere with `n` holes is a wedge of spheres of dimension `n − 4` (Harer) whose links are joins of such complexes, hence Cohen–Macaulay, and rank selection preserves this; so the union of the strata of dimension `≤ k`, thickened and intersected with `M_{0,n}`, maps `k`-connectedly to `M_{0,n}`.
