The actual extension and its h0/c stability pass. Square and frames
remain unproved at this new layer.

Let V=k^36, k=F8, be the actual Q6 monomial module. Keep its Frobenius

F2-linear H-isomorphism V->V^(2), but not the identity k-linear map.
Set A=V^4 and T=(V^(2))^6, indexed by i<j. Define the finite2 group
    N=A x T,
    (a,t)(b,u)=(a+b,t+u+c(a,b)),
    c_ij(a,b)_w=a_i,w b_j,w.
Coordinatewise multiplication is H-equivariant into the squared-weight
module, so H acts on this exact group. Its commutator is
    [a,b]_ij=a_i*b_j+a_j*b_i.
Taking one nonzero row in a and another in b shows N'=T. Squares lie
inT, so Phi(N)=T and N/Phi(N)=A. This is an actual class-two group,
not a graded Lie-algebra integration assumption.

1. Actual source and joint quotient.

v_ab=R d_r for the four cusp cocycles. Because (1+R+R²)d_r=0,
(1-R)R d_r=d_r in characteristic2. Thus this has the prescribed

coproduct Pi=C2 amalg C3 supplies the continuous source map.
The four cusp classes restrict independently toR because H1(C,V)=0;
semisimplicity of V^4 and End_H(V)=k make its residual abelian image
allA. Frattini then gives q(R)=N. Therefore the source map onto
N semidirect C is genuinely surjective.

Pair N with A_infty=L_infty^4 plusV^4 over the reduction of its four
free cusp rows, A. The resulting pro-two group P=N x_A A_infty has
derived subgroupT and Frattini quotient A_infty/2A_infty=V^8.
The actual joint residual source map has this full Frattini image,
so it is ontoP. Equivalently, its projection toN is onto, its

    q(W_infty)=T.

A62's U_infty satisfies
    [W_infty,Pi] <= U_infty <= W_infty.

2. Nonsplitting and six independent directions.

Suppose E'->D_infty had a section. Its second projection would give
a homomorphism eta:D_infty->N semidirect C overC. On the residual
P_infty=R/U_infty, eta maps ontoA, hence ontoN by Frattini.
Consequently eta(D_infty)=N semidirect C. But
P_infty'=M_infty is globally central inD_infty. Its image lies both
inN'=T and in the center of N semidirect C, hence inT^H=0. This
contradicts eta(P_infty')=N'=T. No assumed C-compatible section or
unproved splitting theorem is used.

For every nonzero H-linear quotient T->V^(2), the induced
commutator is still nonzero (choose a coefficient i<j with nonzero
weight), and its image is the whole simple V^(2). The same proof
therefore makes every nonzero k-linear combination nonsplit. The
six resulting extension directions are independent. Their inflation
toPi is zero because the actual source map lifts them.
