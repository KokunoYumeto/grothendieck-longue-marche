SAME-ACTION MULTIPLIER-ONE PRESCRIBED-FRAME OBSTRUCTION
16 November2026. Research proof; no assertion about freely selectable frames.

1. Exact source and original quotient.
Let F be the full free profinite group on x,y, c(x)=x^{-1},
c(y)=y^{-1}, and E=F semidirect<c> (equivalently C2 coproduct C2
coproduct C2). In Q=SL2(F5), take
 X=(0,4;1,4), Y=(0,1;4,4), J=(0,1;1,0).
The exact matrix checker verifies |Q|=120, Q perfect, <X,Y>=Q,
|X|=|Y|=3, J^2=I, JXJ^{-1}=X^{-1}, JYJ^{-1}=Y^{-1}.
Thus the actual epi q:F->Q, x->X,y->Y has a c-stable kernel N.
This stability is direct, not inferred from an unproved realization.
The source E consequently maps onto Q semidirect<c_Q>, where
c_Q=Ad J. This is an actual original three-reflection quotient.

2. Why the ACTUAL orientation-central quotient is Q x Zhat^2.
The primary ATLAS A5 entry records Mult(A5)=2 and identifies
2.A5=SL2(5). The verified perfect double cover Q->A5 is therefore
the universal central extension: the universal cover maps onto Q,
and both have order120. A universal central cover of a perfect
group is centrally closed. Here is the argument needed for arbitrary
profinite central kernels, rather than an unsupported finite-only
comparison. If D->Q is any central extension with kernel M, then
D=M D' and D' is perfect (abstract derived groups suffice). The
composition D'->Q->A5 is central: for an element above the central
kernel of Q->A5, its commutator with D' is a central-valued
homomorphism on the perfect group D', and is therefore zero.
Universality gives a map Q->D' over A5. Its composite with D'->Q
is the identity by uniqueness for the universal cover. Thus it is
a section. Its image is finite, hence closed, and it is continuous
because Q is finite. Perfectness gives D'=Q in this splitting, so
D=Q x M. The section is unique, since two sections differ by a
homomorphism from the perfect group Q to the abelian group M.

Apply this to D=F/[N,F] (all subgroup operations closed). Its
abelianization is exactly F_ab=Zhat^2, because [N,F] is contained
in F'. Hence M=Zhat^2. The canonical joint map
   D -> Q x F_ab,  g -> (q(g),ab(g))
is an isomorphism, and the actual source generators are
   x=(X,e1), y=(Y,e2).
The c-action is literally (Ad J,-1) under this canonical map.
There is no full-versus-pro-p replacement in this argument.
Killing also [N,c] reduces the central Zhat^2 factor modulo2.
Consequently E/[N,E] is the ACTUAL finite group
   (Q x C2^2) semidirect<c>, of order960.

3. Compatible identity action with a bad prescribed frame.
Let theta be the identity on the entire D=Q x Zhat^2, and on its
extension by c. Set beta=(-I,0). It is central, c-fixed and has
order2, so beta c(beta)=1. It has abelianization0. Prescribe beta
as the x-cusp conjugator, and prescribe1 for the y and z=(xy)^{-1}
cusp conjugators. The named reflection xc may also have conjugator
beta, while c and y^{-1}c have conjugator1. Every conjugacy,
pair-product and quotient norm equation then holds literally,
both on the entire actual orientation-central quotient and on
the actual order960 full-source-central quotient. In particular
the external-c frame is1. This is multiplier ONE and the SAME
action c on both sides.

4. Why no full equivariant peripheral lift retains these frames.
Allow even the weaker demand that the full conjugator frames reduce
to the prescribed elements only in Q. Suppose a full peripheral
automorphism of E with multiplier1 has those frames. Its external-c
conjugator a_c belongs to N because its prescribed Q-frame is1.
Normalize the automorphism by conjugation with a_c^{-1}. This fixes
c literally and leaves every prescribed Q-frame unchanged. (On the
orientation-central quotient, a_c is central, so the identity
quotient action is unchanged as well.)

For the normalized restriction Phi to F write
 Phi(x)=a x a^{-1}.
Commutation with c gives a^{-1}c(a) in C_F(x)=<x>, say x^t.
Writing a_ab=r e1+s e2 gives t=-2r and s=0. Therefore a x^{-r}
is c-fixed. The known FULL fixed-subgroup equality C_F(c)=1 gives
a=x^r. Thus Phi(x)=x. The identical y calculation gives Phi(y)=y,
so Phi is the identity. Every x-conjugator for it belongs to <x>.
Its Q-image must belong to <X>, which has order3, whereas the
prescribed beta has Q-image -I of order2. This is impossible.

The equivalent reflection-conjugator obstruction is even sharper:
C_F(xc)=1 in the full negative extension, whereas the finite
orientation-central quotient has the spurious centralizer beta.
The cusp argument above already proves the result and needs no
extra reflection-normalizer theorem.

5. Exact scope and relation to earlier attempts.
This refutes an unrestricted rule that every prescribed frame on
the actual central quotient, even with its involutory quotient norm,
lifts c-equivariantly with multiplier1. It does NOT refute existence
when frames can be changed: the identity is an immediate lift with
all frames1. It does not refute ordinary non-equivariant R2, nor does
it give a nonconjugate negative involution. The obstruction is an
actual failure of centralizer/frame lifting, not a missing original
source epi or an arbitrary abstract nonsplit extension.

The earlier dyadic order2048 example has the same conclusion only
after killing [N,c]; its w^2 need not centralize x in F/[N,F]. The
present example removes that weakness. The earlier theta1(x)=xw^6
candidate on the order13824 quotient fails the named reflection
class of xc and must not be used for the stronger rule.
