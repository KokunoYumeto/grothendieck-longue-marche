Assessment so far: all five supplied scans of cote 134-1 (002, 004–007), and cote 134-2 scans 146–152. This section will be updated after the remaining pages are decided.

The cover is comparatively clear, except for an isolated terminal point whose intentional status is uncertain. The first letter and its continuations are materially less secure where the copy has clipped an edge, thickened a joined stroke, or merged cancellation with the underlying letters. The obscured date on scan 004, the cancelled groups, short word endings, the last formula on scan 006, and the clipped opening on scan 007 are among the least secure places so far. Enlargement makes the surviving ink easier to inspect; it cannot recover strokes removed or fused in the supplied copy. Scan 007 also shows how uncertainty about a single accent or punctuation mark can survive after the surrounding word or phrase is settled.

On scan 146 the personal opening is mostly legible, but vertical copying streaks and thin or merged joins affect several small words. Continuous g descenders can resemble underlining; the inspected words cogitations and longue have no separate rule.

C has usually supplied a careful reading and useful uncertainty markers. Several have been resolved, while others have been retained or had their alternatives reordered from the visible strokes. New doubts were added where an unmarked form or state was not secure. H has supplied useful competing readings, but the adjudicated places include expanded abbreviations, extra punctuation or text, omitted correction states, and different mathematical alphabets. H's editorial note can itself record a genuine insertion, so the absence of an insertion macro from its body is not automatically an omission.

These observations concern the inspected pages and locations. Counts in the changes file include changes of uncertainty status and physical state, and a place may differ from both witnesses. They are not an independently measured word-error rate. Agreement among the three readers or between C and H was never used as a substitute for their required inspection of the scan.
