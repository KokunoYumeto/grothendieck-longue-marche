# Hand and notation log

Observations are restricted to the attached scans. A habit is not a licence to alter a clear letter.

### Scan 026: omega, primes, and cancellation

Omega has two shallow lobes and may resemble a w. A separate short rising stroke is a prime; in (19) several such positions are blackened. This distinction holds for the legible live omegas on this page, but buried cancelled letters remain uncertain. H reversed several omega/omega-prime indices and described cancellation blots as possible stars; C missed the NB prime.

### Scan 026: coiled group capital

The two group symbols resemble a coiled S with an upper flourish; C calls them G_J and H tilde-fraktur-S_J. This was provisional at the first reading. The completed comparison, recorded below, resolves both occurrences as G_J; it does not establish a universal rule for every unrelated coiled sign.

### Scan 027: gamma versus minus-gamma

In the live alpha definition (24), gamma begins with a curved horizontal entrance that is part of the letter. In the cancelled version a detached minus precedes gamma. C imported the minus into the replacement; H did not. This observation is local to these two occurrences.
