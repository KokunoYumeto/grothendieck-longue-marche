## Remaining doubts

Remaining marks default to low: this labels unresolved ink, not uncertainty that a passage, insertion, or deletion is present. An explicit undecided-decision confidence is reused only when the scan and complete reading, including alternatives, match exactly. Optional `DOUBT_CONFIDENCE.tsv` entries override those values; its columns are `scan`, `payload`, and `confidence`, with the full unsure argument as payload (or the literal illegible macro). Divergence-row confidence is never used to infer remaining-doubt confidence.

## Legibility and witness comparison

The main, uncancelled prose is generally readable at the supplied resolution.
The more open displays and the larger path labels are also substantially clearer
than the overwritten drafts. Legibility varies sharply within a single sheet:
a secure live formula can stand beside a cancelled superscript for which no
complete reading survives. Confidence in the existence or placement of a
passage is therefore different from confidence in every letter under its
deletion strokes. The remaining-doubts table records the latter conservatively.

The least secure material is concentrated in the following places:

- Scan 026: the cancelled product in (14), the marginal sketch, and the
  overwritten prime positions in (19). A surviving short tick does not warrant
  supplying every prime that the formula might require.
- Scans 028–029: the slanted proposal and starred note beside (28)–(29), and the
  tiny rho subscript in the lower NB on 029. The main Roman groupoid names are
  clearer than those tiny index strokes.
- Scan 030: the pole subscripts in (34); scan 031: the overwritten operator in
  (43) and the partly cancelled right margin.
- Scans 033–037: small inked-out symbols, crowded changes of variables, and
  revisions to inverse exponents. Scan 035's cancelled explanatory sentence is
  especially weak. These passages should be checked before using the
  transcription to reconstruct an earlier state of the argument.
- Scans 039–042: compressed articles, locally cancelled exponents, and overwritten
  numbering. The final number on 041 was resolved as 79 during the second reading,
  at medium confidence; the extra article on 042 remains le/les.
- Scan 044: two crossed-out words in the tau sentence and the scalar exponent.
  The initial tight crops clipped the two cancelled words; the complete second
  reading did not justify removing their doubt marks.
- Scan 048: the deleted symbol after the tangent-space expression and the
  cancelled symbol in (15). The whole-page audit supports représentera, but the
  first crop had clipped the bottom of that word, and its medium confidence is
  retained.
- Scan 050: six overwritten quarter-arc superscripts, the cancelled upper pole
  label, the tiny extra tangent subscript and deleted tail in (19), the second
  orientation-set glyph, and the last word of the cancelled lower paragraph.
- Scan 051: part of the upper slanted note and the lower deleted marginal text.
  The Dec/Dic forms differ visibly; an accent or a uniform spelling has not been
  supplied from the expected French term.

Witness C provided the more useful diplomatic and diagrammatic starting point:
it retained many cancellations, insertions, abbreviations and TikZ objects that
the supplied H text omitted, expanded or only described. It nevertheless
contained wrong words, missing insertions, incorrect arrow directions, shifted
signs and primes, and readings in which both witnesses agreed against the ink.
Witness H independently helped expose several of these places, including the
positive gamma in the replacement on 027, disques unités on 048, and words in
the cancelled lower draft on 050. Its supplied text also repeatedly smoothed
grammar, expanded short forms, moved wording, confused adjacent margins with
the main column, and treated the flourished G as a tilded fraktur S. Neither
witness was used as an authority for agreement.

The drawings are explicit TikZ reconstructions, not geometric facsimiles. Their
objects, directions, labels, cancellation states and relative arrangement were
read against the scans; dimensions, curvature and spacing remain typesetting
approximations. No TeX compilation or rendering was performed, as requested.
Mechanical syntax checks do not demonstrate a rendered layout or prove a
mathematical statement.
