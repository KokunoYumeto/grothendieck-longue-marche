## Conventions

- For a field `F`: `G_F = Gal(F^sep/F)`, `χ : G_F → Ẑ^×` the cyclotomic character, `G_F° = ker χ` (his `Γ°`, `E°`), `T = Ẑ(1)`.
- A *hyperbolic curve* over `F` is `U = Ū ∖ D` with `Ū` smooth proper geometrically connected of genus `g`, `D` a reduced divisor finite étale over `F` of degree `ν`, `2g − 2 + ν > 0`; its type is `(g, ν)`. `Π_U = π_1(U_{F^sep})` (his `π`), `E_U = π_1(U)` (his `𝓔_U`), `1 → Π_U → E_U → G_F → 1`. A *section* is a continuous homomorphic section of `E_U → G_F`; its *class* is its orbit under conjugation by `Π_U`. `s_x` is the section of `x ∈ U(F)`, defined up to that conjugation. A section is *cuspidal* if its image lies in the decomposition group `D_c = N_{E_U}(I_c)` of a cusp, `I_c ≅ T` the inertia group. `C(s) = C_{Π_U}(s(G_F°))`.
- In characteristic `p`: `Π^(p′)` is the maximal prime-to-`p` quotient of `Π_U`, and `E_U^(p′) = E_U / ker(Π_U → Π^(p′))`, an extension of the full `G_F` by `Π^(p′)`: the *geometrically prime-to-`p` group*. Sections and their classes (under `Π^(p′)`) as above.
- A closed procyclic subgroup `A` of the kernel of an extension `E` of `G_F` is *essentially cyclotomically normalized* if a closed subgroup of `N_E(A)` maps onto an open subgroup of `G_F` and acts on `A` through `χ`.
- **The trait.** `R` a henselian discrete valuation ring, `K` its fraction field, `k` its residue field, `ϖ` a uniformizer, `I_K` the inertia group; (30) is `1 → I_K → G_K → G_k → 1` and (31) is `1 → Π → E_U → G_K → 1` for a hyperbolic curve `U = Ū ∖ D` over `K`, `Π = Π_U`. His `S, η, 𝓔_η, 𝓔_k, T, π` are `Spec R`, `Spec K`, `G_K`, `G_k`, `I_K`, `Π`; in residue characteristic 0, `I_K ≅ T`. *Good marked reduction*: `(Ū, D)` extends to a smooth proper curve over `R` with a relative divisor finite étale over `R`. *Potentially good*: after a finite extension of `K`. A *stable marked model* is a stable pointed curve `(𝒞, 𝒟)` over `R` extending `(Ū, D)`; it is *split* if the components of `𝒞_k`, its nodes, the branches at the nodes and the marked points are rational over `k`; the *thickness* `m_e ≥ 1` of a node `e` is given by the local equation `xy = ϖ^{m_e}`. For a continuous splitting `s` of (30): `E_s = E_U ×_{G_K, s} G_k`, an extension of `G_k` by `Π`, and `ρ_s : G_k → Out(Π)` its outer action.
