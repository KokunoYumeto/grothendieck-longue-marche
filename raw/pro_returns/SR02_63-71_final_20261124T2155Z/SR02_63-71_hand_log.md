# Hand and notation log

Habits are local to the actual writing hand. The Atkin typescript has printer/proofreader annotations; archival location alone does not establish Grothendieck's authorship of that hand. Earlier logs are hypotheses checked afresh.

Here **first** means MAIN's recorded first reading and **blind** the isolated second reading; neither means supplied witness C or H. Box strings may omit surrounding source context. C/H attribution below is limited to explicit MAIN records and stated witness comparisons. Page/box references resolve to decisions or reviewed comparisons, not necessarily to placement rows.
