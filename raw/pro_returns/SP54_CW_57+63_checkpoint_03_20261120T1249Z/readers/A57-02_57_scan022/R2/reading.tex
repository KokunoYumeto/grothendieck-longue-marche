This is easily seen to be true if the \uline{Pic} functor of either factor is representable, by a simple use of dimension theory (namely, we have a morphisme $X\quad\underline{\mathrm{Pic}}_{Y/S}$ whose image has a general fiber of dimension zero, hence the same holds for the special fiber ...).
