## Cote 120, scan 6

Defines moderate maps by their graphs and states identity, composition and inverse-bijection properties, then Mod_{M_*} and the transported category Esp_{M_*}. The upper oblique note overlaps other writing and contains several heavily overwritten words: the attempted readings applications/surjections and linéaires des s. are low-confidence; three overlapped words remain physically unreadable. It appears to weaken the invoked coordinate axiom; do not use the uncertain wording as a proved restriction without image review. Both the index-union glyph and the K/n subscript are explicitly marked. The examples at the head and the margin excluding c2 must be checked together in later notes.

## Cote 120, scan 7

Transport of structure is formulated by unique lifting of a bijection |X| to E. A large crossed block discusses products, a hypothesis written M5 or M5 prime (R_0 nonempty), and a one-point final object; later marginal additions overlap its boundary. The bottom introduces optional M0, R in M_1, with its finite-index consequence. The two oblique revision blocks are the least secure readings in cote 120 so far: several words are physically obscured by crossing strokes and overlapping base text, and the remaining attempted readings are all explicitly marked. No research inference should rely on these reconstructed phrases without checking the scan.
