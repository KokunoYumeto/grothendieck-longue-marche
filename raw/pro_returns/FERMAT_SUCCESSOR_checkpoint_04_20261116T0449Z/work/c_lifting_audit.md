## The field-intersection sublemma in Lead 7 is true

Let \(K_1=\mathbb Q(i)\), \(K_2=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), inside a common algebraic closure, and let \(L=K_1^{ab}\cap K_2^{ab}\). Then
\[
L=\mathbb Q^{ab}(3^{1/4}).
\]
Proof. Each \(K_j^{ab}\) is Galois over \(\mathbb Q\), and contains \(\mathbb Q^{ab}\). Thus \(G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)\) has two distinct abelian open subgroups \(H_j=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K_j)\), each of index two. Set \(H_0=H_1\cap H_2\). The groups \(H_1,H_2\) generate \(G\), and \(H_0\) commutes with both, hence is central. Choose \(a\in H_1\setminus H_0\), \(b\in H_2\setminus H_0\). Then \(G=\langle a,b,H_0\rangle\); \(a^2,b^2\in H_0\), and \(G'\) is the closed group generated by the central commutator \([a,b]\). Since \([a^2,b]=[a,b]^2=1\), this commutator has order at most two. Therefore \([L:\mathbb Q^{ab}]\le2\).

Put \(\alpha=3^{1/4}>0\). The splitting field \(L_0=\mathbb Q(i,\alpha)\) of \(T^4-3\) has group \(D_8\), generated by \(r(\alpha)=i\alpha,r(i)=i\) and \(s(\alpha)=\alpha,s(i)=-i\). Its subgroup fixing \(K_1\) is \(\langle r\rangle\simeq C_4\). Since \(\sqrt{-3}=i\alpha^2\), the subgroup fixing \(K_2\) is \(\langle r^2,rs\rangle\simeq C_2^2\). Both are abelian, so \(L_0\subset L\). The field \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) is not Galois, hence is not contained in the abelian extension \(\mathbb Q^{ab}/\mathbb Q\). Thus the bound is attained, proving equality. The exact finite action law is independently checked by the same program. This proof establishes the field identity, but it does not identify the images of the two CM representations.

## LM7_PUSH D.50: proof audit

Read in full: `LM7_PUSH_return/sections/D_pronilpotent_lift_agent.tex`; also `referee/REFEREE_A_D50_E58.md`. No error was found in the core lifting argument. The following independently reconstructed reasoning explains why its unusually strong conclusion is not contradicted by the usual finite central obstructions.

For a finite extension \(1\to A\to E\to D\to1\), retain every subgroup \(J\le E\) surjecting to \(D\) and every irreducible representation \(\rho\) of \(M=J\cap A\) fixed by \(J\). Its projective intertwiner obstruction is realized by a finite **central** extension \(Q_\rho\to D\): take pairs \((j,B)\) implementing conjugation on \(\rho\), restrict \(\det B\) to roots of unity of order \(\exp J\), then divide by the graph of \(M\). The joint image of all source maps to these scalar covers is a finite central refinement \(D^\dagger\to D\).

The exact pants character count is
\[
\frac1{|M|}\sum_{[\rho]\text{ fixed}}\frac1{\dim\rho}
\prod_{i=1}^3\sum_{a\in U_i}\operatorname{tr}\Psi_\rho(a,R_\rho(x_i)).
\]
To derive it, let \(B_i=\sum_{a\in U_i}a\) in \(\mathbb C[J]\). Each \(B_i\) centralizes \(\mathbb C[M]\). If the quotient value of \(x_i\) moves an irreducible block, \(e_\rho B_i=e_\rho B_ie_\rho=0\). Because the first two quotient values generate \(D\), only invariant blocks remain. In such a block the lifted sums are scalar by Schur's lemma. The three scalar denominators, the trace of their product, and the regular-character factor give precisely the displayed exponent \((\dim\rho)^{-1}\). The product of the three second coordinates is one because \(R_\rho\) is a homomorphism of the actual source. Thus there is no omitted projective scalar.

Transporting the peripheral conjugators through \(D^\dagger\) leaves every trace unchanged. This applies separately to **every** subgroup image \(J\), so Möbius inversion on the finite subgroup lattice preserves the number of epimorphisms to \(E\), not only the number of homomorphisms. The reference epimorphism makes this number positive.

For \(A=\gamma_\infty(E)\), \(D\) is nilpotent and the central extension \(D^\dagger\) is nilpotent as well. In the relative situation replace \(A\) by \(\gamma_\infty(U_0)\), the residual of the finite image of the retained open normal subgroup \(U\). The image of \(U\) in \(D^\dagger\) is again nilpotent. Thus the given **whole** relative-pronilpotent automorphism acts on the required scalar refinement; a map known only at the original finite level need not do so.

Compactness of the product of three full-source conjugator spaces then imposes simultaneously: all relations, prescribed images of all conjugators in the retained relative-pronilpotent quotient, and generation of every finite quotient. Each finite family is soluble by the finite theorem above. The resulting continuous endomorphism is onto, hence an automorphism by Hopficity of finitely generated profinite groups. The modular torsion relations hold because the chosen images are conjugates of the actual order-two and order-three source generators. This gives the literal conjugator lifting claimed in R13.

This is an audit of the proof supplied, not a reliance on the referee verdict. The auxiliary positive-genus formula D.50.12 is unnecessary for this argument; its novelty and all possible generalizations have not been asserted.
