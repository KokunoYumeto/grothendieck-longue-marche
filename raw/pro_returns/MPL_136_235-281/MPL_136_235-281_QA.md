## Mechanical verification

The final structural validator reported no errors or warnings. Its checks cover the expected filenames and scan markers, exact concatenation, the inventory's schema and permitted categories, unique page-local element identifiers and order, completion/audit coverage, required final files, balanced literal braces and environments, explicit math delimiters, allowed custom macro vocabulary, and all 47 recorded hashes.

Additional structural checks found valid relative arrow endpoints in the tikz-cd diagrams, consistent array column counts, balanced left/right delimiters, and no raw math-only tokens outside the checked math contexts. These are static source checks. They do not establish that every reading matches the image.

**No successful LaTeX compilation or page rendering is claimed.** The available TeX installation lacked a usable class/package tree and formats for the required packages. The requested deliverable is a fragment whose custom macros are to be defined by the recipient's typesetting setup; no wrapper or rendered PDF has been substituted for it.

The last hash verification is recorded in *progress.log* immediately before packaging. It confirms that the frozen page files and combined fragment remain unchanged after all context and literature work.

## Reading procedure and limitations

The principal transcriber personally viewed every whole-page image and both supplied halves in the first reading, then personally checked every page again against its transcription before freezing it. Rotation, enlargement, and cropping were used for difficult or inverted material. No OCR was used on the supplied manuscript images. Transcription and the visual second reading were not delegated.

Parallel assistance was used only for static structural checks and, after the freeze, research, bibliography, and mathematical review. Those workers did not inspect manuscript scans or amend the frozen text.

Two procedural departures are disclosed in the doubts file and log: the ZIP central-directory names were initially listed before the prompt had been fully read, although context contents remained unopened until the freeze; and the complete notation table was consolidated at the start of the second reading, rather than fully maintained from the first page. Neither is represented as strict compliance with the requested timing of those steps.

The less secure material remains explicitly marked, especially the cancelled PD calculations, faint scan 254, the overlapping computations on 279, the inverted cancelled block on 281, and the natural/sharp or b/flat-like marks on reused typed pages. TikZ reproductions of strokes and signatures are approximations, not pixel facsimiles. A row marked “yes” in the inventory means that its identified element has been represented in the transcription; it is not a claim of infallible decipherment.

## Research controls

The full supplied results register was read before writing the notes. Earlier-run statuses are kept separate from independent checks and specifically reviewed results. The Hua finding is assessed with its ambient-category qualification: finite-tail full faithfulness and the nonnegative unit are proved in the supporting memo, while the unrestricted all-integer-graded unit is false.

The research notes preserve the distinction between the manuscript's own doubts, cancelled proposals, gaps identified by the present analysis, and explicitly conditional repairs. In particular, the abstract derived inference, the finite divided-power repair, and the two different infinite negligible classes are not conflated. The ordinary cofree cotensor formula is proved with its counit assumptions rather than dismissed by a generic flatness warning.

Literature comparisons use identified primary sources or explicitly identified primary follow-up papers. The separate literature memo records the original-full-text access limits for Miller and Dwyer, and distinguishes Horel's published year from the date of a later arXiv version. The later context and published texts did not alter any frozen reading.
