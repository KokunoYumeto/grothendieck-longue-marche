## Legibility and witnesses

The uncanceled main prose and most large formulas are reasonably legible in the supplied scans. The weakest readings are the heavily canceled opening of scan079, words and indices crossed by several strokes, small overwritten operators in065/074/075, and cramped slanted margins. Darkening and enlargement expose strokes but cannot recover letters physically covered by ink. The transcription retains in-place doubts where those strokes do not decide; graphical traces preserve a few unresolved marks without inventing an underlying word or symbol.

The blind-audit findings on066 and071–079 distinguish recovered continuity from residual uncertainty. On066 the full sloping context resolves the marginal Isom formula and its arguments. Elsewhere, additional inspection preserves doubt: the raised inline J mark on071, explicite on072, canceled deux and I-prime on073, notations plus the first canceled comparison on074, and définissent on075. On076, la/le remains unresolved and action is a candidate whose ending is cut by the right scan edge. The raised Or on a on074 is retained as an insertion. The chain’s open point, literal b_{-1} and absent upper weight remain separate visible features; mathematical expectations do not fill them in. On077 the audit extends the raised insertion to dans une and qualifies the canceled de. On078 the full la flèche/l’hom. group and the retraced third-margin Gamma exponent remain unresolved. On079 the canceled l’infini/l’âme and de la réunion/sur le germe groups, the live Ils sont donc prefix, and both star/left-arrow signs in (100) remain qualified. The visible hyphen in multi-plicités is retained.

All 24 supplied pages have completed independent readings of their designated boxes and main-reader adjudication of the differing boxes. The final text retains the unresolved readings recorded in those audits.

Witness C usually follows the physical order, abbreviations and running French more closely, but it is not an authority: it sometimes supplies punctuation or a plausible index, misses small insertions or extra strokes, flattens layout, and regularizes local underlines. Witness H sometimes preserves a useful detail—for example the underline inside S(G) and the recorded vertical last isomorphism on077—but often paraphrases compressed prose, expands abbreviations, or assigns readable ink to an illegible or canceled span. Their agreement can conceal a shared omission or normalization. Confidence belongs to each local reading and its evidence, not to either witness as a whole.

The drawings are diplomatic TikZ approximations of visible objects, labels, gaps and cancellation strokes. They do not certify the mathematical meaning of every pen lift or overworked mark. The notes distinguish manuscript claims, limited arithmetic or proof checks actually performed, and places where mathematical expectations prompted another look but did not determine the transcription.
