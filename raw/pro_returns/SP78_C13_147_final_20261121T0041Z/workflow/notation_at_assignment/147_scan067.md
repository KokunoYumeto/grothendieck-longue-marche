# Working notation table

Observations are grounded in the reviewed scans listed in each row. They are reading aids, never permission to normalize a clear contrary glyph. This table is updated as later pages are reviewed.

| Written sign or form | Transcription and local use | Source / caution |
|---|---|---|
| G, with or without a short underline | `G`, `\underline G` | 056 introduction and heading underline G; not every G has that underline. |
| δ with a looped lower return | `\delta`; `\delta_\alpha` | 056 (67); modular dimension contributions. |
| g with a long descender | `g`, usually `g_\alpha` | Genus in 056–057; do not infer a subscript 0 when the ink has only α. |
| ν as a broad descending check-like stroke | `\nu_\alpha` | Marked weight in 056 (67)–(69). |
| μ with a long first downstroke, wavy mark above | `\widetilde\mu_\alpha` | Order in 056 (68)–(69); distinguish hat from tilde on each occurrence. |
| ν with an angular mark above | `\widehat\nu_\alpha` | Total weight in 057; the hat is distinct from μ’s tilde. |
| Angular Γ followed by raised upright and dot | `\Gamma^!` | 057 (70), exclamation, not prime. |
| Large looped symmetric-group letter | `\mathfrak S` | 057 (70), subscript 4; retained alphabet convention for the looped S. |
| I with angular hat, A with wavy mark | `\widehat I`, `\widetilde A` | 057, note adjoining (71); full accents included in blind rereading box. |
| dim mod / dim amb | `\dim\operatorname{mod}`, `\dim\operatorname{amb}` | 054 opening has mod; the three rows of (65) have amb. Preserve the distinction. |
| MD- | `MD-` | Capital M and D; in 057 inserted before two occurrences of graphes. |
| Two small quotation strokes | TeX opening/closing double quotes | 057 rationnels; do not replace with French guillemets. |
| top, homéom., isom., nb, dim, can., i.e. | Preserve these short forms as seen | 054–057; punctuation of inserted top in 054 remains uncertain. |

| Curving insertion connectors beside words | `\inserted{...}` at the connector's endpoint | 058 both `ou poids marqué` groups attach after `genre`; the curved line above `zéro` does not strike it. |
| Punctuation of dim and mod | Preserve the point only where a separate dark dot is present | 058 has both `dim` and `dim.` in different places; abbreviation punctuation is not normalized globally. |
| Literal alpha and mu where another notation might be expected | `\alpha=S`, `(\alpha_i,\widehat\mu_i)` | 059 case2: two horizontal bars are written; alpha has no g-descender; mu has a long first leg. Preserve the apparent slips. |
| Short inserted index alpha | `\widetilde\mu_\alpha` | 059 genus1 order insertion has no terminal zero, unlike the clear neighboring alpha0 insertion. |
| Tall Pi with top bar and two straight legs | `\Pi_1` | 060 final paragraph: capital, not lower-case pi. |
| Z with separated doubled diagonal/outline | `\mathbb Z` | 060 two display locations, three glyphs: preserve double-struck alphabet rather than merely bold. |
| Short final t in action word | `trivialt` | 060 (75), no written ement; abbreviation expansion belongs only in the list. |
| Double-stroked projective-space P and base Z | `\mathbb P`, `\mathbb Z` | 061 (77): separate doubled uprights/diagonals; follow each glyph rather than C bold styling. |
| Capital Codim and lower-case codim | Preserve local case | 061 table headers have a large capital C; later headers must be checked on their own ink. |
