## More specific patterns within the broad categories

- Scans 061–063: an arrow crosses a table divider. Array-cell placement in both witnesses can preserve the named source and target yet change the visible relation to the ruled table. Read the rules and arrow together, not as independent decorations.
- Scans 060 and 062: both witnesses complete mathematical punctuation that the source leaves incomplete or mismatched. In 060 an opening subscript parenthesis has no visible mate; in 062 a point-set closes with a parenthesis and has no comma before the group. These are retained as written, with uncertainty where needed.
- Scans 058 and 061: an insertion connector can run above a live word or end in a hook resembling punctuation. Identify its continuous path before treating it as a strike or a comma.

- Scans 072–073: a long descender from the preceding line meets an accent position. In072 both independent arms establish a real hat; in073 the neighboring inserted M retains a hat/no-hat doubt. The crossing alone does not establish cancellation or the absence of a hat.
- Scans 070–075: replacing a drawing by a generic graph can lose filled or open point shapes, short pen-lift gaps, a pointed loop subscript, canceled trial strokes and small extra marks. These are read and drawn individually; a visible gap is not automatically interpreted as topological disconnection.
- Scans 072–073 extend the incomplete-delimiter pattern: a canceled nested parenthesis is not completed in072, and the upper dimension expression in073 has no separately visible opening parenthesis. The notes distinguish literal syntax from the expected formula.
- Scans 074–075: expected sequences can supply absent or repeated labels. Scan074 retains the literal b_{-1}, its open vertex and the missing upper weight; scan075 repeats c_{i-1} in the upper local chain. Other thickened plus/minus strokes remain explicit doubts. Neither the intended chain nor its arithmetic supplies missing ink.

- Scans 076–077: the same proper name has locally different accent evidence (Teichmuller / Teichmüller). A confirmed spelling on one leaf must not erase the visible form on the next.
- Scans 077–078: underlines disappear locally within repeated G notation, while neighboring occurrences retain clear bars. Both witnesses sometimes restore them by pattern. The lack of a separate bar is recorded at the particular occurrence, not generalized to the whole symbol family.
- Scans 077–078: an apparently incomplete map shaft is still a source object. The headless bar in077(96) and hooked but blunt-ended left vertical in078(97) are not completed by copying the neighboring arrows.
- Scan 078: both drafts insert a syntactically useful en where the crop shows only the raised une. A nearby overwritten trace is preserved separately without being assigned the missing word. This is a concrete instance of shared plausible-text invention, beyond ordinary spelling correction.

- Scan 079: both drafts collapse two separately written ou groups across a line break. The first reduced group remains doubtful, but the repetition is preserved. A separate prose asterisk and detached closing curve are also retained without inventing a footnote or a matching delimiter.

- Scan 066: a sloping marginal formula continues into an area that resembles an interlinear prose insertion. The complete rotated crop joins the arguments \((\{0,1,\infty\},I'')\) to Isom. Reading the cropped fragments separately split one formula and encouraged a false primed-pair reading.
- Scans 071–074: familiar repeated forms do not settle a locally obscured mark. The inline J on071 retains prime-plus-colon/bang alternatives; explicite on072 and notations on074 retain word uncertainty; the canceled deux and I-prime on073, and the first canceled ≥/> comparison on074, remain qualified. These certainty refinements are recorded separately from substantive witness corrections.
- Scan 074: the live Or on a is a raised restart above the canceled a il y a. Retaining its insertion status preserves the revision sequence even when a linear reading gives the same words.
- Scans 075–076: compressed endings and clipped text remain qualified even where a familiar phrase is plausible. Définissent on075 has a densely retraced ending; la/le on076 has an ambiguous final vowel, and action loses its ending at the right scan edge. The visible beginnings do not certify the missing or merged letters.

- Scans 077–078: insertion and uncertainty can extend across more than one word. On077 both dans and une belong to the raised addition, while the canceled de and par remain qualified. On078 the article and noun form one unresolved la flèche/l’hom. group; the overlapping strokes do not support a separately certain article. The retraced Gamma exponent in the third margin remains qualified despite clearer bangs elsewhere.

- Scan 079: the canceled l’infini/l’âme and de la réunion/sur le germe groups remain whole alternatives, while the live prefix Ils sont donc is separately qualified. A recognizable beginning or a plausible sentence does not make the overlapped internal letters certain.
- Scan 079: both raised signs in (100) retain star/left-arrow alternatives, although the M bodies are independently bare. The clear stars in the opening pair do not resolve these retraced signs. The separate hyphen in multi-plicités is retained at the physical word division.

These are concrete recurring subpatterns, not additional mutually exclusive counting categories. The ten-category table counts each logged substantive place only once.
