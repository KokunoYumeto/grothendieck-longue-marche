# A split Birman extension and the exact remaining nonabelian cocycle

Research note, 16 November 2026. This is not a proof that K is trivial.
The formal B73/B74 sources are frozen for checkpoint10; this note is a
subsequent structural reduction with its remaining topology gate explicit.

## 1. Actual markings and groups

Retain B65/B73/B74's actual twelve-point marking and write

    r=(1 3)(2 5)(6 8)(12 10)(9 11)(4 7),
    s=(1 2)(3 5)(4 6)(7 8)(9 10)(11 12).

Both belong to the actual tetrahedral T, and they commute. Let d be the
chosen order-five rotation, J=<d,r>, and G=C_Gamma(J). B74 identifies
G with C2*Z and its embedded full completion with the actual completed
J-centralizer. The original pole labels1,3 are fixed individually by G.

In the quotient by r, label the ordinary orbits

    U={1,3}, V={2,5}, W1={6,8}, W2={12,10},
    W3={9,11}, W4={4,7}.

The two branch marks form an unordered pair. The induced sbar swaps U,V,
swaps W1,W4 and W2,W3, and swaps the branch marks. The last assertion
also follows geometrically: two different commuting sphere involutions
interchange each other's two fixed points.

Let R8 be the colored eight-point mapping-class group fixing U,V
individually, preserving the branch pair, and permitting all permutations
of W1,...,W4. It is the index-five stabilizer of V in Q8-tilde from B74.
Let G_V be the corresponding index-five subgroup of G. Thus

    H5 <= G_V <= R8,       [G:G_V]=5,       [G_V:H5]=2.

The second inclusion means the actual based quotient embedding from B74.
It is faithful on full completions. Every element of H5 fixes V; the
additional elements of G_V need not be pure. In fact the nontrivial
permutation in G_V/H5 interchanges original labels2,5. This is why a
possible deck discrepancy must be addressed below rather than suppressed
by assuming that all lifts are pure.

## 2. The split actual Birman extension

Forget W1,...,W4. The retained labels are U,V and the unordered branch
pair, with target Q4=C2*Z. The Birman sequence is

    1 -> N -> R8 --pi--> Q4 -> 1,
    N=B4(S0,4).

Here B4(S0,4) is the ordinary unordered four-strand surface braid group
on the sphere punctured at the four retained marks. There is no low-
complexity central quotient in this kernel: the retained four-point
surface has contractible Teichmueller space, and the standard Birman
sequence identifies its configuration-space fundamental group directly.

The explicit restriction to G_V is particularly simple. As established
in forgetful_retraction_obstruction.md, its orbifold parameter is w with
one cone point2 at0 and four cusps4,infinity,a,b, where

    a=-4-(8/5)sqrt(5),   b=-4+(8/5)sqrt(5).

The map to Q4 fills a,b. With based meridians at w*=4/5, choose a cone
meridian e, one surviving cusp meridian a0, and the two extra cusp
meridians c1,c2. Then the exact homomorphism is

    G_V=<e,a0,c1,c2 | e^2=1> -> <e,a0 | e^2=1>=Q4,
    c1,c2 |->1.

Its restriction to <e,a0> is an isomorphism. This gives an actual group
section j:Q4->G_V->R8 and therefore

    R8 = N semidirect Q4.

The section does not retract the ambient group onto G_V. Its image is
only the smaller C2*Z generated by the surviving e,a0. In particular the
two extra cusp generators are precisely the obstruction to that immediate
forgetful retraction.

## 3. Exact comparison after full profinite completion

N is finitely generated: its pure subgroup is an iterated extension of
finite-rank free groups by the Fadell--Neuwirth fibrations, and the
unordered quotient is finite. For any finite-index subgroup of N, take
a characteristic finite-index subgroup N0 inside its core. Such subgroups
are cofinal because a finitely generated group has only finitely many
subgroups of each fixed finite index. The Q4-action preserves N0 and
acts through the finite group Aut(N/N0). The resulting finite semidirect
quotient of R8 induces on N exactly the prescribed finite quotient N/N0.
Thus N has its full profinite topology in R8.

The retraction pi and its section also give the full induced topology
on j(Q4). Profinite completion is right exact for the normal quotient
by N; the preceding full topology identifies the completed kernel with
N-hat itself. Consequently the actual completed Birman extension is

    1 -> N-hat -> R8-hat -> Q4-hat ->1,
    R8-hat = N-hat semidirect Q4-hat.

This does not use the full profinite completion of the infinitely
generated kernel of G_V->Q4. Its closure inside G_V-hat is the kernel
of G_V-hat->Q4-hat, by right exactness, and its embedding into N-hat is
the actual one coming from B74. No comparison of that closure with the
abstract infinite-rank kernel's full completion is made.

## 4. The involution and the nonabelian cocycle

sbar normalizes R8. On the retained four points it is the double
transposition (U V)(branch1 branch2). The pure four-point mapping-class
group centralizes the canonical Klein four subgroup, and the one allowed
branch transposition also fixes this double transposition. Hence sbar
acts trivially by conjugation on Q4. Therefore

    delta(g)=sbar g sbar^(-1) g^(-1) in N,       g in G_V,

is a well-typed based map. It satisfies the nonabelian cocycle identity

    delta(gh)=delta(g) [g delta(h) g^(-1)].

It is not asserted to be a group homomorphism. Conjugation by sbar is an
actual automorphism of R8, so after completion this formula defines the
continuous map delta-hat:G_V-hat -> N-hat using the exact completed
Birman kernel in Section3.

## 5. Removing the possible deck discrepancy

The double-cover quotient is based using the B73/B74 section fixing
original label1. The full preimage of R8 in the symmetric twelve-point
group is its direct product with the deck group <r>: every lift can be
changed uniquely to fix label1. Its completion has the same finite deck
kernel. The actual s normalizes this preimage, though it need not
preserve the label1-fixing section. Thus delta-hat(g)=1 initially implies
only that conjugation by the actual lifted g takes s to either s or rs.

The second possibility is impossible. The exact original permutations
give

    order(sd)=3,       order(rsd)=5.

The finite group <d,r,s> is the actual A5. Since g lies in the actual
completed G=C_Gamma(J), it centralizes d and r. If it conjugated s to rs,
it would conjugate the element sd of order3 to rsd of order5. This
contradicts invariance of order under conjugation, also inside the full
completion. Therefore every downstairs zero lifts to actual commutation
with s. This argument covers the nonpure elements of G_V; it does not
silently invoke the all-label-pure section.

## 6. The two zero-set statements, in both directions

For a discrete g in G_V, delta(g)=1 now implies that g centralizes
<d,r,s>=A5. The actual discrete A5-centralizer is trivial by B52, so

    delta^(-1)(1)={1}.

More generally delta(g)=delta(h) if and only if h^(-1)g is fixed by
conjugation by sbar. The deck argument just given proves that delta is
injective as a map of discrete sets.

For a completed g in G_V-hat, delta-hat(g)=1 implies actual commutation
with A5. Its permutation centralizes the transitive A5-action on twelve
marks. That permutation centralizer has order2; its nonidentity swaps
original labels1,3. But every element of G_V-hat fixes these two labels
individually. Hence g is pure. It follows that g lies in

    K=C_(P-hat)(A5).

Conversely, K is contained in the actual H5-hat by definition, and H5
is contained in G_V. It centralizes s, so its actual downstairs quotient
has delta-hat equal to1. We have therefore proved the exact comparison

    delta-hat^(-1)(1)=K

under the faithful B74 based embedding. Its fibers are the sets hK
(left cosets) in G_V-hat. No normality of K in G_V-hat is asserted.

## 7. What remains to be established

Discrete injectivity of this nonabelian orbit map does not prove
injectivity on full completions. A precise finite-cover target is:

For every finite-index normal U0 in G_V, find a characteristic
finite-index N0 in N such that, for every g in the discrete G_V,

    delta(g) in N0  implies  g in U0.

This condition is equivalent to K=1. Necessity follows by compactness
from the continuous completed map with trivial zero fiber; sufficiency
follows by density and the clopen finite quotients, using the cofinal
characteristic subgroups of the finitely generated N. Merely separating
each individual nonidentity delta(g) in a finite quotient of N is weaker:
the required quotient must separate the entire complement of U0 at once.

The value of the reduction is the exact, lower-complexity target and
its genuine split full-completion comparison. It does not yet supply the
uniform finite-cover separation just stated, a virtual retraction, or a
nonidentity element of K.
