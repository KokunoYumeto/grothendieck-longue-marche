Use DE's source-contained Nielsen basis
  A=x3^-1*x2^-1*x1*x2*x3, B=x2, D=x4, E=x6,
  a=x3, b=x5, c=x7.
Let U=<A,B,D,E> and let Gamma be the group generated by the six certified
source-based fiber automorphisms.  Their actual source-kernel image is I.
DE's exact source expansions prove U <= I.

One can sharpen the description of I without a normality inference:
  I = < gamma(U) : gamma in Gamma >.
The first two source seeds, after inversion, are E^-1 D E and E^-1 A E.
For the action alpha_1 about q=1, put p=c^-1 E^-1 b^-1 E.  Its restriction
to U is Inn(p) theta, where theta is the free-product automorphism
  theta|<A,B> = Inn(BA),
  theta|<D,E> = Inn(DE).
The other two inverse seeds are p(E^-1 B E)p^-1 and p E p^-1, so both are
in alpha_1(U).  Conversely I is Gamma-invariant and contains U.  The source
image in F7 semidirect F6 is consequently exactly <U, the six base lifts>.

This description retains the whole Gamma-orbit, not merely a finite set of
peripheral generators.  It may make a uniform rewriting proof more concise.
It does NOT imply that any retained generator normalizes I.  In particular,
the fact that alpha_1(U)=p U p^-1 gives normalization information for the
finite free factor U only; applying it to gamma(U) requires additional
relations between alpha_1, gamma and the retained conjugators.

The root-half-twist alpha_0 sends A,D by conjugation with a b^-1, fixes B,E,
and sends (a,b,c) to (a b a^-1,a,c).  The other four nontrivial-root actions
induce identity on F(a,b,c), but their conjugating words contain shifted
deleted meridians.  Those words cannot be discarded on the assumption that
I is normal: that is precisely the missing assertion.
