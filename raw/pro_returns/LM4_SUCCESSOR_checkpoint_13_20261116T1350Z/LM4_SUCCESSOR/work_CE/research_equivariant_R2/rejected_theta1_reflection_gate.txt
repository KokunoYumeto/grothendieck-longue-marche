Rejected stronger interpretation of the theta1 candidate.

On D† of order13824, with normal forms x^a y^b w^d c^e and
a,b mod24,d mod12, the automorphism x->xw^6,y->y,c->c is an
involutory c-equivariant multiplier-one ORIENTATION-peripheral
automorphism. It does not preserve the named reflection class xc.

For an even conjugator g=x^r y^s w^d, direct collection gives
 g(xc)g^{-1}=(2r+1,2s,-s(2r+1),1).
To obtain (1,0,6,1), the first two coordinates force r,s divisible
by12, and the third coordinate is then0 modulo12, a contradiction.
Odd conjugators give the same class because c sends xc to x^{-1}c,
which is conjugate to xc. Hence this candidate cannot refute the
fully reflection-labelled rule. It is retained only as an explicit
failed gate, not promoted as a theorem of that stronger scope.

The later binary-icosahedral identity/bad-frame construction is
different: every named reflection class is fixed literally, and the
obstruction concerns retaining a prescribed central conjugator frame.
