THE SUM PENCIL OF THE SAME ACTUAL ORDERED SURFACE
Research proposal; no source-kernel equality or computed braid words claimed.

Work after the already justified finite-cover changes and scaling zeta=1:
 Y={(v,w) in C2: v,w notin{0}cup mu5; v!=w; vw notin mu5}.
The actual source is the line v+w=2. Instead of the ratio pencil, project
by T=(v+w)/2 and use v as fiber coordinate, so w=2T-v.

No deleted component is an entire T-fiber: the deleted lines are vertical,
horizontal, or v=w, and the product hyperbolas are not sum fibers. Thus
this pencil has base ALL C. No finite value is excluded merely to keep
the whole fiber nonempty. The generic finite punctures are
 v=0, 2T;
 v=u, 2T-u foru inmu5;
 v=T;
 v^2-2Tv+q=0 forq inmu5.
There are23 of them, hence generic fundamental group F23. Every polynomial
is monic in v or affine with finite coefficients; there is no finite
escape value. Infinity is the one additional puncture on the fiber P1.

The complete critical set can be enumerated without a braid calculation:
 (a) T=0;
 (b) T inmu5;
 (c) T in -mu5;
 (d) T=u/2, u inmu5;
 (e) T=(u+v)/2, distinct unordered u,v inmu5.
There are1+5+5+5+10=26 distinct values. For(e), adjacent and nonadjacent
pairs have distinct radii cos(pi/5) and cos(2pi/5), giving two disjoint
five-element orbits. Those radii differ from0,1 and1/2; the two sets
mu5 and -mu5 are disjoint. Thus no listed type overlaps another.

Exhaustion follows by checking pairs of divisor components:
- vertical/horizontal intersections give sums of points in{0}cupmu5;
- diagonal intersections with vertical/horizontal give0 ormu5;
- diagonal/product intersections give T^2=q, hence plus/minus mu5;
- vertical/product and horizontal/product intersections have both
  coordinates inmu5, hence were already counted;
- distinct product hyperbolas do not intersect; products never meet
  the zero axes in the affine surface.
The branch points of each monic quadratic are exactly T^2=q, already
included. This accounts for all finite changes of the fiber punctures.

Local types and puncture deficits:
- AtT=0, the two zero axes and diagonal meet at(0,0): three distinct
  deleted lines, three generic punctures become one, deficit2.
- AtT=u inmu5, the verticalv=u, horizontalw=u, diagonalv=w and
  hyperbola vw=u^2 meet at(u,u). They are four distinct transverse
  divisor branches. The chosen sum fiber is tangent to the hyperbola,
  so its nearby fiber has FIVE punctures in the cluster: three linear
  points and two quadratic roots. These become one, deficit4.
- AtT in -mu5, only the diagonal and one product hyperbola meet. The
  sum fiber is again tangent to the hyperbola: three points become
  one, deficit2.
- AtT=u/2 there are two ordinary nodes, at(0,u) and(u,0): deficit2.
- AtT=(u+v)/2 withu!=v inmu5 there are two ordinary triple points,
  at(u,v) and(v,u), involving one vertical line, one horizontal line
  and their product hyperbola: deficit4.

The total deficit is2+5*4+5*2+5*2+10*4=82. Thus the constructible
Euler count is -22+82=60, matching the original free7-by-free11
bundle. This checks the enumeration but does not establish asphericity.

At the ACTUAL source T=1, the five retained cluster punctures at(1,1)
merge; no U puncture is involved, since U means the vertical/horizontal
roots other than1. The generic23 finite punctures become19, exactly
the original source rank. A common based cut would identify the source
free factor by replacing those five generators by their boundary
product. Four retained generators are initially missing. Locally one
quadratic half twist identifies its two hyperbola meridians, leaving
three independent missing directions, in agreement with the ratio
presentation's missing b plus its two retained base generators.

This alternative could produce a finite presentation on23 fiber
meridians with82 independent local variation relators and NO retained
base stable letters: all26 critical values are filled. The local
counts are4 relators at each ordinary four-component/tangent cluster,
2 at0 and each negative-root cluster,2 at each paired-node value, and
4 at each paired-triple value. However the actual based whiskers,
local braid words, and equivalence of a proposed minimal relation
list still need to be constructed and certified. No such word file
has been produced in this note.

This is not a new relative Lefschetz theorem. Special-fiber images can
remain proper, and a proof of the desired deletion-kernel equality
would still have to control all four missing retained directions.
The distinction from B.76 is concrete: B.76 has an entire deleted
fiber parallel to its selected source, producing an excluded base
value. Here the sum pencil has no such component. That difference
motivates this presentation but by itself proves no surjectivity.
