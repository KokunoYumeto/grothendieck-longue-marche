### Q01 — Is the category built from cote 16’s Lefschetz correspondence rings?

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Witness:** 12.pdf, scans 056 and059, “anneau des correspondances” and “la catégorie des motifs”; CT16/018 and049, “anneau … de correspondances Lefschetzien” and “category of motives which is semi-simple”. **Context search:** catégorie; correspondances; Lefschetz; projecteurs; enveloppe. **Context:** CN S3, S13–S16 and HU’s later “L’enveloppe pseudo-abélienne” / “L’exemple des variétés”, pp.143–151, outside this packet. **RS:** WG2 §2.4 #3; MPL_11_043-058+56 N031; MPL_14_087-132 M01; MPL_15_087-138 L01; MPL_60_002-035 C01.

**The current pages use a category of motives; they do not construct it from the single Lefschetz operator-ring formalism.** A correspondence construction needs morphisms between varieties, an equivalence relation on cycles, projectors, tensor products, and Tate twists; the classical objects \((X,p,m)\) are described in [J92, pp.447–448]. CT16’s rings supply compatible operator and decomposition data, while HU locates a broader categorical construction on later, unsupplied leaves. The precise addition here is the Galois-commutant formulation on scan 056 and the proposed tensor-categorical Lie pro-motive on scan 059.

### Q07 — A precise modern version of that exact sequence

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Witness:** 12.pdf, scan 059, “structures tensorielles”, as the comparison point. **Context search:** Nori; Ayoub; Galois; extension; composante. **Context:** HU’s preceding out-of-packet discussion and RS’s S15/MB10 comparison leads; the theorem is supplied by [K25, Theorem 4.6 and §9], not by P12. **RS:** MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 S15: and M07; MPL_18_018-062 MB10.

For rational Nori motives, compatible embeddings into \(\mathbf C\), and the corresponding Betti fibre functors, the checked descent theorem gives
\[
1\longrightarrow G_{\mathrm N}(\overline{\mathbf Q})
\longrightarrow G_{\mathrm N}(\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow \underline{\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf Q}/\mathbf Q)}
\longrightarrow1.
\]
Here the underline denotes the proconstant affine group scheme, the arguments indicate the base fields of the categories, and the quotient is detected by Artin motives. Identifying this geometric kernel with the neutral component is a further component statement; exactness alone neither supplies that identification nor a canonical splitting.

### N010 — What the double-commutant argument actually establishes

**Kind:** unproved claim. **Witness:** 12.pdf, scan 056, “Le deuxième énoncé + injectivité … entraîne tout”. **Context search:** commutant; semi-simple; Hodge; correspondances. **Context:** HU §14 p.55 = scan 056 names the double-commutant theorem; CT16/049 explicitly connects the Hodge index relation with semisimplicity of the correspondence ring. **RS:** WG2 §2.3 #7 and #2.

Once the correspondence algebra is finite-dimensional semisimple and acts faithfully, equality of the Galois enveloping algebra with its commutant gives the reverse commutant equality by the semisimple bicommutant theorem.
