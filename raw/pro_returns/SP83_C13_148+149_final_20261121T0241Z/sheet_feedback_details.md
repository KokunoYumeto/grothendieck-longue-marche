
### Recurrences worth carrying into the next run

No additional primary error class outside the supplied sheet was established. The following more specific recurrences refine its existing classes; they are not extra places to add to the totals above.

| Recurrence | Observed places and consequence |
|---|---|
| A terminal pen return becomes an extra letter or a lost plural ending. | 148:048, 051–052, 056–059, 063–065. Compare the end itself with a same-page ending; preserve a local doubt when the final hook cannot be separated. Neither grammar nor a generally recurring habit resolves every occurrence. |
| A curved attachment leader is treated as a parenthesis or cancellation. | 148:053, 057–059, 061, 064–065; 149:003–004. Identify the text, its live/struck state and its attachment separately. A descending letter in a raised replacement can cross the old strike without being cancelled itself. |
| A vertical equality is changed into a downward arrow or flattened into a baseline relation. | 148:050, 062, 071. The two parallel upright strokes and the vertical arrangement of the adjacent objects decide the layout. |
| The same category abbreviation is silently regularized between occurrences. | 148:048, 052–053, 062. Spaces, a low extra a, missing vowels, points and final hyphens vary in the ink; the notation table records those differences instead of imposing one spelling. |
| A model's verbal description of a drawing loses the individual object-to-label mapping. | 148:068–069, 072, especially H's prose descriptions. Read every marked vertex, edge, numerical annotation and enclosure in place. On 072, separate 1 and 9 annotations become 19 in H's list. |
| Different drawing layers are collapsed into smooth schematic curves. | 148:069 and 072. Pencil joins, darker retracing, red contours, pen gaps and small local projections need separate attention. On 069 a missing finite join changes the graph; on 072 a grey doubled trace is wrongly made into a ninth extended red contour in C. A physical retracing is not automatically a second incidence. |
| Source spelling is lost despite C/H agreement. | 149:003–004. Both drafts omit the detached initial accent in réconstitue / réconstituer. These are two direct examples of agreement failing to protect a small source feature. |
| A raised abbreviation ending is flattened or read as an extra plural. | 149:003–004. The point abbreviation has p with a raised crossed t. Both drafts flatten it on 003 and at the first occurrence on 004; at the second occurrence on 004 both read pts. Preserve the raised t, the point after the first 004 occurrence and the absence of a point after the second. The raised ts in 148:064 is a distinct form. |
| A repeated overwritten mark is treated as a settled symbol because its mathematical role is plausible. | 149:004, six raised target marks. Repetition supports comparing shapes but does not reveal stroke order. Cancelled-prime/live-star alternatives remain explicit. |
| Show-through becomes a new recto drawing. | 148:071. C promotes the pale background contours into a separate figure. The decision is based on their uniformly faint appearance beneath the dark writing; no unchecked exact verso match is asserted. |

### Production limits to keep separate from reading errors

Colour-separated vector outlines were used only after visual reading of the large coloured drawings. Thresholds can omit pale brown/red stretches or include fragments of nearby text. The 072 preview review caught four omitted continuous stretches and a clipped fragment of a nearby numeral; these were corrected against the source. This is a limitation of the drawing-production process, not an additional manuscript error category or a claim that a threshold decides what is ink.

Some initially tight crops clipped a target's ascender, descender or edge. Their boundary-completion versions are retained beside the originals and documented in the manifests. Distinct places, retained crop files and independent second-reading boxes are different counts. The scheduled checkpoints include all retained evidence versions.
