# Legibility and limitations

All 22 whole-page images and their 44 supplied halves were read visually by one reader, without OCR, and every page received a second visual audit after the first pass was complete. Enlarged and rotated crops were used for compressed marginal writing, cancelled passages, diagram heads, and ambiguous numbers. Doubt marks preserve the proposed ink reading even where its mathematics or grammar is surprising; they do not certify that the alternative list is exhaustive.

The least secure areas are the vertical and cancelled marginalia on 146, the dense rewrites and small diagram labels on 155–157, the overwritten bottom-left note on 156, and the editorial references covered by geometric drawing on the reused typed sheet 158. Estimated unreadable-word counts occur only in physically obscured prose; uncertain mathematical symbols instead receive a proposed reading. The six-page report is generally much clearer, though copying streaks cross several words on 278–280; the faint upper-right traces on 277 and 279 cannot safely establish authorial pagination.

The TikZ drawings are visual reconstructions of the strokes, labels and relative positions, rather than geometric repairs. Fine pen texture and exact stroke width are approximate, especially in the overdrawn complexes on 157–158. The 15/17-page cover count on 144 is a doubtful pencil annotation, not a verified count of the present packet or an authorial page number. See the separate notes for post-freeze reading questions prompted by context; those questions do not amend the frozen LaTeX.
