## Counting and scope

The total of 102 is a count of distinct logged comparison places. One place differs from both C and H, so 14 + 89 is not the distinct-place total. A whole paragraph or display absent from H is one place. The 70 omissions are all against H on its seven partially transcribed pages; H's descriptions on the other 19 scans are excluded. The 85 certainty/scope entries in the doubts file are separate from these counts. A zero in an error category means no new difference of that category was established here, not that the page had no cancellations, insertions or abbreviations to check.
