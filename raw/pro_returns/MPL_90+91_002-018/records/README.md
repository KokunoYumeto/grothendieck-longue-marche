# Status of the working records

The five requested deliverables at the archive root and the 39 fragments in `pages/` are the final deliverables. The combined transcription is the exact ordered concatenation of those fragments. `frozen_transcription_sha256.json` records their pre-context hashes; the final validation reports and `progress.log` check that all 40 files remain unchanged.

The per-page JSON files and `reading_notes.md` preserve working records from the visual reading and audits. Their preliminary doubt descriptions or interpretive leads can be superseded by the final page fragments, the final exhaustive doubts index, and the final research notes. They are retained to document the work, not as alternative final transcriptions.

`research_91.md`, `research_comparisons.md`, and `research_closing.md` are assembly drafts. The authoritative reviewed text is `MPL_90+91_002-018_notes.md` at the archive root; subsequent refinements were applied there. In particular, the final standing index does not count the cross and uncertain “par t.f.” on 91/007 as a question mark.

`context_searches.jsonl` and its Markdown rendering record all 50 item-specific searches across the three supplied context files. Match excerpts document retrieval only; the reader's substantive comparisons are in the final notes. `context_source_manifest.json` identifies the supplied files by hash without including those source files in this archive.

The checkpoint receipts document the two hourly saved ZIPs. Validation reports are mechanical checks; they do not supply a second handwriting reader or a proof of the manuscript's mathematics. The scripts operate on saved files and records, and no script performs OCR. The compilation wrapper is provided for a fully installed TeX environment; successful compilation was not available in this environment, as explained in the final doubts file.
