Audit date: 16 November 2026. This note does not edit the assembled sections.

## Verdict

The mathematical arguments in RH.1–RH.3 and LH.1–LH.2 are valid with their
stated inputs. In particular, the genuine tangential representation is
unramified outside 2 and 3 at modulus 576, and the explicit full-multiplier
bound

`n_* = 2^51942 * 3^14136`

follows. The sign line lies in the permutation module on finite ordered
homology bases. LH.2 correctly excludes a characteristic-zero Delta

It does not make that exclusion after restricting to multiplier one.

FP.37's S4 character and its necessary full-GT equality are sound, subject
to the exact finite matrix certificate described below. Its cubic-frame

the centre in the index-two square-pair subgroup, not in the whole group
of order 48. Its map to the quotient S4's derived A4 is an isomorphism.

## 1. Relation module and cohomology

The graph has 576 edges, 288 vertices of stabilizer type C2, and 192 of
type C3. Its free fundamental-group rank is 97. Because the graph's
integral homology is free, its two exact lattice sequences remain exact
after profinite completion. For finite Q the continuous cochain groups
are finite products, so the usual long exact cohomology sequences apply
without a discrete-versus-profinite coefficient problem.

The Q-invariant vertex augmentation has image
`288 Zhat + 192 Zhat = 96 Zhat`. Its kernel is generated by `(2,-3)`.
The invariant edge norm maps to exactly that generator, because each
type-C2 vertex has two incident edges and each type-C3 vertex has three.
Thus H1(Q,M)=0. Shapiro also gives H1(Q,C0)=0, and the two long exact
sequences give H2(Q,M)=Z/96.

For g != 1, the trace deficit is `96 + number_of_fixed_vertices`.
Only one vertex orbit type can contribute, since an element fixing both
types would have order dividing both 2 and 3. Hence the deficit is in
[96,384], and cannot be divisible by 576. This proves the faithful deck
action on M/576 independently of any computed rank or group census.

Minor typography: `Zhat^{,2}` in RH.1 should be `Zhat^2`.

## 2. The modulus-576 cocycle argument

Equivariance and faithful deck action imply Phi_Q=1 whenever h_576(Phi)=1.
An automorphism of the extension that is identity on kernel and quotient
then defines a well-defined cocycle Q -> M/576. The connecting maps
identify H1(Q,M/nM) with H2(Q,M)[n]. In the comparison from 576 to 6,
the left vertical coefficient map is multiplication by 96 and the middle
map is identity. Thus the cocycle class reduces to zero because H2(Q,M)
has exponent 96.

It follows that the action on Gamma_6 is inner by a kernel element.
The FP.23 module extension is a quotient of Gamma_6 because its image
of N is abelian of exponent dividing 6. Its two centralizer-projection
lemmas then force q(beta) and q(A) to lie in H2 and H3. These intersect
q(F) trivially, as is seen in the genuine mod-2 quotient S3. The positive
signed convention and rho mod3 then give W=T=I. This proves the asserted
kernel containment and does not require characteristicity under every
automorphism of B.

The primitive cusp class of tau^12 is also valid: its embedded cycle
contains an edge outside a spanning tree with coefficient +/-1. An
integer functional on the cycle lattice therefore takes that class to
1 or -1. This proves primitivity over Zhat and at every finite modulus.
Its multiplier eigenvalue detects lambda exactly in integral homology,
and modulo n in finite homology.

## 3. Genuine arithmetic ramification

N is contained in F because Q admits the mod-2 quotient S3 and q(F)
is its kernel. The normal subgroup N'N^576 is stable under the exact
Galois action; its quotient in F is finite, with all prime divisors in
{2,3}. The literal restriction of the braid action in R4 E.65.8 sends
x=u^2 to x^lambda and y=v^2 to f^-1 y^lambda f, matching the specified
tangential action. No change to an arbitrary outer representative is
used.

For every p>=5 the pair `(P1_Zp,{0,1,infinity})` is smooth with relative
normal-crossing boundary, and the tangent vector toward 1 at 0 is a
unit integral tangent. Petrov's Lemma 8.7 applies to this integral
tangential fibre functor. Its specialization isomorphism is equivariant
for the actual G_Qp action, with action on the special fibre factoring
through G_Fp. The proof explicitly preserves the fibre functors, rather
than giving only an outer action. Thus inertia acts identically on the
entire geometric prime-to-p fundamental group. The finite {2,3} quotient
above factors through that group, and inertia acts identically on its
subgroup M/576. This proves exactly the unramified statement used in
LH.1 at all multipliers.

## 4. Explicit cyclotomic conductor bound

Let K be the kernel of the finite Galois homology action and Lambda its
cyclotomic image. The index of Lambda is the degree of L intersect Qcyc,
so divides `D=|GL_97(Z/576)|`. At p>=5 the inertia group is contained in
K. Its cyclotomic image is precisely the full Zp^times factor: roots of
unity of prime-to-p order are unramified locally, whereas the p-power
cyclotomic extension is totally ramified. Closedness of Lambda includes
the whole product of these factors, not just the direct sum.

The finite image of 1+4Z2 has exponent dividing 2^v2(D), and the image
of 1+3Z3 has exponent dividing 3^v3(D). The logarithms on those principal
unit groups give exactly the two power-image formulas used in LH.1.
Hence Lambda contains the principal kernel of
`2^(v2(D)+2) * 3^(v3(D)+1)`.

An independent arithmetic calculation gives

- sum v2(3^i-1), i=1..97: `49+3*48+v2(48!)=239`;
- sum v3(2^i-1), i=1..97: `48+v3(48!)=70`;
- v2(D)=51940 and v3(D)=14135;
- n_* exponents 51942 and 14136;
- |Gamma_n*| exponents 5038380 and 1371194.

If h_n*(g)=1, the primitive cusp gives lambda(g)=1 mod n_*. Choose
sigma in K with exactly the same full cyclotomic multiplier, which is
possible by the proven containment in Lambda. Then g sigma^-1 has
multiplier one and trivial h_576, so RH.2 applies. Arithmetic vanishing
of Delta(sigma) finishes the proof. Matching only a finite multiplier
would not suffice here; the text correctly matches the full multiplier.

## 5. LH.2: raw homology has no trivial Galois constituent

The subgroup N semidirect G_Q is an open subgroup of the arithmetic
fundamental group using the specified genuine section. It projects
onto G_Q and intersects the geometric group in N, so its corresponding
finite etale cover Y/Q is geometrically connected and has degree 96.
Its homology is the stated Galois module M. This avoids an unspecified
descent datum for a complex modular curve.

Dualizing the Gysin sequence gives the boundary term exactly as printed:
Ql(1) tensor the permutation module on boundary points modulo its
diagonal line. At a good prime p != ell with unramified boundary, its
arithmetic Frobenius eigenvalues are p times roots of unity, none 1.
The proper term is V_ell Jac(Ybar), with the stated Galois action.
On the special fibre, Frob_p-1 has finite etale kernel J(F_p), hence
is an isogeny and is invertible on the rational Tate module. Thus it too
has no eigenvalue 1. The middle term cannot have eigenvalue 1, regardless
of whether the extension splits. Every Galois subquotient is Frobenius
stable, so none can be trivial. No semisimplicity assertion is needed.

Since Delta is trivial on G_Q, a full-gentle Delta constituent would
give such a trivial Galois constituent. This proves LH.2. It does not
exclude finite-coefficient constituents, since the nonzero Frobenius
determinant need not be a unit modulo n. It does not exclude invariants
after restricting the Galois group to its cyclotomic kernel.

## 6. FP.37: quadratic character and the cubic limitation

For p=(123), t=(1234), a commutator value R is in A4. Write R=v p^a.
The peripheral order condition is equivalent to ord(p v^-1 t v)=4.
Exactly v=1 or t^2 satisfies it. Thus R=t^(2nu4)p^a uniquely.
The induced normalized action on S4 is Inn(p^-a), and the GT
composition rule adds both nu4 mod2 and a mod3. This proves an actual
continuous gentle character, not merely a finite assignment.

The central-strip and three-cycle formulas in FP.37 have the correct
conjugation order. In particular, H=f(z,X4) gives
f(X5,z)=z^-1 H^-1 z and hence f(X4,X5)=z^-1 H z H^-1.
The harmonic frame image lies in the 192-element group used in the
certificate. Enumerating a possibly larger set of allowed frame images
is harmless for this necessary implication, and the certificate checks
that the stated four F values are exactly obtained by its conditions.

I independently reran `check_burau_quadratic_character.py`; it completed
with no failed assertion. Its complete output is preserved in
`code/audit_burau_quadratic_character.out`. I requested direct assertions
for centrality of the stripped h against X3, and for H48'=H12; these
already follow from the prior exact pair-derived-series computation,
but make the small certificate's two lifting inputs explicit.

The cubic limitation follows by Goursat because the pure Burau image
is generated by order-three elements and has no elementary-two quotient.
Thus the joint image with P5ab/2 is the direct product. The actual source
element with Burau value 1 and abelianization e5 has signed forgotten
residue -I modulo3 and I modulo4, hence [5I] modulo12. In the stated
finite joint quotient its image centralizes d and flips the candidate
cubic frame bit. This is a finite-centralizer ambiguity; it does not
give an element of the actual profinite centralizer or refute a global
comparison. The claimed limitation on kappa_d is correctly restricted.
