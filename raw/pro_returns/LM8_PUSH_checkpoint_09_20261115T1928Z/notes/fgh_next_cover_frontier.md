# Candidate residual cover after A.48

This note records an **unresolved candidate**, not a theorem about its fixed groups or normalizers.

Let H_3 be the Heisenberg group of upper-unitriangular 3 by3 matrices over F_3. It has order27, is generated by x=1+E12 and y=1+E23, and has central commutator z=1+E13 of order3. The geometric free profinite group of U maps onto H_3 by its two handle generators. Its kernel therefore defines an actual connected degree27 geometric Galois cover of U, descending to some finite number field. Boundary inertia is the commutator (up to inverse), hence has order3 in the deck group. The proper compactification has nine cusps and, by Riemann--Hurwitz, genus10: `2g-2=27*(1-1/3)=18`.

The coefficient-three fixed subgroup is covered by A.22, because this geometric Galois closure is a3-group. No all-prime fixed-group or normalizer classification is presently asserted here. In particular the map on pro-prime groups at a different coefficient prime is not to be treated as an inclusion.

A possible next route is to examine the action of the original order-four CM automorphism on the Heisenberg cover and its Jacobian. Its stable reduction, Jacobian decomposition and non-three-prime Galois action have not yet been calculated in this note. The new rational-path criterion requires an actual suitable pointed compact-type model; it must not be assumed from the cover's group alone.
