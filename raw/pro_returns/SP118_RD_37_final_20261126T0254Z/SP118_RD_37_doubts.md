# Legibility and witnesses

The legibility varies sharply. The covers and printed headings are clear; the short bibliographical notes are generally recoverable at the supplied resolution. The most fragile readings are the compressed manuscript formulas and overlaid corrections on017,038,047 and049, and the cancelled or overtyped cells on the two typescripts. These are AI-agent visual readings, without human verification. A larger displayed image makes existing pixels easier to inspect but cannot separate ink that the scan has merged.

The least secure items remain explicitly marked: partly obliterated letters beneath replacements, the final factor in the cohomology display on 038, small prose groups crossed by heavy ink, the uncertain function of a few terminal strokes, and text cut by the paper edge. A grammatical or mathematically expected expression was not enough to remove uncertainty. Where a sign is clear but mathematically surprising, its literal form remains and the issue is discussed in the notes.

C is more extensive than H on several diagrams, short forms, marginal passages and cancellation layers; H supplies useful competing word readings elsewhere. Their agreement was checked visually and was never treated as independent evidence. Some H files contain only editorial summaries, and section headings belonging to the next editorial division are not source text of the current scan. H-only changes of confidence on an otherwise identical proposed word are excluded from substantive change counts. The counts therefore measure recorded reading loci under this edition’s conventions, not comparable error rates for the two systems.

Printed rules, parentheses, source line divisions, abbreviation stops and the extent of underlining receive the same attention as words when they differ. Pale reversed type on reused sheets is distinguished from writing on the photographed face. Archival foliation is recorded separately from authorial numbering. Both reading stages and the main-reader adjudications are documented; the resulting transcription still contains the uncertainties that the supplied images do not settle.
