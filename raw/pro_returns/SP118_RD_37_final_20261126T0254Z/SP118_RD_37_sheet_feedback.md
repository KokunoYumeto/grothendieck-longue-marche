## Recurring and newly encountered issues

The existing error sheet covers the recurring substantive problems found here. No additional general error class is established from this small selection. The following local manifestations should inform a further reading of this cote, with the stated limits:

- **A shortened form is not a missing word.** Preserve reference and mathematical short forms, including `réf`, `noeth`, `ens`, `comm.`, `cri` and `spec`, and read each stop separately (019, 022, 026, 047, 049). The same writer does not give every occurrence the same punctuation.
- **A terminal stroke can imitate an extra letter or an arrowhead.** Long returns occur at word endings and at the ends of diagram shafts. The physical stroke is preserved even when its function remains doubtful (017, 033, 047, 049). These findings do not justify deleting all small terminal marks.
- **A whole-block cancellation and a local correction are separate layers.** The crossed memoranda on 021 and 036, the upper scheme on 047, and the typescripts on 048 and 050 contain local live/deleted distinctions beneath a larger cancellation. Replacing all layers with one strike loses information.
- **Editorial description is not transcription.** H’s stationery notices and diagram summaries sometimes describe an element without setting its wording or geometry; its trailing section headings can belong to the following editorial division (028, 038, 050). Record the distinction when counting omissions.
- **The location of an insertion matters independently of its letters.** Examples include VI_B in the page estimate (020), the reordered contribution labels (026), Deligne’s open attachment curve (038), the typed interlinear additions (048), and the raised sieve letter beside `parties` (049).
- **A typescript can preserve an unfilled mathematical blank.** Operator spaces are source evidence, and an expected operator cannot be supplied. Corrected reference numerals and overwritten letter cells also require separate underlying/live readings (048, 050).
- **A notation family can contain both ornamental and plain capitals.** The calligraphic sieve letters on 038 and the plain/script X forms in 047 must be compared locally. A mathematical expectation does not authorize replacing the clearly plain X or adding a hat to the bare I on 049.
- **Stationery and foliation are independent of the main text’s orientation.** The upside-down letterhead on 037 and inverted typescripts on 048 and 050 coexist with archival numbering in scan orientation. The blank cover on 051 still has a visible archival number.

The category counts above count each logged physical locus once by its primary kind; one locus may differ from both witnesses. They include explicit resolutions of C’s old doubt marks, exclude purely syntactic macro differences, and exclude added uncertainty alone. They should not be compared directly with token-change percentages from a different counting procedure. Independent second-reading differences and changed boxes are separately reported in `second/` and `progress.log`.
