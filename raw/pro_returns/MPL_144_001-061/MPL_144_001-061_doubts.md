# Doubts and notation

## Recurring notation: comparison before the freeze

This table records graphic conventions across all 59 retained scans. It is a reading aid, not a mathematical identification imposed on the manuscript; the contextual interpretation of the groups is reserved for the research notes. A hat, tilde, prime, exclamation mark, change of alphabet, or apparently inconsistent index has been retained locally even when it makes a formula surprising.

| Ink / usage | LaTeX encoding | Scans and reading caution |
|---|---|---|
| Double quotation marks | Two backticks followed by two apostrophes | Used throughout for his double quotes; no conversion to French guillemets. |
| A plain connecting stroke, arrow, double arrow, inclusion | `no head`, `\arrow`, `Rightarrow`, `hook` in `tikzcd` | The absence of an arrowhead is retained, particularly 013–025. The bottom pair of Π objects in 013 (5) has a double right arrow. |
| Prime versus exclamation exponent | `'` versus `^!`, `^{+!}` | The six exclamation exponents at the foot of 013 and `\mathcal N^!_{0,3}` in 014 are exclamations; `(2') (3') (5')` in 013 are primes. Primes on image groups, torsors, and generators elsewhere remain primes. |
| Ordinary, pointed, and arithmetic moduli notation | `M`, `\mathcal X`, `\mathcal M`, `\mathcal N` | Especially 013–028. Plain moduli `M` and calligraphic `\mathcal M` are distinct; the written assignments are not globally regularized. |
| Compactification hat | `\widehat M` | 004–005; the hat here is not a declaration of group completion. |
| Completion hat | `\widehat\Pi`, `\widehat\Gamma`, `\widehat{\mathcal G}` | 013–028. Local unaccented geometric groups are retained unaccented even where profinite. |
| Tilde on an algebraic closure or lifted group | `\widetilde{\mathbb Q}`, `\widetilde\rho`, `\widetilde\sigma`, `\widetilde\tau`, `GL(2,\mathbb Z)^{\sim}` | 015–025 and 032–033; tilde and bar are not interchanged. |
| Gamma and looped G | `\Gamma`, `G`, `\mathcal G` | 004, 006–007, 010, 013–028, 034 and 052–061. The binary icosahedral Γ, the finite Γ in the letter, the Galois Γ, the section-groupoid Γ, and Teichmüller Γ do not receive one imposed meaning. |
| Surface group and fundamental-group symbol | `\Pi_{g,\nu}`, `\Pi_{X,x}`, `\pi_1`, `\Pi_1` | 005, 013–028, 050, 058–061. The doubtful upper-right object in 019 is `\unsure{\Gamma_{X,x}\|\Pi_{X,x}}`. |
| Superscript plus, circle, star, and exclamation | `^+`, `^\circ`, `^*`, `^!` | They remain distinct. In particular `G_2^+` and `G_2^\circ` alternate in 052–061; this is not silently repaired. |
| The parameter nu | `\nu` | Distinguished from Latin v in the moduli indices and from a prime; repeated `g,\nu+1,\nu` indices in 013 retained. |
| Path and reduction torsors | `P`, `P'`, `P^\varphi`, `Q`, `Q'`, `R`, `\widehat P`, `\widehat Q` | 016–028. The doubtful `R_X\|Q_X` quotient in 017 and `Q_X\|Q_{X_0}` in 020 remain marked. |

| Form of the formal completion | `\mathfrak X` | 005; alphabet is a graphic interpretation, not an identification imported from context. |
| Rho and its prime | `\rho_i`, `\rho'_i`, `\widetilde\rho` | 030–040, then 052–061. The changed convention between the 1981, 1982 and 1983 sheets is left visible. |
| Epsilon | `\varepsilon_i` | 030–040; distinct from σ and from the Roman generators r. |
| Sigma and tau, large forms | `\sigma_i`, `\tau_i`, `\tau'_i` | 030–040: primes on tau have short detached strokes. 052–061: broad horizontal tau forms in the presentations are read as tau. |
| Sigma and tau, thin small forms | `\unsure{\sigma_i\|\tau_i}` and corresponding indices | 052, 054, 055, 057–059. The small looped forms resemble sigma; they have not been normalized to tau from their algebraic role. |

| Plus/star on the basepoint | `\unsure{P_+^*\|P_*^+}` | 061 (19); not conformed to the following prose `P_*`. |
| Symmetric-group alphabet | `\mathfrak S` | Especially 007, 013–014, 061; calligraphic appearance can be compressed in the pen strokes. |
| Manuscript page numbers and internal section numbers | `\mspage{...}`, `\textcircled{...}` | Both folio sequence and internal pagination retained; circled internal article/section numbers are not merged into folio numbers. |
| Superscript abbreviation and barred m | `\textsuperscript{...}`, `\overline{\mathrm m}` | Abbreviations remain contracted; the barred m in 047 is not expanded in the transcription. |
| Cross-outs and interlinear replacements | `\deleted{...}`, `\inserted{...}` | Both states retained; apparent errors are not grounds to delete the live reading. |
| Drawn curves, arrowheads, hatching, signatures | `\figure{\begin{tikzpicture}...}` | Geometry is an approximation to pen strokes. Labels are transcribed separately inside the drawing, without embedding raster images. |

## Legibility and limits

The main prose is often recoverable, but the rapid black/brown handwriting compresses word endings, punctuation and indices. The first discussion sheets (003–009), the overwritten torsor/discretisation notes (013–028), the crowded matrix formula sheets (030–035), and the slanting margin on 052 deserve the most caution; the cancelled passages in 003, 007, 013 and 028 contain the remaining physically unreadable word estimates. The reused printed sheets are substantially clearer, although their signatures and a few administrative letters remain uncertain.

All retained whole-page images and both supplied overlapping halves were examined in the sequential transcription, followed by a second visual audit of every page. Additional enlargements and rotations were used for difficult marginal writing; no OCR was used. The notation comparison checked the detached primes against the exclamation exponents, the sigma/tau forms against their larger examples, the h/ell/lambda family, Γ versus G, and the hats/bars/tildes across the component texts. The drawings preserve topology, relative placement, directions, labels and cancellations as closely as possible in TikZ, but do not claim facsimile accuracy of pen trajectories or signatures.

An explicit doubt is evidence of uncertainty, not a guarantee that every remaining unmarked word is correct. Mathematical inconsistencies, absent arrowheads, repeated equation numbers, altered group names, and apparent spelling or agreement errors were not corrected from mathematical expectation. Dates on reused printed circulars are dates on the paper, and are not automatically dates of the mathematical writing on associated sheets.
