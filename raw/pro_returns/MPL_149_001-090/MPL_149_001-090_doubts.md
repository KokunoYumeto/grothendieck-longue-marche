## Reading method and limits

No OCR was used. Each transcriber examined the whole page and both supplied halves, checked the text against them, and committed the page with its inventory; another reader then audited every page. Enlarged, rotated, and contrast-adjusted crops were used only for visual inspection. Show-through from the reverse and physical folds were distinguished from face-side writing; no hidden text was reconstructed from context or mathematical expectation. The supplied context file contents were not opened until the page files and combined transcription were audited, reconciled, and hashed. During initial intake, before the detailed protocol had been read, only the context archive member names and sizes were listed; this metadata-only access is disclosed in progress.log and supplied no textual readings.
