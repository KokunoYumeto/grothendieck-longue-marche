# Input-scope incidents

## Cote 29, scan 102

During the three reader runs, the main reader found that the prior-notes extractor had retained other-page rows in a page-number/title table. In addition to the correct scan-102 row, the excerpt named scan 113's heading “Théorème de Bertini et variantes.” and described scans 114–116 as continuations. It did not include their images or body transcriptions. This exceeded the intended one-page metadata scope. The extractor was corrected to filter numeric first-column table rows; the current input was regenerated and all three readers were explicitly told to disregard those rows, record the limitation, and not reopen images. No scan-102 witness text or reader candidate was changed by that administrative correction. The fresh-reader independence between the three scan-102 runs was maintained; the extraneous title metadata exposure is not concealed.

## 29/113 — shared bullet paragraph
At 10:19 UTC, reader 1 reported that the regenerated prior-questions file still contained two scan-116 prose bullets, one quoting an admitted failure to prove a conjugacy statement and one about a general pencil. The paragraph-level filter retained them because the same paragraph also contained the valid 113 remark. All three readers were immediately told to ignore the extra bullets, disclose the limitation, and not reopen any image. The source extractor was changed to split individual bullets before scan matching; 113 was regenerated and checked to retain its own surjectivity reservation while excluding Scan 116. This is a real input-scope breach: unlike the 102 metadata incident, two short other-page note quotations were visible. No other-page images or full witness texts were assigned. Independent reader outputs were never shared. The error does not justify claiming perfect page-only inputs for this run.
