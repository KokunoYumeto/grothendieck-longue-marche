# Conditional torsor classification check, cote 29 scan 038

Statement location: the paragraph describing finite sets with commuting left G_i and right H actions, the latter a principal homogeneous action, and its classification by Hom(G_i,H)/H.

Assume E is a nonempty right H-torsor. Choose e_0 in E and identify H with E by h -> e_0 h. If a left G_i-action commutes with the right H-action, there is a unique phi(g) in H satisfying g e_0 = e_0 phi(g). Then

    (gg')e_0 = g(e_0 phi(g')) = (g e_0)phi(g') = e_0 phi(g)phi(g'),

so phi is a homomorphism. Conversely a homomorphism gives g.(e_0 h)=e_0 phi(g)h, which commutes with the right action. Choosing e_1=e_0 a changes phi to a^{-1} phi a. A right H-equivariant isomorphism is determined by the image of e_0, and it is also G_i-equivariant precisely when the homomorphisms are conjugate. Thus isomorphism classes have the asserted orbit-set description under these assumptions.

Scope: this verifies the finite-set/torsor classification itself. It does not prove the earlier local-to-global description, the unquoted full-faithfulness criterion, or the claimed reduction in the examples.
