The transcription distinguishes uncertain identification from uncertain meaning. A visible capped shaft can be drawn faithfully even when it is unclear whether the author meant a dash or an unfinished arrow. Conversely, an obscured letter, index or cancellation remains marked in place. Small accents, detached dots, primes and crossing strokes have been judged locally; grammatical or mathematical plausibility alone has not supplied them. Hand-drawn geometry is approximated in TikZ, with the observed labels, connectivity, directions, terminal details and surviving revisions retained.
