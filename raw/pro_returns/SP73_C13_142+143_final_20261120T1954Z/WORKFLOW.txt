SP73_C13_142+143 — diplomatic collation work package

Authoritative deliverables are pages/*.tex and SP73_C13_142+143_transcription.tex,
plus the named reports, decisions.tsv, CHECK_EVENTS.tsv, and progress.log.
The combined transcription is a fragment and uses the supplied PREAMBLE.tex;
the source preamble itself is intentionally not copied into this package.

readings/ contains each first reader's proposed page and evidence record, including
main-reader adjudications made before acceptance. audits/ contains the independent
second readings. state/*_accepted.json is the structured source used to regenerate
the current reports; state/*_main_review.json documents personal main-reader review.
A proposed reading in readings/ or audits/ is not authoritative over pages/.

crops/ contains enlarged source details and their original pixel boxes are recorded
in the evidence files and reports. Halves and original attachments are excluded.
The supplied scans and texts must be restored separately if the environment is lost.
No OCR, external research, or LaTeX compilation is used in this workflow.

scripts/prepare_page.py creates each page's native overlapping halves once and its
single-page packet. scripts/make_crop.py makes one crop from original coordinates
and refuses to overwrite a saved crop. accept_page.py and accept_audit.py require
explicit saved main-reader adjudication records. build_reports.py regenerates the
reports from accepted records. make_checkpoint.py permits a new numbered ZIP only
after 45 minutes from the start or preceding checkpoint, excludes source material
and previous ZIPs, and never overwrites earlier checkpoints. Uploading that ZIP is
a separate persistence operation. Checkpoint numbers and timing are in state/.

All dates and progress timestamps in this package are UTC. Model effort settings
are not exposed in this environment and are reported as such, rather than guessed.
Mathematical check events describe only the checks actually performed; transcription
and comparison of a formula are not classified as proof verification.
