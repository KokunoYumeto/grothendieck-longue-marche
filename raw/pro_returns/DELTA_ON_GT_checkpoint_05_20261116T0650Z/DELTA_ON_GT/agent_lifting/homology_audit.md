**Verdict: the stated homology argument is sound.** The stronger full-multiplier finite modulus is proved separately as LH.1 in `lifting_homology_bound.tex`.

## Exact cohomology

The finite Bass–Serre graph for the normal congruence kernel has576 edges and vertex orbits of sizes288 and192. Its integral first homology is free of rank97. The two exact sequences obtained from its chain complex remain exact after profinite completion because every relevant integral homology group is free.

For the augmentation kernel L in the vertex module, L^Q consists of coefficients (a,b) with288a+192b=0. Since multiplication by96 is injective on Zhat, this is the primitive line generated by(2,−3). The invariant norm edge sum maps to that same primitive generator. Shapiro and the first exact sequence therefore give H¹(Q,M)=0. The second exact sequence gives H¹(Q,L)=Zhat/(288,192)=Z/96, hence H²(Q,M)=Z/96. This is an exact profinite-coefficient cohomology computation.

The deck action is faithful modulo576: for every nonidentity g, its trace deficit from97 is96 plus the number of fixed vertices. Fixed vertices can occur in at most one of the two orbit types, and the deficit lies strictly between0 and576. A matrix congruent to identity would have trace deficit divisible by576. The complete graph program obtains the sharper actual maximum104, but no sharper bound is needed.

## Genuine normalization and full multipliers

R4, E.65.8 gives the genuine braid action u↦u^λ and v↦f(u²,v²)⁻¹v^λf(u²,v²), preserving the braid relation and central full twist. Because τ=u⁻¹ in B, the descended action sends τ literally to τ^λ. ## Explicit upgrade

Petrov(2023), Lemma8.7, states the genuine Galois-equivariant prime-to-p specialization isomorphism for both classical and tangential integral basepoints. Its proof preserves the fibre functors, so it is stronger than an outer-action statement. Apply it to the standard unit tangential point of P¹−{0,1,∞} over Z_p for p≥5.

Since N⊂F, the quotient F/(N′N^576) is a finite group whose order has only primes2 and3. The normalized Galois action on this quotient is exactly the restriction of the standard tangential action, and hence unramified at every p≥5. Its invariant subgroup M/576 is consequently unramified there. This avoids a separate comparison of the orbifold basepoint, and applies at arbitrary multipliers.

The cyclotomic image of the kernel of this finite Galois homology representation has index dividing |GL₉₇(Z/576)| and contains the full prime factors at p≥5. Principal-unit power bounds at2 and3 then give the sufficient modulus2^51942·3^14136. Matching a gentle element's full multiplier by a Galois element in that kernel reduces the finite homology kernel to RH.2. LH.1 gives the complete proof and exact quotient order.
