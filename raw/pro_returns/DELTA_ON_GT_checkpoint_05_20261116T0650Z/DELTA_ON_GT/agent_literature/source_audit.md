## Consequential conclusions

1. **The pointed category cannot detect the missing group pentagon.** In the explicit category `(Z/12,q(a)=a²/24)`, the two pure monodromies on three homogeneous objects commute. Every `f` in the completed free commutator subgroup evaluates to one. At multiplier one the Drinfeld modification is the identity for every gentle pair, including R2. Thus the assertion that R2 must fail this category's pentagon is false. The missing assertion is compatibility between the separately defined CM frame permutation and the category's modular operation. See the complete argument in `A2_and_AA_proofs.tex`, A.2-L3--L5.
2. **The literal scalar-frame claim is false already on Galois.** A Galois element over `Qab` moving `r0=(3/4)^(1/4)` has both bits one. Its order-two corrected frame is `±S` modulo three, which is not central even projectively. A nonempty compact coset specifying such elements is constructed in A.2-L1. The sign pair has a CRT encoding by `1,5,7,11`; the frames themselves are not scalars in one common CM algebra.
3. **The metaplectic abelianization assertion has a stronger proof than the suggested one.** Every multiplier-one order-three pure frame lies in `F′`: the order-three action on `F^ab` has `det(1-r)=3`, hence no fixed vector. For R2 the order-two frame also lies in `F′`. Their homomorphic lifts to any central extension consequently have trivial abelianized class, independently of the lift choices. This separates the bit from every scalar metaplectic character. The eta-squared phase on the centre modulo twelve is nevertheless exactly `chi_-4`; see A.2-L6--L7 and the exhaustive finite certificate.
4. **The AA theorem proves the desired kernel statement.** The precise source is Hoshi--Mochizuki--Tsujimura (2025), Definition 5.12 and Theorem 5.17(ii). Every closed AA subgroup lies in a GT-conjugate of `G_Q`, so every continuous character trivial on `G_Q` kills it. “AA” means *algebraically ample*. The stronger uniqueness statement for the maximal AA subgroup containing `c` follows from the classical centralizer of `c` and conjugacy of Galois involutions; A.3-L1 supplies the proof.
5. **Two E64 source inputs check out with their stated restrictions.** Boggi's stabilizer sequence and genus-at-most-two congruence result give the selected two-boundary sewing isomorphism. NTY2010 Props. 4.11 and 4.13 give the full-group formula for `D3` on Galois, including its `(-I)^rhoK` sign. Section 4.12 is not a proposition. Details are in `B7_comparison_proofs.tex`.
6. **No checked source closes the all-GT comparison.** NTY's triangle action is defined on all GT, but the comparison with the standard braid action is Galois-only. LNS2026 Theorem 1.1 constructs individual full profinite mapping-class actions; Section 8 does not assert all the cutting compatibilities needed here. Neither theorem supplies the identification of the two independently constructed CM actions.
7. **The literal current §47 map differs from the original map.** LM2 uses `(x_L,y_L)=(y_P^-1,x_P^-1)`. Thus its physical quotient is `φ_L(x_P)=τ^-1, φ_L(y_P)=s`, whereas the prompt literally defines `φ_P(x_P)=s, φ_P(y_P)=τ`. The corresponding endomorphisms satisfy `D_L=Ad(c)∘D_P`. The first fixes the Galois image; the second acts there by `σ↦cσc`. A compact coset of elements over `Qab` moving `2^(1/4)` refutes both the literal-current Galois fixed-point assertion and `GT⊆Fix D_P`. Full typed proofs, an independent NT check, and the exact GF0/HF0 comparison are in `A4_marking_correction.tex`.

This is a statement about the search performed, not a mathematical theorem that no such literature exists. Do not promote it into “first”, “new”, or an unconditional historical priority claim.

The parent proof of P.2 must stand on its own pro-prime construction and R1 lifting. The literature result of Harbater--Schneps supplies an inclusion, not its strictness. A finite shadow outside an arithmetic image is not a non-Galois element of the full profinite group without a compatible lift. R2 plus the repaired R4 does provide such an actual full-group element in the supplied development. That deduction is stronger than any finite-shadow computation, but its bibliographic priority is not established here.

For R1, no exact primary analogue with arbitrary prescribed three peripheral conjugators was found in this search; unrestricted automorphism lifting is a different statement. The detailed lifting audit belongs to the other agent. For R7, the classical source below proves the centralizer of the standard involution, not that all finite-order elements are involutions or that all involutions are conjugate. The latter distinctions must remain explicit.

## The finite-character statement that can be proved exactly

Let `G` be GT or GT0, and let `K_G` be the kernel of the multiplier on its profinite abelianization. The Galois map and Kronecker--Weber give a section of `G^ab→Zhat×`, so `G^ab≅Zhat××K_G`. Every finite character of the Galois subgroup extends by the multiplier. Finite characters of `G` vanishing on Galois are precisely the finite continuous characters of `K_G`. This reduction is proved in A.2_and_AA, P-L1.

For GT0, R4 provides a nontrivial quotient `K_GT0→C2`. For GT, determining whether this particular quotient restricts nontrivially is exactly the main task; neither the reduction nor the literature search decides it. A complete classification of all the characters asked for in B.2 is stronger than the existence of R4 and must not be replaced by it.

## Exact A4 comparison and corrected markings

**This section is updated by `A4_marking_correction.tex`, which supersedes the earlier unconverted fixed-D formulas in this audit.** The current prompt's basis is `x_P=(sr)^-2`, `y_P=(rs)^-2`; LM2's basis is `x_L=(rs)^2`, `y_L=(sr)^2`. Keeping the same written assignment `x↦s,y↦sr` in both bases changes the actual quotient. Write `φ_L(x_P)=τ^-1,φ_L(y_P)=s` for the original physical map and `φ_P(x_P)=s,φ_P(y_P)=τ` for the literal current map. The corresponding endomorphisms satisfy

`D_L(g)=c D_P(g)c`.

The original orbifold cover and open immersion, with the same positive loop marking, prove that `D_L` fixes the Galois image. Therefore `D_P(σ)=cσc` there. Elements over `Qab` moving positive `2^(1/4)` give explicit counterexamples to `D_P(σ)=σ`; the Kummer exponent is `2 mod4`, and negation changes it modulo eight. Thus the literal-current compatibility subgroup intersects Galois in `{1,c}` and cannot equal `ker Delta`. These statements do not settle `GT⊆Fix D_L` or `Fix D_L⊆ker Delta`.

At multiplier one, with `a=τ^-1`, set `P=f(a,s)∈F` and `P^ab=(p,q)`. The normalized harmonic frame of `D_L` is `x^(p-q)P^-1`. The fixed-point condition is therefore exactly the NT GF0 primitive form `g=P x^(q-p)`, with `g=β^-1`. A4.M6 proves its equivalent HF0 form and the inclusions from the explicitly imposed equation pairs `(I′)+(V)` and `(II′)+(IV-bis)`. This is a proved dictionary and conditional inclusion, not a theorem that the pentagon supplies the added equations.

The rational map `w=4λ(1−λ)` has local degrees two at `λ=1/2` and `∞`, and degree one elsewhere. It is an orbifold covering from the sphere with cone orders `(3,3,2)` at `(0,1,∞)` to that with orders `(3,2,4)` at `(0,1,∞)`. Its unramified punctured restriction is

`P1\{0,1/2,1,∞} → P1\{0,1,∞}`,

and gives an index-two inclusion `Fhat3→Fhat2`. The harmonic equations compare the action through this covering with the action through a puncture-forgetting inclusion. The prompt's `φ:Fhat2↠C2∐C3` is a different homomorphism. Neither a rational formula nor the subgroup inclusion `A4<S4` identifies these action comparisons.

The lifting agent's new common-word finite test retains the required evaluations and the signed normalization of `D_P`; it still has candidates of both parity values. It is therefore not a proof that `Delta∘D_P` vanishes. Since `Delta` is invariant under conjugation by `c`, the composed characters `Delta∘D_P` and `Delta∘D_L` are equal. A vanishing theorem for that common character would put either fixed subgroup in `ker Delta`; this remains unproved.
