\begin{abstract}
We define the CM sign obstruction independently of its vanishing and
identify its cancelling character with the supplied $\Delta$.
Using the stipulated lifting theorem, we prove
$\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}\subsetneq\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0$
and construct nonpentagonal elements in both sign fibres.
We correct the marking of the descent equation of \S47,
give an actual Galois counterexample to its literal-current
fixed-point formulation, construct finite quotients detecting
the actual frame signs, and compute the three arithmetic CM
field intersections. The categorical, adelic and higher-point
arguments are proved or corrected with their exact domains.
We derive a genuine quadratic pentagon identity from a finite
five-point quotient and reduce the remaining comparison to
an integral cubic-frame character. We construct one actual
mixed-sign gentle element invisible to all the specified
solvable and adelic Burau pentagon tests, and satisfying the
pentagon in the entire relative pronilpotent Ferrari quotient
with its full profinite forgetful base. Its braid formulas
define an actual automorphism of that quotient and of a
subsequent relative quotient retaining the full profinite
abelianizations of characteristic covers of ranks $129$ and $4097$.
For the latter, we correct a failed cyclic seed inside its actual
source normal closure, using forty explicitly certified simple
proper images and a simultaneous quotient argument retaining
all thirty peripheral evaluations.
The full character
is detected by an explicit finite action on rank-$97$
congruence-cover homology, although it has no constituent in
that raw homology in characteristic zero.
The principal assertion $\Delta|_{\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}}=0$
remains undecided in this incomplete return: no surviving
finite shadow is asserted to be a profinite pentagon solution.
\end{abstract}
