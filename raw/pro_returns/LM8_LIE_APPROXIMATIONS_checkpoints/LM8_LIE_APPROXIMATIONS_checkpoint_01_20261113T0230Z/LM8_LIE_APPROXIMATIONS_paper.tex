\begin{abstract}
We distinguish the adelic algebraic representations defined in \S52 of
\emph{La Longue Marche} from nilpotent, relative, and weighted completions.
We give proofs, counterexamples, and reproducible finite computations, and
record exactly which larger assertions have not been established.
This is a working research return. No claim of priority is made.
The accompanying report and statement ledger record the verification scope.
