# Part D report

D.1–D.10 are written in `D.tex`; the register contains 6 Proved, 3 Refuted and 1 Open statement. The package independently rechecks the LM1 divided-power and positive-power arguments rather than treating the earlier review as authority.

New decisive corrections are the primitive-lattice counterexample `k ↦ 2k`, the failure of the proposed generators/relations even for `Ga²` (the characteristic-p Heisenberg test), and the fact that scan 231 is a modular-cover construction whose actual presentations occur on scans 234–237. The `Z/4` nonlinear test is settled positively by the square-zero kernel argument; the corresponding arbitrary nonlinear target over `Q[epsilon]/epsilon²` is not settled here.

The two computational levels are explicit rational Malcev classes 1 and 2 with a specified integral truncated-Magnus representation. They are not claimed to be canonical first and second levels of §52. The torus twist requires dilation by 2 on the second Magnus lattice, but is integral at 3. The four-puncture separating twist has an integral square-zero logarithm. `d_checks.py` verifies exact words and independent matrices through nilpotent class 4; all outputs are rational or integral, with no numerical tolerance.

The broad existence of smooth models with every extra hypothesis of scans 211–219 remains open in this package. This is explicitly a limitation of the work, not a claim that the question is open in the literature.
