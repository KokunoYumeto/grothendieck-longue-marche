                                                                              Abstract
                                                   In this paper, we study certain characters arising from the Galois ac-
                                               tions on the pro-p geometric fundamental groups of once-punctured CM
                                               elliptic curves. These characters may be regarded as analogues of the
                                               Soulé characters, which arise from the Galois action associated to the
                                               thrice-punctured projective line. We study various arithmetic assump-
                                               tions under which such characters are nontrivial or even surjective.
                                               Keywords. once-punctured elliptic curve, complex multiplication, étale
                                               fundamental group, Galois representation.
