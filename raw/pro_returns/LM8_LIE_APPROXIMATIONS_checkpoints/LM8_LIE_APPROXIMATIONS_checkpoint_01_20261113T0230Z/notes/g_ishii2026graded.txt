                                                                       1. Introduction and main result
                                              The aim of the present paper is to study the structure of a certain Lie algebra arising
                                           from the Galois action on the pro-p fundamental group of a once-punctured elliptic curve
                                           with complex multiplication. Among other things, we provide a minimal generating set
                                           of this Lie algebra under suitable assumptions, using (a slight variant of) the theory of
                                           weighted completion developed by Hain and Matsumoto [HM03].
