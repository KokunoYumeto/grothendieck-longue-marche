## Why the sigma coordinate survives

The quotient centralizer contains Zhat(x+y), while the full centralizer C_C(sigma) is only the finite cyclic group generated by sigma. Thus the abelianized conjugator can carry information invisible in the first-stage generator images.

For multiplier one, strip the inner e0 action and normalize the two conjugators into F. The rho conjugator has zero abelianization. The sigma conjugator has abelianization k(x+y). Passing to the integral class-two quotient gives 2q+k^2=0, forcing k to be even. This proves a source restriction, including at2, without using rational logarithms.

The matching arithmetic realization uses the fixed point1/2 of z↦1-z on U=P1 minus{0,1,infty}. The quotient [U/S3] has geometric fundamental group C/omega. The subgroup above the involution is Zhat freeprofiniteproduct C2. Herfort–Ribes Theorem1 conjugates its order-two stabilizer to the factor, and the conjugator can be chosen in F. Theorems1–2 were reopened in the original1984 primary announcement, printedpp31–34; the exact reduction is in the proof.

The path cocycle to1/2 has two Kummer coordinates equal to the class of1/2, as in Wickelgren Lemma3.16 and directly in the covers z^(1/n),(1-z)^(1/n). A basepoint-path change adds(chi−1)v. It therefore changes neither the image on the cyclotomic kernel nor the parity at any multiplier. The possible omega discrepancy when lifting the involution from C/omega to C is excluded by the forced sigma sign in C_ab. These points are essential; the image is not inferred from a finite quotient or a heuristic fixed-point analogy.

The Kummer character of2 on G_Q(mu_infty) has image exactly2 Zhat. Its square root is cyclotomic, while every higher positive real radical2^(1/m), m>2, has an Eisenstein minimal polynomial with nonreal conjugates and cannot lie in an abelian extension. Modulo2 its value is the quadratic character of2, read from sqrt2=zeta8+zeta8^-1. This proves the exact parity cosets for all multipliers.

A splitting would require an affine cocycle t(mu) whose parity is that character. Commuting mu with−1 forces2t(mu)=(1−mu)t(−1); because t(−1) is even, every t(mu) would be even. This contradicts a multiplier congruent3 modulo8 at2.
