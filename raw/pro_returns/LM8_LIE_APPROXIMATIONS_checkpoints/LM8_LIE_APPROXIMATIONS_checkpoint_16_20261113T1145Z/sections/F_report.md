Power completion of Q* is not completion over all finite quotients. Unit tangential cusp sections at 0 and 1 coincide even in the profinite abelian approximation; this does not identify their full nonabelian sections.

The nonpentagonal q7 element is the identity through class seven and first changes y by [y,q7] at class eight. Cocycles and their Galois action must be transported together. It is invisible at the stated finiteness thresholds.
