## Exact readings used in B.25–B.28

At equation (1), physical page 229, the three images are

`φ̂(ℓ0)=ε0=σ∞ρ; φ̂(ℓ1)=σ∞; φ̂(ℓ∞)=ρ^−1`.

With the supplement's X=ℓ1,Y=ℓ0,s=σ∞,r=ρ, these are exactly φ(X)=s,φ(Y)=sr,φ(Z)=r^−1. This is a quotient map. The other occurrence of the free group is the subgroup generated by ℓ1=(ρσ∞)² and ℓ0=(σ∞ρ)², as in the LM2 dictionary.

At equation (12), physical page 233, the manuscript defines φ-bullet as the composite π̂0,3→Ŝ⁺0,3→S3. The subsequent paragraph describes its kernel as the closed normal subgroup generated by ℓ0²,ℓ1²,ℓ∞³. Thus the rank-seven relative source retains a finite quotient already explicitly present in the original. The abstract kernel computation in B.25 is independent of this reading.

The boxed pair on physical page 234 is

`(14) ξρ(ξ)ρ²(ξ)=1; (15) ξ=(φ̂(ξ)σ∞(φ̂(ξ))^−1)ℓ1^ν`.

The text writes ν in Zhat and requires 2ν+1 to be a unit. The σ∞ notation here denotes its conjugation action; this is σ=Int(s) in B.28. Both equations are repeated together at (30) on physical page 240. The reconstruction does not replace their conjunction with one equation alone.

At equation (20), physical page 235, the manuscript writes

`φ̂∘uξ=uξ∘φ̂`.

The immediately following explanation states that the first uξ is its restriction to π̂0,3. This is decisive for the two-map distinction: the target action and its restricted source action are related by the subgroup inclusion, while φ̂ is the separately specified surjection. The modern relative-completion proof preserves exactly this distinction.
