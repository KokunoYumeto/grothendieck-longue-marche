# Part D report

D.1–D.11 are written in D.tex; the register contains 7 Proved, 3 Refuted and 1 Open statement. The package independently rechecks the LM1 divided-power and positive-power arguments rather than treating the earlier review as authority.

Decisive corrections are the primitive-lattice counterexample k ↦ 2k, the failure of the proposed generators/relations even for Ga² (the characteristic-p Heisenberg test), and the fact that scan 231 is a modular-cover construction whose actual presentations occur on scans 234–237.

Both named nonlinear tests are settled positively. Over Z/4 the proof uses the square-zero identity kernel. Over characteristic-zero dual numbers, D.11 constructs Weil restriction without a flatness assumption, computes its reduction kernel as a vector group, restricts to the actual algebraic image, and proves that the inverse image of the cyclic unipotent closure is unipotent. Exponential and adjunction give the unique additive one-parameter subgroup through the section. The nonflat group Spec A[t]/(epsilon t) checks why surjectivity onto the whole special fibre must not be assumed.

The two computational levels are explicit rational Malcev classes 1 and 2 with a specified integral truncated-Magnus representation. They are not claimed to be canonical first and second levels of §52. The torus twist requires dilation by 2 on the second Magnus lattice, but is integral at 3. The four-puncture separating twist has an integral square-zero logarithm. d_checks.py verifies exact words and independent matrices through nilpotent class 4; all outputs are rational or integral, with no numerical tolerance.

The general nonlinear positive-power statement over positive-dimensional bases, and the broad existence of smooth models satisfying every extra condition of scans 211–219, remain unresolved in this package. D.9 specifies the residual problem. This is a limitation of the work, not a claim that these questions are open in the literature.
