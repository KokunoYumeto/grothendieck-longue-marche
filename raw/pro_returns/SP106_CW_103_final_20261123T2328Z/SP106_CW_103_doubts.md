## Legibility and witness comparison

The two handwritten leaves are generally legible, but the right physical edge of015 removes endings, and small cancelled or lowered forms remain difficult. The typed leaves have repeated patchy impressions; the fixed character positions and surviving portions matter as much as apparent word outlines. Underletters obscured by later strokes, the place-name ending, and isolated low marks are the least secure readings. Enlargements do not recreate absent pixels.

C is the more diplomatic witness in its handling of corrections, typography and the address line, but it supplied several absent edge endings and miscounted the m cells of évidemment. H helps at that word and at the period after the map on016, while often giving normalized spelling, punctuation or incomplete correction states. Agreement between the two was never used as proof of the ink. Counts in the changes log refer to localized places, with certainty-only additions separated from reading corrections.
