## Elementary comparison calculation — our observation

Let B be the ring of integer-valued polynomials in one variable, filtered by polynomial degree, with basis b_n(x)=binom(x,n). Leading coefficients give

\[
b_r(x)b_s(x)=\binom{r+s}{r}b_{r+s}(x)+\text{lower-degree terms},
\qquad \operatorname{gr}B\cong\Gamma_{\mathbb Z}(u).
\]

**Confidence: high for this graded-ring calculation**, since b_n has leading coefficient 1/n! and the binomial basis is integral; **low for an equivalence of homotopy models**. Such a comparison would require compatible differentials, simplicial operators and filtrations. This is a proposed comparison experiment, not a claim of novelty or of identification with the manuscript. The primary reader assigns the manuscript scans and context IDs in the main notes.
