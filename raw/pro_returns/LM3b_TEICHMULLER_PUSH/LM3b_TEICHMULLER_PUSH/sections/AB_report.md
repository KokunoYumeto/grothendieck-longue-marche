## Completed elliptic strengthening

We opened Hain–Matsumoto, Universal Mixed Elliptic Motives, arXiv:1512.03975v4, and verified publication metadata in Cambridge Core (JIMJ 19(3), 2020, 663–766, DOI 10.1017/S1474748018000130). `ab_elliptic.py` independently implements their explicit epsilon derivations, checks the boundary identity, computes each entire SL2 orbit, and generates all geometric brackets. The geometric dimensions through ordinary degree twelve are 0,0,0,3,0,5,0,10,0,24,0,50. A.12 lifts this derivation while fixing the full group commutator exactly. A.13 proves the saturation and separation needed for torsion-freeness of outer IA. A.14 then refutes pro-prime elliptic normality at every prime, explicitly including 2. The geometric central involution is characteristic, whereas it negates the odd leading derivation. No full profinite transfer is claimed.

The geometric degree-fourteen dimension is 109; adjoining the three tested brackets raises the rank to 111. The exact upper bound one matches the arithmetic lower bound in Hain–Matsumoto Remark 29.3.
