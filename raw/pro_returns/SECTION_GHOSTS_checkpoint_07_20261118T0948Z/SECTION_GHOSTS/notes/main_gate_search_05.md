# Fifth-hour main-gate source search

18 November 2026. Searches for pronilpotent sections, ordinary nilpotent sections, prosolvable birational tripod sections, and finite-field pro-ell point/cusp comparison were performed in both available search engines. No source found in these queries settles the full ordinary completed-pair question. This is a scoped search outcome, not a universal claim of absence.

The finite-field queries led again to Hoshi absolute cuspidalization and Wakabayashi point-removal correspondences. As already recorded, their recovery of decomposition groups or existence of some point correspondence does not imply injectivity of individual pro-ell section classes, and does not identify an interior section with a specified cuspidal complement. E150 remains the exact proved counterexample to the stronger general interior-point injectivity premise.

Holzschuh, arXiv2510.13325v1, was opened as a recent primary comparison for E157. The source has an introduction-numbering inconsistency: Theorem A is called4.9 in the introduction, but its actual proof is Theorem4.8 on printedpp17-18; Corollary4.9 onp18 gives the component bijection. Corollary3.22 onp16 identifies the ordinary pro-ell fundamental-group formulation for incomplete smooth curves. Theorem4.17 onp20 includes smooth and affine schemes. The E157 proof instead uses Pal's already published graph theorem with every hypothesis checked; no new real section theorem is claimed.

Primary URLs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.13325 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.1205 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206203 .
