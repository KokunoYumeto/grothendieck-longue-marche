## E.115: the generalized Albanese and its Tate module

They hold for the smooth elliptic compactification, the reduced boundary divisor, and the distinguished point over 3. The condition that each boundary component be geometrically irreducible belongs only to A.8(ii); it is not a condition of A.8(iii). Here the affine curve has free profinite geometric group of rank 28, hence torsion-free abelianization, so the source's qualifier causes no loss. ## E.118: coefficient-five exclusion

The following potentially delicate steps were checked.

1. The finite cup for (a_1,b_1) vanishes because the exponent-120 local solutions identify both mod-5 classes with a Steinberg pair. The finite residue surjectivity from A.25 permits the prescribed finite central inertia corrections. The claimed ambiguity group vanishes: the coefficient sequence with integral H¹ and H² equal to zero gives H¹(G_Q,{5,∞},F_5(2))=0. At 2 and 3 the residue invariant spaces vanish, since q²−q is a unit modulo five.
2. Keeping the integral primitives before reduction is essential and is done correctly. The ratios q_1/P and r_1/R at the old primes, and the later primes constrained to be 1 modulo q_0 r_0, are principal units. Their integral five-adic primitives, multiplied by the tail exponents divided by 5, form convergent series. Thus every integral central lift has the same reduction, not merely the same abelian prefix.
3. A nontrivial rational odd-prime Kummer class cannot become trivial in a finite abelian extension of Q: otherwise its nonnormal prime-degree pure radical field would be a subfield of an abelian Galois extension. This justifies the fifth-power and cube independence after adjoining the cyclotomic CRT field F.
4. The finite Heisenberg field L_* has maximal abelian subfield Q(μ_5), so L_*F/F has group H_5. Its intersection with the abelian radical field R_* is exactly its maximal abelian subextension F(a_1^(1/5),b_1^(1/5)). The prescribed central nonzero action is therefore compatible with the radical-fixing action. The remaining extension over R_* has degree five; it cannot absorb the independent cubic extension F(3^(1/3)). Consequently the central defect and noncube condition can be imposed simultaneously by Chebotarev.
5. At five the stage-one error bound is at least six and v_5(120)=1. The choice ξ_5=1 and the Newton root η_5≡1 modulo 5^5 is allowed and yields the required q_1≡1 modulo 25. The later near-one choices at old support primes are compatible with the original induction; they do not change its exponent convention.
6. At q_1, the actual finite Frobenius class is central nonzero with trivial abelian coordinates. Hence α is divisible by five and γ is a unit modulo five. In the B.23 specialization,

       Z_D = 120(γ−qαu) + (q²−1)u binom(120,2),

   the first term has valuation one and the second has valuation at least two. Since v_5(q²−1)≥2, its residue is nonzero modulo 25. Central conjugacy does not alter the needed residue. The argument covers every integral class-two lift, as asserted.
7. The metabelian-quotient exclusion follows from K″⊂[Γ_2Π,Γ_2Π]⊂Γ_4Π and the actual covering inclusion. The five-primary witness is lost when taking only the pro-three quotient, so the stated limitation is necessary and correct.

## P.4: repeated check of the profinite lifting group

Reopened Hoshi Lemma 3.2, author p.14, and Pacheco–Stevenson Definition 4.2/Proposition 4.3, printed p.149: https://msp.org/pjm/2000/192-1/pjm-v192-n1-p08-p.pdf , web `turn787300203view2`. The latter permits arbitrary homomorphisms into arbitrary profinite extensions with pro-p kernel. It does not require the graph map to be onto, nor the kernel to be finite. Thus its use supplies a compatible full lift directly, rather than only finite-depth lifts.

The definition of Ω in P.4 is the maximal pro-p extension of Q(μ_p) unramified outside p, not the unrestricted maximal extension. It is Galois over Q. Both the pointed action and the Kummer cocycle factor through Γ_p: after adjoining μ_p their relevant images are pro-p and they are unramified away from p. Hoshi gives freeness for its pro-p subgroup; index p−1 implies cd_p Γ_p≤1. The graph map and the semidirect products are therefore the correct profinite objects for the lifting theorem.

For p=2, the cited Gras–Jaulent theorem concerns the maximal extension allowing complexification of real places, and gives Z_2*C_2 for Q. Its involution factor represents the real decomposition group; the free-product decomposition may be conjugated so that it is the chosen complex conjugation. Compatible positive roots of 2 make the specified abelian graph trivial on that factor. An arbitrary lift of the Z_2 generator extends continuously because the target semidirect product is pro-2. The free-product universal property then gives the full lift. No projectivity assertion for G_Q or the full unrestricted Galois group is being made.
