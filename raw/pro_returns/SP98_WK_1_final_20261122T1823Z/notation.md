## Local distinctions retained after the audits

The open p/r parameter in143’s range, the0/1 lower summation index there, and the independent upper n/bar doubt are separate readings. Scan146 repeats its range with different upper relations:0≤r<1 first,0≤r≤1 later. Scan148’s tiny A indices in its first live sum remain i/n, and its cancelled second S sum has an i−1/i+1 doubt followed by unindexed A. None of these is made uniform from the expected argument.
