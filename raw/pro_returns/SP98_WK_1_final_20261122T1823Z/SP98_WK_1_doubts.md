## Local distinctions retained after the audits

The open p/r parameter in143’s range, the0/1 lower summation index there, and the independent upper n/bar doubt are separate readings. Scan146 repeats its range with different upper relations:0≤r<1 first,0≤r≤1 later. Scan148’s tiny A indices in its first live sum remain i/n, and its cancelled second S sum has an i−1/i+1 doubt followed by unindexed A. None of these is made uniform from the expected argument.

## Legibility and witnesses

The body of these handwritten notes is often readable, especially the larger live formulas and well-spaced prose. Reliability falls sharply in interlinear additions, cancelled drafts, compressed word endings, shallow superscripts, and heavily retraced relations. High confidence is a local judgment about visible strokes; it is not a claim that a displayed assertion is mathematically correct. The remaining alternatives are part of the transcription and should not be treated as resolved readings.
