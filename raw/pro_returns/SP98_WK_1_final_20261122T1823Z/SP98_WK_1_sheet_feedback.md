## Relation to the supplied error sheet

The ten primary categories above map respectively to the sheet's words, symbols and indices, omissions, text not on the page, struck text, insertion placement, expanded abbreviations or modernized spelling, readable ink left illegible, drawings and diagrams, and other. They count adjudicated witness-difference places, with one primary category per place. They are neither a count of all occurrences of these phenomena on the sheets nor a token error rate. A row involving several phenomena is assigned its principal cause; the full changes table preserves the subsidiary features. Blind-reading adjudications are listed separately in that table and do not double-count the same witness difference.

The wording “readable ink left as \\ill{}” is the required collation category; it includes partly recovered words or formulas for which H used its illegibility macro. Recovery need not imply that every part of the recovered group is certain. Conversely, retaining a doubt or changing only its certainty is not counted as a substantive witness correction.

All23 leaves are handwritten subject notes in this selection. There are no typed overstrikes, handwritten completions on a typescript, letters by another writer, administrative personal lists, or printed names to assess here. The typed-text branches of the error sheet therefore have no instances in this run. A zero in such a branch is a statement about this selection, not evidence that the risk is small elsewhere.

## Scope of the hand log

The page-by-page hand log distinguishes established local comparisons from unresolved look-alikes. The shared notation table records the recurring forms, while review/notation_comparison.md records the final visual comparisons. No local allograph is promoted to an archive-wide rule. In particular, r/v, p/rho, the trace-like signs and heavily flattened indices remain locally qualified wherever the inspected strokes do not settle them.
