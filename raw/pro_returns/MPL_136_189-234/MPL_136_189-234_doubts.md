### Consistency review

Before freezing, the recurrent signs were compared across the page files and the doubtful occurrences were checked against their images during the audits: primes versus `^!`; stars versus dots; 0 versus o; Λ/Γ/Φ/Ψ; the script and ordinary A/M; underlined M; headed and unheaded arrows; parenthesized divided powers; and quotation marks. Differences that are visible or unresolved in the ink remain local differences, with doubt marks where appropriate. Double quotation marks are consistently encoded by two backticks and two apostrophes; double quotation marks used as TikZ label syntax are not transcribed punctuation.

## Legibility and confidence

All 45 supplied whole-page images and their two supplied halves were inspected directly, followed by a second audit of every page; no OCR was used. The reused typed pages are generally the most legible, but their handwritten symbols, small circular 0/o glyphs, pasted foliation labels, and Stoia's editorial alphabet marks require care. The handwritten calculations vary from clear large formulas to rapid cursive prose with dense additions; typography in this fragment is diplomatic, not a reconstruction of the intended theorem.

The least secure readings are the rotated and crowded corrections on scan 194, the lower marginal argument on scan 200, the crossed-out intermediate calculation on scan 202, the overwritten degree annotation on scan 219, the faint writing across music staves on scan 225, and the cancelled text and left margin on scan 226. The retraced suspension sign on scan 229 and crowded superscripts and insertions on scan 231 also remain doubtful. Low confidence identifies an attempted reading with substantially obscured strokes; medium confidence records a plausible reading or an unresolved pair of visible forms; high confidence is used sparingly for a reading whose main strokes are clear despite a minor residual uncertainty.

Scan 224 carries the pencil number 223 on the face presented. The very pale computer-printed characters are reversed show-through from the other face; they have not been mirrored and supplied as a second text. The staves, punched holes, tape, and paper edges on the supplied sheets are physical supports, not authorial diagrams; written brackets, separators, curves, and editorial legends are transcribed or reproduced in TikZ.

Some formulas, wording, indices, and agreement are surprising or incomplete. They have been retained, including missing signs, unfinished parentheses, abandoned phrases, and coefficients not written on the page. A local doubt mark is a statement about the ink, not an instruction to repair the mathematics; any mathematical or context-based alternative belongs exclusively in the research notes.
