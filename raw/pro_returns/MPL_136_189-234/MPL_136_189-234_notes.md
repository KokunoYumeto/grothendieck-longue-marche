### R003 — The point already detects the product qualification

**File/scan:** cote 136, scan(s) 204, 217, 231, 233. **Location:** A_0=S and divided-power multiplication.  
**Kind:** own idea. **RESULTS_STATE connection:** WG2 §2.4 #5; Hua: 136-pd-de-rham-shadows. **Confidence:** high.

For $X=\Delta^0$ the presentation gives $\mathcal A_0=k\{T\}$ in differential degree zero, and its weight-$p$ generator satisfies $T^{(p)}T^{(q)}=\binom{p+q}{p}T^{(p+q)}$; thus identifying each copy with $H^0(\Delta^0,k)=k$ does not identify their products with ordinary multiplication without that coefficient.

Confidence is **high** because both conclusions follow from the displayed presentation and the elementary divided-power identity, without assuming an unidentified comparison theorem.

Context comparison: this **adds** a product test to **WG2 §2.4 #5** and **136-pd-de-rham-shadows**, while `ps6.tex`, §119, manuscript pp. 501–503, likewise **differs** between divided-power and binomial algebraic structures rather than identifying them.

### R004 — A stronger unweighted PD claim has a concrete obstruction

**File/scan:** cote 136, scan(s) 215, 217, 233. **Location:** PD ideal and integral comparison.  
**Kind:** own idea. **RESULTS_STATE connection:** WG2 §2.4 #5. **Confidence:** high.

Suppose the proposed interpretation were an ordinary integral cochain algebra with the usual cup ring and differential-compatible divided powers on every even positive-degree cocycle: then a cocycle $x$ would give $[x]^2=2[\gamma_2(x)]$, because $d\gamma_2(x)=x\,dx=0$.

For the degree-two generator $u$ of $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3)$, $u^2$ is not twice another integral class, giving a counterexample to that stronger interpretation (Hatcher, 2002, Theorem 3.19).

Confidence is **high** because this only uses the PD identity and the explicit cohomology ring; it does not require divided powers of boundaries to be boundaries, and it does not refute the weighted assertion actually written on scan 233.

Context comparison: this **adds** an obstruction relevant to **WG2 §2.4 #5**; `ps6.tex`, §120, manuscript pp. 506–507, already discusses divided powers on a suitable ideal, a restriction that the stronger claim would have discarded.
