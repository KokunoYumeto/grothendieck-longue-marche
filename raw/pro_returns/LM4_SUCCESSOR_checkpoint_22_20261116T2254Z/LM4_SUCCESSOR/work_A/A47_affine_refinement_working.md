# A.47 working proof: a genuine noncentral elementary abelian two layer

**Status:** independently reviewed by the parent and integrated as formal A.47. This extends the regular tripod construction of A.40--A.43. It does not prove a full involution.

## A concrete perfect affine tripod quotient

Let V be the even-weight subspace of F2^9, with the natural action of A9, and put S=V semidirect A9. Its dimension is eight. The module is simple: for every nonzero even-weight vector, a suitable three-cycle changes it in exactly two positions; the A9 orbit of such a pair-vector spans V. In particular V has no invariant or trivial quotient, and S is perfect and centerless.

Moreover H1(A9,V)=0. The permutation module F2^9 splits as F2 plus V because nine is odd. By Eckmann--Shapiro its H1 is H1(A8,F2)=0, and the trivial summand also has zero H1 because A9 is perfect. Brown2010 Proposition3.3 is the exact primary source for Shapiro, reopened and verified at PDF pages19--20.

Use B.57's explicit permutations

    X=(1234567), Y=(1254389), Z=(XY)^(-1).

They generate A9 and each is a seven-cycle. Let vx=0, vy=e1+e2 and vz=e1+e9. Then

    Xtilde=(vx,X), Ytilde=(vy,Y), Ztilde=(vz,Z)

satisfy Xtilde Ytilde Ztilde=1 and all have order seven. The exact script check_A47_affine_A9.py verifies these identities, all256 possible simultaneous V-conjugators, the simplicity test, and generation of the head A9 by exhaustive181440-element closure. In particular the pair (vx,vy) is not a simultaneous coboundary ((1-X)w,(1-Y)w).

The subgroup generated by Xtilde,Ytilde projects onto A9; its intersection with V is an A9-submodule, hence either zero or V. If zero it is a complement, and H1(A9,V)=0 makes it V-conjugate to the standard complement. That contradicts the explicit non-coboundary check. Thus the pair generates S. This is an actual noncentral 2-module quotient of a three-cusp filling. The certificate also counts1024 order-seven lift pairs, versus256 simultaneous coboundaries, giving a two-dimensional quotient of allowable lift parameters.

## The full finite invariant family is still perfect

Take all epimorphism kernels K -> S supported on a retained three-subset consisting of an antipodal pair and one other cusp, with all three positive cusp images of order seven. There are finitely many. The just-constructed triple proves nonemptiness. Every full GT0 element permutes this family: it permutes the cusps and their antipodal relation and replaces their images by unit powers; order seven and surjectivity are preserved.

Each map has its distinguished simple head K -> A9. Group the kernels by their head kernel. For a fixed head kernel, identify the simple quotient with A9 and form the joint image P of all its S-valued maps. Automorphisms of A9 are induced by S9 and therefore preserve the natural module V, so this is a subgroup of

    V^n semidirect A9

projecting onto A9. Its kernel N is an A9-submodule of the semisimple module V^n. It is a direct sum of nontrivial simple modules; hence [N,A9]=N. Since A9 is perfect, P is perfect. Also P is centerless: a central element has trivial A9 image and lies in N^A9=0. Its unique nonabelian simple head is A9. The projection onto every original S is onto, so at least one noncentral V layer remains.

For clarity, splitting P itself is not needed. One can also obtain a splitting from H1(A9,V^n/N)=0 by viewing P/N as a complement in the quotient of the displayed semidirect product, but the perfectness argument already suffices.

Different head kernels are independent. Indeed a proper subdirect product of their P factors would, by finite Goursat, give a common nontrivial quotient. That quotient is perfect and has an A9 simple quotient. A surjection from a product of perfect factors to the simple group A9 factors through one factor (the factor images commute and are normal). Thus a common quotient would identify two head kernels, a contradiction. Induction gives the full product.

The A5 action on the head kernels is free: every head retains the same three nonzero cusp supports, and an antipodal-pair triple has trivial A5 stabilizer. Choose one entire P bundle over each head orbit and induce it to Pi by the regular wreath construction. The resulting actual epimorphism is

    q:Pi -> Q=M semidirect A5,

where M is a finite product of the perfect centerless P bundles and A5 permutes them regularly on each orbit. Its kernel is the intersection of the full S-kernel family and is GT0-invariant.

Let R and C=Pi/R be as in A.40. The finite quotient M/q(R) is a quotient of the open normal subgroup K/R of C. Therefore it has no A9 composition factor. But M is perfect and all its composition factors are A9 or C2. Hence M/q(R) is both solvable and perfect, so is trivial. Thus q(R)=M and the actual joint quotient is

    Pi/(R intersect ker q) = Q x_(A5) C = M semidirect C.

## The actual central refinement and the negative action

The proof of A.40 requires only that M is finite and perfect and that the A5 action regularly permutes its bundles. It does not require the bundles to be simple. Its universal central cover is finite by Brown2010 Section2 and Corollary3.4, as already proved in A.41.

For W=R intersect ker q, the preimage of M in E#=Pi/[W,Pi] is the canonical finite perfect image M# of that universal cover: R=[R,Pi] removes the remaining central factor. The actual s/r elements are M-conjugate to pure C-elements because the corresponding regular bundle permutations have only cycles of lengths two/three and the cycle products are one. Lifting those conjugators to M# and using R=[R,Pi] gives a genuine section C -> E#, exactly as in A.40. Hence

    E#=M# semidirect C= (M# semidirect A5) x_(A5) C.

The map id_(M#) semidirect h_C is an actual automorphism. It fixes the actual cusp literally by its two fiber coordinates, and its s/r conjugators can retain the full C-frames by the same finite-centralizer surjections. A.3/A.34 therefore gives a full positive h inducing this action on the joint quotient, and a=h c has Delta1 and actual order two there. The finite invariant family is c-stable, so c acts genuinely on Q; the negative joint image is (c_Q,beta_C).

A.43 also applies without change: M is centerless, so the central kernel in its cover is its full center; perfectness makes the next center preimage central, and the action on its two central subquotients factors through A5 and is killed by A5-perfectness. Thus [[W,Pi],Pi]=[W,Pi], and the actual first central refinement also carries the compatible order-two image.

## Finite families and scope

One may join this family with any finite union of A.41's simple tripod families. For overlapping A9 heads, the simple quotient is already a quotient of the P bundle; for all other heads the same Goursat argument gives independence. The joint base remains perfect and centerless, with regular A5 head orbits. Hence the closed-set compactness argument of A.41 retains the entire earlier congruence/semisimple tower together with this new noncentral two layer and its actual scalar central refinement.

More generally the argument works for any finite union of tripod kernels with targets V semidirect S, where S is a finite nonabelian simple group excluded from the composition factors of C and V is a finite nontrivial irreducible S-module, together with the simple targets themselves. After aligning identical head kernels, the module kernel is a submodule of a finite semisimple sum of nontrivial irreducibles (possibly of several characteristics). It has no trivial quotient, so the joint bundle is perfect and centerless. Each head still has the same free A5 support orbit. Finite unions and compactness then work exactly as above. The explicit 2^8:A9 example proves that this enlarged tower genuinely contains a noncentral elementary abelian two layer; the general family is not claimed nonempty for every possible module.

No assertion of cofinality or full a^2=1 follows. The regular action on the head orbit remains the precise hypothesis used to split the actual central refinement over C. The next investigation is an orbit-one quotient of Pi itself, where the actual finite s/r components cannot be conjugated to pure C-elements.
