# Primary-source scope audit for the full ambient route

16 November2026. These are attempted routes, not new formal inputs.

1. Minasyan and Zalesskii (2016), *Virtually compact special hyperbolic
groups are conjugacy separable*, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici91(4),
609–627, DOI10.4171/CMH/397; publisher primary PDF
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/43424.
Proposition3.2 assumes an extension H⋊<x> with H torsion-free and x of
prime order. In that setting the finite-subgroup comparison concerns
the resulting prime-order groups. It is not an all-A5 conjugacy theorem.
Theorem1.5 and Corollary3.5 do not remove the missing A5 step for the
mapping-class groups here.

2. The general finite-group fixed-point route does not follow from the
Sullivan theorem retrieved in Lurie's primary course notes, Lecture30:
https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-917-topics-in-algebraic-topology-the-sullivan-conjecture-fall-2007/26a33455223cfb611001acc4e1f507bb_lecture30.pdf.
The equivariant comparison there is for a finite p-group and its
p-profinite homotopy theory. The later arbitrary-finite-group argument
for maps from BG with trivial action cannot be substituted for the
nontrivial A5 action needed here.

3. Leininger and McReynolds (2007), *Separable subgroups of mapping
class groups*, Topology and its Applications154(1),1–10,
DOI10.1016/j.topol.2006.03.013; primary publisher metadata and abstract:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166864106001155.
The named classes include solvable, handlebody/Heegaard, subsurface
geometric, and Johnson-filtration subgroups. No full-topology or
refined-product theorem for the two Veech-like source curves has been
verified from this statement. Individual subgroup separability would
in any event not replace B46's refined-product quantifier.

4. John Crisp, *Injective maps between Artin groups*, Research Report
97-10,27 March1997, primary institutional page:
https://www.maths.usyd.edu.au/u/ResearchReports/GeoTop/Cri/1997-10.html.
The stated LCM criterion proves discrete injection and gives a
simplicial-complex realization. Its abstract supplies no completed
injection. The I2(5) folding is a plausible precise description of
the cyclic family after both branch points have been forgotten, but
that is not the faithful eight-point quotient and requires a based
geometric identification before it can be used.

5. Myriam Finster (2013), *Veech Groups and Translation Coverings*,
doctoral dissertation, primary KIT record,
https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000036365,
DOI10.5445/IR/1000036365. Its stated result realizes specified
partition-stabilizing congruence subgroups by translation covers.
It is not a statement that every finite-index subgroup of every
Veech group has been realized, so it does not supply the required
full induced profinite topology for the possible pentagon subgroup.

The common-C2 quotient itself uses only the valid prime-power
comparison already established in B30. The exact additional homology
calculation is retained alongside this audit. Its anti-invariant
directions do not settle the full ambient intersection.


6. Edmund Heng and Luis Paris (2026), *Representations of Coxeter groups
over fusion rings and hyperplane complements*, arXiv:2511.04836v2,
21 October2026; full primary text
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04836v2.
We read Theorem4.20, Proposition5.4 and Corollary5.6, including the
I2(5)-to-A4 example. The theorem identifies the induced fundamental-group
homomorphism by the standard generator products. The stated spherical
Artin-group injectivity is attributed to Crisp. No finite-cover extension,
profinite comparison or virtual retraction is supplied. In particular,
this geometric identification does not replace the full induced-topology
comparison required after forgetting the two branch marks. The faithful
common-C2 quotient above already has its own valid prime-power topology
proof, whereas this additional folding remains a different map.


## A new holomorphic-retraction announcement does not close the group gate

On 2026-11-16 we opened the primary author page https://julietaygun.com/ and the primary Fields Institute abstract https://www.fieldsinstitute.ca/talks/Holomorphic-retracts-Teichm%C3%BCller-space. The latter records Juliet Aygun's 14 October2026 talk, announcing the converse of Kra's holomorphic-retraction criterion outside rare explicit exceptions. The author page lists the joint work with Apisa, Lamel, Masanta, Novikov and Zimmer as “Kra’s conjecture in genus zero and generically in strata”, explicitly In preparation, with no manuscript linked. Web retrievals turn642view0 and turn642view1. We have not obtained an exact theorem or proof that could be applied.

Independently, no implication from an abstract group virtual retraction in a finite-index mapping-class subgroup to a holomorphic retraction of the associated Teichmueller disk has been established here. The source disk is a pillowcase-cover disk, so even the announced exceptional cases would require exact inspection. This is a recorded lead, not a source for any formal statement or a proof that a virtual retraction is impossible. The group-theoretic finite-index refinement question stays Open.


## Relative Lefschetz source-membership lead: exact available scope

Primary source opened: Madhav V. Nori, Zariski’s conjecture and related problems, Annales scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure, fourth series16(2),305–344 (1983), https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1983_4_16_2_305_0.pdf, web turn649view0/1/2. Introduction statement I gives surjectivity from a neighborhood of an ample divisor with an arbitrary closed subset removed. It does not identify that neighborhood complement with the punctured divisor itself. WLT(B) gives finite-index image for the compact divisor when its image is disjoint from the removed subset. Lemma5.1 and Proposition5.4 impose precise nodal/self-intersection hypotheses for finite-cover bounds.

For the actual signed drilling, the curve p=a(s) is a Mobius graph in P1×P1, while the forbidden old labels are the square-Mobius graphs v_u(s). At old parameter punctures several forbidden graphs collide and the source passes through those singular boundary strata. Thus the usual generic transverse hyperplane-section surjectivity cannot be inserted merely because the graph is ample before boundary resolution. A relative boundary/finite-cover argument would have to control these specified singular points. We have not proved it, nor inferred K_sign=R_sign from ampleness or local simple intersections at the eight new collisions. This is a potential finite-cover mechanism to investigate beyond the attached affine tests, not a new theorem.
