D.10. Arbitrary-frontier descent in the exact comparison                 Open.
    Let 𝑈 be a simply connected open orbit in the original surface. The prime-end disk
need not map continuously onto its closure. When the interior identification has such an
extension, exists, its boundary map may fail to be injective. We have not proved that an
intrinsic centralizer filling can be chosen to extend over every such original frontier, with jointly
continuous inverses. An interior conjugacy alone does not suffice. One must retain all original
approach limits and prescribed frontier values; a boundary equivalence relation describes only
the cases where a continuous boundary map exists. This is the arbitrary-frontier descent
obligation listed in LM4 C.12.

D.11. Nonfixed-frontier pasting and infinitely many orbit types                  Open.
    The remaining LM4 C.12 comparison requires simultaneous fillings on all open orbits
agreeing with a deforming, possibly nonfixed, nonopen locus, including infinitely many operator-
inequivalent remaining components and accumulation outside the treated closed sets. We have
not proved or refuted this assertion. The missing conditions are (i) existence of mutually
compatible restrictions on every common frontier, (ii) extension of these restrictions over the
incident open orbits, and (iii) uniform control of both maps and inverses across arbitrary
accumulating families. D.2 controls one original disk with fixed boundary and does not supply
these three comparisons.
