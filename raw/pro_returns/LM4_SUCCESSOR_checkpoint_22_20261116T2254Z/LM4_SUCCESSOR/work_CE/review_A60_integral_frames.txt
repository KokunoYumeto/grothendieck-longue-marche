1. Literal r-frame lift at the Bockstein level.

Gauge each free integral cocycle by d'=d-(1-rho(g))a so that d'(r)=0;
this is possible because 3 is invertible in O=GR(4,3). The negative
affine parameter becomes q'=q-a-Ca, and Cq'=q'. If J is the retained
base r-frame, b(r)=Jr^{-1}J^{-1} and J b(J)=1. A lift (p,J) conjugates
the actual r^{-1} to b(r) precisely when
    (1-R')(p-q')=0,       R'=rho(b(r)).
Its norm is
    p-rho(J)Cp+(rho(J)-1)q'=(1-D)(p-q'),
    D=rho(J)C.
The retained head identities give D^2=1 and D R' D=R'^{-1}.
Thus <R',D> is a quotient of S3. Its matrices are monomial with
Teichmuller seventh-root entries. A point stabilizer has order dividing6
and therefore no nontrivial character into C7. Transport of one basis
vector per orbit gives an integral permutation basis. Joint invariant
reduction is consequently onto. The actual mod-two frame supplies the
required invariant residue, so it lifts. Undoing the gauge retains the
literal original r and the original frame reduction. The sign in q'
is tied to the stated minus-coboundary convention; the opposite gauge
has plus signs throughout. This proof passes independently.
