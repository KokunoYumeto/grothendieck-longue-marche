==========================================================

In G3=P/gamma4P the layer W=gamma3P/gamma4P is central. If a in HT and b in
H5 have the same class2 image2w, then delta=ab^-1 lies in W. All lifts of that
fixed class2 element from the two subgroup images differ from a,b respectively
by the images of HT intersect gamma3P and H5 intersect gamma3P. Their possible
discrepancies are exactly delta+I_T+I_5. Thus compatibility is equivalent to
delta belonging to I_T+I_5. Since a,b lie in gamma2G3, they commute, and the
obstruction for the indicated m-th powers is exactly m delta.

If H5 has injective degree-one and degree-two maps, then h in H5 intersect
gamma3P first belongs to gamma2H5 by degree-one injectivity, then to gamma3H5
by degree-two injectivity. Hence the stated equality of intersections is
valid. No corresponding simplification is allowed for HT with its reported
nontrivial degree-one kernel.

The completion/topology gate has a primary reference already in the CE
bibliography: Ismael Morales (2024), On the profinite rigidity of free and
surface groups, Mathematische Annalen390,1507-1540,
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-023-02785-6 . The publisher article was reopened;
its Section3 opening explicitly states that all subgroups of polycyclic groups
inherit their full profinite topology, as a consequence of Malcev subgroup
separability. Theorem3.2 proves the per-prime version for every subgroup of a
finitely generated nilpotent group. The publisher web references of this
read are turn459view0, turn459view1, turn459view2.

For the exact full-topology deduction, let H be any subgroup of a finitely
generated nilpotent G and let N have finite index in H. Both H and N are
finitely generated. For each of the finitely many nonidentity N-cosets in H,
subgroup separability of N in G supplies a finite-index overgroup of N that
excludes its representative. The intersection L has L intersect H=N. Taking
the G-core of L gives an open normal subgroup of G whose intersection with
H lies in N. This is exactly the cofinality needed for full induced topology.
Applying it also to the correction subgroups and using right-exactness of
completion gives the required embedded completions and exact kernel images
inside the finitely generated nilpotent quotients.
