The transcription is a personal visual reading of all 38 whole scans and both supplied halves, without OCR or delegated reading. All 38 pages received a separately logged second visual audit before the context archive was opened. The typed letter 031–032 is substantially clearer than the rapid handwriting, although overtyping and apparent spelling errors remain marked rather than corrected; faint reverse-side show-through was distinguished from foreground writing.

The least secure passages are the minute margins on 019 and 027; the heavily revised outline 037; the intersection notes 039; the crowded construction and uncertain cancellation 041; the descent/ampleness margins 044–045; the cancelled dimension discussion 052; the clipped last line 055; and the compressed annotations and abandoned drafts 056–058. Several multiword tentative readings there are low confidence even if a recognizable mathematical interpretation exists. The short superscripts, overwritten field letters, local rank indices, Gamma/P, script/plain I and O, and the looped f-subscript 057–058 also remain vulnerable.

The doubt register reproduces every in-place uncertainty and illegibility mark, including nested and repeated marks. Confidence describes the visual reading, not the truth of a mathematical assertion. “Medium” means a plausible visual candidate with unresolved strokes; “low” covers long compressed/cancelled readings, explicit unreadable spans, and the specially difficult marginal passages. No confidence rating certifies unmarked surrounding words. The one-word unreadable span at the foot of 055 and other illegible cancellations were inspected at additional magnification after attempted readings.

Diagrams retain their written direction, partial objects and cancellation state; enclosures and nonverbal marks have TikZ approximations. The supplied fragment has no preamble or macro definitions. Mechanical checks cover page order, concatenation, markers, inventory, braces, delimiters and environments; a full LaTeX render was not available because this environment lacks the LaTeX class/format and TikZ packages. Thus typography, especially nested diagram/callout placement, has not been render-verified.

Several apparent mathematical inconsistencies are intentionally preserved, including 028's product ordering, 046's doubtful quotient ordering, 052's displayed dimension relation, and 053's disputed étale-map subscript. Any interpretation suggested by the later context belongs only in the research notes. The transcription hashes are the boundary between the visual work and that later research.
