\subsection*{The three full contact orbits in the remaining kernel}
\statement{B.150}{Proved}{The source part of the remaining ordinary fixed kernel has an exact three-orbit presentation}
\label{stmt:B150}
Use the actual groups and marked section of B.149. Define the corrected
contact words
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B150-contact}
\begin{aligned}
 A_s&=s_1s_4^{-1}s_1^{-1},&
 B_s&=A_s^{-1}s_1^{-1}A_s,\\
 D_s&=s_2s_1s_3^{-1}s_1^{-1}s_2^{-1},&
 E_s&=s_2^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For $j\in J_a$, let $B_j,D_j,E_j$ be formal symbols and put
$\mathcal F=F(B_j,D_j,E_j:j\in J_a)$. There is an actual subgroup
identification
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B150-kernel}
 I_{\rm src}:=H_{\rm src}\cap K_{\rm rem}
       =\mathcal F/\mathcal R\ \le K_{\rm rem}^{\delta},
\end{equation}
where $X_j$ evaluates to $\sigma(j)X_s\sigma(j)^{-1}$ for
$X=B,D,E$, and $\mathcal R$ is exactly the full actual relation
preimage described below. In particular these three whole $J_a$
orbits generate $I_{\rm src}$ as a subgroup; they are not replaced
by an ambient normal closure. The ordinary local fixed-source equality
is equivalent to surjectivity of the inclusion in
\eqref{eq:B150-kernel}. Moreover
\[
 \mathcal R\cap F(D_{m^n}:n\in\mathbb Z)=1.
\]
\begin{proof}
The inverse of the free contact change \eqref{eq:B150-contact} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B150-inverse}
\begin{gathered}
 s_1=A_sB_s^{-1}A_s^{-1},\qquad s_2=E_s^{-1},\\
 s_3=s_1^{-1}s_2^{-1}D_s^{-1}s_2s_1,
 \qquad s_4=s_1^{-1}A_s^{-1}s_1.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Both compositions freely cancel to the identity. The frozen literal
one-family source certificate gives
\[
 (s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4,v,m)\longmapsto(1,1,1,A^{-1},v,m).
\]
Thus $(A_s,B_s,D_s,E_s,m,v)$ projects exactly to
$(A,1,1,1,m,v)$, without a nodal word oracle.

Let $P_{\rm src}=J_a*F(B,D,E)$ and let
$\rho:P_{\rm src}\to J_a$ kill the last three letters. Evaluation
on the actual corrected source gives a surjection
$e:P_{\rm src}\to H_{\rm src}$ with $p_ae=\rho$ and
$e|_{J_a}=\sigma$. The only imposed nonfree relation is the proved
$[A_s,m]=1$. Therefore $R:=\ker e$ lies in $\ker\rho$ and meets
$J_a$ trivially.

Here is its complete relation typing. In B.123's actual prefilled
$F_{11}\rtimes S$, evaluate the six free source generators by their
literal words to obtain
\[
 \Psi:F(A,B,D,E,m,v)\longrightarrow F_{11}\rtimes S.
\]
If $N$ is the full nodal normal closure, including all forty seeds,
both sheets and every signed source and deck translate, then
\[
 R=\Psi^{-1}(N)/\langle\!\langle[A,m]\rangle\!\rangle
 \quad\hbox{inside }P_{\rm src}.
\]
The denominator is contained in the numerator by B.130. The map
$\Psi$ is deliberately defined on the free six-generator source,
not on $P_{\rm src}$ before the nodal relation is imposed.

The entire kernel $\ker\rho$ is $\mathcal F$: form
$\mathcal F\rtimes J_a$ with left translation
$j_0(X_j)=X_{j_0j}$, map $X_j\mapsto jXj^{-1}$ and the section

universal property supplies the inverse, and both composites fix
every generator. This proves a simultaneous whole basis, indexed by
elements of $J_a$, not by unrestricted free words on $A,m,v$.
Let $\mathcal R$ be the inverse image of $R$ under this identification.
It is normal in $\mathcal F$ and invariant under all of $J_a$.
Taking kernels of the actual projection gives
$I_{\rm src}=\mathcal F/\mathcal R$. Every evaluated generator is
fixed by $\delta$, proving the inclusion in
\eqref{eq:B150-kernel}. B.149's two split decompositions give its
stated equivalence with fixed-source equality.

Finally B.132 proves that the actual pair $\langle m,D_s\rangle$ is

free factor of $I_{\rm src}$.
\end{proof}
