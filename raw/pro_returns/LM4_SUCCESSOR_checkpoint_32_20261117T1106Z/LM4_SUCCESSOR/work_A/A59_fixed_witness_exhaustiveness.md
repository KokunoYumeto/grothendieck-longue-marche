# Exact scope of the fixed-datum failure

This note is a proposed exhaustiveness proof for review. It is not integrated as an impossibility theorem. The existential A61/A62 constructions change the newer central frames and are fully consistent with this calculation.

## Prescribed datum

Fix the actual A56 quotient action and the literal s-frame in the unchanged `Q6_actual_central_solution.json`, together with the original C action and frames. Consider a full positive peripheral lift h with those data and with tau fixed literally. Its negative companion is b=hc. The question here is whether the induced s-normalizer can have square1 on the actual A59 scalar quotient while retaining that exact old s-frame.

## Every affine action correction

The integral free cusp cohomology action is forced to be the identity by A60; the binary s-block is fixed by the old quotient action. Thus the action on the mixed affine quotient has the same linear part as the baseline. Its inner vector differs from the baseline by2w in the four free cusp rows. Literal cusp fixation forces w to lie in the tau-invariant space modulo2. This gives the complete16-dimensional k source for positive corrections.

Any two maps from Pi into the scalar quotient with the same affine action differ by a central homomorphism into its center. Since Pi_ab=C6 is generated by tau, the value at tau determines this homomorphism. For the correction P=(2w,0), the only possible character restoring the literal cusp is the negative of its central commutator with tau. The source s has zero free cusp coordinate. Every doubled S-fixed free correction centralizes actual s: mixed products reduce0 and doubled quadratic products vanish modulo4. Therefore a nonzero restoring character at s cannot be removed by changing an s-conjugator that retains the old affine data. The necessary simultaneous class/cusp constraint is precisely the six-coordinate commutator map imposed in the CE calculation.

## Every lift of the prescribed s-frame

Write the corrected conjugator as P beta Q times a new central element, where Q lies in the doubled free kernel. Affine s-conjugacy makes its coordinate S-fixed. The affine involutory-normalizer condition makes it fixed also by D=beta*C. Thus Q ranges over the complete48-dimensional k joint-fixed source used in the calculation. The extra central element belongs to N=2O^6 because the old literal frame is fixed. The b-action on N is the identity, so its norm is zero and it cannot change the square.

For these complete corrections the new square changes by the sum of the two norm maps. Their polar terms vanish because both arguments are doubled. The positive correction is constrained by the cusp/s-class equation above. Direct evaluation of every binary scalar direction using the original group laws agrees with the two bilinear formulas:144 s-frame directions and48 positive directions were checked independently in work_CE/code/verify_A59_columns_direct.py/.out.

## The remaining invariant

The s-frame source has image rank3 overk. The16-dimensional positive source, after imposing its rank6 cusp constraint, has dimension10 and norm image rank3. Their combined image has rank5 in k^6 and has annihilator

    (1,1,0,0,0,0).

The baseline square, after transporting the complete old central frame rather than only its affine part, is

    (4,2,7,6,3,1).

Its annihilator value is6, which is nonzero in F8. Hence this exact old framed datum cannot be extended to an involutory s-normalizer within the stated full peripheral lifting scope. No choice of the r-frame can alter this one s-normalizer calculation.

If one wants to derive a full-involution consequence, one must also use the following exact implication rather than assume it: for a full involutory b and the prescribed beta, d=beta^-1 b centralizes s, so d² lies in C_Pi(s)=<s>. Its S3 head is trivial because beta has trivial S3 image and b acts trivially there. Thus d² cannot equal s and must equal1. This uses the full profinite free-product centralizer theorem already registered in the paper. Only after that additional input does the fixed-datum failure exclude a full involution in this particular framed fiber.

The larger existence problem remains untouched by such a conclusion. Multiplication of the new old frame by a suitable central k^6 element changes the displayed invariant. A61 explicitly lifts that changed datum by central halves while preserving the quotient action and every older C/finite-family frame. A62 then chooses compatible valid frames by finite common refinements and compactness; it never demands that this failed tuple lift.
