# Exact scope of the finite-centralizer recognition literature

Research record, 2026-11-16. No new recognition theorem or counterexample to the actual pants extension is claimed.

## Primary full-category warning

Herfort and Zalesskii, *Virtually free pro-p groups whose torsion elements have finite centralizer*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 40 (2008), 929–936, DOI 10.1112/blms/bdn070, was checked in the authors' full preprint, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.4244 (Section 5, Lemma 5.1 and proof; retrieval turn786view1/turn787view0/1). The main decomposition theorem is expressly pro-p. Their full profinite example is the amalgam S3 *_(C2) S3. Every torsion centralizer is finite, but this group has no finite free factor. The proof identifies its orientation subgroup as C3 * C3 and uses the standard free-product torsion and intersection theorems.

The example has odd torsion. In particular it does not satisfy the actual hypothesis that the orientation subgroup is a torsion-free full free profinite group of index two. It blocks a general finite-centralizer shortcut, but neither refutes nor proves recognition in our narrower setting.

Zalesskii, *On virtually projective groups*, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 572 (2004), 97–110, https://mat.unb.br/pz/arquivos/virproj.pdf, introduction pp.97–98 (turn785view0), explicitly distinguishes full profinite groups from the pro-p decomposition theorem. Its mixed-action Zhat-by-C2 example has an infinite involution centralizer, so it also does not decide the current self-centralizing setting. Its main theorem concerns the quotient by the closed torsion-generated normal subgroup and gives projectivity, not a marked free-product decomposition.

## Exact unclosed implication

Haran's primary 1990 paper was rechecked at Proposition 4.1 and its proof (turn790view3). The implication from real projectivity to real freeness there is a pro-two result, using the pro-two Frattini quotient. The full-category theorem gives an embedding in a real free group, not a decomposition of the given group.

For the actual E, A82–A84 establish compact augmentation projectivity, dyadic freeness, and full projective duality. The selected incidence map still has the explicit reduced coset-module cokernel. No source checked here turns these properties into surjectivity of that particular map. Abstract orientation-preserving recognition, if eventually proved, would still require a separate argument to recover the specified peripheral marking.

The current exact algebraic target is the actual full completed-ring unit B in `actual_marked_Fox_unit_gate_15h.md`. Its involution identity is established; its invertibility remains open.
