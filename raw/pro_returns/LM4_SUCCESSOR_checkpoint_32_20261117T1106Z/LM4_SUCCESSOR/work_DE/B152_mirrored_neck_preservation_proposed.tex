\subsection*{The mirrored free neck and its full coset position}
\statement{B.152}{Proved}{The complete mirrored neck is a retract through both initial families, and its ordinary kernel is a full coordinate free factor}
\label{stmt:B152}
In the notation of B.151, put
\[
 J_*=\theta(\mathfrak d(K))=aCKCa^{-1}.
\]
Its actual image in \(V_i=V/M_i\), and then in the complete
initial quotient \(V_*\), is a free group of rank five with
the literal marked basis below. Both images are retracts
and carry their full induced profinite topologies.
The kernel of its retained-height character in \(V_i\) is a
coordinate free factor of B.151's entire ordinary free family
kernel. Survival of this free neck in the later final group
already follows from B.121; the additional information here
is its exact relative coset position. No survival of the
larger local group containing the extra center is inferred.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.152.1. A character of the complete first quotient.}
The common retained retraction \(V\to K\) kills every \(P_i\).
It induces \(\mathcal B_i\to K\). Let
\(\chi:\mathcal B_i\to\mathbb Z\) be its \(f\)-exponent.
It satisfies
\[
 \chi(h)=1,\qquad
 \chi(k)=\chi(a)=\chi(d)=\chi(e)=\chi(V_4)=0.
\]
Every generator in B.151's four complete \(Q_j\) has
zero \(h\)-height. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B152-stabilizer-height}
 \chi(S_{ji})=0\qquad(j=\alpha,\beta,\delta,\epsilon).
\end{equation}
This is a character of the actual quotient by the full normal
closure, so every retained direction is included.

\emph{B.152.2. The whole based neck marking.}
Because \(a^{-1}\eta(K)a=K\), the exact formula in B.151
gives \(J_*=aCKCa^{-1}\). A five-element marked basis before
filling is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B152-basis}
\begin{aligned}
 z_a&=aCaCa^{-1}
       =(ah^{-1})\lambda_\alpha(ah^{-1})^{-1},\\
 z_B&=aCBCa^{-1}=a\lambda_\beta a^{-1},\\
 z_d&=aCdCa^{-1}
       =(ah^{-1})\lambda_\delta(ah^{-1})^{-1},\\
 z_e&=aCeCa^{-1}
       =(ah^{-1})\lambda_\epsilon(ah^{-1})^{-1},\\
 g&=aCfCa^{-1}=ak^{-1}ha^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
These are literal equalities from the chosen family
representatives, and \(CfC=k^{-1}h\). In particular
\(g\in\mathcal B_i\) and \(\chi(g)=1\). No independently
chosen family or corner conjugator is substituted.

\emph{B.152.3. An actual free-target retraction.}
Let \(\mathcal F=F(A,B',D,E,G)\) be an abstract free group.
Define on the whole presentation in B.151
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B152-map}
\begin{gathered}
 b\in\mathcal B_i\longmapsto G^{\chi(b)},\\
 \lambda_\alpha\longmapsto GAG^{-1},\qquad
 \lambda_\beta\longmapsto B',\\
 \lambda_\delta\longmapsto GDG^{-1},\qquad
 \lambda_\epsilon\longmapsto GEG^{-1}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Every stabilizer \(S_{ji}\) maps to one by
\eqref{eq:B152-stabilizer-height}, so every defining
centralizer relation holds. Thus this is a homomorphism
of the complete \(V_i\), regardless of any unresolved
presentation of its base quotient.

The three \(ah^{-1}\) conjugators have height \(-1\);
the \(a\) conjugator has height zero. Therefore
\eqref{eq:B152-map} sends the five whole words in
\eqref{eq:B152-basis} to \(A,B',D,E,G\), respectively.
The reverse assignment defines a homomorphism from the
free group \(\mathcal F\), and its composite is the identity.
It follows that the image of \(J_*\) is free on exactly the
displayed basis, its original embedding survives, and there
is a retraction
\[
 V_i\longrightarrow J_*.
\]
Equivalently \(J_*\cap M_i=1\) in the original \(V\).
Every prescribed finite \(J_*\)-target extends by this
retraction. Hence the image carries the full induced
profinite topology, and the completed retraction fixes its
actual closure, canonically \(\widehat F_5\).
No infinite-coordinate retraction is used for this conclusion.

\emph{B.152.4. The entire ordinary coordinate free factor.}
Put
\[
 b_\alpha=b_\delta=b_\epsilon=ah^{-1},
 \qquad b_\beta=a.
\]
The kernel of \(J_*\to\langle g\rangle\) has the free basis
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B152-entire-basis}
 g^n z_jg^{-n}
      =X_{g^n b_j S_{ji},j}
 \qquad(n\in\mathbb Z,\ j=\alpha,\beta,\delta,\epsilon).
\end{equation}
For fixed \(j\), equality of two cosets would put
\(b_j^{-1}g^{n-n'}b_j\) in \(S_{ji}\); applying \(\chi\)
and \eqref{eq:B152-stabilizer-height} gives \(n=n'\).
Different types lie in different components of the disjoint
union of coset sets. Thus \eqref{eq:B152-entire-basis} is
literally a subset of the full free basis in B.151. Its span
is a coordinate free factor, including all positive and
negative levels. This statement is ordinary; it does not
identify the completion of the infinite family kernel.

\emph{B.152.5. The opposite family and the previous final retract.}
The map \eqref{eq:B152-map} sends \(k'=p(k)\) to one,
because it is a base element of retained height zero.
Each remaining leaf is a commutator
\([k',\theta(x_i)]\), and the remaining square is
\([(\theta(\gamma)k')^2,k']\). The map therefore kills
all five and every one of their ambient conjugates.
It descends to \(V_*\) and still fixes the five marked
neck generators. The same arbitrary-target argument gives
its full induced topology there.

This does not furnish a new final-group survival claim:
B.121 already gives the actual final ordinary retraction
onto \(K\). Conjugating its subgroup and retraction by
the actual \(aC\) supplies survival of \(aCKCa^{-1}\)
through the later quotient as well. The new relative
description is \eqref{eq:B152-entire-basis} inside B.151's
complete one-sided free kernel. Both center generators
are killed by this detector. It therefore cannot decide
additional relations involving their joint action, the
survival of the larger local \(E_1\), or the original
fixed-source and icosahedral comparisons.

\end{proof}
