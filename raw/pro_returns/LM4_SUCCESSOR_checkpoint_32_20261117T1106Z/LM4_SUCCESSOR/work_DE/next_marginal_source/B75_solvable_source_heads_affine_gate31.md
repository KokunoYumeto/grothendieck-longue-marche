## 2. The actual finite head has an automorphism component

Let N0 be a characteristic finite-index subgroup of the actual finitely generated Birman group N=B4(S0,4). Put Q=N/N0. Conjugation by G_V induces alpha:G_V->Aut(Q), and the actual B75 crossed map induces delta_Q:G_V->Q satisfying

    delta_Q(gh)=delta_Q(g) alpha_g(delta_Q(h)).

Thus

    Psi_Q(g)=(delta_Q(g),alpha_g) in Q semidirect Aut(Q)

is an actual finite-image homomorphism. Under its affine action on Q, a point x is sent to delta_Q(g) alpha_g(x). The zero test delta_Q(g)=1 is exactly stabilization of the identity point. B75 requires this entire stabilizer preimage to lie inside each prescribed U0; retaining only the kernel of Psi_Q is weaker.

A solvable, or even elementary abelian, Q does not force the source image of Psi_Q to be solvable: its automorphism component can be nonsolvable. Therefore Section1 gives NO obstruction to characteristic finite p-group Birman quotients or to all abelian Birman targets. Such an inference would discard precisely the action that may carry a nonsolvable source quotient.

## 3. An abstract finite detector, and its missing actual realization

For any prescribed finite quotient pi:G_V->D and any prime p, take the additive regular permutation module V=F_p[D], with D acting on the basis by left multiplication. The map

    c(g)=e_{pi(g)}-e_1

satisfies c(gh)=c(g)+pi(g)c(h) and has zero set exactly ker(pi). Hence (c(g),pi(g)) is an affine detector of the required source quotient, even though its translation module is elementary abelian. Its existence as an abstract cocycle does not make it a quotient of the actual Birman crossed map.

For example, a proposed realization by an equivariant group homomorphism phi:N->V with phi(delta(g))=c(g) necessarily factors through H1(N,F_p). The values c(g), as g ranges over the source, span the full augmentation subspace of dimension |D|-1. Therefore such a realization requires

    dim_Fp H1(N,F_p) >= |D|-1.

The left side is finite because the actual N is finitely generated. For fixed p this prevents using this particular regular-module construction uniformly for source quotients of unbounded order. It does not exclude different affine detectors, nonabelian Birman quotients, passage to further actual subgroups with a justified comparison, or other characteristic refinements.
