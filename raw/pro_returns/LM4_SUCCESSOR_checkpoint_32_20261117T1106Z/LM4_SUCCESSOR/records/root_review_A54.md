# Root review: A.54, the exact cohomology cost of adjoining a layer

Reviewed 16 November 2026, approximately 06:26–06:29 UTC. Accepted
after reading the complete working proof, rerunning the updated
filiform certificate, and receiving CE's independent review.

We reopened Poonen (2017), Rational points on varieties, Theorem
6.7.3 and Corollary6.7.4, printed page194, in the author-hosted text
https://math.mit.edu/~poonen/papers/Qpoints.pdf. The statements
explicitly apply to profinite groups, closed normal subgroups and
continuous modules. The normal pro-p-prime averaging argument gives
all-degree vanishing under the stated zero-invariant condition;
the five-term sequence then supplies the claimed restriction
isomorphism. No splitting of the extension is required.

For C'=Pi/W_m and P=R/W_m, the module T is inflated from C and
therefore has trivial P-action. Thus H1(P,T) is Hom(Pab,T), and
A.53 identifies Pab with V. The resulting exact formula is
H1(C',T)=Hom_C(V,T). In particular H1(C',V)=End_C(V) is nonzero
when V is nonzero. This establishes a real failure of automatic
vanishing on the enlarged base.

The filiform next layer is an actual residual quotient. The new
normalized dual cocycle has r-value zeta² e1, independent of the
old r-value e1: the exact ranks are21,22,23. The old inflation
subspace has dimension one by the proved formula, so the new class
restricts nontrivially to the stabilized residual. Its restriction
is an equivariant homomorphism because that residual acts trivially
on the coefficient module; irreducibility makes it surjective.
The dual coefficient required for a new A.53 application has
nonzero H1, exactly as claimed.

The result obstructs a naive repeated use of the automatic-H1 clause.
It does not obstruct every scalar refinement or the negative norm.
A simultaneous finite abelian enlargement over the original base
still falls within A.53, as the statement explicitly retains.
