3. A genuine one-operator nonlinear half-space decomposition

Let chi:F->Z send x to1 and all ten H_m generators to0, and let
Vchi=ker chi. Its full free decomposition is

 Vchi=*_(n in Z) H_n,  H_n=x^n H_m x^-n.

Set Vminus=*_(n<=0)H_n and Vplus=*_(n>=1)H_n. This gives an
actual free product Vchi=Vminus*Vplus before nodes. Delta sends
H_n to H_(1-n), so it exchanges the two factors.

Both signed m operators preserve Vminus and Vplus exactly. To see
the complete nonlinear statement, write alpha(x)=x r for either
signed m map, with r in H_m, and alpha(H_m)=H_m. For n>0,

 alpha(x^n)x^-n=(x r x^-1)(x^2 r x^-2)...(x^n r x^-n).

This conjugating word uses only H_1,...,H_n. For n<0 the inverse
product uses only H_0,H_-1,...,H_(n+1). Therefore alpha(H_n)
lies in Vplus for n>=1 and in Vminus for n<=0. Applying the
same argument to the inverse operator gives equality, not merely
inclusion. This proves an all-level nonlinear splitting for m,
with every connector still present.

4. Why this is not a common source-invariant splitting

The actual freely reduced word alpha_v(a), expanded in the
Schreier basis of Vchi, has letters at both levels0 and1.
Hence alpha_v(Vminus) is not contained in Vminus in the free
pre-node model. Also alpha_m(B), expressed in the literal
H_v*<x> basis, contains noncancelling x letters and is not in H_v.
Thus neither regional free factor is silently treated as invariant
under the other source operator.

These are exact free-word statements before the node quotient.
They do NOT prove corresponding nonmembership after N is imposed.
Nor does the shifted two-cut picture itself give a tree of groups
or a right-angled presentation for J. The twenty plus nodes couple
the two central half-spaces, and their complete mixed source
translates must still be controlled. This is the precise remaining
compatibility problem for a common nonlinear level decomposition.
