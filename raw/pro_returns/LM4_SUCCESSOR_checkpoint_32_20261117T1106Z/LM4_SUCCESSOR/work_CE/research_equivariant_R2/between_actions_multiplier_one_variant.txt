Multiplier-one identity data between two different full negative actions
16November2026. Typed consequence; same-action multiplier-one remainsopen.

Fix the finite actual source-central quotientDdagger of the preceding
note. Choose an arithmetic Grothendieck-Teichmuller element
 psi=(lambda,f), f inF',
whose cyclotomic multiplier islambda_2=-1 andlambda_p=1 foroddp.
Existence uses the usual arithmetic embedding and surjectivity of the
cyclotomic character; no finite head is postulated without a full lift.
In particularlambda=7mod24, whilelambda is neither1 nor-1 globally.
In every class-two quotient, f is central. Therefore the actual fullpsi
acts there EXACTLY byx->x^lambda,y->y^lambda, not merely on their
conjugacy classes. On the entire orientation subgroup ofDdagger this
is exactly thetheta7 automorphism. This proves the required ordinary
full lift matching all source-central data.

Letalpha=psi c psi^-1, a genuine full negative involution. Power
substitution commutes withc in the class-two quotient, soalpha andc
have identical actions onF/gamma3(F). SinceN containsF', the subgroup
[N,F] containsgamma3(F). Hencealpha andc induce the same action on
N/[N,F]. It follows that
 [N,F semidirectalpha C2]=[N,F semidirectc C2].
The actual source-central quotients for these two different full
actions are therefore literally identified, with the same orientation
kernel and external reflection. The identity map of that quotient is
an equivariant multiplier-one peripheral map. It fixes the three named orientation cusp elements and the external
reflection literally. The other named reflection classes agree because
the actual matching map theta7 preserves them by the explicitx^3 and
y^-3 conjugators in the finite certificate; no equality of arbitrary
previously chosen full reflection representatives is asserted.

Suppose a full multiplier-one peripheralg conjugatedc toalpha. Then
psi^-1g centralizesc in the outer peripheral group. The established
full centralizer is{1,c}; its multipliers are1,-1. But the multiplier
ofpsi^-1g islambda^-1, neither1 nor-1, a contradiction. Thus the
identity data on the actual common central quotient do not lift to a
full multiplier-one equivariant map BETWEEN THESE TWO ACTIONS.

This conclusion does not contradict ordinary R2, which does not demand
that the lift intertwine the two involutions. It also does not refute
the multiplier-one equivariant rule for a single fixed actionc: the
source action here isalpha while the target action isc (or conversely,
depending on the direction ofg). Its role is to make the scope of any
proposed equivariant lifting theorem explicit. The involutionalpha is
already conjugate toc bytheknownfullpsi; no new counterexample to the
main unrestricted conjugacy question is obtained.
