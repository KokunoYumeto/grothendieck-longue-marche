1. The actual base and common neck

Let B_-={0<|q|<1}, B_+={1<|q|<infinity}. Retain every inner/outer
critical fiber. Choose the same small regular neck N about
q_*=exp(pi*i/5) as in B108, and enlarge the two annuli slightly into
opposite halves of N. Their overlap is contractible. Write U_ann for
their union. This base retains both annular fundamental-group generators
and only the first unit-circle neck. It omits the other four necks and
all old fifth-root boundary points. Its fundamental group is free of
rank two, not trivial.

Transport the actual lower disk and its disjoint meridian system through
this one regular neck. The common disk subgroup is K=F5, generated by
the four literal A peripherals and the selected branch peripheral. At
the two ends isotope the transported disk within the lower half-plane
into the appropriate bad side, with the gap connector fixed. The common
orbifold fiber group, based by those same paths, is

  H_neck = C2(c_+) * C2(c_-) * K.

Here c_+ is the cone at t=0, and c_- is the cone at t=infinity, with
their actual common-neck whiskers. These symbols do not silently denote
the q0-adapted cone C in a separate Nielsen basis. Each map of H_neck
below is the actual inclusion after the specified based transport. No
injection of H_neck into a filled regional group is assumed.

2. The two annular regional groups before gluing

Let R_- be the bad-region orbifold group over B_-, with its cone c_-,
and let R_+ be the bad-region group over B_+, with its cone c_+.
These are the complete annular bad groups, including every local
critical relation and the old retained monodromy; they are not the

The good-side equivariant contraction of B107 gives a product of the
base with its good fixed cone. The actual boundary collar intersection

  E_-=(C2(c_+) x <k_->) *_<k_-> R_-,
  E_+=(C2(c_-) x <k_+>) *_<k_+> R_+.

Equivalently E_- is generated by R_- and the opposite cone c_+ with
c_+^2=1 and [c_+,k_-]=1; the analogous assertion holds on the other
side. This is precisely the extra relation lost by replacing an
annular good-side factor with C2 alone.

4. The exact categorical elimination

The map H_neck -> E_- sends c_- and K to their bad-region images,
and c_+ to the good product cone. The map to E_+ has the symmetric
description. Orbifold van Kampen gives their group pushout. In a
presentation of this pushout, first identify the two K copies using
the one transported disk basis. The free good cone on either side
is then identified with the corresponding bad cone on the other side.
Eliminating these redundant generators gives exactly

  G_ann = (R_- *_K R_+) /
          normal_closure([c_+,k_-], [c_-,k_+]).                 (1)

The ordinary amalgam in (1) is meaningful because the actual K maps
are injective (and in fact are ordinary annular retracts, by the
independent DE detector). No injection of either R vertex AFTER the
two added relations is claimed. Equation (1) is an equality of based
presentations, proved by categorical elimination; it does not treat
the final quotient as an amalgam or apply a Bass--Serre tree to it.
Its full completion has the corresponding universal profinite
presentation with the CLOSED normal closure of the same two words.
This last assertion follows on every finite target and includes no
properness assertion for that final profinite quotient.

6. The source count is a constraint, not a proof of equality

The selected source t=0 over U_ann has no inner contact puncture and
four outer contact punctures. It contains both annular base loops.
It is therefore the actual surface with free fundamental group F6,
with four contact peripherals and two base generators. Its images lie
in the even centralizer of c_+; the good inner generator is k_-, and
the outer five come from the bad fixed source of R_+. A proposed
centralizer which retains only that vertex F5 misses this geometric
sixth source direction. This observation is only a necessary check:
equality of ranks or the source count does not prove generation or
injectivity.

The other four necks remain absent from this model. Neither (1) nor
the regional annular centralizer theorem decides the full main marginal
or joint-source question. The exact new gate is the interaction of the
two additional commuting relations in (1), with their actual connectors.
