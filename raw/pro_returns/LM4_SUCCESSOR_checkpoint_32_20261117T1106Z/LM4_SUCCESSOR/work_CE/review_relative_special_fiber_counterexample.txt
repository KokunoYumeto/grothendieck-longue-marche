Independent review: a retained-only special collision does not imply
relative deletion-kernel surjectivity
2026-11-16. Research theorem/counterexample review for the marginal route.

Verdict: PASS. Root and this reviewer independently found the same
example. It refutes the proposed GENERAL relative special-fiber lemma;
it does not decide the actual seven-point marginal source image.

Let B=C minus{1}, and define
 Y={(lambda,z):lambda!=1, z!=0, z!=lambda, z!=1},
 X={(lambda,z):lambda!=1, z!=0, z!=lambda}.
Thus X is obtained by filling the deleted divisor U={z=1} in Y.
Use the special fiber lambda=0. Its only collision is that of the
two RETAINED punctures z=0 and z=lambda. The U puncture z=1 remains
distinct. The defining polynomial z(z-lambda)(z-1) has constant
degree three and nonzero leading coefficient. A continuous far-point
section over B is z=2+|lambda|, which avoids all three punctures.

The exact product chart is
     (lambda,z) |-> (z,r=(lambda-z)/(1-z)).
Its inverse is lambda=z+r(1-z). The conditions lambda!=z and lambda!=1
become r!=0 and r!=1, respectively. Hence
     Y is isomorphic to (C minus{0,1}) x (C minus{0,1}).
At lambda=0 the source is the graph r=z/(z-1). This Mobius map
permutes {0,1,infinity} and induces an isomorphism of its free groups.
The basepoint (lambda,z)=(0,2) has product coordinates (2,2), so no
unmentioned basepoint change is needed. Consequently the actual source
pi1 image in pi1(Y)=F2 x F2 is the graph of an isomorphism F2->F2.

For completeness, pi1(X)=Z^3 can be proved directly by the pencil.
Project to lambda in C minus{1}. Away from lambda=0, the z-fiber has
the two punctures 0,lambda. A loop around lambda=1 has trivial braid
action: its motion of the second puncture contracts in C minus{0}.
A loop around zero is the two-puncture full twist. Filling this base
value, using the far-point section, kills its section lift and imposes
the relation [a,b]=1 on the two fiber meridians. The remaining base
generator about lambda=1 commutes with both. Thus pi1(X)=Z^3.
Equivalently, X is the complement of the three nonparallel affine
lines lambda=1,z=0,z=lambda, whose three intersections are nodes.
The pencil argument supplies the needed group calculation directly.

Let a,b be a free basis in the first factor of pi1(Y). The element
     k=([a,b],1)
is nontrivial and belongs to the actual deletion kernel
ker(pi1(Y)->pi1(X)), because pi1(X) is abelian. It does not lie in the
source graph: a graph of an isomorphism has trivial intersection with
the first factor. In particular it cannot be in the image of the
source deletion kernel. That source kernel is nontrivial (it kills
the meridian about z=1), but its entire image remains in the graph.
This proves strict failure of containment, not merely a failure to
identify some chosen finite list of normal generators.

Exact profinite conclusion.

Identify the second graph factor through the inverse Mobius-induced
isomorphism, so the source graph becomes diagonal. Send the two free
generators in each factor to (12),(23) in S3. This is an actual finite
quotient pi1(Y)->S3 x S3. The source image is diagonal S3, whereas k
maps to a nontrivial three-cycle in the first factor and one in the
second. Thus even the closure of the source image misses k. The same
is true for the image of its closed profinite deletion kernel.

No unproved completion embedding for an infinite-index kernel is
needed. The completion of F2 x F2 is Fhat2 x Fhat2, and the graph
embedding is a retract after the displayed isomorphism, hence carries
the full induced topology. The ambient closed deletion kernel is
ker(Fhat2 x Fhat2 -> Zhat^3), and it contains k by continuity.
The finite S3 x S3 quotient separates k from the source graph. Its
action on the six cosets of diagonal S3 gives a transitive degree-six
finite-cover certificate for the failure.

Why this is relevant but does not settle the actual marginal.

At the EXCLUDED base value lambda=1, U meets the moving retained
puncture and the retained vertical divisor in a triple point. Its
monodromy remains a base action; it is not imposed as a relation.
Therefore local retained-only degeneration at lambda=0 and a generic
fiber van Kampen theorem do not control all conjugations of the
deletion kernel. The actual ratio pencil likewise retains the base
directions around0 and1. Its precise U triples and their transported
monodromies must be used. The counterexample proves no failure of the
actual marginal map and no nontrivial element of the original K.

Control comparison: if lambda=1 is restored to the base in this example,
the coordinates (z,z-lambda) identify the unfilled surface with
(C minus{0,1}) x C* and the filled surface with (C*)^2. The special
source has second coordinate z. Its deletion-kernel image is then
exactly the ambient normal closure of the z=1 meridian in the first
free factor, with trivial second coordinate. Thus the relative
containment becomes true after that base hole is filled. This locates
the failure in the retained base-loop action, rather than in the
retained-only collision by itself.

An optional focused finite target, not a concluded calculation: the
degree-six left/right S3 action suggests testing two actual F7->S3
maps with equal eight peripheral-word values and compatible common
conjugators for the eleven source actions (allowing a factor swap if
the actual monodromy requires one). A mismatch on a deleted peripheral
would then give an actual marginal counterexample. No such pair has
been constructed here.
