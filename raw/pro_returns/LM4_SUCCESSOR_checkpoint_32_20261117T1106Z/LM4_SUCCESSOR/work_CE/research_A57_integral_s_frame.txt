Proposed integral s-frame lift on the actual A57 quotient
16 November 2026. Under independent review; no A59-center or full R2
framed realization is asserted here.

Use the normalized source cocycles from research_A57_integral_norm.txt.
The actual joint quotient has coordinates (w,g), with w in L^4,
g in C_new, and w mod2=e(g), where e=(e_1,...,e_4). Multiplication is
    (w,g)(w',g')=(w+rho(g)w',gg').
The actual source s is S=(0,s), after choosing e_i(s)=d_i(s)=0.
The accepted integral proof gives
    b(w,g)=(-Cw+(1-rho(bg))q,bg),     Cq=q,
where b on the base is the retained involution and b^2=1 on this quotient.
Here C acts diagonally on the four copies.

Let beta be the prescribed C_new s-frame, so
    b(s)=beta s beta^-1,    beta b(beta)=1.
Put D=rho(beta)C. Since the H-image of b is c, and c fixes s,
    D^2=1,    Ds=sD.
Both operators are monomial, with all nonzero entries Teichmuller seventh
roots. A lift B=(p,beta) must satisfy p mod2=e(beta).

The s-conjugacy equation B S B^-1=b(S) is precisely
    (1-s)(p-q)=0.
For the norm, direct multiplication gives
    B b(B)=(p-Dp+(rho(beta)-1)q,1).
Writing t=p-q and using Cq=q reduces this to
    B b(B)=((1-D)t,1).
Thus both required equations hold exactly when t is fixed by s and D.

The required residue t0=e(beta)-q mod2 is already fixed by both. The
s-fixed assertion follows by applying the actual cocycle e to
b(s)=beta s beta^-1 and using e(s)=0. For D, apply e to
beta b(beta)=1 and use the displayed b action modulo2. One obtains
    (1+D)(e(beta)-q)=0.
These are necessary consequences of the ACTUAL retained base frame,
not independent finite assignments.

A commuting pair of monomial involutions with entries in the Teichmuller
C7 group is orbitwise equivalent over O to a permutation representation.
Indeed its generated group is a finite2-group, and the stabilizer of a
basis line acts there by a character into C7; that character is trivial.
Choose an orbit representative and transport its basis vector. The
trivial stabilizer character makes this choice well-defined and yields
an ordinary permutation basis. Its invariant module is free on orbit
sums, and reduction to the mod2 invariant module is onto.

Consequently t0 has a joint invariant lift t in L^4. Set p=q+t. Then
B=(p,beta) is an actual frame with the original literal base beta,
    B S B^-1=b(S),       (B^-1 b)^2=1.
It is also an s-conjugator for the positive action, since c(s)=s.

Limits and the r-frame.
The new frame need not be the image of a particular already chosen full
s-conjugator. Its realization by a full R2 lift requires the actual
central refinement, which A59 shows has an additional18-dimensional
binary center. No lifting through that center is claimed here.
If the retained C_new datum includes a pure transporter conjugating its
s d_s to d_r, A16 supplies the corresponding r-frame in this pro-two
extension: lift the transporter, retain the resulting involution in an
S3 lift, and use coprime conjugacy to move the order-three complement to
actual r. This is conditional on the specified base transporter and
retains its literal r-frame. Failure for an arbitrary stored r-frame
without that refinement is not a full obstruction.
