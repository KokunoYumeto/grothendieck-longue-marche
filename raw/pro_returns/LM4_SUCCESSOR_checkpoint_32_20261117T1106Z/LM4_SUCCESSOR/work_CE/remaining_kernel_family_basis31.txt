An actual family-indexed refinement of the paired relative fiber

Cycle31; companion to one_fixed_coset_relative_kernel31.txt. This is
an ordinary free-product description retaining the precise deleted
family cores. It neither straightens the full node relators nor proves
remaining fixed generation. There is no compact free-product assertion
for the infinite-index subgroup constructed here.

Use B139's exact generic orbifold tuple

    E=F(a,b,d,e,f)*C2(C)*C2(tau), delta=Ad(tau).

Let x=tau C and write z*=tau z tau for each ordinary letter z. The
orientation kernel is the literal free group

    F11=R*D,
    R=F(x,a,a*,f,f*), D=F(b,b*,d,d*,e,e*).

This follows by the two-coset Schreier rewriting with representatives
1,tau. Delta inverts x and swaps each displayed starred pair; hence
both free factors are delta-stable. Deleting D is exactly the actual
one-family filling before its node relations. In particular the
surviving ordinary fiber W_a is the quotient of R by its full projected
node/source relation closure, denoted N_a. The subgroup R need not be
invariant under the original source actions in F11. Only the deletion
map is equivariant. This distinction retains the previously proved
whole-filling nonsplitting and all nonlinear source corrections.

Let U=ker(J_a -> S) be the actual one-family norm group, and put

    R_U=preimage_R(U), Q=preimage_F11(U).

The map to W_a factors through the deletion R*D -> R. Consequently Q
is exactly the preimage of R_U under that free-product retraction.
There is the following explicit inverse presentation:

    Q = R_U * ( *_{R_U t in R_U\R} t D t^-1 ).          (1)

Here one representative t is chosen for each right coset R_U t, with
t=1 for the identity. A direct Bass--Serre verification has one R-type
vertex with stabilizer R_U and one D-type leaf for each right coset;
every edge group is trivial and the quotient graph is a star. Its
fundamental group is (1), so there is no extra free factor and no
unstated relation. Equivalently the usual reduced-word normal form
of R*D gives the two inverse presentation maps.

The cosets R_U\R identify with U\W_a. Their delta action has exactly
one fixed coset, by the proved one-family fixed subgroup and the
torsion-free argument in the companion note. Therefore representatives
can be chosen in exact delta-pairs except for 1. On the identity D
factor, delta swaps b/b*, d/d*, e/e*. On every other factor it sends
t z t^-1 to delta(t) z* delta(t)^-1. Thus all six actual deleted-family
cores and all their coset conjugates occur as explicit free-basis
letters in exchanged pairs.

The retained factor R_U itself has a free basis exchanged in pairs:
apply the single-cone Kurosh argument of the companion to the smaller
orbifold C2(C)*C2(tau)*F(a,f). Its preimage of U times <tau> contains
one tau factor and no C factor, and orientation rewriting gives R_U.
Together with (1) this yields the complete paired basis of Q while
retaining the deleted cores literally, rather than just abstractly.

The actual final node closure N is still the one in the companion
note, not merely the normal closure of the projected N_a. In the
presentation (1) every t^-1 rho t must be rewritten with its actual
source corrections. This gives an exact relative presentation

    Q/N = (R_U * F(actual deleted cores at every coset)) / N,
    Q/N -> U,  ker=K_rem.

The quotient R_U/N_a is U. The map R_U -> Q/N is NOT asserted to
factor through U: a projected one-family relator can represent a
nontrivial remaining-kernel word before the other family letters are
deleted. The actual section sigma(U) exists by B149/B154, but it need
not be this literal retained free factor. Substituting that free
factor for sigma would erase the very correction words at issue.

One can see why a blanket graph-relator inference is invalid even
before computing any new words. The one-family node closure lies in
[R,R], so W_a_ab=R_ab. Source actions preserve the a and a* peripheral
abelian classes, and U is generated by the norms of their paired
conjugates. Its abelian image is therefore contained in the diagonal
line generated by [a]+[a*]. A retained peripheral core a or a*, with
any actual conjugator, has abelian image one of those two unit vectors
and is not in U. Thus it is not in R_U or Q. A node involving such a
core cannot be read as a commutator of two Q-basis vertices simply
because its full commutator lies in Q. Its complete Schreier path and
connector must be retained in (1).

By contrast every deleted core is in Q, and (1) gives the whole
simultaneously free family of its coset copies. This is the concrete
next input for the entire node rewriting. No subset of nodes, signed
source action, covering sheet, or retained-deck translate is omitted;
no resulting filled-RAAG or fixed-generation claim is made.
