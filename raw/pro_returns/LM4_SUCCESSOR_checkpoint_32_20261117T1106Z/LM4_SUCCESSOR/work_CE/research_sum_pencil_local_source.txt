ACTUAL SOURCE INCLUSION AND LOCAL FIVE-POINT MONODROMY
Research proof for the sum pencil; no global normality claim.

Scale zeta=1. Let T=1+epsilon and center the local fiber at v=T.
The five points near(1,1), in increasing order for epsilon=r>0 small,
are
 y1: -sqrt(2epsilon+epsilon^2),
 y2: -epsilon,
 y3: 0,
 y4: +epsilon,
 y5: +sqrt(2epsilon+epsilon^2).
The outer two are the product-hyperbola branches; the inner three are
respectively v=1, v=w, and w=1. A positive epsilon loop sends the outer
pair through a positive half rotation and the inner triple through a
positive full rotation. The factor sqrt(1+epsilon/2) has zero winding
and can be homotoped to1 while the outer radius stays strictly larger
than the inner radius. The common moving center is removed by a
contractible translation supported inside the local disk.

Choose standard real Artin cuts at epsilon=r, with a positive half
twist taking the left point through the lower half-plane. A rigid
half rotation of all five points is Delta5; the additional inner
half rotation is Delta3 on positions2,3,4. Thus the literal local
braid in this marking is
 beta=Delta5*Delta3_inner,
 Delta5=(sigma1 sigma2 sigma3 sigma4)(sigma1 sigma2 sigma3)
        (sigma1 sigma2)(sigma1),
 Delta3_inner=sigma2 sigma3 sigma2.
The two factors commute; its exponent is13 and its permutation is
only(1 5), as required by the one quadratic branch exchange.

Put R=y1*y2*y3*y4 and C=y1*y2*y3*y4*y5. With the pullback Artin
convention used in B.78, the exact action is
 beta(y1)=R*y5*R^-1,
 beta(yi)=R*yi*R^-1 fori=2,3,4,
 beta(y5)=y1.
The checker verifies these as literal free words, verifies the
inverse action, and verifies beta(C)=C.

Consequently the local variation quotient is exactly
 <y1,y2,y3,y4 | R=y1*y2*y3*y4 central>, with y5=y1.
It is Z(R) times F3: eliminate y4=(y1*y2*y3)^-1 R. Of the four
commutation relations with R, one is redundant. Thus the local
five-generator presentation has four independent listed relators:
one branch identification and three central-product commutators.
This agrees with the ordinary four-component divisor intersection,
while retaining the fact that the selected line is tangent to one
of those components.

For the actual special source at T=1 choose one based boundary cut
around the whole local disk. Its puncture meridian is LITERALLY C,
not a choice of one hyperbola meridian. The other eighteen punctures
remain outside that disk. Therefore its generic-fiber image is the
free factor F19 generated by C and the other eighteen puncture
meridians in F23. Algebraically replacing y5 by C is a Nielsen basis
change, so the other four local meridians give a complementary free
factor before imposing variation relations.

In the local variation quotient the source meridian is
 C=R*y1.
Its four-component exponent vector is(2,1,1,1). In particular it is
not the central radial meridian R; the hyperbola occurs twice. The
local quotient therefore leaves three missing retained directions
relative to the source cyclic subgroup. No assertion that this
subgroup or its global deletion kernel is normal follows from the
local calculation.

An actual global common basepoint and whisker from the original
source to this local positive-real epsilon marking still need to
be fixed in the complete sum-pencil certificate. The present proof
provides a compatible LOCAL marking and the exact source boundary;
