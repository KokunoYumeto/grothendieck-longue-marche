\statement{B.86}{Proved}{A whole deleted kernel under the diagonal and all four retained product directions}
Use the actual ordered surface Y, source image H, constant-family
source-kernel image \(J_c\), and common w-marking of B.83--B.84.
There is a specified actual six-generator subgroup L for which a whole
deleted normal closure lies in \(J_c\). Its intersection with the
actual source image is also determined exactly. These statements hold
for the actual closed images in full completions.

Write the source-adapted fiber basis and four retained product words as
\[
\begin{gathered}
 z,c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4,p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4,a,d,b,\qquad T_0=badb,\\
 P_1=bc_1p_1c_1^{-1}b^{-1},\qquad
 P_2=bc_1c_2p_2c_2^{-1}c_1^{-1}b^{-1},\\
 P_3=bc_1c_2T_0^{-1}zp_3z^{-1}T_0c_2^{-1}c_1^{-1}b^{-1},
 \qquad P_4=b^{-1}c_4^{-1}p_4c_4b .
\end{gathered}
\tag{B.86.1}
\]
These are the actual source entries17,19,21,23, respectively;
they are not freely chosen representatives of peripheral classes.
Put
\[
 C=bc_1^{-1}b^{-1},\qquad Q_4=a^{-1}P_4a,\qquad
 L=\langle d,C,P_1,P_2,P_3,Q_4\rangle .
\tag{B.86.2}
\]
Let \(F_6\) be the abstract free group on the six displayed symbols,
with the epimorphism \(\varphi:F_6\to L\) given by their actual words.
Let \(K\) be the image under \(\varphi\) of the free retraction kernel
that retains only d, and let N be the image of the free retraction
kernel that kills only C. Then
\[
\begin{gathered}
 K=L\cap H,\qquad L/K\simeq\mathbb Z,\\
 N=\langle\!\langle C\rangle\!\rangle_L
       \ \subseteq\ J_c\cap L .
\end{gathered}
\tag{B.86.3}
\]
No equality of N with \(J_c\cap L\) is asserted. In particular, the
containment controls arbitrary mixed diagonal/product conjugations of
C, without asserting normality of the whole \(J_c\).

\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual corrected positive triple.}\par
The complete product-one strip for \(c_1\) in B.83 gives \(C\in J_c\).
As in B.84, \(g_i\) is the actual source lift over
\(w=\zeta^{i-1}\), and \(\gamma_i\) is its conjugation action.
The actual positive1 action has the literal restriction
\[
 \gamma_2|_{\langle a,P_1\rangle}
                   =\operatorname{Inn}(aP_1^{-1}).
\tag{B.86.4}
\]
In particular, \(\gamma_2(P_1)=aP_1a^{-1}\). Consequently its
diagonal-normalized triple correction is the actual source word
\[
 v=[a,P_1^{-1}]
   =\gamma_2(P_1^{-1})P_1=[g_2,P_1^{-1}],
 \qquad v^{-1}g_2=P_1^{-1}g_2P_1 .
\tag{B.86.5}
\]
Here commutators mean \(xyx^{-1}y^{-1}\).
The common-marked triple is \((d,C,P_2^{-1})\), in that order.
The literal source action is conjugation by \(vB\) on this triple,
where \(B=dCP_2^{-1}\). Thus the corrected source element
\[
                  e=P_1^{-1}g_2P_1\in H
\]
acts on \(L_0=\langle d,C,P_2\rangle\) as \(\operatorname{Inn}(B)\).
The actual forward and inverse restrictions on all three generators
are checked in the certificate below. This uses a source conjugate of
\(g_2\), rather than an assumed action by a newly normalized local loop.

The subgroups \(H\cap L_0\) and \(J_c\cap L_0\) are invariant under
conjugation by e, hence by B on \(L_0\). They are invariant under C
and \(P_2\): both lie in H, and \(J_c\) is normal in H.
Since \(d=BP_2C^{-1}\), both intersections are normal in \(L_0\).
In particular, for every integer n,
\[
             d^nCd^{-n}\in J_c,\qquad
             d^nP_2d^{-n}\in H .
\tag{B.86.6}
\]
This is an all-powers subgroup argument, not a finite orbit calculation.

\emph{Three filled nodes and the other retained product directions.}\par
The actual common-marked filled nodes at
\(w=-\zeta^3,-\zeta^4,-\zeta^2\), respectively, give
\[
 [d,P_1]=1,\qquad [d,CP_3C^{-1}]=1,\qquad
                       [d,a^{-1}P_4a]=1 .
\tag{B.86.7}
\]
Each equation is obtained from the two transported peripheral
meridians at that node by their common diagonal conjugator.
The source-based marking is retained; these are not the unbased
commutation relations of arbitrary representatives.

The actual positive4 action and inverse satisfy
\[
 \gamma_5|_{\langle a,P_4\rangle}
       =\operatorname{Inn}(P_4^{-1}a),\qquad
              Q_4=\gamma_5^{-1}(P_4)\in H .
\tag{B.86.8}
\]
Thus \(Q_4\) is an actual source word and commutes with d.
For \(P_3\), put
\[
                  j_n=(d^nC^{-1}d^{-n})C\in J_c.
\]
The middle relation in (B.86.7) implies, for every integer n,
\[
                   d^nP_3d^{-n}=j_nP_3j_n^{-1}\in H .
\tag{B.86.9}
\]
Together with (B.86.6)--(B.86.8), this proves that the entire
d-strip of each retained word \(P_1,P_2,P_3,Q_4\) lies in H,
whereas the entire d-strip of C lies in \(J_c\).

\emph{The quantified kernel argument.}\par
We use the following elementary free-group observation. Suppose a free
group \(F(t,S,P)\) maps into an actual group, \(J\triangleleft H\),
every image \(t^nst^{-n}\) with \(s\in S\) lies in J, and every
image \(t^npt^{-n}\) with \(p\in P\) lies in H, for all integers n.
Then
\[
 \operatorname{im}\ker(F(t,S,P)\to F(t,P))\subseteq J.
\tag{B.86.10}
\]
Indeed the exponent kernel \(\ker(F\to\langle t\rangle)\)
is generated by the Schreier letters \(s_n=t^nst^{-n}\) and
\(p_n=t^npt^{-n}\), so its image lies in H. The normal closure of
all \(s_n\) inside this kernel is t-stable and therefore normal in F.
It is exactly the kernel killing S. Its image lies in J by normality
of J inside H. This proves (B.86.10), including arbitrary alternating
conjugations by t and the retained generators.
The all-powers hypothesis for the retained generators is essential;
knowing only that they normalize the final subgroup would not justify
this argument.

Apply this with \(t=d\), \(S=\{C\}\), and
\(P=\{P_1,P_2,P_3,Q_4\}\). The preceding actual identities prove
all its hypotheses. They show both that \(K\subseteq H\) and that
\(N\subseteq J_c\), as required.

\emph{The exact intersection with the actual source image.}\par
The actual regular unit
\[
                       f_d(v,w)=\frac{v-w}{w-1}
\tag{B.86.11}
\]
maps Y to \(\mathbb C^*\) and is identically \(-2\) on the source
\(v=2-w\). At the transported diagonal meridian d its winding is
primitive: the local numerator is a transverse parameter and the
denominator is nonzero. Let \(\epsilon_d\in\{1,-1\}\) be that
winding in the actual inverse-conjugator marking, and define
\(\chi_d=\epsilon_d\,\operatorname{wind}(f_d)\).
Thus \(\chi_d(d)=1\) and \(\chi_d(H)=0\), with no unrecorded
change to the orientation of d.

The other five generators of L lie in H. Hence
\(\chi_d\circ\varphi\) is exactly the free d-exponent homomorphism.
It follows that \(K=\ker(\chi_d|_L)\) and \(L/K\simeq\mathbb Z\).
The inclusion \(K\subseteq H\), already proved, and
\(\chi_d(H)=0\) now give \(K=L\cap H\). This does not identify
the smaller N with \(L\cap J_c\).

\emph{Actual compact images.}\par
Use the free profinite group on the same six actual words and its
continuous epimorphism onto their ambient closure \(\overline L\).
Its d-exponent kernel is the closed subgroup generated by the
integer-indexed strips. To see this directly, that closed subgroup
is d-normal, contains all non-d generators, and has procyclic
quotient; the d-exponent map and its section identify the quotient
with \(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\).
The closed normal closure of all deleted strip letters inside this
kernel is d-stable by continuity, hence is the full closed kernel
killing C. Thus the proof of (B.86.10) applies to closed H and \(J_c\).

The images of these compact free retraction kernels are precisely the
closures \(\overline K,\overline N\). The completed actual character
\(\chi_d\) gives
\[
 \overline K=\overline L\cap H
       =\ker(\chi_d|_{\overline L}),\qquad
 \overline L/\overline K\simeq\widehat{\mathbb Z},\qquad
 \overline N\subseteq J_c\cap\overline L .
\tag{B.86.12}
\]
Here H and \(J_c\) denote their actual compact source images.
No full induced topology on the abstract L, or embedding of the
generic free rank-six subgroup after node filling, is assumed.
