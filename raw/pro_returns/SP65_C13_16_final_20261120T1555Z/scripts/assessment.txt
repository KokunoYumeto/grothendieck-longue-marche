All 25 supplied scans were read in sequence against C and the 21 available H pages, then read again against the consolidated text in the same order. The scan remained the authority at both readings. No OCR, text-recognition service, external bibliography, or TeX compilation was used. The records preserve 180 distinct crop boxes; the images themselves are excluded from the delivery.

The clearest material is the uncancelled typescript, especially the bibliography on scan 028 and most of the reused sheets 041, 043 and 052. Their visible source spellings, accents, underlines and blanks are often easier to settle than a mathematically expected wording. The manuscript formulas are frequently legible, but very small indices, close baselines, and retraced letters require more caution. The handwritten notes on scans 017, 029 and 034 have especially crowded insertions. Pale reversed type through several sheets and the printed background on scan 042 complicate the visual field without supplying missing foreground letters.

The least secure readings are the small faithful-module insertion on 017; the flattened exponent on 018; parts of the cancelled drafts on 022 and 033; the final interlinear qualification on 029; the overwritten diagram and extra symbol on 034; densely hatched words and map labels on 043; and the overtyped letter passages on 044–050. The three repeated degree-shift symbols on 049 remain δ/d/j. On 050 the proposition capital, critical-dimension word, conjunction, and cancelled bottom-line traces retain explicit uncertainty. The corollary inequality on 051 remains <|≤. These limits are marked in the transcription and located in the doubts table; an enlarged image cannot recover a stroke hidden beneath an opaque overstrike.

Witness C preserved much more of the page structure and of the earlier cancelled states. Its doubts were useful pointers, but corrections also arose outside them: the live implication chain on 018, the i/j sequence on 025, the open frame on 025, the typed product sign on 033, letter overtyping, and small drawn marks. The repeated crossed typewriter parameter on 044–050 accounts for many changes from C's preferred ℓ reading. The two handwritten ℓ subscripts on 045 remain ℓ; the plain heading subscript on 048 remains 1.

Witness H sometimes supplied the visible reading where C was doubtful or preferred another one, including several typed χ forms and Analoguous on 050. It also omitted or lost numerous cancelled states, insertions, margin notes and drawings, used illegibility marks for readable ink, and altered indices or the placement of decorations. Examples are the missing pencil additions on 040, the omitted degree insertion on 051, and the repeated ζ readings there. Neither witness's agreement nor its greater certainty was used as evidence. H has no supplied text for 028, 041, 043 or 052; those pages have no H-collation rows.

The changes log records 446 distinct loci: 110 differ from C and 384 from H, with 48 differing from both. Confidence across these loci is high at 261, medium at 140 and low at 45. Pure certainty changes and macro equivalents are excluded from those counts. Of C's 211 live marks, 95 retain the first reading, 65 select a listed alternative, 6 have another reading, and 45 remain undecided. Some resolved live marks coexist with doubt about an earlier deleted character at the same place.

The 25 mathematical check events record only the local computations, explicit maps and proof steps actually checked. The notes identify broader assertions left as claims of the manuscript. The transcription preserves degree discrepancies, source slips, incomplete expressions, blank slots and the author's own questions. A second reading and structural validation improve the documentation but cannot establish perfect recovery of obscured ink.
