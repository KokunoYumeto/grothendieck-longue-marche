# MPL 106+107 — Notation and doubts

## Package notation table

This table is maintained during the single-reader transcription and will be checked against every scan before freezing. Distinctions in the ink take precedence over inferred mathematical consistency.

| Manuscript sign | LaTeX | Policy / observations |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary capital C, W | `C`, `W` | Do not turn these into calligraphic letters. |
| Cof, cof | `\mathrm{Cof}`, `\mathrm{cof}` | Capitalization follows the ink; ambiguous cases carry a doubt. |
| Weak equivalences W with e below | `W_e` | Distinct from plain W and from `W_{\mathrm{Cof}}`. |
| Tilde over x, y | `\tilde x`, `\tilde y` | Preserve separately from primes. |
| Prime | `'` | Exclamation-mark exponents will be written `^!` if encountered. |
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes | Guillemets only where visible. |
| Squiggle by an arrow | `\sim` as a separate arrow label | Do not infer an isomorphism arrowhead. |
| Empty-object sign | `\varnothing` | Check against phi in diagrams; mark uncertain instances. |
| Script-like Phi functor | `\Phi` | Provisional reading on 106/002. |
| Coproduct sign | `\amalg` | Distinguish from union / vee from the ink. |

## Additions during the first reading of cotes 106–107

| Written notation | LaTeX | Reading policy |
|---|---|---|
| Category of two-arrow chains, underlined Ch with subscript zero | `\underline{\operatorname{Ch}}_0(x,y)` | Preserve underline only where visible; the definition is revised in 106 scan 012. |
| Component symbol | `\pi_0` | Distinguish from the handwritten product sign. |
| Class W with a zero subscript | `W_0` | Introduced in 106 scan 012; do not replace by W. |
| Fl | `\operatorname{Fl}` | Preserve abbreviation. |
| Open V coproduct-like mark | `\vee` | Preserve its shape where distinct from the barred coproduct `\amalg`, including 106 scan 016. |
| Relative underlined Hom | `\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A`, `\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_B` | Underline is part of the written notation. |
| Tensor product | `\otimes` | Distinguish from a plain multiplication cross. |
| Primes in factorization diagrams | `'`, `''` | Overwritten primes are individually marked; no change merely to obtain expected indices. |

Additional abbreviation leads, to consolidate after the visual audit: `tt` (tout), `ttes` (toutes), `tjs` (toujours), `néc.` (nécessaire), `suffis` with raised ending (suffisant/suffisante), `can.` (canonique), `str.` (strict/strictement), `cocart` (cocartésien), `w.k` (weak, uncertain reading), `id` (identité), `comp.` (composante), `préc.` (précédent/précédemment), `iso` (isomorphisme). These are proposed expansions for the doubts record only, never for the transcription.
