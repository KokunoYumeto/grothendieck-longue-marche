If a tight box covers multiple mandatory categories, list combined kind and explain. Keep them in page order and choose coherent words/expressions rather than isolated unreadable marks.
For changes include every substantive disagreement against either witness and every resolved C doubt with its crop. Adding uncertainty alone is a doubt entry, not a change row. Record punctuation, accent, abbreviation, underlining and deletion/placement differences if they change what is transcribed. Do not mechanically turn differences.tsv markup noise into findings.
For marks collect every unsure and illegible occurrence in C (including nested/struck ones); exact strings and short contexts let root match to MARKS.tsv. If no visual resolution, keep the mark, decision undecided, honestly narrow alternatives where supported.
