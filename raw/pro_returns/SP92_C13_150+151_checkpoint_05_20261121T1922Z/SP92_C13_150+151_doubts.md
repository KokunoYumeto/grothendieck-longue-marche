## Legibility and witnesses

The ordinary running prose is usually readable, but the small revisions and the bottom and side edges are substantially less secure. Dense cancellations sometimes leave the original letter bodies visible; elsewhere their crossings merge several strokes and only a narrow alternative can be retained. An explicit uncertainty inside a cancellation means that not every proposed letter is recoverable. The cropped lower names and handwritten signatures on the two inverted letters are especially weak evidence: the visible tops of a likely surname are not enough to transcribe the complete name. Their formal signatures remain separate illegible items.

The least secure mathematical places are tiny overtraced indices, the flat/natural-like superscripts on scans150087 and150098, the crowded deletion in150084's margin, the clipped lower underlabel on151003, and the q/p underlabel on151005. Long continuation strokes, arrows without a definite chevron, and rules under limits are recorded as the strokes seen, rather than converted automatically to ellipses or standard colimits. A visible wrong index is preserved even when an explicitly recorded computation shows what would make the statement true.

Witness C is generally the fuller diplomatic witness: it already preserves many revisions, marginal formulas and unusual abbreviations that H omits or rewrites. That did not make an unmarked C reading safe. This pass found additional omissions, changed letters and numbers, expanded abbreviations, misplaced punctuation, and readings where C and H agree against the ink. Witness H is useful for independent word alternatives and sometimes restores a live word absent from C, but its prose bridging, added punctuation, omission of struck work and redistribution of diagram labels require particular care. Its editorial synopses and notes are excluded. H is absent for151004 and151006, which were independently read as complete typed letters.

The counts in the changes log describe documented places, including resolved C doubt marks, not measured error rates or a percentage of accurate text. A place can differ from both witnesses; one complicated formula can contain several separately documented changes. Uncertainty and the evidence for choosing between alternatives are retained explicitly so that a later reader can distinguish a clear stroke from a contextual preference.

The blind phase is documented separately, with immutable first readings and box lists, independently written second readings, a token comparison that ignores whitespace only, and a crop decision for every differing box. Its final totals and effects are recorded in progress.log and the per-page audit sections of the changes log.
