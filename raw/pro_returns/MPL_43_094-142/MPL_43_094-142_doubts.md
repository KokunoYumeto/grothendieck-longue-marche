## Legibility and limits

The mathematical sheets range from clear headings and carefully retraced formulas to very rapid cursive with words reduced to horizontal strokes. Scans 94–98, the canceled draft on 108, the canceled algebra on 129, and the crowded marginal discussions on 133/135 carry substantial reading uncertainty. Dense overwriting on 109/113/114/116 and small degree, index and arrow marks on 130/132/136/138/140 remain especially sensitive. The 133 infinitesimal-duality prose and the lower unfinished argument on 135 should be checked against their doubt marks before being quoted as precise statements.

The reused typed sheets are generally clearer, but their clipping, interlinear additions, erased words and unusual script characters still require caution. The last bibliography quotation on 105, portions canceled on 110/121, and the script alphabets on 124/141 are explicit limits. Several formulas and sentences are genuinely unfinished in the supplied images; their visible beginnings have been retained.

Every kept page has a separate fragment and an element inventory. All readings are attempts from the visible ink; neither the mathematical plausibility of a phrase nor later context was used to complete it. The inventory and second audit record coverage, not a guarantee that every difficult stroke has been interpreted correctly. Confidence in the table below concerns the proposed transcription only. Single proposals on 108/129/133/135 have been rated low after the final visual review, even where only one plausible attempt could be offered.

The source ellipses are retained, and physically blacked-out word groups use estimated word counts only after attempted readings. No formula symbol or diagram object is replaced by an illegible-word count. Drawing and arrow shapes are approximated in TikZ; very small hooked marks, especially the inner return in 136, remain explicitly doubtful.
