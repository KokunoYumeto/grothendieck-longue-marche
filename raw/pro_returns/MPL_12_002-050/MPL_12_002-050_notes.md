### The exact sequence over \(\mathbf Q\): three levels of assertion

With a compatible neutral fibre functor, write \(\Gamma_{\mathbf Q}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf Q}/\mathbf Q)\), viewed as a proconstant group scheme over the coefficient field. The fully faithful Artin subcategory described at **12/009** supplies, in the manuscript's proposed Tannakian setting,

\[
1\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow G_{\mathrm{mot}}(\mathbf Q)
\xrightarrow{\rho}\Gamma_{\mathbf Q}\longrightarrow1,
\qquad N=\ker\rho.
\]

The étale-descent assertion at **12/009–010**, together with passage through finite extensions and compatible base change, is the route to identifying \(N\) with the motivic group over \(\overline{\mathbf Q}\). The stronger assertion \(N=G_{\mathrm{mot}}(\mathbf Q)^0\) is equivalent to having no extra finite quotient over an algebraically closed base; **12/009** invokes Tate or Hodge for precisely this issue. Accordingly, replacing the kernel by the identity component without that qualification overstates these pages. This distinction also corrects the earlier cancelled unconditional isomorphism at **12/008**.

With a comparison to the \(\ell\)-adic fibre, **12/010 and 012** construct \(\varphi_\ell:\Gamma_{\mathbf Q}\to G_{\mathrm{mot}}(\mathbf Q)(\mathbf Q_\ell)\) and write \(\psi_\ell\varphi_\ell=1\). This is a section on the indicated topological groups of points, with its comparison data; it is not a displayed section of affine group schemes over \(\mathbf Q\), a rational point of a variety, or a solution of the anabelian section conjecture. The manuscript's “Tate (avec action semi-simple de Galois !)” criterion at **12/012** further identifies the Zariski closure in each algebraic quotient under its finite-type hypotheses. H5/H6 restate this programme; RS **MPL_15_087-138 §3**, **MPL_18_018-062 §3**, and **MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 B04** are comparison leads, not substitute proofs.

### A. The first categorical outline: 12/002–004

#### N001 — The correspondence construction is not given

**Witness:** 12/002, §§1–5; “par propriété universelle”. **Kind:** reading question.

The opening describes a semisimple rational category and a universal property, but does not choose an equivalence relation on correspondences and then perform the quotient and idempotent completion. CT 16/014–018 supplies a graded correspondence-ring model, which makes the comparison natural without completing that categorical identification. The absent construction is a substantive question, rather than a licence to insert the usual modern definition.

**Context relation:** T12 **adds** categorical requirements to CT 16/014–018; H1 **restates** the programme. **RS:** WG2 §2.4 #3; MPL_11_043-058+56 N031; MPL_15_087-138 §2; MPL_60_002-035 C01 (planning/first-pass leads). **Search:** `correspondance|universelle|pseudo.abélienne`.

#### N002 — Weight one and abelian varieties up to isogeny

**Witness:** 12/002, §4; “L'équivalence”; “Isab”, with an opposite-category superscript. **Kind:** unproved claim.

The asserted weight-one anti-equivalence isolates abelian varieties up to isogeny as the first concrete test of the category. It should be compared with actual Hom theorems with the base field fixed, not with an unrestricted reconstruction theorem for curves. The later Jacobian question at 12/049 passes beyond what this weight-one equivalence says.

**Context relation:** CT 44/007–009 concerns abelian duality; H1 **restates**, H16 **adds** the later reconstruction question. **RS:** MPL_14_002-044 M24a; MPL_15_087-138 §3; LM7 C.1 [Known claim, for the Fermat Picard 1-motive]. **Search:** `Isab|abéliennes|Jacobi`.

#### N003 — Algebraically closed base extension

**Witness:** 12/002, §4; “pleinement fidèle”. **Kind:** unproved claim.

Full faithfulness under extension of algebraically closed fields requires invariance of the chosen motivic Hom spaces, and therefore depends on the missing construction and equivalence relation. It does not follow merely from the existence of faithful cohomological realizations. The cancelled level-invariance remark on 12/003 is a separate, stronger compatibility to keep visible.

**Context relation:** H1 **restates** the assertion; CT 16/018 **differs** by working with a correspondence ring rather than this base-change functor. **RS:** MPL_14_002-044 M01 and MPL_15_087-138 L01 are category/effectivity leads, not proofs of this assertion. **Search:** `algébriquement clos|pleinement fidèle|extension`.

#### N004 — What is included in the universal property?

**Witness:** 12/002, §5; “pseudo-abélienne”; “Künneth”. **Kind:** unproved claim.

The proposed factorization through a pseudoabelian tensor target presupposes the stated cohomological compatibilities and an invertible Tate object. A proof must specify which relations on algebraic correspondences are forced by those conditions and whether all the claimed Hom information is then automatic. This is a question about the universal property's input, not an identification with Nori's different diagram-based universal property.

**Context relation:** CT 16/014–017 **adds** a universal ring model; H1 **restates**; [NA17] **differs** in its construction. **RS:** WG2 §2.3 #2; MPL_15_195-228+18_002-017 S15. **Search:** `universelle|Künneth|Nori`.

#### N005 — Purity by level and unique decomposition

**Witness:** 12/003, §6; “se décompose”; “motifs purs”. **Kind:** unproved claim.

The definition makes level depend on a Tate twist of an effective motive of bounded degree, then asserts a unique decomposition into pure pieces. Semisimplicity alone provides simple summands, but identifying and separating their levels needs the promised compatibility of weights, twists, and effectivity. This early use of “pur” and “niveau” should be distinguished from the later weight and dimension-type filtrations.

**Context relation:** H2 **restates**; H12–H14 **add** the later reorganized filtrations, 12/031–041. **RS:** MPL_14_002-044 M01; MPL_15_087-138 L01 (first-pass leads). **Search:** `niveau|pur|décompose`.
