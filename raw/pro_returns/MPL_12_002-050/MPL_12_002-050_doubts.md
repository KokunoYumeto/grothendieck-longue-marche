# Legibility and limits

All 47 retained pages were read in order from the whole-page image and both full-resolution halves, then read again against the saved fragments. No OCR was used. The two-stroke prose abbreviation, categorical versus ring M, reinforced coefficient letters, duality marks, primes and shrieks were reconciled against the ink before the transcription was frozen.

The ordinary running text is generally readable, but the densely revised outline (002–019), the rotated marginal additions, the cancelled box on 012, and the overwritten marginal formulas on 040–044 remain the least secure passages. Heavy cancellation occasionally destroys complete words; those losses are counted locally, while doubtful mathematical symbols always retain a candidate reading. A medium-confidence row means the proposed reading remains uncertain, not that the rest of its line has been certified infallible; low confidence marks especially weak, overwritten or fragmentary evidence.

The positions in the table refer to lines in the individual transcription fragment, so that a reading can be located reproducibly; page layout and marginal positions are also recorded in the inventory. The inventory is a coverage aid, not an independent proof that every reading is correct. The files are LaTeX fragments using the requested annotation macros; static syntax and package structure were checked, but a full typesetting pass was unavailable because the installed TeX executables lacked their format and package resources.
