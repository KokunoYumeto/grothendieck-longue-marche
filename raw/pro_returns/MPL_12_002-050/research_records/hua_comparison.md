# Hua comparison against the frozen cote 12 packet

## Scope, provenance, and evidence rules

Research comparison made after the transcription freeze announced at **19:23:39 UTC**. I read context/README.md, context/notes/RESULTS_STATE.md, context/hua_project/README.md, and **all 2,934 lines of context/hua_project/12.modern.tex**. I compared the modernised reading with frozen page fragments, especially scans 002–019 and 042–050. I did not reopen source scans or alter any frozen transcription, page fragment, or hash record.

The source is **Hua, modernised reading of cote 12**, with its exact subsection named at each use below. It is a modern interpretation from Michel Hua's project, not our transcription and not our result. Its editorial preamble explicitly permits changes of notation, supplied hypotheses, and mathematical correction of a statement judged false. Its modern mathematical claims and bibliographical references require their own checks. The results register likewise labels most related previous-package notes first-pass leads; the referenced packages themselves are not supplied here.

Hua's displayed “page” numbers are generally one less than the current scan number: his page 1 corresponds to **12/002**, and his page 49 to **12/050**. This is a crosswalk for the present range, not an assertion about all later scans. Even a subsection beginning within this packet may import later evidence: especially “11) Le coniveau (pages 41, 42 et 57)” and “12) L'homomorphisme … (pages 42 à 51).”

**Classification:** “supports” means agreement between the two supplied texts; “adds” means explanation or mathematics supplied by Hua; “difference” means the supplied texts differ; “reading question” is a candidate for future independently authorized source inspection, never a post-freeze change; “scope exclusion” means a later leaf cannot establish a claim about the present packet. Confidence separates documentary confidence from confidence in a mathematical inference.

## Principal conclusions

1. The component-group identification is conditional in the frozen text itself. The categorical map is an epimorphism; Tate, or Hodge in generic characteristic zero, is then invoked. The later ℓ-adic left inverse is a different statement and does not produce an unconditional algebraic splitting over \(\mathbb Q\).
2. The Tate-density statement explicitly includes **semisimplicity of the Galois action** and a **localization of a scheme of finite type over \(\mathbb Z\)**. The Hodge comparison concerns the tensor category generated by a motive, with polarizable Hodge structures already specified.
3. Neither the current packet nor the whole supplied Hua file states a period genericity/transcendence conjecture. Torsors and comparison functors are ingredients, not that conjecture. The register's cote 18/059 lead is a stronger locator.
4. Hua systematically repairs Tate-twist signs, degrees, parity, purity shifts, and primitive-index formulas. These are consistency checks, distinguishable from readings of the manuscript.
5. On birational faithfulness the current pages pose questions. **\(E\times E\)** at 12/050 is the manuscript's counterexample; the genus-two and complex Enriques examples are Hua's own additions. Larsen–Lunts concerns a related quotient, not literally the associated-graded isomorphism at 12/050.
6. Important documentary disagreements remain at 12/042–043: “infinis” versus an initial-term warning, projectivity versus smoothness in the margin, \(n-p\) versus \(n-2p\), and cancellation of the earlier dense-open birational box. None is adjudicated by reopening the ink.

### HC04 — Component group: epimorphism first, conjectural identification second

**Current witness:** 12/007–010, especially 009: “épimorphisme canonique”; “Utilisant conjecture de Tate (ou de Hodge …)”; “c'est un isomorphisme.” **Register:** LM6 A.22; MPL_11_043-058+56 N104; WG2 §2.4 #3. **Context search:** composantes, G/G, connexe, niveau et poids zéro, Tate, Hodge, Artin, procyclique.

\[
G\longrightarrow\pi_1(S,\xi),\qquad
\varprojlim_\alpha G_\alpha/G_\alpha^0\twoheadrightarrow\pi_1(S,\xi).
\]
The second map becomes an isomorphism after the conjectural characterization of finite-monodromy motives as level-and-weight-zero motives. For \(S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q\), this conditionally gives the direction
\[
1\longrightarrow G^0\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow1,
\]
with a proconstant last term and the chosen compatible fibre data.

**Difference:** Hua's footnote says the page immediately boxes an isomorphism. The frozen text already says **epimorphism** before the box. The cancelled isomorphism at 12/008 is an earlier layer. Thus Hua's footnote is itself a documentary disagreement, not a correction the frozen version requires. **Confidence:** high; no conclusion for LM6 A.22's procyclic-normalizer problem follows.
