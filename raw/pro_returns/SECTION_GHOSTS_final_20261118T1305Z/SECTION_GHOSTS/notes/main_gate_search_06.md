## Arithmetic presentations and relative Riemann existence

Gras and Jaulent, *Note on 2-rational fields* (2009), Theorem and Definition0, authorp2, and Theorem2 with its complete proof, authorp3, were reopened at https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.1168 . The theorem gives the free pro-two product Z2*(C2) for the maximal two-ramified complexified pro-two extension of Q. This confirms the arithmetic source used in the dyadic approach; the proof ends on authorp3, with references onp4. The earlier authorJ51 URL was intermittently unavailable. The existing bibliography key is LitGrasJaulent2009, so no duplicate entry is added.

Schmidt, *Rings of integers of type K(pi,1)* (2007), Proposition8.1 and Corollary8.2, printedpp467–468, were opened with their proofs at https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/26010?nt=1 . The result identifies a relative kernel with a free product of inertia groups when the stated K(pi,1), non-splitting and prime hypotheses hold. It is not a free presentation of the whole arithmetic group. The paper's standing restrictions, and the separate modified-site remark for real places, prevent an unqualified application to Q at two.

Schmidt, *On pro-p fundamental groups of marked arithmetic curves*, English author version16January2009, Theorem1.1 and Remarks1.2, authorp2, were opened at https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~schmidt/papers/marked.pdf . The published German version is in J. reine angew. Math.640(2010),203–235. The theorem adds auxiliary primes and realizes local pro-p extensions, but explicitly assumes p odd; Remark1.2 attributes the exclusion of two to the then unavailable mild pro-two theory. Its relative free-product assertion still retains the arithmetic quotient action.

The exact failed inference is: an abstract homomorphism from a free inertia kernel does not extend to a homomorphism on the arithmetic group unless it respects conjugation by the arithmetic quotient. For a tame generator tau_q and arithmetic Frobenius sigma_q, it must satisfy sigma_q tau_q sigma_q^-1=tau_q^q in the target. Those are the old-place constraints that remain in E.148/E.159. Passing to Q(i) removes real places for some source theorems but adds a descent condition; it does not eliminate these equations. The cohomology audit is reconstructing the exact dyadic presentation directly, including the shared Frobenius words for multiple support primes.

## Projective quotients with infinitely many possible ramification primes

The attempt was to replace the finite-S projective arithmetic quotient in P.4 by an infinite-ramification projective quotient, and then lift the ordinary abelian cocycle through the free geometric group. A.32 proves the obstruction to this attempt for every virtually pro-ell quotient retaining the cyclotomic character: nonzero tame inertia at q different from ell would generate, with a Frobenius subgroup, a closed Zell semidirect Zell subgroup with nonzero second cohomology. Such a quotient cannot have ell-cohomological dimension at most one. Hence this lifting mechanism produces no ordinary ghost even when finite ramification is not assumed. This eliminates one proposed method; it does not prove A.2 or A.3.

## Local ramification theorem: corroboration of an existing reduction

Hoshi, *Conditional results on the birational section conjecture over small number fields* (2014), July2012 author version, AppendixA, PropositionA.6 and proof, authorpp37–38, were opened at https://maths.dur.ac.uk/events/Meetings/LMS/2011/GRAF11/papers/hoshi.pdf . The proposition places nontrivial specialized inertia of a pro-Sigma section in cusp inertia at good reduction with residue characteristic outside Sigma. Its hypotheses were checked. Hoshi–Mochizuki (2011), Lemma1.6 and full proof, authorpp15–16, at https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Nodally%20Nondegenerate%20Outer%20Representations.pdf , give the peripheral-recognition input. The lemma uses all finite covers and compactness of their finite cusp sets.

For the tripod this gives a second route to A.31: after cusp recognition, the procyclic cusp normalizer and the nonzero abelian valuation force the same cyclic Kummer section class as the prescribed ordinary point. A.30–A.31 already prove this directly with Magnus series. Thus the newly opened sources corroborate an existing reduction; they do not narrow the main gate further. At a zero-support place both Frobenius complements are unramified, so this theorem says nothing about equality of the prescribed interior-point complement with a cusp complement. At the coefficient place it does not apply.

## Other all-depth proposals checked

The harmonic-point idea that odd-weight generators make the nonabelian Lang map surjective is invalid. Brackets of two odd-weight generators have even weight. The concrete class-two countermodel is the odd-prime Heisenberg group in coordinates(a,b,z), with automorphism(a,b,z) mapped to(-a,-b,z). Above zero abelian class every central z remains a distinct twisted-conjugacy class: a conjugator preserving zero abelian coordinates has a=b=0 and cannot change z. This prevents a degree argument on generators from proving an all-depth point/cusp collision. The actual finite-field cover tests are kept by the ghost audit in its dedicated notes and executed code.

A proposed compactness argument that allows the support primes themselves to move has no compact domain preserving infinite support. Fixing nonzero ordinary integer valuations at designated support primes would give closed conditions, but the fixed-depth constructions do not prove nonemptiness for every depth with those same valuations. Their sufficient divisibility conditions grow with depth. No interchange of these quantifiers is used.

Choosing each new prime by finite Chebotarev conditions only controls finitely many levels of its Frobenius. It does not put that prime's Frobenius in a designated procyclic subgroup of the full arithmetic source at all levels. Similarly, changing high q-adic digits of an already fixed ordinary local point cannot supply arbitrary corrections to its off-diagonal Kummer class: the unit ell-primary quotient of Q_q^times is finite when q differs from ell. No full old-place matching theorem follows from such digit changes.

## Current-source scope check

Holzschuh, Schmidt and Stix, *Anabelian geometry in families*, authorpreprint21August2026, TheoremA, p2, was opened through the current author publication page at https://www.math.uni-frankfurt.de/~stix/research/preprints/HOLZSCHUH-SCHMIDT-STIX-Anabfam20260821.pdf . It compares dominant morphisms with maps that are open on the full profinite fundamental group. A section of the arithmetic sequence has infinite index in the total group because the geometric group is infinite, and hence is not open. A pronilpotent section is additionally a map to a different quotient. Therefore this theorem does not decide the present section question. Only this stated scope is used; its general proof is not imported as a premise.


## The exact obstruction to untwisting by a global arithmetic splitting

E.185 gives the actual dyadic presentation, with one shared arithmetic generator and coupled local Frobenius words. E.186 strengthens the diagnosis: a splitting of G_Q(2) onto G_{Q,{2,infinity}}(2)=C2*Z2 that preserves an actual complex conjugation can identify an actual local Frobenius lift for at most one odd prime. A compatible prime must be3 or5mod8, and one such prime can be attained. The proof uses positive quadratic Kummer characters: their restrictions along the splitting are all multiples of kappa(2), so two compatible primes would have the same quadratic residue character on every other positive prime. CRT and factorization contradict this. Thus path terms cannot be discarded by choosing a different single real-preserving splitting. An equivariant map retaining the path terms remains possible; no all-depth nonexistence follows.

## Dimension-growth and source-automorphism proposals

A fixed finite-S unipotent dimension estimate does not apply directly to the unrestricted ordinary pair: its weight-one Kummer cocycle can ramify at infinitely many primes, although every finite coefficient reduction has finite ramification. Making the visible support grow slowly with coefficient depth supplies no uniform relation between coefficient precision and nilpotent class. No quantitative theorem was proved that bounds the precision needed to detect failure while preserving a nonzero frozen integer valuation. Thus a comparison of Lie dimension with the finite visible support alone does not decide the limit question.

Precomposing a rational section cocycle with an automorphism of the maximal pro-two arithmetic group would retain the actual action only if that automorphism preserves its entire Ihara representation. No such automorphism producing an infinitely supported ordinary pair has been constructed, and arbitrary automorphisms of a free arithmetic quotient do not suffice. This proposed construction therefore stops before an actual section, rather than providing another survivor.
