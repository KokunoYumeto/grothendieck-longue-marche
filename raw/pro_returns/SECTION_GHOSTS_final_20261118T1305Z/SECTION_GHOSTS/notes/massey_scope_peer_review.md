# Independent review of P14

Reviewed on 18 November 2026 by the literature agent. The substantive comparison is correct. This is a review of the cited theorems' scope and the elementary coefficient comparisons, not a new existence theorem for sections.

## Primary-source checks

Harpaz and Wittenberg's author version, revised 9 December 2021, was independently opened at <https://www.math.univ-paris13.fr/~wittenberg/globalmassey.pdf>. Section 7, Example 7.2 (p.25), Proposition 7.3 (pp.26–27), and Theorem 7.4 with its proof (pp.27–28) give exactly the relevant rank-one twisted result. Taking N_i=F_p(-i) makes Hom(N_j,N_i)=F_p(j-i). A defined product has at least one vanishing defining system; this does not require every already selected defining system to vanish. The kernel represented by the top off-diagonal entry is central in the unipotent subgroup, but generally only normal in the full triangular group with diagonal characters. P14's phrase “top central entry” should therefore be replaced by “top off-diagonal subgroup.” Opened refs: turn406902509view1, turn804155087view0, turn804155087view1, turn639529660view1.

Maire, Mináč, Ramakrishna, and Tân's published article was independently opened at <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-026-03518-1>. Definitions 1.1–1.3 and Theorems 1.1, 1.2, 3.2, and 4.2 were checked. The last theorem and its proof choose the residual representation's new ramified primes using the requested coefficient modulus. Part (i) lifts into GL; the unipotent conclusion in part (ii) has an additional local roots-of-unity hypothesis. The paragraph immediately following its proof expressly says that the construction does not pass to a projective-limit lift. Proposition 4.3 explains a necessary local restriction for a ramified adjacent entry. The odd-prime, missing-root, trivial-coefficient and finite-p-group hypotheses are retained correctly in P14. ## Independent comparisons

Over Q, a finite arithmetic target retaining the residual cyclotomic action has a quotient F_p^×. For odd p this quotient is not a p-group. Restricting to Q(ζ_p) removes this particular quotient but fails the missing-root hypothesis of the 2026 theorem. This is a mismatch of the quoted theorem's hypotheses, not a proof that a differently formulated twisted theorem is false.

For a fixed lower section, a higher-weight additive correction has inertia coefficient v in Z_p(w). At q≠p the tame Frobenius relation and the unramified cyclotomic action give q^w v=qv, hence (q^(w−1)−1)v=0. For w>1 the scalar is nonzero and Z_p(w) is torsion-free, so this correction has no such auxiliary inertia. The finite-modulus annihilator phenomenon therefore does not directly construct one compatible integral correction. This elementary check and the defining-system distinction justify P14's cautious conclusion.
