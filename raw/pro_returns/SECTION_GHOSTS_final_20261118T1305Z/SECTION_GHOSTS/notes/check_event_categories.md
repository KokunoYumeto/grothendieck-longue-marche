# Event categories

On 18 November 2026, `code/merge_checks.py` was adjusted to emit the six
categories requested in the prompt. The original source event tables remain
unchanged. A descriptive activity such as a complete peer review or an exact
source-locator check is retained verbatim at the beginning of its merged
outcome. Full peer reads are categorized as targeted proof checks, not silently
promoted to independent reconstructions. Compilation and syntax runs are
categorized as computations with an explicit statement that they do not
certify the mathematics. Scope-only background records retain that weaker
status. This is a reporting normalization, not a new proof event and not
qualifying progress under the stopping rule.
