## What was independently checked

1. For a continuous map P:G→T of compact profinite spaces and continuous bit Δ, the existence of one open normal V⊂T excluding P(Δ⁻¹(1)) is equivalent to Δ vanishing on P⁻¹(1). The proof uses the compact image being closed, and its converse is immediate. Thus A.4.1 is mathematically valid **conditional on exactly the A.7 vanishing assertion**. It cannot be used as an independent proof of that assertion. A cofinal family of finite tests on the same compact locus has the finite-intersection property when all finite joint tests pass. A noncofinal relative tower or different framing/order constraint does not enter that argument.

2. In A.4.39, the finite cusp-centralizer refinement via the actual free-cover graph is correct. For R0=C2*Z, a finite quotient Q retaining its cone involution, and K its kernel, K has rank1+|Q|/2. The mod2 homology class of d^ord(d modQ) has literal edge support <d modQ>. Its deck stabilizer is precisely that cyclic subgroup. Hence the centralizer at R0/K′K² projects onto exactly <d modQ>. The swapped-cusp and iterated versions follow. The subsequent coefficient extraction from kd^t h=v^s, in the retained index-two abelianization, correctly gives t=−4c mod2e and s=4c mode, assuming the geometric inner-equivalence input. The argument never assumes finite-level centralizers are equal to their infinite counterparts.

3. The missing ordinary two-forgetting geometry A.T19 is supported by an independent exact coordinate calculation. With H=<z↦−z,z↦1/z>, the orbit coordinate is u=z²+z⁻²; exceptional orbits lie over ∞,2,−2. The quartet {0,∞,z,1/z} yields cusps∞,2 and an order2 cone−2. Sending {1,−1,z,−z} to {0,∞,r,r⁻¹}, r=(z−1)/(z+1), gives r²+r⁻²=2(u+6)/(u−2), swapping cusps and preserving the cone. This checks the geometric cusp interchange, with basepoint inner ambiguity retained. It does not prove that the whole finite simultaneous centralizer is the image of the ordinary one, or produce the named finite fusion corrector.

4. Exact signed CM calculation: the two Cartans in SL2(F3) have orders6 and4; there are six pairs in each of the four parity classes. Their full GL2(F3) normalizers have orders12 and16 and preserve the indicated bits. The signed pure mod4 image is V4 and omits −I; its centralizer of R0 is trivial. The code checks all twelve shear covariances and all144 cases of the common-exponent equation, including both integral comparison orientations. Exactly72 cases solve it, precisely the equal-bit cases. The T=5I,W=I witness never solves it. This supports A.C16⇒A.C17 and identifies the actual remaining premise: the **one common conjugator** furnishing A.C14 (or its finite compiler counterpart).

5. The CM division fields are directly re-proved. For r⁴=3/4, K=Q(i,r), E3:y²=x³+1, the point (−1+√3,√3(√3−1)r) doubles to (−1,0). It and its ζ3-multiple generate E3[4], while the Weil pairing and the same coordinates recover i,√3,r. For E4:y²=x³−x the roots of its third-division polynomial are ±a,±b, with a=(r⁻¹+2r)/2 and b=(r⁻¹−2r)/(2i); conversely √3=2/(a²−1), i=√3ab and r=(a+ib)⁻¹. Thus Q(E3[4])=K=Q(x(E4[3])). The exact quartic-to-cubic birational substitutions in A.C6 also pass an independent symbolic check. These arithmetic identities do not alone identify arbitrary abstract GT frames.
