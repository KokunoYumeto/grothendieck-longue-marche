## Stix finite geometric support

APA: Stix, J. (2015). On the birational section conjecture with local conditions. *Inventiones mathematicae, 199*(1), 239–265. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-014-0514-3.

Publisher primary metadata: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-014-0514-3 (web ref turn115110157view1). Online publication 8 April 2014; journal year 2015. Author record https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3236 (turn608283266view0) identifies v3, 17 September 2015, as the journal version. Latest 16-page PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.3236 (turn777831113view0/2) has Corollary 5 on author p.5, proof pp.5–6; Section2.2.1 pp.3–4 defines Selmer, Section3 p.5 defines finite geometric support. Its automatic title-page date 1 November2018 is not the arXiv version date. Earlier author PDF https://www.math.uni-frankfurt.de/~stix/research/preprints/STIX-birSCoverQthmAB20140227.pdf is dated27February2014 and has the same Cor5 pagination. No typeset-journal page for Cor5 is asserted.

Cor5 concerns full profinite Selmer sections of any hyperbolic curve over any number field. Noncuspidal plus finite geometric support implies rational-point section. Neither birational lift nor finite ramification is an additional hypothesis. This does not apply to a pronilpotent section or merely abelian local ordinarity. For a full locally ordinary section with rational pair(t,1−t), local Kummer injectivity gives xp=t at finite places, real signs give its component, and support is{t}. Finite geometric support is not a finite set of valuation primes.

Their infinitely ramified finite-coefficient-field representations can have integral pure Frobenius polynomials at all but finitely many unramified primes and be potentially semistable at the coefficient prime. Two-prime density-one compatibility is also available, but equality of ramified Weil–Deligne parameters is not claimed. This refutes the abstract purity shortcut, not the section problem. In the proposed section application, zero-support abelian ordinarity does not identify the full unramified local section with an interior point; hence fibrewise Weil purity is not supplied. Coefficient-prime de Rham and cross-coefficient compatibility are also unproved. E218's family changes with the coefficient prime.
