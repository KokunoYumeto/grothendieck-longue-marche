\subsection{The actual faithful homology map of the compact detector}

\begin{result}{E.207}{Proved}
{The missing quaternion homology certificate is reconstructed exactly}
Retain the literal groups $\Sigma,N_8,T,L_c,R_c,P_c$ of E.203.
Put
\[
 A=T/(T'T^3)=H_1(T,\mathbb F_3),\qquad
 H=(\mathbb Z/3)^2\subseteq\Sigma/T=Q_8\times H.
\]
Let $z=(-1,0)$ be the unique involution in $\Sigma/T$, and
write $A=A^+\oplus A^-$ for its eigenspaces.  The actual
surface-word map induces an equivariant linear map
\[
 \Theta:A\longrightarrow N_8/L_c,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}\Theta=12,\qquad
 \dim A^+=37,\quad\dim A^-=36.                                  \tag{E.207.1}
\]
The image is an abelian group of exponent three.  If
$\mathfrak m_H$ is the augmentation ideal of $\mathbb F_3[H]$,
then the exact kernel is
\[
                 \ker\Theta=A^+\oplus\mathfrak m_H^2A^-.
                                                                    \tag{E.207.2}
\]
The second summand has dimension twenty-four.  On a specified
primitive eighteen-dimensional quaternion multiplicity space
$M\subset A^-$, the kernel is exactly $\mathfrak m_H^2M$,
of dimension twelve, and the image has dimension six.
Every linear normalizer of $H$ on this module preserves that
kernel.

All thirty-six actual peripheral lifts have zero $\Theta$ image;
their homology classes span a thirty-five-dimensional space.
There is also the actual characteristic common source
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 K^- =\ker(T\longrightarrow A/A^+),\qquad
 E^- =\Sigma/(K^-\cap\Sigma'\Sigma^{12}),\\
 |E^-|=288\cdot3^{36},\qquad E^-\twoheadrightarrow P_c,
 \end{gathered}                                                   \tag{E.207.3}
\]
where the epimorphism is induced by the identity of $\Sigma$.
This independently confirms the faithful-homology,
augmentation-kernel and characteristic-source clauses of
historical GT A.4.18.  Together with E.203 it reconstructs that
finite detector from its definitions.  It does not prove the
unavailable numerical BCV/HCF/KFU compiler bounds of A.4.57.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
We retain the actual based words throughout.  The map
$\Sigma\to Q_8\times(\mathbb Z/3)^2$ is surjective: its image
projects onto each factor, whose orders are coprime.  Thus
$[\Sigma:T]=72$.  The Cayley cover has $144$ positive edges
and $72$ vertices; its free fundamental group has rank
$144-72+1=73$.

The independent program
\path{code/check_compact_quaternion_homology.py} constructs
this cover directly, using the same breadth-first letter order
$a,b,a^{-1},b^{-1}$ as E.203.  Every non-tree positive edge
gives its literal Schreier word.  Rewriting each such word in
the nine-generator quaternion basis and applying the
$14\times45$ quotient map of E.203 gives a specified
$14\times73$ matrix $\Theta$.  Its rank is twelve.  The
certificate checks every bracket between its seventy-three
generator images; all are zero.  The target group has exponent
three, so this proves that the actual group map factors through
$T/(T'T^3)$.  It is not a linear map introduced only by a
dimension comparison.

Conjugation on $A$ by a representative of $\Sigma/T$ is
independent of that representative, since inner $T$ actions
are trivial on its homology.  We use the following actual lifts:
\[
 z:a^6,\qquad i:a^9,\qquad j:b^9,\qquad
 h_1:a^4,\qquad h_2:b^4.                                        \tag{E.207.4}
\]
Their labels in $Q_8\times H$ are respectively
$(-1,0),(i,0),(j,0),(1,(1,0)),(1,(0,1))$.
The code rewrites their conjugates of all seventy-three
Schreier words and retains the five resulting $73\times73$
matrices.  It checks the quaternion relations, both order-three
relations, commutation of the two translation matrices, and
commutation of the translations with the quaternion action.
It also rewrites the four surface substitutions of E.203
and verifies the four literal intertwining equations
\[
                   \Theta D_\gamma=M_\gamma\Theta
 \quad\bigl(\gamma=u,l,\operatorname{Ad}(a),
                         \operatorname{Ad}(b)\bigr),             \tag{E.207.5}
\]
where the right matrices are exactly the previously reconstructed
$14\times14$ quotient matrices.  In particular all the
deck equivariances used below are consequences of actual
surface-word maps.

\paragraph{The exact kernel.}
Over $\mathbb F_3$ the two projectors are
$p_\pm=2(I\pm D_z)$.  The program checks their idempotence
and obtains ranks thirty-seven and thirty-six.  It retains
actual column bases $C_+$ and $C_-$, and verifies
\[
                   \Theta(I+D_z)=0,\qquad
                   \Theta p_-=\Theta.                            \tag{E.207.6}
\]
Thus $A^+$ is killed and the whole image is faithful for the
central quaternion involution.

Put $X=D_{h_1}-I$ and $Y=D_{h_2}-I$.  The two verified
commuting order-three actions give
\[
 \mathbb F_3[H]=\mathbb F_3[X,Y]/(X^3,Y^3),\qquad
 \mathfrak m_H^2=(X^2,XY,Y^2).
\]
The exact image of that ideal on $A^-$ is therefore the
column space of
\[
               \mathcal J=[X^2C_-\ \ XY C_-\ \ Y^2C_-].          \tag{E.207.7}
\]
The independently reconstructed matrices give
\[
 \Theta\mathcal J=0,\qquad \operatorname{rank}\mathcal J=24,
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}(\Theta C_-)=12.
\]
Inclusion in the kernel and these ranks prove equality on
the thirty-six-dimensional space $A^-$.  With (E.207.6),
this proves both inclusions of (E.207.2).

Here is a literal choice of the multiplicity space.  On $A^-$
the quaternion matrices satisfy
$D_i^2=D_j^2=-I$ and $D_iD_j=-D_jD_i$.  Hence
$(D_i+D_j)^2=I$ and
\[
                   e=2(I+D_i+D_j)p_-                            \tag{E.207.8}
\]
is an idempotent.  It is a primitive idempotent in the
faithful quaternion block.  One can see this without any
character convention: that block is the full matrix algebra
generated by
\[
 i\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 j\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}
       \quad\text{over }\mathbb F_3,
\]
and (E.207.8) has rank one in that two-dimensional model.
The two matrices and their product, together with the identity,
are linearly independent.  Thus they identify the
four-dimensional block with $M_2(\mathbb F_3)$.

The certificate gives a column basis $C_M$ of $M=eA$ with
eighteen columns.  Since $e$ commutes with $H$, the exact
augmentation-square image is
$[X^2C_M\ \ XY C_M\ \ Y^2C_M]$.  Its rank is twelve,
it is killed by $\Theta$, and
$\operatorname{rank}(\Theta C_M)=6$.  This proves the
asserted kernel and quotient on $M$.  If $g$ normalizes $H$
as a linear group on $M$, then
\[
 g(h-1)(k-1)v=(ghg^{-1}-1)(gkg^{-1}-1)gv.
\]
Applying this equality also to $g^{-1}$ proves invariance
with equality of $\mathfrak m_H^2M$.  It does not identify
such a linear normalizer with a geometric one.

\paragraph{The peripheral and characteristic-source clauses.}
The word $c$ has label $(-1,0)$ of order two in $\Sigma/T$.
Thus the seventy-two cosets split into thirty-six pairs
under multiplication by that label.  Choose one actual
Schreier transversal word from each pair.  The words
$w c^2w^{-1}$ are precisely the thirty-six peripheral
lifts.  The code retains all thirty-six words, rewrites
their homology vectors, and verifies rank thirty-five
and zero image under $\Theta$.  These are the boundary
loops of the actual compactification, with the original
commutator marking.

The group $T$ is characteristic in $\Sigma$.  The involution
$z$ is the unique element of order two in $\Sigma/T$, so
every automorphism preserves it.  Its action on
$T/(T'T^3)$ is independent of its chosen lift, and its
positive eigenspace is consequently preserved.  This
proves characteristicness of $K^-$ and of the final
intersection in (E.207.3), also in the full profinite
free group.  The quotient $T/K^-$ has order $3^{36}$,
so $|\Sigma/K^-|=72\cdot3^{36}$.

Its exponent lattice is exactly $6\mathbb Z^2$.
Containment follows from $K^-\subseteq T$.  For the
reverse containment use
$w_a=a^6c$ and $w_b=b^6c$, both in $T$.
For either word $w$ and any lift $\widetilde z$ of $z$,
the elements $w\widetilde z w\widetilde z^{-1}$ and
$w^3$ lie in $K^-$.  The first has homology in the
positive eigenspace, while the second dies modulo three.
Their exponent vectors are twice and three times that
of $w$; an integer combination gives the vector of $w$.
This yields $(6,0)$ and $(0,6)$.  The abelian modulo-twelve
join therefore adds exactly four, proving the order in
(E.207.3).

Finally (E.207.6) shows $K^-\subseteq L_c$: the actual
map on $T$ factors through $A$ and kills $A^+$.
Intersecting with the abelian modulo-twelve kernel gives
the displayed epimorphism to $P_c$.  This proves an
identity-induced source map, rather than merely matching
the orders of two finite groups.
