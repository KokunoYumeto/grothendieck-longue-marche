# Checkpoint 10: D/E research progress

D.70–D.73 advance the original section questions on the actual Fermat curves and on the specified once-punctured CM elliptic curve, rather than only on abstract free-group models.

- **D.70** proves generic singleton full pro-prime cusp fibres over every number field for curves with at least three geometric cusps and a rational chosen cusp. It includes every odd Fermat curve and every coefficient prime, with the actual covering map and the sign term at two retained. Its full profinite consequence uses the already proved primewise comparison of two actual sections.
- **D.71** reaches exactly the requested curve over Q(i). The cusp packet is invisible in abelianization but is injected into class two; generically its full pro-prime and full profinite class-two fibre is a singleton. The proof handles the nonprimitive commutator boundary by an integral centralizer argument, including at two. For a once-punctured elliptic curve it also computes the entire zero-abelian class-two fibre as the cusp packet.
- **D.72** proves compactness of every exact nilpotent fibre after fixing the actual class-two cocycle, with unrestricted ramification. Separate liftability at every nilpotent stage therefore implies a compatible actual pro-prime lift. The actual-coboundary polynomial argument does not assume that global second cohomology is separated. Applied to D.70/D.71, it gives a genuine finite nilpotent obstruction for every other finite-stage class, uniformly away from the cusp class.
- **D.73** closes the one- and two-cusp gap in generic class-two rigidity. For two cusps the linear map to the exterior square of proper homology retains the surface bivector while killing conjugator corrections; passing to the proper class-two fundamental group would incorrectly lose that information. The resulting theorem applies to every affine hyperbolic curve with a rational chosen cusp.

All four statements received full independent proof audits before integration. D.72 and D.73 additionally received a third audit. The checkpoint-ten visual review actually inspected all fifteen assigned final pages and found no layout defect; the exact pages and hashes are in `notes/de_checkpoint10_visual_review.md`.

The remaining barriers are explicit and unchanged. These generic results do not classify every completed tangent parameter, every Alexander-module fibre, or arbitrary full profinite section classes. The first obstructed finite **derived** length and an explicit obstruction cocycle for the nonzero D.20 Fermat families remain unknown; D.72's nilpotent compactness does not answer that mixed-prime question. General arbitrary-twist first-cohomology ranks, the retained compact terms in global second cohomology, and the abstract nonseparated-kernel type for infinite ramification also remain open. The full higher nonmetabelian reading of D.6 and the general relative-pro-five/cubic-twist assertions of D.9 are not relabeled as solved.

The next D.5 attack is an integral operator-ring argument for finite ramification. The finite arithmetic presentation has positive deficiency, while its actual Alexander operators lie in a three-dimensional solvable pro-five Iwasawa ring. Ore clearing may produce integral cocycle families without assuming that growing rational ranks survive an inverse limit. The outstanding proof checks are faithful action, the absence of finite-rank nonzero operators, and descent through the residual C4 action. This remains notes-only until those bridges and the exact primary ring-theoretic inputs are verified.
