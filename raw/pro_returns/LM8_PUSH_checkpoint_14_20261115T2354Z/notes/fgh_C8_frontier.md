## An actual cyclic-four factorization over the original elliptic curve

For the minus-six member there is the explicit map

    x=(w^8−1+v)/2,  y=w^4(x−1)

to E:y²=x³−x, with exact Kummer equation w⁴=y/(x−1). It has degree4. The function y/(x−1) has divisor

    (0,0)+(−1,0)−(1,0)−O,

so its four ramification points are exactly the four cusps. Composing this map with [2] gives

    x([2]P)=(w^8−1)²/(4w^8),
    y([2]P)=v(w^16−1)/(8w^12),

which is exactly the previously displayed two-isogeny cover after its explicit scaling to E. The plus-six member is obtained after adjoining a sixteenth root of unity and scaling w.
