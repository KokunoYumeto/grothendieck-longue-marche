Proposition 2.4(vii) says that for a closed H inside G_K, with H intersection I_K admitting a continuous surjection onto Z_l for some l in Sigma distinct from residue characteristic p, a section H to the geometrically pro-Sigma arithmetic group fixes at most one primitive p-valuation. The open-image hypothesis on the l-adic cyclotomic character appears only in the additional existence clause. Thus it does not obstruct use of a subgroup of an abelianization kernel when a fixed valuation is already supplied by a rational point or cuspidal section.

Its uniqueness proof reduces via RNS to stable center chains, invokes Hoshi–Mochizuki *Topics surrounding the combinatorial anabelian geometry of hyperbolic curves IV: Discreteness and sections*, Corollary 1.15(iv), and then Proposition 2.3(ix). The latter says distinct primitive chains have infinite distance. Corollary 1.15(iv) was opened independently at the author PDF, printed pp.37–41; its global hypothesis asks for open image of H intersection I_K in pro-Sigma-dagger inertia. Cyclotomic fullness is imposed in (iii) for existence, not in (iv) for bounded distance. Taking Sigma-dagger={l} gives precisely the use in MT.

For a decomposition group D_v=G_{K_v} inside a number-field group G_K, its closed commutator is contained in D_v intersection G_{K^ab}. In the tame local quotient T semidirect hat(Z), the image of these commutators contains (q−1)T, because Frobenius acts on tame inertia T by q. Every l-component l different from p is therefore nonzero and open; projection supplies the required surjection onto an abstract Z_l. The same holds after replacing this subgroup by any open subgroup.

The composite valuation at a chosen lift of a point or cusp is primitive: if A is the horizontal valuation ring, with residue algebraic closure of K_v, let R be the inverse image of its valuation ring. The horizontal maximal ideal is p-divisible and lies in pR. Every prime of R containing p therefore contains that horizontal ideal; the quotient has rank one with radical of (p) equal to its maximal ideal. Also R[1/p]=A, so a geometric automorphism stabilizing R stabilizes the horizontal point. Ordinary-point geometric stabilizer is 1 and cuspidal geometric stabilizer is inertia. This verifies the critical reduction in the supplied ANABELIAN C.7h proof for J=D_v intersection G_{K^ab}, not just the larger cyclotomic kernel.
