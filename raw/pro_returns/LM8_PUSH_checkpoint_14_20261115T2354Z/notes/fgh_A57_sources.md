# A.57 exact primary source checks

This is a separate candidate from the unchanged, fully audited A.56.

## Raynaud

Michel Raynaud, *p-groupes et réduction semi-stable des courbes*, original printed pages 179--180. Publisher preview: <https://page-one.springer.com/pdf/preview/10.1007/978-0-8176-4576-2_7>.

Verified directly from both page images and text, refs `turn902view0`, `turn903view0`, `turn903view1`, `turn903view2`. Theorem 1 gives potential good Jacobian reduction, a tree dual graph, and strictly interior crystalline slopes for every component contracted to a point of the good base. Proposition 1(i) makes the inverse image of a geometrically unibranch branch divisor geometrically unibranch. Part (ii) gives constant inertia on a component and the inertia quotient étale on a neighborhood of its image; the induced component quotient is finite étale of group D/I. The candidate proves rather than assumes that there is one special component and that this neighborhood covers the entire special fibre, so the quotient is globally étale.

## Crew

Richard M. Crew, *Étale p-covers in characteristic p*, Compositio Math.52(1984), 31--45. Original primary PDF: <https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1984__52_1_31_0.pdf>.

Verified through `turn901view0` and `turn902view1`; original page image `turn902view2` is printed page 36. Corollary 1.8 and its unramified specialization (1.8.2) give the exact p-rank formula. The proof uses only the unramified p-group case. It does not apply an ordinary-cover assertion to a ramified quotient or assume that the whole cover has good curve reduction.
