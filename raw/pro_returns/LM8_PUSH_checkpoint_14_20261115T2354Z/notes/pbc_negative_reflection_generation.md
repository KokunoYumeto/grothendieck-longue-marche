# Negative-involution follow-up: the exact reflection-generation obstacle

Research note, 2026-11-15 around20:45 UTC. **No new lifting or conjugacy
theorem is claimed.** This records a concrete attempted route for the
remaining B4/B5 involutive-lifting question while the C31 replay runs.

Suppose an actual full normalized negative peripheral involution alpha
exists, with `alpha(x)=x^-1` and `alpha(y)=f^-1 y^-1 f`, where `f` is in
the full commutator subgroup. Squaring its y-action gives conjugation by
`f alpha(f)`. The primitive centralizer of y and its trivial intersection
with the commutator subgroup therefore give the exact identity
`f alpha(f)=1`.

In `E=F semidirect <t>` for this involution, all three elements
`t`, `x t`, and `y f t` have order two. Put
`K=closure(<x,y f>)`. Then alpha inverts both displayed generators of K,
and the subgroup generated by those three reflections is exactly
`K semidirect <t>`. There is a canonical epimorphism from the free
profinite product of three copies of C2 onto this subgroup.

The generators x and y f have the ordinary abelian basis. Consequently
their images generate every free pro-p quotient of F, by the pro-p
Frattini criterion. This does **not** prove `K=F`: a full profinite
generation assertion cannot be inferred from all separate pro-p images.
If `K=F` were independently proved, restriction from the index-two free
subgroup of `C2 coproduct C2 coproduct C2` would be an epimorphism between
two free profinite groups of rank two, hence an isomorphism. Even that
would initially give conjugacy in the entire automorphism group, not in
the pure peripheral subgroup: the third peripheral class still needs a
separate comparison.

For the proposed metabelian residue tau0, the outstanding step is thus
genuine full reflection generation together with preservation of the
third peripheral marking. The pro-p virtually-free structure theorems do
not supply it. The previously opened full-profinite counterexample and
the source-scope distinction are already recorded in `notes/pbc.md`
(Herfort–Zalesskii, arXiv0712.4244, Lemma5.1; and the separately scoped
Wykowski recognition theorem). A fresh literature search did not find a
primary theorem eliminating this obstacle. Neither a full involutive
lift of tau0 nor a finite obstruction to it is proved here.
