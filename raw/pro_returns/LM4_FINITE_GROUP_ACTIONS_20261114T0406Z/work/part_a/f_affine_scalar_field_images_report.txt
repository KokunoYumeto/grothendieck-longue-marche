F.25: equality of the actual affine images with a common scalar field
===================================================================

Completed assertion
-------------------
For either actual inclusion mathcal K <= R <= V, take any continuous
rho:V -> Aff_1(k), where k is any finite field of characteristic p.
Write M=rho(mathcal K), N=rho(R), D=chi(V), C=chi(R), and identify the
translation intersection M intersect T_k with an additive subgroup U
of k. Put k0=F_p(D) and k1=F_p(C).

The proof establishes that U is always k1-linear. It proves M=N for
the exact class in which U is k0-linear. Therefore M=N whenever C=1
or k0=k1. In particular it proves the full equality for every map
V -> Aff_1(F_p), for every prime p. There is no translation-intersection
restriction in that prime-field corollary. Characteristics two and
three are included.

Exact constructions in the proof
--------------------------------
The actual map D+ -> R from F22 transports F21 normal generation;
then rho|R and the scalar projection give normal_N(M)=N and chi(K)=C.
For C=1, N is abelian and the equality follows immediately.

For C nontrivial, conjugation by an actual element (b,c) of M sends
the translation u to cu. This proves C-stability and k1-linearity of U.
The proof gives the elementary finite-field argument that C is cyclic.
Choose its generator c and an actual lift m=(b,c) in M. The point
z=b/(1-c) is fixed by m. Conjugation by the explicitly specified
translation tau_z puts m at (0,c), leaves U literally unchanged, and
gives the exact group M_z=U semidirect C with both coordinate maps.
This constructs the complement used in the argument inside the actual
marginal image.

When U is k0-linear, every k0-linear map ell:k -> k0 vanishing on U
makes ell(d_z) an actual continuous chi-crossed homomorphism, since
chi(V) lies in k0. It vanishes on mathcal K. F24 injectivity over k0
therefore makes it zero on V. Extending a basis of U shows that the
intersection of the kernels of these exact coefficient maps is U.
Hence d_z(V) lies in U, and N_z lies in U semidirect C=M_z. The
reverse inclusion is the original subgroup inclusion. The inverse
translation conjugation gives M=N.

The field equality k1=k0 supplies the needed k0-linearity by the
already proved C-stability. This is the exact transfer used for
the stated scalar-field class; it is not presumed for other images.
For a prime field both generated subfields equal F_p.
