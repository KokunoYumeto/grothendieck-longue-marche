PROVED TRACE REDUCTION FOR THE ACTUAL AFFINE MARGINAL PROBLEM
===========================================================

Status and scope
----------------
This is a separate proved research subclaim, retained for root review.
It leaves the F24/F25 sources and their manifests unchanged. It proves
an exact reduction and an exact coefficient comparison. It does not
prove the remaining trace-kernel equality or any new full marginal
surjectivity assertion.

Throughout, choose either of the actual inclusions
  K = mathcal K_sign <= R = R_sign <= V = V_sign
from F19--F25. The exact maps D+ -> R carry K+ onto K. F21 therefore
gives closed normal generation of R by K. All representations and
crossed homomorphisms below are continuous; finite fields and finite
groups have their discrete topologies. In particular the scalar
images of K and R agree under every character of V.

Proved subclaim: every failure has a full-translation trace realization
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Let k be a finite field of characteristic p and rho:V -> Aff_1(k) an
actual representation with rho(K) properly contained in rho(R). Put
  H=rho(V), N=rho(R), M=rho(K), D=chi(V), C=chi(R)=chi(K),
  k0=F_p(D), k1=F_p(C), U=M intersect (k,+).
Then C is nontrivial: when C=1 the group N is abelian and actual
normal generation gives M=N. F25 also gives k1 properly contained in
k0 for every remaining failure, and U cannot be k0-linear.

Choose a generator c of the finite cyclic group C and an actual lift
m=(b,c) in M. Conjugate the entire representation by the inverse of
the translation tau_z(w)=w+z, where z=b/(1-c), exactly as in F25.7.
The conjugated marginal is literally M_z=U semidirect C; the element
m becomes (0,c). This conjugation fixes every translation, hence
does not change U. Let A_H and A_N be the additive translation
subgroups of H_z and N_z.

The group N_z is normal in H_z because R is normal in V. Every scalar
d in D is the scalar of an actual element of H_z. Its conjugation
sends a translation a to da, and preserves both A_H and A_N.
Both are therefore F_p[D]=k0 vector subspaces. The equality of that
finite ring with the field k0 is the elementary finite-domain
argument written in F25.

For a in A_H the literal commutator with (0,c) is
  [(a,1),(0,c)] = ((1-c)a,1).
It lies in N_z, by its normality, so (1-c)A_H is contained in A_N.
The nonzero scalar 1-c has inverse in k0, and A_N is k0-linear.
Thus A_H=A_N; call their common value A.

For every h=(b,d) in H_z, the same exact multiplication gives
  [h,(0,c)] = ((1-c)b,1) in N_z intersect (k,+)=A.
Consequently b is in A. Since H_z contains all translations A and
has scalar image D, it contains (0,d) for every d in D, obtained by
multiplying an actual lift (b,d) by (-b,1). Therefore
  H_z=A semidirect D,  N_z=A semidirect C,  M_z=U semidirect C.
The last two are unequal precisely because U is a proper k1-linear
subspace of the nonzero k0-vector space A.

Choose a k1-linear functional L:k -> k1 which vanishes on U and is
nonzero on A. To construct it, choose a in A outside U, extend a
k1-basis of U by a and then to a basis of k, send a to1, and send
every other basis vector to0. This is an actual specified linear
map after the named finite basis choices.

We next prove and apply the exact factorization
  L = Tr_(k0/k1) composed with ell,
where ell:k -> k0 is k0-linear.

Write q1=|k1| and m=[k0:k1]. The finite-field trace is the literal
polynomial
  Tr(x)=x+x^q1+...+x^(q1^(m-1)).
Its values lie in k1 since its q1th power is the same sum, using
x^(q1^m)=x. It is k1-linear. It is not the zero map: its polynomial
has nonzero leading coefficient and degree q1^(m-1)<|k0|, so cannot
vanish at every element of k0. Hence it is onto k1. Choose beta in
k0 with Tr(beta)=1. For every a nonzero in k0,
  Tr(a*(beta/a))=1.
Thus the pairing (a,b) -> Tr(ab) is nondegenerate. The resulting
k1-linear map
  k0 -> Hom_(k1)(k0,k1),  a -> (b -> Tr(ab))
is injective and both sides have k1-dimension m, so it is an
isomorphism. This proves the trace-pairing assertion in every
characteristic, including when p divides m.

Choose a k0-basis v1,...,vr of k. For each i, the actual functional
Li:k0 -> k1, t -> L(t*vi), has a unique coefficient ai in k0 with
Li(t)=Tr(ai*t). Define
  ell(sum ti*vi)=sum ai*ti.
This is k0-linear and satisfies L=Tr composed with ell by the
displayed basis expansion. Since L is nonzero on A, ell(A) is a
nonzero k0-subspace of k0, hence ell(A)=k0. Also
  ell(U) is contained in ker Tr,
and this trace kernel is a proper k1-subspace of k0.

The exact map of affine groups is
  P_ell:k semidirect D -> k0 semidirect D,
  (b,d) -> (ell(b),d).
It is a group homomorphism because ell(d*b)=d*ell(b) for d in D.
Its targets and sources are finite, so it is continuous. Compose
the original representation with the stated translation conjugation
and with P_ell. The resulting actual representation rho' satisfies
  rho'(V)=k0 semidirect D,
  rho'(R)=k0 semidirect C,
  rho'(K)=ell(U) semidirect C <= (ker Tr) semidirect C.
The last subgroup is proper in rho'(R), because Tr is onto. All
scalar values are unchanged by these maps, so k0=F_p(chi(V)) and
k1=F_p(chi(R)) still hold literally.

This proves the asserted reduction to a translation field of scalar
dimension one, with the marginal translations lying in one explicit
trace kernel. Every map and every finite-basis choice has been given.

Proved subclaim: the exact remaining trace-cocycle equation
----------------------------------------------------------
Fix a finite field k0, a continuous chi:V -> k0^* such that
k0=F_p(chi(V)), and C=chi(R) nontrivial. Put k1=F_p(C) and
m=[k0:k1]. These data specify an actual k1-linear map
  T_chi: Z1_cont(V,k0_chi) -> Z1_cont(K,k1_(chi|K)),
  d -> Tr_(k0/k1)(d|K).
The target is exactly as written: on K the character takes values
in C<=k1, so the k1-linearity of Tr proves the crossed-homomorphism
equation after composition. Continuity is preserved because all
coefficient fields are finite and discrete.

The subspace
  P_chi={d_b:g -> (chi(g)-1)b : b in k0, Tr(b)=0}
is contained in ker T_chi. Its k1-dimension is m-1, since chi is
nontrivial and the trace is onto. The rank target for T_chi on the
7m-dimensional k1-space of derivation values at the seven actual
free generators is therefore 6m+1.

The universal scalar-affine marginal image equality is equivalent
to the following exact equation for all the displayed field and
character data:
  ker T_chi = P_chi.                                     (TC)
Here is the complete proof of this equivalence.

A failure of the scalar-affine equality gives rho' from the preceding
subclaim, with translation cocycle d'. The trace of d' vanishes on K.
It is not principal: a principal affine representation fixes a point
of the field, whereas rho'(V) contains every nonzero translation,
each of which moves every point. Thus d' belongs to ker T_chi outside
P_chi, and (TC) fails for these exact data.

Conversely take a d in ker T_chi outside P_chi and form the actual
continuous homomorphism
  rho_d:V -> k0 semidirect D,  g -> (d(g),chi(g)).
It has nonzero translations. Indeed its translation subgroup is
stable under D and therefore is a k0-subspace of k0. If that subgroup
were zero, its scalar projection would identify rho_d(V) with the
cyclic group D. A generator lift (b,a), a!=1, fixes b/(1-a); every
power has that same fixed point, making d principal. The condition
Tr(d|K)=0 and C!=1 would then force the principal parameter's trace
to be zero, contrary to d being outside P_chi. The translation
subgroup is therefore nonzero and equals k0.

The R image is normal and contains an element with nontrivial scalar
c in C. Commuting this with every translation as above forces it to
contain all translations k0. Its scalar image is C, so
  rho_d(R)=k0 semidirect C.
Since T_chi(d)=0, every element of rho_d(K) lies in
  (ker Tr) semidirect C,
which is a proper subgroup. Hence rho_d(K) is properly contained in
rho_d(R), giving an actual affine marginal failure. This proves
the equivalence without asserting that either failure set is nonempty.

For m=1, equation(TC) is precisely the already proved F24 injection,
with P_chi=0. The remaining data have m>=2. Equation(TC) has not been
proved for those arbitrary field data in this research note.

Proved subclaim: exact Galois coefficient splitting
--------------------------------------------------
The m characters chi_j=chi^(q1^j), 0<=j<m, are pairwise distinct:
an equality for two would make the corresponding nonidentity
power of q1-Frobenius fix every chi(g), hence the generated field
k0, which it does not. They all restrict to chi on R and on K.

The exact coefficient-ring isomorphism is
  S:k0 tensor_(k1) k0 -> product_(j=0)^(m-1) k0,
  b tensor a -> (a*b^(q1^j))_j.
To prove this, choose a multiplicative generator gamma of k0^*.
Its minimal polynomial over k1 has degree m and the distinct roots
gamma^(q1^j). Identify the tensor ring with k0[X]/f(X), where the
first gamma maps to X and the second factor supplies coefficients.
Evaluation at those m roots is S. Its explicit inverse uses the
Lagrange polynomials
  e_j(X)=product_(r!=j) (X-gamma^(q1^r)) /
                            (gamma^(q1^j)-gamma^(q1^r)),
  (a_j)_j -> sum_j a_j e_j(X) modulo f(X).
All denominators are nonzero because the roots are distinct.

Giving the first factor the chi action carries that action under S
to chi_j on factor j. Because V is the actual free profinite group
on seven generators, continuous cocycles are precisely their seven
coefficient values: a tuple gives a homomorphism into the finite
semidirect product, and hence extends uniquely from the discrete
free group to its full completion. Thus S gives the exact vector
space comparison
  Z1_cont(V,k0_chi) tensor_(k1) k0
       -> direct_sum_j Z1_cont(V,k0_(chi_j)),
  d tensor a -> (a*d^(q1^j))_j.

On the target, expanding a k0-valued cocycle on K in a fixed k1-basis
of k0 identifies
  Z1_cont(K,k1_(chi|K)) tensor_(k1) k0
       -> Z1_cont(K,k0_(chi|K)).
Coordinate projections and their inverses preserve both the cocycle
identity and continuity, since the basis and all fields are finite.
Under these two isomorphisms, T_chi tensor 1 is exactly
  (d_0,...,d_(m-1)) -> sum_j d_j|K.                 (S_chi)
Indeed a simple tensor has image a*sum_j d^(q1^j)|K, which is the
literal trace formula. Simple tensors span the source.

The base change of P_chi is the subspace
  {(b_j*(chi_j-1))_j : sum_j b_j=0}.
This follows by applying S to the exact trace-kernel sequence;
under S the trace map becomes summation of the m coordinates.
Extension of scalars by the field k0 is exact and detects a nonzero
vector space by extending a basis. Therefore equation(TC) is
equivalent to saying that the kernel of the exact summed-restriction
map (S_chi) is exactly that principal subspace. Its rank target is
6m+1 on 7m columns over k0. This coefficient splitting proves the
comparison, while leaving the asserted kernel description unresolved.
