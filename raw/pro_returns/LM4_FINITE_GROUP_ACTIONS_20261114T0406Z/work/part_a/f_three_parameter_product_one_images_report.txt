repeat. No unproved general semisimplicity is invoked.

For a nonzero intertwiner between two such subspaces, scalar source

proves all comparisons between different Frobenius factors:

  Boundary versus W or any deleted type: source 0 compares 1 with
  r_l!=1.

  W versus W: sources 0 and 7 determine r_j and t_j, hence j=l.

  Boundary versus boundary: source 7 determines t_j, and source 2

6. SUMMED RESTRICTION AND THE FINITE AFFINE CONSEQUENCE

The summed restriction direct_sum_j Q_j -> H1_cont(K,k0_chi|K)
is well typed because all characters agree on K. It commutes with
the actual source precomposition operators. Each individual restriction
is injective by F.24 because its scalar character on R is the same
nontrivial C.

A nonzero invariant kernel would contain a nonzero invariant subspace
T of smallest dimension. Every nonzero coordinate projection p_j|T
is injective. Two such projections would produce the actual
intertwining isomorphism p_l composed with (p_j|T)^-1, whose inverse
is p_j composed with (p_l|T)^-1. All cross-factor comparisons exclude
this. Thus T has only one nonzero coordinate, where individual F.24
restriction injectivity excludes it. The summed H1 restriction is
therefore injective.

Zero summed cocycle restriction consequently forces d_j=b_j*(chi_j-1).
Since the common character on K is nontrivial, their parameter sum
must be zero. Conversely those tuples have zero summed restriction.
The kernel has dimension m-1 on 7m columns, proving rank 6m+1.

A trace-zero cocycle gives the Frobenius tuple (d^(q1^j))_j and
hence is principal, with trace-zero parameter. This proves exactly
the trace kernel of F.26 on the present locus. Every proper original
affine marginal image would give a nonprincipal such cocycle through
the F.26 conjugation and coefficient homomorphism. Those explicit maps
retain the original scalar character and thus preserve the locus and
fields. The trace result excludes every proper image, even for a larger
original coefficient field. The F.23 based isomorphism and inverse
carry the actual negative K/R pair to the positive pair and complete
the sign transport.
