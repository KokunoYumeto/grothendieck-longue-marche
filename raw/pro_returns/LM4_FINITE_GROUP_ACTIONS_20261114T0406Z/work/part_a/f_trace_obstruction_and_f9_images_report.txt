For a proper marginal, C is nontrivial and cyclic. Choose an actual

over k1 = F_p(C). Put k0 = F_p(D). Normality of N in H makes the

M_z = U semidirect C, with U properly contained in A.

Choose a k1-linear functional L on the coefficient field, zero on U
and nonzero on A, by extending a basis. The trace polynomial

including characteristics dividing m.

The map (b,d) -> (ell(b),d) is an actual affine-group homomorphism.
Composing with the specified conjugation and rho gives rho' with

  rho'(V) = k0 semidirect D,

  T_chi: Z1_cont(V,k0_chi) -> Z1_cont(K,k1_chi|K),

kernel subspace is P_chi = { b*(chi-1) : Tr(b)=0 }, of k1-dimension

Its translation group is a k0-subspace of k0. If it were zero, a

both directions of the exact criterion.
