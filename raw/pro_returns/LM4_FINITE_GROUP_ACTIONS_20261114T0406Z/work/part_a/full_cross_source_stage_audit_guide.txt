1. THE ACTUAL SOURCE BASIS
--------------------------
The source is the original Y-dagger of F17, now based at s*=2+2i through
the original F20 polygon eta. There are twenty-seven finite punctures:
0, the ten points +/-zeta^j, and the sixteen roots of s^2=A_u or B_u.
The script lists twenty-seven rational centers with denominator 10^6.
Each lasso uses the square of half-side 10^-4 about its listed center,
with the straight tail from 2+2i to its northeast corner; the square
is traversed counterclockwise and the same tail is returned in reverse.

For the eleven old punctures, exact Q(theta) distance proves the known
root lies within radius r=1/(2*10^4) of its rational center. For each
collision square V, its center c satisfies |c|>1/2 and
|c^2-V|<10^-5 by exact number-field inequalities. On |s-c|=r,
the linear term 2c(s-c) has norm greater than r, while the remaining
terms have norm less than r^2+10^-5<r. Rouche's theorem therefore
gives exactly one quadratic root, counted with multiplicity, in that
circle. The two disjoint centers for each V account for both roots.
The circle is strictly inside its square.

All twenty-seven squares are disjoint. Every tail is at squared
distance greater than 2*(10^-4)^2 from every other center, so it
misses the entire other square. The tail directions have pairwise
nonzero exact cross products. The source basepoint is northeast of
every square, so each tail first meets its own square at its northeast
corner. These data give an actual embedded common-base star and a
geometric free basis of pi1(Y-dagger). Filling the sixteen collision
punctures retains the eleven old lassos as a free basis of pi1(Y).

The inequality implies |P*(z_i-z_j)|>|P*(delta_i-delta_j)|, which

The perturbed paths have only simple pairwise projection events.
The accepted F20 Bernstein/Descartes procedure certifies every root,

3. THE ORIGINAL ENDPOINT WHISKERS ARE RETAINED
---------------------------------------------

the configuration with the same seven old points, positive point

isotopy, and the subsequent T isotopy, by local disk isotopies. The
image of a disk containing all old points and p while excluding n
is the disk used for the F22 section. Its inverse chart is the
specified inverse basepoint/coordinate isomorphism.

For any configuration z at an endpoint, write
  H_z(t)=(z_i+t*delta_i)_(0<=i<=8), 0<=t<=1.
The basepath producer retains the actual values and offsets for
H_r_std, H_r, and H_b. It checked the original and offset projection

The orders agree throughout every endpoint whisker; these endpoint

Let gamma be any original source-configuration loop at b and let
subscript 1 denote its offset path. The certified two-parameter

6. THE EXHAUSTIVE S3 AND A4 SCANS
--------------------------------
F21 implies that the image of each marginal normally generates the
image of its R target in every finite quotient. In S3, a proper subgroup
normally generating S3 is a two-element transposition subgroup; all
proper subgroups of S3 are nilpotent. A possible failure in a map
V7->S3 therefore has full V7 and R image S3, all four deleted puncture
values of order one or two, and marginal image in a fixed C2. Global
conjugation makes it sufficient to test one C2. There are exactly
54,720 generating tuples with at least one deleted transposition.
The producer enumerates all of them.

The eight actual signed collision seeds reduce them to 624 tuples
per sign whose seed images lie in that C2. The eleven actual old
source generators act on seven-tuples by the evaluated conjugation
automorphisms above. A tuple represents a marginal image contained
in C2 precisely when its entire source orbit keeps every seed in C2.
The producer computes the largest forward-invariant subset of the
seed-good finite set: repeatedly discard a tuple whose image under
some source generator is outside the current set. On a finite set
forward invariance under these permutations includes their inverses.
Every one of the 624 tuples is discarded in the first step, for each
sign. Therefore the staged actual marginal map has equal images with
its R target under every continuous homomorphism V7->S3.

For A4 the producer enumerates its full subgroup lattice from the
permutation table. Every proper subgroup is abelian, and the proper
subgroups normally generating A4 are exactly its four order-three
subgroups. A possible failure therefore has full V7 and R image A4,
at least one deleted order-three value, every deleted value of order
one or three, and marginal image in one fixed C3. Global conjugation
makes that one C3 sufficient. The complete generating candidate count is
  12^3*(9^4-1) - 4*3^3*(3^4-1) = 11,327,040.
The script enumerates them in finite batches. The signed seed tests
leave 15,552 generating tuples for each sign. The same exact source-
orbit test discards every one of them in its first step, for each sign.
Thus the staged actual marginal maps have equal images with their R
targets under every continuous homomorphism V7->A4 as well.

The finite quotient statements concern these two specified groups and
these actual homomorphisms. They give no equality of the full marginal
subgroups and no source-membership statement for the interaction
commutators in W. The product-image theorem already proved in F22
combines the two actual marginal images; it does not enlarge them.
