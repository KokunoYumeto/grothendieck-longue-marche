The specified incidence map can fail in the standard group
==========================================================

This unnumbered research record concerns the arbitrary representative choice
in D76. It does not replace D76's distinguished cusp-aligned triple.

Let

  E0=C2(a) coproduct C2(b) coproduct C2(c),
  F0=ker(E0 -> C2),  a,b,c -> 1.

The exact index-two kernel maps are

  Fhat(x,y) -> F0,  x -> ba, y -> ca,
  E0 -> Fhat(x,y) semidirect_delta C2(a),
  a -> a, b -> xa, c -> ya,

where delta(x)=x^-1 and delta(y)=y^-1. The inverse semidirect-product map
sends x to ba, y to ca, and a to a. Both composites fix the displayed
generators.

Put

  g=x[x,y]x^-1 in F0',
  T0=<a>, T1=<b>, T2=<g c g^-1>,
  Y=g y delta(g)^-1.

These are one representative of each of the three actual section classes.
Conjugation retains the class of c, and the three classes are distinguished
by E0 -> C2^3. The equality

  g c g^-1 = Y a

gives H=<T0,T1,T2> = <x,Y> semidirect <a> exactly; it is the same actual
incidence construction as D76. Equation delta(Y)=Y^-1 follows by substitution.

Since g lies in F0', the pair x,Y has the identity matrix on F0_ab=Zhat^2.
For every prime p, its images consequently generate the Frattini quotient
of the free pro-p group F0^(p). The actual induced endomorphism
x->x, y->Y of F0^(p) is onto and is an automorphism, by the pro-p Frattini
criterion and finite-generation Hopficity. Thus <x,Y> maps onto every
maximal pro-p quotient of the named full F0.

The full subgroup is proper, detected by the following specified quotient.
Use permutations of {1,2,3,4,5}, with (uv)(i)=u(v(i)), and send

  a -> (12)(34),
  b -> (13)(25),
  c -> (14)(23).

These generate A5. The corresponding x=ba and y=ca also generate A5.
Direct multiplication gives

  g -> (132),
  g c g^-1 -> a,
  Y -> 1,
  ord(x)=5, ord(y)=2.

Therefore the chosen three involutions have image <a,b>, of order 10,
and <x,Y> has image <x>, of order 5. The full orientation kernel has image
A5, of order 60. This proves both proper inclusions using the actual quotient.

D76 gives a free rank-two section augmentation module for this triple,
but its specified incidence map has nonzero cokernel: it is exactly the
augmentation kernel of F2[[E0/H]]. The A5 quotient supplies a finite
coset quotient with six H-cosets, so this augmentation kernel is detected
by a nonzero five-dimensional augmentation space.

The same finite quotient also proves that this Y is not conjugate to the
original y: their images have orders one and two. Consequently this example
does not satisfy the additional original-cusp equation in the distinguished
triple of D76. It demonstrates the need to retain that equation while
attempting to prove the specified map invertible.

The producer work/part_c/code/pants_incidence_limit.py verifies the free-word
identities, both abelian coordinates and all named finite images, generation
orders and orders of elements. No finite sample is used for the all-prime
Frattini argument.
