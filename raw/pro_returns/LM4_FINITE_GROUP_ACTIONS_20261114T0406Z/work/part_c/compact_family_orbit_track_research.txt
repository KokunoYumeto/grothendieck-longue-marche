ACTUAL SMALL ORBIT TRACKS AFTER THE INITIAL FIXED REGION
Unnumbered research continuation, saved before the F32 peer audit.
This file is not sealed or independently accepted.

Scope
-----
The construction below concerns an actual homotopy of the restriction
to one full Q-orbit, including its original circle limits and the inverse
of its parameterized embedding. It does not supply an ambient correction
with a quantitative displacement bound. That ambient estimate is the
remaining step; no infinite deformation or accumulated estimate is asserted.

Retain the actual endpoint family produced by
compact_family_initial_fixed_region.tex (source af88a37d...).
Write h:P->C_Q(x) for that endpoint, with P compact, connected and based,
h(u0)=identity. The application to based spheres S^n has n>=1.
It fixes F_X=pi^{-1}X pointwise, where X is the specified compact
subpolyhedron containing a disk neighborhood of p.
Its descents b_u have the relative-X component witness IFR.14.
The fixed-set group Homeo_X(N)_0 is contractible by the fully checked
Yagasaki Theorem1.1, because X contains a disk. The normalized contraction
IFR.7 therefore gives an actual ordinary family B_{u,t}, relative to X,
from identity to b_u and constant at u0. Its unique C92 pointed lift
W_{u,t}=lambda(B_{u,t}) is jointly continuous on O and commutes with
every original q. Constant tracks prove it fixes F_X pointwise.
This W is used only on the interior; no original circle extension of
the whole W-family is being asserted.

Fix the same original closed-disk coordinate iota as FCE.1, with
e(z,w)=|iota(z)-iota(w)| and e_Q=sup_q e(qz,qw).
The actual forward envelope is
  a^+(pi z)=max_u e_Q(h_u z,z).
It is continuous and C0 by CFT/FCE. Choose the already constructed
positive C0 majorant eta=a^++a^-+tau. In particular eta(y)>a^+(y).

1. A literal orbit carrier and its radius
----------------------------------------
Fix v in O minus F_X and set
  A_v={h_u(v):u in P},  K_v=Fill(A_v).
A_v is compact and connected, since P is compact and connected.
It contains v because h(u0)=identity. It misses F_X: h_u fixes F_X,
and h_u(v) in F_X would imply v=h_u^{-1}(h_u(v)) in F_X.
FCE's exact filling map applies to this specified A_v. It gives compact,
connected K_v and
  qK_v=Fill(qA_v)
for every original q. The boundary of K_v is contained in A_v:
the boundary of the exterior component of O minus A_v is contained
in A_v, and filling removes the other complement components.

For each q and u,
  e(qh_u(v),qv)<=a^+(pi v).
Thus iota(qA_v) lies in the closed Euclidean ball of that radius
centered at iota(qv). FCE.4 puts the filled set inside its Euclidean
convex hull, which remains in that same ball. Consequently the exact
radius bound is
  e_Q(z,v)<=a^+(pi v)       for every z in K_v.
This strengthens the diameter bound to a radius bound at the actual
original point v. It uses the actual parameter orbit, not a new path
substituted for h.

2. A small Jordan disk with boundary avoiding every old fixed point
------------------------------------------------------------------
Here is a direct neighborhood construction for the filled compact
connected set K_v in the original Euclidean coordinate.
For every open neighborhood U of K_v, there is a closed Jordan disk
D with K_v contained in int D and D contained in U.

To verify this assertion, use that K_v has connected exterior in the
plane. Choose a large closed round disk B_R containing K_v well inside
its interior. Every point in B_R minus U has a polygonal path in the
exterior of K_v to outside B_R. Such paths exist because an open
connected planar set is polygonally connected: the points reachable
by finite polygonal paths form a relatively open and closed nonempty
subset. The compact path avoids K_v by a positive distance. A small
neighborhood of its initial point can be attached to it with the same
property. Finitely many of these neighborhoods cover the compact set
B_R minus U. Hence one positive neighborhood thickness of K_v is
disjoint from all those paths and initial neighborhoods.

Cover K_v by finitely many open round disks with centers on K_v and
radius less than that thickness and the distance to the outside of
B_R. Choose a common radius from a sufficiently small open interval,
keeping the cover but avoiding the finitely many radii causing pairwise
tangencies or triple boundary intersections. Their union W0 has
connected interior: its intersection graph is connected because its
open disks cover the connected K_v and each center belongs to K_v.
Its boundary is a finite disjoint union of piecewise circular Jordan
curves. At a transverse intersection only the two exterior arcs lie
on the boundary; tangencies and triple vertices have been excluded.
The outer boundary surrounds all of W0, because its interior is connected.
Fill the other bounded complement components. This gives the closed
Jordan disk D, containing K_v in its interior.

The chosen paths from B_R minus U and the region outside B_R remain
in the exterior of W0. Filling W0 therefore adds no point outside U.
Thus D is contained in U. This proves the required neighborhood
construction in the actual original plane.

Now let eps>0. The set of points at e_Q-distance less than eps from
K_v is open in O by C81's exact metric-topology comparison.
Since boundary(K_v) is contained in A_v and A_v is compact and
disjoint from the closed F_X, choose an open V containing that
boundary and disjoint from F_X. Apply the construction with
  U={z:dist_{e_Q}(z,K_v)<eps}
        intersect (int K_v union V).
This is an open neighborhood of K_v inside O. We obtain D_v with
  K_v subset int D_v,
  D_v subset {z:dist_{e_Q}(z,K_v)<eps},
  boundary(D_v) intersect F_X = empty.
The last assertion follows because K_v is already inside int D_v,
so its new boundary lies in U minus K_v, hence in V.

Choose eps=(eta(pi v)-a^+(pi v))/2>0. For z in D_v choose k in K_v
with e_Q(z,k)<eps. The preceding radius bound gives
  e_Q(z,v)<a^+(pi v)+eps<eta(pi v).
All q-translates have the same e_Q diameter, and
  diam_{e_Q}(D_v)<=2a^+(pi v)+2eps<2eta(pi v).
The Q-translates of D_v are not asserted disjoint.

3. A specified retraction avoiding the entire old fixed set
----------------------------------------------------------
Use the planar Schoenflies homeomorphism for the constructed finite
piecewise circular Jordan disk: let
  s:R2->R2,  s(D_v)=closed unit disk,
with its inverse s^{-1}. Here D_v denotes its iota-coordinate image.
The particular planar source locator for this standard input remains
to be recorded before sealing this research note.
Define the actual retraction in that coordinate by
  r_v(z)=s^{-1}(s(z)/max(1,|s(z)|)).
Restrict the input to iota(O), and transport it back by iota^{-1}.
The formula is continuous, fixes every point of D_v, and sends every
point outside D_v to boundary(D_v). No metric estimate for s or s^{-1}
is used: all output points lie in the already controlled original D_v.

It maps O minus F_X into D_v minus F_X. Indeed an input in D_v is
unchanged, while an input outside D_v goes to its boundary, which
was chosen disjoint from F_X. This keeps the actual obstacle set,
including all its Q-translates and all components.

4. A genuinely controlled one-orbit embedding homotopy
-----------------------------------------------------
Define
  w_{u,t}=r_v(W_{u,t}(v)).
The W-track avoids F_X because W fixes F_X and is a homeomorphism.
Therefore w_{u,t} lies in D_v minus F_X. At the two ends and basepoint,
  w_{u,0}=v,  w_{u,1}=h_u(v),  w_{u0,t}=v.
These equations use that r_v fixes A_v pointwise. Its values obey
  e_Q(w_{u,t},v)<eta(pi v)
for all u,t, with the original e_Q and its original operator labels.

On the whole actual orbit put
  E_{u,t}(qv)=q w_{u,t}.
This is unambiguous because Q acts freely. Each map is injective:
q w=r w implies r^{-1}q fixes w, so q=r.
Properness of Q makes its image a closed discrete orbit in O, and
its inverse on that image is the exact map q w_{u,t}->qv.
It misses F_X by Q-invariance. It is Q-equivariant and has exactly
the original endpoint h_u restricted to Qv. Its original uniform
displacement is bounded by eta(pi v), since
  sup_q e(E_{u,t}(qv),qv)=e_Q(w_{u,t},v).
For two parameter values s,s',
  sup_q e(E_s(qv),E_{s'}(qv))=e_Q(w_s,w_{s'}).
Thus the actual parameter continuity is measured by the exact
original-orbit restriction metric, not merely quotient convergence.

The variable-image inverse is also continuous. More precisely
  (u,t,qv)->(u,t,qw_{u,t})
is a homeomorphism onto its image in (P x I) x O. For a compact
neighborhood of any target point, properness allows only finitely
many q with qD_v meeting it. Near a specified parameter, continuity
of w and freeness separate those finitely many orbit points.
The inverse q-label is therefore locally constant, giving the stated
inverse continuity.

5. Original circle comparison for this restriction embedding
-----------------------------------------------------------
The set T_v=Qv union boundary(Dbar) is closed in Dbar and compact:
the orbit has no interior accumulation point by properness.
Extend E_{u,t} to T_v by identity on the original circle.
C81.8 applies to the compact source {v} and compact target D_v.
It gives a compact original boundary-fixed centralizer family P_v
with every qD_v contained in P_v(qv). Consequently qv approaching
an original circle point forces the whole qD_v to that same point,
uniformly over u,t. This proves joint circle continuity of the
extended E, without extending any individual q to the circle.

The total map
  (P x I) x T_v -> (P x I) x Dbar,
  (u,t,z)->(u,t,E_{u,t}(z))
is continuous and injective from a compact space to a Hausdorff
space. Its inverse on the compact image is continuous. This gives
both topology directions, including the varying image and the
original circle. The inverse displacement on that image equals
the same pointwise e-distance bound eta(pi v).

6. Exact step still missing
--------------------------
This construction supplies an actual small embedding homotopy of
one original orbit after the common fixed region. It uses a
retraction of the specified ordinary residual tracks into a filled
carrier built from the given actual family itself.

IFR's local extension products can extend the projected one-point
embedding homotopy with compact quotient support and with the old
fixed region retained. Their lifted ambient displacement has not
been bounded by the radius of D_v. Overlapping translates of D_v
may form large chains, and the finite extension product can revisit
such chains. A bound by the diameter of the entire lifted support
would not give the required original uniform estimate.

The next construction must control the actual ambient factor
trajectories, then their accumulated displacement while successive
vertices, edges and cycles are fixed. The present embedding maps
and inverse topology do not assert that estimate or assume a
uniform number of disjoint-disk stages. C83's exact infinite-evaluation
obstruction remains unchanged.
