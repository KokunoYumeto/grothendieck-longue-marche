2. Actual ambient-operator prime-end functor.
An ambient homeomorphism taking O to itself sends each crosscut
closure, its two endpoints, and its complementary crosscut region
to their actual images. Uniform continuity on compact closure(O)
preserves null diameters; the inverse has the same property.
Disjointness, nesting, and the equivalence inclusion relations
between fundamental chains are all preserved. The change of the
interior reference point affects only a finite initial part of a
chain, because its impression lies in the frontier. Consequently
the extension is a genuine homeomorphism of the same prime-end
compactification. Basic crosscut neighborhoods prove continuity
in both directions.

The displayed interior conjugation determines the extension
uniquely. It gives q hhat = h q on the entire closed disk by
density and continuity, and it proves the group composition law.
The kernel is exactly the original restriction kernel on O.
This argument also applies to an operator transporting O to a
different domain, with its independently specified target Riemann
coordinate; all transport labels are retained.

3. Pointwise frontier fixing implies pointwise prime-end fixing.
A plane homeomorphism fixing the complement of bounded O has
positive global orientation because it is identity on a nonempty
open exterior. Its prime-end circle map r is therefore increasing
and satisfies the exact equation q_boundary r = q_boundary.

The boundary map cannot be constant on an open arc. Subtracting
that constant from the holomorphic interior map, straightening
the circular source arc, and using Schwarz reflection gives a
holomorphic extension which vanishes on a segment; the identity
theorem would contradict injectivity of the Riemann map.

For each fixed interior point, sufficiently large concentric
source circles enclose its preimage, hence their conformal images
have positive winding one. Uniform convergence to the boundary
map and positive distance from the interior point to the compact
frontier give degree one for (C.67.8). Its real lift R satisfies
R(t+1)=R(t)+1. For any increasing lift P of r, the difference
R(P(t))-R(t) is one constant integer. Translating P by its negative
gives the stated normalization R P = R exactly. Since R(t)-t is
bounded and periodic, all positive and negative iterates of each
P-orbit are bounded. Monotonicity and continuity then supply a
fixed point. At any projected fixed point of r, the integer shift
of P must be zero, since otherwise that orbit is unbounded.

The periodic nonempty fixed set of P has bounded complementary
intervals. On such an interval all forward iterates converge to
one fixed endpoint. The exact q_boundary invariance and
continuity force the entire projected open arc to have the same
boundary image. This contradicts the no-constant-arc statement.
Thus r is identity, including at all multiply accessible frontier
points. The proof does not equate those prime ends beforehand.

4. Algebraic and topological group comparison.

The interior formulas prove the homomorphism law and injectivity.
Conversely the preceding prime-end result puts the extension of
each b in K_O inside the boundary-fixed disk group and proves

The displayed modulus-of-continuity estimate proves forward

No uniform bound on an unproved inverse of the frontier map is
