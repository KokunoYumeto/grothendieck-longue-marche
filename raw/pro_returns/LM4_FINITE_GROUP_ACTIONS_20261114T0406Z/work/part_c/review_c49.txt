Scope checked
-------------
I read the current c49.tex and its result metadata and bibliography, reread
the closure-preservation argument of C.36 and the actual metabelian image
classification in C.37, and independently opened Yamabe's original
published paper. The theorem proves contractions on each indicated whole
orbit closure, compatibility on their overlaps and with X_2, operator
transport, and finite closed-set pasting. Its last sentence and metadata
correctly leave the infinite accumulation of new homothety orbits and the
remaining one/two-foliation frontier problem unresolved.

Primary source
--------------
Hidehiko Yamabe, "On an arcwise connected subgroup of a Lie group",
Osaka Mathematical Journal 2(1) (1950), 13--14,
DOI 10.18910/6228.
Original version of record:
https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/6228/omj02_01_03.pdf
The opening paragraph states precisely the arcwise-connected-subgroup
theorem. The proof constructs the Lie subalgebra from limiting directions
of small identity-component arcs, proves containment in the associated
analytic subgroup, and then proves the reverse containment by a local
topological argument. This supplies an analytic subgroup, without an
automatic closed-subgroup or induced-topology conclusion. C.49 explicitly
supplies both remaining conclusions afterward by classifying the actual
transitive analytic subgroups. Thus its application retains the precise
source conclusion.

1. Exact based lifts and transitivity on the source cover
-------------------------------------------------------
The source isotopy and every target isotopy are the based covering lifts.
The commutator with a source deck map is deck-valued and continuous in t,
so it is constant and equal to the identity. The line target endpoint is
the identity directly; the circle target endpoint is the identity by
C.29's ambient-null winding comparison. Injectivity of the two-quotient
map then forces the source endpoint to be the identity.

The generated group is path connected by shortening each factor in a
finite word. The compact symmetric set P includes the identity. Every
point of the source plane belongs to delta P^n x for some deck map delta
and n, because the projected G-action is transitive. There are countably
many deck maps, and every displayed word-track image is compact. Baire
therefore gives interior to one generated orbit. Translating that
interior by the generated group makes the entire orbit open. Commutation
with the deck group makes every other orbit a deck translate of it, and
the same transitivity argument then makes all orbits open. Connectedness
of the source plane leaves exactly one. This proves the required
transitivity on the covering plane itself.

2. The connected web group and its stabilizers
----------------------------------------------
C.47 gives the actual full web group as a Lie group of dimension two or
three. Its identity component is open. The open orbit map makes its
orbits open, so it acts transitively on the connected source plane.
For J=W_x, the homogeneous bundle W -> W/J=R^2 gives connectedness of J
from the homotopy sequence. In dimension two, J is also discrete and
therefore trivial. Evaluation then identifies W with the simply
connected plane. The two-dimensional Lie algebra classification gives
exactly the additive plane and Aff^+(R), and simple transitivity makes
the already-transitive generated subgroup equal to W.

In dimension three, J is a connected one-dimensional subgroup of the
ordered-loop automorphism group. A compact group of increasing line
homeomorphisms fixes each point: a compact point orbit has a largest
point, every group element fixes that largest point, and transitivity on
the orbit then makes the orbit a singleton. Hence J is R, not a circle.
The bundle with contractible base and contractible fiber makes W simply
connected.

Each real quotient stabilizer K_i is closed, connected and
two-dimensional: W/K_i is the actual real target and its orbit map is
open. Equality of two stabilizer Lie algebras would give equality of
the corresponding connected subgroups, hence equality of the fibers
through x and then all translated fibers. This contradicts the selected
distinct partitions. Thus the three quotient tangent lines are distinct.

3. Exact homothety identification
--------------------------------
For nonzero Z in the stabilizer algebra, ad(Z) preserves the three
distinct lines in the two-dimensional quotient and is consequently
scalar there. Zero scalar would make the stabilizer algebra an ideal.
The connected stabilizer would then be normal and would fix the entire
transitive space, contradicting the faithful homeomorphism action.

The nonzero scalar gives an actual eigenspace decomposition into the
one-dimensional zero eigenspace and a two-dimensional scalar eigenspace
V. The derivation identity puts [V,V] in the absent eigenvalue 2 lambda,
so V is abelian. Scaling Z supplies the specified bracket [Z,v]=v.
The simply connected Lie-group identification sends the actual
stabilizer J to the dilation subgroup. Therefore W/J is equivariantly
the standard scalar homothety plane, with precisely the action and
multiplication in C.49.5--6.

Yamabe applies to the generated subgroup. A transitive analytic subgroup
has dimension at least two. A two-dimensional Lie subalgebra with
nonzero dilation projection is R(Z+v_0) plus a translation line L. Its
induced quotient-line flow fixes -v_0 mod L, so the subgroup preserves
the proper affine line -v_0+L. This excludes transitivity. The other
two-dimensional subgroup is the entire translation plane. A
three-dimensional analytic subgroup is open in the connected homothety
group and therefore is the whole group. These are actual subgroup
equalities. Evaluation at one or two fixed points proves closedness and
equality with the induced homeomorphism-group topology.

The exponential formula is correct in both affine dimensions, including
its value at zero. The scalar (exp(u)-1)/u is strictly positive, so the
exponential is globally invertible. The group used thereafter is exactly
the subgroup generated by the original based lifts, independent of the
chosen triple.

4. The action on the whole closure
---------------------------------
The closure L of the global group preserves every open G-orbit O:
forward and inverse limits preserve its boundary; a point originally
off that boundary cannot be sent onto it by an injective boundary-
preserving homeomorphism. Both directions then give l(O)=O. This is the
exact argument recorded in C.36.

The subgroup E_O of pairs satisfying p n = l p is closed in the Polish
product L x N. Its projection to N is onto because every n in N is an
actual finite word in the based lifts, rather than merely a limit of
such words. The identical global word supplies a preimage in G. The
Polish open mapping theorem therefore applies. The kernel fixes O
pointwise and hence fixes its closure pointwise. The action descends
jointly continuously because the projection times the identity of the
closure is an open quotient. This proves the action without requiring a
continuous section of that projection.

Every descended element still has a global G preimage. Consequently its
action on any original orbit in the closure is exactly the original
G-image, it commutes with the original operator stabilizer, and every
original quotient identity remains valid. The fractional-power path
starts and ends as claimed, retains both based edges, and has a jointly
continuous inverse through the group action.

5. Intrinsic boundary topology and exact common images
----------------------------------------------------
For a nontrivial nonopen orbit B, N/N_z maps continuously and bijectively
onto its underlying subset. Connectedness excludes dimension zero.
Dimension at least two would give, by restricting to an embedded
coordinate disk and applying invariance of domain, interior to B and
thus make it an open transitive orbit. The domain is therefore a
one-dimensional homogeneous manifold.

A compact coordinate interval maps to an embedded ambient arc. C.28's
intrinsic arc description makes its interior an intrinsic neighborhood.
This verifies equality of the homogeneous-space and intrinsic
topologies, including the local argument on a circle, rather than merely
an abstract bijection of sets. The resulting action is jointly continuous
in the intrinsic topology.

All three possible N are metabelian and connected. Their boundary image
is exactly G|_B. I reread the complete C.37 argument: a transitive
abelian group has free Polish closure, whose evaluation map identifies
it with the line or circle, forcing the original group to be the entire
translation group. In the nonabelian metabelian case, the path-connected
derived group has no common fixed point and has open connected orbits;
it is transitive and hence the full translation group. Its normalizer
is the positive affine group on a line and just rotations on a circle.
Connectedness of the nontrivial multiplier image forces all positive
scales. This applies unchanged to the homothety group in C.49. It proves
the actual closed intrinsic image and topology in C.49.12.

The resulting homomorphism rho_B from simply connected N has the unique
based lift to the universal cover of that exact common image. Both
exponential maps are globally invertible. Exponential naturality gives
C.49.15 with the original based target path, so it retains the circle
covering label and does not silently discard winding.

6. Compatibility, transport and precise scope
---------------------------------------------
Two distinct open-orbit closures intersect only in nonopen original
orbits. Their boundary images are the same G|_B with the same intrinsic
topology. Their based target paths are therefore identical, and C.49.15
proves equality of the contractions on the intersection. The same
formula is exactly C.36--37's formula. If O is already in U_2, the
simply transitive C.36 action has the identical based source lifts and
the same transitive generated subgroup, so agreement holds also on O.
An open orbit outside U_2 is disjoint from X_2, since it is an open
neighborhood disjoint from U_2.

An original operator carrying O to O' lifts to a homeomorphism of their
source covers. Covering uniqueness makes conjugation carry the based
lifted path to the based lifted path. It therefore carries the actual
generated subgroup, its exponential, logarithm and contraction to their
counterparts. The equality extends to the closures by density. A
different source lift changes nothing because deck maps commute with
the generated group. The displayed Phi formula transports the complete
actual boundary-quotient family, not an arbitrary invariant selected
triple.

Finally, each closure and X_2 is closed in the plane. The matching maps
and matching inverse maps paste over finitely many closed sets. They
preserve each piece and are mutually inverse, giving the claimed
homeomorphism family. No passage from finite pasting to an infinite
accumulation is used or claimed.
