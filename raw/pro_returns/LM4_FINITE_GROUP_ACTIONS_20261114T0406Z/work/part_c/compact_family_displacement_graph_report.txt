FINITE CORES AND INVERSE CORES

For each nonempty compact K in N, finitely many actual covering sections produce compact C in O with pi(C)=K. Then pi^{-1}(K)=QC. Equivariance and d_Q invariance give the exact equality of the supremum over pi^{-1}(K) and the maximum over C. Outside K, the triangle inequality uses the same original point z and bounds the map difference by delta_b(pi z)+delta_c(pi z). This yields CFT.5.

For a compact quotient-continuous parameter family b_u and a chosen compact core K, the inverse core is exactly K^P={b_u(y):u in P,y in K}. It is compact as a continuous image of P times K. The literal identity delta_{b_u^{-1}}=delta_{b_u} composed with b_u^{-1} proves the same inverse tail bound outside K^P. Applying the preceding finite-sheet estimate to maps and inverses gives CFT.8.
