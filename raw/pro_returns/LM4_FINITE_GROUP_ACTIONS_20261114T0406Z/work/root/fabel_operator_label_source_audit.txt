PRIMARY SOURCE AND EXACT OPERATOR-LABEL COMPARISON

Paul Fabel, Isotopies of planar compacta need not extend to isotopies of planar continua, arXiv:math/0202163v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0202163v1. Root opened the primary seven-page PDF and read Corollary 7 and its construction, printed pages 5–6. The arXiv margin labels the version 17 February 2002, while the first page displays 21 November 2018; these observed dates have not been identified with each other.

The construction specifies a convergent sequence and moves a single designated point around an adjacent point during each active interval. The other sequence points remain stationary. Only this explicit motion pattern enters the comparison. Theorem 6 and the asserted ambient nonextension conclusion are not used in any accepted LM4 proof.

For the original Q-labelled evaluation map, z_(qv)(t)=q z_v(t) and z_v(0)=v. Injectivity of q proves z_v(t) differs from v exactly when z_(qv)(t) differs from qv. The moved-label set is therefore a union of Q-orbits. Every orbit of a free infinite Q-action is infinite. Consequently the specified single-moving-label intervals cannot obey these equations on an actual nonempty union of free Q-orbits. This is the explicit obstruction to using that literal construction in the current centralizer comparison; no assertion about all possible modified constructions is made.
