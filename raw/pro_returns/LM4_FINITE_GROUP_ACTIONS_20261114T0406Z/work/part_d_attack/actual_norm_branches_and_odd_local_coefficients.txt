ACTUAL CYCLIC NORM BRANCHES AND THE ODD-PRIME COEFFICIENT MAP
==========================================================
Unnumbered proved continuation, retaining the original GT0 involution.
No previously frozen note, producer, output, or review is changed.
The exact original full solution set T remains unproved nonempty.

1. The original word as a cube in a specified full free product
--------------------------------------------------------------
Retain F=Fhat(x,y), z=(xy)^(-1), the actual unique h of D74, and
omega(x)=y, omega(y)=z. Put
  L=F semidirect_omega <eta | eta^3=1>,  b=x eta.
There are explicit inverse continuous homomorphisms
  C3(A) amalg C3(B) -> L,       A->eta, B->x eta,
  L -> C3(A) amalg C3(B),
        x->B A^(-1), y->A B A^(-2), eta->A.                 (1)
Indeed (x eta)^3=xyz=1. In the reverse direction z maps to A^2 B,
and conjugation by A sends the displayed x-image to the y-image and
the y-image to the z-image. The two composites fix every named
generator, proving the isomorphism. The original F is the kernel
of the displayed quotient L->C3 sending both A and B to 1 modulo 3.

Let K={k in Zhat:2k+1 belongs to Zhat^times}, and retain
  D(k)=x^k omega^2(h)^(-1) z^k omega(h)^(-1) y^k h^(-1).
For the actual continuous curve
  a:K->L,       a(k)=h y^(-k) eta,
direct multiplication gives
  a(k)^3=h y^(-k) omega(h) z^(-k) omega^2(h) x^(-k)
        =D(k)^(-1).                                       (2)
Its image under L->C3 is the nonzero element 1. Thus D(k)=1 is
exactly the assertion that this specified a(k) has order three.
The parameter map k->mu=2k+1 is the same homeomorphism to the full
unit group used in the frozen transporter note.

The two full free factors in (1) have trivial mutual conjugate
intersections and are malnormal. The full-profinite statement of
Herfort--Zalesskii Theorem 5 supplies that property. Every nontrivial
finite subgroup of L is conjugate into one of the two factors, by
the unnumbered Corollary on printed page 598 of Scheiderer's Appendix
to Lochak--Schneps. Both source clauses and their exact read scope
are retained in Section 7 below.

In particular an order-three a(k) is conjugate to eta or b. The
other nonidentity powers are excluded by the given quotient to C3.
The conjugator can always be chosen in the original F: replace an
arbitrary conjugator g by g eta^(-j), or by g b^(-j), where j is
its image in L/F and the factor generator is the one being
conjugated. These changes preserve the conjugated element and put
the conjugator in F. The F-conjugator is unique, because
  C_F(eta)=C_F(b)=1:                                      (3)
malnormality puts a centralizer inside its order-three factor,
whose intersection with F is trivial.

The full abelianization of L in (1) is C3(A) x C3(B). The image of
h is zero, since h belongs to F'. The image of y is B-A, so
  [a(k)]=(k+1)A-kB.                                      (4)
For k in K, its residue modulo three is 0 or -1. Formula (4)
assigns the eta branch exactly to k=0 modulo three, and the b branch
exactly to k=-1 modulo three.

Define the closed compact spaces
  R0={(k,w) in K x F:k=0 mod 3,
                     h=w omega(w)^(-1) y^k},
  R1={(k,w) in K x F:k=-1 mod 3,
                     h=w x omega(w)^(-1) y^k}.           (5)
There is an exact homeomorphism
  R0 disjoint_union R1 -> T,       (k,w)->2k+1.            (6)
To prove both directions, the first equation in (5) is equivalent
to a(k)=w eta w^(-1), and the second to a(k)=w b w^(-1).
Either equation therefore gives D(k)=1 by (2). Conversely D(k)=1
gives the indicated factor class by (4), and then its unique
F-conjugator by (3). This proves bijectivity. The domain is compact,
the target is Hausdorff, and the displayed map is continuous, so
its inverse is continuous as well. Equivalently, the two orbit maps
F->eta^F and F->b^F are homeomorphisms onto their compact images.

The inverse in (6) reads k=(mu-1)/2, chooses its residue branch,
and reads the unique w from the corresponding actual orbit map.
Composing (6) with the frozen transporter parameterization gives
all original boundary solutions, with their exact fiber:
  (k,w,lambda) ->
     (mu,u)=(2k+1, x^k omega^2(h)^(-1) z^lambda),
                        lambda in Zhat.                 (7)
Here w is uniquely determined by k, whereas lambda records the
original right cusp-centralizer freedom. No point of either R_i
has been constructed by the free-product identification alone.

2. A useful actual section identity, before any quotient
-------------------------------------------------------
In the original E retain the proved order-three automorphism
  Omega|F=omega,  Omega(s)=y^(-1)f s.
Then Omega(xs)=fs. Put q=Omega(fs), as in the frozen class-two note.
The original normalized z-reflection has the additional exact form
  r=qz=Omega^2(s).                                       (8)
Indeed Omega(qz)=Omega^3(xs)omega(z)=(xs)x=s. Applying Omega^2
proves (8), since Omega^3=1.

There is a direct proof of the first transporter formula that now
uses only the actual coboundary equality. In E, and after the exact
quotient maps of Section 3, a coboundary solution H gives
  Omega(xs)=H^(-1)sH.
Applying Omega^2 gives
  r=omega^2(H)(xs)omega^2(H)^(-1).                        (9)
Likewise, with Y_H=H y H^(-1), direct substitution gives
  q=(omega(H)^(-1)y^(-1)H^(-1)) (Y_H s)
                         (omega(H)^(-1)y^(-1)H^(-1))^(-1).
                                                              (10)
The maps in (9)--(10) use the induced original alpha and Omega.
In E their F-conjugators are unique by D74; in a quotient these
identities remain valid without asserting uniqueness there.

3. Exact original maps to an odd-prime completion
------------------------------------------------
Fix an odd prime p. Let
  q_p:F->P=F^(p)
be the canonical maximal pro-p quotient. Its characteristic kernel
is preserved by alpha, theta and omega, so these give actual
automorphisms alpha_p, theta_p, omega_p of P. The group P is free
pro-p on x_p=q_p(x), y_p=q_p(y), by the free universal property.
The homomorphism of extensions is explicitly
  Q_p:E->E_p=P semidirect_alpha_p C2,
                   g s^epsilon -> q_p(g)s^epsilon.       (11)
The formula for Omega descends through this map. All elements f_p,
h_p,z_p and all original peripheral maps are their q_p images.

Put c_p=[x_p,y_p]. The same inverse coordinate maps as in the
class-two note give
  P/gamma3(P) <-> UT3(Z_p) <-> Z_p^3.                    (12)
For completeness, x_p^a y_p^b c_p^t fills the quotient as a compact
subgroup containing its generators; the two elementary matrices
separate the three coordinates and fill UT3(Z_p). Multiplication
is (a,b,t)(a',b',t')=(a+a',b+b',t+t'-a'b).
The map (12) is exactly the p-coordinate of F->UT3(Zhat), factored
through q_p. The original alpha_p induces (a,b,t)->(-a,-b,t).
Thus every alpha_p-fixed element has zero abelian coordinates and
lies in P'. On C_p=C_P(alpha_p) there is a continuous homomorphism
  chi_p:C_p->Z_p,
        v mod gamma3(P)=c_p^{chi_p(v)}.                  (13)

4. A split surjection from the actual pro-p fixed group
-------------------------------------------------------
Every element of a pro-p group with odd p has a unique square root.
The map is continuous: in every finite p-quotient it is the power
with exponent 2^(-1) modulo the exponent of that quotient. These
maps commute with all quotient maps and with automorphisms.

Define the explicit continuous map
  M_p:P->P,
      M_p(g)=g (g^(-1)alpha_p(g))^(1/2).                 (14)
Write a=g^(-1)alpha_p(g). Then alpha_p(a)=a^(-1), and
  alpha_p(M_p(g))=g a a^(-1/2)=M_p(g).
Also M_p(g)=g for every fixed g. Hence (14) is a continuous
retraction of spaces onto C_p. It is not being used as a group
homomorphism.

For g=c_p^u with u in Z_p, the element g^(-1)alpha_p(g) belongs to
gamma3(P), by (12). Its unique square root also lies in gamma3(P),
because it belongs to the closed procyclic subgroup generated by
that element. Therefore
  chi_p(M_p(c_p^u))=u.                                   (15)
In particular v_p=M_p(c_p) has chi_p(v_p)=1, and the continuous
homomorphism
  Z_p->C_p,           u->v_p^u                           (16)
is a right inverse to chi_p. It is injective and a homeomorphism
onto its compact image.

The original full fixed-group map is also specified:
  C_F(alpha)={1} -> C_p -> Z_p.
Its composite is zero, whereas (13) is onto by (16). Thus no
nonzero element constructed by (16) is the q_p image of an
alpha-fixed element of the original F. These are statements about
the displayed original quotient map, not an identification of its
image with the target fixed group.

5. The local coboundary and first symmetry allow every coefficient
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Now retain alpha in GT0 and its same original h. Write t_p for the
coefficient of h_p in (12). The actual map
  psi_p=Inn(h_p) theta_p:P->P                             (17)
is an involution commuting with alpha_p. Indeed h theta(h)=1 gives
its square, and theta alpha theta=Inn(f)alpha together with
alpha(h)=hf gives the commutation relation. These identities pass
through the named quotient (11).

For every r_p in Z_p define
  g_r=M_p(c_p^{(t_p-r_p)/2}),
  e_r=g_r^(-1)psi_p(g_r),
  H_r=e_r h_p.                                           (18)
Here division by two is the continuous inverse on Z_p, since p is
odd. The map g_r takes values in C_p. Since psi_p preserves C_p,
e_r also belongs to C_p, and e_r psi_p(e_r)=1 by substitution.
Consequently (18) satisfies both full local group equations
  H_r^(-1)alpha_p(H_r)=f_p,
  H_r theta_p(H_r)=1.                                   (19)
The second follows by writing its left side as
e_r psi_p(e_r) h_p theta_p(h_p).

On the central coordinate of (12), psi_p acts by -1: theta_p sends
c_p to c_p^(-1) there, and h_p is central there. Thus e_r has
coefficient -2(t_p-r_p)/2=r_p-t_p, and H_r has coefficient r_p.
Every H_r lies in P', depends continuously on r_p, and H_{t_p}=h_p.
Thus (18) is an explicit continuous section of the coefficient map
on the space of simultaneous solutions (19), through the actual
point h_p.

Equations (9)--(10) hold for these same H_r in the fixed quotient
extension (11). The original images Q_p(r) and Q_p(q) there are
unchanged; the second target section is Y_{H_r}s with
Y_{H_r}=H_r y_p H_r^(-1). This formula specifies exactly which
peripheral conjugate changes with H_r. No equality
Y_{H_r}=q_p(h y h^(-1)) is asserted for r_p different from t_p.

There is also an exact map from the original simultaneous solution
space to this local space: it sends its unique element h to h_p.
D74's uniqueness makes its coefficient image exactly {t_p}.
Formula (18) supplies the additional local points; it does not
provide other full solutions or alter the original t_p.

6. A concrete local obstruction, with an actual finite image
-----------------------------------------------------------
For the original standard involution alpha=delta one has f=h=1.
Apply (18) at p=5 and r_p=1. It produces an actual element H_1 of
the free pro-5 group satisfying (19), with coefficient one. All
maps (11), (17) and the original quotient involution remain fixed.
For this local element the same cyclic word has class-two image
  D_{H_1}(k)=c_5^{-3-k(k+1)/2}.
Its unit zero locus is empty: a zero would imply
  mu_5^2=1-24=2 modulo 5,
whereas the nonzero squares modulo 5 are 1 and 4. This is an exact
obstruction for the constructed local H_1. The original full h=1
maps instead to H_0=1, and has the original solutions mu=1,-1.

The retained producer code/actual_odd_local_midpoint.py verifies
these maps in an actual finite quotient of P. Use the associative
algebra
  A=F_5<X,Y>/(all words of degree at least four),
and the group H generated by 1+X and 1+Y in its units. The explicit
homomorphism sends x_5->1+X, y_5->1+Y. The ambient augmentation
unit group is a finite 5-group; hence this is an actual continuous
pro-5 quotient. The producer enumerates its 3125 image elements.

Substitution of x^(-1),y^(-1); of y,x; and of y,(xy)^(-1) in the
truncated algebra gives the induced delta,theta,omega maps. Each
substitution intertwines the original free-source maps on the
generators. The program verifies preservation of H and their
orders on every element of H. Inverse multiplication, exponent
five, and the square-root map are checked directly on every image
element; the exponent test does not reduce its input exponent.

For each r modulo five the code evaluates the exact formulas (18)
with original h=1, checks both equations (19), verifies both
original z-endpoint transporter equations in H semidirect_delta C2,
and checks (2) and the class-two coefficient at all four unit
residues modulo ten. These include every possible Z_5 unit image.
The coefficient-one case has no zero in this actual finite image.
The retained run passes 27 named checks. Its output JSON and
captured standard output are byte-identical.

This construction records a specific limit of a proposed odd-prime
argument: (19), even with the induced original extension and the
displayed endpoint maps, admits the additional local points (18).
Using them to constrain the full original t_p requires controlling
the image {h_p} of the actual full solution space. No such further
image constraint, no all-prime square root for the original t, and
no nonempty branch in (5) is established in this note.
