THE ACTUAL MARKED V4 EXTENSION AND ITS CUSP-FIXING ENDOMORPHISM
=============================================================
Separate unnumbered proved continuation; no numbered claim is assigned.
It uses the frozen actual-J centralizer calculation. No earlier file
is changed. All maps below retain the original alpha, h, x, y and z.
The original D74.40 norm set T remains unresolved.

1. The actual extension and its positive subgroup
-------------------------------------------------
Use the exact D44 extension
  E=F semidirect_alpha <s>,
  G=E semidirect_Theta <tau>,
  Theta|_F=theta, Theta(s)=fs, theta(x)=y, theta(y)=x.
Here s^2=tau^2=1. Write the quotient G/F as V4 with coordinates
  s -> (1,0), tau -> (0,1).
The original h satisfies alpha(h)=hf and h theta(h)=1. Thus
  Y=h y h^(-1), t=h tau, j=st=ts,
  Q0=<s,t> is a specified subgroup isomorphic to V4.
Conjugation by t on F is psi=Inn(h)theta; conjugation by j is
the original J=alpha psi in the frozen negative-transposition note.
The quotient images of t and j are (0,1) and (1,1).

Let G_+ be the inverse image of <(0,1)>. It is the same actual
subgroup both in F semidirect_theta <tau> and F semidirect_psi <t>.
The full coproduct maps
  C2(T) amalg Zhat(P) -> G_+,  T->tau, P->p=x tau,
have the explicit inverse
  x->PT, y->TP, tau->T.
The inverse respects tau x tau=y and tau y tau=x; both composites
fix the displayed generators. Thus the identification is full,
continuous, and has the indicated continuous inverse. In particular
  p^2=xy=z^(-1), x=p tau, y=tau p.                       (1)
The original F is the kernel of G_+->C2 sending both p and tau to 1.
The subgroup <p> is a full Zhat: the map G_+->Zhat sending p to 1
and tau to 0 splits its exponent map.

2. The exact original z-cusp normalizer in G
--------------------------------------------
Retain the normalized original z-reflection from the accepted GT0
note:
  u1=omega^2(h) in F', r=u1 x s u1^(-1),
  r^2=1, r z r=z^(-1), rF=(1,0).                       (2)
The outer multiplier on G/F is (-1) to the first coordinate.
Therefore the image of C_G(z) in G/F lies in <(0,1)>: an odd first
coordinate would make z F-conjugate to z^(-1). Their original
abelian vectors (-1,-1) and (1,1) are unequal. Its kernel
is C_F(z)=<z>, by A.7; p supplies the other coset. Using p^2=z^(-1),
  C_G(z)=<p>.
Every normalizer of <z> has one of the four quotient coordinates.
The elements p and r supply all four, and its kernel in F is <z>.
Moreover r p r belongs to C_G(z)=<p> and has square p^(-2).
Writing it p^a gives 2a=-2, hence a=-1 in the full Zhat. Consequently
  N_G(<z>)=<p> semidirect_-1 <r>, r p r=p^(-1).          (3)
The maps (a,e)->p^a r^e, and their inverse obtained from parity and
the p-exponent, give this equality as an isomorphism of profinite
groups. These maps in particular retain the original embedded L_z
as <p^2>; no extra choice of an abstract cusp is used.

Put
  v=p r.
Then v^2=1 and vF=(1,1), so v belongs to the actual negative-
transposition extension F<j>. The fixed-cusp relations are
  v z v=z^(-1), r v r=p^(-1)r=zv.                      (4)

3. The unique J transporter supplies an actual V4
------------------------------------------------
The frozen J calculation applies to v in the same actual extension:
all its sections are F-conjugate and C_F(v)=1. There is a unique
  g in F with g v g^(-1)=zv.
On the original abelian basis write [g]=(a,b). Its equation gives
  a+b=-1.                                              (5)
The exact character chi(x)=chi(y)=-1 has chi(g)=1, and the frozen
primitive-character proof gives C_F(g)=N_F(<g>)=<g>.

Define
  q=g^(-1)r.
Equations (4) and the g equation show that q centralizes v. Its
quotient coordinate is (1,0), so q^2 lies in F and centralizes v.
Since C_F(v)=1, q^2=1. Thus
  Q=<q,v> is a specified V4, and qv has coordinate (0,1). (6)
The quotient identifies Q with G/F, since the two nonzero coordinates
of q and v are independent.

The element q belongs to the original E and is F-conjugate to s.
To see the particular class without choosing representatives,
compute its F-component in the split coordinates E=F<s>.
Equation (2) has abelian F-component (1,0), because u1 is in F'.
Therefore q has abelian F-component
  (1-a,-b), whose coordinate sum is 2.
Conjugating an E-involution by an element of F changes this vector
by twice an integral profinite vector. D74's three full F-classes
are represented by s, xs and Ys, with mod-two vectors respectively
  (0,0), (1,0), (0,1).
Only the first has even coordinate sum. This proves that q is in
the class of s, and also proves
  a=1 modulo 2, b=0 modulo 2.                            (7)

Let w be the unique element of F with
  q=w s w^(-1).
Existence was just proved; uniqueness is the full C_F(s)=1.
The full C_G(s) equals Q0: its map to G/F is injective by C_F(s)=1,
and Q0 already supplies four distinct quotient elements. Since
v centralizes q and has coordinate (1,1), it follows exactly that
  v=w j w^(-1), t'=qv=w t w^(-1).                      (8)
In particular t' is an actual involution of G_+.

The involution h tau is conjugate to tau inside the already identified
C2 amalg Zhat=G_+: its torsion lies in a conjugate of the C2 factor.
The conjugator can be taken in F, because G_+=F<tau>. This is the
same full torsion-factor clause used in D44. Hence t' also is
F-conjugate to tau. No assertion about a generated subgroup follows
from this conjugacy alone.

4. A specified full coproduct map and its exact image
-----------------------------------------------------
Let
  P0=C2(R) amalg V4(Q,V),
where R,Q,V are involutions and QV=VQ. The actual elements in (2)
and (6) define a continuous homomorphism
  iota:P0->G, R->r, Q->q, V->v.                        (9)
Its image H is compact, hence closed, and is precisely <r,q,v>.
It contains p=vr. Both its image in G/F and its image in G/G_+
are the whole indicated finite quotients.

For the latter quotient put
  epsilon:P0->C2, epsilon(R)=epsilon(Q)=epsilon(V)=1,
  P_+=ker epsilon, P_0=VR, T_0=QV.
There is the following explicit full presentation, including inverse
maps:
  P0=(Zhat(P_0) amalg C2(T_0)) semidirect_sigma <R>,
  sigma(P_0)=P_0^(-1), sigma(T_0)=P_0^(-1)T_0 P_0.       (10)
The automorphism sigma squares to the identity. In the right-hand
group put V=P_0 R and Q=T_0 P_0 R; these are commuting involutions.
Conversely P_0=VR and T_0=QV recover its named generators in the
left-hand group, and conjugation by R has the displayed formulas.
Thus both full continuous homomorphisms are inverse on generators.
In particular P_+=Zhat(P_0) amalg C2(T_0) with the indicated basis.

The restriction iota_+ has exact images
  P_0->p, T_0->t'.                                     (11)
Moreover iota(P_+)=H cap G_+. To verify the reverse inclusion,
write any preimage of an element of H cap G_+ using (10). Its R
coordinate must be zero, since iota induces the identity on these
C2 quotients. Thus it already lies in P_+.

Use the explicit identification in Section 1 to identify P_+ with
G_+ by P_0->p and T_0->tau. The actual resulting endomorphism is
  eta:G_+->G_+, eta(p)=p, eta(tau)=t'=w h tau w^(-1).     (12)
This is a specified endomorphism arising from the original data and
the two proved unique transporters. In particular it is not an
arbitrary selection of a conjugate factor generator.

The image statement (11) gives the exact equivalences
  iota(P0)=G  <=> eta(G_+)=G_+ <=> eta is an automorphism. (13)
For the first equivalence, H contains r, whose image generates
G/G_+; thus H=G exactly when H cap G_+=G_+. For the second, a
surjective endomorphism of the finitely generated profinite G_+
is injective. Indeed for every
index n there are finitely many open subgroups of index n, by their
coset actions and finite generation. Surjective pullback is injective
on this finite set and hence permutes it. Thus for each open subgroup
U some positive iterate has eta^(-m)(U)=U; ker eta is contained in
that set U. Intersecting all open U makes ker eta trivial. Therefore
eta has its continuous inverse by compactness. At this point iota
also is an isomorphism: its kernel maps trivially to C2 and its
restriction to P_+ is identified with the injective eta. These are
equivalences for the specified maps; no side of (13) is asserted to
hold for a remaining actual nonstandard input.

5. The original F map, its cusp product and its exact determinant
----------------------------------------------------------------
The quotient G_+->C2 defining F sends t' to 1, since t' is conjugate
to tau, and sends p to 1. Thus eta preserves this quotient and F.
Using t'=qv=g^(-1)rv=g^(-1)p^(-1), (1) gives
  eta_F(x)=p g^(-1)p^(-1), eta_F(y)=g^(-1),
  eta_F(xy)=p t' t' p=p^2=xy,
  eta_F(z)=z.                                          (14)
There is no replacement of the original z generator in these maps.

Conjugation by p=x tau on F has original abelian matrix swapping
x and y. Therefore the exact abelian matrix of eta_F is
  [[-b,-a],[-a,-b]], det(eta_F,ab)=a-b,                 (15)
where a+b=-1. Equation (7) makes a-b odd at the prime 2. It does
not establish that a-b is a full unit. The endomorphism eta induces
the identity on the full G_+ abelianization Zhat(p) direct_sum C2(tau),
because t' is conjugate to tau; this latter statement alone supplies
no further determinant equality on the index-two subgroup F.

For completeness, eta_F(F)=eta(G_+) cap F, by the same quotient
coordinate argument as for (11). Both eta(G_+) and G_+ map onto
G_+/F. Consequently full surjectivity of eta_F is equivalent to
that of eta. No full surjectivity is obtained from (15).

There is also an exact automorphism-intertwining equation.
In (10), conjugation by Q=T_0P_0R fixes T_0 and sends P_0 to
T_0 P_0^(-1) T_0. Hence it inverts the two original-domain generators
  x_0=P_0T_0, y_0=T_0P_0
of the kernel of P_+->C2 sending P_0,T_0 to 1. The full inverse
maps of Section 1, with these symbol names, identify that kernel
with Fhat(x_0,y_0); it has index four in P0. Transporting (9) gives
  Inn(q) eta_F = eta_F delta,
where delta(x)=x^(-1), delta(y)=y^(-1).
Since q=w s w^(-1), the specified continuous homomorphism
  beta=Inn(w^(-1)) eta_F:F->F
satisfies
  alpha beta=beta delta, psi beta=beta theta,
  beta(z)=w^(-1)z w.                                   (16)
For the second equivariance, extend the formula for beta to G_+.
It sends tau to w^(-1)t'w=t, so conjugation by tau in the source
is carried to conjugation by t in the target. Restriction to F is
exactly the stated psi/theta identity.
Its two other original cusp images are exactly those in (14)
conjugated by w^(-1); no unit-power alignment with x and y has
been proved. Thus (16) is not supplied as a solution of D74.40.

6. Exact change of the original reflection
------------------------------------------
For any d in the full Zhat, replace r by
  r_d=z^d r z^(-d).
Keep the same p and put v_d=p r_d. Since p commutes with z,
  v_d=z^d v z^(-d), g_d=z^d g z^(-d),
  q_d=z^d q z^(-d), w_d=z^d w, t'_d=z^d t' z^(-d).      (17)
The g_d formula follows by conjugating the entire marked
g v g^(-1)=zv equation; the uniqueness C_F(v_d)=1 proves it
is the conjugator selected by the new construction. The q_d
formula follows from its definition. The w_d formula solves
the new s-conjugacy equation and is unique by C_F(s)=1.
The remaining formulas are then direct products.

Consequently the resulting maps have the exact comparison
  eta_d=Inn(z^d) eta on G_+,
  beta_d=beta on F.                                    (18)
For eta_d, check both p and tau; conjugation by z^d fixes p.
For beta_d, substitute w_d=z^d w into its definition. The
original abelian pair (a,b), and hence its difference a-b,
is unchanged. This keeps the entire same-section-class
reflection choice through actual homomorphisms.
