The inverse coset map takes these named generators to the named

2. An actual S3 normalizer and its transport to j
------------------------------------------------

  s xi s=k xi^(-1)k^(-1).
Put d=k^(-1)s. This is an explicit element of M_all with
  d xi d^(-1)=xi^(-1).
Its square is in C_M(xi)=<xi>. Since d inverts this group,
d^2=d d^2 d^(-1)=(d^2)^(-1), so d^2=1. Hence
  N_M_all(<xi>)=<xi,d> = C3 semidirect_-1 C2 = S3.      (FO.4)
The asserted isomorphism has mutually inverse normal forms
(xi^a,epsilon) <-> xi^a d^epsilon. The parity map to M_all/M
and the inclusion of <xi> make these six forms distinct.
Every normalizer element is accounted for: its positive part
is <xi>, and multiplication by d sends a negative normalizer
element to that positive part.

In (FO.2), k has a transposition as its S3 coordinate, since

The construction uses a known existing conjugator in each

Then the full set is exactly

  C=(a c)<kappa_0>(a c)^(-1)

subgroups are j-normalized. They are distinct: equality would
put t in N_M(<kappa_0>)=<kappa_0>, impossible for an involution.
Each has exactly the two generators displayed in (FO.6).

Its inverse first takes the unique subgroup coordinate u above,

4. Four actual maps and one canonical closed image
---------------------------------------------------
For each kappa in K the full coproduct defines
  Phi_kappa:M->M, tau->t, xi->kappa.
All four maps have exactly the same closed image
  H3=<t,kappa_0>.
Indeed inversion of kappa does not change the generated subgroup,
and conjugating kappa by t does not change it either. The image
is closed since it is the image of the compact M. No assertion
H3=M is made here.

Let J_* be the source automorphism tau->tau, xi->xi^(-1), and
let delta_M be the source automorphism
tau->tau, xi->tau xi^(-1)tau. Then
delta_M=Inn(tau) J_*, and J_*|F=theta delta. On the actual
codomain, j=st and s,t commute. The generator identities give
  Inn(j) Phi_kappa=Phi_kappa J_*,
  Inn(s) Phi_kappa=Phi_kappa delta_M.                   (FO.7)
The second equality follows by composing the first with Inn(t)
and using Phi_kappa(tau)=t. On the original F this says
  alpha rho_kappa=rho_kappa delta,
  psi rho_kappa=rho_kappa theta,
where rho_kappa=Phi_kappa|F. The restriction is well-defined:
kappa F=B^epsilon for a unique epsilon=+1 or -1, and the
induced quotient map A->A,B->B^epsilon is an automorphism of
the named S3. Therefore
  H3 intersect F=rho_kappa(F),
  rho_kappa(x)=(t kappa)^(-2),
  rho_kappa(y)=kappa(t kappa)^(-2)kappa^(-1).           (FO.8)
The kernel/image equality in (FO.8) uses that quotient
automorphism: a source element mapping into F must itself lie
in F. It is not inferred from a numerical rank.

There is also an exact amalgam comparison. Let
  P0=(C2(A0) amalg C3(B0)) semidirect_{J_*}<j0>,
where j0 fixes A0 and inverts B0. Its finite subgroups
V0=<A0,j0>=V4 and S0=<B0,j0>=S3 share <j0>. The universal

fixing A0,B0,j0 on both sides: the two vertex presentations

The actual map

has image <H3,j>; it maps the two finite vertex groups

5. The actual abelian coefficient and its two finite signs
-----------------------------------------------------------
Write epsilon_kappa for the sign in kappa F=B^epsilon_kappa.
The abelian matrix of rho_kappa commutes with the coordinate

by the xi conjugation relation. Using the exact omega matrix

  [rho_kappa]=nu_kappa theta    for epsilon_kappa=-1.    (FO.10)
For example a swap-commuting matrix is [[u,v],[v,u]];

The coefficient has the original continuous character formula
  nu_kappa=chi_x(rho_kappa(xy))=chi_y(rho_kappa(xy)).

One has nu_kappa=1 modulo 2. To see this with the original

xi^2 s0 xi^(-2)=x s0. Its image is kappa^2 s kappa^(-2),

The mod-two abelian section vectors of these classes are
(1,0) and (0,1). Formula (FO.10) therefore forces the assertion.

6. The exact finite-factor comparison with the original T
----------------------------------------------------------
Keep the original w and p from the accepted marked construction.
Define the closed space

             kappa^(-1)t kappa^(-1)=w^(-1)p^nu w}.     (FO.12)

as the map. Here are both directions, without an image assumption.
For a point of (FO.12), Phi_kappa agrees with gamma_nu on tau
and p, hence on the actual G_+=C2(tau)*Zhat(p). The accepted
order-three comparison then sends its literal extension to nu
in the original T.

Conversely, take nu in the original T and the unique actual
extension Phi of gamma_nu constructed in that comparison.
The homomorphisms Inn(j)Phi and Phi J_* agree on F because
alpha gamma_nu=gamma_nu delta and psi gamma_nu=gamma_nu theta.
The images of xi under these two maps induce the same conjugation
on their common F-image. That image is gamma_nu(F), whose full
centralizer in M was proved trivial in the accepted comparison.
Thus the xi images coincide. If kappa=Phi(xi), this says
j kappa j=kappa^(-1); the extension proof already gives its
order exactly three. Hence kappa belongs to the actual K.
The source identity p=xi^(-1)tau xi^(-1) gives (FO.12).

The map to the former three-defect space retains nu and sends
  kappa -> (epsilon_kappa,c=kappa xi^(-epsilon_kappa)).
Its inverse sends (nu,epsilon,c) to (nu,c xi^epsilon).
These are continuous inverse maps between the two closed spaces.
The explicit inverse from T is therefore the same original-h

branch, and kappa=x^(-(nu+1)/2)omega^(-1)(h^(-1))xi^(-1)
on the +1 mod-four branch. No new h,r,g or w enters this map.

One can remove the continuous parameter from (FO.12). Squaring
its equation and taking the original abelianization forces
nu=nu_kappa. The number nu_kappa is odd, so the actual word
  C_kappa=kappa^(-1)t kappa^(-1)
                      w^(-1)p^(-nu_kappa)w             (FO.13)
lies in the original F even before its unit coordinates at

                                      C_kappa=1}.      (FO.14)
For this word, the full original abelian projection is
  [C_kappa]=((nu_kappa-m)/2,-(nu_kappa-m)/2)
                                    for epsilon=+1,
  [C_kappa]=(-(nu_kappa+m)/2,(nu_kappa+m)/2)

To obtain it, write Phi_kappa(p)=rho_kappa(x)t and use the

selects nu_kappa=epsilon_kappa m before its remaining full word
