3. Actual maximal pro-p quotient maps and their inverses
---------------------------------------------------------
For every prime p take the canonical map q_p:F->P_p=F^(p).
Its kernel is fully invariant: composing any finite p-group
homomorphism with b again gives a finite p-group homomorphism.
Thus there is a specified endomorphism b_p of P_p with
  b_p q_p=q_p b.                                      (CO.7)
Equation (CO.6) makes b_p the identity on the elementary abelian
quotient P_p/[P_p,P_p]P_p^p.

Here is the full generation argument for this particular target.
A proper closed b_p(P_p) would have a proper image in a finite
p-group quotient: separate a point outside it by a finite quotient
and use compactness of the closed image. That proper finite image
lies in a maximal subgroup of index p. Pulling back the quotient
to C_p gives a nonzero continuous character P_p->C_p vanishing
on b_p(P_p), contrary to the elementary abelian identity. Therefore
b_p is surjective.

A finitely generated profinite group is Hopfian by the following
finite-set argument. It has finitely many open normal subgroups of
each given index, since there are finitely many homomorphisms from
a fixed finite generating set to any finite group of that order.
Inverse image under a surjective endomorphism permutes this finite
set. Iterating backwards through that permutation shows its kernel
is in every open normal subgroup, so its kernel is trivial.
Apply this to P_p. The map b_p is a continuous automorphism, with
its unique continuous inverse b_p^-1. Equations (CO.5) transport to
  b_p^-1 alpha_p b_p=delta_p,
  b_p^-1 omega_p b_p=omega_p.                          (CO.8)
Every term is induced by the named original quotient map (CO.7).

These statements also retain the full finite extension map.
Let G60,p and G6,p be the quotients by the respective characteristic
kernels F0->F0^(p) and F->F^(p). The map Upsilon descends to
  Upsilon_p:G60,p->G6,p.                              (CO.9)
It is b_p on the kernel and the identity on C2 x C3. Thus it is
an isomorphism. Its inverse sends the kernel element u to b_p^-1(u)

with all the relations because they invert (CO.9), and normal

b_p^-1 images; they do not supply additional full original inputs.

Every finite nilpotent quotient of F is filled by b(F). For its
