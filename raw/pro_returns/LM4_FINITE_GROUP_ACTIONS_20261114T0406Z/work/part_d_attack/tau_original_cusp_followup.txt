A. Full centralizers and normalizers of the actual image cusps
-------------------------------------------------------------
Here is the elementary centralizer argument used for these images.
Use a full free profinite group W and an element t in W admitting a
continuous character chi:W->Zhat with chi(t)=1. The instances below
are R, F0, and the explicitly identified F_* in Section E. The power map
Zhat-><t>, lambda->t^lambda, is an isomorphism: composition with chi
is the identity. Set C=C_W(t), K_t=ker(chi|C). The explicit inverse
continuous maps
  K_t x <t> -> C, (k,t^lambda) -> k t^lambda,
  C -> K_t x <t>, u -> (u t^(-chi(u)),t^chi(u))
give C=K_t x <t>.

For completeness K_t is trivial. A nontrivial profinite K_t has a
nontrivial finite quotient, so for some prime p one of its pro-p Sylow
subgroups is nontrivial. Choose a nonidentity element v in that
subgroup. The free profinite W has cd_p(W)=1; consequently it has no
element of order p. The closed subgroup <v> is therefore Z_p. The
p-factor <t>_p is another Z_p, centralizes <v>, and has trivial
intersection with it by chi. Thus multiplication embeds the compact
group Z_p x Z_p as a closed subgroup of W. Its nonzero degree-two
cohomology contradicts cd_p(W)=1 and inheritance of that bound by
closed subgroups. Hence
  C_W(t)=<t>.
For u in N_W(<t>), write u t u^(-1)=t^lambda with
lambda in Zhat^times. Applying chi gives lambda=1, so also
  N_W(<t>)=<t>.                                             (A1)
This argument concerns the entire generator t. No assertion about
centralizers of individual p-components or malnormality is made.

For i(c_j), use the character on R obtained from q4|R followed by,
respectively, the x-coordinate, the y-coordinate, or minus the
x-coordinate of F_ab. It takes value 1 on the indicated element.
For tau(c_j), use the corresponding character on F0; the identity
abelianization of tau gives the same value 1. Thus (A1) applies to
all six actual image elements.

Choose an actual reflection r_j in D_j. The maps i and tau preserve
parity, so i(r_j) and tau(r_j) are outside the respective parity
kernels, have order two, and invert their image rotations. Every
normalizer element inside the parity kernel is a rotation by (A1).
Multiplication by the displayed reflection sends every outside
normalizer element inside. Therefore the exact full normalizers are
  N_P4(i(L_j))=i(L_j) semidirect <i(r_j)>=i(D_j),
  N_P3(tau(L_j))=tau(L_j) semidirect <tau(r_j)>=tau(D_j).     (A2)
The restriction tau|D_j is injective: a kernel element cannot have
odd parity; a rotation c_j^lambda in its kernel has lambda=0 after
applying the character. Compactness then makes it a homeomorphism
onto the closed subgroup in (A2). In particular rho|i(D_j) is the
specified isomorphism onto tau(D_j), for all three original cusps.
