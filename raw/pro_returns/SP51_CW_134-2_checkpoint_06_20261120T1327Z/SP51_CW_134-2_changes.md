## Consequential corrections

- Scan 172, L7 addition, complete parenthesis: Against H: restores the specified sheaf coefficients, sets or modules, left unread in H, and attaches the parenthesis to faisceaux. The C-side differences here concern punctuation and doubt resolution.
- Scan 175, Diagram, left parenthetic comparison: Against H: restores the left comparison glyph as \underline{\approx}, two waves above a straight rule, rather than \simeq. This corrects the mathematical sign without assigning a different equivalence semantics.
- Scan 175, Diagram, right parenthetic comparison: Against H: restores the right comparison glyph before X_1 as \underline{\approx}, two waves above a straight rule, rather than \simeq; its exact semantic convention is not inferred.
- Scan 178, D1–D2 target objects in the corner diagram: Against H: removes the two supplied terminal object bullets from the corner diagram. The ink has one source point and two outgoing arrows with unwritten targets; no mathematical absence of target objects is asserted.
- Scan 180, L13, adjective after morphismes: Against C: the adjective qualifying the diagram morphisms does not support verticaux. The ink favours virtuels, with mutuels retained, so the consolidated text does not supply a definite verticality condition.
- Scan 181, L18, des types: Against H: des types d’homotopie fin restores the explicitly plural types where H has du type. The source’s singular fin is retained; no additional quantifier or claim is inferred.
- Scan 182, L20, connector after H^j(X,N): Against C/H: the product connector has a right terminal upright; dashv-shaped is preferred with arrow/plain-line alternatives. H’s resolved right arrow is not supported at that certainty. This is a graphic reading, not an assertion of an adjunction relation.
- Scan 182, L21, middle sign of output degree: Against H: the degree sign is not securely plus. Preserve the ink-led preferred i·j with i+j and ij alternatives; the usual expected product grading does not decide the tiny mark.
- Scan 183, L5 argument separator: Against C/H: the argument separator of H^i has an additional upper point. Semicolon is preferred, with comma plus a detached point retained; expected cohomology notation does not determine the mark.
