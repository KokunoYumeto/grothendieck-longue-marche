## Belyi

Original 1979; English translation 1980. **Theorem 4, §4, p.255**: algebraic-number definability and three-point covers. **Corollary, p.256**, with **§1, pp.248–249**: outer injectivity. Later restatement: **2002, Theorem 1, p.329**. Access: English PDF text opened directly; extraction loses overbars. Preserve the distinctions between \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) and \(\mathbb Q\), and between at most three branch values and exactly three.

## Scope cautions

Do not infer \(G_{\mathbb Q}=GT\), GT faithfulness on the narrower \(C_2^+\) category, or transfer between completions. Retain orientation; quotienting can obstruct descent.
