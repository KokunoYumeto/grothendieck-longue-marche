
## Primary references and exact verification limits

The literature was consulted only after the transcription was frozen. These author–date references supply comparison sources, not evidence of what an ambiguous manuscript stroke says. Source webpages and excerpts were retrieved on **16 November 2026**; the full source files are excluded from the ZIP. The suffixes 2026a–2026e distinguish the Stacks Project entries alphabetically by title.

**P1.** Deligne, P. (1974). Théorie de Hodge: III. *Publications Mathématiques de l’IHÉS, 44*, 5–77. [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02685881](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02685881). [Original journal record](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1974__44__5_0/) and [original article PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1974__44__5_0.pdf). The extracted primary text of §§10.1.5–10.1.10 and 10.2.1–10.2.6, printed pp. 56–64, was inspected for the two-term, torsion and pairing comparisons; §10.2.1, p. 60 is the precise locator used in N34. This supplies a published comparison, not an attribution or dating of the present manuscript. No visual inspection of those journal pages is claimed: retrieval returned textual extraction, while image calls returned no usable image payload.

**P2.** The Stacks Project. (2026e). *Kummer theory* (Section 59.28, tag 03PK). Retrieved November 16, 2026, from [https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK). Lemma 59.28.1 treats invertible n on the étale site; Lemma 59.28.3 gives the flat/syntomic version. These exact distinctions are used in N45, without identifying the manuscript’s zeta-shaped coefficient by typography alone.

**P3.** The Stacks Project. (2026a). *The Artin–Schreier sequence* (Section 59.63, tag 0A3J). **P4.** The Stacks Project. (2026d). *Ext sheaves* (Section 20.43, tag 0BQP). Retrieved November 16, 2026, from [https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BQP](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BQP). ### Limits of the SGA collation

The original full SGA 7 I scan at [the SLMath archive](https://library.slmath.org/nonmsri/sga/sga/pdf/sga7-1.pdf) was located, but retrieval refused its approximately 37.4 MB size. Individual image pages were located at [printed page 242](https://wstein.org/sga/sga/7-1/7-1t_242.html), [341](https://wstein.org/sga/sga/7-1/7-1t_341.html), and [300](https://wstein.org/sga/sga/7-1/7-1t_300.html); image calls yielded no usable pixels in this environment, and a direct image transfer was denied. Consequently this return does **not** claim an independent visual or full-text verification of VIII §2, VIII 7.1(b), or IX 2.6(a), and does not upgrade CT N97’s reported comparison into a newly verified result. The successful comparisons are the specific Deligne and Stacks passages identified above; modern retranscriptions or translations encountered in search were not used as replacement authority for unseen original pages.

The abbreviated Serre reference on scan 007, the Néron-completion reference requested on 008, the precise source of the reused Lie text on 038, and “[T.F.]” on 049 remain unidentified. These are explicit bibliographic limits, not evidence of novelty or falsehood. The current task supplies a diplomatic witness and research leads, not an exhaustive originality review of the surrounding archive.
