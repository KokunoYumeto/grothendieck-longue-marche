SIX-CUP QUOTIENT: ACTUAL SCALAR CLOSURE AT EVERY INTEGRAL LEVEL
Independent proof audit, 2026-11-16.  The original base C is retained.

Notation and inputs already established.
Pi=C2 coproduct C3, R=R_nil(K0)=[R,Pi], C=Pi/R.  Let N be the
actual normal pro-odd subgroup of C whose finite image is the diagonal
odd weight subgroup of H.  The integral residual abelianizations are
A_n=L_n^4 plus V^4, where L_n is the O_n=GR(2^n,3) monomial lattice.
Their N-invariants and N-coinvariants vanish.  The A62 residual P_n
is class two, with abelianization A_n and globally central derived
subgroup M_n.  The exact old center theorem gives

             (exterior^2_Z A_n)_{O^2(H)} = M_n,

and H acts trivially on M_n.  The six-cup quotient adds
T=(V^(2))^6, central inside the residual but nontrivial under H.
Its actual residual Q_n has

                 Q_n^ab=A_n,    Q_n'=M_n plus T.

The surjectivity and direct-product assertion in the second formula
hold in the actual joint source quotient: the old center is retained,
and the image of the old relatively perfect kernel on the new group
is all T.  Let W_n=ker(Pi->the actual joint quotient over C).

Claim.
Every W_n is relatively perfect: [W_n,Pi]=W_n.  Consequently their
descending intersection W_infty, the actual six-cup kernel over
D_infty, is relatively perfect as well.  This is a scalar-kernel
statement; action/frame realization still uses its separately checked
source automorphisms and A3/R2 hypotheses.

1. Reduction to a finite central refinement.
Take any finite quotient Z of W_n/[W_n,Pi], and form the actual
central refinement Pi/W, where W is its kernel in W_n.  The residual
M=R/W is a finite central extension of Q_n by Z.  Since R=[R,Pi],
the C-coinvariants of M^ab are zero.  If a scalar prime is odd, the
central extension of the finite two-group Q_n by that scalar group
splits by coprime averaging, as a direct product.  Its odd factor
is characteristic and fixed by the entire conjugation action, giving
nonzero C-coinvariants unless it is zero.  Thus it suffices to consider
a dyadic scalar group Z, and M is a finite two-group.

2. The abelianization is still exactly A_n.
The kernel J of M^ab->A_n is a quotient of the globally central Z,
so C acts trivially on J.  Averaging over the actual finite odd image
of N is exact and C-equivariant (N is normal).  Since A_n^N=0,
the invariant subgroup (M^ab)^N is exactly J.  Averaging splits it
as a C-module direct summand.  It would therefore survive in the
C-coinvariants of M^ab, which are zero.  Hence J=0 and M^ab=A_n.
In particular Z is contained in M'.  This also recovers the relevant
original-base A53 abelianization argument without assuming that the
new quotient over D_infty has vanishing coefficient H1.

3. The possible third commutators and their exact Jacobi vanishing.
Since Q_n is class two and Z is central, M has class at most three.
The third lower-central subgroup is central and is a C-equivariant
bilinear image

                    (M_n plus T) tensor_Z A_n -> gamma3(M).

The M_n summand pairs to zero: it is C-trivial and (A_n)_N=0,
while gamma3(M) is C-trivial.  The T summand factors through A_n/2,
and its image has exponent two.  Thus extend scalars from F2 to
k=F8 for the remaining calculation; faithful field extension loses
no information.

Restriction of scalars from k yields the three Frobenius weight
families lambda, 2lambda, 4lambda.  The coordinatewise cup bracket
has only within-family, same-weight terms.  In each family its T
weight 2lambda is attained by the bracket of two different cusp-row
vectors of weight lambda, with arbitrary scalar coefficient.  To
pair this vector invariantly into a trivial scalar module, an A-vector
must have weight -2lambda.  These weights have order seven, so
-2lambda differs from lambda (3lambda is nonzero).  It is in a different weight/family from
the two chosen lambda vectors.  Consequently the other two T brackets
in the Jacobi identity vanish.  They may have old-M_n bracket
components, but those components have already been shown to pair
trivially with A_n.  The class-three commutator Jacobi identity now
forces the desired [T,A_n] pairing to vanish.

This argument applies to each of the three Frobenius families and all
six cup coordinates, which generate T.  It explicitly retains the
Frobenius twist: it does not incorrectly claim Hom_H(T tensor A_n,F2)
is zero.  That abstract space can be nonzero; its maps are eliminated
by the actual cup bracket and Jacobi identity.  Thus gamma3(M)=1.

4. Exact old-center comparison kills the scalar kernel.
Now M is class two, so M' is a finite H-equivariant quotient of
exterior^2_Z A_n; its H-action is determined by its action on A_n.
Odd averaging gives an exact surjection

            E0=(M')^N -> (Q_n')^N=M_n

with kernel Z, because T^N=0 and Z is globally central.  The action
on E0 is an extension of the trivial actions on M_n and Z.  Its
image is a finite two-group (the kernel acts through homomorphisms
M_n->Z).  Therefore O^2(H) acts trivially on E0.  The surjective
map from the exterior square to E0, obtained by composing with the
averaging projection, factors through its O^2(H)-coinvariants.
The exact old-center calculation identifies that quotient with M_n.
Hence E0 is a quotient of M_n and simultaneously surjects onto M_n.
Finiteness gives equality of orders and Z=0.

Every finite scalar quotient is therefore zero, proving
W_n/[W_n,Pi]=1 and the claim at level n.  No nonsplitting or scalar
depth statement was substituted for this actual kernel calculation.

5. The full integral intersection.
The actual kernels W_n descend with n and W_infty=intersection W_n.
For every finite quotient q of Pi, compactness gives
q(W_infty)=intersection q(W_n); the finite descending sequence on the
right stabilizes.  Since [W_n,Pi]=W_n, its stable value is relatively
perfect in q(Pi).  Therefore [q(W_infty),q(Pi)]=q(W_infty) in every
finite quotient.  It follows that [W_infty,Pi]=W_infty in Pi.

All literal negative-square and normalizer requirements remain the
separate finite-law checks, which now pass for the new zero-t tuple.
