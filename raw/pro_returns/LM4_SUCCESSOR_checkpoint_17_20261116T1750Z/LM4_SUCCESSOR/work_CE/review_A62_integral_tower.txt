2. Independent audit of the all-n s-centralizer step.

The chosen N7-character has stabilizer <s> in Q6=S3 x A4. Its negative
is beta0 times that character. If s exchanged an opposite pair, then
q^-1 s q would lie in beta0<s>. But beta0 has trivial S3 component,
whereas s does not; beta0*s has trivial A4 component, whereas s has a
nontrivial double-transposition component. Neither can be conjugate
to s in this direct product. Odd translations alter the scalar phases
but not the weight permutation. Therefore no opposite pair is
exchanged by s. Orient pair orbits equivariantly; fixed pairs have
both weights individually fixed, so this introduces no ambiguity.
In this orientation the monomial class-two lift of s has zero carry.

An s-fixed vector a in 2L_n^4 now lifts to an actual s-centralizer as
(a,0). The source s has zero free cusp part, mixed binary products
vanish because a reduces0, and the orientation carry is identically0.
This is the decisive all-n repair. The n=2 argument based merely on
quadratic terms being divisible by4 would not extend by itself.

4. Compactness does not require surjective frame transitions.

Let F_n be the solution set of the two conjugacy/norm equations at
level n with fixed original C frame images and any fixed finite
collection of older-family constraints. The kernel over that base is
finite, so this is a nonempty compact finite set after the action is
fixed. Reduction sends a solution at level n+1 to one at n, but the
map need not be onto. Nevertheless any finite collection of
compatibility equations is satisfied by choosing a solution at its
largest level and projecting downward. Thus the usual finite
intersection property in the product of the F_n gives a compatible
point. The known fixed-witness failure is fully consistent with this.

Aut(Pi) is profinite: finite generation gives cofinal characteristic
finite quotients, and compatible automorphisms on them reconstruct a
continuous automorphism. The peripheral conditions and all literal

Put U_infty=intersection_n U_n. Compactness gives

There is also a useful direct perfectness fact. For any finite quotient

    [q(U_infty),q(Pi)] = q(U_infty),

Thus the closed relative R2 criterion can also be applied directly to

assumption that arbitrary intersections of unrelated relative kernels
