Reviewer: CE / torus_real
Status: PASS; formal sources read in full after checkpoint14 release.

A82. The actual C43 hypotheses are stated for the one-section case. Compactness refines the finite action head so that projected finite centralizers act through each actual C2, while finite involution heads remain distinct and nontrivial. The allowed-class embedding problem consequently prescribes local H1 classes, rather than just arbitrary conjugacy classes. The H2 splitting argument applies to finite continuous cocycle extensions. The Ext/Shapiro sequence gives projectivity against every finite p-primary coefficient, and finite generation plus compact finite lifting sets upgrades this to compact projectivity.

Restriction to the full free subgroup gives rank n+1. The induction counit, not a sign-idempotent decomposition, is split at p=2. Reduction modulo2, finite-length cancellation, and common-refinement compactness give an actual compatible basis. Compact Nakayama and the projective splitting then give R2 free. The incidence cokernels identify exactly the reduced E/H component modules in both the three-section and regular one-section cases. A surjective incidence map between the resulting equal-rank free modules is injective by its finite ring reductions. None of these facts proves the selected incidence map surjective.

A83. The exact homology sequence is valid because the finite subgroup K meets all section stabilizers trivially. The boundary module is the signed permutation module modulo the total-sum line, with no claimed splitting when p divides the cusp count. Compact homology carries the actual orientation-twisted nondegenerate intersection form. The finite dihedral cellular sequence remains valid when the rotation image has order1. Summing the six endpoints uses exactly the audited C28 triangle; cancellation of the integer2 is in the free abelian group G0, independent of characteristic. The final equality is only a G0 identity in the modular case. In the semisimple case the self-dual multiplicity is 2 dim_End_Q(S) S, as required over nonsplitting finite fields.

Scope retained: no finite graph recognition, no full marked generation, no compact odd self-duality or universal odd freeness. The explicit cokernel formulas isolate rather than close the generation gate.
