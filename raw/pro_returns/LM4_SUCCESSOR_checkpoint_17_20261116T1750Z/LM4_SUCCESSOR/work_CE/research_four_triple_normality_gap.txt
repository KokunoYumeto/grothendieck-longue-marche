2. Exact partial presentation

Consider ONLY the retained-node relation [c,b]=1 and these four
transported triple-point relation families, before imposing the other
filled critical values or the retained base actions. The local family
says that its ordered three-meridian product commutes with all three
local meridians. Put R_i=P_i Q_i. Its relation with b first says
[R_i,b]=1. The other two actual meridians generate, after allowed
conjugations by b and by Q_i, precisely <P_i,Q_i>. Hence the family is
equivalent to
 b P_i b^-1=R_i^-1 P_i R_i,
 b Q_i b^-1=R_i^-1 Q_i R_i.
The fourth product bR4 gives the same equations. Conversely these two
equations imply [R_i,b]=1 and all original local commutators, so no
extra relation has been inserted.

Define eta on A to fix c and conjugate each free rank-two factor
<P_i,Q_i> by R_i^-1. This is an actual automorphism of A. The partial
group is therefore the HNN extension
 G_partial=<H,b | b a b^-1=eta(a), a in A>
           =(A semidirect_eta Z)*F10.
This identifies the quantified gap, rather than merely warning that
normal generation and subgroup generation differ.

Indeed take u=Q4=x9 and t=x3. Then k=t u t^-1 belongs to K. The word
b k b^-1=b t u t^-1 b^-1 is outside H by the reduced free-product
normal form above (equivalently Britton's lemma). Thus b does not
normalize the image of the ENTIRE H-normal deletion kernel in this
partial group, although it normalizes each of the four designated
pair subgroups and those pairs normally generate K under H.

3. Small finite certificate, including profinite scope

There is a single explicit quotient of this partial presentation:
 b -> (34), Q4=x9 -> (12), t=x3 -> (13),
 all other original special-basis generators -> identity.
All four actual triple relation families and [c,b] hold. The source
H maps onto S3 on {1,2,3}; the partial ambient image is S4. But
 k=t u t^-1 -> (23),
 b k b^-1 -> (24),
and (24) does not belong to the source S3. This finite separation also
proves failure of b-normality for the corresponding actual source
images in the full profinite completion of the partial presentation.
No comparison between abstract and induced completions of an
infinite-index subgroup is used.

4. Why this does NOT settle the actual marginal

The checker also evaluates the two actual retained base monodromies
using the original generic basis; its old x10 image must be c b^-1,
which here equals (34). The base2 monodromy fixes the entire tuple.
The base1 monodromy changes positions3,10,11, and no element of S4
conjugates the original tuple to the changed tuple. Thus this S4
representation cannot be extended to the actual total-pencil group.
It is a precise counterexample to using the four triples ALONE, not
an actual marginal counterexample. The remaining filled relations
and/or the actual retained base actions must be used in any successful
uniform-normality proof. No finite-group census has been performed.
