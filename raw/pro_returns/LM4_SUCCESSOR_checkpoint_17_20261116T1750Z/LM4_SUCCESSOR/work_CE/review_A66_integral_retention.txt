Independent integral-tower audit of A66, 16 November 2026
Verdict: the nonzero free t causes no norm-lifting obstruction. The
proposed paragraph needs the following explicit compatible-action
construction; an arbitrary full h0 need not have integral W=0.

Let D_n be the actual scalar-stable eight-class integral quotient of A62,
and D_n^f the analogous quotient retaining only the four free cusp
classes. Both are actual Pi quotients, by A58. Forgetting the four binary
classes gives D_n -> D_n^f, naturally compatible with reduction. The
actual quotient identity is
  D_n = D_1 fiber-product_(D_1^f) D_n^f.
It can be checked over the common initial C on residual abelianizations
and centers. The abelianization identity is
 (L_n^4 + V^4) = (V^8) fiber-product_(V^4) L_n^4.
The full center is O_n^6 + k^30, while the free center is O_n^6 + k^4
and its mod2 reduction is k^10. All mixed/binary terms occur only modulo2.
The natural maps on these central coordinates are injective into, and
onto, the corresponding fiber product. The same then holds for the
actual groups. Equivalently it follows directly from their compatible
source cocycle/group laws; the binary s carry belongs to the D1 factor.
The free source s has zero free cusp value, and the s-invariant
opposite-pair orientation gives no free orientation carry. The free r
source is the prescribed conjugate of pure r.

The old W0 positive action is literally identity on D_1^f: its free
linear block is identity, its free inner vector is zero, and its free
central character is zero after source normalization. Root independently
checked this with the actual free-center10 projection in
code/check_diagonal_scalar_frame_lift.py. Pair that identity with the
identity on D_n^f and the given old action on D1. This constructs
compatible actual actions on D_n with W_n=0 for all n, rather than
assuming that an arbitrary full old lift has that property. Canonical c
commutes with the natural projections; on the free factor it is the
actual source involution. The negative action squares to one on the
fiber product.

Now let p=wc+t be the desired free affine s-frame. Although the negative
coefficient action is -C integrally, A60's exact affine norm equation is
 (1-D)t=0,  D=rho(beta) C,
with the positive monomial D. The conjugacy equation is S t=t. These
are two commuting Teichmuller monomial involutions. Their stabilizers
have order dividing4 and no nontrivial character into the order-seven
unit group. Orbitwise rescaling makes their joint action a permutation
module. Thus every mod2 jointly fixed t lifts to an integral jointly
fixed vector, at every n and compatibly by compactness. One must not
replace this argument by a generic assertion that mod2 fixed vectors
lift.

For the central conjugacy equation, compare with any actual source
conjugator at that level. The free affine difference is doubled and
S-fixed, and A62's no-opposite-pair-flip orientation lemma lifts it to
an actual s-centralizer. Mixed binary terms vanish on the doubled
difference. The remaining normalizer square lies in2 O_n^6. The negative
action is +1 on this center, so independent central halves kill it.
These halves have zero coordinate in the new K20 factor. They may alter
the discretionary D1 central frame; they retain the selected initial C
and older unrelated families. This is exactly the existential compact
compatibility used by A62, not surjectivity for every prescribed new
central frame.

The new K20 frame is retained by the fiber product, and its s norm was
separately checked. The r-frame is obtained from the selected initial
transporter/A16 at each finite common refinement. Finite intersections
and maximum-level arguments then give simultaneous integral retention.
No realization through the still-uncomputed next scalar refinement is
claimed by this paragraph.
