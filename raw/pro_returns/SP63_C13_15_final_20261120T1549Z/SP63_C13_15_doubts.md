## Legibility and witness comparison

The two readings cover all 25 supplied scans. The three reused typed sheets (160, 162, 164) are generally clear after rotation; their small origin glyphs on 160 and 162 remain o/0. The covers (157, 172) contain only short titles and archive numbers. Most live manuscript text is readable, but several small interlinear words, overwritten exponents, and heavily cancelled passages remain insecure. These limitations are marked in place and listed above.

The least secure material is the nested cancellation on 148, the small extra tensor mark and flattened ξ on 161, the cancelled lower half of 163, overwritten symbols on 165, the unexplained mark after Q_l and three compressed p-grps qualifications on 173, the leading operator and lower twist on 174, and the damaged connecting degree and trailing fragments on 177. Scan 179 has several obscured words within the cancelled draft and a partly obscured coefficient in the surviving connecting formula. Those readings have not been completed from mathematical expectation.

C supplied the fuller diplomatic structure, including many cancellations, insertion states, and drawn connectors.
