# Comparison before freezing

The root transcriber alone performed the visual comparisons. No contextual file contents, OCR, published transcription, or external mathematical reference was used to decide these readings.

## Comparisons and decisions

- The gamma/chi/beta/xi group was compared in the first mathematical sheet and in scans 148, 150, 152 and 156. Small looped gamma and crossed descending chi remain distinct; the overwritten xi/beta and the chi/gamma coefficients in 146 remain doubtful. Delta on 156 has a taller, open-topped stroke than the gamma forms.
- The barred and unbarred variables, pi, tau, sigma and psi forms were compared across those sheets and 160–174. No bar was supplied from the involution formulas. The cap over the double-loop varpi forms was checked in 164, 166, 168, 170 and 174; local omega/varpi and barred-pi alternatives on 154 and 164 remain.
- The exclamation-mark exponent on 154 was enlarged and compared with both four-shaped marks on 160 and the overwritten vertical mark on 174. The clear 154 mark is `!`; 160 remains `4|+`; 174 remains doubtful, with `!|4`, because its small overwritten body is not a secure ordinary prime. The low prime/dot in 182 and the added prime/1 after 112 on 193 were also retained as local doubts.
- The small bent opposed-arrow symbol was compared on every page where it occurs: 172, 176, 178, 180, 184 and 190. Enlarged samples show a left-pointing upper part and right-pointing lower part. The approximation `\leftrightarrow` remains first; `\leftrightarrows` replaces the differently oriented `\rightleftarrows` as the alternative. This comparison does not resolve the author's intended name or mathematical meaning for the sign.
- Vee and wedge forms were compared on 160, 168, 170, 174 and 192. Dual superscripts and the ordinary primes are kept distinct; cancellations where the sign itself is overwritten retain their alternatives.
- O-shaped coefficients, zero and underlining were compared on 172, 178, 180, 182, 184, 188, 190 and 192. The O/0 alternatives remain unresolved, and lower strokes use the requested `\uline` macro. Earlier looped O forms and the possible base k in 160 remain as recorded in the notation table.
- The A-shaped objects in the lower diagram of 160 were compared with those on 174 and later sheets; the preferred glyph is now the same Latin A, with the calligraphic alternative explicitly retained. Heavy overwritten E/A/F forms are not taken as intentional boldface.
- The identity-like marks on 168 remain locally uncertain; no arrow or equality was inferred from the diagram's expected commutativity. Full-size double-headed arrows on 174, 180, 186 and 192 remain distinct from the compact opposed-arrow operator.
- The lower horizontal labels on 154, the central curved-arrow label on 186, and both lower labels and the upper circle on 190 were compared directly against the ink. Their final layout preserves the observed sides of the shafts and the superscript outside the upper circle. The independent worker validated the generated layouts only, using the root's positional descriptions.
- All 48 light edge numbers 145–192 are consistently marginal notes. The printed typescript pagination remains `\mspage`; authorship is not inferred for the pencil foliation. Typed quotation marks consistently use two backticks and two apostrophes; no guillemets were introduced.

## Coverage and limits

The notation table at the top of the doubts file includes the recurring Latin and Greek forms, exponents, arrows, bars, tensor and join signs, numbering, quotation marks and abbreviations. The comparison resolves only variations supported by the ink and leaves the others explicit. Tabular blocks are wrapped with `\diagram`; simple equation cases remain standard mathematical arrays. Inventory classifications were brought into agreement with the final tabular and marginal elements, including nested and cancelled diagrams.

The technical validation is a separate activity. It does not constitute another palaeographic reading, and successful TeX compilation does not validate the mathematics.
