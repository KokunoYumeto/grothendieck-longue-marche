# Notation table

This table records the signs compared during the single-reader transcription and the second reading of all 47 scans, before the context archive was opened. The encoding follows the visible strokes at each occurrence; competing readings remain marked in the page fragments. Scan numbers refer to cote 72. A change of letter or indexing made on the manuscript is retained, together with its cancellations and warnings.
