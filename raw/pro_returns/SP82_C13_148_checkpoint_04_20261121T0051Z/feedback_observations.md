The following observations are based on the pages completed so far and will be reconciled after the blind readings.

- A drawing crossing a text block can be mistaken for cancellation: scan 026, covering sentence. The deletion status must be decided independently of the word.
- Hollow centres in heavy boundary points can disappear in schematic drawing transcriptions: scans 026 (three-point ovals and ring chain) and 027 (central paired circles).
- A short free stroke can be turned into a joining edge: scan 024, middle small sphere.
- Witness H occasionally appends material from the following physical sheet: scan 028, terminal M_{0,5} heading, to be located on its actual sheet.
- A separate cancelled glyph can be conflated with an overwritten live letter: scan 024, the doubtful F before the first quoted J.
