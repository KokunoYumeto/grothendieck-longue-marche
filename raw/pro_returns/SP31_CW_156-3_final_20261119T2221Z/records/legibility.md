The difference counts are counts of localized recorded decisions, sometimes grouping linked letters or states in one passage; they are not word-error rates and cannot validate the benchmark rates quoted in the task. Agreement of C and H was never treated as proof of a reading.

Remaining uncertainty may concern a word, alphabet, punctuation, or the deletion state itself. Confidence in the remaining-doubt table concerns the preferred ink reading: medium is tentative, low is weak; no unresolved reading is labeled high merely because its mathematical meaning is plausible.
