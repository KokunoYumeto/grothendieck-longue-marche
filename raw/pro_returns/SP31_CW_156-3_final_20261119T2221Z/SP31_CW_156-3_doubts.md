## Legibility and witnesses

The main hand is usually readable, but its compression increases sharply in interlinear revisions and at the lower edges. Scans 003, 008, 010, 012 and 018–022 have particularly difficult slanted or crossing notes; the NB tail of 018, the lower side note of 019, the last line of 020 and the cancelled draft of 025 deserve the least confidence. Faint outline watermarks are incidental paper evidence and remain low-confidence, with no complete brand name inferred. Crops are retained so a human reader can revisit decisions; this remains an AI collation, not a human-certified edition.

C supplied much of the diplomatic structure and many valuable doubts, but it also omitted ink, confused some cancellation states and occasionally attached crossing material to the wrong note. H was useful as an independent alternative, while often expanding abbreviations, normalizing syntax, omitting old layers or leaving legible material unread. The difference counts are counts of localized recorded decisions, sometimes grouping linked letters or states in one passage; they are not word-error rates and cannot validate the benchmark rates quoted in the task. Agreement of C and H was never treated as proof of a reading.

All 517 source-C doubt occurrences have a separate root disposition with crop evidence in `audit/source_C_unsure.tsv`, reproduced in the changes log. The alignment fields in that ledger are mechanical candidates, not independent ink judgments; explicit root reasons control, especially for removed or relocated material. Remaining uncertainty may concern a word, alphabet, punctuation, or the deletion state itself. Confidence in the remaining-doubt table concerns the preferred ink reading: medium is tentative, low is weak; no unresolved reading is labeled high merely because its mathematical meaning is plausible.

No OCR, text-recognition library, manuscript PDF text layer or TeX compilation was used. Structural validation checks files, macros, delimiters, inventory and audit coverage; it does not certify mathematical correctness or typographic rendering. The primary reader alone inspected the scans in one sequential first pass and one sequential second pass. A helper performed only mechanical file/TeX/ledger work before the freeze.
