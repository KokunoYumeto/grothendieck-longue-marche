## Cross-page notation comparison before freezing

The single reader compared recurring signs during the second visual reading of all 43 pages, then returned to enlarged examples on scans 091, 092, 100, 110 and 127. The distinctions below were checked against the ink before the context archive was opened; unresolved individual occurrences remain marked in place and in the doubt register. A difference genuinely present on the page has been retained rather than standardized away.

| Recurring sign or alphabet | LaTeX convention and comparison | Principal scans checked |
|---|---|---|
| Prime, double prime and triple prime | `'`, `''`, `'''`; separate small upper strokes on schemes, sheaves and morphisms. These were compared with the filled or crossed strokes used for stars; no unequivocal exclamation-mark exponent was identified in this package. The double upper marks in the added formula on 124 remain uncertain between a double prime and repetition marks. | 094, 102–103, 106–110, 117, 119, 124, 126–131 |
| Star versus prime | `^*` and `_*` for visibly asterisk-shaped marks; the crowded upper kernel on 091 and overwritten signs on 092 retain explicit doubts. Unit-group stars and pullback stars have the same reading, while primed objects remain distinct. | 091–092, 100, 116, 118–119, 123, 127, 130 |
| Small circles in exponents | `^0`, `^{00}`, and `^\omega` remain distinct; the two small circles on 091 are not read as a double prime. | 089, 091, 097, 101, 114 |
| Arrowheads and headless strokes | `\longrightarrow`, `\xrightarrow`, and TikZ arrowheads where visible; plain dashes, `dash`, or `no head` where a head is absent. Doubt remains at the explicitly marked weak arrows on 089 and 094; the upper q-arrow on 127 remains headless. | 089–094, 102–103, 105, 111, 118, 124, 126–131 |
| Parallel arrows and implications | Separate parallel arrows for relations/descent; `\Leftarrow` for the left implication on 105. The three return arcs on 127 point left and remain separate. | 105, 111, 117, 119, 127–129 |
| Inequalities and containment | `\geq`, `\leq`, `\subset`, `\supset`, and `\not\supset` follow the visible orientation; blank inequality positions on 132 have not been completed. | 097, 099–100, 108, 116, 120–124, 130–132 |
| Lowercase Greek | `\alpha`, `\beta`, `\eta`, `\lambda`, `\mu`, `\pi`, `\rho`, `\sigma`, `\tau`, `\omega`, `\varepsilon`, `\varphi`, `\psi`, `\theta` distinguish the visible forms; small ambiguous indices and the map sign on 122 remain marked. | 089–095, 097, 099–100, 105–110, 116–124, 126–132 |
| Uppercase Greek | `\Gamma`, `\Delta`, `\Theta`, `\Sigma`, `\Omega`; angular Sigma in the descent pages is distinguished from Z, and Delta in the difference of divisors from D. | 090, 095, 099–100, 110, 112, 126–128, 130 |
| Roman capitals and small indices | Ordinary X/Y/Z and i/j/1 are kept distinct where the ink allows. Z at the start of 121 was resolved in audit; the overwritten X/Z restrictions on 122–123 remain doubtful. | 089–132, especially 102, 121–124, 126–129 |
| Upright group/operator letters | `\mathrm` and `\operatorname` both encode upright Pic and operator names; this command-level variation is not a proposed distinction in the ink. TN, LN and N retain their separate labels. | 089–103, 105–131 |
| Underlining of sheaves and Pic | `\underline` for mathematical underbars; bare, singly underlined and doubly underlined occurrences are retained individually. The double Pic underbar on 110 is kept, and underlining is not propagated to later unmarked occurrences. | 089–094, 097, 099–103, 106–110, 117–132 |
| Structure-sheaf alphabet | `\mathcal O` for the script/looped structure-sheaf letter, with a separate underbar where visible. No generic rule supplies missing underbars. | 089–094, 099–103, 105–110, 117, 119–120, 126–128 |
| Bold/doubled letters | `\mathbf` for the manuscript's heavy or doubled P, Z and R; `\mathbb C` for the typed complex-number symbol. These are typographic representations of the visible alphabets, not substitutions of mathematical objects. | 090, 095, 099, 118, 121, 123 |
| Bars and tildes | `\bar A`, `\widetilde A`, `\widetilde\Omega`, `\overline{Z'_{\eta'}}`, and barred projections remain distinct; the completion bar on typed 117 is not a hat. | 100, 107, 112, 116–117, 127, 131 |
| Double quotation marks | Two TeX backticks and two apostrophes for paired double quotes. The isolated opening pairs used as ditto/replacement marks on 127 remain isolated; mathematical double primes are not quotation marks. | 090, 095, 100, 116–117, 122–123, 127 |
| Underlining in prose | `\uline` and `\uuline` distinguish one and two visible strokes, including Main Theorem and Démonstration. | 096–132, especially 106 and 113 |
| Limits and global-section symbols on reused typescripts | `\varprojlim` on 130 has a visible handwritten left arrow; plain `\lim` on 132 does not. Literal typed `\mathrm I` on 132 is not silently replaced by `\Gamma`, which is written by hand on 130. | 130, 132 |
| Abbreviations | Abbreviations and superscript endings remain abbreviated in the page text; possible expansions occur only in the abbreviation register below. The symbolic abbreviation `de $=$` is preserved. | 089–132, especially 101, 104, 107, 121, 127, 131 |
