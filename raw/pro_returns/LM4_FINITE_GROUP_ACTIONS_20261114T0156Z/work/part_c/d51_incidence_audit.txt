D.51: exact remaining section incidence and two finite-cover checks
=================================================================

This note records proved maps and character calculations. It does not
identify the projective sibling U with Fhat_2, and it does not assert
existence of an order-three action with a nontrivial multiplier.

1. The missing incidence is an actual group equation.

Use D51 and rescale its character so b1=1. Thus the three relevant lines
in Eab,(3), in coordinates (u,chi), are:

  first section S0:       (1,0);
  second section S1:      (1,1);
  cusp normalizer N:      (1,-1).

Let s generate S0 with u(s)=1, and let v be any element of N with u(v)=1.
The element vs has coordinates (2,-1)=(2,2), on the second section line.
This is an equality in the displayed abelian quotient only. It does
not say that vs has finite order or belongs to the second full section
class. Its cube lies in V, but is not known to be the identity.

The exact roots attached to the three s-translates of the cusp are

  v0=v, v1=s v s^{-1}, v2=s^2 v s^{-2}.

They lie in the three specified normalizers inside U, with the same
generator label in U/V. Since s^3=1, multiplication gives exactly

  v0 v1 v2 = (vs)^3.

Consequently the geometric three-root relation requires an element
of the labelled coset {v in N : u(v)=1}s that actually has order three.
The other section has the required abelian line, but D48-D51 do not
prove that its full conjugacy class meets that coset. Replacing v by
v c^z, changing the section representative by full F-conjugation, or
passing to a maximal pro-p quotient does not supply the missing full
equation. Even a chosen three-root relation would still require the
generation and peripheral identifications to be proved explicitly.

2. The characteristic abelian q-cover.

Let q differ from 3 and let lambda_q be a primitive cube root.
Let Q=F/F'F^q=(Fq)^2, N_q=ker(F -> Q), and let M be the matrix induced
by the actual order-three representative alpha. Since det M=lambda_q,
its eigenvalues are either {1,lambda_q} or {lambda_q^2,lambda_q^2}.
Their fixed-point counts on Q are respectively f=q and f=1.

Write alpha(c)=h c^lambda h^{-1}. On the cusp labels Q the induced
affine map is a -> M a + h_Q. Its cube is the identity: the full cube
conjugator centralizes c and lies in L, whose image in Q is zero.
Thus (I+M+M^2)h_Q=0. Since 3 is invertible on Q, h_Q is in the image
of I-M. Conjugating alpha by Inn(g), with g lifting the required
vector in Q, preserves its actual order and replaces h_Q by zero.
The resulting action on cusp labels is the specified linear M.

The exact cover has q^2 cusp lifts of degree one and compactification
a torus. Over Z3 the boundary classes give the augmentation module
of Z3[Q], and the deck-invariant summand identifies with the homology
of the original torus: the covering degree q^2 is a 3-adic unit.
Averaging over Q gives the actual invariant direct sum. The
order-three module is therefore

  H1(N_q,Z3) = Z3[zeta3]
              + Z3^(f-1)
              + Z3[C3]^((q^2-f)/3).

Here the first summand is the original two-dimensional torus lattice,
and the remaining summands come from the f fixed cusp labels and
the free triples of labels. In particular the rational trace is
f-2 and the high positive-degree mod-three cohomology of
E_q=N_q semidirect C3 has dimension f+1.

For the last assertion, the trivial, cyclotomic and regular summands
reduce to the Jordan blocks of lengths 1,2,3 respectively. Positive
cyclic cohomology sees one dimension from each of the first two
block types and none from the third. The two-row Hochschild-Serre
sequence has no differential into the row of the quotient C3:
its actual section makes every inflated quotient class nonzero.
There are no other possible differentials.

The scalar case f=1 has exactly two full section classes again, and
their centralizers are the same groups as in D48. Indeed each image
section in Q semidirect C3 is Q-conjugate to the specified section,
and its Q-centralizer is trivial. The full fixed groups therefore
map trivially to Q and are contained in N_q. There is one lifted
class above each of the two global classes. Their first mod-three
cohomology is still zero. The trace -1 and the high cohomology
dimension two are fully consistent with this calculation.

In the nonscalar case f=q, put C_i=C_F(S_i) after arranging both image
sections to be the specified C3 in Q semidirect C3. Their images lie
in Q^M=Cq. Let h_i be the size of that image (1 or q), and let
r_i=dim H1(C_i intersection N_q,F3).
The exact transporter cosets Q^M/image(C_i) show that the i-th full
section class splits into q/h_i classes in E_q.
Full Brown restriction and the preceding cohomology calculation give

  (q/h_0)(1+r_0) + (q/h_1)(1+r_1) = q+1.

In particular both images cannot be trivial: that would already
give 2q section classes. At least one of the full fixed groups
surjects onto the specified q-fixed line. This forces a nontrivial
finite quotient of a full fixed group but does not contradict
D48's vanishing of its maximal pro-three quotient.
No claim about arbitrary finite-index first-cohomology ranks of
these full fixed groups is inserted here.

3. The scalar branch in the characteristic Heisenberg q-cover.

Let Q_q be the class-two exponent-q quotient of order q^3 and suppose
the vector part M is lambda_q^2 I. The induced automorphism has the
form

  theta(r,z)=(lambda_q^2 r, lambda_q z + ell(r)).

It has no nonidentity fixed element: the first equation forces r=0,
and the second then forces z=0. Every order-three section of
Q_q semidirect C3 is Q_q-conjugate to the given section, by the
finite coprime complement theorem. Equivalently, in this particular
fixed-point-free case the map x -> x^{-1}theta(x) is a bijection:
equality of two values implies that their quotient is theta-fixed,
and finiteness then proves bijectivity. The full fixed groups in E
thus have trivial images in Q_q, and the corresponding cover
extension again has exactly the same two full section classes and
the same mod-three-acyclic centralizers.

There is also an exact independent homology trace check. Over a
splitting field of characteristic zero the nontrivial linear
characters of Q_q are indexed by nonzero vectors of the dual
abelian quotient. The scalar vector action places each in an
orbit of length three. The nonlinear irreducible representations
are indexed by the nontrivial central characters; multiplication
by lambda_q also places each in an orbit of length three.
Thus the induced alpha has trace zero on every orbit of nontrivial
deck isotypic summands. The deck-invariant homology is exactly the
base torus homology and has trace -1. Therefore

  tr(alpha | H1(ker(F -> Q_q),Q3)) = -1.

The q^2 cusp labels are Q_q/Z(Q_q); their affine action has a unique
fixed point because M-I is invertible. The boundary augmentation
module has trace zero. Filling those cusps consequently leaves
trace -1 on the compact-cover homology as well.
This supplies no contradiction to an order-three action and does
not determine its full multiplier.

The separate finite calculations in torus_order3_sibling_d51.py
already verify the two possible q-homology eigenvalue patterns.
The arguments in this note specify the additional full subgroup
and finite-cover maps; no finite group enumeration is promoted to
a full profinite incidence or generation theorem.
