An exact limit of the mod-two PD1 recognition route
==================================================

This is an unnumbered research record. It does not assert that the group below
occurs as the remaining fixed subgroup in D75.

Actual group and maps
---------------------

Let P be the free pro-3 group on x,y. Let

  A = product_{p != 3} Z_p,

with its named topological generator t, and let A act on P through
A -> Z_2 -> C_2 by the automorphism

  tau(x)=x, tau(y)=y^{-1}.

The full profinite semidirect product D=P semidirect A therefore has the actual
relations t x t^{-1}=x and t y t^{-1}=y^{-1}. The second equation means the
action of the named generator; the whole continuous action factors through
the named C_2 quotient.

Put a=x t and b=y. Since x and t commute, the full primary powers give

  a^{e_3}=x,   a^{1-e_3}=t.

Thus a,b topologically generate D, and the map

  pi: Fhat(X,Y) -> D,   X -> a, Y -> b

is an epimorphism. The closed subgroup generated by a is the direct product
<x> x A, and the map

  Zhat -> <a>,   n -> x^{n_3} t^{n_(3')}

is an isomorphism, with inverse given by the two named primary coordinates.
In particular it is a full procyclic group, not merely a pro-2 subgroup.

Projectivity, torsion and full abelianization
-------------------------------------------

The normal Sylow 3-subgroup is P. For p != 3 the named Z_p factor of A is a
Sylow p-subgroup: it maps onto the Sylow p-subgroup of A, and the kernel P is
pro-3. All Sylow subgroups are free pro-p groups. Hence D is projective by
the Sylow criterion for projectivity, explicitly recalled in Haran, Closed
Subgroups of G(Q) with Involutions, J. Algebra 129 (1990), 393-411, beginning
of Section 5, printed page 405:

  https://www.tau.ac.il/~haran/Papers/closed_subgp_invs.pdf

The criterion can also be checked prime by prime using the exact extension
P -> D -> A. For p=3 the quotient A has p-cohomological dimension zero and
P has dimension one. For p !=3, P has p-cohomological dimension zero and A
has dimension one. Therefore cd_p(D)<=1 for every p.

A finite-order element of D projects trivially to the torsion-free A and
then belongs to the torsion-free P. Thus D is torsion-free.

In abelianization, conjugation by t kills the y coordinate because
2 y_ab=0 in the torsion-free Z_3 module. It leaves the x coordinate.
Consequently the specified map

  D_ab -> Z_3 x A,
  x -> (1,0), y -> 0, t -> (0,1)

is an isomorphism. Its inverse is induced by the commuting images of x,t.
It identifies D_ab with Zhat, and a maps to its full generator.

Projectivity supplies a continuous section iota:D -> Fhat(X,Y) of pi.
The map on full abelianizations also splits. The vector iota(a)_ab maps
under pi_ab to 1 in Zhat and is therefore primitive in Zhat^2: its two
coordinates generate the unit ideal at every prime. This is the same
precise ambient primitivity condition used in D62. No free-basis claim for
iota(a) is needed.

The finite-coefficient mod-two duality comparison
------------------------------------------------

Let B be the closed normal pro-odd subgroup

  B=P x product_{p !=2,3} Z_p

inside D. The displayed direct product is literal because the odd-primary
factors of A act trivially on P. The quotient map D -> Z_2 kills B.

For every finite continuous 2-primary D-module M, averaging over the finite
odd image of B makes B-invariants exact. Restriction/inflation gives the
actual natural comparison

  H^n(D,M) = H^n(Z_2, M^B).

For finite F_2 modules, (M^*)^B -> (M^B)^* is the restriction map and is an
isomorphism: its inverse extends a functional after the canonical averaging
projector M -> M^B. The evaluation pairing and cup products are respected.
Thus the degree-one Poincare duality pairing of Z_2 transports to the
corresponding pairing for D, for every such finite module. The orientation
character is trivial. The same averaging works at every finite 2-power
coefficient level.

For the usual compact-coefficient formulation, normalized averages over
finite odd quotients of B form a compatible central idempotent in Z_2[[D]].
Its summand is the inflation of Z_2[[Z_2]]; the two-term Z_2 resolution of
the trivial module inflates to finitely generated projectives over
Z_2[[D]]. The compact-coefficient duality calculation is therefore the
same as for Z_2. This records PD1 at 2 with its actual quotient and
averaging maps.

In particular H^1(D,F_2) has dimension one.

Exact remaining scope
---------------------

D contains the nonabelian group P, so it is not procyclic. The inclusion
P -> D is the actual semidirect-product kernel map, and x,y do not commute
in the free pro-3 group. It therefore has all of the properties just proved:
projectivity, torsion-freeness, two generators, a full procyclic subgroup
mapping isomorphically to D_ab=Zhat, a split embedding into Fhat_2 with that
generator of primitive full ambient homology, and PD1 at 2.

These computations isolate what a proposed PD1-at-2 continuation of D75
would still have to use: an additional equation from the actual fixed
subgroup, the original cusp, or the full extension. They do not provide
an occurrence of this D as a surface-action centralizer.
