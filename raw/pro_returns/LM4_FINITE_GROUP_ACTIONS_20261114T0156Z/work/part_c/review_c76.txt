The already audited C.37, C.62 and C.63 constructions are used with
their stated actual source paths and real, rather than circle, fibres.
This theorem is qualitative; it makes no executable-computation claim.

1. Actual endpoint image and the six-increment neighbourhood.

For one or two finite endpoints, c is an actual character on G because
all those base points are fixed. Its image V is an additive subgroup.
Every original finite word has a path from the identity inside G.
Applying C.37's planar three-word identity to that character path proves
closure of V under fractions, hence under all real scalars. Thus V is a
real vector subspace of R^k, k <= 2, and is closed before any closure of
G is taken. Generation proves the asserted span by the original loop.

The parameter band |s-t| <= delta is compact and path connected.
J_delta is symmetric, contains the identity, and tends uniformly to
the identity in the actual map-and-inverse topology. Telescoping the
original parameter interval proves that c(J_delta) spans V for every
delta > 0. Choose at most two basis vectors in this set. The symmetric
three-word interval identity gives each [-v_i,v_i] in three such
increments; their sum is realized by six actual source increments.
This supplies a neighbourhood of zero in V without a continuous
section or a quotient-topology assertion. The same argument with the
prefix path gives r c(f_tau) in c(P^3), uniformly as the strip varies.

2. The closed source group and a single compact family.

The relative closed-disk centralizer is a closed subgroup of the
Polish disk homeomorphism group: boundary fixing and each labelled
interior commutation equation persist under map-and-inverse limits.
The principal-coordinate skew-translation form, increasing base,
fixed set A and preserved complementary intervals also persist.
Endpoint evaluations are continuous. Their image on the closure L is
exactly V, because V was already proved closed and c(G)=V. The exact
translation comparison on a retained strip passes pointwise to L.

In a complete compatible metric on L, compactness of each finite
product family permits the next short increment to move every
previous product by less than 2^-n. Uniform Cauchy convergence on the
product of compact parameter spaces gives a compact family K in L.
Identity later factors put every K_n in K. The parameter spaces for
P, each time band and their finite products are path connected, so
their countable product and its continuous image K are path
connected. This construction controls the inverse maps as well.
It is the same K for all strips and all endpoint ranks.

Every required based-face singleton belongs to every interval.

For (C.76.10), C.63's actual short-increment correction applies on the
