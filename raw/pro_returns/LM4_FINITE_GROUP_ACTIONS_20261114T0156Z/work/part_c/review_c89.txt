Reviewed sources
The complete 453-line C.89 TeX proof and its result metadata were read. The
actual C.81 group and covering hypotheses, all of C.82's null-triangulation
and pointwise-stabilizer construction, the parameter-normalization passage
of C.85, and C.88's statement and displacement topology were compared.
The Radó theorem statement on printed page182 was independently reread in
the retained source extraction; it gives uniform convergence on the closed
disk for normalized Riemann maps under Fréchet convergence of Jordan curves.
The existing inspected source PDF and bibliography entry are retained.

1. Hypotheses and domains
C.89 uses the original closed disk and its interior, the original free proper
closed discrete group Q, and the original boundary-fixed centralizer C_Q.
The transitive subgroup is used through the already proved C.82 nullity.
No extension of q in Q to the original circle is required. The fixed oriented
coordinate iota and its inverse are explicit, with uniform moduli comparing
the two compact-disk metrics. Every final face formula returns through iota.
The lifted triangulation is locally finite in O. Its one-skeleton is connected,
and nullity shows that every original boundary point lies in its closure.
Thus X is indeed its compact connected closure in the original disk.

2. Face labels and image characterization
The covering is injective on each closed lifted star and hence on each
triangle. A deck transformation preserving a triangle must fix its projected
labelled vertices, so freeness gives a trivial triangle stabilizer. The
expression q Delta_sigma is therefore unique, exactly as used in C.89.8.
The intrinsic target face conditions force disjoint open Jordan disks: an
intersection forces the connected first interior into the second; its boundary
cannot enter the second interior because it is in b(X). It must lie in the
second Jordan curve, hence equals that curve. Injectivity on X and the
triangulation then identify the triangles. These arguments cover triangles
sharing an edge or a vertex as well as disjoint triangles.
Every compact boundary curve lies inside the open Euclidean unit disk. Its
bounded Jordan disk lies in its convex hull, hence inside O. The same convex
hull observation proves the diameter identity C.89.9. No metric control of
the unbounded complementary component is used.

3. Extension formula, labels, orientation and inverse
Three specified boundary vertices in positive cyclic order uniquely normalize
the conformal disk map for each representative face. Formula C.89.6 gives
the complete boundary reparametrization, and its radial extension has the
displayed radial inverse. Formula C.89.7 consequently extends the entire
map b on the boundary. It fixes every point when b=id_X.
Conjugation by the literal q in C.89.8 agrees on common edges because bq=qb
there. A homeomorphism of O carries the compact Jordan disk to the compact
Jordan disk with its image boundary. Its two appearances in q F q^{-1}
retain orientation of the extension even when q reverses orientation.
Reindexing the unique labels proves commutation with every individual q.

4. Original-circle continuity and surjectivity
At a sequence tending to the original circle, the containing source faces
leave every finite list and have diameters tending to zero. Uniform
continuity of b on compact X, followed by C.89.9, controls all points in
their target faces with the correct original boundary limit. The pasted
map is therefore continuous on the entire original closed disk.
The disjoint-face result and the empty-interior condition give injectivity
across faces and skeleton. The interior image is nonempty and open by
invariance of domain. It is closed relative to O because the whole compact
image is closed and precisely the source circle maps to the target circle.
Connectedness gives surjectivity onto O and hence onto the closed disk.
The resulting compact-to-Hausdorff bijection is a homeomorphism. The stated
inverse formulas agree with that inverse everywhere. Conversely restriction
of an actual C_Q element satisfies all intrinsic face conditions. This
proves equality with the exact restriction image, not only containment.

5. Parameter dependence of marked Riemann maps
Uniform convergence of b_n on X gives explicit uniformly convergent boundary
parametrizations for each fixed representative face. A point in the limiting
Jordan interior remains interior by invariance of winding number. Translating
that point to zero makes the cited Radó theorem apply literally; translating
back gives closed-disk uniform convergence in the original fixed coordinate.
Boundary preimages of each marked vertex converge by compactness and
injectivity of the limiting boundary map. The unique Möbius maps carrying
the fixed cyclic triple to those three preimages converge because the three
limiting points remain distinct. Composition gives exactly the originally
defined three-point-normalized maps. The temporary common interior point
introduces no choice into the final map.
The inverse evaluation in C.89.6 is justified with compact preimages even
though the target domains vary. Any convergent input subsequence has its
unique limiting preimage. This proves uniform convergence of eta on S^1,
and the radial extension preserves that convergence. Thus every fixed face
map converges uniformly. Conjugation by any fixed q preserves this assertion
on a compact interior neighborhood of the relevant images.

6. All faces and the actual inverse topology
The convergent compact sequence of embeddings is equicontinuous on compact
X. Nullity consequently makes all but finitely many boundary-image
diameters small, uniformly for the whole sequence. C.89.10 uses a common
source boundary point and exactly those two diameters plus the uniform
skeleton distance. It controls every target point of every remaining face.
The finitely many other faces are handled by the fixed-face convergence.
This proves uniform convergence on the whole original disk. Formula C.89.11
is valid by writing y=u_n x and applying the modulus of u^{-1} to u_n x and
u x; it proves uniform convergence of inverses. The coordinate metric moduli
then prove continuity in the specified original map-and-inverse topology.
Both spaces are metrizable, so the sequential argument establishes the
claimed global continuity. No Whitney topology or uncontrolled inverse
limit of finite evaluation bundles is imported.

7. Product and deformation
Restriction is continuous. The element s(h|X)^{-1}h fixes X pointwise, so
the second product coordinate lies in exactly C.82's stabilizer. Composition
and inversion are continuous in the original disk group. The two formulas
C.89.3 compose to the identity with the displayed order of multiplication.
The C.82 Alexander contraction is used in the direction k to1; explicitly
this is its displayed A_r with r=1-t. Its identity-fixed property makes
C.89.12 a strong deformation retraction, fixing every point of s(B).

8. Scope and remaining work
The proved object is the actual restriction image B and its global continuous
extension section. The product is a homeomorphism of the displayed spaces.
The theorem does not assert that the section is a group homomorphism. It
does not assert a contraction of B, a compact quotient cutoff for every
family, or a resolution of C.12. C.88 retains its own exact uniform
vanishing-displacement characterization; C.89 supplies a compatible second
model for the same original group because both maps take values in C_Q.
All remaining scope statements in the metadata agree with the proof.

No proof correction or frozen-source edit is required by this review.
