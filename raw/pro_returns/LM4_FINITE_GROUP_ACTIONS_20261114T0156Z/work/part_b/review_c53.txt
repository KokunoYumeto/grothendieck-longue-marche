1. Scope of the global quotient transfer

C.42 explicitly allows every z fixed by L_x whose actual G-orbit is
an intrinsic line or circle. It does not require z to be in the
frontier of the source orbit. Its proof establishes intrinsic
continuity, openness, the equality Lz=Gz, continuity of restriction
L -> Homeo(Gz), and all compatible source/target lifts in this scope.
Consequently the common compatible lift group used for several such
maps is closed Polish and transitive on the source covering plane.
The proper-fiber, flowbox, product and pair-coordinate proofs in
C.43--C.45 require precisely these maps and actions. Their transfer
does not add a compactification or an unproved topology identification.

The change of basepoint in (C.53.3) is exact, and keeps the same target
orbit and target covering. Equations (C.53.4)--(C.53.5) prove invariance
and transport of the complete global family, including the normalizer
condition needed in the one/two-foliation constructions.

2. Three global partitions

C.47 uses only the common maps, pair domains and transitive compatible
group. Neither of its local-compactness arguments uses the location
of the target inside the plane. For the based paths, a circle target
still has the zero-winding endpoint from the ambient-null plane loop,
regardless of its location relative to O. Pair injectivity gives the
closed based source loop. The countable-deck/compact-word Baire
transitivity argument and the Lie isotropy classification therefore
have exactly their earlier hypotheses.

In (C.53.6), every element of N is a finite word in the actual based
source lifts; the identical global word gives a preimage in E_O.
Thus the Polish projection is onto and open. An element of its kernel
fixes O pointwise and, by actual quotient equivariance, fixes the whole
image of each global Phi_z. The already proved intrinsic restriction
continuity makes the induced N action continuous in the actual target
homeomorphism-group topology. Its image is exactly G restricted to that
target, as every source element has a global word preimage. Hence the
metabelian line/circle classification and natural based covering lift
give (C.53.8), including real angles at intermediate circle times.

3. Enlarging the exponential region

All inputs of C.50 remain unchanged: the source groups are the stated
simply connected Lie groups (or the already specified covering group
in C.36); the original global word paths provide the uniform bound;
commutators of commutators fix the open union and its closure; and
each integer residual is an exact fractional power in that same
source group. Its word loop is null there. Thus the exact two-point
winding argument retains the real, not merely modular, limiting
circle angle. The inverse-loop proof applies as before. Agreement
with the old X_star family follows on the old dense open union and
then by continuity. No extra global quotient compatibility for an
arbitrary compactification is assumed in this passage.

4. Finitely many cocompact operator representatives

Outside X_dagger there are at most two global partitions and operator
dimension at most one. The intrinsic C.52 replacement depends on
the full-family normalizer equations and the exact C.42--C.45 maps,
not on a frontier-position hypothesis. It therefore gives every
equation (C.53.9) in the enlarged global family. Empty family means
only constant equivariant quotients can appear at nonopen limits.

The finite number of representatives permits a subsequence with one
fixed O_j and one fixed compact fundamental set C_j. All contraction
and inverse images of C_j form the compact S_j. Approximation in
(C.53.13) is made after composition by the fixed h_n, so it is an
actual open neighborhood condition in Homeo(O_j) and requires no
uniform modulus for the varying h_n. The approximants g_n are actual
global operators commuting with L.

Open evaluation gives l_n -> 1 with l_n y=y_n. The commutation
identity in (C.53.14) then proves g_n y -> z. It both places z in
Fix(L_y) and yields compact-open convergence to the exact Phi_z^y.
The basepoint formula identifies that limit with a global quotient
included in the family. Its target is nonopen: an open orbit about
z would contain all sufficiently late x_n and hence belong to U.

Equivariance, the post-composition approximation and (C.53.9) now give
the unique limiting hybrid value. Every subsequence has a further
subsequence of this kind. Diagonal approximation from U proves
continuity for boundary-only sequences without prior continuity of
the hybrid rule on the whole nonopen locus. The same compact set and
the exact inverse quotient equation give joint inverse continuity.

5. Final relative pasting and scope

X and X_dagger are closed. Their intersection consists of nonopen
orbits, and every such target image in X_dagger is metabelian.
Both constructions therefore use exactly the same based fractional
target path on the intersection. The two closed-set pasting argument
and the inverse pasting argument are valid. H-commutation extends
from the dense open union. Arbitrarily many translates are covered,
while finitely many H-inequivalent representatives and compactness
of their quotients remain explicit assumptions of this proved case.

The proof does not assert an extension through the remaining
nonopen region, arbitrary noncocompact quotients, or infinitely many
unrelated representatives. C.12 correctly remains unresolved.
