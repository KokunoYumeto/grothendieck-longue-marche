1. Actual compact family.
The union K K^{-1} union the original loop union its inverse loop is
compact and path connected, with the common identity. C64.1 gives an
actual open neighborhood of x inside P(x). Applying each original
p in P gives a neighborhood of p(x) inside pP(x), so P(x) is contained
in int P^2(x). This does not infer interior from connectedness alone.
All source labels, operator commutators, original orbit preservation,
and fixed-point conditions survive these actual products.

2. Filled hull and its components.
For compact K the complement has exactly one unbounded component.
The fill is compact and lies in the Euclidean convex hull. A Jordan
curve in an interior component has its entire bounded disk in the
fill, because the connected unbounded complementary component lies
on its exterior side. The disk's interior joins the same interior
component as its boundary. Polygonal approximation and a finite
planar regular neighborhood then give simple connectivity for every
interior component. Thus connected P(x), already in the interior,
determines the unique component V_x asserted in the claim.

3. Exact fundamental-group inclusion.
For U a component of O intersect V with V simply connected, the
outer Jordan disk of the finite regular neighborhood is in V.
All its inner holes are therefore in V as well. Filling exactly
the holes wholly contained in O gives M' in the actual U.
A chosen excluded point in each remaining hole is avoided by the
original null-homotopy in O. The specified inner and outer
Schoenflies radial deformations retract the resulting punctured
plane onto M', fixing the boundaries and basepoint. This proves
injectivity for the actual U-to-O map. C32 supplies nullity of the
original evaluation loop in O, so it gives a filling in this U.

4. Exact operator transport.
Plane homeomorphisms preserve the unbounded complementary component,
and hence the fill. Together with hP(x)=P(hx), this transports V_x
and its selected intersection component U_x with the exact labels.
An H-stabilizer of x fixes every point gx of its original G-orbit,
since H commutes with G. It consequently fixes the entire chosen
filling in O_x. This proves independence of the representative in
the set-indexed H-equivariant selection.

Minor wording correction in this paragraph:
The sentence "an unbounded sequence has no compact subsequence" is
literally too broad. Replace it by "choose a sequence escaping every
compact set". Such a sequence exists in the unbounded component.
Properness of the inverse then prevents its images from being
bounded. This proves the desired unboundedness with no change to
the theorem or its argument.

5. Uniform fixed-frontier bound.
The one compact family P^2 fixes y. Joint evaluation on its product
with a convergent sequence and its limit is uniformly continuous,
so the supremum of |p(x_n)-y| tends to zero. Its ball is convex and
contains the fill, hence every carrier and every selected filling.
The estimate is independent of all choices and all H transport.
