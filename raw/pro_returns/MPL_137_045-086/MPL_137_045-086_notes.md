## Universal complexes and relative exactness


### A01 — The universal evaluation argument is local to additive generators

**Cote/scan:** 137/045; middle–bottom; TeX lines 10–47. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

**Bears on:** Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed)


The proof reduces the evaluation map to the displayed additive generators and ends “va à l’identité, qfd.”, while the generator superscript remains uncertain in the frozen reading. This is an additive-generation/Yoneda mechanism; applying it to a larger completion would require checking that the relevant sums, products or retracts are still controlled. It supplies a precise mechanism for recovering a functor from its universal complex, without yet recovering a space. Context: this restates the argument in Hua, modernised reading of cote 137, “Les réalisations de $\mathbb K$” (our 045), and adds a scope boundary to its universal-property finding.

**Bears on:** Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed); WG2 §Week #3 (unchecked plan)

### A08 — Finite duality does not require reflexivity of the arbitrary test complex

**Cote/scan:** 137/056–057; 056 example; 057 full text. **Kind:** own idea.

**Bears on:** Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed)

**Confidence and reason:** High: the tensor–Hom adjunction for a finite-free target gives the inverse explicitly; this is a standard calculation, not a novelty claim.


The example calls the formula $\operatorname{Hom}(\lambda,L)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}(L^\vee,\lambda^\vee)$ “élémentaire”. For bounded finite-free $L$, transposition uses the reflexivity of $L$, not a claim that an arbitrary $\lambda$ is reflexive; degreewise compatibility with the differential then gives the displayed chain-map correspondence. This is a useful check against inadvertently adding a finite-type assumption to the variable $\lambda$. Context: this restates Hua, final example of “Le théorème”, and adds an explanation of which variable carries the finite-duality hypothesis.

### A11 — The homology-free boundary separates P from Q

**Cote/scan:** 137/060–063; 060 lines 24–29; 061 lines 24–29; 063 opening. **Kind:** own idea / reading question.

**Bears on:** Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed)

### A12 — A kernel can be admissible without its image inclusion being admissible

**Cote/scan:** 137/062; top; lines 3–16. **Kind:** own idea.

**Bears on:** Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed)

**Confidence and reason:** High in the stated finite-free PID setting; not asserted for arbitrary base rings.


The warning “le mono $A'\longrightarrow B$ ne sera pas en général un $\Sigma$-mono” has a concrete explanation over a principal ideal domain: a degreewise image in a finite-free module is free, so the kernel sequence splits degreewise, but the subsequent inclusion can have torsion quotient. Already $2:\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z$, placed in degree zero, has zero kernel and zero categorical cokernel in finite-free modules, yet is not invertible. This explains the deleted claim that the category is abelian and the surviving warning about bimorphisms. Context: this adds an explicit test to Hua, “Les conditions a) à e)”, concluding kernel remark; the principal-ideal-domain restriction is retained rather than generalized silently.

### M08 — A possible factorization proof using the source exact structure

**Cote/scan:** 137/071; square; TeX lines 7–17. **Kind:** own idea.

**Bears on:** MPL_133 B06 (first-pass lead); Hua finding 137-perfect-complexes-universal-sigma-exact (candidate; not independently reviewed)

**Confidence and reason:** Medium: the kernel argument is formal under the stated assumptions, but those assumptions and full coherence have not been established here.


Choose admissible injective presentations $X\hookrightarrow I^0\to I^1$ and $Y\hookrightarrow J^0\to J^1$, and suppose $X*Y$ is the admissible kernel of $I^0*J^0\to(I^1*J^0)\oplus(I^0*J^1)$ and $F$ preserves this kernel. Naturality of the product on injective objects makes the map $F(X)\otimes F(Y)\to F(I^0*J^0)$ vanish under both components, hence factor uniquely through $F(X*Y)$ using only biadditivity of the target tensor product at this step. Verifying that source-kernel presentation and its functoriality is the remaining task; this is not a proof of the whole extension theorem. Context: this adds a conditional route to Hua, “L'unité, et l'extension aux foncteurs exacts à gauche”, and differs from a claim that preservation of kernels by $\otimes_B$ is already known to be necessary.


### M09 — The resulting cup product has the stated laws up to homotopy

**Cote/scan:** 137/072; final paragraph; TeX lines 16–20. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

**Bears on:** MPL_133 B06 (first-pass lead); WG2 §Week #3 (unchecked plan)


The page says “le tout à homotopie près” after listing associativity, commutativity and the unit, with an explicit warning about signs. Context: this restates Hua, “Le cup-produit”, supports MPL_133 B06, and adds the precise boundary between individually stated homotopies and a coherent operadic structure.


### M10 — A minimal calculation separates cosimplicial and DGA commutativity

**Cote/scan:** 137/065–072; comparison with072 product. **Kind:** own idea.

**Bears on:** MPL_133 B06 (first-pass lead)

**Confidence and reason:** High: direct evaluation of the two endpoint formulas; standard example, with no novelty claim.


On the oriented edge $[01]$, take a zero-cochain $f$ with $f(0)=0,f(1)=1$ and a one-cochain $e$ with $e([01])=1$. The Alexander–Whitney product gives $(f\smile e)([01])=0$ but $(e\smile f)([01])=1$, although the pointwise function rings in every simplicial degree are commutative [BF04, §2.2.8]. This tests a distinction of products, not a claim that the interval admits no other commutative model. Context: this adds an elementary illustration to the MPL_133 B06 lead and to Hua, “Le cup-produit”; it does not change the manuscript's own product formulas.

### M12 — Good truncation is a plausible interpretation; naive truncation is not interchangeable

**Cote/scan:** 137/073; last half of paragraph. **Kind:** own idea / reading question.

**Bears on:** MPL_136_189-234 §2 (first-pass lead); WG2 §Week #3 (unchecked plan)
