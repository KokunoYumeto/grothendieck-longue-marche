## Legibility and reliability

The black-ink closing sheets (084–086) are much more legible than the faint pencil/blue material written across printed music staves. Scan045, the long marginal additions on077, the small additions on073 and079–083, the rewritten claim on070, and the cancelled portions on052–053/059 are the least secure. The abbreviation and font tables are editorial aids, not evidence that those weak readings have been resolved.

All 42 whole-page images and all 84 supplied half-page images were inspected by the single reader. A second image audit was completed for every page, with enlargements and rotations for difficult writing. No OCR or delegated manuscript reading was used. The inventory records the represented elements, including cancellations and drawings; `yes` means the element has a transcription, not that every letter within it is certain.

The register is deliberately conservative: many repeated entries concern the same unresolved P alphabet, while other entries concern distinct words, indices, arrows or entire faint phrases. “Low” is assigned to alternatives, physically unreadable words, and the weak-page group; other explicitly doubtful readings are “medium.” A confidence label is not a calibrated probability. Text outside doubt macros is a reading, not a guarantee against error, and this should not be treated as a critical edition without independent human collation.

Locations in the register are source-line positions within a page fragment; paragraphs were allowed to reflow, so these are approximate vertical guides, not a claimed one-to-one count of handwritten lines. Nested doubt macros are retained and their alternatives are split only at a top-level bar.

The supplied context was kept sealed until the transcription was frozen. The progress log records the page and combined-file hashes; later contextual reading questions belong exclusively to the research notes. The first-pass scripts in `support/manual_sources/` are historical records and must not be rerun over the audited page files.
