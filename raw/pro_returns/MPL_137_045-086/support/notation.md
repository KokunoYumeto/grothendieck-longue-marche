# Notation table

This table was begun with scan 045 and completed before the context archive was opened. The transcription was read in one stream by one reader, then checked against the images a second time. The entries below record visible recurring forms and unresolved typographic distinctions, not a reconstruction of the intended mathematics. Local doubt marks take precedence over a convention.

| Recurring ink | LaTeX rendering | Cross-page decision / limits |
|---|---|---|
| Looped capital P | `\unsure{\mathcal P\|P}` | The looped P recurs from the exact-category discussion through the polynomial-category discussion (049–086). Earlier plain/calligraphic renderings were compared with the ink and made uniformly doubtful: the letter is P, but its intended alphabet is not securely distinguishable. This does not identify the different categories or morphism sets mathematically. |
| Degree dot | `^\bullet`, `_\bullet` | Dot and position are checked separately. The possible dots on K(x), scan045, are explicitly doubtful. A dot is not converted into a prime. |
| Star | `^*`, `_*`; multiplication `*` | Stars for realizations, total graded objects and the transported product are retained in their written positions. No single mathematical interpretation is imposed. |
| Prime / double prime | `'`, `''` | Visible prime strokes are retained, including formula labels (1'), (1''). No exclamation-mark exponent was securely identified in this package; punctuation `!` is not treated as an exponent. |
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes | No conversion to guillemets. The closing quotation strokes on067 occur without an added opening pair. Formula labels with primes are not quotation pairs. |
| Opposite sign | `^\circ` | Small open raised circle, distinct from a degree dot. |
| Dual sign | `^\vee` | Separate from the opposite circle and from the lower/upper star. |
| Underlined Hom | `\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}` | Underlining is kept where visible; printed music-staff lines make some occurrences uncertain. |
| Hom with degree dot or star | `\operatorname{Hom}^\bullet`, `\operatorname{Hom}^*` | Subscripts, superscripts and argument alphabets are retained locally. |
| Roman C, F, K, L, M, N, Q, X, Y, Z | `C`, `F`, `K`, … | Handwriting shape does not justify inventing a uniform bold or script alphabet. Ambiguous letters, indices and overwriting have local doubt marks. |
| Lowercase k / uppercase K | `k`, `K` | Not interchanged merely because a coefficient ring or a category is expected;045 is especially uncertain. |
| B and B with a hat | `B`, `\widehat{B}` | Hat, opposite circle, prime and degree signs are independent features. |

| Gamma | `\Gamma` | Divided-power-looking glyph and the functor notation in064 retain their local indices. Its mathematics is considered only in the notes. |
| Sigma | `\Sigma` | Class of sequences, with the written subscripts and intersections; not replaced by a summation sign. |
| Lambda / exterior-power wedge | `\Lambda`, `\bigwedge` | Size and placement follow the local writing;047 and068 compared. The uncertain star position in047 is retained. |
| rho / sigma | `\rho`, `\sigma` | Adjoint-looking maps in053–056 compared; their order is not repaired from the expected adjunction. The symmetry label in066 is separately read as sigma. |
| delta / beta | `\unsure{\delta\|\beta}` within the full labels | Unit-constraint labels in066 remain ambiguous. |
| epsilon | `\varepsilon` | Augmentation-looking label in058,060,062; cancelled occurrences remain cancelled. |
| e / gamma on universal maps | `\unsure{e_{\nu_i}\|\gamma_i}`, `\unsure{e}` | Labels on080 were compared with081. The e-like loop and degree index are preferred where visible; the alternatives remain explicit. |
| alpha | `\alpha` | Lower and upper indices and arrow direction retained. The questionable superscript in081 is not corrected. |
| nu / i; p / r; r / rho | `\unsure{…\|…}` at occurrence | These local similarities are not resolved by mathematical expectation. See052,076,080–082,086. |
| Tensor, direct sum, product | `\otimes`, `\oplus`, `\prod` | Bases, positions and overwritten signs retained. Transported `*` is not substituted for a visible tensor. |
| Unit | `1`, `\mathbf1` | Follows the local stroke; no global replacement by an object name. |
| Isomorphism / equality | `\simeq`, `\sim`, `=` | A drawn arrow and its head are separate from an isomorphism mark. Standalone equivalence signs use phantom labels when needed in diagrams. |
| Plain stroke without an arrowhead | `\mathbin{-}` or a TikZ-CD `dash` arrow | An arrowhead is not supplied because a map is mathematically expected. Ambiguous heads are marked locally. |
| Inclusions and inequalities | `\subset`, `\hookrightarrow`, `\leq`, `\geq` and local variants | Direction checked at each occurrence. No reversal from the argument. |
| Written dots / unfinished phrases | `\ldots` or explicitly doubtful literal dots | Only written trailing dots are represented; they are never placeholders for omitted transcription. |
