# Additional primary-source audit for A.127

Opened the author-hosted Kiran Kedlaya, Notes on class field theory,
https://kskedlaya.org/cft/sec_localrecip.html on 16 November 2026.

Verified Theorem 4.1.2: the local reciprocity map has the finite abelian
extension norm group as its kernel. Verified Example 4.1.4 (particularly
the unit component displayed after the local cyclotomic decomposition):
for Q_p the reciprocity action on the p-power cyclotomic factor sends a
unit u to u^{-1}. Therefore, on Q_2(mu_(2^r)), its unit kernel is exactly
u=1 modulo2^r, including the sign. This is the precise implication used
in A.127.9–10; no norm-limitation statement for a non-Galois field is used.
