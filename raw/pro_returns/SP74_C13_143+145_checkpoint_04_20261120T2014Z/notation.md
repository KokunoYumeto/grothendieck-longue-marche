## Audit-stage graphical convention

Visible insertion guides and carets are drawn explicitly in TikZ, in addition to retaining the inserted words. They are distinguished from tildes, parentheses, underlines and arrowheads. When a marginal note is rotated upright for transcription, compare a separator relative to that same upright note; do not mix native-sheet and rotated coordinates.
