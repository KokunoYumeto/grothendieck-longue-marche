### Repeated subtypes worth naming more explicitly

The broad error-sheet kinds already cover most findings. The following are recurring, more specific mechanisms observed in this run; they should not be added to the totals again.

### What the counts mean

Each table row has one primary error-sheet kind. A locus can differ from both witnesses, but it is counted once in the kind and confidence totals. Certainty-only resolutions are kept in the doubts file and original MARKS decisions, outside material-change totals. Thus a newly readable doubtful word that confirms C's preferred reading may appear in the certainty record, while the same locus can still be a material row against a different H word. Macro wrappers or font conventions alone are excluded. The H-free numerical sheet has no H collation rows.

The hand log is the complete place-indexed record of the habits encountered, including those already anticipated by the error sheet. It scopes each observation to inspected occurrences and records exceptions or unresolved cases; none is a license to normalize a future page without seeing it.
