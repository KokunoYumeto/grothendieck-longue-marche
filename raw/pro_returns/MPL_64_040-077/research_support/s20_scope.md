## Short research notes

### S20.N01 — The planning guess remains unconfirmed in this packet [scope finding]

`context/README.md`, “What our planning document asks,” explicitly calls S20 a guess made from titles, dates and neighbouring folders before these pages were read. Scans 071–077 provide the concrete sequence Kummer cover → tangent tensor power → Legendre pinning, while the earlier scans build finite configurations and elliptic level data. No comparison with a connection, a Cartier operator, or the p-curvature of a uniformizing differential equation is written in scans 040–077, so this packet neither proves S20 nor contradicts the possibility of such a connection elsewhere.

### S20.N02 — Identify the coefficient object before interpreting cohomology [scope finding]

At scan 072, formula (4), the displayed first direct image concerns torsors under the finite étale group scheme already introduced on that page, on the punctured relative curve. It is therefore not the displayed construction of relative de Rham cohomology equipped with Gauss–Manin. Likewise the projective-line bundle in scans 075–077 and its tangent-bundle formula do not by themselves define a projective connection; an actual differential operator or connection and its compatibility would still have to be supplied.

### S20.N03 — Tangent pinning is a substantial positive result of the passage [unproved manuscript claim; scope qualification]

Scans 071–074 explicitly formulate a local-to-global reconstruction programme for tame marked covers from inertia, a punctured-cover class, and a root of the tangent line, ending with “je dis que ce foncteur est une équivalence de catégories.” This deserves analysis on its own terms, including the compatibility of the chosen inertia embedding with the punctured-cover class and the exact morphisms carrying the tangent-line identification. The root agent handles the local formulas and those category conditions separately; neither an incompletely specified target category nor its repair constitutes evidence for the differential-equation component of S20.

### S20.N04 — A tangent line on an elliptic curve does not settle the deformation-sheet questions [scope finding]

The line in scan 075, formula (5), is explicitly the tangent line of the elliptic curve at its zero section, and scans 076–077 modify a candidate square root of the quotient's tangent line. No formal deformation ring, coefficient expansion of a coarse invariant, or order of such an expansion is displayed here. Consequently these pages do not settle E14 or test the unspecified two orders of E16 or the first-order assertion C29 merely by using the word “tangent.”

### S20.N05 — The closest contextual continuity is Kummer, conormal and norm data [textual comparison]

`context/LM_140-2_text.tex`, scans 073–078, develops Kummer classes and local roots of uniformizers; scan 077 maps nonzero conormal classes to splitting classes compatibly with the Kummer homomorphism, and scan 078 expressly asks for the relation with the norm of differential one-forms to be explicated.
