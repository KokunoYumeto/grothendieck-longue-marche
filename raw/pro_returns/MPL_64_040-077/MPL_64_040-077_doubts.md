# Legibility

The main text is usually recoverable, but this is fast working handwriting with dense revisions. The least secure material is the crowded summary on scan 040, especially its rotated insertion; the first group identifications on 042; the cancelled affine computations on 050; the layered parenthesis of Corollary 2 on 053; the modified Sigma and cohomology insertion on 055–057; the clipped end of 066; the small cancelled words on 071–074; and the two heavily cancelled lines on 077. These are reading risks, not invitations to repair the text from mathematical expectations.

All 38 whole-page images and their 76 supplied halves were viewed by the primary transcriber in the prescribed page order, without OCR. Every page was saved before moving on, and all 38 pages received a further visual audit; enlarged and rotated crops were used where necessary. A second agent performed only mechanical checks of saved text, never reading or transcribing scan images. There are no omitted-text placeholders in the transcription: written ellipses are retained, and the tiny terminal marks on 074 are explicitly doubtful punctuation. Some cancelled material admits only a low-confidence proposed reading, and the exact attachment of the Corollary 2 insertion on 053 remains uncertain.

The inventory has one row per identified element, including subparts of long insertion macros; consequently macro counts need not equal inventory counts. The whole-page transcriptions are diplomatic fragments, not facsimile page layouts. The nine diagrams use tikz-cd, with portable ampersand replacement for use inside the requested annotation macro; relative geometry is approximate where the source is irregular.
