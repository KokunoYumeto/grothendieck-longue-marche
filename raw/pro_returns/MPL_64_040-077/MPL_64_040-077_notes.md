### RN017 — The three-way correspondence survives with opposition retained

**Source:** cote 64, scans 051–053, regularity and theorem. **Kind:** unproved claim.

### RN018 — The orientation cover is canonically μ4*, without choosing i

**Source:** cote 64, scans 051, orientation margin; 053, Corollary2. **Kind:** unproved claim.

### RN025 — The relation between I and K removes the constancy hypothesis

**Source:** cote 64, scans 055–057, (11), (14), (16). **Kind:** unproved claim.
