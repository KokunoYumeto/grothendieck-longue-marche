## A whole-prime Frobenius obstruction (AC.33)

The semilinear obstruction has been extended beyond the case in which2 is primitive moduloell. If a Frobenius power is a primitive scalar on all J[ell], then its stability would put all nonzero multiples of any thetaell point on theta. The genus-two difference bound excludes this for ell>=5, so every new factor is ordinary. Its new2-module is free of rank1 over the reduced algebraR=F2[X]/Phi_ell, which may be a product of fields. The primitive scalar's automorphism permutes every geometric character transitively, leaving fixed algebraF2. Its full semilinear norm is therefore1. With the stated exact first-field period, every primeell cover has rational new2-torsion at its first constant-deck field, and every later eligible field fails too. The geometric_routes agent independently reviewed the algebra and the irreducible-polynomial corollary.

If the Frobenius polynomial is irreducible moduloell and q generatesFell*, reciprocity on the four conjugate eigenvalues gives lambda^(ell²+1)=q. The scalar subgroup inside<lambda> is then allFell*, and all nonzero vectors have the same exact period. This proves an explicit family of bad primes and shows why independent-uniform character-value heuristics cannot be used as a proof of A3.

A separate proved arithmetic constraint explains the remaining weakness of L3. For q=p^e and p primitive moduloell, a rational ell-torsion rankr>g forces q=1 moduloell by the Weil pairing, hence ell-1 divides e and ell<=e+1. Such ranks supply at most polynomially many cosets as e grows. In the lower-rank cases, the sufficient counting inequalities demand large prime divisors of Jacobian orders with the required residue order. No theorem guaranteeing those primes for every curve was obtained. This names the exact gap in that attempted universal proof; it is not a counterexample to A3.

The finite etale Kummer torsor z^(q-1)=A records that norm as its Frobenius value. The gauge change A'=A b^q/b([q]a)=A(b^(-q))^(q-1) proves independence of basis. This is a concrete finite etale object, rather than an unsupported appeal to dynamical Chebotarev.

For y²+y=x⁵ overF16, the existing exact order13 point of degree3 has norm of order3. The program was extended and rerun to check every16-power coefficient and its4096/16 norm.
