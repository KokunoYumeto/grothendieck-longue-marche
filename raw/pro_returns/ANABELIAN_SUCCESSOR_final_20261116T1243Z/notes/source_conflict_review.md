# Source conflict under active review

Observed during the sixth-hour A1 review on 16 November 2026.

The primary June 2007 author PDF of *Absolute Anabelian Cuspidalizations of Proper Hyperbolic Curves* contains Proposition 1.8(ii), numbered Proposition 1.6(ii) in the alternative LaTeX PDF. Together with its displayed definition of the configuration quotient, the fiber-preimage assertion appears to imply full prime-to-p fiber injectivity when Sigma is the set of primes different from p. This is incompatible with the complete AG39 proof, which root and all three agents have rechecked through its finite r-group core and monodromy steps. The February 2014 comments repair four other points and do not alter this assertion. We have not treated the paper title or theorem statement as permission to omit this discrepancy.

The attached original source of Saidi–Tamagawa 2009 was also read: Corollary 2.3 constructs its named cuspidal comparison only for puncture sets outside the exceptional set. Theorem 2.7 refers to that comparison despite more general opening wording. Its proof is a reference to Mochizuki's Theorem 3.10. Thus this citation alone is not used here to create a comparison at two hypothetically colliding exceptional points.
