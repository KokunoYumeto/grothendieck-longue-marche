## Concrete fixes and scope clarifications

1. **C06 and the Wildeshaus bibliography annotation — qualify Proposition 2.4 as well as Theorem 6.1.** Proposition 2.4 constructs a canonical compactification morphism over the fixed perfect base field; its identification as an isomorphism requires that the field admit resolution of singularities. Thus replace the unqualified “gives a compactification description” by “gives a compactification morphism, an isomorphism under resolution of singularities,” and change the bibliography's implication that only the last cited statement needs that hypothesis. Primary author preprint: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0408295v3 , Proposition 2.4 and §6 opening/Theorem 6.1; web refs `turn54view2`, `turn54view1`.

2. **LF07 — make the local coefficient/category hypothesis explicit at the new cohomological justification, and cite it there.** Use finite-dimensional continuous representations over a finite extension of \(\mathbf Q_\ell\), with \(\ell\ne p\), of the local decomposition group of the curve, and continuous inertia cohomology. This prevents the assertion from being read as a theorem for every abstract \((D,I)\) and characteristic-zero field of039; cite Deligne (1973), §§10.7–10.8, pp.584–587, at the local-cohomology sentence. The formula itself is correct: \(H^1(I,E^\vee)\cong(E^I)^\vee(-1)\), so the stated Euler-class expression for \(\mu\) is additive locally; finite-inertia factors give the displayed cancellation, without requiring their globalization. Primary PDF: https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/Number20.pdf ; web ref `turn52view2`.

3. **LF11 — specify the finite boundary and the symbols used in the GOS formula.** State that \(i:U\hookrightarrow X\) is a nonempty open immersion, \(E\) is lisse of rank \(r\) on \(U\), \(g=g(X)\), and the sum runs over closed points \(x\in X\setminus U\). The displayed formula and the subtraction of \(\operatorname{rank}\alpha_x\) from the usual Artin conductor are then correct; this is a scope/definition fix, not a change of sign or conductor normalization.

4. **C05 — avoid implying an unstated line-bundle existence/descent result.** Replace “the corresponding line bundle is ample” by “a line bundle inducing such a homomorphism, when supplied, is ample,” or simply say that symmetry does not establish ampleness. The current sentence can otherwise be read as asserting that every symmetric homomorphism over the field comes from a line bundle over that same field. The intended positivity distinction is correct and does not need that extra assertion.
