## Assessment of the supplied Hua finding

**Hua, findings register, 60-inertia-average-duality** retains the supplied status **“conjecture (unsearched)”**, with intake “new to us”; the visible entry ends after “plus…” and supplies no complete continuation to audit. Its account of local averaging, a virtual sheaf and a duality correction is supported by039–051, while037 supplies the rejected global approach that motivates locality. At the level of L-functions, additivity means multiplicativity across sums of virtual classes, not arithmetic addition of rational functions.

Two qualifications affect endorsement of the visible claim. First, `[2]` is cancelled on049: a conventional derived expression containing an even shift can be compatible with the virtual identity, but no canonical derived object is constructed here (LF06, C02). Second, the conditional argument in LF07–LF08 gives \(\mu=0\) under the ordinary-invariant-stalk interpretation and the local category specified there; one cannot infer a nonzero correction from the symbol alone, or infer that the author explicitly retracted it.

A targeted search identified the public repository and the path `src/content/findings.ts`, but neither its raw nor its GitHub blob version could be retrieved, and the deployed findings page supplied no readable entry. Consequently no claim is made about the unseen remainder. The supplied register also gives a verbal page offset inconsistent with its enumerated “their pp.38–58 / our scans039–059” range; this return uses the user-supplied PDF scan numbers throughout rather than extrapolating that offset.
