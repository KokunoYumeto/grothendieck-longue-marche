## Legibility and comparison of witnesses

Most live prose and the larger formulas are readable at the supplied resolution once their local context is preserved. The least secure material consists of tiny interlinear cancellations, densely retraced indices, abbreviated endings, short arrow endpoints, slanted marginal text, and writing cut by a physical scan edge. Examples include the barred I/A forms on scans096–097, the lower cancelled groups and clipped right-hand index in diagram146 on scan103, the ending of the diagonal note on scan104, the crowded Δ insertion on scan106, and the repeated retraced parameter on scan107. Visible edge prefixes are retained without supplying their expected endings, notably normalis[at] on090 and normalisat on099, together with transf and automatiq with a raised t on102. A locally clear surrounding formula does not make an obscured component certain. Such components remain marked in place.

Witness C generally preserves the arrangement, abbreviations and revision states more closely than H on these supplied pages, but it is not treated as authority. The review also changes readings where C had expressed no doubt and where both witnesses agreed: examples include punctuation, source delimiters, individual indices, and the unusual connector geometry on scan103. H frequently expands short forms, changes générisation to a more familiar word, loses interlinear or marginal content, relocates text, or gives cancelled material as live. Its editorial remarks can acknowledge ink that its transcription does not reproduce. Each counted departure is tied to its local evidence; macro differences and changes of certainty alone are excluded from substantive totals.

The comparison is between two AI drafts and the supplied images, not an evaluation against a separate human-verified transcript. No outside text, OCR or TeX compilation has been used. The retained doubts are substantive limits of this reading, and the notes do not convert plausible mathematical repairs into manuscript text. The page reports and blind adjudications preserve the decisions and their evidence so that these limits can be reviewed directly.
