## D1: attribution correction and exact limitation

The supplied OpenAI preprint, `The-p-adic-section-conjecture-October-6-2026/sections/08-global-sections.tex`, was read in full. It contains no argument replacing strongly positive density by positive density. Its Proposition 8.1 identifies the image of localization with the finite-cover descent locus. Its final paragraph asserts finite images at individual finite places under the no-CM-subfield hypothesis and explicitly declines both localization injectivity and global finiteness.

LM6-D.6, read directly in the supplied LM6 paper, attributes the criticized intersection step to the unnumbered corollary following Corollary 7.10 of Porowski, *Families of curves separating points* (arXiv:2607.27746, 2026). Thus the prompt's phrase assigning that density gap to Section 8 of the supplied local-theorem paper is incorrect. The two texts have been compared directly. This is a correction of attribution, without a claim that the finiteness problem has thereby been decided.

The density inference is false as an inference about sets of primes. Take K=Q, L=Q(i), Omega the rational primes congruent to 3 modulo 4, and T the primes congruent to 1 modulo 4. Then Omega has density 1/2, T becomes density one after pullback to L, and Omega intersect T is empty. To check the pullback claim explicitly: primes above inert rational primes have norm p^2; their number up to norm X is at most pi(sqrt(X)), hence zero density relative to the prime ideals of L. The finitely many ramified primes do not affect density. The primes over the split rational primes therefore have density one in L. This refutes the intersection assertion, without furnishing a counterexample curve to the claimed corollary.

The source that is actually opened from the public web, Porowski, *Locally conjugate Galois sections*, arXiv:2312.08005v1, Theorem 1.1, gives a cleaner independent way to justify the injectivity needed in R14: for a smooth curve or a semiabelian variety over a number field, two sections conjugate at all primes of a density-one set are globally conjugate. For proper C and the stipulated local theorem, equality of localization tuples gives equality of the local section classes at every finite place, so this theorem applies. No density-zero omission needs a separate reduction theorem. Combined with Harari–Stix Theorem 2.1, U=1 and S=empty, this proves that the localization map is a bijection onto the finite-cover descent locus. The Harari–Stix statement and its geometric-conjugacy clause (iii) were read in the attached full source. Thus the equivalence between finiteness of Sec(C/K) and of that descent set is valid; it supplies no cardinality bound.

Public primary source opened: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.08005 (Theorem 1.1, page 1). It was published subsequently with DOI 10.1515/crelle-2024-0047; the preprint statement is the one checked. The Porowski 2026 abstract and metadata were found by search, but the full primary text was unavailable through the public retrieval paths attempted. Its exact corollary attribution is therefore from the supplied LM6 source and should not be labeled as a fresh direct check of Porowski 2026.

D1 remains undecided in this work: neither a uniform bound on reduction-image cardinalities on a strongly positive set, nor a proof that the finite-cover descent set is finite for every proper hyperbolic C/K, has been obtained. The already supplied LM6-D.7 arithmetic compact-space model shows precisely why finite individual images and the stated partition refinements alone do not prove finiteness. We have not repeated that result as new progress.

A further arithmetic test, completed after the initial audit, is recorded in `work/notes/density_jacobian_counterexamples.md`. The smooth genus-two curves `y^2=x^6+8x^4+16x^2+8` and `y^2=x^6+8` have Jacobians of different potential toric ranks at 5 (two and zero), but equal Frobenius traces at every good prime inert in Q(sqrt(2)), a set of density one half. The note gives the exact elliptic quotient maps proving the Jacobian isogenies, rather than merely asserting a representation-theoretic analogy. This shows that the trace-separation set in the actual arithmetic argument cannot in general be promoted from potentially density one to density one. A separate pair of CM elliptic curves has full characteristic-polynomial equality on a density-one-quarter set and no potential isogeny. Neither example asserts that a proper hyperbolic curve has infinitely many Selmer sections. Exact code and output are `work/code/density_jacobian_check.py` and `work/code/density_jacobian_check_output.txt`.

## C2: continuous descent (proved, using the stated notion of continuity)

Let Omega be the union of the function fields of finite geometric covering levels of a geometric universal pro-curve. Put the topology of pointwise convergence on the discrete set Omega on its semilinear automorphism group: a basic identity neighborhood fixes a finite collection of functions and constants. A G_K-action is continuous exactly when every element of Omega has an open stabilizer.

Choose generators f_1,...,f_r for an initial finite covering field over bar(K). Their G_K-orbits are finite, because G_K is compact. Let L be the field generated over bar(K) by these finitely many orbit elements. All elements lie at finite covering levels; their compositum is one finite etale covering level. Thus L defines a hyperbolic curve Y over bar(K) that is a finite etale cover of the original level. It is G_K-stable by construction. The subgroup H=Aut_L(Omega) is normalized by the action, since for g in G_K one has gHg^{-1}=Aut_{gL}(Omega)=H.

The original pro-curve action descends to an action on the actual open curve Y. To check the boundary, extend the induced rational map to the smooth proper compactification. Every geometric point of Y has a lift to the universal pro-cover, and the action sends such lifts to points whose projection remains in Y. The same argument for the inverse shows the open curve and its boundary are preserved. A finite coefficient extension K'/K can be chosen, Galois over K, so that its open subgroup fixes all selected generators and all the equations defining the marked model. On that subgroup the action is coefficientwise. The remaining finite Galois action is an effective descent datum for the marked curve, and gives a curve Y_0/K. Its deck group is H. The map G_K to its arithmetic automorphism group is a section; its continuity follows from the open stabilizers of functions. Conversely a continuous section acts this way on every finite function field. These constructions prove the exact continuous B'/B comparison, even with a quotient over K after descent, and in particular the version allowing finite extensions requested in C2.

The mathematical reading supports the pointwise-discrete continuity proposed in cote149 scan59; scan63 removes the normalization hypothesis and does not supply an instruction to discard continuity. The uncertain first word in scan63, `Soient|Soit`, has no mathematical effect here. The uncertain word `simple-discrete` in scan59 is supported by the finite-level/functionwise topology just defined.

## C2: the abstract action with no fixed geometric pro-point (proved)

The full proofs of AP-D.12–D.14 in `D_ultralimit_extension.tex` were checked line by line. The construction is valid. Here is the complete chain needed to use it.

Set a=1+sum_{r>=1}3^{r!} in Q_3. For A_N=1+sum_{r=1}^N3^{r!}, one has v_3(a-A_N)=(N+1)! and 1<A_N<2*3^{N!}. For a nonzero integer polynomial P of degree d, eventual nonzero P(A_N) has ordinary absolute value at most C*2^d*3^{dN!}. If P(a)=0 then it is divisible by 3^{(N+1)!}, a contradiction for large N. Thus a is transcendental over Q. It belongs to U(Q_3), with v_3(a)=0 and v_3(a-1)=1.

Recursively choose q_n different from 3, dividing none of the earlier x_i, and impose x_n=a modulo 3^n, x_n=q_n modulo q_n^2, and x_n=1 modulo every earlier q_i. CRT gives x_n>=2 and v_{q_i}(x_j)=delta_ij. Hence the square classes are independent and x_n tends to a in Q_3. The regenerated exact program checks the first twelve terms and all 144 entries of this valuation matrix; its source and output are in `code/local_ultralimit_check.py` and `code/local_ultralimit_check_output.txt`.

For arbitrary continuous representatives s_n:G_Q->E_U of these rational point sections, choose a nonprincipal ultrafilter and define s(g) as their pointwise ultralimit in the compact group E_U. Continuity of multiplication in the target proves that s is an abstract homomorphism, and projection to G_Q proves it is an abstract section. No continuity of this new homomorphism is claimed.

At every fixed finite arithmetic quotient E_U->F_0, finite etale fibres at points sufficiently close to a are G_{Q_3}-equivariantly isomorphic to the fibre at a. This follows by Hensel's inverse-function theorem after passing to a finite splitting field of the fibre at a, and by taking one small ball for its finitely many sheets and deck transformations. The isomorphism preserves the constant quotient, so the conjugator is in the image of Pi, not just in the arithmetic finite quotient. The finite geometric-conjugacy orbit is closed in the pointwise function space on G_{Q_3}. The ultralimit remains in that orbit. The nonempty closed subsets of Pi giving each such finite conjugacy have the finite intersection property, because a common finite quotient refines finitely many of them. Compactness yields one gamma in Pi with gamma s|_{G_{Q_3}} gamma^{-1}=s_a. Thus this local restriction is continuous and is the exact point section at a.

The character tau:E_U->F_2 associated to the etale cover z^2=t satisfies tau s_n=kappa_2(x_n). Independence gives a continuous surjection kappa:G_Q->product_n F_2. The ultrafilter functional epsilon on this product kills its dense direct sum and takes the constant-one sequence to one. Thus epsilon is not continuous. Since a surjective continuous map between compact Hausdorff groups is a quotient map, epsilon*kappa is not continuous. The identity tau s=epsilon*kappa proves that s itself is not continuous. It defines an abstract semilinear action on the original universal pro-cover, normalizing its original deck group. The stabilizer of sqrt(t) is the exhibited nonopen kernel; this identifies the exact failure of the functionwise-continuity condition.

For completeness, the local Kummer assertion used to determine the fixed set is proved as follows. Let L/Q_p be finite and H the kernel of its full cyclotomic character. For b in L^*, vanishing of kappa_p(b)|_H means its chosen continuous Kummer cocycle c is zero on H, because H acts trivially on Z_p(1). It descends to the compact abelian group A=chi(G_L). Pick a_0 with chi_p(a_0)!=1. Commutativity gives (chi_p(a_0)-1)c(a)=(chi_p(a)-1)c(a_0), so the descended cohomology class is killed by a nonzero p-adic scalar, and then by a power of p. Hence b^{p^m} is divisible by every p^n in L^*. Writing L^*=varpi^Z times mu_{q-1} times U^1, the valuation and finite p-group quotients of U^1 show intersection_n (L^*)^{p^n}=mu_{q-1}. Therefore b is a root of unity.

Suppose a completed geometric closed pro-point were fixed by s(G_{Q(mu_infinity)}). Its projection x on P^1(bar(Q)) is algebraic over Q. Choose a finite L/Q_3 containing x, and use the geometric conjugation above to replace the local restriction by s_a. Put H=ker(chi:G_L->Zhat^*). The fixed pro-point gives, by evaluating the compatible Kummer covers, equality of the restricted Kummer classes of f(a) and f(x), where the unit f is chosen by the following exact table:

| x | f(t) | f(x) |
|---|---|---|
| x in U(bar(Q)) | t | x |
| 0 | 1-t | 1 |
| 1 | t | 1 |
| infinity | t/(t-1) | 1 |

In each case f is regular and nonzero at x; its p-power Kummer covers extend etale there and kill the cusp inertia. Thus kappa_3(f(a)/f(x))|_H=0. The local lemma makes this ratio a root of unity. Since f(x) is algebraic and each listed f is an invertible fractional-linear function over Q, this would make a algebraic, a contradiction. The fixed set is empty.

This settles the clause left undecided by LM6-F.41 alone. LM6-F.41 gives a different abstract action with a unique fixed point; AP-D.11 gives one normalizing no finite-quotient deck group with a unique fixed cusp; AP-D.12–D.14 gives the empty fixed set above. The exact comparison with continuous actions is the inclusion of action sets together with the nonopen stabilizer just proved. No counterexample to the continuous A1 statements is produced by this abstract construction.

## D2 and D3: present scope

No proof or counterexample for the full non-isotrivial generic-reconstruction assertions of D2/D3 was obtained. The supplied LM6-B.38–B.42 were read at the exact reduction steps: a generic section factors through the relative arithmetic fundamental group of a smooth proper model; every closed-fibre restriction is a point section under the local theorem; the remaining morphism of the base into the family is not constructed by those maps alone. The constant/isotrivial proof uses the geometric-image trichotomy followed by the curve-target Hom theorem. Its target ceases to be constant in the non-isotrivial case, and that same comparison no longer supplies a morphism to a fixed curve. No assertion is made that all other mathematical methods fail.

Primary literature searched: Saidi/Tyler on birational field reduction; Saidi on proper section conjectures over function fields; Bresciani on fields of finite type; Carlson–Stix on reconstruction from the etale topos; non-isotrivial curves over finitely generated extensions of Q_p. No source opened in this audit supplied the missing unrestricted theorem. The section 8 correction and the C1/C2 computations do not settle D1–D3.

The published version of Bresciani, *On the section conjecture over fields of finite type*, Mathematische Nachrichten 298 (2025), 3476–3493, DOI `10.1002/mana.70049`, was also opened and checked (first published 8 October 2025). Its Theorem B and Corollaries A–C give exactly the stated constant-abelian-cover route, projective hyperelliptic cases, hyperbolic genus at most two, and a basis of affine opens. Sections 4.1–4.2 identify the missing generic reconstruction as the coherence property. The proof of Proposition 4.5 first uses Faltings's Tate-module Hom theorem, then finite generation of Mordell–Weil groups to turn a profinite Hom class into an integral one. Lemma 4.7 uses Hilbert irreducibility to recover a generic section from a finite cover whose maps on all closed residue fields are surjective. Corollary 4.8 then applies these maps to a family mapping nontrivially into a constant abelian variety. The unrestricted coherence result in Proposition 4.3 uses the additional weak Bombieri–Lang input in Theorem 4.2. Thus that published proof does not remove the extra input from D2 and cannot be imported as a proof of unrestricted D3.
