Independent research reviews checked the cancellation argument, the exactness-without-splitting model, the Frobenius subline example, the Tate-module warning, and the proposed first-order rigidity route. The rank statement in C29 was refined to refer to the coefficient Hom bundle, with the ambient lift fixed; C13 was refined to refer to categories of locally free crystals rather than an equivalence of sites.

No target manuscript image was read by a research worker. None of these comparisons changed a frozen page or the combined transcription. Later theorem hypotheses and the unverified portions of the old programme remain explicit in the main notes.
