### E06 — What the Legendre polynomial computes

**File/location:** cote 93, scan 004, “Hasse” formula near the bottom. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

The sheet writes \((-1)^n\sum_i\binom ni^2\lambda^i\), with \(n=(p-1)/2\), for the Legendre family. For odd \(p\), direct coefficient extraction gives this polynomial as the coefficient of \(x^{p-1}\) in \([x(x-1)(x-\lambda)]^{(p-1)/2}\), providing a short check of the stated formula and a route to its Hasse interpretation; \(\lambda=0,1\) must be excluded for a smooth Legendre curve. Context comparison: README.md's elliptic/Cartier theme is relevant, but notes/RESULTS_STATE.md's **MPL_5+6_001-037 Cote 6, scan 007, item 10** is a different Cartier comparison and contains no Legendre calculation.

**Context queries:** Hasse; Legendre; lambda; Cartier; elliptic.

### E08 — A conditional weighted-count interpretation

**File/location:** cote 93, scan 004, “deg \(\omega=1/24\)” and Hasse weight. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** medium; the degree calculation is formal, but its geometric hypotheses are not proved here.

### E11 — A concrete failure of differential rigidification in characteristic two

**File/location:** cote 93, scan 012, beginning of §4. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the example; it does not prove every case of the printed general assertion.

### E12 — Nilpotent coefficients are not zero coefficients

**File/location:** cote 93, scan 013, the two opening hypotheses in §5. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high; elementary examples over dual numbers.

The distinction “\(a_2^2=0\). Supposons qu'on ait même \(a_2=0\)” is substantive, as is the separate assumption \(a_1=0\) after \(c_4=a_1^4\) in characteristic two. Taking \(a_2=\epsilon\) or \(a_1=\epsilon\) over the corresponding \(k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\) shows that vanishing \(c_4\) need not give the stronger normal-form hypothesis on a nonreduced base. Context comparison: README.md's moduli theme does not supply this infinitesimal qualification, and notes/RESULTS_STATE.md has no matching result; these pages explicitly retain it.

**Context queries:** nilpotent; parfait; perfect; moduli; infinitésimal.

## VII. What this return establishes, and what remains a research lead

The return establishes a complete physical/textual census of the supplied package, the location of its explicit questions and qualifications, and the independent algebraic checks recorded in E09–E19, P02, P05, and C18–C21. Those checks are intentionally narrower than the manuscript's formal-moduli and classification problems. The supplied research register contains no matching settled item to re-prove, and no result from another run is promoted here merely because its status says “observation.”

The most useful next comparisons are the exact text of the two English analysis sheets; the deformation quotient on 018 against actual cote 64 material; the projective notation on 036 against actual cote 159 material; the unqualified base scope of Problem 1; the extra structures and finite-group deformation variant in Problems 2 and 3; and the nonnilpotent divided-power alternative on 042. The canonical-comparison and rigidity steps on 046 have identifiable later literature, but the full old formulation has not been proved in these notes. All post-freeze reading questions, including the published parallels, remain here rather than in the transcription.
