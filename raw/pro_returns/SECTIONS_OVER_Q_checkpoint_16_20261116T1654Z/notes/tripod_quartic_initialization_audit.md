# A.96: the complete quartic two-coordinate initialization calculation

## Outcome

The common abelian field of a rational quartic radical fibre is generated by
the individual quadratic fields and the fields Q(sqrt(2r_J)) attached to
negative-square subset products product D_i = -r_J^2. For two old
coordinates, all such subsets have one or two members. Every associated
cyclic Brauer class has an explicit constant expression. A.85 therefore
constructs the new quartic coefficient while retaining any prescribed finite
collection of actual old local points.

The source is work/sections/quartic_tripod_initialization_agent.tex.
No higher-exponent constancy or simultaneous preservation of an arbitrary
infinite list of local points is asserted.

## The exact identity that prevents the product-relation search

On aX^4-bY^4=1 with T=aX^4, put D0=c/a and D1=(c-1)/b.
For D0 D1=-r^2, let u=a(1-c) and k=br/(1-c). Then

    T-c = u (X^4+k^2Y^4),
    X^4+k^2Y^4 = (X^2+kY^2)^2-2k(XY)^2,
    (2k)/(2r) = D0/r^2.

The exact norm and squareclass maps give

    (T-c,2r) = (u,2k)+(a,D0).

Thus the joint character supplied by this negative-square product has a
constant Brauer evaluation. This is the structural reason the search below
did not produce a local variation.

A negative singleton D0=-r^2 gives

    T-c = a(X^4+r^2),
    X^4+r^2 = (X^2+r)^2-2r X^2,
    (T-c,2r) = (a,2r).

For D0=-r^2 and D1=s^2, the apparently additional character sqrt(s) is the
sum of the characters sqrt(2rs) and sqrt(2r). The product and singleton
identities therefore show that its evaluation is constant too.

## Exact finite common-field calculation

Use F=Q(mu8,sqrt P), L=F(P^(1/4)), and the actual restriction
Gal(L/Q)->Gal(F/Q). The generated root stabilizer images compute the common
abelian field, as in A.57.

If i is absent from K=Q(sqrt D_i), A.80 gives B=K. Otherwise a group element
fixing K has u=1 or 5 modulo 8. Write a lift exponent as k_P=epsilon_P+2h_P.
The literal fixed-root equations modulo 8 become

    W h = -W epsilon/2 - ((u-1)/4) delta   modulo 2.

Every left-kernel relation t gives the character of r_t for a positive-square
subset product, or of 2r_t for a negative-square subset product. These
constraints are character kernels, so the union of admissible images is
already their subgroup. One fixed negative relation expresses each positive
extra character as two negative ones modulo K. This proves the exact field
formula, with no enlargement of the root stabilizers.

## General certificate

work/code/quartic_common_character_certificate.py and its retained output
verify 1,699 finite Galois cases, 18,088 actual quotient-group elements,
897,280 literal root congruences, and 218,384 fixed root tuples counted
exactly. They include 598 cases where i lies in K and 332 cases with an
actual extra common field. All 810 positive-relation eliminations agree
with the negative-relation formula. Four symbolic norm/root/cover
identities, 108 rational squareclass identities and 144 exact affine fibre
comparisons also pass.

The A.69 three-coordinate example appears in the certificate with degree
K=8 and degree B=16. Its extra character survives the field calculation;
the two-coordinate norm proof does not address a negative subset with three
members.
