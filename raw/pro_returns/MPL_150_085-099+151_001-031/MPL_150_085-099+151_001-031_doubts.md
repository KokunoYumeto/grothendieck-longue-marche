# Legibility and limits

All 43 included pages received a first transcription from the whole scan and its two supplied halves, followed by a separate complete visual audit before the context archive was opened. Rotated and enlarged crops were also used for diagonal margins and upside-down letters. No OCR was used. The research context did not supply or correct any transcription reading.

Main prose is often readable, but rapid word endings, tiny indices, overwritten letters and diagonal additions remain difficult. The least secure passages are the diagonal margins of 150:85–86, the small footnote of 150:96, the densely cancelled formulas at the bottom of 150:99 and 151:25, overwritten calculations on 151:3 and 20, and especially the end of the diagonal note on 151:23. Clipped signatures/names on reused letters (151:4,6,8) are explicitly uncertain. Each repeated letter was checked independently on its own scan.

Every explicit doubt is conservatively assigned low confidence: these are requests for further human palaeographic review, not claims that all alternatives are equally probable. The first alternative is the preferred graphic reading. Repeated S/G, V-font, xi/zeta and pi/Pi alternatives make unresolved alphabet choices visible. No claim of error-free transcription is made.

Diagrams and the cover logo are reconstructed with TikZ-cd, arrays and TikZ paths. Relative placement, ovals, brackets, dotted arrows and cancellations are retained as closely as the fragment format permits; geometric placement is approximate. Source checks verified balanced braces/environments, allowed page-state macros, diagram cell separators, label grouping and file concatenation. A full TeX render was not available because the runtime's LaTeX/TikZ package installation was incomplete. The delivered text is a fragment with no preamble; the reader supplies definitions of the specified state macros, including math-capable underline/deletion/doubt wrappers.

Unfinished sentences, repeated equation numbers, unmatched punctuation and surprising indices remain. The separate research notes never alter the frozen files.
