## Carlson, Müller-Stach, and Peters (2017)

*Period Mappings and Period Domains*, second edition, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 168.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781316995846.
Opened publisher preview:
https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781108118989_A30888647/preview-9781108118989_A30888647.pdf.

Exact passage: Section 1.1, printed pp.20–21, preview PDF pages 36–37, derives the Legendre upper-2 and lower-minus-2 monodromies by Picard–Lefschetz squares. D.32 uses these matrices only. The following Theorem 1.1.7's literal Gamma(2) wording has a central-sign issue for the full torsion representation; the needed finite odd-level group is proved directly. Independent openings included tool refs turn869view0, turn870view0–1, and turn874view2.

## Conrad, Math 248B notes

Undated Stanford course-notes copy, 47 pages:
https://virtualmath1.stanford.edu/~conrad/248BPage/handouts/modularcurves.pdf.

Exact passages: Section 17, Proposition 17.6, pp.38–39, finite étale level-isomorphism spaces; Section 19, p.44, Galois-equivariant Tate uniformization, the split multiplicative converse, and the q-expansions of the discriminant and j-invariant. The Tate inertia and twist matrices in D.32 are derived from that description; the finite-field count and GL2 generation are explicit calculations. Openings included turn865view0, turn866view0–2, and turn876view1.

## Sutherland and Zywina (2017)

“Modular curves of prime-power level with infinitely many rational points.”
*Algebra & Number Theory* 11(5), 1199–1229.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.2140/ant.2017.11.1199.
Opened publisher PDF:
https://msp.org/ant/2017/11-5/ant-v11-n5-p05-p.pdf.

Exact passages: Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 2.2, printed pp.1206–1207, publisher PDF pages 10–11. The former describes cusps by primitive residue vectors modulo sign; the latter describes the modular function field and determinant action on cyclotomic constants. D.32 separately derives the two-sided level-isomorphism action and its action on left inertia cosets. The author-copy pagination differs and is not substituted for the publisher pagination. Openings included turn857view0 and turn874view0.

## Drinfeld (1973)

“Two theorems on modular curves.”
English publication metadata: *Functional Analysis and Its Applications* 7(2), 155–156.
Original Russian: *Funktsional'nyi Analiz i ego Prilozheniya* 7(2), 83–84.
Metadata:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=faa&option_lang=eng&paperid=2422&wshow=paper.
Opened original two-page PDF:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=faa&option_lang=eng&paperid=2422&what=fullt.

Exact passage: Theorem 1, printed p.83, and its proof through the first page. It proves torsion of cusp differences for congruence modular curves. D.32 gives the separate norm descent from geometric torsion to a finite-index lattice of rational principal cusp divisors. The full-cover rank2 calculation does not need Manin–Drinfeld. Openings included turn857view1, turn858view0 and turn874view1. The original Russian, not an English full text, was inspected.

## Scope of the source use

All five source references have exact opened passages. None is asserted to prove the section-specific rank2 theorem by itself. Its arithmetic twist action, absence of a quotient of order2 in the odd-level special linear group, full5-adic E3 example, rational-unit power relation, and conditional bounded-index variant are proved in D.32.
