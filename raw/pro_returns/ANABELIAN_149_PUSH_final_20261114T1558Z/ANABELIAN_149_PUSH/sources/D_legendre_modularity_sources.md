# Opened primary sources for D.30

All sources below were opened before integration. The full source-version caveat for KLR is retained in the manuscript and bibliography.

1. **Pan2022, primary author version arXiv:1901.07166v2.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.07166. Conjecture1.0.1, p2, explicitly includes finite ramification, potential semistability and oddness. Theorem1.0.4, p5, adds distinct Hodge-Tate weights and has an extra residual condition only at coefficient3. The theorem thus applies without another residual condition at every coefficient prime at least5. Local files: tmp/D_Pan2021.pdf and .txt. Root independently reopened and checked these passages.

2. **Serre1989, original1968, primary McGill book scan.** https://www.math.mcgill.ca/darmon/courses/18-19/gs/serre-mcgill.pdf. ChapterIII AppendixA.7 Theorem3, printedIII-52–53, PDF144–145, gives local algebraicity for abelian Hodge-Tate representations with semisimple inertia. Its proof explicitly reduces to one-dimensional E-valued characters. The local-algebraicity definition is III.1.1. IV.1.3 gives the Néron-Ogg-Shafarevich criterion. Local files: tmp/D_Serre1989.pdf and .txt. Root and degeneration independently checked the character application.

3. **Berger2002, primary author preprint arXiv:math/0210184v1.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0210184. SectionII.4.4, pp18–19, includes the Kummer calculation proving every extension of Qp by Qp(1) semistable, with the unit classes crystalline. The argument covers arbitrary completed Kummer classes. Local files: tmp/D_Berger2002.pdf and .txt.

4. **Brinon–Conrad2009, primary author notes.** https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/notes. Proposition2.4.4, p19, proves subobject/quotient stability of the Hodge-Tate category and additivity of weights. Example6.3.9, pp81–82, also checks the scalar character case by Tate–Sen.

5. **Stix2015, supplied primary source.** background/literature/stix_birational_local_conditions/src_arXiv_1203.3236/STIX-birSCoverQthmAB-arXivv3.tex, lines967–1092, subsections “Families of elliptic curves and l-adic representations” and “Almost an l-adic representation.” It distinguishes conductor, integral trace and compatibility issues. Its local table assumes semistable boundary reduction; our actual Legendre proof instead retains a potentially ramified quadratic twist at infinity. Existing key D_Stix2015.

6. **Stein2007, primary author book.** https://wstein.org/books/modform/stein-modform.pdf, Section9.2 Theorem9.4 and paragraph after Example9.5, pp162–163. The new subspace has multiplicity one already for the Hecke algebra away from the level. Section10.2, pp178–179, constructs the modular abelian variety over Q. The author HTML counterpart was also opened: https://wstein.org/books/modform/modform/newforms.html.

7. **Milne2017, primary author notes, version1.31.** https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/MF.pdf. Proposition11.21, p117, supplies existence of an elliptic curve from a rational weight-two newform; Sections10–11 give the Eichler-Shimura context. We use existence up to isogeny, not the proposition's compressed literal bijection wording.

8. **Khare–Rajan2000 primary author preprint.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0011272. Theorem1, p1, gives density-zero ramification for a continuous semisimple representation with finite coefficient field. Corollary3, p4, gives the stronger finite-ramification assertion in the abelian semisimple case. These are not conflated.

9. **Khare–Larsen–Ramakrishna2003 primary author preprint.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0309283. Main Theorem and full proof, Section2.2, pp19–21, allow 1≤k≤p−1; the trace-selection bound works at k1. Section2.4 and Corollary14, pp23–24, construct density-one compatible pairs and explicitly withhold strict Weil-Deligne compatibility at ramified primes. Local files: tmp/D_KLR2003.pdf and .txt. Root independently read all these passages. **Not opened:** final AJM127(4)(2005),709–734, DOI10.1353/ajm.2005.0026. Crossref resolves its publisher location to https://muse.jhu.edu/article/185366; retrieval did not supply the final PDF. Exact theorem numbering and weight range refer only to the opened author preprint.

10. **Milne2020 Class Field Theory.** https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf. Primary author text was reopened for the Kronecker-Weber/class-field background. The elementary dense-direct-sum and torsion-free target argument used after that identification is proved in D.30, rather than attributed to a stronger unexamined character theorem.

No priority claim is made. The searches did not establish a theorem removing finite ramification from Pan's hypotheses for section-induced Legendre representations. The explicit condition remains in every statement of the deduction.
