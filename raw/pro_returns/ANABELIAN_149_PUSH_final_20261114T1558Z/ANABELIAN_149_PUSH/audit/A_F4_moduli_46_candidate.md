## 1. Statement and precise scope

Among the48 geometric ordinary genus-two moduli classes fixed by the fourth-power Frobenius,46 satisfy A1 by the constructions below. Equivalently, every ordinary genus-two curve over F4 satisfies the claimed point-section injectivity unless its geometric normalized coefficient triple has one of the two exceptional symmetric polynomials

    Z³+alpha*Z²+alpha²,    Z³+alpha²*Z²+alpha,
    alpha²+alpha+1=0.

The assertion includes every finite extension, every finitely generated form of each of the46 geometric classes, and every hyperbolic open subcurve. The two exceptional geometric classes remain undecided by this enumeration; neither is a counterexample to A1.

## 2. Why there are exactly48 coefficient classes

Over an algebraic closure, every ordinary genus-two curve in characteristic two has reduced Artin–Schreier form

    C(a,b,c): y²+y=a*x+b/x+c/(x+1),  abc≠0.

Indeed its hyperelliptic double quotient has three branch points (two-rank=number of Artin–Schreier poles minus one). The genus-two different formula then forces all three reduced pole orders to be one. Normalize their coordinates to0,1,infinity. A constant term is removed over a finite extension.

An isomorphism must preserve the unique hyperelliptic pencil and permute these three branch points. The two generating coordinate changes x→x+1 and x→1/x permute the coefficients a,b,c, up to a removable constant. Over the identity base map an Artin–Schreier generator can only change by y→y+v(x): the coefficient of y forces any multiplicative factor to be one. A nonconstant v has a pole, and v²+v then has a positive even pole order, which cannot equal a difference with only simple poles. Thus v is constant. Consequently the unordered triple {a,b,c} is exactly the geometric moduli datum.

If a curve descends to F4, fourth-power Frobenius preserves this unordered triple. Conversely the list of such triples is precisely the root-multiset list of monic cubic polynomials over F4 with nonzero constant coefficient. There are4*4*3=48. This count includes repeated coefficient roots; the three branch coordinates themselves remain distinct. The splitting types are10 fully split triples,18 rational-root plus irreducible-quadratic triples, and20 irreducible-cubic triples. Their coefficients therefore lie respectively in F4,F16,F64. The proof below only needs that every actual F4 curve belongs to the list; it does not require a separate descent theorem for arbitrary stable moduli points.

Encode elements of F4 by0,1,2=alpha,3=alpha². A three-digit key s1s2s3 denotes the cubic Z³+s1 Z²+s2 Z+s3. The46 positive keys and witness annihilators n are given below. All48 keys occur exactly once; the omitted keys are203 and302.

## 4. A direct finite-field norm certificate for each row

The complete data are code/A_theta_F4_moduli_witnesses.json. Its46 records give:

- the binary defining polynomial of the coefficient field and the exact coefficient triple(a,b,c);
- an irreducible polynomial q(T) over that field, its root t, and delta=phi(t);
- coefficient lists A(x),B(x), in ascending powers of x, with degA≤d and monic B of degree d=(n−3)/2;
- exact nonzero values B(t),B(0),B(1), and A(t)=0.

Let u=x(x+1) and F=b+(b+c)x+a x²+a x³=u phi(x). Each record proves the full polynomial identity

    u(A²+AB)+B²(F+delta*u)=a(x+t)^n.             (M48.1)

The coefficient fields are represented by binary moduli7,19,67, namely F2[w]/(w²+w+1), F2[w]/(w⁴+w+1), and F2[w]/(w⁶+w+1). The coordinate field is the polynomial quotient by q(T). Each integer field element is encoded by consecutive blocks of e bits for its coefficients in1,T,...,T^(degq−1), where e is2,4,or6. All coordinate factors in the table have degree at most3. The largest fields needed are F4096, arising in the two degree49 rows; no large implicit extension is required.

The verifier code/A_theta_F4_moduli_witnesses.py uses ordinary polynomial coefficient lists and does not import the binary-mask determinant engine. It verifies finite-field inverses, selects and certifies an irreducible factor, solves the original local power-series equations, verifies every jet, and then multiplies both sides of(M48.1) in full. Its output is PASS for all46 rows. This gives fixed exact algebraic witnesses independently of how they were discovered.

For a direct geometric interpretation choose beta satisfying beta²+beta=delta and put R=(t,beta), h=A+B(y+beta). Then Nm(h)=a(x+t)^n/u. Since A(t)=0 and B(t)≠0, h vanishes at R and its conjugate does not; the x-map is étale there. The norm has its only zero at t, of order n. Thus h has a sole zero of order n at R. Its simple poles at W0,W1 follow from B(0)B(1)≠0. The monic term x^d y has unique largest infinity pole2d+1=n−2. Consequently

    div(h)=nR−W0−W1−(n−2)Winfty,
    div(h/u^((n−1)/2))=n(R−Btheta).            (M48.2)

The theta class eta=O(R−Btheta) is nonzero: if R~Btheta, then2R is canonical, forcing R to be Weierstrass, contrary to t≠0,1,infinity. Its exact order d0>1 divides n. Reduction (Z/n)^*→(Z/d0)^* is surjective, so the full-generation property of2,−1 modulo n descends to d0. A.C2.17's compact criterion, or A.C2.19, proves A1 for this curve. Therefore the positive conclusion uses only(M48.1), the listed local values, and the already proved primitive-Prym criterion. It needs no negative torsion scan or assertion of exact order n.

Every witness, isomorphism and deck cover is defined after a finite extension of the relevant field of definition. The lifting criterion A.3 and the geometric contradiction in A.C2.17–20 then give the stated finite-extension, finitely generated form and open-subcurve scope.

## 6. The exact remaining pair

Key203 has normalized coefficients

    F16=F2[w]/(w⁴+w+1), alpha=w²+w,
    (a,b,c)=(1,w³+1,w³+w²+w)=(1,9,14).

Fourth-power Frobenius swaps b,c, hence its branch permutation interchanges0 and1. An actual F4 model is

    v²+v=z+alpha²(z+1)/(z²+z+alpha).           (M48.3)

Indeed put x=z+w and y=v+k with k²+k=w; such k exists in F16. The rational part transforms exactly to(M48.3). Key302 is its coefficientwise square conjugate, with alpha replaced by alpha², and normalized coefficients(1,11,13).

These two cases have not been decided by the positive constructions in this table. Bounded exact Hankel scans record no suitable hit through the displayed tested orders67. ## 7. Discovery, verification, and scope of computation

The complete geometric Hankel criterion is already proved in A.C2.18 and audit/A_theta_Hankel_scan.md. The exploratory program code/A_theta_F4_moduli_scan.py enumerates all48 monic-cubic keys and computes maximal-minor gcds over the algebraic closure. Original separate-determinant data are preserved in code/A_theta_F4_moduli_original_minors.json. An augmented-Bareiss adjugate method was checked against189 identical exact gcd polynomials from that implementation, with PASS output code/A_theta_F4_moduli_adjugate_check.json.

For the augmented method, choose an invertible square block A of the rectangular matrix and let b be its remaining row. Fraction-free elimination on[A^t|b^t] and exact back substitution compute det(A)A^(-t)b^t; its entries are the other signed maximal minors. In characteristic two the signs disappear. All divisions are checked exact. A partial unit gcd proves emptiness; a positive result uses every maximal minor. This justifies the exploratory scan's algorithm but is not needed in the positive table once the direct norm witnesses have been independently verified.

The table leaves universal A1, and the two named F4 classes, unresolved. It does not replace the conditional primitive-zero criterion by the weaker assertion that some odd theta torsion exists.
