### R27 and the two definitions

CHW Definition 2.19 says that pinned morphisms preserve closed points. Its Notation 2.20 defines the corresponding full subgroupoid of relative morphisms. Specifying the commutation isomorphism over BG_k is already part of being a relative geometric morphism; it does not impose this condition. LM5-F.5 uses the correct definition. Consequently the error is in the prompt's R27 summary, not a discovered error in the intended statement of LM5-F.50.

The explicit splitting G_k -> G_{k(t)} produces a relative geometric morphism Spec(k)_et -> (P1_k)_et with generic image. It cannot arise from a scheme map, and universal homeomorphisms do not change the obstruction. Thus no characteristic-zero field has the literal all-morphisms property in R27. The universal criterion in F2 cannot both use that literal definition and contain the asserted F.50.

The stronger construction X_et -> B pi1(X) -> B G_{k(t)} -> X_et for X=G_m acts as the identity on pi1 and cannot factor through BG_k. Its point map is constant generic, however. This is recorded as such; it is not counted as the required nonconstant endomorphism in the intended F2.
