## Exact classification source

Cardona, G., Nart, E., & Pujolàs, J. (2005). Curves of genus two over fields of even characteristic. Mathematische Zeitschrift,250(1),177–201. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-004-0750-0

Primary author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0210105 . Opened as turn827view0; precise passages reopened as turn828view0–2 and turn831view0–1. Exact loci:

- Proposition14, authorp14: the rank-one normal form ax³+bx+c/x+d, scaling action(a,b,c,d)↦(lambda³a,lambda b,lambda^-1c,d), and automorphism group generated by the hyperelliptic involution.
- Section2.1 and Theorem20, authorpp18–19: geometric Igusa invariant coordinates; the three ramification strata are j1≠0, j1=0/j2≠0, and j1=j2=0. In the rank-one normalization c=1 the invariants are(0,A,B).
- Theorem22/Corollary23 and proof, authorpp21–22: every invariant triple over the perfect base has a model there, with the explicit rank-one equation j2x³+j3x+1/x. Thus the geometric F4 moduli classes total64, partitioned48+12+4 by two-rank.
- Theorem17, authorp16:146 arithmetic F4-isomorphism classes. This is a different count from64 geometric classes; the theorem under consideration covers every twist because its hypothesis and cover criterion are geometric.

Publisher record independently opened: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00209-004-0750-0 , turn832view0. It confirms the author order Cardona,Nart,Pujolàs, the journal/volume/pages and DOI. The UAB repository record lists the authors in a different order; the primary PDF and publisher order are used.

This source establishes classification and descent. It does not prove a geometrically prime-to-two point-section injectivity statement. The A1 inference in candidateA.C2.26 is the explicit norm plus primitive-cover argument, not an attribution to this classification paper.
