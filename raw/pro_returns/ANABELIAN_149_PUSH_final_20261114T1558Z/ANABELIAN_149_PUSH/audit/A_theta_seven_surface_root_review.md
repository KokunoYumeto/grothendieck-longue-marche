# Root review of the ordinary theta-seven surface

The root read the complete `A_theta_seven_surface_candidate.md`, including the exact parameter algebra, principal-part construction, norm/divisor, character and section proof, finite moduli fibres, converse chart, one-parameter family and finite example. The two complete independent reviews are `A_theta_seven_surface_independent_review.md` and `A_theta_seven_surface_topos_review.md`. Their distinct certificates are retained. The candidate is accepted in its stated scope as A.C2.15.

For the degree claim, the polynomial lambda*(x+t)^5*(x^2+t)^2+u^3 has unit leading coefficient and degree9 over the specified base. Each excluded factor is nonzero at every geometric point, hence a unit in the finite algebra. Modulo the polynomial, its derivative is u^2*(x^2+t)/(x+t), also a unit. The finite etale degree9 algebra therefore exists over the entire base; removing the diagonal of its fibre square gives rank72. No claim of geometric irreducibility of this cover is needed. Every component has dimension2.

At a simple root r of B, the reciprocal B^2 has a double-pole term and no simple-pole term, a crucial characteristic-two point. With kappa/(r+s)^2=H_t(r), the two principal coefficients are exactly rho_r^2 and rho_r. The same holds at s. Their Artin–Schreier cancellation leaves only the three named simple poles. Evaluation at t fixes the remaining constant. This proves the all-parameter norm identity, rather than inferring it from a finite search.

The norm zero at t lies on exactly one unramified sheet because B(t) is nonzero. Its order is exactly7. The two finite branch poles of h have order1, and the unique x^2*y term has pole order5 at infinity. Thus dividing by u^3 gives exactly7(R−B_theta). The theta class is nonzero because R is not a Weierstrass point. The two doubling cycles modulo7 are negatives, so symmetry of this genus-two theta kills both stable character cycles. The resulting cover has genus8, two-rank2 and trivial odd deck action on all two-primary torsion. The already proved A.C2.7 applies with the correct finite extension of the actual finitely generated field of definition, giving all stated forms and open subcurves.

The moduli dimension is supported by an actual finite-fibre argument. A fixed curve has only finitely many theta7 points. Each specifies the divisor of h, hence h up to scalar. Its monic quadratic y-coefficient removes that scalar and has at most two ordered root choices. The six-dimensional Riemann–Roch space used to extract this coefficient is correct. Passing to isomorphism classes introduces only six branch normalizations, since a nonconstant Artin–Schreier coordinate correction would introduce an uncancellable even pole. The checked finite-field point has nonzero cubic obstruction, so its surface component has a two-dimensional image with a nonempty open part outside that locus.

The converse is confined to the stated separable quadratic-B chart. The separate inseparable-B family is proved by its own universal identity; no exhaustion of the double-root boundary is asserted. The construction gives a positive family and does not supply a useful theta torsion point on every ordinary genus-two curve. No priority is claimed.
