# Independent root review of the finite-cusp criterion

Verdict: PASS for the sufficient criteria and the stated conditional arithmetic reduction. This is not a proof of cofinite integrality for all sections or of the exclusion of two or three cusp labels.

For the tripod, the functions 1-t, t and t/(t-1) are regular and nonzero at the respective cusps0,1,infinity, with value1. At an arbitrary continuous local cusp splitting, their Kummer classes evaluate to that actual leading value because the associated roots are unramified at the cusp; no rationality of the tangent is needed. Under cofinite ordinary integrality and a single cusp label, D.3 makes the global class rational. A cuspidal localization then makes it1 by injectivity of local Kummer theory. Each displayed function omits1 on the tripod, contradicting any ordinary local point. There are ordinary places by the cofinite hypothesis. D.5 then applies with every finite localization ordinary.

For a general curve over Q, a nonconstant global unit with zero order at each used boundary centre similarly gives one rational Kummer value. All finite local support points lie in a finite fibre of the extension of that function to the proper completion. The previously audited finite-place finite-support argument D.5/D.22 then makes the section geometric; noncuspidality puts its point in the open curve. The boundary divisor map is injective modulo Q*, so unit rank greater than the number of relevant boundary centres supplies such a unit. For a finite étale section neighbourhood, noncuspidality transfers because a boundary decomposition group maps to a boundary decomposition group; after spreading the finite map over the integers away from finitely many primes, ordinary integrality transfers by the valuative criterion for a finite map. Thus the neighbourhood unit-rank criterion is valid, but existence of such neighbourhoods is not proved.

For the remaining tripod case, the exact exponent description of D.3 places both Kummer coordinates in the profinite completion of the finitely generated S-unit group. Compactness and density give precisely the closure of that group in the outside-S local unit product. The exceptional cusp relations are B_p=1, A_p=1, and A_p/B_p=-1. They do not force the tangent-dependent profinite valuation coordinates to be integers. If the outside-S tuple is diagonal, D.26 detects both global Kummer coordinates and excludes every cusp; this implication does not assert the converse or lift arbitrary closure points to sections.
