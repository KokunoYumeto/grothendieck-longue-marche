## Primary passages opened

1. Bary-Soroker, L. (2008). On the characterization of Hilbertian fields.
   *International Mathematics Research Notices, 2008*, Article rnn089.
   https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnn089
   Primary copy: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0451
   Opened version: arXiv0803.0451v3, 12June2012, 9pages. Root read §4,
   Definition4.1 and Example4.2, pp8--9, Theorem4.4 and its complete proof,
   p9, and Remark4.5. **Theorem4.4**, not the old-version Theorem12, is the
   numbering in this opened copy. It proves that small Galois extensions
   of Hilbertian fields are Hilbertian. The proof itself cites finite
   extension preservation and credits Fried--Jarden, Proposition16.11.1.
   The publisher metadata was retrieved separately. Root web records:
   turn882view0 and turn886view0/3; the finite-field reviewer opened the
   same exact version independently.

2. Bary-Soroker, L., Fehm, A., & Petersen, S. (2023).
   *Hilbert properties under base change in small extensions*
   [Preprint, arXiv2312.16219v1]. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.16219
   Root opened Theorem1.2, p1, the bounded-degree-compositum proof in §4,
   and Corollary4.3 with its explanatory paragraph, p5. This proves the
   Hilbert property and weak Hilbert property persist under small
   algebraic extensions of characteristic-zero fields, and identifies the
   projective-line/Galois case as the already known Fried--Jarden result.
   Root records: turn882view1 and turn886view4. The later 2026 journal
   metadata was returned by institutional records; the mathematical copy
   actually read is the stated 2023 preprint. We do not silently replace
   it by an unread journal version.

3. SGA1, supplied original source
   `background/literature/SGA1/src_arXiv_math_0206203/smf_doc-math_3_01.tex`.
   Root directly reread ExposéXII, Theorem5.1, printed p333, and
   ExposéXIII,4.5.3, printed p421. These give Riemann existence and the
   geometric base-change comparison used for the universal-tripod
   example, with all primes allowed in characteristic zero. The existing
   SGA1 reference remains the primary citation. A finitely generated free
   group calculation then gives the smallness and open-subgroup claims;
   neither reconstruction nor its arithmetic normality is being read
   into SGA1.

of an arbitrary field uses the previously opened Bary-Soroker--Paran
source at the exact loci recorded in F.45. Finite-extension preservation
and full-cover irreducible specialization use the previously opened

independently reviewed within this return. It retains the full
closed-point-preserving topos convention, a normal algebraic model,
roots of unity and a weaker divisible-kernel inclusion. It gives a
sufficient field class and strict examples. It does not prove an intrinsic
necessary-and-sufficient field classification, and F.9 remains unresolved.
