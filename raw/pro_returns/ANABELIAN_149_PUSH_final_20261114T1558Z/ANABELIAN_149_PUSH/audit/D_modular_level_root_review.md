## Arithmetic and geometric distinction

The symplectic-isomorphism cover is an actual finite étale neighbourhood
of the section: the pulled-back identity is Galois fixed. Its geometric
monodromy is the full SL2 over Z/N, generated by the two Legendre
unipotents since N is odd. The horizontal inertia groups have orders
N,N,2N. Keeping the central sign at infinity is essential; projectivizing
at the beginning would give the wrong degree and cusp counts for the
stated full torsion cover.

With the left-coset convention, the semilinear action is correctly
rho_c(sigma)*h*rho_s(sigma)^(-1). On the cyclotomic kernel the local Tate
representation is a quadratic scalar twist of an inertia unipotent.
The quadratic-twisted image has full projective image. An index-two
subgroup would yield a quotient of SL2(Z/N) of order2, which is impossible
because that group is generated by odd-order unipotents. This proves
transitivity on each of the three cusp fibres even with nonsplit
multiplicative tangential models. No arithmetic element changes the
rational cusp labels.

The degree-zero rational boundary-divisor lattice has rank2. The two
independent base units already attain that rank. Clearing denominators
then proves v^m=q*lambda^a*(1-lambda)^b for every rational unit. Hence a
unit regular and nonzero at two distinct original labels must be
constant. This conclusion for the full cover does not need
Manin--Drinfeld. That theorem is correctly used only to explain the
analogous general congruence-cover rank formula.
