\begin{result}{B.1}{Open}{The literal descent subgroup}
We retain the quotient $\varphi_P(x)=s$, $\varphi_P(y)=\tau$
and its physical comparison $\varphi_L$ in B.1.1.
For the literal subgroup $C^\varphi=C_P$, the inclusion
$\widehat{GT}\subseteq C^\varphi$ and both equalities
$C^\varphi=\widehat{GT}$ and $C^\varphi=\ker\Delta$
are refuted by the actual arithmetic element in B.1.2.
The reverse inclusion $\ker\Delta\subseteq C^\varphi$
is refuted in B.1.3. The remaining requested decision is
$C^\varphi\subseteq\ker\Delta$. We prove the normalized
$d_2$ vanishing on its multiplier-one subgroup and record
the exact unresolved $\widetilde e_3=\epsilon_3(T)+\Psi_3/2$ comparison below.
The physical descent fixes full GT, and the literal descent
satisfies $D_P(F)=cFc$, by B.1.8. B.2.5 identifies the remaining
restriction as $\Delta|_{C_P}=(\Delta\circ D_P)|_{C_P}$ and
proves that $\Delta$ is independent of the image of descent
pullback on the quadratic annihilator.
\end{result}
