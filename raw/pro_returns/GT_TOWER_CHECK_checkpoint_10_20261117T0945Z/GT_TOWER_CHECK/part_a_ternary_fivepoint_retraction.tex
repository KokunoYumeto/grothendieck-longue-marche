\subsection{A split five-point map retaining the ternary modular centralizer detector}

Let $\Gamma=\widehat\Gamma_{0,5}$ have the five
generators
\[
 (a,b,c,d,e)=(A_{13},A_{23},A_{14},A_{24},A_{34}),
 \qquad A_{12}=(abcde)^{-1}
                                                               \tag{TMR.1}
\]
and the six actual free-by-free relations used in
\path{build_fivepoint_cm_level.py}.
Put $F=\widehat F(x,y)$ with its original signed
map $\vartheta_{12}(x)=U^2$, $\vartheta_{12}(y)=L^2$.
The signed map $q:\Gamma\twoheadrightarrow Q$ of
that certificate has $|Q|=192$; write
\[
 N=\ker q,\qquad K=\ker\vartheta_{12},\qquad
 \Gamma_m=\Gamma/(N'N^m),\quad F_m=F/(K'K^m).
                                                               \tag{TMR.2}
\]
All powers and commutators denote closed subgroups.

\begin{result}{A.4.45}{Proved}{The old alternating-prime five-point target excludes the identity cubic frame}
For every integer $m\geq2$ there are actual split
maps
\[
 \Gamma_m\xrightarrow{\ \pi_m\ }F_m
       \xrightarrow{\ \iota_m\ }\Gamma_m,
 \qquad \pi_m\iota_m=\operatorname{id}_{F_m},
                                                               \tag{TMR.3}
\]
where $\iota$ is the fifth pentagon insertion
$x\mapsto A_{12}$, $y\mapsto A_{23}$.
The subgroup $K$ is exactly the kernel of the
original projective map
$B\to\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z/12)/\{\pm I\}$;
in particular $K'K^3$ is normal in $B$.
The finite ternary modular source
$B/(K'K^3)$ is therefore retained by the actual
$B$-core source of this single insertion into
$\Gamma_3$.

In that modular source the projection of the
pure $r$-centralizer to the original signed pure
image is precisely the identity:
\[
 \operatorname{im}\left(
 C_{B/(K'K^3)}(r)\cap F_3
             \longrightarrow\vartheta_{12}(F)\right)=\{I\}.
                                                               \tag{TMR.4}
\]
Consequently the identity source automorphism has
no pure cubic frame with signed value $5I$ at
this actual five-point input.  More directly,
every actual normalized multiplier-one gentle
element whose fifth pair evaluation is one in
$\Gamma_3$ has $\vartheta_{12}(A_g)=I$.
This is an exact exclusion of the identity pair
record with the earlier cubic frame.  Identity
pair records with other harmonic frames, and
general nonidentity pair records, remain separate
canonical-cover lifting questions.
\end{result}
\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The free retraction and its signed marking.}\quad
Define the continuous map on the actual presentation
by
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccccc}
             &a&b&c&d&e\\ \hline
 \pi         &x^{-1}y^{-1}&y&y&y^{-1}x^{-1}&x\\
 \epsilon    &1&0&0&1&0 .
 \end{array}                                                    \tag{TMR.5}
\]
The bottom row has values in $C_2$.
The six relators, written with the same commutator
convention as the target presentation, are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 aca^{-1}&=e^{-1}ce,&
 ada^{-1}&=[c,e]_*d[c,e]_*^{-1},&
 aea^{-1}&=[c,e]_*e,\\
 bcb^{-1}&=c,&
 bdb^{-1}&=e^{-1}de,&
 beb^{-1}&=[d,e]_*e,
 \qquad [v,w]_* =w^{-1}v^{-1}wv.
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{TMR.6}
\]
Each becomes the identity after free reduction
under $\pi$, and each has zero $\epsilon$-value.
Also $\pi((abcde)^{-1})=x$ and $\pi(b)=y$.
Thus these are actual homomorphisms and
$\pi\iota=\operatorname{id}_F$.

Evaluate the original signed four-braid marking
$\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3\mapsto U,L,U$.
On the five named generators it gives exactly
\[
                   q(h)=(-I)^{\epsilon(h)}
                                      \vartheta_{12}(\pi(h)).
                                                               \tag{TMR.7}
\]
This is an equality on generators and hence on
the entire profinite source.
The signed image $\vartheta_{12}(F)$ has order
$96$ and has modulo-four image
$\langle U^2,L^2\rangle=C_2^2$.
That four-element image omits $-I$.
Therefore $-I\notin\vartheta_{12}(F)$ and the
signed and projective kernels on this named
free group coincide.
The target image in (TMR.7) is the direct product
$\vartheta_{12}(F)\times\langle-I\rangle$,
of order $192$.
It follows in particular that
\[
         \pi(N)\subseteq K,\qquad \iota(K)\subseteq N.
                                                               \tag{TMR.8}
\]
The second inclusion uses the actual signed
equality $q\iota=\vartheta_{12}$; no projective
sign is discarded.
These two inclusions carry commutators and
$m$th powers into the corresponding subgroups
in (TMR.2).  They induce (TMR.3) by their exact
quotient maps.

The projective $B$ image has order $576$, and
its reduction modulo two is the marked quotient
$B/F\cong\mathfrak S_3$.
Its kernel is therefore contained in $F$.
On $F$ it is the projectivization of
$\vartheta_{12}$, which was just shown injective
on its signed image.  This proves the asserted
equality of kernels and all normality statements.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{An exact finite quotient detecting all six pure centralizer residues.}\quad
Set $Q_B=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z/12)/\{\pm I\}$,
and denote its marked images of $s,r$ by $S,R$.
Let $\Omega=Q_B/\langle R\rangle$ be its $192$
left cosets and $V=\mathbb F_3[\Omega]$.
The following assignments are literal homomorphisms
from the original source:
\[
 s\longmapsto(0,S),\qquad
 r\longmapsto(e_{\langle R\rangle},R)
                  \quad\text{in }V\rtimes Q_B .
                                                               \tag{TMR.9}
\]
Indeed $S^2=R^3=1$, the displayed basis vector
is fixed by $R$, and three copies sum to zero.
The image of $K$ lies in the elementary abelian
group $V$.  Hence (TMR.9) kills $K'K^3$ and
is an actual quotient of $B/(K'K^3)$.

Here is the full finite calculation used in (TMR.4).
The pure base image has order $96$.
Its free kernel $K$ has rank $97$, obtained by
the usual spanning tree on that actual Cayley
graph.  The $97$ resulting source words evaluated
under (TMR.9) span a subspace $W\subset V$ of
dimension $96$.
For each of the six pure base residues $h$
commuting with $R$, choose its recorded pure
source word.  Its value is $(t_h,h)$.
All pure lifts of that residue in the image of
(TMR.9) are exactly $(t_h+w,h)$ with $w\in W$.
Writing $e=e_{\langle R\rangle}$, commutation
with the displayed lift of $r$ is exactly
\[
       t_h+h e-e-Rt_h\ \in\ (R-1)W.
                                                               \tag{TMR.10}
\]
This follows by multiplying both orders in the
specified semidirect product.  Thus it tests the
actual source fibre, not arbitrary vectors of $V$.

The exact calculation
\path{check_ternary_modular_centralizer.py}
uses the named mod-twelve multiplication table,
the $97$ actual Schreier words and their literal
permutation-module images.
It obtains $\dim(R-1)W=62$.
Among all six residues, only $h=1$ satisfies
(TMR.10).  For each other residue the JSON
certificate records a vector $\ell\in V^*$ with
\[
     \ell((R-1)W)=0,
     \qquad\ell(t_h+h e-e-Rt_h)\ne0.
                                                               \tag{TMR.11}
\]
In particular the signed scalar $5I$ fails this
test.  The identity residue has the identity
commuting lift.  The quotient (TMR.9) therefore
proves exactly (TMR.4).
All six rows and their dual vectors are retained
in the executed certificate; this assertion is
a complete finite linear-algebra calculation.

The free reductions, the two signed image orders,
the omission of $-I$, and both source markings
were independently replayed in
\path{check_fivepoint_signed_retraction.py}.
These are complementary checks of the exact
maps (TMR.3), not numerical dimension comparisons
between unspecified quotients.

Finally, $\iota_3$ is injective by its retraction,
so its actual source kernel is precisely $K'K^3$.
It is already $B$-normal.  The joint source for
any larger finite list containing this insertion
therefore maps onto $B/(K'K^3)$.
An identity source automorphism with a pure
cubic frame maps that frame to the centralizer
in (TMR.4), ruling out signed value $5I$.
For an actual normalized gentle element with
the stated identity fifth evaluation,
$(f_g)_{F_3}=1$ by (TMR.3).
The literal modular formulas
$\Phi_g(s)=s f_g$, $\Phi_g(r)=f_g^{-1}r$
then make its induced action on $B/(K'K^3)$
the identity.  Its genuine pure frame satisfies
$\Phi_g(r)=A_g rA_g^{-1}$ and is therefore
in the centralizer in (TMR.4).
This proves the asserted signed-frame restriction.
\end{proof}
