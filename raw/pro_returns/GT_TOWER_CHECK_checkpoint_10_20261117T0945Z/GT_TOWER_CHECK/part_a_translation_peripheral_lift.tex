\subsection{A characteristic finite peripheral lift at the nine-point cover}

\begin{result}{A.4.9}{Proved}{A degree-three peripheral lift has exactly the actual geometric class-two image}
Use the free surface group
$\Pi=F(A,B,C_1,\ldots,C_8)$ and its nine literal peripheral
classes from (TFC.1)--(TFC.3), with
$C_9=([A,B]C_1\cdots C_8)^{-1}$.
There are characteristic finite quotients
\[
 G_3=\Pi/\bigl(\gamma_4\Pi\,(\gamma_2\Pi)^3\,\Pi^9\bigr)
 \longrightarrow
 G_2=\Pi/\bigl(\gamma_3\Pi\,\Pi^3\bigr),
 \qquad |G_3|=3^{395},\quad |G_2|=3^{55}.
                                                        \tag{TPL.1}
\]
Powers here mean the subgroups generated by all indicated powers.
Thus the kernels are verbal and remain characteristic in the full
profinite free group.  In particular the generators of $G_3$ have
order nine; its third layer has not been replaced by an
exponent-three class-three group.

Consider outer automorphisms of $G_3$ which send each of the nine
named peripheral elements to a conjugate of its $\lambda$th
power, for one common $\lambda\in(\mathbb Z/9)^\times$.
Among their images in $\operatorname{Out}(G_2)$, the subgroup
acting identically on $H_1(G_2,\mathbb F_3)$ is exactly the image
of actual pure surface mapping classes acting identically on
that homology.  It is elementary abelian of dimension $91$.
Its inclusion in the $440$-dimensional outer IA space is given
by the literal matrices in the certificate below.

More generally, the complete image in $\operatorname{Out}(G_2)$
of these peripheral automorphisms is the actual image of the
pure extended mapping class group, allowing reversal of
orientation while fixing every puncture label.  The two signs
are related by the explicit reflection in (TPL.8).
This finite image has order $48\cdot3^{107}$; its
orientation-preserving subgroup has order $24\cdot3^{107}$.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
We give the finite groups, the peripheral obstruction, and actual
geometric witnesses for every element in its kernel separately.

\paragraph{The finite group and its power relations.}
Take ten letters $X_i$ in the order of the surface basis.
In the associative algebra over
$\mathbb F_3$ on these letters, quotient by all monomials of
degree at least four.  The finite subgroup generated by
$1+X_1,\ldots,1+X_{10}$ in its group of units realizes $G_3$.
Indeed it has nilpotency class at most three,
\[
 (1+X_i)^3=1+X_i^3,\qquad (1+X_i)^9=1,
 \qquad (1+U)^3=1\quad\text{for }\deg U\geq2.
                                                        \tag{TPL.2}
\]
For any $U$ of positive degree, $(1+U)^9=1$ as well.
Every weight-three commutator is central.  Commutator collection
therefore writes a group element as a product of ten generators
with exponents modulo nine, forty-five basic commutators with
exponents modulo three, and weight-three commutators with
exponents modulo three.  Skew symmetry and the weight-three
Hall--Witt identity leave $330$ such commutators: for each
$i<j$ use $[[X_i,X_j],X_i]$ and $[[X_i,X_j],X_j]$, and
for each $i<j<k$ use $[[X_i,X_j],X_k]$ and
$[[X_i,X_k],X_j]$.  Their number is
$2\binom{10}{2}+2\binom{10}{3}=330$.

Their degree-three associative tensors are linearly independent.
This follows by comparing their distinct monomial coefficients,
and is also checked by the rank-$330$ tensor matrix in the
certificate.  None contains a monomial $X_i^3$, so the ten
cubic powers in (TPL.2) supply ten further independent central
directions.  After the degree-one and degree-two terms of a
collected word have been removed, these $340$ degree-three
coefficients determine its remaining word.  Thus the collected
normal forms are distinct and the order is
$3^{10+45+330+10}=3^{395}$.
The same collection gives an upper bound of this order for the
verbal quotient in (TPL.1), while the displayed finite algebra
gives a surjective image of exactly that order.  They are
therefore the same group, with the stated explicit quotient map.
Killing the third layer gives $G_2$, of order $3^{10+45}$.
These arguments also show directly that the construction
commutes with passage to the full profinite free group.

For the calculation, use the further quotient
\[
 R=G_3/\langle[\gamma_2G_3,C_i]:1\leq i\leq8\rangle.
                                                        \tag{TPL.3}
\]
This is a quotient only of the central third layer.  Its killed
space is the literal span
$\sum_{i=1}^8[L_2,C_i]\subset L_3$, of rank $304$.
Consequently $|R|=3^{91}$ and $R\twoheadrightarrow G_2$.
Including $C_9$ in the generating list in (TPL.3) gives exactly
the same subgroup: its linear part is $-\sum_i C_i$, and its
remaining part has weight at least two.  The subgroup is
preserved by every permutation of the nine peripheral classes
and by their common invertible powers.  In particular it is
preserved by both translations and by every peripheral
automorphism under discussion.  The characteristic input of
the result remains $G_3$; (TPL.3) is its explicit peripheral
quotient, with no assertion that it is characteristic under
arbitrary automorphisms of a free group with forgotten
peripheral data.

\paragraph{The exact peripheral obstruction.}
Write $V=H_1(G_2,\mathbb F_3)$ and
$L_2=\bigwedge^2 V$.  The IA automorphisms of $G_2$ form
$\operatorname{Hom}(V,L_2)$, and quotienting by inner
automorphisms gives
\[
 \mathcal V=\operatorname{Hom}(V,L_2)/\operatorname{ad}V,
 \qquad \dim\mathcal V=450-10=440.                    \tag{TPL.4}
\]
Denote a representative by $D$, with columns $D_A,D_B,D_i$.
Preserving the eight named peripheral conjugacy classes at this
level says $D_i=[y_i,C_i]$ for some $y_i\in V$.
For $S=\sum_iC_i$, preserving the ninth says
$\sum_iD_i=[y_9,S]$.  These are explicit linear equations;
their solution in (TPL.4) has dimension $117$.
Conjugate the automorphism by an element whose homology is
$-y_9$.  This is an actual inner change, and its effect on
$D$ is subtraction of $\operatorname{ad}y_9$.  The resulting
representative satisfies
\[
 \sum_iD_i=0,
 \qquad D_i=[y_i,C_i],\quad
 y_i\in\langle C_1,\ldots,C_8\rangle.                 \tag{TPL.5}
\]
The last assertion follows by comparing the independent
$[A,C_i]$ and $[B,C_i]$ coefficients in the first equation.
The remaining ambiguity in this inner change is a multiple of
$\operatorname{ad}S$.

Now let this class actually come from a peripheral automorphism
$\varphi$ of $G_3$.  Its multiplier satisfies
$\lambda\equiv1\pmod3$.  Choose an actual conjugator for the
ninth peripheral element and remove it by an inner automorphism,
so that, with $T=[A,B]$ and $P=C_1\cdots C_8$,
$\varphi(TP)=(TP)^\lambda$ exactly.  Its effect at level two
is precisely the normalization (TPL.5).
We perform the next comparison in the finite group $R$.

In $R$ every weight-two commutator commutes with every $C_i$.
The weight-three Hall--Witt identity then implies that each
$[C_i,C_j]$ is central in all of $R$.
Cubes are also central: collection gives
$[x^3,y]=[x,y]^3[x,y,x]^3=1$.
The puncture conjugators in (TPL.5) therefore act on $C_i$
exactly as words in the puncture generators; their factors of
weight at least two and their cubes contribute no conjugation.
The total central correction is $\sum_iD_i=0$.
It follows that
$\varphi(P)=\prod_iC_i^\lambda=P^\lambda$.
For the last equality, the subgroup generated by the $C_i$ is
class two, its commutators have order three, and
$\binom{\lambda}{2}\equiv0\pmod3$.
Moreover $T$ commutes with $P$, and $T$ has order three, so
$(TP)^\lambda=T P^\lambda$.
The ninth peripheral equation now gives $\varphi(T)=T$.

Collecting this last equality in weight three yields the
necessary equation
\[
 \mathcal O(D)=[D_A,B]+[A,D_B]=0
 \quad\text{in}\quad
 L_3\big/\sum_{i=1}^8[L_2,C_i].                       \tag{TPL.6}
\]
This derivation used group commutators and powers in (TPL.2);
it did not use the class-three Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff
formula at the prime three.

The target of (TPL.6) has dimension $26$.
Its two directions containing no puncture letter are
$[[A,B],A]$ and $[[A,B],B]$.
For each $C_i$ it has three further directions, represented
by $[[A,C_i],A]$, $[[A,C_i],B]$ and
$[[B,C_i],B]$; the Jacobi relation identifies the other mixed
ordering, and terms involving two puncture letters vanish.
This accounts for $2+8\cdot3=26$ directions.
The entries of $D_A,D_B$ independently attain all of them.
Thus the obstruction has rank $26$ and kernel dimension $91$.
It is independent of the remaining normalization by
$\operatorname{ad}S$, since the resulting change is
$[S,[A,B]]$, which vanishes in the quotient.

\paragraph{Actual geometric witnesses for the entire kernel.}
The lower bound is supplied by literal surface mapping classes,
not by lifting arbitrary finite tuples.  On the chosen free
basis take the two handle twists
\[
 (A,B)\longmapsto(A,BA),\qquad
 (A,B)\longmapsto(AB^{-1},B),                          \tag{TPL.7}
\]
fixing all $C_i$, the two Ferrari pointpush words from the
double-cover calculation in the proof of A.4.8, and the seven
disk half twists
$C_i\mapsto C_iC_{i+1}C_i^{-1}$,
$C_{i+1}\mapsto C_i$.
These are eleven actual automorphisms of the surface free
group, with all peripheral words retained in the certificate.
Conjugating the two pointpush words by the disk twists and
using their explicitly invertible $2$-by-$2$ homology matrix
gives sixteen pointpushes whose homology matrices are the
elementary entries of the block $B$ in (TFC.2).

The program forms the following $244$ actual words having
trivial homology over $\mathbb F_3$: $48$ handle Schreier
words for the displayed $24$ matrices of
$\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_3)$, the $16$ pointpush cubes,
their $120$ pairwise commutators, $32$ handle-conjugation words
corrected by the appropriate ordered pointpush product, and
$28$ pure disk pair twists.
Every word is retained as a word in the eleven actual
automorphisms, and its homology is checked to be the identity.
Their $244$ outer IA rows have rank $91$; the certificate
retains the indices of $91$ independent rows.
Each such row comes from a mapping class and hence from a
peripheral automorphism of $G_3$.
Their span must lie in the kernel of (TPL.6), and its dimension
is the full dimension of that kernel.  Since the IA group at
level two is elementary abelian, products and powers of these
actual words realize every row in the kernel.
This proves the asserted equality of finite IA images.
The argument uses the $244$ words only as explicit geometric
witnesses; it requires no assertion that they present a
profinite mapping class group.

\paragraph{Both multiplier signs and the complete finite image.}
For an arbitrary peripheral automorphism, its matrix on
$H_1(G_2,\mathbb F_3)$ has the form
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}C&0\\B&\lambda I_8\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
The degree-two coefficient of $[A,B]$ in the ninth peripheral
equation gives $\det C=\lambda\in\mathbb F_3^\times$.
The orientation-preserving matrices with $\lambda=1$ are
exactly (TFC.2), realized by (TPL.7) and pointpushes.
An explicit reflection for the other sign is
\[
 \iota(A)=B,\quad \iota(B)=A,\qquad
 \iota(C_i)=P_{i-1}C_i^{-1}P_{i-1}^{-1},
 \quad P_{i-1}=C_1\cdots C_{i-1}.                     \tag{TPL.8}
\]
It is an involutive free-group automorphism and the reflection
of the standard surface polygon.  Direct substitution gives
$\iota(P)=P^{-1}$ and
$\iota(TP)=T^{-1}P^{-1}=T^{-1}(TP)^{-1}T$;
thus it reverses every peripheral class with the specified
conjugators.  Its matrix has closed block
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
puncture block $-I_8$, and determinant sign $-1$ on the
closed torus.
Consequently every allowed homology matrix is realized by an
actual pure extended mapping class.  Dividing by such a
representative reduces to the IA case just proved.
There are $48\cdot3^{16}$ allowed homology matrices and
$3^{91}$ IA images, giving the stated order and its two
explicitly related sign components.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Finite translation-conjugacy consequence and scope.}
The quotient maps are the literal maps in (TPL.1), followed
only for the obstruction calculation by (TPL.3).
The deck translations of A.4.8 act on all these groups.
The certificate uses their actual free-word restrictions,
not merely their permutations of the punctures.
Thus conjugation of their image by any peripheral
automorphism of $G_3$, when projected to
$\operatorname{Out}(G_2)$, is conjugation by an actual pure
extended mapping class.  In particular the six-dimensional
kernel previously computed for
$H^1(H,W_{91})\to H^1(H,V_{117})$ disappears after imposing
this finite peripheral lift: the permitted ambient IA image
is now exactly $W_{91}$ itself.
This supplies the finite geometric comparison at the specified
class-two level.  The theorem does not identify this quotient
with the larger modular centralizer quotients of A.4.3 and
A.4.6; their common five-point refinement and the resulting
pentagon census still require the actual comparison maps.
