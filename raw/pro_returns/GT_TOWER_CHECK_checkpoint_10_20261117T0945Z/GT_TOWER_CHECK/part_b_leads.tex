\subsection{The fourth and fifth leads}

\begin{result}{B.6}{Open}{Lead 4: all involutions}
The torsion type and the distinguished involution's
centralizer are known with the maps stated in B.3.
The existence of a conjugator for every GT involution
remains the exact unresolved assertion of that result.
The direct use of the E.44 subgroup method fails at
the explicit inner-to-outer comparison in B.3.
\end{result}

\begin{result}{B.7}{Open}{Lead 5: all prime-power quotients and all frame characters}
The marked prime-power quotient comparisons needed
for $\Delta$ have been proved. The quantifiers over
all such quotients and all frame characters in Lead 5
remain unresolved, for the precise parameter-comparison
and restriction-map reasons in B.4. No separating
invariant for $G_{\mathbb Q}\subseteq\GT$ has been
proved by this work.
\end{result}
