\subsection{The third and sixth leads}

\begin{result}{C.4}{Open}{Lead 3: finite support from all-depth survival}
Finite-support reconstruction and fixed-pair
compatibility of integral lifts are proved in
C.1.1 and C.1.7. The further implication from
pronilpotent survival of an ordinary pair to
finite support remains unresolved. The obstruction
and attempted routes are recorded in C.1.
\end{result}

\begin{result}{C.5}{Open}{Lead 6: the rigid-target comparison}
On the gentle-group side, A.2--A.3 exhibit the
relative nilpotent witness, its signed finite
frames and its failure of the pentagon naturality
equation. A.4.1 also proves that some finite five-point
quotient already excludes the actual parity-one locus;
thus the literal assertion of an exclusively infinite-level
obstruction is false. On the section side, C.2.2 gives an
order-$32$ obstruction for one specified factorial
family, while C.3 proves that a single fixed
finite geometric quotient cannot uniformly
exclude the A.72 families. The assertion about
every proposed full cover, configuration or
modular target remains unresolved.
\end{result}
