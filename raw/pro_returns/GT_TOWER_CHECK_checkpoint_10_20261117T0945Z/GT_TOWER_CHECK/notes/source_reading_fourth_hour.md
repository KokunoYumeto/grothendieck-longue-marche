- Leila Schneps, *Automorphisms of curves and their role in Grothendieck--Teichmueller theory*, Math. Nachr.279(2006),656--671, DOI10.1002/mana.200410383. Author PDF: https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/automorphisms.pdf . Opened Definition 1.2, Theorem 3.1 and its actual braid maps, and Theorem 3.4's reduction. The domain in this paper is the subgroup with multiplier 1 and the two Kummer characters rho2=rho3=0. The two special maps are degree-two and degree-three sphere constructions, with explicit maps into the five- and six-point groups. Their exact relation to B.1.7 is the next comparison to write; no unproved extension of the source's domain is being assumed.

- Benjamin Collas, *Action of a Grothendieck--Teichmueller group on torsion elements of full Teichmueller modular groups of genus one*. Author PDF: https://collas.perso.math.cnrs.fr/documents/Collas-Genus1_gt_action.pdf . Opened Definition 1.1, Theorems A--D, Propositions 5.3 and 5.8, and the statement and reduction of Theorem 6.5. The source's acting group is I_Gamma, identified there with GR; the full GT identification in this audit requires the already proved explicit relation comparison. The genus-one theorem concerns prime-order torsion and its power/conjugacy action, and by itself does not determine the parameter on every quotient group.

Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0311308v2 (20 pages, 11 October 2004).
