\subsection{A single finite ten-point quotient carrying all coloured four-point maps}

\begin{result}{A.4.32}{Proved}{The full joint image of the 630 capped four-point maps}
\label{thm:sphere10-joint-capped-quotient}
Let $M=\Gamma_{0,[10]}$ be the discrete orientation-preserving
mapping-class group of the ten-point sphere, and let
$P=\Gamma_{0,10}$ be its pure subgroup.  Let $C$ be the actual
216-element quotient of the mixed four-point group constructed
in A.4.28, with the named images $D,V\in C$.  There is an
explicit homomorphism of discrete groups
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \rho:M\longrightarrow C^\Omega\rtimes\mathfrak S_\Omega,
 \qquad |\Omega|=630,\\
 \Omega=\{(S,\mathcal P):|S|=4,\ 
             \mathcal P\text{ a partition of }S\text{ into two pairs}\},
\end{gathered}                                               \tag{JQT.1}
\]
The permutation action in (JQT.1) is the faithful action of
$\mathfrak S_{10}$ on these labelled objects.  Each stabilizer
coordinate of $\rho$ is the actual forgetful map to the
corresponding coloured four-point group followed by the
quotient of A.4.28; the generator rule below fixes its
transversal and every conjugating ambiguity.

Write $L=\rho(M)$, $G=\rho(P)$, and
$A=P/([P,P]P^4)\cong(\mathbb Z/4)^{35}$.
The pure four-point image is
$C_P=\langle D^2,V\rangle$, of order 108.
Its commutator group $N_3$ is the Heisenberg group of order 27
and exponent three, and its abelianization map is
\[
 \chi:C_P\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/4,
 \qquad \chi(V)=1,\qquad\chi(D^2)=2.                      \tag{JQT.2}
\]
For $\omega=(S,\mathcal P)$ define the actual pure character
$\ell_\omega:A\to\mathbb Z/4$ on a pair-twist class by
\[
 \ell_\omega(e_{ij})=
 \begin{cases}
  2,&\{i,j\}\in\mathcal P,\\
  1,&i,j\in S\text{ and }\{i,j\}\notin\mathcal P,\\
  0,&\{i,j\}\not\subset S.
 \end{cases}                                              \tag{JQT.3}
\]
Then the pure image is the following exact fibre product:
\[
 G=\{(g_\omega)\in C_P^\Omega:
         (\chi(g_\omega))_\omega
          =(\ell_\omega(a))_\omega\text{ for some }a\in A\}.
                                                               \tag{JQT.4}
\]
In particular its indicated kernel is the full direct product
$N_3^{630}$.  There are exact sequences of finite groups
\[
\begin{gathered}
 1\longrightarrow N_3^{630}\longrightarrow G
      \longrightarrow A\longrightarrow1,\\
 1\longrightarrow N_3^{630}\longrightarrow L
      \longrightarrow E_4\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad E_4=M/([P,P]P^4),\\
 |G|=2^{70}3^{1890},\qquad
 |L|=10!\,2^{70}3^{1890}.
\end{gathered}                                                  \tag{JQT.5}
\]
The first sequence splits by the explicit common order-four
complement described below.  All maps extend continuously to
the full profinite completions of their discrete sources,
with the same finite images.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The actual local map and its coordinate convention.}\quad
The cap of A.4.28 sends $(DV)^2$ to one.  We use the
right-word convention for the three-state induced action:
\[
 (x,\sigma)(y,\tau)
   =((x_i y_{\sigma(i)})_i,\tau\circ\sigma).
                                                               \tag{JQT.6}
\]
The three states are the pairings $12|34$, $13|24$, and
$14|23$, in this order.  The folding of the four-strand
group sends its first and third generators to $u$ and its
second generator to $w$.  The actual induction from the
index-three mixed subgroup in A.4.26 gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 u&=((D,1,D^{-1}VD),(23)),\\
 w&=((1,V,D),(12)).
\end{aligned}                                                  \tag{JQT.7}
\]
These are images of the literal source generators with the
Schreier transversal fixed in A.4.26.  Consequently every
stabilizer coordinate is its named conjugate of the mixed
four-point map.  The capped relation makes the central word
$(uw)^3$ trivial.  Direct multiplication in (JQT.6) verifies
$uwu=wuw$ as well.

For completeness the global rule specifies every edge of the
finite automaton.  Label the ten points by $0,\ldots,9$.
For $\sigma_{k+1}$, exchange $k,k+1$ in the ordered four-set
$S$.  When fewer than two of these labels belong to $S$,
the pairing index stays unchanged and the weight is one.
When both belong to $S$, their adjacent positions in $S$
are $r,r+1$.  Apply the state permutation and the weight
of $u$ for $r=1,3$, and of $w$ for $r=2$.
This is the standard forgetful braid groupoid: a crossing
of two retained strands gives its four-strand generator;
a crossing with an omitted strand gives the identity.
The ordered-set rule records the chosen paths between
the groupoid objects.  Composing with (JQT.7) is therefore
the induced forgetful homomorphism asserted in (JQT.1).

The archived program also checks this assertion directly
from the presentation of $M$ used in A.4.29: all 28 distant
commutators, eight adjacent braid relations, and the two
sphere relations are the identity in every one of the 630
states.  It retains all 5670 generator edges with their
weights.  The permutation on each pairing is compared with
the literal image of its two labelled pairs.  The action is
faithful on $\mathfrak S_{10}$: an element fixing every
four-subset fixes each label, since the intersection of
all four-subsets containing a specified label is that
singleton.  It follows that the kernel of the permutation
map on $L$ is exactly $G=\rho(P)$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The local pure group and all 630 characters.}\quad
The multiplication table reconstructed from the 216-element
cap verifies (JQT.2), $\ker\chi=N_3=C_P'$, and
$|Z(N_3)|=3$.  These are the same named matrices and cap
map as in A.4.27--A.4.28.  In the coordinates
$N_3/Z(N_3)\cong\mathbb F_3^2$, conjugation by $V$ has
characteristic polynomial $X^2+1$ and square $-I$.
The inner action of $N_3$ is trivial on this quotient,
so the action factors through the character $\chi$.
The element $h=V^9$ has order four and $\chi(h)=1$:
$V^4\in N_3$ has order dividing three, hence $V^{12}=1$,
and the image of $V^9$ in $\mathbb Z/4$ has order four.
Thus $C_P=N_3\rtimes\langle h\rangle$ with its indicated
concrete complement.

Use the 45 pair-twist generators $e_{ij}$ of pure
abelianization.  Their sphere relations are the ten stars
$s_i=\sum_{j\ne i}e_{ij}$ and the total relation
$t=\sum_{i<j}e_{ij}$ from (QFU.4).  Formula (JQT.3)
annihilates each: at a retained vertex the three incident
weights sum to $2+1+1=4$, and all six retained weights
sum to eight.  It therefore defines a homomorphism on
the actual module $A$.

The literal pure pair-twist word is
\[
 A_{ij}=\sigma_j\cdots\sigma_{i+2}
             \sigma_{i+1}^{\,2}
             \sigma_{i+2}^{-1}\cdots\sigma_j^{-1},
       \qquad 0\leq i<j\leq9.                              \tag{JQT.8}
\]
For $j=i+1$ both outside products are empty.
Evaluating (JQT.8) by the preceding automaton fixes every
state, has every weight in $C_P$, and gives exactly
(JQT.3) after $\chi$.  The code retains all 45 words,
all 28350 weights, and all their characters.  For every
coordinate their generated subgroup is the full $C_P$;
this also follows from forgetting the six omitted points
and the displayed local generators.

The map
\[
 \ell:A\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/4)^{630},
        \qquad a\longmapsto(\ell_\omega(a))_\omega
                                                               \tag{JQT.9}
\]
is injective.  Here is an exact finite certificate: the
630 rows of (JQT.3), on the named 45 columns, have binary
rank 35.  The supplied elimination selects 35 named rows
and 35 named columns and reduces their minor to $I_{35}$
using only odd pivots modulo four.  These characters
therefore span a free rank-35 direct summand of the dual.
Since they annihilate the saturated rank-ten sphere
relation lattice proved in A.4.31, this is the full dual
of $A$.  The file records the selected rows, columns and
reduced matrix, and the replay verifies every equality.
This proves injectivity of the actual map (JQT.9).

The 630 characters are pairwise different even after
identifying a character with its negative.  Their support
recovers $S$; the two entries equal to two recover its
pairing.  The negative has four entries equal to three,
whereas every displayed row has its four remaining
nonzero entries equal to one.  Thus it equals none of
the displayed rows.  Since equality in the dual of $A$
means equality on all named pair-twist generators,
these support comparisons prove the claim on $A$ itself.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The full joint image, including every central coordinate.}\quad
Let $N$ be the kernel of $G\to\ell(A)$ induced by
$\chi$ in all coordinates.  The coordinate containment
$G\subset C_P^{630}$ gives $N\subset N_3^{630}$.
Every coordinate projection $G\to C_P$ is surjective,
so its commutator subgroup projects onto $C_P'=N_3$.
Since $G'\subset N$, every projection $N\to N_3$
is surjective as well.

Put $Z=Z(N_3)$ and $V_\omega=N_3/Z$ in coordinate
$\omega$.  The image $\overline N$ in
$\bigoplus_\omega V_\omega$ is an $A$-stable
$\mathbb F_3$-subspace; the action factors through $A$
because inner automorphisms of each $N_3$ act trivially
on $N_3/Z$.  Choose $\iota\in\mathbb F_9$ with
$\iota^2=-1$.  The two eigencharacters on the
$\omega$ summand are
\[
       a\longmapsto\iota^{\ell_\omega(a)},
       \qquad a\longmapsto\iota^{-\ell_\omega(a)}.
                                                               \tag{JQT.10}
\]
They make $V_\omega$ irreducible over $\mathbb F_3$,
and the just-proved comparisons make these 630
two-dimensional modules pairwise nonisomorphic.
To justify the decomposition used here explicitly,
over $\mathbb F_9$ the operator
$|A|^{-1}\sum_{a\in A}\psi(a)^{-1}a$ projects onto
the $\psi$ eigenspace.  Its denominator is invertible
in characteristic three.  Pairing the two conjugate
projectors descends to $\mathbb F_3$.  Since
$\overline N$ projects surjectively to every summand,
these projectors show
$\overline N=\bigoplus_\omega V_\omega$.

Fix a coordinate $\omega$ and choose two elements of
$N$ whose images in this direct sum are supported only
there and form a basis of $V_\omega$.  In all other
coordinates their entries are central, so their
commutator is one.  In coordinate $\omega$ their
commutator generates $Z$, by the nondegenerate
Heisenberg commutator pairing.  Consequently
$Z^{630}\subset N$.  Combined with the full quotient
image, this proves $N=N_3^{630}$.

The image of $G$ under the coordinate characters is
exactly $\ell(A)$ by (JQT.8)--(JQT.9).  Containing
the full kernel $N_3^{630}$ now proves equality with
the fibre product (JQT.4).  Its splitting is the
literal homomorphism
\[
 A\longrightarrow C_P^{630},\qquad
       a\longmapsto(h^{\ell_\omega(a)})_\omega,
             \qquad h=V^9.                                \tag{JQT.11}
\]
Every value belongs to $G$ by (JQT.4).

Finally $N_3^{630}$ is a normal Sylow three-subgroup of
$G$, by the orders just computed.  It is consequently
the unique Sylow three-subgroup and characteristic in
$G$.  Since $G$ is normal in $L$, it is normal in $L$.
The kernel of $M\to L/N_3^{630}$ lies in $P$,
by the faithful label action.  On $P$ its kernel is
exactly $[P,P]P^4$, by injectivity of (JQT.9).
This gives the indicated canonical isomorphism
$L/N_3^{630}\cong E_4$ and proves both order formulas
in (JQT.5).  Each target is finite, so the universal
property of full profinite completion gives the
claimed continuous extensions with unchanged image.

\end{proof}
