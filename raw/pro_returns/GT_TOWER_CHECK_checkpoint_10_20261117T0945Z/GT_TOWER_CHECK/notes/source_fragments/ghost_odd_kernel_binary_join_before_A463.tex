\subsection{Odd normal refinements joined to the actual binary source}

Keep the actual base $E_1=B_0/J$ and the binary source records
constructed in A.4.61. Consider the directed family of actual finite
quotients
\[
             R=B_0/L\longrightarrow E_1,
             \qquad L\subset J,\quad |J/L|\text{ odd}.
                                                        \tag{OJB.1}
\]
For every member of this family and every binary coefficient
$2^a$, there is a genuine normalized parity-one element whose
pair has the A.4.61 binary record and has value one in $R$.
The element can retain the fixed binary and ternary frames.
One element can satisfy these conditions for all members of
the family and all binary coefficients simultaneously.

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{An exact odd normalization refinement.}\quad
First join $R$ with the actual source $E_3$. The resulting
source $R'$ still has an odd kernel above $E_1$, since that
kernel embeds in the product of the two odd kernels. It
maps to $E_3$ and retains the specified harmonic frame there.
The two pure generators have equal order in $R'$ because
$y=sxs$. Their base images have order six, and the kernel
above $E_1$ has odd order. Their common order is therefore
$q=6o$ with $o$ odd.

Join $R'$ with the actual quotient $B_0/(F_0'F_0^q)$.
Call the resulting group $R^+$. This remains an odd
extension of $E_1$: the additional image of $J$ in the
abelian factor is a quotient of
$6\mathbb Z^2/q\mathbb Z^2$, of odd order.
Both pure generators have exact order $q$ in $R^+$.
Their abelianization maps onto $C_q^2$, while their orders
make it a quotient of that same group. Consequently
\[
                 (\operatorname{im}_{R^+}F_0)^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_q^2,
                 \qquad |x|=|y|=q.
                                                        \tag{OJB.2}
\]
All joins here are the actual joint images of $B_0$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The harmonic frame lifts across the odd kernel.}\quad
Choose a pure lift $b\in R^+$ of the fixed
$\beta_3\in C_{E_3}(s)$. Put $t=bsb^{-1}$ and $u=ts$.
Then $u$ lies in the odd normal kernel of $R^+\to E_3$.
Both $s$ and $t$ are involutions, so $sus=u^{-1}$.
Let $h=\operatorname{ord}(u)$, which is odd, and set
\[
                  v=u^{(h+1)/2},\qquad \beta^+=v^{-1}b.
                                                        \tag{OJB.3}
\]
These are actual finite elements. Since $v^2=u$ and
$svs=v^{-1}$, one has $vsv^{-1}=us=t$.
Thus $\beta^+$ centralizes $s$ and still maps to
$\beta_3$. It is pure because $v$ belongs to the pure
kernel. This proves the centralizer lift without assuming
that the odd kernel is abelian.
Set the odd pair and cubic frame equal to one. Their
source action is the identity on $R^+$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The actual binary--odd join and its canonical cover.}\quad
Join $R^+$ with $E_{2^a}$. Their kernels above $E_1$ have
coprime orders. If their ordinary source kernels are
$L^+\subset J$ and $J_{2^a}$, their product is $J$:
the index of their product divides both of the coprime
indices. Therefore the actual joint source is exactly
\[
 D=E_{2^a}\mathbin{\times}_{E_1}R^+
      =B_0/(J_{2^a}\cap L^+).
                                                        \tag{OJB.4}
\]
The binary automorphism and the identity odd action
induce the same identity on $E_1$. They join to an actual
automorphism of $D$. Their pure frames have the same
base values, so they join as well. In particular the
pair is one in $R^+$ and hence in the original $R$.

The quotient $D\to B_0/(K'K^{2^a})$ has odd kernel:
its two stages have kernels the odd kernel of $R^+$
above $E_1$ and the order-three kernel of
$E_{2^a}\to B_0/(K'K^{2^a})$.
The quotient $D\to R^+$ has a binary kernel.
The actual natural canonical-cover maps therefore
identify the entire binary primary kernel of $D^c$
with the computed binary canonical kernel, and every
odd primary kernel with its counterpart for $(R^+)^c$.
The first defect is zero by A.4.61. The second is the
harmonic class $\kappa_s(\beta^+)$, which is killed by
two because $s^2=1$. Hence its odd primary parts vanish.
The defect is zero in the whole canonical kernel of $D$.

As in A.4.61, conjugated torsion generators define a
positive automorphism of the full finite canonical cover:
factorization follows from centrality of the relation
image, and surjectivity follows from the quotient
automorphism and the stem property. The full
finite-certificate image theorem supplies an actual
positive profinite lift with both prescribed frames.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Literal normalization and the simultaneous element.}\quad
The pure generators in the actual join (OJB.4) have
order $\ell=\operatorname{lcm}(6\cdot2^a,6o)=6\cdot2^a o$.
The two abelian projections have actual joint image
$C_\ell^2$, by the integer coefficient map on
$\mathbb Z^2$. Since the named generator orders also
bound the pure abelianization by $C_\ell^2$, this is
its exact abelianization.
The binary pair has zero abelianization and the odd
pair is one. The raw exponent correction is therefore
a multiple of $\ell$. The corresponding normalization
is identity on the retained source, preserving both
frames and the pair literally. The resulting genuine
normalized element has the fixed parity-one signed
record of A.4.61.

Finite joins of sources (OJB.1) still have odd kernels
above $E_1$. Together with a common binary coefficient,
the construction therefore meets every finite family
of the stated conditions. Their word and frame
evaluations are closed conditions on the compact
genuine group $H$, with frame continuity as proved in
A.4.61. Compactness gives one element satisfying all
of them simultaneously.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The exact application to odd nonabelian five-point layers.}
Let $Q$ be any actual finite quotient of $\Gamma_{0,5}$
mapping onto the named $Q_{192}$ with odd kernel $P$.
Take the common kernel of the eight specified maps
$F_0\to Q$, then its core under the six representatives
of $B_0/F_0$. The resulting source kernel $L$ is
normal in $B_0$ and lies in $J$. Its quotient $J/L$
embeds in the actual product of the $48$ indicated
copies of $P$, so it has odd order.
It is therefore a source in (OJB.1), and the pair value
one in that source makes all eight actual insertions
one in $Q$.
This applies in particular to the literal class-two
ternary quotient
\[
                 \Gamma_{0,5}/(\gamma_3N\,N^3).
                                                        \tag{OJB.5}
\]
The argument also retains every binary relative-abelian
level by (OJB.4). It does not treat a new binary
nonabelian layer as an odd extension; that layer has
its separate actual source comparison.
