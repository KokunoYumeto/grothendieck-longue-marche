\paragraph{The mixed four-point representative for every gentle element.}
Let $A=(\lambda,f)\in\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0$ and put
$m_A=(\lambda-1)/2\in\widehat{\mathbb Z}$.
Division here is the inverse of the continuous isomorphism
$\widehat{\mathbb Z}\to2\widehat{\mathbb Z}$, not division
in the ring $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$ by a unit.
Use the actual modular action $\alpha_A$ on
\[
 B=\langle s,r\mid s^2=r^3=1\rangle^{\wedge},\qquad
 \tau=sr,\quad x=\tau^{-2},\quad y=sxs.
\]
Its pure subgroup is $F=\widehat F(x,y)$, and
\[
 \alpha_A(\tau)=\tau^\lambda,\quad
 \alpha_A(x)=x^\lambda,\quad
 \alpha_A(y)=f(x,y)^{-1}y^\lambda f(x,y).
\]
These are the actual central-quotient formulas from R1,
E.65.8: in $\widehat B_3=\langle u,w\mid uwu=wuw\rangle$
one has $u\mapsto u^\lambda$,
$w\mapsto f(u^2,w^2)^{-1}w^\lambda f(u^2,w^2)$,
and $s=uwu$, $r=uw$, $\tau=u^{-1}$ after killing
$(uw)^3$.  The two hexagons supply the braid relation
and preservation of this central subgroup; the inverse
gentle pair supplies the inverse automorphism.
On the quotient $\mathfrak S_3$ the action is the identity:
$u,w$ are transpositions, $\lambda$ is odd, and
$f(u^2,w^2)$ has trivial permutation.

The actual index-three subgroup
$R_H=\langle x,s\rangle\subset B$ is the inverse image
of $\langle s\rangle\subset\mathfrak S_3$, so it is
preserved by $\alpha_A$.  The marked isomorphism is
\[
 j:R=\langle v,d\mid(vd)^2=1\rangle^{\wedge}
       \xrightarrow{\sim}R_H,\qquad
       v\mapsto x,\quad d\mapsto x^{-1}s.
\]
It is the discrete index-three Reidemeister--Schreier
presentation, followed by full completion; both presentations
are the free profinite product of a procyclic factor and
$C_2$.  Moreover
\[
 K_{\rm std}=\ker(R\to C_2)=\widehat F(v,d^2),\qquad
 j(K_{\rm std})=F,\qquad
 j(d^2)=x^{-1}y^{-1}.
\]
Here the quotient sends $v$ to zero and $d$ to one.

There is an actual continuous action on this marked mixed
group, with the following representative and comparison:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \beta_A(v)&=v^\lambda,\\
 \beta_A(d)&=f(v,d^2)^{-1}d^\lambda f(v,d^2),\\
 j\beta_Aj^{-1}
     &=\operatorname{Inn}(x^{m_A})\,
                     \alpha_A|_{R_H}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
In particular the correction is trivial for multiplier one.
The assertion uses only the gentle action on $B$, and does
not use a ten-point naturality square or a pentagon.

To prove the comparison, set
$\theta=\operatorname{Inn}(\tau)|_F$.
The literal words in $C_2*C_3$ give
$\theta(x)=x$ and $\theta(y)=x^{-1}y^{-1}$, as in
(A.J8).  Since $\tau^{\lambda-1}=x^{-m_A}$,
\[
 \alpha_A\theta
       =\operatorname{Inn}(x^{-m_A})\theta\alpha_A.
\]
Consequently $\alpha'_A=\operatorname{Inn}(x^{m_A})
\alpha_A$ sends the free basis $(x,\theta(y))$ to
\[
 x^\lambda,\qquad
 f(x,\theta(y))^{-1}\theta(y)^\lambda f(x,\theta(y)).
\]
Pulling back by $j$ proves the displayed formula for $v$
and for $d^2$.  It also determines the formula for $d$.
Indeed $C_R(d^2)=\langle d\rangle$: an element has the
form $d^e k$ with $e\in\{0,1\}$ and $k\in K_{\rm std}$;
if it centralizes $d^2$, then $k$ centralizes the primitive
free generator $d^2$ in $K_{\rm std}$ and therefore belongs
to $\langle d^2\rangle$.  The primitive free centralizer
is the one proved by the finite-cover peripheral argument
in A.1.  Since $\lambda$ is a unit,
$C_R(d^{2\lambda})=C_R(d^2)$.
The two proposed images of $d$ have the same square;
after conjugation by $f(v,d^2)$ both centralize
$d^{2\lambda}$, hence belong to $\langle d\rangle$.
Multiplication by two is injective on this procyclic
group, so the two images agree.  This proves existence
as an actual automorphism, since $\alpha'_A$ is one.

Finally this choice gives a homomorphism into
$\operatorname{Aut}(R)$, not merely an outer action.
For the composition convention $\alpha_{AA'}=
\alpha_A\alpha_{A'}$ one has
\[
 m_{AA'}=m_A+\lambda_A m_{A'},\qquad
 \alpha_A(x^{m_{A'}})=x^{\lambda_A m_{A'}}.
\]
These identities imply
$\alpha'_{AA'}=\alpha'_A\alpha'_{A'}$, and hence
$\beta_{AA'}=\beta_A\beta_{A'}$.
Continuity follows from the displayed formulas and the
continuity of the original gentle action.
This specifies the exact change from the modular representative
to the $K_{\rm std}$-normalized representative; it does not
replace the separate $(d,v^2)$ reading by a renamed basis.
