## 1. Exact objects and maps

Put `U = P^1_Q \ {0,1,infinity}`. Fix the rational tangent `01` and geometric tangential base point. Write `E = pi_1(U)`, `G = G_Q`, and `Pi = pi_1(U_Qbar)`, with the resulting split exact sequence of profinite groups. The quotient maps are continuous surjective homomorphisms over G.

The completion used in the supplied section papers is the **power completion**

`K = lim_N Q^×/(Q^×)^N`,

with the power quotients discrete and N ordered by divisibility. It is not legitimate to substitute the product topology on unrestricted valuation vectors. The exact valuation isomorphism is

`K = {+1,-1} x { (a_q)_q in product_q Zhat : for every N, a_q=0 mod N for all but finitely many q }`.

Here `q` runs over rational primes. The proof is immediate at each N from `Q^× = {+1,-1} x directsum_q Z`, and passage to the inverse limit. Conversely a vector with the displayed restriction gives a finite-support class at every N, compatibly. Multiplication becomes addition of valuation coordinates and multiplication of signs. This is a topological isomorphism for the inverse-limit topology on the right, not an assertion that the right side is a closed subgroup of a compact product.

For a finite prime p, localization is the continuous homomorphism `loc_p:K -> K_p=lim_N Q_p^×/(Q_p^×)^N` induced by the field inclusion. The Kummer map `kappa_p:Q_p^× -> K_p` is injective. Indeed a number in all power subgroups has valuation divisible by every positive integer and therefore valuation zero. The finite torsion factor of `Z_p^×` is killed by taking its exponent, and the remaining pro-p unit factor is isomorphic to `Z_p` (use `1+4 Z_2` at p=2), whose intersection of p-power multiples is zero. The same argument also proves continuity of local Nth-power class maps.

Define `G_ord` to be the subset of `K^2` of pairs `(A,B)` admitting, for every finite p, an `x_p in Q_p \ {0,1}` with `loc_p(A)=kappa_p(x_p)` and `loc_p(B)=kappa_p(1-x_p)`. A **global depth-r lift** of `(A,B)` is a continuous section `s_r:G -> E_r` with the specified abelian Kummer class. ## 2. Finite support: complete confirmation of SECTIONS A.32

**Proposition (proved).** On G_ord, finite support of the valuation vector of A, B, or A/B forces `(A,B)=(kappa(t),kappa(1-t))` for one unique `t in Q\{0,1}`; all ordinary finite local centres equal t.

The exact valuation isomorphism above gives `A=kappa(t)`. For every finite p, `kappa_p(x_p)=loc_p(A)=kappa_p(t)`, so injectivity gives `x_p=t`. In particular t differs from 0 and 1. The remaining sign is detected at p=3 because -1 is not a square in Q_3. Hence D=1, proving the formula. The argument beginning with B gives the other case. The ratio case strengthens the stated A.32 reconstruction. Finite support gives `A/B=kappa(u)` for rational nonzero u by the same valuation isomorphism. Local injectivity gives `x_p/(1-x_p)=u`; u=−1 is impossible, and otherwise every local centre is the rational `u/(1+u)`. The preceding localization argument reconstructs both global coordinates.

Consequently, for an ordinary infinite-support pair, the vectors `(v_q(A),v_q(B))` outside EVERY finite set span `Q_l²` for EVERY coefficient prime l. ## 8. Fixed-pair integral compatibility is now proved

The complete LaTeX proof with labels is `ghost_compatibility.tex`. Its exact theorem is:

**Theorem (proved).** For one fixed pair in the power completion of Q×, existence of a global continuous section with that abelian class at every integral nilpotency class is equivalent to existence of a continuous section into the full pronilpotent arithmetic quotient with that class. The equivalence also holds with geometric conjugacy to one fixed family of local point or cusp sections at every place.

Here is the full mechanism. Fix a coefficient prime l and a literal abelian cocycle a representing the pair. Geometric conjugation by a lift of an abelian zero-cochain makes every section with the correct abelian cohomology class have literal abelian cocycle a. Work with these literal fibres S_r(a).

For every weight r≥2, `Z¹_cts(G_Q,Z_l(r))` is a compact finite free Z_l-module. At q≠l, inertia acts trivially; the cocycle becomes a homomorphism on inertia. Wild inertia and the other tame primary factors have zero image. Frobenius conjugation gives `(q^r−q)c(tau)=0`, and torsion-freeness kills the remaining tame image. Thus every such cocycle factors through `G_{Q,{l,infinity}}`. At coefficient exponent l^n, its graph is a homomorphism to the fixed finite semidirect product `Z/l^n(r) ⋊ im(chi_l^r)`. Bounded-degree fixed-ramification finiteness gives finitely many such homomorphisms. Consequently the integral cocycle module is the inverse limit of finite cocycle sets and is compact in the compact-open topology. The reduction kernel is exactly l times this module, since a cocycle with values in lZ_l may be divided continuously by l. Its quotient modulo l is finite. Lifts of a finite basis generate by successive l-adic digit expansion; the remainder tends uniformly to zero. Torsion-freeness then makes the module finite free. This argument proves finite generation itself, rather than inferring it merely from finiteness of some Hom sets.

The free pro-l quotient of class r has Hall coordinates `Z_l^{h_r}`. The discrete collection formulas for multiplication, inversion and powers are rational polynomials with a fixed denominator bound at each class. They extend to integral coordinates by density; no denominator is assumed to be an l-adic unit. The resulting compact nilpotent group is pro-l because every finite quotient is nilpotent and generated by l-elements. To identify the universal completion exactly, a map to a finite l-group of exponent l^e kills every Hall word whose coordinates lie in l^eZ. That coordinate box is a neighbourhood, whether or not it is a subgroup, so the map extends continuously. This proves the claimed pro-l model even at small primes. The actual arithmetic action is polynomial in Hall coordinates by substituting the continuous images of the two generators into these formulas. The central layer of weight r is a finite sum of `Z_l(r)` because the free Lie bracket has weight r and the abelian action is scalar chi_l.

Now parameterize all of S_r(a) by a closed compact subset X_r of a finite power of Z_l, with coordinates polynomial over Q_l in the parameters and continuous in the Galois variable. At class one use the one-point set a. Set the new central coordinates of a lift to zero. Its defect is a finite polynomial family `C_t=Σv_i(t)c_i` of continuous rational two-cochains, where v_i are integral monomials. Only the sum on X_r needs to be an integral two-cocycle.

Let `R={u in Z_l^m: Σu_i c_i is the coboundary of an integral continuous one-cochain}`. This is a submodule of the finite free module Z_l^m. Noetherianity makes it finite free; its basis matrix presents it as the compact image of Z_l^k, so R is closed. Choose an actual continuous integral primitive for each basis vector. A Q_l-linear left inverse of that matrix gives the coefficients of every u in R, continuously and integrally on R. Thus primitives of this finite polynomial family may be chosen polynomially on its exact locus of solvability. The ambient coboundary space need not be closed, and no such ambient claim enters the argument.

The liftable parameter locus `{t in X_r:v(t) in R}` is closed. Add the finite free parameter module `Z¹(G_Q,L_{r+1})` to obtain every lift and the next compact polynomial parameter space. Induction proves compactness of every actual S_r(a). The transition maps are continuous. In the product of the nonempty compact S_r(a), compatibility equations are closed and every finite collection is solved by projection of a section at its largest level. The finite-intersection property gives a compatible family. The inverse limit over r is the free pro-l group, since every finite l-group is nilpotent. Taking the product over all l yields the full pronilpotent arithmetic section.

For local matching, the conjugacy orbit of one local section is the continuous image of the compact geometric group, hence closed in the local continuous-map space. Restriction is continuous, so imposing all places cuts out closed compact global fibres.
