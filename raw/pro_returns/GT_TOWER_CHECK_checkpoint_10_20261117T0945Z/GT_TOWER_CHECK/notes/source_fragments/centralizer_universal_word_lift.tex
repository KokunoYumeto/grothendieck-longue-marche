\subsection{Complete coverage of the simultaneous commutator-word test}

Use the marked group $\mathcal T$ and the actual image
$J_{\mathcal T}\subseteq\mathcal T^6$ of B.2.11.  Let
$P=\langle x,y\rangle\subseteq\mathcal T$; it has order
$24$, its derived subgroup has order $8$, and its abelian
quotient is $C_3$, generated by the equal images of $x,y$.
The following finite set retains the actual positive frames:
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal Z=\{(\alpha,\beta,A):\;&
 \alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal T),\quad
 \alpha(\tau)=\tau,\quad\beta\in P,\quad A\in P',\\
 &\alpha(s)=\beta s\beta^{-1},\qquad
 \alpha(r)=ArA^{-1}\}.
\end{split}                                                    \tag{B.R11}
\]
For $(\alpha,\beta,A)\in\mathcal Z$, define $u\in\mathbb Z/3$
by $\beta P'=x^{2u}P'$, and put $l=-u\in\mathbb Z/3$.
For each of the two $a\in\mathbb Z/6$ with $a\equiv l\pmod3$,
form the actual tuple
\[
\begin{gathered}
 C=x^{-a}\beta,\qquad J_0=\beta^{-1}Ar^l,\\
 w_a=(C,C^{-1},J_0^{-1}C^{-1},J_0,
                 sJ_0^{-1}s,\tau C J_0\tau^{-1})
                    \in\mathcal T^6.
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{B.R12}
\]

\begin{result}{B.2.12}{Proved}{Every normalized target passes the exact word-membership test}
The finite set $\mathcal Z$ has exactly $24$ elements.
For each of them exactly one of the two allowed exponents
$a\in\mathbb Z/6$ gives $w_a\in J_{\mathcal T}'$.

Consequently, for every full normalized $g\in H$ with its
pure frames $\beta_g,A_g$, there are
$a\in\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$, $l\in\mathbb Z/3$, and an actual
word $f\in F'$ such that, in the full target $B$,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 f(s,\tau)&=x^{-a}\beta_g,\\
 f(r^{-1},s)&=\beta_g^{-1}A_g r^l,
 \qquad a\equiv l\equiv-u_g\pmod3,
 \quad\beta_g^{\mathrm{ab}}=(u_g,u_g).
 \end{aligned}                                                 \tag{B.R13}
\]
The other four evaluations of this same $f$ are the other
four displayed coordinates of (B.R12), interpreted in $B$.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
The exact finite census
\path{check_universal_frame_membership.g} enumerates all
$24\cdot8$ pairs $(\beta,A)\in P\times P'$.  It retains
precisely those for which the proposed two generator images
have product $\tau$ and define a bijective homomorphism of
the displayed marked $\mathcal T$.  There are $24$ such
frame triples.  For each, the code finds $u$ using the
quotient $P/P'$, evaluates both permitted $a$ in (B.R12),
and tests membership in the actual subgroup
$J_{\mathcal T}'=\langle\eta(x),\eta(y)\rangle'$.
Exactly one exponent passes in each of the $24$ rows;
twelve pass at $a=l$ and twelve at $a=l+3$.  Every one of
the three residues of $u$ occurs in eight rows.  The
complete output retains all $24$ rows and both Boolean
membership values.  This is an exhaustive finite calculation
on the generators (B.R10), not a replacement of the joint
image by a product of its coordinate images.

The full normalized action of $g$ preserves
$L_0=[\langle\!\langle\tau^2\rangle\!\rangle,
       \langle\!\langle\tau^3\rangle\!\rangle]$,
since it fixes $\tau$.  It therefore induces an actual
automorphism of $\mathcal T$.  Its pure frames reduce to
$\beta\in P$ and $A\in P'$: the latter assertion follows
from $A_g^{\mathrm{ab}}=0$.  Its normalized harmonic frame
satisfies $\beta_g^{\mathrm{ab}}=(u_g,u_g)$, so the
residue $u$ in the finite test is precisely $u_g\bmod3$.
Thus its reduction belongs to $\mathcal Z$ and one of the
two exponents gives the stated finite membership.

Choose that integer representative $a$ and the stated $l$,
and form (B.R12) now with the full elements $\beta_g,A_g$
in $B$.  Its reduction belongs to $J_{\mathcal T}'$.
The exact equality (B.R4), with the order-$144$ quotient
identified in (B.R7), gives
$w_a\in\eta(F')$.  A preimage $f\in F'$ therefore exists
in the full profinite source.  The first and fourth
coordinates of the actual map (B.R1) are precisely
$f(s,\tau)$ and $f(r^{-1},s)$, proving (B.R13) and the
assertion about the remaining four coordinates.
\end{proof}

The proven preimage in (B.R13) is a simultaneous word
preimage under the six displayed source maps.  To obtain a
preimage under $D_P$, one must also impose the two source
cycle relations and the positive automorphism conditions
on the full source.  The finite canonical-cover tests in
B.2.9 specify those remaining conditions exactly.  They
have not been discharged uniformly by this word-membership
calculation.
