\subsection{The uniform binary canonical kernels and their actual transition maps}

Let $K$ be the actual free modular kernel of rank $97$ and
$G=B_0/K$, of order $576$. Put
\[
 \mathcal W=
   (\Lambda^2 K^{\mathrm{ab}})_G\otimes\mathbb Z_2,
 \qquad L_m=K'K^m,\qquad
 M_m=L_m/[B_0,L_m]\quad(m=2^k).
                                                        \tag{UCM.1}
\]
Here $M_m$ is the full canonical central kernel for the
actual finite quotient $B_0/L_m$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Proved input: the binary canonical kernels at every power of two.}\quad
For the specified modular source there are isomorphisms
\[
 \mathcal W\cong\mathbb Z_2^8\oplus C_2^7,
 \qquad
 M_{2^k}\cong C_2\oplus\mathcal W/2^k\mathcal W
             \cong C_2^8\oplus C_{2^k}^8\quad(k\geq1).
                                                        \tag{UCM.2}
\]
They can be chosen using one fixed integral change of the
$97$ power coordinates, independent of $k$. Under these
isomorphisms the actual canonical quotient map is
\[
 M_{2^k}\longrightarrow M_{2^{k-1}},\qquad
   (\epsilon,w)\longmapsto(0,\overline w)\quad(k\geq2).
                                                        \tag{UCM.3}
\]
Its kernel is $C_2^9$ and its image has index two.
The first map $M_2\to M_1=K/[B_0,K]\cong C_2$ is zero.

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The exact rational character and binary wedge module.}\quad
The Bass--Serre graph for $K$ has edges indexed by $G$ and
vertices indexed by $G/\langle s\rangle$ and
$G/\langle r\rangle$. Its two vertex sets have sizes $288$
and $192$. The actual left coset permutations give the
character of $K^{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes\mathbb Q$:
\[
 \chi(g)=1+576\,\mathbf1_{g=1}
          -\#\operatorname{Fix}_{G/\langle s\rangle}(g)
          -\#\operatorname{Fix}_{G/\langle r\rangle}(g).
                                                        \tag{UCM.4}
\]
The finite certificate constructs both coset actions from
the retained actual multiplication table of $G$.
The character values $97,-7,-5,1$ occur respectively
$1,36,64,475$ times. It also retains every square $g^2$.
Consequently
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}(\Lambda^2 K^{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes\mathbb Q)_G
   =\frac1{576}\sum_{g\in G}
                  \frac{\chi(g)^2-\chi(g^2)}2
   =\frac{4608}{576}=8.
                                                        \tag{UCM.5}
\]
This is the ordinary character average over a finite group
in characteristic zero.

The exact coefficient-four wedge calculation in A.4.59
gives
$\mathcal W/4\mathcal W=C_4^8\oplus C_2^7$.
The module $\mathcal W$ is finitely generated over
$\mathbb Z_2$, since it is a quotient of a free module
of rank $4656$. Its rational rank is eight by (UCM.5).
In the structure theorem, those eight free factors already
supply all eight order-four factors of its reduction.
Any additional torsion factor of order at least four
would supply another one. Thus all torsion factors have
order two, and their number is seven. This proves the
first isomorphism in (UCM.2), including the absence of
unseen higher binary torsion.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{One integral power-coordinate reduction.}\quad
Let $S,R$ be the actual integral actions on the $97$
modular Schreier generators. Their stacked power matrix is
\[
             \mathcal P=\begin{pmatrix}S-I\\R-I\end{pmatrix}.
                                                        \tag{UCM.6}
\]
The certificate retains integral unimodular matrices
$U,V$ with $U\mathcal P V$ in Smith form: it has $96$
unit entries, one entry equal to two, and $97$ zero rows.
In particular $K/[B_0,K]\cong C_2$ by these actual
integral relations.

For $m=2^k$, collection in the free class-two group gives
\[
 L_m/[K,L_m]
    =\mathbb Z^{97}\{P_i=k_i^m\}
        \oplus (\mathbb Z/m)^{4656}\{C_{ij}=[k_i,k_j]\}.
                                                        \tag{UCM.7}
\]
The wedge relations give $\mathcal W/m\mathcal W$.
If an action sends $k_i$ to a word with exponent row $a$,
its remaining power relation is the row of $S-I$ or $R-I$
on the $P_i$, together with
\[
 c_m\sum_{j<l}a_ja_lC_{jl},
                   \qquad c_m=-\binom m2.
                                                        \tag{UCM.8}
\]
The original commutator coordinate of the word is multiplied
by $m$ and disappears in the displayed wedge quotient.
Thus the quadratic class in (UCM.8) is independent of $m$;
only its scalar coefficient changes.

Apply the fixed integral row and column operations $U,V$
to these relations. The first $96$ unit rows eliminate
their power variables. The remaining presentation is
\[
 (\mathbb Z P\oplus\mathcal W/m\mathcal W)\big/
       \langle\,2P+c_m v,\ c_m t_1,\ldots,c_m t_{97}\,\rangle,
                                                        \tag{UCM.9}
\]
for fixed classes $v,t_j\in\mathcal W$. The program
\path{check_modular_power_smith.py} uses the same $U$ on
the actual $194$ quadratic rows, projected to the fully
computed $15$-dimensional module $\mathcal W/2\mathcal W$.
Every one of its $98$ residual rows is zero. Therefore
\[
                       v,t_1,\ldots,t_{97}\in2\mathcal W.
                                                        \tag{UCM.10}
\]
This also follows from the independently proved full
$M_2=C_2^{16}$: its order forces the inclusion of
$\mathcal W/2\mathcal W$, and its exponent two forces
the remaining doubled power class to vanish.

For the specified $m=2^k$, $c_m$ is $m/2$ times an odd integer.
Equation (UCM.10) makes every residual quadratic relation
zero in $\mathcal W/m\mathcal W$.
The sole remaining power relation is $2P=0$, giving
$M_m=C_2\oplus\mathcal W/m\mathcal W$.
These are the full finite canonical kernels: the power
coinvariant quotient has order two and the wedge quotient
is a binary group, so no other primary part is omitted.
This proves (UCM.2).

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The actual coefficient transitions.}\quad
For $m=2n$, the quotient map on the collected source sends
$k_i^m$ to $(k_i^n)^2$ and sends each $C_{ij}$ to the
same commutator modulo $n$. Thus its power-coordinate map
is multiplication by two, while its wedge map is reduction.
The fixed integral column change $V$ preserves this assertion
for the final power variable $P$.
The eliminated unit rows are compatible because
\[
                          c_{2n}-2c_n=-n^2,
                                                        \tag{UCM.11}
\]
which acts as zero on $\mathcal W/n\mathcal W$.
The remaining $C_2$ power factor therefore maps to zero,
and the wedge factor maps by its specified reduction.
For $n\geq2$ the seven order-two wedge factors map
identically and the eight free factors contribute one
binary kernel coordinate each. Together with the killed
power factor this gives the exact $C_2^9$ kernel and
index-two image in (UCM.3).
For $n=1$ the wedge output is zero and the same power
calculation makes the entire map to $M_1$ zero.
\end{proof}
