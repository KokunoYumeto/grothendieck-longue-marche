\begin{lemma}[The exact intrinsic kernel of the faithful detector]
\label{lem:faithful-detector-augmentation-kernel}
Retain the literal groups \(\Sigma=F(a,b)\),
\(N_8\), \(K_3\), \(T=N_8\cap K_3\),
and the compact quaternion target of the preceding
construction. Write
\[
 D=T/(T'T^3),\qquad \dim_{\mathbb F_3}D=73,
 \qquad D=D_+\oplus D_-,
 \tag{FK.1}
\]
where the subscripts are the eigenspaces of the
central quaternion involution; their dimensions
are \(37,36\).
Let \(H=C_3^2\) be the specified translation
action and \(\mathfrak m_H\subset\mathbb F_3[H]\)
its augmentation ideal. The actual map of
\(T\) into the compact quaternion target has
abelian image of dimension twelve, and its
induced linear map \(\phi:D\to\mathbb F_3^{14}\)
has precisely the kernel
\[
 \ker\phi=D_+\oplus\mathfrak m_H^2D_-.
 \tag{FK.2}
\]
On the primitive eighteen-dimensional multiplicity
space \(M\), the induced quotient is exactly
\(M/\mathfrak m_H^2M\), of dimension six.
Every linear normalizer of the given \(H\)
action on \(M\) preserves this kernel and
therefore acts on this exact quotient.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source-to-target morphism is induced by
the identity of \(\Sigma\): its target kernel
is obtained from \(L_8T'\) by adjoining
the \(\Sigma\)-normal closure of \([a,b]^2\).
The ordinary abelian kernel \(\Sigma'\Sigma^{12}\)
is intersected afterwards, so the signed period
factor remains the stated separate joint factor.
The assertions below concern the displayed
three-primary \(T\)-image before that abelian
join, and thus do not remove the period factor.

There is an explicit finite linear map for
this assertion. The independent program rebuilds
the quaternion multiplication from
\(i^2=j^2=-1\), \(ji=-ij\), and reconstructs
the literal Schreier bases of ranks nine and
seventy-three. It substitutes each of the
seventy-three words, first through the ten
specified \(K_3\)-basis words in \(\Sigma\),
then through the nine-word \(N_8\)-rewriting.
For a word in those nine letters its class-two
coordinates are accumulated with the exact law
\[
 (v,z)(w,t)=(v+w,z+t+\tfrac12 v\wedge w).
 \tag{FK.3}
\]
Applying the retained central projection of
dimensions \(36\to15\), and then the compact
projection \(24\to14\), gives the actual
\(14\times73\) matrix \(\phi\).
Its rank is twelve. The brackets of every
pair of its seventy-three generator images
are zero. Since the target three-group has
exponent three, these identities prove that
the word map factors through \(D\), rather
than only assigning vectors to free generators.

The thirty-six peripheral loops are the four
quaternion lifts of each of the nine specified
\(K_3\) peripheral words, squared because
their quaternion image has order two.
The program rewrites each actual loop in
the seventy-three generators. Their homology
vectors have rank thirty-five, and all are
killed by \(\phi\). Thus this is also the
actual compact-homology comparison.
For the actual central deck matrix \(Z\),
the identity
\[
 \phi Z=-\phi,qquad
 \phi\,\tfrac12(1-Z)=\phi
 \tag{FK.4}
\]
is checked entry by entry.
In odd characteristic it kills the entire
\(D_+\), and proves the precise factorization
\(D\twoheadrightarrow D_-\twoheadrightarrow
\operatorname{im}\phi\).

Let \(h_1,h_2\) be the two actual translation
matrices. Since
\(\mathbb F_3[H]=\mathbb F_3[X,Y]/(X^3,Y^3)\)
with \(X=h_1-1,Y=h_2-1\), its ideal
\(\mathfrak m_H^2\) is generated by
\(X^2,XY,Y^2\).
For the retained \(73\times36\) faithful
basis matrix \(C\), the program forms
\[
 \mathcal J=
 \bigl[(h_1-1)^2C\ \ (h_1-1)(h_2-1)C\ \ (h_2-1)^2C\bigr].
 \tag{FK.5}
\]
It checks \(\phi\mathcal J=0\),
\(\operatorname{rank}\mathcal J=24\), and
\(\operatorname{rank}(\phi C)=12\).
These give both inclusions and equality in
(FK.2) by the dimension count in \(D_-\).
With the retained primitive multiplicity
basis \(C_B\) of size \(73\times18\),
the same direct calculation gives
\(\operatorname{rank}(\phi C_B)=6\), while
the three augmentation-square image matrices
have joint rank twelve and are killed.
Thus its exact kernel is
\(\mathfrak m_H^2M\), as asserted.

Finally, a linear map \(g\) normalizing \(H\)
obeys, for every \(h,k\in H\) and \(v\in M\),
\[
 g(h-1)(k-1)v
  =(ghg^{-1}-1)(gkg^{-1}-1)gv.
 \tag{FK.6}
\]
This maps \(\mathfrak m_H^2M\) onto itself,
using the same formula for \(g^{-1}\).
It induces the claimed actual linear
automorphism of \(M/\mathfrak m_H^2M\).
For a map carrying \(H\) to another specified
subgroup \(H'\), the identical formula gives
the typed isomorphism
\(M/\mathfrak m_H^2M\to
M/\mathfrak m_{H'}^2M\).
This last assertion concerns the explicit
module quotient; extension to a joint surface
or signed-period normalizer is not inferred
without that additional comparison.
\end{proof}
