\subsection{The integral common source and every coefficient modulus}

Retain the literal groups and ordered insertion maps of
A.4.52 and A.4.58. Thus $B_0=C_2*C_3$, its pure subgroup is
$F_0=\langle x,y\rangle$, the modular kernel $K$ is free of
rank $97$, and the common base kernel $J$ has index $288$
in $F_0$. The actual five-point quotient is
$\Gamma_{0,5}/N=Q_{192}$. Write $\iota_1,\ldots,\iota_8$
for exactly the eight marked homomorphisms (SVM.1).
For every integer $m\geq1$ define
\[
 \Gamma_m=\Gamma_{0,5}/(N'N^m),\qquad
 J_m=\bigcap_{i=1}^8\ker(F_0\xrightarrow{\iota_i}\Gamma_m),
 \qquad E_m=B_0/J_m.
                                                        \tag{ICS.1}
\]

\begin{result}{A.4.60}{Proved}{The eight actual insertion maps have a primitive integral rank-ninety-seven common source, giving exact kernels at every coefficient}
The integral target module and all its coefficient quotients are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 N^{\mathrm{ab}}&\cong\mathbb Z^{100}\oplus C_6^2,\\
 N/(N'N^m)&\cong C_m^{100}\oplus C_{\gcd(m,6)}^2,\\
 |\Gamma_m|&=192\,m^{100}\gcd(m,6)^2.
 \end{aligned}                                            \tag{ICS.2}
\]
On the actual common base kernel, the integral insertion map has
kernel exactly $K'$:
\[
 \ker\left(J\longrightarrow (N^{\mathrm{ab}})^8\right)=K'.
                                                        \tag{ICS.3}
\]
Its image is a free abelian group of rank $97$ whose projection to the
$800$ free target coordinates is split injective over $\mathbb Z$.
Consequently every coefficient has the exact common kernel
\[
 \boxed{\ J_m=K'J^m=(K'K^m)\cap(F_0'F_0^{6m})\ },
                                                        \tag{ICS.4}
\]
and the exact orders and abelianizations are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 J/J_m\cong C_m^{97},\qquad |E_m|=1728\,m^{97},\qquad
 |F_0/J_m|=288\,m^{97},\\
 (F_0/J_m)^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_{6m}\overline x\oplus
                         C_{6m}\overline y,\qquad
 |\overline x|=|\overline y|=6m.
 \end{gathered}                                            \tag{ICS.5}
\]
The matrix certificates retain the integral $s,r$ actions, the
divided exponent map and their exact coefficient maps to the
previously checked modulus-four source.

For coprime positive integers $m,n$, the identity on the original
generators gives the actual fibre products
\[
 \Gamma_{mn}\xrightarrow{\sim}
       \Gamma_m\mathbin{\times}_{Q_{192}}\Gamma_n,
 \qquad
 E_{mn}\xrightarrow{\sim}
       E_m\mathbin{\times}_{B_0/J}E_n.
                                                        \tag{ICS.6}
\]
Over all positive integers ordered by divisibility, the same maps give
\[
 \varprojlim_m\Gamma_m
      \cong\widehat{\Gamma_{0,5}/N'},\qquad
 \varprojlim_m E_m\cong\widehat{B_0/K'}.
                                                        \tag{ICS.7}
\]
The eight completed insertion maps have common kernel
$\overline{K'}$ in $\widehat F_0$ and therefore retain the
actual completed common source $\widehat{F_0/K'}$.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The integral target, with its actual coordinate map.}\quad
The ordinary $192$-sheeted cover has the $769$ named Schreier
generators and the full integral $1152$-by-$769$ relation matrix
$R$ of (CF4.6). An independent exact integral Smith computation
gives rank $669$, with $667$ unit invariants and two invariants
equal to six.

For explicit quotient coordinates, the integral certificate first
clears $661$ unit pivots by integer row operations. The remaining
$479$-by-$108$ matrix has an integral row-Hermite basis with eight
nonzero rows. Both unimodular transformations of that exact
$8$-by-$108$ residual matrix are saved and verified: its Smith
form has six unit entries and two entries equal to six. There
are precisely $100$ remaining free coordinates. Composing these
actual changes gives
\[
 P_N:\mathbb Z^{769}\longrightarrow
          \mathbb Z^{100}\oplus C_6^2,
 \quad
 RP_N^{\mathrm{free}}=0,\qquad RP_N^{\mathrm{tors}}=0\pmod6.
                                                        \tag{ICS.8}
\]
The full pivot and residual transformations identify its kernel
with the integral row lattice of $R$. They prove surjectivity as
well as the displayed identities. The certificate also retains
an integral left inverse for the $769$-by-$102$ representative
matrix before the last two output coordinates are reduced modulo
six. Thus (ICS.8) is a quotient map with a specified marking.
This proves the first line of (ICS.2); taking the quotient by
$m$ times this abelian group proves the other two lines.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The actual integral common lattice.}\quad
Use the same $289$ free Schreier generators of $J$. Rewriting
these words in the actual rank-$97$ modular kernel gives the
integral matrix $Q$, of size $289$ by $97$. Its row lattice
has index three in $K^{\mathrm{ab}}=\mathbb Z^{97}$.
Integral row-Hermite reduction gives a basis matrix $W$ for
that row lattice, with $|\det W|=3$, and matrices
\[
 C:\ 289\times97,\qquad L:\ 97\times289,
 \qquad Q=CW,\qquad LC=I_{97}.
                                                        \tag{ICS.9}
\]
All these identities hold over $\mathbb Z$.
The actual homomorphism $J\to K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ has kernel
$K'$, since $K'\subset J$. Because $W$ is injective over
$\mathbb Z$, $C$ is the marked quotient map
$J\to J/K'\cong\mathbb Z^{97}$.

Substitute each of the eight actual insertion pairs into
each of the $289$ words, rewrite in the target cover, and
apply (ICS.8). This gives a $289$-by-$816$ matrix $T$.
Each block consists of $100$ integral coordinates followed
by two coordinates modulo six. Let $T_{\mathrm f},T_{\mathrm t}$ designate its
$800$ free and $16$ torsion columns. The certificate retains
$A=LT$, with the same column convention, and checks
\[
                  T_{\mathrm f}=CA_{\mathrm f}\quad\hbox{over }\mathbb Z,
 \qquad           T_{\mathrm t}=CA_{\mathrm t}\pmod6.
                                                        \tag{ICS.10}
\]
Thus all eight actual maps factor through $C$, including
their torsion coordinates.

The decisive saturation calculation is integral. The $97$
Smith invariants of the $97$-by-$800$ matrix $A_{\mathrm f}$ are all
one. An explicit integer matrix $G$, of size $800$ by $97$,
is retained and checked entrywise to satisfy
\[
                             A_{\mathrm f}G=I_{97}.
                                                        \tag{ICS.11}
\]
It is obtained from the integer row-Hermite reduction of
$A_{\mathrm f}^{\mathrm t}$. Equations (ICS.10)--(ICS.11) exhibit
both directions of the actual common map: projection to
the free target coordinates followed by $G$ recovers the
source row $C$. They establish (ICS.3) and split
injectivity of the free projection without inferring
saturation from any finite collection of coefficient ranks.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Every coefficient and its exact source formula.}\quad
Let $a\in J$ have coordinate row $v\in\mathbb Z^{97}$.
Its eight target values vanish modulo $m$ only if
$vA_{\mathrm f}$ is divisible by $m$. Multiplication by the actual
integer right inverse $G$ then makes $v$ divisible by $m$.
Conversely $v\in m\mathbb Z^{97}$ kills both the free
coordinates modulo $m$ and the torsion coordinates in
$C_6/mC_6$. Hence the common kernel on $J$ is exactly
the inverse image of $m\mathbb Z^{97}$, namely $K'J^m$.
An element in the kernel of all eight maps must already
belong to $J$, because their base quotient maps are retained.
This proves the first equality of (ICS.4) and
$J/J_m=C_m^{97}$.

For completeness, the second equality has an exact lattice
proof at every integer $m$. The earlier common-base identity is
\[
 J=K\cap(F_0'F_0^6).
                                                        \tag{ICS.12}
\]
Indeed the exponent image of $K$ is
$\{(a,b):a,b\equiv0\pmod2,\ a+b\equiv0\pmod3\}$;
its image modulo $6\mathbb Z^2$ has order three, giving
the exact index $96\cdot3=288$ in (ICS.12).
Let $\Lambda\subset K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ be the inverse image
of $6\mathbb Z^2$ under this exponent map. Then
$J/K'=\Lambda$ and
\[
 m\Lambda
    =mK^{\mathrm{ab}}\cap
        \operatorname{exp}^{-1}(6m\mathbb Z^2).
                                                        \tag{ICS.13}
\]
To check both inclusions, write an element of the right side
as $mu$. Its exponent vector is $m\operatorname{exp}(u)$;
membership in $6m\mathbb Z^2$ is precisely
$\operatorname{exp}(u)\in6\mathbb Z^2$.
Taking inverse images in $K$ proves the second equality
of (ICS.4).

Both factors on that right side are normal in $B_0$:
$K$ is normal, and $F_0'F_0^{6m}$ is characteristic in
the normal subgroup $F_0$. The source $E_m$ is therefore
the stated actual quotient of $B_0$. The base indices give
its order and the pure source order in (ICS.5).
Formula (ICS.4) forces the exponent lattice of $J_m$ into
$6m\mathbb Z^2$. The words $x^{6m},y^{6m}$ belong to
$J^m$, since $x^6,y^6\in J$, and realize its two basis
vectors. Thus the exponent lattice is exactly
$6m\mathbb Z^2$, proving both the abelianization and the
exact orders of the named generators.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Integral action and coefficient compatibility.}\quad
Let $S_J,R_J$ be the actual integral word-substitution
matrices on the $289$ source generators. The retained
integral $97$-by-$97$ matrices satisfy
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 S_JC=CS,\qquad R_JC=CR,\qquad S^2=I,\quad R^3=I,\\
 CW\equiv Q_4\pmod4,\qquad
 SW\equiv WS_4\pmod4,\qquad RW\equiv WR_4\pmod4.
 \end{gathered}                                            \tag{ICS.14}
\]
Here $Q_4,S_4,R_4$ are exactly the previously retained
coefficient-four matrices. Since $\det W=\pm3$, this
coefficient change is invertible modulo four. The actual
exponent rows of the $289$ words are divisible by six;
their quotients factor integrally through $C$. That
$97$-by-$2$ factor is saved as well. These identities
provide the full integral action and exponent data for
every later coefficient calculation.

For coprime $m,n$, the coefficient map on any abelian group
$A$ is the actual isomorphism
$A/mnA\to A/mA\times A/nA$.
Apply this first to $N^{\mathrm{ab}}$ and then to
$J/K'$ to prove both maps in (ICS.6), with their specified
common bases. Every map is induced by the identity on the
ordinary source generators.

Finally the quotients in (ICS.7) are cofinal among the
finite quotients of the indicated ordinary groups.
For a finite quotient of $\Gamma_{0,5}/N'$, the image
of its finitely generated abelian subgroup $N/N'$ has
finite exponent $m$; therefore its kernel contains
$N'N^m/N'$. For a finite quotient of $B_0/K'$, the
image of $J/K'$ has finite exponent $m$; its kernel
contains $K'J^m/K'$. This proves both inverse-limit
identifications. Applying the same cofinality to $F_0$
and using (ICS.3)--(ICS.4) proves the last assertion on
the completed insertion maps and their common kernel.
\end{proof}
