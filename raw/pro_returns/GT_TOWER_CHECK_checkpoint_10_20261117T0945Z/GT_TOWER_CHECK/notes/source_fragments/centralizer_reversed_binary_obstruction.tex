\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The exact rank-ten source.}\quad

\[
 (x^ay^b)t\,(x^{a'}y^{b'})^{-1},\qquad

                                                               \tag{RPO.4}
\]
There are seven nontrivial $x$-edges and three
nontrivial $y$-edges.
Write these basis elements as $g_1,\ldots,g_{10}$

The quotient $J_3/K$ has the unique normal forms
\[
 g_1^{u_1}\cdots g_{10}^{u_{10}}
   \prod_{i<j}[g_i,g_j]^{v_{ij}},
 \qquad u_i\in\mathbb Z/4,\quad v_{ij}\in\mathbb F_2.
                                                               \tag{RPO.5}
\]
For completeness, their multiplication is addition
in the two displayed coordinate groups, with
extra central coordinate $u_j u_i'$ at $(i,j)$
for $i<j$.  Reduction modulo two makes this
bilinear cross term associative.  The resulting
group has central commutators of exponent two
and exponent four; its ten standard elements
satisfy exactly the defining relations above.
Collection gives an epimorphism onto this group,
and its distinct coordinate vectors give the
reverse lower bound on the normal forms.

the order assertions in (RPO.2).

Under $F\to F^{\rm ab}=\widehat{\mathbb Z}^2$,
