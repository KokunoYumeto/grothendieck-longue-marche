\subsection{One genuine survivor through every relative-abelian coefficient quotient}

Retain the actual groups, markings, and eight insertion maps of A.4.60.
Thus $B_0=C_2(s)*C_3(r)$, $\tau=sr$,
$F_0=\langle x=\tau^{-2},y=sxs\rangle$, and $K$ is the free
rank-$97$ kernel of $B_0\to Q_B$, where $|Q_B|=576$.
The common base kernel $J\subset K$ has $[B_0:J]=1728$.
For every positive integer $m$ the actual source and target are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 J_m=K'J^m=(K'K^m)\cap(F_0'F_0^{6m}),\qquad
 E_m=B_0/J_m,\\
 \Gamma_m=\Gamma_{0,5}/(N'N^m),\qquad
 N=\ker(\Gamma_{0,5}\to Q_{192}).
 \end{gathered}                                                   \tag{UAS.1}
\]
Every source map in this subsection is induced by the identity on
$B_0$ or by one of those eight specified insertion maps.

\begin{result}{A.4.61}{Proved}{A genuine parity-one element satisfies every pentagon in the full relative-abelian tower}
There is one actual normalized element $g=(1,f_g)\in H$ such that
\[
 \Delta(g)=1,\qquad
 \operatorname{Pent}_{\Gamma_m}(f_g)=1
                    \quad\hbox{for every }m\geq1.                 \tag{UAS.2}
\]
Equivalently, its pentagon is the identity in the actual completed
quotient
\[
                  \widehat{\Gamma_{0,5}/N'}.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.3}
\]
The element can retain the corrected binary pair and both frames of
A.4.55, together with its ternary record $f_3=A_3=1$ and the same
harmonic frame $\beta_3$.  In particular the signed values in
(CMC.5) remain the specified values.  This element does not belong
to full $\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}$, since $\Delta$ vanishes there.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{An exact integral affine source lattice.}\quad
Use the integral basis of $J/K'\cong\mathbb Z^{97}$ from A.4.60,
and the actual source base residues $0,252,0$ for $f,\beta,A$.
The $252$nd base word is the named harmonic word retained in
A.4.55.  Let $\sigma=\operatorname{Ad}(s)$ and
$\rho=\operatorname{Ad}(r)$.  Their actual row matrices on $J/K'$
and on $K^{\rm ab}$ are the integral matrices in the certificates;
no identification of their two integral lattices is implicit.
The map between the lattices is the retained matrix of determinant
three in (ICS.9).

The four literal target expressions used in A.4.55 are evaluated in
the integral group $N^{\rm ab}=\mathbb Z^{100}\oplus C_6^2$.
Their $400$ free-coordinate equations, the two exponent equations
for $f$, the $194$ frame equations
\[
                 \sigma(\beta)=f\beta,
                 \rho(A)=fA,                                    \tag{UAS.4}
\]
and the two exponent equations for $A$ give the exact integral system
\[
     \mathcal A z=b,\qquad z=(f,\beta,A)^{\rm t},\qquad
     \mathcal A\in M_{598,291}(\mathbb Z).
                                                                    \tag{UAS.5}
\]
The remaining target coordinates give eight explicitly retained
congruences modulo six.  In the binary component these are exactly
their eight congruences modulo two.  The tree-word constants for
the base residue of $\beta$ are included in $b$.

The program \path{check_integral_affine_system.py} constructs this
matrix from the actual integral insertion maps and proves that its
nonzero Smith invariants are $194$ copies of $1$ and one copy of $2$.
The augmented rank is also $195$.
The exact Hermite transformation in
\path{check_integral_affine_lattice.py} supplies an integer solution
and a saturated kernel basis $k_0,\ldots,k_{95}$.
Both the transformation and the solution are retained with arbitrary
precision integers.  In particular
\[
 \ker(\mathcal A:\mathbb Z^{291}\to\mathbb Z^{598})
          =\bigoplus_{i=0}^{95}\mathbb Z k_i.                       \tag{UAS.6}
\]
The integer solution is chosen to reduce to the fully corrected
binary pair and frames of A.4.55.  It therefore satisfies all eight
binary torsion congruences.  Adding any $2\sum u_i k_i$ preserves
these congruences and all the free equations exactly.

There is an integer solution $z_*$ in this binary class for which
the full coefficient-four canonical cusp defect is zero.
The program \path{check_honest_hensel_seed.py} constructs it by
adding twice kernel vectors with indices $1,2,3,5,11$ to its
retained initial integer solution.  It verifies every equation in
(UAS.5) over the integers, all the binary torsion equations, and
membership of the collected cusp defect in the full integral
Hermite relation lattice of $M_4$.  The actual integer point and
the integral relation witness are retained; thus this assertion
does not extrapolate from a tangent calculation.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Where the honest cusp defect lies.}\quad
Write
\[
 \mathcal W=(\Lambda^2 K^{\rm ab})_{B_0/K}\otimes\mathbb Z_2,
 \qquad T=\mathcal W_{\rm tors},\qquad
 \overline{\mathcal W}=\mathcal W/T.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.7}
\]
The preceding uniform canonical-kernel lemma proves, through the
actual commutator maps,
\[
 \mathcal W\cong\mathbb Z_2^8\oplus C_2^7,
 \qquad
 M_{2^a}\cong C_2\oplus\mathcal W/2^a\mathcal W.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.8}
\]
Here $M_{2^a}$ is the full canonical kernel for
$B_0/(K'K^{2^a})$.  Its transition kills the displayed power
$C_2$ and reduces the wedge coordinate.

For an integral solution of (UAS.5), form the actual word
\[
 d(z)=\beta\,\sigma\!\left(\beta^{-1}A\rho(A^{-1})\right)
     =\beta s\beta^{-1}ArA^{-1}(sr)^{-1}.                          \tag{UAS.9}
\]
The frame equations hold in $B_0/K'$.  They imply that the two
displayed torsion images multiply to $sr$, so $d(z)\in K'$.
Its image in $K'/[B_0,K']$ is independent of the choices of the
three lifts, since that subgroup is central in
$B_0/[B_0,K']$.
The actual free class-two presentation identifies
\[
 K'/[B_0,K']=(\Lambda^2K^{\rm ab})_{B_0/K}.                         \tag{UAS.10}
\]
Indeed quotienting $K'$ by $[K,K']$ gives $\Lambda^2K^{\rm ab}$,
and the remaining relations are exactly the conjugation
coinvariants for $s,r$.
Consequently the defect of every honest affine point lies in the
wedge term of (UAS.8).  It has no power-coordinate component.
The injection $\mathcal W/2\mathcal W\hookrightarrow M_2$ and
the fixed binary record show that all these defects vanish modulo
$2\mathcal W$.  Their $C_2^7$ torsion coordinate is therefore zero.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The exact polynomial and its derivative.}\quad
Use the actual integral solutions
\[
               z(u)=z_*+2\sum_{i=0}^{95}u_i k_i.                  \tag{UAS.11}
\]
In $K^{\rm ab}$ let $b_i,a_i,h_i$ be the respective variations of
the harmonic frame, cubic frame, and pair from $k_i$.
The actual row actions $S,R$ satisfy
\[
       h_i=b_iS-b_i=a_iR-a_i,
       \qquad h_iS=-h_i.                                        \tag{UAS.12}
\]
The passage from the integral common-source coordinates to these
modular coordinates is the explicit map already specified above.

Here is the polynomial calculation including its denominator.
Project the class-two group to the torsion-free wedge quotient and
take its rational logarithm.  Changing a chosen section of
$K^{\rm ab}$ changes a lift by a central element, which cancels
from (UAS.9).  The central correction of either action is linear
in the logarithm.  The Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula has only
the term $[v,w]/2$ beyond addition.  For a homogeneous frame
variation $(b,a,h)$ satisfying (UAS.12), its homogeneous quadratic
contribution to (UAS.9) is
\[
       \frac12(aS-b)\wedge h
                   =-\frac12 a\wedge(aR)
                   \quad\hbox{in }\overline{\mathcal W}\otimes\mathbb Q_2.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.13}
\]
To verify the equality, coinvariance gives
$b\wedge bS=(bS)\wedge b=-b\wedge bS$, so this class is zero
in the torsion-free quotient.  Also
$aS\wedge h=a\wedge hS=-a\wedge h$.
Substitution of $h=bS-b=aR-a$ gives (UAS.13).
Equivalently, expanding the two factors
$\beta\sigma(\beta^{-1})$ and
$\sigma(A\rho(A^{-1}))$ gives quadratic terms
$-b\wedge bS/2$ and $-a\wedge aR/2$; their first-degree
terms are opposite, so their final BCH cross term vanishes.

Put $a(u)=\sum u_i a_i$.  The factor two in (UAS.11) makes the
quadratic contribution exactly $-2a(u)\wedge a(u)R$.
Thus
\[
 F(u):=\frac{\overline{d(z(u))}}2
   =\frac{\overline{d(z_*)}}2+L(u)-a(u)\wedge a(u)R
      \quad\hbox{in }\overline{\mathcal W}\cong\mathbb Z_2^8,
                                                                    \tag{UAS.14}
\]
where $L$ is linear.  Its coefficients belong to $\mathbb Z_2$:
the constant and every value at $u=e_i$ are integral by the
vanishing in $\mathcal W/2\mathcal W$, and the displayed quadratic
coefficients are integral.  This proves that (UAS.14) is an actual
polynomial over $\mathbb Z_2$; no integer-valued binomial with an
uncontrolled denominator has been substituted for its derivative.
The identity extends from integer points to $\mathbb Z_2^{96}$
by continuity through the specified finite coefficient maps.

The free reduction has the literal injective doubling map
\[
 \overline{\mathcal W}/2\overline{\mathcal W}
           \longrightarrow 2\mathcal W/4\mathcal W,
           \qquad \bar w\longmapsto2w.                           \tag{UAS.15}
\]
The certificate constructs this map inside the full integral
canonical $M_4$ presentation.  Its dimension is eight.  Directly
collecting (UAS.9) at $z_*$ and at all $z_*+2k_i$ gives the
finite differences of $F$ through precisely this map.
Their rank is eight.  The diagonal quadratic classes
$a_i\wedge a_iR$ are all zero in the actual free quotient modulo
two, and so are all $4560$ cross classes
$a_i\wedge a_jR+a_j\wedge a_iR$.
It follows from (UAS.14), rather than from rank alone, that these
finite differences are the actual derivative modulo two.
The retained invertible minor uses directions
\[
                         0,1,2,3,4,5,9,11.                       \tag{UAS.16}
\]
The independent program \path{check_integral_frame_jacobian.py}
checks the doubling map and all quadratic classes.
The additional \path{check_honest_seed_jacobian.py} directly
recomputes all $96$ full-canonical differences at the chosen
honest integer seed and verifies the same minor.

Restrict (UAS.14) to these eight variables, setting the other
variables to zero.  The seed has $d(z_*)=0$ in $M_4$, so
$F(0)\equiv0\pmod2$.  The Jacobian is invertible modulo two.
For completeness, construct a root successively: given a vector
$u_n$ with $F(u_n)\equiv0\pmod{2^n}$, choose its unique correction
$v_n\pmod2$ in these eight directions satisfying
\[
       DF(u_n)v_n\equiv-2^{-n}F(u_n)\pmod2,
       \qquad u_{n+1}=u_n+2^n v_n.                               \tag{UAS.17}
\]
The polynomial identity makes the new residual divisible by
$2^{n+1}$.  The Jacobian stays invertible because all $u_n$ reduce
to zero modulo two.  Starting with $u_1=0$, these compatible
vectors converge in $\mathbb Z_2^8$ to a root $u_\infty$.
The torsion coordinate of the defect was already zero, and the
power coordinate is absent by (UAS.10).  Therefore the resulting
honest source record has zero defect in every $M_{2^a}$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Actual finite automorphisms at the binary levels.}\quad
The two frames at $z(u_\infty)$ define a homomorphism on the
actual source generators by
\[
       s\longmapsto\beta s\beta^{-1},\qquad
       r\longmapsto ArA^{-1}.                                   \tag{UAS.18}
\]
They fix $sr$ by (UAS.4).  The induced map on $B_0/J$ is the
identity.  Its factorization through the finite source has the
following literal kernel check.  Choose an ordinary lift of the
finite pair value in $J\subset K$ and form the free-group
endomorphism $x\mapsto x$, $y\mapsto f^{-1}yf$ of $F_0$.
It is the identity on $F_0/K$, so preserves $K$, $K'$ and $K^m$;
it also preserves $F_0'F_0^{6m}$ by its identity action on the
abelianization.  Consequently it preserves the actual intersection
$J_m$ in (UAS.1).  Its restriction is exactly the pure map supplied
by (UAS.4), and the displayed positive torsion frames extend that
map to $s,r$.  This proves the factorization through each actual
binary source $E_{2^a}$ directly.
Their matrices on $J/J_{2^a}$ reduce to the verified invertible
binary automorphism in A.4.55.  Their determinants are therefore
odd, so each is an automorphism of $(\mathbb Z/2^a)^{97}$.
Together with the identity base map, this proves that (UAS.18)
is an actual automorphism of $E_{2^a}$.
The four target free-coordinate equations hold over $\mathbb Z_2$
by (UAS.5).  Their binary torsion equations remain the seed's
equations.  Hence their literal pentagon is one in every
$\Gamma_{2^a}$, including its two residual order-two coordinates.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Odd companions and both canonical primary maps.}\quad
Use the fixed ternary record $f_3=A_3=1$, $\beta_3$ from A.4.55.
For any odd $n$ divisible by three, the kernel of $E_n\to E_3$
is an abelian group of odd order.  There is a pure lift
$\beta_n\in C_{E_n}(s)$ of $\beta_3$.
Here is the actual correction: start with a pure lift $b$ and
write $\sigma(b)b^{-1}=e$ in that kernel.  Then
$\sigma(e)=-e$ in its additive notation.  Replacing $b$ by
$(e/2)b$ changes this defect to
$\sigma(e/2)+e-e/2=0$.
This supplies the required lift inside the specified group.
Set $f_n=A_n=1$.  The resulting source action is the identity
on $E_n$, with the retained harmonic frame $\beta_n$.

For $m=2^a n$, $a\geq1$, the actual CRT map of A.4.60 is
\[
       E_m=E_{2^a}\mathop{\times}_{B_0/J}E_n.                    \tag{UAS.19}
\]
The two actions and their frames agree on this actual common base,
so they join to an automorphism and pure frames of $E_m$.
Their pentagons are one in both target factors, hence one in
$\Gamma_m$ under its corresponding CRT map.

Write $B_{2^a}^{\rm mod}=B_0/(K'K^{2^a})$.
The following are quotient maps induced by the identity of $B_0$:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_m&\longrightarrow B_{2^a}^{\rm mod},&
             |\ker|&=3n^{97},\\
 E_m&\longrightarrow E_n,&
             |\ker|&=(2^a)^{97}.
 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{UAS.20}
\]
For every group $T$ here, its source kernel $K_T\subset B_0'$
and its full canonical cover are
\[
 T^c=B_0/[B_0,K_T],\qquad
 M_T=K_T/[B_0,K_T]\cong H_2(T,\mathbb Z).                         \tag{UAS.21}
\]
The isomorphism is the natural integral five-term map, since
$H_2(C_2*C_3,\mathbb Z)=0$ and the map on abelianizations is the
identity of $C_6$.  These finite central kernels are stem kernels.
The maps (UAS.20) induce the actual maps of these canonical covers.
Localized integral homology for their kernels identifies the
$2$-primary part of $M_{E_m}$ with $M_{2^a}$, and, for every odd
prime, identifies that primary part with the corresponding part
of $M_{E_n}$.  Indeed the positive homology of a finite group is
killed by its order, and the localized homology spectral sequence
has only its zero row when the kernel order is invertible.
These are the same natural edge maps as in (CMC.15)--(CMC.16);
the transfer and spectral-sequence statements are
\cite[Theorem~6.5.8, Corollary~6.5.10, Theorem~6.8.2 and
the five-term sequence~6.8.3]{CA_WeibelHA1994}.

The cusp defect in the first target of (UAS.20) is zero by the
honest binary construction.  In the second target it is
$\kappa_s(\beta_n)$ because $f_n=A_n=1$.
This class is killed by two: its lift commutes with $s$ modulo
the central kernel, and the relation $s^2=1$ gives twice that
commutator equal to zero.  Thus all its odd primary components
vanish.  Both actual primary comparisons show that the entire
cusp defect in $M_{E_m}$ is zero.

Pure lifts of the two frames consequently define an endomorphism
of the full finite group $E_m^c$ fixing $\tau$ and inducing the
automorphism of $E_m$.  To see the factorization, the source
kernel $J_m$ maps to the central kernel, so $[B_0,J_m]$ maps to
one.  To see surjectivity, if $I$ is the image then
$IM_{E_m}=E_m^c$.  Centrality gives $(E_m^c)'=I'\subset I$,
and the stem containment $M_{E_m}\subset(E_m^c)'$ gives
$I=E_m^c$.  This is therefore an actual positive automorphism
of the whole canonical cover with the prescribed pure frames.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{Full profinite lifting and the exact normalization.}\quad
Apply the proved finite-certificate image equality of B.2.7 and
B.2.9 to this full canonical automorphism.  It gives an actual
positive automorphism of $\widehat B_0$ preserving the finite
source record and both frames.  Its raw parameter has exponent
vector $(-t,t)$.  The finite parameter has zero abelianization,
where A.4.60 supplies the exact abelianization and generator orders
\[
        (F_0/J_m)^{\rm ab}=C_{6m}^2,
             \qquad |x|=|y|=6m.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.22}
\]
It follows that $t\in6m\widehat{\mathbb Z}$.
The actual normalization $\operatorname{Inn}(x^t)$ of (B.M13)
therefore preserves the parameter and both frames in $E_m$
literally, since $x^t=y^t=1$ there.
The result is a normalized genuine $g_m\in H$.
Its retained ternary frame has zero exponent coordinates modulo
three, so the exact signed formula $V_{12}=U^{8\rho}$ gives
$V_{12}=I$.  The corrected binary record and the named ternary
frame are those of A.4.55, so its signed cubic and harmonic
values are exactly (CMC.5), and $\Delta(g_m)=1$.
Its parameter has the verified pentagon in $\Gamma_m$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{One full element, by compactness.}\quad
For a positive integer $m$ let
\[
 \mathcal C_m=\{g\in H:\Delta(g)=1,
                         \ \operatorname{Pent}_{\Gamma_m}(f_g)=1\}.
                                                                    \tag{UAS.23}
\]
These are closed subsets of the actual compact profinite group
$H$: the parameter coordinate, each specified finite word
evaluation, and $\Delta$ are continuous.
The construction above makes $\mathcal C_m$ nonempty whenever
$m$ is divisible by six.  Every finite family of the conditions
is met by taking $m$ to be six times a common multiple and using
the actual quotient maps (UAS.1).
The finite binary and ternary frame conditions can be included
in the same closed sets.  Their continuity follows from the
unique pure torsion frames, equivalently from the compact closed
graph of their defining equations and the centralizers
$C_B(s)=\langle s\rangle$, $C_B(r)=\langle r\rangle$.
They are retained at every constructed finite stage.
Compactness now gives one element in the intersection, proving
(UAS.2) with the stated finite records.
Finally A.4.60 identifies the inverse limit of these literal
targets with (UAS.3).  The pentagon maps to one in every finite
coefficient quotient, so it is one in that inverse limit.
The vanishing of $\Delta$ on full $\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}$
then proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}
