\subsection{A closed formula for the actual common mod-two, mod-three and mod-six sources}

Use the marked discrete groups
$B_0=\langle s,r\mid s^2=r^3=1\rangle$ and
$F_0=\langle x,y\rangle$, with $x=(sr)^{-2}$ and $y=sxs$.
Let $K$ be the kernel of the specified map
$B_0\twoheadrightarrow Q_B=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z/12)$.
Thus $K\subset F_0$, $[B_0:K]=576$, $[F_0:K]=96$, and $K$
is free of rank $97$.  Use $q:\Gamma_{0,5}\twoheadrightarrow Q_{192}$
and $N=\ker q$ from A.4.45.  For $m\in\{2,3,6\}$ put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \Gamma_m=\Gamma_{0,5}/(N'N^m),\qquad
 J_m=\bigcap_{i=1}^8
       \ker(F_0\xrightarrow{\iota_i}\Gamma_m),\\
 J=\bigcap_{i=1}^8\ker(q\iota_i),\qquad
 E_m=B_0/J_m,\qquad P_m=F_0/J_m,
 \end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFK.1}
\]
where the eight maps $\iota_i$ are exactly the ordered substitutions
(SVM.1).  Normality of $J_m$ in $B_0$ is included in the result.
All groups in the conclusion are finite, so their quotient maps also
specify the corresponding maps from the profinite completions.

\begin{result}{A.4.52}{Proved}{The eight insertion maps have an exact common source kernel}
For each $m\in\{2,3,6\}$ the actual source kernel is
\[
             J_m=(K'K^m)\cap(F_0'F_0^{6m}).
                                                               \tag{CFK.2}
\]
Consequently the map sending the two original source generators to
their eight actual insertion values has the precise quotient
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 |\Gamma_m|=192\,m^{102},\qquad
 |P_m|=288\,m^{97},\qquad |E_m|=1728\,m^{97},\\
 P_m^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_{6m}\overline x\oplus C_{6m}\overline y,
 \qquad |\overline x|=|\overline y|=6m.
 \end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFK.3}
\]
There is the specified full modular quotient map and exact kernel
\[
 1\longrightarrow C_3\longrightarrow E_m
      \longrightarrow B_0/(K'K^m)\longrightarrow1.
                                                               \tag{CFK.4}
\]
On that kernel conjugation by $s$ is inversion and conjugation by
$r$ is identity.  In particular the kernel centralizes the pure
source image.  Formula (CFK.2) identifies $E_m$ with the actual joint
image of the full modular quotient and the marked abelian source
$B_0/(F_0'F_0^{6m})$.

The maps for the three moduli are related by the literal reductions
of every named generator.  In particular
\[
 E_6\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
 E_2\mathbin{\times}_{B_0/J}E_3,
 \qquad [B_0:J]=1728,
 \qquad J/J_6\simeq C_6^{97}.
                                                               \tag{CFK.5}
\]
The isomorphism in (CFK.5) is induced by the identity of $B_0$ in both
coordinates.  Thus it preserves all eight insertion maps and both
named torsion generators.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The actual two prime-coefficient maps.}\quad
The positive spanning tree in the joint base quotient gives $289$
literal Schreier basis words for $J$, as in (SVM.4)--(SVM.5).
The base has order $288$ on $F_0$ and is stable under the two actual
conjugations
$s:(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$ and
$r:(x,y)\mapsto(y,(xy)^{-1})$.
For each prime $p=2,3$, lifting the six five-point relators from
all $192$ target base states gives a $1152\times769$ relation matrix
of rank $667$.  The target homology quotient has dimension $102$.
The eight inserted source matrices have the following exact ranks:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr|r|r}
 p&i=1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8&\text{joint}&\text{six-deck joint}\\ \hline
 2&3&3&97&2&97&97&97&97&97&97\\
 3&2&2&96&2&96&96&96&96&97&97.
 \end{array}                                                   \tag{CFK.6}
\]
These are the maps from the named $289$ source loops to the eight
named target homology groups.  Both executed programs retain a
$289\times97$ projection $P$, a left inverse $L$, the complete
joined matrix $T$, and the identities $LP=I$ and $PLT=T$.
They retain the induced $s,r$ matrices and check their intertwining
with $P$ coefficientwise.  Thus the actual common kernel is
$B_0$-normal and $J/J_p=C_p^{97}$, proving
$|E_p|=1728p^{97}$.

The exact divided exponent matrix of the $289$ source loops,
$(\operatorname{exp}_x/6,\operatorname{exp}_y/6)$ modulo $p$,
also factors through $P$.  The images of $x^6,y^6$ give its two
standard basis vectors.  Since $J$ has exponent lattice
$6\mathbb Z^2$ and $J'J^p\subset J_p$, this proves
\[
       \operatorname{exp}(J_p)=6p\mathbb Z^2,
       \qquad P_p^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_{6p}^2.
                                                               \tag{CFK.7}
\]
The powers $x^{6p},y^{6p}$ vanish in each insertion: their sixth
powers already lie in $N$, whose image in $\Gamma_p$ has exponent
$p$.  The abelianization in (CFK.7) proves that these two orders
are exactly $6p$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The two exact factors in the kernel formula.}\quad
The split fifth insertion of A.4.45 gives
$J_p\subset K'K^p$.  The abelian map (CFK.7) gives
$J_p\subset F_0'F_0^{6p}$.
These are inclusions of actual source kernels, induced respectively
by the free retraction and the divided exponent map.

For the reverse inclusion by the exact indices, compute the exponent
lattice of $K$.  The signed free image modulo twelve is the direct
product of its modulo-four Klein group and
$\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_3)$; its order is $4\cdot24=96$.
Its abelian quotient records the two exponents modulo two and their
sum modulo three.  Indeed the images of $x$ and $y$ at three are
conjugate noncentral elements of order three, and the commutator
subgroup there is the quaternion group of order eight.  Hence
\[
 L:=\operatorname{exp}(K)
     =\{(a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2:
              a\equiv b\equiv0\pmod2,\ a+b\equiv0\pmod3\}.
                                                               \tag{CFK.8}
\]
There is also a literal source-word verification of equality in
(CFK.8).  Two of the $97$ retained modular Schreier words are
\[
 x^6,\qquad yxy^2x^{-1}y^{-1}x^{-2};
 \qquad\operatorname{exp}=(6,0),\ (-2,2).
                                                               \tag{CFK.9}
\]
Both have identity signed matrix, and the determinant of their
exponent vectors is $12$.  All $97$ exponent vectors satisfy the
congruences in (CFK.8), whose lattice has index $12$.  This proves
the claimed lattice using the actual named words.

For any of the three displayed values of $m$, the exponent image of
$K'K^m$ is exactly $mL$: commutators have zero exponent, and the
$m$th powers of all elements of $K$ supply $mL$.  Because
$6\mathbb Z^2\subset L$, the image of this subgroup in
$F_0/(F_0'F_0^{6m})$ is precisely
\[
       mL/(6m\mathbb Z^2)
       =\{(0,0),(2m,4m),(4m,2m)\}\simeq C_3.
                                                               \tag{CFK.10}
\]
On the other hand, the freeness and rank of $K$ give
$[B_0:K'K^m]=576m^{97}$.  Therefore
\[
 [B_0:(K'K^m)\cap(F_0'F_0^{6m})]
                         =1728m^{97}.
                                                               \tag{CFK.11}
\]
For $m=p=2,3$, this is the already proved index of $J_p$.
The kernel inclusions preceding (CFK.8) and equality of finite indices
prove equality of the kernels in (CFK.2) for both primes.
In (CFK.10), $s$ swaps the two exponent coordinates and $r$ sends
$(a,b)$ to $(-b,a-b)$.  These respectively act by inversion and
identity on the displayed three-element group.  This proves
(CFK.4), including its exact action and its source map.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The mod-six map and its fiber product.}\quad
For every abelian group $A$, the actual map
$A/6A\to A/2A\times A/3A$ is an isomorphism.  Apply it to
$N/N'$ to get the actual quotient map
\[
 \Gamma_6\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
        \Gamma_2\mathbin{\times}_{Q_{192}}\Gamma_3.
                                                               \tag{CFK.12}
\]
Surjectivity onto this fiber product is explicit: choose a common
source lift of the base value, then prescribe its two kernel
coordinates using the just displayed Chinese remainder map.
Consequently $J_6=J_2\cap J_3$ for the eight named substitutions.
The same abelian argument applied to $K/K'$ and $F_0/F_0'$ gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 (K'K^2)\cap(K'K^3)&=K'K^6,\\
 (F_0'F_0^{12})\cap(F_0'F_0^{18})&=F_0'F_0^{36}.
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{CFK.13}
\]
Intersecting the two proved prime formulas yields (CFK.2) for $m=6$.
The quotient $J/(J_2J_3)$ is a common quotient of the elementary
two-group $J/J_2$ and the elementary three-group $J/J_3$.
Its order divides both a power of two and a power of three, so it is
trivial.  Hence $J_2J_3=J$, proving the surjectivity and the exact
fiber product in (CFK.5).  Its kernel over $B_0/J$ is
$C_2^{97}\times C_3^{97}\simeq C_6^{97}$ by the literal scalar CRT.
This proves the mod-six order in (CFK.3).

Finally, the marked abelian quotients $C_{12}^2$ and $C_{18}^2$
have joint image $C_{36}^2$ under the same two exponent coordinates.
In $\Gamma_6$ the sixth powers of the source generators have order
dividing six, so the generator orders in $P_6$ divide thirty-six.
The joint abelian image proves the reverse divisibility and excludes
any further abelian relation.  Thus the last line of (CFK.3) holds
also for $m=6$.  The target orders follow from the target kernel
dimensions $102$ at both primes and (CFK.12).

records in this finite source.
\end{proof}
