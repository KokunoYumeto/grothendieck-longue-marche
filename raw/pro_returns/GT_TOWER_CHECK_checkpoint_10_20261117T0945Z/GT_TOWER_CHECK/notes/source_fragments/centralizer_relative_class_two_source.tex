\subsection{The actual common source before the next binary class-two quotient}

Retain the ordinary groups $B_0,F_0,J,K,N$ and the eight insertion
maps $\iota_i$ of A.4.60.  Thus $J$ is free of rank $289$,
$J/K'\cong\mathbb Z^{97}$, and every $\iota_i$ sends $J$ into
$N$.  Define the actual finite target and a common source kernel by
\[
 \Gamma^{(2)}=\Gamma_{0,5}/(\gamma_3N\,N^4),\qquad
 L^{(2)}=[J,K'](K')^2J^4.                              \tag{RCS.1}
\]
The exponent subgroup notation denotes the subgroup generated by
powers.  All three factors of $L^{(2)}$ are normal in $B_0$,
since $J$ and $K'$ are normal in $B_0$.

\begin{lemma}[An actual finite source for all eight binary class-two maps]
Each of the eight maps $F_0\to\Gamma^{(2)}$ factors through
$F_0/L^{(2)}$.  The group $B_0/L^{(2)}$ is a finite common
$B_0$-source.  Its kernel above the named base $B_0/J$ has order
\[
 |J/L^{(2)}|=4^{97}2^{4656+192}=2^{5042},\qquad
 |B_0/L^{(2)}|=1728\,2^{5042}.                         \tag{RCS.2}
\]
Before imposing powers, the actual central sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\Lambda^2\mathbb Z^{97}
 \longrightarrow K'/[J,K']
 \longrightarrow\ker(\mathbb Z^{289}\to\mathbb Z^{97})
 \longrightarrow0.                                   \tag{RCS.3}
\]
Its middle group is free abelian of rank $4848$.
The $B_0$ action on it factors through the actual group $B_0/J$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A.4.60 identifies the kernel of the integral map
$J\to(N^{\rm ab})^8$ with the actual subgroup $K'$.
Consequently $\iota_i(K')\subset N'$ for every named insertion.
The subgroup $N'$ is central after quotienting by $\gamma_3N$,
so every map kills $[J,K']$.
In a class-two group of exponent four the commutator subgroup
has exponent dividing two: the identities
$[u^4,v]=[u,v]^4$ and
$(uv)^4=u^4v^4[v,u]^6$ imply this by taking the greatest common
divisor of $4$ and $6$.
Thus the same maps kill $(K')^2$ and $J^4$, proving the exact
factorizations through (RCS.1).

The integral homology sequence of the actual epimorphism
$J\to J/K'=\mathbb Z^{97}$ gives (RCS.3).
Its left endpoint is zero because $J$ is free.  Its right map
is the integral matrix of A.4.60 with its retained integral
right inverse.  The exterior-square term has rank $4656$ and
the right kernel has rank $192$.
Both are free abelian, so the middle group is free abelian of
their total rank.  Conjugation by $J$ is trivial by the defining
quotient, giving the stated action of the actual base $B_0/J$.

For the exact finite order, apply integral Smith operations to
the specified surjective exponent map
$\mathbb Z^{289}\to\mathbb Z^{97}$.
Each source basis operation is realized by the corresponding
Nielsen operation on the named free Schreier generators.
This gives an actual free basis
$a_1,\ldots,a_{97},b_1,\ldots,b_{192}$ with images the standard
basis and zero, respectively, in $J/K'$.
The subgroup $K'$ is the normal closure of the $b_j$ and
$[a_i,a_l]$.  Quotienting by $[J,K']$ makes all $b_j$ central
and makes the $a_i$ generate the free class-two nilpotent group.
There are no additional relations: sending the same generators
back gives the inverse homomorphism from that presentation.
The relations $(K')^2J^4$ make each $b_j$ and each commutator
$[a_i,a_l]$ an involution and give each $a_i$ order four.
The ordered normal form therefore has $97$ independent
coordinates modulo four and $4656+192$ independent central
coordinates modulo two, proving (RCS.2).
These coordinate changes apply to the specified integral
exponent map; the eight quotient maps remain the original
$\iota_i$ throughout.
\end{proof}

This gives a finite source for the next actual eight-map image.
Its order bounds that image; no equality with the image or
lifting of a proposed gentle record is asserted here.
