\subsection{Deck reconstruction with the explicit quaternion input}

\begin{result}{A.4.13}{Proved}{An explicit smaller input for the three-division normalizer map}
Retain $\Sigma$, $K_3$, $N_8$, $L_8$, $R_8$ and
$\mathcal P_8=\Sigma/R_8$ from A.4.12.  The finite
deck-normalizer construction of A.4.11 applies to this
particular surface quotient with the explicit input
\[
 m=32\cdot3^{45},\qquad
 B_m=(m!)^{(m!)^2},\qquad q=B_m3^{B_m+1}.                \tag{QD.1}
\]
More precisely define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 N_0&=\kappa_m(\Sigma),&P_0&=\Sigma/N_0,\\
 N_1&=N_0'N_0^3,&E_1&=\Sigma/N_1,\\
 M&=\kappa_q(K_3),&Q_1&=K_3/M,\qquad E_2=\Sigma/M.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{QD.2}
\]
Here $\kappa_s(G)$ is the intersection of the kernels of all
homomorphisms $G\to\mathfrak S_s$, exactly as in (DR.3).
There are the actual quotient maps
\[
 E_2\twoheadrightarrow E_1\twoheadrightarrow P_0
      \twoheadrightarrow\mathcal P_8,
 \qquad 1\longrightarrow Q_1\longrightarrow E_2
      \longrightarrow H=C_3^2\longrightarrow1.           \tag{QD.3}
\]
The specified outer deck subgroup $H\leq\operatorname{Out}(Q_1)$
is faithful, and its finite normalizer has the actual map
\[
 N_{\operatorname{Out}(Q_1)}(H)
          \longrightarrow\operatorname{Out}(\mathcal P_8).
                                                        \tag{QD.4}
\]
This map agrees with ordinary descent for every geometric
normalizer.  The explicit size bounds are
\[
 |P_0|\leq B_m,\qquad |E_1|\leq q,\qquad
 |Q_1|\leq(q!)^{(q!)^{10}},\qquad |E_2|=9|Q_1|.
                                                        \tag{QD.5}
\]
These are concrete bounds on the specified reconstruction;
no assertion that the groups attain the bounds is made.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
The regular representation of $\mathcal P_8$, whose order is
exactly $m$, shows $N_0\subseteq R_8$.  In particular
$N_0\subseteq K_3\cap N_8$.  All of the graph-homology and
centralizer arguments in (DR.5)--(DR.11) use only this
containment and the bound $|\mathcal P_8|\leq m$.
They apply to the exact groups (QD.2): the rank of $N_0$ is
$|P_0|+1$; its regular graph homology modulo three has faithful
deck action; and
\[
 C_{E_2}(Q_1)=Z(Q_1)\subseteq N_0/M.
                                                        \tag{QD.6}
\]
Thus the inner-action extension
$\mathcal E=E_2/Z(Q_1)\leq\operatorname{Aut}(Q_1)$ is
exactly the preimage of the specified outer $H$, and
\[
 \mathcal E/\kappa_m(\mathcal E)=P_0.                    \tag{QD.7}
\]
These identities prove the faithful deck assertion and descent
of every normalizer to an actual automorphism of $P_0$.
They also give all bounds in (QD.5) by the same literal
diagonal maps; the number of generator-image tuples is
$(m!)^2$ for $\Sigma$ and $(q!)^{10}$ for $K_3$.

It remains to prove descent to the particular quotient
$\mathcal P_8$, since a characteristic kernel in $\Sigma$
is not by itself an intrinsic characteristic kernel in $P_0$.
In $P_0$ define
\[
 J_8=\bigcap_{f:P_0\twoheadrightarrow Q_8}\ker f.
                                                        \tag{QD.8}
\]
Every displayed surjection lifts to a surjection from $\Sigma$,
and every surjection $\Sigma\to Q_8$ factors through $P_0$:
its kernel contains $N_0$, by the regular action of $Q_8$
on eight letters and fixed extra letters up to degree $m$.
The characteristic-kernel proof in A.4.12 shows that these
surjections all have the same kernel.  Consequently
\[
 J_8=N_8/N_0,
 \qquad
 R_8/N_0=
 \bigl(\gamma_3(J_8)J_8^3\bigr)
      \cap\bigl(P_0'P_0^{12}\bigr).                      \tag{QD.9}
\]
The first factor on the right is $L_8/N_0$: the projection
$N_8\to J_8$ is surjective and carries the indicated verbal
subgroup onto its corresponding verbal subgroup.  The second
factor is $(\Sigma'\Sigma^{12})/N_0$, because $N_0$ is
contained in that kernel.  Their intersection is exactly
$R_8/N_0$ by definition (QS.2).  This proves every equality in
(QD.9), including its actual quotient maps.

Precomposition permutes all surjections in (QD.8), so $J_8$
is characteristic in $P_0$.  The two factors in (QD.9) are
therefore characteristic, and so is their intersection.
Every descended automorphism of $P_0$ consequently induces
an automorphism of
$P_0/(R_8/N_0)=\mathcal P_8$.  Inner automorphisms of $Q_1$
descend to inner automorphisms of this same quotient.  This
proves (QD.4), with all maps induced by (QD.3).
The geometric compatibility follows by restricting the
original automorphism of the based extension
$K_3\leq\Sigma$ throughout this construction, as in A.4.11.

The upper extension class remains the explicit cocycle
(DR.13)--(DR.15), now for the groups (QD.2); its coefficients
are still killed in $P_0$ by (QD.6).  The theorem reconstructs
the specified deck normalizer.  The finite fusion of a
competing subgroup $H'$ into this specified $H$ is an
additional comparison, and is not inferred from this map.
\end{proof}
