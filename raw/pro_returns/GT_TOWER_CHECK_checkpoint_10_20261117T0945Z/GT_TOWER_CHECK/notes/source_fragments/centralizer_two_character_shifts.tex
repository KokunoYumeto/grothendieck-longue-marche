\subsection{Two independent descent families and the physical fixed subgroup}

Let $G=\widehat{GT}_0$, $H=\ker\lambda$, $D=D_P$, and
write $\eta=\overline d_2$ for the full continuous
character of B.2.26.  Define the actual characters
\[
 \chi_n=\Delta\circ D^n,\qquad
 \theta_n=\eta\circ D^n\qquad(n\geq0).
 \tag{B.AA1}
\]

\begin{result}{B.2.28}{Proved}{An actual double-shift quotient and its fixed-subgroup restriction}
All characters in (B.AA1) vanish on full
$\widehat{GT}$, and the map
\[
 \Xi_2:G\longrightarrow\prod_{n\geq0}\mathbb F_2^2,
       \qquad g\longmapsto(\chi_n(g),\theta_n(g))_{n\geq0}
 \tag{B.AA2}
\]
is a continuous surjective homomorphism, already
surjective on $H$.  It intertwines $D$ with the
left shift.  Its finite prefix of length $m+1$ has
the exact images
\[
 \Xi_{2,m}(G)=\Xi_{2,m}(H)=\mathbb F_2^{\,2m+2},
                   \qquad \Xi_{2,m}(\widehat{GT})=0.
 \tag{B.AA3}
\]
There are actual $e_{i,j}\in H$, for $i\geq0$
and $j\in\{0,1\}$, such that
\[
 \bigl(\chi_n(e_{i,j}),\theta_n(e_{i,j})\bigr)
                  =\delta_{ni}\,\mathbf e_j
                         \quad(n\geq0).
 \tag{B.AA4}
\]
Consequently the map
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb F_2[t]^2&\longrightarrow V,\\
 (P,Q)&\longmapsto P(D^*)\Delta+Q(D^*)\eta
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{B.AA5}
\]
is injective, for the actual quadratic annihilator
$V$ of B.2.25.  In particular the family generated
by $\Delta$ alone does not span $V$.

For the physical fixed subgroup $C_L=\operatorname{Fix}(D_L)$,
one has the full-domain inclusion
\[
             C_L\subseteq\bigcap_{n\geq0}\ker\theta_n
 \tag{B.AA6}
\]
and the exact image formula
\[
 \Xi_2(C_L)=
 \left\{\bigl((e,0),(e,0),\ldots\bigr):
                                      e\in\Delta(C_L)\right\}.
 \tag{B.AA7}
\]
The group $C_L$ has continuum index in $G$ and is
closed and nowhere dense there.  These assertions
do not assign a value to the still undetermined
subgroup $\Delta(C_L)\subseteq\mathbb F_2$.
\end{result}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual elements of B.2.27 and put
$e_{0,0}=k$, $e_{0,1}=ak$.  Their two initial
character vectors are the two standard basis
vectors, and both lie in $\ker D$.
Surjectivity of $D:H\to H$ from B.2.24 gives
\[
 (D,\Delta,\eta):H\twoheadrightarrow H\times\mathbb F_2^2.
 \tag{B.AA8}
\]
Indeed choose any preimage of a specified first
coordinate and multiply it on the right by the
appropriate powers of $e_{0,0},e_{0,1}$ to set
the other two coordinates.  These multiplications
preserve its $D$-image and change the two bits
independently.

Inductively choose $e_{i,j}$ with
$D(e_{i,j})=e_{i-1,j}$ and with both initial
bits zero, using (B.AA8).  Then
$D^{i+1}(e_{i,j})=1$, and induction gives
precisely (B.AA4), including all coordinates
after the $i$th one.  Products of these actual
elements prescribe every finite prefix of
(B.AA2), proving the first equalities in (B.AA3).
For an arbitrary infinite target sequence,
the closed subsets of the compact group $H$
prescribing its first $m+1$ coordinates are
nonempty and nested.  Their intersection is
nonempty.  This proves full surjectivity in
(B.AA2), with no compatible-choice hypothesis
left over.

The action of $D$ on (B.AA2) is the literal
left shift by its definition.  Evaluation on
$e_{i,0}$ and $e_{i,1}$ proves that every finite
linear relation among its coordinate characters
has all coefficients zero.  This proves
(B.AA5).  The full GT vanishing of $\eta$ is
B.2.26, and that of $\Delta$ is A.7.
The exact geometric descent comparison B.1.8
gives $D(\widehat{GT})\subseteq\widehat{GT}$;
therefore every iterate in (B.AA1) has the
same vanishing.  This proves the remaining
equality in (B.AA3).

We prove (B.AA6) on its full multiplier domain.
On $H$, equation (B.C24) is the literal identity
\[
 d_2(h)=\epsilon_2(W_h)+
                         \epsilon_2(W_{D_Lh}).
 \tag{B.AA9}
\]
Thus $\eta(h)=d_2(h)=0$ for
$h\in C_L\cap H$.  For any $g\in C_L$,
choose $\gamma\in G_{\mathbb Q}$ with
$\lambda(\gamma)=\lambda(g)$.
The physical descent fixes $\gamma$ pointwise,
so $h=g\gamma^{-1}$ belongs to $C_L\cap H$.
The arithmetic vanishing of $\eta$ then gives
$\eta(g)=\eta(h)+\eta(\gamma)=0$.
This also proves the exact factorization
$C_L=(C_L\cap H)G_{\mathbb Q}$ used here.

Finally the actual relation between the two
descent maps is $D=\operatorname{Ad}_c\circ D_L$,
and $D(c)=c$.  Thus, for $g\in C_L$,
\[
                    D^n(g)=c^n g c^n\qquad(n\geq0).
 \tag{B.AA10}
\]
Both $\Delta$ and $\eta$ are characters and
therefore invariant under inner conjugation.
It follows that $\theta_n(g)=\eta(g)=0$ and
$\chi_n(g)=\Delta(g)$ for all $n$.
This proves both (B.AA6) and the exact formula
(B.AA7).

The projection of (B.AA2) to its second sequence
is a surjection $G\twoheadrightarrow\prod_{n\geq0}\mathbb F_2$
and kills $C_L$.  It induces a surjection from
the actual coset set $G/C_L$ to that binary
product, so the index is at least the continuum.
The countable finite-action embedding of $G$
used in B.2.25 gives the opposite cardinal bound.
Thus the index is exactly the continuum.
The fixed subgroup of a continuous endomorphism
of the Hausdorff group $G$ is closed.
A subgroup with nonempty interior is open and
has finite index in a compact group; the index
just proved excludes that.  Hence $C_L$ is
nowhere dense.
\end{proof}

The map (B.AA5) describes a specified free submodule
of the actual annihilator.  No assertion that this
two-generator polynomial module exhausts $V$ is
made; the abstract dimension of $V$ remains the
countably infinite dimension proved in B.2.25.
