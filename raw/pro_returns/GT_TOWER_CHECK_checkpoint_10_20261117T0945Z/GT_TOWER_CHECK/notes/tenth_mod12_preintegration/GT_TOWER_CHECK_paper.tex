\begin{abstract}
We audit the claimed vanishing of the continuous character
$\Delta:\GTz\to\F_2$ on $\GT$. The ten-point centralizer
comparison uses the prime-power theorem directly; two
label comparisons in the three-division argument require
repair. We give those repairs and the ordinary tower
comparison used in the vanishing proof. An additional
integral degree-nine certificate and an exact finite-image
calculation, with a smaller degree-seven witness, prove $\GT\subsetneq\ker\Delta$.
We also prove compactness of the integral section fibres
over one fixed completed unit-equation pair, global class-two
lifting of every ordinary pair, and an exact continuous
class-three obstruction test. Explicit recursive prime
choices with the original factorial exponents have their
first unrestricted integral obstruction in class three.
Such failures contain an open dense subset of the original
prime-choice space, and finite prefixes can force the failure.
We also reconstruct the variable-exponent arithmetic input
and certify actual full integral survival at every fixed
nilpotent depth, retaining any specified finite Magnus congruence.
The physical modular descent fixes full GT, and every
marked genus-zero prime-power quotient has the original
GT parameter. Exact canonical central-cover computations,
together with full positive lifting, prove that the literal
descent is surjective. Every iterated pullback of $\Delta$
is independent of all the others, and the full space of
continuous quadratic characters vanishing on GT has dimension
$\aleph_0$. A further harmonic character detects an actual
element in the descent kernel and proves that the iterates
of $\Delta$ do not span this space. Two independent
character families give an actual quotient onto the shift
on pairs of bits. Further prime-indexed source factors
give countably many independent descent generators and
prove that the full character module is not finitely
generated under descent. We also construct one finite ten-point
quotient retaining all 630 coloured four-point maps, and
an explicit refinement recognizing the image of its
geometric Klein-four centralizer. The elliptic
target also gives point-order tests at every support prime;
an explicit original-factorial family passes all those tests.
The full
task remains incomplete: the finite pentagon census,
the remaining subgroup and finite-image decisions, and
the finite-support assertion for all-depth ordinary
section ghosts have unresolved parts explicitly recorded
in the numbered statements and report. No priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
