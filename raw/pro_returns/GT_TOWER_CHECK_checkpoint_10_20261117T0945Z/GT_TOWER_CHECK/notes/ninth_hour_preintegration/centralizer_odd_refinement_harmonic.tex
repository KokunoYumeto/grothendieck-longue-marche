\subsection{A harmonic identity record through every odd refinement of the full source}

Retain the marked ordinary source $B_0=C_2*C_3$, its
pure subgroup $F_0$, and the actual finite source
$q_D:B_0\twoheadrightarrow D$ of B.2.8.  Write
$K_D=\ker q_D$.  This source has
$D^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_6$, pure abelianization $C_{12}^2$
with the two named generators, and $x_D,y_D$ of
order twelve.  Let $b^c\in D^c$ be the actual pure
$s$-centralizer element of B.2.26, and let $b_D$ be
its image in $D$.

Define the following family of actual source kernels
and its profinite intersection:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal N_D
   &=\{J\triangleleft B_0:J\subset K_D,
          \ [B_0:J]<\infty,\ [K_D:J]\text{ is odd}\},\\
 R_D^{\mathrm{odd}}
   &=\bigcap_{J\in\mathcal N_D}\overline J
                         \quad\subset\widehat B_0.
 \end{split}                                             \tag{HOR.1}
\]
Every map in this definition is the quotient map of
the same marked $B_0$.  The family is closed under
finite intersections: the kernel of
$B_0/(J_1\cap J_2)\to D$ embeds in the product of
the two odd kernels.

\begin{result}{A.4.50}{Proved}{A simultaneous full harmonic survivor in the odd refinement family}
There is an actual full $g=(1,f_g)\in H$ with
\[
 \Delta(g)=\overline d_2(g)=1,
             \qquad f_g\in R_D^{\mathrm{odd}}.
 \tag{HOR.2}
\]
At every single quotient $E=B_0/J$ in (HOR.1), an
element realizing (HOR.2) at that quotient is supplied
by the identity automorphism of a full canonical
central cover, retaining cubic frame one and a pure
harmonic frame over $b_D$.  Thus (HOR.2) is a
simultaneous assertion about genuine normalized
profinite parameters.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
Fix $J\in\mathcal N_D$ and $E=B_0/J$.
Let $n$ be the order of $x_E$.  Since $y=sxs$, it
is also the order of $y_E$.  The map to $D$ and its
odd kernel give
\[
                      n=12m\quad\text{for an odd }m.
 \tag{HOR.3}
\]
Indeed the cyclic subgroup generated by $x_E^{12}$
lies in that odd kernel, whereas its image in $D$
has exact order twelve.

Adjoin the actual abelian source quotient by putting
\[
 J^+=J\cap(F_0'F_0^n),\qquad
 E^+=B_0/J^+,\qquad P^+=F_0/J^+.
 \tag{HOR.4}
\]
Here $F_0'F_0^n$ is characteristic in the normal
subgroup $F_0$, hence normal in $B_0$.
The kernel of $E^+\to E$ is the image of $J$ in
$F_0/(F_0'F_0^n)=C_n^2$.
Since $D$ retains the specified $C_{12}^2$
abelianization, every element of $J$ has both
exponents divisible by twelve.  Its image in
$C_n^2$ lies in $(12\mathbb Z/n)^2\cong C_m^2$;
it has exponent dividing $m$ and order dividing
the odd integer $m^2$.  Thus $E^+\to D$ still has odd kernel.
The two generators in $P^+$ have exact order $n$:
their orders divide $n$ in both factors of the
join, and the added abelian factor has that exact
order.  The same abelian factor proves
\[
               (P^+)^{\mathrm{ab}}
                       =C_n\overline x\oplus C_n\overline y.
 \tag{HOR.5}
\]

Apply the actual canonical-cover comparison of
(HTS.6)--(HTS.14), replacing $Q_B$ by $D$ and $E$
by $E^+$.  All its hypotheses are present as exact
source maps: $J^+\subset K_D\subset B_0'$,
the kernel of $E^+\to D$ is odd, and $b^c$
already centralizes $s$ in the full canonical
cover of $D$.  Explicitly, the induced map
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (E^+)^c=B_0/[B_0,J^+]
      \longrightarrow D^c=B_0/[B_0,K_D],\\
 H_2(E^+,\mathbb Z)_{(2)}
          \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H_2(D,\mathbb Z)_{(2)}
 \end{gathered}                                        \tag{HOR.6}
\]
is induced by the same quotient of the two named
generators.  Sylow two-subgroup conjugacy first
lifts $b_D$ to a pure $\beta\in C_{E^+}(s)$.
For a pure lift $\widetilde\beta$ to $(E^+)^c$,
the central commutator $[\widetilde\beta,s]$ has
order dividing two, and its image in $D^c$ is one.
The second line of (HOR.6) forces that commutator
to be one.  This proves the required full-cover
harmonic frame.

The identity automorphism of $(E^+)^c$, with this
$s$-frame, cubic frame one, and cusp frame one,
now gives an actual positive $\tau$-fixed
automorphism by B.2.7--B.2.9.  Before normalization
it has $f_{P^+}=1$.  Consequently its abelian
normalizing exponent $t$ is in
$n\widehat{\mathbb Z}$, by (HOR.5).
The exact correction
\[
       \Phi_0=\operatorname{Inn}(x^t)\Phi,
       \qquad f_0=y^t f x^{-t}
 \tag{HOR.7}
\]
preserves every $E^+$-value and gives an actual
normalized gentle element.  Its record in $D$
is exactly the identity pair with harmonic frame
$b_D$ and cubic frame one.  The signed covariance
and affine calculation (B.Y9)--(B.Y10) and the
paragraph following them give
$\Delta=\overline d_2=1$ for this same element.
In particular $f_0$ is one in $E$.

For the simultaneous statement, the multiplier-one
group $H$ is compact.  The two characters are
continuous, and reduction of the normalized
parameter $f_g$ to every finite source is
continuous.  Thus
\[
 \{g\in H:\Delta(g)=\overline d_2(g)=1,
                         \ (f_g)_{B_0/J}=1\}
 \tag{HOR.8}
\]
is closed for every $J\in\mathcal N_D$.
The preceding construction applied to the finite
intersection of any chosen source kernels proves
the finite-intersection property for these sets.
Their intersection is nonempty by compactness,
which is exactly (HOR.2).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The actual joint cap and ternary five-point source.}
Let $E_3=B_0/J_0$ be the eight-map source of
A.4.48.  Let $C$ be the actual capped group of order
$216$, with its two literal evaluations
\[
 \eta_1:(x,y)\longmapsto(D,V^2),\qquad
 \eta_2:(x,y)\longmapsto(V,D^2),
 \tag{HOR.9}
\]
and retain all six deck translates of both maps.
Also retain all six deck translates of the actual
Schreier quotient
$F_0/(J_x'J_x^3)$, where $J_x$ is parity of $x$.
Let $E_*$ be the image of $B_0$ in the joint
source defined by $D$, $E_3$, and these finite
evaluation cores.  This definition means the
intersection of their named $B_0$-normal source
kernels, so all its coordinate maps are fixed.

The map $E_*\to D$ has odd kernel.  For the $E_3$
coordinate this follows from
$E_3\to Q_B$ with odd kernel and the retained
map $D\to Q_B$.  For a cap coordinate, the
derived subgroup $C'$ has order $27$, and
$C/C'$ has exponent dividing four, as certified
by the complete capped multiplication table.
Every element of $K_D\cap F_0$ has both abelian
exponents divisible by twelve; the same holds
after every source deck conjugation.  Hence its
value in every cap coordinate lies in the odd
group $C'$.  In a Schreier coordinate the
remaining kernel over the retained parity
quotient is $J_x/(J_x'J_x^3)=C_3^3$, again odd.
The kernel of the joint map embeds in the product
of these odd groups, which proves the claim.

The source generators have exact order thirty-six
in $E_*$.  They have order twelve in $D$, order
eighteen in $E_3$, order dividing twelve in every
cap coordinate, and order dividing six in every
Schreier coordinate.  The first two coordinates
give the lower bound $\operatorname{lcm}(12,18)=36$.
Their abelian quotients are the specified
$C_{12}^2$ and $C_{18}^2$, whose marked joint image
is $C_{36}^2$.  Since the two source generators
have order thirty-six, the pure abelianization
of $E_*$ is exactly $C_{36}^2$.

Applying the preceding proof to this actual source,
or reducing the simultaneous element in (HOR.2),
gives one genuine normalized $g$ satisfying
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \Delta(g)=\overline d_2(g)=1,\qquad
 (f_g)_{E_*}=1,\\
 \iota_i(f_g)=1\quad\text{in }\Gamma_3
                              \quad(1\leq i\leq8),\\
 f_g(D,V^2)=f_g(V,D^2)=1\quad\text{in }C,
              \qquad c_2(f_g)=0\pmod3.
 \end{gathered}                                        \tag{HOR.10}
\]
In particular both the full five-point pentagon
at $\Gamma_3$ and the cap-$216$ IV defect at
(HOR.9) are one for this element.  This conclusion
uses the actual common source and its full
canonical central-cover lift.
