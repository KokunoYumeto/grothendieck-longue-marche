# Source-sign audit: the inspected 2003 printing

We opened the author-hosted 2003 printing, Mathematical Research Letters 10, pp. 97–108, at https://www.math.nagoya-u.ac.jp/~furusho/paper/GTA-mrl.pdf. The actual rendered printed pages 102 and 105 were inspected. In the following record Ψ means the author’s Ψ₂^(0).


No arithmetic Kummer sign is needed in (A.K1)--(A.K3).
The author-hosted rendered Furusho paper defines
$f_+(xyx^{-1},x^2,y)=y^{-2\Psi}f(x,y)$ in equation (1), p.~102.
Consequently its two substitutions in Lemma 4.1 give the product
$y^{-4\Psi}f(x,y^2)x^{-4\Psi}f(y,x^2)$.
The first display of that lemma, p.~105, instead prints positive
$4\Psi$ exponents.  This records the exact mismatch in that indicated
derivation; we do not use it to refute the theorem stated there.
Our proof below uses (A.J11), (A.K2), and the actual quotient maps,
so it does not require either printed sign identification or the
Anderson duplication theorem.
