\subsection{The full profinite distribution theorem with its exact map}

For $N\geq1$, put
\[
 H_N=\ker\bigl(\widehat F(x,y)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/N,
                  \ x\mapsto1,\ y\mapsto0\bigr).
\]
The full induced topology on this open subgroup identifies it with the
free profinite group on
\[
 X=x^N,\qquad y_a=x^a yx^{-a}\quad(0\leq a<N).
\]
Define the actual continuous surjection and character
\[
 \delta_N:H_N\twoheadrightarrow\widehat F(x,y),\qquad
 X\mapsto x,\quad y_0\mapsto y,\quad y_a\mapsto1\ (a>0),
 \qquad c_N=\operatorname{exp}_y\circ\delta_N.       \tag{A.K1}
\]
These are the covering and opening maps for $t\mapsto t^N$ and
$t\mapsto t$ on $\mathbb P^1\setminus\{0,\mu_N,\infty\}$,
with the positive Schreier marking displayed above.  The alternative
negative Schreier generators satisfy
$x^{-a}yx^a=X^{-1}y_{N-a}X$ for $0<a<N$; hence the map which kills
all of them except $y$ is literally the same map, not just a map with
an isomorphic source.

\begin{result}{A.5.3}{Proved}{Full profinite Kummer distribution}
Every $(\lambda,f)\in\widehat{GT}$ satisfies
\[
                 \delta_N(f)=y^{c_N(f)}f.               \tag{A.K2}
\]
In particular the normalized Kummer descendant
\[
 D_N(\lambda,f)=\bigl(\lambda,y^{-c_N(f)}\delta_N(f)\bigr)
                                                               \tag{A.K3}
\]
is the same full GT element.  For the multiplier-one elements in
(A.J3), $c_2(f)=\rho$, with exactly the modular normalization used
there.  Moreover $c_{MN}(f)=c_M(f)+c_N(f)$.
\end{result}
\begin{proof}
Equation (A.K2), with precisely (A.K1), is Enriquez's full profinite
Theorem 8.1 and equation (50), not a statement only about a
prounipotent or pro-prime image
\cite[\S8, Theorem 8.1, pp.~30--32 of the arXiv version]{CA_Enriquez2007}.
Its source is the finite-index pure braid subgroup associated with
$\{(z_i):z_i\ne0,\ z_i\ne\zeta z_j\}$; the power covering and the
open inclusion define the two braid homomorphisms.  The modified
strand-doubling maps of Lemmas 8.2--8.5 carry the pentagon into that
subgroup.  Applying the opening map and erasing the zeroth strand
gives $\delta_N(f)=y^{c_N(f)}f$.  Thus all the maps occur before
completion, and the finite-index topology supplies their full
profinite versions.  The formula for $D_N$ follows directly.  Its
conjugating word is normalized: the abelianization of $\delta_N(f)$
is $(0,c_N(f))$.  Finally
$\delta_{MN}=\delta_M\delta_N$ on $H_{MN}$, as is checked on its
displayed Schreier generators.  Applying (A.K2) twice and using the
infinite profinite order of $y$ gives the asserted additivity.
\end{proof}
