\subsection{A positive lift to the universal six-coordinate quotient}

Put $K=\bigcap_b N_b$, with the six actual kernels in (B.R1),
and put $U=B/K$.  The set of six kernels is permuted by
conjugation in $B$, so $K$ is normal in $B$.  The actual
pure subgroup of $U$ is $F/K=J\subseteq B^6$, and
$U/J=S_3$.  By (B.R7), the subgroup $L_0^6\subseteq J$
is normal in $U$.  Write
\[
 E=U/L_0^6,\qquad |E|=6|J_{\mathcal T}|=11943936.                \tag{B.R14}
\]
This is the actual induced quotient obtained from the six
maps $F\to\mathcal T$ and their source $S_3$ permutation
action.  Its distinguished $s,r$ retain their original
orders two and three.

\begin{result}{B.2.13}{Proved}{Every normalized target lifts positively to $U$}
For every $g\in H$, choose $a,l$ and the full tuple $w\in J'$
supplied by B.2.12.  Then
\[
 \alpha_U(s)=sw,\qquad\alpha_U(r)=w^{-1}r                     \tag{B.R15}
\]
defines an actual continuous automorphism of $U$.  It fixes
$\tau$, and there are actual pure elements $\beta_U,A_U\in J$
such that
\[
 \alpha_U(s)=\beta_U s\beta_U^{-1},\qquad
 \alpha_U(r)=A_UrA_U^{-1}.                                    \tag{B.R16}
\]
Under the first-coordinate quotient $\varphi:J\twoheadrightarrow
B$, this action descends to
$\operatorname{Inn}(\beta_g^{-1})\alpha_g$.  Thus its
descended outer class is the specified full target $g$.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
Use $C=x^{-a}\beta_g$ and $J_0=\beta_g^{-1}A_g r^l$, and
write the six coordinates of $w$ as in (B.R12).  The target
product relation gives the literal identity
\[
                     C s J_0 r J_0^{-1}C^{-1}=\tau.            \tag{B.R17}
\]
Conjugation by the source $r$ cyclically permutes the
coordinate indices as $(1,3,4)(2,5,6)$.  Conjugation by
the source $s$ changes a tuple $(w_1,\ldots,w_6)$ to
\[
 (w_2,w_1,\tau^{-1}w_6\tau,
                  sw_5s,sw_4s,\tau w_3\tau^{-1}).             \tag{B.R18}
\]
These are exact Schreier formulas for the transversal
$1,s,r,r^2,sr,sr^2$.  Its four nontrivial $s$-weights are
$y^{-1},x,x^{-1},y$, and their target values are
$\tau^{-1},s,s,\tau$.

Substitution in (B.R18) gives
$sws=w^{-1}$.  In the first cubic orbit,
$w_4w_3w_1=J_0(J_0^{-1}C^{-1})C=1$.
In the second cubic orbit,
\[
 w_6w_5w_2
    =\tau C J_0r^{-1}J_0^{-1}sC^{-1}=1
\]
by (B.R17).  The cyclic rotations give the other four
coordinate equations.  Hence both source relations in
(B.R15) hold: $(sw)^2=(w^{-1}r)^3=1$.  Their product is
$\tau$.  They define a continuous homomorphism from $B$
to $U$.

Let $\psi=\operatorname{Inn}(\beta_g^{-1})\alpha_g$.
It is an actual target automorphism, with
\[
 \psi(s)=s,\quad\psi(\tau)=C^{-1}\tau C,\quad
 \psi(r)=J_0rJ_0^{-1}.
\]
On the pure subgroup the proposed map fixes $x$ and sends
$y$ to $w^{-1}yw$.  Using the actual coordinate pairs
(B.R10), direct substitution gives, for $v=x,y$,
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \varphi_b(\alpha_U(v))
      =c_b\,\psi(\varphi_b(v))\,c_b^{-1},\\
 (c_b)_b=(1,C,C,J_0^{-1},1,sJ_0^{-1}s).
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{B.R19}
\]
For the last $y$-coordinate both sides reduce, using
(B.R17), to $\tau J_0^{-1}sJ_0s$; the other eleven
identities follow immediately from their displayed
generator values.  Thus the source map kills $K$ and
defines an endomorphism of $U$.  Its restriction to $J$
is the restriction of the product of the six actual
automorphisms $\operatorname{Inn}(c_b)\psi$ of $B$.
Since $J$ has finite index in $B^6$, this product takes
$J$ onto $J$: it takes it into $J$, and an automorphism
preserves its finite index.  The quotient $U/J=S_3$ is
fixed because $w\in J$.  These two statements make the
endomorphism of $U$ bijective.  The first coordinate of
(B.R19) proves the asserted literal descent to $\psi$.

It remains to verify the positive frames in (B.R16).
The exact script \path{check_universal_positive_source.g}
constructs the actual group $E$ in (B.R14), using the
same Schreier weights as (B.R18).  It checks all $24$
selected target-frame tuples from B.2.12.  Each tuple
belongs to the actual pure derived subgroup of $E$;
each satisfies both cycles, gives a bijective finite
automorphism, and has both positive torsion conjugacy
classes.  The output retains all $24$ rows.  The finite
conjugators can be made pure: their $S_3$ residues
centralize the respective torsion element and therefore
belong to $\langle s\rangle$ or $\langle r\rangle$.
Multiplication on the right by the corresponding power
of that same element removes the residue without changing
the conjugation.

Choose a lift in $J$ of the finite pure $s$-conjugator.
After this conjugation, $\alpha_U(s)$ lies in the coset
$L_0^6s$ and still has order two.  Conjugation by $s$ on
$L_0^6$ permutes its six factors in three pairs, with
isomorphisms between the factors.  On each pair the two
isomorphisms compose to the identity because $s^2=1$.
Every order-two element of this coset is conjugate to
$s$ by $L_0^6$: set one coordinate of a conjugator to
one and solve the other coordinate; the order-two
relation is exactly the equation closing that pair.
This constructs the required actual profinite conjugator,
without a further inverse-limit choice.  The same
argument for $r$ uses its two three-cycles of factors:
set one coordinate to one, solve successively for the
other two, and use the order-three relation to close
the cycle.  Thus each finite positive frame lifts to
an actual pure frame in $U$, proving (B.R16).
\end{proof}

The quotient $U$ is infinite.  Any full lift preserving
$K$ induces a positive automorphism of the actual central
cover $U^{\mathrm c}=B/\overline{[B,K]}$, so this supplies
a further necessary lifting problem.  The proved exact
full-image criterion remains the entire family of finite
canonical covers in (B.Q7)--(B.Q8).  The present result
does not verify that family of positive lifts, and does
not assert surjectivity of $D_P$.
