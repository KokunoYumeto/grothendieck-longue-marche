\subsection{Every compact-passing original family reaches the affine abelian quotient}
\label{sec:ghost-boundary-local-norm}

\begin{result}{C.3.10}{Proved}{The full compact-passing family survives the affine-cover abelian quotient}
\label{prop:ghost-all-passing-genjac}
For each boundary field \(K_i\), the algebra
\(K_i\otimes\mathbb Q_3\) is a field of degree nine
and its actual local norm is surjective onto
\(\mathbb Q_3^\times\).
Every output of the original A.44 recursion satisfying
all the finite elliptic order tests (16) admits a full
profinite section of
\(\pi_1(U)/\overline{[K,K]}\), factoring through the
generalized-Jacobian quotient of \(Y\) and having proper
Tate-module class zero. In particular this conclusion
holds for every original family constructed in
Proposition~\ref{prop:ghost-elliptic-passing}; no
additional congruence at three is needed.
\end{result}
\begin{proof}
The classes of \(3,-2\) are independent in
\(\mathbb Q_3^\times/(\mathbb Q_3^\times)^3\).
Valuation first detects the exponent of three, and
\(-2\equiv7\pmod9\) is not a cube of a three-adic
unit, whose cube is \(1\) or \(-1\) modulo nine.
Restriction of rational local cube classes to
\(F=\mathbb Q_3(\mu_3)\) is injective: a class killed
there has its square killed by the norm, and
multiplication by two is invertible on the cube-class
group. The same holds for the independent pairs
\((-2,3)\) and \((1/3,2/3)\).

Apply the actual boundary Galois-set identification
of Proposition~\ref{prop:ghost-boundary-hasse-norm}
over this local field. The abelian image over \(F\)
is all of \(\mathbb F_3^2\), so commutators give all
of \(H_3\); the cyclotomic character adds the
involution. Thus the decomposition group at three
of each boundary normal closure is the whole
\(G=H_3\rtimes C_2\). Its action on the nine cosets
is transitive, proving that \(K_i\otimes\mathbb Q_3\)
is a field, with stabilizer \(H\simeq S_3\).

Let \(X_i\) be the norm-one character module (38).
The commutator subgroup of \(G\) is \(H_3\):
commuting either abelian generator with the
involution gives a nonzero multiple of that
generator, and their commutator then gives the
centre. Hence \(G^{\mathrm{ab}}=C_2\), and
restriction to \(H\) identifies the two sign
character groups. The exact character sequence
therefore gives
\[
 H^1(G,X_i)=
 \ker\bigl(H^2(G,\mathbb Z)
       \longrightarrow H^2(H,\mathbb Z)\bigr)=0.
                                                        \tag{44}
\]
Here Shapiro makes \(H^1(G,\mathbb Z[G/H])=0\).
Inflation identifies this finite-group cohomology
with \(H^1(\mathbb Q_3,X_i)\): over the splitting
field the action is trivial, and a continuous
homomorphism from its compact absolute Galois
group to the torsion-free discrete lattice is zero.
Local torus duality
\cite[Corollary I.2.4, printed pages 28--29]{GhostMilneADT}
identifies the dual of (44) with
\[
 H^1(\mathbb Q_3,R^1_{K_i\otimes\mathbb Q_3/\mathbb Q_3}
             \mathbb G_m)
 =\mathbb Q_3^\times/
        N(K_i\otimes\mathbb Q_3)^\times.
\]
The latter identity is the actual norm exact
sequence and Hilbert theorem ninety. This proves
surjectivity of the local norm.

Now take any original A.44 output satisfying (16).
At \(q_j\), the point \(-P_0-T\) has prime-to-three
order in \(E'(\mathbb F_{q_j})\), hence is divisible
by three there. Formula (41) and etale Hensel lifting
give a rational local point of \(D_0\), so the norm
from \(K_0\otimes\mathbb Q_{q_j}\) is surjective.
The identical argument for \(P_0-T\) works at
\(r_j\) with \(K_1\). At every other prime greater
than three these rational primes are unit norms
from the unramified boundary algebras. At two they
are ninth powers of odd units, and at the real place
the odd-degree algebra has a real factor. At three
use the surjectivity just proved. Thus each \(q_j\)
and \(r_j\) is an actual everywhere local norm from
the corresponding boundary field, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:ghost-boundary-hasse-norm}
supplies the global norm preimages (42).

The products (43) now converge with the original
factorial exponents, without any further choice
of congruences. The exact image formula (27) with
\(a=0\), together with the block-constant
construction at every coefficient prime other
than three, gives the claimed full profinite
section exactly as in
Proposition~\ref{prop:ghost-original-genjac-survival}.
All of its proper coordinates are zero.
\end{proof}

The precise remaining map is
\(\pi_1(Y)\to\pi_1(Y)/\overline{[K,K]}\).
The proposition constructs its target sections
for the whole stated family. Their lifts through
the displayed commutator kernel, and the integral
class-three obstructions of the original free
geometric group, remain to be calculated.
