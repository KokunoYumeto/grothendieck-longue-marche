\subsection{The complete pure peripheral image at the first cubic sphere level}

\begin{result}{A.4.30}{Proved}{The actual class-three pure ten-point sphere image has order $3^{119}$}
Put
\[
 F=F(x_1,\ldots,x_9),\qquad
 x_{10}=(x_1\cdots x_9)^{-1},\qquad
 Q_{10}=F/\bigl(\gamma_4F\,(\gamma_2F)^3F^9\bigr).
                                                               \tag{SP3.1}
\]
This is the literal ten-punctured-sphere group with the displayed
positive peripheral marking, followed by the stated verbal quotient.
Its order is $3^{294}$.  Let $\mathcal A_{10}$ be the subgroup of
$\operatorname{Out}(Q_{10})$ represented by automorphisms carrying
each $x_i$, for $1\leq i\leq10$, to a conjugate of itself.  Then
\[
 \mathcal A_{10}
   =\operatorname{im}\bigl(\Gamma_{0,10}
                       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Out}(Q_{10})\bigr),
 \qquad |\mathcal A_{10}|=3^{35+84}=3^{119}.             \tag{SP3.2}
\]
The map is the actual Artin surface action followed by the quotient
in (SP3.1).  On the further quotient
\[
 Q_{10,2}=F/(\gamma_3F\,F^3)
                                                               \tag{SP3.3}
\]
its image has dimension $35$ over $\mathbb F_3$.  The kernel of
$\mathcal A_{10}\to\operatorname{Out}(Q_{10,2})$ is elementary
abelian of dimension $84$, generated by the actual words
\[
 [A_{ij},A_{jk}],\qquad 1\leq i<j<k\leq9,\qquad
 A_{ij}=\sigma_{j-1}\cdots\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i^2
                    \sigma_{i+1}^{-1}\cdots\sigma_{j-1}^{-1}.
                                                               \tag{SP3.4}
\]
Here $\sigma_i$ acts on $F$ by
$x_i\mapsto x_ix_{i+1}x_i^{-1}$,
$x_{i+1}\mapsto x_i$, fixing the other generators.
The assertion is also the exact common-power-one-modulo-nine
peripheral assertion, because every $x_i$ has order nine.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The finite group and its weight coordinates.}\quad
Use the integral Hall basis of the free nilpotent group of class
three on the nine named generators.  There are nine generators
of weight one, $\binom92=36$ of weight two, and
$(9^3-9)/3=240$ of weight three.  In (SP3.1) their respective
exponents can be taken modulo $9,3,3$.  These coordinates are
independent.  Indeed Hall collection with these moduli defines
a group: the commutator relations for ninth powers have
coefficients $9$, $\binom92=36$, and $\binom93=84$, all
zero modulo three in the commutator coordinates.  Third powers
of weight-two elements commute with every generator, since the
weight-three coordinates have exponent three.  Thus the stated
power relations introduce no additional weight-three relation.
Conversely these Hall coordinates satisfy the defining verbal
relations: every element has ninth power one and every
commutator has third power one.  This gives both the collecting
upper bound and the group attaining it.  Hence
$|Q_{10}|=9^9 3^{36+240}=3^{294}$.
In particular each $x_i^3$ is central: the class-three collection
formula for $[x_i^3,x_j]$ has the coefficients $3$ and
$\binom32=3$ in the weight-two and weight-three factors.

Let $V=\mathbb F_3^9$ with its ordered basis $e_i$, and put
$S=\sum_i e_i$.  The weight-two and weight-three layers of
$Q_{10}$ are the ordinary free Lie spaces $L_2(V)$ and $L_3(V)$,
with dimensions $36$ and $240$.  They are realized inside the
associative tensor spaces by the bracket $[u,v]=uv-vu$.
This assertion uses integral Hall collection and reduction
modulo three; no Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff denominator is used.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The complete first-degree bound and its actual image.}\quad
At (SP3.3), a pure peripheral map has the form
\[
 e_i\longmapsto e_i+D_i,\qquad D_i=[v_i,e_i],
 \qquad \sum_iD_i\in[V,S].                              \tag{SP3.5}
\]
The last condition is precisely the preservation of the tenth
peripheral conjugacy class.  Each $[V,e_i]$ has dimension eight,
so the individual conjugators supply $72$ parameters.  Their
sum map surjects onto $L_2(V)$, of dimension $36$; the allowed
last-peripheral subspace $[V,S]$ has dimension eight.
Consequently the solution space in (SP3.5) has dimension
$72-(36-8)=44$.  Inner automorphisms give the injective
nine-dimensional subspace $\operatorname{ad}(V)$, leaving the
upper bound $35$ in the outer group.

For the actual word $A_{ij}$ in (SP3.4), direct substitution in
the displayed Artin action gives
\[
 D^{ij}_i=-[e_i,e_j],\qquad D^{ij}_j=[e_i,e_j],
 \qquad D^{ij}_k=0\quad(k\notin\{i,j\}).                \tag{SP3.6}
\]
The thirty-six rows (SP3.6) have rank $36$, and their span
with the nine inner rows has rank $44$.  These are exact ranks
over $\mathbb F_3$ checked in the retained certificate.  Thus
actual pure braids attain the complete outer space of (SP3.5).
The full twist acts by conjugation by $x_1\cdots x_9$, so
these outer actions factor through the actual pure sphere
mapping class group $\Gamma_{0,10}$.

\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{The complete cubic kernel.}\quad
Take a peripheral automorphism whose outer action at (SP3.3)
is trivial.  Lift the corresponding inner correction to
$Q_{10}$; after applying it, its action at (SP3.3) is the
identity.  Write its image of $x_i$ as $g_ix_ig_i^{-1}$.
The linear coordinate of $g_i$ modulo three is a multiple of
$e_i$.  Multiplication of $g_i$ by a power of $x_i$ removes
that multiple without changing its conjugation of $x_i$.
All remaining linear coordinates are divisible by three and
can be removed using the central cubes just proved.
Consequently the remaining conjugator is in $\gamma_2Q_{10}$,
and its effective parameter is an element $Y_i\in L_2(V)$.
The resulting degree-two derivation has
\[
 Z_i=[Y_i,e_i]\in L_3(V),\qquad
             \sum_i Z_i\in[L_2(V),S].                 \tag{SP3.7}
\]
The same argument applied to the tenth peripheral proves
exactly the final condition: its conjugator's linear part is
a multiple of $S$, which can be removed by a power of that
peripheral, followed by central cubes.  There is no independent
cube coordinate in (SP3.7), since conjugation contributes only
commutators.

For each $i$, the map $L_2(V)\to L_3(V)$,
$Y\mapsto[Y,e_i]$, is injective.  The direct sum of these nine
maps therefore has dimension $324$.  Their sum has image all
of $L_3(V)$, of dimension $240$, and
$[L_2(V),S]$ has dimension $36$.  Thus (SP3.7) has dimension
\[
             324-(240-36)=120.                         \tag{SP3.8}
\]
These dimension assertions can either be read in the free Lie
basis or checked in the explicit tensor matrices retained by
the certificate.  The inner degree-two maps
$Z_i=[Y,e_i]$ for one common $Y$ form an injective
$36$-dimensional subspace.  The outer upper bound is therefore
$120-36=84$.  Such kernel maps fix the lower layers and add
central weight-three coordinates, so they compose by addition
and have exponent three.

There is no unresolved nonlinear term in identifying the
actual words (SP3.4) with this kernel.  On the filtered tensor
algebra through degree three, write two actual IA substitutions
as $1+D+E$ and $1+D'+E'$, where $D,D'$ raise degree by one
and $E,E'$ raise degree by two.  Direct multiplication gives
\[
 (1+D+E)(1+D'+E')(1+D+E)^{-1}(1+D'+E')^{-1}
        =1+DD'-D'D                                      \tag{SP3.9}
\]
through degree increase two: the terms $E,E'$ cancel, and
$(1+D+E)^{-1}=1-D+(D^2-E)$ at this degree.  This calculation
uses only addition and multiplication over $\mathbb F_3$.
Thus the word $[A_{ij},A_{jk}]$ has exactly the cubic
parameter $[D^{ij},D^{jk}]$.

The eighty-four resulting rows have rank $84$; adjoining the
$36$ inner rows gives rank $120$.  Every row is tangential
as in (SP3.7), and its sum is zero, because the original Artin
words fix the boundary product.  Hence actual geometric words
attain the entire cubic outer kernel.  Combined with the
already complete first-degree image, this proves equality
(SP3.2) and its order.

The executed certificate

It independently expands every one of those literal

checks (SP3.9) entry by entry.  All ranks use Gaussian
elimination over $\mathbb F_3$ in the $81$ and $729$
associative tensor coordinates.  The JSON, NPZ matrices and
text output are retained beside the source.  The earlier
checkpoint source is retained as
\path{sphere10_class3_peripheral_cp06.py}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The precise obstruction to a primewise product for the coloured mixed target.}
There is an exact source-map obstruction to recovering the
order-$216$ target of A.4.28 solely from separate nilpotent
pure images of the ten-point sphere.  Retain the actual
centralizer $N=C_G(H)$, its deck subgroup $H$, and the
isomorphism $N/H\cong F(v,d)$ of (A.T12).
The image of $N$ in the permutation centralizer has order
sixteen, while $H$ has order four and intersects the pure
subgroup trivially.  The fixed-pair discriminants in A.1 show
that the remaining label quotient is $C_2^2$.  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(N\cap\Gamma_{0,10}\to N/H\bigr)
                  =F(v,d)'F(v,d)^2.                    \tag{SP3.10}
\]
Indeed the quotient map from the free rank-two group onto
this $C_2^2$ is its full abelianization modulo two.
The actual surjection $q_b:F(v,d)\to R_0$ in (A.T20) sends
this subgroup onto $R_0'R_0^2$.

In the actual capped target $C_q$ of order $216$, the image
of $R_0'$ is the full Heisenberg group $N_3$ of order
$27$, by A.4.28.  The square subgroup also contains the
image $D_{\rm cap}^6$; this element has order two, and its
conjugation on $N_3/Z(N_3)$ is $-I$.  This follows from
(LI.5), since the action matrix has order four, and the
central capping does not change the injected $N_3$ action.
Consequently the image of (SP3.10) in $C_q$ has
noncommuting elements of coprime orders and is not nilpotent.
In contrast, the image of the pure sphere group in a direct
product of finite nilpotent pure images is nilpotent, as are
all of its subgroups and quotient groups.  A homomorphism
factoring the specific map (SP3.10) through such a product
would make its just-computed image nilpotent, a contradiction.
This proves the nonfactorization for that exact source map.
In particular, joining (SP3.1) with an independent finite
two-group pure image does not retain the full capped mixed
target by this route.  The outstanding finite construction
must retain the nontrivial binary action on the ternary
layer in one actual source quotient.
