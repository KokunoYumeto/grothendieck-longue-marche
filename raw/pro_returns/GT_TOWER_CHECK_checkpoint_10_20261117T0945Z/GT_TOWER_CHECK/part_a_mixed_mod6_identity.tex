\subsection{The exact identity fibre at the mixed modulus-six five-point level}

Retain the marked groups $B_0,F_0,Q_B,K$ and the
split fifth insertion $F_0\to\Gamma_{0,5}$ of
A.4.45.  In particular $|Q_B|=576$, $K$ is free
of rank $97$, and $K\subset F_0$ has index $96$.
Let $N$ be the kernel of the signed order-$192$
five-point quotient, as in (HTS.2).  Put
\[
 \Gamma_6=\Gamma_{0,5}/(N'N^6),\qquad
 B_p=B_0/(K'K^p)\quad(p=2,3).
 \tag{MIF.1}
\]
All these are the named ordinary finite quotients
and hence also quotients of their profinite
completions.

\begin{result}{A.4.51}{Proved}{No parity-one element in the actual identity fibre at modulus six}
For every actual multiplier-one gentle element
$g=(1,f_g)\in H$, the following implication holds
through the literal fifth-insertion morphism:
\[
 \iota_5(f_g)=1\text{ in }\Gamma_6
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \theta_{12}(\beta_g)=\theta_{12}(A_g)=I,
 \quad V_g=T_g=W_g=I,
 \quad \Delta(g)=0.
 \tag{MIF.2}
\]
Thus the identity fibre of the normalized parameter
at this specific five-point quotient contains no
actual parity-one gentle element.  The conclusion
does not enumerate the other source fibres.
\end{result}

\begin{proof}
We give the binary centralizer comparison first.
Write $S=q_B(s)$ and $R=q_B(r)$, and let
\[
 \Omega_2=Q_B/\langle S\rangle,\qquad
 V_2=\mathbb F_2[\Omega_2],\qquad
 e=e_{\langle S\rangle}.
 \tag{MIF.3}
\]
Use the actual left permutation action of $Q_B$
on these $288$ cosets and the product law
$(v,h)(w,k)=(v+h\cdot w,hk)$.
The generator assignment
\[
 \eta_2:B_0\longrightarrow V_2\rtimes Q_B,
 \qquad s\longmapsto(e,S),\qquad
 r\longmapsto(0,R)
 \tag{MIF.4}
\]
is a homomorphism: $S$ fixes $e$, so the square
of its displayed lift is zero in the vector
coordinate, and the cubic relation for $r$ is
immediate.  Projection is the specified $q_B$.
The restriction to $K$ lands in $V_2$ and therefore
kills $K'K^2$.  It consequently factors through
the actual $B_2$ in (MIF.1).

The $96$-state pure Cayley graph, with the specified
$x,y$ edges, has $97$ non-tree edge loops.  These
are the retained free basis of $K$.  Evaluating
them by (MIF.4) gives a matrix of rank $97$ in
$V_2$.  Write its row space as $W_2$.
Thus the induced $K/(K'K^2)\to V_2$ is injective
as well as onto $W_2$.  In particular the image
of (MIF.4) is exactly the marked group $B_2$,
of order $576\cdot2^{97}$.

For a pure residue $h\in q_B(F_0)$, retain the
fixed based word $w_h\in F_0$ in the spanning
tree and write
$\eta_2(w_h)=(t_h,h)$.
The actual fibre over $h$ consists of
$(t_h+w,h)$ with $w\in W_2$.  A lift centralizes
$\eta_2(s)$ precisely when $hS=Sh$ and
\[
 d_h:=t_h+h\cdot e-e-S\cdot t_h
                         \ \in\ (S-I)W_2.
 \tag{MIF.5}
\]
This is obtained by multiplying the two displayed
semidirect-product elements in both orders.
There is no replacement of the source fibre by
the ambient permutation module.

The executable certificate
\path{check_binary_modular_centralizer.py} forms
all these vectors from the actual source words.
It checks $\dim W_2=97$ and
$\dim(S-I)W_2=45$.  There are eight pure base
residues centralizing $S$.  Their exact projective
matrix representatives, using the fixed choice
of the smaller of $M,-M$ modulo twelve, give
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}

                  \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}
                       &d_h\in(S-I)W_2\\ \hline
 0&(1,0,0,1)&\text{yes}\\
 372&(3,10,2,3)&\text{no}\\
 376&(3,4,8,3)&\text{no}\\

 550&(3,2,10,3)&\text{no}\\

\end{array}                                            \tag{MIF.6}
\]
For each negative entry the numerical archive

$\ell_h((S-I)W_2)=0$ and $\ell_h(d_h)=1$.
The identity entry has the actual identity lift.
These exhaustive source-fibre tests prove
\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(
 C_{B_2}(s)\cap\operatorname{im}(F_0\to B_2)
                        \longrightarrow Q_B\bigr)=\{1\}.
 \tag{MIF.7}
\]

Now suppose the left side of (MIF.2) holds.
The exact retraction of A.4.45, with $m=6$,
is the map
\[
 \Gamma_6\longrightarrow F_0/(K'K^6)
                   \longrightarrow B_p,
 \qquad \iota_5(w)\longmapsto w\longmapsto w
                    \quad(p=2,3).
 \tag{MIF.8}
\]
The second arrows exist because $K^6\subset K^p$.
Thus $f_g$ has identity value in both $B_2$ and
$B_3$.  The genuine multiplier-one source action
and its unique pure torsion frames satisfy
\[
 \alpha_g(s)=s f_g=\beta_gs\beta_g^{-1},\qquad
 \alpha_g(r)=f_g^{-1}r=A_grA_g^{-1}.
 \tag{MIF.9}
\]
Consequently the image of $\beta_g$ in $B_2$
centralizes $s$, while the image of $A_g$ in
$B_3$ centralizes $r$.  Formula (MIF.7) forces
$q_B(\beta_g)=1$.  The ternary vertex-module
centralizer comparison of A.4.45 forces
$q_B(A_g)=1$.  Since both frames lie in the actual
pure subgroup $\widehat F_0$, and $q_B|_{F_0}$
has exactly the signed kernel of $\theta_{12}$,
these are the literal signed identities
$\theta_{12}(\beta_g)=\theta_{12}(A_g)=I$.

The signed cubic covariance gives
$T_g=\theta_{12}(A_g)^{-1}V_g=V_g=U^{8\rho_g}$
centralizing $R_0$.  As in (HTS.17), checking its

$\rho_g=0$ modulo three.  It is already identity
modulo four, so $V_g=I$ modulo twelve.
Then $T_g=W_g=I$, and the defining character
formula gives $\Delta(g)=0$.  This proves (MIF.2)
on the explicitly stated domain $H$.
\end{proof}
