## Primary literature

Bux, K.-U., Ershov, M. V., & Rapinchuk, A. S. (2011). The congruence subgroup property for Aut \(F_2\): A group-theoretic proof of Asada's theorem. *Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics, 5*(2), 327–353. https://doi.org/10.4171/GGD/130

Inspected: https://m-ershov.github.io/Research/asada_final.pdf, §1, author-PDF pp. 1–3, especially the Main Theorem on p. 2 and the distinction from classical linear congruence on p. 3; publisher metadata at https://ems.press/journals/ggd/articles/4445. Statement, definitions, and finite-index application checked; the whole general proof was not independently reconstructed.

Grothendieck, A. (1960–1961a). Techniques de construction en géométrie analytique. I. Description axiomatique de l'espace de Teichmüller et de ses variantes. *Séminaire Henri Cartan, 13*(1), Exposés 7–8, 1–33. https://www.numdam.org/item/SHC_1960-1961__13_1_A4_0/

Inspected copy: https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/AG/AG-87.pdf, §§1–3, especially §2 on Torelli/Jacobi rigidification and §3 on the moduli functor. Used for historical terminology and family-level context, not as an independent proof audit of representability.

Grothendieck, A. (1960–1961b). Techniques de construction en géométrie analytique. X. Construction de l'espace de Teichmüller. *Séminaire Henri Cartan, 13*(2), Exposé 17, 1–20. https://www.numdam.org/item/SHC_1960-1961__13_2_A4_0/

Inspected PDF: https://www.numdam.org/item/SHC_1960-1961__13_2_A4_0.pdf, introduction and §3; the introduction explicitly discusses the elementary genus-one lattice approach. The Serre appendix on pp. 18–20 was located, but no unquoted higher-dimensional rigidity theorem from it is needed by the present notes.

Hain, R. (2014). *Lectures on moduli spaces of elliptic curves* (Version 3) [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.0812.1803

## Retrieval limitation

Nielsen's 1917 article was sought for the classical \(\operatorname{Out}(F_2)\) identification: the bibliographic record at https://zenodo.org/records/2282348 was accessible, but the PDF retrieval failed with a decoding error and EuDML returned 403. The original proof was therefore not claimed as read or independently checked; the precise background identification used here is instead located in Bux, Ershov, and Rapinchuk's primary author paper, with the explicit generator calculations independently supplied.
