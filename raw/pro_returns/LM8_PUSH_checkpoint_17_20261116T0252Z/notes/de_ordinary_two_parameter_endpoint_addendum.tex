\subsubsection{The complete lattice stratum and its infinite local-index endpoint}

This addendum proposes a precise strengthening of D.94. Let
\(s=(a,b)\) be any completed pro-five Kummer pair over
\(\mathbb Q\), with arbitrary \(c_0\) support, satisfying
\[
 (a_5,b_5)=(0,0),\qquad
 L_s=5^r\mathbb Z_5\oplus\mathbb Z_5,\quad r\ge1,
 \qquad z_s=(\alpha,\beta),\quad\beta\in\mathbb Z_5^\times.
                                                        \tag{D94E.1}
\]
Here \(L_s\) is the actual closed away-five valuation lattice,
and \(z_s\) is the actual local unit-logarithm vector in the
common integral gauge of D.83/D.87. Put
\(N=v_5(\alpha)\), with \(v_5(0)=\infty\). The uniformly
convergent local sum lies in \(L_s\), so \(N\ge r\).
The compact cohomology terms are defined for this coefficient
pair whether or not its metabelian section fibre is nonempty.

For finite \(N\), the full D.94 formula holds unchanged:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal S_s&\simeq
      \left(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{r-1}\mathbb Z/5^{N-r+j}\right)
                         \oplus\mathbb Z/5^{N+1},\\
 \mathcal K_s&\simeq\mathcal S_s\oplus\mathbb Z_5^{N-r}.
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{D94E.2}
\]
At the infinite endpoint it instead gives
\[
                \mathcal S_s\simeq\mathbb Z_5^r,
       \qquad \mathcal K_s\simeq\prod_{\mathbb N}\mathbb Z_5.
                                                        \tag{D94E.3}
\]
Every assertion is an isomorphism of compact modules. The
second isomorphism in (D94E.3) absorbs a finite free factor;
it does not identify the canonical inclusion of \(\mathcal S_s\)
with a specified coordinate subproduct. No metabelian lift
or unrestricted first-cohomology rank is asserted for an
arbitrary pair in (D94E.1). The actual ordinary-point examples
and their finite-support section fibres remain exactly those
proved in the main D.94 statement.

\paragraph{Why the finite proof depends only on these data.}
The first part of D.94 proves that its explicit ordinary
points satisfy (D94E.1), with the prescribed finite \(N\).
Everything after its arithmetic paragraph uses only the
actual images
\(I_s=L_s\rtimes I_0\), \(I_5=\mathbb Z_5z_s\rtimes I_0\),
with zero valuation graph, and the natural map of D.87.
These identities hold for all completed pairs by that theorem,
including infinite \(c_0\) support and its uniform finite
local-primitive argument. Eliminating the unit \(\beta\)
gives exactly the same ring, retained generator, action and
map (D.94.6)--(D.94.12). The integral module and norm-transfer
proof is independent of the remaining Kummer coefficients.
D.77 gives the same boundary ranks \((N-r,0,0)\).
This proves (D94E.2) without inferring existence of a section.

\paragraph{The endpoint has an actual inclusion of two ideals.}
Now \(\alpha=0\). The local relation is \(t_2^\beta=1\),
so in the notation of D.94 one has
\[
 R=\Lambda/(t_2^\beta-1)=\mathbb Z_5[[u]],\qquad
       t=1+u,\quad J=q_rR,\quad q_r=t^{5^r}-1,
       \qquad PR=0.                                    \tag{D94E.4}
\]
The actual beta boundary identity is \(\beta_g(t,1)=1\),
and the common graph has no valuation term. Therefore
D.87 identifies \(\mathcal S_s\) with the kernel of
\[
 \left(R,S_\chi(t^{5^r})S_\chi(t)\sigma_\chi\right)_\Gamma
       \xrightarrow{\;q_r\;}
 \left(R,S_\chi(t)\sigma_\chi\right)_\Gamma.
                                                        \tag{D94E.5}
\]
As always the source scalar coordinate retains the actual
\(q_r\) generator. Multiplication by \(u\) identifies the
target equivariantly with \(I=uR\), with ordinary substitution.
Multiplication by \(u q_r\) identifies the source with
\(uJ\), also with ordinary substitution. Both are genuine
injections in the torsion-free power-series domain. Thus
(D94E.5) is exactly the inclusion
\[
                         (uJ)_\Gamma\longrightarrow I_\Gamma.
                                                        \tag{D94E.6}
\]
The quotient \(J/uJ\) is the rank-one module generated by
\(q_r\), with exact character \(\chi\). Its order-four
tame character is nontrivial, so all its \(\Gamma\)-homology
vanishes. The inclusion \(uJ\hookrightarrow J\) induces an
isomorphism on coinvariants. Under it, (D94E.6) becomes
\emph{the actual inclusion}
\[
                              J_\Gamma\longrightarrow I_\Gamma.
                                                        \tag{D94E.7}
\]
This simpler first-order intertwiner suffices at the endpoint;
it agrees with the \(u^3\) and low-jet comparisons in D.94.

\paragraph{Integral invariant measures and the exact kernel.}
Identify \(R\) with the completed group ring
\(\mathbb Z_5[[\mathbb Z_5]]\), whose substitution action
is pushforward by multiplication by units. Its only invariant
integral measures are
\[
                              R^\Gamma=\mathbb Z_5\delta_0.
                                                        \tag{D94E.8}
\]
Indeed, at finite level \(\mathbb Z/5^m\), the coefficients
are constant on each nonzero valuation shell. A residue of
valuation \(j<m\) has five lifts of that same valuation at
the next level. Compatibility makes its coefficient divisible
by \(5^k\) for every further depth \(k\); hence it is zero.
All nonzero residues vanish at every level. The remaining
coefficient at zero is compatible and arbitrary, proving
(D94E.8) integrally. Since \(I\) is the augmentation kernel,
\[
                                  I^\Gamma=0.           \tag{D94E.9}
\]

For compact \(\mathbb Z_5\)-modules with continuous
\(\Gamma\)-action, exact tame averaging followed by the
completed procyclic two-term resolution, after fixing a
topological generator of the pro-five factor, identifies
\(H_1(\Gamma,M)\) with \(M^\Gamma\). Equivalently one
can dualize the usual continuous resolution for discrete
five-primary modules; all displayed endomorphism images
are compact and hence closed. This is the same compact
homology calculation used in D.88 and D.91. It is not a
claim that unrestricted global continuous cohomology has
no derived-limit term.

Put \(R_r=\mathbb Z_5[\mathbb Z/5^r]\), with augmentation
ideal \(I_r\). The actual quotient map gives
\[
            0\longrightarrow J\longrightarrow I
                      \longrightarrow I_r\longrightarrow0.
                                                        \tag{D94E.10}
\]
The beginning of its exact homology sequence, together with
(D94E.9), identifies the kernel in (D94E.7) canonically with the homology group, and then, using that
generator, with the invariant module:
\[
 \ker(J_\Gamma\to I_\Gamma)
        \simeq H_1(\Gamma,I_r)\simeq I_r^\Gamma.
                                                        \tag{D94E.11}
\]
The finite group-ring module has \(r+1\) unit orbits, one
of them zero. If \(O_j\) is the sum of the basis vectors
on the orbit of elements of exact additive order \(5^j\),
then the invariant augmentation ideal has the integral basis
\[
                O_j-(4\cdot5^{j-1})\delta_0,
                         \qquad 1\le j\le r.            \tag{D94E.12}
\]
Thus it is precisely \(\mathbb Z_5^r\) with its compact
topology. The injection in (D94E.11) is the actual connecting
map: choose a tame-invariant lift to \(I\), apply
\(\gamma-1\) for a generator of the pro-five cyclotomic
factor, and retain its class in \(J_\Gamma\). Its
independence of the lift follows from exactness. This supplies
the natural kernel map, not an argument from equal ranks.
Equations (D94E.5)--(D94E.12) prove the first endpoint claim.

The first boundary parameter has global depth \(r\) and
zero local class, so D.77 adds its countable free compact
factor. The second and diagonal local coefficients are units
and contribute zero difference. Its continuous splitting
therefore gives
\(\mathcal K_s\simeq\mathbb Z_5^r\oplus\prod_{\mathbb N}\mathbb Z_5\),
which has the asserted abstract product form. No finite-rank
summand of the canonical map is silently discarded.

For a concrete coefficient pair realizing the endpoint,
use the already verified units \(\lambda_2,\lambda_3\), and set
\[
 a=5^r\left(\kappa(2)-\frac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_3}\kappa(3)\right),
                       \qquad b=\kappa(11).              \tag{D94E.13}
\]
The supports are disjoint across the two coordinates, so its
actual lattice is \(5^r\mathbb Z_5\oplus\mathbb Z_5\).
Its first local class is zero, while
\(v_5(11^4-1)=1\) makes \(\lambda_{11}\) a unit.
The five-valuation vector is zero. This is a genuine finite
support completed coefficient pair; a metabelian lift of this
additional example is not asserted.

No additional numerical premise is needed for the endpoint.
The integral measure and homology arguments prove it directly,
and they keep the arbitrary-support and section-existence
quantifiers separate.
