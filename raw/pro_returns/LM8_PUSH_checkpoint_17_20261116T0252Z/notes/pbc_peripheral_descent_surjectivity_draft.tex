\subsection{Surjectivity before imposing the geometric symmetry}

\begin{statement}{C.45}{Open}{Candidate: the larger peripheral descent map is onto}
The continuous multiplier-preserving homomorphism of C.1
\[
                    D:\mathcal P\longrightarrow
                       \widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0
\]
is onto. Its restriction from the multiplier-one subgroup of
$\mathcal P$ is onto the multiplier-one subgroup of the target.
Here $\mathcal P$ is the full pure peripheral outer group of the
rank-two free profinite group. This does not assert that the
endomorphism $D|_{\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0}$ is onto.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}[Notes-only candidate, independent audits pending]
Let $g$ be a multiplier-one target gentle class. By the extension
comparison of C.1, choose its actual positive peripheral modular
extension $\Psi$ on
$\Gamma=\widehat{C_2*C_3}$. Let
\[
 F=\langle x,y\rangle,\quad z=(xy)^{-1},\quad
 \varphi(x)=s,\quad\varphi(y)=\tau=sr,\quad
 \varphi(z)=r^{-1},\qquad N=\ker\varphi.
\]
In this paragraph $F$ is the source free group with the question's
marking; the target pure subgroup of $\Gamma$ is its separately
marked characteristic index-six subgroup. Form the actual source
centralization
\[
 E=F/\overline{[F,N]},\qquad
 K=N/\overline{[F,N]}.
\]
The finite cyclic periodic resolution and free-product homology
sequence give $H_2(\Gamma,\widehat{\mathbb Z})=0$, coefficient
prime by coefficient prime, exactly as in B.27 and C.40.
The continuous five-term sequence consequently gives an injection
\[
                  K\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
                       F^{\rm ab}=\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{2}.
                                                               \tag{C.45.1}
\]
This is the actual abelianization map of $E$:
$E^{\rm ab}=F^{\rm ab}$ because $[F,N]\subset F'$. Its image
is the kernel of $F^{\rm ab}\to\Gamma^{\rm ab}$.

Choose any three positive peripheral conjugators for $\Psi$,
and lift them to $B_x,B_y,B_z\in E$. Put
\[
 X=B_x\bar x B_x^{-1},\qquad
 Y=B_y\bar y B_y^{-1},\qquad
 Z=B_z\bar z B_z^{-1}.
\]
Their product belongs to $K$, since their three images in
$\Gamma$ are the images under $\Psi$ of a product equal to one.
Its abelianization in $E$ is $x+y+z=0$. Injection (C.45.1)
therefore implies
\[
                               XYZ=1.                 \tag{C.45.2}
\]
Thus $x\mapsto X$, $y\mapsto Y$ defines an actual homomorphism
$F\to E$ which also takes $z$ to $Z$. Its map modulo $K$ is
$\Psi\varphi$. It sends $N$ into the central $K$, hence kills
$[F,N]$ and factors through an endomorphism $\widetilde\Psi$ of
the actual $E$.

Its action on $E^{\rm ab}$ is identity. By (C.45.1) its action
on $K$ is identity as well. Its action on $E/K=\Gamma$ is the
automorphism $\Psi$. It is consequently an automorphism of $E$:
kernel injectivity and quotient injectivity give injectivity,
while the two surjectivities successively lift every element of
the target. This conclusion does not assume that $E$ is a universal
central extension of an arbitrary quotient.

Apply the central criterion proved in B.11. Its proof uses only
normality of $N$ in the finite refinement, as stated there and
spelled out in C.40; full characteristic invariance under every
automorphism of $F$ is unnecessary. It produces an actual full
positive peripheral automorphism $\alpha$ of the source $F$
covering $\Psi$ and retaining the three chosen conjugators.
Its pure outer class belongs to $\mathcal P_1$. By the definition
of $D$, descending it along $\varphi$ and restricting to the
characteristic pure subgroup of $\Gamma$ gives exactly $g$.
All outer normalizations are the ones in C.1. This proves
surjectivity at multiplier one.

For general multiplier, choose an actual Galois element $\sigma$
whose cyclotomic character equals the target multiplier. The
cyclotomic character is onto and C.1 proves $D(\sigma)=\sigma$.
Apply the preceding assertion to $g\sigma^{-1}$ and multiply its
preimage by $\sigma$. This proves the full assertion.

The constructed $\alpha$ is pure peripheral but has not been
proved to commute with the geometric $S_3$ outer action. Imposing
that extra symmetry is precisely the additional requirement for
the source class to belong to $\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0$; the
proof supplies no such assertion.
\end{proof}
