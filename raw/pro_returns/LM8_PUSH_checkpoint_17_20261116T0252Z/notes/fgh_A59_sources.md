# A.59 source scope

The new group-theoretic factorization and induced-topology proof are supplied in full. All external sources were already opened and checked for A40/A56/A57. Silverman VII.5.5 gives potential good reduction of E at every prime from j=1728. SGA1 XIII2.9--2.12 supplies prime-to-residue-characteristic specialization of the good pair (E,O); the explicit local t=z^e proof that the compactification is smooth is repeated. A57 then applies to the actual intermediate curve, with genus-one cases supplied by A40.

The main completion step is the exact core argument of A22, reproduced for the arbitrary intermediate geometric group. It gives an injection on actual geometrically pro-ell arithmetic groups after finite descent, not only on rational Lie algebras. The inertia intersection is the genuine finite-cover cusp branch.

The finite certificate enumerates every subgroup of D8 x C3, Q8 x C5, and H3 x C2, including non-normal ones, and verifies both core formulas for coefficients2,3,5,7. This corroborates but does not replace the coprime-power/direct-product proof for all finite nilpotent groups.
