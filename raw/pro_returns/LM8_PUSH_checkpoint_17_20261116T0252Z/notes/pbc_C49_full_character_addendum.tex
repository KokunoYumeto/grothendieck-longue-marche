\paragraph{Full-group character consequence of the computed positive images.}
Let $\xi:\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ be a
continuous finite-order character trivial on the specified Galois
image. Suppose only that its restriction to
$\mathcal H=\ker\lambda$ factors through one of the actual framed
residue maps in C.49, at $N=24$ or $N=60$. Then
\[
                           \xi=0\quad\hbox{or}\quad\xi=\Delta,
\]
where $\Delta$ is identified with the character valued in
$\{0,1/2\}\subset\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$.
Indeed C.49 gives $\xi|_{\mathcal H}=\varepsilon\Delta|_{\mathcal H}$
for one $\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}$. The continuous character
$\xi-\varepsilon\Delta$ therefore factors through the actual
multiplier quotient $\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\times}$. The Galois
multiplier is onto this quotient, and this difference vanishes on
the Galois image. It is consequently zero. Conversely both displayed
characters have the required restriction and arithmetic vanishing.
This assertion includes any full-group framed character whose
restriction is one of the computed finite residue characters. It does
not specify or calculate the arbitrary-multiplier framed images.
