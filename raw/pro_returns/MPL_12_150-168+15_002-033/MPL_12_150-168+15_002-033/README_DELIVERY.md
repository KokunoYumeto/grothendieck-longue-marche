## Freeze and interpretation

The primary transcriber viewed each whole-page image and both detail images, transcribed all pages in one stream without OCR, and conducted the second visual audit before opening the context. The transcription was frozen at 2026-11-17 00:29:05 UTC; the context was opened at 00:29:10 UTC. The final verification checks every frozen hash.

The notes never amend the transcription. A mathematically preferable formula, a later published version and a difficult handwritten reading are different evidence. Original research memoranda are working records; the final notes incorporate the quotation and scope corrections documented in records/research_review.md.

The transcription deliberately remains a fragment using the requested editorial macros. It must be included in a host document that defines those macros and loads the required standard mathematics and TikZ/tikz-cd facilities. The combined fragment begins with cote 12 and scan 150.

Structural checks passed. A compiled rendering was not available: the local pdflatex executable lacked its format and TeX package tree, and stopped before reading the fragment. No successful compilation is claimed.

The supplied scans, source ZIPs and context documents are not duplicated here. Context-search records identify the supplied files by path and SHA-256. The hourly checkpoint is separate from this final deliverable.
