The printed and typed material on scans 009 and 015 is among the most secure text in this package. Their small exceptions remain marked: the printer's imprint on 009 and the faint pen signature on 015. In the manuscripts, uncertainty is concentrated in overwritten or heavily crossed deletions, faded pencil, cramped or slanted annotations, and small attached indices or punctuation. The clipped final line of scan 008 and the very faint, horizontally reversed lower strip on scan 024 deserve particular caution. The separate hands and successive correction layers are retained where the recorded evidence distinguishes them; unresolved letters, insertion scope and erased forms remain marked in place.

Witness C generally supplies the fuller diplomatic structure, including cancellations, insertions and drawings. Witness H sometimes resolves useful individual readings, but its treatment of the other-hand memoir often consists of an editorial synopsis with selected annotations rather than continuous source wording. The many main-text omission rows on those sheets therefore record a difference in coverage and editorial policy. The difference totals combine those omissions with actual reading disagreements and should not be interpreted as comparative error rates. Agreement between witnesses was not used to settle the ink. The remaining doubts identify the places where the recorded image evidence did not support a secure choice.
