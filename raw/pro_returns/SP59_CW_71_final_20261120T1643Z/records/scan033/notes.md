N1 (independent re-proof of the first lemma). K=E(Gamma) is compact convex with nonempty interior. For x outside K, choose an interior point o: the outward ray from x in direction x-o remains outside K, since otherwise convexity would put x inside K. Move along these rays to a sufficiently large circle enclosing K; that circle joins the rays outside K. Thus R2 minus K is connected and unbounded, misses Gamma, and lies in the unbounded component ext Gamma. This proves the claimed inclusion, conditional on the J-polygon component convention already in the manuscript.

N2 (targeted check of the second lemma's strategy). Reuse and recheck the supporting-line argument of 71-032-N2: an open gap in partial E(Gamma) minus Gamma lies in a single hull side because hull vertices belong to Gamma. Its endpoints are Gamma vertices, since a point interior to a Gamma edge on a supporting line forces that edge into the line and cannot be the endpoint of a gap. An outward supporting ray from a gap point reaches infinity without meeting Gamma, so the open segment is in ext Gamma. Nonconvexity of the polygonal boundary ensures at least one such gap: if the hull boundary were contained in Gamma, the no-proper-circle-containment argument of 71-032-N1 would give equality. This supplies the existence asserted by the second lemma. The manuscript's local open-set step assumes relative openness on a common supporting line; arbitrary crossing segments would not justify it.
