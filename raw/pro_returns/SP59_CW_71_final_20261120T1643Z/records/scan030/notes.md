N1 (independent re-proof of the initial geometric existence assertion, for a simple closed polygon). Choose a linear functional that takes distinct values on the finitely many vertices, and let s be its unique maximizing vertex. Both adjacent vertices lie strictly in its lower open half-plane. Their two incident rays therefore enclose one sector of angle less than pi and a complementary sector of angle greater than pi. They cannot be opposite rays, so s is nonflat. In a sufficiently small disk, no nonincident edge occurs. The larger local sector is connected and contains the upper functional ray from s, which continues to infinity without meeting any point of the polygon, since every edge lies in the lower closed half-plane. Consequently that local sector belongs to the unbounded complementary component. This checks the geometric core of the first assertion under the simple closed polygon hypothesis; it does not make the whole draft into a complete proof.
