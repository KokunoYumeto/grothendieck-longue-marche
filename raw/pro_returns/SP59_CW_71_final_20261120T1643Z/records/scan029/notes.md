N1 (independent re-proof of Lemma V). Choose coordinates on the line d with y at the origin, s=(−a,0), s′=(b,0), a,b>0, and x in the upper open half-plane. If C contained w=(u,v) with v>0, convexity would put T=conv{s,s′,w} in C. For any sufficiently small positive epsilon, epsilon x has height epsilon x_2 less than v and lies between the two sloping edges of T: their horizontal interval converges to [−a,b] as height decreases to zero, whereas epsilon x_1 tends to 0 in its interior. Thus a point of ]y,x[ lies in C, contradicting visibility. Hence C is contained in the opposite closed half-plane. For every z on d, ]x,z[ lies in the upper half-plane and misses C, proving the stated conclusion. This proof validates the triangle step without requiring the stronger marginal assertion that s,s′ lie on an edge of a full-dimensional polyhedron.
