# Notation table

Maintained by the sole manuscript reader, then compared across the completed page fragments and the images before freezing. This table describes visible notation; it does not supply definitions from the contextual files. A recurring form remains doubtful wherever its local ink is inconclusive. Coordinates in TikZ are reconstruction coordinates, not mathematical data.

| Visible form | LaTeX | Comparison and retained distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Latin capital D; successive short raised strokes | `D`, `D'`, `D''`, or `D^{\prime}` | Compared in scans 77–80, 104, 112 and 119. Prime notation is retained where the raised stroke is clear. The two LaTeX spellings of a prime render the same sign. |
| Raised mark beside barred D | `\bar D\unsure{'|^!}` | Scan 112, left of the second coloured construction. The additional ink does not securely distinguish prime from exclamation; it has not been resolved by analogy. |
| Bar, tilde and dual superscript | `\bar D`, `\widetilde D`, `X^\vee`, `\omega_\alpha^\vee` | These are distinct visible marks, particularly in scans 78–80, 88–94 and 112; they have not been substituted for one another. |
| Plain X and more looped capital X | `X`, `\mathcal X`, with local `\unsure` | Recurs throughout scans 77–95 and 118–120. The distinction between the underlying X and the looped letter is often legible, but not consistently secure in isolated labels. |
| D with doubled/strengthened upright | `\mathbb D_{2n}`, `\mathbb D_n^+` | Scan 86, group notation. The chosen alphabet represents the strengthened form; its typeset shape is necessarily conventional. |
| Underlined omega and sigma | `\uline{\omega}`, `\uline{\Sigma}` | Underlining belongs to the manuscript and is retained, including the omega expressions in scans 80 and 86 and Sigma in scan 78. |
| Omega with subscripts and in parentheses | `\omega_D`, `\omega_{D'}`, `\omega_F`, `\omega(D)` | Compared in scans 77–80, 86, 94 and 119; subscript versus parentheses remains as written. |
| Rho, usually with a subscript | `\rho_\alpha`, `\rho_D` | Scans 80 and 119. Rho is not identified with omega even where nearby formulas relate them. |
| Orientation loop or small a | `o`, `a`, with `\unsure{o|a}` or the locally recorded alternatives | Most vulnerable in scans 77 and 105. A mathematical expectation is not used to choose between compressed loops. |
| Alpha, beta, gamma, delta | `\alpha`, `\beta`, `\gamma`, `\delta` | Compared in running text and drawings, especially 79–84, 94–97 and 104–119. Alternatives gamma/delta/theta remain local in the register. |
| Nu, sometimes resembling gamma or Latin r | `\nu`, `\unsure{\nu|\gamma}`, `\unsure{\nu|r}` | Scans 84, 86–87, 94–95, 105 and 114–120. Count letters have not been made consistent merely because a formula would then simplify. |
| Small epsilon, open-loop epsilon, xi | `\epsilon`, `\varepsilon`, `\xi`, with local `\unsure` | Compared in scans 86, 95, 111, 114–116. The chosen epsilon shapes follow the visible open or compressed form; xi is retained where a separate curling stroke is seen. |
| Phi or script F | `\varphi`, `\Phi`, `\mathcal F` | Scan 88 contains a locally doubtful capital `\Phi|\mathcal F`; scans 92 and 121 contain small phi-like labels. They are not silently normalized. |
| Chi and indexed chi | `\chi`, `\chi_0`, `\chi_2` | Scans 82 and 120. The indices and Euler-type expressions remain exactly as read, including cancelled expressions. |
| Lambda, mu, and primes | `\lambda`, `\mu`, `\lambda'`, `\mu'` | Compared across the drawings in 95–98, 105, 108 and 117. Labels are attached to their drawn positions, not reassigned by a presumed symmetry. |
| Tau, sigma and local transport subscripts | `\tau_X`, `\tau_{\mathcal X}`, `\sigma` | Scans 94–95. An antipodism exponent is retained separately from the transport symbol. |
| Multiplication/product and wedge | `\times`, `\wedge` | Distinct in scans 95 and 119; a doubtful isolated cross is recorded as doubtful rather than promoted to a product. |
| Equality, similarity/isomorphism, inequalities | `=`, `\simeq`, `\le`, `\ge`, `\ne` | Directions compared against the images in each audit; surprising expressions, notably scan 105, have not been algebraically repaired. |
| Arrows and plain connecting strokes | `\to`, `\longrightarrow`, `\mapsto`, TikZ directed or plain paths | Arrowheads are retained only where visible. The pencil path near scan 119's diagram is drawn separately and is not a third diagram arrow. |
| Polygon name | `\operatorname{Pol}(D)` | Recurs in scans 86, 92 and 119; capitalization and parentheses retained. |
| St followed by a number | `\mathrm{St}` or visible text `St` with its index/number | Scan 97 has a local reading doubt; isolated St6 on scan 121 was re-examined in a rotated crop. These are not expanded into a theorem name. |
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes | Uniform LaTeX convention for paired quotation strokes. No typographic quotation style is inferred from contextual sources. |
| Underlining and double underlining | `\uline`, `\uuline` | The number of strokes is preserved where separable. Crossing-out is represented by `\deleted`, not by an underline. |
| Pencilled sheet numbers | `\margin{76}` through the corresponding visible numbers | All visible numbers are transcribed. Authorship of the numbering cannot be established from these images alone, so none is asserted as an authorial `\mspage`. Scan numbers always refer to the supplied PDF sequence. |
| Circuits superréguliers / positions supersingulières | the two French words as written | Compared directly in scans 77–78, 86–87 and 94. These are distinct expressions, not a spelling inconsistency to be normalized. The doubtful summit-word on scan 78 remains marked. |
| Arc d'inertie | `arc d'\unsure{inertie}` where uncertain | Direct comparison of scans 90, 92 and 120 favored inertie over the first-pass alternatives identité/incidence. The reading remains doubtful on 90 and 120. |
| p.r. and p.v. | the literal abbreviation, locally `\unsure{p.r.|p.v.}` | The final consonant is sometimes compressed, particularly scans 78–79. The expansions below do not decide the transcription. |
| standard / standart | `\unsure{standard|standart}` | Scans 88 and 90. Word endings are not corrected to dictionary spelling. |
| Printer characters, stars, spacing and job numbers | `verbatim`, monospaced text, or literal character arrays | Reused listing-paper text is transcribed as foreground print. Similar but genuinely distinct job listings are retained; scans 82 and 122 were compared directly for the repeated dataset table. |
