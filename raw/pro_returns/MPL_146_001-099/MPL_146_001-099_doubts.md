## How to locate an entry

Each occurrence of `\unsure` or `\illegible` has its own row below. “L” is a line in the individual page’s `.tex` fragment, not a reconstructed physical manuscript line; the state column identifies the enclosing margin, insertion, deletion, diagram, or drawing where applicable. These stable references are supplemented by the regional rereading notes in the second table. A nested doubtful symbol and the doubtful phrase containing it may therefore have separate rows.

Confidence describes the proposed reading, not the mathematical plausibility of the statement: **medium** means a credible but unresolved reading; **low** means substantial paleographic ambiguity, obliterated writing, or unresolved alternatives. Regional rereading entries may give **high** confidence when they document a resolved or nearly resolved feature. Absence of a second reading means that no defensible competing transcription was identified.

## Legibility and limits

The printed administrative sheets are generally clearer than the rapid manuscript, although signatures, stamps, show-through, and overwritten figures remain uncertain. The main mathematical prose is often readable; tiny indices, distinctions between calligraphic and ordinary letters, primes, hats, tildes, arrowheads, and words written through earlier cancellations are less secure. A well-formed formula is not evidence that its uncertain letters are correct.

The least secure passages include the dense oblique margins on scans 003, 005, 009, 016, 017, 043, 045, 046, and 090; the overwriting in the large path drawing on scan 020; the crossed and diagonal annotations on scans 051–052; faint reversed show-through on scans 071–072; overwritten exponents on scan 073; the heavily revised passage on scan 097; and some covering and local-system symbols on scan 099. Independent rereads were used for several of these regions, but the remaining uncertainty is retained in place.

TikZ drawings preserve the visible components, their relative arrangement, paths, decorations, and written labels as closely as the scans permit. Their geometry is a redrawing, not a facsimile; slight curvature, stroke weight, crossing order in dense overdrawn regions, and handwriting-like signatures are necessarily approximate. Uncertain labels are marked within the drawings.

An inventory row marked “yes” records that the corresponding visible element has been represented; it does not claim that every uncertain reading has been resolved. The transcription should be consulted together with this register, especially for mathematical use of a sign, index, or cancelled alternative.
