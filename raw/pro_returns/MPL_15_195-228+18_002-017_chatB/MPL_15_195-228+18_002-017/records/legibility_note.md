The confidence column records the reader's review of the local reading after both passes. Explicit competing readings have low confidence; single doubtful readings generally have medium confidence, with weak multiword cancellations and the least legible passages assigned low confidence. A confidence label concerns the stated reading, not a probability that the surrounding mathematical statement is correct.
