## Legibility note

All 49 whole-page images and their 98 supplied half-page images were inspected in the prescribed order during transcription. Every page subsequently received a separate reading against its saved text; the progress log and per-page audit records record these stages. Enlarged and rotated crops were used for small, oblique, overwritten and crossed-out writing. No OCR was used. Context material remained unopened during both readings and the notation comparison.

The typescripts are generally clearer than the handwriting, although some copied letters, accents and duplicated strokes are weak. The handwritten text alternates between very clear large symbols and compressed words whose endings are only short strokes. The least secure passages are the lower cancellation and vertical margin of 15/195; the revisions at 15/219 and 221; the cancelled proof attempt and added name at 15/225; the densely written marginal and footer text of 18/011; the crossed draft at 18/012; and the overwritten degrees, support indices and X/Y letters in 18/017. These areas carry local doubt marks, including the remaining small counts of physically unreadable words. No mathematical symbol was replaced by an illegible-word count.

The confidence column records the reader's review of the local reading after both passes. Explicit competing readings have low confidence; single doubtful readings generally have medium confidence, with weak multiword cancellations and the least legible passages assigned low confidence. A confidence label concerns the stated reading, not a probability that the surrounding mathematical statement is correct. Page positions are approximate visual regions or named blocks, accompanied by exact TeX-source line locators.

The small pencil folios in the outer lower margins have been transcribed as marginal content because their authorship is not established by these scans. Printed or typed page numbers, headings and bibliographical numbering remain in the text. Blank paper, verso show-through and unlettered paper blemishes were distinguished during inspection from the written side. Cancellation does not remove a passage from the transcription. The output retains source incompletions, missing or unmatched punctuation, uncertain arrows and indices, and statements later corrected within the notes.
