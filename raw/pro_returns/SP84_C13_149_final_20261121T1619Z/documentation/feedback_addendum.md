## Recurring tendencies and useful refinements

The evidence from scans005–027 does **not establish a genuinely new recurring manuscript-error category outside the supplied sheet**. The sheet already names the principal problems encountered here: tiny formula signs, literal short forms and punctuation, missing additions, uncertain cancellation, insertion attachment, marginal writing, and agreement between witnesses on unsupported text. A record classified as “Other” is not, by itself, evidence for a new category. The following are more precise cautions suggested by this cote, not additional kinds to count alongside the existing categories.

Change identifiers below refer to `reviews/149_scanNNN.json`; crop identifiers refer to that scan’s files in `crops/`.

### Refinements within the existing categories

**A neighbouring stroke can change the apparent construction of a letter.** The problem is sometimes more specific than mistaking a leader for a strike. In scan020, the leader from the inserted words supplies an apparent extra leg beside a Gamma; following the stroke back to its attachment distinguishes that leg from the letter itself. In scan013, small strokes near inserted rounded group symbols can belong to descenders from the preceding line rather than to primes. The useful refinement is to allocate each competing stroke to its baseline, letter or leader before assigning an alphabet or decoration. This remains a symbols/indices and insertion-placement problem. Evidence: scan020, opening continuation, change c02 and crop `crops/149_scan020_c02.png`; scan013, first small interlinear line, change c13 and crop `crops/149_scan013_c13.png`.

**Correction state may change inside a single formula.** A continuous mathematical expression need not be one continuous insertion or deletion. In scan020, the baseline Gamma with subscript K lies between two raised additions: Gamma-equals on the left and an inclusion with indexed Gamma on the right. Marking the whole expression as inserted loses that distinction. In scan015, the cancellation of the extension index is separate from the long leader above it, and the state of the final K remains uncertain even though that K is visible. In scan013, the raised Gamma chain and the remaining baseline inclusion likewise occupy distinct levels. A useful addition to the sheet would say explicitly: record the state of each component before grouping the formula into macros; visibility of overlapping letters does not establish their writing order. These are refinements of struck text and insertion placement, not new categories. Evidence: scan020, change c07 and audit box b09; scan015, change c06 and crop c10; scan013, changes c10–c11 and crop c11. The corresponding page reports retain the complete formulas and state doubts.

**A long returning descender can imitate an underline.** In scan018, the stroke beneath `pro` belongs continuously to the following g and does not establish underlining. The g descender in scan021’s `ss-groupe` also needs to be kept separate from emphasis. Conversely, scan020 has an independent underline beneath `extérieurement`, which H omits. The useful local caution is therefore to trace an apparent underline to both ends, just as the sheet already requires for apparent cancellation. It does not justify a rule that every long low stroke is either always emphasis or always a descender. Evidence: scan018, change c08 and crop c08; scan021, change c04 and crop c04; scan020, change c03 and crop c02.

**A recurring outline does not necessarily settle its typographic alphabet.** The rounded centralizer sign repeats on scans014–015 and021, but repetition alone does not provide the missing contrast between ordinary Z and calligraphic Z. Rounded E/epsilon forms present a similar limitation. By contrast, scan007’s map to B has visible doubled construction that can be compared with the ordinary B elsewhere on that page. The useful refinement is to record both the recurring hand pattern and a contrasting example, when one exists; otherwise consistency can preserve an uncertainty rather than suppress it. This belongs to symbols/indices and the hand log. Evidence: scan014, marks M0987–M0996 and their decisions; scan015, marks and doubts for the centralizer signs; scan021, doubts for the repeated Z and E-like forms; scan007, change c07 and crop c07.

### Confirmations of cautions already explicit in the sheet

These examples are useful reminders for a later run, but they should **not be presented as newly discovered kinds**:

- Reduced or capped map terminals recur in scans005–006 and026–027. The distinct left bar or hook, right head and label have to be read separately. The sheet already says this. The clear headed and unheaded examples on the same leaf are especially useful comparisons: scan006, crops c10–c11; scan026, chain diagrams in crops c19–c20; scan027, the two y maps in crop c08.
- Written short forms, quotation strokes and stop counts resist regularisation even when C and H agree. At scan018, both give expanded `ensemble` for a much shorter group; at scan025, both supply stops after `hom` and `cqfd`. H also repeatedly substitutes guillemets for paired raised pen strokes, for example scans009,011 and022. These are already covered by symbols, abbreviations and text not on the page. Evidence: scan018, change c07; scan025, changes c07 and c20; scan009, crop c10; scan011, change c01; scan022, change c26.
- A leader can become an invented parenthesis in both witnesses: scan011 before `Notons`, and scan020’s second `extérieures` insertion. The supplied sheet already names this exact danger. These are useful local counterexamples to trusting witness agreement. Evidence: scan011, change c03; scan020, change c16.

### Workflow cautions, separate from manuscript-error categories

**A neutral rectangle can still be incomplete.** At scan017, audit box b11 cut the top of a raised cluster. The frozen second reading records that limitation; a separately documented supplementary source rectangle was needed for adjudication. Some other boxes cut ascender or descender tips. A lexical difference caused by different target extents is not automatically a reading error. For the next run, check the full target, decorations and attachment strokes before freezing the neutral boxes, and preserve an independently reported boundary loss rather than completing it from context. Evidence: `second/149_scan017_second_notes.md`, `documentation/audit_scope_crops.tsv`, and scan017 change c09; further boundary observations are recorded in `second/149_scan021_second_notes.md` and `second/149_scan027_second_notes.md`.

**Transport and provenance require their own checks.** The supplied literal TSV needed parsing without CSV quote interpretation. The initial parser joined some rows and exposed reader B to later-page witness rows while preparing scan017; the packet was corrected, and that reader was excluded from independent readings of scans018–022. This was a data-handling incident, not a new manuscript-error category (`documentation/packet_parser_correction.md`). All first-crop coordinate gaps have now been closed. The previously unlocated rectangles were accepted only after the recorded processing pipeline reproduced the saved pixels exactly, not from estimated display coordinates. Future crop creation should save the original rectangle and the contrast, scaling, sharpening and rotation steps immediately. Evidence: `documentation/first_crop_coordinates.tsv` and `documentation/coordinate_recovery_reader_a.md`, `coordinate_recovery_reader_b.md`, and `coordinate_recovery_reader_c.md`.
