# Independent root review of C.36

Reviewed 2026-11-16 05:20–05:24 UTC. Both claims pass review.

The A4 third-cusp assertion holds for every continuous map and every
full unit. A proper image is abelian, so the commutator word is one.
For a generating pair, C.35 and preservation of closed cyclic
subgroups under unit powers keep generation. An order-three
conjugacy class is determined by its nonzero C3 abelianization;
the substituted product has the required class. If the original
product is a double transposition, the new product is a nonidentity
V4 element, because identity would contradict generation. All
three such elements form one class. No choice of a finite
conjugator is asserted to be compatible across other quotients.

For U3=ker(F→C3²), the exact Cayley-graph chain sequence over
F2[C3²] and Maschke splitting give a trivial module of dimension
two plus four distinct simple modules of dimension two. The
canonical substitution has identity base action and is therefore
equivariant on the Frattini module. The invariant part is
identified naturally with H1(F,F2), on which it is identity.
Each nontrivial simple quotient gives an actual A4 quotient of
the split finite extension. Vanishing on one block would make
that composite kill U3 and factor through the abelian base,
contradicting C.35. Each block is consequently invertible.

The pro-two residual is fully invariant, as required for an
endomorphism rather than only an automorphism. Frattini gives
surjectivity on the free pro-two kernel; identity on the finite
base then gives surjectivity on the whole relative group.
Finite generation and Hopficity turn it into an automorphism.
For every other unit multiplier, the two generator images
generate exactly the same closed subgroup. This transfers
surjectivity and the canonical intertwining identity, but
does not establish the third cusp in the relative group.
