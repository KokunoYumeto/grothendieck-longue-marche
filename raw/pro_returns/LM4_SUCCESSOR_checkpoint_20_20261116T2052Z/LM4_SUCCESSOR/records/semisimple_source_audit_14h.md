The elementary construction chooses a primitive integral polynomial f
for a finite Galois field containing the coefficient entries. For any
finite forbidden set, enlarge its product m to exclude the discriminant
and every bad denominator. The integer polynomial f(f(0)mX)/f(0) is
one modulo m. A prime divisor of a value with absolute value greater
than one lies outside that set and gives a simple root of f. The
verified factorization and Galois facts show that it splits completely.
This argument requires no density statement and selects no numerical
prime for the very large counterexample family.
