# Primary-source reading for the B100 universal-cover conclusion

Hatcher, A. (2002). *Algebraic topology*. Cambridge University Press.

Author's primary text: https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf.
Author's edition record: https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATpage.html.

Read Proposition4.1 and proof, printed342, and Theorem4.32 and its
complete proof, printed366--367. The actual use is the degree-two
comparison between higher homotopy and homology in a universal cover.
Both root and DE independently opened the primary text at this step.
The book page confirms Cambridge University Press2002.

B100 separately proves the mod-two assertion directly from its lifted
two-dimensional cellular chain groups, dividing a finite nonzero cycle
by the maximal common power of two. It makes no assertion that its
particular cube sphere is primitive. No equivariant or completed
coefficient comparison is inferred from these ordinary homology facts.
