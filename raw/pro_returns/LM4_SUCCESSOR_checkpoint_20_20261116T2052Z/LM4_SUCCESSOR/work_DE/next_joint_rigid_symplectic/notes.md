## Why these finite tests do not restrict the full3-adic image

Let C be the closure of the actual H5 image under B.64's genus-five projective3-adic homology map. Its reduction modulo3 has order1440 by B.62, and the principal congruence kernel is pro3. Hence every finite continuous quotient of C has order dividing1440 times a power of3. The same conclusion holds for every closed subgroup of C, in particular the closure C' of the actual H5 commutator image.

Let rho be the combined two rigid finite quotients, with target S×S. Both rho(H5) and rho(H5') equal S×S, since this target is perfect. The image of the relevant compact source subgroup in C×S×S (or C'×S×S) is a closed subdirect product. If it were proper, the elementary compact Goursat lemma would give a nontrivial common finite quotient. Any nontrivial quotient of S×S has a quotient S. This is impossible for C or C': the2-part of |S| is64, whereas that of1440 is32. Therefore both joint images are full direct products.

Consequently the full3-adic cyclic image persists after imposing these two rigid **finite** homology tests, even with the exact source commutator condition. This does not assert that it persists after imposing the full discrete or completed rigid monodromies. In particular it does not upgrade B.64 to a source in the actual full cyclic rigid kernel, and produces no element of the actual residual intersection K.
