Inertia fixes i and every 2-power root of unity and 2-power radical of2, because the corresponding splitting fields are unramified at3. Put sigma0=gamma c gamma^-1 c. It is multiplier one, is a c-cocycle, moves r0 by -1, and has dyadic Kummer character of2 equal to0. Let e2 in Zhat have component1 at2 and0 at every odd prime, and sigma=sigma0^e2. Then sigma is still a genuine Galois c-cocycle, both CM bits remain1, and its complete Kummer character of2 vanishes. Indeed the character is additive on the multiplier-one subgroup, so all odd components are killed by e2 and the dyadic component was already zero.

Marin2008, Section4 p9, opened at https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.2507, identifies the signed modular congruence shear on Galois elements with -8 times this Kummer character. Thus sigma acts identically on every actual principal congruence quotient Q_N of B. The full GT0 covariance from refereed E65.15 proves preservation of each U_N=ker(B→Q_N); these subgroups are not being called characteristic under arbitrary Aut(F).
