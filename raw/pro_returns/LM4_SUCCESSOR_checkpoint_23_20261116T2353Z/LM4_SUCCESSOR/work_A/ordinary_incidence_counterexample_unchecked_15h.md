This is an unverified research lead, not a result or counterexample certificate.
No formal claim or source-status change is made.

For \(Y=\operatorname{Conf}_2(\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\})\), source
\(y=1-x\), and filling the diagonal, the cross-ratio map
\(f=x(1-y)/(y(1-x))\) restricts to \((x/(1-x))^2\). The latter is the
double cover of \(\mathbb P^1\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}\) with two points
above 1. One is the source/diagonal intersection; the other is source infinity.
A proposed \(D_8\) quotient sends a base meridian at 0 to a rotation and one
at 1 to a reflection. The expected source-kernel image is the order-two
reflection subgroup, while the ambient filling-kernel image is the Klein four
normal closure. Exact based maps and ambient surjectivity still require audit.

blocks the broad relative-transversality shortcut. The actual tangential
