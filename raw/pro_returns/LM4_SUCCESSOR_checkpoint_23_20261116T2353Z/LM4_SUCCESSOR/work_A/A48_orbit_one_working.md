# A.48 working proof: nonregular orbit-one quotients

**Status:** independently reviewed by the parent and incorporated as formal A.48. The crucial elementary lemma is the injectivity of A into the abelianization of a central extension of C. This is a different argument from the regular-bundle section of A.40.

## Central extensions of the retained quotient

Let Pi=C2 amalg C3 with named s,r,tau=sr, let R=[R,Pi] be a closed normal subgroup, and set C=Pi/R. Let h0 be a full positive automorphism of Pi, fixing tau literally, preserving R, and with prescribed full s/r conjugators. Write h_C for its induced action. Since R is contained in Pi', C^ab=C6.

Let 1 -> A -> C# -> C ->1 be any central extension with A finite abelian. For arbitrary lifts b_s,b_r of s_C,r_C, put b_tau=b_s b_r. Then:

1. A injects into (C#)^ab.
2. h_C has an automorphism lift k of C# fixing A, preserving the conjugacy classes of b_s,b_r with the prescribed C-frames lifted, and fixing b_tau literally.

Here are elementary proofs that avoid an unproved equivariant lifting claim.

Pull back the central extension along Pi -> C, obtaining P=C# x_C Pi. Its kernel over Pi is A, and the subgroup R0={(1,r):r in R} is normal. We have R0=[R0,P] because its conjugation action is exactly that of Pi on R. Hence R0 lies in the closed commutator subgroup and P^ab -> (C#)^ab is an isomorphism.

A central extension of the free profinite product Pi has the presentation

    P=<A central, S,T | S^2=a, T^3=b>,

where S=(b_s,s), T=(b_r,r), a=b_s^2 and b=b_r^3 lie in A. This follows from the universal property of the amalgamated product of the two finite central extensions of C2 and C3 over A: its quotient by A is Pi, and the map to P is identity on A and Pi, hence an isomorphism.

In the abelianization, the only relations involving the new generators are2S=a and3T=b. They do not kill a nonzero element of A. Equivalently, embed the finite abelian group A in a divisible torsion abelian group, choose a half of a and a third of b, and take the finite subgroup generated by A and those chosen roots. The resulting finite abelian target of the displayed presentation embeds A. Thus A injects P^ab and hence (C#)^ab. This is the precise injectivity used below.

Lift the two full h0 conjugators arbitrarily from Pi to P. Send S,T to their conjugates by those lifts and fix A. The displayed presentation gives an endomorphism phi of P covering h0. It is an automorphism: it covers an automorphism of Pi and is the identity on its central kernel A, so is both injective and surjective.

For r in R0, phi(r) differs from the canonical element of R0 over h0(r) by an element chi(r) of A. Centrality makes chi a homomorphism R -> A, and equivariance of phi makes chi invariant under conjugation by Pi. It therefore factors through R/[R,Pi]=1. Thus phi preserves R0 and descends to an automorphism k of C# covering h_C and fixing A. Its specified conjugators project to the given full C-frames.

The product ST projects to tau. Since h0 fixes tau, phi(ST)(ST)^(-1) lies in A. But phi is the identity on P^ab, because it fixes A and conjugates S,T. Injectivity of A into P^ab forces this difference to be one. Consequently k fixes b_tau literally. This proves both assertions without assuming that C# splits over C.

## An actual orbit-one noncongruence quotient

For the C and R of A.40, take an epimorphism q0:Pi -> A9 defined by

    s=(12)(34)(56)(78),
    r=(138)(249)(576),
    tau=(149)(23758), of order15.

The script check_A48_orbit_one.py enumerates the generated181440-element group and verifies all orders. The restriction K -> A9 is onto: its image is normal, and a trivial image would make A9 a quotient of A5. Its kernel is Pi-normal, hence its A5 orbit has length one. Every one of the twelve cusps maps to a conjugate of tau^5 of order three, so this is not one of the regular tripod-support heads.

Take all kernels of epimorphisms Pi -> A9 whose cusp has order15. This is a nonempty finite GT0-invariant family. Distinct simple kernels have full product joint image T=A9^n. Joining the A5 quotient gives T times A5 by the same Goursat argument. R maps onto T, since C has no A9 composition factor. Thus for W=R intersect ker(q), where q:Pi -> T is the actual joint map,

    D=Pi/W=T times C.

The action of C on this finite head is now trivial, but the actual s/r components in T are nontrivial and cannot be removed by M-conjugacy. This is exactly where the regular proof did not apply.

## The actual central refinement is a central product

Set E=Pi/[W,Pi], and A=W/[W,Pi]. As in A.40, R=[R,Pi] forces M=R/[W,Pi] to be the finite perfect image of the universal central cover of T. Let C# be the full preimage in E of the pure C-section of T times C.

It centralizes M. Conjugation by an element of C# induces the identity on T; an automorphism of the perfect group M inducing the identity on its central quotient T is the identity, since its difference would be a homomorphism M -> A. Hence

    E=M C#,  [M,C#]=1,  M intersect C#=A.

This is a central product, with no assertion that C# -> C splits.

Choose m_s,m_r in M lifting the actual T-components of s,r, and put b_s=m_s^(-1)s, b_r=m_r^(-1)r in C#. Since the two factors commute, the actual cusp equals

    tau=m_s m_r b_s b_r.

Apply the preceding lemma to C# and the already constructed full positive h0 from A.33. The resulting k fixes A and b_s b_r and retains the prescribed C-frames. Therefore

    psi_E=id_M times k

is a well-defined actual automorphism of the central product E. It covers id_T times h_C, fixes the actual cusp literally, and sends actual s,r to conjugates with those prescribed C-frames: the chosen conjugators lie in C# and commute with m_s,m_r.

A.3/A.34 now gives a full positive h retaining the joint action and frames, and a=h c has Delta1 and actual negative involutory image on D. The invariant finite family is c-stable, so the image is exactly(c_T,beta_C).

## The order-two assertion at the actual refinement

Genuine c preserves E, M and C#, and has square one. Let b=psi_E c_E. On M, its square is the identity, since psi_E fixes M. On C, its square is beta_C^2=1. On A its square is also the identity. Therefore b^2 on C# is multiplication by a central character C -> A. It fixes the actual cusp, and fixes M, so fixes b_s b_r. Since tau_C generates C^ab=C6, that character is trivial. Hence b^2=1 on E itself.

There is no further scalar central layer. With W1=[W,Pi] and W2=[W1,Pi], let M2=R/W2. Relative perfectness makes M2 a finite perfect central cover of M. The preimage of A in M2 is central: its commutator with M2 would be a homomorphism from the perfect quotient M to the new central kernel. The preimage of C# in Pi/W2 also centralizes M2, because it acts trivially on M and M2 is perfect. Together these subgroups generate Pi/W2, so W/W2 is central there. Thus W1=W2.

## Scope and finite families

The same argument applies to any finite invariant family of simple Pi quotients excluded from C's composition factors. Distinct kernels give a full product of simple groups; no regular A5 orbit assumption is used. It can be imposed together with any finite regular tripod family from A.47: their K-head kernels have different cusp supports (all twelve versus three), hence are independent; after first taking the regular family's actual stabilized central refinement as the new base, the residual kernel is still relative-perfect and still maps onto the orbit-one simple factors. The central-extension lemma applies to that new base without requiring composition-factor exclusion there.

Finite unions and compactness thus retain the previous tower as well as the indicated orbit-one simple families and their scalar refinements. This still does not control general noncentral chief modules over a mixed solvable/congruence head. No full involution is asserted.
