## Exact passages opened and checked

- Proposition4.1, printed page342: a covering map induces isomorphisms
  on homotopy groups in degrees at least two.
- Theorem4.32, printed page366, and the complete proof on page367:
  for an (n-1)-connected space, n>=2, the first Hurewicz map is an
  isomorphism. The page367 discussion explicitly applies this to the
  universal cover and identifies pi2(X) with H2 of that cover.
- Theorem3A.3 and Corollary3A.4, printed page264: the homology universal
  coefficient short exact sequence. With H1 of a simply connected
  space zero, its degree-two sequence identifies mod-two homology
  with integral H2 tensor F2.

## Application and limits

B100 independently proves pi2(Z) nonzero and verifies the hypotheses
of Milnor's two-dimensional CW theorem using the closed smooth graph
sD(v,w)=1 in C3. A universal cover of that CW model is simply connected
and two-dimensional. Hurewicz identifies its nonzero H2 with pi2(Z).
Its H2 is the kernel of C2->C1 and hence free abelian. Consequently
H2 with F2 coefficients is nonzero. This disproves mod-two acyclicity
of the actual universal cover, as well as contractibility.

No claim is made that the particular torus sphere is primitive, that
its own mod-two reduction is nonzero, or that it has been identified
with a class in B92's presentation kernel or equivariant obstruction.
