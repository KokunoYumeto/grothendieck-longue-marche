Let T_A,T_B be the actual Cayley trees of the two free groups R_A,R_B,
using the audited common-R basis indexed by (retained F5 word,slot).
The standard product-tree two-cell module is

  P=C1(T_A,Z) tensor_Z C1(T_B,Z).

For every vertex a!=1 of T_A let L(a) be the unique oriented path from
1 to a. The boundary isomorphism

  C1(T_A,Z) -> ker(Z[R_A]->Z)

sends L(a) to a-1. Hence the L(a), a!=1, form a FREE ABELIAN BASIS of
C1(T_A). This is not just a spanning circuit system: it is a genuine
unimodular change of integral basis. Retain the standard positive
B-edge basis e(v,j), where v is a B vertex and j a positive R generator.
Then

  L(a) tensor e(v,j),    a!=1,

is a free abelian basis of the ENTIRE product-tree module P. A collected
finite dictionary of triples(a,v,j) is therefore an exact unique normal
form. Equality of these dictionaries decides equality of full chains.
No expanded rectangle enumeration is required.

Schreier-rewrite a paired F21 word with zero retained head using the

positive edge start v (v=b for s=+1; v=b*j^-1 for s=-1), add

  s L(a) tensor e(v,j).

At A letters add no two-chain term. If the word has trivial E image,
this sum is its COMPLETE two-chain filling in the product tree.
The path and generator words use persistent freely reduced tries, so
construction is linear in the literal word, apart from dictionary costs.

The full boundary identity, with H(a,v)=L(a) at B vertex v, is

  boundary(L(a) tensor e(v,j))
    =H(a,v)-H(a,v*j)+e_B(a,v,j)-e_B(1,v,j).

The H terms telescope against the actual A edges of the input word;
the projected B edges at A=1 cancel because its B projection is a
closed path in a tree. The remaining B edges are exactly its actual
B edges. This verifies the entire boundary without expansion. Since
the product of the two trees is contractible and has dimension2,

More usefully, a whole G monomial acts sparsely on the new basis.
Write g0=(u,v,t) in G=(R_A times R_B) semidirect T. Its action is

   =[L(u*tau_t(a))-L(u)]

This follows because translating a path from1 to a gives the path
fromu to u*tau_t(a), and a tree path fromu to x is L(x)-L(u).
The edge assertion is literal in the audited retained splitting:
x_(g,j)=g*a_j*g^-1, so tau_beta(t)(x_(g,j))=x_(beta(t)g,j).
Thus a positive B edge maps to one positive B edge. No arbitrary
old-base or source operator is substituted for this retained action;
those more general automorphisms may send an edge to a longer path.
Each monomial sends one normal-form term to at most TWO terms.
Consequently arbitrary finite Z[G] combinations of full node columns

mandatory standard-rectangle expansion. This is a reusable full-module
