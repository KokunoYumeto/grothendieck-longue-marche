4. Four fixed generators and their actual source classes

For each of the four fixed rows, a_nu=(b_nu,b_nu^(-1)).  The letter b_nu
here names a coefficient word in R, not a retained base loop.  In
particular b_nu=1 for nodes(12,1) and(16,1); it must not be confused with
the nontrivial actual source word rho_nu below.

Set

  t_nu=(b_nu^(-1),1),
  e_nu^half=(1,b_nu^(-1)),
  K_nu=t_nu R_nu,
  p_nu=e_nu^half e_nu.

The precise ordered equations are

  F(t_nu) F(a_nu)=t_nu,
  F(K_nu)=K_nu,
  (1+F)p_nu=K_nu.                                     (3)

Therefore v_nu=d(p_nu) is an intrinsic anti-invariant chain in the actual
image of d.  Equivalently, its half coefficient satisfies

  F(e_nu^half) a_nu=e_nu^half.

The order of these noncommuting coefficients matters.  It is checked
literally in both CE and DE independent certificates.

The four full anti-chains have respectively53,125,297,1 nonzero cells.

Applying that map to the full chains gives exactly

5. Conditional full E-span consequence

This paragraph is CONDITIONAL on the separate root elimination proving
that the twelve relations(1) span ker(d).  CE's twelve-relation program
proves their independence and involution structure, not that spanning
assertion.  The conditional conclusion is included so the elimination
certificate has a precise cohomological target.

If ker(d) is their direct free E-span, then it is the sum of four exchanged
rank-two E-summands and four fixed rank-one E-summands.  After the unit
change in(3), F acts on each fixed summand by coefficient interchange.
Each exchanged summand is induced as a Z[C2]-module, even when its table
sign is-1: choosing one generator and its F image gives a signed
permutation basis with free C2-orbits.  Its positive cohomology vanishes.

On a fixed summand Z[E], the E-basis element(u,v) is exchanged with(v,u).
The only fixed basis elements are the diagonal(u,u).  Thus

  H^2(C2,ker(d)) = F2[R]^4,

with its four summands represented by diagonal translates of K_nu.
The whole original domain Q0 is induced as a Z[C2]-module, since F
exchanges e_nu and f_nu.  Hence its H^1 and H^2 vanish.  The exact sequence
0 -> ker(d) -> Q0 -> L0 -> 0 then identifies H^1(C2,L0) with the displayed
H^2.  Formula(3) computes the connecting primitive explicitly, and(4)
computes its image in H^1(C2,P).

Consequently the INTRINSIC Tate image of the entire E-span L0 is exactly

  sum_(nu=(0,1),(8,1),(12,1),(16,1)) F2[R](rho_nu-1),  (5)

and is therefore zero after passage to the quotient by J_I.  This proves
no additional obstruction from this entire E-span, if the separate
spanning certificate succeeds.  It does not exhaust the old-base
translates of the64 node columns, nor prove I=R or nonlinear fixed lifting.

The direct cohomology argument gives the same conclusion as section5.
For a fixed raw generator C_nu, put v_nu=(1,b_nu^(-1))C_nu as in section4.
Then F(v_nu)=-v_nu.  Its free E-summand therefore has negative coefficient
interchange.  The basis elements(s,t) with s!=t occur in induced pairs;

It follows that H^1(C2,L0)=F2[R]^4 and H^2(C2,L0)=0.

  Tate((s,s)v_nu)=s(rho_nu-1).

E-span.  The four rho_nu are actual positive first-sheet source meridians.
