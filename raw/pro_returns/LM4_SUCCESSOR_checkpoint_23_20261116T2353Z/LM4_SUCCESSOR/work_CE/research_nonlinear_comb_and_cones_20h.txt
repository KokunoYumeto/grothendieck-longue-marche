2. Exact nonlinear rewriting

factorization

  prefix = n * b * a * t,

where n is a word in the displayed N_gen basis, a in R_A, b in
R_B, and t in the retained free group. Retained letters update t.
An A letter at head t becomes the appropriate A Schreier letter
and updates a. A positive B letter x gives

  b*a*x = c(a,b,x)*(b*x)*a,

so append c(a,b,x). A negative B letter gives

  b*a*x^-1 = c(a,b*x^-1,x)^-1*(b*x^-1)*a.

identities hold literally in the free product R_A*R_B. For a
negative step the generator is based at the FOLLOWING B vertex.
Immediate inverse free cancellation is performed in n. At an
N_gen word the final a,b,t are1, leaving the exact free word n.
This is a nonlinear identity, not a homological calculation.

After abelianization each c(a,b,x) is the root-path chain
L_A(a) cross e_B(b,x). Thus the abelianized dictionary is exactly
B103's unique normal form, including all retained word labels.

7. Uniform nonlinear coefficient transport, with its section cocycle

The group G has elements g=(u,v,t), with u in R_A, v in R_B and
t in T, and product

  (u,v,t)(a,b,s)=(u*tau_t(a),v*tau_beta(t)(b),t*s).

Use the literal comb section into W

  q_g=v_B*u_A*t.

It is a set section, NOT a group homomorphism. Write rho_g for
conjugation by q_g on N_gen. For h=(a,b,s), direct free reduction
gives

  q_g*q_h=omega(g,h)*q_(gh),
  omega(g,h)=v*[u,tau_beta(t)(b)]*v^-1 in N_gen,
  rho_g*rho_h=Inn(omega(g,h))*rho_(gh).

All commutators in this note are [x,y]=x*y*x^-1*y^-1. Associativity
of the three literal section words gives the full nonabelian
cocycle identity

  omega(g,h)*omega(gh,k)
    =rho_g(omega(h,k))*omega(g,hk).

Thus replacing this crossed action by a strict G action on N_gen
would discard an actual correction. The G action on its
abelianization is strict because these inner corrections vanish
there. Different W lifts of the same g also differ by an inner
N_gen action; this is why the whole normal closure in section4

Here is a uniform exact formula on every nonlinear comb generator.
For c(a,b,x)=b*[a,x]*b^-1, put

  a'=tau_t(a), b'=tau_beta(t)(b), x'=tau_beta(t)(x),
  P_g(b)=v*[u,b']*v^-1.

Then

To verify the formula, use

  [u*a',x']*[u,x']^-1=u*[a',x']*u^-1,

and expand both sides. The correction P_g(b) is itself a finite
