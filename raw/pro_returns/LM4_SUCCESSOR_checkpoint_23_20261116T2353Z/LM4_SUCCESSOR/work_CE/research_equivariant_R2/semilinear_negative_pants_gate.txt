Actual negative pants actions exclude a nontrivial PSL2 field component
Research consequence,16November2026; no full group classification input.

Let F be the free profinite group on x,y,z with xyz=1. Let q:F->PSL2(k)
be an epimorphism, k a finite field of order at least4. Suppose a full
peripheral automorphism Phi of multiplier -1 stabilizes ker(q), and
q Phi=beta q, where beta is induced by a projective semilinear matrix
(G,sigma). Then beta is induced by a projective LINEAR matrix. In
particular the nonsplit diagonal-field outer involutions used in the
M10 example cannot occur, for any odd square field, in an actual
negative peripheral source action.

Proof. Choose determinant-one lifts X,Y of q(x),q(y), and let q2:F->SL2(k)
be their free evaluation. Put Z=(XY)^-1. The semilinear automorphism
beta2(A)=G A^sigma G^-1 of SL2(k) lifts beta. Since q2 Phi and beta2 q2
have identical projective images, their pointwise ratio is a continuous
central character eta:F->{+1,-1}; in characteristic2 this character is
trivial. In particular eta(x)eta(y)eta(z)=1. Write X'=q2 Phi(x) and
Y'=q2 Phi(y). The peripheral inverse-class conditions in F imply
 tr(X')=tr(X), tr(Y')=tr(Y), tr(X'Y')=tr(XY),
because determinant-one inversion preserves trace and conjugation does
not change it.

Here is the elementary simultaneous-conjugacy lemma required at this
step. For an absolutely irreducible pair X,Y inSL2(k), set
 a=tr X,b=tr Y,c=tr XY. Cayley-Hamilton gives
 X^2=aX-I, Y^2=bY-I,
 YX=-XY+bX+aY+(c-ab)I.
Thus all words lie in the span of I,X,Y,XY. Absolute irreducibility
makes the generated algebra M2(k), so these four matrices form a basis.
The same is true for X',Y': their projective images generatePSL2(k),
which acts absolutely irreducibly. All multiplication in the displayed
basis is determined by a,b,c. Therefore X->X',Y->Y' extends to an
algebra automorphism of M2(k). Every matrix-algebra automorphism is
conjugation by some B inGL2(k): for completeness, transport the standard
matrix units, choose a nonzero vector in the image of E11, and use the
image of E21 to construct the corresponding basis. It follows that
 X'=BXB^-1, Y'=BYB^-1.
Projectivizing removes eta and proves beta=Ad(B) on both q(x),q(y), hence
on the whole PSL2(k). This is the asserted projective linearity.

The absolute irreducibility input does not require the chosen lifts to
generate allSL2(k). Their projective image is the wholePSL2(k), so their
matrix algebra contains the standard projective elementary-unipotent
behavior and is M2(k). Equivalently, no common line over an algebraic
closure can be invariant under all ofPSL2(k). The same holds afterPhi.

Scope. This does not settle arbitrary finite simple residuals, nor a
transported reflection-tuple count. It explains why the entire most
immediate nonsplit-semilinear family fails the actual source gate, not
just the particular(4,4,4)A6 head. It also does not assert that a negative
full source action is involutory: only the multiplier -1 peripheral
conditions and the stable quotient are used. The argument is consistent
with A.28's explicit three-sign spin obstruction.
