\statement{A.99}{Proved}{The original regular cusp frame in the same sphere kernel}
In the literal rank-two copy of A.98, write
\(\alpha_* =\gamma|_{\widehat F(A,S)}\),
\(\varphi=\varphi_\alpha(A,S)\), \(u=u_\alpha(A,S)\).
Let \(\iota_*\) swap A,S and put
\[
 v_*=\operatorname{Inn}(u^{-1})\iota_*,\quad
 \tau_*=\alpha_*v_*,\quad L=SA,\quad z_*=(AS)^{-1}.
 \tag{A.99.1}
\]
Let t be the A.98 normalized negative-cusp transporter. The unique
zero-abelianization regular transporter of A.86 is exactly
\[
 h_{\rm reg}=S^{-1}tA\varphi^{-1}Su,
 \qquad \tau_*(z_*)=h_{\rm reg}^{-1}z_*^{-1}h_{\rm reg}.
 \tag{A.99.2}
\]
If \(h_{\rm reg}=q_*\tau_*(q_*)^{-1}\) is the actual A.86 norm
frame, with \((q_*)_{\rm ab}=(\xi,-\xi)\) in the (A,S)-basis,
then
\[
\begin{gathered}
 h_0=S q_*\tau_*(q_*)^{-1}u^{-1}S^{-1}
                  \varphi A^{-1}p,\\
 (q_*)_{\rm ab}=(-\xi,2\xi,0)
                    \quad\hbox{in the basis }(L,A,C).
\end{gathered}
\tag{A.99.3}
\]
These are full-word identities retaining the same h0 and both based
cabling factors; they do not by themselves determine xi.

\begin{proof}
In this literal copy,
\(\alpha_*(A)=A^{-1}\),
\(\alpha_*(S)=\varphi^{-1}S^{-1}\varphi\), and
\(\alpha_*(u)=\varphi^{-1}u\). The A.85--A.86 commuting
involutions transport through the actual free-factor isomorphism.
Their definitions give
\[
 \tau_*(A)=u^{-1}S^{-1}u,
 \qquad v_*(L)=u^{-1}ALA^{-1}u.
\]
Apply alpha* to the second equality in its displayed order and
use \(\alpha_*(L)=t^{-1}L^{-1}t\). This gives
\[
 \tau_*(L)=k^{-1}L^{-1}k,
 \qquad k=tA\varphi^{-1}u.
 \tag{A.99.4}
\]
A.98 gives \(t_{\rm ab}=-A\), and phi,u are commutator words;
therefore k has zero abelianization.

The exact basepoint identity is \(L=A^{-1}z_*^{-1}A\).
Applying tau* shows that
\(A^{-1}h_{\rm reg}\tau_*(A)\) transports L to its inverse.
Its abelianization is \(-L\). Left multiplication by L preserves
the transport equation and makes its abelianization zero.
The full centralizer of the primitive L proves uniqueness of the
zero-abelianization transporter, so
\[
 k=S h_{\rm reg}\tau_*(A)
      =S h_{\rm reg}u^{-1}S^{-1}u.
\]
Solving this equality proves (A.99.2). Substituting the actual
A.86 frame and \(h_0=tp\) proves the first equation of (A.99.3).
Since \(S=LA^{-1}\), the coordinate change gives the second.

For the standard reflection \(\varphi=u=1,t=A^{-1}\), the formula
gives \(h_{\rm reg}=1\), agreeing with the literal negative-swap
regular involution. More generally the odd A-coordinate of h0 is
cancelled in (A.99.4). Abelianizing (A.99.3) recovers
\((h_0)_{\rm ab}=-A\) independently of xi. Thus this bridge alone
does not identify \(a_{\rm reg}=1-4\xi\) with
\(d_\alpha=2k_\alpha+1\). The latter is proved separately in A.100;
even that equality does not imply \(\Omega_\alpha=1\).
The relative pro-two kernel sees only the dyadic image of these
coordinates, and its action factors through the pro-two base by
A.98. No odd information or missing positive-cusp relation is
introduced by replacing the actual correction with its normalized
reflection.
\end{proof}
