\statement{A.131}{Proved}{The explicit elliptic pentagon transporter and vanishing of the actual common word}
Use the actual full profinite elliptic marking of A.121 and A.126.
For every profinite commutator word \(w\) satisfying the original
marked four-strand pentagon, the unique pure transporter of its
elliptic cubic is explicitly
\[
 p_w=w(X_3^{-1},K_2)w(X,\sigma^2)
     =w(\sigma^2,\tau^2)^{-1}w(X,K)w(X_2,\tau^2).
 \tag{A.131.1}
\]
It belongs to the full pure derived subgroup and has all three
based deletion images equal to \(w(X,K)\). Consequently the
actual common word of A.126 satisfies \(a_w=1\).
For the canonical \(u\) at the full-GT scope, this gives
\(a_u=1\); the actual base parameter \(b_0\), specified boundary
and original CM class are not evaluated.
\begin{proof}
Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 a=\sigma^2,\quad b=\tau^2,\quad C=\sigma\tau,
 \quad L=C^{-1},\quad \Pi_0=LY,\\
 K=[Y,X]=\sigma\tau^2\sigma,\quad K_2=\tau\sigma^2\tau,
 \quad z=YX^{-1}Y^{-1}.
\end{gathered}
\]
For the word values write
\[
\begin{gathered}
 U=w(X,K),\quad Z=w(X_2,b),\quad A=w(X,a),\\
 E_w=w(X_3^{-1},K_2),\quad B_w=w(a,b).
\end{gathered}
\]
The original mixed relation gives
\(YX_2Y^{-1}=X_2a^{-1}=\operatorname{Ad}(C)(X)\).
Also \([Y,b]=1\) and \(\operatorname{Ad}(C)(a)=b\).
Therefore the following is an exact based pair conjugation:
\[
 \Pi_0(X_2,b)\Pi_0^{-1}=(X,a),\qquad
 A=\Pi_0Z\Pi_0^{-1}.                              \tag{A.131.2}
\]
For the other pair, \(z=X^{-1}K^{-1}\) and the definitions give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 LzL^{-1}
 &=\tau^{-1}\sigma^{-1}X^{-1}\sigma^{-1}\tau^{-1}
   =\tau^{-1}X_2^{-1}\tau^{-1}=X_3^{-1},\\
 LKL^{-1}&=K_2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the original cubic word is
\[
 \Pi_w=Lw(z,K)YU^{-1}B_w=E_w\Pi_0U^{-1}B_w.
\tag{A.131.3}
\]
The original type-B image of the marked \(P_4\) pentagon,
proved with all five pairs in A.121.5, is precisely
\[
 B_wE_wA=UZ.                                         \tag{A.131.4}
\]
It uses the homomorphism
\((B_4/Z(B_4))\times_{S_4}S_3\to B_{1,3}\),
\(\sigma_0^2\mapsto X,\sigma_1\mapsto\sigma,
\sigma_2\mapsto\tau\), and only the commuting-decoration
property of a commutator word. Combining (A.131.2)--(A.131.4),
in their printed order, gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Pi_w
 &=E_w\Pi_0ZA^{-1}E_w^{-1}\\
 &=E_wA\Pi_0A^{-1}E_w^{-1}
  =(E_wA)\Pi_0(E_wA)^{-1}.
\end{aligned}                                      \tag{A.131.5}
\]
This proves both expressions in (A.131.1). The base \(\Pi_0\)
has exact order three: the original braid and \(Y_i\) relations
give \((YL)^3=YY_3Y_2=1\), while its permutation is a three-cycle.
Thus (A.131.5) also independently proves the cubic, without the
long cancellation repaired in the separate A.121 correction.

Every argument in \(E_w,A\) is pure. Since \(w\) is a closed
commutator word, \(E_wA\in P'\). A.124 gives
\(C_G(\Pi_0)=\langle\Pi_0\rangle\), whose intersection with
\(P\) is trivial. Hence two pure transporters in (A.131.5)
coincide, so this is the actual unique \(p_w\), not a chosen
lift of a quotient tuple.

For the entire based tuple \(q=(\pi_1,\pi_2,\pi_3)\), A.126 gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
 q(X)=(1,X,X),\quad q(X_2)=(X,1,X^{-1}),\\
 q(a)=(1,1,K),\quad q(b)=(K,1,1),\quad q(K)=(1,K,K).
\end{gathered}
\]
Since \(XX_2X_3=1\), one has \(q(X_3^{-1})=(X,X,1)\).
Also \(K_2=LKL^{-1}\), and the exact inverse cyclic based
formula with \(j(K)=K\) gives \(q(K_2)=(K,K,1)\).
Consequently
\[
 q(E_w)=(U,U,1),\qquad q(A)=(1,1,U),\qquad
 q(p_w)=(U,U,U).                                     \tag{A.131.6}
\]
The same tuple follows independently from the second formula in
(A.131.1): \(q(B_w)=1\), \(q(U)=(1,U,U)\) and
\(q(Z)=(U,1,1)\). The common word was defined by
\(q(p_w)=(Ua_w,U\theta(a_w),Ua_w)\). Its first coordinate
in (A.131.6) proves \(a_w=1\) in the full group.
Continuity follows directly from the displayed word evaluations.

For the canonical full-GT \(u\), A.37 supplies its original
pentagon. The formulas of A.127 therefore reduce exactly to
\[
\begin{gathered}
 t_u=b_0,\qquad \ell=b_0^{-1},\qquad
 [z_u]=\partial(b_0^{-1}),\\
 q(h_u)=(Ub_0U^{-1},U\theta(b_0)U^{-1},Ub_0U^{-1}).
\end{gathered}
\]
This uses the original pentagon, beyond the reduced cubic/tuple/norm
conditions that allowed arbitrary common words. It does not evaluate
\(b_0\), imply \(h_u=1\), establish the specified boundary
\(a(k)=a_B\), or identify the elliptic class with the original
CM frame. No canonical-u pentagon is asserted at the bare GT0 scope.
\end{proof}
