\subsection*{An actual noncommuting source pair and a strict one-sided quotient}

\statement{B.131}{Proved}{The corrected d-contact does not commute with the inner source loop, discretely or in the actual full completion}
\label{stmt:B131}
Use the complete actual presentation of B.123 and the actual kernels of
B.127--B.128:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 F=F(U_1,\ldots,U_8)*T,\quad T=F(f,g,x),\quad L=\ker(F\to T),\\
 W=F/N,\quad J=L/N,\quad \Gamma_0=W\rtimes S,\quad S=F(m,v).
\end{gathered}
\]
The ordered family labels are
$(a,B,d,e,\bar a,\bar B,\bar d,\bar e)$, where
$\bar U=cUc$. The normal subgroup $N$ includes all forty actual ordinary
node seeds, all their signed source translates, and their full
$F$-normal closure. Write $s_1,\ldots,s_4$ for the four actual source
contact words of B.123, and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-contact-basis}
\begin{aligned}
 A_s&=s_1s_4^{-1}s_1^{-1},&
 B_s&=A_s^{-1}s_1^{-1}A_s,\\
 D_s&=s_2s_1s_3^{-1}s_1^{-1}s_2^{-1},&
 E_s&=s_2^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-noncommutation}
 [m,D_s]\ne1\quad\hbox{in }\Gamma_0
 \quad\hbox{and in }\widehat\Gamma_0.
\end{equation}
More precisely, there is a split epimorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-free-retract}
 q:J\longrightarrow F(X,Y),\qquad
 X\longmapsto B,\quad Y\longmapsto x\bar d x^{-1}
 \quad\hbox{under its section},
\end{equation}
which sends $[m,D_s]$ to $YX^{-1}Y^{-1}X$. There is a continuous split
analogue from the \emph{actual} closed family kernel
$\mathcal J=\ker(\widehat W\to\widehat T)$ onto
$\widehat F(X,Y)$. No identification $\mathcal J=\widehat J$ is made.
The word is detected by the order-eight group $\operatorname{UT}_3(\mathbb F_2)$.

Let $H_5=\langle s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4,m\rangle\leq\Gamma_0$.
Its image under the actual one-sided filling is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-one-sided-group}
 P_1=\bigl(F(A_s,D_s)\times\mathbb Z_m\bigr)*F(B_s,E_s),
\end{equation}
and the induced epimorphism $H_5\to P_1$ is proper. If $E_5$ is the
abstract free group on $A_s,B_s,D_s,E_s,m$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-kernel-sandwich}
 \langle\!\langle[m,A_s]\rangle\!\rangle_{E_5}
 \ \subseteq\ \ker(E_5\to H_5)
 \ \subsetneq\
 \langle\!\langle[m,A_s],[m,D_s]\rangle\!\rangle_{E_5}.
\end{equation}
No equality or strictness is asserted at the first inclusion. The
specified failure of injectivity also holds for abstract full completions
and for the actual closure map induced by the whole one-sided filling,
without identifying subgroup completions with their ambient closures.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.131.1. The complete relation family and its uniform character.}
Define
\[
 \chi_\Sigma:T\to\mathbb Z,\qquad
 \chi_\Sigma(f)=\chi_\Sigma(g)=1,\quad\chi_\Sigma(x)=0.
\]
The exact retained actions in equation~\eqref{eq:B128-retained-actions}
preserve this character. The heads in equation~\eqref{eq:B128-head-table}
have values independent of the family label:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-uniform-heads}
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 s&m&m^{-1}&v&v^{-1}\\\hline
 \chi_\Sigma(h_{s,i})&0&0&-1&1.
\end{array}
\end{equation}
For the inverse operators this follows also from
$h_{s^{-1},i}=\phi_s^{-1}(h_{s,i}^{-1})$. The full nonlinear formula is
\[
 \alpha_s(X_{t,i})
   =q_{s,t,i}X_{\phi_s(t)h_{s,i},i}q_{s,t,i}^{-1},
 \qquad X_{t,i}=tU_it^{-1},\quad q_{s,t,i}\in L.
\]
Thus the difference between the two retained $\chi_\Sigma$ values of
a pair of peripheral cores is unchanged by every signed source word.
Retained-deck conjugation shifts both values equally, and conjugation
by $L$ changes only their nonlinear connectors.

The complete forty-seed factorization of B.127 has exactly two seeds
with absolute colors $B,\bar d$. Putting $B$ first, their exact heads are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-two-seeds}
\begin{array}{c|c|c|r}
 \text{seed}&B\text{ head}&\bar d\text{ head}&
      \chi_\Sigma(B\text{ head})-\chi_\Sigma(\bar d\text{ head})\\\hline
 \text{minus, pair\_b\_hdh, sheet 1}&1&g&-1\\
 \text{plus, pair\_b\_hdh, sheet 1}&x^{-1}&g^{-1}&1.
\end{array}
\end{equation}
Every signed operator preserves the positive peripheral color.
Consequently every relation in the whole defining family with these
two colors has odd character difference. This assertion includes
arbitrary source words, arbitrary retained translates, factor signs,
and all nonlinear conjugators; it is not a bounded orbit calculation.

\emph{B.131.2. A free quotient that kills the whole normal closure.}
Let $D_8=(C_2)^3$ record the individual retained $f,g,x$ exponents
modulo two, and let
\[
 E_{64}=F(y_{i,r}:1\leq i\leq8,\ r\in D_8).
\]
The group $D_8$ acts by translation of the residue index. The free
universal property gives
\[
 F\longrightarrow E_{64}\rtimes D_8,\qquad
 U_i\longmapsto y_{i,0},\qquad f,g,x\longmapsto
 \text{the three standard translations}.
\]
Its image on $L$ is in $E_{64}$. Project this free fiber to $F(X,Y)$
by retaining only
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-selected-pair}
 y_{B,(0,0,0)}\longmapsto X,
 \qquad y_{\bar d,(0,0,1)}\longmapsto Y.
\end{equation}
All other generators are sent to one. This projection is used only on
the free fiber; no retained or source action on $F(X,Y)$ is assumed.

For a node commutator to survive, both its peripheral cores must survive.
They would have to have the colors and residues in
\eqref{eq:B131-selected-pair}. Both residues have even $\chi_\Sigma$,
contrary to \eqref{eq:B131-two-seeds} and its uniform all-word consequence.
At least one core is therefore killed. Its entire conjugate is killed,
regardless of the nonlinear connector, so the complete commutator is
killed. This works for every generator of $N$ and every full normal
conjugate. The map descends to $q:J\to F(X,Y)$.
The literal words $B$ and $x\bar d x^{-1}$ map to $X$ and $Y$, giving
the section and proving \eqref{eq:B131-free-retract}.

\emph{B.131.3. Exact source evaluation and the finite detector.}
Here the convention is exactly B.123's
$\alpha_s(w)=sws^{-1}$. To evaluate $q$ on a full $F$ word, scan it
from left to right, update its three retained parity coordinates on
$f,g,x$, and emit a signed $X$ or $Y$ precisely at the two positions
specified in \eqref{eq:B131-selected-pair}. Retained letters emit nothing.
Substitution of the complete raw source words and complete signed
operators of B.123 gives the following small table:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-source-image-table}
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 &s_1&s_2&s_3&s_4\\\hline
 q&X^{-1}&1&Y^{-1}&1\\
 q\alpha_m&1&1&Y^{-1}&1\\
 q\alpha_{m^{-1}}&1&1&Y^{-1}&1.
\end{array}
\end{equation}
This computation uses whole words; it does not discard source or
peripheral conjugators. The same scan is equivalently evaluation in
$E_{64}\rtimes D_8$ followed by its free-fiber projection, so free
cancellation during or after the scan gives the same result.
In particular
\[
 q(D_s)=X^{-1}YX,\qquad
 q\alpha_m(D_s)=q\alpha_{m^{-1}}(D_s)=Y.
\]
Both signed defects therefore map to $YX^{-1}Y^{-1}X$. The full input
defect lengths are $798$ and $648$; the reduced output is the displayed
four-letter word. The already proved $A_s$ commutation gives an empty
output as a separate control.

Write elements of $\operatorname{UT}_3(\mathbb F_2)$ as triples, with
\[
 (a,b,c)(a',b',c')=(a+a',b+b',c+c'+ab').
\]
The assignments $X\mapsto(1,0,0)$ and $Y\mapsto(0,1,0)$ send
$YX^{-1}Y^{-1}X$ to $(0,0,1)$. This is an explicit nonidentity element
of a group of order eight and proves discrete noncommutation.

\emph{B.131.4. Actual compact maps and a whole-group finite target.}
The finite translation action extends to $\widehat E_{64}$, so the
universal continuous map
\[
 \widehat F\longrightarrow\widehat E_{64}\rtimes D_8
\]
exists. Its restriction to
$\mathcal L=\ker(\widehat F\to\widehat T)$ lands in
$\widehat E_{64}$. Apply the continuous free-fiber projection onto
$\widehat F(X,Y)$ defined by \eqref{eq:B131-selected-pair}.
It kills $N$ by the preceding proof and therefore kills its actual
closure $\mathcal N$ in $\widehat F$: its restricted kernel is closed
in the closed subgroup $\mathcal L$. B.128 gives the exact identity
$\mathcal J=\mathcal L/\mathcal N$. We obtain the claimed continuous
split epimorphism, with the same literal section. The order-eight
map detects the compact defect. B.123 supplies $\widehat W$ as the
actual closed fiber of $\widehat\Gamma_0$.

For completeness this detection also has a direct finite-index
construction. Let $F^{(8)}=\ker(F\to D_8)$. Its entire image lies in
$E_{64}$. The free-two projection followed by the order-eight map
kills $N$ and is a finite quotient of $F^{(8)}/N$, an index-eight
subgroup of $W$. Core its kernel in $W$; this finite-index normal
subgroup still excludes $[m,D_s]$. Since $W$ is finitely generated,
the intersection of all subgroups with index at most that finite index
is a characteristic finite-index refinement. The actual $S$ action
then factors through the finite automorphism group of the resulting
finite $W$ quotient. Its finite semidirect product with that image of
$S$ is a finite quotient of $\Gamma_0$ detecting the specified word.
This proves \eqref{eq:B131-noncommutation} without identifying the
completion of an infinite discrete kernel with an actual closure.

\emph{B.131.5. The exact whole one-sided subgroup.}
Kill the four barred family generators in the entire B.123 presentation.
Each signed source operator preserves their normal closure and every
node has a barred core, so all forty seeds and all their translates
are killed. This is a quotient of the whole group, with free fiber
$F(a,B,d,e,f,g,x)$ and the actual source action.
Put
\[
 E=fde d^{-1}f^{-1},\qquad h=a^{-1}ga,\qquad y=xaf.
\]
Then $(a,B,d,E,f,h,y)$ is a free basis, with inverse
$e=d^{-1}f^{-1}Efd$, $g=aha^{-1}$, $x=yf^{-1}a^{-1}$.
The exact contact images are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-one-sided-contacts}
\begin{aligned}
 s_1&=aB^{-1}a^{-1},&s_2&=E^{-1},\\
 s_3&=(aBa^{-1}E)d^{-1}(E^{-1}aB^{-1}a^{-1}),&
 s_4&=aBa^{-1}B^{-1}a^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Their literal inverse basis is \eqref{eq:B131-contact-basis}, mapping
to $a,B,d,E$. The positive source operator is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-partial-inner}
\begin{aligned}
 &a\mapsto a,\quad d\mapsto d,\quad y\mapsto y,\\
 &B\mapsto\kappa B\kappa^{-1},\quad
 E\mapsto\kappa E\kappa^{-1},\qquad \kappa=f^{-1}h,\\
 &f\mapsto f^{-1}hf,\qquad h\mapsto f.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Its inverse fixes $a,d,y$, conjugates $B,E$ by $hf^{-1}$, and sends
$f\mapsto h$, $h\mapsto hfh^{-1}$. These are substitutions in the
actual B.123 maps, not an action chosen for the desired conclusion.

Write the free fiber as
$A*Q*P$, where $A=F(a,d,y)$, $Q=F(f,h)$ and $P=F(B,E)$.
For the recursively chosen conjugators
$\alpha_m^n(P)=\kappa_nP\kappa_n^{-1}$, set $q_0=hf$ and
$q_1=fh=fq_0f^{-1}$. Direct calculation gives
$\alpha_m(q_0)=q_0$, $\alpha_m^2|_Q=\operatorname{Inn}(q_0^{-1})$,
and $\kappa_2=q_0^{-1}q_1$. Thus, for every integer $n$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B131-all-integer-conjugators}
 \kappa_{2n}=q_0^{-n}q_1^n,
 \qquad
 \kappa_{2n+1}=q_0^{-n}(f^{-1}h)q_1^n.
\end{equation}
The inverse recursion proves the same formulas for negative $n$.
Even and odd terms have different abelianizations. Equality of two
even terms would make a nonzero power of $q_0$ centralize $f$, using
$\kappa_{2n}=q_0^{-n}fq_0^nf^{-1}$. The centralizer of the free generator
$f$ in $Q$ is $\langle f\rangle$, and the $h$ exponent excludes this.
For odd terms use
$\kappa_{2n+1}=q_0^{-n}(f^{-1}hf)q_0^nf^{-1}$.
After conjugation by $f$, equality would put a nonzero power of
$q_1=fh$ in $\langle h\rangle$, excluded by its $f$ exponent.
All the $\kappa_n$ are therefore distinct. The used generator
centralizers follow immediately from reduced words in a free group.

The kernel of $A*Q*P\to Q$ is the free product of all $Q$-indexed
conjugates of $A*P$. Hence the subgroup generated by $a,d$ and all
integer translates of $B,E$ is exactly
\[
 F(a,d)*\mathop{*}_{n\in\mathbb Z}F(B_n,E_n),\qquad
 (B_n,E_n)=\kappa_n(B,E)\kappa_n^{-1}.
\]
At the identity index of $Q$, the $A$ and $P$ factors are still distinct free factors;
the omitted generator $y$ introduces no relation. The element $m$
fixes $a,d$ and shifts $B_n,E_n$ to $B_{n+1},E_{n+1}$. Conversely the
presentation in \eqref{eq:B131-one-sided-group} unfolds to exactly this
free product. This proves the exact one-sided subgroup description,
with every signed power of $m$ included.

\emph{B.131.6. The strict actual comparison and its limits.}
B.130 proves the actual relation $[m,A_s]=1$. The one-sided quotient
and the preceding exact presentation give both inclusions in
\eqref{eq:B131-kernel-sandwich}. The upper one is strict because
\eqref{eq:B131-noncommutation} shows that its generator $[m,D_s]$
is not in the actual source kernel.
The finite whole-group detector restricts to $H_5$, so the same word
survives in $\widehat H_5$ while mapping to one in $\widehat P_1$.
For actual ambient closures the continuous whole one-sided filling
likewise kills a nonzero point of $\overline H_5$. No claim that
$\widehat H_5=\overline H_5$ or that the target closure is
$\widehat P_1$ is needed. The sixth source generator $v$, mixed source
words, the full source presentation, and the selected centralizer
remain outside this conclusion.
\end{proof}
