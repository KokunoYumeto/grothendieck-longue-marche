\subsection*{The actual regional retraction kernels and the mixed square filling}

\statement{B.141}{Proved}{The regional neck-retraction kernel has an explicit graph presentation, and the complete initial filling obstructs the specified opposite-kernel retractions}
\label{stmt:B141}
Let $r:R^0\to K=F(a,B,d,e,f)$ be the actual B.114/B.117
neck retraction, with $B=CbC$, $h=fk$ and $k_1=CkC$.
Its kernel $N_{\rm reg}$ is the explicit graph group described below.
The actual subgroup $E_1=C\langle K,k\rangle C$ meets this kernel
in a full coordinate graph subgroup. The entire actual neck action
is given by retained translations, the height shift and the ordered
section correction in \eqref{eq:B141-cocycle}.

For the actual unfilled $V=R_L*_KR_R$ of B.134, its common-retract
kernel therefore has a faithful ordinary graph presentation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-ambient-kernel}
\ker(V\to K)=N_L*N_R=A(\Sigma_L\sqcup\Sigma_R).
\end{equation}
All ten initial relations have the exact mixed form
\eqref{eq:B141-mixed-family} and its actual $\theta$-image.
In particular the existing retraction $V\to R_L$, identity on
$R_L$ and the common-neck retraction on $R_R$, does not descend
through the actual initial quotient $V_*$, either discretely or
continuously through its full completion. The mirrored retraction
fails in the same way. This is failure of specified retractions;
it does not assert that their nontrivial regional images are killed
in $V_*$, or that either local quotient fails to embed.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.141.1. The actual height graph and the same kernel.}
Use the complete B.112 height presentation
\[
R^0=A(\Gamma)\rtimes\langle h\rangle,
\quad U_n=h^nUh^{-n},\quad\bar U_n=h^nCUCh^{-n},
\quad k_0=k,\quad k_1=CkC.
\]
Its vertices are $U_n,\bar U_n$, for $U=a,b,d,e$ and every
$n\in\mathbb Z$, and $k_0,k_1$. Its entire edge list consists of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-original-edges}
\begin{gathered}
U_n--\bar U_n\quad(U=a,b,d,e),\\
a_n--b_n,\quad\bar a_n--d_n,\quad
\bar a_n--e_n,\quad d_n--\bar e_n,\\
b_n--d_{n+1},\quad b_n--e_{n+1},\qquad
k_{n\bmod2}--U_n\quad(U=a,b,d,e),
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
and all barred images. The bar exchanges the two k vertices.
There is no edge between them. Conjugation by h shifts family
levels and exchanges the k vertices.

Put $A_n=a_n$, $B_n=\bar b_n$, $D_n=d_n$, $E_n=e_n$ and
\[
\Delta=\{A_n,B_n,D_n,E_n:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]
This is an independent full vertex set. Deleting all other vertices
is h-equivariant and gives an actual retraction
\[
q_h:R^0\to F(\Delta)\rtimes\langle h\rangle
       =K_h=F(a,B,d,e,h).
\]
The last free presentation follows by eliminating the level names
using the h action. Identify this abstract free group with K by
h$\mapsto f$, fixing the other four generators. The composite
fixes the actual $f=hk_0^{-1}$ and a,B,d,e; it kills k and the
opposite four family generators. These are precisely the actual
retraction values on the whole generating set of $R^0$. Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-same-kernel}
N_{\rm reg}=\ker q_h=\ker\bigl(A(\Gamma)\to F(\Delta)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Only the auxiliary section changed; h and f were not equated in $R^0$.

\emph{B.141.2. A full graph-kernel presentation.}
For any graph $\Gamma$ with induced subgraph $\Delta$, put
$T=A(\Delta)$ and let $\mathcal D$ be the other vertices.
For $v\in\mathcal D$, set
$L_v=A(\operatorname{link}_\Gamma(v)\cap\Delta)\le T$.
Define $\Sigma$ with vertices
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-expanded-vertices}
(gL_v,v)\qquad(g\in T,\ v\in\mathcal D).
\end{equation}
For each original deleted/deleted edge $v--w$ and each $g\in T$,
join $(gL_v,v)$ to $(gL_w,w)$. The T action is left translation.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-general-kernel}
A(\Gamma)=A(\Sigma)\rtimes T.
\end{equation}

Here are both presentation maps. Send $(gL_v,v)$ to $gvg^{-1}$.
Changing its representative by $L_v$ has no effect, since every
retained neighbor of v commutes with v. Edges become conjugates
of original commutators, and the T action agrees with conjugation.
Conversely send retained vertices into T and a deleted vertex v
to $(L_v,v)$. A retained/deleted edge holds because its retained
letter stabilizes that coset; a deleted/deleted edge is the case
g$=1$; retained relations hold in T. The maps are inverse on every
named vertex and T. This proves \eqref{eq:B141-general-kernel},
also for infinite graphs. Applying it in
\eqref{eq:B141-same-kernel} gives $N_{\rm reg}=A(\Sigma)$.

\emph{B.141.3. The complete actual link data.}
Write
$\alpha_n=\bar a_n$, $\beta_n=b_n$,
$\delta_n=\bar d_n$, $\epsilon_n=\bar e_n$.
The retained links freely generate the following $L_v$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-links}
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
v&\text{retained link generators}&\text{deleted neighbors}\\\hline
\alpha_n&A_n,B_n,D_n,E_n&k_{n+1\bmod2}\\
\beta_n&A_n,B_n,D_{n+1},E_{n+1}&k_{n\bmod2}\\
\delta_n&A_n,D_n,E_n,B_{n-1}&k_{n+1\bmod2}\\
\epsilon_n&A_n,D_n,E_n,B_{n-1}&k_{n+1\bmod2}\\
k_0&A_{2j},D_{2j},E_{2j},B_{2j+1}&
 \beta_{2j},\alpha_{2j+1},\delta_{2j+1},\epsilon_{2j+1}\\
k_1&A_{2j+1},D_{2j+1},E_{2j+1},B_{2j}&
 \beta_{2j+1},\alpha_{2j},\delta_{2j},\epsilon_{2j}
\end{array}
\end{equation}
The last two rows include every $j\in\mathbb Z$.
For example $B_{n-1}$ in the third row is the barred image of
$b_{n-1}--d_n$; the other entries follow directly from all the
edge types \eqref{eq:B141-original-edges}. This specifies every
vertex and edge of the actual kernel, with no height cutoff.

The graph is bipartite between expanded centers and expanded families,
but it need not be a forest. Indeed $\delta_n,\epsilon_n$ have
identical retained links. If $\kappa=k_{n+1\bmod2}$, the retained
letter $A_n$ belongs to both links but not to $L_\kappa$.
The two distinct center vertices $(L_\kappa,\kappa)$ and
$(A_nL_\kappa,\kappa)$ are therefore each adjacent to both
$(L_{\delta_n},\delta_n)$ and $(L_{\epsilon_n},\epsilon_n)$.
Their full induced graph is a square, so its full coordinate
subgroup is $F_2\times F_2$ in $N_{\rm reg}$.
This concerns this regional retraction kernel, not the separately
defined geometric kernel M.

\emph{B.141.4. The actual local factor and the whole neck action.}
Let $\Gamma_{\mathcal D}$ be the full deleted subgraph.
The actual $E_1$ is generated by
$\bar a,b,\bar d,\bar e,f_1,k_1$, with
$f_1=CfC=hk_1^{-1}$. Conversely h$=f_1k_1$ and
$k_0=h^{-1}k_1h$. Thus
\[
E_1=A(\Gamma_{\mathcal D})\rtimes\langle h\rangle,
\qquad E^0:=E_1\cap N_{\rm reg}=A(\Gamma_{\mathcal D}).
\]
Inside $A(\Sigma)$ it is exactly the full coordinate subgroup on
all $(L_v,v)$. A pair in this set can be adjacent only when its
original deleted vertex types are adjacent, and g$=1$ supplies
every such edge. The graph $\Gamma_{\mathcal D}$ consists of the
two infinite stars listed in \eqref{eq:B141-links}. Hence
\[
E^0=(\mathbb Z(k_0)\times F(\mathcal L_0))*
       (\mathbb Z(k_1)\times F(\mathcal L_1)),
\]
where $\mathcal L_i$ is the corresponding complete leaf set.

Let $\sigma_h$ be the shift of T and $\Sigma$:
$(gL_v,v)\mapsto(\sigma_h(g)L_{\sigma_h(v)},\sigma_h(v))$.
Conjugation by a,B,d,e is left translation by its retained
level-zero letter. For the actual f and its inverse,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-branch-action}
\operatorname{Ad}(f)
=\operatorname{Inn}(k_1^{-1})\sigma_h,
\qquad
\operatorname{Ad}(f^{-1})
=\operatorname{Inn}(k_0)\sigma_h^{-1}.
\end{equation}
This follows from $f=hk_0^{-1}$ and $hk_0h^{-1}=k_1$.
Both correcting centers are retained.

For any actual neck word x, substitute f$\mapsto h$ to obtain
$\psi(x)\in K_h$. Its actual section is uniquely
$x=\omega(x)\psi(x)$, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-cocycle}
\begin{gathered}
\omega(a)=\omega(B)=\omega(d)=\omega(e)=1,\quad
\omega(f)=k_1^{-1},\quad\omega(f^{-1})=k_0,\\
\omega(xy)=\omega(x)\operatorname{Ad}(\psi(x))(\omega(y)).
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The $\psi$ action is the actual graph action generated by retained
translations and the shift. This gives all signed neck words,
including their ordered nonlinear corrections.

\emph{B.141.5. Rewrite both full initial families inside the common kernel.}
The common-retract decomposition, with the actual right eta marking,
gives \eqref{eq:B141-ambient-kernel}. Use
\[
a_1=CaC,\quad v_0=p(aa_1^{-1}),\quad
\sigma(CuC)=p(C\eta(u)C),\quad s=v_0^{-1}kv_0.
\]
For every $u\in K$, literal multiplication gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-d-map}
d(u)=v_0\sigma(CuC)v_0^{-1}
    =p\bigl(aC(a^{-1}\eta(u)a)Ca^{-1}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Indeed $(aa_1^{-1})C=aCa^{-1}$. The map d is an injective
free-group map, with image $p(aK_1a^{-1})$, retaining the
invertible neck substitution $u\mapsto a^{-1}\eta(u)a$.
Put
\[
(V_1,V_2,V_3,V_4)=(a,d,e,f^{-1}Bf),\qquad
\gamma=d(f),\quad x_i=d(V_i).
\]
Conjugating the five B.134 words by the same v0 gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-mixed-family}
P_0=[(\gamma k)^2,k],\qquad P_i=[k,x_i]\quad(1\le i\le4),
\qquad
N_*=\langle\!\langle P_i,\theta(P_i):0\le i\le4\rangle\!\rangle_V.
\end{equation}
Both sheets and both square relations remain present.

The common right retraction is $r_R(p(g))=\eta(r(g))$.
Write $r(CuC)=\zeta_0(u)$, where $\zeta_0$ kills a,B,d,e
and fixes f. The literal reflection reverses f-exponent, so
$\zeta_0\eta(u)=f^{-\chi_f(u)}$. It follows that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-heads}
r_R(d(u))=q^{\chi_f(u)},\qquad
q=\eta(af^{-1}a^{-1}).
\end{equation}
Thus $x_i\in N_R$, $k\in N_L$, and $\gamma=cq$ with the
specified correction $c=\gamma q^{-1}\in N_R$.
This c is not a cone generator. Let
$\alpha=\operatorname{Ad}(q)$,
$\kappa_j=\alpha^j(k)$ and $c_j=\alpha^j(c)$.
The $\kappa_j$ are q-orbit elements, not the regional k vertices.
Multiplication in the exact kernel-times-K normal form gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B141-recurrence}
\begin{gathered}
(\gamma k)^2=c\kappa_1c_1\kappa_2q^2,\\
P_0=c\kappa_1c_1\kappa_2c_1^{-1}\kappa_1^{-1}c^{-1}\kappa_0^{-1},
\quad
P_0=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\kappa_0=(c\kappa_1c_1)\kappa_2(c\kappa_1c_1)^{-1}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The kappa elements are in the left factor and the c elements in
the right; their actual q action preserves each factor. The theta
mate exchanges the factors with its complete eta marking.

Every word in \eqref{eq:B141-mixed-family} has trivial common
K image. Therefore $N_*$ is exactly the normal closure in
$N_L*N_R$ of all its actual K translates. Equations
\eqref{eq:B141-branch-action}--\eqref{eq:B141-cocycle} specify these
actions at every word and height, separately on each factor.
The two correcting conjugations cannot generally be replaced
by a single inner automorphism on a mixed word. Thus the graph
presentation of the unfilled kernel does not make this a coordinate
graph filling.

\emph{B.141.6. The specified regional retractions fail to descend.}
The unfilled map $\pi_L:V\to R_L$ is identity on the left and
$r_R$ on the right. It sends the four leaf words in
\eqref{eq:B141-mixed-family} to one, but sends the square word to
\[
\pi_L(P_0)=[(qk)^2,k].
\]
Put $\ell=afa^{-1}$, so $q=\eta(\ell)^{-1}$.
B.136--B.138 prove that $\langle k,\eta(\ell)\rangle$ is
the actual free pair, with full induced topology in the regional
group. In its free basis k,q, the displayed image is the
nonempty reduced word
\[
qkqkq^{-1}k^{-1}q^{-1}k^{-1}.
\]
It is therefore nontrivial both discretely and in the actual
regional completion. This proves the asserted nonextension of
$\pi_L$ and, by theta, of the mirrored retraction.
It does not say that this regional word is killed by $V\to V_*$:
it is the image of a mixed relator under a map which does not descend.

Deleting expanded family vertices in $\Sigma$ and retaining every
expanded center is a retraction onto a free group. It respects the
actual K action because the graph actions preserve vertex types and
every cocycle correction is a word in centers. Its extension is
the established color retraction $R^0\to L=\langle K,k\rangle$.
It fixes k and q, so the same obstruction is already present there.
It cannot be attributed to an omitted opposite-family relation.

No completion of the infinite graph kernel in
\eqref{eq:B141-same-kernel} has been identified with its induced
compact closure. The compact nonextension conclusion only uses the
separately proved full topology of the specified free pair. Neither
local filling survival, kappa-invariance of the actual ambient kernel,
nor a fixed-source equality follows from this proposition.
\end{proof}
