\subsection*{The recursive degree-five heads do not supply the full source topology}
\statement{B.157}{Proved}{The actual recursive dihedral-cover heads are not cofinal for B.75's source}
\label{stmt:B157}
Let \(G_V=\langle e,a_0,c_1,c_2\mid e^2=1\rangle\) be the
actual based source of B.75. The topology generated solely by the
permutation heads of all iterates of its degree-five rational cover,
including finite products, restrictions, and subquotients of these heads,
is strictly weaker than its full profinite topology. In particular it
cannot replace the characteristic-neighborhood quantifier in B.75.6.
This does not decide the actual completed residual group \(\mathcal K\).

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.157.1. The complete marked cover.}
The actual parameters of B.74--B.75 satisfy
\[
 q=\frac{(u^5+1)^2}{u^5},\qquad
 w=4\left(\frac{u+1}{u-1}\right)^2,
\]
and the intermediate map is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B157-map}
\begin{aligned}
 F(w)&=\frac{4w(w^2+40w+80)^2}{(w-4)^5},\\
 F(w)-4&=\frac{16(5w^2+40w+16)^2}{(w-4)^5}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The cover from the \(u\)-sphere is Galois with transformations
\(u\mapsto\zeta u\) and \(u\mapsto u^{-1}\), where \(\zeta^5=1\).
It has group \(D_5\) of order ten. The intermediate \(w\)-sphere is
the quotient by the indicated reflection. Thus the monodromy of
\(F\) is the faithful five-point action on reflection cosets of
this actual \(D_5\), with the common basepoint of B.75 retained.

The fibers over zero and four have local degrees \((1,2,2)\),
and the fiber over infinity has the single local degree five.
Their ramification contributions are \(2+2+4=8=2\cdot5-2\),
so no other critical value occurs. The forward images of
\(0,4,\infty\) are \(0,\infty,4\), respectively. Every iterate
therefore restricts to a covering off this same postcritical set.
The order-two cone relation at zero is respected: zero is an
unramified fixed point, and a backward branch can encounter a
critical point mapping to zero at most once. Such a point maps
directly to zero, and cannot later be another critical point
over zero. Its total local degree over zero in any iterate is
therefore at most two. Equivalently one may first take the
punctured-sphere monodromy and then factor through the actual
order-two orbifold relation.

\emph{B.157.2. A uniform restriction at every depth.}
The monodromy of a composition preserves the blocks supplied by
the intermediate covering. On each block it is a subgroup of the
monodromy of that stage, transported by a path. Induction embeds
the monodromy of \(F^n\) into the \(n\)-fold permutational wreath
product of \(D_5\). Equality with that wreath product is not needed.
Every such finite group has order divisible only by the primes
two and five. The same is true of subgroups, quotients, and finite
products. Restriction to the actual \(G_V\) consequently has the
same property.

Thus the kernel \(T\) of any finite collection of these source
actions has index \(2^a5^b\). Any subgroup containing such a
kernel also has index divisible only by two and five. This
includes all neighborhoods obtained by taking stabilizers or
finite intersections in the topology generated by these heads.

\emph{B.157.3. An explicit missing source neighborhood.}
The actual presentation gives the epimorphism
\[
 G_V\longrightarrow C_3,\qquad
 a_0\longmapsto1,\quad e,c_1,c_2\longmapsto0.
\]
Its kernel \(U_0\) has index three. If one of the preceding
finite-index subgroups \(T\) were contained in \(U_0\), then
\[
 [G_V:T]=[G_V:U_0]\,[U_0:T]
\]
would be divisible by three, a contradiction. Hence the recursive
heads are not cofinal in the full source topology.

B.75.6 instead allows every characteristic finite-index subgroup
of the actual Birman group \(N=B_4(S_{0,4})\), with its actual
source action and crossed map. The preceding obstruction concerns
only the stated recursive permutation heads. It neither excludes
those general characteristic quotients nor produces a nonidentity
element of \(\mathcal K\). Pointwise separation or a contraction
property of the rational correspondence does not supply the
missing uniform finite-target implication.
\end{proof}
