\subsection*{The complete family kernel and its paired Schreier coordinates}

\statement{B.127}{Proved}{The actual family kernel has an exact all-orbit Schreier presentation, a literal retained section, and a paired discrete abelianization}
\label{stmt:B127}
Use the actual free-eleven presentation of B.123. Write
\[
 \mathcal U=(a,B,d,e,\bar a,\bar B,\bar d,\bar e)
 =(a,B,d,e,cac,cBc,cdc,cec),\qquad T=F(f,g,x),
\]
where \(g=cfc\), \(x=\tau c\), and bars exchange the two four-label
halves. Thus \(F=F(\mathcal U)*T\). Let \(\pi:F\to T\) kill the
eight family letters, let \(L=\ker\pi\), and let \(N\) be the
entire defining normal subgroup of B.123, including every signed
source translate of all forty marked ordinary node words.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-section}
 N\subseteq[L,L],\qquad J=L/N,\qquad W=F/N=J\rtimes T.
\end{equation}
The section of \(T\) is the literal subgroup on \(f,g,x\).
It is a retained-fiber section, not a section of the whole
coarse one-neck group into the unfilled group.

The exact Schreier free basis of \(L\) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-basis}
 X_{t,i}=tU_i t^{-1},\qquad t\in T,\quad U_i\in\mathcal U.
\end{equation}
The selected involution \(\delta=\operatorname{Inn}(\tau)\) acts
on this entire basis by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-pairing}
 \delta(X_{t,i})=X_{\delta_T(t)x,\bar i},\qquad
 \delta_T(f)=xgx^{-1},\quad
 \delta_T(g)=xfx^{-1},\quad \delta_T(x)=x^{-1}.
\end{equation}
It is a fixed-point-free permutation of the basis.
There is an exact discrete module identification
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-ab}
 J_{\rm ab}
   =\bigoplus_{\substack{t\in T\\i\in\mathcal U}}\mathbb Z e_{t,i},
 \qquad
 \delta(e_{t,i})=e_{\delta_T(t)x,\bar i}.
\end{equation}
In particular its Tate cohomology for this \(C_2\)-action
vanishes in every degree.

All nonlinear source corrections admit the uniform formula
\eqref{eq:B127-nonlinear} below, and the exact relation subgroup
is \eqref{eq:B127-L-closure}, with no cutoff on either source
or retained-deck words. On actual full completions the retained
section gives
\[
 \widehat W=\overline J\rtimes\widehat T,\qquad
 \overline J=\ker(\widehat W\to\widehat T).
\]
No equality \(\widehat J=\overline J\), no completed version of
\eqref{eq:B127-ab}, and no lifting of invariant abelian classes
to fixed group elements is asserted here.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.127.1. The exact free basis and retained section.}
The free-product normal form for \(F(\mathcal U)*T\) gives
\[
 L=\mathop{*}_{t\in T}tF(\mathcal U)t^{-1}.
\]
Equivalently, in the coset graph for \(\pi\), the retained
\(T\)-edges form its spanning tree and each family edge is a
loop at one vertex \(t\). This proves \eqref{eq:B127-basis}.
For a literal word of \(F\), scan from left to right, keeping
its current retained prefix \(t\). A letter
\(U_i^\epsilon\) emits \(X_{t,i}^\epsilon\); a retained letter
updates \(t\). The result is the exact normal form \(nt\),
with \(n\in L\). Expanding the emitted letters gives the
inverse verification and retains every nonlinear connector.

Each marked node factor of B.123 is an actual conjugate of a
family peripheral. The signed source maps preserve the family
filling, so every seed and every translate belongs to \(L\).
Thus \(N\subseteq L\), and the inclusion of the literal free
factor \(T\) descends to a section \(T\to W\). This proves
\(J=L/N\) and \(W=J\rtimes T\). The continuous extension of the
section has left inverse \(\widehat W\to\widehat T\); the
quotient property of full completion gives the displayed
compact splitting with its actual closed kernel.

\emph{B.127.2. Pairing on every index.}
In the original free group,
\[
 \delta(U_i)=xU_{\bar i}x^{-1}
\]
and the three retained images are exactly those in
\eqref{eq:B127-pairing}. Direct substitution proves that formula
for every \(t\in T\). Applying it twice returns \(t\), because
\(\delta_T(x)=x^{-1}\). No color is fixed by \(i\mapsto\bar i\).
Taking all \(t\) and the first four colors as representatives
therefore gives a free basis in exchanged pairs. This is a
uniform free-word identity, not an inference from a finite
collection of encountered indices.

\emph{B.127.3. Every actual node and its connector.}
The ten local pairs in each regional marking of B.123 are
\[
 (U,CUC)\ (U=a,b,d,e),\quad
 (a,b),\ (CaC,d),\ (CaC,e),\ (d,CeC),\quad
 (b,fdf^{-1}),\ (b,fef^{-1}).
\]
Take their twenty actual orbifold commutators and both ordinary
sheets, retaining the full regional substitutions. For each
factor the resulting literal free-eleven word is
\[
 wU_i^\epsilon w^{-1},\qquad \epsilon\in\{1,-1\}.
\]
Writing \(w=nt\) by the scan in B.127.1 gives exactly
\[
 nX_{t,i}^{\epsilon}n^{-1}.
\]
For the two factors of one node, conjugation of the entire
relator by \(n_1^{-1}\) yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-connector}
 [X_{t_1,i_1}^{\epsilon_1},
   (n_1^{-1}n_2)X_{t_2,i_2}^{\epsilon_2}(n_1^{-1}n_2)^{-1}].
\end{equation}
The connector \(n_1^{-1}n_2\) is an essential part of the
actual relation. It is not discarded or replaced by an
augmented incidence label.

The finite input checks use the complete substitutions in
B.123: each of the eighty factor words is expanded back to its
original free-eleven word, and its commutator is compared with
the corresponding one of all forty stored node words. The
Schreier scan and expansion are inverse on every such word.
These checks certify the whole finite presentation input;
the infinite relation family is retained below.

\emph{B.127.4. Uniform signed source operators.}
For each \(\sigma\in\{m,m^{-1},v,v^{-1}\}\), the actual
partial-inner substitutions of B.123 give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-peripheral}
 \alpha_\sigma(U_i)=
       w_{\sigma,i}U_iw_{\sigma,i}^{-1}
       \quad (i\in\mathcal U).
\end{equation}
All thirty-two cores are the same positive family letter.
One completely specified choice of \(w_{\sigma,i}\) is obtained
by freely reducing the actual image and peeling equal inverse
outer letters until its one-letter core remains. The defining
substitutions, this deterministic operation, and the exact
free-word expansion specify its full connector; homology is
not used to choose the core. The twelve retained generator
images are also kept as full free-eleven words.

Let \(\phi_\sigma\) be the induced automorphism of \(T\), and
put \(h_{\sigma,i}=\pi(w_{\sigma,i})\). For arbitrary \(t\in T\)
define \(q_{\sigma,t,i}\in L\) by the unique decomposition
\[
 \alpha_\sigma(t)w_{\sigma,i}
   =q_{\sigma,t,i}\phi_\sigma(t)h_{\sigma,i}.
\]
Then direct substitution gives the complete nonlinear action
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-nonlinear}
 \alpha_\sigma(X_{t,i})
 =q_{\sigma,t,i}
   X_{\phi_\sigma(t)h_{\sigma,i},i}
   q_{\sigma,t,i}^{-1}.
\end{equation}
In particular \(\alpha_\sigma(t)\) is not silently replaced
by its retained projection. With
\(\alpha_{st}=\alpha_s\alpha_t\), exact recursive conjugators
can be chosen as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-composition}
 w_{st,i}=\alpha_s(w_{t,i})w_{s,i},\qquad
 h_{st,i}=\phi_s(h_{t,i})h_{s,i}.
\end{equation}
These equations follow by applying \(\alpha_s\) to
\eqref{eq:B127-peripheral}; they preserve every signed word
without an orbit-depth bound.

For specificity, the retained heads of the peeled conjugators
are as follows. For \(m\), the head is \(f^{-1}g\) on
\(B,\bar a,\bar d,\bar e\) and \(1\) on the other four letters;
for \(m^{-1}\), replace \(f^{-1}g\) by \(gf^{-1}\). The other
sixteen heads are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 U_i&h_{v,i}&h_{v^{-1},i}\\ \hline
 a&f^{-1}&xgx^{-1}\\
 B&xg^{-1}x^{-1}&f\\
 d&f^{-1}&xgx^{-1}\\
 e&xg^{-2}x^{-1}f&xg^{-1}x^{-1}f^2\\
 \bar a&f^{-1}&xgx^{-1}\\
 \bar B&f^{-1}gx^{-1}f^{-1}x&xgx^{-2}f^{-1}xg\\
 \bar d&f^{-1}&xgx^{-1}\\
 \bar e&f^{-1}gx^{-1}f^{-2}xg&xgx^{-2}f^{-2}xg^2
\end{array}
\]
The finite checks verify these heads together with the full
conjugators, not instead of them.

Writing any ambient conjugator as \(nt\), \(n\in L,t\in T\),
now gives the exact alternative form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B127-L-closure}
 N=\left\langle\!\left\langle
       t\alpha_s(r)t^{-1}:
       t\in T,\ s\in F(m,v),\ r\in\mathcal A
       \right\rangle\!\right\rangle_L ,
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal A\) contains all forty marked nodes.
This retains every source translate, every retained-deck
translate and every normal conjugator. A simultaneous
compatible change removing all the connectors in
\eqref{eq:B127-connector} is not supplied by this presentation.

\emph{B.127.5. Exact abelianization.}
Both factors of every node belong to \(L\), so each seed is in
\([L,L]\). This commutator subgroup is normal in \(F\), since
\(L\) is normal, and it is preserved by every signed source
automorphism. Thus the entire subgroup \(N\) in
\eqref{eq:B127-L-closure} is contained in \([L,L]\). Hence
\[
 (L/N)_{\rm ab}=L/[L,L],
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:B127-ab}. The retained action is
\(e_{t,i}\mapsto e_{ut,i}\), while the actual signed source
action is
\[
 e_{t,i}\longmapsto e_{\phi_\sigma(t)h_{\sigma,i},i}.
\]
Only at this abelian stage do the \(q_{\sigma,t,i}\) disappear.
The involution module is a direct sum of copies of the
regular integral \(C_2\)-module. Its two-periodic Tate complex
is exact on each exchanged pair, and kernels and images
commute with this direct sum. This proves Tate acyclicity in
every degree.

Neither this calculation nor the existence of the retained
section lifts invariant abelian classes to fixed group
elements. The actual closed-kernel topology and the nonlinear
fixed-source gate remain those explicitly distinguished in B.123.
\end{proof}
