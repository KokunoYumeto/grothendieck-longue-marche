 F_{11}=F(a,B,d,e,f,\bar a,\bar B,\bar d,\bar e,g,x).

\([p,q]=pqp^{-1}q^{-1}\).

and let \(\chi_\Sigma\) vanish on the other nine fiber generators.
The map
\[

 \quad(w\in F_{11},\ s\in S)

\begin{equation}\label{eq:B174-index-two}

 F_{11}^{(2)}=\ker\chi_\Sigma,\qquad
 S^*=\langle m,v^*\rangle\cong F_2,
 \quad v^*=f^{-1}v.
\end{equation}

pro-two completion. The entire actual \(H_5\) lies in \(E^*\).

Equivalently, its fiber is

   \beta_s(r_-),\beta_s(r_+):s\in S^*
                         \rangle\!\rangle_{Q_8},

\eqref{eq:B174-full-saturation}; the reverse arrow is not asserted.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.174.1. Actual parity and the whole lifted source.}
The thirty-two actual signed family rows say that each of
\(m,m^{-1},v,v^{-1}\) sends each positive family core to a conjugate
of that same positive core. The conjugator has \(\chi_\Sigma\)-value
zero for the two \(m\)-rows and one for the two \(v\)-rows.
All four source maps preserve \(\chi_\Sigma\) on the eleven fiber
generators. These are the literal rows in the frozen B.123/B.132
inputs; they include every barred core as well as every unbarred core.
They prove that \(\epsilon\) is a homomorphism.

The two elements \(m,f^{-1}v\) lie in its kernel. Projection to
\(S\) restricts to the isomorphism from their free subgroup onto
\(F(m,v)\): freeness follows because a relation would project to
the identical relation in that free group. Every element of \(E^*\)
is the product of an element of \(F_{11}^{(2)}\) and this lift of
its source projection. This proves \eqref{eq:B174-index-two}.
In particular the actual signed actions are
\[
 \beta_m=\alpha_m,\quad \beta_{m^{-1}}=\alpha_{m^{-1}},\quad
 \beta_{v^*}=\operatorname{Ad}(f^{-1})\alpha_v,\quad
 \beta_{(v^*)^{-1}}=\alpha_{v^{-1}}\operatorname{Ad}(f).
 \tag{B.174.1}
\]
The last formula keeps the order in the inverse action.

The Schreier transversal \(\{1,f\}\) gives the sixteen family
generators \(c,fcf^{-1}\), for the eight family cores \(c\), and
the five generators
\[
 t=f^2,\quad u_o=gf^{-1},\quad w_o=fg,\quad x_o=x,
 \quad y_o=fxf^{-1}.
 \tag{B.174.2}
\]
Delete the thirteen family generators other than
\(a,B,\bar d\) on the even sheet. For the adjusted source maps
in (B.174.1), an even family core has an even conjugator, and its
odd Schreier partner has the corresponding odd conjugator.
Thus every deleted core maps to a conjugate of a deleted core.
The same assertion for all four signed maps gives equality of the
deletion kernel with each of its images. This proves the genuine
equivariant quotient, first in the free basis
\((a,B,\bar d,t,u_o,w_o,x_o,y_o)\). No coarse stable section before
the deletion has been used.

Make the reversible Nielsen substitutions
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B174-nielsen}
 \begin{gathered}

 u=a^{-1}u_o,\quad w=w_o a,\quad X=x_o a,\quad y=y_o,\\

\end{equation}

\(y=fXf^{-1}\); these latter equalities use the killed odd
\(a\)-core. This defines every coordinate in
\eqref{eq:B174-generic-quotient}.

\emph{B.174.2. The full forty-node and deck calculation.}

\end{equation}

For completeness, the family/parity reason for the complete
