\subsection*{A mixed contact beyond the single compact orbit family}
\statement{B.168}{Proved}{A specified mixed contact lies outside the entire compact \(m\)-orbit kernel of \(D_s\), with an actual finite ambient separator obtained from one prescribed \(S_4\) fiber map}
\label{stmt:B168}
Use the commutator convention \([x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}\).
In the notation of B.164 and B.167, set
\[
                     r=[m,B_sD_sB_s^{-1}].
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B168-strict}
 r\in\mathcal I\setminus\mathcal U_D,\qquad
 \mathcal U_D\subsetneq\mathcal I,\qquad
 \partial(\mathcal U_D)\subsetneq\partial(\mathcal I).
\end{equation}
The last two cohomological subspaces are compact and closed.
This is a strict comparison inside the known contact image, not a
counterexample to the remaining equality \(\mathcal I=\mathcal K^\delta\).
The separate even-sum free-seven detector of B.132 kills \(r\);
it is therefore not faithful on this actual mixed contact subgroup.

\begin{proof}
\emph{The whole actual one-sided quotient.}
B.131.5 gives an ordinary quotient of the entire actual group
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B168-whole}
 \Gamma_0\longrightarrow
 F_7(a,B,d,E,f,h,y)\rtimes S,\qquad S=F(m,v).
\end{equation}
All four barred colors are killed. Every signed source operator
preserves their normal closure, and every node has a barred core.
Thus the whole node family, including both sheets and every signed
source translate, is killed. This is not only a homomorphism from
the five-generator source subgroup.

The basis and its inverse are literal:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 E=fde d^{-1}f^{-1},\qquad h=a^{-1}ga,\qquad y=xaf,\\
 e=d^{-1}f^{-1}Efd,\qquad g=aha^{-1},\qquad x=yf^{-1}a^{-1}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
The actual contact basis maps to
\((A_s,B_s,D_s,E_s)\mapsto(a,B,d,E)\).
For the actual positive source operator,
\[
 \alpha_m(d)=d,\qquad
 \alpha_m(B)=\kappa B\kappa^{-1},\qquad\kappa=f^{-1}h.
\]
Therefore \(r\) has the exact free-fiber image
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B168-fiber-word}
 r_1=\kappa B\kappa^{-1}d\kappa B^{-1}\kappa^{-1}
                       B d^{-1}B^{-1}.
\end{equation}

\emph{One finite free-fiber witness.}
Define a homomorphism \(\eta:F_7\to S_4\) by
\[
 \eta(a)=\eta(E)=\eta(f)=\eta(y)=1,\qquad
 \eta(h)=(12),\quad\eta(B)=(13),\quad\eta(d)=(34).
\]
Then \(\eta(\kappa)=(12)\), which commutes with \((34)\), while
\(\eta(BdB^{-1})=(14)\). With permutations composed right to left,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B168-S4}
               \eta(r_1)=[(12),(14)]=(124)
                    \notin\langle(34)\rangle.
\end{equation}
This is one prescribed assignment, not a search over finite groups.
The map \(\eta\) is only a free-fiber map; no source equivariance or
extension to the same finite codomain is assumed.

\emph{An actual whole-group finite quotient.}
Let
\[
 C=\bigcap_{\varphi\in\operatorname{Hom}(F_7,S_4)}\ker\varphi.
\]
There are \(24^7\) homomorphisms, since the displayed fiber is free
of rank seven. Thus \(C\) is a finite-index characteristic subgroup
and \(C\le\ker\eta\). Put \(P=F_7/C\).
All actual signed source automorphisms descend to \(P\), with finite
image \(A_S\le\operatorname{Aut}(P)\).
Equation \eqref{eq:B168-whole} consequently induces a genuine finite
ambient homomorphism
\[
                       \Gamma_0\longrightarrow P\rtimes A_S.
\]
Its fiber has a further map to \(S_4\) induced by \(\eta\).
Equation \eqref{eq:B168-S4} proves that the image of \(r\) is outside
the cyclic subgroup generated by the image of \(d\).
The characteristic intersection is a proof of finiteness; its
homomorphisms need not be enumerated.

The continuous extension of this ambient map sends every generator
\(m^zD_sm^{-z}\) of B.167 to the same element \(d\).
Indeed \(m\) fixes \(d\) literally in the actual one-sided quotient;
the identity for all \(z\in\widehat{\mathbb Z}\) also follows by
continuity from the integer identities.
The image of the entire compact \(\mathcal U_D\) is therefore
contained in \(\langle d\rangle\). It cannot contain the image of
\(r\), proving \(r\notin\mathcal U_D\).

On the other hand \(m,B_s,D_s\) are actual fixed source elements.
The filled one-family map kills \(B_s,D_s\) and retains \(m\);
thus \(r\in\overline{H_{\mathrm{src}}}\cap\mathcal K=\mathcal I\).
This proves the first two assertions of \eqref{eq:B168-strict}.
B.164's connecting map is injective and continuous, so the last
assertion follows; compactness gives closedness of both images.

Finally B.132's different quotient has the literal markings
\[
                 B_s\longmapsto B,\qquad
                 D_s\longmapsto B^{-1}D_0B,\qquad [m,D_0]=1.
\]
It sends \(r\) to \(1\), whereas the just-constructed actual finite
ambient target detects \(r\). No claim about the whole three-contact
relation kernel or its generation follows from either restriction.
\end{proof}
