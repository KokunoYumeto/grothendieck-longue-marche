\statement{B.97}{Proved}{The two actual source sheets form a free rank-128 block, and its returned-source orbit is the full node module}
\label{stmt:B97}
Retain the discrete groups, modules, markings and involution of
B.94--B.96.  In particular $\Omega=\mathbb Z[G]$, $b=b_1$ is the
positive old lasso about $q=1$, and $\alpha_b$ denotes geometric push.
Put
\[
 L=L_{\mathrm{ret}},\qquad L_-=\alpha_b^{-1}(L),\qquad
 L_8=L+L_-.
\]
Then these actual submodules of $P$ satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B97-block}
 L\cap L_-=0,\qquad L_8=L\oplus L_-\cong\Omega^{128}.
\end{equation}
Both summands are $F$-stable, and the direct sum is equivariant.
With the actual first-sheet elements $\rho_i$ of B.93 and the
second-sheet elements $\sigma_i$ of B.96, define
\[
 J_8=\sum_{i=1}^4\mathbb F_2[R](\rho_i-1)
       +\sum_{i=1}^4\mathbb F_2[R](\sigma_i-1).
\]
The positive-degree integral involution cohomology of this block is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B97-cohomology}
 \begin{gathered}
 H^1(C_2,L_8)\cong\mathbb F_2[R]^8,
 \qquad H^2(C_2,L_8)=0,\\
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(H^1(C_2,L_8)\longrightarrow H^1(C_2,P)\bigr)
       =J_8\subseteq J_I.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Let $B_0\leq B$ be the index-two actual source-cover subgroup and
$S_t$ its returned-source operators, with the conventions of B.96.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B97-source-orbit}
 \overline L=\sum_{t\in B_0}S_t(L_8),\qquad
 \sum_{t\in B_0}\operatorname{im}\bigl(H^1(C_2,S_t(L_8))
                  \longrightarrow H^1(C_2,P)\bigr)=J_I.
\end{equation}
Each $S_t$ commutes literally with $F$.  The first sum is a sum of
actual submodules, with finite support for each element; the second
is the sum of their individual cohomology images.  Their overlaps,
and hence the possible excess $U/J_I$, are not determined by this
statement.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.97.1. The actual augmentation rows and their sheet parity.}
Write $\mathfrak a=I_{\mathbb Z[E]}\Omega$, where $E=R\times R$.
The rows of the free $\Omega$-module $P$ are
$\epsilon_{i,j,z}$, $i,j\in\{2,3,5,6,7,8,10,11\}$, $z\in T$,
as in B.94.1.  Modulo $\mathfrak a$, each of the $64$ actual
columns has one row with a signed $T$-unit coefficient.  Its ordered
physical slot pair is $(i,j)$, and the $64$ pairs are distinct.

We verify why the second sheet has different rows.  Let
$\varepsilon:T\to\mathbb F_2$ send $y_1$ to $1$ and the other
four retained generators to $0$.  The actual retained action of
the positive old lasso $b$ is
\[
 \varphi(y_1)=y_1,\quad \varphi(y_2)=y_2,\quad
 \varphi(y_3)=y_3,\quad
 \varphi(y_4)=y_1^{-1}y_4y_1,\quad
 \varphi(y_5)=y_1^{-1}y_5y_1.
\]
Thus $\varepsilon\varphi=\varepsilon$; also
$\varepsilon\beta=\varepsilon$.  Let $h_{A,i},h_{B,j}\in T$
be the actual retained heads of the peripheral conjugators of this
operator, before identifying the $B$ labels by $\beta$.  In the
stated marking their exact values have the following parities:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|rrrrrrrr}
Slot&2&3&5&6&7&8&10&11\\\hline
$\varepsilon(h_{A,i})$&0&0&0&0&0&0&0&0\\
$\varepsilon(h_{B,i})$&1&1&1&1&1&1&1&1
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These are reductions of the literal free-word heads, not a choice
of unbased conjugators.  The heads, full peripheral words and inverse
identities are in the actual row-action certificate named below.
For the inverse operator the head is
$\varphi^{-1}(h_{A,i})^{-1}$, respectively
$\varphi^{-1}(h_{B,j})^{-1}$, so the same parity table holds.

For clarity, the induced row calculation takes place only after
$E$-augmentation.  Peripheral labels $g$ on the first side and
$h$ on the common second side give $z=\beta(g)^{-1}h$.
Their transported labels are
$\varphi(g)h_{A,i}$ and
$(\beta\varphi\beta)(h)\beta(h_{B,j})$.  Therefore the new
relative row word is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B97-row-action}
 z\longmapsto
 \beta(h_{A,i})^{-1}(\beta\varphi\beta)(z)\beta(h_{B,j}).
\end{equation}
The inverse action has the same formula with the inverse data.
Consequently either direction changes $\varepsilon(z)$ by $1$,
for every ordered physical pair.  In particular the augmented
row of $\alpha_b^{-1}(c_{i,j})$ differs from that of $c_{i,j}$.
Physical pairs are unchanged by this pure transport.  There are
therefore $128$ distinct augmented unit rows for the two specified
layers.

The complete input check reconstructs all $64$ original nodal
commutators and all $64$ actual $\alpha_b^{-1}$ images in $F_{21}$.
It computes their full integral product-tree chains and checks every
edge boundary.  An independent checker integrates at the other
family of edges, verifies all $128$ chains, all $256$ boundary
identities and all $128$ inherited $F$ identities, and then compares
the complete $G$ coefficients.  Its $128$ distinct unit rows agree
with the parity proof.  Thus the proof does not infer a full-column
identity from its augmented image.

\emph{B.97.2. Independence over the full integral coefficient ring.}
Select those $128$ rows.  The resulting coefficient matrix is
$D+M$, with $D$ a diagonal matrix of signed group units and every
entry of $M$ in $\mathfrak a$.  Suppose a finite left relation
among the $128$ columns has coefficients $a_j\in\Omega$.
Reducing its selected rows modulo $\mathfrak a$ gives
$a_j\in\mathfrak a$ for every $j$, since the diagonal entries
are units.  If all coefficients lie in $\mathfrak a^n$, the
off-diagonal error terms lie in $\mathfrak a^{n+1}$, and the
same row argument, with multiplication on the correct right side
of each left coefficient, gives $a_j\in\mathfrak a^{n+1}$.
By induction all $a_j$ belong to $\bigcap_n\mathfrak a^n$.
B.94.3 proves that this intersection is zero in the actual
$\mathbb Z[G]$, with integral coefficients and all retained deck
words.  This proves \eqref{eq:B97-block}.  We use separatedness
to prove injectivity; we do not invert $D+M$.

\emph{B.97.3. Cohomology and the source-orbit presentation.}
B.94 proves $F$-stability and the entire cohomology of $L$.
B.96 proves the corresponding facts for the canonical odd pullback
$L_-$.  Since \eqref{eq:B97-block} is a direct sum of these actual
$F$-stable submodules, its cohomology is their direct sum.  The
first summand contributes the four $\rho_i-1$ and the second
the four $\sigma_i-1$, with the inversions specified in B.96.3.
In characteristic two an inverse difference generates the same
left ideal, since $\sigma^{-1}-1=\sigma^{-1}(\sigma-1)$.
This proves \eqref{eq:B97-cohomology} with the actual source
marking retained.

B.96 expresses every even old translate as $S_t(L)$ and every
odd one as $S_t(\alpha_b(L))$.  The actual returned source
operator $S$ over $b^2$ satisfies
\[
 S=\operatorname{Inn}(p_1)\alpha_b^2.
\]
Inner conjugation acts on $P$ by a $G$-unit, and therefore
\[
 S\bigl(\alpha_b^{-1}(L)\bigr)=\alpha_b(L)
\]
as actual $\Omega$-submodules.  As $t$ ranges over $B_0$, its
products with this source operator range over the same group of
returned operators, with geometric-push composition understood
in the opposite order.  We obtain the first equality in
\eqref{eq:B97-source-orbit}.  All eight peripheral source elements
and their orbits under the eleven returned source generators and
their inverses generate $I$ as a subgroup, by B.96.1.  Naturality
of the Tate target from B.96.4 and
$gh-1=(g-1)+g(h-1)$ now prove the second equality.

\emph{B.97.4. Exact scope of the parity argument.}
The parity in B.97.1 distinguishes selected augmentation rows.
It is not a homogeneous grading of the full integral columns.
Relative to the original pivot for each physical pair, the exact
full-row check finds both parity colors in $39$ of the $64$
original columns and in all $64$ odd columns.  Hence separating
the two specified unit minors does not separate arbitrary sums
of their further source translates.  A cancellation within one
such sum may remove its leading augmentation terms.  No claim
about that cancellation follows from \eqref{eq:B97-block}.

For a finite set $T_0\subset B_0$ put
$V_{T_0}=\sum_{t\in T_0}S_t(L_8)$.  B.96.6 shows that adding a
new block contributes no additional class if the connecting image
of $H^2(C_2,V_{T_0}\cap S_s(L_8))$ has zero diagonal modulo
$J_I$.  This is an intersection with the whole preceding sum.
It remains uncomputed, as do the nonlinear and compact comparisons.
