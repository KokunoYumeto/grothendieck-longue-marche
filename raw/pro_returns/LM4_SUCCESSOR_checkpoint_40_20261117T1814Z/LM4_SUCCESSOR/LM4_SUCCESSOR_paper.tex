\begin{abstract}
We study the involutions of the full profinite three-cusp group,
the icosahedral centralizer in the full profinite mapping class group,
and the associated torus, noncompact comparison and real-structure
questions. This is an incomplete research return. Neither conjugacy
of every negative involution to complex conjugation nor finiteness of
the icosahedral profinite centralizer is established. A gap in a
published proof is distinguished throughout from a counterexample to
its statement.

For the first question, we give exact marked generation, norm and
boundary criteria, and distinguish full profinite realization from
its prime-by-prime and finite-level shadows. We evaluate the original
elliptic pentagon and negative involution transporters, identify an
explicit Brunnian cobounder, and prove the full profinite embedding
of the actual annular source by finite-target refinement. The
negative fixed groups and both specified fixed axes are determined.
We compute the transverse annular intersection and the negative
modular frames, and prove a precise obstruction to extending a pure
modular representation across the whole elliptic pure group. These
results do not imply the remaining positive marked cube, the
specified nonlinear third-cusp identity, or the original coherent
class and complex-multiplication parity.

For the second question, we audit the nonsolvable centralizer step,
construct the actual comparison maps and retain the distinction
between a centralizer in a completion and the closure of a discrete
centralizer. We compute contact-source images and prove the full
induced topology of a four-vertex path subgroup. Whole-source mixed
words, with every signed source action and node relation retained,
give strict bounds on the actual contact presentation kernel.
Finite fiber separators, characteristic refinements and finite
amalgams prove separation in genuine ambient finite quotients;
the large final quotients are not claimed to have been numerically
enumerated. These image calculations do not establish exhaustion
of the fixed-point or cohomology fiber, or the separate uniform
global Birman condition required for the main centralizer decision.

The remaining parts give scoped corrections and results for the
punctured torus, uniform-tail topology, genus-three real structures
and the five-point census. Every original task retains an explicit
outcome and, where unresolved, its exact obstruction and attempted
approaches. The final register contains 401 statements: 357 Proved,
19 Refuted, 19 Open and 6 Known, with 117 bibliography entries and
49 task rows. At the scheduled progress review we stop further
exploration because the current auxiliary refinements no longer
advance either main decision. We retain the complete proofs,
computations, unsuccessful attempts and source audits, and claim
no priority.
