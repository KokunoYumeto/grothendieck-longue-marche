# The actual partial-source images and the remaining nonabelian gate

names use the larger ordered root at q*=1/100. All maps are the actual

## Exact source decomposition

The unordered source is free on the six old base loops and four new
crossings. Its ordered-cover character is odd exactly on alpha1. Its
index-two subgroup is F19. A Schreier basis consists of eleven even old
loops, four original crossing meridians, and their alpha1 conjugates.
The eleven even old loops are

    alpha_k (k=0,2,3,4,5), alpha1^2,
    alpha1 alpha_k alpha1^-1 (k=0,2,3,4,5).

Filling the four moving crossings gives the actual F19->F15 partial
source map. Filling the other four gives the other F15. Filling both
gives F11. In particular each partial source kernel is normal in F19,
and its image J_mov or J_const is normal in the entire actual ordered
source image, including the common full filling-kernel image I.

The four inverse original moving source words are V4,V1,C^-1V2C,
C^-1V6C. The last two equal alpha5^-1(V2),alpha3^-1(V6), respectively.
Thus their orbit under the even old group equals the orbit of V. The
opposite crossing generators can similarly be replaced by alpha1(V).
Consequently J_mov has the following **exact finite operator description**:
start with V1,V2,V4,V6 and take the subgroup generated by their orbit
under the eleven even old automorphisms and the four inner automorphisms
Inn(alpha1(V_j)), including all inverses. Inner conjugation by the
moving subgroup itself adds nothing to a subgroup. This is precisely
the image of the normal moving source kernel, rather than an ambient
normal closure substituted for that image. J_const=alpha1(J_mov), and
I=J_mov J_const. Both factors are normal in I. The actual compact images
have the analogous product and normality statements: the source fillings
are free retractions, their completed kernels are closures of their
discrete kernels, and compact images are closed.

All22 even actions/inverses and the eight original/opposite source words
are in tangent_even_family_actions.json and tangent_partial_source_words.json.
The original action convention is retained throughout.

## A source-contained ordered Nielsen basis

Use the standard ordered Schreier basis

    Y1,...,Y4=V1,V2,V4,V6;
    Y5,...,Y8=A V1 A^-1,A V2 A^-1,A V4 A^-1,A V6 A^-1;
    Y9=C, Y10=E=A C A^-1, Y11=A^2, Y12=P=B A^-1, Y13=Q=AB.

Replace Y5,...,Y8 by

    O1=alpha1(V1), U2, O4=alpha1(V4), U6.

These four elements lie in the actual constant partial-source image.
For O1,O4 this is the based family exchange. For U2,U6 it follows from
the previously certified literal source expressions, not merely from
recognizing their ambient peripheral cores. If r_i denotes the ith
stored depth-one source orbit word in tangent_bundle_nielsen_actions.json,

    U6 = r1 r48 r1^-1,
    U2 = (r21 r48) r36 (r21 r48)^-1.

Here r36 and r48 have old action path[-2], hence are constant-family
source meridians; r1 and r21 have even family character. Normality of the
constant source kernel proves the asserted membership. The corresponding
U1,U4 expressions are conjugates of moving source meridians.

The thirteen new elements are a literal free basis of F13. The exact
producer tangent_source_ordered_basis.py verifies both inverse
substitutions on all thirteen generators. A triangular explanation is
available: U6=P Y8 P^-1, U2=P Y8 Y6 Y8^-1 P^-1 recover Y8,Y6. O4 is a
conjugate of Y7 by a word in Y4,P,Q,C; then O1 is a conjugate of Y5 by a
word in the now known Y7 and the other retained/selected generators.
Thus all old generators are recovered without a determinant-only test.

Let L be the rank-eight free factor generated by these first eight new
basis elements. Then L<=I. Since J_mov and J_const are normal in I,

    J_mov intersect L = ker(L -> free group on its four constant generators),
    J_const intersect L = ker(L -> free group on its four moving generators).

The upper bounds follow from the actual ambient partial fillings; the
lower bounds follow from normality under L. Both are whole infinite
normal-kernel statements. They also hold for actual compact intersections
by the explicit free-factor retractions and density of the discrete
filling kernels. They are not statements that J_mov or J_const is normal
in the whole F13.

## The restricted obstruction is not a full-source obstruction

The smaller Gamma0 subgroup omits alpha1^2 and the other-family source
conjugations. Its proposed equality J0=N_mov is refuted by the exact D8
quotient in tangent_restricted_family_obstruction.md. Alpha1^2 changes the
missing E-index by−1 on every selected peripheral, so that particular
finite separator does not apply to the actual even source.

The even old group alone has a rank-seven invariant index lattice in
Q9_ab. For selected label1 its full primitive annihilator is generated by

    W2+2E,
    −W1+W2+E+A^2+AB,

where W1,W2,W4,W6 are the four complementary peripheral coordinates.
The actual opposite words O1,O2 have character values (0,−1),(1,1),
respectively, a determinant-one pair. Labels2,4,6 give the corresponding
swaps/pairs. The genuine code tangent_even_source_lattice_completion.py
checks that the actual inner-source operators fill both missing
coordinates integrally. Thus no character from this restricted test
survives the full operator set. Saturation proves neither transitivity
of the free nonabelian index group nor equality of either source image.
No additional affine-index test is proposed.

## Current target

The useful sufficient target remains J_mov=N_mov (or the exact actual
compact equality). Its alpha1 transform would supply the other family,
and their product is the full ambient filling kernel R. We now retain
all source operators and have actual whole-kernel intersections in the
rank-eight and rank-nine free factors above. A proof must control the
remaining retained directions on the **whole** partial source image,
or supply a direct finite-cover/relative-variation comparison. No such
uniform normalization has been established.

A tentative implication “D normalizes I, therefore the C orbit induction
automatically closes” is not used. Even if D normalizes the final I,
a stage known C-normal need not stay C-normal after D conjugation. The
simultaneous normalizer argument would need its own invariant stages.
If both C and D normalize I then A,B do, because alpha0(I)=I and
alpha0(D)=AC; that sufficient implication itself is exact.

## Rejected stronger target: normality of the whole source group

A subsequent suggestion to prove normality of the whole source image H
by checking conjugation of its finite generating list is false as a target.
Root identified the exact quotient obstruction. After filling both U
families, H maps onto the source section H0 in the retained split bundle
F3 semidirect F6 (or its ordered F5 semidirect F11 comparison). If a
section H0 were normal in a semidirect product N semidirect H0, then
[n,h] would lie in both N and H0, hence would be identity for every n,h.
Its monodromy action on N would therefore be trivial. The actual root
half-twist alpha0 acts nontrivially on the retained fiber; in the
unordered marking it sends B to A and A to A B A^-1. Therefore H0,
and hence H, cannot be normal in the full ambient group.

This invalidates the proposed finite whole-H conjugation target. It does
not invalidate the required weaker target: uniform normalization of the
partial kernel J_mov or the full filling-kernel image I. Common local
relations can still give seed conjugations, but their extension to all
H-conjugates must not be achieved by falsely assuming H itself normal.
