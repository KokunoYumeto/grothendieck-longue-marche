Nonmatched nodes impose further relations, and their full based braid
conjugators remain part of the presentation. A local primitive meridian
or local free-product factor need not remain a free-product factor after
other blocks are attached. Therefore the local amalgam description does
not by itself give a graph-of-groups structure for the whole orbifold
group, a normal form for arbitrary mixed words, or a fixed-source theorem.
