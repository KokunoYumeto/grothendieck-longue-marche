This review does not claim an independent re-execution of those computations.
The integral equations, dimensions, source-word membership, and the passage
from the certified first layer to all levels were reviewed independently.

1. The actual lattice and the Eichler order.

The even-strand branched-cover homology lattice is ker(epsilon)/Z*1, not the
unreduced 12-dimensional module. The rank-ten basis and quotient operation
used by the certificate agree with that lattice. The actual signed A5 lifts
have character twice the irreducible degree-five character in characteristic
zero. No semisimplicity or irreducibility assertion in characteristic three
is used; indeed that assertion would be false for the degree-five factor.

The commuting algebra has dimension four, and its symplectic Lie algebra
dimension three. The displayed I,H,E,F are integral at three and independent
modulo three in the ambient matrix lattice. Together with the dimension
calculation, this proves they span the full saturated Z_3 order: a larger
finite-index lattice would make their reductions dependent. The multiplication
identities identify this order with [[Z_3,Z_3],[3 Z_3,Z_3]], with H=diag(1,-1),
E=E_12, F=3E_21. The symplectic adjoint gives the ordinary two-by-two norm.

The filtration must be the filtration of this order in Mat_10(Z_3). Thus
g-I in 3O corresponds to [[1+3a,3b],[9c,1+3d]], not to an unrestricted
lower-left entry divisible by three. The draft preserves this distinction.
The determinant equation determines d uniquely from a,b,c, so the resulting
principal group is homeomorphic to Z_3^3. Projective centralization adds no
extra components: conjugation signs on the chosen linear A5 lift would give
a homomorphism A5 -> C2, which is trivial. Projectivization is injective on
the linear principal identity subgroup, where the sign is uniquely fixed.

2. The two algebraic derived centralizers.

Restriction of the degree-five character to A4 is 1'+1''+3, while its
restriction to C5 is the regular character. With multiplicity two and the
symplectic form, their connected centralizers over a splitting field are
GL2 x SL2 and SL2 x GL2 x GL2. Their derived dimensions are therefore six
and nine. These are not the dimensions of the full centralizers, which are
seven and eleven. The centralizing SL2 of A5 lies in both derived groups
because its map to either abelian torus quotient is trivial.

Source lifts centralize the linear A4 and C5 lifts: any projective ambiguity
would give a character from either group to C2, and both character groups
are zero. The full algebraic centralizers are connected by the displayed
decompositions, so source commutators lie in the connected derived groups.

Each of B.63's n_j is a difference of conjugates of the actual rigid word h,
including the commutators [e_1,h] and [e_3,h]. Thus it lies both in the normal
closure of h and in the source commutator subgroup. All nine c_j are actual
commutator words in H5. This source condition is stronger than merely having
zero ambient homology, and is exactly the condition needed in B.64.

3. Saturated logarithm lattices and generation at every 3-power level.

For either connected derived centralizer G, L=Lie(G) intersect Mat_10(Z_3)
is saturated and has rank six or nine. Matrix logarithm and exponential
converge on 1+3Mat and 3Mat at p=3. The centralizer and symplectic equations
are respected. In a splitting field, each derived factor is characterized
by determinant one, whose logarithm is trace zero. Although isotypic
projectors can have denominators divisible by three, the eigenvalues of a
restricted block of an integral ambient matrix remain algebraic integers:
they are among the ambient eigenvalues. Consequently the determinant
characters of a matrix I+3X belong to 1+3O_K. Scalar logarithm is injective
on this subgroup (normalized valuation at least one, strictly beyond the
p-adic exponential radius). The derived-group condition is therefore
equivalent to the Lie condition on this neighborhood, even over the
ramified splitting field. Saturation then gives exactly exp(3^r L).

Squaring the actual B.63 source words removes the possible central sign of
a linear lift of a projectively trivial mod-three matrix. If the normalized
first layer is X, the squared word has log/3 congruent to 2X modulo three.
The scalar two is a unit, so the six and nine independent first-layer vectors
remain independent. Saturation and the matching rank identify them with
bases of L/3L, not merely bases of a possibly smaller source lattice.

For U_r=exp(3^r L), the quotient U_r/U_(r+1) is L/3L and cubing carries
exp(3^r X) to exp(3^(r+1) X). Thus powers of the chosen actual words give a
basis in every layer. Successive approximation gives a convergent product
matching any element of U_1. This proves the closed subgroup generated by
the words is U_1. The argument directly supplies the required generation;
it does not assume a finite-endomorphism or a characteristic-zero density
criterion in place of the integral first-layer computation.

4. The upper-bound gate is correctly avoided.

I initially flagged that six or nine first-layer directions alone cannot
exclude new torus directions in deeper principal layers of the complete
individual H_T or H5 images. The present draft does not need that inference.
It proves lower bounds for the actual derived source images, and bounds
their common image by the A5 centralizer. An element in both image closures
centralizes both A4 and C5, hence the A5 generated by them. Conversely the
principal A5 centralizer lies in the two derived principal groups already
generated by the actual source words. These two containments prove the
stated exact intersection without any assertion that an individual full
monodromy principal fibre has no deeper torus direction.

5. Full/profinite and actual-K scope.

The closure of R_T inside hat(H_T), and the closure of H5' inside hat(H5),
map onto their respective compact 3-adic image closures. This uses compactness
and density, not a claim that the possibly infinitely generated R_T has a
separately embedded full profinite completion. Each element of the displayed
common principal group therefore has a compatible lift in each source
completion separately. The proof does not equate those lifts in hat(P), and
does not construct a nonidentity element of the actual residual group K.
B.62 and finite-group centralization give only q_infinity(K) contained in
the displayed principal A5 centralizer. The statement and final paragraph
retain this exact limitation.

Conclusion: all substantive steps of the current B.64 draft pass. Its
nontrivial common 3-adic image is an all-level obstruction to deciding K by
this representation alone, not evidence that K itself is nontrivial.
