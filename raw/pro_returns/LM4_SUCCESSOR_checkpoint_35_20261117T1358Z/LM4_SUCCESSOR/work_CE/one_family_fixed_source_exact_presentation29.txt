single free generator a_(sH). Consequently the norms A_(sL), for all

reduced word in them remains a nontrivial reduced word in distinct

In the original Gamma0, let A_src=s1 s4^-1 s1^-1 be the actual corrected

             A -> A_src,   m -> m,   v -> v             (5)
extends to a homomorphism J_a->Gamma0^delta if and only if
             [A_src,l]=1 for every actual l in L.       (6)
If it extends, its composition with the fixed quotient is the identity

The condition (6) is not proved here. The known <m> subset L accounts

An arbitrary section of the fixed-subgroup surjection might use different
fixed lifts of its generators, so failure of (6), if later found, would
not alone exclude every possible fixed section. Conversely, no existence
is inferred just from fixed surjectivity.
