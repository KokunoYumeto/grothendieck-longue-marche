An actual finite source-central counterexample to unrestricted equivariant R2
16November2026. Research theorem, with an explicit mixed-multiplier limit.

Let E0=F semidirect<c>, F=Fhat(x,y), cxc=x^-1,cyc=y^-1. Thus E0 is
C2 coproduct C2 coproduct C2, with named reflections c,xc,y^-1c. Put
N=ker(F->(Z/12)^2). There is an automorphism theta of the ACTUAL central
source refinement E0/[N,E0] which preserves every named reflection
class and every named pair-product cusp class with one common profinite
unit multiplier, and which fixesc. It lifts an automorphism of E0/N.
Nevertheless no full reflection-class-preserving peripheral automorphism
of E0 induces it. Even an infinite-order full lift is impossible.

This does not refute the multiplier-one same-action equivariant rule.
It is a finite-base version of the mixed-sign phenomenon of A.18/A.39,
strengthened to absence of any c-equivariant lift rather than merely
absence of a full involutory lift.

1. Compute the exact actual central refinement.
Write w=[x,y]. Killing[N,F] makes F class2, since F' is containedN,
and forces w^12=1. Conversely those relations make x^12,y^12,w central,
so they make allN central. Killing[N,c] imposes x^24=y^24=1, becausec
inverts x^12,y^12, and imposes no additional relation onw because
c(w)=[x^-1,y^-1]=w in the class2 quotient. Consequently
 Ddagger=E0/[N,E0]
has normal forms x^a y^b w^d c^e, a,b modulo24,d modulo12,e modulo2.
Their exact multiplication is
 (a,b,d,e)(u,v,k,f)=
 (a+(-1)^e u,b+(-1)^e v,d+k-b(-1)^e u,e+f).
This constructs the converse quotient and proves that no hidden relation
reduces its order. Thus|Ddagger|=13824, its orientation subgroup has
order6912, and its kernel overQ0=(Z/12)^2 semidirectc has order48,
isomorphic toC2^2 xC12, generated byx^12,y^12,w. It is central.

2. The transported automorphism already exists on that entire refinement.
Set theta(x)=x^7,theta(y)=y^7,theta(c)=c. It fixesw because49=1mod12,

It transports the original quotient tuple by an actual automorphism

3. No full equivariant lift, of any order.
Suppose Phi were a full reflection-class-preserving peripheral lift.
It preserves the orientation subgroupF, because the common parity
character takes value1 on each named reflection class. Its image ofc
is conjugate toc. The conjugator may be chosen inF: multiply an odd
conjugator byc if necessary. ConjugatingPhi by this inner element
normalizes it to fixc without changing its common multiplierLambda.
The image on the abelianization of the orientation subgroup ofDdagger
forcesLambda=7mod24, in particularLambda=7mod12.

NowPhi|F commutes withc. WritePhi(x)=a x^Lambda a^-1. Commuting withc
implies a^-1c(a) centralizesx, and therefore equalsx^t; here we use the
full profinite centralizer of a primitive free generator, which is its
procyclic subgroup. Writea_ab=(r,s). Abelianization gives(t,0)=(-2r,-2s),
so s=0 andt=-2r. Thena x^-r is literallyc-fixed. The established full
fixed-group theorem C_F(c)=1 givesa=x^r. ConsequentlyPhi(x)=x^Lambda.
The identical argument givesPhi(y)=y^Lambda. This uses full profinite
fixedness, not a claim that every finite fixed group is trivial.

Finally evaluate inS4 atX=(234),Y=(1234), rightmost permutation first.
WhenLambda=7mod12, X^Lambda Y^Lambda has order2, while(XY)^Lambda has
order4. These cannot be conjugate. The third-cusp condition fails.
Hence no normalized full lift exists, and therefore no original one
exists either. No assumption that the full lift squares to1 was used.

5. Precisely what counting assertion is refuted.
The source is the actual three-reflection free product. The ORIGINAL
quotient epimorphism is E0->Q0, and the desired transported head tuple
is theta applied to its three named reflection images. The desired
central-source action exists on E0/[N,E0], with all named classes.
A rule asserting that such central data suffice for a full equivariant
peripheral lift would givePhi and is therefore false. This counterexample
uses a mixed unit, so it does not decide whether a restricted theorem
holds for common multiplier1, or for a prescribed full negative action
and a narrower choice of frames. It also does not give an explicit
minimal finite larger target at which the proper transported tuple
count vanishes; compactness guarantees some finite obstruction, and
its extraction is a separate finite-count question.
