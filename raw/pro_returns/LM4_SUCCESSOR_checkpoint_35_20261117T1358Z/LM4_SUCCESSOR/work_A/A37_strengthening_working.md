## Peripheral endomorphisms are automatically automorphisms

Let F=pi1hat(S0,m), m>=3, with oriented peripherals x1,...,xm and product1. Suppose a continuous endomorphism phi sends each xi to a conjugate of x_pi(i)^lambda, where pi is a permutation and lambda is a common profinite unit. Then phi is onto and hence an automorphism.

If its closed image H were proper, choose a proper open subgroup U containing H. Let U0=U intersection pi1(S0,m) be the corresponding discrete cover, of degree d. Every peripheral image lies in U and is a unit power of an F-conjugate of its base peripheral generator. Therefore the U-coset represented by its conjugator is a width-one cusp above that base puncture. A profinite conjugator can be replaced by a discrete representative of its U-coset; the difference belongs to U, so the resulting elements represent exactly the corresponding oriented boundary classes in U_ab. These m classes are distinct because they lie above the m different base punctures.

Applying phi to x1...xm=1 and then abelianizing U gives lambda times the sum of those m selected boundary classes equal0. In the homology of any connected oriented finite cover with boundary, the only relation among all boundary classes is their full sum0. This integral statement remains exact after profinite completion: the boundary submodule is Zhat^r/Zhat(1,...,1). Since lambda is a unit, the selected sum vanishes only if these m selected classes are ALL r boundary classes. Each has width1, and there is one above each base puncture. The sum of cusp widths over any fixed base puncture is d, so d=1. This contradicts U proper.

The proof uses the common multiplier orientation essentially, and uses a full open subgroup of F, not a pronilpotent replacement. It does not assert that finite quotient maps preserving three conjugacy classes are automatically onto.
