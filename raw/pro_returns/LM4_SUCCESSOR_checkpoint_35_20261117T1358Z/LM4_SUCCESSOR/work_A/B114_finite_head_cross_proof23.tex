\emph{B.114.6. Both new relators are genuinely killed elements.}
The base winding on $R_-$ is zero on $K$ and one on $k_-$.
Hence $k_-\notin K$. The element $c_+$ is odd, so $c_+\notin K$.
Therefore $[c_+,k_-]$ is a reduced alternating word of length
four in $\mathcal A$ and is nontrivial. The reflected argument
applies to $[c_-,k_+]$.

These elements also survive in the full completion. In Section~2
prescribe $M_-$ to lie in the kernel of inner base winding modulo
two, and $M_+$ in the analogous outer kernel; retain orientation
as well. The compatible refined finite heads still put $k_-$
outside $D$ in $A_-$ and $k_+$ outside $D$ in $A_+$. The cones
remain odd and lie outside $D$. Both length-four words are
therefore reduced and nontrivial in $V$. Its residual finiteness
gives finite quotients detecting each word; the product of those
quotients detects both. Composing with $\mathcal A\to V$ proves
their nontriviality in $\widehat{\mathcal A}$. This conclusion
uses no residual-finiteness assumption on the original vertices.
