## Mathematical reading check actually performed

The displayed relation for eta_(i+1) was substituted into the displayed expression −eta_(i+1)+e_(i+1); collection of the e coefficient gives 1−alpha. The independently inspected dash in crop 09 agrees with that result. The check did not license any transcription change. The ambiguous i−1/i+1 below the rule remains ambiguous, and the beta=0 and alpha=±1 conclusions were not treated as verified proofs.
