## Collation and remaining uncertainty

The principal H reading change is the six individual i-versus-1 decisions in the lambda expansion. H also omits the upside-down block, both bottom-left delta starts, several local cancellations, the lower evaluations/brackets, the cancelled RHS in the vertical chain, the circle, underline, initials, and two isolated ticks. Its expanded `impair`, omitted `si` readings, literal punctuation, and descriptive wrappers are separately located in `report.json`. H's `\note` text is treated as apparatus; its explicit acknowledgement of the page cancellation is preserved in the assessment.

For C, the equals sign below the left box and the isolated cancelled `xf_1` are resolved. The extra paired ticks, the superposed minus at the cancelled upper-right loop, and the physical alignment of the vertical chain are reflected in the proposed page. The remaining marked loci are the descended η/γ, extra top mark, lower-annotation minus, cancelled terminal zero, cancelled a/0, cancelled f₁, cancelled −eᵢ, even-branch eta index, following e/a, initials, and the function of the paired ticks.

The visible written coefficients and indices remain in the transcription. The reading did not perform a proof check, so `check_events` is empty. All 31 supplied inventory items are accounted for, plus separate entries for the underlining, circle, paired ticks, archive numeral, and frame.
