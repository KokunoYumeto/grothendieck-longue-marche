## H emphasis — clarified during scan 005

The supplied `hua/transcripts/preamble/grothendieck.sty`, lines 45–46, says that H transcriptions use `\emph` for Grothendieck’s own emphasis. Accordingly, H `\emph{words}` and our `\uline{words}` carry the same emphasis reading when their exact marked span agrees. That macro conversion alone contributes no collation row. The ink still determines the underlined span and the single or double stroke in our transcription. This clarification was sent individually to all three independent scan005 readers. No reader’s ink decision was supplied to another reader.

An audit of completed scan002–004 decision records found no H-emph rows to reclassify.
