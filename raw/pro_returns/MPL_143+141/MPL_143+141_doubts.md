# Legibility and limits

Overall assessment: The printed covers and notices are substantially clearer than the hurried manuscript. The least secure readings are the rotated or clipped marginal qualifications on 143/006, 008, 012, 015, 030 and 031, the altered categorical notation on 143/018–026, and the dense sketches and cancellations on 141/010 and 014–015. Every whole scan and both supplied halves were personally inspected without OCR; all 50 pages received a separate second reading, followed by a cross-page notation check. The 291 doubt entries are explicit reading uncertainties, not claims that all other readings are error-free. TikZ reconstructs the visible geometry and labels, but line shapes and physical spacing are approximations; cancellations and clipped passages remain particularly vulnerable to residual error.
