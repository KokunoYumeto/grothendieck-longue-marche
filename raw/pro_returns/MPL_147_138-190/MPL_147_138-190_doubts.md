# Doubts, abbreviations, and reading limits

## Scope and location conventions

This record accompanies a visual diplomatic transcription of scans 138–190. Every supplied whole-page image and both overlapping halves was directly inspected; enlarged and rotated crops were used for difficult passages, and each page received an independent visual comparison. No OCR was used. Readings below are the readings retained in the transcription, including the author's unusual notation, repeated numbers, abbreviations, and cancellations.

The line references locate the reading in its separate `pages/MPL_147_138-190_scanNNN.tex` file; they are not a claim that LaTeX source lines reproduce the handwritten line breaks. A nearby equation number is an additional locator and may precede, rather than contain, the reading. The table includes every retained `\unsure` and `\illegible` occurrence, including nested annotations and repetitions. An uncertainty can be a letter, an ending, an arrow, a numeral, or several words; a plausible mathematical interpretation was not treated as sufficient evidence to remove it. Confidence labels are conservative editorial assessments of the ink, not probabilities or mathematical truth values; canceled or extensively overwritten passages are generally classified low.

## Legibility and limits

The larger prose and most unaltered formulas are appreciably more legible than the cramped diagonal notes, overwritten indices, and canceled attempts. The least secure areas include the opening of 138, the last phrase of 143, the diagonal note on 144, the bibliographic numerals on 145 and 148, the arrow in (240), the canceled comparison on 147, the canceled corollaries on 151–152, the layered definition and notes on 161, both canceled notes and the discarded table on 163, the revisions on 166, the long margin on 177, and the revisions on 185 and 189–190. The retained single unreadable-word span is in the canceled upper margin of 161 and remained obstructed after enlargement; no formula or diagram symbol is replaced by an illegible-word count.

Repeated manuscript page numbers, the repeated equation label (303), locally changing stars and primes, a visibly surprising `d−1` in a canceled formula, and arrow directions have been preserved. TikZ drawings approximate the geometry and relative placement of the pen drawings; they are not photographic facsimiles. The curved marks around the discarded table on 163 remain particularly ambiguous between cancellation, enclosure, and relocation marks, and have not been promoted to additional mathematical morphisms.

Balanced source groups and matching environments were checked, and the page sequence, annotations, inventory coverage, and file assembly were checked separately. A typesetting attempt could not run: the available TeX executables lack their format files, document classes, and TikZ packages. Thus this delivery has source validation and visual source comparison, but no successful compiled-layout validation; it is a fragment and intentionally supplies no preamble or definitions of the requested editorial macros.
