## Source and verification boundaries

The local manuscript claims and context comparisons are distinguished throughout from independently checked elementary calculations and published theorems. In particular, no claim of priority, universal nonexistence, or formal verification is inferred from the register's “new to us” field, from an unsuccessful reference search, or from a report of Lean files absent from the supplied package.

The primary Horel, Toën, Borger–Wieland, Richter, and Isbell texts were consulted at the cited statements or sections. Mandell and Berger–Fresse were checked against their journal records and abstracts for the particular claims used; the Dress–Siebeneicher comparisons are restricted to publisher metadata and abstracts because direct full-page access was blocked. Sullivan's publication list and indexed author-hosted text support the provenance lead, but the exact typescript issue and Appendix H pagination have not been independently established.

The published Richter article, rather than the directionally misleading introductory sentence in arXiv v1, is the version used for the comparison in R083. That comparison concerns simplicial chains and a transported tensor product; it is not applied to the manuscript's cosimplicial/cochain setting without an additional variance argument.

The text was structurally checked for the required scan order, macro vocabulary, inventory coverage, braces, environments, and math delimiters. This was not a typesetting check: the environment lacked the LaTeX support packages required for the fragment's `tikz-cd` and related commands, so no successful compiled rendering is claimed. The source drawings are manually reconstructed TikZ approximations, with the permitted crop-candidate comments on scans 040 and 043; no source image is embedded in the transcription.
