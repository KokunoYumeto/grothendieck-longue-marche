# Post-freeze research review

The transcription and its 43 hashes were frozen before any of this research. Three research workers assisted with primary-literature verification, contextual comparison, and independent mathematical or editorial checks. They did not read the scans or write any transcription file. The primary reader retained responsibility for the manuscript readings and all decisions in the final notes.

## Mathematical checks retained

The explicit divided-square computation in R014, the contractible-complex examples in R022/R024/R026, the boundary-sphere model in R039, the nonadditive homogeneous-map example in R047, and the finite-free bimorphism in R055 received independent checks. These checks validate the stated conditional mathematical examples, not the uncertain underlying ink or an interpretation of the full unseen manuscript.

The review sharpened the fiber-rank formulation in R023 to distinguish each selected prime from the generic point and unselected primes. It supplied the Tor calculation in R025, since an infinite displayed resolution alone does not prove infinite projective dimension. It also restored the second chain-map equation in R036 and identified the kernel of the projection to the first component as torsion in the cycle module, rather than in an arbitrary term.

The weak-target-Σ example in R100 was checked against each supplied Sex1–Sex2 axiom. Its scope is deliberately conditional: it does not satisfy the stronger condition that every morphism have an admissible target kernel, and it is not a counterexample to an unseen exact-category formulation. “The same disks” was sharpened to the same disk cochain complex including the differentials; the broader extension in R099 explicitly assumes a target with the needed kernels.

R098 was corrected to state only the valid one-way limitation on conservativity. Full recovery of mapping spaces would imply reflection of equivalences; no contrary independence claim remains in the final note. R107 independently distinguishes cotensors constructed by kernels from tensor objects that can require cokernels.

## Editorial checks retained

The final 107 notes have distinct IDs, short one-to-four-sentence bodies, a kind label, source scan locators, and context relationships. Every note has at least one exact anchor in the frozen page text, and each has its own supplied-context search in the ledger. Source-focused notes retain brief words or displayed expressions; doubtful quotations remain subject to the transcription's uncertainty.

Own-idea items give confidence and a reason. The two mandatory standing sections distinguish the manuscript's own question marks from editorial doubt, and distinguish printed or archival numbering from autograph pagination and page totals.

External-source summaries were consolidated in the principal notes. The research memoranda retain access records, contextual evidence, and independent checks without repeating the same theorem summaries. The final note loader reads the canonical Markdown bodies rather than embedding duplicate literature exposition in executable code; a byte comparison confirmed that this refactor left the canonical notes unchanged.
