## Numbered notes


## R001 — Projectivity is a statement about the category of complexes

**Witness:** 137.pdf, scan 002, page-file L6 ff..  
**Kind:** unproved claim / proof clarification.

The lemma says that a degreewise projective, “flasque” simplicial module is projective as a simplicial object, and the NB allows projective \(\pi_0\) with higher homotopy zero. Its connective normalized complex can be split inductively into projective identity disks and a projective degree-zero summand, so the missing proof needs boundedness on the lower side and projectivity of the cycles obtained by that induction, rather than acyclicity alone. This restates the mathematical reconstruction in **Hua, modernised reading of cote 137**, “Préliminaires : projectivité, annulation, et une comparaison,” while adding the categorical distinction; the supplied register has no separate settled lemma, and **WG2 §Week #3** remains an unchecked program item (see also The Stacks Project, n.d.-a, Tag 019G).

## R014 — A direct divided-square obstruction

**Witness:** 137.pdf, scan 010, page-file L7 ff.; scan 012, related passage.  
**Kind:** own idea / independent check of a contextual counterexample; confidence high.

For the standard simplicial \(K(\mathbb Z,1)\), the normalized quotient of its degreewise divided square has \(C_1=\mathbb Z\gamma_2(e)\), \(C_2=\mathbb Z(e_1e_2)\), and no higher terms, because every degree-two monomial in three or more coordinates lies in a degeneracy image. The three faces in degree two send \(e_1e_2\) to \(0,2\gamma_2(e),0\), hence the differential is multiplication by \(-2\) and \(H_1=\mathbb Z/2\), disproving the unrestricted \(\Gamma^2\)-closure claim over \(\mathbb Z\). This independently checks the divided-square example already given by **Hua, modernised reading of cote 137**, “Stabilité par produits tensoriels et puissances”; confidence is high because the faces are explicit, but it does not resolve the separate exterior-power claim or identify the ink's intended “faux” target, and **MPL_133 B06** remains a first-pass contextual lead.

## R015 — Why the acyclic-generator argument does not finish the power argument

**Witness:** 137.pdf, scan 012, page-file L8 ff..

## R016 — Ranks alone do not classify arbitrary projective coefficient modules

## R023 — Pointwise polynomial fiber ranks do not imply perfection

**Witness:** 137.pdf, scan 014, page-file L41 ff.; scan 015, related passage.  
**Kind:** own idea / counterexample; confidence high.

Choose distinct primes \(p_i\), \(i\geq1\), let \(N_i=\mathbb Z/p_i\) with zero differential, and let \(L=\mathrm{DK}(N)\), so \(L_n=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n(\mathbb Z/p_i)^{\binom ni}\) is finitely generated. At each selected residue field \(\mathbb F_{p_i}\) its ordinary fiber rank is \(\binom ni\), and at the generic point and any unselected prime it is zero, yet \(H_i(N)=\mathbb Z/p_i\) in unbounded degrees prevents perfection. Confidence is high because the formula is explicit: it contradicts the pointwise criterion as transcribed and also the assertion in **Hua, modernised reading of cote 137**, “Définitions,” that individual constructible coefficients plus noetherianity supply a uniform degree bound, while leaving Hua's separately bounded-degree implication untouched. This adds a counterexample to **WG2 §Week #3** (unchecked), with full details in records/research_counterexamples.md; it does not address the projective-term hypothesis in part c).

## R024 — Derived perfection can coexist with exponential ranks

**Witness:** 137.pdf, scan 014, page-file L41 ff.; scan 015, related passage; scan 017, related passage.  
**Kind:** own idea / counterexample; confidence high.

Let \(N_i=\mathbb Z\) for every \(i\geq0\), with \(d_i=\mathrm{id}\) for odd \(i\) and \(d_i=0\) for even \(i\); this is a locally finite direct sum of identity disks and is contractible. Its inverse Dold–Kan object has \(L_n\cong\mathbb Z^{2^n}\), so all terms are finite free while the derived class is perfect and the rank is not polynomial. Confidence is high from the contraction and binomial identity; this strengthens the strict/derived warning in **Hua, modernised reading of cote 137**, “Définitions” and “Le cas d'un anneau principal,” and refutes necessity in the literal non-strict formulations of scans 014–015 and 017 without challenging their strict versions. The relevant register item is **WG2 §Week #3** (unchecked), with no supplied settled result covering the distinction.
