## Legibility and relative performance of the witnesses

These sheets are mainly continuous handwritten mathematical notes. Most uncancelled prose can be followed at the native resolution, but individual word endings, small indices, high marks and connector tips often cannot be settled from the sentence alone. Enlarging a crop exposes existing strokes; it cannot restore information absent from the scan. The least secure readings are heavily crossed mathematical clusters, revised indices, tiny interlinear additions, the slanted margin qualifications, and words compressed against the right edge. These are kept locally doubtful rather than completed from grammar or mathematical expectation.

WitnessC usually preserves more of the revision history and local uncertainty than H, but it also misses unmarked letter, index, punctuation and placement details. H sometimes supplies a better symbol or recognizes a revision in an editorial note, while elsewhere expanding short forms, supplying words or punctuation, flattening a diagram, or moving a group label. Neither witness is a control transcription made from an independent physical source: both were supplied as AI readings of the same images. Their agreement is therefore not independent evidence for a difficult word.

The confidence labels concern the recorded local reading, not the truth of a mathematical assertion. Some clear manuscript forms are mathematically surprising; they remain clear readings. Conversely a mathematically natural interpretation can remain uncertain when the strokes allow more than one form. The notes and CHECK_EVENTS.tsv distinguish these two questions and delimit every proof, computation, conditional consistency check and background assertion.
