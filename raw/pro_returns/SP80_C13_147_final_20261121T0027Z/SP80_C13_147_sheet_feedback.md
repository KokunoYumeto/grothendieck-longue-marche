## Observations beyond or more specific than the sheet

The ten broad categories cover the accepted locations; no additional disjoint category is needed for these sheets. The following more specific patterns should be added as prompts within those categories. They are observations from this set, not estimated error rates for other papers.

| More specific pattern | Places | Reading practice supported by these pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-sided terminal pen turns treated as complete arrowheads | Short connectors on scans112,117,119–120,122,124,126,129–130 | Distinguish a shaft with a single terminal turn from a two-sided head. Reproduce the visible path and leave its intended map interpretation in the notes where necessary. A nearby fully headed arrow does not establish a head on every other shaft. |
| Nested vertical levels of a group-base subscript flattened into one level | Group labels in(184),(188),(190), scans121–123 | Read the low D base relative to the already lowered m in G_m. Grouped subscripting preserves a real second level without adding a comma. |

A short hook can be drawn without inventing a matching delimiter or duplicated closing parenthesis. |

Shared readings still require inspection. The shortened revêt on scan128 and the opening retraced range digit were not settled by agreement between C and H. Several literal grammatical mismatches and absent articles likewise remain in the edition; grammatical fluency is not the criterion of a diplomatic reading.

The independent rereading records literal tokens. Its raw difference count includes alternative TeX encodings, crop-edge fragments, uncertainty wrappers and different represented extents. The accepted-change count must therefore come from the crop adjudications, not from the raw token distance. Remaining doubts are evidence limits, not proof that either witness is correct.

## Completed blind-reading results

All23 pages have a frozen first reading and an independently written image-only reading for every fixed box. Across441 boxes,352 comparisons differ after ignoring whitespace. Root individually adjudicated those352 and also inspected and changed one equal-reading box,128 B15. The resulting353 adjudications comprise273 retained first readings and80 changed box readings. Seventy-nine changes arose among the differing boxes; the eightieth corrected the shared ellipsis normalization on128 B15. Boxes overlap in some crowded formulas, so80 is a count of changed boxes, not80 independent manuscript errors or a token error rate.

The final changes include letter alternatives, cancelled text, literal punctuation, index punctuation, diagram endpoints and annotation placement. Examples outside simple witness disagreements include the single-prime group base on117; the two separate annotations on130; the agreed continuation normalized by both readers on128; the fibres/flèches uncertainty on130; and commodum/mémoire on131. Independent agreement therefore remained a prompt to inspect the source, never a substitute for it.

The counts distinguish the required fresh crops from later viewing aids: each of the441 fixed boxes has exactly one fresh source crop. A documented additional context crop was made for root's110 B03 adjudication; two115 files are rotated views derived from existing fresh crops. No duplicate fresh crop of a fixed box was silently substituted, and no frozen first or second reading was revised after comparison.
