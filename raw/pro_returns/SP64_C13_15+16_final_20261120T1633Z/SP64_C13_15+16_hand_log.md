# Hand and notation log

Started before scan 15/180. Each observation is confined to the supplied pages.

## 15/180

The isolated motive letter has an N-like pair of sloping strokes and a curled foot; C explicitly offers Z/N. This is a single occurrence, so no general hand rule has yet been inferred. The n inside the parentheses has a rounded low valley.

## 15/183

ζ has a small upper turn and a long sinuous descending stroke in the class and all three later torsion occurrences; H reads ξ in each. The superscript or has an o followed by a low shoulder r, repeated four times; H reads 0u. J has a tall upright and leftward bottom hook, visible in J_R; H reads i_R. These observations hold at the cited occurrences; the possible prime beside i and several struck letters remain unresolved.

## 15/184

Blue mathematical writing on the reused typescript: the subscript φ sometimes has its lower stroke faint enough to resemble a small m; clearer occurrences in the displayed sequences show the descending stem. This observation applies to the blue hand on this page, not automatically to the dark manuscript hand.

## 15/185

The ζ torsion label from 183 recurs in every group and polarization exponent here with the same long descending curve; both witnesses use ξ on this page. These observations are specific to their listed occurrences.

## 15/187

## 15/189

## 15/190

## 15/191

## 15/192

The blue typescript uses separate underscores for many Lie-algebra letters, but g-modules in item 4 has none. Symbols filled by hand share the blue colour of the machine text; colour alone does not determine their hand. The isolated theta retains its capitalization doubt.

## 15/193

## 15/194

## 16/003

## 16/004

## 16/005

The heavily retraced index in the final displayed line has a separate dot, supporting i. ## 16/006
