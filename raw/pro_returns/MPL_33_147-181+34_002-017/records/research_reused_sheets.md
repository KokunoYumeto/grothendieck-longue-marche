### R05. The Jacobson comparison preserves the site of opens, not all points

**File / scans / TeX lines:** cote 33; 163:23–25. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

### R09. An explicit dense subspace realizes the Hilbert-ball sketch

**File / scans / TeX lines:** cote 33; 155:26. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high, because density, separation and the missing support functional can each be checked directly.

For real H = ℓ²(ℕ₀), x = e₀ and G = span{eₙ + n e₀ : n ≥ 1}, the subspace G excludes e₀ but is norm dense, since n⁻¹(eₙ + n e₀) converges to e₀ and then every basis vector lies in its closure. Thus σ(H,G) is Hausdorff and weaker than the ordinary weak topology, while both topologies agree on the weakly compact unit ball by the compact-to-Hausdorff argument. Its continuous dual is G, whereas every supporting functional of that ball at e₀ is a positive multiple of e₀, so no continuous supporting hyperplane remains. This explicit construction is ours and does not recover the missing original conjecture.

**Context relation / status:** **adds** an own construction with no exact earlier counterpart: the dedicated Hilbert/dense-subspace/supporting-hyperplane searches have zero hits in all supplied context. A **N67**, scans 005–049 odd, prevents automatic authorship transfer; no existing result-register status is changed.

### R22. Preserving the exact solution set is an extra step in Fredholm regularization

**File / scans / TeX lines:** cote 33; 179:9–19. **Kind:** own idea / reading question. **Confidence:** high for the logical distinction; no conclusion about the missing continuation.

This is our identification of a proof obligation, not a counterexample to the stated theorem.
