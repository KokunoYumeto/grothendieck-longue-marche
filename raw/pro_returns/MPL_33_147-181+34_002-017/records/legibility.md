## Legibility and confidence

All 49 supplied leaves were read personally in order, first as whole pages and then from both supplied halves, with additional rotations and enlarged crops for small writing. Every leaf received a second visual audit before context was opened. No OCR or other text-recognition system was used, and the transcription was not divided among workers.

The reused typed leaves are usually legible, but their faint carbon impressions, overwritten mathematical alphabets, handwritten accents and corrections remain possible sources of error. Their spelling, grammar, unexplained variables, reversed arrows and incomplete continuations were retained. The handwritten duality leaves are markedly less secure: fluent connecting prose can lose several letters, and cross-outs frequently obscure the difference between the discarded and substituted wording. The SGA 7 sequence has more continuous prose, but the dense cancellation on 34/014 is among the least reliable readings in the package.

The passages I trust least are the narrow rotated margin of 33/152; short prose on 33/148 and 150; the parenthesis about permutation and a sign on 33/158; the proof's “quotient” and “produit/générateur” on 33/172; the very faint “poncti…” on 33/180; the interlinear first-part condition and the sign before “strictement” on 34/003; the abandoned roots-of-unity paragraph on 34/006; the overwritten state of “il existe” on 34/011; the cancelled proof, excellence qualifications and margin on 34/014; “de K” on 34/015; and the cancelled opening of 34/017. Readings in these places are hypotheses about ink, not reliable quotations without consulting the source.

There is one `\illegible{5}` at a physically blacked-out deletion on 33/161; the estimated word count is approximate. Mathematical marks have attempted readings and explicit doubts instead of illegibility placeholders. Medium confidence means a plausible reading whose local strokes do not decide it; low confidence is used for the most obscured or cancelled readings and the passages identified above. The table does not claim that every unmarked character is certainly correct. Its line references locate the final per-page LaTeX source, not reconstructed manuscript line numbering; a containing doubt and a nested doubt may both have entries.

Unfinished formulas and open parentheses are source features. They have not been completed to satisfy a theorem or a later context note. The archive contains several unrelated reused texts, and their presence beside the handwritten research is not evidence of common authorship or a mathematical connection.
