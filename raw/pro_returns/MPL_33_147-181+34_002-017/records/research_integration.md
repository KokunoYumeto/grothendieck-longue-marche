## I02. Resolution hypotheses provide a precise S19 comparison, not a silent upgrade

**File:** 33.pdf. **Scans / approximate TeX lines:** 154:17–20; 156:3–11. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

The qualification “la résolution des singularités à la Hironaka est possible” makes the local Künneth argument conditional, and the next leaf actively seeks finite maps to smooth schemes to avoid increasing dimension. Relative to **MPL_33_098-146 N15** (118), **N20–N21** (122) and **N41** (130–146), first-pass leads in the register, these pages add a concrete dependence of the proposed Verdier pairing on that conditional theorem. Grothendieck's published Exposé III introduction explains that an earlier formula used resolution and incompletely verified compatibilities, whereas the published treatment uses Deligne's finiteness results to remove that restriction (Grothendieck, 1977, pp. 73–74); this is a checked comparison of mathematical scope, not evidence that the present draft already contains the later proof.

## I04. The distinction between a canonical map and an isomorphism remains a useful audit target

## I06. Tame inertia does not answer the full profinite torsion question

**File:** 34.pdf. **Scans / approximate TeX lines:** 010:1–30; 016:1–30; 017:1–40. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

## I07. The two meanings of completion must be kept separate

**File:** 34.pdf. **Scans / approximate TeX lines:** 014:19–26; 015:4–11. **Kind:** unpursued implication.

The claim “3.13” and its rewritten proof concern replacing a henselian discretely valued field by its valuation completion while preserving finite étale extensions. **LM4 F.7** instead asks about a centralizer in a profinite completion of a mapping class group (Open, return claim; §30, folios 194–196, scans 383–387, Parts D(b) and F(b)), so the field-theoretic theorem supplies no missing nonsolvable $A_5$ centralizer argument. The manuscript's completion result has a direct primary comparison in SGA 4 X, Lemma 2.2.1 (Artin, 1973), but that verification leaves the register entry unchanged.
