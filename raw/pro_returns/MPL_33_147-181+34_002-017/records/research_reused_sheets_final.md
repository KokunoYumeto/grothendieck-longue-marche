### R09. An explicit dense subspace realizes the Hilbert-ball sketch

**File:** cote 33 · **Scans / TeX lines:** 155:26 · **Kind:** own idea · **Confidence:** high—direct density, separation and supporting-functional calculations · **Context:** new construction; zero exact Hilbert/dense-subspace/supporting-hyperplane hits in all four supplied context files; A **N67**, scans 005–049 odd, relevant only to authorship caution; no register-status change · **Attribution:** our construction, not a recovery of the missing conjecture

For real H = ℓ²(ℕ₀), x = e₀ and G = span{eₙ + n e₀ : n ≥ 1}, G excludes e₀ but is norm dense, since n⁻¹(eₙ + n e₀) converges to e₀ and then every basis vector lies in its closure. Thus σ(H,G) is Hausdorff and weaker than the ordinary weak topology, while both topologies agree on the weakly compact unit ball by the compact-to-Hausdorff argument. Its continuous dual is G, whereas every supporting functional of that ball at e₀ is a positive multiple of e₀, so no continuous supporting hyperplane remains.
