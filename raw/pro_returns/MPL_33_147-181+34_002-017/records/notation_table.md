# Notation table

This table was maintained during the single-stream transcription and checked across the images before the transcription was frozen. It records visible distinctions and local doubts; it does not standardize the mathematics. An ordinary complex letter, a doubled derived-functor letter, an underline, a cancellation, and a retraced stroke are not interchangeable.

| Recurring manuscript form | LaTeX encoding | Cross-page evidence and cautions |
|---|---|---|
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes, ` ``...'' ` | Used throughout both cotes, including handwritten quotations and typed quotations. No guillemets were substituted. |
| Doubled-stem derived R | `\mathbb{R}` | 33/148, 150, 154, 156, 158, 160, 162, 167, 170, 176, 178, 180; the initial roman convention was corrected after visual comparison. |
| Thin or incompletely doubled R | `\unsure{\mathbb{R}\|\mathrm{R}}` or reversed alternatives | 33/150 rotated margin; 33/167 final Hom; 33/180 second top-row R and final unfinished RH. Alternative order follows the local ink. |
| Ordinary dualizing-complex R | `R_X^{\bullet}` | 33/165, 167, 170, 178; not changed to the derived-functor alphabet. |
| K / R complex-letter ambiguity | `\unsure{R\|K}` | Three opening letters on 33/154 have rounded bowls favouring R; 33/152, 158 and 162 have separate K readings. No mathematical harmonization is made. |
| Doubled L above a tensor or external product | `\overset{\mathbb{L}}{\otimes}`, `\overset{\mathbb{L}}{\boxtimes}` | 33/150, 154, 156, 170, 178, 180; ordinary products without the superscript are retained separately. |
| D with a long low stroke | `\unsure{\underline{D}\|D}` where a separate underline is unresolved | 33/150, 152, 154, 167, 170. Later D on 33/178 and 180 has a continuous low part of the letter and is encoded as D. Category and duality uses were not unified semantically. |
| Internal Hom with a looped H | `\mathcal{H}om` | 33/148–180 handwritten duality leaves; external Hom with an ordinary H is `\operatorname{Hom}`. |
| Sheaf Ext versus ordinary Ext | `\mathcal{E}xt`, `\operatorname{Ext}` with local alternatives | 33/172; the long initial stroke alone does not settle the alphabet. |
| Dot low beside a complex letter | `L_\bullet`, `F_\bullet`, `G_\bullet` | 33/148–170. These are not converted into upper cohomological dots. The tiny low mark at F on 33/178 is doubtful. |
| Dot high beside a complex or functor | `K^\bullet`, `G^\bullet`, `\mathcal{H}om^\bullet` | Position read independently at each occurrence; no normalization of lower and upper grading conventions. |
| Exceptional exclamation mark | `^!`, `_!` in its written position | 33/148–162, 167, 176, 180. R^!f and R_!f retain the mark on R; marks on f, g, i and pr remain on those letters. Cramped marks at 33/154 and 156 have local doubts. |
| Prime | `'` | Especially K′, V′, I′, π′ in 34/012–017; never substituted for a visible exceptional exclamation mark. |
| Pullback star or lower star | `^*`, `_*` | Kept distinct from exclamation marks and grading dots. Typed-page handwritten stars are individually marked as insertions. |
| Restriction dot on i | `i_{X\bullet}`, `i_X^\bullet`, `i_{Z\bullet}` | 33/151, 153, 159, 161: dots are not silently changed to stars. The extra typed i on 33/153 is retained. |
| Doubled coefficient letters | `\mathbb Z`, `\mathbb Q`, `\mathbb P`, `\mathbb N` | 33/154, 155, 165 and the SGA 7 leaves. Z diagonal doubling, P stem doubling and N diagonal doubling were checked from ink. |
| Typed singular-operator letters | `\mathbb H`, `\mathbb S`; doubtful filled forms with `\mathbf` alternatives | 33/177 and 179. Uneven overtyping can fill the doubled stroke; doubtful filled forms have explicit alternatives. |
| Script operator algebra / function spaces | `\mathcal G`, `\mathcal F`, `\mathcal S_M`, `\mathcal L`, `\mathcal M` | Reused typed sheets 33/157, 171, 177, 179. |
| Gothic maximal-ideal letter | `\mathfrak m` | 34/003, 006, 013, 014. It remains distinct from a plain subscript m on reused algebra pages. |
| Residue / geometric-point bars | `\bar k`, `\bar K`, `\bar x`, `\bar\eta` | Placement retained, including uncertain bar/tilde on 34/008 and unexpected denominators on 34/012. |
| Henselization tilde and completion hat | `\widetilde V`, `\widetilde K`, `\widehat V`, `\widehat K` | 34/006–016. Nested tilde/hat expressions in the 34/014 margin are retained. |
| Tilde or hat on a category | `\widetilde{...}`, `\widehat{...}`, or `\overset{\inserted{\sim}}{...}` | 33/147–163 reused topology text; added marks are distinguished from typed letters. |
| Isomorphism-like wavy sign | `\simeq`, or `\xrightarrow{\sim}` / `\xleftarrow{\sim}` with a directed shaft | Direction and wavy equality read separately, including the leftward boxed arrow on 33/154. |
| Shaft without a secure arrowhead | `\unsure{\text{---}\|\longrightarrow}` or `\unsure{-\|\longrightarrow}`; `no head` in diagrams | 33/148, 152, 156, 172; 34/007, 012 and surrounding diagrams. The first alternative can deliberately be a dash. |
| Inclusion / hooked arrow ambiguity | `\unsure{\hookrightarrow\|\subset}` | 34/012; not decided by the intended extension of valuation rings. |
| Projective and inductive limits | `\varprojlim`, `\varinjlim`; doubtful underlined limit with alternative | Typed topology pages and 34/003, 004, 007, 010. The little arrow under lim on 34/003 is insecure. |
| Lower-case round index versus zero | `\unsure{o\|0}` where unresolved | 33/181; ordinary x_o and opposite-category superscript o elsewhere are not automatically zeros. |
| Spectral indices and truncations | literal p, q, pq, p+q, 1, 2 with local doubts | 33/176, 178: unfinished formulas, extra factors and uncertain spectral-sequence page retained. |
| Greek inertia and root notation | `\pi`, `\pi_0`, `\Theta_n`, `\varepsilon`, `\mu_n`, `\ell` | 34/003–017; π and a uniformizer π can coexist, while Θ, ε and ℓ are not regularized by expected meaning. |
| Numbers written on a sheet | `\mspage{...}` | Original numbering, including uncertain 9/8 on 34/010; archive edge numbers use `\margin{...}`. Neither is inferred from sequence. |
| Cancellation / interlinear addition | `\deleted{...}`, `\inserted{...}` | Nested edits remain nested. Uncertain cancellation versus underline or insertion is explicit on 34/011 and 015. |
| Underline and double underline | `\uline{...}`, `\uuline{...}` | Extent follows the source, especially the italics instructions on 33/181. No double-underline occurrence was securely distinguished. |
| Curves, brackets, axes, separator strokes | `\figure{\begin{tikzpicture}...}` | Geometrically reproduced with labels, not replaced by descriptions. |
| Directed diagrams and arrays | `\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}...}` or an array | Labels, directions, headless connectors, cancellations and insertions represented within the diagram. |

Abbreviation expansions appear after the doubt register. They are editorial hypotheses and do not enter the transcription.
