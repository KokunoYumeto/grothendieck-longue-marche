## Further recurrent difficulties



The ten supplied categories cover the primary causes found here. No new primary category is claimed. The following recurring local mechanisms deserve explicit attention in later runs; they refine the sheet rather than replace its categories.

- **A doubled letter is an occurrence-level fact.** The first Gamma on 084 (084-07) and the first product Gamma on 085 (085-10) have two stems, while later Gammas on those pages do not. Both rational-field Q forms on 096 (096-09 and 096-13) are doubled; on 099 the barred first Q is doubled and the final Q is plain (099-21). Neither a uniform font substitution nor an inferred difference of mathematical objects is justified.
- **The same small Pi-like outline can have two notational roles.** Capital acting-group Pi and the component functor pi-zero occur on 092 and 097–098. On 092, the upper phrase names components of fibres, while the later definition separately names the acting group Π₀. Its first-consolidation capitalization was withdrawn after the full second reading. The local explanation on 097 and the functor applied to a map in (52) likewise distinguish the component functor. A curved terminal stroke is supporting evidence, not a universal case test. See withdrawn 092-02, 097-31, the annotation beneath (51), and display (52) on 098.
- **An upper flourish can dominate recognition of the whole letter.** Ornate C is repeatedly mistaken for T in groupoid names on 094–097; its lower open bowl is decisive. The ornate G on 085–087 similarly varies between a broad loop and a simpler return. Each ornament is checked with the lower letter body, not from the upper stroke alone. See 094-03/13/47/50/55, 095-08/11, 096-20, and 097-02/05.
- **Crossing text can manufacture a false annotation.** Interlinear strokes on 093 (093-10 and 093-33), densely crossed marginal material on 094 (especially 094-37/39/51/62), the withdrawn purported insertion M0424 on 096, and the slanted margin of 100 require separating strokes by baseline and direction. A stroke from a neighboring line can look like a tilde, prime, quantifier, insertion leader, or an additional marginal word. The supposed separate right-hand additions in C’s scan-100 margin are especially vulnerable to this error.
- **Numbering can be inconsistent even when every digit is clear.** Display numbers and references must be checked individually. The run repeats 34, 41 and 45, and the added degree sentence on 098 visibly cites (52). Correcting these to the argument’s expected numbering would erase source evidence. The notes distinguish the written references from the apparent intended ones.
- **A looped delimiter need not be a grammatical parenthesis.** The two tall signs on 099 have loops and retracing that leave their identity uncertain. Both witnesses’ normal punctuation cannot establish the form; equally, a brace-like outline does not establish a mathematical bracketing function. The isolated sign and the opener before “des paramètres” remain separate doubts.
- **An i point is not automatically the accent expected by spelling.** The full second readings found compact points in forms of apparait and connait on 082, 085–086, 088, 093 and 098. Oblique or compressed marks retain a plain-i/circumflex alternative where their shape does not settle it. On 088, sous-groupoide has one point while the later groupoï-/de has two; lui-même also shows that a genuine circumflex can join the letter. The final groupoide on 096 and the body groupoide on 100 also have a single i point; both 099 forms retain two detached points. Each occurrence therefore needs its own stroke evidence.
- **The existence and number of punctuation strokes are separate decisions.** The cancelled ie. on 080 and second ie. on 086 lack an internal point, whereas the first i.e. on 086 and the i.e. on 090 have it. On 091, an apostrophe and a dotted initial i had been overread as an additional opening quotation pair; the closing pair remains without an invented matching opener. The 093 abbreviation has its internal point but no terminal one. On 094, the two endom occurrences and the second ext lack points that nearby ext. and endom. ord. possess. A sentence or quotation need not be typographically balanced, and adjacent abbreviations need not be pointed alike.
- **An illegibility count can itself be unsupported.** The two crowded marginal runs at M0335/M0341 on 082, the middle marginal run M0428 on 096, and the cancelled run C098-D02 on 098 do not securely expose the word boundaries assumed by a witness or the first consolidation. Their unknown extent is recorded as illegible{?}. Where individual word-like groups are actually separable, their count is retained; an unreadable run is not automatically assigned two or three words.
- **Small independent pen marks can survive outside the prose baseline.** The isolated upright on 079 and short wave below voudrais on 087 were missed by both witnesses and are preserved graphically without an invented word or function. The low point-pair on 088 remains explicitly uncertain as punctuation. Presence of dark ink, shape, attachment and semantic function are separate questions; recording a trace does not establish a colon, underline or correction sign. The two high points beside equality on 093 are reproduced neutrally, whereas two points directly beneath the Theta contour on 095 have no independent textual position and are excluded as incidental traces.

- **A low letter body can be mistaken for another underline.** In the slanted first note on 095, the connected bodies of un lie above one independent straight underline. C and the first consolidation counted both as underlines; the second reading and root review correct 095-37 to a single underline. H’s italic emphasis is equivalent here and creates no substantive difference.

- **A shared t-crossbar can resemble a cancellation.** The long upper line of pourtant on 098 joins its two t stems above the low a/n bodies. The first consolidation treated it as a strike; the full second reading and root review prefer live text while retaining a deleted alternative. Existing 098-36 and C098-D07 record this reopened state, without inventing another comparison locus.

- **A proper name’s familiar spelling does not settle a connected diacritic.** On 100 the name is transcribed Teichm\unsure{û|ü}ller: the single joined arch favors circumflex, while a joined umlaut remains possible. Both witnesses give ü without doubt. This is a retained disagreement at a changed preferred reading, not an established error excluding the alternative. See 100-A01.
