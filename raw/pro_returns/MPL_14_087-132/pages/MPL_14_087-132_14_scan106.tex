\[
f\in\unsure{\mathfrak r A|\operatorname{rad}A},\qquad
\left\{\begin{array}{l}X=S-\text{pt fermé}\\Y=X\cap V(f)\end{array}\right.
\]
\(X,Y\) réguliers
\[
U=X-Y
\]

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
