### P05 — Cote 14, scan 094, marginal “Critère numérique” — Own idea: the curve-and-top test in dimension three

### M16 — Cote 14, scans 087 and 089, `Hom`; C 16/021 — Unpursued implication: Tate and anabelian Hom

### M17 — Cote 14, scans 092 and 117; C 16/049 — Unpursued implication: semisimplicity and centres

For a connected affine group scheme over a characteristic-zero field, semisimplicity of its finite-dimensional representations is equivalent to pro-reductivity, which does not imply a trivial centre (`G_m` is an immediate counterexample) (Deligne & Milne, 1982/2012, Proposition 2.23). For a hyperbolic curve in characteristic zero the geometric profinite group `Δ` is slim, hence centre-free, and its arithmetic group is slim if `G_K` is slim; pulling back `1→Δ→Aut(Δ)→Out(Δ)→1` along the outer Galois action recovers the arithmetic extension (Mochizuki, 2004, Lemma 1.3.1 and Corollary 1.3.2). C 16/049's “semi-simple” motive programme and C 16/005's centre of a correspondence algebra are distinct from this profinite-centre assertion, which was not found in current 14. **R WG2 §2.3 #7** and **WG2 §2.4 #3** remain unchecked, while **LM1 A.8** records an unreviewed refutation of an overstrong `GL₂`-uniqueness claim, another reason to retain central data.
