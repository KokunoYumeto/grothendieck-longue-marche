1. The compact fundamental set
------------------------------
The quotient map for a group action is open. Images of relatively
compact open neighborhoods cover O/Q; compactness gives a finite
subcover. Their finitely many closures form a compact C contained in
O and meeting every Q-orbit. Thus QC=O follows with no orbit-section
or fundamental-domain assumption.

2. Actual approximants and a small lift of the moving point
---------------------------------------------------------
Given x_n=q_n y_n tending to a frontier point z, a subsequence has
y_n tending to y in C. Density of the actual H_O restrictions in Q
supplies global h_n whose restrictions approximate q_n at y_n.
Each h_n commutes with every element of L=closure(G), since it
commutes with all loop values and hence with G and its closure.

L preserves O and acts transitively. The stated open Polish orbit
map permits a selection l_n tending to the identity with l_n y=y_n.
The neighborhood-basis construction in the text proves that
selection for the specific sequence without asserting a continuous
local section. Commutation gives exactly
h_n y=l_n^{-1}h_n y_n.
The points h_n y_n tend to z; the inverse l_n converge uniformly
on an ambient compact neighborhood of z. Hence h_n y tends to z.

Every member of J_y=L_y fixes h_n y and therefore z. The restrictions
h_n|O are continuous L-equivariant maps O->R^2. C.42's evaluation
homeomorphism consequently makes them converge uniformly on every
compact subset of O to the actual map Phi_z^y. This is valid also
when the range is a point. No arbitrary q_n has been assigned a
global extension; the global maps are exactly the chosen h_n.

3. The fixed-basepoint comparison and the empty family
----------------------------------------------------
For a in L with ax=y, the stabilizer is J_y=a L_x a^{-1}.
Thus z_0=a^{-1}z is fixed by L_x. L preserves O and its boundary,
so z_0 remains on the frontier. The equation
Phi_{z_0}^x(l y)=Phi_{z_0}^x(l a x)=l a z_0=l z
proves equality with Phi_z^y on all of O, including its exact range.

C.42 identifies the range with Gz=Lz. It lies in the boundary.
No open G-orbit can meet the boundary of a different open orbit,
since such an open neighborhood would be disjoint from O but would
contain a point of closure(O). Thus a nontrivial range is precisely
an intrinsic line or circle of the kind defining the original
partition family. Each frontier point can be approached by a
sequence from O, so an empty family makes every frontier range a
point. This proves that all frontier points are fixed by G.

4. Intrinsic contractions and full quotient equations

5. Joint frontier continuity and the inverse
------------------------------------------
The union S of C and all its images under the intrinsic contraction
and inverse is compact in O, because both maps are jointly continuous
on the compact parameter square times C. Density now chooses h_n
approximating q_n uniformly on that single S. This stronger choice
retains every preceding convergence argument.

Commutation gives K_n x_n=q_n K_n y_n. Uniform approximation on S
compares this point with h_n K_n y_n with error less than 1/n.
The points K_n y_n converge to K y and remain in S. Compact-open
convergence of h_n to Phi_z^y then gives
h_n K_n y_n -> Phi_z^y K y = Q^{Gz}_{s,t} z.
All equalities use the actual quotient equation; the point-orbit
case uses its constant specialization.

Every subsequence has a further subsequence with a convergent
fundamental-set representative, and the resulting limit is
independent of every choice. This proves the entire sequence
converges. The argument permits varying parameters and therefore
proves joint frontier continuity. Sequences in the boundary are
handled by C.34. Splitting a sequence into its interior and boundary
subsequences proves continuity on the complete closure.

The exact quotient equation rearranges to the corresponding equation
for inverse maps. Their images of C were already included in S, so
the identical argument gives jointly continuous inverse maps on the
closure. The maps preserve O and each frontier orbit, and their
piecewise formulas are mutual inverses. Hence they are actual
homeomorphisms, with the asserted based edges and endpoint values.

6. Operator transport and finite pasting
--------------------------------------
Conjugation by an original operator identifies the actual restriction
groups, their quotient spaces and complete boundary quotient families.
The cocompactness and partition-count hypotheses are therefore
preserved. Choose the intrinsic construction on one component from
each operator orbit. The exact stabilizer commutation makes its
transport independent of the transporting element. Median naturality
makes the transported frontier value the same global C.34 value.

Distinct treated closures meet in the closed nonopen locus Y. Their
maps and inverse maps equal the corresponding C.34 formulas there.
Finite closed-set pasting gives continuous mutually inverse families
on Y and any finite collection of the closures. An operator may
carry such a finite collection to a different collection; the proof
correctly states the induced intertwining map between the two unions
rather than silently assuming each finite collection is invariant.

There is no unproved step from this per-orbit compact fundamental
set to uniform control over infinitely many components. Likewise
the median/exponential comparison on X_* is not inferred. The exact
scope and the remaining C.12 obligations are correctly retained.
