# D.35: exact involution-conjugator criterion

The TeX result gives a proved parametrization, not a claim that all its solution spaces are nonempty.

Let F=Fhat(x,y), let P be the full pure three-cusp group, and let delta invert x and y. D.34 supplies a normalized involution alpha=(-1,f) and a solution h0 in F' of f=h0^-1 alpha(h0).

## The exact remaining equation

All coboundary solutions are h=c h0 with c in the actual fixed subgroup C_F(alpha). This fixed subgroup lies in F' because alpha acts by -1 on full abelianization.

The normalized endomorphism beta(x)=x^mu, beta(y)=h y^mu h^-1 intertwines alpha beta=beta delta exactly when h^-1 alpha(h)=f. Its third-cusp requirement is

    x^mu h y^mu h^-1 = u (xy)^mu u^-1.

The closed image is generated by two elements with independent ambient mod-two characters, hence has first cohomology of dimension exactly two. The third-cusp equation puts all three full cusp Z_2 parts in this image. D.32 therefore forces the image to be F; the finitely generated profinite Hopfian property makes beta an automorphism. This closes the full-generation implication once that displayed relation is present.

The solution space modulo the explicit right action u -> u(xy)^a maps homeomorphically to the actual P-conjugator set. Thus no normalization or change of conjugacy group is implicit.

## Exact bridge from P-conjugacy to GT_0-conjugacy

The key auxiliary equality is C_{P^1}(delta)=1, where P^1 is the multiplier-one kernel. A normalized pair (1,k) commuting with delta has delta(k)=k. The explicit isomorphism

    F semidirect_delta C2 = C2 coproduct C2 coproduct C2

sends the three factors to s, xs, ys. Full-profinite free-factor malnormality makes the centralizer of s exactly C2, so C_F(delta)=1 and k=1.

Harbater-Schneps Propositions 3-4 identify GT_0 exactly with C_P(S3), with theta(x)=y, theta(y)=x and omega(x)=y, omega(y)=(xy)^-1 as the specified outer S3 generators. For alpha in GT_0 and a P-conjugator beta, the element r=beta^-1 sigma beta sigma^-1 lies in P^1 and centralizes delta. Hence r=1 for every sigma in S3. Therefore beta belongs to GT_0. The Harbater-Schneps equations I and II follow, rather than being suppressed.

## Finite criterion

For every open characteristic N, take Q=F/N and m=exp(Q). Enumerate triples (a,b,v) in (Z/m)^units x Q' x Q satisfying the two displayed equations modulo N. All such finite sets are nonempty exactly when the full conjugator set is nonempty. The proof uses a common characteristic refinement for each finite collection of conditions and compactness of Zhat^units x F' x F. The map F' -> Q' is onto; unit residues lift by CRT.
