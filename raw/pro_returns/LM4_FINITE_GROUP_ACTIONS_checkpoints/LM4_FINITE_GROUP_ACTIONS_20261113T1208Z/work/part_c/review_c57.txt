1. Actual lift group and preservation of the full family.

For a lift q of a local centralizer element, q f_t q^{-1} is a lift of
the same downstairs path as f_t and has the same identity initial value.
Thus it equals the specified based lift. This uses all source lifts,
including lifts of orientation-reversing local maps, and does not assume
that q extends to the ambient plane. C.55's equation gives preservation
of the full two-element global family. The two C.45 partitions therefore
give exactly a coordinate pair or a coordinate exchange. Deck maps
preserve each selected partition separately through the original
target-cover equation.

The C.49 transitivity argument does not use a Lie conclusion: compact
word tracks and countably many deck translates cover the source. Baire
gives interior to one orbit. Transitivity downstairs and commutation
with decks make all source orbits translates, hence all open; connectedness
then leaves one orbit. Both target groups are consequently transitive.

2. Full line centralizers.

An element of the centralizer of a transitive line group that fixes one
point fixes the entire line. A decreasing map has a fixed point, so every
nonidentity centralizer element is increasing and fixed-point free.
The previously proved free proper centralizer topology applies to the
closed transitive target group. The actual centralizer is therefore a
Lie group of dimension at most one.

If its dimension is one, its identity component has open orbits on the
line and hence acts transitively. Freeness excludes other components,
and evaluation gives the real translation action. In dimension zero,
properness makes each orbit closed and discrete. A nonidentity element
and its inverse give points on both sides. The nearest positive orbit
point determines a group element; its iterates exhaust the orbit because
there can be neither an intervening orbit point nor a finite accumulation
point. Freeness then identifies the group with Z. Interpolation over a
fundamental interval gives the stated line conjugacy. The identity case
completes the exact list, including the induced topology.

3. Full-plane case.

If q exchanges coordinates, q(x,y)=(a(y),b(x)), commutation with the
path gives a b_{2,t}=b_{1,t} a. The graph of a is a proper nonempty
invariant subset for the entire generated source group. This contradicts
source transitivity, independently of the orientation of a. Thus all
lifts are coordinate pairs in D_1 x D_2.

The source deck group is included in this same product. Its factors are
abelian by the exact line classification, so every element of the
product commutes with the original deck subgroup and descends through
the specified covering. The descended map commutes with the whole
generating path, hence with G. This proves equality of groups, not only
an embedding in a proposed product.

The topology of the quotient by the deck subgroup is the actual map-
and-inverse compact-open topology by the covering comparison of C.48.
The maps from X to X/P and from X through O to O/C_O are open quotient
maps with identical fibers. Their induced bijection is a homeomorphism.
The six coordinate quotient maps displayed in the proof give exactly
the six spaces in the table. No choice or omission of the original deck
embedding is involved. The source deck group being 1 or Z follows from
C.44 and is not used to replace its actual position in the product.

4. Half-plane case.

Transitivity on x<y makes the stabilizer T_x transitive on both rays
about x, so its fixed set is precisely {x}. Commutation of a coordinate
pair or exchange with the diagonal source path puts each coordinate
function in the full line centralizer, hence makes it increasing.
An increasing exchange fails to preserve the half-plane by letting
the second input tend to infinity with the first input fixed.

For a coordinate pair, bijection of the complete vertical ray over x
onto the ray over a(x) forces b(x)=a(x). A diagonal centralizer value
c(x) is fixed by T_x and thus equals x. Consequently the full lift
group and its deck subgroup are trivial. The explicit logarithmic
coordinate is a homeomorphism to the plane.

5. Band case and deck labels.

In x<y<x+1, diagonal source transitivity makes T_x transitive on
(x,x+1). The commuting unit translation transports this statement to
every adjacent interval and fixes all x+n. Hence Fix(T_x)=x+Z exactly.
A line centralizer element is therefore a specified integer translation,
as agreement with that translation at one point extends over the entire
transitive orbit. This also proves primitivity of the unit period.

The nonexchanged coordinate pairs preserving the band are exactly
(x+m,y+m); exchanged pairs are exactly (y+m,x+m+1). These are all powers
of e(x,y)=(y,x+1). Every source deck transformation preserves the
orientation inherited from the orientable surface O, whereas e reverses
it. Thus the actual deck subgroup is 1 or <e^{2m}>, m>=1. Each power
of e commutes with that subgroup and the diagonal path, so every
candidate descends and equality of the full lifted group follows.

The coordinates u=x+y, v=y-x-1/2 and their displayed inverse are exact.
They turn e into (u+1,-v); the quotient is the open Mobius band.
For deck group <e^{2m}>, the intermediate quotient makes u periodic
with period 2m and is an open annulus, with local centralizer C_{2m}.
For trivial deck group the source is a plane and the centralizer is Z.
Both final orbit quotients are the same specified glide-reflection
quotient. These statements preserve all covering labels.
