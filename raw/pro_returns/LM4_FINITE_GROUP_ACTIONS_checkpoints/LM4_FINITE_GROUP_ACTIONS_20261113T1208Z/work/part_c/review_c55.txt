1. Actual local centralizer and topology

The closure of G|_O in Homeo(O), with maps and inverses controlled on
compact sets, is a closed Polish transitive group. Centralizing that
closure is equivalent to centralizing the original restriction image.
C.30 consequently applies directly to the full local centralizer C_O:
its evaluation image is closed, evaluation is a homeomorphism onto that
image, and its action is free and proper. It is locally compact with the
specified subspace topology. C.33 applies the faithful product action
on O times R and proves that it is a Lie group of dimension at most two.

The actual restriction H_O -> C_O and its pointwise restriction kernel
are retained. Its closure is exactly the previously defined Q_O; this
is a closed subgroup of C_O. The proof makes no claim that every local
centralizer element has a plane extension.

The global closure L preserves O, by the forward and inverse boundary
argument. Restriction L -> Homeo(O) is continuous, including inverse
convergence. Approximating a fixed l in L by global words from G proves
q(lu)=lq(u) for every q in C_O. This is an equality between the indicated
maps on O and is sufficient for every later use of a local q.

2. Preservation of the full global quotient family

3. Dimension-two local centralizers

The identity component of a two-dimensional C_O has free properly
embedded two-dimensional orbits in the connected surface. These orbits
are open, so there is one orbit. Freeness then excludes additional
components of C_O. Evaluation identifies C_O with O.

Its full centralizer N_O is the opposite group acting by right
translations, with the exact evaluation homeomorphism. Every element
of N_O agrees at one point with a word from G, by transitivity of G,
and evaluation is injective on N_O. Hence G|_O=N_O. The restriction
L -> N_O is a continuous surjective homomorphism of Polish groups and
is open.

Its kernel fixes closure(O) by continuity. For each actual global
quotient Phi_z, equivariance also makes that kernel fix the entire
image of Phi_z pointwise. The continuous actions therefore descend
through the open quotient to actions of N_O with the specified
topologies. This establishes the action comparison on all global
targets, including those away from the frontier.

The universal covering group of N_O is R^2 or Aff^+(R), with global
exponential and logarithm. The actual based path has a closed lift:
C.32 gives the null evaluation loop in O and evaluation N_O -> O is
a homeomorphism. C.36's fraction therefore applies with exactly that
cover. Its action on each line or circle quotient is the exponential
branch of C.52, with the based real circle angle retained.

6. Direct local-equivariant limits

meeting every local-centralizer orbit, using the open quotient map

For an accumulating sequence in the H-saturation, finitely many

The H-saturation is disjoint from X-double-dagger by H-invariance of
