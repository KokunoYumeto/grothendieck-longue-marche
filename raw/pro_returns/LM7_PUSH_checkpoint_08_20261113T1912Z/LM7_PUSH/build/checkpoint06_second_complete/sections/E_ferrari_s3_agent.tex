\begin{statement}{E.27}{Proved}{The generating-pair \texorpdfstring{$S_3$}{S3} Ferrari quotient and its exact source factor}
Let $\mathcal P$ be the set of all eighteen ordered generating pairs of
$S_3$, and let
\[
 K=\im\bigl(\langle\xi_1,\xi_2\rangle\longrightarrow S_3^{\mathcal P}\bigr),
 \qquad \xi_1\longmapsto(g)_{(g,h)},\quad
 \xi_2\longmapsto(h)_{(g,h)}.
 \tag{E.27.1}
\]
The literal Ferrari Nielsen automorphisms in E.17 permute $\mathcal P$.
Their image $A\leq\operatorname{Aut}(K)$ has order $162$, and the resulting
four-strand quotient $T=K\rtimes A$ has order $17\,496$.
The free image has order $108$ and
\[
 [K,K]=\gamma_3(K)\simeq(\mathbb Z/3)^3.
 \tag{E.27.2}
\]
In particular $K$ is nonnilpotent.

For the six pure evaluation pairs of E.17, both entries have order
three and the two entries commute. Consequently their evaluations
kill $F'$. Their common induced six-coset source map is exactly a
faithful representation of
\[
 B/(F'F^3),\qquad |B/(F'F^3)|=54,
 \tag{E.27.3}
\]
where $F\triangleleft B=C_2*C_3$ is the free subgroup of index six
used throughout Part E. Since $K_6=N'N^6\subset F'F^3$, its reduction
from $G_6$ is the specified quotient homomorphism
\[
 B\longrightarrow G_6\longrightarrow B/(F'F^3).
 \tag{E.27.4}
\]
Every $f\in\widehat F'$ has six identity values in $T$. For every
profinite unit multiplier, the resulting four-strand braid map is
surjective and preserves the two full twists with that multiplier.
In particular all twenty-four retained $G_6$ points of E.17, and
every lift of those points used in E.24, pass this finite test.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
Write permutations as functions and use their ordinary composition
for multiplication in $S_3$. Direct breadth-first enumeration gives
the eighteen generating pairs and the order-$108$ subgroup in
(E.27.1). The two substitutions on the pair set are
\[
 p_a(g,h)=(g,hg),\qquad p_b(g,h)=(gh^{-1},h).
 \tag{E.27.5}
\]
For an evaluation vector $k$, the automorphism corresponding to $a$
has value $k_{p_a(g,h)}$ at $(g,h)$, and similarly for $b$.
Thus composition of two such automorphisms is represented by
$p_{\alpha\beta}=p_\beta\circ p_\alpha$. The program enumerates
all $162$ permutations in the generated image, checks their
multiplication and action on every element of $K$, and checks
faithfulness by their distinct pairs of images of $\xi_1,\xi_2$.
It then uses the actual semidirect product multiplication, with
\[
 a=(1,p_a),\qquad b=(1,p_b),\qquad c=(\xi_1,p_a).
\]
The three braid relations and the literal Ferrari words for both
free generators are verified. Enumerating the subgroup generated
by $a,b,c$ yields every element of $K\rtimes A$, proving its stated
order and surjectivity from $B_4$.

The derived subgroup of $K$ is computed from all pairwise
commutators in the completely enumerated group. It has $27$
elements, is abelian of exponent three, and taking all its
commutators with $K$ produces the same subgroup. This proves
(E.27.2). In particular the quotient (E.27.1) cannot factor through
any nilpotent quotient of the Ferrari free group: the image of a
nilpotent group is nilpotent, whereas its displayed nonzero lower
central subgroup persists at every further step.

The six pairs are formed from the actual pure Artin words, with no
linearization. For each pair $(X_i,Y_i)$, the program checks
$X_i^3=Y_i^3=[X_i,Y_i]=1$. Therefore the homomorphism
$\theta_i:F\to T$ factors through $F/(F'F^3)$.
Induce their product from $F$ to $B$ using the same six coset
representatives $b_q$ as E.19:
\[
 b\longmapsto
 \left(\overline b,
 \bigl(\theta_i(b_qb\,b_{q\overline b}^{-1})\bigr)_{q,i}\right).
 \tag{E.27.6}
\]
Here the base factors are multiplied with the explicit Schreier
permutation action, so (E.27.6) is an actual homomorphism.
The subgroup $F'F^3$ is characteristic in $F$, hence normal in $B$,
and is killed in every induced coordinate. Thus (E.27.6) factors
through the order-$54$ group $B/(F'F^3)$. A separate enumeration of
its two generating images has exactly $54$ elements, proving
faithfulness of this factor. As an independent check of the required
$G_6$ comparison, all $4656$ commutators of the $97$ prescribed
$N$ generators and all $97$ sixth powers are evaluated to the
identity in all thirty-six coordinates. Their complete normal
generating property proves (E.27.4). The image of $N$ in this
factor has order three.

Since all six maps kill $F'$, their extension to $\widehat F'$
also has identity value. The pentagon therefore holds identically.
The braid generators of $T$ have order six, so a unit multiplier
has only the residues $1,5$ in this target. Direct complete
enumeration verifies that $(a^\lambda,b^\lambda,c^\lambda)$
generates $T$ for both residues; the three braid relations and
the images of $(ab)^3$ and $(abc)^4$ are checked exactly.
Both full twists have order three and their images are their
$\lambda$th powers.

Finally, abelianizing $K$ modulo two and taking the induced linear
action gives the affine action on $\mathbb F_2^2$. In the ordered
list $(0,1),(1,1),(1,0),(0,0)$, the images of $a,b,c$ are respectively
$(12),(23),(34)$. This is the standard permutation map of $B_4$.
Hence $\ker(B_4\to T)$ is already contained in $PB_4$,
$|B_4/\ker(B_4\to T)|=17\,496$, and the image of $PB_4$ has order
$729$.

The certificates are \texttt{de\_ferrari\_s3\_model.py} and
\texttt{de\_ferrari\_s3\_factor.py}, with their actual outputs,
full multiplication/action tables, and induced source matrices.
They also evaluate each retained literal commutator word directly.
The assertion concerns this actual target and its source factor;
joint four-strand generation with the other finite targets has
not been inferred from separate component generation.
\end{proof}
