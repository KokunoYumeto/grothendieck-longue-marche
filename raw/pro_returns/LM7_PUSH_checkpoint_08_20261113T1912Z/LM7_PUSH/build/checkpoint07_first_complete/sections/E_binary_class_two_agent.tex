\begin{statement}{E.36}{Proved}{The complete exponent-four refinement and its joint exponent-twelve fibres}
With $H\triangleleft F\triangleleft B$ and $\mathscr D$
as in E.33, put
\[
 P_4=B/\bigl(\gamma_3(H)H^4\bigr),\qquad
 P_{12}=B/\bigl(\gamma_3(H)H^{12}\bigr).
 \tag{E.36.1}
\]
Every one of the $432$ multiplier-one points of E.24
has exactly $2^{10}$ complete correction images giving
generating lifts to
$\operatorname{im}(B\to\mathscr D\times P_4)$.
All four abelian boundary solutions modulo four lift;
each has an affine eight-dimensional space of central
corrections over $\mathbf F_2$.

Every pair consisting of one of these solutions and one
of the $3^{11}$ solutions in E.33 is realized by one
commutator word.  The complete joint correction fibre
in $\operatorname{im}(B\to\mathscr D\times P_{12})$
therefore has
\[
                 2^{10}3^{11}=181398528
 \tag{E.36.2}
\]
images over each retained source point.
Every such solution extends compatibly and simultaneously
through
\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(
 B\longrightarrow
 \mathscr D\times P_{12}\times B/[H,H]H^m\bigr),
                       \qquad m\ge1.
 \tag{E.36.3}
\]
All these finite shadows are generating and retain
the complete binary and ternary Ferrari coordinates.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We keep the exact ordered Schreier basis
\textup{(E.33.5)}.  At exponent four the correct
Hall coordinates are
\[
 H/\bigl(\gamma_3(H)H^4\bigr)
       =(\mathbb Z/4)^{10}\times(\mathbb Z/2)^{45},
 \qquad
 \prod_i\eta_i^{v_i}\prod_{i<j}[\eta_i,\eta_j]^{z_{ij}}.
 \tag{E.36.4}
\]
The multiplication and powers are
\[
 \begin{split}
 (v,z)(w,t)&=(v+w,\ z+t-(v_jw_i)_{i<j}),\\
 (v,z)^n&=\bigl(nv,\ nz-\tbinom n2(v_iv_j)_{i<j}\bigr),
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.36.5}
\]
with the first coordinate reduced modulo four and the
second modulo two.  In any class-two exponent-four
group, the identities for $(ab)^4$ and $[a,b]^4$
give $[a,b]^2=1$, since $\gcd(6,4)=2$.
Conversely \textup{(E.36.5)} has exponent four,
and its independent collected coordinates give
the inverse to the generator map from the free
class-two exponent-four group.  This proves the
isomorphism \textup{(E.36.4)}.
Its order is $2^{65}$, and its central Frattini
subgroup consists of the $55$ binary coordinates
$(v/2,z)$ with $v$ even.
In particular the square of $(v,z)$ contains the
term $(v_iv_j)_{i<j}$; it is retained throughout.
At modulus two there is no corresponding new
commutator layer: $(ab)^2=a^2=b^2=1$ gives $ab=ba$,
so $\gamma_3(H)H^2=H^2$.

Use the actual substitutions \textup{(E.33.8)}
and the same nine-coset carries
\textup{(E.33.10)}, now collected by
\textup{(E.36.5)}.  If the ten images under an
action have abelian columns $A_i$ and central
columns $J_i$, the image of $(v,z)$ is the
ordered product of their $v_i$th powers and
their pairwise commutators to the powers $z_{ij}$.
Equivalently its central part is
\[
 \sum_i v_iJ_i
 -\sum_i\tbinom{v_i}{2}
                  (A_{\ell i}A_{ki})_{k<\ell}
 -\sum_{i<j}v_iv_j
                  (A_{\ell i}A_{kj})_{k<\ell}
 +(\Lambda^2A)z .
 \tag{E.36.6}
\]
This formula, reduced modulo two, preserves all
the square terms and gives the exact action on
every Hall coordinate.  The literal calculations
verify $s^2=r^3=1$ and verify that the negative-square
words for $x,y$ induce their actual inner actions.

Here is the complete source-kernel computation.
Let
$R_2=\ker(B\to D_{2,6})\cap F'$.
The binary image of $K_6\cap F'$ is the actual
seven-dimensional elementary-abelian group of
E.24.  Its complete normal presentation has
\[
 4656+95+7+21+7\cdot97=5458
 \tag{E.36.7}
\]
relators: the original commutators and primitive
integral-$F^{\rm ab}$ kernel sixth-power products,
the seven basis squares, the twenty-one basis
commutators and all $N_B$ conjugation relations.
For each original generator or conjugate, subtract
its actual seven-basis representative by group
multiplication.  The proof that these normally
generate the kernel is the same exact presentation
argument as in E.31: after imposing them the
quotient is generated by seven commuting elements
of order two and still maps onto the actual
group of order $2^7$.

Evaluate all $5458$ relators by
\textup{(E.36.5)--(E.36.6)}.  Their complete
normal image is computed using the parity quotient
$(\mathbb Z/2)^{10}$ and the central Frattini group.
For a dependent parity vector multiply by the
inverse of the ordered product of its chosen
parity representatives; the resulting even
abelian vector and central coordinate belong to
the central Frattini group.  Retain each parity
representative's square and every pairwise
commutator, then close under the actual $s,r$
actions.  These operations give precisely the
generated subgroup, since they include the
central collection corrections between all
parity representatives.

The resulting image has parity dimension six,
central Frattini intersection dimension $45$,
and high-bit abelian dimension six.  Its order
is therefore $2^{51}$.  Its abelian image is
exactly $\mathcal D/4\mathcal D\simeq(\mathbb Z/4)^6$,
where $\mathcal D$ is the primitive lattice of
E.28; all six columns are explicitly lifted in
the certificate.  The intersection with the
commutator centre has dimension $45-6=39$.
If $U=\mathcal D/2\mathcal D\subset\mathbf F_2^{10}$,
that intersection is exactly
\[
             C_2=U\wedge\mathbf F_2^{10},
                    \qquad\dim C_2=39 .
 \tag{E.36.8}
\]
This follows both by the complete calculation
and by the explicit preimages below.

The joint source kernel has this same image.
Every $u\in R_2$ belongs to $K_6$, whose full
ternary source image has exponent three.
Thus $u^9$ kills that ternary image, retains
the binary source kernel, and has the same
image as $u$ in the exponent-four group
\textup{(E.36.4)}.  This proves the exact
comparison with
$\ker(\widehat B\to\mathscr D)\cap\widehat F'$.
The discrete-to-profinite finite-image comparison
is the open-kernel density argument of E.33.

The abelian norm equation on $\mathcal D/4$
has exactly four solutions for each retained
word, by the Smith factors
$(1,1,1,1,3,0)$ in E.28.  For a chosen abelian
correction, first multiply the selected parity
representatives, then solve for the required
even abelian difference in the central Frattini
group.  This supplies an actual element of the
complete source-kernel image with that abelian
coordinate.  All other such elements differ by
$C_2$.  The two central norm equations on
$C_2$ form a binary linear map of rank $31$.
The literal $432$-word calculation checks all
four abelian choices for each word: every
right-hand side lies in that map's image.
Its kernel has dimension $39-31=8$.
This proves the exact count $4\cdot2^8=2^{10}$.
The induced action on the ten-dimensional
Frattini quotient of the geometric two-group
has rank ten in all $1728$ abelian cases.
The central choices do not change that action.

There are actual zero-abelianization preimages
for the entire space \textup{(E.36.8)}.
The six parity representatives can be taken
to be the normal relators with zero-based
indices
\[
                    96,\ 97,\ 98,\ 100,\ 103,\ 104
 \tag{E.36.9}
\]

Write them as $u_1,\ldots,u_6$.
Their parity vectors form a basis of $U$.
The sixty literal words

The independent preimage checker constructs

Their ninth powers preserve these central

Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(
 R_2\cap[H,H]\longrightarrow
 [H,H]/(\gamma_3(H)H^4)\bigr)=C_2 .
 \tag{E.36.10}
\]
This supplies the integral kernel comparison
required to prescribe a central value while
retaining every abelian modulus.

We spell out the exponent-twelve comparison,
including its coordinate change.  The Hall

$P_{12}$ are

collected-coordinate construction proves
independence.  The exact maps to the two

\[
 \begin{split}
 (v,z)&\longmapsto(v\bmod4,\ z\bmod2),\\
 (v,z)&\longmapsto
       \bigl(v\bmod3,\ z+2(v_iv_j)_{i<j}\bmod3\bigr).
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.36.11}
\]
The second line is the actual change from
ordered Hall coordinates to the BCH coordinates
of E.33.  Its inverse, together with the first

\[
 \begin{split}
 v&=9v_4+4v_3\pmod{12},\\
 z&=3z_4+4\bigl(z_3-2(v_iv_j)_{i<j}\bigr)\pmod6 .
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.36.12}
\]
Both lines respect multiplication.
The literal $B$ actions respect these reductions,
so they give the actual isomorphism
\[
                   P_{12}\simeq P_4\times_{B/H}P_3 .
 \tag{E.36.13}
\]

The common source-kernel image also splits as

The two norm equations are tested coordinatewise

For simultaneous lifting through

Finally we prove joint generation without
assuming a fixed-kernel automorphism on
$\mathscr D$.  Each of the boundary maps
descends to its displayed quotient, since
it preserves $H$ and kills the appropriate
characteristic class-two or abelian subgroup.
The rank-ten Frattini test makes the
$P_4$ map an automorphism; E.33 gives the
same conclusion for $P_3$, and E.31 for
the retained abelian factors.
Their actual common boundary quotient
therefore also has an automorphism:
the intersection of these kernels is
preserved, and the induced map has trivial
kernel in every factor.

First retain only moduli $2^a3^b$.
Let $T$ denote this finite common boundary
quotient, and let $T_N$ be its $N_B$ image.
Choose $r\ge5$ after the lower-central series
of $T_N$ has stabilized, and write
$L=\gamma_r(T_N)$.
The retained $G_6$ map places the corrected
$N_B$ image inside $T_N$; its automorphism
of the finite group $T$ then makes this
inclusion an equality.  The corrected map
is therefore onto $T_N$, whereas
$\gamma_r$ of the $N_B$ image in
$\mathscr D$ is one.  Hence the corrected
joint image contains exactly the pure
factor $\{1\}\times L$ at this lower-central
stage.  The quotient of the full joint
$N_B$ image by that factor is finite
nilpotent with prime divisors two and three.
It is generated by the $97$ source
generators and still maps onto
$(\mathbb Z/6)^{97}$ through $G_6$.
Both Sylow Frattini dimensions are therefore
$97$, and the retained $G_6$ automorphism
forces joint surjectivity.
The quotient by $N_B$ then gives the
whole common group.
For additional moduli prime to six,
the old geometric $H$ image has order
divisible only by two and three, whereas
the new abelian $H$ image has order prime
to six.  The actual CRT map and the

generation at every level of
\textup{(E.36.3)}.
