\begin{statement}{E.24}{Proved}{A complete joint binary and ternary Ferrari refinement}
Use (E.21.1)--(E.21.2) in the actual algebra
\[
 \mathcal A_{2,d}=\F_2\langle X,Y\rangle/
                 (\text{monomials of degree}>d),
 \qquad 2\leq d\leq6,
 \tag{E.24.1}
\]
and denote the resulting four-strand target by
$T_{2,d}$. All square terms are retained.
Let $D_{2,6}$ be the three-strand quotient
formed by the six evaluations, their six
$B/F_2$ conjugates, and intersection with
$K_6$, exactly as in (E.19.1). Then
\[
 \ker(D_{2,6}\to G_6)\simeq\F_2^9,
 \qquad
 \im(K_6\cap F_2'\to D_{2,6})\simeq\F_2^7.
 \tag{E.24.2}
\]
The actual common source quotient with
E.23 is the fibre product
\[
 D_{2,6}\mathbin{\times_{G_6}}D_6,
 \tag{E.24.3}
\]
whose kernel over $G_6$ is
$\F_2^9\times\F_3^{19}$.

For every one of the 24 retained
$G_6$ points, all compatible multiplier
residues in (E.24.3) are $1,73$ modulo
144. For each residue the complete
binary correction system has an
affine line of two solutions.
Every one combines with every
point of E.23's nine-point ternary
plane by an actual $F_2'$ word.
Thus each retained CM fibre has
36 simultaneous points, for a
total of 864. All give surjective
three-strand shadows, satisfy both
complete target pentagons, and
give surjective four-strand braid
maps preserving the full twists
with the specified multiplier.
In particular every extra-coset
CM fibre survives this refinement.

The exact binary target order is
\[
 |T_{2,6}|=6\cdot2^{40}
          =6\,597\,069\,766\,656.
 \tag{E.24.4}
\]
The actual joint four-strand image
is $T_{2,6}\times T_6$. Its
kernel is already contained in
$PB_4$, and its order is
\[
 880\,164\,249\,471\,192\,366\,606\,672\,986\,112.
 \tag{E.24.5}
\]
These counts and comparisons concern
the entire finite correction fibres
over the specified $G_6$ points.
They do not enumerate other $G_6$
points or other initial multiplier
residues in a CM matrix fibre,
and do not assert full profinite
lifting.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
The free-group substitutions of
(E.21.1) and all six evaluation
pairs are used literally in
(E.24.1). In particular
$(1+X)^2=1+X^2$ is retained.
No logarithmic coordinates are
used in the binary group.
The programs verify the braid
relations, the two literal
Ferrari generators, their four
action formulas, and actual
word and inverse truncations.
At degree two the free-unit
kernel has order 32 and the
automorphism image has order
48, giving target order 1536.
In degrees two, three and four,
all 4656 $N$-commutators and
all 97 sixth powers vanish
in the full induced core.
Thus the six evaluations at
those levels factor through
$G_6$. All 24 retained points
pass the pentagon and every
braid relation there.

The kernel of reduction from
degree six to degree four
has only unit terms and
generator-image changes in
degrees five and six. Its
group products add: a product
of two unit terms has degree
at least ten, and substituting
one automorphism change into
another has degree at least
nine. It is therefore an
elementary abelian two-group.
This is an exact assertion
about the displayed algebra
and substitution maps.

The complete source image
is obtained from all
$[n_i,n_j]$, all $n_i^6$,
and their normal closure
under $N$. For its
$F_2'$ intersection, use
the entire integral rank-95
abelianization kernel of
E.19 for the sixth-power
products. At degree five
the commutator, full and
$F_2'$ ranks are $3,6,4$;
at degree six they are
$6,9,7$. Literal basis
words, all additive

$s,r$ stability are
checked. This proves
(E.24.2) without omitting
power corrections.

Fix the selected ternary
word $S_0$ of E.23. For
each multiplier residue
put
\[
 R_0=S_0^{-1}t^\lambda,
 \qquad S_0R_0=t^\lambda.
 \tag{E.24.6}
\]
The seven-dimensional

The complete norm matrix
on $M_2$ has rank five,
the pentagon matrix has
rank five, and their

rank six. Every augmented

The braid matrix has
rank zero. All 128 values
of the correction space
are substituted to check

and every solution is
checked in both complete

The normalized word is

The binary and ternary

We give the common-word

Every binary correction

 S=S_0u^{-2}v^3,
 \qquad R=S^{-1}t^\lambda,
 \qquad f=sx^gSx^{-g},
 \tag{E.24.7}
\]

All the correction

The two projection

(E.24.7) retains every

The source product

\end{proof}
