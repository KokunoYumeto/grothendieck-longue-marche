\subsection{The full integral affine return tower}

\begin{statement}{D.48}{Proved}{An entire return-prime lattice tower with its literal inverse-limit fibre}
Retain the distinct primes \(\ell,p\geq5\), the integer
\(a=\operatorname{ord}_p(\ell)\), and the actual sources
\(\Gamma,D\) of D.46. Let \(\mathcal O\) be the
unramified extension of \(\mathbf Z_\ell\) of degree \(a\),
with the lift \(\widetilde\zeta\) of the specified
order-\(p\) element of its residue field. For
\(1\leq n<\infty\), put
\(\mathcal O_n=\mathcal O/\ell^n\mathcal O\), and
write \(\mathcal O_\infty=\mathcal O\).
Use the same cyclic shifts and diagonal actions to form
\[
 V_n=\mathcal O_n^\ell,\quad
 H_n=V_n\rtimes P,\quad
 q_{H_n}(x)=(e_0,c),\quad q_{H_n}(y)=(0,n_0).
 \tag{D.48.1}
\]
Let \(E_n\) be the actual closed six-coset image of
\(\widehat B\), using precisely D.46.5, and put
\[
 W_n=V_n^6,\qquad J_n=\Gamma\mathbin{\times_D}E_n.
 \tag{D.48.2}
\]
Then all these maps are epimorphisms, and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \ker(E_n\to D)=\ker(J_n\to\Gamma)=W_n,\
 E_n=E_\infty/\ell^nW_\infty,\qquad
 J_n=J_\infty/\ell^nW_\infty,\qquad
 J_\infty\simeq\varprojlim_nJ_n.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{D.48.3}
\]
The equalities retain the actual maps from \(\widehat B\).
In particular, for \(K=O^p(O^\ell(F))\), there is an
actual epimorphism
\[
 \widehat B/O^\ell(K)\twoheadrightarrow J_\infty
       \twoheadrightarrow\Gamma,
 \qquad\ker(J_\infty\to\Gamma)
          =\mathcal O^{6\ell}.
 \tag{D.48.4}
\]

Fix any one entire normalized old D.44 automorphism
\(\alpha\) of \(\Gamma\), including its literal
conjugator and prescribed old peripheral conjugators.
It has a normalized peripheral automorphism on this
whole \(J_\infty\) retaining all that data.
The maximal pro-\(\ell\) pure quotient is still the
original \(P_\ell\), with the specified original pair.
Thus the R8 weight-nine realization and the chosen
whole relative pro-\(p\) lift are retained through
this complete affine return tower.

Let \(\mathscr X_n\) be the full literal fibre of
normalized peripheral source maps to \(J_n\) above
this same old \(\alpha\), with \(\tau\) fixed.
Every map in the fibre is generating and factors
through an automorphism of this same \(J_n\).
After choosing one origin \(\sigma_0\) in the
infinite fibre, the actual maps give compatible
torsor isomorphisms
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_\infty^\tau\simeq\mathcal O^\ell
       &\longrightarrow\mathscr X_\infty,
       &w&\longmapsto\operatorname{Inn}(w)\sigma_0,\\
 W_n^\tau\simeq\mathcal O_n^\ell
       &\longrightarrow\mathscr X_n,
       &\bar w&\longmapsto
          \operatorname{Inn}(\bar w)(\sigma_0\bmod\ell^n),\\
 \mathscr X_\infty&\simeq\varprojlim_n\mathscr X_n,
       &&|\mathscr X_n|=\ell^{n\ell a}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{D.48.5}
\]
Every finite normalized map lifts to the entire
compatible tower. Before fixing \(\tau\), the full
peripheral fibre is a torsor under \(W_n\), with
\(\ell^{6n\ell a}\) literal maps at finite precision.
These are the full ordinary centralizer unions and
also the fibres with prescribed old conjugators;
no conjugator freedom is discarded.
The exact word-to-image fibres are specified in
\textup{(D.48.12)} below.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We give integral source maps before using their finite reductions.
The derivative of \(T^p-1\) at the chosen residue
root is a unit, so its root has a unique lift in the
unramified degree-\(a\) extension. This gives
\(\widetilde\zeta^p=1\) literally, and
\(\widetilde\zeta^b-1\) is a unit whenever
\(b\not\equiv0\pmod p\).
The coefficient ring is
\(\mathcal O=\mathbf Z_\ell[\widetilde\zeta]\).
Thus every local multiplication in D.46 is defined
over this actual ring and reduces compatibly to
\(\mathcal O_n\).

The closed subgroup generated by the two elements
in \textup{(D.48.1)} maps onto \(P\).
The \(\ell\)-th power of the first is the
constant vector, whose conjugates span
\(\mathcal O^\ell\): apply the unit
\(\widetilde\zeta-1\) in one coordinate and
then use powers of \(\widetilde\zeta\) and
the cyclic shift. Integer powers and their
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-limits supply these coefficients
inside the closed source image. This proves
\(q_{H_\infty}\) is onto and supplies all its
finite quotient maps.

\paragraph{The actual whole kernel and all reductions.}
The source \(E_\infty\) is a closed subgroup of
\(H_\infty^6\rtimes\mathfrak S_3\), and its
kernel over \(D\) lies in \(\mathcal O^{6\ell}\).
Its reduction modulo \(\ell\) is the entire
kernel of D.46.3. One can see this with actual words:
the conjugates of the three cusp powers of exponent
\(\ell p\) retained in D.46 have a basis of the
modulo-\(\ell\) kernel among their vector images.
Lift those same words to \(E_\infty\).
The determinant of their vector matrix is a unit
in \(\mathbf Z_\ell\), so their
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-linear span is the whole
\(\mathcal O^{6\ell}\).
They commute inside this kernel, and continuous
powers realize all those coefficients in the
actual closed subgroup. Hence its kernel is
\(W_\infty=\mathcal O^{6\ell}\).
The same conclusion follows from the closed-module
form of Nakayama's lemma, applied to this specified
submodule of a finite free \(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-module.

Reduction of all six components now has kernel
exactly \(\ell^n W_\infty\), which proves
the asserted \(E_n\) maps in
\textup{(D.48.3)}. Every finite image and
every transition is therefore the image of the
same two source generators.
The commutator map satisfies
\[
 [E_{\infty,F},W_\infty]=W_\infty,
 \qquad W_n^D=0\quad(1\leq n\leq\infty).
 \tag{D.48.6}
\]
For the first equality its reduction modulo
\(\ell\) is onto by D.46.8. The sum of the
images of the two integral operators for
\(x-1,y-1\) is a closed submodule and has
a unit full-rank minor, hence is all of
\(W_\infty\). For the second, a fixed vector
at finite precision reduces to zero modulo
\(\ell\) by D.46.8; divide by \(\ell\)
and induct on the precision. The infinite
assertion follows from all these reductions.

\paragraph{Integral centralizer equations and the entire union.}
The local equations used in D.46 remain exact over
every \(\mathcal O_n\). For the cyclic shift
\(C\) on \(\mathcal O_n^\ell\),
\[
 \ker(1-C)=\mathcal O_n\sum_i e_i,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{im}(1-C)
      =\{(v_i):\textstyle\sum_i v_i=0\}.
 \tag{D.48.7}
\]
Both equalities follow by successively solving
the coordinate differences, and use no division
by \(\ell\). The \(Y\) fixed space consists
of the vectors with first coordinate zero,
because \(\widetilde\zeta-1\) is a unit.
The operator \(1-Z\) is invertible: since
\(Z=(XY)^{-1}\), its linear action satisfies
\(Z^\ell=\widetilde\zeta^{-1}I\), and its inverse is
\((1+Z+\cdots+Z^{\ell-1})/(1-\widetilde\zeta^{-1})\).
The displayed denominator is a unit.

Summing the \(X\) centralizer equation still
forces the constant \(N\)-coordinate to vanish,
so the first local image in D.46.10 is again
exactly \(\langle c\rangle\).
The \(Y\) and \(Z\) centralizer projections
are still onto. The unchanged actual
\(F^{\rm ab}/p\) quotient and the two-cycle
coordinate equations of D.46 then give
\[
 C_{E_n}(s)\twoheadrightarrow C_D(s),\quad
 C_{E_n}(r)\twoheadrightarrow C_D(r),\quad
 C_{E_n}(\tau)\twoheadrightarrow C_D(\tau)
       \quad(1\leq n\leq\infty).
 \tag{D.48.8}
\]
All corrections are in the actual full kernel
\(W_n\).

The invariant modules at \(s,r,\tau\) are free
over \(\mathcal O_n\) of ranks
\(3\ell,2\ell,\ell\), respectively.
For \(\tau\), choose one coordinate in each
of its three two-cycles. The three choices
are a constant vector for \(X\), a vector
with first coordinate zero for \(Y\), and
zero for \(Z\). The partner coordinates
are prescribed by the actual \(\tau\)
action. This is an explicit compatible
isomorphism
\(W_n^\tau\simeq\mathcal O_n\oplus
\mathcal O_n^{\ell-1}\).
In particular every invariant vector at
finite precision lifts invariantly to
\(W_\infty\).

\paragraph{Existence, full fibres, and generation over the ring.}
Choose lifts in \(E_\infty\) of the prescribed
three conjugators in \(D\). Their product
defect lies in \(W_\infty\).
The exact correction map D.23.8 is now a
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-linear map
\(W_\infty^3\to W_\infty\).
Its reduction modulo \(\ell\) is onto by
D.46.12; a unit minor therefore gives an
actual integral right inverse.
Cancelling the defect produces a continuous
peripheral source map \(\widehat B\to E_\infty\).
It factors through \(\widehat B\) from the
three-boundary source because its first two
images are conjugate to the actual elements
of orders two and three.

Every such source map is onto, at every
precision. Its image projects onto \(D\)
and contains the \(2\ell p\)-th power of
the prescribed \(\tau\) conjugate.
This is a vector whose reduction in
\(W_1\) is nonzero. Its orbit spans
the irreducible \(D\)-module \(W_1\).
The image's intersection with \(W_n\)
is a closed \(D\)-invariant
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-submodule; it therefore
surjects onto \(W_1\), and Nakayama gives
that it is the whole \(W_n\).
This proves generation, including at
\(n=\infty\).

At finite precision the correction solutions
in conjugators number \(|W_n|^2\).
Using the three free invariant modules just
computed gives exactly
\(\ell^{6n\ell a}=|W_n|\) distinct maps.
The surjections \textup{(D.48.8)} identify
this with the full ordinary conjugator union.
By \textup{(D.48.6)} the kernel-conjugation
action is free, so the maps form exactly
one \(W_n\)-orbit.
The infinite fibre is also one free
\(W_\infty\)-orbit: after choosing one
solution, its comparison with any other
has a unique conjugator at each finite
precision. Uniqueness makes these conjugators
compatible, and their inverse limit gives
the required element of \(W_\infty\).
Fixing \(\tau\) is precisely restriction
to the invariant subgroup \(W_n^\tau\).

\paragraph{Actual automorphisms and the whole retained old quotient.}
The pure rigidity proof of D.46 works over
\(\mathcal O_n\) without a new equation.
The first translation has coordinate sum
\(\widetilde\zeta^b\); equation
\textup{(D.48.7)} arranges it in the first
coordinate, a constant \(N\)-element
removes its scalar, and the remaining
\(V_n^c\) action removes the second
translation using the unit
\(1-\widetilde\zeta\).
Every pure peripheral source map to
\(H_n\) over the identity on \(P\)
is consequently an inner conjugate of
the specified source map.

For a normalized source map to \(E_n\),
\textup{(D.48.6)} places its normalized
conjugator in \(E_{n,F}'\). Its identity
coordinate is therefore a pure map of
the preceding kind. The six actual
conjugates in D.46.13 prove preservation
of the same closed normal core, also
when \(n=\infty\). Thus the source map
factors through an onto endomorphism of
this same \(E_n\). These groups are
finitely generated profinite quotients
of \(\widehat B\), and hence Hopfian;
the endomorphism is an automorphism.
Kernel conjugation handles the full
unnormalized fibre as well.

The image of the old source kernel \(K\)
in \(E_\infty\) is the entire
\(W_\infty\). It is contained there
because \(D\) is an old quotient.
Its reduction is all of \(W_1\) by
D.46.14. It is a closed
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\)-submodule, so the
same unit-basis argument gives
\[
 q_{E_\infty}(K)=W_\infty.
 \tag{D.48.9}
\]
Consequently the actual joint source
is the complete fibre product in
\textup{(D.48.2)}. Componentwise
automorphisms on \(\Gamma\) and
\(E_n\), agreeing on \(D\), give
its actual automorphisms, and every
peripheral source map above the fixed
old automorphism has this factorization.
Pairing the old conjugators with upper
conjugators having their exact
\(D\)-images retains those whole old
conjugators. Since \([J_{n,F},W_n]=W_n\),
the new normalized conjugator belongs
to \(J_{n,F}'\) and has precisely the
given old image.

The source kernel of \(J_\infty\)
contains \(O^\ell(K)\), since its
image on \(K\) is the pro-\(\ell\)
group in \textup{(D.48.9)}.
This proves \textup{(D.48.4)}.
The source kernel also lies in
\(O^\ell(F)\). Every finite
\(\ell\)-quotient of the new pure
group, composed with its source map
from \(F\), factors through the
original \(P_\ell\), which is itself
a quotient of the new pure group.
This proves the exact maximal
pro-\(\ell\) comparison and retains
the original action on it.

\paragraph{The full inverse-limit and word fibres.}
The quotient maps in
\textup{(D.48.3)} identify each
\(\ell^nW_\infty\) as the literal
source descent kernel. An automorphism
above the specified old map preserves
\(W_\infty\), and therefore preserves
each of these characteristic subgroups
of its kernel. All the displayed
transition maps on full fibres are
thus actual descent maps.
With one infinite origin selected,
their parameters are exactly the
reductions of \(W_\infty^\tau\).
Every such finite parameter lifts,
and a compatible collection has
one inverse-limit parameter.
This proves all comparisons in
\textup{(D.48.5)}, including their
topology: the continuous bijection
from the compact parameter group
to the closed source-map fibre is
a homeomorphism.

Writing \(f_0=s\sigma_0(s)\) for
the new infinite origin, the
conjugator image at parameter
\(w\in W_\infty^\tau\) is the
literal group expression
\[
       f(w)=(sws)f_0w^{-1}\in J_{\infty,F}'.
 \tag{D.48.10}
\]
Its old image is the specified
old \(f\), because \(w\) lies
in the kernel over \(\Gamma\).
The parameterization is injective:
equality of these conjugators
would give the same normalized
map on both \(s\) and \(\tau\),
hence on \(s,r\); its kernel
stabilizer is zero by
\textup{(D.48.6)}.
At finite precision the same
formula uses \(\bar w\), and
\[
     \mathscr X_{n+1}\longrightarrow\mathscr X_n
        \quad\hbox{is the surjective reduction}
        \quad\mathcal O_{n+1}^\ell\to\mathcal O_n^\ell.
 \tag{D.48.11}
\]
Finally each image in
\textup{(D.48.10)} has, after
choosing one source preimage
\(\widetilde f(w)\in F'\),
the complete profinite word fibre
\[
 \widetilde f(w)\,
    \ker\bigl(F'\longrightarrow J_{\infty,F}'\bigr).
 \tag{D.48.12}
\]
The analogous expression applies
at every finite precision.
This separates the full word
fibres from the affine lattice
of conjugator images.
\end{proof}
