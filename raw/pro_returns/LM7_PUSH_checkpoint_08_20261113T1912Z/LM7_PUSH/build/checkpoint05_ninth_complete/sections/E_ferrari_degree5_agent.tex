\begin{statement}{E.22}{Proved}{A compatible relative class-three test preserving every retained CM point}
Extend the actual Ferrari Magnus representation
(E.21.1)--(E.21.2) to $d=5$, retaining its joint
image $T_5$ with the modulus-nine target. Define
$K_{3,5}$ by all six evaluation maps, all six
$B/F_2$ conjugates, and intersection with $K_6$,
as in (E.19.1). Put $D_5=B/K_{3,5}$. The
specified reduction maps give
\[
 D_5\longrightarrow D_4\longrightarrow G_6,
 \qquad
 T_5\longrightarrow T_4\longrightarrow\widetilde H_3.
 \tag{E.22.1}
\]
The kernel of $D_5\to D_4$ is $\F_3^6$.
Its entire image is represented by words in
$K_{3,4}\cap F_2'$. The kernel of $D_5\to G_6$
has order $3^{13}$. The actual lower-central
images of $N$ in $D_5$ have dimensions
\[
 \dim\im(\gamma_2N)=9,\qquad
 \dim\im(\gamma_3N)=3,\qquad
 \im(\gamma_4N)=1,
 \tag{E.22.2}
\]
where the two nonzero images are elementary
abelian three-groups.

All three points of each of the 24 correction
lines in E.21 are included in the next test.
Every one of those 72 points has a gentle lift
to $D_5$. In each old line exactly one point
has a simultaneous pentagon lift; its top
correction is unique. Thus every one of the
24 particular E.17 $G_6$ shadows, including
all twelve extra-coset points, has a verified
compatible lift through (E.22.1). These
literal commutator words give surjective
three-strand shadows and surjective
four-strand braid maps satisfying the
complete pentagon in $T_5$.

The full target orders are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |T_5|&=6\,778\,308\,875\,544,\\
 |B_4/(\ker(B_4\to T_5)\cap PB_4)|
       &=162\,679\,413\,013\,056.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E.22.3}
\]
The three- and four-strand full twists
are preserved. The multiplier calculation
is exhaustive over lifts of the retained
$G_6$ shadows: the exact cusp order in
$D_5$ is 72, so all compatible values
$\lambda\equiv1\pmod{72}$ and
$m=(\lambda-1)/2\equiv0\pmod{36}$
give exactly the same finite equations.
This does not enumerate other multiplier
residues or other $G_6$ points within a
CM matrix fibre.

The natural quotient $B\to D_5$ factors
through none of the finite quotients in
E.20.1. Thus this is a concrete relative
class-three extension beyond that family,
with positive witnesses in all 24 CM
fibres. It is one specified finite
refinement, with no all-quotient
lifting assertion.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}

We give the complete kernel

 \tag{E.22.4}
