The first Q20 comparison incorrectly assumed that q4 factors through the old Ferrari A5 base. Its literal x,y images have order15, so their nontrivial order2 q4 values cannot factor through that base. The actual joint base is q5 x q2. Its Q20 kernel has rank3 over F2: choose the unique representative congruent to I modulo5, then write its modulo4 matrix as I+2[[a,b],[c,a]]. The first Schreier discrepancy was [[9,10],[10,9]], whose three-bit coordinate is(1,1,1). The one-bit run stopped on that assertion and produced no signed comparison certificate. All three bits are retained in the replacement calculation. The residual and projective q5 certificates do not use the failed assumption.
