The named primary texts used in P.6–P.7 were opened in their actual versions. The current Boggi mapping-class paper is arXiv2011.15075v6,23April2026. The Lochak–Nakamura–Schneps paper is arXiv2602.12462v1,12February2026. Its Section8 explicitly retains image-preservation and injectivity conditions for cutting maps. These were not silently replaced by the existence of the Theorem1.1 actions.

Both Collas2012 papers were opened in full PDF form; their theorems concern torsion in the mapping-class groups acted upon. The genus-one operator group in the stated general theorem is IΓ≅GR. The special-locus author PDF was checked at Theorems3.1 and3.4. Drinfeld's original Russian PDF was opened at Section4, including the cycle, pentagon and arithmetic construction. The two 2024 Boggi-related papers were distinguished: symmetric mapping-class congruence, and Boggi–Zalesskii finite subgroups of completions of good groups.

Ihara's second beta/gamma paper was obtained from the original institute file https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/preprint/file/RIMS1224.ps.gz (50,950compressedbytes), decompressed, rendered with Ghostscript using a writable temporary directory, and all13preprint pages read.
