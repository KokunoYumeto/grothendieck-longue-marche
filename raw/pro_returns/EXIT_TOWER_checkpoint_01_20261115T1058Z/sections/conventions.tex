\section*{Conventions and manuscript notation}
We work over $\mathbb C$ for the topological comparison, with labelled marked points and orientation-preserving mapping classes. A stable type satisfies $2g-2+n>0$. The moduli spaces below are Deligne--Mumford stacks; their topological realization retains their isotropy groups. We use paths only through explicitly specified groupoids and record the conjugations introduced by choices of base points.

The manuscript's hats for compactified or infinitesimal objects are retained in quotations. Completion of a group or category is always specified separately. The symbol $\widehat\Gamma$ denotes full profinite completion; a procongruence completion is denoted $\check\Gamma$.
