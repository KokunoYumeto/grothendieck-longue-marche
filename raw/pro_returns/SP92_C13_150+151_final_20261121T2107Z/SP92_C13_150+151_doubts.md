## Legibility and witnesses

This qualitative assessment incorporates the completed blind audits through 150_scan099 and of the selected 151 leaves, including the now-completed root adjudications of 151_scan003, 151_scan005 and 151_scan007. Useful first-pass observations are retained where the audits support them, and remaining local uncertainty is stated explicitly. No final assembled statistics are claimed here.

Ordinary running prose is usually readable, but small revisions, overlapping annotations and the bottom and side edges are substantially less secure. Dense cancellations sometimes preserve individual letter bodies; elsewhere the crossings merge several strokes and only a local alternative can be retained. An uncertainty inside a cancellation means that not every proposed letter or symbol has been recovered. The blind audit exposed substantive weaknesses in the first reading, including an operator's identity and state, the distinction between words and mathematical terms, and the orientation of a whole canceled passage.

Three completed adjudications illustrate both recovery and its limits:

- On 150_scan091, the construction after alors contains a canceled les-like word followed by a live coproduct and its live lower index. Neither witness preserves that live construction correctly. The audit separates the operator's two uprights and lower bar from the nearby cancellation; this is a substantive reading and state correction.
- On 150_scan096, two canceled groups previously treated as short words are mathematical V terms. The later group is V_i; the earlier V retains an ij/j index uncertainty. Recovering the mathematical bodies does not certify the remaining obscured index.
- On 150_scan099, the bottom canceled block is inverted relative to the main text. Reading it in the restored orientation reveals ordinary upper stars and lower L feet, two union expressions and the ending Z/2 = X_L*. The opening, several indices, the Z/2 forms and the lower relation remain doubtful. A separate reader unfamiliar with the page inspected only the existing rotated previews and corroborated the provisional shapes. That corroboration does not resolve the crossed interiors.

The least secure mathematical places still include tiny overtraced indices, the flat/natural-like superscripts on 150_scan087 and 150_scan098, and the crowded deletion in 150_scan084's margin. The completed 151 audits retain the clipped lower 2/1 index on 151_scan003, the overtraced q1/p1 underlabel and attached 3-like oval closure on 151_scan005, and crossed x/z alternatives on 151_scan007. On the last leaf, the crowded side caption remains uncertain between application and canceled appl followed by constante; its final small subscript remains 1/q. Those alternatives do not certify a recovered complete phrase or index. Clear source geometry must be recorded occurrence by occurrence: points and plain rules in continuations, uncertain limit endpoints, square-ended annotation brackets, and the placement of h above the parentheses surrounding a wave are distinct features. A standard ellipsis, colimit or homotopy symbol can conceal those differences.


The completed 151_scan003/005/007 audits make the graphical distinctions more precise. Many annotation lines have a straight middle and turned ends, so they are square-ended brackets rather than cusp braces; the genuine cusped p21 underbrace on 151_scan005 remains distinct. Frames retain interrupted corners, doubled returns and arrowed edges. On 151_scan007 the smaller compartment shares the large frame's right and bottom boundaries, while adjacent notes and the lower diagram cross that same frame. Its first canceled coordinate arrow has a barred tail and two separate right-facing tips; the later loop-ended and square-ended shafts are different forms. These are changes to recorded source geometry, separate from the later repairs to TeX nesting or matrix syntax.

Cancellation state also required correction. On 151_scan005 the upper inventory's left p0/p1 and delta0 are live, while the middle and right groups are canceled; the commas beside the canceled composites remain live. The separate boxed upper p-pair is canceled, and the lower oval p-pair is live. On 151_scan007 the abandoned upper calculations and complete diagrams, including their arrow paths, are canceled as a block. Nearby border strokes and annotation rules are not automatically deletions.

The completed audits retain the live G-shaped group letters, the literal sigma3 stabilizer on 151_scan003, sigma1 on 151_scan005/007 and the right-hand p11 on 151_scan007. A visible wrong index remains literal even when a separate mathematical check identifies what would make an assertion true. The ambiguous final sigma index on 151_scan005 is a different case: its short upright admits 1, and the recorded limited coordinate check supports that admissible reading. That check does not turn the overtraced q1/p1 label into a certainty.

In the first-pass collation, C was generally the fuller diplomatic witness, preserving many revisions, marginal formulas and unusual abbreviations that H omits or rewrites. That observation concerns coverage, not a measured accuracy ranking. Unmarked C readings also required correction. H provides useful independent alternatives and sometimes preserves a word or source feature missed by C, but its prose bridging, added punctuation, omitted struck work and redistributed labels require care. Both witnesses can agree wrongly, and shared wording or conventional mathematical notation cannot substitute for the ink. Editorial narration is kept separate from source-bearing information about a visible enclosure, annotation or revision.

The two typed letters offer clearer alignment and more separable characters in their ordinary text. Their completed blind adjudications justified no transcription change, but the autographs and the separate edge-clipped lower lines remain unreadable. Visible stroke tops do not establish a complete surname; a fully visible sender name elsewhere does not supply missing letters. Copy specks do not establish an additional accent or typed overstrike. H is absent for both 151_scan004 and 151_scan006, so these outcomes do not represent agreement between two textual witnesses. The inverted typed faces and the differently oriented, later foliation are recorded separately.

The changes log documents places and their evidence, including resolved C doubt marks; it is not a measured error rate or a percentage of accurate text. A place can differ from both witnesses, and one formula can contain several separately documented changes. A newly recovered word, operator or cancellation state must be distinguished from a certainty-only refinement, such as broadening an unchanged first reading's alternatives or leaving a challenged reading unresolved. Geometry and encoding corrections are also identified by what actually changed. A differing blind box or token run does not by itself establish a new C/H reading error.

The blind phase has immutable first readings and box lists, independently written second readings, a comparison that ignores whitespace only, and root decisions for differing boxes. Current per-page audit records preserve the adopted readings, the reasons for retaining alternatives and the cases where no change was justified. Final assembled counts remain for the later checkpoint/final assembly. Residual crossed words and image-edge loss remain explicit limits of the transcription.
