\begin{abstract}
We compute equations, integral Groebner bases, fibres and point
groups for the final modular group scheme in sections 48--50 of
\emph{La Longue Marche}. It is flat of relative dimension six and
smooth exactly away from two. We prove pinning and parametrization
results, correct the integral sheaf presentation, compute geometric
central extensions and the vanishing manuscript Brauer class, and
construct exponential counterexamples and the virtually abelian envelope.
Every statement carries a proof status. Remaining comparison, extension
and universality questions have proved cases and executed computations.
No priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
