\section*{Conventions and scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conventions and scope}
All rings are commutative with identity. Unless a discrete or profinite group
is expressly named, a group is a group scheme and exactness means exactness
of fppf sheaves. An identity on all geometric fibres does not assert an
identity on a nonreduced base. We write $[a,r]=ara^{-1}r^{-1}$ and use
conjugation, rather than multiplication, for the manuscript's $r(a)$.
Our fixed matrices and principal notation are as follows.

In particular $r^3=s^2=-I$, whereas their projective images have orders
$3,2$. The phrase ``elements of orders $3,2$ in $\SL_2(\Z)$'' is not used
for these lifts. The earlier convention satisfies $r+s_-=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$;
the final convention satisfies $rs^{-1}=L_1(1)$ and $s^{-1}r=L_0(-1)$.

We cite the manuscript \citep{D_Grothendieck1981} by cote, scan and autograph
folio where legible, distinguishing transcription from ink. Every numbered
statement appears in the results CSV. ``Open'' means unresolved here.
Partial tasks have proved cases, executed computations and exact residuals;
the report identifies any unfinished task. Verified references were opened
at the recorded passages. The attached LM3, BISITE and GALOIS returns and
reviews are leads to check, not authorities. We claim no priority.
