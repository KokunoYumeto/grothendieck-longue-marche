## 106, scan 010

Own explicit obstruction: “le seul ennui” is that alpha∈W need not lie in cof, so alpha i=f need not lie in cof. The attempted repair by postcomposition beta is rejected: “Visiblement, ce n’est pas tjs le cas”, taking cof to be monomorphisms and f nonmonic; “Ça se présente mal !” The approach to inverses inside pi_0 Ch_0 is thereby abandoned locally, replaced by the canonical functor tilde C→W^-1 C and a proof of surjectivity on arrows. “il est immédiat” asserts identity/composition compatibility without displaying the check. The NB explicitly notes use of y∈Cof in weak lifting. The final alpha i=f calculation is given, not merely promised. Distinguish this proved surjectivity from faithfulness, not proved on this page.

## 106, scan 016

Common domination of factorizations is drawn through a coproduct pushout followed by a cofibration/weak-equivalence factorization. The text says a common dominating weak equivalence has a section if either original one has a section, then reduces the choice issue to a marked statement (*): for a weak equivalence f:x→y with section i, the component of (1_x,i) in pi0 Ch0(x,y) is independent of i. The manuscript calls the condition necessary and sufficient for faithfulness (hence isomorphism) of tilde C→W^{-1}C, but qualifies the completion by “comme on doit pouvoir vérifier aisément, en construisant bel et bien le foncteur cherché C→tilde C.” This is a self-flagged expected verification, not a completed proof on this page. In the middle, section primes are overwritten and remain uncertain. The composition formula visibly uses (f,id_bar y), retained without replacing f by f0.
