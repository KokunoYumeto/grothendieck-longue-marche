A noncentral obstruction to an unrestricted equivariant central-lifting rule
16 November 2026. Research note; no source-specific R2 refutation.

1. An elementary family, and its smallest useful member.
Let p be odd, k=F_(p^2), sigma(a)=a^p, and let epsilon:PGL2(k)->C2
be the determinant square character. In PGL2(k) semidirect <sigma>, put
  E={(g,e): epsilon(g)=e},   N=PSL2(k).
Then 1->N->E->C2->1 is exact. A representative of its outer coset is
(diag(eta,1),sigma), where eta is a nonsquare in k. For p=3 this is
M10=A6.2c, of order720, with N=PSL2(9)=A6 of order360.

No element of E\N has order two. Indeed, if (g,sigma)^2=1 projectively,
then a matrix representative G satisfies G G^sigma=lambda I. Applying
sigma and comparing with G^-1(G G^sigma)G shows lambda^sigma=lambda,
so lambda belongs F_p^*. Taking determinants gives
 Norm_(k/Fp)(det G)=lambda^2.
The quadratic character of Norm(t) equals the quadratic character of t,
since Norm(t)^((p-1)/2)=t^((p^2-1)/2). Hence det G is a square, contrary
to the chosen diagonal-field coset. This is a direct calculation, not
an inference from a group name or a character-table label.

The independent certificate uses k=F3[i]/(i^2+1), encoded a+3b, and all
720 projective matrices. Their action on P1(k) is faithful; adjoining
Frobenius gives1440 distinct permutations. The diagonal-field subgroup
has720 elements. Its kernel has orders
  1:1, 2:45, 3:80, 4:90, 5:144;
the other coset has180 elements of order4 and180 of order8. Perfectness,
centerlessness, and simplicity of the kernel are verified directly:
elementary upper/lower unipotents generate360, their commutators generate
360, and the normal closure of every nonidentity conjugacy-class
representative is the whole kernel. Thus all45 involutions of E lie in N.

2. What a scalar quotient loses.
Because N is perfect, [N,E]=N. Every homomorphic image of E in which
the image of N is central kills N altogether. Thus the maximal such
quotient is simply E/[N,E]=C2, where the nontrivial element is an
involution. Nevertheless this involution has no lift to E, even without
any generation or peripheral conditions.

For the source R=C2 coproduct C2 coproduct C2, send all three named
reflections to the nontrivial element of C2. This is an epimorphism and
all pair products map to1. The embedding problem through E has zero
weak solutions: already one reflection cannot lift. The corresponding
problem through the maximal central pushout has a solution (indeed,
exactly one tuple over the prescribed reflection heads). Relative
perfectness of a finite kernel therefore cannot by itself make an
equivariant lifting problem central.

This is deliberately NOT a counterexample to A.3 or R2. E is not a
quotient of R, since all its involutions generate a subgroup of N.
Nor has this note identified the actual universal central refinement
of a source quotient R/W: there is no epimorphism R->E to begin with.
The ordinary finite count in A.3 starts with an ACTUAL source
quotient and an existing epimorphism; those hypotheses may not be
removed. Any proposed reflection-source version must preserve that
hypothesis and must at least impose the lifting of the relevant
involution classes, in addition to a scalar condition.

3. Negative peripheral classes do not repair the missing involution.
There is a generating pair x,y in N for which x,y,z=(xy)^-1 all have
order4. The exact certificate supplies that pair and elements a_i inN
such that, for beta=conjugation by the chosen diagonal-field h,
 beta(x_i)=a_i x_i^-1 a_i^-1,  x_i=x,y,z.
The outer class of beta has order2 because h^2 belongsN. It has no
involutory representative: every representative is conjugation by
nh, n inN, and the center of E is trivial; all nh have order4 or8.
Hence even negative peripheral class compatibility on an actual
free rank-two finite head does not imply an involutory action.
This remains finite data; it is not claimed to arise from a full
GT0 element, a canonical R1 word, or an actual peripheral full lift.
The actual source central gate can also be audited, and FAILS for this
particular head. Lift x,y to X,Y in SL2(F9) and put Z=(XY)^-1. Each
projective order4 lift has nonzero trace in iF3. The semilinear lift of
beta sends its trace to its negative, while inversion preserves trace.
Consequently, if a full negative peripheral source map induced beta,
the ratio between its SL2 evaluation and the semilinear evaluation
would be a central character taking value -1 on EACH of x,y,z. Their
product is1, a contradiction. The exact matrices and traces are stored.
Thus this refinement is the same spin gate as A.28, now in a direct
SL2(F9) model; it does not create a counterexample to source-specific
R2. The new point of the broad embedding example remains the explicit
noncentral extension and failure of involution lifting despite perfect
kernel, before an actual source epimorphism is imposed.

4. Exact counting gate for a possible source-specific theorem.
For an actual extension pi:E->Q, a named involution tuple tbar_i inQ,
and prescribed pair-product classes C_ij inE, the proper lift count is
 sum over (t0,t1,t2):
   product_i 1[pi(t_i)=tbar_i and t_i^2=1]
   * product_(i<j)1[t_i t_j in C_ij]
   *1[<t0,t1,t2>=E].
The analogous weak count omits the last indicator. In the example both
counts are zero because each nontrivial-head set is empty. A prospective
equivariant analogue of D.50 would need an equality of these real tuple
counts under the proposed transported central data; the ordinary
oriented peripheral count alone supplies no such equality. No claim
is made here about equality once all local reflection classes lift
and both tuples come from actual source epimorphisms. That is the
remaining precise source-specific counting problem.

5. Correct transported-head formulation (clarifying the next gate).
A source-specific count comparison starts with an ORIGINAL epimorphism
rho:R=C2*C2*C2 ->E and quotient pi:E->Q. Put q_i=pi(rho(t_i)). A NEW
automorphism alpha ofQ and specified class conjugators transport these
to the desired heads alpha(q_i). The desired epimorphism toE is NOT
assumed. Its reflection and pair-product classes must be prescribed
using the original classes and the selected conjugators, and its proper
lift count is to be compared with the known positive original count.
An appropriate central source refinement must carry a lift over alpha;
asserting only that E/[ker(pi),E] admits a formal involution is weaker.
The M10 example fails the original-epimorphism hypothesis outright.
No counterexample to this correctly transported, actual-source count
comparison has been constructed here.
