REGULAR PANTS: AN EXACT GLOBAL UNIT AND RELATIVE PRO-TWO GENERATION
Research proof prepared after checkpoint08; not yet a numbered theorem.

Setup and scope.
Let F be the full free profinite group on x,z.  Let sigma be the regular
negative pants involution considered in C.43, with sigma(z)=z^{-1}
literally and sigma inducing the identity on A=F/<<z>>=Zhat.  The local
peripheral sections sigma and z sigma are involutions.  The one-section
theorem gives a conjugating g; replacing that conjugator by its sigma
image if necessary fixes the convention

                    sigma(g) g^{-1}=z.                    (1)

C.43's full fixed-group theorem makes this g unique.  Existence and
uniqueness here are in the actual full profinite F, not merely in a
pro-two quotient.  The conclusion below does not claim <g,z>=F.

1. The exact completed Alexander module.
Put K=<<z>>, Lambda=F_2[[A]], and write T for the image of x in A.  Then

                  K/Phi_2(K) = Lambda * [z],              (2)

as a compact Lambda-module, where Phi_2(K)=closure(K^2[K,K]).

Here is a direct verification retaining the full procyclic deck group.
For each positive integer m, U_m=ker(F->Z/m) is the full completion of
the ordinary degree-m covering subgroup.  The Schreier basis is x^m
and x^i z x^{-i}, 0<=i<m.  Thus U_m/Phi_2(U_m) is F_2 plus F_2[Z/m].
For m|m', d=m'/m, the inclusion-induced transition sends the x^{m'}
coordinate to d times the x^m coordinate and sends a z-coordinate to
the coordinate with its index reduced modulo m.  Taking m'=2m kills
the first coordinate in the inverse limit.  The remaining transitions
are precisely those defining Lambda.

For completeness, this inverse limit is actually K/Phi_2(K).  Every
continuous homomorphism K->F_2 extends to some open U containing K:
choose an open normal V of F with K cap V in its kernel, and extend
to KV by killing V.  The definition is well-defined; commutators of
V with K lie in K cap V.  Every open neighborhood of K contains a U_m.
Consequently Hom_cont(K,F_2) is the direct limit of Hom(U_m,F_2), and
Pontryagin duality gives the asserted inverse-limit identification.
The conjugation action of x shifts the z-basis, proving (2) as a
Lambda-module, including its full induced topology.

Because sigma is the identity on A, its action on (2) is Lambda-linear.
It sends [z] to -[z]=[z] over F_2, so it acts as the identity on (2).

2. Continuous divided differences over the full group ring.
Multiplication by T-1 gives a topological isomorphism

             Lambda --(T-1)--> ker(augmentation Lambda->F_2). (3)

Surjectivity follows since its compact image projects onto the
augmentation ideal of every F_2[Z/m].  For injectivity, a vector killed
by T-1 has constant coefficient c_m in each finite cyclic group ring.
The transition from 2m to m sends that constant vector to zero, hence
c_m=0 for every m.  Compactness makes the inverse in (3) continuous.

For every a in Zhat, there is therefore a unique continuously varying
D_a in Lambda with

                    (T-1)D_a=T^a-1.                     (4)

For nonnegative integer a, D_a=1+T+...+T^{a-1}; by continuity these
integer identities determine the formula for all profinite a.  Its
augmentation is a modulo2, also by integer density.

3. The section conjugator has a FULL profinite unit exponent.
Write sigma(x)=kappa x, theta=[kappa] in Lambda, and g=k x^a with
k in K and a in Zhat.  The crossed-homomorphism identity on powers
of x, first for integers and then by continuity, gives

             [sigma(x^a)x^{-a}] = theta D_a.

Since sigma acts identically on K/Phi_2(K), the k contribution cancels
modulo2.  Equation(1) consequently becomes

                         theta D_a=1 in Lambda.          (5)

In particular D_a is a unit.  Its augmentation shows a is odd.  For
any odd prime q, choose a primitive q-th root zeta in a finite field
of characteristic2.  Evaluation T->zeta is a continuous homomorphism
Lambda->F_2(zeta), factoring through F_2[Z/q].  If a were zero modulo
q, equation(4) would give (zeta-1)D_a(zeta)=0, hence D_a(zeta)=0,
contrary to invertibility.  Thus a is nonzero modulo every prime:

                         a in Zhat^times.                (6)

This tests all odd prime characters of the full procyclic quotient;
it is stronger than detecting only the pro-two exponent of g.

4. Actual generation over the full procyclic head with pro-two kernel.
Let O^2(K) be the intersection of kernels of all continuous finite
two-group quotients of K.  It is characteristic in K and normal in F.
Set G=F/O^2(K), P=K/O^2(K).  Then P is pro-two and G/P=A=Zhat.
Let H be the closed subgroup of G generated by the images of g,z.
Equation(6) gives H->A surjective.  The subgroup H cap P contains
g^t z g^{-t} for all t in Zhat.  Their images in
P/Phi(P)=K/Phi_2(K)=Lambda are T^{at}[z].  Since a is a full unit,
these generate Lambda as a closed F_2-module.  Hence H cap P maps
onto P/Phi(P), and the pro-two Frattini property yields H cap P=P.
This Frattini argument does not need finite generation of P: a proper
closed subgroup is contained in a proper open subgroup, and passage
to a finite two-group quotient puts it in an index-two maximal subgroup.
Therefore H=G.

Equivalently the endomorphism phi of F defined by phi(x)=g, phi(z)=z
descends to a surjective endomorphism of G.  Indeed phi(K) is in K and
every finite two-group homomorphism of K precomposed with phi|_K is
again such a homomorphism, so phi preserves O^2(K).  The finitely
generated profinite group G is Hopfian; thus the descended map is an
automorphism.  If rho(x)=zx and rho(z)=z^{-1}, then rho^2=1 and (1)
gives phi rho=sigma phi.  Thus sigma is genuinely conjugate to the
standard regular involution in this relative pro-two quotient.

Limitations.
Neither the full-generation conclusion <g,z>=F nor free-product
recognition of F semidirect_sigma C2 follows from this argument.
The surviving kernel O^2(K) is exactly the full mixed/non-two
obstruction left uncontrolled.  The quotient head here is the full
Zhat, not its maximal pro-two quotient; this distinction is essential.
