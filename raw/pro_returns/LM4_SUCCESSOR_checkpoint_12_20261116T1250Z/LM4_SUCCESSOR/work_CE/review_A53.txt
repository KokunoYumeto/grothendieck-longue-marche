1. Coinvariants and the maximal primary action quotient
-------------------------------------------------------
Since R=[R,Pi], the finite moduleV and every primary part have zero
Pi-coinvariants. Let H be the actual finite action image. Its maximal
p-quotient is trivial orC2 forp2, trivial orC3 forp3, and trivial otherwise:
any map Pi=C2*C3 to a p-group kills the other factor. For Kp=O^p(H),
(Vp)_Kp is a finite abelian p-group with a finite p-group action and zero
coinvariants. The stated elementary argument is valid: if U=[U,P],
then U lies in every lower-central term of the finite p-group U⋊P,
so U=0. This proves (Vp)_Kp=0 without semisimplicity.

2. Cofinal finite abelianization induction
------------------------------------------

retaining the prescribed V quotient ofMi, quotienting by the image ofAi
gives a finite quotient of the preceding stage that still retainsV.
The full preimage ofMi-1 isMi because Ai is contained inMi, so no
unwanted outside element is introduced in this restriction.

The preceding finite abelianization is exactlyV: it is a quotient of
Mi-1^ab=V and still surjects onto that same V. Consequently the kernel

This remains valid ifA' has primary components outside those ofV;

3. Class-two and wedge induction for all finite abelian exponents
----------------------------------------------------------------
At the first stageZi is globally central, so the ordinary commutator
pairing is alternating and bilinear onV. Its image topologically
generatesZi, making it an actual finite quotient of wedge²_Z V.

At a subsequent stage, gamma3(Mi) lies in the new centralAi and
gamma4(Mi)=1. In particular [Zi,Zi]=1. The remaining commutator

kernel fixing the two successive center subquotients embeds in

a p-group and the new scalar is trivial, so the full action image

for everyi, including mixed primes and non-elementary exponents.

4. Exact augmentation powers and the numerical bound
----------------------------------------------------
OnceZi is abelian, images of the actual relative commutator subgroups
are exactly augmentation powers: (Wj/Wi)_p=Ip^j Zi,p. This follows
inductively from the group commutator definition, not only from a
dimension estimate.

5. Automatic H1 over an actual relative-pronilpotent base
--------------------------------------------------------
For R=R_nil(K0), A34 gives [R,K0]=R. The actual normal
N=K0/R is pronilpotent. Its characteristic factors Np,Np'
are closed normal subgroups ofC. OnVp, zeroN-coinvariants

A continuous cocycle onNp' with values in the finite p-module

an open subgroup on which both the action and the cocycle are
zero. Finite averaging kills it. Subtracting that coboundary
from the cocycle onC leaves one vanishing onNp'. Normality
then forces every value into the zeroNp'-fixed subspace.
This proves H1(C,Vp^vee)=0 for eachp and hence the full
finite abelian dual vanishing.

The conclusion controls all scalar layers of a specified finite

It does not manufacture an action with prescribed peripheral frames,

family. The explicit scope limitations in the working theorem are
necessary and correct.
