Define I_T and I_5 as the images in V of HT intersect gamma3P and H5 intersect gamma3P. Compatible lifts of2w in the two subgroup images in G3 exist exactly when delta is in I_T+I_5. Indeed multiplying a and b by elements with class2 image0 changes delta precisely by these two central subgroups. For an integer multiple m, the obstruction is m delta because a,b lie in gamma2G3 and commute there.

The H5 degree-one matrix is injective of rank6 and its bracket matrix is injective of rank15.
