\subsection*{The first $3$-adic lift and a smaller open refinement}

\statement{B.63}{Proved}{A three-dimensional first-lift obstruction with actual rigid-kernel witnesses}
Use B.62's branched-cover homology action and define
\[
 q_9:P\longrightarrow Q_9
 :=\operatorname{im}\bigl(P\longrightarrow
          \operatorname{PSp}_{10}(\mathbb Z/9)\bigr).
                                                               \tag{B.63.1}
\]
Let $A_9=q_9(H_T)$ and $C_9=q_9(H_5)$.
In the identity fibre of reduction modulo three, write
$I+3X$ for its unique representative reducing to $I$;
then $X\in\mathfrak{sp}_{10}(\mathbb F_3)$.
The two identity-fibre images are vector spaces
$V_T,V_5$ of dimensions six and nine, respectively,
and
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb F_3}(V_T\cap V_5)=3.
 \quad
 W_9:=\{I+3X:X\in V_T\cap V_5\}\cong C_3^3.                 \tag{B.63.2}
\]
For the actual residual group,
\[
            \widehat q_9(\mathcal K)\subseteq W_9.            \tag{B.63.3}
\]
If $M_9=q_9^{-1}(W_9)$, then $M_9$ is a proper finite-index
subgroup of $N_3=\ker(q|_P)$ and is not normal in $P$.
For any $L_1,L_2\leq_f P$ containing $M_9$, the product
\[
                   (H_T\cap L_1)(H_5\cap L_2)               \tag{B.63.4}
\]
is closed in the full profinite topology of $P$.
In particular the two factors for $L_1=L_2=M_9$ have indices
$3888$ and $1049760$ in $H_T$ and $H_5$.

Every direction of $W_9$ has matched actual subgroup words:
the tetrahedral word can be chosen in the normal closure
of B.56's rigid-kernel word $h$, and the cyclic word in
$[H_5,H_5]$.  Both have zero ambient integral first homology.
These are finite-quotient witnesses; they are not asserted
to agree in $P$, in $\widehat P$, or in a joint quotient
also retaining the forgetful tests.

\begin{proof}
\emph{The comparison map.}
The integral construction in B.62 defines (B.63.1) on the
same marked genus-five homology lattice.
Projectivization here means quotient by $\{I,-I\}$;
the full twist acts by $-I$ integrally, so it is killed
also modulo nine.
Reduction $Q_9\to q(P)$ is the actual mod-three map.
An element of its identity fibre has a unique choice of
sign reducing to $I$ and hence has the form $I+3X$.
The symplectic equation reduces to
$X^tJ+JX=0$ over $\mathbb F_3$.
Multiplication in this fibre is addition of the $X$'s.

Use exactly the nineteen common-base geometric braid
words in B.61; their relative marking is not changed.
For each source free group, enumerate its mod-three
image and choose a word $t_d$ representing each image
element $d$, with $t_1=1$.
For every source generator $g_j$, the words
\[
                    t_d g_j t_{dg_j}^{-1}                  \tag{B.63.5}
\]
generate the kernel of the mod-three source map.
Their mod-nine images lie in the elementary abelian
identity fibre, so their vectors span its entire image.
This computes an upper bound by an exhaustive Schreier
generating set, without enumerating the much larger
mod-nine groups.
The $144$ tetrahedral image elements give $1872$ directed
edges in (B.63.5), and the $1440$ cyclic elements give
$8640$ edges.  Exact elimination over $\mathbb F_3$
gives dimensions six and nine.

\emph{Short witnesses with the actual source conditions.}
Write $h=e_6e_{11}e_6^{-1}e_5^{-1}$, as in B.56, and
use the convention $h^u=uhu^{-1}$.
The following six words lie in its normal closure:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 n_0=[e_1,h],& n_1=[e_3,h],\\
 n_2=h^{e_7}(h^{e_5})^{-1},&
 n_3=h^{e_1e_2}(h^{e_2})^{-1},\\
 n_4=e_2n_2e_2^{-1},&
 n_5=h^{e_5^2}(h^{e_2e_5})^{-1}.
\end{array}                                                 \tag{B.63.6}
\]
The six words reduce to the identity modulo three.
Their independent mod-nine vectors span $V_T$.
Each word is a product of conjugates of $h$ and $h^{-1}$,
so it belongs to the actual common rigid-test kernel.
In fact its full tetrahedral source abelianization is zero:
the two conjugates have opposite exponent sums.

The nine cyclic words are
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
 c_0=[f_0,f_3],& c_1=[f_0^3,f_1],&c_2=[f_0^3,f_2],\\
 c_3=[f_1^3,f_2],&c_4=[f_1^3,f_3],&c_5=[f_1^3,f_4],\\
 c_6=[f_1^3,f_5],&c_7=[f_1,f_2^3],&c_8=[f_1,f_3]^2.
\end{array}                                                 \tag{B.63.7}
\]
They also reduce to the identity modulo three, and
their nine mod-nine vectors are linearly independent.
They therefore span $V_5$.
Every $c_j$ is a commutator word, so its full source
abelianization, and hence ambient first homology, is zero.
Equations (B.63.6)--(B.63.7), together with the full
Schreier upper bounds, establish these exact image
equalities with the actual source conditions.
Reducing an integral homology equation modulo three
would not by itself establish those equalities.

Here are three matched pairs, with factors in the
displayed order:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
 i&a_i\in H_T&b_i\in H_5\\\hline
 0&n_1n_3^2&c_1c_2\\
 1&n_0n_1^2n_2n_3^2n_4^2&c_1c_3^2c_4^2c_5c_6\\
 2&n_0^2n_2n_3^2n_4n_5&c_1c_3^2c_5^2c_7c_8
\end{array}                                                 \tag{B.63.8}
\]
Exact multiplication gives
\[
                 q_9(a_i)=q_9(b_i)=I+3X_i.
                                                               \tag{B.63.9}
\]
The three $X_i$ are independent.
The combined matrix of the six tetrahedral and nine
cyclic vectors has rank twelve, so these three vectors
form the entire intersection, proving (B.63.2).
The freely reduced source lengths in (B.63.8) are
$(38,94,88)$ and $(16,54,54)$, respectively.

All entries are specified by the integral matrices of
B.62 and the actual geometric words of B.61.
For reproducibility,
\nolinkurl{next_3adic_symplectic/first_lift.py}
records both Schreier transversals, every directed edge,
the full vector spaces and the exact integral source
homology lattices.
The separate programs

record the normal-closure construction and the shortened
words (B.63.6)--(B.63.8).
Their actual standard output and complete JSON
certificates are retained.

\emph{The first-lift Lie algebra.}
The bracket inherited from the ambient matrix Lie
algebra is
\[
 [X_0,X_1]=X_0,\qquad
 [X_0,X_2]=-X_0,\qquad
 [X_1,X_2]=X_1+X_2.                                        \tag{B.63.10}
\]
Put $H=X_2$, $E=X_0$, and $F=X_1+X_2$.
Then
\[
                 [H,E]=E,\qquad [H,F]=-F,\qquad [E,F]=0.
                                                               \tag{B.63.11}
\]
Thus the common three-dimensional Lie algebra is
solvable, with a two-dimensional abelian derived ideal;
it is not $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_3)$.
This is a matrix Lie bracket, rather than the group
commutator in $W_9$, which is abelian.
For lifts to the next level it occurs in the identity
$[I+3X,I+3Y]=I+9[X,Y]\pmod {27}$.
No identification of the full integral $A_5$ centralizer
is deduced from this first layer.

\emph{The completed residual group and the refinements.}
By B.62, every $k\in\mathcal K$ has trivial mod-three
image.  Since it lies in both completed actual subgroup
closures, its mod-nine image belongs to both finite
identity-fibre images.  Finite quotients have the same
images on a subgroup and on its closure, so (B.63.2)
proves (B.63.3) directly in the full profinite topology.
This uses neither completion of an infinite-rank
discrete kernel nor a pro-nilpotent comparison.

The subgroup $M_9$ is open and contains $\mathcal K$
after taking closure.  It is strictly smaller than
$N_3$, since $V_T$ has dimension six and its intersection
with $V_5$ has dimension three.
The same exact certificate checks that
$q(e_1)X_0q(e_1)^{-1}$ is outside the intersection.
Thus $M_9$ is not normal in $P$.
For any two finite-index subgroups containing $M_9$,
the factorization ambiguity in B.52 lies in
$\mathcal K\subseteq\overline{M_9}$ and hence in both
completed subgroups.  The identical argument used in
B.58 and B.62 proves (B.63.4).
Finally
\[
 [H_T:H_T\cap M_9]=144\,3^{6-3}=3888,
 \qquad
 [H_5:H_5\cap M_9]=1440\,3^{9-3}=1049760.
\]
These follow from the exact finite image orders and the
surjection of both factors onto $W_9$.

The nontrivial common image has therefore survived all
the displayed source conditions at this level, while
yielding a smaller open subgroup that bounds the actual
residual image.  There is no assertion that a nonzero
vector of $W_9$ lifts to $\mathcal K$, that all such
lifts can be chosen compatibly at higher levels, or that
vanishing in the mod-nine normal quotient has been proved.
\end{proof}
