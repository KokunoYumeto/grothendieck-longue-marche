## Cross-page comparison of recurring notation

The main reader compared the recurring families below across the complete first readings and their original-image crops. This comparison preserves local slips and unresolved forms; it does not use an expected mathematical identity as a substitute for a stroke. The independent second readings are recorded separately and may refine individual entries.

In particular, the source's final nonbase-case index on025, the distinct fibre index on032, the `Z,z` order and starred target on034, and incomplete edge writing remain visible. Conditional mathematical checks and counterexamples are recorded in the notes and `CHECK_EVENTS.tsv` rather than applied to the transcription.
