These observations are grounded in the completed first readings and will be checked against the independent readings before the final archive is frozen.

- **Do not read a partial arrow terminal as a complete conventional head.** Scans 019 and 020 contain blunt ends, one-wing terminals, rounded returns and detached nearby strokes. The resulting endpoint geometry matters even when a plausible map can be inferred. Scan 025 repeats this problem in its first square. The arrow ends are recorded as geometry, with labels kept as text.
- **Separate a retraced shaft from a different arrow type.** The narrow two-stroke image map on scan 024 has one head; its second close stroke is reproduced without assigning it a logical double-arrow meaning. Mere increased stroke weight without a change in the arrow reading is retained graphically but is not independently counted as a source correction (scan 027).
- **The side of an arrow label can change while the words remain the same.** In scan 027, can is to the left of the left vertical shaft and its wavy equivalence mark is to the right. Combining both in one label loses that placement. The wave in the inline isomorphism below the square sits over an explicitly directed shaft.
- **A punctuation or delimiter error can survive several otherwise agreeing witnesses.** Examples include the unmatched opening/closing pair in scan 022, the rightward arrow in the complement-looking display of scan 024, and the additional closing parenthesis in that page’s quantified phrase. Mathematical reconstruction is documented separately and does not complete or repair the visible ink.
- **Editorial explanation is not missing manuscript text.** H’s explicit synopses, connective prose and descriptions of omitted cancelled nodes are evaluated as editorial material. They are excluded from source-correction counts when they merely explain the chosen editorial presentation. Literal quotation marks, commas or words attached to a purported source sentence are still compared with the scan.
- **Treat token-alignment fragments as prompts to inspect a complete element.** TikZ labels containing quotes, matrix separators, environment boundaries and macro options can split a single graphic or textual comparison into several difference rows. Every supplied row is dispositioned, but the same actual source place is counted only once. The original difference TSV is literal tab-separated data, so its quote characters must not be interpreted as CSV quoting.
- **Printed letterhead does not by itself make a sheet a reused typescript.** Blank institutional stationery carrying these mathematical notes is classified as text, with the printed header separately inventoried. The wholly cancelled mathematical draft on scan 018 is classified cancelled_draft.
- **An emphasis macro can agree with an underline while its scope disagrees.** H's emph is not counted against C's uline merely for using another macro. On scan032, however, the actual underline stops before the fibre formula, whereas H includes that formula in the marked span. On scan035 the source excludes the initial le and underlines de separately. Compare endpoints as well as the presence of emphasis.
- **An angular lowercase allograph is different from a clear local case reversal.** On scan033 the prose itself writes lowercase z with an angular 2-like form, so angular subscript shape alone does not prove uppercase Z. On scan034 the last two pairs reverse the distinct larger/smaller forms relative to the opening pairs; that written Z,z order is preserved. A global substitution based only on expected point/space roles would lose the distinction.
- **Visible edge fragments do not license completion.** Scan033's terminal sequence ellipsis is clipped to a dot and low run, and its connexe-like word ends at a crossed cluster. The visible fragment is represented and the limitation described; absent final strokes are not supplied. Scan035's crowded final index is handled as a local doubt rather than completed from the surrounding formula family.
- **Line division is not another word.** Source essen-/tiellement, fonda-/mentaux, homo-/topiques and intervien-/nent divisions on030–033 are accounted for as physical layout. Joining the same word in another witness is not a lexical correction and does not generate a new crop or correction row.
- **Nested insertion layers need separate attachments.** Scan035 has a covering parenthesis inserted by a long curved leader and a condensed adjective inserted inside that parenthesis by another leader. A line passing underneath the whole note does not make it cancelled. Its long leader includes a visible pen gap, which is retained without treating the gap as a new symbol.
- **Standalone punctuation should not be forced into the nearest familiar glyph.** The two raised points plus a lower comma before varphi in scan035 are represented in their actual relative placement rather than silently reduced to an ordinary one-dot semicolon. The dark, retraced terminal punctuation of formula16 remains locally doubtful.
