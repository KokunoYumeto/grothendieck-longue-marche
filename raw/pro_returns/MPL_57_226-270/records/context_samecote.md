## Scope of the supplied research register

**LM7 E.23** is about continuous section classes of specified geometric metabelian/marked depth-two arithmetic quotients of the Fermat cubic; **LM6 E.21** concerns evaluating ramified class-two inputs and associated continuous tests. The current formal Picard ML statement may contribute background in a later construction, but no section map, Fermat cubic, marking, arithmetic action or computation of an image/fibre appears here. Even the use of H¹(k,N) at239 concerns a particular unipotent transition kernel, not those section classes.

**WG H1** asks a nonabelian centralizer assertion under vanishing of Tate-module invariants. The present integral component-group stabilization and characteristic-zero vector-group arguments neither give the required Tate invariants nor control a full nonabelian centralizer. S D01–D03 and E08 at291–292/311 contain closer Tate/integral comparisons, yet that previous run likewise labels their connection only a boundary.

**WG2 §1.1 #3** is the appropriate general placement reference for most of these notes; its presence is not a claim that the analytic/formal packet supplies all the planned Book II conclusions. CR's cote43/149/B11 material is a planning lead, and those images are not among the supplied sources.
