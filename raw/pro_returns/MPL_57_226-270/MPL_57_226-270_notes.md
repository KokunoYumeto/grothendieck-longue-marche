## 2. The analytic criterion and a proof audit

### A01 — The criterion is asserted as a theorem, with Grothendieck's own finite-length condition

**File/scan:** P57, 57:227, theorem (iii); 256–258, Th. 1.7; 229 and 264, algebraic versions. **Kind:** unproved claim, in the sense of an asserted theorem whose full proof inputs require audit.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** finite length | longueur finie | necessary | il f. et s.

The letter says “it is nec and suff” and immediately identifies the condition as a “module of finite length”; the handwritten theorem's 57:258(iii) again says “il f. et s.” for the surjectivity of the comparison map. H57, “La lettre,” correctly reports an iff, while its §2 formulation weakens the wording to a sufficient condition. This supplies the earlier witness that SN P03 lacked and corrects the documentary impression of RS's “conjecture (unsearched)” label, without claiming that every proof step or the literature status has been settled. The zero stalk is included explicitly by 57:227's alternative that the support does not contain \(y\).

### A02 — The germ Picard group is not the Picard group of the scheme-theoretic fibre

**File/scan:** P57, 57:227 and 256, definitions. **Kind:** unpursued implication.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** germ | germe | varinjlim | X_y | X_n

The manuscript defines its \(\operatorname{Pic}(X_y)\) by the direct limit over neighbourhoods of \(y\), “\(R^1f(\mathcal O_X^*)_y\),” whereas \(X_n=X\times_Y\operatorname{Specan}(\mathcal O_y/\mathfrak m_y^{n+1})\) are thickenings of the fibre. H57, “La lettre” and “§ 1. Le théorème analytique,” preserves this distinction, and SN P03 correctly declined to reconstruct the germ map from scan 272 alone. A reading that substitutes the ordinary Picard group of \(X_0\) would change both the comparison and the finite-length criterion.

### A03 — Mittag–Leffler and equality of finite images do not assert full surjectivity

**File/scan:** P57, 57:227(i)–(ii), 258(ii) and margin. **Kind:** unpursued implication.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** universal images | images universelles | Mittag | même image

“\(\operatorname{Im}v_n=\operatorname{Im}v_nu\)” says every finite component of a formal Picard class can be matched by a germ, with the germ allowed to depend on \(n\). H57, “La lettre,” restates these separate assertions, and SN P02's questions over henselian rings separate finite-image descent from complete Picard comparison in the same way. The margin on 57:258 gives a finite-stage stabilization refinement, not an unrestricted assertion that every class on \(X_n\) lifts.

### A05 — Saturation explains the stabilization argument

**File/scan:** P57, 57:254, “son quotient ... est sans torsion.” **Kind:** own idea.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** sans torsion | stationnaire | rang | type fini

For a finitely generated abelian group \(L\), a descending chain of saturated subgroups becomes constant once its ranks stabilize: if \(K'\subset K\) have equal rank and \(L/K'\) is torsion-free, then the finite group \(K/K'\) must vanish. This supplies the elementary lattice step behind 57:254, conditional on the queried finiteness input in A04. H57's phrase about stationary subgroups “de type fini” is too abbreviated without this saturation condition, as \(2^n\mathbb Z\subset\mathbb Z\) shows. **Confidence: high**, because the finite quotient argument proves the claimed lattice implication directly; it does not settle A04's topological input.

### A10 — A finite-free-complex test isolates the logical overreach

**File/scan:** P57, 57:250(ii) and 256, “est surjectif.” **Kind:** own idea.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** surjectif | Mittag | changement de base | complexe

Take \(A=\mathbb C\{t\}\) and the complex \([A\xrightarrow{t}A]\) in degrees 1 and 2: its \(H^1\) is zero, but after reduction modulo \(t^{n+1}\), its \(H^1\) is \((t^n)/(t^{n+1})\simeq\mathbb C\). All transition maps on these \(H^1\)'s are zero, so the system is Mittag–Leffler with zero limit and still receives no surjection from \(H^1\) of the original complex. This algebraic test demonstrates why the formal-functions-shaped statement in A09 alone is insufficient; it is not offered as a constructed proper analytic space with structure sheaf realizing this complex. **Confidence: high**, by direct kernel calculation, and it tests an inference omitted from H57's analytic proof discussion rather than contradicting that discussion's main theorem.

### A11 — Stable images suffice for the correction argument

### A15 — A standard proof supplies the analytic-module converse

**File/scan:** P57, 57:227(iii), 256(D), 258(iii), “it is nec and suff.” **Kind:** own idea, standard proof reconstructed here; no novelty claim.  
**Bears on:** Hua finding 57-analytic-pic-completion-criterion. **Search:** finite length | longueur finie | artinien | Krull | complété

For a finite module over \(A=\mathbb C\{z_1,\ldots,z_N\}/I\), exactness of completion and Krull intersection pass completeness to finite subquotients, so a prime filtration of a complete module of infinite length would contain a complete positive-dimensional analytic domain \(R\). Analytic normalization makes \(R\) finite over \(B=\mathbb C\{t_1,\ldots,t_d\}\), \(d>0\), and normalized field trace splits the normal ring \(B\) off \(R\); the equivalent maximal-ideal topologies would therefore make \(B\) complete, contradicted by the divergent formal series \(\sum j!t_1^j\). This proves completeness iff finite length, with no reducedness assumption on \(A\), and supplies the step left implicit by H57's “§ 1. Le théorème analytique.” **Confidence: high**, with the complete proof in `records/analytic_module_lemma.md` and the inputs checked in Demailly II.2.7, II.2.10, II.4.8–4.13 and Stacks Tags 00MA, 00IP, 00L0, 032L; the separate finite-level claim in A09 is not thereby validated.

### G03 — The extra unipotent-kernel hypothesis matters

**File/scan:** P57, 57:237(iii) and insertion. **Kind:** reading question and own idea.
