## Scope and procedure

The controlling instructions are PROMPT(20261124-134408).md. Earlier prompts are retained as historical context only. The source archive contains 30 specified pages. Three readers receive only a scan, its neutral box coordinates and transcription conventions; they do not receive text or witnesses. The main reader compares their TSV readings by script and adjudicates the substantive differences on the enlarged crops. No OCR, text-recognition library or external transcription is used.

The supplied H comparison table often aligns source text against an editorial synopsis. Page labels, descriptions and TeX syntax are not manuscript text. Every supplied row is reviewed in page context; the audit records their disposition separately from actual reading changes. Entire pages are also read visually against the supplied text.

All timestamps are actual UTC. CHECK_EVENTS records source-transcription checks, never mathematical proof verification. Blind reading alone is documented in reader logs and is not labelled an independent proof or a completed adjudication.
