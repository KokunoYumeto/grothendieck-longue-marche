# Final comparison of notation

Completed by the root reader before any context contents were opened. The complete page-by-page audit preceded this comparison. The comparison covered the full set of page files and revisited representative and discrepant glyphs in the source images, particularly scans 092/134 (cursive H), 108/112/113/129 (E), 101/108/110/115/126/129 (doubled capitals), 114–115 (literal dots), and the arrow, inverse-image, and derivative occurrences already checked in the audit.

The first pass had used `\mathbf` for some doubled capitals and `\mathbb` for others. The doubled alphabet is now encoded with `\mathbb`. This affects C, projective-space/projectivization P, affine-space E, and G. The plain variable P and plain principal-parts P are not globally converted. Clear E forms replaced the proposed A alternatives on scans 108, 112, 113. The last-page Lie-algebra letter was compared with scan 092 and now has the single doubtful reading `\mathcal H`, not an unrelated Gothic-g alternative.

The review also distinguished the cursive torsor T from Gamma, underlined omega from Omega, primes from inverse-image negative exponents, ordinary from divided derivatives, and written dashes from maps. Quotation marks use the same TeX convention throughout. Powers, factors of 2, signs, derivative orders, grammatical slips, and seemingly surprising assertions were not made mathematically consistent: discrepancies present on the pages remain in the transcription and are reserved for the research notes.

The original doubt rows are retained as a reading history. A later row or this table can resolve an earlier alternative; the frozen page files contain the final readings.
