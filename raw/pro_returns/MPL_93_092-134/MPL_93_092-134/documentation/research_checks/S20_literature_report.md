### F03 — The family question must distinguish fibrewise algebraicity from holomorphic variation

**Location:** scan 115:7, “Quand X … varie avec un paramètre algébrique … Sont-elles algébriques ?” **Kind:** conjecture/question; own comparison with published obstruction. **Prior IDs:** P04, P09. **Hua:** VIII “Le dictionnaire,” final family-question footnote.

McMullen’s §2, paragraph “Projective structures,” explicitly states that the Fuchsian section of projective structures over Teichmüller space is real analytic and not holomorphic [M00]. Thus, for the full positive-dimensional moduli variation, fibrewise algebraic uniformizing data cannot simply be assumed to form a holomorphic, hence algebraic, section; this is a negative answer to that general interpretation, not a denial of constant or otherwise specially restricted cases. The manuscript’s question and its phrase “fonctions analytiques” should therefore be retained as a question about the parameter dependence, and Hua’s general warning is supported by a directly read primary source.

### F05 — Cartier descent requires zero p-curvature

**Location:** comparison prompted by scans 121–124; not a named theorem in those scans. **Kind:** own comparison, high confidence in the cited distinction. **Prior IDs:** MPL_93_002-046 E26, P06; MPL_5+6_001-037 Cote 6, scan 007, item 10. **Hua:** XIV “Connexions et stratifications intégrables : problèmes divers,” outside this scan range.

For (S	o T) smooth in characteristic (p), Katz’s Cartier theorem identifies quasi-coherent sheaves on (S^{(p)}) with integrable connections on (S/T) having **zero** p-curvature, through relative Frobenius pullback [K70, Theorem 5.1, pp. 190–191]. Ordinary flatness or Frobenius invariance of a scalar/projective expression alone does not establish these hypotheses. This rechecks P06’s categorical distinction; E26’s Hasse scalar and the earlier cote-6 manuscript assertion remain unrechecked first-pass leads.

### F06 — Gauss–Manin nilpotence has genuine geometric hypotheses

**Location:** proposed bridge to the present projective equation. **Kind:** own comparison, high confidence in the primary statement, no claimed application. **Prior IDs:** P06, P07, P08, C25, C33. **Hua:** XII “Le complexe de De Rham filtré, et la connexion de Gauss–Manin,” outside this range.

Katz’s Theorem 10.0 assumes (T=operatorname{Spec}R), with (R) an integral domain finitely generated over (mathbb Z) and characteristic-zero fraction field, (S/T) smooth and connected, (X/S) proper smooth, and locally free Hodge and de Rham cohomology sheaves; it gives global nilpotence of the Gauss–Manin connection [K70, §§9.0–9.1 and Theorem 10.0, pp. 208–210]. The conjugate graded-piece statement uses either the indicated arbitrary-base-change property or flat absolute Frobenius [K70, Theorem 7.4 and Corollary 7.5, p. 203]. None of these hypotheses supplies the missing identification of the manuscript’s uniformizing equation with such a family.
