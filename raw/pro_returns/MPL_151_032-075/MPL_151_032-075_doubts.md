## Legibility and limits

The main uncancelled prose and large displays are generally readable, but the handwriting is rapid and often contracts several letters into one stroke. Every full scan and both supplied halves were read by the same transcriber, followed by a separate second reading of all 44 pages and a comparison of recurring notation; no OCR was used. These checks do not remove all uncertainty: the dense cancellations on 038, the diagonal or overlapping notes on 036, 055, 059, 069, 071–072, the small morphism labels on 064, and the revised indices on 060, 067–068, 071, 073–075 are the least secure parts. The three-word physically obscured cancellation on 038 is the only remaining \illegible marker; attempted readings are supplied for the other crossed material. In particular, the continuous marginal reading on 071 remains weak even after enlargement and rotation. Typeface choices for the flourished F, G, and P, several limit decorations, and the abbreviated même forms retain explicit alternatives instead of being settled from mathematical context. All diagrams and drawings have been typeset and visually inspected in a scratch rendering; the delivered LaTeX remains a fragment using the requested macros.
