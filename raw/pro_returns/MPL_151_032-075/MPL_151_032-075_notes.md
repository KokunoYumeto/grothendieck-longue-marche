# MPL_151_032–075 — Research notes

These notes accompany the frozen diplomatic transcription of cote 151, PDF scans 032–075. They are separate from the transcription: mathematical checks, external terminology, and later reading suggestions below have not changed any frozen page.

**Principal outcomes.** Scans 041–043 contain Grothendieck’s own answer to the sphere objection in the earlier “Remords,” using gerbes and an explicit automorphism calculation; the month of that calculation is not established by the located dating evidence. The requested words are used, with palaeographical doubt, in the supplied cote 149 scan 133, while the present search located neighbourhood-germ and conical constructions but did not establish their first lexical introduction. Cotes 120–121 give several coordinate, compact-category, and finiteness drafts; the verified comparison with o-minimal geometry is stated through actual coordinate and graph maps. Shiota supplies the compact object comparison and, through the specified preprint constructions, the compact homeomorphism-isotopy comparison; the additional higher mapping-space assertions remain unverified here.

Two concrete tests deserve particular attention: note 053-03 gives a finite closed-embedding diagram satisfying the written abstract corollary’s hypotheses whose canonical maps into the colimit identify distinct points; note 071-04 gives a four-point failure of the claimed R-base-change formula under the earlier non-strict closed-family axioms. Each test is attached to its exact hypotheses and map, and the strict sign preserved in scan 070 is treated separately.

#### Q3-03 — Cote 121 scans 056–058, compared with 010

**Kind:** own idea. **Anchor:** Identity inclusion of compact coordinate models

Let C_n be compact semialgebraic subsets of [0,1]^n and K_n all compact subsets; the inclusions C_n→K_n induce the faithful functor sending each space and continuous graph map to itself. Both obey the verified compact coordinate block: finite intersections stay closed, inverse images under its continuous coordinate maps are closed in compact cubes, and direct images stay compact; the arithmetic graphs also belong to both. The Cantor set lies in K_1, is infinite and contains no nondegenerate interval, so this block alone does not impose finite points-and-intervals decomposition, while the separate condition f) excludes its infinitely many singleton components. Confidence: high, because the inclusion functor and this compact example are explicit; no claim is made about a model of every clause in all drafts.

**Context:** Adds a scoped test of the compact axiom block; the supplied context contains no matching 121 test, and WG2 B15 (§1.2) is the related open tame-topology comparison.

#### Q3-04 — Cote 120 scan 005; cote 151 scans 045 and 059; parameter comparison

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Anchor:** « R0 »

#### Q3-05 — Cote 121 scans 054–058; modern named comparison

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Anchor:** Nash geometric categories and Shiota X-families

Schürmann’s Theorem 0.1 gives explicit constructions: \(\mathcal X\mapsto\mathcal N(\mathcal X)\) by local Nash charts, \(\mathcal N\mapsto S(\mathcal N)_n=\{A\subset\mathbb R^n:A\in\mathcal N(\mathbb P^n(\mathbb R))\}\), and affine restriction \(\mathcal N\mapsto\mathcal X(\mathcal N)\), giving inverse correspondences with local X-families satisfying X(v). Its compactification criterion uses \(\tau_n(x)_i=x_i/\sqrt{1+x_i^2}\): the o-minimal core consists of A for which τ_n(A) is locally an X-set at every ambient point, including cube-boundary points. Every bounded X-set and every X-map between bounded X-sets belongs to this core, and the reverse inclusion is supplied by the theorem, so the compact continuous-map categories coincide by the identity on objects and graphs. Applying this comparison to a complete 121 draft still requires verification of the full X-axioms, including difference and one-dimensional finiteness; that verification has not been completed here. [S]

**Context:** Adds a precise primary-source comparison route to C147-T 181–190 and the compact model above; bears on WG2 B15 (§1.2) and WG2 §1.8 #5, without declaring the manuscript axiom systems equivalent wholesale.

#### Q3-06 — Cote 121 scans 019–024 and 054–058; local/global comparison

**Kind:** own idea. **Anchor:** A concrete projective-boundary test

For the semialgebraic structure, the associated Nash category admits locally semialgebraic sets, and the inclusion of globally semialgebraic subsets into that affine family sends each subset and continuous graph map to itself. The subset \(\mathbb Z\subset\mathbb R\) belongs locally at every finite point; in the projective chart \(t=1/x\) around infinity its positive tail becomes \(\{1/n:n\geq1\}\), which has infinitely many isolated points accumulating at 0 and therefore fails local semialgebraic finiteness there. Confidence: high, because the chart map and accumulating set are explicit; this illustrates the specified compactification comparison without identifying the manuscript’s entire sheaf-of-families construction with it.

**Context:** Adds an explicit local/global test to C147-T 182–184 and WG2 B15 (§1.2); the context’s restriction claims do not decide the boundary-at-infinity condition.

### 1.4. The comparison with Shiota

#### Q4-01 — Cote 151 scans 044–045 and 059–061; Esquisse §5, printed 29–30 and note 6,46

**Kind:** conjecture. **Anchor:** The actual Isom comparison

The Esquisse asks for the canonical map \(\operatorname{Isom}_{T}(X,Y)\to\operatorname{Isom}_{T^{\prime}}(X,Y)\) to induce a bijection on isotopy components and a homotopy equivalence of semisimplicial mapping spaces; it also envisages other map classes and stratified systems. #### Q4-02 — Cote 151 scans 044–045 and 059–061; compact objects

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Anchor:** The compact object-class map

For a real-field o-minimal structure S with real parameters, inclusion induces \(\{\text{compact polyhedra}\}/\cong_{\rm PL}\to\{\text{compact S-definable sets}\}/\cong_S\). Definable triangulation gives surjectivity, and Shiota’s published Theorem 1.1 gives injectivity by turning a definable homeomorphism between compact polyhedra into a PL homeomorphism. This bijection realizes the compact object-class comparison described in note 6, with its actual map specified. [Sh14]

**Context:** Adds a verified literature theorem to the earlier context’s tame-topology programme; bears on WG2 B15 (§1.2) and WG2 §1.8 #5.

#### Q4-03 — Cote 151 scans 044–045 and 059–061; compact homeomorphism isotopies

**Kind:** unpursued implication. **Anchor:** The map on isotopy classes

For compact polyhedra P,Q and an o-minimal expansion S of the real field with real parameters, write H_S(P,Q) for S-definable homeomorphisms modulo S-definable isotopy through homeomorphisms, and H_PL for PL homeomorphisms modulo PL isotopy: \(H_{\rm PL}(P,Q)\to H_S(P,Q)\) is surjective by the Complement of Theorem 2.1 in Shiota’s 2010 author preprint and injective by the endpoint-preserving, level-preserving construction in the proof of Lemma 3.3, pp. 44–46. For o-minimal expansions T⊂T′ of this same field and compact T-definable X,Y, choose T-triangulations a:P→X and b:Q→Y; conjugation \(p\mapsto bpa^{-1}\) identifies both H_T(X,Y) and H_T′(X,Y) with H_PL(P,Q), and the commuting triangle proves that the actual inclusion H_T→H_T′ is bijective. Thus the compact homeomorphism π0 comparison has this precise positive answer; the whole semisimplicial mapping-space equivalence and the additional map classes requested in the Esquisse have not been verified from a stated theorem here. [Sh10]

**Context:** Adds a proof of the specified compact π0 comparison to the context’s open tame programme; bears on WG2 B15 (§1.2), with no claim that this finishes that broader entry.
