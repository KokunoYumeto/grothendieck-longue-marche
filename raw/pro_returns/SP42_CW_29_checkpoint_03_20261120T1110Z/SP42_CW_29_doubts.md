## Legibility and limits — pages decided so far

Scans 003–004 are typewritten letter pages with distinct blue additions. The typed prose is generally legible, but mechanical overtyping, small hand-added symbols and the compressed signature require more caution. On scan 003 the order of several overlapping impressions remains unresolved; a two-loop group letter has no secure alphabet key, and the small primed Hom subscript does not settle lowercase versus capital. On scan 004 the initials are clear while the low surname strokes remain qualified. The isolated mark beneath scan 003 is least securely identified and may be incidental.

The returns show why agreement does not substitute for inspection: two readers and both witnesses overlooked the overstrike in giving, while one reader missed a plainly doubled p. C largely preserves the typed slips and correction states on these pages; H sometimes gives the corrected or normalized result and omits a typed page label. These observations concern only the completed pages and are not an estimate of the witnesses’ overall error rates. Counts in the changes log count loci and encoding of physical state, not errors per word.

Scans 005–006 are handwritten and mostly readable, but tightly joined letters, case at line beginnings, small detached points, and the compressed signature tails are less secure. The final text marks the uncertainty at the affected letters or mark, rather than extending it to an entire otherwise legible word or signature. On scan 006, the separator is visibly two points but its interpretation as paired periods or an oblique colon remains open. The fibres/fibers ordering is similarly unresolved. Marginal strokes have been drawn without assigning them a mathematical meaning or a writer whose identity the image does not establish.

H omits several marginal marks and underlines, supplies dashes in blank gaps, and normalizes some spelling or punctuation. C is generally closer to the visible state, while its uncertainty sometimes covers more letters than the image warrants. Neither witness nor majority agreement determines the final reading: a main-reader crop on scan 006 also exposed a faint possible point absent from all three returns. This mark remains explicitly doubtful.

The typewritten scans 008–009 again make overtyping the principal uncertainty. On scan008, the photographed edge contains only proo of a cancelled word, so the expected final f is not supplied. On scan009, one reader found an additional f-like impression in connected that the other readers and both witnesses had omitted; main crop inspection confirmed the overlapping forms. The very faint graphite date on scan009 is the least dependable date reading so far. Colour-channel contrast enhancement enlarged compression artifacts without settling its digits or separators, and the full uncertainty remains.

Scans 010–011 show a recurring distinction between readable superposed components and unreadable correction order. The l/o, f/w and paired n/i impressions are clearer than their sequence. Other dense cells, including the middle of standard, the sixth cell of reduced and the extra r in intergers, support only provisional component or cancellation analyses. Both witnesses can preserve the expected resulting word while losing a residual impression. The separate faint A/lambda-shaped cell on scan011 is real but cannot be assigned a secure identity or live/cancelled state; it is no longer treated as one double-struck A. Faint nontextual curves and two small raised spots are retained under local doubt without a definite hand attribution.

Physical cutoffs are treated consistently: surviving incomplete letters remain qualified, while wholly absent terminal letters or punctuation are not restored. The missing group base on scan010 and five blank relation positions on scan011 are deliberate transcription gaps supported by crop inspection. Their conventional mathematical completions in H are excluded from the text. These are observations about the visible source, not a claim that the author's mathematics is false.

On scan012 the ordinary typed prose is clear enough to confirm considred against C’s considered. The cancelled w can be read, but initials under x cancellation and the order of the i/o overprint remain less secure. The missing formula brackets, group bases and second arguments are clear absences, distinct from uncertain glyphs.
