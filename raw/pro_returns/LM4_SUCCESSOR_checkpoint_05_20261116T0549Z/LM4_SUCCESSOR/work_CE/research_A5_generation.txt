Working argument: completion of canonical A5 generation
=======================================================
Status: proposed, sent to root and A for independent review during the
formal source freeze. No formal theorem has yet been inserted.

Let alpha=(-1,f) in full GT0 be an involution, u=f alpha(u) its unique
full R1 parameter, y'=u^-1 y u. C35 proves u(y,x)=u^-1. Put
 v=Inn(u^-1) iota, where iota swaps x,y.
Then v^2=1, v(x)=y', v(y')=x. Moreover alpha v=v alpha:
 iota alpha=Inn(f) alpha iota and alpha(u)=f^-1 u give
 alpha v=Inn(alpha(u)^-1 f^-1) iota alpha=Inn(u^-1)iota alpha.
Thus tau=alpha v is an involution. On H1(F,F2), tau acts by swap.
It preserves the third cusp up to conjugacy with exponent-1, because
v is an inner modification of the positive swap and alpha is negative.

Lemma needed (the counting calculation already used in C8):
If an involution sigma of full Fhat2 acts on H1(F,F2) as the regular
F2[C2] module, then E=F semidirect_sigma C2 has exactly one F-conjugacy
class of sections. Indeed the two-row Hochschild--Serre spectral sequence
has H^n(E,F2)=F2 u^n for n>=2, since the positive cohomology of the regular
module vanishes. Its degree-zero localized algebra is F2. The exact full
finite-coefficient restriction theorem of Kochloukova--Zalesskii2007,
Theorem2, used verbatim in C8 and C28, then gives one section class.
This count uses neither a torus cusp nor the later relative-duality
centralizer argument of C8. No assertion about a centralizer is needed.

Consequence: if such sigma inverts a primitive boundary z and acts
identically on a finite quotient q:F->Q, then q(z)=1. The finite map
E->Q times C2 sends the chosen section to central(1,c). Every section
is conjugate to it, hence has that same finite image. A local lift
inverting z has square in its full procyclic centralizer and fixed by
inversion; torsion-freeness makes it an involution. It and z times it
are the two local reflections, whose product is z. Both map to(1,c),
so q(z)=1. This is the proposed final contradiction below.

The small exact script check_a5_duality_orbits.py classifies the19
Aut(A5) generating-pair orbits and checks the binary duality possibilities.
It does not enumerate any full word constraints. The classification is:
* eight orbits with generator orders3 and5: every conjugated pair still
  generates, since no proper A5 subgroup contains both orders;
* six orbits with one generator of order2 and the other of order3 or5;
* one orbit(3,3,5);
* four orbits with both generator orders5, of passports(5,5,3) twice,
  (5,5,5), and(5,5,2).
The two5/5 orbits with different A5 five-cycle conjugacy classes are
(5,5,2) and one(5,5,3). The other two have equal classes.

For each of the six one-order2 orbits, the negative peripheral alpha
preserves the quotient kernel: its inverse-power triple has the same
passport, which determines the Aut(A5) orbit. The induced automorphism
is inner, since it preserves a five-cycle conjugacy class and the outer
automorphism interchanges those two classes. Write it as Inn(h), h^2=1.
If the canonical substituted pair generates a proper subgroup M, the
only possibilities are A4, S3, D5. Since alpha inverts the two substituted
generators it normalizes M. These maximal subgroups are self-normalizing.
An element of A4 cannot invert a3-cycle, so M cannot be A4. In S3 or D5,
h must be the order2 substituted generator: it centralizes that reflection
and inverts the odd rotation, and its reflection centralizer is C2.
In E=F semidirect_alpha C2 -> A5 semidirect_Inn(h) C2 = A5 times C2,
the associated full X or Y section therefore has central image(1,c).
C28's base incidence triangle makes the third cusp's reflections belong
to X and Y. Their finite product has order at most2, contrary to the
order3 or5 image of that cusp. Thus all six orbits generate.

For any5/5 orbit, swap preserves its quotient kernel. If the substituted
pair generates a proper subgroup, it is cyclic C5, say b'=a^r. The induced
v swaps a,b' and has order2, so r^2=1 mod5. In the two orbits with different
five-cycle classes, r is2 or3, which is impossible. This settles those two.

It remains to consider (3,3,5), same-class(5,5,3), and(5,5,5). Their
negative and swap automorphisms preserve the quotient kernel and are
inner: the relevant passport plus equal five-cycle classes specify the
orbit, and preserving a five-cycle class excludes the outer automorphism.
For the3/3 case a proper image A4 is excluded exactly as above; hence the
proper image is cyclic C3. In the5/5 cases the only proper image is C5.
Write b'=a^r. Again v^2=1 forces r=+1 or-1 (for3 this is automatic).

The plus case is excluded by full duality in SL2(F5). Let the swapping
inner automorphism be Inn(t), with t a nontrivial involution of A5. If
u conjugates b to a then, from v=Inn(u^-1)swap and v centralizing a,
the induced inner involution v lies in C_A5(a), an odd cyclic group;
thus v=1 and q(u)=t. Lift the pair to SL2(F5). The swapping automorphism
is conjugation by a lift T of t, up to central changes of the input
matrices; such changes do not affect a commutator word. Every lift U of
q(u)=t is +/-T, and T^2=-1 (SL2(F5) has unique involution-1). Duality
would give U Ad(T)(U)=1, but its left side is U^2=-1. Contradiction.

In the remaining minus case, both induced alpha and v are inner
involutions normalizing the same C3 or C5 and inverting its generator.
They commute, since alpha and v commute in the full group. In an odd
dihedral normalizer two commuting reflections coincide. Hence induced
alpha and v agree, and tau=alpha v acts trivially on this A5 quotient.
Its mod2 action is swap and it inverts the primitive third cusp. The
one-section lemma therefore forces that cusp image to vanish, contradicting
its order3 or5. This would complete all19 orbits.

Points for independent audit: exact applicability of the existing full
restriction theorem to the regular mod2 module (only section count needed);
inner versus outer class distinctions in the three remaining passports;
and lifting the swapping automorphism to SL2(F5) up to central signs.
