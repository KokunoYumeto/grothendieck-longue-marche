## Scope of the actual standing-section check

All retained frozen page files were text-searched for explicit interrogation, verification, pending-reference and pagination markers; the full relevant passages were then read in their page files, and the corresponding Hua sections and `RESULTS_STATE` orientation were checked. The distribution and diagonalizable reused sheets were also text-searched for a direct contextual counterpart: Hua provides no substantive distribution or diagonalizable subsection in this supplied modern reading, so none has been invented. This pass adds no new mathematical check beyond the already documented BR, P, FORMAL and root memoranda, and it leaves every uncertain or cancelled wording in the frozen transcription unchanged.
