**Source:** 150/073. **Kind:** unpursued implication / literature comparison. **Anchor:** “topologie modérée.” **Context search:** o-minimal, geometric category, projection; C150 Q3c–Q3d and T147 181–184; verifies a comparison framework, not an attribution. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan].

The comparison should check Boolean families in every finite power, coordinate projections, field-operation graphs, one-dimensional finiteness, and the parameter convention (van den Dries & Miller, 1996, §2). Analytic-geometric categories correspond to o-minimal expansions of \(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{an}}\), while Schürmann's broader dictionary relates o-minimal real-field expansions, Nash geometric categories, and Shiota families with the additional locality axiom (van den Dries & Miller, 1996, D.10; Schürmann, 2002, Theorem 0.1). The correspondence changes the treatment of local charts and infinity; it is not literal equality of the original affine-set families.

### T03 — Local membership and global definability differ

**Source:** 150/073, unspecified tame context. **Kind:** own diagnostic deduction, restating a relevant context test. **Anchor:** “un contexte.” **Context search:** local, global, o-minimal; C150 Q3d and T147 182–184; applies that existing diagnostic to the present absence of an axiom list. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan]. **Confidence:** high, directly from one-dimensional finiteness.

The set \(\mathbb Z\subset\mathbb R\) is locally semialgebraic at every ambient point but cannot be globally definable in an o-minimal expansion of the real field. Thus unrestricted local-to-global membership and global o-minimality are different literal axioms, consistent with the dictionary in T02. Likewise, if the inherited M9 literally makes every permitted subset compact, the usual global family containing all of \(\mathbb R\) is not that literal family. Neither diagnostic identifies the unseen full theory with a named modern variant.

### T04 — Definable triviality does not automatically construct the prescribed retractions

**Source:** 150/074 and 076. **Kind:** unpursued implication / literature comparison. **Anchor:** “ajusté de façon cohérente”; `\inserted{de \unsure{locale} trivialité}`. **Context search:** famille, trivialité, équisingularité; C150 R19–R20, R26–R27, R41, T147 190; identifies useful modern input and its limitation. **Register:** WG H4 [hunch]; WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan].

For a continuous map definable in a fixed o-minimal expansion of the ordered real field, generic triviality gives a finite definable partition of the image over which the map is a definable product, without requiring compactness or properness in that statement (van den Dries & Miller, 1996, Theorem 4.11 in the author revision). It does not automatically keep the manuscript's prescribed stratum partition or construct a coherent family of tubular retractions. The Esquisse itself separates tubular-neighbourhood questions from the equisingularity required for local fibrations (Grothendieck, 1984/1997, §5, pp. 30–31). That separation agrees with the explicit final parenthesis on 076.

### T05 — The exact compact Hauptvermutung statement

**Source:** 150/073–076. **Kind:** unpursued implication / verified literature comparison. **Anchor:** “topologie modérée.” **Context search:** compact, polyhedra, Hauptvermutung; C150 Q4b–Q4c, T147 190; checks the earlier partial-match lead. **Register:** C150 Q4b [FP]; WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan].

For compact polyhedra in an o-minimal field setting, a definable homeomorphism implies a PL homeomorphism; the author's marked-family restatement preserves a finite family of compact subpolyhedra (Shiota, 2014, Theorem 1.1; Shiota, 2016, restatement on printed p. 8). The homeomorphism must be definable, and the conclusion does not say that the given map is already PL. In the checked 2010 formulation the field is real closed, “compact” means closed and bounded, and compact polyhedra are finite unions of simplices, not necessarily compact in the order topology of a non-Archimedean field (Shiota, 2010, §§1–2). This is a conditional modern realization of part of the programme, not an identification of the manuscript's unspecified category.

### T06 — The isotopy supplement is a real strengthening

**Source:** 150/074, coherence of choices. **Kind:** unpursued implication / verified literature comparison. **Anchor:** “de façon cohérente.” **Context search:** isotopy, Supplement, Hauptvermutung; C150 Q4c, T147 190; checks the stronger version of the earlier lead. **Register:** C150 Q4b [FP]; WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan].

The compact-family homeomorphism can be definably isotoped to a PL homeomorphism **through homeomorphisms**, preserving the marked family (Shiota, 2016, printed p. 8). Shiota explicitly says that this qualification was omitted from the earlier Supplement's statement although present in its proof. This task checked the author's later restatement and correction, not the inaccessible published page 227 itself. The refinement is relevant to comparisons of choices, but it is not automatically a theorem about every parameterized family of retractions used on 076.

### T07 — A compact component comparison follows

**Source:** 150/074–076, as a comparison target. **Kind:** own deduction from checked relative results. **Anchor:** “choix”; “rétractions.” **Context search:** isotopy, relative, compact; C150 Q4d, T147 190; confirms the restricted earlier deduction. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan]; no general comparison status change. **Confidence:** high for fixed compact marked polyhedra and the specified definable isotopies.

Straightening provides a PL representative of each definable isotopy class of homeomorphisms, and the compact relative injectivity argument converts a definable isotopy between PL homeomorphisms into a PL isotopy through homeomorphisms with the prescribed fixed subsets (Shiota, 2010, pp. 44–46). Consequently the PL and definable isotopy classes agree in that setting, including for nested o-minimal structures on the same field. This concerns the stated admissible isotopies, not arbitrary continuous compact-open families. It also does not make the inclusion of raw PL-map categories full.

### T08 — The Esquisse proposes a stronger mapping-space comparison

**Source:** 150/073, compared with the later Esquisse. **Kind:** unpursued implication / primary programme comparison. **Anchor:** current “topologie modérée”; later Isom comparison. **Context search:** isotopique, Isom, comparaison; C150 Q4a and Q4e, T147 188–190; verifies the larger target. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan].

The Esquisse asks for a comparison \(\operatorname{Isom}_T(X,Y)\to\operatorname{Isom}_{T'}(X,Y)\) inducing both a component bijection and a homotopy equivalence, and allows semisimplicial models and analogous geometric mapping spaces and systems (Grothendieck, 1984/1997, §5, p. 29). It also calls the axiomatic system provisional and emphasizes tame triangulability. Therefore compact object-level Hauptvermutung, even supplemented by the component result T07, does not by itself certify the entire stated programme.

### T09 — The remaining verification is relative and parameter-preserving

**Source:** 150/074–076. **Kind:** own idea / sufficient comparison protocol. **Anchor:** “de façon cohérente”; “inclusions quelconques.” **Context search:** parameter, relative, comparaison; C150 Q4e and R51, T147 190; makes the outstanding test explicit. **Register:** WG2 B15 (§1.2) [plan]. **Confidence:** high as a sufficient verification target, without claiming the wider problem is unknown in all literature.

For a chosen semisimplicial family model, seek straightening of definable homeomorphisms \(F:P\times\Delta^n\to Q\times\Delta^n\) over \(\Delta^n\), through definable homeomorphisms over that parameter space to a jointly PL homeomorphism, preserving the marked subspaces and fixing prescribed boundary families that are already PL. Arbitrary non-PL boundary data require a prior compatible replacement, and straightening the total-space map while preserving finitely many faces need not preserve each parameter fibre. Such relative control in every required dimension would support a weak-equivalence comparison, with a further model-dependent step if an actual homotopy equivalence is asserted. The checked literature supports T05–T07; this task does not certify the full assertion for all proposed tame theories, map types, and systems.
