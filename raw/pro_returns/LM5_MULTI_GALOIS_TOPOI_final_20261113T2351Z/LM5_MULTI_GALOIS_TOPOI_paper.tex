\begin{abstract}
We prove corrected recognition and reconstruction theorems for the
multi-Galois, mixed, stratified, and higher topoi in Grothendieck's
manuscripts. Exact cohomological defects, sharp flag bounds, sphere
Postnikov invariants, and arithmetic inertia counterexamples identify
where the provisional claims fail. A separate argument proves pinned
etale reconstruction over subfields of finitely generated extensions
of $\mathbb Q^{\mathrm{ab}}$. The paper retains
two unresolved obligations: a further base-field test and one literal
cross-return audit. Every numbered statement has a status and a
manuscript locus in the accompanying registers. No priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
