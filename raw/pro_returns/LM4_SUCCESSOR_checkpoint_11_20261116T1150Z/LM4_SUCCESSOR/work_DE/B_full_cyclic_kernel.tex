\subsection*{An exact kernel of the full cyclic rigid family}

\statement{B.69}{Refuted}{Injectivity of the full cyclic rigid map and its completion}
Use B.65's actual geometric basis
$H_5=F(f_0,f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4,f_5)$ and its specified map
$\theta_5:H_5\to PB_6$.
Put
\[
 \begin{split}
  g&=f_5^{-1}f_0^{-1}f_2^{-1},\\
  h&=f_5^{-1}f_1f_5,\\
  k&=h^{-1}g=f_5^{-1}f_1^{-1}f_0^{-1}f_2^{-1},\\
  w_0&=[k,f_1].
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{B.69.1}
\]
Then $w_0\ne1$ in $H_5$ and in $\widehat H_5$, whereas
\[
                    \theta_5(w_0)=1\quad\text{in }PB_6.
                                                               \tag{B.69.2}
\]
Consequently the full joint rigid-seven test family on
the cyclic centralizer is not faithful, discretely or
after full completion.  This concerns the actual full
map of B.65, not only the intermediate quotient of B.67.

\begin{proof}
With signed index $j$ denoting $f_{j-1}$, the word is
\[
                 (-6,-2,-1,-3,2,3,1,2,6,-2).
                                                               \tag{B.69.3}
\]
It is freely reduced of length ten.
There is also an explicit finite witness: send
$f_1$ to $(1\ 2)$, $f_5$ to $(2\ 3)$, and all four
other generators to the identity in $S_3$.
The word maps to $(1\ 2\ 3)$.
Thus it is nonidentity in the full free profinite
completion as well as in the discrete free source.

The standalone verifier
\nolinkurl{next_joint_rigid_symplectic/refute_full_theta5.py}
reads the original common-base eleven-strand words of
B.61, imports no discovery code, and performs its own
strand deletion and faithful Artin evaluation.
The retained finite labels are $(4,11,5,1,10,6)$.
Substitution of (B.69.3) gives a freely reduced braid
word of length $132$.  Its six Artin images are exactly
\[
                         x_i\longmapsto x_i,
                         \qquad 1\leq i\leq6.                 \tag{B.69.4}
\]
Faithfulness proves (B.69.2) before any central quotient.
The complete six source braids, the substituted braid,
all six Artin images, and the finite $S_3$ witness are
retained in
\nolinkurl{full_theta5_noninjection_certificate.json};
the accompanying standard output is from the actual
fresh run.  We independently reread the proof and
reran this complete certificate successfully.

We also give the structural derivation, which explains
why this relation occurs.  Move label $5$ to the last
strand as in B.68, using
$\tau=\sigma_3\sigma_4\sigma_5$ and
$b\mapsto\tau^{-1}b\tau$.
The order becomes $(4,11,1,10,6,5)$, and the last-strand
Birman kernel has free coordinates $x_1,\ldots,x_5$.
Set
\[
                       B=x_1x_2x_3x_4x_5,
                       \qquad q=x_3x_5.
                                                               \tag{B.69.5}
\]
The last-conjugator extraction for $f_1$ is
$qB^{-1}q^{-1}$.  As audited in B.67 and B.68, this
extraction is antihomomorphic.  Inverting it and
conjugating the resulting fiber identification by
$q^{-1}$ therefore sends $f_1$ to $B$ literally.

For a source element $s$, write its last Artin image
as $\widetilde Q_s x_6\widetilde Q_s^{-1}$, and let
$Q_s$ be the image of that conjugator after killing $x_6$.
Let $D_s$ be the induced automorphism
on the other five generators after killing $x_6$.
The actual conjugation action on the fiber is
\[
 \alpha_s(x)=Q_s^{-1}D_s(x)Q_s.
\]
Because the total Artin boundary is fixed,
$\alpha_s(B)=Q_s^{-1}BQ_s$.
In the $q$-normalized fiber this becomes
\[
 \begin{split}
  \alpha'_s&=\operatorname{Inn}(q^{-1})\alpha_s
                                  \operatorname{Inn}(q),\\
  \alpha'_s(B)&=R_sBR_s^{-1},\\
  R_s&=q^{-1}\alpha_s(q)Q_s^{-1}.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{B.69.6}
\]
The exact original-word evaluation gives
\[
 R_{f_5^{-1}}=B^{-1}x_1B,
 \qquad R_g=B^{-1}x_1Bx_1^{-1}B
                   =R_{f_5^{-1}}BR_{f_5^{-1}}^{-1}.            \tag{B.69.7}
\]
Since $f_1$ is $B$, the right-hand side is precisely
the actual fiber image of $h=f_5^{-1}f_1f_5$.
Hence conjugation by $h^{-1}g$ fixes $B$, giving
$[h^{-1}g,f_1]=1$ in the actual braid image.
The verifier checks this equality of full fiber words,
in addition to the independent complete Artin test.
No inference from a quotient relation module is used.

Finally B.65 identifies the kernels of all ten nonconstant
rigid-seven restrictions on $H_5$ with $\ker\theta_5$;
the other two restrictions are zero.  The same kernel
identification holds after full completion.  The word
(B.69.3) therefore proves the stated nonfaithfulness
of both joint test families.
It is not an element of $\mathcal K$: B.28 embeds the
free source in $P$, whereas B.7 gives $C_P(A_5)=1$.
Its failure to centralize some element of $A_5$ also
survives in the full completion by residual finiteness.
\end{proof}

\statement{B.70}{Proved}{The cyclic joint rigid kernels are free of countably infinite rank}
Define the actual embedded kernels
\[
       N_5=\ker(\theta_5)\leq H_5=F_6,
       \qquad
       M_5=\ker(\widehat\theta_5)\leq\widehat H_5.
                                                               \tag{B.70.1}
\]
Then $N_5$ is an abstract free group of countably infinite
rank, and $M_5$ is a free profinite group of countably
infinite rank, with the convergent-basis convention.
These are respectively the joint discrete and completed
kernels of all twelve rigid-seven tests on $H_5$.
The assertion $M_5=\overline{N_5}$ remains open;
$M_5$ is not the full profinite completion of the
abstract group $N_5$.

\begin{proof}
B.69 gives a nontrivial finite word in both kernels.
Both kernels have infinite index.  Indeed the same
exact original-braid certificate gives
\[
       \ell_{4,5}(\theta_5(f_5))=1,
       \qquad \ell_{4,6}(\theta_5(f_5))=0.
                                                               \tag{B.70.2}
\]
Each pair-linking homomorphism takes the central full
twist to $1$.  Thus $\ell_{4,5}-\ell_{4,6}$ descends
to $PB_6/\langle\Delta_6^2\rangle=P\Gamma_{0,7}$,
and has value $1$ on the image of $f_5$.
The discrete image is infinite, and its completed image
has the infinite quotient $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$.
No assertion about the full center of a completed
braid group is needed for this argument.

By Nielsen--Schreier, $N_5$ is free.
A nontrivial normal subgroup of infinite index in
a finite-rank free group cannot be finitely generated.
For completeness, a finitely generated subgroup would
have a finite core in its covering of the finite rose.
A nonempty cyclically reduced word in the normal
subgroup labels a reduced closed loop at every vertex
of that cover, by normality.  Every vertex would
therefore belong to the finite core, forcing finite
index.  This contradiction proves infinite rank;
countability of $F_6$ makes the rank exactly $\aleph_0$.

For the completed kernel use the precise finite-word
theorem of \citet[Theorem~1.2; Corollary~3.7 in the
primary preprint]{DE_Shusterman2016}, already applied
with its full hypotheses in B.56.
The ambient $\widehat F_6$ is finitely generated and
free profinite, with the six geometric meridians as
a profinite basis.
The subgroup $M_5$ is closed and of infinite index.
It contains the closed normal closure of the nontrivial
finite basis word (B.69.3); its nontriviality is
independently detected by the displayed $S_3$ quotient.
These are exactly the theorem's hypotheses.
It follows that $M_5$ is free profinite of rank
$\aleph_0$.  Closed normality and infinite index alone
would not suffice: the finite-word hypothesis is
essential to this application.

B.65 supplies the exact joint-kernel identifications
in the statement, including removal of the central
twist by the fixed-pair linking homomorphism.
The six specified source braid words also give an
exact discrete membership procedure: substitute a
finite source word and accept precisely when all
six faithful Artin images are the respective free
generators.  Thus (B.70.1) refers to a completely
specified homomorphism, not just abstract isomorphism
types of unspecified subgroups.

The equality of the closed completed kernel with
$\overline{N_5}$ would require a further comparison
with the induced topology on the actual braid image.
No such equality has been proved here.
There is also a strict distinction from
$\widehat{N_5}$: a countably generated abstract free
group of infinite rank has uncountably many maps
to $C_2$, by independent choices on a free basis.
Its full profinite completion is consequently not
metrizable.  In contrast, $M_5$ is a closed subgroup
of the metrizable finitely generated free profinite
group $\widehat F_6$.
Neither the kernel descriptions nor their rank
calculations determine the residual centralizer
$\mathcal K$.
\end{proof}
