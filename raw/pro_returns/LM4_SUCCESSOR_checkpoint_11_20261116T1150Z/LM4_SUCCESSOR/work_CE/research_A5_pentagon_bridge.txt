The marked/joint A5 gap: exact pentagon reduction and the first affine gate
=======================================================================
Research note, 2026-11-16, sixth-hour formal freeze.
This is not a newly numbered formal theorem and does not solve the full
third-cusp or canonical-conjugacy problem.

1. The exact question.

C.37 proves generation in each individual A5 quotient for a full GT0
involution and its canonical u. It does not prove that the resulting
19 quotient kernels are permuted, or that the third cusp has the correct
unit exponent d. The finite false-positive table from
check_a5_finite_constraints.py has f=1, changes just the dual coordinates
4 and 10, and sends passports (3,5,2), (5,3,2) to third-cusp order five.
It merges those coordinates with unchanged ones. Full C_F(c)=1 rules out
this table as a canonical full c-word. Its role is to test which finite
conditions detect that failure.

For a full GT involution, A.36 additionally proves that the canonical u
satisfies the full pentagon. This extra hypothesis is not available for
an arbitrary GT0 involution. The present probe keeps that distinction.

2. Complete small-head classification, rather than arbitrary sextuples.

Use the standard pure generators
 (a,b,c,d,e,f)=(A12,A13,A23,A14,A24,A34).
The normal Fadell--Neuwirth free subgroup is generated by d,e,f. In an
epimorphism P4 -> A5 its image is normal, hence either 1 or A5.
If it is 1, the remaining P3 epimorphism has abc=1 and is one of the
19 generating-pair Aut(A5) orbits.

If the fiber image is A5, a,b,c are uniquely determined, if they exist,
by their conjugation on the generating triple d,e,f. The exact Artin
conditions require a^-1 to induce
 (d,e,f) -> (Ad(de)d, Ad(d)e, f),
b^-1 to induce
 (d,e,f) -> (Ad(df)d, Ad([d,f])e, Ad(d)f),
and c^-1 to induce
 (d,e,f) -> (d, Ad(ef)e, Ad(e)f).
Center-freeness gives uniqueness. The separate P3 relations are still
checked explicitly in the certificate, rather than inferred.

The complete finite reduction has 1,862 Aut(A5) orbits of triples, 1,668
of them generating, and exactly 76 of the latter admit the required
three inner actions. Together with the 19 trivial-fiber cases this gives
95 epimorphism orbits. Every one admits a lift to SL2(F5): the canonical
six matrix lifts satisfy the exact pure Artin relations. Changing any
lift by its central sign leaves those relations and every commutator-word
evaluation unchanged.

The checker verifies explicit factorization, not just 95=5*19. Exactly
19 epimorphisms satisfy each of the following mutually exclusive patterns:

 * forget strand 1: a=b=d=1 and cef=1;
 * forget strand 2: a=c=e=1 and bdf=1;
 * forget strand 3: b=c=f=1 and ade=1;
 * forget strand 4: d=e=f=1 and abc=1;
 * fill the sphere point at infinity:
       (a,b,c,d,e,f)=(f,(df)^-1,d,d,(fd)^-1,f).

The fifth is the standard spherical quotient on free generators d,f.
It is also verified directly from the Artin presentation. These are the
five forgetful F2 quotients of P4 modulo its center, namely PΓ0,5.

3. Consequence for all binary-icosahedral pentagons.

The five arguments of the pentagon are
 (a,ce), (bc,f), (c,f), (ab,ef), (a,c),
with the first two on the left and the last three on the right.
For every one of the 95 epimorphisms exactly two argument pairs generate
A5. When commutator-word values on all proper projected subgroups are 1,
the 95 equations reduce to 76 equalities of the same transported value
on opposite sides and 19 binary-duality equations on one side.
The script checks this for every allowed binary-duality coordinate value,
not merely for the particular false-positive table: 9,050 assignments.

The particular table with U_4=(0,2;2,0), U_10=(0,3;3,0), and all other
generating/proper values 1 passes every one of these finite pentagons.
It also passes the previously checked binary duality and finite c-norm.
There is no conclusion that it is a full canonical u. In particular a
finite pentagon test must not be promoted to the full P4 pentagon.

The finite evaluation table can be realized by some actual commutator
word, without asserting any full equation on that word. Indeed the joint
map to the 19 binary groups is onto SL2(F5)^19: the distinct A5 kernels
give the full A5 product, and a subgroup surjecting that quotient differs
from the binary product only by its center; perfectness then gives the
entire binary product. All proper binary subgroup images are solvable.
Their finite joint image has no common nontrivial quotient with the
perfect binary product, so Goursat gives a direct product. Taking
commutators allows the prescribed binary coordinates while killing all
proper-subgroup evaluations. This is a finite-word realization only;
full c-fixedness would force that word to be trivial.

4. Cohomological gate for the proposed 2^4:A5 extension.

Let pi:PΓ0,5 -> F2 be one of the five forgetful maps, with free rank-three
kernel K. The pure base acts trivially on K_ab. Let M=F2^4 be the nontrivial
simple A5 module, inflated through a fixed F2 -> A5 epimorphism. Then

 Hom_F2(K_ab,M)=0,
 H^1(F2,M) -> H^1(PΓ0,5,M) is an isomorphism.

The first assertion follows from the trivial base action and M^A5=0;
the second is inflation--restriction (or the split free-base extension).
Consequently every affine lift of the A5 epimorphism to M semidirect A5
still factors through the same forgetful F2 map. The P4 center also
vanishes in the lift: its image would lie in M and centralize the full
A5 image, hence in M^A5=0.

The same statement holds for a nonsplit extension of A5 by M. Its pullback
to the free base F2 splits. Comparing any PΓ0,5 lift with an inflated
lift gives a cocycle, whose restriction to K is zero by the displayed
calculation. Thus nonsplitting does not evade this particular gate.

More generally, for any abelian kernel module the image of K under a
lift lies in the invariant subgroup of the module, hence is central in
the image. The simple four-dimensional module therefore supplies no new
pentagon specialization over an epimorphic A5 head. A useful next test
must instead exploit the actual negative-hexagon/canonical-norm action
on its relative module, or a genuinely later nonabelian layer. This is
a reason to avoid a raw enumeration of the 960-element affine group.

5. Existing relative-dyadic rigidity, with attribution.

A.29 already proves, for every open normal U in the geometric
W=C2 coproduct C2 coproduct C2 contained in its free orientation kernel,
that the relative-dyadic quotient W/O^2(U) has exactly the three factor
involution classes, each with centralizer C2. In particular its even
fixed subgroup for c is trivial. Therefore the f=1 false-positive table
cannot extend coherently while retaining f=1 through every relative-two
layer above its joint A5 head. This is an application of A.29, not a new
centralizer theorem. It does not exclude a genuine negative parameter f
whose A5 value is 1 but whose subsequent module values are nontrivial.

The unresolved issue is exactly the compatibility of those nontrivial
corrections with the peripheral involution, the canonical norm, and the
correctly normalized third cusp. No main-A solution is claimed.
