# Research after A.49: irreducible chief modules and a new torsion-frame target

Status: research notes during checkpoint05 freeze. No additional formal conclusion or cofinal lifting theorem is asserted.

Write N=K_cong/R_nil(K_cong), so N is a normal pronilpotent subgroup of C=Pi/R. Its Sylow factors N_l are characteristic and normal in C. The retained positive h_C is identity modulo N_2, because it is identity on the common congruence quotient and on all odd relative components.

Let V be an irreducible finite F2-representation of a finite quotient of C. Normal N_2 acts trivially, by the standard normal-p-subgroup fixed-vector argument in characteristic p. Thus h_C acts trivially on the representation quotient. If some odd N_l acts nontrivially, then V^{N_l}=0 by irreducibility; coprime cohomology and Hochschild--Serre give H^j(C,V)=0 for every j. The same holds for the dual. This is the structural source of the H1 vanishing used concretely in A.49.

Conversely, if the entire N acts trivially, then a new irreducible residual image V cannot occur at an elementary-abelian layer above C: R=[R,K_cong] would give V=[V,q(K_cong)]=0. Thus a genuinely new irreducible characteristic-two layer must be sensitive to an odd pronilpotent factor. This does not prove an equivariant lift: the actual s-frame can still act nontrivially on H1(<s>,V), and the central refinement may have invariant commutator forms or further norm constraints.

A.49 had V restricted to <s> free over F2[C2], so H1(<s>,V)=0 and every s-lift was conjugate to pure. A next exact target with a nonzero torsion-frame space is the odd Heisenberg group of order7^3, extended by C6 acting on its two degree-one directions by primitive inverse characters. It is a quotient of the relative pro-seven part and hence belongs to C. The Schrödinger representation over F8 has dimension7, with s acting by j->-j on the seven basis indices. It has one H1(C2) direction over F8, rather than zero. The center acts by a primitive seventh root, and the order-three element permutes the nonzero indices in two triples. The two generator norm spaces have dimensions4 and4 over F8, while coboundaries have dimension7, suggesting H1(Pi,V) dimension1 over F8 once the actual head lifts are fixed.

Here retaining the prescribed s-frame can require a scalar correction on that nonzero torsion class. For the simplest Schrödinger representation the scalar group F8^× has odd order7, so its norm equation may still be harmless; this is not yet checked with the actual C-frame. A larger multiplicity or a different odd nilpotent representation may be required for a genuine two-primary correction. No existence or obstruction is claimed from these dimensions alone.

The parent's proposed distinction between a head on which h_C is trivial and one on which it is nontrivial must be retained carefully. Adding a dyadic head factor makes h_C nontrivial on the finite head, but an irreducible characteristic-two module kills its normal dyadic factor; merely adding that factor does not change the module test. The next useful step is therefore the actual torsion-frame/norm calculation, not a larger finite head without a new representation-theoretic effect.
