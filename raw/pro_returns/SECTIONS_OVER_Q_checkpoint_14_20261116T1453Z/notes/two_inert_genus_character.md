# Explicit genus characters and their exact limitation

This note supplements the completed A165/A168 calculations. It does not
exhaust the balanced characters in A155 and does not determine the balanced
class \(e_3\).

Put \(d=649\), \(\alpha^3=3\), \(K_n=\mathbb Q(\rho_n)\) with
\(\rho_n^{3^n}=3d^{24}\), and \(E_n=K_n(\zeta)\), where
\(\zeta=\zeta_3\). Write \(F=E_1=\mathbb Q(\alpha,\zeta)\).
The involution \(\tau\) fixes \(K_n\) and inverts \(\zeta\).

## The literal radical and cubic polynomial

Set
\[
 u_{11}=\frac{2+\zeta\alpha}{2+\zeta^2\alpha},\qquad
 u_{59}=\frac{2+2\zeta\alpha-\zeta^2\alpha^2}
              {2+2\zeta^2\alpha-\zeta\alpha^2}.
\]
The identity \((2+x)(2+2x-x^2)=1+6x\) at \(x^3=3\) gives
\[
 u=u_{11}u_{59}=\frac{1+6\zeta\alpha}{1+6\zeta^2\alpha}.
\]
All three radicals satisfy \(\tau(u_i)=u_i^{-1}\).
If \(r^3=u\), extend \(\tau\) by \(\tau(r)=r^{-1}\).
This involution commutes with the Kummer automorphism \(r\mapsto\zeta r\).
Thus its fixed field is an actual cyclic cubic extension of \(K_1\).
It is generated by \(s=r+r^{-1}\), with equation
\[
 s^3-3s-\frac{2-12\alpha-36\alpha^2}{1-6\alpha+36\alpha^2}=0.
\]
Its discriminant is the literal square
\[
 \left(\frac{108\alpha(1-3\alpha)}
                   {1-6\alpha+36\alpha^2}\right)^2.
\]
This polynomial and the specified involution provide the field map;
the square-discriminant calculation is a separate check.

## The exact local calculation at three

Now complete \(F\) at its unique three-prime and normalize its valuation
by \(v_F(3)=6\). Then \(v_F(\alpha)=2\),
\(v_F(\lambda)=3\) for \(\lambda=\zeta-1\), and
\(\alpha^2/\lambda\) is a uniformizer. Direct subtraction gives
\[
 v_F(u_{11}-1)=v_F(u_{59}-1)=5,\qquad v_F(u-1)=11.
\]
The depth-eleven unit is a cube: write \(u=1+C\), put \(c=C/3\), and
use \(v_F(c)=5\). At \(1+c\), the polynomial \(X^3-u\) has residual
\(3c^2+c^3\) of valuation fifteen and derivative of valuation six.
The strict inequality \(15>12\) is the one-variable Hensel criterion.

An explicit representative of the unramified cubic Kummer line is
\(1+\lambda^3\). Indeed
\[
 \frac{(1+\lambda Z)^3-1}{\lambda^3}
 =Z^3-\zeta^2\lambda Z^2-\zeta^2Z.
\]
Equating this to one has reduction \(Z^3-Z-1\), irreducible and
separable over \(\mathbb F_3\). It therefore gives the unramified
cubic extension. The representative has depth nine.

A unit cube cannot have depth five. For \(v_F(b-1)=k\), the expansion
\(b^3-1=3(b-1)+3(b-1)^2+(b-1)^3\) has depth \(3k\) for \(k<3\),
depth at least nine for \(k=3\), and depth \(6+k\) for \(k>3\).
A cube with residue one has a cube root with residue one.
Multiplication by a power of the depth-nine unramified representative
cannot alter a depth-five leading term. Consequently the span of
\(u_{11},u_{59}\) meets the unramified local Kummer line only in its
zero class: its only zero combinations are the equal-exponent ones,
because \(u_{11}u_{59}\) is a cube.

## Why a higher pure level does not remove this ramification

The full splitting field has the actual holomorph group established
in A155. Above \(F\), write
\[
 J=(3\mathbb Z/3^n)\rtimes U_1,\qquad
 U_1=\{v\in(\mathbb Z/3^n)^\times:v\equiv1\pmod3\}.
\]
The subgroup fixing \(E_n\) is \(H=U_1\).
The normal closure of \(H\) in \(J\) is
\((9\mathbb Z/3^n)\rtimes U_1\): conjugation by translation three
produces \(3(1-v)\), and \(v=4\) produces a generator of
\(9\mathbb Z/3^n\). Hence the largest Galois subextension of
\(E_n/F\) is \(E_2/F\), of degree three.

The same calculation is valid in the local splitting field at three.
That splitting field is totally ramified, so it is disjoint from the
unramified cubic extension of \(F\). In their compositum the group is
the direct product with an extra \(C_3\). It follows from the same
normal-closure calculation that every cyclic cubic \(F\)-extension
which becomes unramified after adjoining \(E_n\) has Kummer class in
the span of the \(E_2/F\) radical and the unramified Kummer line.
The first radical has valuation two modulo three.
The \(u_i\) are units; their span can therefore meet that two-dimensional
space only along the unramified line, already treated above.

Thus among precisely these two candidate radicals, the combinations
unramified at three at any higher level are the equal-exponent ones.
This assertion concerns this specified two-dimensional global span.

## An explicit unramified cyclic class quotient

At each of eleven and fifty-nine, the three primes of \(F\) have
valuation vector \(0,1,-1\) on \(u\), in an order fixed by the displayed
conjugate factors. The \(E_2/F\) radical has valuation two at all
three primes. Thus \(u\) is not in its Kummer line.
The global normal-closure calculation above proves that \(u\) is not
a cube in any \(E_n\). The cyclic cubic field obtained above remains
nontrivial after every base change to \(K_n\).

For \(n\ge2\), all changed-prime valuations of \(u\) in \(E_n\) are
divisible by three, because those branches are totally ramified of
degree \(3^{n-1}\) over \(F\). At a place of residue characteristic
different from three, a unit cubic Kummer extension is unramified.
At three the radical is already a cube in \(F\).
At all other finite primes its valuation is zero.
It follows that the resulting cyclic cubic extension of \(K_n\)
is unramified everywhere.

At every two-prime the expression \(u=(1+6\zeta\alpha)/
(1+6\zeta^2\alpha)\) is one modulo two. The cube map is an
automorphism on the principal units, so these places split.
In particular the resulting ordinary class character annihilates
the distinguished two-prime.

At the changed linear primes, \(\alpha\equiv-2\pmod{11}\) and
\(\alpha\equiv-1/6\pmod{59}\). Both give \(u\equiv-\zeta\).
Over \(E_n\), the cubic residues are respectively
\(\zeta^{40}=\zeta\) and \(\zeta^{1160}=\zeta^2\).
The residue degree of \(E_n/K_n\) at these linear primes is two.
Consequently the arithmetic Frobenius values over \(K_n\), in the
generator \(r\mapsto\zeta r\), have exponents two and one.
This explicitly retains the restriction-of-Frobenius map.

At level two the quadratic changed primes are principal by the
literal \(\sigma_{11},\sigma_{59}\) in A165, so their character values
are zero. At every higher level the character is pulled back by ideal
norm; those branches have residue-degree-one norm maps and retain zero.
Its nonzero linear values therefore violate the balanced relation
\([P_{q,2}]-2[P_{q,0}]\). This explicit ordinary class quotient does
not factor through the balanced quotient of A155.
