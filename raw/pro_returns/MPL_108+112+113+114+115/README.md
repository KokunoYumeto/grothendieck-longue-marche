## Transcription and verification boundaries

The sole transcriber read every supplied whole-page image and both halves, completed a second visual audit, reconciled the shared notation, and froze the transcription before opening context contents. The forty page fragments and combined fragment retain their original frozen SHA-256 values. Later research interpretations, objections and suggested readings appear only in the notes; they do not revise the transcription.

The LaTeX is intentionally a fragment using the requested editorial macros, together with standard mathematical LaTeX and TikZ/tikz-cd. A local harness checked syntax with an underline fallback and without final rendering; this is not a claim of final typographic verification. The structural checks verify order, markers, braces, inventory coverage, exact concatenation and the frozen hashes. The quotation checks establish literal presence of the cited cues, not the correctness of every mathematical interpretation.

The supplied scans, archives and context sources are excluded from this ZIP. The notes cite them by their supplied filenames and manuscript or scan locators; the work records preserve the comparisons and searches needed to follow the research. Primary external references are linked in the research file. The two chronology questions and the exact availability limit for STRATA C.1 are answered explicitly there.
