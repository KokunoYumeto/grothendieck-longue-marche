### Primary external references opened after freezing

**[PS] Alexander Grothendieck, *Pursuing Stacks*.** The typed edition at [arXiv:2111.01000](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.01000), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01000), was consulted after the scan transcription was frozen. Particularly relevant are §§42–43 (original ms 74–78, PDF indices 116–120), on ordered-set modelizers and the finite-subset weak-test candidate; §81 (original ms 260–261, PDF index 265) defining Loc4 by compatibility with π₀; §95 Proposition3 (ms 346–347, PDF index 331) on nontrivial contractors; and §95 Proposition4 with the opening of §96 (ms 349–351, PDF indices 332–334). The retained Proposition4 is not the proposition printed on supplied114/004, so this edition is comparison evidence, never a replacement source for that scan. The supplied edition identifies its transcription as CC0.

**[Ara] Dimitri Ara, *The groupoidal analogue \(\widetilde\Theta\) to Joyal's category \(\Theta\) is a test category*.** [Author-hosted PDF](https://www.i2m.univ-amu.fr/perso/dimitri.ara/files/thetatld.pdf), §§4.4–4.6, printed pp. 15–16: definitions of weak, local, test and strict test categories, and the model-structure statement. These definitions are the external basis for the elementary initial-object check in R10808; the proof itself is given in that note and is credited as verification of Hua's lead.

**[Cavallo–Sattler] Evan Cavallo and Christian Sattler, *Relative elegance and Cartesian cubes with one connection*.** [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X25101466), Definitions 7.9, 7.11 and 7.12, with Remark 7.10 specifying the ordinary weak-homotopy localizer. These supply the direct asphericity definition used in the independent initial-object verification recorded in `work_records/research_secondary_review.md`; the proof is an elementary application of those definitions.

**[Cisinski] Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Les préfaisceaux comme modèles des types d'homotopie*, Astérisque 308 (2006).** [Publisher record](https://smf.emath.fr/publications/les-prefaisceaux-comme-modeles-des-types-dhomotopie), [Numdam bibliographic record](https://www.numdam.org/item/?id=AST_2006__308__R1_0), [primary PDF hosted by Georges Maltsiniotis](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~georges.maltsiniotis/ps/ast.pdf). Remark 8.4.33, printed p. 308 in that PDF (PDF index 331), explicitly warns that Cartesian products of cubical sets need not preserve weak equivalences and gives the product of two one-cubes as a non-simply-connected example. R11414 combines that verified example with an explicit connectedness argument and the independently checked PS Loc4 definition; the cubical convention is stated there. The separate Corollary 4.2.22 citation in a known-after-all notice is retained as supplied credit, not claimed to have been verified in this external check.

**[Hubert] Léo Hubert, *Homotopical Dold–Kan Correspondences* (2025).** [arXiv:2505.08321](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08321), version 1, submitted 13 May 2025; [Aix–Marseille doctoral record](https://college-doctoral.univ-amu.fr/soutenance/674). The abstract states a homology functor from abelian presheaves to nonnegative derived abelian groups, homology model structures for Whitehead local test categories, and a derived Dold–Kan equivalence for Whitehead test categories. R11205 uses this as a route to compare hypotheses; neither the abstract nor this note is asserted to settle the manuscript's precise twisted-coefficient or Δ/X question.

**[Saorin-Stovicek] Manuel Saorín and Jan Šťovíček, *t-Structures with Grothendieck hearts via functor categories*, Selecta Mathematica 29, article 77 (2023).** [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00029-023-00872-9), §5 opening: the definition there permits a set of small projective generators, and the following paragraph explicitly distinguishes Grothendieck categories with enough projectives from these module categories. This checks the many-object scope relevant to R11304.

**[Bazzoni] Silvana Bazzoni, Ivo Herzog, Pavel Příhoda, Jan Šaroch and Jan Trlifaj, *Pure Projective Tilting Modules*, Documenta Mathematica 25 (2020),401–424.** [Primary PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/26531), p. 412 before Corollary 4.5, Corollary 4.5, Proposition 6.2, Theorem 6.3 and the example on pp. 420–421. These provide the original counterexample used in R11304; the separate secondary-review report checks why a whole family of compact projectives, not merely one compact generator, is excluded.

**[Spaltenstein] Nicolas Spaltenstein, *Resolutions of unbounded complexes*, Compositio Mathematica 65 (1988),121–154.** [Primary PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1988__65_2_121_0.pdf), §5, especially Propositions 5.6–5.8 on K-flat resolutions and K-projectivity. **[Stacks]** [The Stacks Project, tag 06Y7](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06Y7), Definition 20.26.2 and Lemmas 20.26.3, 20.26.9: K-flatness, preservation of quasi-isomorphisms, and the bounded-above case. These are the modern scope references for R11326, not hypotheses inserted into the manuscript.
