# Legibility note — scans 026–033

This note summarizes the adjudicated records for manuscript pages 25–32. It is based on those records and the working notation table, without a new image reading. It incorporates the recorded main-reader decisions and all eight completed blind adjudications for026–033. Completion is documented in the audit records and progress log; this note adds no image decision.

The main live text and most formula structures are sufficiently legible for a detailed diplomatic transcription. The records identify repeated ordinary-capital forms, separate primes, different order signs and a phi-shaped empty sign. Difficulty is concentrated in flattened word interiors, very small raised marks, page-edge clipping, and places where deletion strokes, an insertion or an older word overlap newer writing. Legibility therefore varies within each page; a clear formula does not make its cancelled predecessor equally recoverable.

**Relative witnesses.** C often preserves abbreviated or syntactically awkward wording that H expands, smooths or replaces: examples include the 029 compatibility sentence without a supplied sont, the 031 compressed élément/seult forms, and 032 can., tjrs and the unchanged article Le. H remains a useful comparison witness. It retains the v in 028 d'équiv., keeps the opening sentence of that page outside the broad cancellation, and correctly reads the restated cancelled universal quantifier on 031. C also supplies an extra cancelled On at the 032 proof heading, where the adjudication finds only one live token. Both witnesses replace the literal empty-set glyph by a conventional empty-set symbol, and both omit some graphical strokes. These examples support local comparison, not a numerical accuracy ranking. H's explicit editorial descriptions of large crosses are acknowledged evidence, even when its macros arrange the state differently. [026–032]

**The hardest surviving places** are the 026 slanted note, compressed adverbs and clipped final formula; the 027 small cancelled proof note and bottom identifier; the 028 overdrawn capitals and tiny lower indices; the 029 cancelled warning word, phrase and footer overtrace; the 030 clipped G/L, short left-note label and hatched abandoned term; the 031 thick superscript and densely cancelled lower attempt; and the 032 crowded oblique note and overwritten identité beneath inclusion. The broad curve through the last note has an unresolved function as well as difficult crossings with the text; the preferred live note carries an explicit whole-note cancellation alternative. The newly recorded032 pr./pro. start and str.te-like form also remain locally uncertain. Several clear mathematical mismatches and grammatical irregularities remain literal because the records distinguish an unexpected reading from an illegible one. [026–032]

The unresolved alternatives are substantive limits of the present transcription. A mark retained as doubtful should not be described as fully deciphered, and the records' local mathematical checks do not certify the arguments or remove those palaeographic limits. References [026–032] are the corresponding `scan026_adjudicated.json` through `scan032_adjudicated.json` files in this review directory; the detailed evidence pointers are listed in `hand_synthesis.md`.
