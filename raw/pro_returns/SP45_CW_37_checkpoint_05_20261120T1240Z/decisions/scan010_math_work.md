This is a limited check of item 1 using the conditions on this page, not a proof of the whole exercise and not a reason to normalize any glyph.

Let the proposed covering families be the covering families of S whose arrows lie in the class denoted by the underlined M. Identities lie in the class by (b). Isomorphisms are base changes of identities, so (a) includes them. Pullbacks of a proposed family exist because its arrows are quarrable by (a); its pulled-back arrows remain in the class by (a), and the family remains covering by the site axioms. A composite of two such covering families remains covering and has arrows in the class by (b). These are the pretopology closure conditions. Every proposed family was already covering in S, and every covering family of S has a refinement of this kind by (d); hence the generated topology is the original topology. Condition (c) is not needed for this limited check of item 1.

The type direction in item 2 is also intelligible from (a): for u:X→Y, an object Z→Y of S(Y) pulls back to Z×_Y X→X in S(X). This only checks that the object assignment and displayed direction are well typed; it does not independently prove the full continuity claim or the equivalence begun in item 3.
