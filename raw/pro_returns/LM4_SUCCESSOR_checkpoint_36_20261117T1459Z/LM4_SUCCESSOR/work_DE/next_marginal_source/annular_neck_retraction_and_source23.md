## 1. Literal common-neck subgroup and free-basis change

At the certified inner base $q_b=4/5+i/2$, the actual first-neck disk
subgroup is the subgroup generated by the original five ordinary
comb meridians $x_1,x_3,x_4,x_5,x_6$. In the common q0-adapted basis

\[
a=Y_1,\ b=Y_2,\ C=Y_3,\ f=Y_4,\ d=Y_5,\ e=Y_6,
\qquad \bar x=CxC,
\]

the exact table is

\[
\begin{aligned}
x_1&=a\bar b a^{-1},&x_3&=a,\\
x_4&=f d e d^{-1}f^{-1},&x_5&=d,\\
x_6&=d^{-1}fd.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]

It follows, with literal inverse words, that

\[
K=\langle a,\bar b,d,e,f\rangle,
\quad
\begin{aligned}
a&=x_3,&\bar b&=x_3^{-1}x_1x_3,&d&=x_5,\\
f&=x_5x_6x_5^{-1},&
e&=x_6^{-1}x_5^{-1}x_4x_5x_6.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

Both compositions of (1)--(2) are identities in an abstract free
group of rank five. The separate checker also substitutes (1) into
the actual full six-generator old-to-Y dictionary, reducing only
the cone square, and obtains all five literal recorded words.

The already proved actual annular ordinary detector $D$ sends

\[
(a,\bar b,d,e,f)\longmapsto(X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4,H).
\]

Thus the homomorphism from the abstract free group on this neck
basis into the actual vertex is injective: its composite with $D$
is the identity basis map. Identifying the target free group with
this actual subgroup gives a retraction

\[
r_-:R_-^0\longrightarrow K,\qquad r_-|_K=1_K.
\tag{3}
\]

In particular $K$ has its full induced profinite topology in the
ordinary vertex, and hence in the orbifold vertex by finite-index
cores. This conclusion uses the complete annular detector, including
all node and q0 mapping relations, not its slit restriction.

For the q0-adapted cone $C$, the induced endomorphism on $K$ is

\[
r_-\operatorname{Inn}(C)|_K:
(a,\bar b,d,e,f)\longmapsto(1,1,1,1,f).
\tag{4}
\]

It is not zero. The original comb cone is instead

\[
c_{\rm old}=P C P^{-1},\qquad P=a\bar b^{-1}\in K.
\tag{5}
\]

This identity is also checked literally. Changing from $C$ to this
cone conjugates the endomorphism (4) accordingly. Neither cone
choice is silently substituted for an independently chosen
boundary-gap cone in a later pushout.

## 2. Actual transport to the neck

The inner computational marking is the one used in B110. Move from
$q_b$ radially to a point just inside the unit circle on the same
ray, then use the regular first neck and the first open unit-circle

nodes cannot occur there, and the remaining collision factors have
no zero before the old unit-circle punctures. Within the first arc

are distinct. The stronger upper-y assertion is used only on the short bridge ending at
$\exp(i\pi/5)$, or at $(4+3i)/5$ for the later calibration: there
$\operatorname{Im}Q\geq3$ and
$3(1-\cos(2\pi/5))-\sin(2\pi/5)>1$. It is not asserted

disk, its five meridian arcs, and its common connector together.

Define B108's allowed first-neck disk and its basis by this transport.

## 4. Actual source topology over two annuli and one neck

Let $U$ be the union of the full inner annulus, the full outer annulus,
and one sufficiently small first-neck disk, avoiding the old unit
punctures. The two enlarged annular regions intersect in one
contractible set. Van Kampen gives

\[
\pi_1(U)\cong\mathbb Z*\mathbb Z=F_2.
\]

Choose the inner generator $a_0$ on a small circle about zero and
the outer generator $b_0$ on a circle of radius strictly between
$1$ and the smallest modulus of the four outer source contact
points. The chosen connectors run through the one neck.

The selected quotient source is

\[
D_{0,U}\cong U\setminus\{\alpha_u:u\in\mu_5\setminus\{1\}\}.
\]

Its inner piece is an annulus, and its outer piece is an annulus
with four points removed, with the same contractible overlap.
Consequently its abstract source group is

\[
\pi_1(D_{0,U})\cong F_1*F_5=F_6,
\]

with geometric basis $a_0,b_0,p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4$, where the $p_i$
are the four actual outer contact lassos. This is a topological
statement about the source; it does not yet claim injection of its
image into the full one-neck ambient group.

The actual original selected source cover adjoins $\sqrt{1-q}$.
Its character is zero on $a_0$ and the $p_i$, and one on $b_0$:
the outer circle encloses $q=1$, while the inner circle and small
contact lassos do not. The source cover is therefore connected.
Its rank is $2(6-1)+1=11$. An explicit Schreier basis, using
representatives $1,b_0$, is

\[
a_0,p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4,\quad b_0^2,\quad
b_0a_0b_0^{-1},\ b_0p_i b_0^{-1}\ (1\le i\le4).
\]

The component containing the original basepoint is thus the entire
connected selected source cover. The literal lift is the original
$F$ fixing that basepoint and the selected source, not $F\sigma$.
If a later actual ambient comparison proves the quotient-source
fixed equality, the source character is surjective here, so B106's
two-coset transfer is applicable. This differs from B110's paired
slit model, where the source cover had two components. No such
ambient equality is inferred from these rank calculations alone.
