## Mathematical qualifications retained

The unrestricted prime in the printed centerless construction cannot be used for a noncyclic finite p-group when the chosen prime is p: every constructed subgroup and quotient remains a finite p-group, and the nontrivial final quotient still mapping onto the original group cannot be centerless. Quotienting by a center also does not generally produce a centerless group. The later embedding into the product of peripheral quotients requires a separate normal-closure comparison; we do not import it without proof. These qualifications concern the indicated proof steps and do not refute the genus-zero congruence theorem.

The amended audit supplies two independent replacements for precisely the four-punctured-sphere input. The compact proof normalizes a primitive peripheral automorphism, kills that peripheral element, and uses the exact marked Birman inner-action identity; continuity follows from a compact quotient and requires no continuous choice of representatives. The finite proof chooses a prime coprime to the original finite quotient, uses its relation module and averaging to obtain a centerless refinement, and then uses one actual lamp target followed by characteristic refinement. The latter avoids the product-normal-closure step entirely.

For the cover argument we retain the explicit characteristic index-27 starting cover with all four peripheral orders three. Its 36 punctures and Euler characteristic -54 give compact genus ten. Further covers therefore avoid the overbroad low-genus assertion about arbitrary ramified four-punctured-sphere covers.

These primary investigations remain useful research history. The final actual compact-L proof instead uses the exact correction `q_s = delta(h_s)` and its marked free-basis isomorphism.
