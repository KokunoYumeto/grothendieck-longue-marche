# Explicit local scalar cover for the next ordinary cup

Research note after the ninth formal-paper freeze. This note proves a local finite-group construction. Its actual source attainment, compatibility with the selected full action, and normalizer equations are separate tasks.

## The actual group and cocycle

Let k=F8, with X^3+X+1, and let Tr:k to F2 be its absolute trace. On k^2 define

    (a,z)(b,w)=(a+b,z+w+a^2 b).

The second-coordinate function is F2-bilinear, so this is an associative group S of order64. Its square is (0,a^3), and its commutator is (0,a^2 b+b^2 a). In particular its center and derived subgroup are the eight elements with first coordinate0, and S/Phi(S) is the additive k.

Consider the k-valued normalized cochain

    f((a,z),(b,w))=z b^4+a^2 w^4.

It is an actual two-cocycle. In the cocycle equation on ((a,z),(b,w),(c,v)), all terms cancel except

    a^2 b c^4 + a^2 (b^2 c)^4,

which is zero because b^8=b in k. Thus the central extension k times_f S is an associative group of order512.

For every nonzero d in k, the map (a,z) to (d a,d^3 z) is an automorphism of S. The two terms of f acquire factors d^7 and d^14, respectively, so f is invariant. This is a strict C7 action on the extension, acting identically on its central k.

## Removing the one trivial scalar direction

The absolute trace of f is a coboundary:

    Tr(f)=delta q,   q(a,z)=Tr(a^4 z)+a^7.

Here a^7 is0at a=0 and1otherwise, regarded as an F2-value. Expanding delta Tr(a^4 z) leaves the extra trace Tr(a^6 b+a^2 b^5). For a,b,a+b all nonzero this is1, and otherwise it is0; this equals delta(a^7). One may verify the trace assertion on the six nonzero ratios b/a other than1, or by the polynomial identities in k. The attached program checks every pair independently.

Since Tr(1)=1, the map pi(t)=t+Tr(t) identifies k/(F2*1) with L=ker(Tr), a group of order4. Quotienting the central extension by F2*1 gives the explicit group

    G={(a,z,t):a,z in k, t in L},
    (a,z,t)(b,w,u)=(a+b,z+w+a^2b,t+u+pi(z b^4+a^2w^4)).

Thus |G|=256. The triple commutator on the lifted old central subgroup is exactly

    [(0,z,0),(a,0,0)]=(0,0,z a^4+a^2 z^4).

Its value has trace0: the traces of its two terms are equal by Frobenius invariance. For a=1, the image z+z^4 is already L, because its kernel is F2 and it has dimension2. Consequently the central L is generated by actual third commutators. The center is exactly L, the derived group consists of all (0,z,t) and has order32, and the lower central orders are256,32,4,1. The Frattini quotient has order8, equal to the additive first-coordinate k. These assertions are also verified from the exact multiplication, without using classification.

The three scalar components of f span a two-dimensional cohomology subspace. We independently check this by enumerating all nine C7-orbits of nonidentity elements of S. Their invariant one-cochains have coboundary rank9, and adjoining the three components of f raises the rank by2. The sole nonzero linear functional on k making f a coboundary is Tr. Averaging over C7 shows that an invariant scalar cocycle which is an unrestricted coboundary is also an invariant coboundary, so this rank test is not confined to a smaller artificial notion of equivalence. This proves the displayed two independent classes, without asserting that they exhaust every local cohomology class.

## Literature identification and exact source scope

The local base is a known Suzuki2-group. In Eaton's (2024) Section2, page3, the type-A multiplication is (alpha,beta)(gamma,delta)=(alpha+gamma,beta+delta+alpha theta(gamma)). With theta(t)=t^4, the substitution (a,z) to (a^2,z) identifies our law with that definition: a^2 (b^2)^4=a^2b. The same paragraph identifies this type at order64 with the Sylow two-group of Sz(8). We use only this explicit definition and comparison, not the block-theoretic classification or subsequent automorphism claims.

The primary ATLAS entry records the Schur multiplier of Sz(8) as C2^2 and provides covering-group presentations. This is consistent with the two scalar directions found here, but we do not infer our cohomology calculation or a source lift from that consistency. The explicit cocycle is proved and tested above.

A MathOverflow answer on capability of Suzuki2-groups supplied a useful formula to compare. We transported its coordinate convention and proved the resulting formula directly; the answer is not used as theorem authority. Higman's original1963paper was sought at its publisher. The retrieved pages displayed an access iframe, so its exact proof was not inspected and is not marked verified.
