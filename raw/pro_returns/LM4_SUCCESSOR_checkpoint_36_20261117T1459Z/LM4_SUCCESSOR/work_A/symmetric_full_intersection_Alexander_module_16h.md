## Geometry supplying an actual free presentation

The universal cover of the filled bundle X maps to the universal cover
Btilde of its base. It is the fiberwise universal cover of the pullback
bundle: its fiber is the universal cover of the six-punctured sphere,
a complex disk. Every lift of an A or B component is a section over Btilde,
because the corresponding original divisor is a section over B.

Away from the lifts of the eighteen mixed critical values the symmetric
complement is a genuine bundle. Its generic fiber is a disk with locally
finite punctures indexed by all lifted A and B components. Each family
alone has no collisions. Each mixed collision is a transverse node, with
two sections crossing and no branch interchange. At the actual generic
basepoint let a_C and b_D be meridians of those punctures.

The proposed exact van Kampen presentation is

    F=Free({a_C}) * Free({b_D}) -> K_s,
    K_s=F/L,

where L is the normal closure of the actual transported node commutators.
Each such word is [u a_C u^-1, v b_D v^-1] in this same fiber marking.
The transports u,v must be retained; replacing them by1 would be an
unjustified RAAG assertion.

Here is the intended justification of surjectivity and completeness.
Over the punctured simply connected base Btilde, the fundamental group is
generated by the fiber and lifted base lassos. Around each critical value,
a local section through a regular fiber point extends across that value.
Its lasso is killed on filling, eliminating the corresponding base generator
up to a fiber word. The remaining local monodromy is the product of full
twists at the disjoint mixed nodes, and its variation ideal is exactly the
normal closure of their transported commutators. There are no other critical
values. Any loop, nullhomotopy, or relation uses a compact subset of the
cover, so it meets only finitely many local nodal charts; the finite
van Kampen calculations are compatible and give the stated presentation.
This compact-exhaustion point must be audited carefully; it is not a claim
that a nonproper map is automatically a bundle at infinity.

Filling one family identifies the two free factors with R_A and R_B,
respectively. Indeed the corresponding one-sided cover is a bundle over
the contractible Btilde, with exactly that punctured-disk fiber. Thus all
node relators belong to

    N=ker(F -> R_A times R_B),

and M=N/L. This uses the actual fiber maps, not arbitrary abstract sections
of a normal-subgroup quotient.

## The full module and its exact chain model

Let T_A,T_B be the Cayley trees of the displayed free bases of R_A,R_B.
The covering of the wedge of their two roses with deck group E is the
one-skeleton of T_A times T_B. Its fundamental group is N. The product
of trees is contractible, so its cellular boundary gives an E-equivariant
isomorphism

    N_ab = C_2(T_A times T_B,Z)
         = I(R_A) tensor_Z I(R_B)
         = direct_sum_(C,D) Z[E] e_(C,D).

Here I denotes the integral augmentation ideal. Therefore the proposed
actual full Alexander module is

    M_ab = (direct_sum_(C,D) Z[E] e_(C,D))
            / < z_r : r is an actual transported node relator >_(Z[E]). (1)

The coefficient z_r is exact, not just its augmentation. It can be computed
from the Fox derivatives of the actual word r after mapping F->E. With
left-module edge conventions,

    d_2 e_(a,b) = (1-b)e_a + (a-1)e_b.

For r in N, its Fox derivative vector is a one-cycle in the product-tree
one-skeleton, and z_r is the UNIQUE finite two-chain having that boundary.
Uniqueness follows because d_2 is injective. Finite computation is possible
for a specified transported word, but no full coefficient matrix or module
membership calculation has been executed here.

The filling quotient actions recover the already proved first module by
taking E-coinvariants: each transported node coefficient reduces to the
single positive tensor of its two lifted components. This consistency
check does not determine the full coefficients in (1).

## Involution and next exact test

The chosen lifted F fixes the generic fiber basepoint and exchanges its
A/B punctures. In disk uniformization it is an elliptic involution; one
may choose paired meridian paths from the quotient orbifold so that F
exchanges the two free bases. On the product-tree C_2 module it acts
semilinearly by negative swap, together with swapping the E factors.
The actual transported relator submodule is stable, because F permutes
actual nodal charts. If a different fiber marking is used, its actual
Artin transport must replace this convenient description.

For a specified fixed target in E, choose its actual word lift x in F.
The word x^-1 F(x) lies in N. Its unique product-tree two-chain, modulo
(1), is the actual first full-M_ab cocycle. A nonzero class in
H^1(C2,M_ab) induced this way would obstruct lifting that target through
the actual abelianized intersection extension. Vanishing would still leave
the nonlinear kernel [M,M]. No action, class, or vanishing has yet been
computed at this stronger level.

This is deliberately different from repeating M/[M,K_s]. It retains all
R_A times R_B coefficients and every transported node whisker. It assumes
neither a RAAG presentation nor asphericity of the symmetric complement Z.

## Exact unrestricted Tate module and defect convention

Identify R_A with R_B by F and call it R. Put J=I_Z[R]. The unrestricted
module N_ab is J tensor J with F(v tensor w)=-w tensor v. The abelian
basis {r-1:r!=1} decomposes this tensor into negative diagonal singleton
orbits and two-element signed orbits. Hence, naturally for the diagonal
left R action,

    H^1(C2,N_ab) = J/2J = I_F2[R],
    H^2(C2,N_ab) = 0.

The first map takes the class of v tensor v to v modulo2; cross terms
are coboundaries, so this is additive and basis independent.

We consistently use the RIGHT defect x^-1 F(x). For the canonical free
lift x=a(r)F(a(r)) of (r,F(r)), its exact word is

    F(a)^-1 a^-1 F(a) a.

With the stated left-edge convention its unique product-tree chain is

    -(a^-1,F(a)^-1) * (D_A(a) tensor D_B(F(a))).

Thus its unrestricted Tate class is r^-1-1, NOT r-1. The inverse
crossed rule is delta(rs)=s^-1 delta(r)+delta(s). The left-defect
convention would give r-1 after the corresponding diagonal transport;
we do not switch conventions mid-proof.

Let Lbar be the exact transported node submodule in N_ab and set

    U=im[H^1(C2,Lbar)->H^1(C2,N_ab)]=a left R-submodule of I_F2[R].

Exactness gives the precise fixed-lift criterion in K_s/[M,M]:
the target (r,F(r)) has an F-fixed lift iff r^-1-1 belongs to U.
This set contains the actual source kernel I. Consequently U contains
the left ideal generated by i-1 for i in I. Equality with that ideal
would identify the projected fixed subgroup at this stronger quotient
with the actual source, but that equality is NOT proved.
