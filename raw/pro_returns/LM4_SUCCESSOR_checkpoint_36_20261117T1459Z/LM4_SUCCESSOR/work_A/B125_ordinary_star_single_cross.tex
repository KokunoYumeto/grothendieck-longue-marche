\subsection*{The actual ordinary star and one remaining cross relation}
\statement{B.125}{Proved}{The pre-cross ordinary cover is a proper full profinite star amalgam, and the actual cross filling is one specified commutator quotient}
\label{stmt:B125}
Use the actual B.112--B.114 regional marking
\[
 R,\quad R^0=\ker\varepsilon_R,\quad K=F(a,B,d,e,f),\quad
 B=CbC,\quad h=fk,\quad c=aCa^{-1}.
\]
Here $\varepsilon_R(C)=1$, every generator of $K$ and $k$ has
ordinary parity zero, and $\eta$ is the actual based neck reflection.
Put $Q=K\rtimes_\eta\langle j\rangle$, with $j^2=1$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-pre-cross}
 A=R*_{K}Q,\qquad
 X=A/\langle\!\langle[jcj,k]\rangle\!\rangle.
\end{equation}
Thus $X$ is the actual final mirror extension of B.114 and B.121;
all regional relations and all five neck-prefix inversions are already
present in $A$. Extend $\varepsilon_R$ to $\varepsilon:A\to C_2$
by killing $Q$, and write $A^0=\ker\varepsilon$.
Let $j_0=j$, $j_1=CjC$, and $K_1=CKC$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-star}
 A^0=Q_0*_{K}R^0*_{K_1}Q_1,
 \qquad Q_0=Q,\quad Q_1=CQC.
\end{equation}
The two edge embeddings and all three vertex embeddings induce their
full profinite topologies, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-completed-star}
 \widehat{A^0}
 =\widehat Q_0\amalg_{\widehat K}\widehat{R^0}
       \amalg_{\widehat K_1}\widehat Q_1
\end{equation}
is a proper full profinite star amalgam.

Define the actual words
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-single-words}
 \begin{gathered}
 a_1=CaC,\quad k_1=CkC,\quad
 t_0=j_0a a_1^{-1}j_1,\\
 j'_0=a^{-1}j_0a,\quad j'_1=a_1^{-1}j_1a_1,\quad
 g=j'_0j'_1,\quad z_*=gh.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The original-parity character descends to $X$, and its kernel has the
exact presentation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-final-single}
 X^0=A^0/\langle\!\langle[z_*,k]\rangle\!\rangle_{A^0}.
\end{equation}
Here $z_*$ is the entire existing word in
\eqref{eq:B125-single-words}, not a new free stable letter.
The selected opposite cone is $\tau=jcj=t_0C$.
The five exact selected regional source words $s_1,\ldots,s_5$
of B.120 give the actual six source prelifts
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-source-prelifts}
 j_0s_1j_0,\ldots,j_0s_5j_0,k.
\end{equation}
Their images are the actual source words after the cross filling.
The mirror character $\mu$ takes $j_0,j_1$ to $1$ and kills $R^0$;
it is distinct from $\varepsilon$. The actual ordinary one-neck
group is $X^0\cap\ker\mu$, and all words in
\eqref{eq:B125-source-prelifts} lie in it.
No vertex survival or selected-centralizer equality after
\eqref{eq:B125-final-single} is asserted.

\begin{proof}
\emph{B.125.1. The actual index-two star.}
The marked neck $K$ embeds in both $R$ and $Q$, so the ordinary
amalgam in \eqref{eq:B125-pre-cross} is proper. In its ordinary
Bass--Serre tree the kernel of $\varepsilon$ has one central
$R^0$ vertex, two $Q$ vertices represented by $1,C$, and the two
edges represented by $K,CKC$. This gives
\eqref{eq:B125-star}. Equivalently, rewriting the two cosets
$1,C$ introduces $j_0,j_1$ and exactly their two sets of neck
inversion relations. There is no relation between $j_0,j_1$.
The inverse maps send the second leaf and its edge to their literal
$C$-conjugates. This proves the full marked presentation, rather
than an injection inferred from a diagram alone.

\emph{B.125.2. Both ordinary sheets of the cross filling.}
Free multiplication gives
\[
 \tau=j_0aCa^{-1}j_0=t_0C,\qquad
 Ct_0C=t_0^{-1},\qquad t_0=a g a_1^{-1}.
\]
Consequently the actual cross relator is
\[
 r_0=t_0k_1t_0^{-1}k^{-1}.
\]
Its $C$-conjugate is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-two-sheet}
 Cr_0C=t_0^{-1}r_0^{-1}t_0.
\end{equation}
Thus the normal closure of $r_0$ inside $A^0$ is already
$C$-invariant. Its quotient is precisely the original-parity
kernel in $X$; no second ordinary cross relation is missing.

The actual regional relations give $[k,a]=[k_1,a_1]=1$.
They transform $r_0$ into $a(gk_1g^{-1}k^{-1})a^{-1}$.
For an explicit comparison before imposing those two commutations,
put
\[
 \alpha=a_1^{-1}k_1a_1k_1^{-1},\qquad
 \beta=a^{-1}k^{-1}ak,
 \qquad r_*=gk_1g^{-1}k^{-1}.
\]
The exact free-word identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-relator-difference}
 \begin{split}
 r_0(ar_*a^{-1})^{-1}
  ={}&(ag)\alpha(ag)^{-1}\\
     &\cdot(agk_1g^{-1})\beta(agk_1g^{-1})^{-1}.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Both factors vanish in the actual regional group. Finally
$k_1=hkh^{-1}$, so $r_*=[gh,k]$. This proves
\eqref{eq:B125-final-single}, including the precise existing word
that occurs in it.

\emph{B.125.3. Cofinal compatible finite kernels.}
The actual ordinary regional retraction $r:R^0\to K$ of
B.112/B.114 has companion
\[
 \zeta=r\operatorname{Ad}(C)|_K,\qquad
 \zeta(a)=\zeta(B)=\zeta(d)=\zeta(e)=1,
 \quad \zeta(f)=f.
\]
Let $A_R\triangleleft R^0$ and $A_i\triangleleft Q_i$ be
arbitrary finite-index normal subgroups. Identify $K_1$ with $K$
by $C$-conjugation. Choose a finite-index normal $N\triangleleft K$
contained in all four prescribed restriction kernels:
\[
 K\cap A_R,\quad K\cap CA_RC,\quad K\cap A_0,
 \quad K\cap CA_1C.
\]
There is a finite-index fully invariant subgroup $L\leq N$:
take the intersection of the kernels of all homomorphisms
$K\to K/N$. There are finitely many such maps because $K$ is
finitely generated and the target is finite; the quotient map is
one of them. Precomposition proves full invariance. Hence $L$ is
stable under $\zeta$, $\eta$ and $\eta^{-1}$.

With $\psi:R^0\to R^0/A_R$, form the finite product map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B125-finite-central-map}
 \Phi(x)=\bigl(\psi(x),\ r(x)\bmod L,\ r(CxC)\bmod L\bigr).
\end{equation}
On $K$ the second coordinate forces its kernel into $L$; conversely
all three coordinates kill $L$ by its choice and $\zeta$-stability.
Thus the exact edge kernel is $L$. On $CKC$ the last two coordinates
are interchanged, giving exact kernel $CLC$. The total kernel
refines $A_R$.

Each leaf has the finite quotient
$(K/L)\rtimes_\eta C_2$ in its own marked coordinates. Combining
this map with the prescribed quotient by $A_i$ refines $A_i$
and still has the same exact edge kernel, since $L$ was chosen
inside that restriction kernel. We have therefore obtained finite
quotients of all three vertices, refining arbitrary prescribed
ones, and identical actual edge quotients on both ends of each
edge.

The resulting finite star of finite groups has a finite permutation
quotient injective on every vertex. Choose free actions of all
three finite vertices on one set whose cardinality is divisible
by their orders. The restrictions to an edge are free actions of
the same finite edge group, so conjugate each leaf action to agree
with the central one on its edge. The two conjugations are
independent because the underlying graph is a star. The resulting
homomorphism preserves every finite vertex faithfully.
It follows that each actual vertex has its full induced topology
in $A^0$. The same then holds for the edges by their actual
retractions or finite-index embeddings. The universal property
on finite targets identifies the completion with the full
profinite pushout, now known to be proper. This proves
\eqref{eq:B125-completed-star} without a residual-finiteness
assumption about an unspecified amalgam.

\emph{B.125.4. Based source and quotient scope.}
The positive regional source words $s_i$ include their entire
selected-gap $a$-conjugation. Their $j_0$-conjugates and $k$ are
exactly the prelifts already compared with the raw actual six
source words in B.120. Their ordinary and mirror parities both
vanish. The cone implementing the selected action is
$\tau=t_0C$, not $C$ alone and not the mirror reflection.

Full completion of \eqref{eq:B125-final-single} is the quotient
of \eqref{eq:B125-completed-star} by the closed normal closure
of its specified relator, by the finite-quotient universal
property. This gives no vertex embedding after filling and no
fixed-element lifting statement. In particular one cannot turn
\eqref{eq:B125-final-single} into a genuine HNN extension by
omitting the equation defining $z_*$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact verification and provenance.}
The universal selected-cone, two-sheet and relator-difference
identities were executed with full free words. The proof of finite
kernel compatibility is the argument above, not a computation.
