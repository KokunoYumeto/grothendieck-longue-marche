# Finite source-operator presentation of the actual coset module

Research, 16 November 2026, checkpoint20 cycle. This records the exact
finite presentation available after adjoining the actual source
operators. It does not infer finite presentation of an augmentation
kernel from finite generation of the stabilizer.

## 1. The actual fixed coefficient group and source subgroup

Use B104's actual even-base quotient P_even=G semidirect S, with
S=F(s_1,...,s_11) the eleven pointwise fixed-source lifts. Its convention
is s_j^-1 r s_j=psi_j(r). Thus the left conjugation action of s_j is
theta_j=psi_j^-1, not psi_j. Both directions are available in the
certified source-word dictionary.

The actual involution fixes S and has G^F=diag(R). Therefore

    K=P_even^F=diag(R) semidirect S,

which we identify with R semidirect S using the diagonal coordinate.
The actual subgroup I in R is stable under every source operator and
its inverse. The source filling F19 -> F11 has the prescribed free
splitting and eight peripheral kernel generators. Its kernel is
generated as a subgroup by all S-conjugates of those eight peripherals,
by the free-product normal form (or its elementary Schreier basis).
Passing to the actual image yields

    I = subgroup generated by {theta_s(rho_j): s in S, 1<=j<=8},
    H=I semidirect S <= K.

Here the rho_j are the eight actual source peripheral images, including
both sheets; no new choices of their based words are made. The group H
is generated by the eleven s_j and the eight rho_j. This is the actual
image of the nineteen-generator source in the fixed coefficient group.

## 2. Cosets and a finite module presentation

Every element of K has a unique normal form r s. Since S is contained
in H, the map rI -> rH is a bijection

    R/I -> K/H.

It is K-equivariant: R acts by left multiplication, and S acts through
its actual conjugation action theta on R/I. Put A=F2[K]. Then

    A^19 -> A -> F2[K/H] -> 0

is an exact finite presentation, where the first map sends its basis
vectors to rho_j-1 and s_i-1. Indeed the left ideal generated by these
nineteen elements is exactly the left ideal generated by h-1 for every
h in H. The identity uv-1=(u-1)+u(v-1), together with
u^-1-1=-u^-1(u-1), proves this without any finiteness assumption on I.

Consequently the *permutation module*, equipped with its augmentation
to F2, is finitely presented over A. Equivalently, quotienting first by
the eleven source-generator relations identifies the remaining module
with F2[R] carrying its actual K-action, and the eight peripheral
relations generate the entire J_I as an A-submodule. As an F2[R]-module
this means the complete source-operator orbit, not a finite truncation:

    J_I = sum_(s,j) F2[R] (theta_s(rho_j)-1).

Thus all required old-source orbit relations have a finite operator
description, despite the previously proved failure of finite generation
over F2[R] alone.

## 3. The augmentation kernel remains distinct

The target of kappa is the reduced permutation module

    ker(F2[K/H] -> F2) = I_(F2[R])/J_I.

Its finite presentation does not follow merely from the preceding
finite presentation of F2[K/H]. The kernel of a map from a finitely
presented module to the trivial module need not be finitely presented
over a non-Noetherian group ring. An adequate additional hypothesis
would be type FP2 over F2 for K, since then its augmentation ideal is
finitely presented and the displayed nineteen-generator ideal can be
factored out. Such an FP2 theorem for the actual fixed group K is not
asserted here.

Even finite generation of this reduced module would require a real
relative generation input. To see the issue directly, suppose finitely
many augmentation-zero coset polynomials generate it. Let L be the
subgroup generated by H and the finitely many coset representatives
appearing in those polynomials. The map F2[K/H] -> F2[K/L] kills all
these polynomials, so it kills the whole reduced module. The augmentation
kernel of F2[K/L] must then be zero, forcing K=L. Since H is finitely
generated, K would be finitely generated as well. No such conclusion is
extracted solely from the nineteen source generators.

The correct unconditional finite object is the cyclic permutation
module with the nineteen printed operator relations and its explicit
augmentation map. Neither Noetherianity, finite kernel generation, a
subgroup equality H=K, nor a profinite fixed-subgroup comparison follows.
