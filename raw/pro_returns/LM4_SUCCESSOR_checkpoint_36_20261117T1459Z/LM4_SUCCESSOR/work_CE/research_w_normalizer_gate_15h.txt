Actual product-one strips and the still-open whole-subgroup gate

The source-adapted w basis is
  z,c1,c2,c3,c4,p1,p2,p3,p4,a,d,b.
The first nine elements lie in the actual source image H. Each c_i lies
in the actual constant-family partial-source image J, which is normal
in H. These statements refer to actual source maps after the five
negative-root node fillings; a free generic fiber is not asserted to
remain embedded after those fillings.

1. Two exact whole two-generator kernel images.

The actual old source action at positive_4 restricts on <b,c1> to
Inn(b c1^-1). The actual positive_1 action restricts on <b,c4> to
Inn(c4^-1 b). These are literal restrictions in the common source-based
marking, checked independently on both generators.

If an actual source action restricts on <b,c> to Inn(b c^-1) or
Inn(c^-1 b), and c belongs to J, then J intersect this two-generator
image is invariant under the displayed conjugation and under c.
It is therefore invariant under b. Hence the image of the entire
free-group filling kernel ker(F(b,c)-><b>) belongs to J. In particular
all b^n c1 b^-n and b^n c4 b^-n belong to J for every integer n.
This is an image-containment assertion; no free-factor embedding after
the node relations or whole-J normalization is claimed.

2. The actual filled w=-1 relation.

Let h_d,h_b be the exact common-transport conjugators of the diagonal
and product-one meridians. After the adapted basis change,
  h_d^-1 h_b=d^-1 a^-1 b^-1 M,  M=z c3 c4.
The node therefore yields
  [d,q0]=1, q0=(ba)^-1 M b M^-1(ba).
Equivalently [(ba)d(ba)^-1, M b M^-1]=1. It is not the unbased
relation[d,b]=1. The distinction remains after the source words M are
recognized as elements of H.

3. A proposed propagation relaxation was rejected.

At15h root proposed that knowing all b-strips of the seed set and
gamma^-1(b)=k_gamma b k_gamma^-1 with k_gamma in a known normalizer
of J would force b-normality of all of J. CE initially accepted this
relaxation too quickly. DE identified the omitted middle conjugation;
CE and root explicitly withdrew the claim before using it.

The actual formula is
  b^n gamma(w)b^-n
   =gamma(k b^n (k^-1 w k) b^-n k^-1).
Knowing k normalizes J gives no bound on the orbit/stage complexity
of k^-1 w k. Thus the proposed orbit-length induction is circular.

DE's exact free-group counterexample confirms the issue. In
F=<b,h,u>, let beta fix b,h and send u to b u b^-1. Put
Gamma=<beta,Inn(h)> and
  J=<h^k b^n u b^-n h^-k : k,n in Z>.
Then H=<h,J> normalizes J. Every generator of Gamma and its inverse
sends b to a conjugate by an element of H; all b-strips of u lie in J.
But b h u h^-1 b^-1 is outside J. Indeed the normal closure of u in
F is freely based by t u t^-1, t in F(b,h). The displayed J uses
only the index words h^k b^n; the index bh is absent.

The stronger stage argument actually used in B82 remains valid:
its correcting k belongs to the already controlled C-normal stage
K_n itself. That puts k^-1 w k in K_n, where the induction applies.
Only the proposed replacement by an arbitrary normalizer was rejected.

4. A separate exact obstruction to source membership of a conjugator.

The actual w=0 action has
  alpha0(b)=h0 b h0^-1,
  h0=b c1 c2 b^-1 (bad)^-1 z c3 c4.
Its a,d,b abelian coordinates are(-1,-1,-1). Every one of the thirteen
source crossing words has zero a,d,b coordinates. All old and filled
actions preserve fiber abelianization, and the filled node variations
are commutators. Therefore the a,d,b characters extend to the actual
total group by sending the old source base generators to zero, and
they vanish on H. No h0 b^n belongs to H, because its a,d coordinates
remain(-1,-1). Thus even the premise of the rejected relaxation would
fail for this b with the proposed normalizer H.

This character argument tests only a particular proposed conjugator;
it is not an abelian separator of the ambient filling kernel and does
not revisit the already excluded affine/metabelian marginal search.

5. Remaining target.

The actual source words plus a,d,b generate the total fiber image.
To deduce equality of the actual partial-source image with the ambient
deletion kernel, one still needs a valid whole-subgroup normalizer
argument (or another full-group mechanism). Individual b-strips,
transported local pairs, and finite source-subgroup membership do not
by themselves supply it.
