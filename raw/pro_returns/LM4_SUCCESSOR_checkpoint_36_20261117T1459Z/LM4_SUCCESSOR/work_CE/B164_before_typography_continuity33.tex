\subsection*{The remaining contact image and its exact finite-refinement criterion}
\statement{B.164}{Proved}{The remaining local fixed-source equality is exactly a contact-image equality in nonabelian cohomology, with a complete ordered continuous source model and an equivalent cofinal finite criterion}
\label{stmt:B164}
Use B.163 and the actual three discarded contacts
$\mathscr C=\{B_s,D_s,E_s\}$ of B.150. In
$\widehat{\Gamma_0}=\widehat G_{\rm gen}/\mathcal N$ put
\[
 \mathcal K=\mathcal K_a/\mathcal N=\overline{K_{\rm rem}},\qquad
 \mathcal I=\overline{I_{\rm src}}.
\]
The connecting map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B164-connecting}
 \partial:\mathcal K^\delta\longrightarrow H^1(\Delta,\mathcal N),
 \qquad t\mathcal N\longmapsto[t^{-1}\delta(t)]
\end{equation}
is an injective continuous map with closed image
\[
 \operatorname{im}\partial
   =\ker[H^1(\Delta,\mathcal N)\to H^1(\Delta,\mathcal K_a)].
\]
Consequently the actual local completed equality is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B164-gate}
 (\widehat{\Gamma_0})^\delta=\overline{H_{\rm src}}quad
 \Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \ker[H^1(\Delta,\mathcal N)\to H^1(\Delta,\mathcal K_a)]
          =\partial(\mathcal I).
\end{equation}
This kernel is nontrivial: it contains the nonbase class of the actual
$D_s$ contact. Thus the right side cannot be replaced by injectivity
of the whole cohomology map. Neither equality in
\eqref{eq:B164-gate} is concluded by this proposition.
\begin{proof}
\emph{Actual closed quotient and section.}
The filled source diagram gives $\mathcal N\le\mathcal K_a$.
The actual generic section $\widehat\lambda$ projects to the
pointwise-fixed marked section $\widehat\sigma$ after killing
$\mathcal N$, because all its defects lie in $\overline R\le\mathcal N$.
Hence
\[
 \mathcal P/\mathcal N=\mathcal K\rtimes\widehat\sigma(\widehat J).
\]
B.139 says that every fixed point of $\widehat{\Gamma_0}$ projects
into the actual $\widehat J$. B.154 therefore gives the two exact
decompositions
\[
 (\widehat{\Gamma_0})^\delta
       =\mathcal K^\delta\rtimes\widehat\sigma(\widehat J),\qquad
 \overline{H_{\rm src}}
       =\mathcal I\rtimes\widehat\sigma(\widehat J).
\]
Here $\mathcal I$ is the actual closure, equal to the intersection
of the source closure and $\mathcal K$ by the net argument in B.154.

For $t\mathcal N$ fixed, $t^{-1}\delta(t)$ is an $\mathcal N$-cocycle;
changing the lift on the right by $n\in\mathcal N$ gives the usual
twisted conjugation by $n$. Every $\mathcal N$-cocycle trivial in
$H^1(\Delta,\mathcal K_a)$ has this form. If the classes from $t_1,t_2$
coincide, their lifts differ, after an $\mathcal N$ correction, by an
element of $\mathcal K_a^\delta$. B.163 proves that this group is $1$.
Thus \eqref{eq:B164-connecting} is injective. Continuity and compactness
make its image closed and prove \eqref{eq:B164-gate}.

\emph{A continuous three-family model of the entire source kernel.}
Let $W$ be the free profinite group on the compact space
$\widehat J\times\mathscr C$. It can be constructed as
\[
 W=\varprojlim_V\widehat{F((\widehat J/V)\times\mathscr C)},
 \qquad V\trianglelefteq_o\widehat J.
\]
The transition maps fold equal labels. Every continuous map of the
compact generating space into a finite group factors through a finite
coset set, proving the universal property exactly as in B.162.
Left translation supplies a jointly continuous $\widehat J$ action.
The two generator-wise inverse maps show
\[
 W\rtimes\widehat J
     =\widehat J\amalg\widehat{F(B_s,D_s,E_s)}.
\]
Send $\widehat J$ by its actual section $\widehat\sigma$ and the
three free letters to the actual contacts. This gives a surjection
onto $\overline{H_{\rm src}}$ compatible with the projection to
$\widehat J$, so its kernel factor $W$ maps onto precisely $\mathcal I$.
Thus the entire compact contact family is retained. No completion of
the infinite discrete contact kernel is identified with $\mathcal I$.

Choose actual generic lifts $t_c\in K_a$ of the three contacts and set
\[
 c_c=t_c^{-1}\delta(t_c)\in N,\qquad
 \kappa_j=\delta\widehat\lambda(j)\widehat\lambda(j)^{-1},\qquad
 T_{j,c}=\widehat\lambda(j)t_c\widehat\lambda(j)^{-1}.
\]
For every $j\in\widehat J$ the \emph{ordered} identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B164-contact}
 c_{j,c}:=T_{j,c}^{-1}\delta(T_{j,c})
 =T_{j,c}^{-1}\kappa_jT_{j,c}\,
       \widehat\lambda(j)c_c\widehat\lambda(j)^{-1}\kappa_j^{-1}.
\end{equation}
All factors lie in $\mathcal N$ after the indicated conjugations.
The factors $\kappa_j$ cannot be removed before filling. For arbitrary
successive lifts one also has
\[
 c_{tu}=u^{-1}c_tu\,c_u.
\]
Equivalently each contact lift acts on cohomology by
$[z]\cdot(t\mathcal N)=[t^{-1}z\delta(t)]$.
This is a well-defined right action of the fixed quotient: normality
of $\mathcal N$ verifies independence of cocycle representative and
lift, and composition gives the product in the stated order.
The subset $\partial(\mathcal I)$ is exactly the closed orbit of the
basepoint under all these contact actions and their inverses, for
\emph{every} $j\in\widehat J$. This characterizes the image without
turning nonabelian cohomology into an additive or normal-span object.

\emph{A nontrivial class already lies in that image.}
The actual $D_s$ is fixed, is killed by the one-family map (B.150),
and is nontrivial even in the actual completion by B.132. For a generic
lift $t_D\in K_a$, its cocycle $t_D^{-1}\delta(t_D)$ therefore defines
a nonbase class in $H^1(\Delta,\mathcal N)$ by the injectivity just
proved. It becomes a coboundary in $\mathcal K_a$. This is the concrete
obstruction to the earlier stronger injectivity formulation; it is a
known contact class, not a counterexample to \eqref{eq:B164-gate}.

\emph{Exact cofinal finite criterion, with all nodes retained.}
Let $V$ range through open characteristic subgroups of
$\widehat G_{\rm gen}$; these are cofinal since the generic group is
finitely generated (intersect the finitely many subgroups of a bounded
index). They are $\delta$-stable. Denote the actual finite images of
$\mathcal K_a,\mathcal N$ by $K_V,N_V$ and put
\[
 Z_V=\ker[H^1(\Delta,N_V)\to H^1(\Delta,K_V)].
\]
Let $I_V\le K_V/N_V$ be the image of $\mathcal I$, equivalently the
subgroup generated by the three whole finite $\lambda(J)$ contact
orbits, and put $Y_V=\partial_V(I_V)\subseteq Z_V$.
These finite images use the normal closure of \emph{all forty actual
node seeds}, both sheets and all signed source conjugations; no
projected one-family relators replace $N_V$.

For a finite acting group, nonabelian first cohomology of an inverse
system of compact groups is the inverse limit of these finite pointed
spaces. Here is the needed compact argument: compatible finite cocycle
classes give nonempty closed sets of lifts satisfying any finitely
many cocycle and class conditions; a common refinement supplies their
finite-intersection property. Their intersection is an actual cocycle.
If two cocycles are conjugate at every level, the closed sets of their
possible conjugators have the same property, giving a single conjugator.
Thus in this actual system
\[
 Z:=\ker[H^1(\Delta,\mathcal N)\to H^1(\Delta,\mathcal K_a)]
       =\varprojlim_V Z_V,
\]
and $\partial(\mathcal I)$ is a compact subset with projection $Y_V$.
It follows that \eqref{eq:B164-gate} is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B164-finite}
 \text{for every }V_0\text{ there exists }V_1\le V_0
 \text{ with }
 \operatorname{im}(Z_{V_1}\to Z_{V_0})\subseteq Y_{V_0}.
\end{equation}
Indeed the decreasing finite images at $V_0$ intersect in the image
of $Z$, by the same compactness argument. If $Z=\partial(\mathcal I)$,
finitely many unwanted points can be excluded at one common refinement.
Conversely \eqref{eq:B164-finite} puts every point of $Z$ in every
finite image of the compact subset $\partial(\mathcal I)$, hence in
that subset itself.
\end{proof}
B.163 controls precisely the coset classes coming from $\overline R$;
its nonbase classes remain nontrivial in $H^1(\Delta,\mathcal K_a)$.
It does not prove \eqref{eq:B164-finite} for all additional nodal
cocycles. That is the remaining local contact-generation gate.
The original full-base B.75 criterion and the global icosahedral
comparison require their own marked transfer and remain open.
