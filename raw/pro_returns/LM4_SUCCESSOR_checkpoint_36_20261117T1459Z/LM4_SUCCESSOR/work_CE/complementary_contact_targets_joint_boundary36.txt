Complementary actual contact targets: the precise joint boundary
Checkpoint36. Separate research note; no formal statement is changed.

Use the actual compact source H5c and the presentation source

  P0=(Zhat_A x Zhat_m) amalg Fhat(B,D,E)

of B170. Write C0=ker(P0 -> H5c). Every normal closure below is a
closed normal closure in the displayed compact ambient group. Use
[g,h]=g h g^-1 h^-1 throughout.

The first actual source target, B169–B170, has composite kernel

  R1=clnormal_P0([m,D]).

The complementary actual source target, B173, has composite kernel

  R2=clnormal_P0(A,E,[m,B D B^-1]).

Both composites factor through the actual H5c, so C0 is contained in
R1 intersect R2. The map from H5c to the product of its two actual
targets has kernel (R1 intersect R2)/C0. This is a literal quotient
identity for these two compact surjections. It does not replace C0 by
a normal closure inferred from either deletion.

The upper bound R1 intersect R2 is nontrivial in P0. Indeed put

  r=[m,D],  w=[r,A].

Normality gives w in R1. Since A is in R2, normality also gives
w in R2. Its ordinary free-product word, written in successive
nonidentity factors of (Z_A x Z_m) * F(B,D,E), is

  m | D | m^-1 | D^-1 | A | D | m | D^-1 | m^-1 A^-1.

Every displayed factor is nonidentity and adjacent factors belong to
different free-product vertices. Thus the word is reduced and nontrivial.
It remains nontrivial in the full profinite free product P0. For a
concrete reduction of the topology needed for this last sentence, map
A and m to the two independent involutions of C2 x C2, D to the
generator of another C2, and B,E to one. The same word is reduced in
(C2 x C2) * C2: the last factor m A is nonidentity. This ordinary
finite-group free product embeds in its full profinite completion
by residual finiteness, and its finite quotients are actual finite
quotients of P0. No infinite-index completion identification is used.

This witness belongs to the joint kernel of the two presentation-source
targets. Its image in H5c is nontrivial if and only if w is not in C0;
neither possibility is proved here. In particular, this does not give
a new nontrivial element of the actual source, a new relation in it,
or a counterexample to fixed-source generation. It shows exactly why
the two complementary targets, without an additional comparison with
C0, cannot by themselves establish injectivity of the presentation
source into the actual group.
