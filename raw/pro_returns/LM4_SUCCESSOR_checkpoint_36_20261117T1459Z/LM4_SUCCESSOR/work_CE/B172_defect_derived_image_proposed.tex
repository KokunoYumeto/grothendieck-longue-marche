\subsection*{A retained derived image of the actual normal defect}
\statement{B.172}{Proved}{The actual one-sided normal defect has an image containing the entire derived subgroup of a free profinite group of rank two and therefore surjects onto every finite two-generated perfect group}
\label{stmt:B172}
Use \(\mathcal I_5,\mathcal K_5\) from B.169--B.170.
The actual compact map of B.131.2--4,
\[
 q:\mathcal J\twoheadrightarrow\widehat F(X,Y),
\]
is defined on the closed eight-family kernel \(\mathcal J\), which
contains \(\mathcal I_5\). It satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B172-exact-images}
 \begin{gathered}
 q(A_s)=q(E_s)=1,\qquad q(B_s)=X,\qquad
 q(D_s)=X^{-1}YX,\\
 q([m,D_s])=YX^{-1}Y^{-1}X.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B172-derived-bound}
 q(\mathcal I_5)=\widehat F(X,Y),\qquad
 \widehat F(X,Y)'\ \le\ q(\mathcal K_5).
\end{equation}
The prime denotes the closed derived subgroup.
For every finite perfect group admitting two generators, there is
a continuous epimorphism from \(\mathcal K_5\) to that group.
No equality for \(q(\mathcal K_5)\) or presentation of \(\mathcal K_5\)
is asserted.

\begin{proof}
The exact B.131 raw-source evaluation table is
\[
 q(s_1)=X^{-1},\qquad q(s_2)=q(s_4)=1,\qquad q(s_3)=Y^{-1}.
\]
Substitution in the actual corrected basis
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 A_s=s_1s_4^{-1}s_1^{-1},\qquad
 B_s=A_s^{-1}s_1^{-1}A_s,\\
 D_s=s_2s_1s_3^{-1}s_1^{-1}s_2^{-1},\qquad E_s=s_2^{-1}
 \end{gathered}
\]
gives the four contact values in \eqref{eq:B172-exact-images}.
The last value is B.131's whole-word signed-operator calculation.
Its compact map is defined on the actual closed kernel, with the
entire original normal family killed; no abstract completion of
an infinite-index kernel is substituted.

Both \(B_s,D_s\) lie in \(\mathcal I_5\), and
\(X,X^{-1}YX\) generate \(\widehat F(X,Y)\).
The compact image \(q(\mathcal I_5)\) is therefore the entire group.
Since \(\mathcal K_5\triangleleft\mathcal I_5\), its compact image
is a closed normal subgroup of \(\widehat F(X,Y)\).
It contains \(YX^{-1}Y^{-1}X=[Y,X^{-1}]\).
The closed normal closure of this word is exactly the closed derived
subgroup: its single defining relation imposes \([X,Y]=1\), and the
resulting profinite presentation is \(\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\,2}\).
This proves \eqref{eq:B172-derived-bound}.

For a specified finite perfect two-generated group \(P\), choose the
corresponding epimorphism \(\widehat F(X,Y)\to P\).
Its image on the closed derived subgroup is \(P'=P\).
Hence its restriction to \(q(\mathcal K_5)\), and then to
\(\mathcal K_5\), is surjective. This is a uniform consequence, not
a census or an additionally executed finite certificate.

No image of \(m\) under \(q\), and no source action on
\(\widehat F(X,Y)\), has been assumed. The map is used only on
\(\mathcal I_5,\mathcal K_5\) inside its proved domain.
This retained two-color map sees part of the noncommuting defect that
both mirrored deletions kill. It leaves the full contact-image
equality open.
\end{proof}
