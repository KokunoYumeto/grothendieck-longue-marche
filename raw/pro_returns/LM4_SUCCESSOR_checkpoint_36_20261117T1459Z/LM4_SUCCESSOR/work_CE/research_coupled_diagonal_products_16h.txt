and a set P of retained generators, mapping to an actual group G.

all images t^n s t^-n lie in J and all images t^n p t^-n lie in H.
Then the entire image of ker(F->F(t,P)) lies in J.

Indeed K=ker(F-><t>) is generated by the Schreier letters s_n,p_n.
Its image lies in H. The normal closure of all s_n inside K is stable
under t, hence normal in all F; it is exactly ker(F->F(t,P)). Its image
is contained in J because the s_n map into J and K maps into H.
This controls arbitrary alternating t/P conjugations, not only separate
one-direction strips. The hypothesis on retained-generator strips is
essential; it is precisely absent in the rejected arbitrary-normalizer

therefore the full closed kernel killing S. Closed H,J give the same
image containment. This argument concerns actual images and does not
assert an induced full topology on their abstract discrete subgroups.
