3. Arbitrary finite targets of H extend after finite refinement.

  V intersect M <= M0,
  M intersect (T V) <= M0.                                (5)

The second choice is a compactness consequence of (3): the closed sets
M intersect T V decrease to M intersect T=1 as V decreases. If none were
contained in the open subgroup M0, their intersections with the compact

(3). Refining once more ensures the first condition.

Put D=hat(G_Q)/V, with cyclic images Mbar,Tbar. The map psi on M descends

  rho(Mbar intersect Tbar)=1.                             (6)

  [g h,k]=[g,rho(h)k]  (h in Mbar),

5. Exact continuous-family description of the contact-image kernel.

Let J0=Zhat_a times Zhat_m. Let X be the disjoint union of a compact copy
of Zhat labeled d and two compact copies of Zhat^2 labeled B,E.
For each positive n, let U_n be the free profinite group on the finite
labels d_i (i in Z/n), B_(i,j),E_(i,j) ((i,j) in (Z/n)^2).
For divisibility, send a generator to the generator with its reduced
label. Set U=lim U_n with these surjective bonding maps.

The continuous label map X->U has the following universal property:
every continuous map X to a profinite group extends uniquely to a continuous
homomorphism U to that group. For a finite target the label map factors
through some finite residue grid, by compactness and the clopen cylinder
basis. The universal finite free-group map then gives the extension. For
an arbitrary profinite target apply this argument to each finite quotient;
uniqueness makes the maps compatible. The labels topologically generate U,
since their images generate every U_n and compactness gives density in
the inverse limit. This proves the universal property directly; it is not
an assertion that an arbitrary inverse limit commutes with free completion.

J0 acts jointly continuously by

  a^s m^t: d_i -> d_(i+s),
  a^s m^t: B_(i,j),E_(i,j) -> B_(i+s,j+t),E_(i+s,j+t).

the elements a,m,d_0,B_(0,0),E_(0,0) satisfy exactly the presentation

  d_i -> a^i d a^-i,

in hat(H). They intertwine the J0 action because [a,m]=[d,m]=1. The

  hat(H)=U semidirect J0,

This is a full profinite continuous-family statement. It is not the
