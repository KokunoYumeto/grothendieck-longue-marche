## How to use the doubts tables

The first table records the readers' approximate positions on the manuscript and their substantive alternatives. The following exhaustive index records every remaining `\unsure` and `\illegible` occurrence in the page files, with exact **page-file LaTeX line numbers**; these navigational line numbers are not claims about the manuscript's handwritten lineation. Repeated identical doubts on one page are grouped, with all their file-line locations retained.

Confidence is an editorial judgment about the proposed reading: **high** means a substantially resolved reading with a small residual concern; **medium** means a plausible reading whose strokes do not fully discriminate alternatives; **low** means cancellation, crowding, weak ink, clipping, or unresolved form prevents a secure reading. Where a macro occurrence has no separately matched confidence assessment, the index conservatively assigns low; this is not a measured error probability. A blank alternative means that no second defensible reading was supplied, not that the first is certain.

## Legibility and limits of trust

The principal uncancelled prose and most large formulas are legible after viewing the whole page, the overlapping halves, and selected enlargements. Small corrections, dense overwriting and marginal formulas are markedly less secure. Confidence in the mathematical plausibility of a phrase has not been counted as evidence that its letters are visible. Every supplied image was viewed by the coordinator as well as by its assigned primary reader or auditor; OCR was not used.

The least secure early material is scan100's first product accents and unfinished final paragraph,103's overwritten opening and final parenthesis,104's crowded diagram labels, and106's regularity parenthesis. The tiny isolated e/c-like mark after the second boundary symbol on107 is explicitly retained as doubtful. The word and ending corrections made by the independent reader are reflected in both the page text and the log.

On108–116, the diagonal margin on108, additions around109's product, and multiply cancelled passages on113,115 and116 require particular caution. The local sigma/o index on114–115, the n/u correction symbol on116, and the xi/zeta object glyph on116–117 remain open. On117–125, the rewritten classification and cancellations on119–120, the ramification-point digit on121, the cancelled orientation clause on122, the small group subscript and isolated curved mark on123, and the insertion after “tétraèdre” on125 are fragile. The rounded permutation-group capital remains a Gothic S/G doubt throughout the related passages. The final isolated “On” on125 is unfinished in the source and has not been completed.

The graph catalogue127 preserves the row structure, graph incidences, vertex weights, contraction arrows, multiplicities, unfinished marks and cancellations. Fine loops and crossings are vector approximations; the long looping stroke crossing or adjoining a leftward arrow in row(0,6) has been traced without assigning it an unsupported letter. Margins and overwritten clauses on128–131 and133 remain less secure than the main displays. The opposite directions of133's two normalization arrows are retained literally. Its unfinished converse remains unfinished.

The printed notices132 and134 are substantially clearer than the manuscript pages. Both were inspected in their original inverted orientation and after visual rotation; printed text is presented upright, and manuscript pagination remains separately marked. Signatures are TikZ traces of visible strokes, not guessed names. Visible printed line-end hyphens are retained where transcribed. The typography and lineation are an edition of the visible content, not a facsimile of the paper.

All figures are TikZ approximations of observed strokes. Their topology, points and labels are generally more reliable than exact curvature, pen thickness or metric placement; no source image is embedded. The combined file is a LaTeX fragment with no preamble. The caller must define the specified editorial macros and load the ordinary mathematics and TikZ/tikz-cd facilities. Brace balance, environment matching, page markers and command usage were checked, but a temporary typesetting attempt could not compile because the available TeX engine executables lacked their format files and required packages; no successful compilation or rendered-page validation is claimed.

## Process record and deviations

Each primary page was saved separately with its inventory and UTC completion entry; a different reader audited every page before the transcription was frozen. The coordinator additionally inspected all99 supplied images and compared recurring symbols across the assigned blocks before the freeze. The hashes of all33 page fragments and the combined fragment are recorded in `progress.log`. The contextual archive is used only for the separate notes, and the frozen transcription is checked again against those hashes at completion.

Two deviations from the requested procedure are recorded openly. Initial attachment inspection listed the context ZIP's central-directory filenames and sizes before the restriction had been read; no member content was read or extracted at that point. Durable checkpoint ZIPs were captured at14,24 and30 completed pages rather than exactly after10,20 and30, because the parallel page completions and checkpoint capture were not synchronized to those exact thresholds. A further full-transcription checkpoint preserves all33 pages after the freeze. These events and the later first extraction of the contextual contents are distinguished in the log.
