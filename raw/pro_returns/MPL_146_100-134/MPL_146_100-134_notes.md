### R06 — Reconstructing an extension from its one-coordinate quotients

**Scan:** 103, “elle est connue quand on connaît les ext. correspondantes”; frozen page-file line 4. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high under the stated factor-preservation hypothesis, because the kernel and lifting argument is explicit.

### A109-1 — Scan 109, TeX lines 4–9 and 16–19, tangent-direction torsors — unpursued implication

### A111-3 — Scan 111, TeX lines 17–20, why the phase torsor is a different question — own idea
