## Concrete calculations checked

- N029: ordered real closure of Q(t) with t positive infinite. The real closure extends that chosen ordering. The identities t−n=s_n² with s_n nonzero obstruct every unital field embedding into R; algebraicity retains transcendence degree one.
- N030: Spec F3 has the nontrivial étale quadratic torsor given by z²+1. Its degree-one class tests the source's strict upper bound without requiring a global theorem about arbitrary X.
- N033: the finite-field Frobenius quotients yield cyclic degree-p constant extensions of both Fp and Fp(t); the checked characteristic-p cohomological bound gives dimension one for both.
- N037: the map x↦t,y↦it has kernel (x²+y²). The localized real curve has residue R and fraction field C(t); the cited Tsen/Brauer chain and the explicit quadratic character supply its stated cohomological dimensions.
- N057: under integer translation, closed edges [0,1] and [1,2] meet at one endpoint; their interiors are disjoint. Open intervals of radius less than 1/2 give the ordinary quotient covering R→R/Z. The source convention remains a question.
- N073: in C((t))→C((u)), t=u², the extension has degree and ramification index two. Formal unit square roots in C[[t]] give surjective unit norm, while the norm of a unit remains a unit. The example's infinite residue field and valuation-ring units are essential to the stated convention test.
- N080: the additive polynomial automorphism (x,y)↦(x+y^p,y) has the written inverse and has no constant matrix as a group-scheme coordinate expression. Equality on Fp-rational points alone is not used.
- N082–N083: the regular norm of s in C[C2] is the determinant −1; the extra-factor embedding into C[C2]×C has norm one by the block determinant. The irreducibility in N082 concerns the one-dimensional constant F2 module for the absolute Galois group of C.
- N089: the quadratic character of sqrt(t) is continuous and nonzero with roots-of-unity coefficients over C(t), and becomes the explicitly written coboundary after the inclusion into Ksep*. Its application is expressly limited to ordinary continuous H1 with discrete coefficients.
- N025: the triangular presentation over C[[x,y]] gives Ann(e1)=(x) by downward coefficient comparison; regularity of y modulo x then gives the stated annihilator for y^n e1. Identification with all choices in the historical pushout remains a question.
