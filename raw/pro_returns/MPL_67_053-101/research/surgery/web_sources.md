# Primary mathematical references consulted after freeze

These references supplement the supplied context. They do not alter the transcription, and literature novelty was not investigated. No external full source is copied into the deliverable. Retrieved 18 November 2026 UTC.

- **W1. Christopher J. Bishop, Complex Analysis I, MAT536, Spring2024, Chapter12, “Conformal Maps to Jordan Regions,” Theorem12.14, PDF pp.31–32 (viewer indices30–31).** [Author's lecture notes](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~bishop/classes/math536.S24/chap12.pdf). Used for the boundary-extension theorem for a Jordan domain. Its hypotheses were compared with the local Jordan-bank construction; no assertion of a theorem for arbitrary non-locally-finite cutting complexes is inferred.
- **W2. Christopher J. Bishop, “Conformal welding and Koebe's theorem,” Annals of Mathematics166 (2007), 613–656, introduction and §4.** [Published paper](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v166-n3-p01.pdf); [journal record](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2007/166-3/p01). The introduction distinguishes general boundary homeomorphisms from actual weldings; §4 treats the quasisymmetric sufficient class. These statements are background, not a proof in this run of every welding result in that article.
- **W3. Malik Younsi, “On removable sets for holomorphic functions,” EMS Surveys in Mathematical Sciences2 (2015), 219–254, §3.2, Theorem3.5 and Corollary3.6.** [Published article](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/36977?nt=1). Used for removability for continuous functions holomorphic off a finite-length compact set, with the holomorphicity hypothesis explicitly retained. This differs from removability for merely bounded functions.
- **W4. Christopher J. Bishop, Complex Analysis I, MAT536, Spring2024, Chapter10, Theorem10.15 and the following slide, PDF pp.75–76 (viewer indices74–75).** [Author's lecture notes](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~bishop/classes/math536.S24/chap10.pdf). Confirms the normalized covering constant1/4 for a univalent map with f(0)=0,f'(0)=1, used to check Hua's shadow bound.

Its PDF is scanned; this run does not claim a proof audit of that article.
