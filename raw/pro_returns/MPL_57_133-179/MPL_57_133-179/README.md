## Freeze and verification

All whole images and both supplied halves were read by the root transcriber without OCR. The first pass proceeded in one sequential stream, followed by a second visual audit of every page. The transcription was frozen at **2026-11-18 08:54:59 UTC**, before opening the context archive; all 43 page hashes and the combined-fragment hash remain unchanged after the research phase.

The structural checks confirm the page set, exact concatenation, inventory format and completeness flags, check-event format, and hash stability. ## Research outcomes and limits

No completed second manuscript witness of cote 47’s Fourier compatibility check was identified in this selection. The current “à vérifier” concerns a generalized-Jacobian Kummer/transgression class, a different construction. No earlier textual form of any of cote 44’s seven numbered theorems was identified; the notes record the precise ordinary Picard, divisor, and curve connections and the limits of chronology.

The research also tests several literal assertions and preserves every mathematical correction outside the transcription. Earlier research notes and the supplied modernization retain their stated evidential limits. The most uncertain readings remain the compressed margins, overwritten passages, some curve calculations and the page-state contour on scan 151; the doubts file identifies them in place. A complete page inventory and a successful syntax check do not guarantee that every reading is correct.

The original scans and attached context files are not duplicated here. Context paths in the notes refer to the supplied context ZIP. Intermediate reports and research memos are retained as work records; the current root-level deliverables and final reports take precedence over superseded candidates or provisional note identifiers.
