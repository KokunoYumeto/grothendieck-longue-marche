### FW05 — A small complex shows why dimensions and a top-degree identity are insufficient

**File/scan:** P57, P57, 57:144:L23–25, the boxed identity followed by “à vérifier ......”; the point is to preserve, not erase, the distinction between an asserted comparison and its verification.  
**Kind:** own idea  
**Brief source quote:** See the brief quoted words in the locator above.  
**Bears on RS:** WG2 §2.4 #4; WG2 §1.1 #3  
**Context search:** Koszul | cohomology | fibre  
**Context comparison:** CT, 47:7–8; CN F8, “top cohomology and derived restriction to the unit.”  

**Own-idea confidence and reason:** high for the explicit algebra calculation; it is a diagnostic example, not a model asserted to be the Poincaré direct image.

Over \(R=k[[x]]\), let \(K=[R\xrightarrow{x^2}R]\) and \(Q=[R\xrightarrow{x}R]\), both in degrees 0 and 1, with chain map \(c=(x,1):K\to Q\). The chain condition is \(x\cdot x=x^2\), and after tensoring with the residue field both differentials vanish, so source and target have identical one-dimensional cohomology in each degree while \(c\) induces \(0\) in degree 0 and \(1\) in degree 1. Thus an identity in top-degree fibre cohomology and matching dimensions in all degrees still do not make the given map an isomorphism. This confirms the precise logical limitation of using CN F8 alone and says nothing against the actual Fourier theorem.

### P02 — The non-quasi-compact monomorphism warning has a concrete model

**File/scan:** P57, 57:135 L18–20  
**Kind:** own idea  
**Brief source quote:** « un mono n’est donc pas ... affine [ex. Z → G] »  
**Bears on RS:** WG2 §1.1 #3  
**Context search:** monomorphisme | type fini | quasi-affine | noyau  
**Context comparison:** HUA, “Le noyau pour un morphisme surjectif”, L1707–1728, stops at the monomorphism step without reproducing the nonfinite-type warning; CT 44:2’s kernel has a different meaning.

**Own-idea confidence and reason:** high: the diagonal/fibre-product test and failure of quasi-compactness give an explicit example

Over a characteristic-zero field, the map from the constant scheme \(\mathbf Z_k=\coprod_{n\in\mathbf Z}\operatorname{Spec}k\) to \(\mathbf G_a\), taking \(n\) to its image in \(k\), is a monomorphism: different integers remain distinct after every base change. It is not affine, because its inverse image of the affine target is the non-quasi-compact scheme \(\mathbf Z_k\). This verifies the manuscript’s objection to the unrestricted inference from monomorphism to affine morphism, without refuting the Picard-kernel theorem itself.

**File/scan:** P57, 57:173 L6–23; 178 L5–15; 179 L3–9  
**Kind:** unpursued implication  
**Brief source quote:** « Prépic »; « Prépic′ »; « enveloppe exacte »  
**Bears on RS:** WG2 §1.1 #3  
**Context search:** Prépic | enveloppe | sheaf | représentable  
**Context comparison:** HUA, “Le foncteur de Picard (pages 172 à 182)”, L1956–2013, restates the chain; SN P04–P05, reporting 57:276–277, concerns later quotient formulations.

The unprimed functor takes a base change to its absolute Picard group, the primed functor records relative classes after open-cover descent, and the final Picard functor enforces the specified flat descent. The distinction explains why the kernel of the first comparison can contain line bundles from the base and why the second comparison can have a Brauer obstruction. Neither quotienting by the base nor sheafifying is by itself a proof of the representability postponed at 57:179.

### Q02 — The requested cohomological formulation is the low-degree Leray sequence

**File/scan:** P57, 57:174 L3–14; 175 L7–14  
**Kind:** own idea  
**Brief source quote:** « donner une formulation cohomologique des raisonnements »  
**Bears on RS:** WG2 §1.1 #3  
**Context search:** Leray | cohomologique | rigidifié | H^2  
**Context comparison:** HUA, “Le foncteur de Picard”, L1966–1983, puts the Leray sequence beside rigidification; CT 47:3 uses the same removal of scalar automorphisms.

**Own-idea confidence and reason:** high: the exact-sequence argument and the section’s retraction are explicit; no new representability theorem is claimed

Under \(f_*\mathcal O_X^*=\mathcal O_S^*\), the low-degree sequence is \(0\to\operatorname{Pic}(S)\to\operatorname{Pic}(X)\to H^0(S,R^1f_*\mathbf G_m)\to H^2(S,\mathbf G_m)\to H^2(X,\mathbf G_m)\), on the site being used. A section makes the last pullback injective, so the obstruction vanishes and the relative classes lift; rigidifying these lifts kills their scalar automorphisms, making their descent isomorphisms unique. This supplies a precise formulation of the requested mechanism, while the separate flat sheafification and its Brauer example require retaining the correct site.

### Q05 — The absolute Picard functor already fails separatedness for open-cover descent

**File/scan:** P57, 57:178 L12–15; 179 L9
