# Documentation index

## Current final records

- `frozen_sha256.json`: the 43 page hashes and combined hash recorded before the context was opened.
- `final_quality_report.json` and `final_structure_validation.json`: final integrity/structure results and their limits.
- `tex_compile_report_final.json`: final 43-page syntax status, with exact per-page hashes; unchanged pages inherit their earlier successful checks, and the three revised fragments were recompiled before the freeze.
- `reading_record.md` and `second_audit.md`: root reader records for the first and second image readings.
- `research_items.json`: structured data for the 94 final research notes; `root_research_items.json` contains the root-authored subset.
- `context_searches.json`: one navigation search record per research item, with bounded excerpts and explicit comparison targets; it is not an assertion that every search hit was used or that a keyword search proves absence.
- `research_calculation_checks.md` and `research_counterexamples_review.md`: expanded calculations supporting the short note bodies.
- `build_research_notes.py`, `assemble_research.py`, and `build_check_events.py`: research-only assembly scripts. They require the separately supplied context texts and never alter the frozen page fragments.

## Historical and provisional records

`checkpoint_status.md` describes the state at the first hourly checkpoint, not the final state. Initial/audited syntax and inventory reports, inventory backups, and the reconciled candidate preserve the work history; they can contain warnings, readings, or row arrangements superseded before the freeze. The authoritative inventory is the root-level CSV.

`research_fourier.md`, `research_jacobians.md`, and `research_picard_context.md` are delegated comparison memos; their candidate-note labels and proposed ledger rows are provisional. The final notes incorporate their findings with the root’s checks and corrections. The mathematical counterexample memo’s CE170 and CE136 labels correspond to final N03–N04 and U03–U04. `research_notes_locator_review.md` records draft issues subsequently corrected in the final notes.

The syntax wrapper and its reports are deliberately limited to syntax: identity definitions for the editorial macros do not display cancellation, insertion, or doubt formatting. No PDF or image-rendered edition is asserted by those reports.

## Source boundaries

Original input archives, manuscript images, Oda’s supplied PDF, and the complete context sources are excluded. The snippets in the search log are bounded navigation evidence. No research memo or external reference was used to modify a frozen transcription reading.
