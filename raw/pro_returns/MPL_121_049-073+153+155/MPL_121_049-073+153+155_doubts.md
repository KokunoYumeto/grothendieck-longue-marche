# Notation register and doubts

## Package notation register

The register began with the first page and was reconciled across all 44 pages after the second visual reading, before the supplied context was opened. This table describes transcription conventions, not mathematical identifications supplied by later sources. The fragments preserve genuine changes of notation; consistency does not mean replacing a written sign by a mathematically expected one.

| Manuscript sign or usage | LaTeX encoding | Locations / distinction retained |
|---|---|---|
| Double quotation strokes | two backticks and two apostrophes | Throughout; quotation characters in TikZ option syntax are not manuscript quotation marks. No guillemets are substituted. |
| Double-stroked number alphabets | `\mathbb N`, `\mathbb Z`, `\mathbb Q`, `\mathbb R`, `\mathbb C` | 121:049,055–059,065,070–073; 153:002–003,010. First-pass bold encodings were reconciled against visible doubled strokes. |

## Reading confidence

High means the feature can be distinguished positively in the image, including an apparently erroneous or unfinished form. Medium means a plausible visual reading with unresolved detail; low means cramped, cancelled, overlapped, damaged, or insufficiently differentiated ink. Confidence is not a numerical estimate of transcription accuracy and is never based on mathematical plausibility alone.

Two short cancelled prose passages remain marked with word-count illegibility macros. No such macro substitutes for a mathematical symbol or a diagram. Drawings, logos, and spatial diagrams are vector reconstructions, not exact facsimiles; their objects, labels, relative positions, and visible stroke directions were compared against the images. An inventory value of yes means the element has been represented, including an explicit doubt where needed, and does not certify that every tentative reading is correct.
