Searches covered cotes 120, 147, 149, 150, 151, public 121 batches 01–04, and 161 modernized summaries with the present cotes 121, 153, and 155 excluded from that broader summary search. The 121 batches overlap this packet, but were searched for the glossary nouns only, without supplying or changing transcription readings. Exact word boundaries separated “ombre” from “nombre”; no OCR was run on the supplied images.

The bounded negative result about the first definitions of “tubulure” and “ombre” is stated as such. Candidate homonyms in other folders were not converted into an identification. Likewise, inspecting selected undated 151 pages does not prove that no date exists anywhere in its dossier.
