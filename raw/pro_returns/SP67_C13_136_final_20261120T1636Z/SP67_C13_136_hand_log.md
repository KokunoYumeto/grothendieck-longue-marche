## Scope of the learned forms

The cross-page review in the doubts file is the consolidated notation table. The entries below preserve the observations made page by page, including which witness was misleading. The following restrictions are part of the hand log:

- The flattened K reading holds at every inspected occurrence on 358,359,361,362 and363, including the deleted occurrence on 363. It does not decide the differently overwritten342 K/P trial.
- Vertical equality pairs recur on 332,333,334,335,339,340,343,357,358,359 and361. The straight pairs are not prose quotation marks. The wavy relations on 337,343,355 and363 were compared separately; unresolved overtracing stays doubtful.
- C versus doubled C is not an invariant alphabet rule for the whole dossier. The337 ambiguity was settled from the adjacent repeated exterior-power notation, while340’s visible inner strokes and its clear plain-C exception were retained. Every later occurrence was checked rather than copied from this rule.
- A star can resemble alpha, but a closed alpha-shaped loop is not automatically a star. The total Gamma notation on 332/335 and the clear loop in 330’s comparison label have different outcomes. The unresolved330 source exponent is still marked.
- Lowercase c is consistent in the four335 coefficients and the 337 rank calculation. Clear uppercase C elsewhere remains uppercase. Similarly, the small shoulder-shaped r on 362/363 is not made n or2 solely from its silhouette.
- Printed music staves are not handwriting. This affected underlines and apparent equality strokes on 340,345,355 and357. The typed351 sheet has different provenance: its underlined matrix y and its spelling are recorded without using them as evidence about Grothendieck’s hand.
- Cancellation scope and insertion attachment must be decided separately from the letters. The cancelled lower draft on 330, source-only cancellation on 348, erased/circled node on 355, full X-crossing on 358 and qualification pour X variable on 360 illustrate different cases. No universal rule about nearby strokes was applied.

Ambiguous forms not settled by a repeated notation remain local doubts: pale prose on 348/350/352; the 330 h/k map label;337 oblique signs;355 raised and lower Hom labels;359’s class letter and product sign; and several densely cancelled trials. A hand habit is not a license to repair a clear mathematical slip.
