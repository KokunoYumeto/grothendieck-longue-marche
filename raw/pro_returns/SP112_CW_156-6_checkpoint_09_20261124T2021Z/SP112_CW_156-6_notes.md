Let M consist of infinitely many incomparable x_n below a top ω and give it the total compatibility relation. This is symmetric, reflexive and downward closed. The family must contain the principal down-set ↓ω=M and every closed subset of it, hence A={x_n}. A sufficient repair is to require every downward-closed subset of each principal down-set to be finitely generated: then a closed subset of a finite union of principal down-sets is a finite union of finitely generated intersections. Under that condition, the family of finitely generated compatible down-sets has the required closed-subset stability; finite union and the pairwise compatibility condition give the displayed binary-union property. The claim itself is preserved without this addition.
