#### Q2.6 — The automorphism parameter and the connecting map

**Kind:** own explanation — explicit calculation. **Anchor:** 151/043, equation (25).

**Context:** `MPL_147_138-190_transcription.tex`, scan 180; current 121/039, the gluing arrow. **Relationship:** explains the parameter in the newly located calculation. **Register:** EXIT_ONE_TYPE P1d1 [return claim retained]. **Confidence:** high; inverse maps and the relevant exact-sequence terms are displayed.

For an abelian group \(A\), every automorphism of \(0\to A\to A\times\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z\to0\) fixing its two ends is
\[
\phi_a(u,n)=(u+na,n),\qquad a\in A:
\]
its value on \((0,1)\) determines it, and \(\phi_{-a}\) is its inverse. Take \(U=S^2\setminus\{\infty\}\) and a disk \(V\) around \(\infty\); the punctured disk \(U\cap V\) retracts to \(S^1\), and both \(U\) and \(V\) are contractible. The Mayer–Vietoris connecting map
\[
\delta:\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z,A)=H^1(U\cap V,A)
\xrightarrow{\sim}H^2(S^2,A)
\]
is an isomorphism because its neighbouring positive-degree cohomology groups for \(U\) and \(V\) vanish. Thus evaluation of the overlap homomorphism at \(1\) gives the same \(a\) that parametrizes the extension automorphism; this checks the concrete correspondence without re-proving the general return recorded as P1d1.
