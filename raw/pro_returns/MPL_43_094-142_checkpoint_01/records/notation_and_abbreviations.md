## Legibility and limits

The first sheet is written in a very rapid hand, with words reduced to nearly horizontal strokes. The cancelled opening, the description of objects in the Weil space, and several abbreviated phrases are particularly uncertain. Individual proposals are marked in place. This note will be updated after all pages and the second reading.
