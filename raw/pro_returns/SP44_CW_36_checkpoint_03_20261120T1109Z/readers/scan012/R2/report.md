Condition a) asks for a reverse homomorphism of algebraic origin, right inverse on the image of i*. It then mentions a hyperplane-section situation, an algebraic Lambda operation, and lifting to characteristic zero with the polarization. These are transcribed claims, not established results of this pass.

Two narrowly bounded checks were actually reasoned through and are separately logged:

1. **36-012-zero-map-implication — targeted proof check.** Assuming the displayed sheaves, the ordinary additive cohomology functor H¹(S,−), and the stated compatibility, a zero sheaf morphism induces the zero H¹ map. Hence the image identified with u(x) is zero. This checks only that implication. It does not prove construction or compatibility, and it does not prove equivalence with checking a single geometric fibre; the required base/sheaf hypotheses are not fully present on this sheet.
2. **36-012-a-right-inverse — targeted proof check.** Let A and B be the displayed source and target and F=i*:A→B. A reverse map V:B→A restricts to V:Im F→A, and `F∘V|Im F=id_Im F` is correctly typed after corestricting F to its image. Thus the author's `inverse à droite` can be retained coherently. Existence and algebraicity of V were not checked.
