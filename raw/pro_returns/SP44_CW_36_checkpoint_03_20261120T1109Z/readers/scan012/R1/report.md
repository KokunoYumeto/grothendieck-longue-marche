## Mathematical reading and actual checks

The page first gives Z-induced maps on direct images and on H^1(S,−), then says that u(x) is the image of u(x′); hence zero for the displayed map is enough to force u(x)=0. Its asserted equivalence with vanishing on the displayed geometric fibre was **not checked**, because this page alone does not supply the full hypotheses needed to justify that inference.

Section 2 supplies i:Y→X, the contravariant i* between degree 2n−1 groups with rational coefficients after correction, and an algebraic reverse map that is a right inverse on im(i*). The directions and degrees parse without changing the ink. The writer qualifies the standard-conjecture remark with **“sans doute”**, and writes **“conjectures standart”** and **“touhours”**. The hyperplane-section/Lambda and lifting claims were transcribed without proof.

Two narrowly scoped checks were actually performed and recorded separately with the permitted type **targeted proof check**:

1. Assuming (**) is H^1(S,Z) and the printed compatibility holds, Z=0 implies H^1(S,Z)=0 and therefore u(x)=0. This does not verify the construction of Z or the equivalence with one geometric fibre.
2. Writing r=i*:A→B and v:B→A, the printed right-inverse-on-image condition is rvr=r. Direct composition gives (vr)^2=v(rvr)=vr and (rv)^2=(rvr)v=rv. This checks the consistency of the local wording, not the existence or algebraicity of v or any conjecture.
