## Consequential decisions

- Scan 010, L07, live direct-image coefficient: The inline coefficient remains f|F rather than being silently normalized to the named sheaf F.
- Scan 010, L15, degree correction: The live degree correction is 2d′; H’s statement that the replacement is absent is contradicted by the ink. The typed d is reused rather than cancelled.
- Scan 011, D1, coefficient of the left direct image: The left coefficient remains unidentified; it is not silently restored to F from the target coefficient.
- Scan 012, L11, corrected projectifs / propres: The live assumption is proper smooth schemes; the cancelled projective condition and mixed type/pen correction are preserved.
- Scan 012, L14, reverse-map notation after inverse: The reverse map’s label remains uncertain: vk is closest to the visible shape, with v* and v^* alternatives; it is not silently normalized to a star.
- Scan 013, D01, punctuation immediately after C: The first displayed source expression literally closes H^1(C) before the coefficient and retains the later two closes; it is not normalized to H^1(C,Q(1)).
- Scan 013, L13, left coefficient correction: The later left rational coefficient contains a cancelled integer coefficient Z under pen Q; the live Q is unchanged, but the correction history is restored.
- Scan 014, D1 terminal parentheses: The first displayed H-group remains short of one closing parenthesis, as written.
- Scan 014, T8 coefficient alphabet: The inline image-group coefficient may be a pen Q over typed Z; the correction and its uncertainty are retained, not regularized from the trace argument.
- Scan 014, T8 terminal parentheses of the i-star group: The inline i*(H-group) likewise retains its missing outer closing parenthesis.
- Scan 014, T17 second product capital: The two ambient-product capitals are not normalized to a common X or Y: the second has an additional unresolved X/Y correction structure.
- Scan 015, Generic point named after soit: The prose point-name is visibly unbarred and has an unresolved s/t overprint; H’s detached overbar is not supplied from the expected geometric-point notation.
