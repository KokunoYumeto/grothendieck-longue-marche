## Legibility and witnesses

- Scan 010: Ordinary typescript is clear. The least secure material is the overwritten first-page coefficient and Leray layers, the brown prefix, and the old characters after Tate. C preserves substantially more revision history than H. H correctly cautions about a chi-shaped machine glyph, but wrongly says the degree replacement is absent and capitalizes a visibly lowercase prose z. The heavy E/Z alphabet choices remain uncertain.
- Scan 011: Live typescript is largely clear. The fused first coefficient, clipped nulle, cancelled extra e, final accent and x-outs are least secure. C preserves the obvious slips and final cancellation better than H, but both omit three smaller overtyped old letters. R1/R3 also missed those layers; R2 identified them. The type/pen provenance of the first 2 remains uncertain.
- Scan 012: Live prose is mostly clear; overtyped old/live endings, the reverse-map mark, and cancellation cells are less secure.
