# Notation table

This table records the package-wide visual conventions used in the transcription. The whole-page and half-page readings were compared again before the transcription was frozen; differences between typescript and handwriting are retained where the ink differs. A notation convention is not an assertion that every doubtful instance has been resolved. The alternatives in the following doubts register remain part of the transcription.

| Recurring ink / distinction | LaTeX used | Visual comparison and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Double quotation marks | two backticks and two apostrophes, ` ``...'' ` | One encoding throughout both cotes, including the nested, unmatched quotation marks in 39/017. No guillemets have been substituted. |
| Exclamation-mark exponent | `^!` | 36/056: the small vertical stroke and dot after Hom are confirmed by the adjacent explicit words “l’exposant !”; not a prime. |
| Primes on maps, schemes and numbers | `j'`, `A'`, `X'`, `d'`, `u'_n` | Compared with exclamation marks and stars; primes in 36/054–058, 062–064, 074–084 and 39/004–020 remain primes. The first correspondence label on 36/077 is ξ′; later ξ₀ remains indexed. |
| Pullback / dual asterisk | `^*`, `_*` | Distinguished from ! and prime: Δ*, f*, E*, k*, inverse/pullback marks in 39/013. The last inverse-map decoration on 39/013 remains doubtful. |
| Plain connecting stroke versus arrow | `\mathbin{-}`, TikZ `dash` / `no head`, or `\unsure{-|\to}` | No arrowhead is supplied merely because a map is intended. Small but visible heads were restored in 39/014, 016 and 017; two short strokes in 39/020 remain explicitly ambiguous. |
| Arrows in both directions / double implication | `\leftrightarrow`, `\Longleftrightarrow`, `\Leftrightarrow` | Checked independently of the argument, especially the substitutions and reformulations in 39/010–013. |
| Equality, isomorphism, tilde | `=`, `\simeq`, `\cong`, `\xrightarrow{\sim}` | Retain number and shape of strokes, and presence or absence of a shaft. Crowded vertical comparisons in 36/062 and 071 remain doubtful. |
| Equals sign used in prose | `$=$` | Retained as ink, including “de =”, “satisfait =”, and “en général = pour ν=1”; not expanded to “même” or another word. |
| Underlining versus a letter descender | `\uline`, `\uuline`; otherwise ordinary text | Compared long leftward g descenders in 39/006, 011, 013, 017 and 019. These are not independent underlines. Actual separate rules below headings, “conditions logiques”, and full assertions remain; the square-ended mark in 39/020 is drawn in TikZ. |
| Hom and sheaf Hom | `\operatorname{Hom}`, `\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}` | Underline is significant on 36/054–056; subscripts gr, fct, ens are kept separately, with doubts where crowded. |
| Biextension category versus classes | `\operatorname{BIEXT}`, `\operatorname{Biext}` | Upper/lower case, underlining and semicolon retained on 36/054–058. |
| Torsors / bitorsors and rigidification | `\operatorname{TORS}`, `\operatorname{Tors}`, `\operatorname{Bitors}`, `\mathrm{birig}`, `\mathrm{sym}` | Case and individual underlines retained. BIRIG/RIG in 36/054 remains uncertain; no abbreviation is expanded in the transcription. |
| Ext with cohomological degree | `\underline{\operatorname{Ext}}^1` | The superscript is the numeral 1, distinct from prime; separate from the inserted A′ in 36/054. |
| Néron–Severi notation | `\operatorname{NS}`, `\underline{\operatorname{NS}}` | Underlining, base subscripts and written arguments retained independently; no uniform sheaf underline is imposed on all occurrences. |
| Picard notation, P versus Pic | `P`, `\operatorname{Pic}`, `\underline{\operatorname{Pic}}` | The crowded target on 36/058 remains `\unsure{P|\operatorname{Pic}}`; later clear P and Pic are not merged. |
| Typescript small o and capital O | `\underline{o}`, `\underline O` | Small underlined o on the reused quotient typescripts; capital underlined O in the annotated invariant-cycle typescript. These differ from the handwritten script structure sheaf. |
| Handwritten structure sheaf | `\mathcal O` | Cote 39 and handwritten geometric pages; the especially compressed O on 36/081 retains an O/alternate-script doubt. |
| Hat on dual action | `\hat\sigma` | 36/053, 055; the hat belongs to σ, not G. |
| Outlined affine-space letters | `\mathbb A`, `\mathbb B` | Reused typescript 36/055. Not identified with the cursive algebra family in cote 39. |
| Underlined numerical Z in typescript | `\underline Z` | 36/061–064: preserve the typed underlined alphabet rather than replacing it by the handwritten doubled alphabet. |
| Doubled handwritten numerical alphabet | `\mathbb Z`, `\mathbb Q`, `\mathbb N`, `\mathbb F` | Compared across handwritten cyclic groups, Tate modules, lattices and power-series examples; former bold N/Z choices were brought into agreement with the doubled strokes. Ordinary variable N remains plain. |
| Multiplicative group notation | `\unsure{\mathbb G|\mathbf G}_m`, with written base indices | Repeated doubled/outlined Gₘ forms in 36/074–079 compared together; the hand-drawn alphabet cannot be confidently distinguished as blackboard or bold, so every such G is marked consistently. The plain Gₘ in the discarded cover title 36/082 is retained. |
| Vertical divisor lattice / its image | `\Delta`, `\Gamma` | Same Greek capitals across 36/061–071; keep the visible omission before `/Z·X₀` in 36/061 rather than supplying Δ. |
| Torsion quantities and multiplicities | `\delta`, `\delta'`, `\delta''`, `\delta_i`, `d`, `d'`, `d^t`, `d_t` | Do not conflate quantities or normalize a subscript to a superscript. The boxed δ/N in 36/065 and dₜ/dᵗ in 36/062 remain doubts. |
| Left n-torsion versus quotient/index n | `{}_n\Gamma`, `\Gamma_n`, `{}_n\delta`, `\delta_n` | Compared throughout 36/062–071; the revised quotient `{}_n\delta/{}_n\delta'` is distinguished from `{}_n(\delta/\delta')`. |
| Generic / special / geometric fibres | `\eta`, `\bar\eta`, `0`, `s`, `\bar s`, `(s)` | Bars, parentheses and primes belong only where written; the differing X/X′ generic-fibre targets in 36/064 are retained. |
