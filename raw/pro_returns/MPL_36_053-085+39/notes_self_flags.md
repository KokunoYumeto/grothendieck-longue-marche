### Scope markers, desired axioms and incomplete arguments

The cover **36/073** says **“(partiel!)”**. This qualifies the orthogonality result’s scope; it is not a statement that every step of the live proof is unchecked. The circled **“!” on 36/062**, the circled injection on **36/063**, and the exclamation-like mark on **36/079** are attention marks without an explicit statement of what is wrong; N22–N25 and N46–N49 keep that distinction.

On **39/006**, the Ado reduction ends in an ellipsis. On **39/007**, **“On veut qu’on ait une réciproque”** introduces the desired Pfaff integration axiom. On **39/016–017**, **“Condition intégrale”**, **“Heuristiquement”**, and **“bien connu”** introduce the quotient/foliation interpretation. These are unfinished constructions, desired axioms or asserted background, not all instances of an explicit verification question (N66–N69, N89).

### Withdrawals retained in the transcription

The relevant withdrawals include the injectivity proof attempts on **36/067 and 069**, the entirely cancelled orthogonality draft **36/081**, the rearranged Néron hypotheses on **36/083**, the deleted converse/inverse argument on **39/004**, the entirely cancelled retraction draft **39/008**, and the cancelled proof routes on **39/019–020**. Their live replacements or later comparisons are identified in N31–N32, N45, N52, N62–N63, N69 and N95. A deleted route does not by itself retract the surrounding theorem, and the final “cqfd.” on 39/020 remains live.
