# SP94_C13_151: consolidated diplomatic transcription

This archive contains consolidated transcriptions of the 24 supplied scan pages from cote 151, in supplied scan order. The images, witness C, witness H, differences list, marks list and preamble were supplied in the original input archive; they are not duplicated here.

The files in `pages/` and `SP94_C13_151_transcription.tex` are LaTeX fragments for the supplied preamble. `SP94_C13_151_changes.md` records the visible evidence and decisions, `SP94_C13_151_collation.tsv` contains substantive differences from H, and `decisions.tsv` follows the order of the input marks list. The inventory, notes, doubts, hand log and error-sheet feedback accompany the transcription.

`crops/` preserves the enlarged visual evidence, including the independent second readings. Coordinates refer to the original JPEG pixels, before scaling or rotation. `second/` preserves the box lists, the first written readings, independent readings and token comparisons; editorial decisions remain separate from those original readings. `reports/`, `drafts/`, `audit/` and `scripts/` preserve working documentation. The drafts are historical first-pass returns, not the final text; the final text is in pages/ and the combined transcription. The scripts assemble and validate records; they perform no OCR and decide no readings.

`progress.log` records actual completion times, checkpoints and final SHA-256 verification. Reading order and reader assignments are in `run_state.json`. Pale foliation in another hand is distinguished from the author's own numbering. The scans may contain unresolved or struck ink; uncertainty is retained explicitly rather than inferred from the expected mathematics.

## Completed scope and verification

All 24 pages have completed the first collation, main-reader adjudication, independent blind reading by a different reader, and main-reader decision on every recorded difference. The final records contain 851 substantive comparison places (244 against C and 717 against H, with overlap), 82 supplied-mark decisions, 410 inventoried elements, 382 blind boxes and 83 mathematical check events. The doubts file contains 206 remaining doubt records.

The original first and second readings and neutral box lists remain preserved; later decisions are recorded separately. Historical drafts, count snapshots, metadata-review findings and notation inventories document earlier stages and are explicitly distinguished from final records. The final notation table is at the start of the doubts file.

Validation checks file completeness, source order, mark order, record totals, crop paths and boxes, reader separation, original reading integrity, and LaTeX fragment structure. No TeX compilation was performed, as requested. The SHA-256 freeze manifest is `audit/frozen_transcription_sha256.json`; final verification is recorded in `progress.log`.
