## Legibility and witness comparison

The ordinary live prose is generally legible. The least secure readings are overwritten or cancelled formulas; small indices and primes crowded against bars; very short interlinear words; slanted marginal notes; and the clipped right edge and unfinished final calculation of scan124. A clear mathematical inconsistency has not been used to repair clear ink. Several literal statements require correction mathematically, but those corrections are confined to the notes and check events.

Witness C preserves much more of the physical state of the manuscript than H, but its unmarked readings, foliation, index letters, line-end hyphens and attachment of insertions also required changes. H sometimes supplied an independently useful lexical or symbolic alternative, yet also regularized notation and arguments, omitted cancellations or marginal material, and used editorial remarks or ellipses in place of a literal reading. Those editorial remarks are not manuscript text. A difference in macro syntax is not counted as a substantive difference. The change counts are counts of recorded places, not error rates: their granularity varies, and a low-confidence disagreement does not establish that a witness was wrong.

Colour, stroke continuity and crossing direction were used to distinguish insertion leaders, cancellation strokes, figure lines and underlines. Reversed show-through is particularly liable to be read as face writing on scans115 and117. Foliation belongs to another hand and is kept separately from the author's text. Missing mathematical signs, unequal continuation patterns and defective delimiters are preserved rather than silently supplied.

Independent image-only readings and main-reader adjudication are complete for all 24 pages. The original 379 committed boxes produced 306 token-level differences and 67 changed box readings. Two separately committed supplementary boxes on scans120 and122 add one further difference and no changed reading: 381 boxes and 307 differing boxes in all. These are box counts, not counts of manuscript errors; a difference can concern notation syntax, a figure's representation or the degree of uncertainty. Every differing box has a recorded main-reader crop decision, and the immutable first readings, independent readings and comparisons remain alongside those decisions.

Of witness C's 123 supplied doubt marks, 66 have been resolved on crops (46 to the first candidate, 11 to a listed alternative and nine to another reading); 57 remain undecided. The final doubt table contains 298 local records, including doubts newly found outside those original marks. The slanted marginal note on scan122 and the overwritten and unfinished formulas on scan124 remain among the least secure passages. A retained primary candidate inside a doubt mark is not a claim of certainty.
