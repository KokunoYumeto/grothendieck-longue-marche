## Additional distinctions for later runs

The existing sheet already identifies most error families encountered here. The following refinements are useful because they can otherwise produce false differences or conceal physical evidence. They are observations from these pages, not claims about every manuscript leaf.

- **An editorial witness note can acknowledge physical state without supplying a reading.** H occasionally recognizes a cancellation, an interlinear insertion or an unfinished sum in a note while its main transcription omits the words or signs. Compare both body and note before classifying a difference as a state omission. Examples: scans121–124; scan124's interlinear trace, margin fragment and unfinished final sum. A general editorial state note does not by itself mean that the local text was marked doubtful.
- **A continuation stroke is part of the written formula.** A dot followed by a dash, two dashes, and a conventional three-dot ellipsis should not be interchanged automatically. Examples: scans108–109,113 and121–124. The script-level representation can differ while the drawn marks agree; adjudication must identify the actual marks.
- **Reversed show-through can resemble a selected marginal note.** Scans115 and117 have inspected areas with no transcribable face text. An empty independent box reading is valid only with an explicit physical finding, never merely because the text is difficult.
- **Separate the visible letter from the modern interpretation of the claim.** The use of “entière” and the different radii on scans120–122, and the modulus conventions in scan124, need local mathematical explanation without changing the words or operators. A conditional proof of an interpretation is not proof of every ambiguous literal sign.
- **A drawn enclosure does not turn its text into drawing.** Open-corner frames in scan124 and the partial brackets and enclosures in scans112,116–118 are reproduced stroke by stroke; the enclosed words and formulas remain searchable TeX text.
- **Clipped ink and a blank operand remain incomplete.** In scan124, the right-edge wedge expansion and the final sum stop in the image. Do not reconstruct their closures or operands from the preceding calculation.
- **A long shared t crossbar can resemble a strike.** The live forms of tout on scans117,120 and121 were compared on their crops. Their horizontal return belongs to the letter construction, not to a separate cancellation. A strike through nearby words does not extend to them merely because its level is similar.
- **A marginal reading can change the statement's subject.** The second reading of scan122's slanted note led to dimension rather than distance and a finite-dimensional-subspace phrase, with its opening pour/sur and capital/relation still uncertain. The first-pass convergence/distance interpretation is explicitly withdrawn; the final mathematical note does not use it as evidence.
- **A possible text character and a nontext ink mark require different treatment.** Scan124's possible left-margin I/II remain ordinary textual doubts. The separate loaded blob and interrupted vertical stroke on its right have no secure character value and are preserved as drawing components. Neither a generic drawing nor a guessed punctuation mark substitutes for the local decision.

The primary category counts above count each substantive change row once, even when that place differs from both witnesses. Certainty-only additions to the doubt record are not included. Low-confidence different primary readings remain unresolved disagreements rather than settled errors. The independent-audit tables preserve differences in presentation and certainty as well as actual corrections, so their counts are not interchangeable with the primary category counts.

The completed independent audit covers 379 original boxes and two separately committed supplements. There were 307 differing boxes in all; 67 original box readings changed and neither supplement changed. The consolidated witness apparatus contains 1,071 rows: 286 against C and 1,013 against H, with overlap between those two counts. Its confidence totals are 717 high, 163 medium and 191 low. These totals describe the final records, not a measured error rate or proof that every low-confidence witness disagreement is an error.
