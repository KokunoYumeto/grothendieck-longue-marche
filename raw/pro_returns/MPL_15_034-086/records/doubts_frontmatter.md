## How to locate and assess a doubt

“Fragment line” gives the exact line in the individual pages/MPL_15_034-086_15_scanNNN.tex file, including its short heading. These scans have no consistent manuscript line numbering, and the prose is reflowed; the locator and quoted reading identify the approximate place without inventing manuscript line numbers. The inventory supplies reading-order blocks, including separate marginal additions and cancellations.

Confidence describes the first proposed reading, not its mathematical plausibility. “Medium” means a plausible visual reading with a real unresolved stroke or typographic distinction; “low” marks overwritten, cramped, unusually shaped or multiply plausible material. No doubtful reading is assigned high confidence merely because it fits the surrounding mathematics.
