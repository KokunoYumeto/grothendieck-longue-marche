### N02 — Independence of the immersion factorization through adjunction

### N30 — A conditional coherence certificate using mates of adjunctions

**Locator:** Cote 33, scan 058 L36–42, scan 070 L9–12, scan 088 L15–18; admitted comparisons.  
**Kind:** own idea.  
**Context:** No matching coherence certificate in supplied results; methodological S19 addition, not a resolution of an arithmetic register ID (search N30).  
**Confidence:** high for the formal argument once its hypotheses hold; no claim that the manuscript supplies those hypotheses.

Starting from “il faudrait vérifier,” one can organize the missing checks by fixing the adjunction $L_f\dashv R_f$, its counit and coherent composition maps for $L_f=Rf_!$. Define each $R_{gf}\simeq R_fR_g$ as the unique mate of the chosen $L_gL_f\simeq L_{gf}$; the two triple-composition paths then have the same mate because the left-adjoint composition maps satisfy their associativity identity. This proves the desired coherence **for those mate-defined maps**, and reduces the manuscript's remaining work to proving that its geometric comparison maps are exactly those mates. It is a useful proof certificate rather than an automatic repair, because scan 058 and the trace-class comparisons identify precisely that last equality as unverified.

Both stalks of $F$ are $\mathbb Z$, whereas $P_XF$ is the diagram $\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_a}\mathbb Z$, whose endomorphisms have matrices $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}u&0\\v&w\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, giving an additive Hom group of rank 3 rather than the asserted rank 2. In particular the nonzero map $(x,y)\mapsto(0,x)$, zero on the open $\{a\}$, is a sheaf endomorphism not furnished by the two coordinatewise endomorphisms of the original stalks. This refutes the natural product-Hom identification under those hypotheses and suggests checking whether the intended statement instead concerned morphisms between families of stalks before applying their product-of-skyscrapers construction.

### N67 — The order convention on open sets conflicts with its own explanation

**Locator:** Cote 33, scan 065 L5–9; typed p.32, upper/middle.  
**Kind:** reading question.
