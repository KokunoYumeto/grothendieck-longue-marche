## Legibility and comparison of the witnesses

The broad outlines of most main-text lines are legible at native resolution, but the dense cancellations, compressed marginal words and small diagram labels are substantially less secure. The least trusted readings are the partly hidden cancelled words, the ornate group and point alphabets, and small overwritten indices. An explicit `\unsure` therefore remains even where a plausible mathematical or grammatical completion is easy to imagine.

Scan008 has a clipped typed area and an unreadable signature. Scans017–020 concentrate very small indices, retracing, partial arrowheads and intersecting graphic layers. The sparse formulas on021 still leave the point glyph and one coordinate uncertain. The slanted note on028, the condensed choices note on031, the overwritten first index and edge-cut word on033, and the nested additions at the foot of035 require particular caution. The pale foliation is a different graphic layer from the dark authorial numbering. Paper texture, faint show-through and isolated specks have not been promoted to text merely because a sentence or formula could accommodate them.

The witnesses help locate questions but neither is treated as authority. C often retains cancelled material, local short forms and awkward punctuation that H smooths or omits. C nevertheless misses some insertion leaders, alters some deletion states, regularises diagram terminals, and uses lowercase or plain alphabets where the later sheets visibly have capital Pi and calligraphic V. H sometimes preserves the correct alphabet or word against C, including the medium-confidence *des* on033, but it also expands abbreviations, substitutes familiar words and supplies complete delimiters or heads. A note in H that explicitly describes a figure or cancellation is considered when deciding whether H actually omits that source feature.

Correction counts are counts of logged places under the stated principal-kind convention; they are not error rates for the two witnesses. Several signs may belong to one complete formula place, and one source place may differ from both witnesses. Uncertainty-only additions, physical line divisions of the same word, and equivalent macro syntax are excluded from those counts. The independent image-only second readings and all root adjudications are supplied separately, so agreement and remaining doubt can be assessed without treating confidence labels as proof.
