## Local refinements for later readers

The recurring problems on these nine pages fall within the supplied ten classes; no additional general error class is claimed. The following local patterns refine the sheet and should travel with this cote’s notation table.

- **J with a long head and a small foot.** The ordered-set family was repeatedly read as I, especially by H (scans009–014,016–017). The head alone does not decide it. Several clear lower returns support J; the compressed NB occurrence on017 remains explicitly uncertain.
- **A descender mistaken for a new insertion.** On010 the alleged inserted ξ is continuous with a preceding-row descender. On015 and017 long g strokes belong to épinglage/épinglés and are not underlines. Follow a stroke to its parent letter before assigning a revision state.
- **Live material between or below cancellations.** On012 the lower bracket and P_{n−1} survive the strike above. On016 the small de between two cancelled spans and the word ayant remain live; the later les/des also has no separate cancellation stroke. A cancelled phrase should not be extended across a white gap solely to make grammar coherent.
- **A letter replaced without erasing its earlier shape.** Scan016 has five ξ-to-α overwrites. The older ξ descenders and the live α bowls must both be represented. The nearby unchanged ξ and the rank subscript n remain as written even though the new notation would favor α and K.
- **Abbreviation points and spelling omissions.** ens, hom, mono and several other contractions lack stops at particular occurrences. The forms applict/applictions on010–011 and014 lack letters supplied by one or both witnesses. Their location and baseline position matter; neither expansion nor a raised suffix is automatic.
- **Detached arms, primes and capped shafts.** The detached upper diagonal of K on017 is its arm, not a prime. The two Z joining strokes there end in tiny caps, unlike the clearer curved arrowhead in X→X̃. The two different kinds of short stroke should not be normalized together.
- **Small punctuation within an uncorrected sentence.** The repeated-prime pairs on009–010, the incomplete parenthesis on012, the unprimed L in015’s closure condition, and the colon after commute on017 remain despite their awkward mathematical or grammatical context. The crop records the mark; the notes state the possible intended reading separately.

## Scope of the counts

The unit is a documented reading feature at a specified manuscript place, not a whole crop or a whole sentence. One crop can therefore support several different features, such as a word’s letters and its separate underline. A single omitted passage is not counted again for every character within it. Resolving an unchanged preferred C reading, adding an uncertainty mark, and changing only macro syntax contribute no correction against C. H’s explicit editorial text is excluded from source-text correction counts.

The first and independent readings, comparison tokens, and final adjudications are retained so that the counts can be checked against the actual alternatives. Confidence labels express local reading judgments and are not estimated error rates.
