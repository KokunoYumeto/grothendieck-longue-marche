## Local recurring features

See the hand log for the local letter forms and typewriter overstrikes. Source blanks, damaged impressions and editorial expansions are kept separate; no earlier numerical error rate is treated as a prior verdict on an individual glyph.

## Refinements for later runs

No additional broad error class is needed beyond the ten in the supplied sheet. These recurring local mechanisms deserve more explicit checks within those classes:

- **A composite symbol can be constructed rather than corrected.** In 32/008, 32/009 and 32/015, the round and crossed type impressions under the derived-tensor L form one tensor sign. Treating one impression as an abandoned R or K invents a canceled letter. The hand log records the local construction, and the L alphabet and its separate underline are checked independently.
- **Two visible layers need not reveal their chronology.** This recurs in 29/115, 29/119, 29/123, 29/127–128 and 32/007, 32/009–010, 32/013–015. A darker impression is not by itself a later one. The transcription retains both recoverable letters and alternative layer orders; it does not convert each composite into the word demanded by grammar.
- **Underline scope can end inside a word or before its mathematical complement.** Examples include the partial homomorphism underline in 29/123, po/urra écrir/e in 29/127, the first canceled a in 32/012, the separate pour underline before f in 32/013, and the separately underlined class designations in 32/014. One continuous emphasis wrapper can silently widen the source scope.
- **The source can distinguish a drawing from a mark with a textual function.** In 32/010 and 32/013, the marginal words stay text while their outline or leader is drawn separately. The short left-facing mark in 32/012 remains a doubtful textual sign. Physical paper damage at the upper left of 32/015 is excluded from the transcription rather than converted into a figure. The visible pen gaps, separate passes and endpoints of actual marginal contours remain part of the drawing.
- **A complete expected word may occupy fewer keyed cells.** The canceled famill on 29/115, clipped montr on 32/010, canceled pr on 32/014, and six-position clas[s/e]s on 32/013 are retained at their visible lengths. A line-broken word is joined only when its continuation is actually present on the same supplied leaf.

These are observations from the listed places, not estimated error rates for unexamined writing. The per-page reports retain the complete local decisions, including doubts that did not change either witness's primary reading.
