## Recurring observations

The table assigns each counted place one primary error-sheet kind. A place can differ from both C and H but contributes once to this table; a resolved witness doubt is counted only when an image decision is recorded. Newly added doubt, retained candidates, candidate reordering and macro-only differences are excluded. These counts describe the logged corrections and do not estimate an overall manuscript error rate.

The error sheet already names the recurring phenomena found here: low cursive bodies obscuring technical vocabulary, short insertion leaders mistaken for parentheses or strikes, abbreviation endings expanded by grammar, tiny primes and indices, and faint non-face marks treated as content. No additional general error category is established by these thirteen pages. The specific recurring refinements are:

- The phi form of the empty set has a small loop and a long descending stem on043–044,046–050 and052. It must be distinguished from a normalized empty-set glyph at each occurrence.
- Panaché has a descending p and one tall h loop; the middle low strokes repeatedly led H to parallèle, par-dessus or other familiar wording on043–053. The gender and final s remain local decisions.
- Onglet is the manuscript's term on049–051. A clear o initial on050 does not automatically settle the crowded initial vowel on049 or051.
- The actual side and attachment of a short addition must be separated from its lexical reading: the sigma labels and cancelled insertion on046, i.e. on047, the defining phrase on048, relation-family additions on049–051, and Σ on052.
- Plain and decorated subset signs are not silently standardized. In052 condition b carries a strictness modifier while the core-union index has a plain subset curve; the notes explain the proper-containment interpretation.
- Joined plus strokes can have a short downward component merging into the following letter. The right-hand genus sign on054 remains qualified despite a mathematically expected plus and the sweep's unqualified reading.
- Drawing retracings are not all marked points or arrowheads. Examples include the outline gaps on043/045/048, the deliberately heavier portion of the049 arc, the chord junctions on054, and the short junction branches and open contours on055.
- Opposite-face attribution matters even for dark marks:055's upper-right fragments reflect054's bi/bi arrows and stem. No show-through is reproduced as a new055 drawing.

No typescript, typed overstrike, personal administrative list, letter, or cover occurs in this selection. Those special cases therefore provide no new calibration evidence here.
