## Legibility and witness comparison

Most main prose is readable, but the most compressed cursive and interlinear revisions remain materially less certain than the broad formulas. The least trusted passages are the heavily cancelled groups on044,047–048 and051–052; the two slanted notes on049; the sideways pencil note on050; crowded subscripts and the Pi/pi distinction on050–051; and overwritten technical terms and the joined genus sign on054. The pencil contours on055 are clearer in their overall arrangement than in the exact endpoints of faint retracings. A qualified transcription records those limits rather than claiming a uniquely recoverable word or topology.

The witnesses are useful locators of disagreements, not independent confirmation of the handwriting. H repeatedly substitutes familiar terms for the manuscript's technical vocabulary: panaché becomes parallèle or par-dessus, onglet becomes angle-like wording, and monorive/birive is replaced by other terms. H also loses short insertions, abbreviated endings and deletion state, supplies punctuation or connective words, and omits readable material behind illegibility marks. C is generally closer in those passages but retains unsupported or uncertain characters, misses local gaps and drawing strokes, and sometimes treats show-through as face content. They locate contradictions and explain why a plausible normalized formula would be tempting; they do not replace the ink.
