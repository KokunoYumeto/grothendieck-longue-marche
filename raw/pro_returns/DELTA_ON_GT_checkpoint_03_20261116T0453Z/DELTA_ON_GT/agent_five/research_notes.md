# Five-point and Atkin–Lehner audit (work in progress)

1. A.3(a), pure frames for arbitrary multipliers, is false. Under the standard genuine GT action on the full sphere mapping class group, every half-twist maps to a conjugate by a pure element of its lambda power. Thus the action on S_n is the identity, since lambda is odd. If alpha is pure and Phi(t)=alpha t^lambda alpha^{-1}, then permutation(t)=permutation(t)^lambda. For complex conjugation lambda=-1, this fails for cycles of lengths 3,4,5. This is a complete finite obstruction and remains true profinitely.

2. Lochak–Schneps (1997), Theorem 2 (pp.573–574), explicitly distinguishes lambda=+-1 mod5 and lambda=+-2 mod5. In the former case f=rho(k)^-1 k; in the latter it is rho(k)^-1 s k, where s=x34*x51^-1*x45*x12^-1. Proposition 3(iii) says these are distinct classes in H^1(C5, pure Gamma5). Therefore the pentagon is NOT always a coboundary condition. In particular Galois elements with cyclotomic multiplier 2 modulo5 are counterexamples to the universal coboundary assertion. On multiplier-one subgroup, the coboundary claim holds.

3. Collas genus-zero (2012), Theorems A and B / Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.10, concern prime-order torsion only. The conjugator in Theorem 3.10 is in the full profinite mapping class group, not required pure or canonical. His genus-one paper uses the subgroup IΓ=GR, not all GT, in its original 2012 theorem.

4. The harmonic polynomial w=4z(1-z) DOES define an abstract degree-two orbifold cover from cone weights (3,3,2) at (0,1,infinity) to weights (3,2,4) at (0,1,infinity). Its other ramification point is infinity, as well as z=1/2. This does not identify the principal congruence covers X(3), X(4) over the j-line: neither Gamma(3) nor Gamma(4) includes the other. A4 subset S4 alone supplies a quotient of the common spherical S4 action, not a map between those two modular covers. Compatibility with the required frames needs an additional comparison.

5. X0(6) genuinely has cusp parameter set {0,1,9,infinity}; explicit eta Hauptmodul h=eta(tau)^5 eta(3tau)/(eta(2tau) eta(6tau)^5), u=h+9. Atkin-Lehner maps are w2(u)=(u-9)/(u-1), w3(u)=9/u, w6(u)=9(u-1)/(u-9). These form the Klein four group acting on the puncture set by double transpositions. Sources verified: Colò thesis Appendix A, Maier paper to be checked.

6. Forgetting a point is a map of pure mapping class groups, or of the subgroup fixing the forgotten label, not of full unordered Gamma5. The abstract identification of the rank-three fibre with pi1 of P1 minus {0,1,9,infinity} supplies no canonical comparison of GT actions. A path or section at 9 enters; preserving that section is an extra statement. Need exact blocking equation.
