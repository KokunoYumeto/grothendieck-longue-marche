## Legibility and witness comparison

Most main-line prose and large formulas are readable in the supplied enhanced images. Compressed endings, very small old letters under replacements, and long cancelled drafts are substantially less secure. The overlapping upper notes on scan005, the rewritten lower half of scan017, the cancelled slanted note on scan018, the heavily revised proof on scans021–023, and especially the lower slanted margin of scan024 deserve human reinspection. A retained uncertainty is a positive report of these limits; the first alternative follows the visible strokes and may be mathematically unexpected.

Witness C generally follows the ink and the deletion/insertion layers more closely, but this pass changes readings in C as well as resolving or adding uncertainty. H is useful for locating alternative readings, yet often expands abbreviations, supplies smoother prose, drops old writing, or regularizes formulas and diagram strokes. Agreement between these two readings of the same image was never treated as independent evidence. The change counts are editorial loci and include uncertainty-state decisions; they are not measured error rates, independent accuracy estimates, or a validation of the error-rate figures in the task prompt. This is an AI visual collation and same-reader second audit, not human verification. Confidence describes the reading of the ink, not the truth of the mathematics.
