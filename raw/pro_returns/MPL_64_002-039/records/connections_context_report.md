### CC01 — Scans 002, 026, 033, 039: S20 remains unestablished here

**Kind:** own comparison; confidence high about this packet’s evidence boundary.

Scan 002 announces four-element sets, squares, octahedra, and free rank-two modules over Z/4Z, while scan 026 defines the module and its reduction modulo 2. The projective line on scan 033 is P(M) over Z/4Z, with six rational points mapping two-to-one to the three points of P(M/2M), and scan 039 recapitulates this finite construction. This differs from the connection data sought by planning S20: the frozen text supplies no Gauss–Manin connection, Cartier operation, or p-curvature computation. Accordingly this packet neither settles the registered MPL93 E14/C29 questions nor supplies P09’s missing cote 159 comparison; the claim is restricted to 64/002–039.

### CC02 — Scans 026–029: a genuine analytic context, without a differential identification

**Kind:** own comparison; confidence high for the documentary link, not a proof of S20.

The nearest supplied primary context is LM_140-3_text.tex, scans 019–020, which describes a complex elliptic curve by a line and a lattice, and a unimodular rigidification by a point of the upper half-plane. Its scans 049–050 then discuss the analytic modular map, the SL2(Z) uniformization, and reduction modulo 2 to the permutation group of three objects; compare LM3 notes, scan 019 R30 and scan 050 R26–R28. The present rank-two Z/4Z construction can therefore be proposed for comparison with finite level data, but that comparison still needs an identified elliptic family and an actual identification of its relevant local system. These contextual uniformization passages add a real genus-one setting without supplying the Cartier/p-curvature or projective-connection map required by S20.

**Context relation:** adds an explicit primary contextual uniformization lead. **Register reference:** planning S20 and MPL93 P09; the LM3 scan-qualified note labels above avoid confusion with repeated R numbers.

### CC06 — Scans 026–032: Hua’s relevant finding, correctly bounded

**Kind:** cross-witness statement comparison; confidence high as a match of stated objects, not an independent proof.

Hua, modernised reading of cote 64, §5 “Modules libres de rang 2 sur Z/4Z,” identifies the canonical equivalence between rank-two modules and triples (I,Q,k), where k identifies the orientations of I with the codiagonals of Q. This agrees at the level of stated constructions with frozen scans 028–029 and the RESULTS_STATE finding 64-gl2-z4-combinatorial-model; the finite verification agent separately checks the actual mathematics. Its accompanying abstract group identification does not, by itself, establish the stronger canonical functor or a priority claim. Hua’s later geometric and transcendental sections are useful leads outside the packet—especially his pp. 130–151, mapped by the supplied README to our scans 131–152—but their contents cannot be imported into the transcription or treated as evidence for a current S20 proof.

**Context relation:** restates the relevant registered finding while bounding what it supports. Hua’s complete modernized file has no lexical match for Gauss–Manin, Cartier, connexion/connection, Fuchs, p-curvature, or p-courbure; the actual analytic material concerns lattices and uniformization, so the absent differential identification should not be silently supplied.
