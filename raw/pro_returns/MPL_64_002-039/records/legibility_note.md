## Legibility and limits

The main writing is generally readable after comparison of the whole scan with both full-resolution halves. Every page received a separate second visual audit; no OCR was used and no page reading was delegated. Small indices, overwritten letters, cancellation strokes, and the dense diagonal additions require substantially more caution than the main text. The least secure passages are the heavily cancelled final lines of 009, the diagonal addition on 018, the small diagonal note on 028, and especially the final overlapping lines of the marginal NB on 035; these were enlarged and/or rotated repeatedly, and unresolved words or symbols remain marked in place.

The register does not warrant that every unmarked word is beyond doubt. Stroke endings, accents, punctuation, and the distinction between retracing and a separate cancellation remain limitations of a diplomatic rendering from these scans. Drawings are TikZ reconstructions of the visible geometry and labels; their coordinates are approximations, whereas the intended topology, marks and written labels have been checked against the images. No mathematical correction was used to settle a reading, and the later research notes cannot modify the frozen text.
