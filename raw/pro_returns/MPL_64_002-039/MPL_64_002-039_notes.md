### C64-Q07 — The published Gauss–Manin–Cartier relationship has precise hypotheses

**Cote 64, scans:** 002–039, whole-packet scope; literature comparison for S20  
**Kind:** unpursued implication  
**Anchor:** S20 is a planning question, not a quotation from the manuscript

For a smooth proper family over a smooth complex curve, algebraic de Rham cohomology carries the Gauss–Manin connection, represented by Picard–Fuchs equations (Katz, 1970, introduction). In characteristic p, Katz’s Cartier description of the conjugate spectral sequence gives zero p-curvature on its E_r-terms for r≥2 under the stated base-change or Frobenius-flatness hypotheses, hence nilpotence on de Rham cohomology; global nilpotence additionally uses the arithmetic-model and local-freeness hypotheses of Theorem 10.0 (Katz, 1970, Theorem 7.4, Corollary 7.5, Theorem 10.0). On a smooth characteristic-p scheme, ordinary Cartier descent concerns integrable connections with zero p-curvature, a stronger condition than nilpotence (Ogus & Vologodsky, 2007, Remark 2.2). These published relationships make S20 a coherent research direction but provide no documentary identification in the unavailable cote 93 and 159 texts.

## VI. Evidence, reproducibility and limits
