### Refinements supported by this run

These observations refine the supplied categories; they do not establish independent error rates or a new taxonomy. Counts above assign each substantive change-log place to one principal category. A single place can involve several physical causes.

| Recurring phenomenon | Places actually encountered | Practical refinement |
|---|---|---|
| A large cancellation is extended by a witness across a separate live continuation, or a cancelled block is treated as live | 078, proof and Hilbert–Schmidt restart; 082, lower conclusion; 083, live continuation between cancelled drafts; 086, convexity argument; 087, opening condition; 088, live convexity clause; 090, conclusion below the cancelled determinant calculation; 094, live de and final p. ex.; 095, live temps and norm-construction wording; 096, entire lower proof and its earlier local deletions; 097, live proposition above the cancelled proof | Follow the start and end of each cancellation stroke.
