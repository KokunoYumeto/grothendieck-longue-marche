## Image evidence and integrity

The transfer adds `crop_index.html` and `crop_index.tsv` for navigating image evidence. Original PNG reference names remain in the records; the image index, alias map and resolver locate their actual stored representations. The included transfer documentation explains native-pixel crop preparation, lossless encoding, retained inspection images where a safe mapping was unavailable, original metadata, and restoration of PNG paths. No attached full scan or prepared half is included.

The raw-stage and transformed delivery manifests have deliberately different scopes. The raw manifest records the immutable pre-transformation member names and bytes. The transformed manifest records actual delivered names and bytes. Each excludes itself from its own member list. The frozen transcription hashes cover exactly the24 pages and combined fragment; crop representation changes do not change those text hashes.

All readings derive from the supplied images. No OCR, external edition, external research or TeX compilation was used. No human verification is claimed. Uncertainties and clear written slips remain in place, with the image-based reasons recorded beside them.
