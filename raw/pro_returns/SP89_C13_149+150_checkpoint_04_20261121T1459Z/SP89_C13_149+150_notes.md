### N03

Independent radial-truncation counterexample to the two NB identities under the literal conditions (a),(b), using the interval-action mapping-cylinder definition supplied for this run. Take T=[0,1], Y={0}, with scalar action mu·t=mu t, and T-prime=[0,a] for 0<a<1. Condition (a) holds because 0≤mu t≤a for mu∈[0,1] and t∈[0,a]. The map h:[0,1]→[0,a], h(s)=as, is a homeomorphism fixing 0 and satisfies h(mu s)=mu h(s), so the restricted action on T-prime is again the absolute cone action. T-prime is also an ambient neighborhood of 0 in T because it contains [0,a), open relative to [0,1]. Thus the literal (b) is satisfied with the same Y={0}. But the intrinsic outer boundary of T-prime is {a}, whereas T-prime intersect dot T is [0,a] intersect {1}=empty. Also, for 0<lambda<1, lambda T-prime=[0,a lambda], whereas T-prime intersect T_lambda=[0,min(a,lambda)]. Taking a=lambda=1/2 gives [0,1/4] versus [0,1/2]. ### N04

Targeted corrected-scope check of the reconstruction and strict correspondence. In a mapping cylinder C(f) of f:B→Y, write q for the quotient, with outer boundary B×{1}. For a closed subset A of B and a subspace C of Y satisfying f(A)⊂C, define S=q(A×[0,1]) union C. Then S has precisely A as its outer slice and C as its base intersection; it is invariant under the ambient scalar action. For every lambda∈[0,1], lambda S=C union q(A×[0,lambda]), and S intersect T_lambda is the same set. The source’s NB identities therefore hold for these full-ambient-ray saturated subcones. Conversely any such saturated S is uniquely determined by A=S intersect dot T and C=S intersect Y. Under the additional strict condition C=f(A), S is determined by A alone, proving the claimed closed-A/strict-subcone correspondence within this stronger class. The scalar-stable truncated example in N03 is not of this form. Closedness of A is retained as the source stipulates; it is not derived from conditions (a),(b) by this argument.

### N05

Independent topological check that the construction in N04 has the expected subspace topology when A is closed in B, without requiring C closed in Y. The natural continuous injection C(f restricted to A:A→C)→C(f) has image S. To prove it is an embedding, let F be closed in the smaller mapping cylinder. Its quotient preimages F_C⊂C and F_A⊂A×[0,1] are closed and compatible at zero. Extend F_C to a closed F_Y⊂Y with F_Y intersect C=F_C. Since A is closed in B, F_A is closed in B×[0,1]. The subset F_Y disjoint union (F_A union (f^{-1}(F_Y)×{0})) of Y disjoint union B×[0,1] is closed and saturated for the ambient quotient: zero-level membership agrees with F_Y, and compatibility of F_A at zero prevents an additional base identification. Its quotient image is closed in C(f), and its intersection with S is exactly F. Thus every closed set of the smaller cylinder is closed relative to S, which proves the embedding. This establishes the conditional construction used in N04, not the unrestricted classification asserted in the source NB.
