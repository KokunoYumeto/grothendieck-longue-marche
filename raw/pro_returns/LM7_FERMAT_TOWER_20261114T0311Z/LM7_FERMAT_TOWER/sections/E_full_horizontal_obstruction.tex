The proof gives both coordinate directions,

\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual obstruction on every horizontal input.}
Choose an ordered geometric Hall lift $g_v$ of
any $v\in V$ into $N_2$. The actual cusp norm
identity of E.85 gives, in the central lattice,
\[
  (u_0g_v)T(d)(u_0g_v)^{-1}d^{-7}=7[v,e_{B_0}].
 \tag{E89.14}
\]
This identity uses only the abelian coordinates
of $g_v$, so its proof applies to all of $V$.
Adding a relative central factor $w_a$ adds
$(T_2-7I)a$. The actual E.77 relation is
therefore precisely
\[
 (I-J_2)\bigl(7[v,e_{B_0}]+(T_2-7I)a\bigr)=0.
 \tag{E89.15}
\]
Its factor-one-half cohomology normalization
gives (E89.2). The bracket identity
\[
 7[(I-T_1)b,e_{B_0}]
       =-(T_2-7I)[b,e_{B_0}]
                   \quad(b\in V^{J_1})
 \tag{E89.16}
\]
proves descent through the entire geometric
abelian gauge. Every bracket contains a cusp
vector, so its free elliptic coordinate is zero.

The exact retained E.74 Smith matrix sends this
ten-column defect to a fifteen-row matrix with
moduli $(3^{11},9^4)$. Rows $4,8,15$ are exactly
(E89.3), with moduli $(3,3,9)$. Their columns
$f_1,B_1,f_2$ form a matrix of determinant $-16$.
Its inverse gives a literal section onto these
three residue coordinates. The complete
fifteen-row map factors through them, with the
entrywise moduli retained in the certificate.
Thus they give both the full image $C_3^2\oplus C_9$
and its exact integral kernel $Q\mathbb Z_3^{10}$.
Here $Q$ is the saved Hermite matrix, with
$\det Q=81$ and local Smith factors
$1^7,3,3,9$.

The actual gauge matrix is
$G=(I-T_1)(I+J_1)$; multiplication by $I+J_1$
has image $V^{J_1}$ because two is a unit.
The exact matrix $Q^{-1}G$ has four unit
Smith factors and six zero factors. Both
inverse matrices are saved. This proves the
free rank-six zero locus and the absence
of a remaining torsion coordinate there.
Direct multiplication proves that (E89.7)
is integral on $Q\mathbb Z_3^{10}$, kills $G$,
and is inverse to the six columns (E89.4).
It therefore gives every cohomological
coordinate, including every preimage outside
the coefficient kernel of E.87.

The relative correction (E89.8) is obtained
from the exact E.75 Smith inverse for

E.85. More directly, both equations in
(E89.9) are exact rational matrix identities
over $\mathbb Z_{(3)}$, verified for all

\emph{The actual finite character fixing the target residue.}
For $((x^Ay^Bc^n),(x^{A'}y^{B'}c^{n'}))\in\mathcal J_2$
put $p=A$ and define
\[
          \vartheta=n+n'+p(p-1)\pmod3.
 \tag{E89.17}
\]
This is a genuine continuous homomorphism
$\mathcal J_2\to C_3$. To verify it, use
the exact Hall product
\[
  (A,B,n)(D,E,r)=(A+D,B+E,n+r-DB).
 \tag{E89.18}
\]
In the joint abelian lattice the two pairs
are $(p,p-L)$ and $(3m-p,3m-p-L)$.
The two central cross terms reduce to
$pq$ modulo three. The difference of the
two polynomial terms $p(p-1)$ under
addition is $2pq=-pq$ modulo three.
They cancel, proving the homomorphism.
The genuine operators of E.80 give
\[
    \vartheta\mathcal T=\vartheta+p\pmod3,
             \qquad\vartheta\jmath=\vartheta.
 \tag{E89.19}
\]
These follow by substituting
$T_\mu(A,B,n)=(7A,7B,49n+7\mu B)$
and $I(A,B,n)=(-A,-B,n)$ in (E89.17).

The pullback of this character to $N_2$
factors through $V$, since it is a homomorphism
to an abelian group. Its values on $A_i$
are zero: the two maps fill these cusps.
Its values on $C_1,C_2$ are zero as well:
one factor is a conjugate of a third power
of the infinity peripheral generator,
and the other is trivial. Formula (E89.17)
vanishes on every such conjugate. Its value
on $B_0$ is zero by the actual normalization
$d\mapsto(y,y)$.

Write the remaining possible character row as

 \tag{E89.20}

 \tag{E89.21}

This retains every possibility for the actual

\[
                   \vartheta(g_v)=2m\pmod3.
 \tag{E89.22}

Thus no unevaluated handle coefficient enters
