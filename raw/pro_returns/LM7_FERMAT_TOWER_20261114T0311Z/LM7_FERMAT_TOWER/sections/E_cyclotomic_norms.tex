\subsection{Infinitely many ramified directions from exact polynomial norm systems}

\begin{statement}{E.48}{Proved}{Cyclotomic-polynomial norms and a compact product of independent section classes}
Use E.46 with a rational $a\ne0$, the first cocycle
$A=3\kappa_3(a)$, and the actual algebras
$E_{A,n}=\mathbb Q[X_n]/(X_n^{3^n}-a^3)$.
For every $m\ge1$ prime to three the rank-three
algebra norm has the exact identity
\[
 N_{E_{A,n+1}/E_{A,n}}\Phi_m(X_{n+1})=\Phi_m(X_n).
 \tag{E48.1}
\]
There is no omitted sign or unit. Whenever
$\Phi_m(a^3)\ne0$, these elements are units of the
finite etale algebras and
\[
        u_{n,m,z}=\Phi_m(X_n)^{3z},\qquad z\in\mathbb Z_3,
 \tag{E48.2}
\]
is an actual norm system in (E46.4). Its scaled
toric coordinates are
$\bigl(\kappa_3(a),z\kappa_3(\Phi_m(a^3))\bigr)$.

In particular fix an integer $a\ge2$, and enumerate
the primes at least five as $\ell_0,\ell_1,\ldots$.
Put $B_j=\Phi_{\ell_j}(a^3)$. The formulas
\[
 \begin{split}
 u_{n,\mathbf z}
     &=\prod_{j\ge0}
           \Phi_{\ell_j}(X_n)^{\,3^{j+1}z_j},
       \qquad\mathbf z=(z_j)_j\in\prod_{j\ge0}\mathbb Z_3,\\
 (\kappa_3(a),\beta_{\mathbf z})
     &=\left(\kappa_3(a),
              \sum_{j\ge0}3^jz_j\kappa_3(B_j)\right)
 \end{split}
 \tag{E48.3}
\]
give a closed topological embedding of the full compact
product $\prod_{j\ge0}\mathbb Z_3$ into this global
first-coordinate section fiber of $H$. The series and
products converge in the multiplication-by-$3^r$
completions specified in E.46. The image is torsion-free.
The toric projection itself is injective on this family.

The integers $B_j$ are greater than one, pairwise
coprime, and prime to $3a$. For each $j$, choose any
prime $p_j\mid B_j$. Its toric inertia coordinate is
exactly
\[
       v_{p_j}(\beta_{\mathbf z})
          =3^jz_j\,v_{p_j}(B_j).
 \tag{E48.4}
\]
Thus these are independent ramified directions at
distinct primes away from $3a$. The choice $z_j=1$
for all $j$ gives a continuous section with nonzero
toric inertia at infinitely many primes.

For clarity, the universal rank-three norm for the
remaining cyclotomic indices is also determined:
\[
 N_{Y^3=X}\Phi_m(Y)=
 \begin{cases}
     \Phi_m(X),&3\nmid m,\\
     \Phi_{m/3}(X)^2,&3\mid m,\ 9\nmid m,\\
     \Phi_{m/3}(X)^3,&9\mid m.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{E48.5}
\]
The geometric target and its comparison kernels are
the actual $H$ and (E47.8). All section classes in
(E48.2)--(E48.3) are obtained through the proved
norm-to-section isomorphism (E46.4).

\emph{Manuscript: \S51.16, folios733v--736v,
scans172--178, (371)--(379), Part E(c),(d).
The program \texttt{e\_cyclotomic\_norms.py} retains
the exact resultant and convergence certificates.}
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
Work first over the polynomial ring $\mathbb Q[X]$
with the free rank-three algebra
$\mathbb Q[X,Y]/(Y^3-X)$. Over a ring containing
a primitive cube root of unity, the three embeddings
send $Y$ to $Y,\omega Y,\omega^2Y$. For every
root of unity $\xi$ their product is
\[
          N(Y-\xi)
          =(Y-\xi)(\omega Y-\xi)(\omega^2Y-\xi)
          =X-\xi^3.
 \tag{E48.6}
\]
The leading product $\omega^3$ is one, and the
constant term is $-\xi^3$, fixing the sign.
Multiplicativity of the determinant norm gives
\[
       N\Phi_m(Y)
          =\prod_{\operatorname{ord}(\xi)=m}(X-\xi^3).
 \tag{E48.7}
\]
When $3\nmid m$, cubing is a permutation of the
primitive $m$th roots. For $m=3s$ with $3\nmid s$,
its image consists of the primitive $s$th roots,
each with multiplicity two. When $9\mid m$, its
image is the primitive $(m/3)$th roots, each with
multiplicity three. This proves every case of
(E48.5) as an identity over $\mathbb Q[X]$.
Specialization commutes with the determinant norm,
including for products of fields, so (E48.1)
holds in the exact algebras of E.46.

Iteration down to level zero gives the total norm
$\Phi_m(a^3)$. Its nonvanishing is exactly
invertibility of $\Phi_m(X_n)$ in the finite
etale algebra. Taking continuous three-adic
powers proves norm compatibility in (E48.2).
The level-zero element is the cube
$\Phi_m(a^3)^{3z}$; the map (E46.7) divides
this Kummer coordinate by three, giving the
asserted toric coordinates.

Let $B=a^3>1$. For distinct primes $\ell,r$,
a common prime divisor $p$ of $\Phi_\ell(B)$
and $\Phi_r(B)$ cannot divide $B$. The order
of $B$ modulo $p$ then divides both $\ell$
and $r$, so $B\equiv1\pmod p$. But
$\Phi_\ell(B)\equiv\ell$ and
$\Phi_r(B)\equiv r\pmod p$, forcing $p$
to divide both distinct primes. This proves
pairwise coprimality. Each sum
$\Phi_\ell(B)=1+B+\cdots+B^{\ell-1}$
is greater than one and prime to $a$.
For $\ell\ge5$ prime, its value modulo three
is $1$, $\ell$, or $1$ as $B$ is respectively
$0$, $1$, or $-1$ modulo three. It is
therefore also prime to three.

At coefficient exponent $r$, every factor
of the product (E48.3) with $j+1\ge r$
is trivial. Thus the product exists in
every completed algebra, varies continuously
with the product topology on $\mathbf z$,
and is compatible under all norms. Its
level-zero element is again a cube.
This proves that it defines actual global
section classes by E.46, including when
infinitely many coordinates are nonzero.

The Kummer valuation at $p_j$ receives a
contribution only from $B_j$, giving (E48.4).
The positive integer $v_{p_j}(B_j)$ is a
nonzero element of $\mathbb Z_3$, so equality
of toric coordinates forces equality of
every $z_j$. This proves injectivity and
torsion-freeness. The target cohomology
quotient is Hausdorff: coboundaries are
the continuous image of the compact module
$J_A$ in the Hausdorff uniform cocycle
space, hence form a closed subgroup.
A continuous injection from the compact
product is consequently a homeomorphism
onto a closed image. This proves the
topological assertion, as well as the
claimed infinite ramification.

The program checks (E48.5) by exact
resultants for $1\le m\le45$, verifies
108 total norms in the actual radical
etale algebras, and checks 84 coprimality
identities with explicit supporting
prime factorizations. It also records
the exact coefficient bounds for the
infinite product. The universal
determinant identity and the preceding
convergence proof establish all levels.
\end{proof}
