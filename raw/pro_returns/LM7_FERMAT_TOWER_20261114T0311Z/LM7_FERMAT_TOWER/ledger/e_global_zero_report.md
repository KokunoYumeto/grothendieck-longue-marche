## Exact maps and proof

The first cocycle is normalized uniquely by the geometric first coordinate s=-a(c)/2. Residual conjugation is precisely a coboundary in J=(3,U)Z_3[[U]]. The Galois-module isomorphism sends a measure to its augmentation and its restrictions to the annuli 3^j Z_3^times. Its inverse is the centered series

nu delta_0 + sum_j (mu_j - aug(mu_j) delta_0).

At positional level Z/3^k, every term with j>=k is zero. This proves actual continuity and explains why unrestricted products, with no convergence condition on their coordinates, occur.

Finite Shapiro evaluation at the position one identifies an annular class with Kummer classes of K_n=Q(zeta_(3^n)); the positional pushforward is actual corestriction and hence the field norm. The Shapiro identity explicitly proves that natural field Galois action corresponds to inverse deck translation. This is retained in both the root-of-unity line and the group-ring coordinates.

Every norm-coherent multiplicative class has zero valuations away from 3: after finitely many layers, each fixed prime has residue degree multiplied by 3 at every step, forcing each compatible valuation into all powers of 3. The kernel of all completed valuations is exactly the completion of the 3-units, proved using the free principal-divisor image and the prime-to-3 S-class group.

The 3-primary class group of every K_n vanishes. Lemmermeyer's opened Theorem 1 applies with a single totally ramified prime and no infinite ramification. Its ambiguous class number has no factor 3 by induction from the Eisenstein PID. A nonzero finite 3-group under a group of order 3 has nonzero invariants. The real subfield's 3-primary class group injects into the complex one because ideal extension followed by norm is multiplication by 2.

The opened proof of Sharifi's Theorem 4.3.3 gives the exact real circular-unit index and normalized independent generators. For epsilon_(n,a)=(1-zeta_n^a)(1-zeta_n^-a), the exact relation epsilon_(n,a)/epsilon_n = eta_(n,a)^2 shows that the subgroup of real 3-units generated by all epsilon conjugates has index 2^(3^(n-1)-1) h(K_n^+), prime to 3. These conjugates therefore give a basis of the completed real 3-units. The minus part of the completed complex 3-units is exactly mu_(3^n), by the quotient u/conj(u) and the elementary root-of-unity criterion. Thus the finite completed 3-unit group is actually mu_(3^n) + Z_3[Gamma_n].

Norm sends zeta_(n+1) to zeta_n and epsilon_(n+1) to epsilon_n. On their exponents these are reduction and group-ring projection, respectively. This evaluates the norm limit as Z_3(1) + Z_3[[Gamma]], with explicit generators at every level.
