E.33 computes the continuous arithmetic kernels left in E.30. Every finite cyclotomic stage is a free prounipotent group of rank r2+1. Its abelianization is explicitly the scalar line plus the cyclotomic field's complex-conjugation minus subspace, and the transition is the field trace. Compatible free generators on the cofinal sequence of conductors 4j! turn the entire inverse system into free groups with deletion maps.

The proof derives unramifiedness away ell from the tame relation and unipotent adjoint eigenvalues, uses the opened Brumer application in Sharifi's author notes, derives degree-two vanishing from Milne's exact Euler characteristic, and proves the required unipotent-completion cohomology injection directly. It covers ell=2 as well. The abstract rational version in E.30 is not identified by scalar extension with this continuous calculation.

The exact cyclotomic-field program computes all divisor trace maps through conductor24, their Galois equivariance and all tower compositions, and the free-Lie ranks through degree8. Saved output and full rational matrices accompany the code.
