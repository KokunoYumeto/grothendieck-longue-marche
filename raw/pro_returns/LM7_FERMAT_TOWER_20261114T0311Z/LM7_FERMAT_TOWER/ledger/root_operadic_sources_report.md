### Further lifting-source checks

For D.13 we checked a purported surjective comparison in Martin Gonzalez,
*Contributions to the theory of KZB associators* (2018), Remark 2.6.13,
printed page 91. The original page was rendered and inspected: the arrow
from the profinite GT group to its pro-l version is indeed double-headed.
The preceding Definition 2.6.10 says those versions are not developed in
the thesis, and the displayed remark supplies no proof of surjectivity.
We therefore did not use that arrow as a lifting theorem. The exact source is
https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~calaque/students-Martin_Gonzalez_Thesis.

We then opened the cited original book of Fresse, *Homotopy of Operads and
Grothendieck–Teichmüller Groups*, Part 1, Theorem 11.1.7 and Chapter 12.
Theorem 11.1.7 identifies the Malcev operadic automorphisms with Drinfeld's
pairs in that completion. Chapter 12 constructs the profinite arithmetic
action. These checked passages do not construct the required reverse map
from a pro-l special leading vector to a full profinite peripheral action.

Horel's original *Profinite completion of operads and the
Grothendieck–Teichmüller group*, Theorem 7.2, Corollary 7.3 and
Theorems 8.2–8.4, were also opened with their proofs. They identify the
profinite operadic endomorphism and automorphism objects with the profinite
GT monoid and group. That identification gives another presentation of the
source; the proofs do not solve the lifting equation from the pro-l target
needed in D.13. The inspected author PDF is
https://geoffroy.horel.org/Profinite%20completion%20of%20operads.pdf.

For comparison we reread Drinfeld's original English translation,
Section 4, printed pages 846–847. It defines the profinite and pro-l groups
as units in their respective equation monoids and constructs natural
homomorphisms between the indicated completions. We retain precisely those
arrows. We also reread Harbater–Schneps, Introduction 0.1–0.3, including the
full profinite Main Theorem and the Belyi subgroup definition; those give
the exact full-source interpretation already used in the paper.

The Coleman original-source route was extended through its verified DOI
10.2969/aspm/01710055 and the Mathematical Society of Japan's Volume 17
catalogue. The DOI resolves to the Project Euclid record
https://projecteuclid.org/euclid.aspm/1529259065. The normal request returned
an access challenge, and the browsing reader did not obtain the original
paper. Its original-proof status remains unverified. No mathematical
conclusion is inferred from the failed access.
