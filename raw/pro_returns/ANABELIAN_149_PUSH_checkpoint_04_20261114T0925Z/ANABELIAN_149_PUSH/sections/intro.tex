\section*{Scope and proof conventions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Scope and proof conventions}
We use the full arithmetic quotient by the maximal geometric prime-to-$p$ kernel, retaining the whole absolute Galois group of the base. Conjugacy of sections always means conjugacy by the geometric kernel. Every restriction to a smaller quotient proves only the corresponding necessary condition unless a converse is supplied. The attached prompt fixes the item labels; our statement identifiers have the prefix of the relevant part.
