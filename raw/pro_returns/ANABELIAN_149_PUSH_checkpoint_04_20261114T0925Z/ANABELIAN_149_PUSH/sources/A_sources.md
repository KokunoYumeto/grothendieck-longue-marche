## All-characteristic cyclic and abelian extension

A.21 now includes characteristic two. Its target x²+x=z^e is treated by a complete direct Cartier calculation, including the absence of geometric 2-primary Jacobian torsion. The chosen nonzero odd-torsion inversion fibres are unramified even when inversion is wild elsewhere. A.23 proves the all-degree cyclic theorem by separate outer degree and inner full-index-branch inductions; A.24 uses character quotients and retains stabilizers at ramified fibres. These extensions use the previously opened sources and the new proofs in the manuscript, rather than an unverified general universality theorem.

A.25 proves the double-cover branch-pair induction over an elliptic base, with auxiliary genus-two covers supplied by divisibility of Pic^0. A.26 gives the S3 quotient criterion; its explicit genus-six branch tuple is realized using the same opened SGA1 XIII2.12 prime-to-p presentation, not an unverified existence assumption. A.27 gives cyclic degrees 3,4,5,8 and abelian exponent dividing 120 over elliptic bases in odd characteristic. Its Kummer norm, even-valuation involution check, unramified quadratic twisting via E[4]→E[2] and E[8]→E[2], the bielliptic reflection quotient in degree eight, branch-count induction, and normalization assertions are proved in the manuscript and independently reviewed by root. A.29 now subsumes this positive conclusion using an independent proof; none of these broader theorems is attributed to an external source.

A.29 removes the elliptic-cover exponent restriction completely in odd characteristic and treats supersingular elliptic quotients in characteristic two. Its divisor identity q_i=binom(i,2), exact periodic second differences, local Kummer ramification, and character/inertia recovery are proved directly. The auxiliary separation inputs are precisely A.19 and the Cartier/logarithmic-differential argument of A.21; the characteristic-two supersingular normal form is justified in the proof by the explicit Weierstrass inversion formula. The étale lifting uses A.3, with its geometric Galois-closure hypothesis checked by a finite wreath-product bound. Root independently reviewed the complete written proof. Exact finite verification is in code/A_elliptic_divisor_certificate.py and .json; it does not replace the universal identities. This result makes no assertion for ordinary elliptic quotients in characteristic two or general nonabelian covers. A.25 and A.27 remain as separate earlier proofs.

A.29a is a direct, exact obstruction for an ordinary characteristic-two elliptic auxiliary. Its flex, genus, Cartier calculation, finite counts, Newton identities, and all cyclotomic exclusions are written out or verified by the standard-library code/A_ordinary_elliptic_obstruction.py and .json. The Frobenius/point-count correspondence is the already opened Milne II, Theorem 1.1; the supersingular Frobenius comparison is the proved A.10. No point-separation counterexample is inferred, and no statement about further odd étale covers of that auxiliary is made.

A.29b treats one such further cover explicitly. Its Kummer rank, genus, Heisenberg action, and the surjectivity of three Jacobian pullbacks are proved directly. The exact norm operator identity, including its distinction from the geometric elliptic-translation relation, is checked in code/A_cubic_refinement_certificate.py and .json. This is a bounded method obstruction, with no external isogeny decomposition assumed and no conclusion about arbitrary odd étale covers.
