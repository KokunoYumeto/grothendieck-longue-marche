The local quotient is valid with arbitrary infinite ramification. The original tripod action is unramified at q different from 5. On inertia the Kummer twist is a homomorphism into the pro-five abelian group; wild inertia and the prime-to-five tame factors act trivially. The pro-five tame resolution therefore gives H1(I,W_r)=W_r/(mu_q-1), including when that operator has a kernel. Arithmetic Frobenius acts on this quotient as q^{-1} rho_s(sigma_q), with the inverse q arising from precomposition on the inertia generator. The sum J_q is the sum of two compact additive images and is closed. Local H1 is finite at each finite coefficient level, so its first derived limit vanishes.

At the global finite levels, odd-primary cohomological dimension at most two makes B_(r+1) -> B_r onto. Finite Poitou–Tate applies separately to each W_r: each finite action has finite ramification even when their inverse limit has no common finite ramification set. Local duality at five makes its H2 summand surject onto the dual global invariants, so it can absorb every reciprocity sum of a finite-support family away from five. The kernel K_r is an extension of two finite groups and is finite.

The inverse systems of finite kernels are Mittag–Leffler, with no need for their individual transition maps to be onto. This proves the stated inverse-limit surjectivity and both compact exact sequences. The finite-precision support condition is exactly the inverse limit of discrete direct sums. The proof of openness is correct: stabilize the image K_t -> K_r, lift a target vanishing at t, and subtract a compatible kernel element with the same r-component.

The uniform-cochain quotient topology agrees with the inverse-limit topology after removing its closure of zero. A finite-valued coboundary witness can be lifted continuously, so each reduction kernel has representatives uniformly in m^r. The inverse limit of countable discrete groups is complete, countably based and separable.

The non-local-compactness argument is valid at every precision. Infinitely many primes split the finite action on W_(r+1) and the indicated cyclotomic field. Their local kernels between levels r+1 and r are the nonzero m^r/m^(r+1) pieces. Surjective finite-level transitions lift every finite-support choice to the inverse limit. Thus every basic open subgroup has an infinite discrete quotient and cannot be compact.
