# A54 primary sources and exact scope

All entries below were opened in the primary text on 2026-11-15.

- **Milne, Abelian Varieties**, version2.00, Theorem10.15 and Lemma10.16, printed pp49–50: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AV.pdf . Web refs turn822view0, turn823view0, turn832view0. The integral map Hom(A,B) tensor Z_l to Hom(T_l A,T_l B) is injective, with torsion-free cokernel. Used for faithful specialization and for a bound on the rational endomorphism centralizer in the special fibre; no Tate-surjectivity theorem is assumed.
- **Milne, Lectures on Etale Cohomology**, version2.21, Theorem25.1 p147 and Theorem29.4 p165: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/LEC.pdf . Web refs turn822view1, turn823view2, turn823view3. Used for the actual primitive Kummer character sum; all four local characters are nontrivial, so the compact-support eigenspace is the proper primitive eigenspace. The F_q² character is the norm extension of the same chosen character.
- **Milne, Shimura varieties and moduli**, Theorems4.4 and7.12, pp489,508; CM-type torus definition p509 and equivalence with CM abelian varieties p519: https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/svhIP.pdf . Reopened refs turn828view0, turn830view1, turn830view3, turn832view1. Used only for polarizable weight-one full faithfulness and the standard CM/Mumford–Tate torus equivalence. No arbitrary higher-weight Hodge-realization implication is used.
- **Ruppert, Torsion points of abelian varieties in abelian extensions**, §1 Corollary1, p1: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9803169 . Reopened ref turn834view0. A rational Galois-stable Tate line has infinite torsion over the maximal abelian extension and hence forces a CM abelian subvariety. Applied after finite extension and after proving the actual B8 factor has no geometric CM factor.
- **Brinon–Conrad, CMI Summer School Notes on p-adic Hodge Theory**, Theorems2.2.3 and2.4.6, printed pp13,20: https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/notes.pdf . Reopened refs turn834view1, turn837view0 and turn837view1; exact labelled-profile argument is the one independently checked in A48. Equality of two global characters after finite extension forces equality of all translated quartic CM-type indicators.

The quotient identity, group-algebra factor identification, cyclic20 model, differential signature, complete character sums and quadratic square-class tests are proved/computed here, not attributed to an external source. Character conventions fix K=Q(zeta20), i=zeta20^5, zeta5=zeta20^4, delta=tau*c^4, and chi(g)=zeta20 at the chosen place zeta20→g^((q−1)/20).

Finite extensions used to construct the map or define all endomorphisms can increase residue degrees. This is why the proof verifies K[pi_q^n]=K[pi_q] for every positive n before comparing the two global quadratic fields.
