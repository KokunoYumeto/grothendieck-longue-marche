D.76: exact full coefficient module after the pants fixed-subgroup theorem

The new proof identifies the actual section augmentation module
Delta_T with F2[[E]]^2. The argument uses only the specified pants
extension, D.74's full self-centralizers, and explicit coefficient
maps. It does not require an additional real-projective decomposition
theorem.

The short exact coefficient sequence
0 -> M -> M tensor F2[C2] -> M -> 0
has the actual quotient character u as its connecting cochain.
Its middle module is induction/coinduction from F, whose degree-two
cohomology vanishes. Hence cup-u is onto in degrees one to two.
Brown restriction in degree two and period-one cohomology of each
actual section imply degree-one restriction is onto. The actual
relative augmentation sequence gives Ext^1(Delta_T,M)=0 for every
finite M. Compatible finite lifts split a compact free presentation
of Delta_T, proving compact projectivity.

Restriction to the full free F gives a free module of rank four.
Frobenius reciprocity with the same coefficient sequence therefore
gives dim Hom_E(Delta_T,M)=2 dim M for all finite M. Every finite
group-ring coinvariant is projective; its semisimple top is the top
of a rank-two free module. Nakayama and projectivity give an actual
finite free isomorphism. Compactness of all finite isomorphism sets,
using common finer quotients rather than onto transition maps,
produces compatible isomorphisms and the claimed full inverse limit.

The proof separately keeps the actual incidence map formed from
e_1-e_0 and e_2-e_0. Its cokernel is precisely the augmentation kernel
of the coset space E/H for the closed subgroup H generated by those
three section representatives. It is onto, hence invertible after
the module identification, exactly when H=E. Thus the proof supplies
an exact module test for the remaining generation question, rather
than replacing that incidence map by an arbitrary free-module basis.

Four actual involutions s, xs, fs, yfs generate E. The unique D.74
element h gives fs=h^-1 s h, exhibiting the duplicated class.
The triple s, xs, h y h^-1 s generates K semidirect C2 with
K=closure<x,h y h^-1>. Neither K=F nor the remaining third-cusp
equation is asserted.
