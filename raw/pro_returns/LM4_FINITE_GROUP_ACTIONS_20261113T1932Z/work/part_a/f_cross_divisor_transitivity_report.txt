Meridian centralizer lifts and normal generation
------------------------------------------------
Remove from each cross divisor its intersections with the other seven.
The remainder is connected, and its fundamental group maps onto that
of the full divisor by loop transversality. A sufficiently small circle
bundle in its complex normal line lies in the ten-point configuration
space. Its fiber generator is central in its fundamental group because
the line has its complex orientation, and its projection to the base
fundamental group is onto. With the selected source whisker, this fiber
generator is exactly the positive primitive source collision meridian.
Consequently its discrete centralizer maps onto the divisor image.
Completing this actual homomorphism gives a compact centralizing image
covering the full divisor subgroup; no injectivity of that completion
is required.

For each selected meridian nu and every g in the full ten-point group,
transitivity factors varpi(g)=gamma(l)*p with p in its divisor subgroup.
Lift gamma(l) by h in H-dagger and lift p by c centralizing nu. Then
g=d*h*c with d in D+, and g*nu*g^-1=d*(h*nu*h^-1)*d^-1. The middle
conjugate belongs to K+, since K+ is the kernel in the actual source
row H-dagger->L. All ambient conjugates of the eight F19 normal
generators thus lie in the closed D+-normal closure of K+. F19
identifies their ambient normal closure with D+, proving the claimed
normal-generation equality. Passing to D+ abelianization kills the
D+ conjugations. The image of compact K+ is closed, so it equals
the entire abelianization.

In every continuous finite nilpotent quotient P of D+, the image of K+
normally generates P. Every maximal proper subgroup M of P is normal:
choose the first upper-central-series term not inside M; one of its
elements outside M normalizes M because the preceding term is inside M.
Maximality forces its normalizer to be P. A proper image of K+ would
lie in such a normal maximal subgroup, contradicting normal generation.
Thus K+ has full image in every such finite quotient. Its image in their
inverse limit is compact, closed, and dense, proving the actual
maximal pronilpotent quotient surjection.
