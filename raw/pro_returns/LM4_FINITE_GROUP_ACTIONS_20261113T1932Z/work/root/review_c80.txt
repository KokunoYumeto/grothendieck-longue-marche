ROOT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT: C.80

Decision: PASS for every based S^n family, n>=1, in the specified
actual closed-disk relative centralizer. The independent audit is
recorded separately. No pi_0 or whole-group contraction is asserted.

The source group is the closed actual map-and-inverse centralizer.
The sphere image together with its inverses is compact and path
connected. Each finite word has a based path in its fixed finite
power, by choosing a path for each of its finitely many letters.
No continuous selection of paths over the sphere is needed or used.
The increasing base maps and transitive complementary intervals
therefore have exactly the same source-level proofs as in C.75.
The derived base image is transitive in the nonabelian case: its
fixed set is invariant under the transitive full image and empty,
and every derived orbit is open by the actual based paths.

The sphere short-pair space with geodesic distance at most delta<pi
is compact. Moving its first coordinate along the unique shortest
geodesic to its second retracts it onto the path-connected diagonal.
The increment image is thus compact and path connected, contains
the identity, and is symmetric by exchanging the pair. Uniform
continuity in the original disk group makes it shrink to identity.
Finite subdivision of any sphere path gives the required telescoping
character identity. The proved five-letter additive-path formula
and at most three basis vectors give the same fifteen-increment
neighborhood. The actual finite-dimensional character image is a
vector space before closure; continuity and its closedness preserve
that exact image on L. Both compact-product constructions retain
the original inverse topology and one common family for all strips.

The closed relative subset is precisely
({0,1} x S^n) union ([0,1] x {*}). Its labels agree at intersections
and are continuous. For the relative interlaced selections, a point
off this closed set has a neighborhood avoiding it; at a point on
the set, the prescribed singleton belongs to the original set and
the original lower-limit inclusion controls nearby off-set points.
Continuity controls nearby on-set points. Thus replacing the sets
on the relative subset by these singletons retains the required
inclusion. The partition-of-unity approximation applies to the
compact metric parameter domain and its product with each interval.

The zero-endpoint-character base map (1-r)t+r beta_xi(t) is strictly
increasing and has the required endpoints and relative values.
Strict bracketing gives its joint inverse. A path in S^n from the
basepoint to xi supplies one original label at every intermediate
base value by the intermediate value theorem. This is only a
pointwise existence input to the compact-fiber selection, so the
absence of a continuously chosen path is immaterial to that proof.
The other base maps retain the joint character translation or the
full-vector positive average. Every rank-two splice uses that one
common base and the same compact actual source family.

The comparison-space actions are the actual vector, interval and
scalar character/base actions. Their path-group orbit maps are
open with the precise stabilizers retained. The C.78 pair-transport
argument supplies the successive small actual corrections. Fiber
selections use the complete closed relative set. C.62 applies to
their common-base convex intervals, including the endpoint splice,
giving exactly MM^-1M and M^-1MM^-1 as forward/inverse bounds.

The original component formula retains d>0, lambda of either sign,
and kappa. The zero-character affine base formula is translation
covariant; the two-character cutoff is covariant as well. The
full-base line-bundle case uses the existing positive norm and
relative bundle selection. The finite endpoint argument treats
one fixed interval and pairwise distinct bounded intervals; the
latter have both endpoints tending to the limiting fixed line.
Compact inverse envelopes reduce inverse limits to the same case.
On fixed lines, connectedness of the sphere displacement image
supplies the fractional displacement in the original compact image.
All resulting compact word families fix the actual original circle,
which proves the boundary limits jointly in every parameter.

The piecewise inverse formulas and all boundary limits give actual
homeomorphisms of the closed disk and continuity into the specified
group. The cone on S^n is the closed (n+1)-disk, and the retained
basepoint ray gives the based null-homotopy. This establishes exactly
pi_n(C_Q,1)=0 for n>=1. The arbitrary-domain prime-end comparison
and the general C.12 extension problem remain outside this claim.
