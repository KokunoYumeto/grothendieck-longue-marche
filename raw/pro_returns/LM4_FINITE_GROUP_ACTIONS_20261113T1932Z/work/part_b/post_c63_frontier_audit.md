# Exact reductions after the fixed-frontier extension C63

C63 is complete and has passed root and independent C audits. This separate note records the remaining scope and an auxiliary fact relevant to the zero-dimensional local-centralizer case. It does not modify the registered C63 source or metadata.

## What the compact-family construction establishes at a nonfixed frontier

The global maps in C63's compact family are limits, in the actual global homeomorphism topology and all selected original source-cover topologies, of words in the original loop. They therefore preserve every original open orbit and fix every point fixed by the original generated group. Their projected point and inverse-point control is exact:

\[
K_{\rho,t}(x)\in\mathcal K\mathcal K^{-1}\mathcal K(x),\qquad
K_{\rho,t}^{-1}(x)\in\mathcal K^{-1}\mathcal K\mathcal K^{-1}(x).
\]

At a fixed frontier point, the compact evaluation set on the right is a singleton. At a nonfixed frontier point the proof supplies a compact evaluation set but does not identify it with one prescribed point. The construction also does not establish that arbitrary infinite-product limits preserve each individual nonopen original G-orbit. Consequently the same compactness argument does not, by itself, prove nonfixed-frontier continuity for the selected lift.

The continuous-selection proof was performed in the actual principal real line bundle. Its relative boundary conditions were the four homotopy-parameter faces, where the prescribed coefficients already belong to the original crossing intervals. A prescribed value on a physical nonfixed frontier has not been shown to satisfy an analogous lower-limit inclusion. No relative selection across that frontier is being asserted.

## Proved auxiliary fact: actual small paths move a point through an open neighborhood

This fact does not require a positive-dimensional local centralizer.

Take the countable collection of original open G-orbits. Retain their original universal covers and based lifted loop paths. As in C63, form the compatible product of the global plane homeomorphism group and the homeomorphism groups of those covers, with every map and inverse controlled on compact sets. Let L be the closure, in this exact product, of the subgroup generated by the labelled original loop. It is Polish. Its global values commute with the original operators, preserve every original open orbit, and fix the original G-fixed set. The covering and operator equations remain exact by closedness.

Let P(L) be the group of continuous paths σ:[0,1]→L with σ(0)=1, with pointwise multiplication and the compact-open path topology. It is Polish. A compatible complete metric on L gives completeness of the uniform path metric; uniform limits remain continuous and based. Separability follows by coding finite values from a countable dense subset and a uniform-continuity scale, selecting one actual path from every nonempty coded neighborhood. Pointwise multiplication and inversion are continuous on compact path-parameter sets.

Fix x in an original open orbit O. The action

\[
P(L)\times O\longrightarrow O,\qquad (\sigma,y)\longmapsto\sigma(1)y
\]

is continuous and transitive. Indeed, every original finite word carrying x to a prescribed point of O is the endpoint of an actual based word path: replace each original parameter t_j by s t_j and keep its inverse sign. This path lies in the labelled group L and retains all original source lifts.

The exact Polish orbit-open-map theorem already used in C30 therefore makes

\[
\operatorname{ev}_x:P(L)\longrightarrow O,\qquad
\sigma\longmapsto\sigma(1)x
\]

an open map. For every neighborhood U of the constant identity path in P(L), U·x is an open neighborhood of x in O. Thus every point of that neighborhood can be reached by some actual global operator-centralizing path satisfying all the controls defining U.

For example, U may require the global maps and inverses to move each point of one specified ambient compact set by less than ε at every time, and may simultaneously impose any finite collection of compact controls on the original source-cover maps and their inverses. All these are open conditions in the stated path topology. The resulting motions fix the original G-fixed set throughout and preserve every original open orbit.

This conclusion supplies existence of small actual paths for nearby endpoints. It does not supply a continuous choice of such paths as the endpoint varies, a local section of the evaluation map, or a lift of an arbitrary parameterized deformation. Those additional requirements have not been deduced from openness.

## Current remaining cases

The new fixed-frontier principal-fiber contraction leaves the prescribed-value problem at nonfixed frontier orbits. Open orbits whose full local centralizer is zero-dimensional also lack the principal one-dimensional fiber used by the selection argument. Their intrinsic contractions from C32 remain available, but a controlled global extension through all their frontiers has not been established. C12 remains open.
