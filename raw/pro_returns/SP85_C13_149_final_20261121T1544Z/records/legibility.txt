The main running text is generally readable at the supplied resolution. Confidence is lower in compressed endings, heavily crossed words, letters retraced over earlier strokes, and small interlinear additions. The least secure passages include the layered revisions on 028–031 and 035–040, the upper decoration of the group formula on 042, the crowded lower revision and clipped condition on 045, the slanted notes on 048–049, the retraced sign on 051, the revised formula and tiny labels on 052, and the overwrites, small geometry addition and cancelled clause on 053. These are identified by their local doubt marks and saved crops; the list is not a claim that all ink on those leaves is poor.

The remaining uncertainties concern both reading and attachment. A visible stroke may be securely present while its role as a prime, punctuation, leader, or decoration remains unsettled. Likewise a word can be readable while its cancelled or inserted status is doubtful. The transcription preserves these distinctions rather than silently completing a phrase, correcting agreement, or substituting the formula that an argument would ordinarily require. Whole formulas were reread when a sign was doubtful. Illegibility markers occur only in nonmathematical text whose underlying ink remains physically obscured, never as substitutes for mathematical symbols.

Witness C was usually closer to the local wording and revision structure, but it was not treated as an authority. Both witnesses supplied abbreviation stops, missed short additions, and flattened the rounded extension capital; C also retained wrong words, indices, punctuation and source slips that required fresh inspection. H often supplied fluent expansions or coherent paraphrases, including admissible for the written anabélien and complete words for short forms. At other places H preserved a detail that C omitted, including the detached conjugaison hyphens on 053. Agreement between C and H did not certify the ink. The independent image-only readings also made mistakes and exposed overconfident first decisions; every recorded disagreement was adjudicated on a saved crop.

Counts in the changes log describe logged physical places, including alphabet and punctuation corrections and resolutions of C's existing doubts. They are not an estimated error rate for all words, a measure of mathematical reliability, or a count of independent discoveries. Newly added doubt without a change of first reading is recorded in this file and the second-reading record but is excluded from material changes against C. No OCR, external mathematical research, or TeX compilation was used.
