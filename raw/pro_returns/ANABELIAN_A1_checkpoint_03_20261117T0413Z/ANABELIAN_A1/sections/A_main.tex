\subsection{Conventions and audit target}
Let \(k=\mathbf F_q\) have characteristic \(p\), and let \(C/k\)
be smooth, proper, and geometrically connected of genus \(g\geq2\).
We write \(\Delta_C=\pi_1(C_{\bar k})^{(p')}\) and
\[
1\longrightarrow\Delta_C\longrightarrow\Pi_C
 \longrightarrow G_k\longrightarrow1.
\]
The arithmetic quotient is the entire group \(G_k\). A rational
point determines a continuous section up to conjugation by
\(\Delta_C\). We reserve \(E_C\) for the exceptional set of
closed points, rather than using it for this arithmetic extension.

\begin{claim}{A.1}{Open}{Audit target}
For every such \(C/k\), distinct rational points have distinct
\(\Delta_C\)-conjugacy classes of sections of \(\Pi_C\to G_k\),
and the assertion remains true after every finite extension of \(k\).
\end{claim}
The proof under audit is AS-A.3, assembled from AG.40--AG.42.
It bears on cote 149, scans 35--36. The elementary compactification
lemma, the finite exceptional-set input, the cuspidal criterion,
the relative configuration exactness, and the boundary comparison
are being checked separately before the assertion is accepted.
