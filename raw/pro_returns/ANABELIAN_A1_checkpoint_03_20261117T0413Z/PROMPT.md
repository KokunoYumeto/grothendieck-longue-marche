## Conventions

- `k` is a finite field of characteristic `p`; `C/k` is smooth, proper, geometrically connected of genus `g ≥ 2`; `Δ = π_1(C_{k̄})^(p′)` is the maximal prime-to-`p` quotient of the geometric fundamental group; `Π_C` is the quotient of `π_1(C)` by the kernel of `π_1(C_{k̄}) → Δ`, an extension of the whole `G_k` by `Δ`. `s_P` is the section of `P ∈ C(k)`, defined up to conjugation by `Δ`. A *collision* is a pair `P ≠ Q` in `C(k)` with `s_P`, `s_Q` conjugate under `Δ`.
- `Σ` is the set of primes other than `p`. On the universal geometrically pro-`Σ` cover of `C`, the *exceptional set* `Ẽ_C` is the set of closed points whose decomposition subgroup of `Π_C` is commensurate with that of another closed point (Saïdi–Tamagawa 2009, Definitions 1.3 (iii) and 1.5); `E_C` is its image in the closed points of `C`.
- `r` is a prime other than `p`. For `x ∈ C`: `F = π_1(C − x)^(p′)`, `K_F = ker(F → Δ)`, and the *maximal cuspidally pro-`r` quotient* is `F_cr = F / ker(K_F → K_F^(r))`. For `U = C × C` minus the diagonal the same construction over `Δ × Δ` gives `E_cr`. The arithmetic forms are extensions of `G_k`.
- For a finite extension `L/k`, a `P`-pointed cover is a finite étale cover of `C_L` with a chosen `L`-rational point over `P`; `h(D) = #J_D(L)`; for `χ : J_C(L) → Z/ℓ`, `D_χ` is the pull-back of the Lang cover by the Abel map based at `P`, and `L_P(χ, T)` its twisted `L`-function.

(2) *Exactness* (AG.41): the fibration sequence `1 → F_cr → E_cr → Δ → 1` is exact. Step 1: if conjugation on `L/[L, L]L^r` is through an `r`-group, `L^(r)` injects into `H^(r)`. Step 2: for a Galois étale cover `C′ → C` of group `A` of order prime to `p`, the family `C′ × C′` minus the graphs of the deck transformations has labelled punctures and trivial monodromy on the homology of the compactified fibre, so the monodromy modulo `r` is a group of shears. Step 3: a finite quotient of `F_cr` is dominated, on the fibre, by a quotient of `E_cr` built from a core. (3) *The criterion* (AG.40): an isomorphism `G_P ≅ G_Q` of the maximal cuspidally pro-`r` quotients of the arithmetic groups of `C − P` and `C − Q`, over the identity of `Π_C` and carrying cusp to cusp, forces `P = Q`. Step 1: `P − Q` has `p`-power order; `f` with divisor `p^e(P − Q)`. Step 2: corresponding finite covers have the same number of rational points over every constant extension (uses (AG.39.15)). Step 3: decomposition groups of non-cuspidal points go to decomposition groups of non-cuspidal points, by compactness over the whole system of covers; by the finiteness of `E_C` the target point is the same point `R` for all `R` outside a finite set. Step 4: the two cuspidally central extensions by `Z_r(1)`; the multiplier of the induced isomorphism is one; `χ(u, v) = β̄(u)v⁻¹` is a cocycle with residues `+1, −1` at `P, Q`; `κ_r(f) − p^e χ` descends to `H¹(Π_C, Z_r(1))`, the constants; on the decomposition group of `R` the cocycle `χ` is a coboundary. Step 5: a point `R` with `f(R) = aζ`, `ζ` a non-trivial root of unity of `r`-power order, outside the finite set, gives a contradiction.
