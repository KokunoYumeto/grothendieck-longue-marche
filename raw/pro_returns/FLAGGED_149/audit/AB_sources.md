## Exact source check for the final genus-two reduction

Milne AV III §9, printed pp113–114, explicitly sets the base field to be separably closed. Proposition9.1 states that the Abel map induces the isomorphism from the curve's abelianized étale fundamental group to that of its Jacobian, and that connected abelian covers are obtained by pullback. Its proof for integers n prime to the characteristic uses Lemmas9.2–9.3; Lemma9.2 explicitly identifies cyclic degree-n covers with n-torsion line bundles. B9 uses only n=2 in odd positive characteristic, so this is within the opened hypotheses. The actual branch-pair Kummer divisor and the proof that the15 Weierstrass-pair classes exhaust nonzero J_C[2] are supplied in B9. The joint norm identity and the character-descent computation of ker π^*=J_C[2] are proved directly, not attributed to a further theorem.
