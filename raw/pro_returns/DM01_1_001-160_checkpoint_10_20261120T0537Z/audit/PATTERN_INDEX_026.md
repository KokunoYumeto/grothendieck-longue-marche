## Recurring unresolved cancellation and contraction limits

Cancellation repeatedly preserves ascenders, descenders and dots while hiding the distinguishing bodies. **M0008/M0010/M0012/M0013** retain uncertainty over covered counters or superposed numeral layers. The useful contrast is **M0327**, where two surviving p descenders support `support`, against **M0326**, where detached fragments do not: only neighboring `compact` is secure. **M0510/M0512/M0514/M0515/M0526** likewise remain U despite surviving high or low strokes.

Uncancelled compression can impose the same limit: **M0229** does not distinguish `en fait|enfin`; **M0284** does not securely distinguish initial n/m. Repeated dots or tall stems therefore support targeted comparisons, not reconstruction of missing interiors. These limits are recorded in PATTERNS.md and the exact current decision rows above.
