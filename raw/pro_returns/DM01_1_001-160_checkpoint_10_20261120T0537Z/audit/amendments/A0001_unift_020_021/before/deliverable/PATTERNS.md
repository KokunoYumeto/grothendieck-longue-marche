# Recurring doubts and errors

Only patterns supported by inspected marks are included. A pattern is a search aid, not permission to change unchecked occurrences.

## Scan 003

### Long connective strokes in compressed French cursive

Marks: M0001, M0006, M0007, M0015.

Several letters are reduced to a horizontal connector. Ascenders, descenders, accents and terminal turns supply the distinguishing evidence; context alone would overresolve the interior.

### Deletion and overwriting can genuinely destroy distinctions

Marks: M0008, M0010, M0012, M0013.

Crossings-out cover counters and merged numeral layers prevent secure separation. Retain uncertainty when enlargement adds no surviving distinction.

## Scan 006

### Closed right oval in varphi, separate central prong in psi

Marks: M0056, M0057, M0059.

Each of these three marks has an enclosed right oval, sometimes filled by the pen, attached to a descending stem. The same-page unmarked psi-varphi pair and Donc psi show that psi instead has a distinct central rising prong between open arms. The three marked looped signs therefore independently support varphi.

### Horizontal cancellation hides low letter bodies

Marks: M0060, M0061, M0062, M0064.

These deletions repeatedly merge with the baseline. Taller strokes, dots and descenders survive while low letter joins disappear. M0060 preserves the later l/e group, and M0061 preserves a first-line l/a group plus dots and two descenders in the continuation, but those remaining landmarks do not establish all the surrounding cancelled letters.

## Scan 013

### Dotted si and open omega

Marks: M0207, M0208, M0210, M0215.

The page shows a low s-like si with a detached dot, and omega as low open lobes distinct from the tall angular M. Those distinctions survive where dense deletion strokes do not intervene.

### Cancellation obscures bodies and operators

Marks: M0212, M0213, M0214, M0216, M0217.

Long crossing rules overlap the x-height of deleted text and mathematical operators; surviving extrema allow partial readings but do not securely recover hidden joins or word boundaries.

### Heavy cancellation obscures baseline letters

Marks: M0219, M0220, M0225, M0226, M0227.

This page uses broad horizontal deletions and large crossing loops. Ascenders, the prime and descenders often remain visible, while short joining letters and positional indices merge into the cancellation.

### Contracted low letters with separate dots

Marks: M0221, M0224, M0228, M0229.

Several low letter sequences become horizontal strokes or small detached marks; M0224 retains two i dots, while M0229 is too contracted for the final sequence to be resolved.

### Open c versus closed a coefficients

Marks: M0231, M0234, M0237.

The upper-formula and prose coefficients each use an open hook with a long low exit for c, whereas the lower-formula coefficient has a closed bowl and right-hand rise for a. The page preserves the differing letters.
