# Legibility and comparative reliability

The ordinary running hand is substantially more legible than the crowded marginal additions and repeated cancellation layers. The least secure readings are short overwritten abbreviations, tiny boundary indices, strokes that may be either a capital’s own upper arm or an additional bar, and words crossed by several revision layers. The cancelled passages and bottom diagram on 150_scan024, the diagonal upper note and overwritten tuple on 150_scan023, and the dense sheets 150_scan003–005 carry particularly many local uncertainties. These assessments combine the first readings with the completed independent audits and root crop adjudications.

Both witnesses supplied useful readings. H frequently expands abbreviations, regularizes mathematical notation or simplifies revision states where the manuscript retains a shorter or less coherent form. C is often closer in those respects, but it also omits or shifts insertions, misreads small symbols and extra bars, and retains alternatives that later inspection resolves. Some accepted readings agree with neither witness. Agreement with a plausible mathematical statement was not treated as visual evidence. The changes log counts local collation decisions, including resolved C doubt marks; those counts are not witness error rates or a measure of certainty for every unchanged word.

The independent pass has produced both retained readings and revisions. Completed audit records include changes to a diagram shaft on 149_scan134, exclusion of the nonsemantic blot graphic on 150_scan014, revised height of the looped formula group on 150_scan016, and a revised doubtful element-label relation on 150_scan017. It also added or retained narrow uncertainties. Fixed first/second readings preserve what each reader committed before comparison; current source files and root adjudications supply the accepted state. Source slips, differing local alphabets, doubtful arrowheads and unfinished statements remain distinct from mathematical checks and proposed repairs in the notes.

All 25 pages are audited: 560 fixed boxes, 472 nonidentical boxes, 2546 token-edit spans and 194 revised boxes. All 195 original marks have decisions: 82 first candidates, 6 listed alternatives, 20 other readings and 87 still undecided. A differing box can contain several issues; token agreement is not proof that every ink element has been recovered.

A wider native margin frame separates the two writing directions.
