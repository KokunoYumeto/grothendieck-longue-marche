## Openness criterion

For a continuous map f, openness is equivalent to f^{-1}(closure B) = closure(f^{-1}(B)) for all B. Continuity supplies the inclusion from the right to the left. Openness supplies the reverse: any neighborhood U of x maps to a neighborhood f(U) of f(x), so f(x) in closure B forces U to meet f^{-1}(B). Conversely this reverse inclusion implies every f(U) is open: if a point y in f(U) were in closure(Y\f(U)), an x in U over y would be in closure(f^{-1}(Y\f(U))), contradicting the open neighborhood U.
