## Delta versus barred D in the incidence notation

Main-reader cross-comparison at approximately 16:03 UTC: `149_scan132_P12.png`, `149_scan133_P01.png`, and `149_scan136_P15.png`. In scan132 both the overbrace label and the final intersection term contain a closed triangular Delta. The scan133 inserted definition instead has the upright stem and rounded bowl of D under an upper horizontal stroke; the barred D forms in scan136’s cancelled incidence passage have the same rounded letter, with detached upper bars clearest in the inserted containment. The two notations serve related incidence expressions, but their source glyphs differ: `\Delta_J` on scan132 and `\bar D_J` on the later leaves are retained.
