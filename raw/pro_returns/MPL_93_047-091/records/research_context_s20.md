## The S20 hypothesis and the wider context

S20 is the hypothesis in the supplied `README.md`: that Gauss–Manin, the Cartier operation, and projective connections are a single line of investigation concerning uniformization equations and p-curvature, also involving cotes159 and64. The context README's older sentence “Only this README” is superseded by its dated additions: this package actually includes the earlier cote93 notes, the results-state register, and Michel Hua's full modernized reading. All of those were first opened after the transcription freeze; the entire `hua_project/93.modern.tex` was read, including its sections beyond the supplied scan range.

### S20-01 — Strong evidence for the projective-connection strand

**File/location:** cote93, scans072–074,078,080–082,084,086–091. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** Schwarzian; projective; homographie; uniformisant; S20.

The explicit differential invariant, its normalization avoiding division by2 and3, and the later `(P,sigma,C)` description make projective connections a sustained mathematical subject within these pages, not merely a catalogue inference. They extend **MPL_93_002-046 P01/P03** at scan036, in `same_cote_93/MPL_93_002-046_notes.md`, by supplying general coordinate-change questions and geometric connection data. Hua, modernised reading of cote93, §§VI–VII (“Invariants différentiels” and “Fibrés en droites projectives et connexions transverses”), is supported at that level, subject to the sign, factor, surjectivity, and characteristic qualifications in the SC and OP notes.

### S20-02 — A crystalline strand is present, but no particular period equation is identified

**File/location:** cote93, scans047–049,051,053–054. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** cristal; extension; Dieudonné; Gauss; Picard.

The universal-extension, isogeny, filtration, and classification material continues the programme on038–046 and therefore adds to **MPL_93_002-046 C25/C30/C33/P08**, rather than merely restating the projective calculation. No supplied page identifies a specific projective connection on072–091 as the Gauss–Manin connection of a specified family, and no family of periods is supplied to make its scalar equation Picard–Fuchs. Hua §IV (“Programme de la théorie de Dieudonné”, pages36–53) and its later §§XI–XII suggest routes for comparison, but that comparison remains an unproved step in S20 for this scan range.

### S20-03 — Integral jets and p-curvature are distinct assertions

### S20-04 — The entire retained sequence is not one continuous argument

**File/location:** cote93, scans050,052,055–070,083, compared with047–054 and072–091. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** orientation; Euler; Postnikov; réemploi; S20.

The Banach exercises, administrative sheets, Euler-characteristic programme, and isolated Postnikov question are distinct strands; none of those sheets proves a link joining every preceding or following subject. This reinforces **MPL_93_002-046 O01** (orientation material on027–033) and **§3** (typed-sheet census): sharing a cote does not establish a common theorem. ## Later subjects in Hua's modernized reading: credited leads and limits

The following short items address topics found in **Hua, modernised reading of cote93**, `hua_project/93.modern.tex`, which the current scan range does not itself develop fully. His page n corresponds to PDF scan n+1, as stated in `notes/RESULTS_STATE.md`; locations beyond091 below refer to his supplied interpretation, not to original images inspected in this task. None of his reported Lean checks was available as source code or executed here.

### HC01 — Later Riccati formulas help connect the two projective descriptions

**File/location:** cote93, scans080,082,090–091; Hua §VII “Sections horizontales”, pages95–100, and §VIII, pages101–107. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** Riccati; facteur 2; sections horizontales; lambda.

Hua's later reading reports the equation `2 lambda′+lambda²=mu` and a sequence of corrections to a missing factor2; the variation formula already on082 and the checked matrix normalization on090 give an internal reason to examine that coefficient. This adds a concrete continuation to **MPL_93_002-046 P01/P03**, but the original later corrections have not been visually checked in this package. The modernized equation is therefore a comparison target, not a replacement for any doubtful sign on the current sheets.

### HC02 — The level-p² claim belongs partly to later pages

**File/location:** cote93, scans072–074; Hua §IX “L'injectivité en caractéristique p”, pages121–123. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** p^2; Hasse; injectivité; homographie; Pic.

The supplied scans state the divided-derivative expression, but the p-versus-p² rigidity claim is supplied by **Hua, findings register,93-integral-schwarzian-level-p2**, and his later §IX, rather than by an explicit assertion on072–074. The SC proof record verifies the integral cocycle and a precisely stated monic local algebra model, including an additional descent argument reviewed in this run, while leaving a general global sheaf theorem and historical novelty unclaimed. This upgrades a limited part of that result-state candidate and preserves the distinction between current manuscript evidence and a contextual continuation.

### HC03 — Canonical analytic uniformization is stronger than a projective connection

**File/location:** cote93, scans086–091; Hua §X “Uniformisation et connexion projective canonique”, pages125–139. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** uniformisation; domaine; canonique; projective.

Hua's §X describes a canonical analytic construction from universal covers and homogeneous domains, followed by algebraicity and regular-singularity questions. The current pages supply the bundle-and-section language needed for that description but leave full connections in a quadratic-differential torsor, so their restricted normal form alone does not select the uniformizing connection. This adds a precise distinction to **MPL_93_002-046 P03/P04/P08** and supports S20's thematic connection without proving its arithmetic part.

### HC04 — Torseurs with connections and the proposed Picard–Fuchs construction

**File/location:** cote93, scans047–048 and090–091; Hua §XI “Connexions, stratifications, et équations de Picard–Fuchs”, pages141–160. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** torseur; stratification; Picard; Manin; principal.

Hua's §XI reports classification of torsors under E equipped with a compatible integrable connection by de Rham hypercohomology and constructions of Picard–Fuchs jets and “sections de Manin”. The present universal-extension material and first-principal-parts operator provide relevant objects, but no map from the specific projective torsor to those period constructions is written on047–091. This is a credited continuation of **MPL_93_002-046 C30/C33/P08**, with new comparison data rather than a result already established by the current scans.

### HC05 — Gauss–Manin from a filtered de Rham complex is a later comparison target

**File/location:** cote93, scans047–048; Hua §XII “Le complexe de De Rham filtré, et la connexion de Gauss–Manin”, pages161–166. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** Gauss; Katz; Oda; filtration; de Rham.

Hua's §XII reads a construction from a filtered relative de Rham complex and records its own warnings about the projection formula and integrability. The current continuation of the crystalline programme could be compared with that connection, but neither the mere existence of a crystal nor a displayed differential on an associated graded complex proves that the two constructions agree. This bears on **MPL_93_002-046 C25/C30/P08** and adds a definite comparison problem to S20; the later original pages are not part of this transcription.

### HC06 — Cartier representatives versus cohomology classes in the modernized context

**File/location:** cote93, scans072–074, compared with Hua §XIII “L'opération de Cartier”, pages168–172. **Kind:** own idea. **Confidence:** high for the elementary countercheck; no claim about the unseen manuscript's wording. **Context queries:** Cartier; psi; formes exactes; Hasse.

Hua's §XIII writes a map to closed forms with `psi(dx)=x^(p-1)dx` for every x, but that assignment is not additive before passage to cohomology: over `F2[x,y]` the defect on `d(x+y)` is the nonzero exact form `x dy+y dx=d(xy)`. The refinement **HC06-R** passes to cohomology or makes explicitly chosen representatives part of the construction; the closed-form-level assignment cannot serve as an established proof connecting the integral jet formula to Cartier. This supports the distinction in **MPL_93_002-046 E26/P06** and is a check of Hua's interpretation, not a correction of any original page beyond091.

### HC07 — The later p-curvature question is a further conjectural bridge

**File/location:** cote93, scans072–074 and086–091; Hua §XIV “Questions”, pages193–197. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** p-courbure; réciproque; monodromie; intégrable.

Hua's §XIV reports a question relating almost-everywhere vanishing p-curvature to finite monodromy, with rank-one and group-torsor variants. That subject strongly supports the wider folder-level theme proposed in S20, but the present projective formulas do not supply the vanishing hypothesis, and this note does not claim to establish the modern status of the general conjecture. It bears on **MPL_93_002-046 P05/P06/P07** and differs from a theorem identifying each current connection as geometric.

### HC08 — Iterated Cartier and eigenvalues need their own hypotheses

**File/location:** cote93, scans072–074; Hua §XV “Itérés de Cartier et valeurs propres de Frobenius”, pages198–203. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** Cartier; valeurs propres; Hasse; Frobenius.

### HC14 — Canonical comparisons of lifts have a sheaf-level descent step

**File/location:** cote93, scans047–048; Hua §XXI “Construction de la connexion canonique en cohomologie de De Rham”, pages233–235. **Kind:** unpursued implication. **Context queries:** cristal; comparaison; homotopie; torseur; Gauss.

This bears directly on **MPL_93_002-046 C30/C33** and adds a specific later comparison to **P08**, without upgrading an unavailable Lean development into a proof of the original construction.
