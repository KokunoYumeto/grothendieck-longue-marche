## Legibility and comparison of witnesses

The ordinary typescript is generally clear. Its most difficult parts are not entire sentences but individual cells where two letters were keyed in the same position, cancellations made with repeated x impressions, and additions crossing earlier formulae. The manuscript often leaves enough of both letters to record them, but not enough to determine their order. Those order alternatives remain explicit. The English typescript leaves are comparatively open, although their small indices, membership signs, and deliberately unfilled symbol positions require care.

The least secure readings are the crowded canceled phrases and overstrikes in cote 29 scans 115, 119–123, 125 and 127–128, and the layered formulae and corrections in cote 32 scans 007–010 and 013–015. The place and date on 29/125, short words beneath dense cancellation, some punctuation at the page edge, and isolated letters under later strokes remain weaker than the surrounding text. A readable phrase around such a cell does not make the cell certain. The doubts table states the local alternatives and confidence rather than treating a recognizable sentence as proof.

Witness C usually preserves more of the canceled writing, insertion structure, and graphics. It nevertheless contains silent spelling repairs, omitted or misidentified overtyped layers, mistaken alphabets or indices, and some drawings whose outlines lose visible gaps or endpoints. Witness H frequently omits canceled material or gives canceled endings as live text, and its editorial explanations sometimes replace source features rather than transcribe them. On the two leaves without H text, no H correction count is inferred. Agreement between the witnesses was never used as evidence in place of the scan.

The enlarged crops preserve the evidence for local decisions. They do not recover strokes that the scan does not contain. Clear but mathematically surprising letters, domains, degrees, signs, wording and blanks remain in the transcription; the notes separately state the limited formal consequences checked in this run. No OCR, text-recognition tool, PDF text layer, external mathematical source, or TeX compilation was used.
