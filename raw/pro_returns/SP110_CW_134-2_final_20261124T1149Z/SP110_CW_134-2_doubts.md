## Legibility and reliability

Most running prose is legible at the supplied resolution. Confidence is lower in the overwritten older layers, compressed word endings, the small top- word and the proper-name ending. Enlarging the crops makes stroke placement easier to inspect but does not recover detail absent from the source pixels.

Both witnesses are drafts. C often preserves the local wording and punctuation better on these two leaves; H helps locate the repeated calligraphic P family and some alternative endings. Agreement was checked against the images and was not treated as evidence by itself. Some differences are only quotation, underline or divider representation and should not be read as lexical error counts. The final changes table separates actual preferred-reading/state differences from changes of certainty.

The original first-reader reports, the root pre-audit pages, and the committed blind readings are preserved. Where the blind reading differs, the adjudication table states the final crop-based decision. Remaining uncertainty is visible in the page files as well as in this table.
