## What the counts do and do not mean

- Punctuation and source-mark realization account for several differences: four quotation forms, the Hom underline, a comma, a point count, the single hyphen and the divider. They are not lexical corrections.
- The overwritten older letters omitted from H's main transcription are classified as omissions, not as a false deletion state. All four deleted/overwritten places on scan 184 were independently inspected and included in the blind audit.
- Three insertions on scan 184 were checked for both their letters and their attachment. The first P requires an insertion state in C; H's claimed insertion of baseline X is not supported. The raised replacement prefix and ss- are also covered.
- The initial C/c on scan 185 and the cannelés/canelés ending on scan 184 remain uncertain. Reordering their alternatives is reported as a preferred-reading change, with medium confidence, not as a certain correction.
- Resolving C's doubt without changing its preferred letters is recorded in the changes log but excluded from the against-C correction count. Added doubt alone is recorded in the doubts table.
- No drawings, diagrams, typed overstrikes or typed spellings occur on these two handwritten leaves. Zero in those categories is a scope observation, not evidence that the risk was ignored.

## Further patterns to carry to the next run

No new recurring error class beyond the supplied sheet was established from these two pages. The useful local refinements are already instances of its cautions:

1. A derived L can be doubled on one leaf and single on the next; consistency of mathematical role must not regularize the alphabet (184 L24; 185 L1).
2. A source mark described in H's editorial note may still be absent from its main typeset transcription (Hom underline on 184; divider on 185). Record the representation difference without claiming H failed to notice it.
3. A final downward hook can distinguish s from a flat exit, while an expected final e or s can be absent (184 L15, L20, L23 and L24). The audit corrected L15 constants to constant and resolved the added L23 constant/constante and L24 les/le doubts from comparative stroke evidence.
4. Point-count comparisons should distinguish ellipsis-plus-period from a literal six-point series (185 L3 versus L9); macro syntax alone must not create a correction.

The separate hand log records the observed letterforms, their local scope and their exceptions. The second-reading comparison and adjudication files preserve every literal token disagreement, including harmless context or macro-scope differences.
