## Scan 184 — manuscript page 39

**N184-01 — Proposed recovery of homotopy type (L1–L8).** The writer says that a homotopy type can be reconstructed through its fundamental infinity-groupoid and that all conceivable cohomological operations are already included in the system of n-categories. This is a statement being transcribed, not a theorem proved in this run. The prose explicitly refers to a previous letter; that letter was not researched. The unusual accented spelling `réconstituer` is retained from the raised mark over the first e. Evidence: `crops/scan184_r1_reconstituer.png`.

**N184-02 — Stratifications and higher categories (L9–L16).** The next paragraph proposes an analogous correspondence for homotopy types associated with finite diagrams and stratifications of moderate topological spaces X. Its own tentative wording is `devraient correspondre`. The inserted calligraphic P names the stratification, and the raised replacement prefix is visibly (n+1)- before catégories; the following n-champs remains n-champs. The mathematical dimensional relation prompted checking the whole prefix, but it did not recover the cancelled older text, which remains doubtful. All three P forms were compared locally. Evidence: `scan184_r1_prefix.png` and the three `P_` crops in crops/.

**N184-03 — Derived-category proposal (L17–L24).** The conditional wording is `Si plus haut … marchait bel et bien, on s'attendrait ici …`. The writer proposes recovering the mixed homotopy type from a subcategory of a derived category of abelian sheaves, with extra operations. The subject makes a cohomological condition plausible, but it does not settle the compressed abbreviation: `\unsure{coh.|ch.}` is kept unexpanded. The associated final adjective is `constant`: the audit crop shows a final crossed t and its low exit, without a separate e. The same ending occurs at L15, where the first reading constants was corrected to constant. If the abbreviation means cohomologie, ordinary agreement would favor constante; that expectation does not add an e to the ink. The short noun `\unsure{topos|topis}` is likewise not repaired from its possible conceptual role. Evidence: `scan184_r1_article_topis.png`, `scan184_r1_ch.png` and `scan184_r1_constante.png`.

**N184-04 — Literal forms and operations (L20/L24).** The second category word on L20 is transcribed `catégori`: its final dotted i is followed by a terminal return and blank paper, without the separate e visible in the preceding catégorie. The inserted `ss-` is retained as an abbreviation. The derived tensor has a doubled L above it; the next operator has a doubled R and an underline confined to Hom. These are glyph and placement checks, not a proof that the proposed reconstruction works. Evidence: `scan184_r1_categori.png`, `scan184_r1_ss.png`, `scan184_r1_Ltensor.png` and `scan184_r1_RHom.png`.

**N184-05 — Page join.** The final article is retained as `les` before scan 185's `\unsure{Cas|cas} échéant`. The ordinary phrase would be le cas échéant. The pronounced downward hook and short rightward return distinguish the terminal s from the flat exit of le on L20. The audit resolved the added les/le doubt on this comparison, preserving a possible authorial slip. The capitalization on the next leaf is independently uncertain. Evidence: `scan184_b2_B184_24.png`, `scan184_b2_B184_16.png` and `scan185_r1_cas.png`.

## Scan 185 — manuscript page 40

**N185-01 — Four operations and the inclusion notation (L1–L3).** The explicit numeral 4 matches the four written terms: two Rg terms with lower ! and *, a single-stroke Lg with upper *, and g with upper !. Both R capitals are doubled; this L is single, unlike the doubled L above tensor on scan 184. There is no comma between the first two terms. The following word is inclusions, and the connector in `g:Z'-Z''` is a horizontal shaft without a visible arrowhead. The description of g makes a map the natural intended role, but it does not supply the missing arrowhead. The single and double primes and all four upper/lower attachments were checked together. Evidence: `scan185_r1_four_operations.png`, `scan185_r1_inclusions.png` and `scan185_r1_map.png`.

**N185-02 — Expressed conceptual reservation (L3–L9).** The writer's parenthesis says `on ne sait même pas encore ce que c'est qu'une catégorie triangulée`, and similarly questions `la version non commutative`, which he calls `sans doute plus simple et plus fondamentale`. He describes a “catégorie homotopique” with singular “fibre” and “cofibre” operations. This historical first-person reservation is reported as written, not assessed as a present-day mathematical claim. No definition or missing qualification has been supplied. The six marks after strates and the four after cofibre were counted separately; a single underline belongs to une. Evidence: the strates, une, homotopique, fibre and cofibre crops.

**N185-03 — Closing question (L10–L19).** The writer calls the letter `de plus en plus vaseuse`, asks about the `mirifique formule de \unsure{Bloch|Blod}-Quillen`, says he has never heard of it, and ends with his greeting and signature on three separate baselines. The formula itself is not stated and was not identified from outside knowledge. The proper name's final ch/d cluster remains uncertain. The short written hyphen is retained across the line join. Juste is retraced, but no distinct cancelled earlier word is recoverable. Evidence: `scan185_r1_Bloch.png` and `scan185_r1_Juste.png`.
