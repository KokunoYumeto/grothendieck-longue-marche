# Legibility and limits

All whole images and both supplied halves were inspected without OCR, and all 44 pages received a further image-against-text audit before freezing. Enlarged, rotated or mirrored inspection was used for small writing and reverse-side print. Repeated computer-print rows and character banners remain especially fragile: counts, spaces, letters and physically unreadable word counts are estimates where marked. The 1982 dates in that printing date the reused printed sheets; they do not establish a date for the handwritten mathematics.
