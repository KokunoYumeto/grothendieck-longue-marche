## Legibility assessment

The ink is often clear at stroke level, but rapid cursive, overwritten symbols and abbreviated words create reading uncertainty. Explicit doubt marks record these limits; a plausible mathematical interpretation has not been used to remove a visual doubt. The final assessment follows the full audit.
