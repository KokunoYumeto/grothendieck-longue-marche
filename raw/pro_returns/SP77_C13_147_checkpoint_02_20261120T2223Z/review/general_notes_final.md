## Scope and editorial observations

The supplied scans are handwritten mathematical notes. Head page numbers, formula numbers and separate pale footer numbers are retained where present. A pale footer number is recorded without assigning its hand when the scan alone does not establish the writer. Scan numbers are editorial identifiers and are not substituted for the numbers written on the sheets.

The supplied instructions identify the paper watermark as EXTRA STRONG. Its letters are paper features, not part of the manuscript, and none is transcribed. Specks and bleed-through are not promoted to punctuation.

The notes describe the author’s claims within these 24 pages. Unless a check event explicitly says otherwise, a statement was carried as background while reading, not proved or independently verified. The recorded local arithmetic check on scan 053 has only the limited scope stated there. Apparent slips in primes, variable names, grammatical agreement and spelling remain in the transcription when the ink is clear.
