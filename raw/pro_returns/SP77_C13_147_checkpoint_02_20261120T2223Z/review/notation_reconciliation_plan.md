# Cross-page notation review plan

The table will be consolidated after the last first reading and before the final freeze. Each local reading has already been checked in its page review; the independent boxes include the uncertain occurrences.

Families to compare without regularizing clear local differences:

- Hatted and unhatted moduli symbols, and the extent of the closure bar.
- Plain and underlined G; high prime plus separate low dot; cancelled prime clusters.
- Parenthesized alpha at baseline versus a genuinely lowered bare alpha.
- Pi-one versus pi-zero; g versus forked gamma; sigma versus round o.
- Tildes versus hats, including overwritten A/B layers.
- Underlined map labels versus the return stroke of a descender.
- Literal flat-ended shafts, kinked endpoints and clear arrowheads; vertical wavy identification signs and double shafts.
- The actual short forms tt, equals, equals with lower s, and barred m; retain uncertain individual forms.
- h with subscript 1 versus superscript 1; retain each visible position.
- def., déf. and dfn as written; quotation marks and abbreviations without expansion.

No single glyph rule is to be inferred from a disputed occurrence. Apparent slips such as an unprimed local graph or a clear B where the construction suggests C-prime belong in notes and remain in the text.
