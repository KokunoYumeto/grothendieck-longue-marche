## Mapping-torus reduction under audit

Choose alpha actual by a profinite conjugation. A representative of t_alpha acts on the full completed surface group Pi. If its outer action preserves the oriented conjugacy class of beta, choose a conjugating correction so that a lift u of the stable letter commutes with a generator b of beta. Squaring removes the possible orientation reversal. The commuting group generated by u,b is Zhat^2: the mapping-torus projection detects the first coordinate, and the primitive curve subgroup detects the second.

The ordinary Dehn-twist mapping torus is a graph manifold whose JSJ vertex groups are the cut surface groups times Z, with edge groups Z^2. Its profinite JSJ tree is expected to supply the following exact reduction. Theorem5.2 and Remark5.3 of Wilton–Zalesskii, Distinguishing geometries using finite quotients, Geometry & Topology21(2017),345–384, force a Zhat^2 subgroup acting acylindrically on a profinite tree to fix a vertex: otherwise its action is faithful and the group has projective soluble form Z_pi semidirect Z_rho with pi and rho disjoint, contrary to cohomological dimension2 at each prime. This source was opened at https://msp.org/gt/2017/21-1/gt-v21-n1-p08-s.pdf ; local agents/sources/WZ2017Geometries.pdf/txt. The precise efficiency and acylindricity references for this particular JSJ tree still need full proof-depth checking before promotion.

A fixed vertex would imply that beta is conjugate into a completed cut-component surface subgroup. Applying the same argument with alpha,beta reversed gives mutual ellipticity of their one-edge profinite surface splittings.

## 15:51 UTC: closed-surface mapping-torus reduction checked

For a closed surface S_g, g>=2, the carried-subgroup reduction now has a complete source-level derivation. This does not establish the remaining link statement.

Let alpha be an actual essential curve, and let beta be a profinite geometric curve whose primitive twist commutes with t_alpha. E47 identifies the centralizer of each primitive twist with its curve stabilizer. Thus t_alpha preserves beta. Valid Boggi2014 Section4 identifies the oriented stabilizer as an index at most two subgroup, so t_alpha^2 acts trivially on an oriented generator b of beta up to inner conjugation of Pi_hat. Choose v in Pi_hat so that u=v t fixes b in M_hat, where M=Pi semidirect_<t_alpha^2> Z is the actual mapping-torus group.

The identification M_hat=Pi_hat semidirect Z_hat is exact: every finite-index subgroup of the finitely generated Pi contains a characteristic finite-index N; the automorphism of Pi/N has finite order m, and the quotient (Pi/N) semidirect C_m induces that finite quotient on Pi. This gives the full induced topology and injectivity of Pi_hat; the split projection to Z supplies exactness and the completed semidirect product.

The map Z_hat^2 -> M_hat, (r,s) -> u^r b^s, is injective. Projection to the mapping-torus Z_hat detects r, and b generates a copy of Z_hat in Pi_hat, detecting s. It therefore identifies its image with a closed Z_hat^2 subgroup.

The actual JSJ pieces of this mapping torus are S_v times S^1, where S_v are the components cut along alpha. Every base has negative Euler characteristic and boundary. Along a JSJ torus the two fibre slopes differ by the nonzero twist exponent2, so the splitting is reduced and the pieces are large Seifert pieces. Opened and read Wilton--Zalesskii, Profinite properties of graph manifolds, arXiv0807.3727v3, TheoremA (the detailed Theorem3.7 proof, PDF pp15--16) and Lemma5.5 with proof (PDF p21). TheoremA supplies full vertex/edge topology. Lemma5.5 supplies the profinitely2-acylindrical standard JSJ action: the two successive peripheral intersections are the respective centre copies Z_v_hat,Z_w_hat, and their intersection in the middle edge is zero. The literal finite-slope argument applies here. Source URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.3727 ; local agents/sources/WZ2010Graph.pdf/txt.

Wilton--Zalesskii2017 Theorem5.2 and Remark5.3 (opened PDF pp19--20, printed363--364) now apply to the closed abelian subgroup P=<u,b>. A nonelliptic acylindrical action would identify P with Z_pi semidirect Z_rho with pi and rho disjoint; each of its pro-p Sylow subgroups has rank at most1, whereas each Sylow subgroup of Z_hat^2 is Z_p^2. Hence P fixes a vertex. The corresponding vertex group is a conjugate of H_v_hat times Z_hat, and its intersection with the completed surface fibre is the conjugate of H_v_hat, because the piece fibre maps isomorphically to the mapping-torus Z_hat. Consequently b is carried by a completed cut-component subgroup. Applying the same construction after conjugating beta to an actual curve gives mutual ellipticity of the two cyclic surface splittings.

The missing geometric conclusion is still: a globally profinite-geometric cyclic subgroup, elliptic in an actual cut splitting (and with the symmetric ellipticity just proved), belongs to the completed link. No common-refinement or relative simplicity theorem has been supplied yet.
