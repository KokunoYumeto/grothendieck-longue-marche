15:36 UTC, 15 November2026. Root searched both engines and opened the primary arXiv v2 and journal landing page of Funar–Pitsch, Finite quotients of symplectic groups vs mapping class groups (2022). Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.04129 and https://nwejm.univ-lille.fr/index.php/nwejm/article/view/13 . Web refs turn298view0, turn298view1, turn299view1–3.

Read Section4.4, Propositions4.9–4.16 and their displayed proofs. Theorem1.9 concerns the closed group in genus at least4 and trivial finite modules. It kills classes coming from H²(Γ_g;A) after restriction. D35 needs every class at every finite-index subgroup H, namely the entire colimit of H²(H;A). The paper explicitly separates its stronger E_n restriction-colimit condition from the A_n comparison at the initial group. Its cofinal coefficient-kernel argument converts E_n for constant modules to E_n for arbitrary finite modules, but supplies no implication from the proved A₂(trivial) to that E₂ condition.

The exact remaining map is H²(H;A)→colim_{L≤f H}H²(L;A), for every finite-index H≤Γ_g. No vanishing is asserted. This investigation therefore does not strengthen D35. The quantum-representation input to Proposition4.11 was not proof-depth audited; no new theorem is registered from this search.
