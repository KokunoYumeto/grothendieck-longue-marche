\subsection{The positive-genus compact crossed-module comparisons}

\begin{result}{D.38}{Proved}{All 24 and 198 compact cone expansions}
The genus-two and three-pointed-torus diagrams of D.27 and D.29
have explicit cellular comparisons with their compact global
presentations.  They supply, respectively, all $24$ and $198$
open-stratum cone identities as signed conjugate-relator
sequences in the compact free crossed modules, together with
their Peiffer reductions.  Every lower local and sewing
two-cell has its specified compact filling.

The complete artifacts are
\path{code/d_genus_two_peiffer.json} and
\path{code/d_elliptic_three_peiffer.json}.
The counts, obtained from the executed lists, are
\[
\begin{array}{l|rr}
 & (2,0)&(1,3)\\\hline
\text{objects, including open}&20&109\\
\text{oriented one-cells}&155&755\\
\text{open crossing tree edges}&19&108\\
\text{generators after tree collapse}&136&647\\
\text{compact relators}&17&7\\
\text{boundary local relators}&200&370\\
\text{boundary sewing relators}&178&922\\
\text{local-generator crossing relators}&89&339\\
\text{boundary triangle relators}&24&198\\
\text{open-face relators}&42&304\\

\text{distinct compact certificates}&62&91\\
\text{factors in these certificates}&24823&86087\\
\text{selected cone identities}&24&198\\
\text{boundary two-cell prisms}&402&1490
\end{array}                                                     \tag{D.79}
\]
These are the specified mapping-cylinder cellular comparisons,
with all coherence fillings recorded, as in D.30.  They retain
the other cells of the local classifying spaces and their products.
No assertion that the selected cones exhaust the three-cells,
or form a basis of the entire positive-genus identity module,
is included.
\end{result}
\begin{proof}
We first specify the compact relators and their fillings.
For $(1,3)$ use the three Artin braid relators on
$a_i,b$, the three commutators $[a_i,a_j]$, and
$\Delta=(a_1a_2a_3b)^3$.  This is the actual presentation
of D.28.  The program \path{code/d_artin_d4_fill.py}
retains the relator factors in its Garside reduction.
Every change between two positive reduced expressions
of a Coxeter simple is a path in its finite Matsumoto
graph; each edge records one of the six actual Artin
relations, its sign and its preceding prefix.
For the transfer
$AB\to AD B'\to A'B'$, with
$d=\gcd_{\rm left}(\partial a,b)$,
the certificate is the $B\to DB'$ certificate conjugated
by $A$, followed by the $AD\to A'$ certificate.
To replace $x^{-1}$ by $\partial x$, take the inverse
certificate for $x\partial x=\Delta$, include its
$\Delta$ cell and conjugate by $x^{-1}$.
A literal $\Delta$ block is deleted with that same
seventh relator.  Successive prefix-conjugated changes
therefore telescope to the actual free-word difference.

For $(2,0)$ use the ten Artin relations of $B_6$,
the five commutators $[W,\sigma_i]$, $W^2$ and
$\omega_6$, with the exact $W$ of E.15 and D.27.
The program \path{code/b_genus2_fill.py} retains each
signed Artin cell in right and left word reversing.
Pure braid combing uses the exact star words
\[
 W_i=t_iWt_i^{-1}
     =\prod_{j\ne i}A_{\min(i,j),\max(i,j)},\qquad
 t_i=\sigma_{i-1}\cdots\sigma_1.
\]
Their Artin certificates and the displayed centrality
relations replace each $A_{i6}$ by $L_i^{-1}W$, where
$L_i$ is the same ordered star product with $j\leq5$.
Collecting the remaining $W$ factors uses those five
fixed commutators, with their actual signs and prefixes.
The identity $\omega_6=\omega_5W_6$ then gives
$\omega_5=W^{-1}$ with a recorded certificate.
The remaining even power of $W$ is filled by $W^2$.
Each intermediate equality in this description has
its own freely checked relator sequence in the program.

In both cases a certificate is a list of triples
$(c,j,\epsilon)$, with zero-based compact relator index
$j$ and $\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$.  Every certificate used
below was checked against the exact equality
\[
 \prod_{(c,j,\epsilon)\in N(w)}
            cR_j^\epsilon c^{-1}=w
       \quad\text{in the free group on the compact letters}.
                                                               \tag{D.80}
\]
Thus the finite claimed fillings are proved by their
actual free expansions, independently of any claim
about termination on inputs outside the executed lists.
The original normalizers also check their intermediate
word transformations.

Here are the full comparison maps.  Use a named local
presentation at every boundary flag object, the stated
compact presentation at the open object, each actual
face letter $e_a$, and each crossing $t_i$ to the open
object.  The one-cell map is
$x_i\mapsto\mu_i(x_i)$, $e_a\mapsto q_a$, $t_i\mapsto1$,
and is the identity on the compact letters.
Here $\mu_i$ and $q_a$ are exactly D.27 or D.29's
recorded words; every nonidentity target twist is retained.
The two-cell map sends each local or sewing relator
to its certificate (D.80), each compact relator to
its own cell, and each generator-crossing or open-face
relator to the empty certificate.  The latter images
are freely empty, so this is an actual map on the
whole two-skeleton.  Taking the free Fox boundary
of (D.80) verifies the degree-two chain boundary
equation for every column.

For $a:i\to j$, $b:j\to k$, $c=ba$ and target twist
$f=f_{b,a}$, write
\[
 S=e_be_ae_c^{-1}f^{-1},\qquad
 O_a=t_je_at_i^{-1}q_a^{-1},\qquad
 C_k(z)=t_kzt_k^{-1}\mu_k(z)^{-1}.
                                                               \tag{D.81}
\]
The last expression is expanded in the named
generator-crossing relators by the signed prefix
rule of D.30.  Fix the certificate $T=N(\mu(S))$.
Use its literal inverse as the open coherence disk;
its boundary is
$\mu_k(f)q_cq_a^{-1}q_b^{-1}=\mu(S)^{-1}$.
The complete signed source cone identity is
\[
\begin{split}
 &(t_kSt_k^{-1})\,C_k(f)\,
   \bigl(\mu_k(f)O_c\mu_k(f)^{-1}\bigr)\,T^{-1}\\
 &\hspace{20mm}\cdot
   (q_bO_aq_b^{-1})^{-1}O_b^{-1}=1 .
\end{split}                                                     \tag{D.82}
\]
Its factors are individually indexed in the artifacts.
Free cancellation verifies its boundary before any
source relator is imposed.  The two-cell map sends
its factor list literally to $TT^{-1}$, since all
crossing and open-face certificates are empty.
Successively cancelling the middle adjacent inverse
factors is the recorded Peiffer reduction.  This
proves zero of the corresponding class in the
compact presentation's second homotopy group,
not only zero of its word boundary.

For genus two the $24$ triangle certificates are all
empty; its source cone sequences have $103$ factors.
For $(1,3)$ three triangle certificates are nonempty;
its source cone sequences have $3447$ factors and
their compact images have $5260$ factors.
All these counts and all signed factors were executed
and checked.  The same construction supplies every
one of the $402$ and $1490$ boundary two-cell prisms:
for a boundary relator $R$ based at $i$, its identity
is
$(t_iRt_i^{-1})N(\mu(R))^{-1}C(R)^{-1}$,
where $C(R)$ is its fully expanded side annulus.
Its compact image is again its certificate followed
by its inverse.  Grouping the side annulus of a
triangle by its three faces gives (D.82), by the
same Peiffer interchange as in D.30.

Finally these choices have one simultaneous CW
realization.  Let $X=P_2(g,n)$ for either type,
$L=E\Gamma\times_\Gamma X$, and let $p:L\to B\Gamma$
be the actual Borel projection.  The full diagram
with its open stratum is its mapping cylinder:
$[e,(x,t)]$ corresponds to $([e,x],t)$, and the
cone endpoint is $[e]$.  Choose a classifying-space
CW model $K$ whose two-skeleton is the specified
compact presentation.  Such a model is obtained
by attaching higher cells to kill its higher
homotopy groups; no finite identity-module basis
is required.  The displayed one- and two-cell maps
define $f:L\to K$ through dimension two and extend
over all higher cells since $K$ is aspherical.
They induce the actual homomorphism of $p$, so
the resulting map is homotopic to $p$ by extending
their comparison in the contractible universal
target.  This fixes the full classifying-map scope.

Use $M_f$ as the CW replacement of the full cone
diagram.  Its retraction is the identity on $K$
and sends $(z,t)$ to $f(z)$.  Its two-skeleton is
exactly (D.81).  The top face of the prism over
a boundary two-cell is its one already fixed
compact certificate, with the reverse orientation;
thus all open coherence disks are simultaneously
the prescribed inverses.  Each selected
three-dimensional prism maps into $K^{(2)}$.
The other cells of $L$ and its local classifying
spaces remain and map by the extended $f$.
Inclusion of $K$ is a strict right inverse for
this retraction.  These are the claimed cellular
comparison maps with the exact scope stated.

The separate program
\path{code/d_positive_genus_peiffer_verify.py}
imports neither normalizer nor constructor.  Reading
the serialized artifacts alone, it has checked
all $550$ and $2140$ relator images against (D.80),
all $24$ and $198$ source identities, their exact
mapped factor lists, and all $402$ and $1490$
prisms.  Every expansion and every adjacent inverse
Peiffer cancellation passed.  This completes both
compact expansions and the additional comparison
previously recorded in D.24.
\end{proof}
