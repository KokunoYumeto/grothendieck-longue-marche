### N030.1 — What the letter records

On the printed letterhead of “UNIVERSITE DES SCIENCES ET TECHNIQUES DU LANGUEDOC” and “INSTITUT DE MATHEMATIQUES”, a typed letter bears “Villecun le 30.01.78”. The printed field “Montpellier, le” is unfilled. The sender line names Monsieur Alexandre Grothendieck, Professeur; the recipient is the Ministre de l’Education Nationale. The text says that a copy of a same-day letter to M. Rouzaud is being sent, and names a further copy to M. Peyrefitte. It discusses the problems it attributes to article 21 of the ordinance dated “2.11.1945” and asks the minister to take a public position. This paragraph reports the supplied letter’s contents; the legal or historical assertions were not independently checked.
