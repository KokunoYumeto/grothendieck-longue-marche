## Critical points checked

1. **The coboundary submodule is actually closed.** For finitely many fixed rational continuous two-cochains, the set of integral coefficient vectors whose combination has an actual integral continuous primitive is a submodule of a finite free `Z_5` module. Noetherianity makes it finitely generated; its basis map has compact image, so it is closed. This does not assert that the global space of coboundaries is closed. A rational left inverse to its basis matrix supplies continuous, integral-on-the-allowed-set primitives and preserves the polynomial finite-span property. A possible denominator in that inverse is fixed, not parameter dependent.

2. **All exact central cocycles are included.** In weights at least two, the tame relation `q b = q^j b` kills every inertia value away from five, including nonprimitive values. Thus every actual integral cocycle factors through the finite-ramification Galois group. Finite-coefficient finiteness and the stated continuous inverse-limit comparison make integral `H^1` compact and finitely generated by compact Nakayama. Actual representatives of module generators, together with the coboundaries of a coefficient basis, generate the entire actual `Z^1`, including any torsion-class representatives. This is the required finite family for the induction; selecting only cohomology representatives without coboundaries would have been insufficient.

3. **The finite-span property survives every stage.** Hall multiplication, inversion, and powers are finite rational polynomials in each fixed class. The actual arithmetic action is obtained by substituting the two generator images and hence has only finitely many fixed continuous coefficient functions in that class. After substitution, the obstruction consists of finitely many parameter monomials with fixed continuous two-cochain coefficients. Bounded rational denominators can be absorbed into those coefficient cochains. The inverse image of the actual coboundary submodule is closed; the chosen primitive family and all central cocycle parameters remain finite polynomial families. This proves compactness of the exact fixed-abelian fibre, even when the original abelian cocycle is infinitely ramified.

4. **The group model and topology are genuine.** The Hall-coordinate completion is the actual free-pro-five nilpotent quotient by the finite-quotient universal property. No integral BCH correspondence in class at least five is used. Every finite quotient of the full free pro-five group is nilpotent and factors through one of these quotients. Consequently the uniform topology on compatible full cocycles is exactly the inverse-limit topology of the finite-class fibres.

5. **Conjugacy does not lose compactness or compatibility.** A class above the fixed abelian class can be conjugated to an exact representative. Any conjugator between exact representatives has invariant abelian image and therefore zero abelian image. The class fibre is the quotient of the compact exact fibre by the compact commutator subgroup; it is Hausdorff. Exact lift spaces for a fixed lower representative are closed and nonempty whenever a class lift exists. Every finite compatibility constraint is met at the highest stage, so compactness supplies an actual compatible tower. Nested compact conjugator sets also give the asserted class-level injectivity.

6. **The metabelian version satisfies the same hypotheses.** Its discrete derived Alexander module is free of rank one over the two-variable Laurent ring; truncation by the augmentation ideal gives torsion-free finite-rank central grades, with rank `n-1` in grade `n` and action `chi_5^n`. Hall's theorem therefore applies. The completed Alexander module is separated for this augmentation filtration, and compactness gives the full inverse-limit identification.

7. **The generic finite-stage conclusion follows at the stated strength.** The descending compact images in a fixed stage have intersection exactly the full cusp class, using D.67 (or D.66 in the metabelian case) and the newly proved compatibility statement. Their complements outside any cusp neighborhood eventually become empty. The class-three fibre is the compact `Z_5^2` torsor: the class-two fibre is a singleton, and the positive-weight associated graded kills all stabilizers. This yields a uniform finite stage outside each `5^h Z_5^2`, but provides no numerical bound or first obstruction. The draft retains that limitation.
