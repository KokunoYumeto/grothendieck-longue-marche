# Integral detection in cyclic covers: the split-prime A.54 route

Working note, 15 November 2026, after checkpoint09 source freeze. This is a proved elementary comparison lemma and an explicitly unfinished application. It is not a new registered normalizer theorem.

## Exact lemma

Let X be a smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus g at least two over a characteristic-zero field embedded in C. Let ell be a prime, m=ell^n, and chi:H_1(X,Z/ m)→Z/m a surjection. Let q:Y→X be the corresponding connected unramified cyclic covering, with deck group D=C_m. Let d be the Poincare dual of chi, a primitive vector of H_1(X,Z/m), determined up to the sign convention for the alternating pairing. Then

q_*(H_1(Y,Z_ell)^D) modulo m = (Z/m)d.

Equivalently the integral pushforward of the invariant lattice is the inverse image of that line under H_1(X,Z_ell)→H_1(X,Z/m). In particular, if a closed loop in Y has proper class w, its proper pushforward v is not in that inverse image, and w_Q denotes its rational ell-adic class, then the projection of w_Q to the nontrivial deck summand is nonzero.

All maps are actual continuous maps induced by q after the characteristic-zero comparison, and the deck invariant rational summand is exactly q^!H_1(X,Q_ell), where q^! is transfer. This is a statement about proper homology of the actual cover, not a representation that has merely the same dimension.

## Direct proof

Choose a symplectic surface presentation with generators a_1,b_1,...,a_g,b_g and relation product_j[a_j,b_j]=1 so that chi(b_1)=1 and chi vanishes on all other generators. For a general primitive chi modulo ell^n this choice is legitimate: symplectic elimination over Z/ell^n sends its primitive dual to a_1, and the elementary symplectic operations lift to integral symplectic operations, hence to changes of surface generators. No single integral basis needs to work for all n simultaneously.

Use coset representatives 1,b_1,...,b_1^{m-1}. Reidemeister--Schreier gives generators B=b_1^m and a_{j,i}=b_1^i a_j b_1^{-i}, b_{j,i}=b_1^i b_j b_1^{-i} for j≥2, together with a_{1,i}, for 0≤i<m. The m lifted surface relations abelianize to a_{1,i}-a_{1,i+1}=0, with indices modulo m. These relations have rank m-1 and their quotient is torsion-free. Thus the proper integral homology has basis

h=[a_{1,0}], B=[b_1^m], and [a_{j,i}],[b_{j,i}] for 2≤j≤g and 0≤i<m.

The deck generator fixes h and B and cyclically permutes each of the remaining m-tuples. At the wraparound any extra conjugation by B vanishes in abelianization. Consequently the invariant lattice is freely generated by h, B, and the sums over i of each a_{j,i} and b_{j,i}. Their pushforwards are respectively

a_1, m b_1, m a_j, m b_j.

This proves the asserted integral lattice exactly. Its rank is 2g, and the entire homology has rank 2+2m(g-1), as required by unramified Riemann--Hurwitz. Tensoring with Q_ell, the identities q_*q^!=m and q^!q_*=sum_{d∈D}d identify the invariant summand with transfer. The rational invariant subspace meets the integral lattice in the integral invariant lattice because H_1(Y,Z_ell) is torsion-free. This proves the detection assertion.

## Consequence and the unresolved divisibility issue

For the genus-two curve G:w^5=t(t-1) arising from A.54, fix a split coefficient prime ell≡1 mod5, initially ell=11. The four CM eigenlines form two symplectic pairs. Suppose an actual normalized procyclic subgroup has nonzero proper generator image v=ell^r v_0, with v_0 primitive on one eigenline. Choose chi_n whose Poincare dual belongs to the OTHER symplectic pair; it annihilates v, and its dual modulo ell is not parallel to v_0. The actual subgroup therefore lifts to the unramified cyclic ell^n cover. For n>r the displayed lattice computation forces its proper class to have a nonzero component in the new Prym. After a finite field extension defining the cover, the covering projector is Galois equivariant; normalization would give a one-dimensional Galois subrepresentation in that new Prym.

The missing arithmetic assertion is that these new Pryms have no such line, uniformly at the necessary depths. Proving the assertion only for the first degree-ell cover does not suffice: a topological generator of a procyclic subgroup may have ell-divisible proper homology without admitting an ell-th root in the fundamental group. There is no permission to divide that element in the group. Conversely, choosing chi that annihilates v but not its symplectic dual makes PD(chi) parallel to v; that is precisely the exceptional line allowed by the integral invariant lattice, and does not force new homology.

The first degree-eleven cover has group C_11 semidirect C_5 over the tripod, genus12, and a genus-two quotient by C_5 according to the FGH working route. Its new Jacobian factor and the higher-depth arithmetic exclusion remain to be computed and proved. No all-split-prime normalizer conclusion is claimed here.
