# First-pass observations — historical working record

These observations were written during the first visual pass, before the separate full audit. Some readings were subsequently corrected or marked uncertain. They are retained to document the work, and **must not override the frozen page files, final doubts table, or consolidated research notes**; in particular, the final injection/all-map distinction on scans 020–023 supersedes preliminary shorthand here.

## Scan 006

Title: Groupes de Witt et variantes. Explicit self-flagging: «Même dans ce cas j’ignore si c’est un lambda-anneau (et pas seulement un pré-lambda-anneau)», referring to invertibility of 2. Proposed reduction: compute WG of classifying topoi of O(m) and products. The phrase «anneau binomial» occurs explicitly for H^0(S,Z), but no homotopy-type/cochain statement is made here. The parentheses in the connected-base paragraph and final condition are retained without supplied closing punctuation.

## Scan 007

Self-flagged: «il n’est pas clair que l’hom. précédent se factorise par K(S)» and «il n’est pas clair si on a φ(E)=φ(E’)+φ(E’’) dans WG(S)», with «non splittée» inserted. W(S) is defined as the cokernel. The assertion that (M,q) plus (M,-q) is hyperbolic when 2 is invertible is accompanied by the diagonal/antidiagonal construction. The last sentence continues onto scan 008.

## Scan 008

## Scan 009

Withdrawn in the same text: the paragraph asserting «Un calcul facile montre que w circle alpha=1» and the induced map from W(S) are crossed out, as is a marginal splitting argument. Live text instead records the Stiefel–Whitney map on WG(S), its normalization and additivity-to-multiplicativity, and a lambda-hom assertion involving ranks and completed cohomology. This is evidence about characteristic classes of quadratic modules, not a Fourier transform. The hard upper-right insertion must not be used as secure evidence without further visual checking.

## Scan 020

Defines k_0(G) as ring of virtual finite G-sets, k_n(G)=k(G×S_n), and graded induction product combined with diagonal restriction on G. Defines tau^n(E)=cl_n Hom(I_n,E), I_n=[1,n], and states exponential identity for disjoint union. Several discarded coefficient annotations illegible as symbols, best read(K_0). ## Scan 021

Proof decomposes Hom(I_n,E disjoint F) into invariant pieces indexed by subsets. Corollary extends tau* uniquely from finite sets to group-completed k_0(G). Defines orbit quotient q_H and composite q_H^n, described as polynômiale homogène de degré n. The latter descriptor is a claim needing careful meaning for a Burnside ring, not supplied proof on this sheet.

## Scan 022

## Scan 023

## Scan 024

Category E consists of finite sets with injective maps. Functor category H=Hom(E,Ens_f) called a para-topos. Distinguishes representable h_I=Mon(I,E) from H_I=Hom(I,E), and begins proposition on connected objects. This gives a clear ink model for Mon vs Hom, to be used during source-only notation audit.

## Scan 025

Special functors defined by finite disjoint sums of representables, alternatively direct summands of H_I for I large. Claims stability under finite sums/products, an insertion adding finite inverse limits, and under composition; no proof supplied on this sheet. Says every special functor lands in the injection category E.

## Scan 026

Distinguished functors are finite disjoint sums of orbit quotients Mon(I,-)/H, said to share special-functor permanence. Blue note gives species-like coproduct Mon(I_n,E) wedge_(S_n)X_n with each X_n finite, but no visible finite-support qualification. Defines equivalence of functors as eventual objectwise isomorphism for all finite sets of cardinal≥n, then two-out-of-three and base-change stability. Cancellations in earlier proposed condition kept.

## Scan 027

Blue page defines outlined Omega=End(E,E) as a non-full subcategory of the functor category, with finite values and injections on maps, and only objectwise injective natural transformations. Three operations: disjoint union, cartesian product and composition. First formula has a heavily inked plus/coproduct sign, explicitly doubtful.

## Scan 028

Blue K-theory sheet states universal expression of tau^n in exterior-power classes and units rho_i of integral symmetric-group representation rings, using symmetric polynomials Sigma_alpha. Diagonal margin explicitly asks about effectiveness/direct-summand interpretation and mentions H. Weyl commutation theorem, with wording uncertain. No proof of universal formula on this sheet. Variable and clipped final factor doubtful; do not repair from known symmetric-function identities.

## Scan 029

Need later literature/context check and preserve direction Z→K(k).
