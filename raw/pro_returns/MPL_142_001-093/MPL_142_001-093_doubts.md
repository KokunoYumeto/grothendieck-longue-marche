# Shared notation table — MPL 142, scans 001–093

The table records the common transcription conventions after visual comparison across the ninety nonblank scans. It does not normalize the author's notation: differences visible in particular occurrences remain in the page files, and unresolved alphabet or stroke distinctions remain marked locally. Scan numbers are those of the supplied archive images; scans 023, 043 and 065 were omitted from the supplied set as blank.

| Visible form or recurring distinction | LaTeX encoding | Reading constraint and reconciled observations |
|---|---|---|
| Roman italic capitals and lowercase letters | `A`, `B`, …; `a`, `b`, … | Preserve the visible case and distinguish E/F, u/n, l/1 and J/I from their strokes. A mathematical role does not determine an alphabet. |
| Script or calligraphic capitals | `\mathcal{C}`, `\mathcal{G}`, etc. | Script/plain distinctions that the ink does not settle are marked with `\unsure`. |
| Curled automorphism-group G | `\unsure{\mathcal G_J|G_J}`, or the corresponding unsubscripted form | Direct comparison of 024, 025, 039, 044, 045 and 085 favors the curled/script reading. The live terminal curl in 025 and 039 is continuous with G, not a separate prime; the heavily cancelled possible G-prime in 044 remains a separate doubt. |
| Gothic/fraktur letters | `\mathfrak{g}`, `\mathfrak{G}`, etc. | Used only where the visible letter form supports it. |
| Double-stroked number-system capitals | `\mathbb{Z}`, `\mathbb{Q}`, `\mathbb{R}`, `\mathbb{C}`, etc. | Retain a plain capital where the source is plain; heavy or retraced ink is not automatically a distinct alphabet. |
| Single and doubled left stem of capital Gamma | `\Gamma`; locally `\unsure{\mathbf{\Gamma}|\Gamma}` for the doubled form | The bold form is a typographic stand-in for the handwritten doubled stem, with the local alternative retained. Distinct forms are especially visible in 068–070; 022, 084 and 085 show why the alphabet must not be assigned solely from the group's meaning. |
| Large Pi and small pi | `\Pi_1`, `\pi_1`; locally `\unsure{\mathbf{\Pi}|\Pi}_1` | Preserve the visible size/case distinction between occurrences, including 085–086 and 091–093. The doubled left stem on the 022 cover is registered by a local bold/plain stand-in; the mathematical role alone does not determine the alphabet. |
| U naming the three-punctured line | `U` with local plain/bold alternatives | The general outline in 022, 024 and 083 agrees, but 022 has substantially heavier retracing. No decisive script form is visible in 024 or 083; local weight uncertainty is preserved instead of imposing one font on all occurrences. |
| Roman numbered groupoids | `\mathrm{I}_J`, `\mathrm{II}_J`, …, `\mathrm{VII}_J` | The capped vertical strokes in 024–043 name successive Roman-numbered groupoids; Roman II is not Greek Pi. Hats and omitted subscripts are retained occurrence by occurrence. |
| Lowercase path letter | `l`, with the indices actually written | Compare the looped letter with numeral 1 and with any explicitly different glyph; a subscript is not supplied from the path's expected endpoints. |
| Phi, psi and capital Psi | `\varphi`, `\psi`, `\Psi` | The looped phi and forked psi remain distinct. Scan 062 explicitly introduces capital Psi; local lower/uppercase ambiguity in 064 is retained. |
| Eta parameter | `\eta` | The long descending parameter introduced in 027 is eta; uncertain overwritten occurrences remain marked. |
| Sigma, tilde-sigma, tau and rho | `\sigma_\infty`, `\widetilde\sigma_\infty`, `\tau_\infty`, `\rho` | Preserve the rounded sigma, flatter tau, descending rho and visible overmark. The marginal `rho^{-1}?` in 031 does not replace the live rho in its formula. |
| Orientation set and its barred counterpart | `\uline{\omega}`, `\uline{\bar\omega}`; compressed forms may contain `\unsure{\bar\omega|\varpi}` | Direct comparison of 044 and 055–064 favors a low omega with a separate horizontal bar; the separate underline belongs to the set. Local bar-omega/varpi uncertainty is preserved. |
| Orientation functor | `\Omega` | Ordinary capital Omega in 044 is distinct from the underlined lowercase orientation sets. |
| Prime, exclamation mark and star | `x'`, `f^!`, `x^*`, `x_*` as visible | A prime is not an exclamation mark; dark overstrikes are not automatically stars. The exclamation in the Euler-characteristic notation on 005 and 047 is a subscript: `\chi_!`. |
| Bar, hat and tilde | `\bar{x}`, `\widehat{G}`, `\widetilde{X}` | Preserve the actual mark and its scope; absence of a mark is not corrected from a neighboring formula. |
| Left action exponents | `{}^u l`, `{}^{uv}l`, `{}^u g_l(v)` | The action superscript is attached on the left, not to the preceding expression; compare 078–079. |
| Ordinary and indexed wedge | `\wedge`, `\wedge_{\pi_0}` | Preserve the symbol and operand order, including the associated-cover formula in 092. |
| Written category or operator names | `\operatorname{Hom}`, `\operatorname{Aut}`, `\operatorname{Ob}`, etc. | Reproduce only the operator actually present, including abbreviated forms such as `sg`. |
| Arrow, double arrow, hook, two heads, or plain segment | `\to`, `\Rightarrow`, `\hookrightarrow`, `\twoheadrightarrow`, `\mathbin{-}`; `no head` in tikz-cd | Heads and direction come from the ink. Plain segments are not promoted to arrows; crossed-out heads or labels remain cancelled. |
| Inclusion, inequality, equivalence and equality | `\subset`, `\subseteq`, `\supset`, `\le`, `\ge`, `\sim`, `\simeq`, `\cong`, `=` | Retain direction and extra strokes; uncertain signs are marked locally. |
| Literal two-stroke equals sign in prose | `$=$` | In several late pages it appears to abbreviate “même”; that possible expansion is recorded only in the abbreviations table. |
| Double quotation strokes | two backticks and two apostrophes | LaTeX ``quoted text'' is used for ordinary double quotation strokes; guillemets are used only where visible. |
| Underline and double underline | `\uline{…}`, `\uuline{…}` | Preserve the number and visible extent of the underlines. |
| Cancellation, addition and independent marginal note | `\deleted{…}`, `\inserted{…}`, `\margin{…}` | Cancelled ink remains present. An insertion is placed at its visible anchor; independent marginal text is kept separate. |
| Manuscript number and separate pencil sequence | `\mspage{…}` and `\margin{…}` | Both visible numbering systems are preserved. Attribution of the separate pencil sequence to archival handling is not asserted by the transcription. |
| Diagrams, drawings and tables | `\diagram{…}` with tikz-cd or an array; `\figure{…}` with TikZ | Objects, paths, heads, labels, decorations and readable cancellations are transcribed; drawing geometry is an approximate reconstruction from the supplied scan. |
| Local uncertainty | `\unsure{reading}` or `\unsure{reading|alternative}` | The first reading follows the ink, including unusual spelling or a surprising formula, rather than a mathematically expected correction. |
| Physically unreadable words | `\illegible{n}` | Reserved for words still unreadable after attempted readings and enlargement; never used as a replacement for a mathematical symbol. |
| Abbreviations | As written, including punctuation and raised letters | Possible expansions appear only in the abbreviations table below. |

The independently checked page files preserve repeated equation numbers, changing indices, inconsistent source/target letters and other apparent mathematical discrepancies. Such discrepancies are research questions rather than grounds for silently altering the text. Every scan was visually read as a whole and in both supplied halves, then audited by a reader other than its transcriber; OCR was not used.

## Legibility and limits of confidence

The main prose is usually decipherable, but the notes were written rapidly, with compressed endings, small indices, retracing and substantial cancellation. The most fragile readings are in dense interlinear corrections and marginal notes, especially the overlapping calculations and labels in 024–043, the small topological drawings and overwritten labels in 050–064, and the crowded marginal material in 071, 077, 082, 086 and 090. Rotated and enlarged crops were used for difficult margins and cancellations; this improves visibility without restoring ink hidden by an overstrike.

Every supplied whole-page image and both supplied halves were inspected, and every nonblank page received an independent visual audit. The resulting inventory records the transcribed elements, rather than certifying that all readings are correct. The confidence labels are local qualitative judgments, not estimated error probabilities: “high” can record an audit resolution, “medium” indicates a plausible reading with a remaining stroke or alphabet ambiguity, and “low” indicates a materially uncertain reading. Short uncertain endings and cancelled fragments deserve particular caution even when the surrounding sentence is clear.

Explicit unreadable-word markers remain in a small number of obscured verbal fragments; mathematical symbols instead retain a best attempted reading with a doubt mark. TikZ drawings reproduce the visible topology, labels and approximate relative placement, but they are not facsimiles, and overlapping arrowheads or cancellation strokes can remain ambiguous. The doubt register preserves those local limitations. No claim of error-free transcription is made, and no mathematical inconsistency was silently repaired to improve apparent readability.
