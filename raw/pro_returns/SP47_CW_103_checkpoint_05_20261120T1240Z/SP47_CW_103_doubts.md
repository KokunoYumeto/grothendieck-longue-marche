# Legibility and witness comparison

Current assessment after scans 002–007 and 009–014 (twelve of the sixteen pages; the final assessment will include the remaining pages).

The typed body text is generally more secure than its overtyped corrections, handwritten additions and small indices. Repeated impressions can merge an older character with its replacement; neighbouring formula lines can also collide. The wholly handwritten letter pages add pale joins, clipped edge words, tiny high or low points, and letters whose loops permit more than one identity or alphabet. The least secure readings so far are overwritten underletters, the clipped date/margin on scan009, small punctuation and individual mathematical letters on scans010–014. The changes log gives the precise remaining alternatives. No grammatical or mathematical completion has been promoted to certainty merely because it is plausible.

C preserves much of the source’s correction state and uncertainty, but several of its doubts were resolved by visible strokes, and some apparent underletters, complete word endings, or relation signs could not be maintained with certainty. H supplies useful independent candidate readings, yet the compared pages include regularised quotation forms, omitted cancelled layers, definite readings where the ink remains incomplete, and changes to mathematical indices or relations. H’s editorial notes were credited when they recorded a visible word or state; editorial supplementation and actual source insertions were distinguished. Neither witness was accepted by agreement alone. Agreement among the three fresh readers also did not settle every locus: the main crops changed or reopened readings where the strokes required it.

The comparison counts include uncertainty added, removed or reordered as well as changes of text or source state. They are counts of adjudicated differences, not measured error rates. Low-confidence alternatives remain in the page files and in the doubts table. The three complete reader returns and the retained crops make those limits reviewable. Visual transcription checks are separate from the limited mathematical checks documented in CHECK_EVENTS.tsv.
