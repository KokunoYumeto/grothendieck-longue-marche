## Retained nonuniformities

The two index lists on scan 027 differ visibly: 1, 2, 3 in the stabilizer list and 0, uncertain 1, infinity in the later savoir list, followed by clear 0, 1, infinity in the live choices of lifts. The cancelled prefix before the first invariant tau retains uncertainty about both its raised 2-like mark and cancellation scope. Those inscriptions are not regularized from the group action.

The domain of the last map on scan 025 lacks the exclamation present on the preceding S-extension name and on the Aut ext target. The two lattice terms in scan 026's oblique NB differ in whether Z is hatted. These local differences remain visible even where a uniform definition would be mathematically convenient.
