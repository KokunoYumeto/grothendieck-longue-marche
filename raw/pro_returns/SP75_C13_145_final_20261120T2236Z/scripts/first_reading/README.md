# Historical first-reading preparation scripts

These 51 scripts are unchanged records of the first readers’ crop preparation and report construction. They are included to document the work. Their original paths, assumptions and draft content are preserved.

Do not rerun them in the finalized edition: they can recreate or overwrite crops and preliminary reports and may refer to the original temporary workspace. The accepted page text, reviewed reports and immutable reading evidence are stored separately in `pages/`, `reader_reports/` and `second/`.

`reader_reports/first_reading_scripts_packaging_receipt.json` records each original path, packaged path, byte count and matching source/copy SHA-256 hashes, together with the inventory of documentation remaining outside the package.
