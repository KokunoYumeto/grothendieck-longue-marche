# Working notation table

Preserve all alphabets, primes, exponents, abbreviations and authorial slips as written. Use the supplied PREAMBLE macros. Double quotation marks: `` and two apostrophes when so drawn. Formula numbers at left.

| Written form | Reading / use | Observed basis and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `$\bar m$` | Abbreviation for « même »; do not expand in the transcription. | Scan 005, « la … relation » and « de … »: upper horizontal bar with a visibly curved, two-humped lower letter. Main reader verified the saved crops. A similar shape occurs in scan 003's left margin; inspect locally before applying. |
| `$\rho$` | Cyclic automorphism / permutation of 0, 1, infinity. | Scan 005 definition; looped head and long descending stem. Do not confuse the rho with the Latin exponent p. |
| `$\gamma$` | Greek gamma, frequently a looped descending character with a small high entry stroke. | Scan 003 formulas and margin beta=gamma; do not change a visible gamma into sigma merely from its open top. |
| `$\operatorname{int}(g)$` | Inner-conjugation notation, written int. | Scans 003 and 005; retain its exact argument and nearby parentheses. |
| Pi letter case | Not yet globally settled. | Large indexed, hatted group signs have tall, straight Pi-shaped legs; small unindexed signs may have the same handwritten outline at x-height. Record local shape and uncertainty rather than mechanically changing every pi. |
| `autom`, `mod` | Abbreviations for automorphisme(s), modulo. | Scan 007 has unpunctuated forms at each inspected occurrence. Scan 005 also has a locally visible stop after autom. Read punctuation locally; do not add stops as a house style. |
| Ornate S with subscript 3 | Permutation-group notation, provisionally `\mathfrak S_3` with local alternative `\mathcal S_3` where unresolved. | Scans 005–009: rounded outer sweep, top-right hook and inner curve. No replacement by plain S solely from its mathematical meaning. |
| `g` and `u` | Distinct letters even where a formula seems to require the other. | Scan 008 opening has a loop with a substantial descending stroke: g. Preserve a clearly written g where a witness regularizes it to u. |
| Loop at the top of `l` | Part of the base letter, not necessarily another exponent. | Scan 009, h=g l₁^q: the apparent extra p is the upper loop of l; q is the single superscript. Compare the complete letter and baseline before adding an exponent. |
| Long leftward foot of `g` | A descender may resemble an underline. | Scan 009 arguments: the low horizontal stroke is continuous with the g descender, with no separate emphasis line. Inspect continuity rather than assuming emphasis. |
| `$\Gamma$` with doubled left stem | The group name introduced in scan 011. | Both the definition and the application have a long left vertical (retraced or doubled), a top bar and only a short downturned right tip. The tip is not a full second leg: retain Gamma against H's Pi. This does not settle the case of the separate pi group signs. |
| Overlapping horizontal and oblique text | Words in one direction can be mistaken for part of the other. | Scan 010 margin: the horizontal `tels que` runs near the oblique phrase `groupe abélien`; it does not supply an additional `, que` in that marginal sentence. Read the two baselines separately. |
