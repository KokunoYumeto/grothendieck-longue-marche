## Additional recurring observations

The observed difficulties fit the sheet’s existing broad categories; this run does not establish an additional independent error class. The following recurrent subcases merit more explicit treatment in future instructions. Their places are included in the counts above, not counted a second time as a new category.

- **A descender mistaken for an underline or cancellation.** The low returning stroke of g at arguments (scan009), signature and Malgoire (scan023), and conjugués (scan027), and the long f descending from forme into a marginal formula (scan010), require a continuity check: does the mark belong to a letter above or to an independent rule? This refines categories4 and7 and the existing warning about correction strokes.
- **Crossing writing directions contaminate one another.** On scans010 and026 the slanted note and horizontal prose occupy overlapping coordinates. On scan010 this supplied C’s extra , que; on026 it complicates the cancelled leurs and the final marginal group sign. Each baseline and its continuation must be traced independently before words or parentheses are assigned. This refines omissions and insertion/placement, rather than introducing a new class.
- **Glyph identity, adornment and revision state are separate decisions.** On scans024–025 a tiny inverse-position mark may be an obscured −1, a cancelled −1, or a cancellation whose underlying glyph is not plain. On scans021,025–026 a wave over a group sign may be a live tilde or an abandoned trace, separately from the Fraktur/calligraphic rendering of S. A single uncertainty label must not silently settle these different questions.
- **Grouping shape and correction attachment can be conflated.** The flat bars on019–020 and025 differ from curved braces. In system(8) on020 a local cancellation was overextended to the entire display, and the second-row replacement l₁ was mistaken for an exponent on the third row. This refines drawings/diagrams, placement and struck text; see s019-19, s020-35, s020-36 and s020-40.
- **Abbreviation stops vary locally.** The unpunctuated autom and mod on scan007, autom versus ext. on010, and gén without a stop on019 and gén versus inserted gén. on023 show that recognizing an abbreviation does not supply its terminal dot. The same applies to continuation marks: the four-point sequences on026–027 must not be reduced automatically to a three-dot ellipsis.
- **A revision can deliberately leave grammatical or algebraic roughness.** The singular profini and discret after plural nouns on015, the repeated des on017, the missing formula parenthesis on013, and the successive partial rewrites on025–027 illustrate the need to keep letter recovery separate from grammatical repair. These are local manuscript features, not a claim that every irregularity is authorial.

The hand log gives the page-scoped evidence and witness consequences. The independent second-reading tables retain disagreements and their adjudications, including differences of certainty or layout that are not counted as substantive C/H corrections.
