## How to use the counts

The ten rows above correspond to the error sheet’s ten categories in the same substantive order. Each change-log place receives one primary category, even where more than one issue occurs together. A place that differs from both witnesses is one place, not two. Counts include changes of certainty where these were recorded; they are not a count of independently verified errors in an author’s text, and they are not error rates per word. Macro syntax alone is excluded from the witness collation. The independent audit has its own box, difference and changed-place counts in the changes log and progress record.

## Recurring observations from these pages

- Short connectors frequently end in a thick pen return without a distinct arrowhead. This occurs beside genuinely headed arrows on the same page: scans 065, 069, 071, 075–077, 079 and 084. The distinction must be read locally. The error sheet anticipates arrow confusion; this run adds many examples of blunt ends being promoted to heads.
- A long wave over a Tate expression may finish noticeably to the right of the expression it modifies. Scans 074, 075, 079 and 081 show why physical endpoint and mathematical attachment must be considered separately. The exact written group and its prime remain independent decisions.
- A small equality sign can be a literal prose abbreviation for “même,” as on scan 077. Scan 074 separately retains literal equals bars after D and after the independently written idem/id.; those contexts are not collapsed into one expanded word. In scan 081, `\unsure{du|de}. =` retains both the short-word doubt and the point before the literal equals sign, rather than being converted to a conventional definition sign.
- Torsion prefixes, quotient subscripts, and primes migrate in plausible normalized readings. Scans 065–071 distinguish left and right n; scans 074–081 distinguish left ell-power torsion from right indices. Scan 081’s last line puts the prime on Y rather than on the right-hand G. The manuscript’s clear placements survive even where a standard formulation would use others.
- Inversion and two independent numbering systems explain more than the archive sequence alone. The typescript’s pages3, 7, 9 and10 occur on scans 072, 070, 068 and066. The last two join in reverse scan order. This is a codicological finding, not a transcriptional correction to be silently applied.
- A thin secondary number is distinct from the main manuscript hand. Pencil numbers appear to run from 64 to 84 across the 21 scans, including covers and stationery, but scan 070 retains 69|63 with 69 preferred; sequence expectation does not settle the second digit. They do not prove that all leaves are one mathematical text or that adjacent scans are recto and verso.
- Multiple layers need separate confidence statements. A clear live A, Z or G can overlay an uncertain older letter; removing the old layer merely because the live reading is secure would lose evidence. Scans 074–077, 079, 081 and 084 provide examples.
- Not every repeated darkening is a second letter or cancellation. Retracing in the title on scan 082 and diffuse offset/show-through below the formula on scan 080 were not transcribed as new words or an equality sign. On scan 084, root B014 instead identifies two low points that remain a possible displaced colon: they are preserved with uncertainty, not treated as either certain punctuation or mere background.

These observations refine categories already present in the error sheet rather than establishing a new universal handwriting rule. The principal additional caution is the interaction of displaced tildes, crossed-out draft layers and independent foliation. The hand log gives local occurrences and exceptions; none of these habits was extrapolated to unread pages or other cotes.

## Findings added by completed blind adjudications

- Serifed handwritten T can resemble Gamma in isolated typescript crops. The completed decisions on scans 066 and068 retain T after inspection of each disputed occurrence. The hand log records the inspected scope rather than treating every capped stem as T.
- Hollow machine full stops can look like zero or a small subscript when isolated. The scan 068 audit keeps its identified sentence stops distinct from handwritten circles and indices.
- Scan 070’s projection sign is pi in seven separately adjudicated occurrences, replacing varpi. Its curved cap does not join the two descending legs into an omega base. The inverse occurrence has no lower beta, which remains a separate literal omission rather than being restored by pattern.
- Abbreviations remain independent of lexical expectation: scan 065’s ss-grpe and grpe replace expanded readings. Its map label remains v|nu, so the audit does not claim that every similar two-stroke form has a settled alphabet.
- The lower annotation on scan 082 remains abstrait|obstruct|obstruent with a separate cancellation-layer doubt. A readable live title does not establish the old annotation’s word or whether the surrounding line cancels it.

- A reduced dotted i can be mistaken for a prime, and its detached dot or terminal return can be mistaken for extra punctuation. Scan 077 B005/B033/B034 retain the low i after comparison, while B035 removes an apparent comma that belonged to the index. This does not settle the separate high mark over U_i, which remains stray ink/prime/star.
- Adjacent words need independent cancellation scopes. In scan 077 B026, et|à and de|si remain live before the separate cancelled un grpe, followed by secure live grpe G_a. Its other correction groups and scan 075’s single prime require coupled alternatives for the old and new layers, rather than placing every candidate inside one deletion.
- Short written forms remain short or explicitly doubtful: scan 077 retains Pour|P., grpes|gr., complet|compl. and th.|thm. The physical-edge `topologi\unsure{es}` ending is a different limitation: a better frame does not recover strokes outside or merged with the paper edge.

- Punctuation on a raised insertion can be assigned incorrectly to its leader or the baseline. Scan 079 B002/B003/B004/B043 keep the terminal commas within the inserted text; B017 distinguishes an underline and final point from the neighbouring curved leader. The top-edge k and the inserted k/K retain their own uncertainties.
- A secure live replacement does not establish its older letter. Scan 079 retains live A with doubtful older T or G_k/G/T, and a live T over G/C. Nearby prime, connector and torsion-prefix uncertainties remain local. Its terminal accouplts/accouplements doubt concerns the written short form without changing the clear sentence join to 080.
- A doubled alphabet requires visible doubled strokes at that occurrence. Root 081 B025 retains `\mathbb G_m` because its left upright is doubled; root 079 B021 retains only `\mathbf G_m` in the cancelled formula. Shared mathematical meaning does not license one font to replace the other.
- Vertical marks must keep their layout. In 081 B058, explicit alignment anchors place two upright strokes on one left axis with ell-T beside the upper stroke. The correction does not turn them into a horizontal equality or arrow. The same leaf independently distinguishes the wavy first lower identification from the straight equality below it, and a headed limit from an uncertain underline/limit shaft.
- Familiar prose can conceal a short written form: 081 retains tor./tore, cont with raised t/contenant/contient and provient/pr-plus-rule alternatives. Its compressed non and morphismes remain uncertain; those words are not supplied merely because the argument would accept them.

- Letter case needs an occurrence-based comparison. Scan 083 retains low-armed k(s) in its H0 formula and old first condition, but high-armed K(s) in two later conditions and K(eta) in the Picard denominator. The tiny degree subscript stays doubtful; the three capital cases are medium-confidence decisions (B004/B010/B022/B026/B036/B039). No rule turns every residue-field letter into lowercase k.
- Short marginal forms can survive better than nearby small symbols. Scan 083 restores both literal pr abbreviations and explicit O/L underlines, while retaining star/alpha, a blotted degree-field subscript, fact./f.s. and composé/compos. uncertainties. On 084 the visible short form is pr with pour/par alternatives; the canonical accouplement continuation is represented once (083 B021/B022; 084 B010).
- Revision layers can cross grammatical or mathematical expectations. Scan 083 keeps the live Psi index s separate from an old-subscript/separate-est ambiguity; it resolves a later cancelled et without resolving the heavily crossed neighbouring fragments. Scan 084 records the inserted Z^r over old uncertain E(s)/E_(s), and inserted Je dis que below cancelled Par suite. A secure replacement is not evidence that every old letter or its position is secure (083 B030/B034/B038; 084 B003/B004).
- A point near an index must be tested locally. Scan 083 retains an actual comma in `(C_i,C_j)`, while 084 removes a supplied separator and keeps literal `(C_i C_j)`. The 084 u connector separately remains long-headless/arrow uncertain, with a possible displaced colon after the expression; the initial colon after u is secure (083 B020; 084 B007/B014).
- Audit uncertainty does not imply that every nearby word is doubtful. On 084, literal p-fait and autoadjoint remain secure and final de is resolved, while the old est liée à remains uncertain under exprime. The widened final crop confirms the existing biextension rather than adding another occurrence (B009/B015/B016/B019/B020).

- Unknown formula material requires symbol-level uncertainty. At 071 B024 and 076 B040, editorial `\unsure{?}` marks an unclassified unreadable sign or continuation, preserving the surrounding candidates and layers without asserting a written question mark or a number of words.

These findings complete the documentary reconciliation of all twenty-one applied blind audits and refine existing error-sheet categories. The audit has 874 recorded entries (873 distinct page/rectangle scopes), 627 differing boxes and 242 changed-box decisions. One frozen duplicate on 067 contributes to these entry counts; changed boxes can also overlap a single page edit or concern uncertainty and placement. No aggregate second-reading error rate is inferred from them.
