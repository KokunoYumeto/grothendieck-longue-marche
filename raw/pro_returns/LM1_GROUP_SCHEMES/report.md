## Principal mathematical conclusions

The exact scan399 scheme is the six-dimensional determinant-constrained product using the commutative Eisenstein unit group and the signed Gaussian normalizer. It is flat over Z, smooth away from2, and nonreduced at2 with multiplicity4. Characteristic3 is a degeneration of the quadratic algebra but not a singular fibre of full M. The connected sign kernel M00 is smooth over every prime. The finer C4 scan41 parameter scheme has a different dimension and is smooth over all Z.

The normalized matrix problem has an explicit integral pinning. Its canonical algebra isomorphism must not be confused with arbitrary automorphisms of a general-linear group. Strict projective unipotent lifting has a scalar infinitesimal ambiguity at2. The wording of A.2 is corrected to the unique line on which the operator acts pointwise as identity; other invariant lines can occur over nonreduced rings.

The proposed constant-cyclic/additive presentation is a genuine fppf pushout, but its bare relation has a noncentral kernel. A reduced matrix-identity word and its nontrivial commutator survive sheafification. Nine explicit Gauss relation families give the exact signed kernel. Projectivization requires the full schematic mu2, and a multiplicative determinant factor supplies GL2. Finite word groups and actual sheaf sections differ sharply, including at F3, F5 and Z/4.

The central double-cover problem for the precise M is solved. With kernel and quotient fixed, there are32 classes over Qbar, a group F2^4 plus two rational square-class parameters over Q, and exactly8 classes over Z. The integral representatives adjoin square roots of delta^a e^b t^c. The proof uses the connected component's Kummer classes, uniqueness of its pointed splitting, affine descent, connectedness of the total mu2-squared scheme over Z, exact Cartier duality, and Minkowski's unramified-extension theorem. These are marked extension classes, not a claim of eight pairwise nonisomorphic abstract middle groups.

The literal cup product on C3-611 is(-3,3)=0. The different constant-C4 obstruction is(-1,-3), of period2, ramified at3 and infinity. A constant Dic3 lift of an imaginary quadratic character is impossible. More generally a full constant Dic3 lift with quadratic field Q(sqrt(d)) exists exactly when(d,-1)=0.
