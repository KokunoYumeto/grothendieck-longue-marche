## Cumulative assessment during the run

Fresh page-specific readers keep earlier images and other readers’ conclusions out of the independent passes. Each return includes a full text, rather than only corrections, so agreement on unaltered passages can also be documented. Comparing actual H drawing files is essential: the prose description alone sometimes understates what its drawing already includes.

C provides a useful geometric starting point, but reusing it can propagate the same small omission into all three returns. On scans 002–003 the main crops found additional endpoint gaps, returns and small counters even when all readers agreed. Conversely, a reader’s proposed extra hook was rejected when the crop showed a plain stroke. Agreement is recorded as agreement, never substituted for the ink.

Short formulas and strongly printed type can often be settled, while overwriting, clipping, ambiguous writing history and isolated marks remain genuine limits. A visibly doubled stem is recorded locally; it does not authorize normalization of every F on the sheet. Graphic traces preserve nonlinguistic marks without inventing illegible words or mathematical meanings.

Very large image batches occasionally produced unavailable outputs. The same existing crops were redelivered in small batches, with those delivery failures documented; decided loci were not recropped. Reader crop processing is recorded as actually performed. Early returns that enlarged already enhanced sources without additional contrast are identified honestly rather than relabelled as autocontrast.

Issue prose alone is insufficient to audit a return: direct textual differences in reading.tex can reveal an added stroke or changed uncertainty that the issue list missed. Later briefs explicitly require this consistency check. Inventory terminology and whole-sheet reused-type classification also needed clarification. TikZ remains a stroke-faithful approximation at the level of distinct paths, gaps, labels and marks, not a pixel facsimile of ink pressure or paper grain.
