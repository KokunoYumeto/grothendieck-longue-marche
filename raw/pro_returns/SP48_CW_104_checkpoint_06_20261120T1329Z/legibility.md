## Legibility and witness comparison

This assessment is cumulative and will be completed after scan 017. On scan 002 the short live names and formulas are legible once isolated. The underlying word, possible prior connector, dense lens loops and origin of the edge marks are least secure. C follows the hand-drawn contours much more closely than H’s regularized drawing. Some small gaps and returning strokes nevertheless needed correction in C. H’s editorial prose is distinguished from its actual drawing and from manuscript text.

Scan 003 has strong foreground ink, but the foot is clipped and several cancelled groups are entangled with live letters. The first lower Delta index, older letters, prime-like isolated mark and one visible relation bar remain least secure. The sideways address is readable; grey background remnants do not establish further continuous type. C captures many more drawing details than H, but still omits some gaps, returns and dots. All three fresh readers occasionally missed the same small contour feature; the main crops supplied additional refinements. Endpoint fill-state disagreements were settled by preserving visible white counters rather than assigning ideal node semantics.

Scan 004 has clear major foreground strokes but heavily retraced indices and composite O/F forms. The top target index, old third-row index, last pair indices and overwrite chronology are the least secure readings. Direct crops resolve several looped arguments against the witnesses' C forms, two wedge-shaped operators and a 2-shaped separator. Doubled F forms require local examination: no alphabet is imposed by the product or inclusion pattern. The small geometric gaps, returns and detached points are better captured by C than H, but both omit several. No continuous readable type can be established from the pale background alone.

Scan 005 is a densely annotated landscape trial sheet. The main foreground prose and many long returning F stems can be resolved, while tiny indices, canceled letters, the isolated low E-like glyph and the lower sketch's unmatched label remain uncertain. Several readers inherited C's reversed vertical-membership rotation; all six are corrected individually from downward-open arches. H omits the small array/maps and many pi annotations, incompletely captures the right column, and supplies arrowheads or ordinary equations where the ink has headless connectors or a single bar. C preserves more of the sheet but leaves some resolvable doubts and omits several literal marks. Pale rotated background impressions do not establish recoverable words or a reliable typed-word count.

Scan 006 combines readable sideways type with a densely annotated handwritten working sheet. The type is complete, including completion signs and an unfilled n–N gap. Clear stems resolve several D/I/F alphabets, but the overwritten map labels, older qualifiers, B alphabet and canceled opening remain least secure. The lower calculation relation remains >/= from its converging strokes even though all three readers and both witnesses had equals. H omits much of the margin and small geometry, expands or abbreviates the footer differently, closes the open loop, and moves the separate right map. C preserves far more detail but leaves some resolvable doubts, regularizes several placements and omits small detached marks. These marks have been recorded without assigning them mathematical meaning.

Scan 007 is a handwritten text sheet with two formula displays and several interlinear changes. Most continuous text is legible. The least secure places are the nearly vertical accents, covered final stop of canceled autom, reduced par, canceled de and e/s, the compound quantifier, the two small marks before cause, and the dotted canceled plui/plus. The main crops resolved Phi, barred I, the old D beneath M, doubled J and several word doubts. H regularizes quotation marks and abbreviations, changes insertion states and misidentifies the final family; C is closer but its centered M and aligned arrow starts do not preserve the map's actual positions. A complete existing crop was needed for classes because the first planned neighboring crop did not include that whole word. No unseen lettering was reconstructed.

Scan 008 is a handwritten text sheet with type and Hom maps, interlinear changes and a final commutativity diagram. Most live words and indices are legible. Least secure are the naming cluster, heavily overworked old capitals and parenthesis, the canceled article, the high 4/asterisk, the short relation trace, the final accent in commutativité and the locally H-shaped quantifier. C is closer in deletion states and literal diagrams; H sometimes supplies punctuation, repeated words, an extra de or ou, changes J to I and gives canceled words live. C also needs corrections: a new quantifier doubt, refined naming alternatives, absent final period in a cancellation, small points, unequal arrow positions and the scope of the lower brackets. The index reversal in the final diagram is manuscript evidence and remains uncorrected. No title, date or author folio is recovered; the archive number remains a comment.
