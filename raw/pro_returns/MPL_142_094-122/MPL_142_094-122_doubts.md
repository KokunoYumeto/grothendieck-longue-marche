# MPL_142_094–122: doubts, notation and abbreviations

## Shared notation table

| Ink / feature | LaTeX policy |
|---|---|
| Small pi with subscript | `\pi_0`, `\pi_1`; distinguish capital `\Pi` by the actual strokes, mark doubt if unresolved. |
| Capital, wide fundamental-groupoid Pi with hat | `\widehat{\Pi}_1`; do not replace by small pi. |
| Prime | `'`; repeated prime `''`; never silently substitute for an exclamation. |
| Exclamation exponent | `^!` only if the stroke and dot show it; otherwise `\unsure{...}`. |
| Theta, lifted Theta | `\Theta`, `\widetilde{\Theta}` and visible indices; do not conflate small theta `\theta`. |
| Broad, cursive T / tau-like groupoid symbol | Provisionally `\mathcal{T}` if a script capital T is visible; use `\unsure{\mathcal{T}\|\tau}` if undecided. |
| Script curve C | `\mathcal{C}` if genuinely script; ordinary C as `C`. Keep it distinct from the preceding groupoid sign. |
| Roman X, E, I and group elements | `X`, `E`, `I`, `g`, etc., preserving case and all hats, bars, indices. |
| Blackboard/gothic/script | `\mathbb`, `\mathfrak`, `\mathcal` only where the letter's visible form supports that alphabet. |
| Isomorphism / similarity | `\simeq`, `\cong`, `\sim` according to the ink; use uncertainty instead of imposing equivalence. |
| Operators written as words | `\operatorname{Im}`, `\operatorname{Transp}`, `\operatorname{int}`, `\operatorname{Aut}`, etc.; preserve abbreviations/case exactly. |
| Map arrows / inclusions | `\to`, `\mapsto`, `\hookrightarrow`, `\subset`, `\subseteq`, etc. only for visible head/bar; plain dash stays `-` with doubt if needed. |
| Double quotation marks | LaTeX two backticks and two apostrophes: ``text''; guillemets only when drawn. |
| Abbreviations | Keep actual manuscript letters and stops, e.g. `gr.`, `rev.`, `ét.`, `resp.`, `i.e.`; expansions ONLY in doubts list. |
| Deletions and insertions | `\deleted{...}` and `\inserted{...}`; reproduce readable erased words, no suppression. |
| Underlining | `\uline{...}` / `\uuline{...}`. |
| Doubt | `\unsure{reading}` or `\unsure{reading\|alternative}`; `\illegible{n}` only for physically unreadable words after a visual attempt. No illegible macro for mathematical symbols. |
| Page marks | `\scanpage{N}` first; `\mspage{n}` at the visible place. |
| Script indexing set and point names (114–121) | Use `\mathcal{R}` for the visibly script set of repères and plain `R` for vertex/point labels such as `R_r^{ij}`; do not merge the two alphabets. |
| Cartographic involutions (114–121) | The flat-topped downstem forms on 114 read `\tau_0`, `\tau_1`, `\tau_\infty`. Curled signs in 115 (7) and 120–121 retain explicit `\sigma` / `\tau` alternatives where the ink does not decide; algebraic identities do not authorize normalization. |
| Rotations (114 onward) | Preserve the visible Greek `\rho`, its indices, inverses and order of composition. Keep it distinct from Roman `R` and from the involution signs. |
| Path and loop letters (106, 114–121) | Preserve lower `\lambda`, capital `\Lambda`, primed `\Lambda^{\prime i}` and distinct capital `A` by their strokes. The visible primes in 117–121 are not exclamation exponents. Do not replace an ambiguous `A` / `\Lambda` without an explicit doubt. |
| Triangular stars / dots (112, 116 onward) | Preserve upper-left, lower-left and right positions: `^{\substack{*\\*}*}` is the three-star form. Replace only the corresponding star by `\cdot` when a dot is visible. On 112 the attached words are sommets, centres faces and milieux arêtes respectively; on 116 these positions are labelled infinity, zero and one. Preserve the geometric ordering without moving a mark between positions. |
| Literal short equals-like abbreviation (94, 102, 114, 116, 120) | Retain `=` when two short horizontal strokes are visible, adding `\unsure{=}` where needed. Do not expand the sign to a guessed word such as même, even when that reading is plausible. |
| Compact product qualifier (97) | The fraction-like sign before direct is `\unsure{\frac12\|s.}`; no expansion to semi-direct in the transcription. |
| Diagrams / drawings | `\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}...\end{tikzcd}}` / `\figure{\begin{tikzpicture}...\end{tikzpicture}}`. No images or verbal substitutes. |

The table was agreed before parallel transcription and completed by comparison of the scans before the context archive was opened. Unresolved letter forms remain explicit alternatives; expected mathematics did not decide them.

## Scope and confidence

This file contains 200 curated doubt entries and a separate exhaustive register of 369 in-text doubt macros: 364 occurrences of `\unsure` and 5 of `\illegible`. The occurrence register includes 3 macros nested inside another doubt macro; these are counted separately. The two registers deliberately overlap and do not count distinct reading problems.

High, medium and low are qualitative reading judgments, not probabilities. A macro occurrence inherits a curated confidence only when its reading can be matched locally on the same scan; otherwise its confidence defaults to low. Approximate image regions come from the curated doubt or the corresponding inventoried margin, diagram, drawing, or nearby prose block. A TeX line and column identify the exact place in the individual page fragment and are a fallback locator, not a claim about manuscript line numbering. No argument is truncated; nested alternatives remain in the outer argument as well as receiving their own occurrence entry.

## Legibility and the least secure passages

Much of the large main prose is readable, but confidence is uneven. The short title and printed sheets are generally easier than the manuscript revisions. The most difficult extended passages are the diagonal marginal notes on 094, 096, 100, 101 and 104, together with the compressed algebraic marginalia on 111 and the revised notes on 115 and 118. Several are explicitly tentative phrase by phrase even after an independent second visual reading. Crossed-out words, compact interlinear additions, and the attachment of insertion strokes are less secure than the surrounding main text.

The drawings on 106 and 110–121 retain their paths, labels, directional marks, cancellations and visible auxiliary lines in TikZ. They remain manual reconstructions: heavily retraced arrowheads, minute superscripts, and near-overlapping vertices are their weakest details. Lambda/Lambda-prime/A and tau/sigma distinctions were compared across the scans, with explicit alternatives retained where shape alone did not settle them. The triangular stars and dots are positional, not a linear string.

Specific cautions matter when citing these pages. The upper inserted sentence on 098 stops visibly at doit; the transcription does not complete it with an equality of fibre cardinalities. The compact fraction-like qualifier on 097 remains 1/2 versus s., rather than being expanded. The overwritten MD/DM letters and the underlined orientations/revêtements in the diagonal note on 100 remain doubtful. The Cyrillic-looking name on 096 and short equals-like abbreviations are preserved without romanization or interpretive expansion. Independent auditing reduces omissions and unmarked guesses but does not turn these unresolved readings into established text.
