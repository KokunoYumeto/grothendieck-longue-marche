\subsection{Nonlinear Wajnryb tests on finite surface covers}
\begin{statement}{E.76}{Proved}{The complete retained fibres pass three nonlinear surface-cover tests}
For $g\in\{2,3,4\}$, let $V_g=F(u_1,\ldots,u_{2g})$, and put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 v_g=u_1u_2^{-1}\cdots u_{2g-1}u_{2g}^{-1},\qquad
 \omega_g=v_g\,\iota(v_g),\qquad \iota(u_j)=u_j^{-1},\\
 \Pi_g=V_g/\langle\!\langle\omega_g\rangle\!\rangle.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.76.1}
\]
The following based automorphisms, with all unlisted generators fixed,
give the literal Wajnryb action:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
 a_i\ (i<2g)&u_i\mapsto u_i u_{i+1}^{-1}u_i,
                  \quad u_{i+1}\mapsto u_i\\
 a_{2g}&u_j\mapsto u_j u_{2g}^{-1}\ (j<2g),
                  \quad u_{2g}\mapsto u_{2g}\\
 d&u_j\mapsto w u_j\ (j\leq4),
                  \quad w=u_1u_2^{-1}u_3u_4^{-1}.
\end{array}
 \tag{E.76.2}
\]
They preserve $\omega_g$.  They satisfy all relations (A), (B), (C),
and, for $g=4$, (C$'$), in the presentation used in
\cite[Theorem 3.1]{DE_LochakNakamuraSchneps2026}.  The capping comparison
is the actual quotient map in (E.76.1); in these based generators
\[
       (a_1\cdots a_{2g})^{4g+2}
                 =\operatorname{Inn}(\omega_g)
                    \quad\hbox{in }\operatorname{Aut}(V_g).
 \tag{E.76.3}
\]
Thus the action on $\Pi_g$ kills the boundary twist.

For $(g,L)=(2,S_3),(3,S_3),(2,S_4)$, let
\[
 \mathscr C_{g,L}
    =\operatorname{Epi}(\Pi_g,L)/\operatorname{Aut}(L),
 \qquad
 T_{g,L}=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_{2g},d\rangle
           \subset\operatorname{Sym}(\mathscr C_{g,L}),
 \tag{E.76.4}
\]
where a point $[\rho]$ acts on the right by precomposition with
the corresponding automorphism (E.76.2).  These are actual finite
surface-cover actions. Their exhaustive sizes and source comparisons
are
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r|r}
 (g,L)&\#\operatorname{Hom}(\Pi_g,L)&
 \#\operatorname{Epi}(\Pi_g,L)&\#\mathscr C_{g,L}&
 \text{raw source degree}&\text{faithful source degree}\\ \hline
 (2,S_3)&486&360&60&1080&48\\
 (3,S_3)&16038&15120&2520&75600&342\\
 (2,S_4)&34176&21600&900&16200&552.
\end{array}
 \tag{E.76.5}
\]
The last source is defined from all $2g-1$ evaluations
\[
 (x,y)\longmapsto
    (y_i,a_i^2),\qquad
 y_i=a_{i-1}\cdots a_2 a_1^2a_2\cdots a_{i-1},
             \quad 2\leq i\leq2g,
 \tag{E.76.6}
\]
and all six actual $B/F$ cosets.  Each of these three specified
source homomorphisms factors through $G_6=B/(N'N^6)$.

All $24$ retained E.17 origins, all $18$ E.24 correction values above
each origin, and both retained multiplier residues pass these tests.
More precisely, in each target the substitution
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 a_1\longmapsto a_1^\lambda,\qquad d\longmapsto d^\lambda,\\
 a_i\longmapsto f(y_i,a_i^2)^{-1}a_i^\lambda f(y_i,a_i^2)
                         \quad(2\leq i\leq2g)
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.76.7}
\]
satisfies every Wajnryb relation and generates the original full
$T_{g,L}$.  This holds on the entire specified fixed-$G_6$ word
fibres and for all the compatible multiplier lifts
$\lambda\equiv1\pmod {72}$.  Each of the three tests has exactly
three distinct corrected generator tuples among the $24$ origins.
Explicit simultaneous point intertwiners certify generation for all
of them.  In the $S_4$ action both original orbits, of sizes $540$
and $360$, are retained.

In particular every later correction in $N'N^6$ preserves these
three tests. The existing retained CM pairs, including the extra
CM pairs, therefore survive these complete finite surface-cover
comparisons. This assertion concerns the displayed actual finite
source maps and their whole specified fibres.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We construct the based surface maps, enumerate the finite cover sets,
and then prove the full source and fibre comparisons.

\paragraph{The based maps and capping.}
For $i<2g$, the inverse of $a_i$ sends
$u_i\mapsto u_{i+1}$ and
$u_{i+1}\mapsto u_{i+1}u_i^{-1}u_{i+1}$.
The inverse of $a_{2g}$ replaces $u_j u_{2g}^{-1}$ by
$u_j u_{2g}$, and the inverse of $d$ replaces $w u_j$ by
$w^{-1}u_j$.  Substitution proves both inverse identities; in
particular $d(w)=w$.

These formulas also give an explicit geometric construction.
Take the double cover of a disk branched at $2g+1$ points.
Its orbifold group is the free product of the involutions
$t_1,\ldots,t_{2g+1}$.  The kernel of their common sign map
is free on $u_i=t_i t_{2g+1}$.  The inverse Hurwitz moves of
adjacent involutions restrict to the first two rows of
(E.76.2).  The preimage of a circle surrounding the first
four branch points has two boundary curves.  For one of
them the twist is the last row.  To see the based formula,
use $v_i=u_i u_4^{-1}$, $1\leq i\leq3$, on that side of the
cut.  Its boundary is $w=v_1v_2^{-1}v_3$.  The edge twist
conjugates the $v_i$ by $w$ and sends the connecting generator
$u_4$ to $w u_4$, fixing $u_5,\ldots,u_{2g}$.  Consequently
it sends each of $u_1,\ldots,u_4$ to $w u_i$, exactly as stated.

The boundary polygon in (E.76.1) pairs each edge oppositely;
all its vertices are identified.  It has one face, $2g$ edges
and one vertex, giving the oriented closed genus-$g$ surface
after attaching the boundary disk.  Thus (E.76.1) is the actual
fundamental-group capping map.  Direct reduced-word substitution
checks boundary preservation and (E.76.3).  Equivalently, the
remaining annular twist becomes conjugation by the boundary,
which is trivial in $\Pi_g$.  The finite actions therefore
factor through capping as claimed; no unproved compatibility
between two profinite surface embeddings is used.

The program \texttt{de\_wajnryb\_free\_model.py} verifies the
complete presentation on the free generators.  There are $11$
relations for $g=2$, $23$ for $g=3$, and $39$ for $g=4$.
All left and right words and all their common reduced generator
images are retained in its JSON.  The literal $d_3$ word sends
$u_i$ to $(u_1u_2^{-1}\cdots u_5u_6^{-1})u_i$ for $i\leq6$
and fixes the remaining generators.  The basis
$e_i=u_i-u_{i+1}$, $e_{2g}=u_{2g}$ has determinant one, and the
program verifies that abelianization gives exactly (E.72.1).
The initial boundary-twist sign guess was rejected by the
free-word assertion; its source and logs are preserved.
The retained equality is the positive sign in (E.76.3),
checked independently in all three genera.

\paragraph{Exhaustive finite surface-cover sets.}
For $S_3$ all $6^{2g}$ tuples are enumerated, the boundary word
is evaluated literally, and tuples generating a proper subgroup
are removed.  Every automorphism permutes the three
transpositions, and that permutation determines it since
the transpositions generate $S_3$.  The six distinct inner
maps therefore give its entire automorphism group.
An epimorphic tuple has trivial
automorphism stabilizer, so the orbits have size six.
For $S_4$ all $24^4$ tuples are enumerated.  The complete
subgroup enumeration gives $30$ subgroups and the eight
maximal proper subgroups of orders
$6,6,6,6,8,8,8,12$; their literal subsets supply the complete
epimorphism test.

The $S_4$ automorphisms are checked without assuming an orbit
count.  Use the generating pair $((12),(1234))$.  An
automorphism must send the first generator to an involution
with conjugacy-class size six and the second to an element
of order four.  All $36$ candidate pairs are propagated
through the full Cayley table.  Exactly $24$ give bijective
homomorphisms, and their maps are precisely the $24$ inner
automorphisms.  Hence all epimorphism orbits have size $24$.
The resulting numbers are (E.76.5).  For $S_4$ an independent
enumeration of products of two handle commutators gives
the same total of $34176$ boundary-identity tuples.

For every representative, every generator and its inverse
are evaluated by (E.76.2), and the result is assigned to its
actual simultaneous-automorphism orbit.  This saves the full
point maps and verifies every Wajnryb relation again in the
finite permutation target.  Our convention is composition
$ab=a\circ b$ on free-group automorphisms.  Precomposition
is therefore the right action $[\rho]a=[\rho\circ a]$,
and for point arrays $p(ab)=p(b)[p(a)]$.  This is the convention
used throughout the saved source and fibre calculations.

\paragraph{The entire induced source.}
Every evaluation in (E.76.6) is an actual homomorphism from
the original $F=F(x,y)$.  The source retains the same six
Schreier words and the same $B/F$ permutation as the earlier
finite sources.  Its generator relations $s^2=r^3=1$ and
all products of $s,r,r^{-1}$ are checked literally.
Compression discards a transitive $B$-set only after constructing
an explicit point intertwiner to a retained set.  All of
these maps remain in the certificate; the degrees are the
last two columns of (E.76.5).

Next evaluate all $97$ actual free generators of $N$.
In every target their sixth powers are identity, and every
one of the $\binom{97}{2}$ pair commutators is identity.
Since the image of $N$ is then abelian and generated by
elements of exponent dividing six, this proves that the
entire $N'N^6$ is killed.  This is the asserted factorization
through the specified $G_6$ quotient, not only a test on
selected elements of a smaller correction subgroup.

\paragraph{Whole fibres and generation.}
The actual E.17 origin words $f_i$ are read with their
original indices and CM labels.  Their two ordinary
exponent sums are checked to be zero.  Each is evaluated
in the compressed source, and every original target
coordinate is recovered using the saved point intertwiners.
The recovery is checked first on the original source
generators $x,y$.  Thus the values inserted in (E.76.7)
are literal evaluations of these words.

Every one of the $24$ old correction words and all $13$
homogeneous correction basis words used to reconstruct
the E.24 family have source value identity.  The reconstruction
formula uses only products, inverses and powers of those
words.  Hence all $18$ points over every origin, for both
multiplier residues, have exactly the same new evaluation
values as that origin.  More generally, the complete
$N'N^6$ factorization proves the assertion on its entire
specified word coset.

All five or seven corrected generator values are retained
for every origin.  Direct substitution in every presentation
relation passes in all cases.  The identity pair is checked
first as an independent control.  The generator orders are
six for the two $S_3$ models and twelve for the $S_4$ model.
The program also explicitly compares the first and
seventy-third powers of every generator.  Thus all multiplier
lifts $\lambda\equiv1\pmod {72}$ give these same powers.

For generation, an actual point permutation $h_i$ is saved
for every corrected tuple, satisfying
\[
       h_i\,p(a_j)=p(a'_j)\,h_i,
       \qquad h_i\,p(d)=p(d')\,h_i
                 \quad\hbox{on every point}.
 \tag{E.76.8}
\]
The program builds and checks the point map along the full
generator graph.  In the $S_4$ model it does this on both
orbits and verifies all generator edges after combining
the maps.  Equation (E.76.8) makes the corrected group
conjugate in the full symmetric group to $T_{g,L}$.
On the other hand every corrected generator is a word in
the original generators, so the corrected group is a
subgroup of $T_{g,L}$.  The two finite groups have equal
order and therefore coincide.  This proves full generation
without assuming a quotient kernel is invariant under
an arbitrary shadow.

The retained programs are the
\texttt{de\_wajnryb\_s3\_model/source/fibres.py} and
\texttt{de\_wajnryb\_s4\_model/source/fibres.py} batches,
with JSON, NPZ and stdout/stderr for each genus.
The original $S_4$ engine metadata is preserved before
its type label and its two-orbit generation handling
were made explicit.  All earlier project artifacts remain
unchanged.  These are complete finite source/fibre results;
the full-GT image of the character in E.65 is not inferred
from them.
\end{proof}
