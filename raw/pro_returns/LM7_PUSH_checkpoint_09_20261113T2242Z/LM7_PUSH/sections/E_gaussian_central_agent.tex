\begin{statement}{E.69}{Proved}{The Gaussian central layer and its exact common finite fibres}
Let $H_3$ be the exponent-three Heisenberg group, written in
logarithmic coordinates as
\[
 (v,c)(w,e)=(v+w,c+e+\tfrac12\det(v,w)),
 \qquad v,w\in\mathbf F_3^2,\quad c,e\in\mathbf F_3.
 \tag{E.69.1}
\]
Here $1/2=2$. Let $d$ have order four and act by
$d(v,c)d^{-1}=(Sv,c)$, with the matrix $S$ of E.67.2.
The actual Gaussian triangle quotient
\[
 \widetilde{\mathrm G}=H_3\rtimes\langle d\rangle,
 \qquad
 x=(e_1,0)d,\quad y=(-e_2,0)d^2,\quad z=d
 \tag{E.69.2}
\]
has order $108$. Keep the Mordell quotient $\mathrm M$ of
E.67 unchanged. Evaluate each word in the same six ordered
pairs E.67.5 in each of these two groups, and denote this
actual twelve-evaluation homomorphism by $\widetilde\Theta$.

Define two automorphisms of $H_3$ by
\[
 A(v,c)=(-v,c+v_1+v_2),\qquad
 B(v,c)=(-Sv,c+v_1+v_2).
 \tag{E.69.3}
\]
For $N=N_B$ and $K_6=\overline{N'N^6}$, the complete
correction image is
\[
 \begin{split}
 L:=\widetilde\Theta(N')=\widetilde\Theta(K_6)
   &=\mathcal M\times\widetilde{\mathcal G},\\
 \widetilde{\mathcal G}
   &=\{(u,v,t,A(t),B(v),B(u)):u,v,t\in H_3\}
     \simeq H_3^3.
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.69.4}
\]
In particular $|L|=3^{12}$. The actual projection which
forgets the six Gaussian central coordinates induces
\[
       L^{\mathrm{ab}}\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
       \mathcal M\times\mathcal G,
       \qquad L'\simeq\mathbf F_3^3.
 \tag{E.69.5}
\]
The right side and its source map are precisely those of
E.67 and E.68.

There are four automorphisms of $\widetilde{\mathrm G}$
which fix $x$, carry $y,z$ to conjugates by elements of
$H_3$, and preserve the positive primitive boundary
commutator. They are conjugation by $1,x,x^2,x^3$.
They have $36$ pairs of such frames. Exactly twelve
framed six-tuples belong to $\widetilde{\mathcal G}$:
if $h_y,h_z\in H_3$ are the frames, their equation is
\[
          (h_z)_c=(h_z)_1+(h_z)_2,
          \qquad (h_y)_c\ \hbox{arbitrary}.
 \tag{E.69.6}
\]
Thus each of the four E.67 Gaussian actions has exactly
three framed lifts in the actual correction image.

Retain the E.66 map $\Psi:N'\to Z$ and exactly the
$24$ E.17 origins used in E.68. The complete common image is
\[
       (\Psi,\widetilde\Theta)(N')=Z\times L,
       \qquad |Z\times L|=189\,321\,481\,108\,517\,289\,984.
 \tag{E.69.7}
\]
Every one of E.68's $1152$ finite combinations has exactly
three lifts to these specified Gaussian framed targets.
All $3456$ combinations have saved actual $N'$-word
preimages. For one such correction $u$ over an origin
$f_i$, the complete word fibre inside $f_iN'$ is
\[
 f_i u\bigl(N'\cap\ker\Psi\cap\ker\widetilde\Theta\bigr).
 \tag{E.69.8}
\]
These conclusions concern the displayed finite maps and
their full correction-image fibres. They retain the
Mordell quotient of E.67 and do not assign a full-GT
lift or generation in an additional unevaluated source.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We identify the quotient, its complete source image and
the common word fibres in this order.

\paragraph{The actual Gaussian quotient and its frames.}
In the original quartic triangle, put
$X=x^2y$ and $Y=z^2y$. After killing the central fourth
power of $z$, the exact free-by-cyclic presentation of
E.64 gives
\[
 dXd^{-1}=Y^{-1},\quad dYd^{-1}=X,\quad
 x=Xd,\quad y=Y^{-1}d^2,\quad z=d.
 \tag{E.69.9}
\]
Pass the free kernel $\langle X,Y\rangle$ to its
class-two exponent-three quotient. Its collection map
is exactly (E.69.1), with $X=(e_1,0)$ and
$Y=(e_2,0)$. Thus (E.69.2) is a quotient of the actual
triangle, with no change of peripheral words. Direct
collection gives
\[
 xyz=1,\qquad y^2=z^4=1,\qquad
 x^4=[X,Y^{-1}]=[X,Y]^{-1}.
 \tag{E.69.10}
\]
Forgetting the central coordinate gives the literal
Gaussian affine quotient of E.67.3.

The finite model lists all $108$ elements and verifies
the multiplication law, all inverses and all
$108^3$ associativity triples. For each of the
$27^2$ pairs $(h_y,h_z)$ it forms
\[
 y_*=h_y y h_y^{-1},\qquad z_*=h_z z h_z^{-1}
 \tag{E.69.11}
\]
and tests $xy_*z_*=1$. It then propagates
$x\mapsto x$, $y\mapsto y_*$ along every edge of the
full $108$-element Cayley graph. Agreement on every
edge proves a homomorphism; $108$ distinct images
prove it is an automorphism. It also checks the image
of $z$ and the positive commutator. Exactly four
distinct automorphisms survive, each with nine frame
pairs; direct comparison with all conjugations by
$x^j$, $0\leq j<4$, identifies them as stated.
Their actual six word values are
\[
 (h_y^{-1},h_y,h_z,h_z^{-1},h_z^{-1}h_y,h_y^{-1}h_z).
 \tag{E.69.12}
\]
This ordering retains both three-cycle and inverse
equations, including the central terms.

\paragraph{The complete correction image.}
Use the unchanged $97$ literal free generators of
$N_B$. Every one of their twelve values has even
deck exponent. The even-deck subgroup of $\mathrm M$
has exponent three, and the even-deck subgroup of
$\widetilde{\mathrm G}$ has exponent six. These
exponent assertions follow from the group laws for
every element of those subgroups. Hence all $N^6$
values vanish and the two images in (E.69.4) agree.

On the deck-zero part use the twelve Mordell
translation coordinates followed by six Gaussian
triples $(v_1,v_2,c)$, a total of thirty coordinates.
The program evaluates all $\binom{97}{2}=4656$
commutators, closes their span under conjugation by
all $97$ actual generators and under Lie brackets,
and retains literal word preimages for every row.
The initial commutator rank is twelve; all subsequent
normal-conjugation and bracket checks remain in that
same span. The following formula realizes each
linear row addition by an actual word in this
class-two exponent-three group:
\[
                  \log\bigl(ab[a,b]\bigr)=\log(a)+\log(b).
 \tag{E.69.13}
\]
Indeed the Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff coefficient is
$1/2=2$, and $2+1=0$ in $\mathbf F_3$; multiplication
by two is inversion. Thus no row operation replaces
the source word map by an abstract linear surrogate.
The closed normal generation of $N'$ by the pairwise
commutators proves that this is its complete image.

Both $A$ and $B$ in (E.69.3) preserve the law (E.69.1):
their linear parts have determinant one and their
central corrections are linear. The twelve-dimensional
span just computed is exactly the direct product in
(E.69.4). This equality is checked in both directions
against its twelve displayed coordinate basis vectors.
The three brackets of the two generators in each
$H_3$ factor form the entire three-dimensional derived
subgroup. Their common kernel under the E.67 projection
also has dimension three. This proves the precise
abelianization isomorphism (E.69.5), including its
source projection.

The same nine-dimensional Gaussian image is already
the image of $\widehat F'$. To check this assertion
with its exact map, start with the single literal
$[x,y]$, close its Gaussian span under conjugation by
the two free-source generators and under brackets,
and compare it in both directions with the nine
Gaussian basis vectors in (E.69.4). The resulting
rank is nine. Consequently each retained origin
also has its Gaussian value in the displayed graph.
Substitution of the $36$ frame pairs (E.69.12) in
that graph yields exactly (E.69.6), with twelve
solutions. For every actual E.17 word, the correction
is evaluated as $f_i^{-1}t$ in the group law, including
its central cross term. The complete source membership
test gives the same twelve solutions. Their Mordell
frame is the uniquely specified one already proved
in E.67.

\paragraph{The common quotient with the retained distribution source.}
Let $J$ be the actual image of
$(\Psi,\widetilde\Theta):N'\to Z\times L$.
Both projections are onto. Goursat's construction
gives a common quotient of $Z$ and the finite
three-group $L$. A nontrivial finite three-group has
a nontrivial cyclic quotient of order three. Its
composition with $L$ factors through $L^{\mathrm{ab}}$.
But (E.69.5) identifies this particular abelianization
map with E.67, and E.68 proves that its actual common
image with $\Psi$ is the full product. Such a cyclic
common quotient cannot occur. Thus the common quotient
is trivial and (E.69.7) follows.

For explicit common preimages, the new certificate
reads E.68's complete Schreier presentation without
changing it. It obtains six words in $N'$ whose
$\Psi$ values are one, whose Mordell values are zero,
and whose Gaussian E.67 values are the six successive
horizontal basis vectors of $H_3^3$. Their three

exactly the three new central basis vectors. Their
old-source identities are replayed on the entire
$5331$-point source. In fact all three commutators
are the identity on all $5223$ old induced points,
which is stronger than the retained $\Psi$ assertion.

Start with each of the $1152$ saved E.68 words and
evaluate it in the actual new twelve groups. For
each of the three central lifts of its Gaussian
frame, multiply by the necessary product of the
three just-constructed central words. All $3456$
resulting words are replayed in the new groups;
their old values are retained by the checked
central identities. The finite target counts
and full word cosets (E.69.8) follow.

The four programs share the prefix

\end{proof}
