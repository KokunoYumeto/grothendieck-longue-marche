\subsection{The complete quaternion correction fibre in the \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$}{SL2(F3)} Ferrari target}

\begin{statement}{E.59}{Proved}{Every retained CM matrix pair survives the complete \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$}{SL2(F3)} Ferrari test}
Retain the actual groups $B,F,N_B,G_6$ and the $24$ indexed,
multiplier-one $G_6$ points of E.17. There is a specified finite
Ferrari quotient
\[
 \pi_{24}:B_4\twoheadrightarrow T_{24},\qquad
 |T_{24}|=2^{29}3^4=43\,486\,543\,872,
 \tag{E.59.1}
\]
obtained from every epimorphism kernel of the free Ferrari group
$\langle\xi_1,\xi_2\rangle$ onto $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$.
Let $\rho_{24}:B\twoheadrightarrow G_{24}$ be the actual induced
source formed from all six E.17 evaluations and all six $B/F$
cosets. Then
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 |G_{24}|=2^{26}3^6=48\,922\,361\,856,\qquad
 P=\rho_{24}(N_B),\quad |P|=2^{20}3^4,\\
 C=\rho_{24}(N_B')=\rho_{24}(N_B'N_B^6)
   =\rho_{24}(F'\cap N_B'N_B^6)\simeq Q_8^3.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.59.2}
\]
The isomorphism in the last line is the product of three actual
evaluation-coordinate homomorphisms described below.

Writing $K_6=N_B'N_B^6$, the common source is exactly
\[
 D_{24}=B/(K_6\cap\ker\rho_{24})
     \simeq G_6\mathbin{\times_{G_{24}/C}}G_{24},
 \qquad \ker(D_{24}\to G_6)=C.
 \tag{E.59.3}
\]
For each one of the $24$ retained normalized words $f_i\in F'$, the
whole finite-value fibre of actual $F'$ words over its $G_6$ value
is $f_iC$, with $512$ elements. In every one of these fibres,
the exact counts at multiplier one are
\[
 \begin{array}{l|r}
 \text{literal finite conditions}&\text{number of values}\\\hline
 (sf)^2=(f^{-1}r)^3=1\text{ in }D_{24}&4\\
 \text{the preceding conditions and the full target pentagon}&2\\
 \text{also all three four-strand braid relations}&2.
 \end{array}
 \tag{E.59.4}
\]
All four first-row points generate the actual three-strand source
$D_{24}$. Both last-row points generate the whole target $T_{24}$
through the specified four-strand braid map and fix the literal
three- and four-strand full twists. Every value has a
retained literal commutator-word preimage in $F'$.

The $24$ base points have the $24$ distinct admissible CM matrix
pairs proved in E.1 and retained in E.17, including all $12$ extra
pairs. Thus each of those matrix fibres has two values passing
this complete finite test. The counts in \textup{(E.59.4)} are
counts in the entire $512$-element fibre over each specified
$G_6$ point. They do not enumerate other affine $G_6$ points or
other multiplier residues in the same CM matrix fibre, and do
not assert a compatible full profinite pentagon lift.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We construct the target and its faithful source, determine the whole
correction image, and then check the exact finite equations and generation.

\paragraph{The actual universal target.}
Put $S=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$. Enumerate its $24$ determinant-one
matrices and the $384$ ordered generating pairs. Conjugation by
$\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$ supplies $24$ different automorphisms.
To verify completeness without assuming an abstract automorphism-group
identification, choose one generating pair of $S$, retain a Cayley
spanning tree, and test the homomorphism given by every proposed
generating-pair image on every Cayley edge. Exactly the same $24$
maps pass. Thus these are all of $\operatorname{Aut}(S)$.

Two epimorphisms from $L=\langle\xi_1,\xi_2\rangle$ onto $S$ have
the same kernel exactly when an automorphism of $S$ relates them:
the reverse map is the actual isomorphism induced from their common
quotient $L/\ker\varphi$. There are therefore $16$ epimorphism kernels.
Use the lexicographic generating-pair representative $(g_j,h_j)$ of
each orbit and the actual evaluation map
\[
 L\longrightarrow S^{16},\qquad
 \xi_1\longmapsto(g_j)_j,\quad
 \xi_2\longmapsto(h_j)_j.
 \tag{E.59.5}
\]
Its image $K$ is retained as that subgroup; no independence of the
$16$ factors is presumed.

The exact Ferrari Nielsen substitutions are
\[
 a:(g,h)\longmapsto(g,hg),\qquad
 b:(g,h)\longmapsto(gh^{-1},h).
 \tag{E.59.6}
\]
For each representative, the resulting pair determines its new
kernel and the unique automorphism of $S$ relating it to the chosen
new representative. This gives a permutation $p$ and automorphisms
$\alpha_j$ in the retained order. A general element of the containing
holomorph is written $(p,\alpha,k)$, with $k_j\in S$. Its product is
\[
 (p,\alpha,k)(q,\beta,l)=
 \bigl(q\circ p,(\alpha_j\beta_{p(j)})_j,
                   (k_j\alpha_j(l_{p(j)}))_j\bigr).
 \tag{E.59.7}
\]
It has the faithful right permutation action on the $384$ points
$(j,t)$, $t\in S$, given by
\[
 (j,t)\longmapsto
       \bigl(p(j),\alpha_j^{-1}(k_j^{-1}t)\bigr).
 \tag{E.59.8}
\]
Faithfulness follows first from the factor label. The image of $1$
then gives the constant $\alpha_j^{-1}(k_j^{-1})$; multiplying it
off the remaining values recovers $\alpha_j^{-1}$, and hence $k_j$.
Substitution
in \textup{(E.59.7)} verifies composition in this right-action convention.

Take the three braid generators to be $a,b,c=\xi_1a$ in this model.
All braid relations, the two literal Ferrari words, and all four
conjugation formulas are checked. In particular
\[
 \xi_1=ca^{-1},\qquad
 \xi_2=abac^{-1}b^{-1}a^{-1},
 \tag{E.59.9}
\]
with conjugation by $a$ fixing $\xi_1$ and sending $\xi_2$ to
$\xi_2\xi_1$, and conjugation by $b$ sending $\xi_1$ to
$\xi_1\xi_2^{-1}$ and fixing $\xi_2$. These checks prove that
the construction is a quotient of the actual Ferrari braid group.
Exact faithful permutation calculations give
\[
 |K|=2^{14}3^2,
 \qquad |\langle a,b\rangle|=2^{15}3^2,
 \qquad |\langle a,b,c\rangle|=2^{29}3^4.
 \tag{E.59.10}
\]
The first factor is normal under the displayed Nielsen action, and
the coordinates of $\langle a,b\rangle$ have $k=1$. Thus their
intersection is trivial, and the product of the first two orders
is the last order. The program independently computes all three.

\paragraph{The complete six-evaluation source.}
Use the positive pure braid words $A_{ij}$ of E.17 and
$\omega=(ab)^3$. The six pairs are, literally,
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (A_{12},A_{23}A_{24}),\quad(A_{13}A_{23},A_{34}),\quad
 (A_{23},A_{34}),\\
 (A_{12}A_{13},A_{24}A_{34}),\quad(A_{12},A_{23}),\quad
 (\omega,A_{34}).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.59.11}
\]
Let $\theta_i:F\to T_{24}$ denote the corresponding actual
evaluation. For the saved section $q\mapsto b_q$ of $B/F=S_3$,
define the component of $\rho_{24}(w)$ at $(q,i)$ to be
\[
          \theta_i(b_qwb_{qw}^{-1}),
 \tag{E.59.12}
\]
together with the coset permutation $q\mapsto qw$. The cocycle
identity for these based words proves that this is a homomorphism.
At the identity coset its restriction to $F$ is exactly $\theta_i$.

The resulting faithful permutation action has degree
$6\cdot6\cdot384=13\,824$. It compresses to degree $1956$ by
discarding only duplicate transitive $B$-sets. Each discarded orbit
has an explicit bijection to its retained copy intertwining both
$s$ and $r$, so the compressed and original maps have the identical
kernel. The retained orbit sizes are
\[
              48,24,432,384,96,432,96,12,432.
 \tag{E.59.13}
\]
The actual original generator multiplication and both torsion
relations are checked before compression. Complete Schreier chains
then give the first two orders in \textup{(E.59.2)}, using the literal
$97$ Bass--Serre generators of $N_B$ for the subgroup image $P$.
Their index is $576=[B:N_B]$. Consequently $\ker\rho_{24}\subset N_B$:
indeed $[B:N_B\ker\rho_{24}]=576$ already equals $[B:N_B]$.
The specified matrix quotient $q:B\to Q$ therefore factors through
$G_{24}$ with its original generator values.

\paragraph{The whole correction image and its actual quaternion maps.}
Evaluate all $4656$ pair commutators of the $97$ retained basis
elements. There are $158$ distinct nonidentity values. Their generated
subgroup has order $512$. In the zero-based basis numbering, six
generating commutators are
\[
 [n_0,n_1],\ [n_0,n_4],\ [n_0,n_5],\ [n_0,n_6],\
 [n_8,n_{11}],\ [n_8,n_{13}].
 \tag{E.59.14}
\]
Every original pair commutator is in this subgroup, and conjugating
these six generators by each original $n_i$, and separately by
$s,r$, stays in the same subgroup. This proves that it is exactly
$C=\rho_{24}(N_B')$, with the asserted normality in $G_{24}$.
An independent complete derived-subgroup Schreier calculation also
gives order $512$.

For each of the $36$ evaluation coordinates of
\textup{(E.59.12)}, restriction to $C$ is a homomorphism, because
$C\subset\rho_{24}(F)$. The three retained coordinate projections
with zero-based indices $(q,i)=(0,0),(0,1),(0,3)$ have
image of order eight, each with exactly one involution and with
explicit generators $i,j$ satisfying
\[
             i^2=j^2=-1,\qquad ij=(-1)ji.
 \tag{E.59.15}
\]
The full multiplication table and the selected coordinate indices
are saved. Each image is therefore the explicitly presented
quaternion group. The combined map separates all $512$ elements
of $C$, and its target has order $8^3=512$. It is consequently
an isomorphism $C\to Q_8^3$ through those actual homomorphisms.
In particular its centre has order eight and its exponent is four.

Every one of the $97$ values $\rho_{24}(n_i^6)$ lies in $C$.
Since $P/C$ is abelian and generated by these basis images, its
exponent divides six. Hence every sixth power in $P$ lies in $C$,
proving $\rho_{24}(K_6)=C$. On the other hand the six generators
\textup{(E.59.14)} have literal preimages in $N_B'\subset K_6\cap F'$.
This gives both inclusions in
$\rho_{24}(K_6\cap F')=C$. A breadth-first enumeration of the
$512$ elements retains a parent and a generator label for every
element, hence an actual $N_B'$ word preimage of each one.

The map $G_6\to G_{24}/C$ is induced from these exact source maps.
For any proposed pair in the right side of \textup{(E.59.3)}, lift
its $G_6$ coordinate to $B$. The discrepancy of its second coordinate
lies in $C=\rho_{24}(K_6)$ and is corrected by an actual $K_6$
element. This proves the fibre-product isomorphism and its kernel.
The same argument with the saved $N_B'$ words proves that every
point of $f_iC$ has an actual $F'$ lift retaining the specified
$G_6$ value. Conversely any such lift differs by $K_6\cap F'$,
whose entire image was just computed. This proves completeness
of the finite correction fibre.

\paragraph{The exact finite equations.}
For every one of these $512$ corrections, evaluate the normalized
three-strand images
\[
                      S_f=sf,\qquad R_f=f^{-1}r.
 \tag{E.59.16}
\]
Their product is the original $\tau=sr$. The original $G_6$ values
already satisfy $S_f^2=R_f^3=1$. Testing these identities in all
$36$ coordinates of \textup{(E.59.12)} therefore tests them in
the faithful common source $D_{24}$. Exactly four corrections
in every fibre pass.

Write $v_j=\theta_j(f)$ for the six target values in
\textup{(E.59.11)}. The full group pentagon, with the retained
conventions, is the literal equality
\[
                  v_1v_2=v_3v_4v_5.
 \tag{E.59.17}
\]
The associated four-strand generator images are
\[
                  a,\qquad v_5^{-1}bv_5,\qquad v_6^{-1}cv_6.
 \tag{E.59.18}
\]
For all four gentle points, the program tests
\textup{(E.59.17)} and each of the three Artin braid relations
for \textup{(E.59.18)}. Exactly two pass all of them, in every
one of the $24$ fibres. These are exhaustive group multiplications
in \textup{(E.59.7)}, including the nonabelian quaternion
corrections. No linearization, selected correction, or omitted
central term is used. The saved output gives every passing index
and both complete literal commutator-word lifts in each fibre.

\paragraph{Source and target generation.}
For each of the four gentle points in each fibre, the complete
permutation calculation on the $1956$ retained source points gives
\[
                  |\langle S_f,R_f\rangle|=|G_{24}|.
 \tag{E.59.19}
\]
Thus the corrected map is onto $G_{24}$ and is already onto $G_6$
through its retained old shadow. These two surjections give a
surjection onto the actual common source, as follows. Their
common composite to $Q$ is $\operatorname{Inn}(g_i)\circ q$,
where $g_i=q(x)^{h_i}$ is the actual integral cusp gauge retained
for the $i$-th old point. This equality is checked on the literal
$s,r$ images. In particular its kernel is $N_B$. The image of
$N_B$ is therefore a subdirect product of the
two actual $N_B$ images. The $G_6$ image is abelian. By Goursat,
their common quotient is abelian, and so kills the commutator
$P'=C$ on the $G_{24}$ side. The corrected image therefore
contains the entire kernel $C$ of \textup{(E.59.3)} and maps onto
$G_6$. It is all of $D_{24}$. This proves the complete
three-strand generation assertion through the specified maps.

For each of the two pentagon points, a separate exact Schreier
calculation on the $384$ target points gives the order
$2^{29}3^4$ for the three images in \textup{(E.59.18)}.
They belong to $T_{24}$, so they generate precisely $T_{24}$.
At all $48$ pentagon points, literal multiplication also verifies
that $(ab)^3$ and $(abc)^4$ retain their original target values.
Every chain retains the original two or three generators and
literal-word directed acyclic graph. All final Schreier relations
are sifted, and the independent Python array reader re-evaluates
every saved strong-generator word. Thus the order equalities
are complete finite generation certificates.

Finally, retain the actual ungauged matrix frames $A_i,\beta_i$
of each E.17 point. Its gauge $g_i$ changes the three frames to
$(g_iA_i,g_i\beta_i,g_i)$. Consequently the CM coordinates are
exactly
\[
 (g_iA_i)^{-1}g_i=A_i^{-1},\qquad
 (g_i\beta_i)^{-1}g_i=\beta_i^{-1}.
 \tag{E.59.20}
\]
The separate literal matrix check retains the nontrivial gauges,
the unique E.1 representatives with mod-four value in $V_4$,
and the positive rational three-torsion line. It obtains $24$
distinct pairs and exactly $12$ unequal sign pairs. E.1's
finite-centralizer calculation shows that reduction to the same
$G_6$ point preserves those matrix coordinates under the current
corrections. This proves the stated CM coverage through the
actual frames, including the original $12+12$ division.

\end{proof}
