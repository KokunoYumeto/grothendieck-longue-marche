\begin{statement}{E.23}{Proved}{A relative class-four refinement with complete correction planes}
Extend the same actual Ferrari Magnus representation to
degree six, retaining its joint image $T_6$ with the
modulus-nine target. Let $K_{3,6}$ be the intersection
of $K_6$ with the kernels of all six evaluations and
all six $B/F_2$ conjugates, and put $D_6=B/K_{3,6}$.
The actual reduction maps are
\[
 D_6\longrightarrow D_5\longrightarrow G_6,
 \qquad T_6\longrightarrow T_5.
 \tag{E.23.1}
\]
The kernel of $D_6\to D_5$ is $\F_3^6$;
its whole image is represented by words in
$K_{3,5}\cap F_2'$. The kernel of $D_6\to G_6$
is elementary abelian of dimension 19.
The successive lower-central images of $N$ are
\[
 \begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 j&2&3&4&5\\\hline
 \dim\im(\gamma_jN)&15&6&3&0.
 \end{array}
 \tag{E.23.2}
\]
Thus this specified relative source quotient has
class four. Its natural map does not factor
through $D_5$, whose fourth-commutator image
is trivial by E.22.

Above each of E.22's 24 simultaneous points,
the entire next correction set is an affine
plane over $\F_3$. All nine points in each
plane are surjective gentle shadows and
satisfy the complete pentagon. All 216
associated four-strand triples generate
the whole target $T_6$ and preserve the
three- and four-strand full twists. In
particular all twelve extra-coset CM
fibres still have positive witnesses.

The exact target orders are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |T_6|&=133\,417\,453\,597\,332\,552,\\
 |B_4/(\ker(B_4\to T_6)\cap PB_4)|
   &=3\,202\,018\,886\,335\,981\,248.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E.23.3}
\]
The cusp period remains 72 in the source
joint quotient. Every multiplier lift
of the retained $G_6$ shadows therefore
has the same equations at this level,
as in E.22. This is the complete
comparison over the stated 24 points,
with all 216 surviving corrections
retained for further refinements.
No assertion of lifting through all
finite quotients follows from (E.23.1).
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}

For the complete source kernel,
the old images of $K_6\cap F_2'$

12 and 13. The normal presentation

12 basis cubes, 66 basis

kernel images have dimension
six and coincide. Literal
re-evaluation, integral
$F_2$-abelianization, and
$s,r$ stability are checked.
This is the program
\texttt{de\_ferrari\_degree6\_kernel.py}.

For each retained degree-five point,
take its actual word $S_0$ and set
\[
 R_0=S_0^{-1}t,\qquad S_0R_0=t.
 \tag{E.23.4}
\]
The complete top correction space

zero. Every combined augmented

Consequently the complete

program asserts (E.23.4)
before constructing the system
and retains all matrices,

are affine because the
degree-six correction kernel

All basis-pair substitutions

The normalized conjugator is

Both the old and new

Every selected word is checked

The lower-central computation
