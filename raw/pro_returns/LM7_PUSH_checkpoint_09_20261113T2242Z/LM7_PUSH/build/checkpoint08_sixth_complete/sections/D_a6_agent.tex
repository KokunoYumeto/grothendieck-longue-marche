\subsection{An \texorpdfstring{$A_6$}{A6} chief obstruction with prescribed base conjugators}

\begin{statement}{D.31}{Proved}{A proper-lift obstruction and its complete forgetful fibre}
There is an actual chief extension
\[
 1\longrightarrow\operatorname{Inn}(A_6)
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(A_6)
 \xrightarrow{\pi}\mathbf F_2^2\longrightarrow1
 \tag{D.31.1}
\]
and a generating peripheral triple $(X,Y,Z)$ of
orders $(2,8,8)$ such that the following holds.
Put $d_1=\pi(X)$, $d_2=\pi(Y)$, and
$d_3=d_1+d_2$.  With the same base epimorphism
$q_1=q_0$, multiplier $\lambda=1$, and prescribed
base conjugators
\[
                    (h_1,h_2,h_3)=(0,0,d_1),
 \tag{D.31.2}
\]
there are exactly ninety weak lifts and no
epimorphic lift.  Every weak lift has image
isomorphic to
\[
       C_8\rtimes_5 C_2
       =\langle r,s:r^8=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^5\rangle.
 \tag{D.31.3}
\]
The original choice $(h_1,h_2,h_3)=(0,0,0)$
has exactly 360 lifts, all epimorphic.

More precisely, write
$h_i=p_i d_1+q_i d_2$, with $p_i,q_i\in\mathbf F_2$.
The complete prescribed-conjugator count is
\[
 \begin{array}{c|cc}
 q_1+p_2+p_3+q_3&\text{weak lifts}&\text{epimorphic lifts}\\\hline
 0&360&360\\
 1&90&0.
 \end{array}
 \tag{D.31.4}
\]
Thus each row occurs for 32 of the 64 possible
base-centralizer triples.  Forgetting the
prescribed base conjugators gives a union of
1800 weak lifts and 1440 epimorphic lifts.
These form exactly two simultaneous
$\operatorname{Aut}(A_6)$-conjugacy orbits, of
sizes 1440 and 360, respectively.  The first is
the orbit of the original generating triple.
In particular the ordinary peripheral lift
fibre is nonempty.

Both groups in \textup{(D.31.1)} have trivial
maximal pro-five quotient.  For every finite
five-group $L$ and every specified
multiplier-one peripheral automorphism $\beta$
of its given two-generated source quotient,
the actual products of \textup{(D.31.1)}
with $L$ have maximal pro-five quotient $L$.
Over the same base shadow
$1_{\mathbf F_2^2}\times\beta$ they have both
the obstructed prescribed-conjugator branch
above and an explicit admitted branch.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
All permutations below act on $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$.
In $S=A_6$, take
\[
            a=(0123)(45),\qquad b=(01234).
 \tag{D.31.5}
\]
They generate $S$.  An automorphism is specified
by the images of this actual generating pair.
The following table defines $X,Y,Z$; a row
$[u_0,\ldots,u_5]$ denotes the permutation
$i\mapsto u_i$:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|cc}
       &a\text{ image}&b\text{ image}\\\hline
 X &[1,0,4,2,5,3]&[4,1,0,2,5,3]\\
 Y &[1,2,3,0,5,4]&[5,1,0,4,2,3]\\
 Z &[1,4,5,0,3,2]&[0,3,4,2,5,1].
 \end{array}
 \tag{D.31.6}
\]
The exact permutation certificate verifies
that these assignments extend to automorphisms,
that $XYZ=1$, and that $X,Y$ generate all
1440 automorphisms.  It enumerates every
automorphism, rather than taking an order
formula as input: any automorphism must
send $a,b$ to elements of orders $4,5$
with the same product and commutator orders.
For each such pair it propagates the proposed
map over every edge of the Cayley graph of
the actual 360-element group.  Consistency
on these edges and bijectivity are necessary
and sufficient for an automorphism.  This
exhaustive calculation gives 1440 maps.
Composition is computed from their two
generator images.  The inner maps form
the actual 360-element normal subgroup,
and its computed quotient is $\mathbf F_2^2$,
with $d_1,d_2$ as the indicated basis.
The same certificate computes all seven
conjugacy classes of $A_6$ and the normal
closure of each.  Every nonidentity class
normally generates all 360 elements.
This proves simplicity, so the displayed
kernel is indeed chief.  A nontrivial
commutator normally generates it as well,
which proves perfectness.

Let $\mathcal X_0,\mathcal Y_0,\mathcal Z_0$
be the conjugacy classes of $X,Y,Z$ under
$A=\operatorname{Inn}(S)$.
Their sizes are $15,90,90$.
Put
\[
 \mathcal X_1=Y\mathcal X_0Y^{-1},\qquad
 \mathcal Y_1=X\mathcal Y_0X^{-1},\qquad
 \mathcal Z_1=X\mathcal Z_0X^{-1}.
 \tag{D.31.7}
\]
For each letter, the two displayed classes
are distinct and of equal size.  Conjugation
by an element above $p d_1+q d_2$ changes
their indices as follows:
\[
 \mathcal X_i\longmapsto\mathcal X_{i+q},\qquad
 \mathcal Y_i\longmapsto\mathcal Y_{i+p},\qquad
 \mathcal Z_i\longmapsto\mathcal Z_{i+p+q}.
 \tag{D.31.8}
\]
These are actions of the actual outer
quotient on the actual inner classes.
They are verified by conjugation of
the saved automorphisms, and do not
replace that action by an abstract
isomorphic group.

The tuple map
$q_E:\widehat F\to E=\operatorname{Aut}(S)$,
$x_1\mapsto X$, $x_2\mapsto Y$,
$x_3\mapsto Z$, is an epimorphism.
For the base conjugators
\textup{(D.31.2)}, take the actual lifts
$(1,1,X)$.  The required images belong
to
\[
                 \mathcal X_0,\quad
                 \mathcal Y_0,\quad
                 \mathcal Z_1,
 \tag{D.31.9}
\]
respectively.  Their base values are
still $d_1,d_2,d_3$, because the quotient
is abelian.  Every other choice of
lifts of \textup{(D.31.2)} gives the
same three $A$-conjugacy classes.
The complete enumeration of
$u\in\mathcal X_0$, $v\in\mathcal Y_0$
with $(uv)^{-1}\in\mathcal Z_1$ gives
exactly ninety triples.  They are one
simultaneous $A$-conjugacy orbit.
For one representative $(s,r,(sr)^{-1})$,
the literal products give
$|r|=8$, $|s|=2$, and $srs^{-1}=r^5$;
its generated subgroup has sixteen
elements and its intersection with
$A$ is $\langle r^2\rangle$ of order
four.  These equalities prove
\textup{(D.31.3)} for every member
of the orbit.

There is also a direct count proof of
nonexistence of a proper lift.
The centralizer of $A$ in
$\operatorname{Aut}(A)$ is trivial:
an automorphism commuting with every
inner automorphism fixes every element
because $Z(A)=1$.  Thus a generating
triple in $E$ has trivial simultaneous
centralizer in $A$.  Its $A$-conjugacy
orbit has 360 elements and remains
inside the required three classes.
It cannot fit inside the ninety
triples in \textup{(D.31.9)}.
The count with $\mathcal Z_0$ is
360, and its triples are exactly the
$A$-conjugates of $(X,Y,Z)$; all of
them generate $E$.

We retain every base-centralizer
choice.  For arbitrary $h_i$ the
profile indices in
\textup{(D.31.7)} are
\[
                  (q_1,\ p_2,\ p_3+q_3).
 \tag{D.31.10}
\]
Simultaneous conjugation by an element
above $p d_1+q d_2$ adds
$(q,p,p+q)$ to these three indices.
These four vectors are exactly the
even-parity vectors in $\mathbf F_2^3$.
They send the original 360-element
orbit to every even-parity profile,
and send the ninety-element orbit
to every odd-parity profile.  This
proves \textup{(D.31.4)} from the
two exact counts above.  Each of the
eight profiles has eight preimages
in the 64 conjugator triples.

The forgetful map is the literal
set map
\[
 \coprod_{(h_1,h_2,h_3)\in D^3}
       \operatorname{Lift}(h_1,h_2,h_3)
   \longrightarrow
 \{(U_1,U_2,U_3):U_1U_2U_3=1,\
              U_i\in X_i^E,\ \pi(U_i)=d_i\},
 \tag{D.31.11}
\]
which discards the conjugator labels
and retains the triple.
It is onto: any $E$-conjugator has
some base image $h_i$, and changing
its lift by $A$ is precisely the
inner-class freedom already counted.
Its image is the union of eight
disjoint profiles, not the sum of
64 labelled counts.  There are
$4\cdot360+4\cdot90=1800$ weak
triples and $4\cdot360=1440$
generating triples.  The simultaneous
$E$-orbits are exactly the 1440
conjugates of the generating source
and the 360 conjugates of the
order-sixteen source.  Their
centralizers have orders one and
four, respectively.  In particular
\textup{(D.31.11)} retains the
original epimorphism itself.

For the pro-five comparison, every
homomorphism from $E$ to a finite
five-group kills its perfect normal
subgroup $A$.  It then factors
through $D$ of order four and is
trivial.  The same holds for $D$.
Thus their actual universal maximal
pro-five quotients are trivial.
Given a finite five-group source
$\widehat F\twoheadrightarrow L$,
write its peripheral values as
$l_i$ and suppose
$\beta(l_i)=k_i l_i k_i^{-1}$.
The paired original source into
$E\times L$ is onto: a subdirect
subgroup would otherwise give a
nontrivial common quotient of $E$
and $L$, contrary to the just-proved
maximal-pro-five calculation.
The projection
\[
 E\times L\longrightarrow D\times L
 \tag{D.31.12}
\]
has the simple kernel $A\times1$.
Take new base values
$(d_i,\beta(l_i))$ and prescribed
base conjugators
$(0,k_1),(0,k_2),(d_1,k_3)$.
Any epimorphic lift would project
to an epimorphic lift of
\textup{(D.31.9)}, which is impossible.
On the other hand the same base
values, with base conjugators
$(0,k_1),(0,k_2),(0,k_3)$, have the
explicit generating lift
$(X_i,\beta(l_i))$.
The coordinate projections
$E\times L\to L$ and $D\times L\to L$
are the universal maximal pro-five
maps, with sections $l\mapsto(1,l)$.
Both branches therefore lie over
the same specified pro-five shadow.

The two certificate programs in \texttt{code}
have the common prefix \texttt{a\_peripheral\_a6\_}
and suffixes \texttt{outer.py} and \texttt{obstruction.py}.
They save the complete automorphism maps,
all inner classes, all ninety bad
triples, their generated subgroup,
and all 64 conjugator cases in
matching output and JSON files.
The latter independently checks every
generator edge of every saved
automorphism and verifies both
simultaneous-conjugacy orbit equalities.
Its final status is
\texttt{PASS\_A6\_PROPER\_OBSTRUCTION\_AND\_FORGETFUL\_UNION}.
An initial, incorrect guess that the
bad image was a Sylow subgroup of
order 32 is explicitly retained in
the files with suffix
\texttt{failed\_sylow32}; the exact
order-sixteen calculation above
supersedes that guess.

D.31 disproves the extension of
D.30's arbitrary-prescribed-conjugator
assertion to all nonabelian simple
kernels.  The forgetful map
\textup{(D.31.11)} proves that this
example does not obstruct its
underlying ordinary base shadow.
No restriction on the rational
pro-five leading image in D.1 or
D.2 is inferred from it.
\end{proof}
