\subsection{The literal \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$}{GL2(F3)} target and its common source with E.59}

\begin{statement}{E.62}{Proved}{The complete joint matrix-cover fibres retain every CM matrix pair}
Let $L=\langle\xi_1,\xi_2\rangle$ be the free Ferrari group, and retain
the actual $B,F,N_B,Q,G_6$, $K_6=N_B'N_B^6$, and the $24$ indexed
multiplier-one words $f_i\in F'$ of E.17 and E.59.  There is an explicitly
specified Ferrari quotient
\[
 B_4\twoheadrightarrow T_{48},\qquad |T_{48}|=2^{39}3^{10},
 \tag{E.62.1}
\]
formed from every epimorphism kernel of $L$ onto the literal matrix group
$\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$.  Write $\rho_{48}:B\twoheadrightarrow G_{48}$
for its six-evaluation, six-coset source.  With the E.59 maps, define
the actual common images
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 G_{24,48}&=\operatorname{im}\bigl(B\longrightarrow G_{48}\times G_{24}\bigr),
 &T_{24,48}&=\operatorname{im}\bigl(B_4\longrightarrow T_{48}\times T_{24}\bigr),\\
 P_{48}&=\rho_{48}(N_B),
 &P_{24,48}&=\rho_{24,48}(N_B).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E.62.2}
\]
Their orders, with these specified maps, are
\[
 \begin{array}{c|r|r}
 &\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)\text{ alone}&\text{common image with E.59}\\\hline
 G&2^{29}3^6&2^{41}3^6\\
 P&2^{23}3^4&2^{35}3^4\\
 C=\operatorname{im}(N_B')&2^{21}&2^{24}\\
 C'&2^3&2^6\\
 T&2^{39}3^{10}&2^{63}3^{12}.
 \end{array}
 \tag{E.62.3}
\]
In both columns, $C'$ is central and elementary abelian, $C/C'$ is
elementary abelian of dimension $18$, and
\[
 C=\operatorname{im}(K_6)=\operatorname{im}(F'\cap K_6).
 \tag{E.62.4}
\]
In particular the full common source with the original homology quotient is
\[
 D_{24,48}:=B/(K_6\cap\ker\rho_{24,48})
 \simeq G_6\mathbin{\times_{G_{24,48}/C_{24,48}}}G_{24,48}.
 \tag{E.62.5}
\]
The fibre of actual $F'$ values above each specified $f_i\in G_6$
has $2^{24}$ elements.  For every one of the $24$ base points, its
complete multiplier-one counts are
\[
 \begin{array}{l|r|r}
 \text{conditions on the whole fibre}&\text{GL alone}&
                    \text{common targets}\\\hline
 \text{fibre size}&2^{21}&2^{24}\\
 (sf)^2=(f^{-1}r)^3=1&16&32\\
 \text{also the full pentagon and all braid relations}&4&4.
 \end{array}
 \tag{E.62.6}
\]
All the gentle source points in this table generate their actual common
sources with $G_6$.  Every final four-strand point generates its entire
specified target.  The literal three- and four-strand full twists are
fixed on every gentle point.  Every correction has a retained word
preimage in $N_B'$, with the literal map of the $N_B$ basis into $F$.

The $32$ common gentle points map two-to-one onto the $16$ GL gentle
points and eight-to-one onto the four E.59 gentle points.  The four
common final points map two-to-one onto exactly two of the four GL-only
final points, and two-to-one onto both E.59 final points.  Thus the
common-source compatibility removes half of the GL-only final values.
Every one of the $24$ CM matrix pairs, including all $12$ extra pairs,
still has four final values in the common test.  These are complete
fibres over the specified $G_6$ points at multiplier one; no compatible
full profinite pentagon lift is asserted by this finite result.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We construct every map from the literal matrix tables and retain the
common word through all target evaluations.

\paragraph{The complete matrix quotient.}
Enumerate the $48$ invertible matrices over $\mathbf F_3$ in lexicographic
order of their four entries.  Matrix multiplication and inversion give
the group table.  The actual determinant map to $\mathbf F_3^\times$
has kernel the determinant-one matrices, and the complete commutator
calculation identifies this same subgroup with the derived subgroup.
There are $864$ ordered generating pairs.  To determine all automorphisms,
choose the first such pair, retain a spanning tree of its Cayley graph,
and send its two generators to every one of the $864$ possible generating
pairs.  Evaluate the tree words and check the proposed map on every
edge of that Cayley graph.  Exactly $48$ maps pass.  Since every word
is obtained by traversing these edges, each passing map is a homomorphism;
its generating image makes it an automorphism.  Conversely an
automorphism has one of the tested generating-pair images.

These $48$ actual maps are the $24$ inner matrix maps and their composites
with
\[
 g\longmapsto\det(g)g.
 \tag{E.62.7}
\]
Their restrictions to the determinant-one subgroup reproduce all $24$
automorphisms in the E.59 table, with restriction kernel of order two.
The projective action on the four displayed lines
$(1,0),(1,1),(1,2),(0,1)$ has image order $24$ and kernel $\{I,-I\}$;
all products are checked under that actual action.  We use no
identification of the matrix group with another double cover.

Two epimorphisms $L\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$ have the same kernel
exactly when their generating pairs differ by one of these automorphisms.
The reverse implication uses the actual isomorphism induced from their
common quotient $L/\ker\varphi$.  The orbit calculation gives $18$
kernels, six for each nonzero pair of determinant characters.  Let
$(g_j,h_j)$ denote their lexicographic representatives.  We retain
\[
 L\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)^{18},\qquad
 \xi_1\longmapsto(g_j)_j,\quad\xi_2\longmapsto(h_j)_j.
 \tag{E.62.8}
\]
The free image is the image of this displayed map.  Its independence
across coordinates is neither assumed nor needed.

For each Nielsen substitution, the unique recorded automorphism
expressing the new generating pair as an automorphic image of its
representative gives the coordinate transporter.  In the notation
$(p,\alpha,k)$ of E.59 the product is
\[
 (p,\alpha,k)(q,\beta,l)=
 \bigl(q\circ p,\ (\alpha_j\beta_{p(j)})_j,
                 (k_j\alpha_j(l_{p(j)}))_j\bigr).
 \tag{E.62.9}
\]
It acts faithfully on $18$ copies of the literal $48$-element group by
\[
 (j,z)\longmapsto
 \bigl(p(j),\alpha_j^{-1}(k_j^{-1}z)\bigr).
 \tag{E.62.10}
\]
For $a,b$ use the substitutions
$(g,h)\mapsto(g,hg)$ and $(g,h)\mapsto(gh^{-1},h)$; put $c=\xi_1a$.
The program checks their three braid relations and the literal identities
recovering $\xi_1,\xi_2$, including
$\xi_2=abac^{-1}b^{-1}a^{-1}$.  It also checks the four Nielsen
conjugation formulas and all products of the six signed braid generators
in the faithful permutation action.  This defines the asserted actual
map from $B_4$.  The computed free, Nielsen, and full target orders are
$2^{23}3^3$, $2^{16}3^7$, and $2^{39}3^{10}$, respectively.

\paragraph{The source and both block projections.}
Substitute the six actual E.17 pairs into this target and induce over the
six actual $B/F$ cosets, as in E.59.  This gives a degree-$31\,104$
permutation action of $B$.  The saved intertwiner of each repeated

Every one of the original $97$ based $N_B$ generators is evaluated in
both actions.  Complete Schreier certificates give the left source
column of \textup{(E.62.3)}.

Take the block sum with the degree-$1\,956$ E.59 source, using the same
literal $s,r$ words.  Take likewise the block sum of the two four-strand
targets, using the same $a,b,c$.  These are exactly the images defined
in \textup{(E.62.2)}.  Their complete certificates give the right
column of \textup{(E.62.3)}.  The source projection kernels have orders
\[
 \bigl|\ker(G_{24,48}\to G_{48})\bigr|=2^{12},\qquad
 \bigl|\ker(G_{24,48}\to G_{24})\bigr|=2^{15}.
 \tag{E.62.11}
\]
The corresponding target kernel orders are $2^{24}3^2$ and $2^{34}3^8$.
These are kernels of the retained block maps.  In particular, neither
specified source quotient map factors through the other: such a
factorization would make their common image the graph of the factor map,
of the order of that projection, contradicting \textup{(E.62.11)}.
The identical graph argument applies to the target maps.

In both source columns $[G:P]=576$.  The map
$B/N_B\twoheadrightarrow G/P$ induced by the actual source map is
therefore an isomorphism.  Inverting this particular quotient map
identifies $G/P$ with the original $Q$, and proves that the preimage
of $P$ in $B$ is exactly $N_B$.

\paragraph{Actual coordinates on the whole correction group.}
The complete derived-image algorithm starts with the original

 \end{split}
 \tag{E.62.12}
\]
The retained strong-generator words are words in these original based

\[
 C'\subseteq Z(C),\qquad C'\simeq\mathbf F_2^k,\qquad
 C/C'\simeq\mathbf F_2^{18},\qquad k=3\text{ or }6.
 \tag{E.62.13}
\]
All $97$ original sixth powers belong to $C$.  Hence $P/C$ has exponent
dividing six, so the image of $K_6$ is $C$.  The actual $N_B'$ word
preimages of its generators prove the last equality in
\textup{(E.62.4)} as well.

Choose the saved ordered basis $z_1,\ldots,z_k$ of $C'$ and then the
saved successive representatives $e_1,\ldots,e_{18}$ in $C$.  Every

\[
 |\langle C',e_1,\ldots,e_j\rangle|=2^{k+j}.
 \tag{E.62.14}

Consequently every element is uniquely
$e_1^{v_1}\cdots e_{18}^{v_{18}}z_1^{w_1}\cdots z_k^{w_k}$.
The basis words, not only their coordinates, are retained in the

Let $s_i$ be the coordinates of $e_i^2$ in $C'$ and $b_{ji}$ those of

 (v,w)(v',w')=(v+v',\,w+w'+B(v,v')),

 \tag{E.62.15}

This follows by moving the second ordered product through the first,

\paragraph{The complete equations, including their central terms.}
Fix one of the original words $f_i$.  For a correction $u\in C$ set
$\sigma=sf_i$, $\rho=f_i^{-1}r$, $A=\sigma^2$, and $D=\rho^3$.
The unchanged $G_6$ conditions give $A,D\in C$.  Write
$\Sigma=\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma)|_C$ and
$R=\operatorname{Ad}(\rho)|_C$.  For the corrected value $f_i u$,
the two source relations are exactly
\[
 \Sigma(u)A u=1,\qquad
 u^{-1}R(u^{-1})R^2(u^{-1})D=1.
 \tag{E.62.16}
\]
For example $(\sigma u)^2=\Sigma(u)\sigma^2u$; multiplying
$(u^{-1}\rho)^3$ gives the second formula in the displayed order.
All action columns and both constants are obtained by conjugating and
powering the actual permutations and recovering their unique coordinates.

Projection to $C/C'$ gives an affine system of rank $15$ in $18$
variables.  Every one of its eight solutions is then substituted into
the complete equations \textup{(E.62.16)}, using
\textup{(E.62.15)}.  The remaining central system has rank two on
$\mathbf F_2^3$ in the GL case, and rank four on $\mathbf F_2^6$
in the common case.  All eight systems are consistent.  This gives
$16$ and $32$ values, respectively.  These computations enumerate
the whole fibre: every element has the unique coordinates already
proved, and the central equations have all been imposed.  Every
answer is additionally checked by powering the actual corrected
source permutations.

Evaluate each coordinate basis word through its retained word graph in

are fixed on all gentle values.

The exact block projection, expressed on the coordinate basis, maps
all $32$ gentle values onto the $16$ GL values twice each.  Its
projection to the stored E.59 correction group maps them onto its
four gentle values eight times each.  On the four final values the

All these projection assertions are checked for every indexed fibre.

\paragraph{Generation and the source with the original homology point.}
There are $768$ common gentle points and $96$ final common target
points.  Each has a separate complete Schreier certificate, with all
strong-generator words independently replayed.  The source orders
are always $2^{41}3^6$; the target orders are always $2^{63}3^{12}$.
The $96$ GL-only final target points likewise generate $T_{48}$.
The GL-only source assertion follows also by projecting the common
gentle points, whose projection covers every GL gentle point.

It remains to retain $G_6$ itself in the source generation claim.
Equation \textup{(E.62.4)} gives the fibre product in
\textup{(E.62.5)} by the actual quotient maps from $B$.  The corrected
$s,r$ words define a map $B\to D_{24,48}$ onto each factor: the
$G_6$ assertion is the retained E.17 point, and the other projection
is the preceding complete generation certificate.  In the original
matrix quotient its composite is
$\operatorname{Inn}(g_i)\circ q$, with the literal old cusp gauge
$g_i$ from E.59.  Therefore the preimage of either factor's $N_B$
image is exactly $N_B$.  The image of $N_B$ is consequently subdirect
in the abelian $G_6$ image of $N_B$ and $P_{24,48}$.  Their common
quotient in Goursat's description is abelian, so it kills
$P_{24,48}'=C_{24,48}$.  Hence the corrected image contains
$\{1\}\times C_{24,48}$, the kernel of
$D_{24,48}\to G_6$.  Surjectivity onto $G_6$ now proves surjectivity
onto $D_{24,48}$.  The same argument applies to the GL-only source.

Finally every correction lies in $K_6$, so it leaves the actual $G_6$
point unchanged.  The E.59 common-left-gauge formulas for the frames
remain literal: $(A,\beta,V)\mapsto(g_iA,g_i\beta,g_iV)$, with the
same cancellations in $A^{-1}V$ and $\beta^{-1}V$.  Thus the CM
matrices are exactly the $24$ distinct pairs already verified there.
