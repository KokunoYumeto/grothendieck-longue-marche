\subsection{The exact prime-five five-strand source and its signed common quotient}

\begin{statement}{E.50}{Proved}{Every retained boundary fibre survives the prime-five symplectic five-strand test}
Let $B=C_2*C_3$, and retain its actual subgroups $F,N,H$
and the finite source $D_{23}$ from E.24 and E.47.
The four chain transvections on $\mathbf F_5^4$, with the
alternating matrix of E.49, give the specified quotient
\[
 B_5\twoheadrightarrow T_{5,\mathrm{sp}}
       =\operatorname{Sp}_4(\mathbf F_5),
       \qquad |T_{5,\mathrm{sp}}|=9360000.
 \tag{E.50.1}
\]
Retaining all eight literal evaluations and all six $B/F$
cosets gives a source $\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}:B\twoheadrightarrow
G_{5,\mathrm{sp}}$ of order $9000000$.
Write
\[
 C_5=\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(F')
       =\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(H'),\qquad
 L_5=C_5',\qquad Q_5=O_5(C_5).
 \tag{E.50.2}
\]
The actual coordinate maps proved below give
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 L_5\simeq\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_5),\qquad
 C_5=L_5\times Q_5,\qquad Q_5\simeq(\mathbf Z/5)^3,\\
 \rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(N)=L_5\times(\mathbf Z/5)^5,\qquad
 \rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(\gamma_jH)=C_5\quad(j\ge2).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.50.3}
\]

Put
\[
 A_m=B/\overline{F'F^m}\quad(m=2,10),\qquad
 Q_{20}=\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf Z/20)/\{\pm I\}.
 \tag{E.50.4}
\]
The maps from $B$ to $Q_{20}$ take $s,r$ to the classes of
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
The actual common quotient with the E.38 double-cover source
$G_L$ is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 J=A_{10}\times_{A_2}Q_{20},\qquad
 |A_{10}|=600,\quad |A_2|=24,\quad |Q_{20}|=2880,
       \quad |J|=72000,\\
 G_{5,\mathrm{sp}}/Q_5\xrightarrow{\sim}J,
       \qquad G_L\twoheadrightarrow J.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.50.5}
\]
Here $A_{10}\to A_2$ is reduction of the actual free
exponent sums modulo two; $Q_{20}\to A_2$ is projective
reduction modulo four, with its values on $s,r$ specified
by the same source map.

Every one of the $432$ multiplier-one points of E.24,
every full two-adic fibre of E.41, and every full
three-adic class-three fibre of E.47 has a simultaneous
profinite commutator word retaining these images and the
complete E.43 double-cover comparison, and satisfying
all five-strand tests in \textup{(E.50.1)}.
The word retains the identity action on every full
relative pro-$p$ boundary factor of E.42 for $p\ge5$.
Its new five-strand action is the action of the same
actual Galois element used in E.43; in particular it is
onto $T_{5,\mathrm{sp}}$ and satisfies the complete
pentagon, Artin, full-twist and periodic-conjugacy tests.
It also retains E.46's Hurwitz quotient through its
specified map from $G_L$.

For every finite old boundary source $G_{\rm old}$ of
E.47 which retains $P_{20}$, set
$G_+=G_{\rm old}\times_S G_L$, where $S=G_L/C_L$
is the actual E.43 quotient.  The new common sources,
and the images of their corrected source maps, are exactly
\[
 G_{\rm old}\times_{A_{10}}G_{5,\mathrm{sp}},
       \qquad
 G_+\times_JG_{5,\mathrm{sp}}.
 \tag{E.50.6}
\]
The second assertion uses the same word throughout the
entire indicated profinite boundary family.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\paragraph{The actual finite source and its residual.}
Use the four matrices
$U_i=I+e_i e_i^{\mathsf t}\Omega$ over $\mathbf F_5$,
where $\Omega_{i,i+1}=1$, $\Omega_{i+1,i}=-1$, and
all other entries are zero.  Their action on the $624$
nonzero vectors is faithful.  The literal matrix products
verify the Artin relations, and the generated order is
$5^4(5^2-1)(5^4-1)=9360000$.
The first three matrices generate a group of order $15000$.
The two periodic words have orders ten and eight, and
their fifth and fourth powers, respectively, are $-I$.

The eight pairs are exactly E.46.12--13, evaluated in
these matrices.  For each pair the source map is the
six-coset formula E.46.15.  Thus it is a homomorphism
with actual specified values on $s,r$.
The $29952$-point induced action is faithfully compressed
to $1830$ points by the saved $300$ orbit intertwiners.
Every deleted orbit has an explicit bijection commuting
with both generators.  The resulting order is $9000000$.
Evaluation of all $97$ literal $N$ generators, the full
primitive $95$-column $F'$ correction lattice, and the
ten literal $H$ Schreier generators gives
\[
 \begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
  \text{image}&F&H&N&F'=H'&N'&[H,H']\\ \hline
  \text{order}&1500000&1500000&375000&15000&120&15000.
 \end{array}
 \tag{E.50.7}
\]
The displayed equalities are checked by containment
and generating sets.  In particular $[\rho(H),C_5]=C_5$,
which proves every lower-central equality in
\textup{(E.50.3)}.

There is a small exact matrix description of the
$120$-element subgroup.  In the third pentagon pair
$(A_{23},A_{34})$, the plane spanned by $e_2,e_3$ is
stable.  Extract its matrix from the identity $B/F$
coset and transpose it.  This defines
\[
 \theta:\rho(F)\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_5),
 \quad
 \theta(x)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix},
 \quad
 \theta(y)=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{E.50.8}
\]
The transpose converts the specified right-permutation
multiplication into ordinary matrix multiplication.
The faithful compression is inverted on this coordinate
using its retained orbit intertwiners.  On $L_5=N'=C_5'$
the map takes exactly the $120$ determinant-one matrices,
each once; it is therefore an actual isomorphism.
The full derived calculation also gives $L_5'=L_5$.

For every retained generator $g$ of $N$ or $C_5$, let
$\ell(g)$ be the unique element of $L_5$ with
$\theta(\ell(g))=\theta(g)$, and put
$v(g)=g\ell(g)^{-1}$.
The certificate checks that these $v(g)$ commute with
$L_5$, commute with one another, and have order dividing
five.  Their complete images have orders $3125$ and
$125$, respectively, and meet $L_5$ trivially.
This proves the direct products in
\textup{(E.50.3)} with these explicit inclusions and
projections.  It identifies $Q_5$ as the actual kernel
of $\theta|_{C_5}$ and as $O_5(C_5)$.

Every noncentral element of $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_5)$
has normal closure the full group.  The independent
matrix calculation records the $120$ normal-closure
orders: one is $1$, one is $2$, and the other $118$
are $120$.  Consequently every perfect quotient of
$C_5=L_5\times Q_5$ kills $Q_5$.
Indeed its image is already the image of $L_5$, while
the image of the central five-group $Q_5$ would be a
central five-subgroup of a quotient of $L_5$;
the displayed normal-closure calculation excludes it.
Also $C_5^{\rm ab}$ has order $125$, so $C_5$ has no
nontrivial solvable quotient whose order has only the
prime divisors two and three.

\paragraph{The abelian and signed source quotients.}
The images of $x,y$ have order ten and
$|\rho(F)^{\rm ab}|=100$.  Their actual free exponent
sums therefore identify this abelianization with
$(\mathbf Z/10)^2$.
Together with the unchanged six-coset map to $B/F$,
this proves
\[
                   G_{5,\mathrm{sp}}/C_5\xrightarrow{\sim}A_{10}.
 \tag{E.50.9}
\]
For precision the program constructs $A_m$ directly:
for a section $b_q$ of $B/F$ it retains the six vectors
\[
       \operatorname{ab}(b_q b b_{q\bar b}^{-1})\pmod m.
 \tag{E.50.10}
\]
Multiplication is section-word cancellation.  Its
kernel is $F'F^m$: the identity coset detects ordinary
abelianization, and the remaining cosets are its
actual conjugates.  Enumeration gives $6m^2$ elements
for $m=2,10$.

On $F$, the specified modular map to $Q_{20}$ has a
unique signed representative whose diagonal entries
are $1$ modulo four.  Its two coordinates are
\[
 \left(
    P\theta(w)P^{-1},\
    \begin{pmatrix}1&2a(w)\\-2b(w)&1\end{pmatrix}\pmod4
 \right),
 \qquad
 P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
 \tag{E.50.11}
\]
where $a,b$ are the ordinary free exponent sums modulo
two.  This equality holds on $x,y$ and respects the

all of $F$.  Chinese remainders give the indicated
matrix modulo twenty.  The full $F$ image is
$\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_5)\times C_2^2$, of
order $480$; adjoining $s,r$ gives all $2880$ elements

The kernel of $B\to G_{5,\mathrm{sp}}$ lies in $F$
and kills both coordinates in \textup{(E.50.11)}.
Thus $Q_{20}$ is an actual quotient of this source.
Its kernel is the rank-five elementary abelian
factor in \textup{(E.50.3)}.

The map $A_{10}\to A_2$ is reduction of
\textup{(E.50.10)}.  Reducing the same signed modular

The resulting two maps have fibres of orders $25$

$C_5\cap\ker\theta=Q_5$.
This proves the first map in \textup{(E.50.5)}.

\paragraph{Corrections that retain the entire boundary system.}
Let
\[
             R_{\rm nil}=\bigcap_{j\ge1}\gamma_j(\widehat H).
 \tag{E.50.15}
\]
For any continuous finite image of $\widehat H$,
the image of this intersection is its stabilized
lower-central subgroup.  Indeed, for each element
in that finite intersection, its inverse images
in the compact groups $\gamma_j(\widehat H)$ are
nonempty nested closed sets.  Their intersection
is a preimage in $R_{\rm nil}$.
It follows from \textup{(E.50.3)} that
$\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(R_{\rm nil})=C_5$.
Every finite nilpotent $H$ boundary image kills
$R_{\rm nil}$.  This includes every finite stage
of E.41, E.47, and all full pro-$p$ $H$ factors
of E.42.

On $G_L$, the image of $R_{\rm nil}$ is exactly
$C_L$.  Its image in $S$ is trivial because
the actual $H$ image of $S$ is the class-two
group $Q_8\times C_{10}^2$ in E.43.
Conversely E.43's closed prosolvable residual
is contained in $R_{\rm nil}$ and maps onto
$C_L$.  Thus both inclusions are actual
subgroup comparisons.

Apply Goursat to the image of $R_{\rm nil}$
in $C_L\times C_5$.  Its common quotient is
perfect because $C_L$ is perfect, and every
perfect quotient of $C_5$ kills $Q_5$ by the
normal-closure argument following
\textup{(E.50.8)}.  Hence
\[
 \rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}
       (R_{\rm nil}\cap\ker\rho_L)\supset Q_5.
 \tag{E.50.16}
\]
The reverse inclusion follows from the common
map to $J$ in \textup{(E.50.5)}.  Intersecting
also with $\ker\rho_{D_{23}}$ retains this
entire image: the two images in question are
a five-group and a group whose order has
only the prime divisors two and three, so
their common quotient is trivial.  Therefore
\[
 \rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}
 \bigl(R_{\rm nil}\cap\ker\rho_L
                     \cap\ker\rho_{D_{23}}\bigr)=Q_5.
 \tag{E.50.17}
\]
Every element of this compact subgroup also
kills every retained boundary factor, and
lies in $\widehat H'\subset\widehat F'$.

The old finite sources retain $A_{10}$:
$\gamma_3(H)H^{20}\subset F'F^{10}$ gives
the actual map $P_{20}\to A_{10}$.
Before adjoining $G_L$, the same argument
with $C_5$ and the solvable $\{2,3\}$ image
of $D_{23}$ gives the whole pure $C_5$.
Together with \textup{(E.50.9)} this proves
the first original-source fibre product in
\textup{(E.50.6)}.
For that family choose the correction with
new image $\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(f_0)^{-1}$;
this element lies in $C_5$ because
$f_0\in\widehat F'$.
The resulting new word value is $1$, so
its multiplier-one new source and braid
maps are the original onto maps.
After adjoining $G_L$, equation
\textup{(E.50.17)} gives the whole pure
$Q_5$, and the common map to $J$ gives
the reverse containment.  This proves that
$J$ is the maximum common quotient through
these specified source maps, and proves
the second original-source fibre product.

Now take the E.47 word $f_0$ with the E.43
comparison
$\rho_L(f_0)=\rho_L(f_\sigma)$ for its
specified actual Galois element $\sigma$.
Their images in $J$ agree, so
\[
       q=\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(f_0)^{-1}
                   \rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(f_\sigma)\in Q_5.
 \tag{E.50.18}
\]
Choose its actual preimage $k$ in the
compact subgroup of \textup{(E.50.17)},
and put $f=f_0k$.
This one profinite commutator word retains
every old coordinate and satisfies
$\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(f)=
\rho_{5,\mathrm{sp}}(f_\sigma)$.
In particular all $48$ induced evaluation
coordinates agree with the same Galois word.
The new five-strand map is consequently
the original quotient in
\textup{(E.50.1)} precomposed with the
actual profinite Galois braid automorphism.
This proves its relations, periodic
conjugacies and surjectivity without a
kernel-invariance assertion for a finite
shadow.  E.46.26 carries the unchanged
$G_L$ value to its Hurwitz source as well.

\paragraph{Generation on the actual joint source.}
The corrected maps to both factors of
the second fibre product in
\textup{(E.50.6)} are onto, and agree
on $J$ with the same Galois source map.
On the old side the $H$ image is onto
because its retained $B/H$ map is the
same automorphism and its whole source
map is onto.  On the new side it is onto
because the corrected map agrees with
the actual Galois map, whose source
automorphism preserves $H$.
Equivalently, its onto $F$ image has
abelianization $C_{10}^2$ and hence no
nontrivial quotient of the exponent-three
group $F/H$.
This verifies the $H$ comparison even
though $G_{5,\mathrm{sp}}$ itself does
not retain $B/H$.

By the compact argument after
\textup{(E.50.15)}, the corrected image
of $R_{\rm nil}$ on the new side is
$C_5$.  On the old side it is the direct
product of $C_L$ with a solvable
$\{2,3\}$ image: the boundary factors
kill it, and perfectness excludes a
common quotient between $C_L$ and
that solvable image.
Every common quotient of this old
residual and $C_5$ is perfect.  Indeed
its abelianization is both a quotient
of the old $\{2,3\}$ abelianization
and a quotient of $C_5^{\rm ab}$,
which is a five-group; it is therefore
trivial.  The same normal-closure
argument forces that common quotient
to kill $Q_5$.
Thus the corrected joint image contains
$\{1\}\times Q_5$.
Its onto projection to $G_+$ now gives
the entire fibre product over $J$.
The version without $G_L$ follows by
the corresponding argument with the
whole $C_5$.
This proves generation for the actual
source maps, rather than presuming
that their finite kernels are invariant.

The independent programs share the prefix
\texttt{bc\_symplectic\_five\_p5\_}, with

They retain the exact source arrays,

The result concerns the specified
multiplier-one family and the stated

full-GT image questions remain those
recorded in E.2 and E.3.
\end{proof}
