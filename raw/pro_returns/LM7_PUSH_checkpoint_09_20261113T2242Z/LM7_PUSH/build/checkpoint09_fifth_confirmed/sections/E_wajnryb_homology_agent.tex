\subsection{An exact comparison for the genus-three homology source}
\begin{statement}{E.72}{Proved}{The genus-three Wajnryb homology evaluations factor through the retained genus-two evaluations}
For $n=4,6$, let $J_n$ have entries $(J_n)_{i,i+1}=1$,
$(J_n)_{i+1,i}=-1$, and all other entries zero.  In the indicated
standard integral basis define
\[
 A_i=I_6+e_i e_i^tJ_6,\qquad
 D=I_6+(e_1+e_3)(e_1+e_3)^tJ_6,
 \qquad
 B_i=I_4+e_i e_i^tJ_4.
 \tag{E.72.1}
\]
The assignment $a_i\mapsto A_i$, $d\mapsto D$ satisfies the
genus-three Wajnryb relations (A), (B), (C) in
\cite[Theorem 3.1]{DE_LochakNakamuraSchneps2026}, with
\[
 Y_k=A_{k-1}\cdots A_2 A_1^2 A_2\cdots A_{k-1},
 \qquad
 Z_k=B_{k-1}\cdots B_2 B_1^2 B_2\cdots B_{k-1}.
 \tag{E.72.2}
\]
In particular, the literal word $d_3$ of that presentation has image
$I_6+(e_1+e_3+e_5)(e_1+e_3+e_5)^tJ_6$.

For every integer $m\geq1$, consider the five homomorphisms
from the original source free group $F=F(x,y)$ obtained by sending
$(x,y)$ to $(Y_k,A_k^2)$, $2\leq k\leq6$, modulo $m$.
Their joint kernel is exactly the joint kernel of the three maps
\[
       (x,y)\longmapsto (Z_k,B_k^2),\qquad 2\leq k\leq4.
 \tag{E.72.3}
\]
This equality also holds for their closed profinite kernels.
After induction through the six specified $B/F$ cosets, it gives
the same equality of the two normal kernels in $B=C_2*C_3$.
Consequently the earlier eight-evaluation symplectic source maps
onto this entire five-evaluation genus-three source through the
same literal generators $s,r$.

The comparison is integral and retains central signs.  Put
\[
\begin{split}
 P_0&=(e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4,e_1+e_3+e_5,e_6),\\
 P_1&=(e_1+e_3,e_4,e_5,e_6,e_1,e_2).
\end{split}
 \tag{E.72.4}
\]
Both matrices have determinant one.  The complete pair maps are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 k&P& P^{-1}Y_kP&P^{-1}A_k^2P\\ \hline
 2,3,4&P_0&\operatorname{diag}(Z_k,I_2)&
                         \operatorname{diag}(B_k^2,I_2)\\
 5&P_1&\operatorname{diag}(Z_3,-I_2)&
                         \operatorname{diag}(B_3^2,I_2)\\
 6&P_1&\operatorname{diag}(Z_4,-I_2)&
                         \operatorname{diag}(B_4^2,I_2).
\end{array}
 \tag{E.72.5}
\]
For $k=3,4$, the old subgroup generated by $(Z_k,B_k^2)$ preserves
$\mathbb Z e_1$; the two generators act on this line by $-1$ and
$1$.  Thus the extra block is the character already present in
the same old evaluation.  It is the identity for every
$f\in\widehat F'$.

For the previously constructed source families whose retained
five-strand symplectic values are those of the specified Galois
word, these genus-three values are those of that same word.
Their Wajnryb images therefore satisfy all the displayed genus-three
relations and generate the actual matrix target.  In particular,
passing from the retained homology source to this genus-three
homology source imposes no further restriction on their CM pairs.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We give the integral maps first and then identify the induced source
kernels, including the right-action convention of the stored finite
certificates.

The matrices in (E.72.1) preserve $J_6$, and the analogous four-dimensional
matrices preserve $J_4$.  Their adjacent braid and nonadjacent
commutation relations follow by multiplying the two rank-one
nilpotent parts.  The vector $e_1+e_3$ has zero pairing with $e_j$
for $j\ne4$, and pairing one with $e_4$, giving the additional
relations involving $d$.  The exact remaining products are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 D Y_5 D Y_5^{-1}=(A_1A_2A_3)^4,\\
 t_1=A_2A_1A_3A_2,\qquad t_2=A_4A_3A_5A_4,\\
 (t_1^{-1}t_2^{-1}Dt_2t_1)(t_2^{-1}Dt_2)
       D A_1^{-1}A_3^{-1}A_5^{-1}
   =I_6+(e_1+e_3+e_5)(e_1+e_3+e_5)^tJ_6.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E.72.6}
\]
The vector on the last line has pairing one with $e_6$, proving
relation (C).  These integral identities give the specified
homology representation of the Wajnryb generators; no conclusion
about a surface-cutting map is used.

Direct integral multiplication gives (E.72.5), as well as
\[
 P_i^tJ_6P_i=\operatorname{diag}
       \left(J_4,\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}\right),
 \qquad \det P_i=1\quad(i=0,1).
 \tag{E.72.7}
\]
For the last two pairs the identities $Z_ke_1=-e_1$ and
$B_k^2e_1=e_1$ define a homomorphism
\[
 \epsilon_k:\langle Z_k,B_k^2\rangle\longrightarrow
       \{1,-1\},\qquad M e_1=\epsilon_k(M)e_1.
 \tag{E.72.8}
\]
Over a ring where $1=-1$, its image has one element; the formula
still specifies the same matrix map.  The required homomorphisms
on the old pair images are therefore
\[
 M\longmapsto P_0\operatorname{diag}(M,I_2)P_0^{-1},
 \qquad
 M\longmapsto P_1\operatorname{diag}
                (M,\epsilon_k(M)I_2)P_1^{-1}.
 \tag{E.72.9}
\]
They agree with every source generator in (E.72.5).  This proves
that the five-evaluation map factors through the three-evaluation
map over every $\mathbb Z/m$.  The first three blocks recover all
three old matrices by projection, so the reverse kernel inclusion
holds as well.  The maps are continuous on profinite images, giving
the asserted equality there.  Equation (E.72.8) also proves the
claim for $\widehat F'$ without discarding a sign coordinate.

Here is the exact induction comparison.  Use the same six coset
representatives $t_q$ of $F$ in $B$ as in the retained sources.
For $b\in B$, the induced coordinate at $q$ is the evaluation
of the actual word $t_q b t_{q\bar b}^{-1}\in F$.
Apply (E.72.9) separately to these six words and retain the same
coset permutation $q\mapsto q\bar b$.  This defines a homomorphism
of the actual induced images.  Its inverse kernel comparison is
the recovery of the first three old coordinates just proved.
Equivalently, induction replaces either common $F$-kernel $K$
by the same intersection $\bigcap_{q\in B/F}t_q^{-1}Kt_q$.
The earlier eight-evaluation source contains exactly the three
specified evaluations (E.72.3), so projection supplies the final
surjection in the statement.

The Drinfeld substitution in
\cite[Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 4.3]{DE_LochakNakamuraSchneps2026}
uses precisely these pairs, with
$a_i\mapsto f(Y_i,A_i^2)^{-1}A_i^\lambda f(Y_i,A_i^2)$ and
$d\mapsto D^\lambda$.  On each retained family matched to the
specified Galois word, (E.72.9) gives equality of all seven
generator values with that word.  The Galois automorphism on the
presented group is onto; composing it with the matrix quotient
therefore has the original full matrix image.  This proves the
stated relation and generation assertion without assuming that
an arbitrary shadow preserves a chosen quotient kernel.

The retained program \texttt{de\_wajnryb\_homology.py} checks every
identity in (E.72.6)--(E.72.9) over $\mathbb Z$, and also checks
the genus-two and genus-four versions of the presentation.
It repeats the presentation checks on the transposed matrices,
which are the right-action matrices of the saved permutation
models.  It compares the four old generator matrices and all
six old pair matrices with the saved five-strand characteristic-three
model.  Finally it reconstructs all $90=3\cdot6\cdot5$ induced
coordinates for $s,r,r^{-1}$ on the full $728$-point vector set.
The source has degree $21840$, and its $s^2=r^3=1$ identities are
checked literally.  The JSON and NPZ retain the two integral
basis matrices, all new generators and all reconstructed source
coordinates.  The successful run took $0.190$ seconds; no old
source or certificate was modified.
\end{proof}
