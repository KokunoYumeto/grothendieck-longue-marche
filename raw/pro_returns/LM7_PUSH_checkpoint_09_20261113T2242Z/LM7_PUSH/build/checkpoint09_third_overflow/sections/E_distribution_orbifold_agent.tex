\begin{statement}{E.68}{Proved}{The exact common source for the distribution and orbifold tests}
Let $N=N_B$ and retain the actual E.66 correction map
\[
 \Psi:N'\longrightarrow Z\subset C\times J,
 \qquad |Z|=356\,241\,767\,399\,424,
 \tag{E.68.1}
\]
where $C$ is the E.62 correction group of order $2^{24}$
and $J$ retains the five coordinate-zero distribution maps
and their coefficients modulo six. Let
$\Theta:\widehat F\to\mathrm M^6\times\mathrm G^6$ be the twelve
literal orbifold evaluations of E.67. Its restriction to
$N'$ has image $\mathcal M\times\mathcal G$ of order $3^9$. The image of the actual common-domain map is
\[
 (\Psi,\Theta)(N')=Z\times\mathcal M\times\mathcal G,
 \qquad
 |(\Psi,\Theta)(N')|=7\,011\,906\,707\,722\,862\,592.
 \tag{E.68.2}
\]

Use precisely the $24$ indexed E.17 words $f_i$ retained
by E.62 and E.66, representing the E.1 CM pairs. For each
of their four final E.62 correction coordinates, each of
its three E.66 coefficient tuples, and each of the four
E.67 orbifold norm targets, there is one common word
$u\in N'$ attaining all the prescribed values.
There are exactly $48$ such finite value combinations
over each indexed origin and $1152$ in total. All have
saved actual $N'$ word preimages. In particular all four
Gaussian norm-one actions remain attainable in every
one of the retained E.66 finite fibres.

For one such combination, fix its saved word $u$. Its
complete word fibre inside $f_iN'$ is
\[
       f_i u\bigl(N'\cap\ker\Psi\cap\ker\Theta\bigr).
 \tag{E.68.3}
\]
The statement concerns these exact finite source maps
and the equations of E.62, E.66 and E.67. It makes no
generation assertion for the enlarged distribution
source and assigns no full-GT lift to these words.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We construct the common finite quotient before taking
its commutator image or any coordinate-zero projection.

\paragraph{One literal source and its exact projections.}
Take the unchanged $97$ free generators of $N_B$.
The E.66 source acts on $5223$ points: its first $4764$
points retain the joint E.62 Ferrari source, and its
remaining $459$ points retain the full induced cyclic
distribution actions for $n=2,3,4,6,12$.
Adjoin the $108$-point E.67 action, whose two free-generator
permutations are exported with the convention
\[
 p(gh)=p(h)[p(g)],\qquad
            (v,A):t\longmapsto A^{-1}(t-v).
 \tag{E.68.4}
\]
The program first compares all $97$ free words
letter for letter between the two source lists and
then replays each in the two exported permutations.
Thus the resulting $5331$-point generators are values
of the same elements of $N_B$.

Let $P$ be their finite image. The complete Schreier
certificate retains a generating subset of thirteen
of these actual words and proves its equality with
the full $97$-generator image. Its complete derived
certificate computes $P'$, which is the image of
$N_B'$ under the original surjection. Only now do
we retain the five distribution coordinate-zero
blocks. This restriction is a homomorphism on $P'$:
every original word in $N_B'\subset\widehat F'$
has zero cyclic deck exponent in each cover.
Together with all Ferrari and orbifold points the
retained action has degree
\[
                    4764+5\cdot17+108=4957.
 \tag{E.68.5}
\]
Its image is exactly the left side of
\textup{(E.68.2)}, with the stated maps to $Z$ and
$\mathcal M\times\mathcal G$.

Both projections are onto. The first follows from
the unchanged $5223$-point source and the fact that
surjections carry the commutator subgroup onto the
commutator subgroup. The second follows from the
same literal $97$ orbifold values and E.67; the new
projected certificate also verifies its order $3^9$
directly. The complete certificate for the $4957$-point
image gives
\[
  |(\Psi,\Theta)(N')|
    =7\,011\,906\,707\,722\,862\,592
    =|Z|\,3^9.
 \tag{E.68.6}
\]
It is a subgroup of the displayed product, so this
order equality proves the literal surjectivity in
\textup{(E.68.2)}. The four complete certificates
retain all original-word DAGs and verify every final
Schreier relation; their independent word replays
use the same right-action multiplication.

\paragraph{Origins, targets and the common correction.}
For each actual E.17 origin $f_i$, the program
reevaluates the word in the two E.67 free-generator
permutations. It compares this value with the
separately exported E.17 origin value. No correction
from the distinct original E.1 word list is used.

Here is the exact membership target. For one of the
three E.66 tuples $\mathbf c=(c_n)$, put
\[
 d_n=
 \psi_n(f_i)^{-1}
       \bigl(q_F(y)^{c_n}q_F(f_i),\ c_n\bmod6\bigr),
 \qquad
 d_{\mathrm{orb}}=t-\Theta(f_i),
 \tag{E.68.7}
\]
where $t$ is an absolute E.67 twelve-translation norm
target and $\psi_n$ is the actual matrix-and-coefficient
map of E.66. The chosen E.62 coordinate $c\in C$
and $(d_n)$ form an element of $Z$ by E.66. The
translation $d_{\mathrm{orb}}$ belongs to
$\mathcal M\times\mathcal G$ by the E.17 evaluation
in E.67. Therefore \textup{(E.68.2)} supplies
$u\in N'$ with
\[
          \Psi(u)=(c,(d_n)),\qquad
          \Theta(u)=d_{\mathrm{orb}}.
 \tag{E.68.8}
\]
Multiplication gives all the required values of
$f_i u$, with the printed correction order and
translation sign. Since $N'\subset K_6$, its
specified old $G_6$ value is unchanged.

The complete finite lists have four E.62 coordinates,
three E.66 tuples for each, and four E.67 targets.
Testing all $1152$ combinations in the actual joint
certificate confirms every membership. These are
complete combinations of the three stated lists,
so the resulting finite count is exact. The inverse
image of a value under a homomorphism is a coset of
its kernel; this gives \textup{(E.68.3)}.

\paragraph{Actual word preimages and reproducibility.}
The witness program constructs each joint preimage
from the projected stabilizer chain. With right
permutations, removing a transversal on the right
requires prepending that transversal to the reconstructed
word. It retains this order explicitly, then substitutes
the projected original generators by their actual
derived-source word DAGs. Consequently every returned
word is an element of $N_B'$, not just an independently
chosen tuple in its projections.

All $1152$ resulting words are replayed on the full
$5331$-point source before restriction. The replay
checks $24363$ ancestor nodes in a $26479$-node DAG
and verifies the exact Ferrari coordinate, all five
distribution values and all twelve orbifold values
for each word. The input record also retains the
thirteen original $N_B$ words.

The new programs use the prefix
\texttt{a\_cm\_distribution\_orbifold}; their two
suffixes are \texttt{.py} and
\texttt{\_witnesses.py}. The frozen E.66 and E.67
inputs are read only. The isolated replay guide and
manifest retain the full source, target, output and
word-certificate comparisons.
\end{proof}
