\begin{statement}{E.71}{Proved}{The complete matrix distribution fibres on the full retained word domain}
Put $N=N_B$, $K_6=\overline{N'N^6}$ and
\[
             J=K_6\cap\widehat F',\qquad
             \widehat F=\widehat F(x,y).
 \tag{E.71.1}
\]
Keep exactly the $24$ indexed E.17 origins $f_i$ used
in E.62 and E.68. Their entire word fibres with the
same $G_6$ value and zero abelianization are $f_iJ$.

For $n=2,3$, let $\delta_n$ be the positive cyclic-cover
Schreier homomorphism of D.5, and put
$c_n=\operatorname{exp}_y\circ\delta_n$.
Evaluate
\[
       q\delta_n(f)=q(y)^{c_n(f)}q(f)
 \tag{E.71.2}
\]
in every literal epimorphism to
$\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$ and
$\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$, represented by the
$18$ and $16$ exact epikernel representatives of
E.62 and E.59. Retain $c_2,c_3$ modulo $24$, as well as
all the finite E.68 conditions and their coefficients
modulo six.

The actual common correction map $\rho:J\to P$,
constructed below, has
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |P|&=2^{105}3^{17},&
 |P_0|&=2^{103}3^{17},\\
 P_0&=\rho(N'),&
 P/P_0&\simeq\mathbf Z/4\mathbf Z.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E.71.3}
\]
Its old projection is exactly the E.68 image
$O=Z\times\mathcal M\times\mathcal G$, of order
$2^{42}3^{13}$. The kernel $P\to O$ has order
$2^{63}3^4$.

For each of the $48$ E.68 values over one origin,
and for each of the $16$ lifts of its pair
$(c_2,c_3)$ from modulo six to modulo $24$, there is
exactly one value in $P$ satisfying all $70$ matrix
and coefficient conditions (E.71.2). Consequently
the complete finite locus has $768$ values over each
indexed origin and $18\,432$ in total. All these
values have saved actual word preimages in $J$.
For a saved correction $u$, the entire corresponding
word fibre is
\[
                         f_i u\ker(\rho).
 \tag{E.71.4}
\]
The retained $N'$ intermediate has exactly four
coefficient lifts over each E.68 value, or $4608$
values in total. The cyclic quotient in (E.71.3)
is the actual map
\[
            [u]\longmapsto c_2(u)/6\pmod4.
 \tag{E.71.5}
\]
These assertions determine the displayed finite
conditions on the whole domain $f_iJ$. They do not
assign a full-GT lift or generation in an additional
source to any of the saved words.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
We construct the finite actions, identify the whole
correction domain, and then determine its fibres.

\paragraph{The exact distribution maps.}
Let $H_n$ be the kernel of
$\widehat F\to\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$ sending $x$ to
$1$ and $y$ to $0$. Its positive Schreier generators
are $x^n$ and $x^j yx^{-j}$, $0\leq j<n$. Define
\[
 \delta_n(x^n)=x,\qquad \delta_n(y)=y,\qquad
 \delta_n(x^j yx^{-j})=1\quad(0<j<n).
 \tag{E.71.6}
\]
These assignments give an actual continuous
homomorphism on $H_n$. Enriquez's Theorem~8.1,
equation~(50), and Remark~8.6 prove the corresponding
full profinite GT identities and
$c_{mn}(f)=c_m(f)+c_n(f)$
\cite{DE_Enriquez2007}; the sign and the comparison
with the other Schreier convention are those of D.5.

Write $r_n$ for the least nonnegative residue. For
$w\in\widehat F$, put
\[
 j_w=r_n(j+\operatorname{exp}_x(w)),\qquad
 s_j(w)=x^jwx^{-j_w}.
 \tag{E.71.7}
\]
The index is reduced before it is used as a section
exponent. Thus the wrap under $x$ contributes $x^n$.
For a literal epimorphism $q:\widehat F\to H$, the
following is a right action on $\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\times H$:
\[
 (j,z)w=
 \bigl(j_w,q\delta_n(s_j(w))^{-1}zq(w)\bigr).
 \tag{E.71.8}
\]
Indeed
$s_j(ww')=s_j(w)s_{j_w}(w')$; applying the two
actions gives the inverse of that product on the
left and $q(ww')$ on the right. This proves the
morphism, including negative letters and all closed
profinite words. On $\widehat F'$ the coordinate
$j=0$ is stable, and
\[
       (0,q(y)^a)f=(0,1)
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       q\delta_n(f)=q(y)^a q(f).
 \tag{E.71.9}
\]
An additional right action on
$\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\times\mathbf Z/24\mathbf Z$
adds $c_n(s_j(w))$ in its second coordinate. Its
condition $(0,-a)f=(0,0)$ is exactly
$c_n(f)=a\pmod{24}$.

The $34$ representative maps retain the literal
matrix groups and their full automorphism orbits.
An equality for a representative transports through
its recorded automorphism to every epimorphism in
that orbit. The least common multiple of their
$q(y)$ orders is $24$. There are therefore exactly
$2(34+1)=70$ specified target points. Reduction of
the coefficient action modulo six agrees with the
E.66 action on each generator in (E.71.6), hence on
the entire cyclic-cover group. The other retained
coefficients are exactly
\[
       (c_2,c_3,c_4,c_6,c_{12})
                 =(a,b,2a,a+b,2a+b)\pmod6.
 \tag{E.71.10}
\]
The new full action has $6360$ points. Adjoining the
unchanged $5331$-point E.68 source gives one actual
$11\,691$-point source. The code first compares the
same $97$ original words and all the literal matrix
tables. No coordinate-zero restriction is made
before the required subgroup is identified.

\paragraph{The entire closed word fibre.}
Let $n_1,\ldots,n_{97}$ be the retained free basis of
$N$. Their actual two integral free-source exponent
rows give
\[
 e:\widehat{\mathbf Z}^{97}=N^{\mathrm{ab}}
                  \longrightarrow\widehat{\mathbf Z}^{2}.
 \tag{E.71.11}
\]
The saved integer column operations, with their
integer inverse checked in both orders, produce
$U\in\operatorname{GL}_{97}(\mathbf Z)$ with
\[
 eU=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&0&\cdots&0\\4&6&0&\cdots&0\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{E.71.12}
\]
The image index is $12$, and its cokernel Smith
factors are $2,6$. They are retained, rather than
replaced by a surjective exponent map. Multiplication
by every nonzero integer is injective on
$\widehat{\mathbf Z}=\prod_p\mathbf Z_p$.
The first two columns in (E.71.12) are therefore
injective, so the last $95$ columns are exactly
$\ker e$ at all primes.

The abelian quotient map identifies $K_6/N'$ with
$6\widehat{\mathbf Z}^{97}$. It induces the exact
surjection
\[
 J\longrightarrow6\ker e,
 \qquad
 J/N'=6\widehat{\mathbf Z}^{97}\cap\ker e=6\ker e.
 \tag{E.71.13}
\]
For the last equality, $6e(v)=0$ implies $e(v)=0$.
For surjectivity, lift any $a\in6\ker e$ through
$N\to N^{\mathrm{ab}}$. Its lift has zero free-source
exponents and has abelianization divisible by six,
so it lies in $J$. This is an actual lift through
the closed profinite quotient.

For each last column of $U$, form the finite word
$g_j=\prod_{i=1}^{97}n_i^{U_{i,j+2}}$ in increasing
generator order. Its two integer exponents are zero.
The closed subgroup generated by $N'$ and all the
$g_j^6$ contains the kernel in (E.71.13) and maps
onto its entire target; hence it is $J$. All $95$
literal words, columns, inverses and sixth-power
values are retained and replayed. Thus their values,
together with the complete $N'$ image, generate
the entire correction image in every finite source.

\paragraph{The complete finite image and its cyclic quotient.}
The complete Schreier calculation first determines
the image of $N$ on all $11\,691$ points and then
its derived subgroup. This is the actual image of
$N'$. On $J\subset\widehat F'$ each coordinate-zero
block is stable. Retaining all old $4957$ points and
the new $2544$ coordinate-zero points gives the
homomorphism $\rho$ of degree $7501$.

Adjoin the $95$ actual sixth powers to the computed
$N'$ image. Complete presentations and independent
word replays give (E.71.3), and show that the old
projection remains exactly $O$. In particular this
enlargement has index four. Its complete coset
multiplication table, also retained, identifies the
quotient as cyclic.

There is a direct coefficient identification of
that quotient. Equation (E.71.12) shows $N\subset
H_2$, so $c_2|_N$ is a genuine homomorphism and
kills $N'$. Its saved integral row on the last
$95$ columns has greatest common divisor one.
It follows through (E.71.13) that
$c_2(J)=6\widehat{\mathbf Z}$. The first kernel word is
\[
 \begin{split}
 g_0&=n_1^{-1}n_2n_3^{-1}\\
    &=y^{-5}x^{-1}yx^{-2}yx^{-1}y^2xy^{-1}x^2y^2x,
       \qquad c_2(g_0)=-3.
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.71.14}
\]
Thus $c_2(g_0^6)=6\pmod{24}$. The retained coefficient
action makes (E.71.5) well-defined on $P$; it is onto
and kills $P_0$. Since $[P:P_0]=4$, its kernel is
exactly $P_0$. This proves the asserted quotient map,
including its integral division by six.

\paragraph{Exact fixed-value fibres.}
The prescribed-base Schreier driver changes no
group algorithm: it installs a specified base prefix
before running the unchanged complete engine.
First use a complete base of $O$, of length $31$.
The corresponding stabilizer is precisely
$K=\ker(P\to O)$, and the complete certificate retains
all its strong generators as words in the original
source. Install the $70$ target points of (E.71.9)
and its coefficient companions as a base for $K$.
The orbit-length product is
\[
           |K|=2^{63}3^4,
 \qquad \operatorname{Stab}_K(\text{all 70 targets})=1.
 \tag{E.71.15}
\]
Every final Schreier relation and every strong word
is independently replayed. Thus this is the entire
kernel, with a trivial simultaneous stabilizer.

For completeness the program rechecks all
$24\cdot4\cdot36\cdot4=13\,824$ old candidates.
Exactly the $1152$ E.68 values occur in $O$.
For an old value $v$, the old-base transversals give
an actual preimage $u_0\in P$. Its full inverse
image is $u_0K$. For each lift $(A,B)$ modulo $24$
of the old pair $(a,b)$, apply $f_i u_0$ to the
$70$ points
\[
 (0,q(y)^A),\ (0,-A),\qquad
 (0,q(y)^B),\ (0,-B),
 \tag{E.71.16}
\]
with their respective matrix and coefficient blocks.
The remaining problem is the literal simultaneous
transporter in $K$ from this tuple to the target
tuple. The complete orbit tables decide it, retaining
each inverse transversal as an actual kernel word.
All $1152\cdot16=18\,432$ transporters exist.
Equation (E.71.15) proves uniqueness of their finite
values. The separate $P_0$ calculation gives exactly
four of them: $A=c_2(f_i)\pmod{24}$ and all four
compatible values of $B$. This retains the smaller
intermediate without using it to exclude a full fibre.

Finally substitute the kernel and old-base
transversals into their source certificates, then
substitute the $95$ lattice words. The resulting DAG
is over the original $97$ words. All $18\,432$ values
are replayed on the full $11\,691$ points. Every
saved correction has zero exact integral free-source
exponents and zero $N$-abelian exponents modulo six;
hence it belongs to $J$. Its retained value is the
one just counted. Equality under the homomorphism
$\rho$ is exactly the kernel coset (E.71.4).

\end{proof}
