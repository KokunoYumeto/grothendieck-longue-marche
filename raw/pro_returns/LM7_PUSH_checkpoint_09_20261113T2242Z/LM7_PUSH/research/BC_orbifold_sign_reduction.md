# The original Mordell sign and a finite Gaussian comparison

This is new research after the E.64 freeze. The old E.64 section and
certificates are unchanged. All matrices below are in the same ordered
Ferrari basis `(xi1,xi2)` as E.64; the two actions being compared retain
their separate source maps.

## 1. An actual order-54 quotient of the Mordell triangle

Let `V=F3^2`, and retain

```
R=[[0,1],[-1,1]],       R^3=-I,       R^6=I.
```

In `V semidirect <R>` use the product `(v,A)(w,B)=(v+Aw,AB)`.
The literal triangle map is

```
x -> (0,R),      y -> (e1,R^3),      z -> (e1,R^2).
```

It satisfies `xyz=1`, `y^2=z^3=1` and is onto the order-54 group.
In fact `zx^-2=(e1,I)` and `x^4z=(e2,I)`. These are exactly the
two Mordell kernel generators of E.64.19 after taking the central
quotient. The quotient is therefore the actual

```
Delta(infinity,2,3)/([K,K] K^3)
       = (K^ab/3) semidirect C6.
```

The split here is proved by the cusp relation: `x^6` is the inverse
primitive boundary commutator, so becomes one on abelianizing `K`.
There is no transfer from the pro-three completion of the whole
triangle group.

For every multiplier-one normalized gentle pair, its original
orbifold action `Omega3` fixes `x` and sends

```
z -> f(z,x) z f(z,x)^-1.
```

The conjugator has trivial deck coordinate because `f` is a
commutator word. Write its translation as `a in V`, and let `B3`
be the induced automorphism of `V`. The latter commutes with `R`.
Comparison of the displayed two images of `z` gives the exact
equation

```
(B3-I)e1 = (I-R^2)a.
```

Now

```
im(I-R^2)=F3(1,-1),       e1 is nonzero in V/im(I-R^2).
```

Every matrix commuting with `R` is `A I+B R`. Its scalar on that
one-dimensional quotient and on the line `F3(1,-1)` is the same,
namely `A-B`. The preceding equation forces this scalar to be one.
Thus the original Mordell orbifold character has **positive sign
on the E.1 line for every multiplier-one gentle pair**. The proof
uses the actual deck-zero conjugator, and applies in particular to
the full-GT orbifold action of NTY III Proposition 4.4.

For the E.64 norm-character comparison this proves the exact identity

```
epsilon3(C3(F)) = epsilon3(D3(F)).
```

The original Mordell beta/Tate factor cannot contribute the negative
sign. This does not evaluate `D3` outside the actual Galois comparison.

## 2. The order-36 Gaussian quotient and its exact finite actions

Put

```
S=[[0,1],[-1,0]],
x -> (e1,S),      y -> (-e2,S^2),      z -> (0,S)
```

in `V semidirect <S>`. This is the actual quotient of the quartic
triangle of E.64 by `[K,K]K^3`: the two explicit translations are
`x^2y=e1` and `z^2y=e2`. Its order is 36. These are the native
Ferrari coordinates, with no unprinted change of origin.

For a genuine multiplier-one orbifold action, the homology matrix
`B4` commutes with `S` and has determinant one, so
`B4` belongs to `{I,-I,S,-S}`. If `v=f(x,y)` is its translation
value in this finite quotient, then the action on `x^2y` gives

```
B4 e1=e1-v.
```

Consequently its four possible values and their parities are

| `B4` | `v=f(x,y)` | Gaussian parity |
|---|---|---|
| `I` | `(0,0)` | 0 |
| `-I` | `(2,0)` | 0 |
| `S` | `(1,1)` | 1 |
| `-S` | `(1,2)` | 1 |

In particular the parity is `v_2^2` in `F3`, with values in
`{0,1}` read as `F2`. This table is an evaluation formula for an
actual original-orbifold character; it is not a claim that an
arbitrary free word produces a gentle pair.

For completeness the four finite automorphisms can be constructed
without any existence assertion about full GT. Given `B` in the
displayed four-element group, fix `x` and send

```
z -> ((I-B)e1,S),       y -> (z x)^-1.
```

These maps induce `B` on the translation subgroup. The matrices
`I-S` and `I-S^2` are invertible over `F3`, so both `z` and `y`
have unique conjugating translations. The program checks the entire
36-element automorphism permutation for all four choices. This
specifies all peripheral finite actions in this determinant-one
family, with their actual frames.

Together with Section 1, the E.65 parity on multiplier-one full GT
has the exact decomposition

```
delta(F)=epsilon3(D3(F))
        +epsilon2(D4(F)) +epsilon2(B4_F).
```

Every factor is a specified continuous character on that source.
The remaining task is to evaluate their sum; the equation does not
set either discrepancy character to zero.

## 3. Actual old-source correction maps

The 97 literal free generators `n_i` of `N_B` in the frozen input
`code/bc_cm_literal_words.json` are evaluated in the two groups above.
The resulting images have orders 27 and 18 respectively. Their
derived groups have orders 3 and 9. The actual joint image of
`N_B'` is their full product of order 27: the subgroup generated by
the 4,656 literal commutators already has this order, which equals
the upper bound supplied by the two derived projections.

In particular the image of

```

        -> Gaussian translation subgroup V
```

is all of `V`. With the input's indices starting at zero, the
commutator words `[n_8,n_30]` and `[n_1,n_19]` map respectively to
`e1,e2` on the Gaussian side and to one on the Mordell side.
Their complete literal `F` words, and preimages of every translation,
are saved in the JSON. They have exactly zero integral ordinary
abelianization and lie in `N_B'`, hence preserve the actual `G6`
source value.

This proves an exact independence statement for these two specified
evaluation maps. It does not discard the other evaluations required
by the gentle equations or pentagon, and does not turn one corrected
free word into a full GT element.

## 4. Program, retained failures, and source scope

the two exact finite quotient maps, the entire Mordell sign table,
the four Gaussian finite automorphisms, and the joint normal-source
image with literal commutator preimages. It also retains all 24

whole-fibre or full-GT statement.

The initial probe before the determinant label and full-product
upper-bound check is preserved in `BC_orbifold_initial_probe/`.
The source defining the original action is the already opened
NTY III equations (4.2) and Proposition 4.4; the explicit quartic
lift and the two Ferrari bases are those proved in E.64. All new
sign conclusions above follow from the displayed group maps.
