\subsection{The exact cyclic-cover source and the simultaneous distribution overlap}

\begin{statement}{E.66}{Proved}{All E.62 final values survive the five simultaneous distribution tests}
Retain the actual group $N_B\triangleleft B=C_2*C_3$, its ordered
rank-$97$ free basis, and the $24$ words $f_i\in F'$ of E.1.
Let $C$ be the actual image of $N_B'$ in the joint E.62 source,
with its displayed ordered class-two coordinates. Thus $|C|=2^{24}$.
For $n\in\{2,3,4,6,12\}$ use the actual cyclic-cover map of D.5,
and put
\[
 \psi_n(w)=\bigl(q_F\delta_n(w),c_n(w)\bmod6\bigr),\qquad
 J=\im\left(N_B'\xrightarrow{(\psi_n)_n}
                (q_F(F)\times\Z/6)^5\right).
 \tag{E.66.1}
\]
Here $q_F(F)$ is the literal order-$96$ matrix group in E.6;
its unique signed representative modulo four has diagonal entries one.
The actual common image
\[
 Z=\im\bigl(N_B'\longrightarrow C\times J\bigr)
 \tag{E.66.2}
\]
has order $356\,241\,767\,399\,424$ and index two in $C\times J$.
It is exactly the fibre product of the following two nonzero characters:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \chi_C(c)&=c_4+c_5+c_6+c_9+c_{10}+c_{11}
                   +c_{13}+c_{15}+c_{17}\pmod2,\\
 \chi_J((h_n,a_n)_n)&=(h_3)_{21}/2\pmod2.
 \end{split}
 \tag{E.66.3}
\]
The indices in the first line are zero-based indices in E.62's
ordered binary coordinates; its six central coordinates have zero
coefficient. The second line uses the lower-left entry of the
specified modulo-four representative, which is even.

For every $i$ and every one of the four final E.62 correction
values $c$ over $f_i$, there is a single $u\in N_B'$ whose E.62
value is $c$ and for which $f_i u$ satisfies all five equations
\[
 q_F\delta_n(f_i u)=q_F(y)^{c_n(f_i u)}q_F(f_i u)
 \quad(n=2,3,4,6,12)
 \tag{E.66.4}
\]
together with
\[
 c_4=2c_2,\qquad c_6=c_2+c_3,\qquad
 c_{12}=2c_2+c_3\pmod6.
 \tag{E.66.5}
\]
For each specified correction there are exactly three coefficient
tuples in this finite target. Their $c_3$ coordinates are $0,2,4$;
their $c_2$ coordinate is the same fixed even value for the given
base point. All $96$ final E.62 values, including all values over
the extra CM coset, are retained.

There is also an exact comparison with the cyclic-three map into
$\mathrm{SL}_2(\F_3)$. Its single-coordinate image on $N_B'$ is
the whole group. Keeping all three based cyclic coordinates gives
an image of order $1536$ with derived subgroup of order $512$.
Keeping their entire six-coset $B/F$ orbit gives an image of
order $2^{27}\cdot3$ with derived subgroup of order $2^{27}$.
Both abelianizations are $C_3$, so the actual common correction
image of either one with $C$ is their full direct product.

These are exact necessary finite tests on the specified source.
They do not assert generation in an additional distribution
refinement or a full profinite GT lift, and do not evaluate
$\Delta|_{\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}}$ from E.65.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We describe the source maps before their finite certificates.
Put $H_n=\ker(F\to\Z/n)$, with $x\mapsto1$, $y\mapsto0$.
The map $\delta_n$ sends the actual free basis
$x^n,y,xyx^{-1},\ldots,x^{n-1}yx^{-(n-1)}$ to
$x,y,1,\ldots,1$. Its $x$-exponent is the original
$x$-exponent divided by $n$, and $c_n$ is its $y$-exponent.
The former is therefore exactly zero on $F'$. D.5 compares
this basis with the negative Schreier convention and proves the
full-GT distribution equation from Enriquez's theorem.

\paragraph{One actual induced source.}
Let $A=q_F(F)\times\Z/6$. For each $n$ use the section
$1,x,\ldots,x^{n-1}$ and the right wreath-product coordinates
\[
 (k;(a_j)_j)(l;(b_j)_j)
       =(k+l;(a_j b_{j+k})_j).
 \tag{E.66.6}
\]
The source images are
\[
 x\longmapsto(1;1,\ldots,1,(q_F(x),0)),\qquad
 y\longmapsto(0;(q_F(y),1),1,\ldots,1).
 \tag{E.66.7}
\]
For $w\in H_n$, coordinate $j$ is the value of $\psi_n$ on
$x^jwx^{-j}$, so coordinate zero is exactly \textup{(E.66.1)}.
Every $u\in N_B'$ belongs to every $H_n$ in question.

The signed order-$96$ group has the actual faithful action on
four points from its modulo-four group and on the eight nonzero
row vectors of $\F_3^2$ from its modulo-three matrix.
Adjoin the actions of $\Z/6$ on two and three points.
This gives a faithful $17$-point action of $A$. Right multiplication
on the four-point group and the rule $v\mapsto vh$ on row vectors
make its permutation multiplication agree with
\textup{(E.66.6)}. The five induced actions have total degree
$17(2+3+4+6+12)=459$.

The program reads exactly the $97$ original $N_B$ words used in
E.62 and forms their common block action on these $459$ points
and E.62's $4764$ points. It computes the full derived image of
this common source. Only then does it project each shift-zero
distribution factor to its coordinate-zero $17$-point block.
Thus the resulting degree-$4849$ image is literally $Z$, rather
than a product of independently chosen corrections. Its two
projections are the already specified $C$ and $J$.

Complete Schreier certificates give
\[
 |J|=42\,467\,328,\qquad |C|=16\,777\,216,
 \qquad |Z|=\frac{|C||J|}{2}.
 \tag{E.66.8}
\]
Every retained generator in the derived certificate is an actual
word in commutators and their conjugates from the original
$N_B$ basis; all final Schreier relations are checked. This proves
both containments required for the displayed image orders.

\paragraph{The common character.}
Because $Z$ is a subdirect subgroup of index two, define
$\chi_C(c)$ to be zero precisely when $(c,1)\in Z$, and define
$\chi_J(j)$ analogously. The two projection-kernel quotients
give nonzero homomorphisms to $C_2$ and
$Z=\{(c,j):\chi_C(c)=\chi_J(j)\}$. Membership of every E.62
ordered basis element gives the first line of
\textup{(E.66.3)}. All basis products are independently checked;
the character vanishes on the six central coordinates.

For the second line, modulo-four multiplication in the displayed
four-element matrix group adds its even lower-left entry after
division by two. Hence the proposed formula is a homomorphism
on the ambient group. The program decodes every strong generator
of $J$ into its five actual $A$ values and compares this formula
with $(1,j)\in Z$. They agree on every generator. Finally it
checks the two character values on every actual strong generator
of $Z$. This proves the exact formula, including its coordinate
convention; it is not inferred solely from the index.

\paragraph{All final fibres and a common word.}
For each E.62 coordinate $c$, construct its actual permutation
$p_c$ from the frozen ordered class-two basis. For a coefficient
tuple $(a_n)_n$, the needed distribution correction is the literal
tuple
\[
 d_n=\psi_n(f_i)^{-1}(q_F(y)^{a_n}q_F(f_i),a_n).
 \tag{E.66.9}

Test $(p_c,(d_n)_n)$ for membership in $Z$. Enumerating all

The complete chain also returns a word in its strong generators

\textup{(E.66.9)} and the exact E.62 coordinate. This supplies

\paragraph{The cyclic-three orbit comparison.}
Replacing $A$ by the literal $\mathrm{SL}_2(\F_3)$ target

\[

 \tag{E.66.10}
\]

\[
 |\im N_B|=13824,\quad |\im N_B'|=1536,
 \quad |\im N_B''|=512.
 \tag{E.66.11}
\]
The three matrix abelian characters have the diagonal image
$\{(a,a,a):a\in\F_3\}$. Their kernel is exactly the product

Induce this $72$-point action through the six actual cosets

\end{array}
 \tag{E.66.12}

The last two columns again give abelianization $C_3$.
No identity is imposed on a conjugate coordinate merely from

For each cyclic-three image just obtained, form its joint
image with E.62's $C$ using the same domain $N_B'$.
Any common quotient is a two-group. A nontrivial finite

\end{proof}
