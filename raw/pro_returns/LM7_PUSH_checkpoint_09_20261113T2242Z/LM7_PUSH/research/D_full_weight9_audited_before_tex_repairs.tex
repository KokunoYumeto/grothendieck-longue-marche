\subsection{The full gentle image in weight nine}

\begin{statement}{D.52}{Proved}{Task D.2 has dimension two for every prime at least five}
For every prime \(\ell\geq5\), the rational weight-nine metabelian
leading image of the full profinite gentle group is
\[
 \mathscr J_{9,\ell}
   =\mathbf Q_\ell G_9(X,Y)[x,y]
       \oplus\mathbf Q_\ell P_9(X,Y)[x,y],\qquad
 \dim_{\mathbf Q_\ell}\mathscr J_{9,\ell}=2,
 \tag{D.52.1}
\]
where
\[
 G_9=\frac{(X+Y)^9-X^9-Y^9}{XY},\qquad
 P_9=X^2Y^2(X+Y)^3.
 \tag{D.52.2}
\]
There is an actual full profinite pair
\((1,f)\in\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0\) whose image in the
free pro-\(\ell\) group has its first nonzero homogeneous term in
weight nine, with metabelian pair coordinate exactly
\(P_9[x,y]\). In the derivation convention
\(D_h(x)=0,\ D_h(y)=[h,y]\), its leading outer derivation has
coordinate \(-P_9[x,y]\). This pair does not satisfy the full
profinite pentagon. Together with D.7--D.8, this settles the
weight-nine comparison in Tasks D.2--D.3.
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We construct the local pair, lift that entire pair through the actual
source of D.50, and then apply the graded maps of D.51.

\paragraph{A literal integral invariant source.}
Use the standard Lyndon brackets \(\mathsf L(w)\) of D.51.3.
Define \(h\in L_9(\mathbf Z[1/2]x\oplus\mathbf Z[1/2]y)\)
by the following complete list of coefficients of \(2h\);
every unlisted Lyndon coefficient is zero.
\[
\begin{array}{lr|lr|lr}
 w&[\mathsf L(w)](2h)&w&[\mathsf L(w)](2h)&w&[\mathsf L(w)](2h)\\\hline
 xxxxxxyyy&2&xxxxxyxyy&-18&xxxxxyyxy&-24\\
 xxxxxyyyy&-6&xxxxyxxyy&18&xxxxyxyxy&-12\\
 xxxxyxyyy&19&xxxxyyxxy&18&xxxxyyxyy&48\\
 xxxxyyyxy&49&xxxxyyyyy&6&xxxyxxxyy&-2\\
 xxxyxxyxy&32&xxxyxxyyy&-1&xxxyxyxxy&20\\
 xxxyxyxyy&17&xxxyxyyxy&11&xxxyxyyyy&-13\\
 xxxyyxxyy&-20&xxxyyxyxy&-12&xxxyyxyyy&-43\\
 xxxyyyxxy&-20&xxxyyyxyy&-105&xxxyyyyxy&-49\\
 xxxyyyyyy&-2&xxyxxyxyy&-41&xxyxxyyxy&-72\\
 xxyxxyyyy&-17&xxyxyxxyy&59&xxyxyxyxy&-18\\
 xxyxyxyyy&-34&xxyxyyxyy&-18&xxyxyyyxy&-68\\
 xxyxyyyyy&12&xxyyxxyyy&-11&xxyyxyxyy&-51\\
 xxyyxyyyy&30&xxyyyxyxy&-10&xxyyyxyyy&40\\
 xxyyyyxyy&60&xxyyyyyxy&24&xyxyxyxyy&6
\end{array}
\tag{D.52.3}
\]
Put \(z=-x-y\) and \(k=-h(z,x)\). Expansion in the free
associative algebra gives all four exact identities
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 h(x,y)+h(y,x)&=0,\\
 [x,h(x,y)]&=[x+y,h(z,y)],\\
 [h(x,y),y]&=[k,x+y],\\
 h(x,y)+h(y,z)+h(z,x)&=0.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{D.52.4}
\]
These are identities over \(\mathbf Z[1/2]\), before reduction
at any prime. The independent certificate listed after the proof
checks every associative coefficient of each identity and retains
the complete Lyndon expansion of \(k\).
The actual quotient by the second derived Lie ideal gives
\[
 h\longmapsto
  (X^5Y^2+3X^4Y^3+3X^3Y^4+X^2Y^5)[x,y]
               =P_9(X,Y)[x,y].
 \tag{D.52.5}
\]
In particular \(h\ne0\), and \(h,k\) are integral over
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\) for every \(\ell\geq5\).

We spell out the outer invariance, rather than identifying the
special equation with invariance. Let \(D_h(x)=0\) and
\(D_h(y)=[h,y]\). The third identity of \textup{(D.52.4)}
makes this a special derivation. On the graded free Lie algebra,
the two actual geometric generators of \(\mathfrak S_3\) act by
\(s(x,y)=(y,x)\) and \(r(x,y)=(y,z)\).
The first and second identities of \textup{(D.52.4)} give the
literal derivation comparisons
\[
 sD_hs^{-1}-D_h=-\operatorname{ad}(h),\qquad
 rD_hr^{-1}-D_h=-\operatorname{ad}(h).
 \tag{D.52.6}
\]
For example the second left-hand side has values
\(-[h(y,z),z]\) at \(x\) and \(-[h(x,y),y]\) at
\(y\); the cabling identity identifies the first value with
\(-[h(x,y),x]\). Thus \([D_h]\), and also \(-[D_h]\),
are fixed in the actual quotient by inner derivations.

\paragraph{The actual pro-\(\ell\) pair and its leading term.}
Let \(P_\ell\) be the free pro-\(\ell\) group on the same
two generators. The integral construction in D.44.32 first
realizes \(-[D_h]\) in the leading quotient of the IA special
outer group. That construction is onto: at each later degree
the error map is \((u,v)\mapsto[u,y]+[v,-x-y]\), with image
the whole integral free-Lie lattice of that degree. The IA group
and its closed filtration kernels are pro-\(\ell\).
The exact coprime fixed-point lifting argument of D.44 therefore
applies to the geometric group of order six and
\textup{(D.52.6)}. It gives an actual special outer automorphism
\(\beta_\ell\) centralizing that \(\mathfrak S_3\), lying
in \(I^9\), and having leading class exactly \(-[D_h]\).

Apply the centreless pullback isomorphism D.44.28 and its
normalization D.44.29--30. This produces an actual automorphism
\(\phi_\ell\) of
\[
 B_\ell=\mathcal B/O^\ell(F),\qquad
 \mathcal B=\langle s,r\mid s^2=r^3=1\rangle^{\wedge},
 \quad F=\ker(\mathcal B\to\mathfrak S_3),
 \tag{D.52.7}
\]
fixing \(\tau=sr\) literally, with a unique normalized pair
\(f_\ell\in P_\ell'\). Its restriction to the actual pure
kernel, in the basis \(x=\tau^{-2}\), \(y=sxs\), is
\[
              \psi_{f_\ell}(x)=x,qquad
              \psi_{f_\ell}(y)=f_\ell^{-1}yf_\ell.
 \tag{D.52.8}
\]

We verify its filtration and coefficient without choosing an
unrelated representative of \(\beta_\ell\). For every
\(r\geq2\) the centralizer of \(x\) in
\(P_\ell/\gamma_{r+1}P_\ell\) is
\[
       \langle x\rangle\gamma_rP_\ell/\gamma_{r+1}P_\ell,
 \tag{D.52.9}
\]
and the corresponding formula holds for \(y\). To prove it,
the degree-two commutator first makes the abelian image a
multiple of \(x\). Remove that power. Any first remaining
nonzero homogeneous term of degree \(j<r\) would commute
with \(x\) in the integral free Lie algebra. In the free
associative algebra a homogeneous tensor commuting with \(x\)
is a multiple of \(x^j\): comparison of the first and last
letters proves this successively. For \(j\geq2\), every
ordinary Lie monomial has zero coefficient at \(x^j\).
The integral Magnus injection therefore rules out that term.
This proves \textup{(D.52.9)} degree by degree over
\(\mathbf Z_\ell\).

The class \(\beta_\ell\in I^9\) makes
\(\psi_{f_\ell}\) inner modulo \(\gamma_{10}\), say
\(\operatorname{Inn}(w)\). Since it fixes \(x\),
\textup{(D.52.9)} gives \(w\in\langle x\rangle\gamma_9\)
in that quotient. Its value at \(y\) gives
\(f_\ell w\in\langle y\rangle\gamma_9\).
The abelianization of \(f_\ell\) is zero, so the two powers
are both zero. Consequently \(f_\ell\in\gamma_9P_\ell\).
Write \(h_f\) for its image in
\(\gamma_9P_\ell/\gamma_{10}P_\ell=L_9(\mathbf Z_\ell^2)\).
The exact group-to-Lie comparison of \textup{(D.52.8)} is
\[
     \psi_{f_\ell}(y)y^{-1}
             \longmapsto [y,h_f]=-[h_f,y].
 \tag{D.52.10}
\]
Thus its leading derivation is \(-D_{h_f}\), and its outer
class is the prescribed \(-[D_h]\). The difference of these
two normalized derivations is inner and has zero value at
\(x\). The same free-Lie centralizer argument in degree nine
makes that inner derivation zero. Its value at \(y\) then
implies \(h_f-h=0\). This proves that the literal pair
coefficient is \(+h\), with no terms of smaller weight.

\paragraph{The full profinite source and every retained coordinate.}
The source comparison in D.50.20--21 is the actual isomorphism
\[
 \mathcal B/R_{\rm nil}(F)
       \simeq\prod_{p,\mathfrak S_3}\mathcal B/O^p(F).
 \tag{D.52.11}
\]
Put \(\phi_\ell\) in its \(\ell\)-component and the
identity in every other component. All these maps induce the
same identity on \(\mathfrak S_3\). The positive
\(s\)- and \(r\)-conjugators at \(\ell\) can be chosen
in \(P_\ell\): the old and new cyclic subgroups are
complements to a pro-\(\ell\) kernel, and their orders two
and three are prime to \(\ell\). Coprime complement
conjugacy supplies precisely these kernel conjugators.
Together with identity components elsewhere they are actual
elements of \textup{(D.52.11)}. The \(\tau\)-conjugator is
one. This verifies every peripheral hypothesis of D.50 on
the displayed pronilpotent source.

D.50 gives an actual automorphism \(\Phi\) of
\(\mathcal B\), fixing \(\tau\), and a full pair
\(f=s\Phi(s)\in F'\), with the exact commuting square
\[
 \begin{array}{ccc}
 F&\xrightarrow{\Phi|_F}&F\\
 q_\ell\downarrow&&\downarrow q_\ell\\
 P_\ell&\xrightarrow{\psi_{f_\ell}}&P_\ell,
 \end{array}
 \qquad q_\ell(f)=f_\ell.
 \tag{D.52.12}
\]
Here (F) is the actual free profinite pure subgroup of
\(\mathcal B\), with the displayed basis. The same word
calculation as D.44 gives, literally in that group,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  \Phi(s)&=sf,&\Phi(r)&=f^{-1}r,\\
  \Phi(x)&=x,&\Phi(y)&=f^{-1}yf,\\
  f(y,x)f(x,y)&=1,&
  f(z,x)f(y,z)f(x,y)&=1,
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{D.52.13}
\]
where in the last line \(z=(xy)^{-1}\) is a group element.
In the Lie identities \textup{(D.52.4)}, its graded image
was \(-x-y\). The two equations and the actual automorphism
of the free pure group are exactly the multiplier-one gentle
pair conditions under the source comparison of D.44.
Consequently \((1,f)\) belongs to the full group
\(\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}_0\).

Apply to \textup{(D.52.12)} the characteristic lower central
quotients, the integral Magnus graded isomorphism D.51.2,
and the actual quotient by the second derived Lie ideal.
Equations \textup{(D.52.5)} and \textup{(D.52.10)} give
the pair coordinate \(+P_9[x,y]\) and the derivation
coordinate \(-P_9[x,y]\), respectively. Thus the local
coordinate is retained by an actual full profinite source,
with its stated sign. One can also place \(f\) in the
full closed subgroup \(\gamma_9F\): its image in
\(\prod_pP_p\) is \(f_\ell\) at \(\ell\) and one
elsewhere, and hence lies in
\(\prod_p\gamma_9P_p=\gamma_9(\prod_pP_p)\).
The equality follows by projection to finite products and
closedness. Since \(R_{\rm nil}(F)\subset\gamma_9F\),
the inverse image of this subgroup is exactly \(\gamma_9F\).
This additional observation does not change the prescribed
pro-\(\ell\) filtration or its coordinate.

\paragraph{The exact dimension and the pentagon map.}
The actual full gentle source induces the geometric
\(\mathfrak S_3\)-centralizing special outer action by
D.44.22 and D.50. The characteristic projection and leading
map used above therefore place its weight-nine image inside
the two-dimensional invariant metabelian space computed in
D.4 and, independently with an integer certificate, in D.8.
This proves the upper bound in \textup{(D.52.1)}.
The actual Galois-to-full-GT map and the same graded and Fox
coordinate maps in D.20 give the nonzero line
\(\mathbf Q_\ell G_9[x,y]\) inside the full gentle image.
Our full pair supplies the other direction. These directions
are independent over every \(\mathbf Q_\ell\): the
coefficient of \(X^7\) in \(G_9\) is nine, whereas it is
zero in \(P_9\), and the coefficient of \(X^5Y^2\) in
\(P_9\) is one. This proves both the asserted direct sum
and its dimension.

Finally, the full profinite pentagon maps under the same
characteristic pro-\(\ell\) projection, rational unipotent
completion, and braid insertion maps to the infinitesimal
pentagon in degree nine. D.8 proves that its complete kernel
has metabelian image exactly \(\mathbf Q_\ell G_9[x,y]\).
The coefficient just constructed lies outside that kernel
image, so this actual full gentle pair cannot satisfy the
full pentagon. The finite comparison D.7 retains the entire
weight-nine stratum and all degree-ten corrections, and
D.8 identifies its leading projection with this same
pentagon kernel. Thus the finite and full comparisons use
the same source, filtration, and quotient maps.
\end{proof}
