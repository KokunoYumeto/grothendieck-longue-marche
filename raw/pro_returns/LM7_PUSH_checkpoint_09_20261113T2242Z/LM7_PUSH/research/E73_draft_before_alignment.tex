\subsection{The actual double-cover and matrix source at the full boundary kernel}

\begin{statement}{E.73}{Proved}{The exact E.62 overlap and the whole deep correction image}
Retain the maps $\rho_{J_2}:\widehat B\to J_2$ and
$J_2\to J$ of E.63, the perfect subgroup $C_{J_2}$,
and the E.62 common matrix source map
$\rho_{62}:\widehat B\to G_{62}$. Put
\[
 C_{62}=\rho_{62}(N'),\qquad
 \eta=(\rho_{J_2},\rho_{62}),\qquad
 I=\gamma_5\widehat N\cap R_{\rm nil}(H),
 \quad N=N_B.
 \tag{E.73.1}
\]
All maps have their original based generators. In particular
$|C_{62}|=2^{24}$, with the ordered class-two coordinates of
E.62. The actual maximum common quotient of $J_2$ and $G_{62}$

\[
                         2^{17}3^6=95551488.
 \tag{E.73.2}
\]
The kernel of the actual joint source over $J$ and $G_{62}$
is precisely the $122$-dimensional perfect-bridge kernel of
E.63. It has zero image in the three-dimensional
$\mathcal E_2^{\rm ab}$ of that statement.

Write $P'/C=V_6\times W$ for the actual E.54 quotient,
where $V_6\simeq\mathbf F_3^6$ and $W\simeq\mathbf F_2^6$.
There is an actual subgroup $C_*=C_{J_2}\times1$ in

\[
 \begin{aligned}

 \{((v,\alpha(c)),c):v\in V_6,\ c\in C_{62}\}\

 \end{aligned}
 \tag{E.73.3}
\]
Here $\alpha:C_{62}\twoheadrightarrow W$ is the following
explicit homomorphism. Code the E.62 ordered coordinates by
$u=\sum_{j=0}^{23}u_j2^j$, with the first eighteen coordinates
the quotient basis and the last six the derived center.

 \begin{split}

 \tag{E.73.4}

In the finite group $\rho_{62}(H)$ define

 \tag{E.73.5}

The actual subgroup

 \tag{E.73.7}

 \tag{E.73.8}

These are the exact common-source and boundary-kernel maps.
The assertion leaves the evaluation of additional prescribed

\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
We identify the finite common source, descend through its actual
perfect subgroup on $N'$, and prove both inclusions for the image
of the closed subgroup $I$.

\paragraph{The complete finite common source.}
Adjoin the entire $4764$-point E.62 action to the accepted
$11010$-point common $A_6/A_5$ base, always evaluating the same
two literal generators $s,r$. The resulting degree is $15774$.
The complete Schreier calculation retains $407$ strong generators,
$13526$ word nodes and all $1279851$ final relations. Above this
base, evaluate every relation in both full lifted Ferrari models.
The relative kernel is an elementary binary subgroup of the
actual $128+42$-dimensional coordinate product, of dimension
$163$. The dimensions of its two projections are $125$ and $41$.

An independent replay evaluates every one of the $163$ basis
words in both full models and on all $15774$ base points.
It checks all $326$ original-generator conjugates. Setting the
signed coordinates to zero leaves dimension $163-41=122$.
The exact binary inclusion matrices identify this whole subspace
with the perfect-bridge kernel of E.63; concatenating their
bases has rank $122$. Its image under the already retained
$\mathcal E_2^{\rm ab}$ matrix is zero. All these are coordinate
maps of the same words.

The orders of the complete base and its $163$-dimensional kernel
give the order of $\eta(\widehat B)$. Division of
$|J_2||G_{62}|$ by that order gives exactly (E.73.2), which is
the order of Goursat's actual common quotient. The calculation
therefore retains the additional $A_5$ source blocks throughout;
no factorization of those blocks through $G$ is assumed.

\paragraph{The precise descent on $N'$.}
Let $M=\eta(N')$. Its first projection is the entire E.63
image of $N'$, and its second projection is $C_{62}$.
Goursat's common quotient is solvable, since it is a quotient
of the finite two-group $C_{62}$. The image of the perfect
normal subgroup $C_{J_2}$ in that quotient is trivial: its
successive derived images are all equal, whereas the derived
series of a solvable group terminates. Thus
$C_{J_2}\times1\subset M$.

Consider the actual homomorphism
\[
 M\longrightarrow (P'/C)\times C_{62},
 \qquad (j_2,c)\longmapsto(\rho_G(j_2)\bmod C,c).
 \tag{E.73.9}
\]
Its kernel contains $C_*$. For an element of its kernel,
the old $J$ value belongs to $C_J$: this is exactly the
$N'$ comparison (E.54.17)--(E.54.18). The surjection
$C_{J_2}\to C_J$ lets us remove that value by an element
of $C_*$. The remaining element is the identity in both
old $J$ and E.62. The complete $122$-kernel computation
above places that remaining element in $C_*$ as well.
Hence the kernel of (E.73.9) is exactly $C_*$. This proves
the required injective descent on the stated domain.

To identify its image, evaluate all $97$ original $N$ words
in the accepted $G/C$ collection and in the full E.62 action.
All $4656$ pairwise commutators are retained. Their ternary
values span exactly $V_6$, whose action on every element of
$W$ is checked to be the identity. The E.62 correction group
has exponent four. The actual ninth power of each commutator
therefore keeps precisely its binary value, and its fourth
power keeps precisely its ternary value. These source powers
prove the direct product assertion between the two primes.

For the binary calculation use the E.62 class-two product
with its eighteen quotient coordinates and six central
coordinates, together with the six coordinates of $W$.
Triangular subgroup collection retains every square carry
and every commutator carry. Each new quotient basis element
is accompanied by its square and all commutators with the
previous basis. Close under the conjugations of all $97$
actual $N$ generators. The resulting group has order
$2^{24}$ and projects onto the entire $C_{62}$, also of
order $2^{24}$. That actual projection is an isomorphism,
so its inverse followed by the $W$ projection defines
$\alpha$.

Solving on the saved ordered basis gives precisely the six
masks (E.73.4). Their central columns vanish, and all $576$
ordered basis-product checks retain the full E.62 carries.
The masks agree on all $4656$ original commutators. The
complete normal-generation argument proves (E.73.3), with
its specified source map.

\paragraph{The stable lower image and its actual source.}
Apply the same exact collection to successive commutators
with the $97$ original $N$ generators. The binary order is
$2^{24}$ for $N'$, $\gamma_3N$, $\gamma_4N$ and

 \tag{E.73.10}
\]

\paragraph{The matching whole-$H$ upper bound.}

 \begin{array}{c|ccccc}

\[
 |R\cap C_{62}|=
 \frac{(2^{15}3^3)2^{24}}{2^{24}3^3}=2^{15}.

Every basis element of the stable lower image is
