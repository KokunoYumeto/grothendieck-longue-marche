# Root mathematical checks — provisional pending the corresponding ink decisions

These are local checks using the eight-page draft readings and their stated data. They do not authorize changing any ink reading. No external literature has been consulted. The attached sweep was consulted only as a lead; its results are not adopted as certificates.

## At 6, scan 006 (S row SA21-K7-01)
If the ink hypothesis is X incident to F, then X is a stratum of F. Reflexivity of refinement gives X in F^X. For Y in F^X, X and Y share the upper bound F, hence are compatible; At 5 gives X <= Y. Thus X itself is already the least element of F^X. This verifies the sweep's observation that this hypothesis makes the intended existence assertion automatic. The nontrivial intended version needs X in M, F in the figure set and X << F; merely changing triangleleft to << in a quantifier allowing every X in the figure set also loses the explicit multistratum restriction. Record that qualification, not a silent correction.

The definition saying X is least in tilde(Y)^X should mathematically say Y: its following condition is X << Y' incident to Y implies Y'=Y. If ink confirms X, preserve X and note the local inconsistency.

## Minimal versus least, scan 007 At_M 4(a,b)
Under the page's interpretation of internal refinement, a minimal Z in tilde(Y)^X satisfies X <<° Z. Two such minima are compatible because both lie under Y, so (b) identifies them. For arbitrary W in tilde(Y)^X, apply (a) below W to obtain a minimal Z_W there; Z_W is internally refined by X and compatible with Z, so (b) gives Z_W=Z. Hence Z <= W. Thus (a,b) together imply a least element; the word minimal does not need emendation to minimum.

## Disjointness by strata, scan 008
Subject to At 2 and At 3 and the nonempty-stratum meaning of strict irreducibility: F parallel G implies every stratum pair is compatible (bounded by F vee G) and has no common stratum. Conversely, all stratum pairs disjoint gives compatibility of F,G by At 3. Any nonempty meet would have a stratum by At 2; that stratum would be disjoint from itself, impossible. This checks the displayed stratum criterion, not the proposed extra axioms.

## Margin argument, scan 009
Conditional on the preceding meet/refinement rules, a common stratum Z <= X',Y' refines both X and Y and hence L=X cap Y. Since Z <= X' and X' is disjoint from L, Z is disjoint from L. At 4 supplies a stratum W of L with Z << W; compatibility and At 5 give Z <= W <= L, contradicting disjointness. The marginal compatibility conclusion remains an additional premise; this calculation only verifies why compatibility would force empty intersection under the displayed hypotheses.
