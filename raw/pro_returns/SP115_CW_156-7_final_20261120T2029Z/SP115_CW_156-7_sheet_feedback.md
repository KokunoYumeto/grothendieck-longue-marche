## Recurrences and qualifications

- Open C-like letters need local comparators. The clear C forms in004–005 are retained even though a G would make the formula coherent. Small c/G forms remain doubtful. This belongs to the sheet’s existing symbol/slip category, not a new rule that all Cs are Gs.
- A revision can cross an internal letter group within a word. The heading on004 illustrates why the deletion boundary must be read independently of the likely completed word.
- H’s editorial descriptions can identify a source correction but still omit its literal text or render its state incorrectly. Editorial notes themselves are excluded; the ink is transcribed.
- Detached paired quotation ticks are often converted into guillemets, and underlining can be extended or omitted. These are source-state differences, unlike an equivalent emph/uline macro choice.
- A map-like shaft may have transverse strokes at both ends without an arrowhead (005 Scholie). Both ends must be read independently.
- No new general error category is established by this small eight-page sample. The recurrent problems fit the supplied categories; the examples above refine their application.
