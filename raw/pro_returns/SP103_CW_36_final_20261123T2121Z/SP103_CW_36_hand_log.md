# SP103_CW_36 — Hand and typewriter log

These observations are grounded in the listed scans. “His hand” identifies the correcting hand conventionally attributed to Grothendieck in this packet; handwriting attribution itself has not been independently established. A habit is never authority for an unread occurrence.
