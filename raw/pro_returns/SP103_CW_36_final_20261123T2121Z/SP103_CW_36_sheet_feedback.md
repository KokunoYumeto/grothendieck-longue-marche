## Recurring issues and refinements

- **Different layers of correction need separate readings.** Scan018’s struck consequence contains insertions that were themselves struck; scans020–021 have inserted phrases crossed by later strokes. The old wording, replacement wording and attachment must be checked separately. A single “deleted paragraph” flag conceals these layers.
- **Adjacent type can create a false familiar word.** Retained examples include ponsidéré (016), coéfficients (018), estchoisieassez (022), femmés and ppouver (023), literal da, resolved in the blind audit (025), and parti s (026). These are not normalized to the language expected by the argument.
- **A corrected letter can coexist with its predecessor.** Examples include the t/s point labels (019,021,023–024), the two overtyped subscript numerals (026), and the swapped letters in automorphismes and several predecessors in avec (027). An alternative a|b can under-record a visible a-to-b correction; chronology remains doubtful where both orders fit.
- **An isolated x is not automatically a deletion.** Scan026’s x after p-linéaire has no separate cancelling stroke or visible old letter, so it is transcribed live. Other isolated x-like remnants, such as the one after the cardinality formula on021, remain uncertain. A typist’s possible intention is not established by its shape alone.
- **Labels and marginal punctuation are text.** The exclamation beside7.5 and words inside ovals remain ordinary text. Only their enclosing or attaching strokes use TikZ. A text-bearing TikZ node would lose the reading and is not used.
- **The precise extent of a bar matters.** The full k(t) and k(s) closure bars on026, the local underline under the family Y on019/023, and the absence of an underline under the first displayed Q on021 are distinct observations. A global style preference cannot settle them.
- **A margin’s orientation can hide a logical qualification.** The “pas prouvé” and “forme forte” notes on018/020 and the long framed Bertini note on020 materially qualify the surrounding argument. Both words and attachment are preserved.
- **The page edge is evidence of loss, not a request to complete text.** Examples include the first formula and fondame/tal join on021, the final E-expression on022, a cancelled point label on024, and the final group subscript on026.
- **Formula errors can repeat without becoming a convention.** The ≥ bounds on024/026, the repeated prime inequality on020, and the product zero-set equality on025 remain written slips. Their mathematical implications belong in the notes and check events.
- **A literal symbol can play two roles.** On025 the same φ-shaped glyph is used for a form and the empty set. The latter is entered once as “φ : l’ensemble vide”; it is not modernized to a different empty-set glyph.

These refinements concern the same broad error-sheet classes rather than a new statistical sample. The per-class table above assigns one primary category to each recorded place to avoid counting a single correction several times. Independent blind-pass changes are added to the same documentary records and their two readings remain available in second/.
