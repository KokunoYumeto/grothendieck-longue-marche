# Source-marked uncertainty, expectations and verification cues

This register was checked against all 44 frozen page files. It records the source's actual questions, qualifications, admitted omissions, requested checks and abbreviated proof cues; the **cue type** specifies what the words do on that page. An ordinary “il suffit,” “on voit,” or “reste à prouver” is recorded together with the argument that follows, so an intermediate proof step is not automatically labelled an open problem. Bracketed **[doubtful reading]** reports uncertainty in our reading of the quoted ink, while the mathematical question or qualification is the author's own.

The **voice** column identifies the printed Jouanolou statements separately from the handwritten annotations and working notes; the author of the reused idèle and English typescripts is not identified by the retained leaves. The last column reports the scope of our research comparison, separately from the historical statement. References **A**, **O** and **E** point to the numbered algebra, offprint and exponential research notes, with their exact context searches and source locators; a local calculation in those notes does not change the status originally recorded in `RESULTS_STATE.md`.
