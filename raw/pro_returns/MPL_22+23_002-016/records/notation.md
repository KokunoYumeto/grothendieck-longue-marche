# Reading record

The principal transcriber personally viewed all 132 supplied images, whole page followed by both supplied halves in the required order, without OCR and without delegating a page. All 44 sheets were then compared against their transcriptions in a separate visual audit. Enlarged and rotated crops were used for the least legible marginal material. The context archive's member names were inadvertently listed during preparation; its contents remained closed until after the recorded transcription freeze.

The least trusted passages are the tightly superimposed cancellations and diagonal notes on 22/020–021 and 027–029, the crowded lower half of 23/011, the interlinear tensor expression on 23/012, and the rotated inner margin on 23/013. Printed sheets are generally clearer, with local damaged type and edge clipping, particularly 22/028; the faint repeated symbols on 23/007 and the raised differential-module strokes on 23/016 remain marked. Doubt markers record uncertainty at a location; unmarked passages still carry ordinary human transcription risk.
