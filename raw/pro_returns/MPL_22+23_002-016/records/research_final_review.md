## Mathematical review and resolved precision points

The review checked the explicit calculations and comparison maps in E01–E23. In particular, the square-zero logarithm test in E08, the cubic-parameter reduced-coproduct computation in E13, the finite real-function algebra calculation in E14, the commuting-operator exponential proof in E17, the uniform nilpotence argument in E18, the explicit differential and quotient maps in E19–E20, and the square-zero group and commutator calculations in E21–E22 are consistent as written with their stated scope.

For E23, the two reduced-coproduct columns are `(-2,-1,-1,1)` and `(1,-1,-1,-2)` in the displayed basis. Substitution into the stated map `L(a,b,c,d)=-(a+b)e_1/3+(a-2b)e_2/3` gives `Lδ(e_1)=e_1` and `Lδ(e_2)=e_2`. Tensoring and composing this explicit left inverse verifies injectivity at every reduced-coproduct iteration. Together with the degree-dropping requirement of Cartier's filtration, this supplies the announced obstruction to exhaustion for that concrete algebra. The root reviewer, rather than this ancillary reviewer, visually checked Cartier's printed definition and Lemma 2 at pp. 2-01–2-02.
