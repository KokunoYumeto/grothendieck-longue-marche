## Additional recurring observations

See the hand log for observed letterforms and notation, including the scope of each observation. No unobserved cross-cote habit is assumed.

- **Crossing baselines can create words that are not independently present.** In 147_scan008, the slanted margin intersects the main text. The apparent strokes behind supplied “se placer” and “remplacer” follow the main-text baselines rather than separate marginal words. A rectangular crop must be read with both writing directions in view; lexical plausibility does not establish a second word.
- **Descenders can resemble underlines.** In 147_scan004, the strokes below “singuliers” and “analogue” continue from their g forms. They do not establish independent underlining. Conversely, the separate long strokes in the section headings do.
- **Insertion connectors can resemble punctuation or mathematical signs.** In 147_scan007, the hook after the raised “sur S” is part of its attachment, not a separate comma. In 145_scan038, the raised “(donc” and its connector belong to the start of the complete three-line condition. In 147_scan010, the connector places the raised quoted words inside the parenthesis before “quadratiques”.
- **A heading can migrate to a neighboring witness file.** H places the heading visible on 147_scan004 at the end of its transcription for 147_scan003. The collation records one placement discrepancy on the destination sheet, rather than counting both a global omission and invented text.
- **Reduced a and à can look like points.** The compact prepositional à on 147_scan004 is sometimes a thick low mark beneath its accent; 147_scan014 has the same local shape. In the raised “alg.” on 147_scan014, the small block before lg is the a, not an additional point after k. These observations do not license treating every pair of dots as à.
- **Exit strokes can change the apparent alphabet of a diagram label.** The origin-map o in 147_scan008 and the four occurrences in 147_scan009 has a closed loop and short curved exit. Its comparison with the angular sigma under a straighter top bar on those sheets supports a consistent o reading. Every labeled arrow still requires its own inspection.
- **Faint footer pagination has an unestablished hand.** The small, light lower-left numbers are transcribed and inventoried separately from the darker upper pagination. Their location alone does not establish authorship.
