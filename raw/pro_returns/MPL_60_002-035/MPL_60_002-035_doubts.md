## Legibility and limits

All 34 whole-page images and all 68 supplied halves were personally inspected in a single sequential transcription stream; no OCR was used on them and no page was assigned to another transcriber. Every page then received a separate second visual reading. Crops and enlargement were used for cramped details. The transcription was completed and its notation reconciled before the supplied context was opened.

The sixteen reused typed faces are generally clear, including their awkward wording, incomplete references and page-edge continuations. The seventeen handwritten text pages combine clear large formulae with highly compressed cursive, overwriting and occasional page-edge loss. The cover is legible. A doubt macro is not a mathematical correction, and a confidently visible oddity is left literal. The two halves overlap; repeated content in that overlap is transcribed once.

Trust is lowest in the tiny insertion on scan 013; the multiplicity letters and abbreviated last sentence on 017; overwritten/cropped terms on 019; the NB insertion on 021; the cancelled formulas and short hypothesis on 025; the bottom notation on 027; the cancelled reciprocal factor on 029; the tiny superscript on A on 031; and the inserted support expression, high star/low y, short right-hand phrase and overwritten bottom twist on 035. Some cancellations admit only a best symbol-by-symbol guess; those guesses remain marked. The normalized ξ/ζ letter cannot be resolved securely and is marked consistently throughout.

TikZ reconstructs the visible doodles, outlines and connector shapes. It is an approximation of hand-drawn geometry, not a facsimile. No raster image is embedded. Scan 035's enclosing contour and its labels were brought together in one TikZ figure during the audit. The small curved marks on scan 005 may be pen marks or edge effects; they are included conservatively. Clear tears, holes, stains and reversed blue show-through are not treated as live writing; the I-like left-margin mark on scan 025 was enlarged and identified as a paper defect.

The pencil numerals 2–34 are recorded with the requested mspage macro, but these images do not establish whose hand added that foliation. Typed headers are kept separately from it. No page-count statement has been inferred from a numeral. The fragment has no preamble or macro definitions, as requested. Structural checks were performed; a full TeX compile was unavailable because the installed engine configuration lacked the needed class/package files. In particular, the deliverable does not claim pixel-level rendered validation.
