# Notation table

Established from the scans alone by the single transcriber. All 34 pages received a second visual reading before this table was finalized and before the supplied context was opened. Differences listed below were compared against the ink; a difference in the source is retained rather than normalized away. A vertical bar inside a doubt macro separates the preferred reading from its alternative.

| Recurring ink / use | LaTeX encoding | Comparison and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Endomorphism with descending loop | `f` | 003–009; distinguished from the Greek weight letter. |
| Characteristic polynomial, reciprocal series, later L notation | `P_f(t)`, `Z_f(t)`, `L_f(t)` | 003, 005, 007; the change from Z to L is in the source. |
| Lower-case lambda exterior operation | `\lambda^i` | 003–005; not replaced by a wedge. |
| Two-legged exterior-power sign | `\bigwedge^n` | 009 and typed algebra sheets, especially 032/034; a genuinely different visible sign. |

| Tensor-series star and ordinary multiplication | `*`, `\cdot` | 003–009; not interchanged. |

| Contragrédient/check accent | `\check f`, `\check E` | 007, 009, 013, 015, 017, 025, 033; not a prime. |
