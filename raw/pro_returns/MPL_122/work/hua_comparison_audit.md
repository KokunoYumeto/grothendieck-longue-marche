## Supported modern descriptions and genuinely additional interpretations

1. **Open-book model is assumed, not established.** Hua, “La construction” (lines 374–434), is supported by the assumed fibrations and trivializations at F013:L13–41 and their gluing at F015:L2–28; this supports the modern open-book description in the circle/disk specialization. The hypotheses do not prove a Milnor fibration for a specified function germ. Hua's cusp equation and trefoil illustration in “Résumé” (lines 83–90) are absent from the frozen manuscript, as are the name Milnor, a Picard–Lefschetz formula, and a theorem on quasi-unipotence.

2. **The complement correction is explicit editorial work.** Hua, “Une variété coupée en deux (pages 6 à 10)” (lines 273–283), replaces F007:L6 `Q=P−P°` by the closure of W−P, matching the later equations W=P∪Q and common boundary. The frozen literal expression must remain, with the intended complementary-piece reading stated only as an interpretation.

3. **Collar reasoning is supplied.** Hua, “La construction” (lines 391–405), separates the boundary-trivialization hypothesis from the homotopy equivalence interior→manifold. F015:L8–12 connects them verbally, while F003 and F005 already explore collars; this is a sound explanatory distinction under ordinary collared-manifold hypotheses, not a new manuscript proof.

4. **Finite coefficients and compactness are added hypotheses.** Hua, “Le dossier, sa charpente et ses notations” (lines 197–211), assumes a field or self-injective coefficient ring so that dual cohomology is the dual of cohomology; “Variétés à bord et dualité (pages 2 à 4)” (lines 237–244) supplies compactness when identifying relative and compactly supported cohomology. The manuscript's perfectness statement F022:L38–40 alone does not justify naive cohomology duals over every coefficient ring: over Z, Ext terms may occur.

5. **The equivariant class correction is substantive and supported as a question.** Hua, “L'application $u$” (lines 616–629), puts u in H^{2n}(π,K⊗^L K), while F022:L26 declares a morphism in D(Λ[π]) and L30 displays only H^{2n}(K⊗^L K), after a cancelled RΓ term. For K perfect over Λ, the equivariant mapping complex is RΓ(π,K⊗^L K); the displayed ordinary class is only the image after forgetting equivariance and does not specify its equivariant lift.

6. **The extra circle shift is a valid supplied calculation.** Hua, “Les triangles (c') et (b')” (lines 661–672), supplies RΓ^π(K∨)≃RΓ^π(K)∨[−1] for π=Z with appropriate finite perfect complexes and dual action. This accounts for the change from −2n at F022:L22 to −2n−1 at F024:L19; the manuscript itself explicitly asks to verify this at F024:L22. It is not ordinary unshifted commutation of invariants and duality.

7. **Tate-twist caution.** Hua, “Les triangles de la fibre de Milnor (pages 19 à 21)” (lines 516–525), supplies L(−1)[−1] in the tame étale interpretation and records L(−1)[−2] for purity. Under the conventions K=RΓ(M,Λ) and K∨=RHom_Λ(K,Λ), for a smooth complex-dimension-n étale object with dualizing complex Λ(n)[2n], the matching formula is RΓ_c(M,Λ)≃K∨(−n)[−2n]; Hua's isolated “une torsion (n)” in “L'application $u$”, line 603, therefore needs a convention/sign check before reuse. This is an algebraic derivation conditional on that dualizing-complex convention, not a claim that the manuscript states an étale theorem.

8. **Transposition sign remains open in the witness.** Hua, “L'application $u$” (lines 631–642), correctly leaves the sign of ρ′ versus the transpose of ρ dependent on triangle-rotation conventions. F022:L42 expressly writes “signe ?”; the standard middle-degree sign (−1)^n under an oriented manifold convention is additional explanatory mathematics, not an explicit sign computation on the page.

9. **The two pairing degrees agree.** Hua, “Les données nouvelles” (lines 682–706), matches the revised exponents of F024:L37–48: λ pairs degree 2n−i with i and yields H^i(W−W0)→H^{i−1}(W0), whereas μ pairs degree 2n−1−i with i and yields H^i(W−W0)→H^i(W0). The witness nevertheless phrases the identifications as “je dis que c'est β/α”; an exact chain-level sign check is not present.

10. **Gysin and sphere criterion require standing geometry.** Hua, “La suite de Gysin” (lines 747–789), agrees with F026:L18–25 and F028:L2–20, while expressly supplying closedness, connectedness and orientation of W to obtain the converse. The isolated critical degree is 2n, and connectedness of W0 identifies its top cohomology with Λ; without those hypotheses, the short form of the criterion should not be quoted as unconditional.

11. **Lefschetz bounds are interpreted, not independently proved.** Hua, “Lefschetz local” (lines 791–821), takes the lower bound n−2 and dual upper bound n+1. These are dimensionally consistent with duality on W0 of dimension 2n−1 and with F028:L27–38, but F028 preserves numeral uncertainty. Hua's identifications with Milnor, SGA 2, SGA 4, and Gabber are external historical/theorem comparisons, not citations found in the manuscript and not checked here.

12. **Vanishing does not follow from a single mapping torus.** Hua, “Les annulations” (lines 851–869), correctly explains that H^i(P)=0 above n+1 only makes T−1 bijective on higher fibre cohomology via the Wang sequence; it need not annihilate that cohomology. F030:L26 specifically says “par passage à la limite”, which may intend a tower of cyclic covers or another limit and must not be reduced to a single mapping torus without investigation. Hua's affine/Stein theorems are extra hypotheses and external comparisons, not a proof supplied by the phrase alone.

13. **The long-exact-sequence successor index is −1.** Under the triangle K∨[−2n]→K→L, the term after H^i(L) is H^{i+1}(K∨[−2n])=H^{2n−i−1}(K)∨ when the simple dual-cohomology identification applies. Hua, “La suite exacte des degrés du milieu” (lines 873–877), uses this index; F030:L33 keeps the physical reading +1 or −1. This mathematical index audit belongs in notes only.

14. **The retained “surj” is restrictive.** Hua, “La suite exacte des degrés du milieu” (lines 897–904), correctly observes that exactness makes the image of H^{n−1}(W0)→H^n(M)∨ equal ker(u); it is surjective iff u=0 in that degree. F032:L15 retains “surj”, so it cannot be a general consequence of the usual intersection-form sequence unless an extra vanishing hypothesis is intended. Hua's ordinary-double-point example is an external illustrative example, not manuscript material.

15. **Six-term reconstruction and integral unimodularity are conditional.** From the surviving F032:L13–17, the relative triangle, simple cohomology duality, and H^{>n}(M)=0, one can reconstruct 0→H^{n−1}(M)→H^{n−1}(W0)→H^n(M)∨→H^n(M)→H^n(W0)→H^{n−1}(M)∨→0; Hua gives this at lines 924–938. To reduce further to kernel/cokernel of u requires H^{n−1}(M)=0; integral homology-sphere/unimodularity equivalence additionally needs the relevant integral homology freeness, endpoint/connectivity conditions and correct integral duality. Neither the word “unimodular” nor an integral criterion appears in Hua's file, and no such criterion should be attributed to the manuscript.

16. **Final V_s top-degree tension is real but interpretation uncertain.** Hua, “En termes de fibres (page 33)” (lines 965–975), flags that F034:L22 omits degree 2n−1 from the possible nonzero cohomology of V_s, unlike F032:L19 and the link interpretation. Hua's repair by reinterpreting it as the Milnor fibre is a possible explanation only: F034:L24 itself preserves uncertainty in the subscript ξ versus barred s, so the witness does not settle that change of object.

17. **Final counterexample remains unfinished and cancelled.** Hua, “Un autre sujet : finitude et noyaux (page 34)” (lines 995–1007), agrees with F035:L5–9 that the entire example is struck out and ends at Q=. Its identification P=M_f/M with H^1_(f)(M), assuming f is M-regular, is mathematically natural; completing a counterexample via multiplication by g would be a new construction and would need definitions of the intended finiteness condition.

## Scope and absence checks

- Hua deliberately omits the reused typescript. The complete frozen transcription adds the seven French typescript pages, the Efimov announcement, five English text pages and Figures 7–8; these cannot be assimilated to the handwritten singularity argument merely because they share sheets.
- Hua's `\pages{1}{34}` and “Trente-quatre feuillets” are editorial range/count descriptions. The present retained scan list contains 32 supplied faces, scans 003–018 and 020–035, with two omitted blank scan numbers; page-mark range, physical leaves and supplied faces should remain distinct.
- The date 25 April 1968 is on the reused announcement and is not a handwritten date of the singularity notes. Hua itself makes that caution in “Le dossier, sa charpente et ses notations”, lines 142–147.
- No direct proof of Picard–Lefschetz, quasi-unipotence, wild-inertia elimination, a characteristic-p analogue, or an integral homology-sphere/unimodularity criterion was found in the frozen passages reviewed. “π” and the geometric-generic dictionary permit these research questions but do not answer them.
