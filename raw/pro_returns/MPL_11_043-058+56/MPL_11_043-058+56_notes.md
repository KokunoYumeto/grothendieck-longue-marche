### 2. Correspondence rings and the construction of motives

Cote 11 adds the operations that the earlier register entries asked to see: the correspondence group `A(I′;I)=A(I ⨿ I′)` at 11/046, pull–multiply–push composition on that page, and the diagonal action at 11/048. Here is the exact geometric comparison in the concrete Chow realization for a fixed smooth projective variety X:

\[
c_{I,J}:X^{I\amalg J}\xrightarrow{\sim}X^I\times X^J,
\qquad (x_t)_{t\in I\amalg J}\longmapsto((x_i)_{i\in I},(x_j)_{j\in J}).
\]

Under these coordinate isomorphisms, the source projections `p₂₁,p₃₂,p₃₁` correspond to `pr₁₂,pr₂₃,pr₁₃`, respectively, and the displayed composition becomes

\[
v\circ u=(\operatorname{pr}_{13})_*
\bigl(\operatorname{pr}_{12}^*u\cdot\operatorname{pr}_{23}^*v\bigr).
\]

For the rational Chow realization, adjoining projector images and integer twists gives summands `(X^I,e,m)` with the explicit groups

\[
\operatorname{Hom}((X^I,e,m),(X^J,f,n))
=f\,\mathrm{CH}^{\dim(X^I)-m+n}(X^I\times X^J)_{\mathbf Q}\,e,
\]

with the same composition; finite formal direct sums of these summands have matrix Hom groups, and tensor product uses external products of cycles and adds twists. This formula provides a precise comparison with the correspondence-based definition used in [J92, p. 447]. CT16/015–018 adds its specified graded-ring homomorphism `Φ→E`, including the degree projectors; the realization of an algebraic correspondence is the map `C↦[x↦pr₂*(C·pr₁*x)]` into endomorphisms of cohomology. These are the exact arrows to compare.
