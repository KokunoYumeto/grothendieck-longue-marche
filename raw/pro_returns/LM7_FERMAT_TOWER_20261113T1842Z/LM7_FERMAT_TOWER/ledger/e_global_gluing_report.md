# E.73 — exact global presentation and coupled local packets

## Result

The actual maximal pro-three arithmetic group over Q(zeta3) has a minimal convergent presentation with two chosen unramified generators and one inertia generator for every prime away from three. The full closed relation subgroup is generated by the actual local words

`Y_q T_q Y_q^-1 T_q^(-Nq)`.

Here `Y_q` is a chosen preimage of the actual local Frobenius in that free group. The proof does not set it equal to the word of its image in the unramified quotient.

Consequently a section over the free unramified quotient, together with a null family of inertia values, extends globally exactly when evaluation of all those actual words gives the local tame relations. These equations are sufficient. The local class spaces already computed in E.61, E.66, E.68 and E.71 are their exact packets. The complete geometric conjugation action, the comparison to the two intrinsic quotients, and rational descent by actual complex-conjugation words are included.

The equations retain their arithmetic coupling. Their proof does not evaluate every higher Frobenius word or solve every coupled global system. Those remain the specific arithmetic inputs for the outstanding global classification.

## Exact support and relation-module proof

The inertia-bundle universal property identifies H1 of its kernel with a direct sum over base primes of locally constant coinduced modules on the prolongation spaces. Continuity forces finite support in the base primes. Since the unramified group is free on two generators, its H1 commutes with this direct sum. The explicit Shapiro cochain and the central-extension edge map identify global H2 with the direct sum of the actual local H2 groups.

The dual relation module is a compact product. The displayed local relators evaluate against its dual classes as the coordinate vectors. Their closed normal closure is the entire kernel: a surviving nontrivial normal pro-three subgroup would have a finite quotient with a nonzero invariant character, contradicting that spanning property. Inertia generators converge because every finite extension ramifies at only finitely many primes. The relators then converge regardless of the chosen Frobenius preimages.

The source/module maps and all conjugation signs are written in the proof. In particular the positive local relation error agrees with evaluation of the coinduced cocycle at the base inertia generator.
