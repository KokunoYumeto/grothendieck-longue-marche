\subsection*{Restoring full arithmetic homology while retaining the repaired invariant block}

\begin{statement}{D.58}{Proved}{The D.57 repair extends through the full arithmetic homology tower and the remaining moving class-two module}
Retain the actual groups, maps and automorphism of D.57.
Use \(G=G_3\), \(K_3\), \(U=K_3^{\mathrm{ab},(2)}\),
\(U^-\), \(V=U/2U\), \(W=U^-/2U^-\),
and \(Z=W\wedge V\) from D.49--D.54.
Let \(\theta_U:U\twoheadrightarrow U_*\) and
\(\theta_Z:Z\twoheadrightarrow Z_*\) be the actual onto
maps of D.56. Set
\[
 \mathcal L=\ker(U^-\longrightarrow U_*^-),\qquad
 S_{57}=\mathfrak R_h\cap M_*\cap L_{*,2},\qquad
 R_{50}=\mathfrak R_h\cap M_\infty.
 \tag{D.58.1}
\]
Then \(\mathcal L\) is a saturated, free
\(\mathbb Z_2\)-module of rank \(w-640\), and
\[
 \operatorname{im}(S_{57}\longrightarrow U)=\mathcal L.
 \tag{D.58.2}
\]
There is an actual continuous peripheral automorphism of
\[
 Y=\widehat B/(\mathfrak R_h\cap M_\infty\cap L_{*,2})
 \tag{D.58.3}
\]
which projects to the complete D.57 automorphism of
\(X_*=\widehat B/S_{57}\), including one compatible pair
of its normalized conjugators. It has multiplier one,
fixes \(t\), and induces identity on \(G\).
It restores the full integral homology source \(U\);
its particular homology action is newly repaired.
The exact comparison kernels are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \ker(Y\longrightarrow X_*)&\simeq\mathcal L,\\
 \ker(Y\longrightarrow\mathcal Z_h)&\simeq Z_*,
       &\mathcal Z_h&=\widehat B/R_{50},\\
 Y&\simeq\mathcal Z_h
            \mathbin{\times}_{\mathcal T_{*,2}/Z_*}
                    \mathcal T_{*,2}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{D.58.4}
\]
All arrows in this display are induced by the same source
\(\widehat B\). In particular the full small class-two
action of D.57 and the entire prescribed
\(\mathcal W_h\) action are retained simultaneously.

There is also a further actual extension. Let
\[
 X=\widehat B/(R_{50}\cap L_2),\qquad
 \mathcal K=\ker(\theta_Z:Z\to Z_*),\qquad
 \mathcal K_0=\mathcal K^A,\quad
 \mathcal K_1=(1-e_A)\mathcal K.
 \tag{D.58.5}
\]
The identification of \(Z\) with
\(\ker(X\to\mathcal Z_h)\) in D.54 is used in this
definition. Thus \(\mathcal K_0,\mathcal K_1\) are actual
normal subgroups of \(X\), with their displayed
\(G\)-module structures. The source map is exactly
\[
                  Y=X/\mathcal K.
 \tag{D.58.6}
\]
The constructed automorphism of \(Y\) extends to an
actual continuous peripheral automorphism of
\[
                         X_{58}=X/\mathcal K_0.
 \tag{D.58.7}
\]
The projection \(X_{58}\to Y\) has the exact kernel
\(\mathcal K_1\). The full remaining class-two map is
\(X\to X_{58}\), with exact kernel \(\mathcal K_0\).

For the actual finite arithmetic group put
\[
 n=576\cdot3^{97},\qquad q=|A|,\qquad
 m=n/(6q),\qquad \rho=1+n/6,\qquad w=m(q-1).
\]
These are the actual quantities of D.49, with
\(3^4\leq q\leq3^{97}\). The exact dimensions are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \dim\mathcal K
    &=w\rho-\frac{w(w+1)}2-210240,\\
 \dim\mathcal K_0
    &=\frac{m^2(q-1)}2-2560,\\
 \dim\mathcal K_1
    &=\frac{w\rho}{2}-207680.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{D.58.8}
\]
Thus the extension retains all 2560 invariant central
coordinates of the solved actual order-3888 quotient,
in addition to the full original moving class-two
module. Its remaining invariant kernel is nonzero:
already \(m\geq96\) and \(q\geq81\) give
\(\dim\mathcal K_0\geq366080\).
The order \(q\) is the order of the actual normal span
of \(f_h\); no new evaluation of that entire span is
asserted in this statement.

The normalized parameter of \(X_{58}\) still maps to
the literal \(f_h^*\) in \(\mathcal W_h\) and hence
retains its established full metabelian and prescribed
CM comparisons. The actual source kernel of (D.58.7) is
\[
 R_{58}=\{b\in R_{50}:
           \text{the image of }b\text{ in }\mathcal T_2
                          \text{ lies in }\mathcal K_0\}.
 \tag{D.58.9}
\]
In particular
\(R_{58}/(R_{50}\cap L_2)=\mathcal K_0\).
The original full profinite realization and the
remaining invariant class-two lifting problem are
not asserted to be complete.

The manuscript passages are Part D(e), section 51.16,
folio 734 verso, scan 174, and section 51.15,
folios 725--727, scans 156--160.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
\emph{The exact image of the new source kernel.}
The onto map \(U^-\to U_*^-\) is the integral map
proved in D.56, with the original marked source maps.
Its target is free over \(\mathbb Z_2\). Its kernel
\(\mathcal L\) is consequently saturated and free,
and its rank is \(w-640\). Odd averaging gives
\(e_A\mathcal L=0\).

We first evaluate an actual image of an intersection,
rather than intersecting two independently computed
images. Every element of \(S_{57}\) maps into
\(U^-\), because it lies in \(\mathfrak R_h\), and
has zero image in \(U_*\), because it lies in \(M_*\).
Its image therefore belongs to \(\mathcal L\).
Conversely take \(u\in\mathcal L\). By D.50's onto
map choose an actual \(b\in\mathfrak R_h\) mapping
to \(u\). Then \(b\in M_*\). In the actual small
class-two quotient its image has zero abelianization
and belongs to the image of \(\mathfrak R_h\), so
it lies in \(Z_*\), by D.51 and D.56.

The map
\[
                 R_{50}\longrightarrow Z_*
 \tag{D.58.10}
\]
is onto. Indeed D.51 gives the onto map
\(R_{50}\to Z\), and D.56 gives the onto marked
exterior map \(Z\to Z_*\). Choose \(c\in R_{50}\)
with the same small class-two image as \(b\).
Then \(bc^{-1}\in S_{57}\), while its full integral
homology image is still \(u\). This proves (D.58.2)
using compatible source preimages.
Its kernel is \(S_{57}\cap M_\infty\).
Because \(M_\infty\subset M_*\), this intersection
is exactly the source kernel in (D.58.3), proving
the first kernel formula of (D.58.4).

The second formula in (D.58.4) follows from the same
onto map (D.58.10): the kernel of \(Y\to\mathcal Z_h\)
is \(R_{50}/(R_{50}\cap L_{*,2})\), identified with
its actual image \(Z_*\) in \(\mathcal T_{*,2}\).
The product of the two original source kernels gives
the maximal common quotient \(\mathcal T_{*,2}/Z_*\).
Closed Goursat then gives the displayed fibre product,
including both original projection maps.

\emph{The common quotient at every integral homology level.}
For \(k\geq1\), put
\[
 M_k=\overline{K_3'K_3^{2^k}},\qquad
 J_k=\widehat B/M_k,\qquad
 U_k=U/2^kU,\qquad
 \mathcal L_k=\mathcal L/2^k\mathcal L.
 \tag{D.58.11}
\]
Saturation identifies \(\mathcal L_k\) with its
actual image in \(U_k\). Equation (D.58.2) proves
that the maximal common marked quotient of \(X_*\)
and \(J_k\) is exactly \(J_k/\mathcal L_k\).

The D.57 automorphism descends to this common quotient.
To verify the assertion directly, \(X_*\) maps to
\(G=G_3\) through its retained \(\mathcal W_h\)
projection; that action is the identity. The image
of \(K_3\) in \(X_*\) is therefore invariant.
Taking its abelian quotient of exponent \(2^k\)
is characteristic. Its actual kernel is the image
of \(M_k\). Hence the resulting quotient is
\(\widehat B/(S_{57}M_k)=J_k/\mathcal L_k\), with
the induced automorphism and the actual images
of the fixed D.57 conjugators. This descent does
not assume a lift of any centralizer element from
\(\mathcal W_h\) to the old D.50 homology action.

\emph{An actual first-level peripheral endomorphism.}
Write \(J=J_1\), \(V=U_1\), and
\(L=\mathcal L_1\). Lift the two prescribed
conjugators from \(J/L\) to \(J\). Their images
in \(G\) centralize \(s,r\), respectively, since
the lower action fixes \(G\). Their two conjugates
have the required orders, and their product differs
from \(t\) by an element of \(L\).

Left corrections \(b,a\in L\) change this error
by exactly
\[
                  (1-s)b+s(1-r)a.
 \tag{D.58.12}
\]
Its image is the augmentation image \(I_GL\).
Since \(e_AL=0\), odd averaging gives \([L,A]=L\),
and therefore \(L_G=0\). Thus (D.58.12) is onto.
Kill the cusp error with an actual pair of these
corrections. The resulting homomorphism from
\(\widehat B\) is identity on \(G\), fixes \(t\),
and sends \(K_3\) to the elementary abelian kernel
\(V\). It kills \(M_1\) and hence defines an
actual peripheral endomorphism \(\Phi:J\to J\).
It retains both conjugators and the automorphism
on the whole quotient \(J/L\).

\emph{Projective generation repair inside the retained kernel.}
We spell out the repair because it must remain inside
\(L\). The actual marked Bass--Serre graph gives
\[
 0\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow \mathbb F_2[G]
 \xrightarrow{\partial}
 \mathbb F_2[G/\langle s\rangle]
       \oplus\mathbb F_2[G/\langle r\rangle]
 \longrightarrow\mathbb F_2\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{D.58.13}
\]
The chosen conjugators define the edge chain map
\(D\) of D.50. Its vertex maps are the actual
permutations given by their \(G\)-coordinates,
which centralize the corresponding stabilizers.
They are invertible. The exact path identity
\(D\mathfrak p(g)=\mathfrak p(\Phi(g))+
(g-1)\mathfrak p(\beta)\) proves that the restriction
of \(D\) to \(V\) is the actual homology map \(T\).

Let \(P\) be the nilpotent Fitting summand of \(D\)
on the regular edge module. Invertibility on the
vertex module forces \(P\subset V\). The induced
map on \(V/L\) is invertible, since the quotient
action on \(J/L\) is an automorphism. Consequently
\(P\subset L\). The Fitting decomposition is
\(G\)-linear, so \(P\) is a direct summand of the
regular module and is projective. Let \(e\) be its
\(G\)-linear projection.

The exact path cocycle has values
\(\mathfrak p(s)=0\) and
\(\mathfrak p(r)=e_1-e_r\). The derivation
\(\delta_0=e\mathfrak p\) restricts to \(e|_V\).
The cusp has order 36 in \(G\), and \(\Phi(t)=t\),
so the cycle \([t^{36}]\) is fixed by \(T\) and
killed by \(e\). Therefore
\[
           (1+t+\cdots+t^{35})\delta_0(t)=0.
 \tag{D.58.14}
\]
Restriction of a projective \(\mathbb F_2[G]\)-module
to the cyclic subgroup generated by \(t\) remains
projective. In a free cyclic-group module the norm
kernel is the image of \(1-t\), by its coefficient
sums; direct summands retain this exactness.
Choose \(b\in P\) with
\((1-t)b=\delta_0(t)\), and subtract the inner
derivation \((1-g)b\). The resulting \(\delta\)
fixes the cusp and has
\[
 \delta(s)=(1-s)b,\qquad
 \delta(r)=(1-r)(e(e_1)+b),\qquad
 \delta|_V=e|_V.
 \tag{D.58.15}
\]
These are actual left corrections to the two
conjugators inside \(P\subset L\). The corrected
map has homology map \(T+e\), invertible on both
Fitting summands, and remains identity on \(G\).
Thus it is an automorphism of \(J\), preserving
the entire prescribed common quotient and the
actual conjugators there. No division by the
even cusp order occurs in this construction.

\emph{All higher levels and compatible conjugators.}
Suppose level \(k-1\) has been constructed together
with its \(X_*\) component. Its source kernel is
\(S_{57}\cap M_{k-1}\). Its image in \(J_k\) is
exactly
\[
 \mathcal L_k\cap2^{k-1}U_k
                       =2^{k-1}\mathcal L_k.
 \tag{D.58.16}
\]
For the first equality, an element of \(S_{57}\)
whose image in \(J_k\) reduces to one already
belongs to \(M_{k-1}\); this is the actual
preimage property. The second uses saturation
of \(\mathcal L\subset U\).

Thus the next maximal common quotient is
\(J_k/2^{k-1}\mathcal L_k\). Its two quotient maps
identify it with the fibre product of \(J_{k-1}\)
and \(J_k/\mathcal L_k\) over their original
common quotient. The previously constructed
action and the D.57 action agree there, and
their conjugators agree as elements. Lift those
two elements to \(J_k\). Their cusp error lies
in \(2^{k-1}\mathcal L_k\simeq L\), where
(D.58.12) is onto. These corrections preserve
both previous conjugators and the entire
\(X_*\) component. The resulting map is an
automorphism: its homology matrix reduces to
the already invertible matrix at level one.

Successive compatible choices give automorphisms,
inverses and one pair of conjugators in the
inverse limit. Closed Goursat and (D.58.2)
identify this limit with (D.58.3). This proves
the first actual extension and all its
comparison assertions.

\emph{The exact full class-two kernel.}
The source inclusion \(L_2\subset L_{*,2}\)
comes from the inclusion \(K_3\subset K_*\)
and the defining class-two exponent-four laws.
Thus there is an actual projection \(X\to Y\).
Its kernel is the image in \(\mathcal T_2\)
of \(R_{50}\cap L_{*,2}\). The image of
\(R_{50}\) is exactly \(Z\). An element of
this image is represented by a source element
in \(L_{*,2}\) exactly when its image in
\(\mathcal T_{*,2}\) is one. Hence the kernel
is exactly \(\ker\theta_Z=\mathcal K\), with
both inclusions furnished by those same
source representatives. This proves (D.58.6).

The constructed \(Y\) action has identity
\(G\)-action and full integral homology
automorphism \(T\). Its map to the retained
small class-two action gives
\(\theta_VT=T_*\theta_V\). Therefore
\(\bigwedge^2T\) preserves \(\mathcal K\),
and it preserves \(\mathcal K_0,\mathcal K_1\)
because it commutes with the \(G\)-action
and the central averaging operator \(e_A\).

Lift the two actual \(Y\) conjugators to
\(X/\mathcal K_0\). Their product differs
from \(t\) by an element of
\(\mathcal K/\mathcal K_0=\mathcal K_1\).
This is an abelian module with action through
\(G\). Its \(A\)-coinvariants vanish, so
\[
 (1-s)\mathcal K_1+s(1-r)\mathcal K_1
                         =\mathcal K_1.
 \tag{D.58.17}
\]
Kill the error by these actual central
conjugator corrections. The finite-order
relations remain exact and the cusp is fixed.

To verify factorization, the resulting source
map already projects to the constructed
automorphism of \(\mathcal Z_h\). Its finite
class-two projection is identity on \(G\)
and sends \(K_3\) into a class-two
exponent-four kernel, so it kills \(L_2\).
On its commutator module the map is
\(\bigwedge^2T\), which preserves
\(\mathcal K_0\), as just proved. It
therefore induces a homomorphism of
\(\mathcal T_2/\mathcal K_0\).
It is an automorphism by its invertible
Frattini map and identity quotient map.
These two automorphisms agree on their
original common quotient. Their fibre
product is exactly \(X/\mathcal K_0\),
so the corrected source map is the claimed
continuous automorphism of (D.58.7).
The conjugators project to the fixed
\(Y\) pair, and hence to the fixed D.57
pair, throughout this construction.

\emph{Dimensions and the remaining actual source kernel.}
The map \(\theta_Z\) is onto. Odd averaging
is exact, so it also gives the onto map
\(Z^A\to Z_*^{A_*}\), whose kernel is
\(\mathcal K_0\); the action on the target
factors through the actual quotient
\(A\twoheadrightarrow A_*\).
D.51 gives
\(\dim Z=w\rho-w(w+1)/2\),
\(\dim Z^A=m^2(q-1)/2\), and
\(\dim Z^-=w\rho/2\).
D.56--D.57 give the respective target
dimensions 210240, 2560 and 207680.
Their differences prove (D.58.8) with
the exact module maps, and (D.58.9)
is the preimage of the remaining
\(\mathcal K_0\) under \(R_{50}\to Z\).

For an actual surviving source element,
choose a nonzero \(z\in\mathcal K_0\),
which exists by the proved positive
dimension, and an actual source preimage
\(b\in R_{50}\) under the onto map to
\(Z\). Then \(b\in R_{58}\), while
its image in \(X\) is the specified
nonzero \(z\). This detects the source
kernel through an explicit quotient map.
For the constructed \(X_{58}\) action,
the next invariant boundary is the
actual class of the lifted cusp error
in \((\mathcal K_0)_G\); changing its
two central lifts changes that error
by the corresponding augmentation
columns. That class has not been
evaluated here. All parameter and CM
comparisons claimed in the statement
follow from the retained
\(\mathcal W_h\) projection and
\(\mathfrak R_h\subset\widehat F'\).
\end{proof}
