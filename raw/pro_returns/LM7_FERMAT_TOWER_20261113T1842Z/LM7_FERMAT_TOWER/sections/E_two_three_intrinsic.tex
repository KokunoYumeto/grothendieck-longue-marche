\subsection{The complete intrinsic class-two locus unramified outside two and three}

\begin{statement}{E.99}{Proved}{All global class-two sections over the actual two-prime arithmetic source}
Let $N$ be the based geometric pro-three group of the cubic
with its nine cusps removed, in the actual marking of E.95.
Put $N_2=N/\gamma_3N$, $V=N^{\rm ab}$ and
$M=\gamma_2N/\gamma_3N=\bigwedge^2V$.
Write $\operatorname{Sect}_{23}(N_2)$ for the geometric
conjugacy classes of continuous sections whose cocycles
factor through the relative arithmetic quotient unramified
outside $\{2,3\}$.
There are explicit continuous mutually inverse coordinates
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \operatorname{Sect}_{23}(N_2)
 \simeq\coprod_{(a,b,c)\in\mathbb F_3^2\times\mathbb Z/9}
 \left(\mathbb Z_3^{36}\times
       (\mathbb Z/3)^9\times(\mathbb Z/9)^5\right).
 \quad}                                                    \tag{E99.1}
\]
The finite representatives, all coefficient maps, the exact
abelian image, and the nonlinear changes of representative
are specified below. In particular (E99.1) is a statement
about actual global sections and their complete geometric
quotient.

Use the basis
$(f_1,f_2,A_0,A_1,A_2,B_0,B_1,B_2,C_1,C_2)$ of E.95;
a cusp label in a linear formula means its positive
peripheral vector. Set
\[
 \begin{split}
 h&=f_1+\tfrac12(A_1+A_2+B_1+B_2+C_1+C_2),\\
 V^+&=\langle h,A_1-A_2,B_1-B_2,C_1-C_2\rangle,\\
 W^-&=\langle A_0,B_0,A_1+A_2,B_1+B_2,C_1+C_2\rangle,\\
 h_0&=(1,1,1,1,1),\qquad
 h_1=(1,0,0,0,0),\qquad h_2=(0,1,0,0,0).
 \end{split}                                               \tag{E99.2}
\]
Here $V^+=V^{J_1}$ and $W^-=W\cap V^{-J_1}$.
Thus $h_0=-e_O$, $h_1=e_{A_0}$ and $h_2=e_{B_0}$
after the displayed inclusion of $\mathbb Z_3^5$ in $W$.
For $p=(a,b,c)$ in (E99.1), use the representatives
$0\leq a,b<3$, $0\leq c<9$ and put
\[
 U_p=(3a-3b-c)h-a(B_1-B_2)+(-a+b)(C_1-C_2).
 \tag{E99.3}
\]
Define $\epsilon_p=0$ at
\[
 p_O=(0,0,0),\qquad p_{A_0}=(2,2,7),\qquad
 p_{B_0}=(2,0,2),                                         \tag{E99.4}
\]
and $\epsilon_p=1$ at the other seventy-eight points.
The complete inertia lattice in the $p$-th stratum is
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \mathscr K_p=9\mathbb Z_3^5+
                    3^{\epsilon_p}\mathbb Z_3h_{\bar c}.
 \quad}                                                    \tag{E99.5}
\]
For every abelian section in the coordinates below, its
class-two preimage is nonempty exactly when its inertia
coordinate belongs to (E99.5). Each nonempty preimage is
\[
          \mathbb Z_3^{25}\times
                  (\mathbb Z/3)^9\times(\mathbb Z/9)^5.
 \tag{E99.6}
\]
Its coordinate origin and inverse are part of the construction.
The other six abelian coordinates are unrestricted. The full
abelian source has additive type
$\mathbb Z_3^{11}\oplus(\mathbb Z/3)^3$; its finite carry
in the representatives (E99.3) is retained by the exact
conjugation map in the proof.

The projection onto the relative inertia coordinate is
therefore the union of the three rational-cusp lines
modulo nine. More precisely, among all
$81\cdot9^5=4\,782\,969$ finite pairs $(p,v\bmod9)$,
exactly $261$ occur. Three strata have nine such residues,
and seventy-eight have three. This is the exact finite
classification, including all nonunit inertia parameters.

There are global class-two sections with abelian class
\[
                    9\kappa_3(2)(e_{A_0}+e_{B_0})
 \tag{E99.7}
\]
which have no global lift to $N/N''$, and consequently no
global lift to $N$. An actual three-generator section and
its central inertia word are given in (E99.32).

The certificate \texttt{a\_s23\_intrinsic.py} retains every
Smith matrix and inverse, all eighty-one inertia lattices
and their particular corrections, all finite residue checks,
the actual tame word checks, and the full central coordinate
inverse. Its input hashes are recorded in the output.

\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual arithmetic source and the original marking.}
Let $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$ and let $G_{23}$ denote the
relative maximal pro-three quotient of the arithmetic group
unramified outside two and three, over
$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)$.
The actual source comparison of E.55 gives
\[
 G_{23}=\left(
       \langle t,\sigma\mid\sigma t\sigma^{-1}=t^4\rangle_3
                   \mathop{\ast}_3\langle\rho\rangle
          \right)\rtimes\langle c\rangle,
 \quad
 ctc=t^{-1},\quad c\sigma c=\sigma,
 \quad c\rho c=\rho^{-1}.                                 \tag{E99.8}
\]
We use the original complex-conjugation marking $c$ and
the original rational Kummer roots, rather than leaving a
change of these choices implicit.

Here is the exact transport from the construction in E.55.
Suppose its local order-two element is $d=g c g^{-1}$.
For either rational Kummer cocycle $\kappa$, with
$\kappa(c)=0$, one has $\kappa(d)=2\kappa(g)$.
The root change making the new roots $d$-fixed has exponent
$e=\kappa(g)$ and changes the cocycle to
$\kappa+(\chi-1)e$.
Its Frobenius $\widehat\sigma$ has character four and
fixes these roots. Consequently
\[
 \kappa(\widehat\sigma)=-3\kappa(g),\qquad
 \kappa(g^{-1}\widehat\sigma g)=0.
 \tag{E99.9}
\]
Conjugate the entire arithmetic presentation by $g^{-1}$.
The old $c$ is now its order-two element. Conjugated tame
inertia has Kummer value $\chi(g)^{-1}$; raising it to the
unit power $\chi(g)$ gives $\kappa_3(2)(t)=1$.
This preserves the tame relation. The same calculation
applies simultaneously to the roots of two and three.
It is a specified isomorphism of the arithmetic sources,
with inverse conjugation and inverse unit power.

For a useful exact choice of the other free generator,
lift the old marked $\tau$ of E.37 and E.49 to this group.
Multiply its lift on the left by the inverse of its
$\kappa_3(2)$-power of $t$, and call the result $\gamma$.
It has the same geometric action as $\tau$, because
the actual based action is unramified at two. Set
\[
                   \rho=\gamma c\gamma^{-1}c.
 \tag{E99.10}
\]
It satisfies the last identity of (E99.8) exactly. The
characters on the three generators are
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
             &t&\sigma&\rho\\\hline
 \chi_3      &1&4&1\\
 \kappa_3(2)&1&0&0\\
 \kappa_3(3)&0&0&2.
 \end{array}                                               \tag{E99.11}
\]
The images of $\sigma,\rho$ freely generate the unramified
group $P$ of E.34: their two Frattini characters are
independent. Define $\rho_1:=\rho^{1/2}$ in this actual free factor.
It has the last Kummer value one. The literal free-factor
automorphism $\rho_1\mapsto\rho_1^2$, with inverse
$\rho\mapsto\rho^{1/2}$.

For completeness, (E99.8) retains all relations of the
actual arithmetic source. The opened
\cite[Theorem2.4 and its proof, pp13--14]{E_WingbergRiemann}
identifies the kernel over $P$ with the continuous free
product of actual inertia groups, indexed by
$P/\langle\sigma\rangle$. The coset tree of the displayed
free product has exactly the same vertex stabilizers and
the same map on each inertia factor. Its universal property
gives inverse maps on the kernels; the quotient map is the
identity on $P$. This is the E.55 source isomorphism, now
with the explicit transport (E99.9)--(E99.10).
It also gives a reciprocal section into the full relative
arithmetic group. Thus solving its relations constructs
global sections, not only compatible local restrictions.
The actual action on $N$ factors through the same based
quotient by E.34 and the tangent transport of E.78.

\emph{The evaluated coefficient operators.}
Write $U_\mathrm{gl}$ for the eight-by-two gluing matrix
