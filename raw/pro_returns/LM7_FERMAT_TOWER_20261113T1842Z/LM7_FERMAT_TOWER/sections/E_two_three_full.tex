\subsection{Every full and intrinsic-metabelian section over the actual two-prime source}

\begin{statement}{E.100}{Proved}{Complete cubic sections unramified outside two and three, with every full-to-metabelian fibre}
Let $N$ be the actual based cubic pro-three group of E.34 and
E.78, let $M=N/N''$, and retain E.99's original arithmetic
marking and rational Kummer roots. Write
\[
 \mathscr S_{23}(H)=H^1_{\rm cts}(G_{23},H),\qquad
 \mathscr S_3(H)=H^1_{\rm cts}(\Gamma,H)
       \quad(H=N,M),
 \tag{E100.1}
\]
where both cohomology sets mean geometric-conjugacy classes
for the genuine based action. The maps from the corresponding
absolute arithmetic groups are the actual quotient maps
proved in E.34, E.55 and E.99. In particular
\[
\begin{gathered}
 G_{23}=(D_4\mathop{*}_3\langle\rho\rangle)
                   \rtimes\langle c\rangle,\qquad
 D_4=\langle t,\sigma\mid\sigma t\sigma^{-1}=t^4\rangle_3,\\
 ctc=t^{-1},\quad c\sigma c=\sigma,\quad
 c\rho c=\rho^{-1},\qquad
 G_{23}/\overline{\langle\!\langle t\rangle\!\rangle}
        =\Gamma=P\rtimes\langle c\rangle .
\end{gathered}
 \tag{E100.2}
\]
Put $J=\Phi_c$, $F=\Phi_\sigma$, $T=\Phi_\tau$ and
$A=\Phi_\rho$. These denote actual group automorphisms,
and satisfy
\[
 \Phi_t=1,\qquad FJ=JF,\qquad
 A=TJT^{-1}J,\qquad \mathcal L:=AJ=TJT^{-1}.
 \tag{E100.3}
\]

The section set (E100.1) has the following complete partition:
its zero-inertia part is precisely the inflated
$\mathscr S_3(H)$, and its nonzero-inertia part consists of
three strata, indexed by the rational cusps
\[
 O=[1:-1:0],\qquad A_0=[0:-1:1],\qquad B_0=[1:0:-1].
 \tag{E100.4}
\]
Choose the actual rational tangents and $c$-fixed paths
constructed below. Let $d_i$ be the resulting positive
peripheral element and
$u_i=p_iF(p_i)^{-1}$. The entire stratum at $i$ has unique
representatives
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
     &t&\sigma&\rho&c\\ \hline
 a_{i,a,y}&d_i^a&u_i&T(y)&1
\end{array}
 \qquad
 a\in\mathbb Z_3\setminus\{0\},\qquad J(y)=y^{-1}.
 \tag{E100.5}
\]
Here $J(d_i)=d_i^{-1}$, $J(u_i)=u_i$ and
$u_iF(d_i)u_i^{-1}=d_i^4$. Every row of (E100.5)
is an actual global section. Its geometric stabilizer
is trivial, and every section in its stratum has one
and only one $J$-fixed geometric conjugator carrying
it to that row.

Define $\operatorname{Anti}_J(H)=\{y\in H:J(y)=y^{-1}\}$.
There are explicit compatible Hall coordinates
\[
 \operatorname{Anti}_J(H)
       \simeq\prod_{r\ge1}\mathbb Z_3^{\nu_r(H)},\qquad
 \mathscr S_{23}(H)_i^{\rm ram}
       \simeq(\mathbb Z_3\setminus\{0\})
                       \times\prod_{r\ge1}\mathbb Z_3^{\nu_r(H)}.
 \tag{E100.6}
\]
These are homeomorphisms of spaces. The anti set is
used with its displayed group equation; no group
operation on that set is imposed.
The ranks are completely evaluated in every degree.
For $r\ge1$ set
\[
\begin{aligned}
 n_r(N)&=\frac1r\sum_{d\mid r}\mu(d)10^{r/d},&
 j_r(N)&=\frac1r\sum_{d\mid r}\mu(d)
                          (4+(-1)^d6)^{r/d},\\
 n_1(M)&=10,\quad j_1(M)=-2,&
 n_r(M)&=(r-1){r+8\choose r}\quad(r\ge2),\\
 j_r(M)&=-2c_{r-1}-c_r\quad(r\ge2),&
 c_s&=[z^s](1-z)^{-4}(1+z)^{-6},\\
 \nu_r(H)&=(n_r(H)-j_r(H))/2.&
\end{aligned}
 \tag{E100.7}
\]
For example,
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 r&1&2&3&4&5&6\\\hline
 \nu_r(N)&6&24&166&1248&10002&83320\\
 \nu_r(M)&6&24&166&744&2580&7504\\
 \nu_r(N)-\nu_r(M)&0&0&0&504&7422&75816 .
\end{array}
 \tag{E100.8}
\]

The actual map $\mathscr S_{23}(N)\to\mathscr S_{23}(M)$
is onto. On each ramified stratum it preserves $i,a$.
For the matched Hall and Chen coordinates constructed
below, its remaining map literally deletes the negative
Hall coordinates not belonging to the Chen subset.
Consequently every ramified target has full fibre
\[
       \prod_{r\ge4}
           \mathbb Z_3^{\,\nu_r(N)-\nu_r(M)}.
 \tag{E100.9}
\]
There is also a coordinate-independent description
with its actual map. Put $R=N''$, fix the target's
$\rho$-value $x_M$, and choose an actual lift
$x_0\in\operatorname{Anti}_{\mathcal L}(N)$.
Then
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \operatorname{Fib}(x_M)
   =\operatorname{Anti}_{\mathcal L_0}(R),\qquad
 \mathcal L_0=\operatorname{Ad}(x_0)\mathcal L,\\
 r\longmapsto r x_0,\qquad
 R/R^{\mathcal L_0}\xrightarrow{\sim}
       \operatorname{Anti}_{\mathcal L_0}(R),\quad
 gR^{\mathcal L_0}\longmapsto g\mathcal L_0(g)^{-1}.
\end{gathered}
 \tag{E100.10}
\]
The quotient in the last line is the space of right
cosets. The map has the continuous section
$r\mapsto r^{1/2}$ before passing to those cosets.

Here are all zero-inertia fibres as well. Give $R$
its induced filtration and put
\[
\begin{aligned}
 K_r&=(R\cap\gamma_rN)/(R\cap\gamma_{r+1}N)
       =\ker(L_r(N)\to L_r(M)),\\
 \mathcal C_r(R)&=
       K_r/(T_r-I)K_r^J,\qquad
 L_r(H)=\gamma_rH/\gamma_{r+1}H .
\end{aligned}
 \tag{E100.11}
\]
These are finite-rank, explicitly presented compact
$\mathbb Z_3$-modules, with free rank
$\nu_r(N)-\nu_r(M)$. Their matrices are restrictions
of the actual integral Hall matrices of E.37 and
E.41 to the non-Chen Hall basis proved below.
Their torsion is killed by
$3^{r+b_r}$, where
$b_r=\max_{r\le j\le2r}(1+v_3(j))$.
Thus Smith reduction modulo $3^{r+b_r+1}$,
with the retained integral basis maps, determines
every finite factor and its inverse coordinates.
There are exact sequences
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal C_r(R)
 \xrightarrow{\iota_r}\mathcal C_r(N)
 \xrightarrow{\bar\pi_r}\mathcal C_r(M)
 \longrightarrow0 .
 \tag{E100.12}
\]
The notation $\mathcal C_r(N),\mathcal C_r(M)$ is
that of E.41. Its full unramified coordinates give
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \mathscr S_3(H)\simeq\prod_{r\ge1}\mathcal C_r(H),\\
 \operatorname{Fib}\bigl(
      \mathscr S_3(N)\to\mathscr S_3(M)\bigr)
       \simeq\prod_{r\ge4}\mathcal C_r(R).
\end{gathered}
 \tag{E100.13}
\]
Every fibre in the second line is nonempty.
Equations (E100.24)--(E100.26) below give the
actual triangular map and every inverse fibre,
including the nonlinear arithmetic corrections.
A full ramified source cannot lie over a
zero-inertia metabelian target, so (E100.13)
is also that target's entire fibre in
$\mathscr S_{23}(N)$.

The topology on the whole partition is the actual
quotient topology specified in (E100.14) below.
In particular its zero-inertia boundary is retained:
in (E100.5), $a\to0$ gives the unramified section
with values $(1,u_i,T(y),1)$. The statement evaluates
the entire cubic source unramified outside
$\{2,3\}$, within the global maps of E.23.
The manuscript anchor is \S51.16, folios733v--736v,
scans172--178, formulas(371)--(379), Part E(c),(d).
\end{statement}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The actual arithmetic presentation and its normalized values.}
E.99 proves (E100.2), including its actual maps
to the relative global arithmetic group and
to the unramified quotient. Its proof retains
the maps on each inertia factor in the
continuous free-product theorem
\cite[Lemma1.1, Theorem2.4 and their proofs,
pp4--5,13--15]{E_WingbergRiemann}.
We use precisely those maps. The element
$\rho=\gamma c\gamma^{-1}c$ there has
$\Phi_\gamma=T$, because multiplying the chosen
lift of $\tau$ by a tame inertia power changes
neither its actual geometric action nor its
image in $P$. Hence (E100.3) is an equality
of full automorphisms. Both $R=N''$ and its
quotient are stable, so the same equality
holds on $M$, on $R$, and on all the induced
filtrations.

Every continuous cocycle under consideration
factors through this source. Over $K$ its
cocycle together with the genuine action
has image in a pro-three semidirect product;
the maximal pro-three arithmetic quotient
therefore supplies the factorization. The
relative order-two quotient is then exactly
the one retained in E.99. Conversely its
actual source map inflates every cocycle
constructed here to the original global
arithmetic group.

If a cocycle has value $v$ at $c$, its
order-two relation is $vJ(v)=1$.
Conjugation by $v^{-1/2}$ makes that value
one. Every element of a pro-three group has
a unique square root, continuously: this
is the inverse of squaring on its procyclic
subgroup, and the inverses agree in every
finite three-group quotient. The remaining
geometric conjugators are exactly $H^J$.
Thus the whole section space is the compact
Hausdorff quotient
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathscr S_{23}(H)=
 \{(\nu,u,x)\in H^3:\;&J(\nu)=\nu^{-1},\
             J(u)=u,\ \mathcal L(x)=x^{-1},\\
             &uF(\nu)u^{-1}=\nu^4\}/H^J,\\
 g\cdot(\nu,u,x)=
       (&g\nu g^{-1},\ g uF(g)^{-1},\
                         g xA(g)^{-1}).
\end{split}
 \tag{E100.14}
\]
The last norm equation follows from
$c\rho c=\rho^{-1}$ and the cocycle identity:
$J(x)=A^{-1}(x^{-1})$. The free-product
presentation proves sufficiency of these
four equations, as well as necessity.

\emph{Rational cusp maps, their whole path torsors, and descent.}
Use $s=z/x$ at $O$, $s_A=x/z$ at $A_0$
and $s_B=y/x$ at $B_0$, with tangent value
one. The rational coordinate maps
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 a_A[x:y:z]=[z:y:x],\qquad a_B[x:y:z]=[x:z:y],\\
 s_Aa_A=s_Ba_B=s,\qquad a_A^2=a_B^2=1
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{E100.15}
\]
preserve the actual cubic and its cusp divisor.
They identify the corresponding tangential
fibre functors at every three-power level.
For the comparison with E.34's parameter
$-x/z$ at $A_0$, the compatible roots of
$-1$ are all $-1$. They are rational, and
the two transports compose to the identity.
This is the same precise basepoint transport
used in E.78 and E90.27.

The based action at each of these tangents
therefore factors through the same $\Gamma$.
Its entire pro-three path torsor does also.
Indeed an element of the kernel acts trivially
on both endpoint groups; the difference between
its translate of a path and that path centralizes
the full endpoint group. The latter is free
pro-three of rank ten and has trivial centre,
as proved by the Magnus first-term argument
in E.90. Thus the difference is one.
This proves the asserted factorization of the
path itself, not just its abelianization.

Choose any such path $p$. For
$\eta=pJ(p)^{-1}$ one has
$\eta J(\eta)=1$, so
$p_i=\eta^{-1/2}p$ is literally $J$-fixed.
Transport the positive local inertia along
$p_i$ to obtain $d_i$. Its full path cocycle
is $p_i g(p_i)^{-1}$, and therefore
\[
 J(d_i)=d_i^{-1},\quad J(u_i)=u_i,\quad
 u_iF(d_i)u_i^{-1}=d_i^4,\quad
 p_i t(p_i)^{-1}=1 .
 \tag{E100.16}
\]
The last equality uses the whole path
factorization just proved and $t\in\ker(G_{23}\to\Gamma)$.
This supplies the actual source and target
maps for each row of (E100.5).

Restrict a normalized section to $D_4$.
For $H=N$, E.61 gives the nine full local
cusp packets over $\mathbb Q_2(\zeta_3)$;
for $H=M$, E.66 gives the identical complete
ramified packets. Their parameters are
$(i,a,\bar b)$ with $a\ne0$ and
$\bar b\in\mathbb Z_3/3$. Choose conjugate
paths for each nonrational pair. On those
packets the actual involution acts as
\[
                 (i,a,\bar b)\longmapsto
                         (\bar i,a,-\bar b).
 \tag{E100.17}
\]
The source inversion of $t$ and the positive
peripheral inversion cancel on $a$.
The source $\sigma$ is fixed, while the
peripheral factor $d_i^b$ is inverted.
These are exactly the three sign and label
changes in (E100.17). The only fixed cusp
labels are (E100.4), and multiplication by
two is invertible on $\mathbb Z_3/3$.
Thus a descended ramified class has
exactly $i\in\{O,A_0,B_0\}$ and $\bar b=0$.

There is an actual unique conjugator
aligning its $D_4$-restriction with
$(d_i^a,u_i)$. Existence follows from
the local classification and the change
$b\mapsto b-3k$ by $d_i^k$.
For uniqueness, the centralizer of
$d_i^a$, including nonunit $a$, is
$\langle d_i\rangle$ in both $N$ and $M$:
E.61 proves the full case, and E66.18--21
proves the metabelian case through its
injective continuous Magnus map.
A stabilizer of the full local section
would then have exponent $k=4k$, hence
$k=0$. Since both local sections are
$J$-normalized, $J(g)$ is another aligning
conjugator whenever $g$ is one. Uniqueness
forces $J(g)=g$. This proves actual descent
and its full conjugacy assertion, without
identifying an invariant class with a
descent datum by assumption.

After that alignment the free $\rho$-value
is any $x\in\operatorname{Anti}_{\mathcal L}(H)$.
The exact equality (E100.3) identifies it
with $x=T(y)$ for a unique
$y\in\operatorname{Anti}_J(H)$.
All rows (E100.5) follow, with no remaining
geometric stabilizer. A different fixed path
$p_i'=h p_i$, $h\in N^J$, changes the row
by the actual conjugation $h$. In the
$y$-coordinate its change is
\[
 y\longmapsto g\,y\,J(g)^{-1},\qquad g=T^{-1}(h).
 \tag{E100.18}
\]
This gives the exact coordinate morphism
for every allowed path change.
The map to E.99 is the actual truncation
of the four entries in (E100.5), followed
by E99.20--22 and E99.29--31.
Its three Frobenius abelian labels are
$p_O=(0,0,0)$, $p_{A_0}=(2,2,7)$ and
$p_{B_0}=(2,0,2)$, and its inertia is
the literal vector $a[d_i]$.
The higher coordinates use the truncated
actual $u_i$ and $T(y)$ in that specified
normalization algorithm. Thus the
comparison retains the actual path
origins and all its central carries.

\emph{An actual $J$-adapted free basis.}
Use the integral $J$-eigenbasis of $V$ in
E99.2 and E99.19: it has four plus columns
and six minus columns. Its matrix and
inverse over $\mathbb Z_{(3)}$ are retained
in the certificate. A fixed abelian column
has a full fixed lift. Inductively, if $w$
lifts a fixed element through a central
lower-central layer, put
$\delta=J(w)w^{-1}$ in that layer.
Then $J(\delta)=-\delta$, and
$\delta^{1/2}w$ is fixed. Ordered Hall
lifts and this correction give a compatible
inverse sequence, hence a fixed element
of $N$. For a minus column, choose any
abelian lift $w$ and use instead
$w^{1/2}J(w)^{-1/2}$. It has exactly that
column and is inverted by $J$.

The resulting ten elements $y_1,\ldots,y_{10}$
form a free pro-three basis, with
$J(y_i)=y_i$ for $i\le4$ and
$J(y_i)=y_i^{-1}$ for $i>4$.
Here is the group-level justification.
Their reductions form a basis of $N/\Phi(N)$,
so their closed subgroup is all of $N$:
a proper closed subgroup lies in a maximal
open subgroup of index three. The resulting
endomorphism from the old free basis is
surjective and injective. To see the latter
directly, a finitely generated profinite
group has finitely many open normal
subgroups of each bounded index. Pullback
by a surjective endomorphism permutes each
such finite set. Some iterate therefore
fixes each chosen open normal subgroup,
and the endomorphism kernel belongs to it.
The intersection of those subgroups is one.
This also describes the inverse as the
compatible inverses on characteristic
finite three-group quotients. Thus the
construction is an actual continuous
change of free basis; the original
arithmetic operators are transported by
that isomorphism throughout.

\emph{Integral Hall deletion and the strict kernel.}
Take the standard degree-ordered Hall basis
in these generators. Its left-comb words
\[
 [y_i,y_j,y_{k_3},\ldots,y_{k_r}],
                 \qquad i>j\le k_3\le\cdots\le k_r
 \tag{E100.19}
\]
are exactly its Chen subset. Every other
Hall word contains an internal bracket
whose two children have degree at least
two. That internal group word is in $N''$;
normality then puts the whole Hall word
in $N''$, as an actual group element.

The continuous Fox isomorphism E41.13
identifies $N'/N''$ with
$\ker(\Lambda^{10}\xrightarrow{(X_i)}\Lambda)$.
E41.15 proves its exact homogeneous
filtration and saturation. Under this
actual map, (E100.19) has leading syzygy
\[
 (-1)^{r-2}X_{k_3}\cdots X_{k_r}
                         (X_i e_j-X_j e_i).
\]
Those are the integral Chen basis from
the homogeneous Koszul presentation;
the same basis and the literal Hall
subset are also displayed in
\cite[\S2.4, equation(2) and the following
paragraph, p5]{E_SatohChen}.
Thus no further integral relation occurs.
At every lower-central level, the actual
quotient of an ordered Hall product is
the ordered product obtained by deleting
all non-Chen factors. The remaining
Chen coordinates are unique, degree by
degree, because their graded modules
are free with the displayed basis.
Continuity extends the equality to all
$\mathbb Z_3$ powers and all limits.

In particular the non-Chen Hall words
give an integral basis of $K_r$ in
(E100.11). This is the induced group
filtration: if a leading Hall class
maps to zero, lift its higher-degree
image to $\gamma_{r+1}N$ and subtract
that lift. Conversely every element
of $R\cap\gamma_rN$ maps to zero.
The quotient $L_r(M)$ is free, so this
kernel is saturated. The preceding
literal Hall-word argument supplies
actual lifts in $R$ for all its basis
vectors. Both groups and $R$ are their
inverse limits for these filtrations:
intersection is one since finite
three-group quotients are nilpotent,
and compactness gives surjectivity
onto the nested inverse systems.

\emph{The anti coordinates and their exact quotient map.}
Write $H_r=H/\gamma_{r+1}H$ and let
$h_r:H_{r-1}\to H_r$ be its ordered
Hall or Chen lift with last-degree
coordinates zero. These lifts commute
with $N_r\to M_r$ by the just-proved
literal deletion map. For an anti point
$z\in H_{r-1}$, put
\[
\begin{aligned}
 u&=h_r(z),\qquad d=uJ(u)\in L_r(H),\\
 \widehat h_r(z)&=u\,d^{-1/2},\\
 z_r&=\widehat h_r(z_{r-1})\,\beta_r(a_r),
       \qquad a_r\in L_r(H)^-,\quad z_0=1 .
\end{aligned}
 \tag{E100.20}
\]
Here $\beta_r$ is the actual ordered product
of the negative degree-$r$ Hall or Chen
commutators, in the central top layer.
One has $J(d)=d$: indeed $J(u)=u^{-1}d$
and $d$ is central. Hence
$\widehat h_r(z)J(\widehat h_r(z))=1$.
Every other anti lift differs by one
and only one element of $L_r(H)^-$.
The inverse of (E100.20) takes the
lower image and then the Hall coordinates
of $z_r\widehat h_r(z_{r-1})^{-1}$.
This proves both directions at every
level, and the compatible limit gives
the homeomorphism (E100.6).

The sign of a Hall word is the product
of the signs of its letters. In the
chosen basis $J$ is diagonal on each
graded Hall or Chen basis, even though
its action on a full noncentral Hall
word also has higher terms. Formula
(E100.20) uses that full action.
Every operation in (E100.20) commutes
with the quotient: the zero-top lifts,
$J$, the central product, its unique
square root, and the retained negative
Hall coordinates. Consequently the
map on the anti coordinates is exactly
deletion of the non-Chen negative
coordinates. Applying the actual $T$
to both source and target gives the
same assertion for (E100.5).
This proves (E100.9) and constructs
an actual full lift of every ramified
metabelian target.

The ranks of the full graded basis
and its involution trace are the
Witt formulas in (E100.7).
The exact syzygy sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow L_r(M)\longrightarrow
 V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{r-1}V
           \longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^rV
 \longrightarrow0
 \tag{E100.21}
\]
is an integral sequence, by the preceding
Fox and Koszul map. Its rank and trace
give the metabelian formulas in (E100.7).
The non-Chen negative count is therefore
their exact difference, not a rational
dimension substituted for an integral
kernel. This proves the table.

For (E100.10), write a second lift of
$x_M$ as $r x_0$. Its anti equation is
exactly
$(\operatorname{Ad}(x_0)\mathcal L)(r)=r^{-1}$.
The operator $\mathcal L_0$ is an
involution because $x_0\mathcal L(x_0)=1$.
The map $g\mapsto g\mathcal L_0(g)^{-1}$
is onto: the preimage of $r$ can be
chosen to be $r^{1/2}$. Two such
preimages differ on the right by
$R^{\mathcal L_0}$. These are literal
equalities in $R$, and prove the
claimed fibre and quotient maps.

\emph{Zero inertia and every unramified lifting fibre.}
If $\nu=1$, the section kills the
geometric cocycle on the full closed
normal inertia subgroup generated by
$t$. Indeed $\Phi_t=1$, and the
cocycle identity then kills every
conjugate of $t$ and its closed normal
closure. It factors through the actual
quotient in (E100.2), so the zero-inertia
set is exactly the inflated E.41 set.
Inflation is injective on geometric
conjugacy since the source quotient is
onto. The transition to E.41's
$(\tau,\tau')$ coordinates is evaluation
on those actual elements of $P$, using
the inverse of its free-basis map
$(\sigma,\rho)\to P$. That inverse
exists by the E.99 Frattini-basis
proof and is the compatible inverse
on finite characteristic quotients.
This retains the same source marking
and all Frobenius words.

Fix an unramified target with E.41
representative $x_M$ at $\tau$.
Choose any actual lift $x_0\in N$.
The free arithmetic group $P$ gives
a full normalized section with that
value, as in E41.9. Put
$\Theta=\operatorname{Ad}(x_0)T$ on $R$.
The entire full fibre is the following
actual orbit space:
\[
 R/\!\sim_{\Theta},\qquad
 k\cdot r=k r\Theta(k)^{-1}\quad(k\in R^J),
       \qquad r\longmapsto r x_0 .
 \tag{E100.22}
\]
Here $\sim_\Theta$ is the displayed
orbit relation. To check that it
is the whole fibre, first align the
projected target by its unique
$M^J$-conjugator from E.41. It has a
fixed lift in $N$, by the same central
half-correction used above. All
remaining conjugators project to a
target stabilizer, which is one by
E41.17. They therefore belong to $R^J$.

The action of $\Theta$ on $K_r$ is
exactly $T_r$, since its inner factor
is trivial on this induced grade.
Every twisted stabilizer in (E100.22)
is trivial: its first nonzero graded
coordinate would be fixed by the
actual arithmetic action, whereas
the Frobenius weight argument of
E41.17 excludes such a vector.
Fixed lifts through the central
layers exist by division by two.
E.41's normal-form induction therefore
applies to the induced filtration of
$R$ with precisely the coefficient
quotients (E100.11), proving the
second line of (E100.13).

For clarity its linear maps and its
nonlinear comparison are both explicit.
Taking fixed parts of the strict sequence
$0\to K_r\to L_r(N)\to L_r(M)\to0$
is exact because two is a unit.
Apply $T_r-I$ to those fixed parts.
It is rationally injective on every
term, since the actual Frobenius at
seven has no eigenvalue one in
positive degree. The resulting
kernel-cokernel sequence is (E100.12).
For its inverse on the kernel, represent
a class by $v\in L_r(N)$ and solve
\[
 \pi_r(v)=(T_r(M)-I)b,\quad b\in L_r(M)^J;
 \quad v-(T_r(N)-I)\widetilde b\in K_r ,
 \tag{E100.23}
\]
where $\widetilde b$ is any integral
fixed lift of $b$. Uniqueness of $b$
and exactness prove independence of
the lift modulo $(T_r-I)K_r^J$.
This specifies every arrow in (E100.12).

Choose the pointed Hall--Smith
representatives of E.41 and a continuous
set section $\lambda_r$ of $\bar\pi_r$.
The latter is obtained from the actual
finite-rank Smith maps: lift free
basis vectors and use the chosen
residue digits for finite factors.
Suppose the source's lower coordinates
are fixed, with canonical word $x_{N,r-1}$,
and let $x_{M,r-1}$ be its target
normal form. Let $g_{r-1}\in M_{r-1}^J$
be the unique lower aligning conjugator.
Lift it by the actual fixed
half-correction to $\widehat g\in M_r^J$,
