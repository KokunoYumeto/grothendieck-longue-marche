structures the functors   $u\colon M_1 \hand{\to} M_2$  which give rise to a commutative
square of functors
\diagram{\begin{tikzcd}
M_1 \ar[r,"u"] \ar[d] & M_2 \ar[d] \\
\overline{M}_1 \ar[r,"\overline{u}"] & \overline{M}_2
\end{tikzcd}\quad ,}
