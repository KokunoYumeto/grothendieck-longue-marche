## Leads (by Claude Fable 5.2; unverified; prove, correct or refute each)

- **Lead 1 (the MDT-structure is the exit category in bimodule form).** Let `σ ⊇ τ` be multicurves on a surface of type `(g, n)` with graphs `G` and `G′ = G/C`, `C = σ ∖ τ`, and `Γ` the mapping class group. Then the fundamental group of `(ΠM_G, Γ_G)` is `Stab(σ)/Δ_σ`; that of `ΠM^*_{G,G′}` (with `Γ^!_{G,G′}`) is `Stab(σ, τ)/Δ_τ`, an extension of `Stab(σ, τ)/Δ_σ` by `Δ_σ/Δ_τ = Z^C`; `φ` and `γ` are the source and target of the groupoid of crossing arrows from the stratum of `G` to that of `G′`; his transitivity (105) is the composition law; and `S_AΠM_G` is the link of the stratum in the open stratum, with group `Stab(σ)`. So his system, for generization, is the exit category of the returns, presented by its automorphism groupoids and bimodules, and the system of the `S_AΠM_G` is the transporter category before division by the twists.
- **Lead 2 (forgetting at infinity).** Forgetting a marked point acts on MD-graphs by stabilization. On the lattices of meridians `Z^{A(G)} → Z^{A(st G)}` it sends the two edges of a contracted rational bridge to the same edge and the edge of a contracted rational tail to 0. The kernel in the first case is the `Z` of RESIDUE H.3. This is "l'effet à l'infini" of (148) that he struck out.
- **Lead 3 (the `BZ` and the filling map).** We asked whether these `BZ` explain why the kernel of the filling map of LM3b B.4–B.6 is larger than the point-pushing group. By RESIDUE H.5 the forgetful functor is smooth for `(0, 3)`: there is no `BZ` for 4 → 3, where LM3b B.5 already finds the failure. So at the first case the answer appears to be no, and the question is open from 5 → 4 on.
- **Lead 4 (levels in genus ≥ 1; our hunch H3 (iii)).** With `d = 3g − 3 + n` and `P_k` the poset of multicurves with at least `d − k` components, `P_k` is `(k − 1)`-connected for `k ≤ 2` (Hatcher–Thurston; Hatcher–Lochak–Schneps) and cannot be for all `k` when `g ≥ 2`, the curve complex having homotopy dimension `2g − 3 + n < d − 1` for `n > 0` (Harer). Guess: `P_k` is `(min(k, 2g − 2 + n) − 1)`-connected. By codimension (dictionary, RG1): the fundamental groupoid of the punctured neighbourhood of the boundary needs the strata of codimension ≤ 3, so Corollaire 3 of scan 152 must be corrected before it is applied to `M̂_{g,ν}`.
- **Lead 5 (the tower, from round 1 of our planning; S1).** For `2g − 2 + n > 0` let `E(g, n)` be the exit category of the Deligne–Mumford compactification stratified by topological type. (a) It is an ordinary category and its classifying space is the compactification (as a stack). (b) Its completion `Ê(g, n)` by finite congruences has as finite representations the finite étale sheaves constructible along the boundary. (c) Forgetting, clutching and relabelling are functors between the `Ê(g, n)`; the automorphism group of the genus-0 system with its decorations is `GT̂`, and the absolute Galois group of `Q` acts on it faithfully. (d) Conjecture: the automorphism group of the whole system is `GT̂`, and the system is generated in modular dimension ≤ 1 with relations in dimension 2. For the procongruence completions (d) is claimed in a 2026 preprint of Boggi, which we have read in its introduction only; for the full completions it needs the congruence subgroup property in genus ≥ 3, which is open.
- **Lead 6 (the maquette of the tower).** The category of components of his structure for `M̄_{g,n}` is the category of MD-graphs with generization morphisms; its classifying space is the boundary complex, the moduli space of tropical curves (Chan–Galatius–Payne 2021; from memory).
