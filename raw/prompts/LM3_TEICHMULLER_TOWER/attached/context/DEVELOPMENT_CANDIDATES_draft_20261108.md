# Development candidates from the checked notes of 36 packages (draft, 8 Nov 2026; not prompts; the book format is not yet settled with the user)

Manuscript pages are his folio numbers; "check" marks literature still to be verified.

1. **Arithmetic Teichmüller group at the base levels and the "conjecture hâtive" (27)/(28)** — scans 311–350, 371–380 (pp. 157–177, 188–191). This covers 𝕀Γ_{0,3} as the centraliser of 𝔖₃ ("et peut-être égal" to the subgroup fixing each puncture) and lifts commuting with the order-3 and order-2 symmetries, which look like the Drinfeld–Ihara relations (I), (II). **Partly known:** Belyi gives injectivity, Drinfeld and Ihara give G_Q ⊂ GT̂, Harbater–Schneps give GT̂ in terms of π̂₁(M₀,₅). Ihara's question is open. At type (0,4) the conjecture looks equivalent to Ihara's question, and at (0,3) it is probably false (both check).
2. **Filling a hole in the profinite setting** — scans 341–349, 395–420 (pp. 172–177, 200–212). His admitted isomorphism T̂̂(π,i) → Ĝ̂(π′), 𝕀Γ_{g,ν} ≅ 𝕀Γ_{g,ν−1}, the exact sequence marked "??" and the faithfulness (f) of Θ_{g,ν}. **Partly known:** (f) follows from Belyi in type (0,4) and probably from Matsumoto 1996 and Hoshi–Mochizuki 2011 in general (check). The isomorphism is a profinite Birman / cuspidalization statement, related to Hoshi–Mochizuki (check); otherwise open.
3. **Finite groups acting on profinite loop groups, and finite coverings** — scans 118, 136, 351–360, 381–400. This covers π^G = 1 ("admis dans le contexte profini"), discrete against profinite classes of lifts, "conjecturer sans vergogne", and his discretification claims under finite coverings. **Partly known:** trivial centralisers (Anderson, Nakamura); finite subgroups of profinite Fuchsian groups (Bridson–Conder–Reid, check); the discrete torsion-free extension claim is known. Profinite Nielsen realisation looks open.
4. **Finite groups acting on surfaces, discrete case (§15, §17, §18)** — scans 131–210 (pp. 64–105).
   - Fixed points against classes of sections ("Cette application est-elle bijective ??").
   - Whether the rotation multiplier μ is intrinsic to the extension ("J'ignore si…"), with his torus and elliptic-curve cases.
   - The lemme fondamental and his conjecture that it also holds in the two abelian cases.
   - The abandoned "Ultra fort" theorem and the lemma he marked "faux!".
   - "Ça doit être le seul contre-exemple."

   **Known in substance** (Kerékjártó–Eilenberg, Nielsen–Fenchel–Kerckhoff, NEC-group theory), but his specific questions still need deciding.
5. **The isotopy 2-category of surfaces with a finite group acting** — scans 261–300 (pp. 131–151). His conjecture that the 2-functor is an equivalence ("3-fidèle"), the conditions on the homeomorphism group A, the H⁰/H¹ vanishing, and the bijection and realisability criterion by drilling holes. **Known in pieces** (Kerckhoff, MacLachlan–Harvey, Birman–Hilden, Earle–Eells, Ladegaillerie for Γ = 1); I have not found the 2-categorical statement in the literature (check).
6. **Real structures** — pp. 74, 89, 168, 188–191, 221–238. Anti-involutions and real components against sections (margin conjecture p. 74), classes in 𝒯_g ("on l'espère!"), where the real punctures lie, and the canonical involutions τ with the Artin–Schreier facts. **Topological parts known** (Weichold); real section conjecture known over ℝ (Mochizuki 2003, Pál 2011); the GT̂ analogue is partly known or open (check). Pages 223–238 of §33 have not had their second pass.
7. **§20, the 2-isotopic description of spheres and sphere bundles** — scans 231–250 (pp. 116–125). The Gr-category and its splitting, the conflicting faithfulness bounds, "Au concours !", forms of ℙ¹ with homotopies through lifts to SL₂, and Azumaya algebras as a 2-category. **Topological part known** (Kneser; obstruction theory); arithmetic part partly known through A¹-homotopy theory (check).
