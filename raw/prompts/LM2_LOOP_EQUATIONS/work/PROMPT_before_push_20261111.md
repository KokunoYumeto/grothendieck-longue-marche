# LM2_LOOP_EQUATIONS: the equation of loops in types (0,3) and (1,1). Grothendieck's conditions on the image of Galois, against the Grothendieck–Teichmüller relations (§§36–47)

DRAFT, provisional (11 November 2026). Development prompt for Grothendieck, *La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois* (1981). File prefix for your deliverables: `LM2_LOOP_EQUATIONS`.

## What is to be settled

The second part of the manuscript, "Variations sur l'équation des lacets", tries to describe the image of `𝕀Γ_Q = Gal(Q̄/Q)` in the group of exterior automorphisms with loops of `π̂_{0,3}` by explicit equations. Its first half (§§38–47, cote 140-3, folios 291–428) is a long calculation, restarted several times with new notation:

- §37–38: moduli of elliptic curves through Legendre, `M_{1,1}[2] ≅ U_{0,3} = M^!_{0,4}`; the isomorphism `π_{0,3} ≅ S⁺_{0,3}` and the group `S_{0,3}` with its "avatars" (§42).
- §39: congruence properties of the action of `𝕀Γ_Q` on `π̂_{0,3}` linked to `N_{1,1} → GL(2, Ẑ)`.
- §41–§44: conditions for `π̂_{0,3} ≅ Ŝ⁺_{0,3}` to commute with the outer Galois actions; outer automorphisms of both groups; cocycles under `ρ` (order 3) and `σ_∞`; the "mise en équations" in `SL(2, Ẑ)`.
- §45: the homomorphism `M̃_{0,3} → GL(2, Ẑ)` and the extension `Ñ_{1,1}` of `𝕀Γ` by `SL(2, Z)^`.
- §46: "Notations définitives (?)": the groups `M̃_{0,3}`, `Ñ^!_{0,3}`; the three fundamental cases and the lifts `K_{1/2}, K_2, K_{−1}`; the subgroups `M̃^!_{0,3}(q)`. §47: the groups with double tilde.

What he asks or leaves open (cote 140-3 scans unless noted):
1. Are the congruence relations on `(0,3)` and `(1,1)` "de nature arithmétique", or consequences of a purely algebraic profinite formalism (scan 54)? "Toute une kyrielle" of conditions (57).
2. The triple of involutions and reflections `σ_∞, ρ` (9–10, 85); only two explicit solutions, `u = 1` and `u = τ_∞`: he cannot "en construire explicitement une seule" other (120).
3. Signs `ε = ±1` and class field theory, `ε' = +1` iff `p ≡ 1 (4)` (155); "je n'ai pas de caractérisation bien plausible à proposer" for `Γ_Q` inside `Γ̃` (170, 178, 185); the "groupe adélique" of triples satisfying the loop relation (204–205).
4. The conjectured unique splitting of the extension `M̃^!_{0,3}` (209); the torus condition `c² + cd + d² ∈ Ẑ*` (209, sign doubtful in the ink).
5. From cote 140-2, §36 (scans 505–524): is the arithmetic action on `𝒯̂⁺_{1,1} ≅ SL(2, Z)^` faithful "mod"; lifting through `±1` compatibly with `Γ`; the anabelian converse (a `Γ`-compatible group-theoretic lift comes from a geometric one, 523); "Je doute qu'il en existe une" (524).

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** An element of his `M̃_{0,3}` is a pair `(λ, f)` in Drinfeld's and Ihara's sense, `λ ∈ Ẑ*`, `f ∈ F̂_2'`, acting by `x ↦ x^λ`, `y ↦ f^{-1} y^λ f`. His conditions from the symmetries of order 2 and 3 of `P^1 − {0, 1, ∞}` (commutation with `𝔖_3`: the cocycle conditions under `σ` and `ρ`) are the 2-cycle and 3-cycle (hexagon) relations of `GT̂`. The 5-cycle (pentagon) relation, which lives on `M_{0,5}`, has no counterpart in §§38–47; his passage to `(1,1)` gives instead conditions of another kind (compare the relations of Nakamura–Schneps for the genus-1 levels).
- **Lead 2.** Item 1: the congruence properties are those coming from the map `F̂_2 → SL(2, Ẑ)` (the congruence kernel of `SL(2, Z)`), and the Galois action on `SL(2, Z)^` factors through data determined by `GT̂` (Ihara; Lochak–Schneps; Nakamura); which of his congruences hold for all of `GT̂_0` (2- and 3-cycle only), and which need the pentagon or are properly arithmetic, can be decided.
- **Lead 3.** Item 2: complex conjugation is the element `(−1, 1)`; no other element of `Gal(Q̄/Q)` or of `GT̂` is known explicitly, which agrees with his remark.
