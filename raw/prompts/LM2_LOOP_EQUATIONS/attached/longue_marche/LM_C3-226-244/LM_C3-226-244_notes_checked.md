### Scans 232–236

- **R11 — scan 233 — unproved claim — correct (the claim is true).** φ̂° sends ℓ₀, ℓ₁, ℓ∞ to σ∞ρ, σ∞, ρ⁻¹, of orders 2,

  order of the relation among ℓ₀, ℓ₁, ℓ∞ does not matter). Revised: proof sketch replaces "should establish equality".

- **R13 — scan 234 — unproved claim — correct.** Uniqueness of α with αρ(α)⁻¹ = ξ is equivalent to int(ρ) having no
  fixed point other than 1 in Π̃^_{0,3}; existence is the triviality of the class of the 1-cocycle given by ξ, whose

  p. 350). The "α ∈ Π̂_{0,3}" on the left of (16) is in the ink; Corollaire 1 and the right side have Π̃^_{0,3}.
- **R14 — scan 234 — own idea — keep.** The margin "en fait, on n'a pas vraiment besoin de α, β" is borne out by (18)

- **R15 — scan 235 — unproved claim — correct.** Corrected reading: "Les automorphismes à lacets de Ĝ⁺_{0,3}". The
  proof is on scan 236 ("En effet…"); it uses two facts not proved there: f normalises L₀ only if f = ℓ₀ⁿ (the
  normaliser of L₀ in Π̂_{0,3} is L₀), and ε₀ⁿ = 1 forces n = 0 (ε₀ of infinite order, ⟨ε₀⟩ torsion-free).
