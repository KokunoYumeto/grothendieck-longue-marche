## New notes — Claude (second pass)

- **N1 — scans 41–51 — own idea.** The ν₂ convention changes. Scans 42–43 have μ = 2ν+1 = 4ν'+2ν₂+1, and (51) has the
  slip 4ν'+2+ν₂. Scans 50–51 have μ = 4ν'−2ν₂+1, in (63) and the scan-50 margin, and the signs in ν = 2ν' ∓ ν₂ on
  scans 41 and 47 are overwritten. Because ν₂ is idempotent, 4ν'+2ν₂ = 4(ν'+ν₂) − 2ν₂: the two conventions differ by
  the reparametrisation ν' ↦ ν'+ν₂ and give the same μ and the same μ ≡ 1−2ν₂ (4).
- **N2 — scans 39–50 — own idea.** The ν₃ sign is not a mere reparametrisation. (28) and (29) put ζ = α₀−ρ(α₀)−λ₁ for
  ν₃ = 1, and scan 50's 2ν'−ν₂+ν₃ ≡ 0 (3) follows from that sign. (33), (35) and (45) write +ν₃λ₁, and scan 47's
  "vrai par hypothèse (cf (31))" needs + in (31). Since 2λ₁ ∉ Im(1−ρ_M), the two signs give different conditions
  mod 3. The overwritten signs in (31) and (32) record the hesitation and stay unresolved.
- **N3 — scan 51 — unproved claim (false as written).** (67) b) states n+N[λ] = n'+N[λ'] ⟺ n+Nλ = n'+Nλ' in ΓG_a.
  Under the contracted product (66), n+N[λ] equals (n+Nk)+N[λ−k] only for locally constant integers k. Equal images in
  G_a therefore do not give equal sections: over Q with N = 2, 0+2[0] and 1+2[−1/2] both map to 0 but are distinct.
  The map to (Z/NZ)^* in (74) and the kernel _N G_a on scans 54–55 both rest on the finer relation; read literally,
  (67) b) would make 𝕆_N → G_a a monomorphism.
- **N4 — scan 45 — own idea (check).** The struck identification 𝔊₀⁺ ≃ SL(2,Z) = {ρ,σ | ρ⁶ = σ⁴ = 1, ρ³ = σ²} is
  correct: SL(2,Z) ≅ Z/4 *_{Z/2} Z/6, with σ = S of order 4 and ρ = ST of order 6. So (48) is the universal property
  of this amalgam, and 𝔊⁺ is its quotient by the normal subgroup generated by [σ,λ_∞].
- **N5 — scans 48–51 — unpursued implication.** The loop equation (1−ρ_M)α₀ − (1−σ_M)β₀ = cλ₁ can be solved locally
  exactly when c ∈ 3·O_S (R06). The struck revision on scan 50 replaces c = 2ν'−ν₂+ν₃ by 3ν'' with
  2ν'−ν₂ = 3ν''−ν₃, and so makes the parameter map onto. This removes the char-3 non-surjectivity that the scan-48
  margin calls "un peu cavalier" and "canular en car. 3". It is the motive for the auxiliary 𝕆_N^* construction that
  follows.
- **N6 — scans 54–55 — unproved claim (incomplete).** "Son noyau est isomorphe au syst. des (77)" covers only the
  sections with n ≡ 1 (N). A section n+N[λ] with n ≢ 1 and n+Nλ = 1 also lies in the kernel of (76): over Q with
  N = 3, 2+3[−1/3] maps to 1. Over Z[1/N] the kernel of (76) is the constant group (Z/NZ)^* (for N = 12, μ×μ), not
  zero. Exactly (77) ≅ _N G_a is the kernel of 𝕆_N^* → G_m × (Z/NZ)^*_{S₀}, the map that (79)–(81) and the Proposition
  (88) go on to use: the "maladresse" of scan 55 in another guise.
