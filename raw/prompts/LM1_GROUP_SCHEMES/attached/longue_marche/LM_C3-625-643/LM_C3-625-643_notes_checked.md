## New notes — Claude (second pass)

**C1. Scans 628–634 — own idea.** By (28), the margin of scan 631 and the NB of scan 633, 𝒢̃_3 has order 12, a central μ, a normal Z/6Z (kernel of sg) and an element σ of order 4 over a transposition: it is the dicyclic group Z/3 ⋊ Z/4, the pull-back of Z/4Z → μ along sg: 𝒢_3 → μ. The obstruction to lifting any φ: Γ_Q → 𝒢_3 to 𝒢̃_3 is therefore the pull-back of the class of Z/4Z along sg∘φ, which is the character of P_0 ≃ Spec Q(j), so it is the same nonzero class for T and for every epimorphism (already at the real place). In the model (27)–(28), with 𝒢̃_3 acting through 𝒢_3, j = 0 would have no Q-rational lift to M_{1,1}, contradicting y² = x³ + 1 and confirming the doubt of scan 628; the model must need a twist over Q (the Legendre substitutions such as λ ↦ 1/λ act on curves only after extracting √λ). Confidence: high for the group theory, medium for the reading of the model.

**C2. Scans 630–632 — own idea.** For the constant extension E″ ≃ Z/4Z and the character χ of Q(j) = Q(√−3), the obstruction is the cup square χ ∪ χ, the quaternion class (−3,−3) = (−3,−1) in ₂Br(Q), ramified exactly at 3 and ∞. This matches the margin argument at 3 (scan 631) and the identification H^2(Q,μ_2) ≃ ₂Br(Q) (scan 630). Over Q(√3) the class still ramifies at both real places, so it does not split there, contrary to the statement on scan 632 unless a different extension is meant. Confidence: high.
