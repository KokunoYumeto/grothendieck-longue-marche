- **N1 — scan 549 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** In (6) the sign is ⊂ (changes, row 3), so "C'est un

  modular case (12) is the model in which {±1} ↪ 𝒢⁺ and L_ρ∩L_σ = {±1} hold.
- **N3 — scan 550 — unpursued implication — keep.** (10) follows from (6) because inversion preserves the cyclic
  subgroups L_ρ and L_σ. The converse is not claimed.
- **N4 — scan 550 — unproved claim — keep.** The text reads "est injectif, donc μ est connu" (row 5), which does not

- **N5 — scan 551 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** Both signs in (14) are inclusions on the image. The argument

### Scans 554–558

- **N10 — scan 554 — unproved claim — correct.** (31) defines i_𝒢 by its value on τ: the prime of τ′ is struck
  (row 11), with τ_Z = (int(τ)|𝒢⁺, −1, −1, −1). Extending it to an embedding needs τ_Z ∈ Z_{ρ,σ}, which the margin
  asserts ("τ′_G ∉ Z_{ρ,σ} par contre on a τ_G ∈ Z_{ρ,σ}"). It also needs i_Z(τ_Z)² = i_{𝒢⁺}(τ²) and conjugation by

- **N11 — scan 554 — unpursued implication — keep.** The margin characterises i_𝒢(𝒢) as the inverse image of

- **N13 — scan 555 — unproved claim — keep.** The maps 𝔻_ρ → N_ρ and 𝔻_σ → N_σ are called "évidents". The relations
  needed are τ′ρτ′⁻¹ = ρ⁻¹ and τ′στ′⁻¹ = σ⁻¹ (scan 548, (*)).
- **N14 — scan 555 — unproved claim — correct.** The predicate is legible as "⟺ 𝒢⁺ est isom à un quotient de Z/4Z",
  with no negation. Suppose, as in the modular case, that 𝒢⁺ is topologically generated by ρ and σ with ρ³ = σ² = −1.
  Then ρ = −1 makes 𝒢⁺ = ⟨σ⟩ a quotient of Z/4Z, and conversely a cyclic 𝒢⁺ of order dividing 4 forces ρ² = 1, i.e.
  ρ = −1. So the last term of a chain that begins "i_ρ injectif ⟺ … ρ ≠ −1" should read "n'est pas isom". As written,
  it states the opposite (a slip, or a negation lost in the revision).
- **N15 — scan 556 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The corrected text reads "des épimorphismes. On a donc
  diagramme comm. de suites exactes (41)" (row 16), and before (43) "dc diag. comm. de suites exactes" (row 19). The
  page therefore asserts the commutativity of (41) and (43), as well as the surjectivity of δ_ρ and δ_σ.
- **N16 — scan 556 — conjecture (author's doubt) — keep, corrected.** The margin's reservation, "Mais celà ne prouve
  pas que l'on ait commutativité!", faces the main text's "On a donc diagramme comm.". The margin computation shows

- **N17 — scan 556 — own idea (first pass) — keep.** The struck conditions a) ρ² ≠ 1 and b) σ² ≠ 1 were replaced by

- **C3 — scan 556 — own idea, Claude (second pass).** The right-hand squares of (41) and (43) commute by the definition
  (40) of the starred groups. For x ∈ N*_ρ, ε′_γ(δ_ρ(x)) = ε′_γ(δ_G(i_ρ(x))) = ε′_G(i_ρ(x)) = ε_ρ(x) by (28), and in

  N_ρ, and the inserted star condition is what removes it. Confidence: high for the right-hand squares, medium for the
  left.
