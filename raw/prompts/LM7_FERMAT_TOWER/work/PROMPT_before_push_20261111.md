## What is to be settled

§51, "Exemple: la tour de Fermat" (cote 140-4, folios 664–737), applies the schematic envelope of §50 to the quotient `𝔖` of `GL(2, Z)` (his table of contents writes `SL(2, Z)`) by `[Π_0, Π_0]`, where `Π_0 ≅ π_{0,3}` is the free group of rank 2 generated by `λ_i = −ℓ_i`, `ℓ_0 = (1 −2; 0 1)`, `ℓ_1 = (1 0; 2 1)` (cote 140-4, scan 20; it lifts the level-2 subgroup modulo `±1`, and has index 12 in `SL(2, Z)`, 24 in `GL(2, Z)`). The abelianized kernel `M = Π_0^{ab} ≅ (Z(0) + Z(1) + Z(∞))/diagonale` carries the action of the finite quotient; the Fermat curve `F_n` is the abelian covering of `P^1 − {0, 1, ∞}` with group `(Z/n)^2`, so the tower of Fermat curves is what the metabelian quotient of `π̂_{0,3}` sees. His subsections: the provisional `Z_{ρ,σ}`; the groups `O*_N` (units of a ring scheme `O_N`); the sequence `1 → N^{0+}_{ρ,σ} → N_{ρ,σ} → O*_{12} → 1` and the relation with `GL(2, Z/12Z)`; `M_{ρ,σ}` through `N_{ρ,σ}`; `M_{ρ,σ}(0)`, `M_{ρ,σ}(1/2)`, `M_{ρ,σ}(−j̄)`; the homomorphism `M̃ → M_{ρ,σ}(Ẑ)` and the Fermat tower, "position du problème" (51.11); the splitting of the extension `G_{ρ,σ}/μ_G` of `Ẑ* × 𝔖_3` by `M̂` (51.12); the "[mo]tivisation" of the Fermat tower (51.13; the first letters of the word are unread); generalized Jacobians (51.14); the interpretation of `𝕀Γ_Q → N_σ ≅ Ẑ* · Ẑ` and a digression on Kummer extensions (51.15).

What he asks or marks (cote 140-4 scans):
1. Level 12 and the order 32 (scan 64); why `GL(2, Z/12Z)` appears.
2. The relation with "la tour des courbes de Fermat" (95–97, 135–145); "Je n'aurais certes pas suspecté" a splitting (136); the freeness of the action of `μ_n^{0,1,∞}` that he first claims and then strikes out (98).
3. `𝕀Γ_Q →` an extension of `Ẑ*` by `Ẑ` (157); the Kummer-type maps: surjective, or with open image; linear independence for distinct primes (167–170).

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** The object he is computing is the Galois action on `π̂_{0,3}/[π̂', π̂']`, studied by Ihara (1986) for the pro-`ℓ` case: it is given by a universal power series `F_σ ∈ Z_ℓ[[u, v]]^×` whose special values are Jacobi sums, with Anderson's and Coleman's explicit formulas and the Anderson–Ihara theory of higher circular units. His extension of `Ẑ*` by `Ẑ` and his Kummer maps are the "Kummer characters" at the roots of `1 − ζ`; surjectivity and independence statements are then statements about circular units (known in the form of Ihara, Anderson, Coleman, Ihara–Kaneko–Yukinari; exact forms to be checked).
- **Lead 2.** The number 12 is the index of `Π_0` in `SL(2, Z)` (and the order of the abelianization of `SL(2, Z)`); the appearance of `GL(2, Z/12Z)` and the "order 32" should come out of an explicit computation with the finite quotient `GL(2, Z)/Π_0` of order 24 acting on `M ⊗ Z/N`.
- **Lead 3.** His "[mo]tivisation" (51.13) and "Jacobiennes généralisées" (51.14) describe `M̂ ⊗` as the Tate module of the generalized Jacobian of the Fermat curve with modulus the cusps, a 1-motive in Deligne's sense, with complex multiplication by `Z[μ_n]^2`; the splitting of 51.12 should be the statement that the weight filtration (toric part against abelian part) splits over the cyclotomic field up to bounded torsion.
