## What is to be settled

Then:

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** Conjecture 5.5 with its correction in §9 is the Hom- or Isom-form of the anabelian conjecture for hyperbolic curves, proved by Tamagawa (affine, finite fields and number fields) and Mochizuki (sub-`p`-adic fields); what §9 corrects is the role of tangential base points, that is, sections "at infinity". The exact relation between his system-of-groupoids formulation and the modern statements is to be written down.
- **Lead 2.** Item 2 is the section conjecture in a 1981 form that differs in detail from the 1983 one (admissibility conditions; Frobenius conditions). Known: injectivity (his own argument is the standard one, by Mordell–Weil and the Kummer sequence); the real case (Mochizuki; Wickelgren; Pál); the birational `p`-adic case (Koenigsmann; Pop); conditional and local results (Stix's monograph). Open in general.
- **Lead 3.** Item 3: the topological side is Weichold's and Harnack's classification of real curves; the bijection for anti-involutions is the real section conjecture, a theorem; the maximal free abelian subgroups of mapping class groups were determined by Birman–Lubotzky–McCarthy (1983).
- **Lead 4.** Item 4 anticipates the nilpotent approach to rational points: Kim's non-abelian Chabauty method on unipotent quotients of `π_1`, Ellenberg's and Wickelgren's 2-nilpotent section obstructions for `P^1 − {0, 1, ∞}`, Deligne's study of the unipotent fundamental group of the projective line minus three points. His first "approximation de Lie" in §51 is the metabelian quotient `π̂/[π̂', π̂']` restricted to the Fermat tower (compare Ihara 1986 and Anderson–Ihara).
