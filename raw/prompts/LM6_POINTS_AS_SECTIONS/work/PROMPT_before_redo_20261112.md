## What is to be settled

The manuscript states, two years before the letter to Faltings, what became the anabelian conjectures, in a form of its own. §5 introduces "le contexte arithmétique" and the "conjecture anabélienne fondamentale": Conjecture 5.5 (scan 23) that the functor from anabelian curves over `K` (with a marked finite set `S`) to the associated systems of groupoids is fully faithful, with his later margin "C'est un peu faux cf §9", and the pointed form 5.5 bis; §6 the local analysis at `s ∈ S`; §7 a reformulation he calls "bordélique", with Kummer data ("comment en termes de groupes extérieurs exprimer les données de Kummer k_i ?", scans 47–48); §8 a taxonomy of cases; §9 the tangential structure at the points of `S` and "sections d'extensions de deuxième type"; §10 adjusted hypotheses; §11 conditions on the systems of groupoids that come from geometry; §13 the Galois formulation. Then:

1. Scan 65: "Ceci suggère une approche de la conjecture de Mordell" (finiteness of conjugacy classes of splittings for `g ≥ 2`), and "Idem pour une approche du th. de Fermat". **Asked:** state the finiteness he means, prove which implications hold between it, the section conjecture and Faltings's theorem, and prove finiteness of classes of splittings unconditionally in the cases within reach (Part D).
2. §32 and scans 404–436, 480–488: points as admissible lifts; his axioms ① to ⑥; "Je dis que cette application est injective"; pathological lifts (413); conditions "faisant intervenir les Frobenius" and an intrinsic description of admissible outer actions (480); admissible germs of splittings and the algebraic points of a moduli object (488); the distinctness criteria for `g > 0` and `g = 0` (487); the withdrawn "Conjecture provisoire profinie". **Asked:** prove injectivity under his axioms; construct the pathological lifts of scan 413; decide which Frobenius conditions make "admissible" equal to "geometric" and test them on the tangential sections of the tripod; decide the statement of scan 488 for the types `(0,4)` and `(1,1)` as far as it can be decided.
3. §33 and scans 136–148, 436–470: anti-involutions of compact oriented surfaces; `π_0(U^Γ) →` classes of sections for an anti-involution, "Cette application est-elle bijective ??" (139, 148); recover algebraically the position of the points at infinity on the components of `X^σ` (437, margin); classes in `𝒯_g`, "Nous montrerons (on l'espère !)" (440); anti-involutions of a cylinder (457); "Ce sont peut-être les sous-groupes abéliens-libres maximaux" (470), with the strips as rank-one subgroups. **Asked:** prove the bijection of scans 139 and 148 in the discrete and in the profinite setting; prove the group-theoretic recovery of the points at infinity; prove the classes of scan 440 distinct; classify the maximal free abelian subgroups and decide scan 470.
4. §51.16 (cote 140-4, scans 172–178, "Réflexions sur le théorème de Fermat"): "si nous arrivions à prouver l'inexistence de scindages de (371) autres que ceux provenant des pts à l'infini, le th. de Fermat serait prouvé pour U_n, même sans avoir établi le yoga anabélien"; "il n'est nullement exclu qu'une analyse assez poussée de M_{0,3} et N_{1,1} … impliquant aussi des approximations de plus en plus fines de π_1(U_n)" would decide it; the richer extension (372) by `Γ_Q × 𝔖_3` and its quotient (373) in the Lie approximation of §51. **Asked:** write the extensions (371)–(373) exactly; compute the sections of each approximation for small `n` by program; determine the first level at which a non-cuspidal section is obstructed.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** Conjecture 5.5 with its correction in §9 is the Hom- or Isom-form of the anabelian conjecture for hyperbolic curves, proved by Tamagawa (affine, finite fields and number fields) and Mochizuki (sub-`p`-adic fields); what §9 corrects is the role of tangential base points, that is, sections "at infinity". The exact relation between his system-of-groupoids formulation and the modern statements is to be written down.
- **Lead 2.** Item 2 is the section conjecture in a 1981 form that differs in detail from the 1983 one (admissibility conditions; Frobenius conditions). Known: injectivity (his own argument is the standard one, by Mordell–Weil and the Kummer sequence); the real case (Mochizuki; Wickelgren; Pál); the birational `p`-adic case (Koenigsmann; Pop); conditional and local results (Stix's monograph). Open in general.
- **Lead 3.** Item 3: the topological side is Weichold's and Harnack's classification of real curves; the bijection for anti-involutions is the real section conjecture, a theorem; the maximal free abelian subgroups of mapping class groups were determined by Birman–Lubotzky–McCarthy (1983).
- **Lead 4.** Item 4 anticipates the nilpotent approach to rational points: Kim's non-abelian Chabauty method on unipotent quotients of `π_1`, Ellenberg's and Wickelgren's 2-nilpotent section obstructions for `P^1 − {0, 1, ∞}`, Deligne's study of the unipotent fundamental group of the projective line minus three points. His first "approximation de Lie" in §51 is the metabelian quotient `π̂/[π̂', π̂']` restricted to the Fermat tower (compare Ihara 1986 and Anderson–Ihara).
