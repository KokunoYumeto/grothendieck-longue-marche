## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

The first three are results of the review, by the author of this prompt. Their dimensions are exact computations (`LM3_review/checks_author/check_lm3.py` and `.out`; do not rely on them: recompute); the arguments were read by no one else.

Notation: `F` is the free pro-ℓ group on `x_1, …, x_r`, `x_0 = (x_1 ⋯ x_r)^{-1}`, the pro-ℓ group of type `(0, r+1)`. A *special* automorphism sends every `x_i`, `0 ≤ i ≤ r`, to a conjugate of itself. A special derivation of degree `n` of the free Lie algebra on `x_1, …, x_r` is `D(x_i) = [a_i, x_i]` with `a_i` of degree `n` and `Σ_{i ≥ 1} [a_i, x_i] = [a_0, Σ_{i ≥ 1} x_i]`; `OutSDer_n` is their space modulo inner derivations.

- **Lead 1 (lifting).** Every special derivation over `Z_ℓ` is the leading term of a special automorphism of `F`: lift the `a_i`, and correct the defect of `Π a_i x_i a_i^{-1} = a_0 (x_1 ⋯ x_r) a_0^{-1}` degree by degree, using that `(b_i) ↦ Σ [b_i, x_i] − [b_0, Σ x_i]` maps `⊕ gr_{m−1}` onto `gr_m`. The same inside the automorphisms inducing the identity modulo the normal closure of `x_1`, because the ideal `I = (x_1)` satisfies `I_m = [Lie_{m−1}, x_1] + Σ_{i ≠ 1} [I_{m−1}, x_i]`.
- **Lead 2 (B.7 fails pro-ℓ).** Type `(0,3)`: `dim OutSDer_n = 0, 0, 1, 0, 3, 0, 6, 4` for `n = 1, …, 8` (it is `2 l_n − l_{n+1}` for `n ≥ 2`, `l_n` the dimension of the free Lie algebra of rank 2), and the `𝔖_3`-invariants have dimensions `0, 0, 1, 0, 1, 0, 2, 1`. In weight 5 the representation is the trivial one plus the 2-dimensional one. A special automorphism with a non-invariant leading term of weight 5 does not commute with `𝔖_3` in `Out`; since `𝔖_3` meets the normal subgroup of label-preserving automorphisms trivially, `𝔖_3` is not normal in `Out_lac` of the pro-ℓ group, for every ℓ.
- **Lead 3 (C.8 fails pro-ℓ).** Type `(0,4) → (0,3)`: `dim OutSDer_n(0,4) = 2, 1, 6, 6` for `n = 1, …, 4`; the Lie algebra generated by the two point-pushing derivations of degree 1 (`D(x) = [y, x]`, `D(y) = [x, y]`, `D(z) = 0`, and the same with `z`) has dimensions `2, 1, 2, 3`; the kernel of filling `x` has dimensions `2, 1, 5, 6` and the image `0, 0, 1, 0`. In weight 3 there are three dimensions of derivations inducing zero on the filled algebra modulo inner and point-pushing ones; lifting one inside the kernel (Lead 1) gives an outer automorphism in the kernel of filling that is not point-pushing. So the pro-ℓ form of the sequence "??" is not exact in the middle.
- **Lead 4 (graded strictness).** If the `𝔖_3`-invariant special outer derivations are the graded form of `GT_0` (relations (I) and (II), no pentagon), then `GT ≠ GT_0` on graded pieces in weight 7, where the invariants have dimension 2 and `grt_1` has dimension 1. The identification is not checked.
- **Lead 5 (an abelian quotient would refute the conjecture).** `Gal(Q̄/Q)^ab = Ẑ^×`. So `Gal(Q̄/Q) = GT̂` forces `GT̂^ab = Ẑ^×` through `λ`. In the pro-ℓ graded picture the coinvariants of `λ` on an abelian piece of weight `w` are `Z_ℓ/(λ^w − 1 : λ ∈ Z_ℓ^×)`, which is non-zero when `ℓ − 1` divides `w` (for ℓ = 2 in every weight). A continuous character of `GT̂` not factoring through `λ` would be a proof that `Gal(Q̄/Q) ≠ GT̂`; its absence in a computed range is evidence the other way. A hunch, not an inference.
- **Lead 6 (the weight argument for D.23).** For a procyclic `L = ⟨z⟩^-` in `π_1(U_K̄)` whose normalizer in `π_1(U)` has open image in `G_K`, the normalizer acts on `L` through a character `ψ`. Choose an open subgroup `H ∋ z` with `z ∉ [H, H]^-`; then `ψ` occurs in `H^ab`, an extension of the Tate module of a Jacobian (weight `−1`) by copies of `Ẑ(1)` (weight `−2`). If the image of `z` lies in the toric part, `ψ` is cyclotomic and Nakamura's theorem (LM3 D.3) makes `L` an inertia group. The other case needs a Galois-stable line in a Tate module: complex multiplication, at a split prime. Whether such a line lifts to a procyclic subgroup of `π_1` with open normalizer image is the question.
