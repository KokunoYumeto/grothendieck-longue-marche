# LM5_MULTI_GALOIS_TOPOI: the topoi of the Longue Marche. Multi-Galois topoi, the topos at the missing points, van Kampen and hole-filling, and the 2-isotopic description (§§1–4, 14, 16, 20)

DRAFT, provisional (11 November 2026); waits for the second pass of the pilot packages (cote 140-2, scans 1–100). Development prompt for Grothendieck, *La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois* (1981). File prefix for your deliverables: `LM5_MULTI_GALOIS_TOPOI`.

## What is to be settled

The manuscript opens with topoi, not with groups: §1 "Topos multigaloisiens", §2 "Application aux revêtements des topos", §3 "Variantes pro-multigaloisiennes", §4 "Compléments, remords". The fundamental groupoid of a topos enters as the multi-Galois topos of its locally constant objects, with no base point chosen, and the systems of groupoids of §§5–11 are diagrams of such topoi. Three later sections return to topoi:

- §14, "Digression cohomologique (sur le bouchage de trous)" (cote 140-2, scans 116–134): for a curve `X` and the open `U = X − S` he introduces a topos `B_{X,U}` (he calls it "topos abracadabra") built from `U` and the local data at the points of `S`, and asks whether its cohomology is computed "galoisiennement" and whether `H^*(B_{X,U}, F) → H^*(X_ét, f^*F)` is an isomorphism for torsion `F` (scan 124); he rejects it in genus 0 for locally constant coefficients, "Non, à cause du genre 0 !!!" (129).
- §16, "Bouchage et forage de trous: préliminaires topologiques généraux" (scans 158–178): a van Kampen theorem for topoi; 2-cartesian squares of topoi (166); the expected acyclicity of `B_{D;S} → B_D` and `B_{X;S} → B_X`.
- §20, "Digression: description 2-isotopique de la catégorie des isomorphismes topologiques" (scans 231–260): Gr-categories describing spheres and sphere bundles up to 2-isotopy (232–239); neutrality of a gerbe, "brr !", to consult Giraud or Breen (240–241); bounds on fidelity in terms of `dim S`, which conflict (247–248), "Au concours !"; what a homotopy means arithmetically for forms of `P^1`: "sans doute un relèvement de u en une section du revêtement simplement connexe" (249); the 2-category of Azumaya algebras (250); "bien profond" for simple connectedness (259).

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** A multi-Galois topos in his sense is the classifying topos of a groupoid with (pro)finite or discrete automorphism groups, equivalently a topos generated by locally constant objects satisfying a finiteness or local-connectedness condition; §§1–3 are then the Galois theory of SGA 1, Exposé V, and SGA 4, Exposé IV, freed from base points and connectedness, and coincide with later treatments (Moerdijk's prodiscrete Galois theory; Bunge; the fundamental pro-groupoid of a locally connected topos; Leroy). Whatever he states beyond these is to be found and proved.
- **Lead 2.** `B_{X,U}` is the topos-theoretic form of "`U` together with its punctured formal neighbourhoods at `S`", glued along the local fundamental groups: in present terms the Kummer-étale or logarithmic topos of `(X, S)`, or the root-stack limit, or Deligne's tangential base point construction made into a space. With that identification his cohomological question has a known answer (Kummer-étale cohomology with torsion coefficients agrees with the étale cohomology of `U`, not of `X`), and the genus-0 failure he notes should be explained by it.
- **Lead 3.** His van Kampen theorem is the statement that the fundamental-groupoid functor (the multi-Galois reflection) sends suitable 2-pushouts of topoi to 2-pushouts; forms of it are in the literature for locally connected topoi and for étale fundamental groups (Stix's and others' van Kampen theorems), and the effective-descent hypotheses matter.
- **Lead 4.** §20 computes the homotopy 2-type of the group of homeomorphisms of the sphere and of sphere bundles (classically `O(3)`, by Kneser and Smale), and its arithmetic form asks for the étale or motivic homotopy type of forms of `P^1`; Azumaya algebras of degree 2 as a 2-category is the 2-gerbe-theoretic description of `B PGL_2`.

C.3: `BX` is the homotopy pushout of `BA ← BE → BB`. C.8: for locally constant coefficients in any complete and cocomplete ∞-category, in particular for sets, `Fun(BX, 𝒞)` is the pullback of `Fun(BA, 𝒞) → Fun(BE, 𝒞) ← Fun(BB, 𝒞)`; for sets this is a van Kampen theorem for the fundamental groupoid of the glued topos, with the link as the third term.
