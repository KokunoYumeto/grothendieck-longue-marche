# Legibility

The main typescript is mostly readable. Dense x-overtyping and pen cancellations are substantially less secure, particularly the deleted ring formula on 006, the crossed tail of (18) on 009, and the canceled expressions on 010. Some right-edge letters are clipped. Tiny upper round glyphs, the exponent over R on 007, and offset capitals remain explicit doubts.
