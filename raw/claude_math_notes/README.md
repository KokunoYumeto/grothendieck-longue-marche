# Claude's mathematical notes

Working notes kept by Claude (Claude Opus 5.5 at effort max, an AI model made by Anthropic) in the main session of
the user's research programme, from 15 November 2026. They record what came in (returns of GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro
mode), checks of other chatbots' answers, books and papers read) and what Claude made of it: its own arguments, its
hunches, and the questions it queued for other models. They are raw provenance, not edited for publication, and
nothing in them is refereed.

## What is here

- `NOTES_20261115.md`: the notes, in numbered sections with dates and times (UTC). Every statement is labelled:
  [thm] a theorem, [read] read in a named source, [recalled] from memory and not checked against a source, [mine]
  Claude's own argument or computation, [hunch] a guess stated as one. A later section corrects an earlier one where
  it says so (for example §15 corrects §6).
- `PROGRAMS.md`: which notes belong to the S6 programme (a complex structure on the six-sphere), which to the
  Grothendieck programme (La Longue Marche, Les Dérivateurs, the Montpellier archive), and which lie outside both and
  are kept as a separate thing for now. It says nothing about which of them is worth pursuing.
- `FOR_FABLE.md`: questions queued for Claude Fable 5.2 agents, with the answers when they came.
- `FOR_ASTRA.md`: candidate items for GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode) prompts.
- `PROMPT_UPDATES.md`: changes Claude made to unsent prompts, and changes it proposes.
- `consults/`: the answers of Claude Fable 5.2 agents at effort high to small consults from these notes.
- `checks/`: the scripts behind the exact checks quoted in the notes (Python with SymPy and mpmath), and the script
  that compares the results tables of successive checkpoints.

Paths in the notes are local paths on the machine where the work runs; the returns they point to are published in
`raw/pro_returns/` of grothendieck-longue-marche and in `pro_returns/` of s6-complex-structure-workbench.
