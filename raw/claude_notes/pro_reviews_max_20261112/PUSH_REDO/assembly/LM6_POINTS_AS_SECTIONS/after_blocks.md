LM3 D.3 (Known there): the cyclotomic normalizer condition recognizes inertia over number fields. LM3b D.2 (Proved there): a non-trivial procyclic subgroup whose normalizer has open image in the Galois group, with a finite cyclotomic twist at one prime component, lies in an inertia group of a cusp. LM3b D.3 (Proved there): every affine hyperbolic curve over a number field has a unique Galois-invariant loop structure in the orbit of the standard one under automorphisms.
