# Translation aids for the English *La Longue Marche*

The translation aids of *La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois* are shared with *Les Dérivateurs* and kept
in one place, the repository of *Les Dérivateurs*:

- current version: [grothendieck-derivateurs/raw/translation_aids](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/grothendieck-derivateurs/tree/main/raw/translation_aids)
- as first published (commit `c5c918c`, 12 Nov 2026): [grothendieck-derivateurs@c5c918c/raw/translation_aids](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/grothendieck-derivateurs/tree/c5c918c3c15b04cf1d0765e09dd15e8524be5b7e/raw/translation_aids)

What is there:

| Folder | What it is | Made | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| `translation_glossary_20261110/` | Terminology, notation and typesetting tables for both works (`GLOSSARY_LM.csv`, 116 entries, with his abbreviations; `NOTATION_LM.csv`; `TYPESETTING.csv`; the same for *Les Dérivateurs*), the decision files and scripts they are built from, and the inventories of the French transcriptions | 10 Nov 2026 | Claude Fable 5.2 (a Claude Code subagent) |
| `translation_comparison_20261111/` | Five scans of *La Longue Marche* (package LM_P049, after its second pass) and five edition pages of *Les Dérivateurs* translated by eight model configurations with one prompt, checked by script and graded blind; it chose GPT-6.1 Sol at reasoning effort max for both works | 10–11 Nov 2026 | Claude Opus 5.5 (set-up, runs, checks); translations by Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol; blind grading by Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 |
