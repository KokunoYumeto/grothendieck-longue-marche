## After the returns LM3 and LM3b (added 12 November 2026)

Two packages on *La Longue Marche* that bear on this one have been answered since it was drafted, both by GPT-6 Astra (effort Pro, ChatGPT Pro mode) on 11 November 2026: LM3_TEICHMULLER_TOWER and LM3b_TEICHMULLER_PUSH (attached under `LM3_return/`, `LM3b_return/`; statements cited by their numbers, with the status the return gives them). Neither has been reviewed line by line yet. Each cited statement is a claim: check its proof in the attached return before you build on it, correct it where it fails, and then go beyond it. A row that only repeats one of them is unfinished.

- **The mixed topos computes the cohomology of `X`, the classifying topos does not (Part B (a), (b); Part C (b)).** LM3 C.10 (Refuted there): the classifying topos of the fundamental group of a curve is not its small étale topos (two subterminal objects against at least three). LM3 C.1 (Proved there): plugging a Galois-stable set of holes is the quotient by the closed normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups, and classifies the finite étale coverings that extend over the plugged points. LM3 C.11 (Proved there): over a sub-`p`-adic field the arithmetic fundamental group of a hyperbolic curve reconstructs its proper completion with its small étale topos, functorially for isomorphisms. LM3b H.6, H.7, H.8 (Proved there): for `P¹` with the points `0, 1, ∞`, the category with one object `u`, `Aut(u) = G` the free group, three objects `p_i` and `Hom(p_i, u) = G/I_i` (the category whose representations are his glued topos `B_{X;S}`) has a cofinal family of strict finite quotients; the cohomology of their nerves with finite constant coefficients `A` is `A` in degrees 0 and 2 and zero elsewhere, the class of degree 2 being the orientation class of `P¹(C)`; and the nerve represents the profinite stratified homotopy type. This is his comparison of scan 124 in genus 0, where his margin says that it fails for locally constant coefficients on `X` itself. Part B (b) starts from it: check H.7; prove from his Proposition of scan 129 that for `S ≠ ∅` the comparison `H^*(B_{X;S}, F) → H^*(X_ét, f^*F)` is an isomorphism in every genus, genus 0 included; and prove that the failure he notes is that of `S = ∅` in genus 0 alone. Part C (b) then decides the "tendance d'être acycliques" with these in hand.
- **Functors up to isomorphism, not up to natural transformation (Part A (f), Part E).** LM3 F.2 and F.4 (Proved there): the strict completion of a category with finitely many objects by its finite congruences, and its finite representations. LM3 F.3 (Refuted there): one may not pass to classes of functors under arbitrary natural transformations; the category `[1]` against the point is the counterexample, with three subterminal objects against two. His classes of pairs `(f, α)` on scan 17 are classes under isomorphisms of pairs: Part A (f) keeps that distinction and proves that the Hom-categories are equivalence relations in his situation, with the hypothesis that makes it true.
- **Stratified versions (Part E).** LM3 F.9 (Known there): the comparison of the finite étale covers of `M̄_{0,n}` constructible along its boundary with the completed exit category. LM3 F.18 and LM3b H.9 (Open there): the same comparison for all finite homotopy types once there are chains of three strata; the first case is `n = 5`. Part E (c) may take the case `n = 4` as proved in LM3b H.8, after checking it, and is to decide `n = 5` or to isolate the step that fails.
