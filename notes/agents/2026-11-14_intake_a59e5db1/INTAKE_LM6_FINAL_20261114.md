  Selmer section of `U_ℓ`, `ℓ ≥ 5`, the mod-`ℓ` Legendre representation is unramified outside `2ℓ`, flat at `ℓ`,

  weight 2 and level dividing 16, and there is none. **Sound (mine)**: the valuation `v_p(j) = 8v_p(2) − 2ℓ|v_p(a)|`
  and the cusp case are right. This is Frey's argument run on a section, with no Frey curve.
