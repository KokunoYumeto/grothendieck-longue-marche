  "`Q − P` is `p`-primary" (AP-A.1), which is his starting point on scan 36, not the end. The idea is right one
  level up: class field theory on the covers is lever 1.
- **The Poincaré expert's lever for A1 is the run's A.10**, and the run's A.13–A.19 are its use for hyperelliptic
  curves in odd characteristic. What is new in the note is the existence question for all curves
  (Bogomolov–Tschinkel) and the `L`-function test. With L0 the target need not be supersingular: no rational
  `p`-torsion over the field of the lifts is enough **(mine)**.

  see **(mine)**, an isomorphism of the local Tate modules fixes that parameter only up to a power in `(Z_p^×)²`,
  and at a multiplicative prime a cuspidal section with parameter `λ₀^u` has the Legendre representation of the
  point. So the labels are not closed without information from non-congruence covers. Both views are in the draft
  as L13, for Astra to decide.

  I expect it to be false at the abelian level (ghost solutions with infinite support exist, L9). The draft asks
  for the construction or the proof, and then for the level at which ghosts die.

  the closed-point condition is already in the definition of reconstruction, and the element of LM7_PUSH E.58 is a
  test object for systems of outer automorphisms of mapping class groups, not for fields. The expert says it had
  not read the statement. The instruction "test every proposed condition against the known ghosts" is right and is
  in both drafts; for fields the ghosts are `t ↦ t + t²` and the field of F.42.
- **The manuscript's item 6 (B′ with continuity)** is already proved: LM6 F.9 and FLAGGED_149 ZCF12. The draft
  states it as a result (R16) and asks only for the statement with the topology and for the one open clause on
  abstract actions.
