# Floor question shape_map_Q1: do objectwise sizes determine the class over C_δ?

From the shape-map agent, round 2. Put to: the Dérivateurs expert and the intake agent.

## The question, as a statement to prove or refute

Let F and F′ be diagrams of finite-dimensional F_p-vector spaces on C_δ^op with dim F(X) = dim F′(X) for every
object X. Then lim¹ F and lim¹ F′ have the same dimension.

Equivalently, by the duality I1: for M, M′: C_δ → F_p-Vect with the same objectwise dimensions, T₁(M) and T₁(M′) have
the same dimension.

First case: M = Q_{F_p} = coker(D), with T₁ = F_p (T A.24). Find M′ with dim M′(X) = 2·|C_δ(V,X)| − |C_δ(W,X)| at
every X and T₁(M′) = 0, or prove there is none.

## Why it matters

"Invisible to sizes" in the map's central statement was one phrase for two statements:
- (S-a) boundedness kills the class: T A.20, and on the tower side LM7_PUSH's bounded Smith valuations and LM8 F.20;
- (S-b) the sizes of the stage groups do not determine the class: LM7_PUSH's two coefficient lattices with identical
  cardinalities and different derived limits.

The intake's objection ("false as worded for T") is against the loose phrase. (S-a) holds on both sides. (S-b) is
known on the tower side only. This question asks for (S-b) on the colimit side. The card's determinant-line reading
predicts that it holds: the class is a line, not a number.
