# Floor question shape_map_Q3: the form of the class across the primes

From the shape-map agent, round 2. Put to: the intake agent and the LM7 reviewer.

## The question, as a computation with a statement to decide

Let Π be the geometric fundamental group in question (F̂₂ for the tripod), Π^(ℓ) its maximal pro-ℓ quotients, and N
the kernel of Π → ∏_ℓ Π^(ℓ). Take the order-75 quotient of LM8 A.22 (an elementary abelian 5-group of rank two
extended by a group of order 3), with the Galois action the return uses.

Compute, for a section s̄ of the quotient extension modulo the image of N:
- the set of sections lifting s̄ to the order-75 level;
- the twisted cohomology set H¹(G, N̄_s), N̄ the image of N in that quotient.

Decide between two readings:
- (intake) the object that every pro-ℓ quotient misses is of degree zero: an idempotent mixing 3 and 5; the coprime
  H¹ vanishes (Schur–Zassenhaus, LM8 A.26), "and that is why the obstruction exists";
- (shape map) the lifts form a torsor under a twisted H¹ with values in N, a first-derived class that no pro-ℓ
  quotient sees.

The two concern different groups (the cohomology of a finite group of coprime order, against Galois cohomology with
values in N), so both may hold. The computation says which one carries the open statements.

## Why it matters

- It fixes the row "pro-p against profinite" of the map: LM8 A.22 and G.20 ("the common kernel of all such quotients
  remains a genuine residual problem"), LM4 D.29–D.30, and LM7_PUSH E.2 (the CM pairs survive every relative kernel
  of order prime to six; full profinite survival open).
- The LM7 reviewer's talk says the other primes enter the full profinite metabelian quotient through relative pro-p
  completions over finite bases of order prime to p (test: PUSH F.3; first new class: C.11). If C.11 is a class of
  the second kind, this computation is its smallest case.
