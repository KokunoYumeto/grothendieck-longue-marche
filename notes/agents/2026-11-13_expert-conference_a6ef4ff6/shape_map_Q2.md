# Floor question shape_map_Q2: are cuspidal sections the points of a homotopy limit?

From the shape-map agent, round 2. Put to: the EXIT_ONE_TYPE reviewer and the LM7 reviewer.

## The question, as statements to prove or refute

1. (Topological.) For a normal crossings pair and a decreasing basis V_n of neighbourhoods of a point x of the
   boundary: holim_n Sing(V_n ∖ D) ≃ Sing(τ⁻¹(x)), the torus of tangent rays. The limit of the spaces is empty; π₀
   of the homotopy limit is the set of components of the torus of rays. A tangential base point is a point of the
   homotopy limit.
2. (Arithmetic.) For a hyperbolic curve U over a number field K with a K-rational cusp c: the cuspidal sections at c
   (the packet of LM7 B.1, CYCLOTOMIC C1–C2, FLAGGED F10) are the K-points, in the sense of homotopy fixed points of
   G_K, of the homotopy limit of the étale homotopy types of the punctured henselian neighbourhoods of c. The packet's
   torsor structure under the completed Kummer group is the H¹(G_K, Ẑ(1)) of that homotopy limit, whose type is
   BẐ(1).
3. (The section conjecture in this language.) Every section is a homotopy fixed point either of U itself (a point)
   or of one of these homotopy limits (a cusp).

## Why it matters

- LM8 E.11: the limit of the sheaf topoi of the punctured neighbourhoods is empty; E.10: the covering germ is BZ.
  The EXIT reviewer's talk, §4 (d): on singular chains the limit is zero and R lim = lim¹[−1] computes the torus of
  rays. The intake reads E.11 as "no class". Statement 1 would file E.11 as the case where the underived limit has
  no points and the derived limit has exactly the tangential ones.
- Statement 2 would put the cusp packets, which four returns treat separately from point sections, under the same
  heading as points: both are homotopy fixed points. It is the profinite form of the EXIT reviewer's statement B
  (compact stages) and of the intake's I2 (coherent topoi).
- The first check is statement 1 for a punctured disc, and the comparison of statement 2 with the proof of
  CYCLOTOMIC C1 (splitting classes form the completed Kummer H¹ torsor).
