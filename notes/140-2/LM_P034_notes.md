# LM_P034 — Editorial and research notes

These notes accompany the diplomatic LaTeX transcription of the ten supplied manuscript images. **Scan numbers follow the filenames, 331–340; the archival numbers printed in the footers are 330–339.** The authorial folio numbers visible at the top are 168 on scan 332, 169 on scan 334, 170 on scan 336, 171 on scan 338, and 172 on scan 340. The corresponding lower-left handwritten archival numbers are not encoded as authorial `\mspage` numbers.

Locations below are approximate physical handwritten lines, including display rows where appropriate. Crowded insertions and marginal notes are located by their adjacent wording rather than by an artificially precise line count. Confidence concerns the **reading or placement of a mark**, not the truth of a mathematical assertion. Repeated script letters and accents remain uncertain even where the surrounding mathematics makes one interpretation attractive. Only the supplied images and task metadata were used; no published transcription or external source was used to supply a reading.
