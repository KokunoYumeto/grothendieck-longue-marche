## Own notes (second pass)

**N1 — scan 142 — Own (second pass), own idea (consistency check).** The ink says Γ° is "d'indice 0 ou 1" in Γ; Γ° is the kernel of the orientation character Γ → {±1}, so its index is 1 or 2. **N2 — scans 141, 142, 145, 146 — Own (second pass), own idea (consistency check).** Four slips of the pen are kept as written: "l'image de U^{Γ′} dans U" (Γ′ acts on Ũ, so Ũ^{Γ′} is meant); "où σ est la conjugaison complexe" under a formula written with τ; D^Γ = U for the quotient D/Γ; and the covering "D̃* → D̃ donné par exp 2iπz", which is ℂ = D̃* → D* = ℂ*. None changes the argument once read as intended.

**N3 — scans 142–145 — Own (second pass), implication (structure of the proof).** With "Supposons d'abord Γ = Γ°, i.e. Γ conserve l'orientation. … On procède" cancelled on scan 142 and its continuation ("Par induction sur l'ordre de Γ", "a) D^Γ ≠ ∅", "a) Cas Γ d'ordre premier") struck on scan 143, the proof runs: a) the conformal argument for any Γ with an invariant C² structure; then, inserted on scan 145, "du moins pour Γ°. Supposons d'abord Γ = Γ°" for the induction b)–c); d) passes to Γ ≠ Γ°. The orientation assumption therefore governs only the "non-sauvagerie" part.

**N4 — scans 146–147 — Own (second pass), counterexample to the stated lemma.** u(z) = z̄ is a homeomorphism of ℂ of order 2, commutes with z ↦ z + 1 (conj(z + 1) = z̄ + 1) and fixes 0, yet u ≠ id. So the lemma as written ("Soit u un homéomorphisme d'ordre fini de ℂ, commutant à z ↦ z+1, et laissant invariant l'origine. Alors u = id") is false; it needs "u conserve l'orientation", which holds where it is used (Γ = Γ°, scan 146). The sentence "Il reste à prouver que si un groupe fini Γ opère sur ℂ en commutant à l'action de ℤ …" has the same gap. Confidence: high (direct check).

**N5 — scan 147 — Own (second pass), own idea (route for the orientation-preserving lemma).** Descend u to the cylinder ℂ/ℤ ≅ ℂ* (z ↦ e^{2iπz}): ū has finite order, fixes the image 1 of ℤ, and acts trivially on π₁(ℂ*) because u carries the segment [0, 1] to a path from 0 to 1; being orientation-preserving it therefore fixes both ends, and extends to S² with three fixed points 0, 1, ∞. By the classical theorem that a finite-order orientation-preserving homeomorphism of S² is conjugate to a rotation (Brouwer 1919, Kerékjártó 1919, Eilenberg 1934), ū = id; then u(z) = z + k with u(0) = 0, so u = id. Confidence: high for the reduction, conditional on that theorem (not checked against the corpus in this pass); it does not repair the manuscript's internal logic, since the lemma fondamental is the plane case of the same theorem.

**N6 — scan 148, margin — Own (second pass), own idea (test of Grothendieck's conjecture).** Take U = ℂ* (orientable, ≠ S²) and Γ = {1, conj}. With the covering z ↦ e^{2iπz}, the lifts of conj are s_n(z) = −z̄ + n (n ∈ ℤ), all involutions, and T_m s_n T_{−m} = s_{n+2m}: two π-conjugacy classes of sections (n even, n odd), each with π^{Γ′} = (1). The fixed line Re z = n/2 of s_n maps to ℝ_{>0} for n even and to ℝ_{<0} for n odd, the two components of U^Γ = ℝ*. So π₀(U^Γ) → classes is bijective here, while the point-level map U^Γ → classes is not injective. Confidence: high (explicit computation).

**N7 — scan 149 — Own (second pass), own idea (check of the NB).** "Celles sur Γ [assurent] que Γ opérant sur D satisfait aux hyp. du lemme fondamental" holds: a section Γ′ ≅ Γ acts faithfully on Ũ ≃ D because ℰ does (Γ acts faithfully on U). If U is orientable, π preserves the orientation of Ũ, so Γ′° ≅ Γ° ≠ (1); if U is not orientable, Γ′° has index at most 2 in Γ′, so card Γ ≥ 3 gives Γ′° ≠ (1). Confidence: high.

**N8 — scan 150 — Own (second pass), implication.** The corrected notation U^! connects this corollary with its continuation (witness text after this package: "soit J (≃ U^!) l'ensemble des classes de π-conjugaison de sections partielles (≠ 1) maximales", and S′ = S ∪ U^!), where the critical points are added to S to reconstruct the loop group of X ∖ S′. Under the first pass's U′ this link was invisible.
