## Second-pass notes

A. **Scan 544, Corollary. Claim checked (second pass).** The ink gives the stabilisers in $\Gamma'_n$ as $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$, whereas all three witnesses print $\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z$. The ink is the mathematically right statement: by (15) every stabiliser in $\Gamma_n$ contains $-1$, which generates the kernel $\{\pm1\}$ of $\Gamma_n\to\Gamma'_n$. Confidence high.

B. **Scans 545 and 547, freeness of $\Gamma'_{2m,2}$ and the gerbe. Claim checked (second pass).** Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $\ne2$, the kernel of $\operatorname{Aut}(E)\to\operatorname{GL}(E[2])$ is $\{\pm1\}$. For $y^2=x^3-x$, the automorphism $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,iy)$ swaps $(1,0)$ and $(-1,0)$. For $y^2=x^3+1$, the order-3 automorphisms permute the three 2-torsion points cyclically. In characteristic 3, $x\mapsto x+1$ and $x\mapsto -x$ make the group of order 12 surject onto $\mathfrak S_3$. Hence $\Gamma'_{2m,2}$ acts freely on $M_{1,1}[2m]$ over $\mathbb Z[1/2m]$, as (19) asserts, and the inertia of $M_{1,1}[2]$ is $\pm1$ everywhere, as the $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$-gerbe of scan 547 requires. Confidence high.

C. **Scan 550, $I_{n-3}$. Claim checked (second pass).** $I_{n-3}=\{i\in\mathbb N\mid 3<i\le n\}=\{4,\dots,n\}$ has $n-3$ elements. This matches $\dim M^!_{0,n}=n-3$ and the factor $U_{0,3}^{I_{n-3}}$, and the ink has $<$ in both definitions. The witnesses' "$3\le i\le n$" in the first definition would give $n-2$ elements and is wrong. Confidence high.

D. **Scan 550, $\underline{\operatorname{Mon}}(I_{n-3},U_{0,3})$. Own idea (second pass).** Read Mon as the scheme of injections of the finite set $I_{n-3}$ into $U_{0,3}$, an open subscheme of $U_{0,3}^{I_{n-3}}$, as the "$\subset$" in (30) indicates. The identification sends a curve with sections to the coordinates of $s_4,\dots,s_n$ after the unique projective transformation that puts $s_1,s_2,s_3$ at $0,1,\infty$ in some fixed order. Disjointness of the sections is exactly injectivity together with avoidance of $0,1,\infty$. $\mathfrak S_3$ then acts diagonally through the six Möbius maps permuting $\{0,1,\infty\}$, and $\mathfrak S_{n-3}$ acts by permuting $I_{n-3}$. Confidence medium-high.

E. **Scan 548, the bracketed expectation. Claim checked (second pass).** "Si cependant on exclut les courbes elliptiques anharmoniques, ça marcherait" is correct, and not only in characteristic 0. Over any field, the curves with automorphisms other than $\pm1$ are those with $j=0$ or $j=1728$, which coincide in characteristics 2 and 3. Removing the inverse images of the two sections of note 5 therefore leaves a $\Gamma'_n$-stable open set on which $\Gamma'_n$ acts freely. There (24) restricts to an equivalence over all of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/n]$. Confidence high.

F. **Scan 549, base of (25). Unpursued implication (second pass).** With the base read as $\mathbb Z[1/2]$ in both places, the margin's comparison of images in $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ says that $U_{0,3}=M^!_{0,4}$ extends over the prime 2 while $M_{1,1}[2]'$ does not. The text does not pursue the question of what, on the elliptic side, the fibre of $U_{0,3}$ at 2 corresponds to. Level structures defined only over $\mathbb Z[1/2m]$, such as $M_{1,1}[4]$ or $M_{1,1}[6]$, do not reach the prime 2. A comparison would need level-2 structures that make sense in characteristic 2, such as Drinfeld-style ones in the sense of Katz–Mazur. Confidence low to medium; this is a direction, not a claim.
