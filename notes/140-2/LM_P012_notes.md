# Research notes — separate from the transcription

These notes are in English. “Unproved claim” means that the supplied ten scans do not provide a proof; it makes no assertion about the rest of the manuscript or the present literature. “Conjecture” also covers the author's explicit questions and expectations. Suggested proof requirements and the items labelled “own idea” are the transcriber's analysis, not additional manuscript text. No item is presented as new or as an established external result.

### R01 — Scan 111, ~5–7 — Unproved claim

The text says that the operation on the fundamental group determines the action on I and that “on récupère donc le K-schéma étale S.” A proof would need a precise definition of the peripheral indexing set and an explanation of the displayed induced-set construction, including the ambiguity in its first factor. This claim should not be reconstructed from that uncertain symbol alone.

### R02 — Scan 111, ~8–16 — Unproved claim

“On récupère tout le diagramme” asserts more than recovery of the boundary set: it includes the indicated fundamental-groupoid/topos diagram. One would need to identify the relevant morphisms, their dependence on base choices, and the equivariance data before asserting reconstruction of the whole diagram. The especially uncertain phrase concerning the toposes remains a textual obstacle.

### R03 — Scans 111–112, final paragraph / ~1–7 — Unproved claim

The morphisms of pairs “se décrivent” by outer homomorphisms compatible with actions and lacet structures, with a map of indexing sets and integers d_i. A proof would need to specify the admissible outer homomorphisms and explain which compatibility conditions are necessary and sufficient, not merely necessary. The margin defining I-prime is important and should not be absorbed into a guessed global condition on all of S-prime.

### R04 — Scan 112, ~8–15 — Unproved claim

“Il faut cependant compléter” introduces an additional cohomological datum when the indexing set is empty. To justify the construction one would need to explain the coefficient module T, the relevant cohomology theory, and the equivariance of the indicated isomorphism. The explicit restriction to l-adic components with l different from p in positive characteristic is part of the stated qualification.

### R05 — Scans 112–113, closing lines / degree diagram — Unproved claim

The degree condition is encoded by the square with vertical maps d and f-star, and the prose also invokes “l'ordre de Coker” in connection with `f^{*}`. A proof must relate this index description to the geometric degree under the hypotheses actually intended. The direction `pi -> pi-prime` at the end of scan 112 is retained as written and should be checked before using the passage as a consistent functorial statement.

### R06 — Scan 113, ~7–12 — Conjecture

The revised sentence includes “il devrait y avoir” a compatibility between the two sorts of data in the mixed boundary/non-boundary case. The relevant S-symbols are not completely secure, but the expectation of an additional compatibility is visible. Its exact domain, coefficient conventions, and behavior under killing lacets are not supplied here.

### R07 — Scans 113–114, ~13–20 / ~1–3 — Conjecture

The author proposes describing a “cohomologique” structure on the quotient obtained by dividing out the lacets, says “il me faudra revenir dessus,” and expects morphisms to preserve the resulting structure with an appropriate degree. A proof would need a construction independent of choices of peripheral representatives and a precise compatibility under the quotient maps. The uncertain adjective transcribed as “harmonicienne” is not silently replaced by another technical term.

### R08 — Scan 114, ~14–15 — Conjecture

The annotations “pro-fini ???” and “discret” with question marks leave the topology and/or intended kind of the automorphism object and projection unsettled on this page. Before interpreting the proposed group extension, one would need to distinguish an abstract group, a topological group, and the continuity requirements on its action. The word transcribed as “noyau/germe” is too insecure to use as a settled technical definition.

### R09 — Scan 114, ~17–20 — Conjecture

The identification of G_0 with the indicated automorphism group of the geometric pair is explicitly qualified by “conjecturalement.” A proof would need both a realization statement for compatible group-theoretic automorphisms and a faithfulness statement, with the exact class of automorphisms specified. The uncertain conjecture epithet is not identified with a named result from outside these pages.

### R10 — Scan 115, ~1–6 — Conjecture

The descent-related interpretation through a splitting is accompanied by “on l'espère, de façon pleinement fidèle.” The desired assertion is therefore stronger than mere existence of a section: it should account for morphisms and for the equivalence relation on splittings. The uncertain word “restriction/restitution” is left unresolved; the research question is based on the displayed maps and the explicit full-faithfulness hope.

### R11 — Scans 115–116, presentation and first cases — Unproved claim

“On déduit de ceci” introduces the freeness, rank, and exceptional abelian cases from the presentation. The displayed relation ends in zero and the later bound reads nu less than or equal to 3; neither has been emended. Any mathematical use should first establish the intended conventions or recheck the original ink rather than silently importing the usual surface presentation.

### R12 — Scan 116, ~4–12 — Unproved claim

The “suite exacte canonique” and the assertion that the projections from T_pi to L_i are isomorphisms require an abelianization calculation and careful control of the exceptional cases. Canonicality also needs independence from choices of representatives of the peripheral conjugacy classes; this issue is taken up, but not fully proved, on the next scan. The hypothesis inserted above T_pi is part of the claim's scope.

### R13 — Scan 116, ~13–19 — Unproved claim

The author promises that “on établira plus bas une relation” between two definitions of T_pi. The comparison is not carried out in this selection. It would need to identify the kernel-based orientation module with the cohomological description in an appropriate common setting and explain how the boundary is being removed or retained.

### R14 — Scan 116, ~20–22 — Unpursued implication

The assertion that g is determined by pi and the cardinality of I makes explicit that the number of peripheral classes is part of the data used to recover the type. It does not, by itself, prove that the abstract group pi determines that cardinality. A reconstruction argument would need to keep this distinction visible rather than dropping the peripheral structure from its hypotheses.

### R15 — Scan 117, ~1–16 — Unproved claim

The passage claims distinctness of the peripheral conjugacy classes and canonical identifications among representatives, using the fact that a normalizing element centralizes the subgroup. The supplied justification is expressly easy only in the range where the map to the abelianization is injective. A full proof must cover the remaining case and show that the induced identifications are independent of a conjugating element.

### R16 — Scan 117, left margin beside ~8–16 — Conjecture

“Voir à part le cas,” followed by `nu=1`, singles out the normalizer of the subgroup generated by the product of commutators in a free group. This is an explicit outstanding check, not a completed proof in the margin. A proof would have to control that cyclic subgroup's normalizer for the particular relator, including any exceptional low-genus cases allowed by the surrounding assumptions.

### R17 — Scans 117–118, orientation character — Unproved claim

The action on the lacet structure is asserted to induce an action on the invertible orientation module and hence a character with values in the two units of Z. To make this precise, the construction must be functorial for the kind of automorphisms under consideration and independent of the choices eliminated earlier. The cancelled continuation on scan 118 should not be treated as an additional settled compatibility definition.

### R18 — Scan 118, ~9–15 — Conjecture

The profinite discussion expressly announces “deux pts à vérifier,” including the displayed normalizer–centralizer equality for a peripheral subgroup. The handwritten side qualification is partly illegible, so its scope must be rechecked rather than inferred. A proof for the discrete presentation is not, on these pages, a proof for the profinite or prime-to-p variants.

### R19 — Scan 118, ~15–18 and left margin — Conjecture

The second displayed condition concerns the center, followed by the stronger statement that the centralizer of every open subgroup is trivial. The margin includes the emphatic phrase “dans aucun cas anabélien profini !”; it is evidence of the author's uncertainty in this passage, not a statement of present-day research status. A proof must respect the anabelian and exceptional-case restrictions rather than asserting this for all completed surface-type groups.

### R20 — Scan 119, ~7–16 — Unproved claim

The reduction to triples is introduced with “Par le topos d'Artin.” To verify the formulation one would need the exact gluing category, the variance of the map `F_S -> j^{*}i_{*}F_U`, and the morphisms between triples. The constructibility additions should be kept separate from the broader statement in the next paragraph.

### R21 — Scans 119–120, last paragraph / first line — Unproved claim

“Toute condition de constructibilité étant mise à part” and “Il n'y a pas lieu de supposer F_U à fibres finies” enlarge the proposed setting. A proof must explain which sheaves are admitted via the fundamental topos and why the gluing construction remains valid there. One should not silently replace the stated class by finite locally constant sheaves.

### R22 — Scan 120, ~2–8 — Unproved claim

The henselian local objects, their punctured complements, and the commutative diagram provide a second local description of the situation. To use this construction rigorously one must settle the local hypotheses from scan 119, where the visible dimension condition involves S, and verify the defining maps. No replacement of that condition by a mathematically preferable one is part of the transcription.

### R23 — Scan 120, ~15–18 — Unproved claim

The bracketed note says that the inclusion from S to D “définit une équivalence” for the indicated class of sheaves. The phrase “ces faisceaux” restricts the assertion: this should not be promoted to an equivalence of all étale sheaves on D with all étale sheaves on S without further justification. A proof would need the exact meaning of the uncertain qualifier “ind loc. constant.”

### R24 — Scan 120, ~9–19 — Unproved claim

The final construction replaces the earlier boundary datum by the map `F_D -> \sigma_{*}\rho^{*}F_U`. Comparing the two formulations requires the appropriate local-to-global and adjunction identifications, including compatibility of maps between triples. The page stops at “ou encore,” so the next reformulation is not available in this selection.

### R25 — Scans 113, 116, and 119–120 — Own idea

A possible organizing test is a relative cohomology construction for a group together with its peripheral subgroups, intended to compare the kernel definition of the orientation module with the closed-case cohomological one. Confidence: medium as a research direction, not as a theorem, because the text separately asks for such a comparison and introduces a gluing construction that might support it. The degree convention, coefficient twists, and profinite or prime-to-p passage would all have to be verified.

### R26 — Scans 115–117 — Own idea

Assuming the displayed freeness-and-rank claim, compare the types (g,nu)=(0,3) and (1,1): both give rank two, while their peripheral data differ. Confidence: high for the formal diagnostic conditional on that claim, because it uses only the displayed expression 2g+nu−1. This is a test of what the proposed reconstruction data must retain, not a correction of the manuscript's exceptional-case line or a novelty claim.

### R27 — Scans 119–120 — Own idea

Try expressing the local gluing datum entirely in terms of the actions attached to the maps from punctured henselian neighbourhoods, keeping the boundary action as part of the data. Confidence: medium-low, because the final map suggests this direction but the exact permitted sheaf category and the needed base-change comparison are not established here. The first test should be compatibility with the two triple descriptions actually written, without assuming finite fibers.
