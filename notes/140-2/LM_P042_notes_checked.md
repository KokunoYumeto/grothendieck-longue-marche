## First-pass notes

1. **Scan 412, condition ① — question. KEEP.** « tt sous-groupe ouvert aussi (et inversement ?) »: the converse, admissibility of Γ deduced from that of an open subgroup, is asked, with a question mark that the witnesses drop.
2. **Scan 412, condition ② — claim stated as axiom. KEEP.** Under « On suppose », bouching a Γ-invariant part I′ gives an admissible operation of Γ on π′. It is a requirement on the notion of admissibility, not a theorem.
3. **Scan 412, lower margin — claim without proof. CORRECT.** The note reads « Il suffit de la poser pour card I′=1, i.e. bouchage d'un trou — du moins si on sait que la condition d'admissibilité ne dépend que de l'action des noyaux des groupes » (tail doubtful). The reduction of ② to one hole is therefore explicitly conditional on that hypothesis, which is nowhere stated or proved on these pages.
4. **Scan 412, ①–② — own idea (first pass). KEEP, reworded.** Testing the one-hole reduction separately for a Γ-fixed hole and for a Γ-orbit of holes is still useful; the corrected margin shows that the author's own reduction rests on a hypothesis about kernels, which is what such a test should probe.
5. **Scan 413, equivalence of systems — claim without proof. KEEP.** « On trouve ainsi une équivalence » between systems (π,a,Γ,ψ,i) of type (g,ν+1) with an admissible exterior operation and a Γ-stable i, and systems (π′,a′,Γ,ψ′) of type (g,ν) with a « vraie » admissible action. No functors on morphisms or natural isomorphisms are given.
6. **Scan 413, pathological lifts — conjecture. KEEP, wording corrected.** « il y aura des relèvements "pathologiques" d'une action cat./ext. admissible donnée… »: the inverse implication is expected to fail; no example is given. The abbreviation before « admissible » is doubtful (cat. or ext.).
7. **Scan 413, condition ③ — claim stated as axiom. KEEP.** If Γ operates effectively and admissibly on π, then π^Γ = (1). Introduced by « On suppose de plus ».
8. **Scan 413, rigidity — implication not proved. KEEP.** « Cela implique que la catégorie des Γ-Pts de π, ou … des sections de B_{π,Γ} (≃ B_u) sur B_Γ, est rigide »: automorphisms of a section are π-elements fixed by Γ, so ③ gives rigidity once every section is shown to come from an admissible effective action.
9. **Scan 413, existence vs rigidity — own idea (first pass). KEEP.** ③ controls automorphisms only; non-emptiness of the category of sections is a separate question, answered on 415 by axiom ④ only after passing to an open subgroup.
10. **Scan 411, opening — explicit doubt. CORRECT.** The margin « pour les isom » (CSG: « [pour les isomorphismes] ») restricts the claimed equivalence of categories to isomorphisms; the author then writes « ce n'est pas du tout évident ».
11. **Scan 411, axiomatic set-up — claim. CORRECT.** The « condition essentielle » is that the ψ_i : T̂̂_{g,ν} → Ŝ̂_{g,ν−1} (script capital G or S) induce M_{g,ν} ⥲ M_{g,ν−1}; the first pass read the ψ without index and left N/M open. This, with closed subgroups N_{g,ν} ⊂ T̂̂_{g,ν} normalising T̂_{g,ν}, is said to suffice to define arithmetisations and the bouchage/forage theory; no verification is given.
12. **Scan 411, inverse functor — own idea (first pass). KEEP.** Comparing « forer des trous » at invariant points with passage to a smaller Γ′ remains a sensible check; the author's own doubt follows immediately.
13. **Scan 414, discreteness — claim without proof. KEEP.** The inductive-limit category of sections of B_{π,Γ} ≃ B_u over the B_{Γ′} « est discrète et correspond à l'ens » Pt_ad(B_{π,Γ^♮}) ≃ lim Pt_ad(B_{π,Γ′}).
14. **Scan 414, groupoid description — claim without proof. CORRECT.** Corrected statement: if Pt(B_{π,Γ}) ≠ ∅, then, because π^{Γ′} = (1) for every open subgroup, « le gr. cat. π peut être décrit can.(?) par un groupoïde profini P̲t̲(B_{π,Γ^♮}) ayant Pt(B_{π,Γ}) comme ens d'objet, lié(s) par le groupe cat. π, sur lequel Γ opère en sens strict, avec le stabilisateur Γ_P de ∀ pt étant ouvert, et Γ_P → Aut(P) (≃ π mod autom. int.) étant continue ». A proof must build the arrows from π, the strict action, and continuity.
15. **Scan 414, NB margin — implication not pursued. KEEP.** The point groupoid « à opérateurs stricts » « dépend fonctoriellement de (π,Γ), pour des morphismes ext. … »; the rest is illegible (CSG: « [??] »), so the class of morphisms is not fixed.
16. **Scan 415, bijection — claim without proof. CORRECT.** The sentence reads « On a ici si à ∀ P ∈ Pt(B_{π,Γ}) on associe la classe d'isom. de sections de B_{π,Γ} sur B_Γ, on trouve une bijection — ce qui caractérise le Γ-groupoïde en question à isom unique près »; it is an assertion, not a definition (the first pass read « par déf. »).
17. **Scan 415, ④ — axiom. KEEP, wording corrected.** An admissible operation of Γ on (π,a) has, on some open Γ′, its « op. cat. » lifted to an effective admissible operation, i.e. Pt_ad(B_{Γ,π}) ≠ ∅.
18. **Scans 415–416, canonical homomorphism of extensions — claim. CORRECT (consequential).** The text says the homomorphism of extensions coming from the exterior action and from p_i « n'est pas la composée » Ŝ̂(π)_i → T̂̂(π)_i →ψ_i Ŝ̂(π′_i); the first pass read the opposite (« qui vient par la composée »). The insertion on 416 also requires the p_i : π → π′_i to commute with the exterior actions of T̂̂(π).
19. **Scan 416, general construction — claim. CORRECT.** It is said to exist whenever a group Γ (here T̂̂(π)) acts exteriorly on two exterior groups π, π′ with a Γ-compatible homomorphism π → π′ such that « le centre de π et le centralisateur de son image(?) dans π′ soient triviaux »; the cancelled « de centre trivial » after « qui » was replaced by this sharper condition. Existence and uniqueness are not separated in the text.
20. **Scan 416, forgotten axiom (g) — claim. CORRECT.** « On a oublié de préciser : (g) L'hom canonique (Ŝ̂_{g,ν})_i → Ŝ̂_{g,ν−1} associé à i ∈ [0, ν−1], envoie (M_{g,ν})_i dans M_{g,ν−1} donc il s'insère dans un hom.(?) de suites exactes » (26). The label is (g) or 9, not 7/2; CSG and IUT misprint the target as M_{g,ν}.
21. **Scan 415, ④ — own idea (first pass). KEEP.** Testing the lifting axiom on finite quotients is reasonable; nothing on the page makes the finite-level choices compatible.
22. **Scan 416, centraliser hypothesis — own idea (first pass). KEEP.** The difference of two candidate extension maps should land in the centraliser of the image of π in π′, so dropping triviality of that centraliser is the natural toy test.
23. **Scan 417, admissibility transported by φ_i — claim. KEEP.** « On conclut aisément de ② que si le premier est admissible, le deuxième l'est »: the argument is not written out.
24. **Scan 417, (27)–(29) — claim. KEEP.** Passing to the limit gives a map of Γ-sets (28) and a homomorphism of Γ-groupoids (29); the transition maps and the action on arrows are not specified.
25. **Scan 417, canonical point P — implication not pursued. KEEP.** P ∈ Pt_ad(B_{π′_i,Γ}) = (Pt_ad(B_{π′_i,Γ^♮}))^Γ is « le pt canonique », presumably the class of the canonical hom Γ → M_{a′}(π′_i) of 418 a).

## Notes added in the second pass

40. **(second pass, own) Scan 416, why the extension map is not the composite — own idea, high confidence.** The composite Ŝ̂(π)_i → T̂̂(π)_i → Ŝ̂(π′_i) kills π (π is the kernel of Ŝ̂(π)_i → T̂̂(π)_i), while the left square of the diagram forces φ_i|π = p_i. So φ_i equals the composite only if p_i is trivial: the corrected reading « n'est pas la composée » is the mathematically necessary one.
41. **(second pass, own) Scan 417, diagram (26) — implication not pursued.** The annotations « non comm.? » (left triangle, with φ_i) and « comm. » (right triangle) say that the dashed lift N_a(π)_i → M_{a′}(π′_i) makes the lower-right triangle commute but not, apparently, the upper-left one. Whether the left triangle commutes up to π′_i-conjugation is the question behind a) and b) on 418.
42. **(second pass, own) Scan 413, which axiom is invoked — open point.** The inserted sentence cites « La condition (1) » (ink), but the inference « si Γ opère effectivement, de façon admissible, alors l'action extérieure déduite est admissible » follows from ② applied to (π°,a°) and the new hole, not from ① (open subgroups). The witnesses print (2). Either the author slipped or the ink is (2); the mathematics needs ②.
43. **(second pass, own) Scan 412, admissibility of the arithmetic groups — implication not pursued.** The admissible acting groups may be open subgroups of 𝕀Γ_g, « valeur commune des 𝕀Γ_{g,ν} », or of 𝕀Γ_a for an arithmetisation a. That 𝕀Γ_{g,ν} does not depend on ν is asserted in passing; it is what makes forage/bouchage compatible with the acting group.
44. **(second pass, own) Scan 416, transport of structure — claim.** The extension homomorphism is identified (« C'est aussi ici ») with the transport-of-structure map sending every automorphism à lacets u of π to the corresponding automorphism of π′_i. This identification is asserted, not checked; it presupposes that u preserves the hole i.
45. **(second pass, own) Scan 420, (6) as a splitting problem — own idea, medium confidence.** With E the extension of Γ by L = Ker((N_a)_{I′} → N_{a′}), ⑥ asks that the partial splitting over Γ′ extend uniquely to Γ; uniqueness follows if L^{Γ′} = 1, a generalisation of ③ (this is the author's own continuation, CSG ll. 8371–8383). Existence amounts to Norm_E(Γ′) → Γ being onto.
