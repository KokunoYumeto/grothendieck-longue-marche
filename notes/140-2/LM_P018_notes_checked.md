## First-pass notes

**R01 — scan 171 — implication not pursued — corrected.** The cancelled critique contrasts $B_U$, the classifying topos $B_{\Pi_1,U}$, with $B_X$, "un topos nettement plus gros". The corrected reading has the letters $U,X$ "(suggérant, en l'espèce, des schémas $U,X$)". It proposes $B_{X;S}$ "(pour suggérer les faisceaux sur $X$ ens.|ess. constants sur $X\setminus S$ et sur $S$)" and, as an interlinear replacement, "et de même $B_{D;S}$". Plain $B_D$, $B_X$ are kept for the classifying topoi. The whole passage is cancelled, so it records a notation that was considered and then dropped.

**R02 — scan 171 — claim without proof — corrected.** The text says the gluing operations are "essentiellement triviales et sans mystère, et indépendantes de ttes [toutes] hypothèses spéciales du type 'groupes à lacets'". The claim is independence from every loop-group hypothesis, not from one "hypothèse spéciale" as the first-pass wording suggested. A proof needs the amalgamated sums defined on scans 168–170.

**R03 — scans 172–173 — claim without proof — kept; quotation corrected.** The quotient topos is written $\mathcal X=(B/\Gamma)$. The text states that an action of $\Gamma$ on $B$ amounts to presenting $B$ as a Galois covering, with group $\Gamma$, of an essentially unique topos $(B,\Gamma)$, but gives no proof. A precise version must fix the 2-categorical data: actions up to coherent isomorphism, the torsor $U$, and the equivalence $B\simeq\mathcal X_{/U}$.

**R04 — scan 173 — claim without proof — kept.** The square $B_{D^*}\to B_U$ over $B_{D^*,\Gamma}\to B_{U,\Gamma}$ is asserted to be 2-cartesian, with $\Gamma$-galoisian vertical arrows. A proof must identify the 2-fibre product. In the profinite case of scan 172, it also needs a continuity convention.

**R05 — scan 174 — claim without proof — kept and sharpened.** The ink reads "on voit que $(B,\Gamma)$ est aussi multigaloisien". This is a claim, not the requirement "on veut que … soit" printed by CSG and IUT. The proof is not given. It needs the definition of a multigaloisian topos, and a description of $(B,\Gamma)$ through the fundamental groupoid of $B$ extended by $\Gamma$.

**R06 — scan 174 — implication not pursued — kept.** "$B_{U,\Gamma}$ est 0-conn. ssi $\operatorname{card}\pi_0(B_U)/\Gamma=1$, i.e. $B_U$ non vide et $\Gamma$ transitif sur $\pi_0(B_U)$." The author states non-emptiness explicitly. The induced action on $\pi_0$ is the only obstruction to connectedness of the quotient.

**R07 — scan 175 — implication not pursued — kept, with an addition.** The arithmetic situation is read as the geometric one, $(\overline X,\overline S)$ over $\overline K$ with $B_{\overline D^*}\to B_{\overline U}$, together with the $\Gamma$-action. Under $B_\Gamma$ the author wrote "$e_{\text{ét}}$". This identifies $B_\Gamma$, $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K/K)$, with the étale topos of the base point $e$ (the notation of scan 163).

**R08 — scans 175–176 — claim without proof — kept.** $B_{(I,\Gamma)}$ is the topos of $\Gamma$-sets over $I$, "un topos induit de $B_\Gamma$". Its connected components correspond to the orbits, and for a transitive action it is $B_{\Gamma_i}$. The underlying fact is standard: a transitive $\Gamma$-set is $\Gamma/\Gamma_i$, and $\Gamma$-sets over it are $\Gamma_i$-sets. In the profinite case, continuity conventions are still needed.

**R09 — scan 176 — claim without proof — kept; quotation corrected.** The corrected reading is "le morphisme de groupoïdes qui le décrit soit injectif sur les flèches". Only "injectif" is underlined. Both directions are asserted without proof: discrete $B$ gives an injective groupoid morphism, and $I=\coprod_j\Gamma/\Gamma_j$ is reconstructed from the injective $\Gamma_j\to\Gamma$.

**R10 — scan 177 — claim without proof — corrected.** The quotient is taken "dans|des chaque composante connexe de $\Pi_1B_{D^*,\Gamma}$" (low-confidence reading) by the kernels of the induced maps. The factorization is "(bi (pour objets), épi (pour hom)) suivi de mono (pour hom)". The witnesses' "épimorphique" in the second factor is a misreading (see S03).

**R11 — scan 178 — claim without proof — corrected.** The ink reads "passage de [$B_{D^*}\to B_U$, with $B_S$] à un $B_{X,S}$, via un $B_X$ ('bouchage des trous')", not "voire un $B_X$". $B_X$ is named as a stage of the passage, not as a further alternative target. **R13 — scan 179 — claim without proof — kept, with additions.** "Conditions équivalentes" a)–d) for a finite group acting on a surface. The labels are written in the order b), b'), c), d), with a) added last at the foot over a d). The heading reads "(Complément au §7|§15)". **R14 — scan 179, M02 — question — corrected.** "Il est prudent de supposer que $\Gamma$ opère en conservant une orientation de $U$ (supposé orientable), sinon on a des ennuis, p. ex. avec $z\to\overline z^{-1}$ … Il faudrait éclaircir|tirer au clair la situation générale (cas abélien) dans le th. … cas d'une anti-involution)." This is the author's open question about the abelian case without orientation preservation. The first pass dropped "Il".

**R15 — scan 179, M01 — conjecture — corrected.** "Ça doit être le seul contre-exemple (dans le cas … renvers. d'une orientation …) En tous cas un contre-exemple doit être tel que (si $\Gamma$ fidèle, $\Gamma\simeq(\pm1)$), opère par anti-involution…" There are two statements. The first is a uniqueness expectation. The second is a necessary condition on any counterexample ("un", not "ce"): a faithful $\Gamma$ has order 2 and acts by an orientation-reversing involution.

**R16 — scan 179, M02 — first pass's own idea — kept and completed.** In polar coordinates, $z\mapsto 1/\overline z$ is $(r,\theta)\mapsto(1/r,\theta)$. This is an orientation-reversing involution of $\mathbb C^*$ that fixes the unit circle and acts trivially on $\pi_1=\mathbb Z$. It is homotopic to the identity, so b′) and c) hold. It is not isotopic to the identity, because isotopies preserve orientation, so b) fails. The lifts $w\mapsto-\overline w+2\pi ik$ commute with $w\mapsto w+2\pi i$, so $\mathcal E\simeq\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/2$ is central and d) holds. But a) fails: a translation of order 2 in a group structure on $\mathbb C^*$ has no fixed point, while this map fixes a circle. So the map is a counterexample to d) ⇒ a) without orientation preservation, exactly as M01–M02 anticipate. Confidence: high.

**R17 — scan 180 — proof sketch — kept.** The case c) ⇒ d) is checked. With $\operatorname{Centre}(\pi)=1$, an extension of $\Gamma$ by $\pi$ is the pullback of $1\to\pi\to\operatorname{Aut}\pi\to\operatorname{Autext}\pi\to1$ along the outer action. A trivial outer action therefore gives $\pi\times\Gamma$. In the abelian case the outer action is the action itself, so c) makes $\pi$ central. The sketch is correct; the bracket "(et … ]" is unbalanced in the ink.

**R18 — scan 180 — claim depending on earlier material — kept; quotation corrected.** The corrected reading is "OPS [on peut supposer] que $\Gamma$ opère fidèlement … Si on avait|avec $\Gamma\ne1$, fixons un scindage de l'extension $\mathcal E$ … par le th. du n° 15 $\pi^\Gamma=|\simeq1$, or le scindage canonique de $\pi\times\Gamma$ sur $\Gamma$ donne $\pi^\Gamma=\pi$, absurde". The reduction to a faithful action replaces $\Gamma$ by its image. The contradiction needs $\pi\ne1$, which holds by $\pi_1(U)\ne(1)$, and the theorem of n° 15 for the splitting chosen. The witnesses' "Si on avait Γ = 1" inverts the hypothesis.

**R19 — scan 180 — claim the author marks unproved — kept; quotation corrected.** "Je dis qu'une action de $\Gamma$ sur $U$ est ici (à ~~isotopie~~ homéomorphisme|conjugaison près) définie par une action de $\Gamma|\pi$ sur le torseur", with "pas prouvé!" in the margin. $\pi$ acts trivially on $U_0=\pi\otimes\mathbb C/\pi$, so only $\Gamma$ gives the sentence a sense, even if the ink there is overwritten.

**R20 — scan 180 — first pass's own idea — kept and strengthened.** Crops at 2.2× confirm $\mathbb C$ in both torseur formulas, so the transcription keeps $U_0=\pi\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb C/\pi$ for $U\simeq S^1\times S^1$ as well. For $\pi\simeq\mathbb Z^2$ this group is $(\mathbb C^*)^2$, of real dimension 4, so a surface cannot be a torsor under it. The torus needs $\pi\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R/\pi$. On the next page (CSG l. 3719–3721) the author writes "π ⊗Z C (ou π ⊗Z R)", so this is a slip in the manuscript, not a transcription error.

## Second-pass notes

**S01 — scan 179 — claim without proof (second pass).** The definition says "anabélienne si $\pi$ non abélien (auquel cas $\operatorname{Centre}(\pi)=1$), i.e.|et si $U\not\simeq\mathbb C^*,S^1\times S^1$". Both parts hold for orientable surfaces but fail without orientability. The Klein bottle group $\langle a,b\mid bab^{-1}=a^{-1}\rangle$ is non-abelian with $b^2$ central. The Möbius band ($\pi_1=\mathbb Z$) and $\mathbb{RP}^2$ ($\pi_1=\mathbb Z/2$) have abelian $\pi_1\ne1$ but are not in the list. The assumption "(supposé orientable)" in M02 is therefore needed already for the definition and for c) ⇒ d). Confidence: high.

**S02 — scan 179 — claim without proof (second pass).** In a), the existence of a topological group structure on $U$ is followed by "(donc $U\simeq\mathbb C^*$ ou $S^1\times S^1$)". This needs the fact that a locally Euclidean group is a Lie group (Gleason–Montgomery–Zippin). The connected 2-dimensional Lie groups with $\pi_1\ne1$ are then, up to homeomorphism, $\mathbb R\times S^1\simeq\mathbb C^*$ and $T^2$. The step is correct but not argued.

**S03 — scan 177 — check by the second pass.** The factorization of $\Pi_1B_{D^*,\Gamma}\to\Pi_1B_\Gamma$ into (bijective on objects, surjective on Hom) followed by (injective on Hom) produces $B_{I,\Gamma}$. Its vertex groups are the stabilizers $\Gamma_i\subset\Gamma$. By the NB of scan 176, $B_{I,\Gamma}\to B_\Gamma$ must be injective on arrows, which confirms the ink "mono" against the witnesses' "épimorphique". Confidence: high.

**S04 — scan 179 — textual question with mathematical weight (second pass).** If the heading reads "Complément au §15", as the author's table has it, §17 completes the theorem on finite groups acting on surfaces in §15. The proof on scan 180 indeed rests on "le th. du n° 15". If the ink "§7" is right, it points to the groupoid set-up of §7 recalled on scan 178 instead. The ink is ambiguous (see the changes file, change 27).

**S05 — scan 178 — implication not pursued (second pass).** The upward-drawn sign under $B_{D^*}$ is more likely the arrow $B_{D^*}\to B_S$ of the gluing datum $B_U\leftarrow B_{D^*}\to B_S$, whose amalgamated sum is $B_{X,S}$. The sentence then states that $B\mapsto(B,\Gamma)$ commutes with forming this amalgamated sum and with "bouchage des trous" ($B_X$). That is the property §18 needs before inverting it as "forage de trous".

**S06 — scan 180 — claim without proof (second pass).** The abelian case of d) ⇒ a) is left open at the foot of the page. The author marks the reduction to a $\Gamma$-action on the torsor "pas prouvé!". The witnesses' continuation ("Corollaire-Scholie … pas tout à fait démontré faute d'avoir établi la classification topologique des opérations d'un groupe fini sur $\mathbb C^*$ ou sur $S^1\times S^1$") shows that the author left this gap open. By R16, without orientation preservation the abelian statement is false for $\mathbb C^*$, so any proof must use orientation preservation.
