Each first-pass note (R01–R19, I01–I06) is kept, corrected or dropped against `LM_P025_checked.tex`; the note text that follows the verdict is the checked version. Notes C01–C07 are new, from the second pass, and are labelled as such. “Unproved claim” means that no proof is given on these ten scans.

## First-pass notes

**R01 — Scan 241, l. 4–17. Kind: claim without proof / question. Verdict: corrected.** Reason: the first pass read “Soit encore”; the ink has “Sauf erreur on a”, so the author himself flags the identity as unchecked.
Checked note: The identity δ′(ξ′) = δ(ξ′) − ρ₀, introduced by “Sauf erreur on a”, reduces the lifting problem to the surjectivity of H¹(S, ℋ′_S) → H²(S, µ), after which Grothendieck writes “je ne m'en tire pas” and proposes to consult Giraud or Larry Breen. ## New notes (second pass)

**C01 — Scan 241, l. 5–9. Kind: claim without proof (second pass).** The identity δ′(ξ′) = δ(ξ′) − ρ₀ is the behaviour of the connecting map of the central extension 1 → µ → 𝒢 → ℋ → 1 under twisting by the torsor ξ₀ whose obstruction is ρ₀. Grothendieck marks it “Sauf erreur”; its sign depends on the convention for twisting and should be fixed before the reduction to surjectivity is used.

**C02 — Scans 241–243. Kind: implication not pursued (second pass).** In the case at hand Γ = π₀ and µ = π₁ of the 2-type are both ℤ/2 (µ = µ₂ on scan 245), and Aut(ℤ/2) is trivial, so the condition “(ainsi que l'action de Γ sur µ)” holds automatically; the only content is the vanishing of the Postnikoff-Sinh class in H³(ℤ/2, ℤ/2) ≅ ℤ/2, which is not automatic. The splittings of Bℋ̿ as K(Γ,1) × K(µ,2) that induce the identity on π₁ and π₂ form a torsor under [K(Γ,1), K(µ,2)] = H²(Γ, µ) ≅ ℤ/2, which is the dependence on a choice that the author notes.

**C03 — Scan 244, margin. Kind: claim without proof, checked (second pass).** The margin's statement that a (ℤ/2ℤ)-torsor amounts to a system of twisted integers holds because Aut(ℤ) = {±1} ≅ ℤ/2: a ℤ/2-torsor η gives the local system ℤ ×^{ℤ/2} η of rank one, and conversely the sheaf of generators of such a local system is a ℤ/2-torsor. This is the orientation local system when χ is the orientation character.

**C04 — Scans 245 and 250. Kind: inconsistency noted (second pass).** “Algèbres d'Azumaya de rang 4” (245) and “de degré fixé (ici 4)” (250) disagree: an Azumaya algebra of rank 4 has degree 2, which matches Sl(2) and µ₂ in the exact sequence of scan 245. The “4” on scan 250 is therefore the rank, and “degré” there is either a slip or used for rank.

**C05 — Scans 246–248. Kind: own idea (second pass).** For ℋ = G̃P(1, ℂ) one has π₂(ℋ) = 0 and π₃(ℋ) ≅ ℤ, so B_α is an isomorphism on π_i for i ≤ 3 and only surjective on π₄. By the standard connectivity count, maps of a relative CW pair of relative dimension d then correspond bijectively for d ≤ 3 and only surjectively for d = 4; applied to (S × S^i, S × pt) this gives injectivity on π₂ of the mapping spaces only for dim S ≤ 1, not for dim(S × S²) ≤ 4 as written for 0-faithfulness, and bijectivity on π₀ for dim S ≤ 3. This would explain both the “??” of scan 247 and the remark that 1-faithfulness “impliquerait alors la 3-fidélité”; it is a heuristic count, not a proof of the optimal bounds.

**C06 — Scans 249–250. Kind: implication not pursued (second pass).** For a form G of GP(1) = PGL₂ the simply connected cover G̃ is the corresponding form of Sl(2), so “un relèvement de u en une section de G̃” is the arithmetic counterpart of the liftings to Sl(2, C̲_S) that define the µ₂-gerbe on scan 245. Every automorphism coming from Sl(2, K) has such a lift, which is why scan 250 concludes that they are all identified with the identity.

**C07 — Scan 241 (and scan 240). Kind: transcription question affecting notation (second pass).** The letter of ξ₀, ξ′ here is read ζ in LM_P024 for the same sentence; the mathematics does not decide between them, but the edition must use one letter for the class in H¹(S, ℋ_S) and its twist across both packages.
