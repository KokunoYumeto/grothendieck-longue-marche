## First-pass notes

**R01. Scan 181. Claim without proof. Corrected.**
Grothendieck says the abelian case "n'est pas tout à fait démontré, faute d'avoir établi la classification top. des opérations d'un groupe fini sur ℂ* ou sur S¹×S¹". The missing piece is a topological classification of finite group actions on ℂ* and on the torus, enough to show that such an action comes from an action on the U₀-torsor.
Reason for the correction: the first pass read "étudié" where the ink has "établi", and left the exponent of ℂ open; it is ℂ*.

**R02. Scan 181. Claim without proof. Corrected.**
Under the extra hypothesis U^Γ ≠ ∅, "il est encore vrai que" trivial operation on π is equivalent to trivial action on U. The argument reduces to a faithful Γ and refers to the anabelian argument "plus haut", which is outside this package.
Reason: the first pass read "il en résulte aussi que". That presents the statement as a consequence of the struck conditions a) b) c); the ink states that the equivalence remains true under the new hypothesis.

**R03. Scan 182. Claim without proof. Kept, with a correction.**
Three correspondences are stated "à isom unique près" without proof: between realisations of the outer group, objects of Π₁B_{U'}, and pairs (Π', L'ᵢ) ↔ objects D̃ᵢ'* of Π₁B_{D'ᵢ*}. The margin note "Je vais provisoirement laisser tomber les "primes" !" (first pass: "voir précisément dans les points") explains the change of notation from Π', L'ᵢ to π, Lᵢ on scans 183–184. The first pass's remark that the passage is unreadable no longer applies to the margin.

**R04. Scans 183–184. Claim without proof. Kept.**
The surjection Isom_{Π₁Vᵢ}(a,bᵢ) ≃ Isom_{Π₁U}(a,bᵢ) → {Lᵢ in class i} and its compatibility with the action of π are asserted; the fibres are not computed. The first pass's warning not to strengthen surjectivity to bijectivity is right: two paths differing by a loop round sᵢ give the same Lᵢ.

**R05. Scans 184–185. Implication not pursued. Kept; quotation corrected.**
The independence of π = π'/⟨⟨L'ᵢ⟩⟩ from the chosen object D̃ᵢ* is reduced ("tt revient à voir", not "il revient") to the triviality of the action of Aut(D̃ᵢ*) ≃ T ≃ Lᵢ. That triviality holds because u ∈ Lᵢ acts by int(u) and u becomes 1 in the quotient. The argument is present. What is left implicit is why a functor on a connected groupoid whose automorphism actions are trivial is "constant", i.e. factors through a point up to unique isomorphism.

**R06. Scan 185. Claim without proof. Kept; detail corrected.**

**R08. Scans 186–187. Claim without proof. Corrected.**
The theorem: if the loop groups of types (g, ν) and (g, ν−1) are anabelian, i.e. 2g+ν ≥ 4 [donc g ≥ 2, ou g ≥ 1 (or = 1), ν ≥ 2, ou g = 0, ν ≥ 4], then Aut ext(π', i) → Aut(π) is an isomorphism. The proof plan has two steps. First, the exact sequence 1 → π → Aut ext(π', i) → Aut ext(π) → 1 "on va définir": it is still to be defined, not already defined as the first pass read. Second, a morphism of extensions into 1 → π → Aut π → Aut ext π → 1, which "sera nécessairement un isomorphisme". The relation sign in "2g+ν … 4" is overwritten in the ink; only ≥ agrees with the bracket.

**R09. Scan 188. Claim without proof. Corrected.**

**R14. Scan 190. Implication not pursued. Kept.**
An action of Γ on π of type (g, ν) "pas seulement extérieurement" gives an outer action on π' of type (g, ν+1) fixing the class i. The reverse passage, from such outer actions to honest ones, is what the isomorphism Aut ext(π', i) ≃ Aut(π) of the theorem supplies. These lines state only the forward construction.

**R15. Scan 190. Claim without proof. Corrected.**
The normaliser ℰ'ᵢ of Lᵢ in ℰ' gives 1 → L'ᵢ (≃ T) → ℰ'ᵢ → Γ → 1 ("on trouve"). Two facts are used without proof. First, ℰ'ᵢ → Γ is onto: Γ fixes the class i, so any lift can be corrected by an element of π'. Second, ℰ'ᵢ ∩ π' = N_{π'}(L'ᵢ) = L'ᵢ: a loop subgroup is self-normalising in a non-abelian loop group. **R17. Scans 181, 187. The first pass's own idea. Kept, made concrete.**

## Second-pass notes (mine)

**S01. Scan 182. Own idea (an inconsistency in the text).**
"card I' = ν" is written for a group of type (g, ν+1), which has ν+1 classes of loop subgroups (the witnesses print ν+1). It is probably a slip of the pen, but the count matters for the index "2ν!" on scan 189 (see S04).

**S02. Scan 182. Implication not pursued.**
The struck parenthesis gives a uniform criterion: a loop group of type (g, ν+1) is non-abelian iff 2g+ν ≥ 2, i.e. iff 2−2g (= EP, the Euler–Poincaré characteristic of the compact surface) ≤ ν. Equivalently, χ of the punctured surface is negative. The case form kept in the text, "si g = 0 on a ν ≥ 2", is equivalent, since for g ≥ 1 the group (free of rank 2g+ν) is always non-abelian.

**S03. Scans 187–188. Claim without proof.**
"qui sera nécessairement un isomorphisme" is the five-lemma step. A morphism between two extensions of Aut ext(π) by π that is the identity on the kernel and on the quotient is an isomorphism. What has to be checked is exactly that the map Aut ext(π', i) → Aut(π) restricts to the identity of π (inner automorphisms) and induces the identity on Aut ext(π). This is the content of the "vérifications de compatibilité" left undone on scan 189.

**S04. Scan 189. Own idea. Confidence: low.**
The index "2ν!" plausibly measures the two rigidifications built into 𝒯^{00}: acting trivially on T (index at most 2, the orientation) and fixing each of the ν loop classes (index at most ν!, their permutations). The text does not say this; it is an interpretation to be checked against the definition of 𝒯^{00}_{g,ν} on scan 188.

**S05. Scan 184. Question.**
The base point is written with an index, Ũ₀ = Ũ(aᵢ) and Ũ(aᵢ) ⥲ ρ_!(D̃ᵢ*(bᵢ)), while the paths run "de a vers bᵢ" and Ũ₀ was defined from a single base point a ∈ U on scan 183. The relation between a and aᵢ (one base point per hole, or a slip) is not stated.

**S06. Scan 181. Own idea. Confidence: medium.**
In the abelian case with U^Γ ≠ ∅, a fixed point identifies the U₀-torsor U with U₀ itself, Γ-equivariantly. If Γ acts by torsor (affine) maps, it then acts by group automorphisms of U₀ = π⊗ℝ/π, determined by its action on π, so trivial action on π gives trivial action on U. The fixed point removes the translation part only. It does not remove the need for the claim that an action on U comes from an action on the torsor, which is the classification gap of R01.
