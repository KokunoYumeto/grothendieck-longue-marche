# Legibility and numbering

The main text is moderately legible; the darker additions are often clearer than the underlying pencil. The least reliable regions are the overwritten formulas and primes on scan 17, the revisions in condition b) on scan 19, and the ending of anabélique/anabélien and marginal f) on scan 20. Word counts inside illegible markers are estimates. Some insertion attachment points remain uncertain even where individual words can be read.

The top-centre handwritten numbers 4–8 are tagged with mspage. The separate lower-left sequence 10–19 is retained as margin entries; its hand is not attributed. Printed archive footers are outside the transcription.
