## [checker] notes

A. **Scan 322, claim without proof — checked [checker].** The asserted equivalence holds with "injective". (11) is an Ŝ̂(π)-equivariant map out of a transitive set. The stabiliser of π₀♮ is the inverse image of T̂(π₀♮) (using π ⊂ Ŝ(π₀♮), scan 321), and the stabiliser of the subgroup T̂(π₀♮) under conjugation is the inverse image of its normaliser. Such a map is injective iff the two stabilisers coincide, i.e. iff T̂ is self-normalising; "bijective" is right only with the target restricted to the conjugacy class of geometric subgroups.

B. **Scans 322–323, conjecture (status) [checker].** The retraction in the margin of 322 was added later ("Cela apparaît clairement par la suite"). The main text of 323, "À l'opposé de ce qu'on peut conjecturer sur T̂ … on supposerait plutôt que T̂⁺ est invariant dans T̂̂", still presupposes the retracted conjecture. Under the margin's "question judicieuse" (T̂ invariant in T̂̂), T̂ and T̂⁺ would behave alike, so the contrast on 323 is not the author's final position.

C. **Scan 323, claim without proof — proof sketch [checker].** For χ(T̂) ⊂ {±1}: T̂(π₀♮) is the image of Ŝ(π₀♮), the closure of the discrete Aut_lac(π₀) (scan 321). Assume, as "de multiplicateur +1" suggests, that the multiplier is the exponent with which loop generators go to conjugates of loop generators. An automorphism of the discrete π₀ preserving the loop structure sends loop generators to conjugates of loop generators or of their inverses, so its multiplier is ±1. χ is continuous and {±1} is closed in Ẑ*, so the same holds on the closure.

D. **Scan 323, claim without proof — reading-independent [checker].** Σ = ker(χ|T̂) is open, as the kernel of a continuous character into a discrete group, and normal of index 2 provided χ|T̂ is onto {±1} (see F). So "ouvert" and "invariant" both describe true properties, and the unsettled reading changes no claim. "Invariant" would, however, use one word in two senses within the sentence (normal in T̂, then normal in T̂̂).

E. **Scan 325, implicit step [checker].** "… on aura|a donc T̂′⁺ = int_g T̂⁺" uses that χ is a homomorphism to the commutative group Ẑ*, so χ∘int(g) = χ and int(g) carries ker(χ|T̂) onto ker(χ|T̂′). With T̂′⁺ = T̂⁺ = Σ this gives g ∈ 𝒩, and the class of g in Γ_Σ conjugates T̂/Σ to T̂′/Σ. The step is valid but not stated.

F. **Scan 325, claim without proof — gap [checker].** For each T̂ in (16) to give an element of order exactly 2, χ|T̂ must take the value −1. This is the "d'indice 2" of 323, stated without proof. It holds if the discrete Aut_lac(π₀) contains an automorphism of multiplier −1, e.g. one induced by an orientation-reversing homeomorphism of the surface of type (g,ν) that preserves the punctures. That justification is mine and is not on these pages.

G. **Scan 328, unstated theorem [checker].** The lower row of (24) identifies Autext_lac(Π_{g,ν}) with T_{g,ν}. This is the Dehn–Nielsen–Baer theorem, in its version for punctured surfaces (outer automorphisms permuting the loop classes), used without mention. The upper row has T⁺_{g,ν} of index 2 in T_{g,ν}. So the "isom|hom. de structures d'extensions" can only be an isomorphism onto the sub-extension over T⁺_{g,ν}, not onto the lower row.

H. **Scan 330, implication not pursued [checker].** The cancelled sentence put the image of the arithmetic group π₁(M_{g,ν}) inside T̂(π₀♮), which the text's own claims exclude. By scan 323, χ takes only the values ±1 on T̂. The multiplier of the image of a Galois element should be its cyclotomic character, which takes other values; this standard fact is not stated on these pages. The replacement keeps only the geometric part π₁(M_{g,ν,Q̄}) → T̂⁺, with complex conjugation giving the opposite orientation.

I. **Scan 329, implication not pursued [checker].** "Exactement une classe de discrétifications orientées" requires the indeterminacy left after choosing Q̄ ≃ Q̄₀ to fix the image of the standard oriented class. That indeterminacy consists of inner automorphisms by π₁(U_{g,ν,Q̄}). Their image in Ŝ̂(π) must lie in the stabiliser of that oriented class. The upper row of (25), with π₁(U_{g,ν,C}) ≅ Ŝ⁺_{g,ν}, supplies this, but the text leaves the inference implicit.
