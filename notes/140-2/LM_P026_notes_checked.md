**R01. Scan 251. Claim without proof. Keep.**

**R02. Scans 251–252. Claim without proof. Keep, with a small correction.**

**R04. Scan 253 d). Claim without proof. Correct.**
In the corrected reading the inserted phrase is "a priori d'une telle op.", attached to "l'existence", not "pourvu qu'une telle op.". The claim is therefore: continuity together with a) determines the operation uniquely, and the a priori existence of such an operation follows from π₁(A°_{g,ν}) = 0. How a lifted continuous group action is obtained from simple connectivity is not argued.

**R20. Scan 260. Claim with a partial argument. Correct.**

## Second-pass notes (my own)

**S01. Scans 257–258. Own check.**
The case values agree with the stated rules. δ = dim_ℂ Aut°: (1,0) → 1, (0,2) → 1, (0,1) → 2, (0,0) → 3. With μ = 3g−3+(ν+δ) these give μ = 1, 0, 0, 0, matching the display. The rule "δ augmente de 1 … (g,ν−1) abélien" gives δ(0,1) = 2 and δ(0,0) = 3, and the marginal ν′ form gives the same μ.

**S02. Scan 258. Own remark on an internal tension.**
"À partir de … M̃_{g,0} avec g ≥ 1, quand ν augmente les dimensions complexes augmentent d'autant" is not true for the step (1,0) → (1,1), which is abelian. The very next parenthesis ("Par contre M̃_{1,0} ⟸ M̃_{1,1} est un homéomorphisme") makes that exception explicit. It agrees with (85), M̃_{g,ν+1} ≃ M̃_{g,ν} for abelian (g,ν).

**S03. Scan 253 margin. Claim without proof.**
"L'opération de A°_{g,ν}×1 se décrit le plus simplement par translations de A°_{g,ν} sur l'espace homogène Ũ_{g,ν} de A°_{g,ν}" uses (73), Ũ_{g,ν} ≃ A°_{g,ν}/A°_{g,ν+1}. It inherits the unproved identification of R02.

**S04. Scan 252 NB. Claim without proof.**
"Ã°_{g,ν} = A°_{g,ν} et π₀(Ã_{g,ν}) ≃ Γ′_{g,ν+1}" is read off the second extension in (75). It needs A°_{g,ν} connected and the topology on the fibre product (74). Neither is discussed on these pages.

**S05. Scan 252. Reading consistency.**
A tilde stands above the U in "Π_{g,ν} = π₁(U_{g,ν}, a_{g,ν})". Read literally as π₁(Ũ, a), the group would be trivial. So either the mark is stray or it is a slip, and the transcription keeps both readings under `\unsure`.

**S06. Scan 258. Implication not pursued.**
The margin note "i.e. sur surfaces (orientées?) sphériques à ν ≤ 3, toutes les structures complexes sont isotopes" is the geometric content of μ = 0 in genus 0. It is the basis of the later reduction to M_{0,3}. No argument is given here.

**S07. Scan 260. Claim without proof.**
Inside the bracket, "il est donc à fibre contractile" relies on Ũ_{g,ν} being contractible as the universal cover of a hyperbolic (anabelian) surface. This is used but not stated as a separate fact on these pages.
