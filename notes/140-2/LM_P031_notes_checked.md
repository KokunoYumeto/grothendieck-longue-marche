# LM_P031 (scans 301–310): research notes, checked against the corrected text

Each first-pass note (numbered as in `LM_P031_notes.md`, §4) is given its scan, its kind and a short statement, then a verdict: keep, correct or drop. Notes added in the second pass are labelled **[second pass]**. Quotations follow `LM_P031_checked.tex`.

## First-pass notes

1. **Scan 301 — claim without proof.** An automorphism u that is the identity near S_{g,ν} gives an element of SΓ^! “en prenant comme γ_i les identités”, using u_•(D̃_i) ≃ D̃_i^*. A proof must show that these choices define an element and a homomorphism. *Verdict: keep.* The disc formula is now read with a doubtful u_• in place of u_i.

2. **Scan 301 — claim without proof.** “Le fait que l'hom obtenu soit trivial sur (SA_{g,ν})^0 est ss doute trivial.” Grothendieck asserts the triviality on the identity component with a hedge (“sans doute”) and gives no argument. *Verdict: correct.* The first pass read “tt d'ordre formel”, which misreads the ink and overstates the claim.

3. **Scan 301 — claim without proof.** Surjectivity says that every orientation-preserving automorphism inducing the identity on S_{g,ν} is isotopic to one that is the identity near S_{g,ν}, “c'est facile”. The isotopy can be chosen, “si on veut”, to be the identity outside a small neighbourhood of S_{g,ν}. *Verdict: correct.* The two illegible words are “si on veut”, so the support condition is an option offered, not part of what must be proved.

4. **Scan 301 — expectation.** “L'injectivité peut-être plus délicate” announces the kernel computation of scans 302–304. *Verdict: keep;* the reading is now secure.

5. **Scan 302 — claim without proof.** The sequence 1 → SA′⁺ → A′^{!+} → ∏ Aut⁺(C_i) → 1 and its long exact homotopy sequence require restriction to the boundary to be a fibration. The annotation adds that Aut⁺(C_i) is homotopic to “le groupe circulaire standard 𝕌, tordu par T”, which supplies the vertical ≀ identifying ∏ π₁(Aut⁺(C_i)) with T^I. *Verdict: keep,* extended with the corrected annotation.

6. **Scan 302 — expectation.** The map A′⁰ → A is defined “par 'recollt des disques'” (gluing the discs back), and the induced maps “sont sûrement des équiv. d'homotopie”. A proof would extend automorphisms across the glued discs, e.g. by the Alexander trick, and check the comparison of the subgroups SA′ → SA. *Verdict: correct.* The first pass read “par 'oubli' des disques”, which reverses the construction.

7. **Scan 302 — implication not pursued.** By exactness, the kernel of π₀(SA′^{!+}) → Γ′^{!+} is the cokernel of π₁(A′⁰) → T^I; scans 303–304 compute exactly this. *Verdict: keep.*

8. **Scan 303 — claim without proof.** In the anabelian case π₁(A⁰) = 0 gives the boxed extension 1 → T^{S_{g,ν}} → π₀(SA) → Γ^{!+} → 1. The vanishing of π_i((SA)⁰) for i ≥ 2 uses π_i((SA)⁰) ≅ π_i(A⁰) and the contractibility of A⁰, which these pages do not prove. *Verdict: keep.*

9. **Scan 303 — implication not pursued.** The correction “quatre” → “deux” comes with the insertion “(sous-entendant que l'on ait I ≠ ∅ !) — on a tjrs g = 0, ν = 1 ou 2”. *Verdict: correct the wording.* “types” is “tjrs” (toujours). See second-pass note B.

10. **Scan 303 — claim without proof.** G ≈→ A⁰: the identity component of the complex automorphism group is asserted to be homotopy equivalent to A⁰. *Verdict: keep.*

## Second-pass notes

A. **Scan 301 — claim without proof [second pass].** SΓ^{!+} is discrete, so “ss doute trivial” amounts to saying that the map SA → SΓ^{!+} is constant along isotopies through automorphisms that are the identity near S_{g,ν}. That needs the map to be locally constant for the topology on SA, and the page does not show this.

B. **Scan 303 — implication not pursued [second pass].** The non-anabelian types are (g,ν) = (0,0), (0,1), (0,2) and (1,0). Of these, only (0,1) and (0,2) have I = S_{g,ν} ≠ ∅, which explains both “quatre” → “deux” and “on a tjrs g = 0, ν = 1 ou 2”. The theorem's inserted “Supposons ν >|≥ 0” repeats this hypothesis, which would favour “>”, but the sign stays doubtful in the transcription.

C. **Scan 304 — implication not pursued [second pass].** In both abelian cases the boxed extension of scan 303 fails: T^I is replaced by the cokernel of π₁(G) → T^I. With Γ^{!+}_{0,2} = 0 this gives π₀(SA_{0,2}) ≅ T, and π₀(SA_{0,1}) = 0. The theorem limits the canonical isomorphism to the anabelian case accordingly, and keeps ∞-connectedness for all cases.

D. **Scans 305–306 — claim without proof [second pass].** The sentence “Bien entendu, SΓ est le ss-groupe des ∏ Aut_lac(π_i), l'image inverse de la diagonale …”, absent from both witnesses, presents SΓ as a fibre product over the diagonal Γ ⊂ Autext_lac(π)^I. The whole inverse image of the diagonal has kernel ∏ Int(π_i) ≅ π^I. The kernel is T^I only for tuples with u_i(L_i) ⊂ L_i (scan 305) and only because L_i is its own normaliser in π_i (“réduite à L_i”, scan 306).

E. **Scans 309–310 — own idea [second pass].** Three steps are one fixed-point argument: the uniqueness on scan 309 (π^{G_Q} = 1), the margin NB of scan 310 (trivial normaliser in π) and the closing “En effet” (g̃ preserves Fix(G_Q) = {Q̃}). An element normalising r(G_Q) preserves the unique fixed point Q̃ of the rotation group, and π acts freely on Ũ. Writing the argument once makes the role of G_Q^+ ≠ 1 explicit, since a reflection alone fixes a line rather than a point. Confidence: medium.

F. **Scan 310 — claim without proof [second pass].** With the restored “et”, the image of Centr(r(G_Q)) “contient 𝒢_Q, et m̄ le relèvement 𝒢_Q → E”. The centraliser would then carry a canonical lift of the whole stabiliser, which is what the next sentence (the small group's lift fixes the large group's lift) relies on. This rests on a low-confidence reading of the struck passage.

G. **Scan 302 — question [second pass].** The diagram leaves π₀(SA′⁺) “à calculer”, with an arrow pointing at it. Scan 303 answers the question only for SA, after the expected homotopy equivalences, so the computation for the bordered surfaces themselves is never written out.
