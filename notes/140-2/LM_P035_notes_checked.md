2. **Scan 341: claim without proof. CORRECT.** The margin, read again as "(et|car) Σ/Σ^! ⥲ N/N^! (≃ 𝔖_I)", gives the reason for bijectivity. The only input used without proof on this page is the surjectivity of Σ → 𝔖_I ("induit un hom surjectif"). *Reason:* the first pass asked for an argument that the margin in fact supplies.

## B. Notes added in the second pass (Claude)

B1. **Scans 342 and 346: implication not pursued (Claude).** The inserted vertical relation reads Ĝ_{g,(ν,ν')} ⊂ Σ_{g,ν}, with "= Σ_{g,(ν,ν')}"; the same line has "Ĝ_{g,ν'} = Σ_{g,ν'}", and scan 346 has "Ĝ^!_{g,ν} = Σ^!_{g,ν}". These equate single-hatted *automorphism* groups with subgroups Σ of the *outer* group T̂̂ (scan 345: Σ_{g,ν} = T̂^+_{g,ν}). Making them precise needs the passage Aut → Out (quotient by π̂) and the orientation condition +. They should not be read literally. Confidence: medium.

B2. **Scan 346: own idea (Claude).** For 0 < ν' < ν, the "pas nécess." is in fact a "not". Ĝ̂(π,I') preserves which subset of I(π) a base sends the first ν' standard loops to, so it cannot act transitively on Bases(π). Likewise Ĝ̂^!(π) acts trivially on I, so it cannot change the bijection a base induces between {1,…,ν} and I(π) unless ν ≤ 1. Confidence: high, granted that a base induces that bijection (scan 342).

B3. **Scan 348: own idea, possible slip in the manuscript (Claude).** Suppose the admitted isomorphism T̂̂(π,i) ≅ Ĝ̂(π') holds and π' has trivial centre (anabelian case). Then the quotient of T̂̂(π,i) by π' (inner automorphisms) is T̂̂(π'), an *outer* group. The third terms written as Ĝ̂(π',i), Ĝ̂_{g,ν−1}, G_{g,ν−1} and G^{!+}_{g,ν−1} are therefore probably meant as the outer groups, and the NB on 349 doubts surjectivity onto T̂̂_{g,ν−1} if that glyph is T. The quotient sets used afterwards are unaffected, because Ĝ̂^!/Ĝ^{!+} and T̂̂^!/T̂^{!+} are the same set Prédiscrét^+(π'). This is a question about the manuscript; the transcription keeps the letters of the ink. Confidence: medium.

B4. **Scan 349: implication not pursued (Claude).** The corollary stops at the minimal types allowed by the standing hypothesis 2g+ν ≥ 3: (g,0) for g ≥ 2, (1,1) and (0,3). The excluded (1,0) and (0,2) are exactly the non-anabelian types. This matches the margin of 348 and the restored "anabélien" of 350. Confidence: high (arithmetic check).

B5. **Scan 350: implication not pursued (Claude).** Formula (2) with its overbrace gives 2g'−2+card I' = n(2g−2+card I). Since anabelian means 2g−2+card I > 0, π' is anabelian whenever π is, so "anabélien comme tjrs" is inherited by finite-index subgroups. Confidence: high (arithmetic check).

B6. **Scan 341: claim without proof (Claude).** The page leaves unproved the statement that T̂̂ → 𝔖_I "n'est pas trivial sur Σ, mais induit un hom surjectif Σ → 𝔖_I". It needs Σ to permute the punctures transitively in every way. That holds for the discrete oriented Teichmüller group, whose image is all of 𝔖_I, and passes to the closure Σ = T̂^+. Confidence: medium; it depends on the definition of Σ on earlier pages.
