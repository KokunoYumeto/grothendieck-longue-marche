## First-pass notes

**R01 — scan 131 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The exponent is now read ⊗−1, so the claim is: the composites T_π ≅ L_i → H²(π, Ẑ)^{⊗−1} do not depend on i, hence T_π is identified with the dual of H²(π, Ẑ) when g ≠ 0. The exponent doubt that the first pass gave as a block to formalisation is settled; the independence of i still needs the preceding pages' definitions.

**R02 — scans 131–132 — conjecture / proposed construction — keep.** "On voudrait en déduire" a diagram of topoi (horizontal links drawn without heads) and a trace homomorphism inducing an isomorphism H²_!(U′, Ẑ) → H²_!(U, Ẑ) that should be the contragredient of the orientation isomorphism attached to f. The degree H²_! is now settled; the construction remains a wish, not a result.

**R03 — scans 132–133 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The section is about "morphismes mixtes" (not "non stricts"), and the datum k identifies the local system of π₁ on B_{D*} with "le système loc. constant T", a locally constant system, not a constant one. This is what is said to define B_D (≃ B_S) and B_{X,U} with complementary open and closed subtopoi; the reconstruction of (X, S) from these data is still asserted, not proved.

**R04 — scan 133 — unpursued implication — keep, corrected.** The ink reads "On aura S′ = S′₀ ⨿ S′₁ (S′₀ = f⁻¹(S)), donc D′ = D′₀ ⨿ D′₁": the splitting of D′ is a consequence of that of S′, not a further datum. The point stands that B_{X′,U′} → B_{X,U} needs data beyond f_π: π′ → π.

**R05 — scan 134 — unproved claim — keep.** The passage from a family of universal coverings of U indexed by S′₁ = I′₁ to a family of π-torsors (P_{i′}) after choosing Ũ is asserted; the action conventions and the change of Ũ remain to be tracked.

**R06 — scan 134 — proof deferred — keep, corrected.** The ink has "l'hom de topos B_{X′,U′} → B_{X,U}" (a cancelled "un" replaced by the definite article): the author expects the square on the 0-part and B_{D′₁*} → B_{D′₁} → B_U to determine the morphism. "Je passe sur le détail de la description" leaves this unproved.

**R07 — scan 134, margin — conjecture — keep, corrected.** Reading settled: "Il semble qu'on soit en droit de faire la description des morphismes de topos B_{X′,U′} → B_{X,U} qui induisent U′ → U et qui sur D′₁* (= D′*|U …) se factorisent par D′₁* → D′₁" (the witnesses' "en train de" is not the ink). It is a characterisation claim without proof; the parenthesis identifying D′₁* with the part of D′* over U is not fully secure.

**R08 — scan 135 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** In the equivariant version the operator group at the head of the paragraph is Γ written over Σ, the source topos is B_{D′₁,Σ} = B_{I′₁,Σ(?)}, and the target B_{U,Γ} is Γ written over Σ. The author is passing from Σ to Γ in mid-paragraph, so the groups in this display must not be silently unified. The required datum is, for each orbit, i.e. for each i′ ("tt i′"), a Γ_{s′}-object of the category of universal coverings of (U, Γ).

**R09 — scans 135–136 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The ink says the datum B_{Γ_{s′}} → B_{X,U} "revient sauf erreur à" a right π-torsor P and a splitting of ℰ^P → Γ over Γ_{s′}: the author marks the equivalence as tentative. ℰ is "une extension de Σ ⊂ Γ par π" (the inclusion was dropped by the first pass). Changes of representative and of torsor still need checking.

**R10 — scan 136 — unproved claim — keep.** An automorphism of (P, q) corresponds to α ∈ π centralising Γ_{s′}, "i.e. qui soit fixé par Γ_{s′} opérant sur π"; the action of Γ_{s′} on π is the one given by the splitting. Under π^{Γ_{s′}} = (1) the object is rigid ("défini à isom unique près") by its isomorphism class.

**R11 — scan 136 — conjecture — keep, corrected.** The parenthesis reads "(dont l'opération extérieure est draconienne !)", not "dite canonique". The expectation that π^{Γ_{s′}} = (1) always holds in the arithmetic case thus rests explicitly on the strength of the outer Galois action on π; it is not proved here (see own note N4).

**R12 — scan 137 — conjecture — keep, corrected.** The formerly illegible phrase reads "et permet de paradigmer ce qu'un tel pt rationnel permettrait d'obtenir": a splitting class should serve as the model for what a rational point of U over K provides. It remains a programmatic statement, not a bijection theorem.

**R13 — scan 137 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The points to check are labelled "cas anabéliens" (not "cas anodins"). The justification reads: known in abstract algebraic geometry "du moment qu'on admet que u conserve [or préserve] une structure complexe — il en est justement ainsi si on admet qu'il n'y a pas de sauvagerie". **R14 — scan 138 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** A structure on Y = X/Γ⁰ gives one on X stable by Γ⁰, and one stable by Γ "si on choisit celle de Y invariée par l'élément non trivial de Γ/Γ⁰". **R15 — scan 138 — unproved claim — keep, corrected.** The insertion reads approximately "si Γ⁰ ≠ 1, [i.e. Γ pas réduit à une … anti-involution …)": U^Γ is finite as soon as Γ is not just an anti-holomorphic involution together with the identity. **R16 — scan 138, NB — unproved claim — corrected.** The group is Γ⁰ (small superscript circle visible): "U^Γ ≠ ∅ implique que Γ⁰ est cyclique". So read, it is the standard fact that a finite group of orientation-preserving automorphisms fixing a point acts faithfully by rotations on the tangent line, hence is cyclic. The witnesses' "Γ est cyclique" would fail for dihedral stabilisers (R23).

**R17 — scan 139 — explicit question — keep.** "Cette application est-elle bijective ??", with the inserted condition Γ⁰ ≠ (1); injectivity and surjectivity are separate problems.

**R19 — scan 139 — proof obligation — corrected.** The word the first pass took as a cancelled "Obs" is "OPS" (on peut supposer), not cancelled: the author reduces injectivity to Γ = Γ⁰ ≅ ℤ/pℤ, p prime, without giving the reason (see own note N3). ## Notes added in the second pass

**N1 — scan 136 — Own (second pass), implication not pursued.** "Une extension ℰ de Σ ⊂ Γ par π", together with the Σ → Γ overwritings on scan 135, shows that a subgroup Σ ⊂ Γ (probably the group acting on I′₁, or a stabiliser) was in play and was then replaced by Γ. Before formalising, decide whether the extension is restricted to Σ or taken over all of Γ.

**N2 — scan 136 — Own (second pass), claim flagged by the author.** "Revient sauf erreur à" marks the torsor-plus-splitting description of B_{Γ_{s′}} → B_{X,U} as unverified by Grothendieck himself. Any use of it should state it as a claim to prove, not as part of the construction.

**N3 — scan 139 — Own (second pass), own idea.** The reduction "OPS Γ = Γ⁰ ≅ ℤ/pℤ" can be justified for injectivity: if Γ⁰ ≠ 1 choose Γ′ ⊂ Γ⁰ of prime order; for x ∈ U^Γ ⊂ U^{Γ′} the splitting class attached to x restricts to the class attached to x for ℰ′ = p⁻¹(Γ′), so equal classes for Γ give equal classes for Γ′, and injectivity for Γ′ gives x = y. Confidence: high for the formal reduction, assuming the fixed-point map is compatible with restriction to subgroups; it says nothing about surjectivity.

**N4 — scan 136 — Own (second pass), unproved claim and possible route.** For the conjecture π^{Γ_{s′}} = (1): in the pro-ℓ completion, a weight argument (the graded pieces of the lower central series have nonzero Frobenius weights) excludes nontrivial fixed elements when the base field is finitely generated over ℚ. This does not by itself give the profinite statement, because π does not inject into the product of its pro-ℓ completions. Confidence: medium; not checked against the literature here.

**N5 — scan 134, margin — Own (second pass), implication.** If "(= D′*|U)" is read correctly, D′₁* is the part of the punctured neighbourhoods D′* lying over U, i.e. around the points of S′₁ sent into U. The condition that the topos morphism factor through D′₁* → D′₁ then says that these punctured discs extend across the puncture in B_{X,U}, which is what "mixte" requires.

**N6 — scan 137, margin — Own (second pass), implication.** The marginal title "Structure des Γ-orbites critiques en termes d'extension…" announces the theme of § 15: orbits with nontrivial stabilisers are to be described by splittings of ℰ. This matches the author's later table of contents ("orbites critiques des scindages d'extensions").

**N7 — scan 132 — Own (second pass), definition implicit in the text.** "Morphismes mixtes" are the f: (X′, S′) → (X, S) with f(X′ ∖ S′) ⊂ X ∖ S but S′ ≠ f⁻¹(S), so that S′₁ = S′ ∖ f⁻¹(S) is sent into U. For them the open-part datum f_π: π′ → π does not determine B_{X′,U′} → B_{X,U}; the missing datum is the family of torsors indexed by S′₁ (scan 134).
