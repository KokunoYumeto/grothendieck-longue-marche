## B. Own notes (Claude, second pass)

**O1 — scans 201–202 — claim without proof (dependency) — own.** Injectivity of Γ̃ → Γ rests on "π^{Γ₀} = (1)". This is question c) applied to Γ₀, which according to CSG is settled on the preceding page only conditionally, on the realisability of cyclic outer actions. It also needs Γ₀ ≠ {1}, since π^{{1}} = π. That fits the hypothesis of d) if CSG's printed "Γ ∩ Γ = {1}" is a lost "≠". CSG drops ≠ elsewhere on these pages, e.g. in (24) and (26).

**O2 — scan 201 — gap — own.** "(car Ɛ! contient le centralisateur de Γ₀ dans Ɛ, donc Γ′ et Γ″)" needs Γ′ and Γ″, as subgroups of Ɛ, to centralise Γ₀. That means Γ₀ must be central in each of them, and the lifts of Γ₀ through Γ′ and through Γ″ must be the same subgroup of Ɛ. Neither point is argued on these pages, although Γ₀ = Γ′ ∩ Γ″ is formed after identifying Γ′, Γ″ with subgroups of Γ. Both hold if Γ′, Γ″ are abelian and Γ₀ is their intersection inside Ɛ. Without this, the passage to Γ! (Γ₀ central) is circular.

**O3 — scan 203 — claim withdrawn — own.** Grothendieck first concluded "Ainsi Γ̃ ≃ Γ est un ss-groupe fini de Aut(π), donc cyclique" and struck "donc cyclique". A finite group acting faithfully on a surface group need not be cyclic, so the deletion removes an overstatement. A dashed line under the struck words could be a restoration mark. If it were one, the claim would need Γ to be cyclic.

**O4 — scan 205, lower-left margin — clarification — own.** Read as "les pts marqués des espaces homog. des premiers membres de (13), (14) s'en vont vers les systèmes de pts {a_{g,0}, …, a_{g,ν−1}} ou (a_{g,0}, …, a_{g,ν−1})", the note fixes the normalisation of (13) and (14). The class of the identity goes to the base configuration, i.e. the maps are u ↦ u(S_{g,ν}) and u ↦ (u(a_{g,i}))_i. "dans/de" and "s'en vont vers" are still uncertain.

**O5 — scans 204, 209–210 — inconsistency to settle — own.** Scan 204 takes U_{g,ν} as "la surface (orientée, ou orientable) type" and A_g = Aut(X_g) without saying whether orientation must be preserved. The equivalence on 209 is with "surfaces compactes orientées", and the punctured analogue on 210 starts with "surfaces orientées" and "surface orientée de type (g,ν)" but ends with "surfaces orientables de type (g,ν)". If A_g is the full homeomorphism group, the pair (G, 𝒳) recovers the surface without an orientation, and the target should be orientable surfaces with all homeomorphisms. An equivalence with oriented surfaces needs A_g to be the orientation-preserving group.

**O6 — scan 209 — claim without proof — own.** "Il revient au m̄ de dire que …" asserts without proof that the two definitions of a Teichmüller pair agree. In the first, (G, 𝒳) ≅ (A_g, class of A_{g,1}). In the second, 𝒳 with its homogeneous-space topology is a compact orientable surface of genus g and G → Aut(𝒳) is a homeomorphism. One direction uses (17) and the self-normalisation (19), which makes the class a copy of X_g. The other direction needs the stabilisers of points of 𝒳 to be exactly the conjugates of G₁.
