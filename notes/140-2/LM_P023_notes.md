## 4. Legibility and limits

The main running text is moderately legible, but the small subscripts, corrections written over earlier symbols, and compressed endings are substantially less reliable. I trust the broad paragraph sequence and most numbered displays more than the slanted notes on scans 221, 222, and 226, the layered first paragraph of scan 223, and the overwritten formulas on scan 225. The large deletion on scan 227 has an uncertain trailing boundary.

This is a working diplomatic transcription with explicit unresolved readings, not a certified critical edition. The doubt table is an index of the marked uncertainties, not a guarantee that every unmarked character is correct. Gaps remain as `\illegible{n}`; their word counts are estimates. In particular, familiar mathematical statements have not been used to repair (49), the rank reading on scan 229, or the unfinished sentence on scan 230. A temporary LaTeX wrapper is used only to check syntax; that check does not validate the handwriting readings.


## 5. Research notes — English; separate from the transcription

### R01 — Scan 221, approximately lines 10–20, diagram (43)
**Kind: unproved claim.** The passage asserts “un hom. canonique” from the surface-group extension to the automorphism/outer-automorphism extension, with the central vertical map obtained by restricting an inner automorphism. A proof would need to check that the indicated subgroup is normal, identify the kernel of the inner-action map, and verify commutativity with the quotient map; the center-trivial hypothesis is explicitly invoked in the text. The exact earlier extension number remains a transcription doubt.

### R02 — Scan 221, approximately lines 21–25, theorem
**Kind: unproved claim.** The theorem described, tentatively, as “bien connu” identifies the mapping-class groups with the indicated automorphisms and outer automorphisms preserving the “structure à lacets.” Establishing this requires both realization of the permitted algebraic automorphisms by surface homeomorphisms and uniqueness up to the relevant isotopy relation. The damaged marginal extension to exceptional types should be checked separately rather than incorporated into the non-abelian statement.

### R03 — Scan 222, approximately lines 2–10, corollary
**Kind: unproved claim.** The statement “est une équivalence des catégories” includes both an unpointed and a pointed version, although the reading of the adjective for the latter is uncertain. Beyond the automorphism-group statement on scan 221, a proof must identify the objects and morphisms on both sides and establish essential surjectivity. The marginal explanation of the arrows is only partly legible, so the exact quotient by isotopy should be recovered from the preceding definitions before using the result.
