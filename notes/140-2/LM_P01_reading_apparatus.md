## Legibility note

The main text of scans 6–10 is substantially more legible than the compressed contents on scans 3–4, but some cancellations cannot be separated confidently from overwritten letter strokes. The least reliable readings are the mathematical expressions in scan 4, the corrected definition at the top of scan 8, and the amendments to Proposition 1.7 in scan 9. These uncertainties are local; they do not justify treating the entire transcription as verified.

Only the clearly visible upper manuscript numerals 1, 2, and 3 are represented by `\mspage`. The lower-corner sequence and printed archive footers have not been treated as authorial text. Scan 5 is not called blank because three isolated ink numbers are visible. Mirrored show-through is not transcribed. The precise authorship of wrapper annotations and isolated numbers is not established by these images.

The diagram in scan 10 is described because the central arrow’s shaft is uncertain. The unusual sign in the last sentence is rendered by its visible arrangement of strokes, not expanded into a word or replaced by a conventional equivalence sign.
