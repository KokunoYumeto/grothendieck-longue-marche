## Second-pass notes

A. **312 — the model's own idea (an ink slip).** "le|un ss-groupe fini Γ^♮ de Γ" follows "Γ^♮ des Γ_Q, qui est un ss-groupe invariant d'indice fini de Γ". In the profinite case Γ^♮ has finite index and is infinite, so "fini" is probably short for "d'indice fini". The transcription keeps "fini" as written.

B. **315 — the model's own idea.** Read the margin "𝒯̂̂ est ici produit ½ direct de 𝔖₃ = 𝒯⁺ par 𝒯^!" with Grothendieck's convention: an extension of A by B has B normal. Then 𝒯̂̂^! is normal and 𝔖₃ is a complement. An element commuting with 𝔖₃ maps to a central element of 𝔖₃, which has trivial centre, so 𝚪_{0,3} = Centr(𝔖₃) ⊂ 𝒯̂̂^{!}_{03}: this is the containment asserted on 315. Equality ("et peut-être égal") holds exactly when 𝔖₃ centralises 𝒯̂̂^!, i.e. when the product is direct. Confidence: high, given the margin's decomposition. Whether it is direct depends on the definition of 𝒯^!, which this package does not contain.

C. **320 — the model's own idea (completes first-pass note 34).** The map 𝔖_{g,ν} = Aut_lac(Π_{g,ν}) → 𝔖̂̂_{g,ν} = Aut_lac(Π̂_{g,ν}) is injective. Surface and free groups are residually finite, so Π_{g,ν} ⊂ Π̂_{g,ν}, and an automorphism that is the identity on Π̂ is the identity on Π. The image is countable, and infinite because it contains Inn(Π_{g,ν}) ≅ Π_{g,ν} (trivial centre in the anabelian case): this is the inserted "tjs infini". A closed countable subgroup of a profinite group is a countable compact Hausdorff group. By Baire it has an isolated point, so it is discrete, so it is finite. Hence 𝔖_{g,ν} is never closed, which gives the "car … n'est pas fermé". Confidence: high.

D. **320 — implication not pursued.** The admitted injectivity of 𝔖̂_{g,ν} → 𝔖̂̂_{g,ν} says that the profinite completion of Aut_lac(Π_{g,ν}) equals its closure in Aut_lac(Π̂_{g,ν}). This is a form of the congruence subgroup problem for these groups. Its present status should be checked in the literature (Asada for genus 0 and 1, and Boggi, are the usual starting points) before any claim; this pass did not check it.

E. **312, 315–316 — the model's own idea.** The inserted value exp(2iπ/6) = e^{iπ/3} is a fixed point of the order-3 automorphisms z ↦ 1/(1−z) and z ↦ 1 − 1/z of P¹∖{0,1,∞}: the equation z(1−z) = 1 has roots e^{±iπ/3}. This explains how ρ can denote both the point (312) and the order-3 symmetry fixing it, or its lift at that base point (315–316: "commute à ρ"). Confidence: high for the computation, medium for the reading of the notation.

F. **316–317 — question (literature lead).** In Ihara's and Drinfeld's Grothendieck–Teichmüller group, the defining relations (I) and (II) express compatibility with the order-2 and order-3 symmetries of P¹∖{0,1,∞}. It is worth checking whether the lifting condition on 316 ("un relèvement qui commute à ρ, et un rel. qui commute à σ₀") corresponds to those relations, and so whether its vacuity on 𝚪_{0,3} (317) can be decided there. Confidence: low; this is a lead, not a checked correspondence.

G. **316 — implication not pursued (notation).** The new letter 𝔖 is the same glyph as 𝔖₃, and the diagram that introduces it uses both in one row ("𝔖_{03} — 𝔖₃ × Z/2"). The collision the notation change was meant to remove therefore reappears at once.
