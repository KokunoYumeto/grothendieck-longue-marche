### Scans 501–503

1. **Scan 501, lines 15–20 — unproved claim.** “J'admets que ces groupes sont ≃ Ẑ” is followed by the inserted justification “c'est d'ailleurs trivial en passant à π_ab”. A detailed proof would need to identify the images of the displayed generators in the abelianization and check that their continuous procyclic parameterizations have trivial kernel; the manuscript supplies only this indication.
2. **Scan 501, lines 20–22, (4) — unproved claim.** “et qu'on a, en analogie avec (2)” introduces the centralizer identities for powers of the closed subgroups Λ_i and Λ′_i. The condition on the exponent is graphically uncertain, so any attempt at a proof must first settle that reading and then distinguish the injectivity of a procyclic parameterization from the stronger centralizer assertion.
3. **Scan 501, lines 13–15 — unproved claim.** The parenthesis “Ils sont d'ailleurs tous conjugués sous Aut(π)” is stated without an argument on this page. A proof would have to realize the needed permutations of the displayed closed cyclic subgroups by automorphisms respecting the relation (3).
4. **Scan 502, lines 2–13 — conjecture / expectation.** “Le résultat le plus fort de fidélité … serait le suivant” proposes faithful outer action for an anabelian curve over a finite extension of Q. The formulation is conditional and prospective; neither the passage's group-letter ambiguities nor the sentence's confidence should be silently converted into a proved theorem.
5. **Scan 502, lines 13–19 — conjecture / expectation.** “mais même l'homomorphisme composé … devrait être injectif” asks for faithfulness after passing to the displayed quotient by the hatted script subgroup. A proof would need to exclude nontrivial Galois elements whose action lies in that subgroup, a stronger issue than faithfulness before the quotient.
6. **Scan 502, lines 17–20 — unproved claim.** “puisqu'il est surjectif par construction même” asserts surjectivity of the quotient map and explains why its hoped-for injectivity would give an isomorphism. The relevant definitions are outside these pages, so this construction-dependent claim cannot be verified from the present extract alone.
7. **Scan 503, lines 1–7 — conjecture / expectation.** “semblent indiquer que ce serait le cas au moins lorsque V est un revêtement étale” limits the heuristic to covers of the displayed U. He explicitly calls the preceding heuristic “moins convainquants sans doute”, so the wording records qualified expectation rather than a completed proof.
8. **Scan 503, lines 9–16 — unproved claim / conditional reduction.** “Si on pouvait montrer que” introduces injectivity of the map from the group indexed by π₀ to the one indexed by π′. A proof would need the actual definitions of these quotient groups, the functoriality of passage to a characteristic open subgroup, and control of the quotient map's kernel; faithfulness of a related automorphism restriction alone would not automatically settle the quotient statement.
9. **Scan 503, lines 16–18 — unpursued implication.** “Donc modulo cette hypothèse … et utilisant [Белый]” makes the ensuing conclusion explicitly dependent on the profinite-group hypothesis. This is a useful place to track which later statements retain that dependency; the name's reading is itself marked doubtful in the transcription.
10. **Scans 502–503, quotient and diagram — own idea.** As a checking strategy, write the proposed kernel as the intersection of the arithmetic image with the subgroup being quotiented out, and then track this intersection under each diagram map. **Confidence: medium**, because this would isolate the additional faithfulness requirement visible in the passage, but the absent definitions may obstruct or change the proposed comparison; this is a suggested analysis, not an established result or a novelty claim.

### Scans 504–506

#### 504-R1 — Scan 504, lines 1–4 — unproved claim

The continuation states that for every algebraic curve there is a nonempty open `V'` for which the displayed `\Gamma \to \mathbb{\Gamma}_{\pi'}` is injective (“pour toute courbe algébrique … ∃ V' … soit injectif”). This is a conditional conclusion carried over from the preceding page, not an independent proof on this page. Its hypotheses and dependence on the proposed profinite-group argument must be retained when assessing it.

#### 504-R2 — Scan 504, lines 4–11 — conjecture / question

Grothendieck asks whether injectivity already holds before shrinking, and singles out `\Gamma \to \mathbb{\Gamma}_g` for `g ≥ 2` and `\Gamma \to \mathbb{\Gamma}_{1,1}`. His stated uncertainty is strong: “je n'ai pas de raison heuristique plausible … peut-être est-ce tout à fait faux ?” This should be recorded as an open question with an explicit doubt, rather than as an asserted faithfulness theorem.

#### 504-R3 — Scan 504, lines 12–19 — unproved claim

The earlier argument, “à supposer qu'on arrive à le justifier,” is said to propagate injectivity from one `π` to its open subgroups `π'`. A proof would need to specify the restriction map on the relevant outer-action quotients and show that its kernel cannot kill the image of the Galois group. The text itself qualifies this deduction rather than claiming that the needed argument has already been established.

#### 504-R4 — Scan 504, lines 16–18 — unpursued implication

The parenthesis “ce qui ne dépend que de son type `(g,ν)`” asserts independence from the chosen curve within the indicated type for the injectivity property under discussion. This raises a separate naturality issue: an identification of geometric fundamental groups alone need not, without further argument, identify arithmetic actions. A later check should distinguish which object or quotient is asserted here to depend only on the topological type.

#### 505-R1 — Scan 505, lines 1–5 — unproved claim / expectation

The preceding knowledge claim is qualified by “modulo peu de chose” and limited to types `(0,ν)`, `ν ≥ 3`. Grothendieck then suggests “on pourrait peut-être le déduire” for types obtained from these by finite covering. A proof would need the precise covering hypotheses and the injectivity-preservation argument discussed on scan 504.

#### 505-R2 — Scan 505, lines 6–9 — unproved claim

He says that the proposed method does not yield `(g,0)` with `g ≥ 2`, or any `(g,ν)` with `g ≥ 1` and `ν ∈ {0,1,2}` (“C'est dire qu'on est loin du compte”). The page gives the numerical exclusion but no calculation establishing it. A check should identify the permitted covering maps and count the preimages of the punctures, with their ramification constraints.

#### 505-R3 — Scan 505, lines 10–17 — conjecture / question

The “premier cas bien intéressant” is type `(1,1)`, annotated “tore à un trou !”. The question is whether the arithmetic action, described as an outer action modulo `\widehat{\mathcal{T}}^+_{1,1} \simeq Sl(2,ℤ)^∧`, is faithful: “est-elle fidèle ?” The qualifier “mod” is underlined and should remain central when comparing this question to an ordinary outer-action faithfulness statement.

#### 505-R4 — Scan 505, lines 11–13 — unproved claim

The page writes the displayed identification of the completed Teichmüller-type group with `Sl(2,ℤ)^∧` and calls the acted-on fundamental group a “groupe libre à deux générateurs”. The precise completion, topology on the acting group, and passage from a discrete action to this completed outer action are not proved on this page.
