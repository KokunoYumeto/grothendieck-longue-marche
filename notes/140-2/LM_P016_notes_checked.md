## Second-pass notes (own)

**S01 — scan 154 — unproved claim (own note).** The left object of the second diagram is B_ℰ, yet the annotation under it is "π-Ens". The identification only makes sense with the Γ-action kept ("compatible avec action de Γ"): an ℰ-set is a π-set with a compatible Γ-action twisted by the extension. The text uses this identification without stating it.

**S02 — scan 155 — unpursued implication (own note).** With the corrected ℤ/nℤ ↪ Γ, the construction of μ(u) needs only that π is torsion-free: the image Γ₁ of u then has the same order n, and its lifted section has a fixed point x. The NB's caveat matters because an orientation-reversing element has no rotation multiplier at its fixed point.

**S03 — scan 158 — own idea.** An orientation-reversing finite-order automorphism of π ≅ ℤ² has determinant −1, hence eigenvalues 1 and −1, hence order exactly 2. The reading "d'ordre 1 ou 2" (the witnesses) therefore contains a vacuous case, while "i.e. 2" would state the fact; the ink does not decide between them.

**S04 — scan 158 — own idea.** T ≃ Λ²_ℤ π is canonically H₂(π,ℤ), dual to H²(π,ℤ) ≃ Λ²H¹(π,ℤ) with H¹(π,ℤ) = π^∨. This supports the reading "dual de" against the witnesses' "dim 2 dans" on mathematical grounds only.

**S05 — scan 160, margin — unproved claim (own context note).** The claim that every outer action of a solvable Γ on π is geometrically realised matches the classical partial Nielsen realisation results (cyclic groups: Nielsen; solvable groups: Fenchel); the general finite case was proved by Kerckhoff in 1983, after this manuscript. This attribution is from general knowledge and was not checked against the literature corpus in this pass.

**S06 — scan 159 — own idea.** For an elliptic curve over Ω and u of finite order fixing 0, the root "dans Ω" that Grothendieck adds is naturally the scalar by which u acts on the one-dimensional tangent space at 0. Over ℂ the two complex eigenvalues of u ⊗ ℂ are exchanged by the choice of orientation of π ⊗ ℝ, which agrees with the marginal "pour Ω = ℂ … orientation(s)". This interpretation is not written in the manuscript.
