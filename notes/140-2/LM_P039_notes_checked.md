## First-pass notes

**R381-1** · scan 381 · claim without proof / expectation · **keep.**
Grothendieck links the abundance of π-conjugacy classes of lifting germs to the "fait naïf" that the curve U has "beaucoup de pᵗˢ rat/Q̄". He then says that it would suffice to treat liftings to a true action of Γ_a on π, whose exterior actions are obtained through M_a → N_a ≃ M_a/π. No correspondence between rational points and lifting germs is proved here. The corrected text changes nothing the note relies on.

**R382-3** · scans 382–383 · first-pass model's idea · **keep, with precision.**
The obstruction to surjectivity of (3) lies in nonabelian continuous cohomology. The Γ′-equivariant sequence 1 → π → Ĝ_a⁺ → T̂_a⁺ → 1 gives the exact sequence of pointed sets 1 → π^{Γ′} → (Ĝ_a⁺)^{Γ′} → (T̂_a⁺)^{Γ′} → H¹(Γ′, π). So the image of (3) is the kernel of the connecting map. Confidence: high for the exact sequence; its use here is the model's proposal.

## Checker's notes

**C1** · scan 382 · structure of the requirements (checker).
Conditions (b) π^{Γ′} = (1) and (c) Z finite are two parallel demands on an admissible Γ: rigidity of points, and finiteness of their automorphism groups. The corrected "On veut sûrement" makes the "fait pendant à (b)" of scan 383 literal: both are wishes, not consequences.

**C2** · scan 382 · verification (checker).
Two splittings s, s′ of (1)′ over Γ′ are identified when s′ = x s x⁻¹ with x ∈ π. The stabilizer of s is the centralizer of s(Γ′) in π, which is π^{Γ′}. So π^{Γ′} = (1) says exactly that germs of splittings have no nontrivial automorphisms, which is the "rigidité" asserted.

**C3** · scan 384 · implication not pursued (checker).
The margin implies that if N_a = Norm_{T̂̂}(T̂_a), it might be automatic that a finite G ⊂ T̂_a⁺ lies in some 𝒯⁺ for a discretification in the class a. That would be a profinite Nielsen-realization statement attached to the arithmetic class a. It is not pursued on these pages.

**C4** · scan 385 · claim resting on conjectures (checker).
The comparison invoked, between the profinite centralizer T̂_a^{+G} and the discrete isotopy-automorphism group of the G-action, is what the "conjectures standard topologiques" are used for here. Any later use of this identification inherits that conditional status.

**C5** · scan 386 · verification of a claim (checker).
For a finite G acting on a hyperbolic surface of type (g, ν), the fixed locus of G in Teichmüller space is isomorphic to the Teichmüller space of the quotient orbifold, of complex dimension 3g′ − 3 + n′. Here g′ is the genus of U/G and n′ counts branch points and punctures. If g′ ≥ 1, hyperbolicity forces 3g′ − 3 + n′ > 0, as Grothendieck states. For g′ = 0 the dimension is n′ − 3, which is 0 for triangle-group actions, so the condition is sufficient but not necessary. Confidence: high for the formula, medium for its match with the uncertain word "schématique".

**C6** · scan 388 · checker's own idea (proof sketch).
Discrete case of (5): realize G (cyclic, orientation-preserving, fixing P) by isometries of a hyperbolic metric (Nielsen–Kerckhoff), and lift it to the disc fixing a lift of P. An element of π₁(U, P) fixed by G is a deck transformation commuting with the elliptic lift of a generator. In PSL₂(ℝ) the centralizer of a nontrivial elliptic element is the rotation group about its fixed point, which meets the torsion-free π₁ only in 1. Confidence: medium. The profinite case is exactly what the text admits without proof.

**C7** · scan 383 · notation check (checker).
In (c), Γ̃^♮ ⊂ N_a and T̂_a⁺ ⊂ N_a, so the equality Z = (T̂_a⁺)^{(Γ♮)} = Centr(Γ̃^♮) ∩ T̂_a⁺ holds with the centralizer taken in N_a, as in the parallel formula on scan 382. The ink of the subscript is retraced and stays marked (N_a|M_a).
