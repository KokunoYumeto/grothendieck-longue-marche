## First-pass notes

1. **Scan 371 — conjecture.** "le groupe s'envoie (on présume injectivement) dans $\widehat{\widehat{\mathcal G}}(\pi_{g,\nu})$": the map from $\pi_1(\mathcal U_{g,\nu,\mathbb Q})$ is presumed injective. **Correct:** the source group is $\pi_1(\mathcal U_{g,\nu,\mathbb Q})$, the fundamental group of the universal curve over Q, with no base point. The first pass's "α|a" was the subscript Q.

2. **Scan 371 — claim without proof.** $M_{g,\nu}$ is a closed subgroup with $\widehat{\mathcal G}_{g,\nu}\subset M_{g,\nu}\subset\widehat{\widehat{\mathcal G}}_{g,\nu}$, $\widehat{\mathcal G}^+_{g,\nu}$ is normal in it but $\widehat{\mathcal G}_{g,\nu}$ is not, and giving $M_{g,\nu}$ is equivalent to giving $N_{g,\nu}$ as in (11). **Correct:** the parenthesis "(mais pas $\widehat{\mathcal G}_{g,\nu}$ !)" is attached by a line to "normal dans $M_{g,\nu}$". 3. **Scan 371 — conjecture.** (13): the surjection $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}\to\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ is presumed bijective, "i.e. que les $\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ sont can. isom. entre eux". **Keep:** the corrected text is unchanged here.

4. **Scans 371–372 — claim without proof.** $\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ contains a canonical element of order 2, $\tau_{g,\nu}$, "correspondant aux éléments de $\widehat{\mathcal T}_{g,\nu}-\widehat{\mathcal T}^+_{g,\nu}=\widehat{\mathcal T}^-_{g,\nu}$", and (19) gives $\tau_\alpha^2=1$, $\tau_\alpha\ne1$. **Correct:** the margin reads "correspondant aux", not "comme provenant des". The element is the common image of all orientation-reversing classes. They have one image because $\widehat{\mathcal T}^-$ is the single non-trivial coset of $\widehat{\mathcal T}^+$ in $\widehat{\mathcal T}$, and that image has order 2 by (12).

5. **Scan 372 — claim without proof.** After transport of structure by an isomorphism $\pi_0\simeq\pi_{g,\nu}$, "le résultat n'en dépend pas". **Keep:** the independence follows from (10). ## Second-pass notes

S1. **Scan 371 — claim without proof (second pass).** The opening recalls that the transcendental construction from a differentiable reference surface gives $\widetilde M_{g,\nu\,\mathbb C}$ and, probably, $\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{g,\nu\,\mathbb C}=\widetilde M_{g,\nu+1\,\mathbb C}$: universal covers of Teichmüller type. Hence it gives canonical universal covers $\widetilde M_{g,\nu\,\mathbb Q}$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{g,\nu\,\mathbb Q}$, relative to which $\pi_1(\mathcal U_{g,\nu,\mathbb Q})$ is taken. The identification of the universal curve over the cover with the cover of type $(g,\nu+1)$ is stated without argument.

S2. **Scan 371 — implication not pursued / probable slip (second pass).** "induisant un isomorphisme entre son sous-groupe $\pi_1(\mathcal U_{g,\nu\,\overline{\mathbb Q}_0})$ avec $\widehat{\mathcal G}(\pi_{g,\nu})$" has no "+" (checked at 2×). The factors in (9) are $\widehat\pi_{g,\nu}$ and $\widehat{\mathcal T}(\pi_{g,\nu})^+$, so the geometric subgroup maps onto $\widehat{\mathcal G}^+$. If the image were $\widehat{\mathcal G}$, it would be normal in $M_{g,\nu}$, contrary to "(mais pas $\widehat{\mathcal G}_{g,\nu}$ !)". The inclusion $\widehat{\mathcal G}\subset M_{g,\nu}$ of (10) is then accounted for by the image of complex conjugation, which is orientation-reversing.

S3. **Scan 374 — probable slip (second pass).** "$N_{g,\nu}$, ext de $\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ par $\widehat{\mathcal T}_{g,\nu}$": by (12) the kernel of $N_{g,\nu}\to\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ is $\widehat{\mathcal T}^+_{g,\nu}$. The plus is missing in the ink, and CSG/IUT supply it silently.

S4. **Scan 374 — implication not pursued (second pass).** The margin "si … injectif !" is exactly the condition for $\pi_1(M_{g,\nu\,\mathbb Q})\to N_{g,\nu}$ to be an isomorphism. The geometric parts $\pi_1(M_{g,\nu\,\overline{\mathbb Q}})\to\widehat{\mathcal T}^+_{g,\nu}$ correspond and $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}\to\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ is onto, so the map of extensions is onto, and it is injective if and only if $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}\to\Gamma_{g,\nu}$ is. This supports Q (not Q̄) in "$\pi_1(M_{g,\nu\,\mathbb Q})$" on scan 374 and in the corrected base field on scan 375.

S5. **Scan 378 — claim without proof (second pass pointer).** The "fait connu" follows from Artin–Schreier: an involution of $\Gamma_{\mathbb Q}$ has a real closed fixed field, and all real closures of Q in Q̄ are conjugate. The centraliser statement follows because an element commuting with τ preserves the real closed field $R=\overline{\mathbb Q}^{\tau}$, and R has no automorphism other than the identity, since its order is defined by squares and Q is dense in it.

S6. **Scan 379 — question (second pass).** "se donner un noyau de relèvement" is an unusual phrase. Scan 380 goes on to liftings given on an open subgroup $\Gamma'$ and to "ces germes de relèvements", so "noyau" may stand here for what is later called a germ of a lifting. The ink supports "noyau", and the sense remains to be fixed from the following pages.

S7. **Scan 380 — conjecture (second pass).** The cancelled "invariantes", replaced by "stables" (and probably "Les propriétés à dégager"), shifts the requirement from invariance to inheritance by restriction to smaller open subgroups. Conditions defining "admissible" germs should therefore pass to restrictions. Whether they can be recovered from a restriction is a separate question that the page does not raise.
