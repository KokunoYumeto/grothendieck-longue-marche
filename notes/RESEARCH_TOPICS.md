# La Longue Marche: an initial map of topics and open questions

An initial guide to the mathematics of the manuscript, compiled from the research notes of all packages (Grothendieck's
own conjectures, questions, expectations and announced programmes, in his words where quoted). References are to the
archive scans of each cote; the notes files of the corresponding package give the context. The pointers to later
work are first indications, to be checked; they are not claims about the state of the art.

## Cote 140-2 (manuscript pages 1–292)

**Groups with loops (groupes à lacets) and the rigidity of their peripheral structure.** Triviality of centres and
of centralisers of open subgroups ("pas prouvé pour le moment dans aucun cas anabélien profini !", scan 118; the
centre of a free profinite group, "je cale sur a)", 496); normalisers and transporters of the loop subgroups,
N_E(L_i) = E_i (84) and Trans_E(L_i^d, L_i) = E_i (69); "toute extension sans torsion d'un groupe fini par un groupe
à lacets est (de la façon canonique) un groupe à lacets" (353).

**Finite groups acting on surfaces and their group-theoretic description.** Essential surjectivity, whether every
finite subgroup of the extended Teichmüller group is realised, "un problème ouvert sur lequel les gens sèchent"
(101–102); the questions c)–e) on finite groups acting on a group with loops (197–198), with a proof of d) (201–203);
the invariant μ on elements of finite order, "J'ignore si cette application μ peut se définir intrinsèquement"
(155–160); "forage de trous" (drilling holes) as the operation to be understood (160, 178); the conjecture that the
2-functor from Γ-actions on surfaces to their group data is a 2-equivalence, "i.e. 3 fidèle" (270–271), its fidelity
conditions (285–289), the realisability criterion (294), and the theorem on the components of the automorphism
groups (304). Pointers: Nielsen realisation (Kerckhoff, 1983); profinite analogues.

**Isotopy and homotopy, topological and arithmetic.** 2-isotopic descriptions through Gr-categories (232–239); the
neutrality of a gerbe (240–241); what a homotopy between isomorphisms of forms of the projective line should mean
arithmetically, "sans doute un relèvement de u en une section du revêtement simplement connexe" (249); the
2-category of Azumaya algebras (250); the "topos abracadabra" B_{X,U} and whether its cohomology is computed in
Galois terms (124, 129).

**Teichmüller groups, discretisations and the "conjecture fondamentale".** Normality of T̂ in the group of outer
automorphisms (322–326, one conjecture retracted as "ultra-faux"); prediscretisations and their bijections under
passage to open subgroups (347, 351–360); the conjecture that the arithmetic groups Γ_{g,ν} are canonically
isomorphic to Gal(Q̄/Q) (341, 356, 371, 376); the expectation that Γ_Q → Γ_{0,3} is an isomorphism, with a result
transmitted by Deligne (479); the "question essentielle", an algebraic description of Γ_{0,3} and of the admissible
splittings (479). Pointers: Belyi (1979); Drinfeld and Ihara on the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group.

**Faithfulness of Galois actions.** For an anabelian curve over a number field (497, 502); on the profinite mapping
class groups of genus g ≥ 2 and of type (1,1), "peut-être est-ce tout à fait faux ?" (504–505).

**Admissible splittings and rational points.** "Ceci suggère une approche de la conjecture de Mordell" and "Idem pour
une approche du th. de Fermat" (65); pathological lifts (413); conditions involving Frobenius (480); admissible germs
of splittings as the algebraic points of a moduli object (481–488). Pointers: the section conjecture.

**Real structures.** Fixed loci of anti-involutions and the classes of sections (136–139, 148, 179); the position of
the points at infinity on the components of X^σ, to be recovered "par voie algébrique" (437); classes of
anti-involutions (440, 457–460); maximal free abelian subgroups of Teichmüller groups and their normalisers, with the
strips ("lanières") in a role like rank-one groups (469–470). Pointers: the real section conjecture; Birman,
Lubotzky and McCarthy on abelian subgroups of mapping class groups.

**Type (1,1) and SL(2,Z)^.** Faithfulness "mod" the geometric part (505); lifts through ±1 compatible with the
Galois action (515–525); the obstruction class and the anabelian converse (523–526); rigidifying covers and levels
(573).

## Cote 140-3 (manuscript pages 293–650)

**Necessary conditions for an outer automorphism to come from Gal(Q̄/Q).** Congruence relations for (0,3) and (1,1)
and whether they are arithmetic or formal (28–60); "toute une kyrielle" of further conditions (57); explicit
solutions, of which only two are known (105, 120); signs and class field theory (C3 146–163, 245–263); the search for
a characterisation of Γ_Q inside the larger group, "je n'ai pas de caractérisation bien plausible à proposer"
(C3 170–185); an adelic group of triples satisfying the loop relation (C3 204–205). Pointers: the relations defining
the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group.

**Group schemes over Z from pairs of matrices.** The schemes M_{ρ,σ} attached to pairs satisfying the loop relation,
their dimension, components, tori and unipotent parts (C3 151 onwards); presentations of group sheaves over Spec Z by
finite cyclic subgroups and G_a, "cela vaudrait bien la peine de tirer cette question au clair" (C3 336); uniqueness
and existence of lifts (C3 340–371); a "procédé systématique" for constructing "représentations motiviques" (C3 489);
central extensions by μ_2, the class in the Brauer group, and the lifting obstructions (C3 596–634).

## Cote 140-4 (manuscript pages 650bis–787)

**Integrality over arithmetic bases.** Unipotent sections and homomorphisms from G_a (C4 6–9); the "lemme
dubitatif" on divided powers in residue characteristics 2, 3 and 5, "un contreexemple … ou un théorème !"
(C4 10–16); smoothness of the parameter schemes (C4 41).

**Level 12 and the Fermat tower.** The congruence subgroup of level 12 (C4 64); the relation of the arithmetic
homomorphisms to the tower of Fermat curves (C4 95–145); surjectivity and open image of the associated Kummer-type
maps (C4 167–170).

**Fermat via splittings.** "Si nous arrivions à prouver l'inexistence de scindages … autres que ceux … à l'infini"
(C4 173), through successively finer nilpotent approximations of the fundamental groups (C4 173–178). Pointers:
nilpotent obstructions to sections; the unipotent fundamental group method.

**L-groups and Galois–Teichmüller structures.** "Il semble que ∀ L-groupe soit isomorphe à un groupe obtenu ainsi"
from a finite group acting on a surface (C4 183); centraliser conditions and characterisations (C4 184–189);
modular multiplicities over Q and Z and their presentations as quotients (C4 193–197); the axioms of
Galois–Teichmüller structures (C4 204–208); quasi-unipotent homomorphisms and integral one-parameter subgroups
(C4 210–221).
