# LM_C4-058-076 — Editorial and research notes

## Scope and reference convention

This package transcribes all 19 supplied manuscript scans, numbered 58–76 by their filenames. The printed archive footers run from 57 to 75, one number behind the filenames. References throughout this package use the filename scan number. Authorial folio numbers visible at the top of a sheet are recorded separately with `\mspage`; the small lower-left archival numbers and the institutional footers are excluded from the manuscript text.

The transcription is a diplomatic LaTeX fragment. Its doubt, deletion, insertion, margin, underline, and diagram macros mark the state and legibility of the supplied images. It contains no editorial reconstruction of missing mathematics. An inventory value of `yes` means that the physical element is represented, including explicit uncertainty where needed; it is not a guarantee that every tentative reading is correct.

All manuscript statements and brief quotations below refer to Grothendieck (1981), at the scan and equation or local position specified in each entry. Bibliographic identification comes from the accompanying task sheet. No published transcription or external mathematical source was used.

## 3. Legibility and editorial limitations

Every scan was read in full and checked against its transcription, with enlarged inspection of crowded revisions and marginal writing. The most uncertain passages are identified below and remain marked locally in the LaTeX. The fragment passed structural checks for braces, environments, mathematical delimiters, and scan order; compilation was not verified in this environment. The requested editorial macros are intentionally left undefined in the fragment.

### Scans 58–62

The principal prose and most displayed coefficients on scans 58–62 are legible after enlargement. The least reliable readings are the extensively cancelled heading of scan 59, the overwritten operator in (90), the small subscript of the base open set on scan 60, and the long sloping marginal note and central cancellations on scan 62. The different signs in (96), (100), and (101) have been retained as read, without algebraic correction. The small handwritten lower-left numbers 57–61 duplicate the archive footers; they are treated as archival pagination, while the clearly distinct top-of-sheet numbers 677 and 678 are transcribed with `\mspage`.

No external transcription or mathematical reconstruction was used. A `yes` in the inventory records that an element has a transcription, including explicit uncertainty or an unreadable-word estimate; it does not certify that every proposed reading is correct.

### Scans 63–67
