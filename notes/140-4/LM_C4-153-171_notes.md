## 4. Legibility and consequential reading decisions

The main prose is usually readable, but the hand is highly compressed and frequent cancellations obscure whole endings, indices, and equation numbers. Whole-page readings were checked against enlarged overlapping regions, with additional crops for dense revisions. Several pages received an independent second reading; this reduces, but cannot eliminate, transcription error. The least secure portions are the overwritten formula numbers on scan 153, the compressed `tri-` word and cancelled indices on scan 155, the group superscripts on scans 156–158, the rewritten coordinate conditions and definition on scan 159, the crowded automorphism condition on scan 162, and the superscript annotations on scan 166. The inserted dimension condition on scan 167 and the revised final paragraph on scan 171 also remain weak points.

The three `\illegible{1}` occurrences concern physically obscured words: the opening cancellation on scan 153, the cancelled word in item 1 on scan 155, and the cancelled annotation on scan 166. Mathematical symbols, including heavily crossed ones, instead carry proposed readings inside `\unsure{…}`. In particular, the last group-superscript shape is left as opposing arrows with a sharp-like alternative where the distinction remains unresolved, and the N alphabet in the later segment is explicitly doubtful.

Several tempting normalizations have deliberately not been made. The apparent missing exponent on the first coordinate in the second line of (332), uncertain signs in that display, repeated or inconsistent equation references, the duplicated-looking authorial page number on scan 157, and the awkward surviving wording on scans 163 and 166 remain visible. Both twists in (357) and the following question on scan 167 read `(-1)` on enlargement; the twists in (358) and (365) also visibly include a minus, while the two less clear occurrences on scan 168 retain explicit alternatives. The full `Π₁` written in the scan-167 question is not changed to its cyclotomic kernel.

On scan 169, the insertion « la tour des » attaches to « images inverses », rather than forming a separate object beside (360); the repeated label (339) is retained. On scan 171, the word before the ramification insertion is « ramification », the three coordinate tuples retain their small superscript dots, and the insertion « de degré n en » is attached to the revised phrase. Ordinary Fermat-curve U and the more ornate universal-curve letter remain distinct. The all-plus equation is transcribed literally, and the excerpt stops before any completed genus formula.
