## Second-pass notes

**A. Scan 58 (9) — second pass, own check.** With (10) and (11) as corrected, (9) as now read is exactly the image under u of the relation ℓ₁ℓ₀ℓ_∞ = 1, conjugated by σ_∞(g₀)^{-1}: u(ℓ₁) becomes ℓ₁^p, u(ℓ₀) becomes int(h₀)(ℓ₀^p), and u(ℓ_∞) becomes int(h₀ℓ₀^{-γ}ρ^{-1}(h₀))(ℓ_∞^p) = int(ℓ₁^γρ(h₀)^{-1})(ℓ_∞^p) by (10). So (9) says that (11) respects the defining relation. The first-pass reading fails already in the abelianization; the cyclic order of the relation (ℓ₁ℓ₀ℓ_∞ = 1 rather than ℓ₀ℓ₁ℓ_∞ = 1) should be checked against [C].

**B. Scan 58, margin — second pass, own idea; confidence medium.** Abelianizing (10) gives (1 + ρ + ρ²)[h₀] = γ([ℓ₀] + [ℓ₁] + [ℓ_∞]) = 0, an identity, since 1 + ρ + ρ² vanishes on H₁ = Ẑ[ℓ₀] ⊕ Ẑ[ℓ₁]; the abelianization of (9) is empty too. So the announced γ = (p−1)/2 and "(9) superflu" can only come from higher commutators: the next test is to write (9) and (10) in Π̂₀,₃ modulo its third lower central term, where commutators of the ℓ's carry γ and p.

**C. Scans 52, 54 — second pass, question.** The ink has "cat." where the first pass read "ext." twice: "l'hom cat. (73)" and "un autom. cat. de Π̂₀,₃". The preceding pages (P063) use "cat" as a superscript on C₁,₁ beside "ext". Which class of homomorphisms or automorphisms "cat." denotes is not defined on these pages; it decides whether the opening question of §41 concerns outer automorphisms of Π̂₀,₃ or a narrower class.

**D. Scan 60 (18) — second pass, own idea; confidence medium.** By (15), u(ρ) = gρ, and (16) is the cocycle condition for g under ⟨ρ⟩ (note 30). For p ≡ 1 mod 3, u(ρ) is conjugate to ρ^p = ρ exactly when g = cρ(c)^{-1} for some c, i.e. when the class of g in the nonabelian H¹(⟨ρ⟩, 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃) is trivial; for p ≡ 2 mod 3 the same holds after twisting by ρ. So (18) is a vanishing statement in H¹, which explains why it does not follow from (16) alone and connects it to the cocycle calculations of §§42–43.

**E. Scan 55 (3) — second pass, question.** The inclusion 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃ ⊂ 𝒯̂̂₀,₃ makes sense for automorphisms rather than outer automorphisms: 𝔖⁺₀,₃ ≅ PSL(2,Z) acts by conjugation on its normal subgroup Π(2) ≅ Π₀,₃, with Π(2) acting by inner automorphisms. That fits "Aut_loc" with "ext" struck, but it conflicts with 𝒯̂̂ standing elsewhere for the outer group Autext_lac (IUT l. 7276: "Autextlac(π) = T̂̂(π)"). The struck word, and so which group 𝒯̂̂₀,₃ denotes here, is unresolved.

**F. Scan 57, margin — second pass, implication.** The margin "ℰ₀,₃ est le groupe noté M^!₀,₃ dans le §46" is a later cross-reference: §46 is "Notations définitives (?)" in the author's table of contents, and "!" is his notation for marked-point variants (M^!₀,₄, CSG ll. 10198–10208). It supports the reading of note 20, that the group divided by ⟨ℓ₁², ℓ_∞³⟩ to give M₀,₃ is ℰ₀,₃ = M^!₀,₃.
