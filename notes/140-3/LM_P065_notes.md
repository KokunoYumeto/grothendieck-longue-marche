## 3. Legibility and limits

Most of the main prose and the principal displayed relations can be read, but many word endings are reduced to a stroke. The least dependable regions are the diagonal notes around (24) on scan 62, the wording after “uniquement déterminé” on scan 63, the heavily overwritten equation (30) on scan 64, the parenthetical group notation on scan 66, the cancelled block and lower-left margin on scan 68, and the overwritten square display and final insertion on scan 70. The recurring group alphabets also remain provisional.

The transcription retains cancelled fragments, incomplete expressions, uncertain arrowheads, ditto marks, and visible but grammatically awkward remnants of revisions. It does not supply a missing label (25), complete an unclosed prose parenthesis on scan 66, or reconstruct obliterated factors of (30) from the argument. The order and attachment of overlapping additions on scans 62 and 68 are less secure than the surrounding main text. The looped generator letter is encoded as `\ell`, and the visibly doubled Z as `\mathbb Z`; these are typographical representations of handwriting, not evidence of a printed font.

Enlarged crops, rotation of diagonal writing, and tonal contrast adjustments were used for inspection. No generated reconstruction of missing strokes was used. Explicit uncertainty remains the appropriate outcome where those inspections did not resolve the ink.

## 4. Research notes — English

Each item is anchored to a supplied scan. Quotes are brief manuscript readings; any uncertainty affecting a quote or its interpretation is stated. “Unproved claim” means that the relevant justification is not supplied at that location in this excerpt, not that the claim is false or absent elsewhere. Later treatment within these ten pages is identified where visible; statements about the rest of the manuscript or the literature have not been inferred. ### Scans 61–63


1. **Scan 61, between (21) and (22); kind: unproved claim.** The step introduced by “donc” identifies an element centralizing `\ell_0'{}^p` with `\ell_0'{}^\beta`. A proof would need the relevant centralizer statement in the profinite target group and the hypotheses on the exponent `p`; those hypotheses are not restated at this step.

2. **Scan 61, (20)–(22); kind: unpursued implication.** The text says that “il suffit d'exprimer cette condition” on the two named generators and then parametrizes the possible conjugating element by `\beta`. One can ask how that parameter changes under changing the representatives of the exterior automorphism, but this page does not carry out that comparison.

3. **Scan 61, marginal addition to (22); kind: unproved claim.** The added equality for `\lambda` points to “plus bas (24 bis).” Its compatibility with the boxed expression depends on the later relation, so the addition should be tracked as a dependent claim rather than used to replace the first written formula.

4. **Scan 62, left margin beside (24); kind: unproved claim.** The note asserts that replacing `g_0` by `g_0\ell_0^\lambda` changes the parameter by `\beta'=\beta-3\lambda`, and says that one can “normaliser le choix de `g_0`” uniquely with `\beta\in\{0,1,2\}`. An arrowed query, tentatively read “Correct?”, makes the author's own reservation visible; a verification should substitute the replacement into all of the defining formulas, retaining the distinction between the generators and their images under `\widehat\varphi`.

5. **Scan 62, end of left-margin note; kind: conjecture / question.** Grothendieck writes “Il faudrait élucider si” the parameter takes “toutes les trois valeurs mod 3” (the word “trois” is uncertain). The question asks for realizability of the possible residue classes, beyond the preceding proposed normalization.

6. **Scan 62, right-margin (24 bis); kind: unproved claim.** “Cette équation équivaut à l'équation plus simple” introduces a boxed expression for `\widehat\varphi(h_0)` containing a power of `\sigma_\infty`. A proof would need to identify the elements fixed by conjugation by `\sigma_\infty` and specify the exponent, whose tiny glyph is read only tentatively as `\varepsilon`.

7. **Scan 62, (26)–(27); kind: own idea.** The operation `\widetilde\mu=\sigma_\infty(\mu)^{-1}\mu` suggests organizing the preceding identities as a nonabelian coboundary calculation for the involution. **Confidence: medium**, because the displayed formula directly has that shape, but the ambient action, continuity requirements, and equivalence relation would need to be specified before making a cohomological statement.

8. **Scan 63, proposition, lines 3–6; kind: unproved claim.** The parameter `\gamma` is described as “uniquement déterminé” by the data involving `g_0` and `p` (the intervening wording is uncertain), with a reference to formulas (11). Establishing this within the present excerpt requires retrieving those earlier equations and checking their existence and uniqueness hypotheses; the star on the set containing `\gamma` should also be checked against the earlier notation, rather than changed here.
