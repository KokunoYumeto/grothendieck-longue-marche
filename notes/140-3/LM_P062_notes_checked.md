# LM_P062 (cote 140-3, scans 31–40): research notes, checked against the corrected text

Text checked: `second_pass/LM_P062_checked.tex`. Scans 31–39 continue §39, "Les conditions de congruence pour l'opération extérieure de Γℚ sur Π̂₀,₃" (ms. pp. 303–307). It asks how much of N₁,₁ and of the homomorphism (21) N₁,₁ → Gl(2,Ẑ) can be rebuilt from a group Γ of outer automorphisms of π̂₀,₃. ## First-pass notes

1. **Scan 31, ll. 1–10 — conjecture (question) — correct.** The "conditions" are not outside the manuscript. They are the congruence conditions defined on scans 29–30 (P061): the outer action of Γℚ on Π̂₀,₃/Π(∞) given by χ and Ẑ*/Ẑ*² is compatible with the action on Π̂₀,₃. The doubt is whether they restrict outer automorphisms of Π̂₀,₃ that commute with 𝔖₃ at all, since all known ones come from Γℚ. The inserted parenthesis widens this to "des élémts non triviaux de 𝒯̂̂^{!*}₀,₃" (one word illegible).

2. **Scan 31, ll. 11–13 — conjecture (expectation) — keep.** "Il faudrait quand même refaire à l'occasion un effort en ce sens" states an intention to look for non-arithmetic constructions. It claims nothing.

3. **Scan 31 l. 14 to scan 32 — conjecture (question) — correct.** The question is whether one can rebuild a) the finer extension N₁,₁ (an extension of N′₁,₁ by {±1}) and b) the homomorphism (21), or at least (22) N′₁,₁ ≃ M₀,₃ → Gl(2,Ẑ)/{±1}. The rebuilding is to use only the profinite groups with loops, the outer automorphisms of π̂₀,₃, or 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃ ≃ 𝒯̂⁺′₁,₁. That group is the cartographic group 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃, which the first pass wrote 𝒢̂⁺₀,₃. The author treats b) first, as announced.

4. **Scan 32, (23)–(24) — unproved claim — correct.** "qui passe au quotient" needs the congruence kernel C = Ker(Sl(2,ℤ)^ → Sl(2,Ẑ)) to be stable under conjugation by N₁,₁. If (21) exists this is automatic, since C is the kernel of (21) restricted to 𝒯̂⁺₁,₁ (P061 note 4). From the bare extension it is a condition, the first of the "conditions assez fortes" of scan 24 (P061 note 9). The page does not say which of the two it relies on.

5. **Scan 32, unnumbered display and (25) — unproved claim — correct (verified).** The kernel of Gl(2,R) → Aut(Sl(2,R)) is the group of scalars R* for every commutative ring R. A matrix that commutes with [[1,1],[0,1]] and [[1,0],[1,1]] is scalar. With R = Ẑ this gives the injection of (25) at all primes at once.

6. **Scan 32, (26) — unproved claim — keep.** "il se trouve que (24) se factorise" records a fact, presumably known from the geometric representation (21), not something deduced from the extension. This fact is what lets φ (27) be rebuilt from N₁,₁.

7. **Scan 33, (28)–(29) — unproved claim — correct (verified).** The kernel of (28) is {λ·1 : λ² = 1}. The 2-torsion of Ẑ* = ∏ ℤ_p* is ∏_p {±1}, also at p = 2 because ℤ₂* = {±1} × (1+4ℤ₂). So ₂Ẑ* ≃ (ℤ/2ℤ)^P, as stated.

8. **Scan 33, (28) and the arrow under GP(1,Ẑ) — unpursued implication — correct (verified).** The image of (28) is exactly the fibre product GP(1,Ẑ) ×_{Ẑ*/Ẑ*²} Ẑ*. If det u = λμ², then μ⁻¹u lifts ū and has determinant λ. The drawn arrow GP(1,Ẑ) → Ẑ*/Ẑ*² is det modulo squares, which is well defined on GP.

9. **Scan 33, (30)–(31) and margin — unproved claim (a condition) — correct.** "se factorise par l'image de (28)" means det φ(u) ≡ χ(u) mod Ẑ*² for u ∈ N₁,₁ (note 8). For the representation (21) it holds because det = χ (the Weil pairing; this is not in the ink). For a bare extension it is a hypothesis.

## Second-pass notes (mine)

A. **Scans 32, 34, 35, (25), (33), (37) — unproved claims, checked (second pass).** The map GP(1,Ẑ) → Aut(Sl(2,Ẑ)′) is injective. If ū acts trivially, then u g u⁻¹ = ε(g) g with ε a continuous homomorphism Sl(2,Ẑ) → {±1}. Comparing traces gives ε = 1 on elementary matrices (trace 2, and 4 ≠ 0 in Ẑ); these topologically generate Sl(2,Ẑ) = ∏ Sl(2,ℤ_p), so u centralises Sl(2,Ẑ) and is scalar. This proves "encore injectif" (33) and (37).

B. **Scans 33–37 — unproved claims, checked (second pass).** The image of GP(1,Ẑ) in Out(Sl(2,Ẑ)′) is GP(1,Ẑ)/(image of Sl(2,Ẑ)) ≅ Ẑ*/Ẑ*² through det, because det(Ẑ*·Sl(2,Ẑ)) = Ẑ*². So the congruence condition (38)–(39) is equivalent to det φ′ ≡ χ mod Ẑ*². That is the factorisation (42) through (43); the same argument derives (31) from (30).

C. **Scans 34–35 — claim in the ink, false as written (second pass).** The insertion on scan 34 identifies 𝒯̂⁺′₁,₁ ≃ 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃ "≃ Sl(2,Ẑ)′" (s34_D). The profinite completion of Sl(2,ℤ)′ is not Sl(2,Ẑ)′: Sl(2,ℤ) has non-congruence subgroups of finite index, so the congruence kernel is non-trivial. On scan 35 the author strikes the same isomorphism ("Utilisant l'isom 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃ ≃ Sl(2,Ẑ)′") and uses the homomorphism (38) 𝔖̂⁺₀,₃ ≀ Sl(2,ℤ)′^ → Sl(2,Ẑ)′ instead.

D. **Scans 34–35 — numbering (second pass).** The display that defines ψ′ is numbered (31) in the ink, duplicating (31) on scan 33. "ψ′ de (31)" on scan 34 refers to it, and scan 35 cites it as "(32)". Renumbering it silently, as the first pass did, would hide which object "(31)" denotes in the question of scan 34.

E. **Scan 38, (48) — unpursued implication (second pass).** Twisting a given lift by a family (η_ℓ) of continuous quadratic characters of Γ, a different one on each ℓ-component of Ẑ, gives another lift. Families that differ by one common character give the same lift in Gl(2,Ẑ)′, so all such families modulo a common twist lie in the indeterminacy Hom(Γ, ν). For the Galois-theoretic N₁,₁ these lifts are Tate-module-like representations with a different quadratic twist at each ℓ. Only the untwisted one is geometric.

F. **Scans 38–39 — own idea (second pass), confidence medium.** Among the lifts of note E, the geometric lift (from the Tate module of the universal or the Legendre curve) is singled out by compatibility across ℓ, for instance Frobenius traces independent of ℓ. A purely group-theoretic substitute for that compatibility would be one form of the "élément un peu astucieux" of scan 39. The description of the twists is exact; whether such a substitute exists is open.

G. **Scan 39, (49) — unproved claim, partly checked (second pass), confidence medium.** Π₀,₃ can be realised as Γ̄(2) = Ker(GP(1,ℤ) → Gl(2,𝔽₂) × {±1}), the map being reduction mod 2 and det. This is a free group of rank 2 with three cusp classes (the loops), normal of index 12 in GP(1,ℤ). Conjugation gives an injection GP(1,ℤ) → Aut(Π₀,₃) that respects the loops; 12 = |𝔖₃ × ℤ/2| matches the outer part. That the image is all of Aut_lac(Π₀,₃) is the claim (49), not checked here beyond these counts.

H. **Scan 40, (52)–(53) — own idea (second pass), confidence medium.** An explicit form of L: L(Π) = Ker(Autext_lac(Π)/±1 → Gl(Π_ab ⊗ 𝔽₂) × {±1}), the map being reduction mod 2 and the determinant of the action on Π_ab. For Π = Π₁,₁ this is the Γ̄(2) ≅ Π₀,₃ of note G. It is functorial for isomorphisms of exterior groups and is the twist of Π₀,₃ by the pushed-out torsor, which should make (53) commute. This is the discrete form of Legendre's λ (level-2 structures). Still to check: the loop structure, i.e. that the three cusps correspond to the three non-zero classes of Π_ab/2.

I. **Scan 38 — unproved claim, checked (second pass).** Because ν has exponent 2, Hom(Γ, ν) depends only on Γ^{ab}/2. So the hypothesis needed after (48) is that χ induce Γ^{ab}/2 ≅ Ẑ*/Ẑ*², weaker than the isomorphism written "Γ/Γ_ab →χ Ẑ*" (doubtful notation in the ink). For Γ = Γℚ both hold by Kronecker–Weber.
