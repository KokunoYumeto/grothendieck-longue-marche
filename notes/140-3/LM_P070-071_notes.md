The `.tex` file is a fragment: it deliberately supplies no preamble or definitions of the requested editorial macros.

## 2. Table of doubts

Locations are approximate manuscript lines or visible anchors such as equation numbers, paragraphs and margins, not line numbers in the LaTeX source. A row may group repeated occurrences of the same abbreviation or letter on one scan; the individual occurrences remain marked in the transcription. The reading and alternative columns sometimes use literal LaTeX to identify symbols exactly. Counts in `\illegible{n}` are estimates; a densely overwritten mathematical cluster may count as one unread unit.

**Confidence concerns the reading, not the mathematical truth of the passage.** High means strongly supported but still marked for a residual doubt; medium means plausible with unresolved strokes; low means the cancellation, overlap or letter form prevents a secure decision. An uncertainty can concern placement or the extent of a cancellation even when some of its words are readable. “No dependable alternative” does not imply that the proposed reading is certain.

## 4. Legibility and limits

Legibility is uneven. Larger matrices and many uninterrupted sentences are readable, while short word endings, small indices, and heavily overwritten clauses are much less secure. The transcription is deliberately conservative. An unmarked reading is the best supported reading after the checks, not a guarantee that it is error-free; no numerical accuracy claim is made.

The least secure areas are the layered opening revisions on **111–112**, the dense parameter annotations and marginal writing on **114–116**, the revised generator-identification sentence on **121**, and the corrected or crowded expressions on **122–126**. Scan122’s equation (9) reaches the right edge. The bent insertion on124 may contain two successive versions of “résulte”; that uncertainty is not silently resolved. The ornate group alphabet on125 and129–130, and the extension-group letter on125–130, remain explicit doubts.

The last scan was checked through **(37)**. Its right-hand completed-group expression visibly bears a tilde and is retained as written; no missing preposition has been supplied in the torsion insertion. The final singleton is tentatively read as **1/2**. Readings in cancelled text and the attachment of some interlinear phrases deserve particular caution even when their individual mathematical symbols are otherwise legible.
