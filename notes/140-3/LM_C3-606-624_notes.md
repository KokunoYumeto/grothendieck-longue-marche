# LM_C3-606-624 — Notes on the diplomatic transcription

## 1. Source, scope, and pagination

The transcription covers all 19 supplied scan images, in filename order, from `LM_C3-606-624_scan606.jpg` through `LM_C3-606-624_scan624.jpg`. The source is Grothendieck's *La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois* (1981), Montpellier archive cote 140-3, the archive file described as pages 293–650. Readings were made from these images and enlargements of their existing pixels; no published transcription supplied missing words or normalized the mathematics.

The scan identifier, upper authorial foliation, and lower archive foliation are distinct. Only the upper authorial foliation is represented by `\mspage`; missing numbers are not supplied from the sequence. The lower-left archive foliation, matching printed page label, and repeated institutional imprint are treated as archival paratext and recorded below rather than included in the autograph text. ### Placement and notation decisions

- **Scan 606:** the large cancelled middle passage and the earlier NB remain deleted text. The extent of some overwritten strokes is uncertain; the revision is not silently converted into a clean proof.
- **Scan 607:** the left-margin operation is written \({}^{t}u=(\operatorname{Tr}u)-u\). The arrow-like correction beneath the cancelled equation number is reproduced in TikZ; its function is not inferred. The deleted transition before the final congruence is tentatively read as “démontrons,” with “supposons” retained as an alternative.
- **Scan 608:** the enlarged reference favors (84), with (89) retained as an alternative. The presence of a raised degree on the first and last Ext expressions is not secure; the second Ext has a separate raised curved mark. These are recorded as reading uncertainties rather than supplied from the usual notation for extensions.
- **Scan 609:** the corrected lower object in (114) is tentatively \(\mu\times\{1\}\); the denominator in the subsequent quotient remains uncertain.
- **Scan 610:** the possible abelianization subscript in the prose after (115), the decorated subgroup chain, and the repeated equation number are retained without mathematical repair.
- **Scans 611–612:** the degree in (115) and the short arguments in (121)–(122) remain doubtful. In (119), the first relation is visibly an inclusion \(N_\rho^*\subset\mathcal M\), not an equals sign. The rightward mark after “larron” is reproduced as a TikZ drawing.
- **Scans 613–615:** the retraced foliation and superscripts are not regularized from the surrounding argument. In (131), the left quotients lack braces and the final lower annotation has \(L_\sigma/\pm\) without a separately visible 1; those forms are retained. The sentence about the action on the order-two quotient is also retained as written.
- **Scan 616:** the small loop at the base of the right-hand M below (132) is preserved as a doubtful subscript. Both image expressions in (134) visibly end in \(N_\rho^*\), although the surrounding text and following sets use sigma. No correction has been made.
- **Scan 617:** the compressed sentence before (140) has doubtful words and a two-stroke sign; its grammar is not repaired. The normality warning at the right is a marginal note.
- **Scan 618:** the repeated zigzag decoration is represented by a doubtful xi/star reading. The differing prime marks and the withdrawn paragraph at the bottom are preserved.
- **Scan 619:** the small tilde-gamma below \(N_\rho^*\) is retained as an insertion with uncertain syntactic attachment. Diagram (144) includes the inserted “sans !” vertex, heavy diagonal arrow, and dashed inclusion. Diagram (145) includes the three square labels and the visible degree labels 2; no 2 is added to its unlabeled first vertical arrow.
- **Scan 620:** the cancelled case condition is represented as a very low-confidence mathematical reading, not by an unreadable-word marker. The inserted “mod” expression has the visible bare calligraphic G.
- **Scan 623:** the second intersection in (14) has an undecorated calligraphic G in the supplied pixels; no plus or completion sign is supplied editorially.
- **Scan 624:** the first centralizer subscript and the overwritten groups in (19) remain uncertain. The normalizer in row 4 may itself be partly cancelled; the surviving strokes do not settle its editing state. In (18), lowercase gamma is primed while no prime is separately visible on capital Gamma. In the last two rows of (19), no detached bar is visible over j. In the cancelled sources at the bottom, the visible order of decorations favors exclamation mark followed by prime, unlike the prime-then-exclamation order in rows 2–3 of (19). The final arrow has no written target and is left unfinished.

## 4. Legibility and verification

The large formulas, many diagram objects, and much of the running French are recoverable, but this is a difficult, heavily revised manuscript. The least secure regions are the cancelled blocks on scan 606; the diagonal margin and sign corrections on scan 607; the small Ext marks on scan 608; the degree and class letters on scan 611; the superscripts and retraced foliation on scan 613; the compressed prose on scan 617; the altered subgroup list on scan 618; the inserted diagram vertex on scan 619; the deleted case condition on scan 620; the character labels on scan 622; and the overwritten groups and cancelled final comparison on scans 623–624. Enlargement clarifies existing strokes but cannot recover ink hidden beneath cancellations. Absence of a doubt mark should not be treated as a guarantee of letter-perfect accuracy.

Each scan was read as a whole, transcribed, reread in enlarged regions, and inventoried. Independent checks were also made of scans 606–610 and 624, followed by targeted rereading of several disputed glyphs in scans 616–620. The fragment has no preamble or editorial macro definitions, as requested; its importing document must supply the stated editorial macros and the usual mathematical and TikZ/TikZ-CD support. Static validation checked page order, braces, environments, math delimiters, diagram wrappers, and the six-column inventory. A full typeset compilation was not available in this environment, so no rendered proof is claimed.

The completed inventory has **368 elements**, all marked `yes`; the fragment contains **173 explicit uncertainty spans**, **43 TikZ-CD diagrams**, and **3 TikZ drawings**. No unreadable-word placeholder is used; heavily obscured content is retained as explicitly tentative readings. These counts describe coverage and editorial treatment, not certainty.

## 5. Research notes — separate from the transcription

These notes are in English and do not emend the diplomatic text. “Unproved claim” means that the cited passage does not give a complete proof there; an argument may occur earlier or later in the manuscript, and a recap may summarize an earlier result. “Conjecture” includes a written question or tentative expectation. Own ideas are explicitly labelled, with confidence and a reason; they are proposals for checking, not established conclusions or claims of novelty. Quotations are short extracts from the supplied manuscript; its scan numbering is used in the citations.

### Research note 1

A proof should reduce \(c^2+cd+d^2\) modulo 3 and use the unit hypothesis; this is separate from determining the ink in the marginal abbreviation (Grothendieck, 1981, scan 606).

### Research note 2

### Research note 13

**Scan 611, (115) and following prose; own idea.**
