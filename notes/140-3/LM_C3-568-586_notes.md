## Table of doubts

Every occurrence of `\unsure` or `\illegible` has an entry below. `D01`, `D02`, and so forth identify successive doubt marks within a scan, including marks inside deletions and marginal notes. Locations are approximate positions on the written page, with manuscript equation numbers where available; they are not line numbers of the LaTeX file. Repeated readings appear repeatedly so that no occurrence is hidden by aggregation. Confidence describes the reading of the ink, not the truth of a mathematical assertion, and is qualitative rather than a calibrated probability.

Where a second reading is not supported by the visible strokes, none is invented. A low-confidence proposed reading is still transcribed in place. “Unreadable words” is restricted to physically obscured prose; uncertain symbols are always given a proposed reading. Alternative alphabet choices and small superscripts remain explicit.
