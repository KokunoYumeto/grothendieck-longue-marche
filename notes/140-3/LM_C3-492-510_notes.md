# LM_C3-492-510 — Editorial and research notes

## Scope and source references

The transcription covers the 19 supplied image files, scans **492–510**, in filename order. The filenames run one ahead of the repeated printed archive page numbers **491–509**. The `\scanpage` markers follow the filenames; `\mspage` records visible handwritten numerals where they occur, including the separate upper numbering on some sheets. Recording a numeral does not establish whose hand supplied it. The repeated modern institutional footer is archive metadata, outside the handwritten manuscript transcription.

The source attribution, date, and manuscript title below follow the supplied task prompt. All page references in the tables and research notes refer to **scan filenames**, unless explicitly described as handwritten page numbers. The source for the observations and brief quotations is the supplied manuscript (Grothendieck, 1981). No published transcription has been used to complete, normalize, or correct its wording.

Uncertainty is local: `\unsure{reading}` retains a tentative reading, a vertical bar inside it separates alternatives, and `\illegible{n}` records an estimated number of words obscured by the ink. Repeated uncertainties in a symbol's alphabet can be grouped in the table below, but remain marked in the LaTeX. A row marked `yes` in the inventory means that the visible element is represented, including any explicit doubt or deletion; it does **not** certify that every doubtful reading has been resolved.
