The first pass's table of doubts and list of abbreviations are superseded by `LM_P060_changes.md` (several of their entries, such as ν_a, "type" and the ornamented group letter, had already been revised in the fixed first pass).

Scans 11–20 are sheets 10–19 of the file, in §38 ("Détermination de Π'_{0,3} ⥲ 𝔖^+_{0,3}", ms pp. 293–298). Scans 11–13 state the quotient theorem for a discrete group 𝒢 with an invariant subgroup G and a signature d_X. Scans 14–18 apply it to X = U^{an}_{0,3}, 𝒢 = 𝒯_{0,3} = 𝔖_3 × ℤ/2, G = 𝔖_3, ℋ = ℤ/2ℤ, and compute the involutive generators τ'_0, τ'_1, τ'_∞. Scans 19–20 begin the comparison of Π̃'_{0,3} ≃ 𝔖_{0,3} with GP(1,ℤ) = Gl(2,ℤ)/{±1} through the half-plane and elliptic curves. No witness covers these pages.

**R1. Scan 11, stabiliser hypothesis. Own idea (first pass). Kept.**
"les stabilisateurs G_x (x∈X) opèrent fidèlement au voisinage de x, en conservant l'orientation locale en x". Testing the quotient construction on a disk with a finite rotation group is a sensible local check; under the hypothesis each nontrivial G_x is finite cyclic, which is what makes ν_x = [G_x] a ramification index. *Reason:* the reading is unchanged.

**R2. Scan 11, d_Y(y) = ν_x d_X(x). Implication not pursued. Corrected.**
The subscript reads ν_x ("si y = f(x), ν_x = [G_x]"). Independence of the lift x is immediate: the points over y form one G-orbit, G_{gx} = gG_xg^{-1} has the same order, and d_X is 𝒢-invariant. *Reason:* the first pass left the subscript and the check open; there is no gap here.

**R5. Scan 12, the two "Remarques". Claim without proof. Corrected.**

**R13. Scan 14, f(z). Own idea (first pass). Corrected: carried out.**

**R14. Scan 15, the six points over y = −j̄. Claim without proof. Corrected: checked.**

The order-3 element z ↦ 1/(1−z) of 𝔖_3 has determinant 1 and fixed points −j̄, −j, and sends 0 → 1 → ∞ → 0 and ½ → 2 → −1 → ½. ## Second-pass notes (own)

**S1. Scan 18 against scans 9–10 (P059). Grothendieck's question "ou ρ^{-1} ?" answered.**
Scan 18 settles the marginal question of scan 9 in favour of ρ^{-1}: with ρ = τ'_0τ'_1 (margin), (23) and (24) give ℓ'_∞ = τ'_1τ'_0 = ρ^{-1}, ℓ'_1 = σ_∞, ℓ'_0 = σ_∞ρ. **S2. Scan 11–13 against §37. Implication not pursued.**
The theorem of scans 11–13 (𝒢 with invariant subgroup G, arbitrary signature d_X) generalises §37's Proposition (𝒢 = G, d_X = 1); footnote 148 of §37 (CSG ll. 10522–10528) announces exactly this "variante" with the application "𝔖_{0,3} ≃ 𝒯'_{1,1} (GL(2,ℤ)^+/±1) (où 𝔖_{0,3} est une extension de 𝒯_{0,3} = 𝔖_3 × ℤ/2ℤ par π_{0,3})", which scans 14–20 carry out.

**S3. Scan 18, margin. Claim checked: ε_i² = ℓ_i.**
From the marginal formulas, ε_0² = (τ'_∞τ'_1)² = ℓ_0, ε_1² = τ'_0τ'_1τ'_∞τ'_1τ'_∞τ'_0 = ℓ_1 (using τ'_0τ'_∞ = τ'_∞τ'_0) and ε_∞² = (τ'_1τ'_∞τ'_1τ'_0)² = ℓ_∞. These are the relations "ε_i² = ℓ_i" announced in (16) on scan 9; they also support the doubtful final factors of the margin.

**S4. Scan 17. Implication not pursued.**
Since λ'_0 and λ'_1 are null-homotopic in U_{0,3}, the generators τ'_0 and τ'_1 are, after the change of base point, the pure elements σ̇_∞τ̇ and σ̇_0τ̇ of 𝒢, the reflections in the great circles (∞, −j̄, ½, −j) (Re z = ½) and (0, −j̄, 2, −j) (|z − 1| = 1) named under the formulas; both fix P = −j̄. Only τ'_∞ carries a path, the arc through ½ from P to τ̇P = −j. This is the geometric content of (22): τ'_0 = σ̃_∞, τ'_1 = σ̃_0 lie over reflections, τ'_∞ = τ_∞ over τ̇.
