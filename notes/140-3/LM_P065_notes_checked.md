## B. Second-pass notes (Claude)

B1. **Scan 62, left-margin NB; claim checked (consequential). Confidence high, relative to the formulas as written.** By (22),
ℓ'0^β = g0^{-1}λ^{-1}φ̂(g0), where the conjugator λ of (19)–(20) depends only on u, and u does not change under
g0 ↦ g0ℓ0^λ (by (11)). With ℓ0 = ε0² (25), φ̂(ℓ0) = ε0 (18) and ℓ'0 = ε0, the new value is ε0^{-2λ}ε0^βε0^λ = ε0^{β−λ}, so
β' = β − λ, not β − 3λ. Every u therefore has a representative g0 with β = 0; Grothendieck's own "Correct?" stands.

B2. **Scan 62, (24 bis); model's idea. Confidence medium.** With φ̂(h0) = σ∞^ε g0 ε0^β, the change g0 ↦ g0ℓ0^λ gives
φ̂(h'0) = σ∞^{-λ}φ̂(h0)ε0^λ, so (β, ε) ↦ (β − λ, ε − λ mod 2) (ε and β are unique for given g0, since ε0 has infinite order).
The invariant of u is β − ε mod 2, i.e. ε after normalizing β = 0. The meaningful form of his mod-3 question is whether this
Z/2-invariant takes both values, for instance on automorphisms coming from ΓQ.

B3. **Scans 60–64; implication for the references. Confidence high.** Two formulas carry the number (18): scan 60
"(18) u(ρ) conjugué de ρ^p", which scan 64 calls "la formule présomptive (18)", and scan 61 "(18)" defining φ̂, which scan 63
cites as "défini par (18)". (28) for x = ℓ∞ is what makes the presumptive (18) of scan 60 precise.

B4. **Scan 63, inserted "(On prolonge u en autom de 𝔖̂+0,3 par int(u), cf (15))"; implication. Confidence high.** The
reference is (15) of scan 59: u(σ∞) = σ∞, u(ρ) = g.ρ with g = σ∞(g0)ℓ1^γρ(g0)^{-1}. The same shape returns as (36) on scan 68,

B6. **Scan 68, splitting of ℰ!; claim without proof. Confidence medium.** Composing u ∈ ℰ! with int(h), h ∈ Π̂0,3, so as to
fix σ∞ needs every element of order 2 above σ̇∞ to be Π̂0,3-conjugate to σ∞; in the discrete Z/2 * Z/3 this is the Kurosh

Herfort and Ribes; to check). Uniqueness of h needs Π̂0,3^{σ∞} = 1, admitted on scan 58; the dependence on choosing σ∞ among
σ0, σ1, σ∞ is the "non canoniquement".
