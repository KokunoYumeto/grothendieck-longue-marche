## First-pass notes

1. **Scan 21, (29) — unproved claim — correct.** The canonical analytic isomorphism D/GP(1,ℤ)⁺ ≃ ℙ^{1an}∖{0} = Y is asserted without proof. The ink settles the normalisation the note left open: the ramification-3 orbit goes to ∞, the ramification-2 orbit to 1, and the removed point 0 is the cusp. Own note A gives the explicit map.

2. **Scan 21, (30) and last paragraph — conjecture / stated intention — correct.** "que je voudrais expliciter" announces an identification of τ′₀, τ′₁, τ′∞ with elements of Gl^{±}(2,ℤ)′ (possibly Gl^{±′}); it is not carried out on these pages. The fundamental group of (30) is now read π₁^{±}(Y, d̲; y) in preference to π₁^{*}, which fits 𝒢 = GP(1,ℤ) containing the orientation-reversing maps.

3. **Scan 21 — own idea (first pass) — keep.** Choosing matrix representatives and checking their orders against the normalisation of (29) is the right test; own note B carries it out for one consistent choice.

4. **Scan 22, (1) — unproved claim — correct.** The vertical map N₁,₁ → Gl(2,Ẑ) is not constructed on the page (presumably the action on the Tate module of the universal elliptic curve, with det = χ by the Weil pairing; this interpretation is not in the ink). Once that map exists, "passe au quotient" follows: the kernel of Sl(2,ℤ)^ → Sl(2,Ẑ) is the kernel of 𝒯̂⁺₁,₁ → Gl(2,Ẑ), hence normal in N₁,₁.

5. **Scan 23, centraliser step — unproved claim — correct (an omitted computation, not a gap).** "commute à Sl(2,Ẑ) … c'est un scalaire" is a two-line matrix computation valid over any commutative ring (own note C). With it, the injectivity of (5) is fully proved by the argument on the page.

6. **Scan 23 — own idea (first pass) — keep.** Repeating the argument over ℤ/nℤ works verbatim; own note C does it, and it proves the NB of scan 24.

7. **Scan 24, (6) — unproved claim — correct.** The notation is now read (Γ_{ℚ ab})₂, i.e. Γℚ^{ab}/(Γℚ^{ab})². "Par la théorie du corps de classes pour ℚ" is the Kronecker–Weber theorem: the kernel of (6) fixes the compositum of all quadratic fields (own note D). "Cette dernière est fidèle" is the injectivity of (5).

8. **Scan 24, marginal NB — unproved claim — keep, verified.** Own note C over R = ℤ/nℤ shows that the kernel of (ℤ/nℤ)* → Autext Sl(2,ℤ/nℤ) is the group of squares, so ((ℤ/nℤ)*)₂ acts faithfully. The modulus letter (n or m) and the parentheses stay doubtful in the transcription.

9. **Scan 24, around (7) — unpursued implication — keep.** The two "conditions assez fortes" (stability of the congruence kernel; an outer action on Sl(2,Ẑ) coming from Ẑ*/Ẑ*²) are independent tests on an outer automorphism. The text does not claim that they characterise the image of Γℚ.

10. **Scan 25, (8)–(9) — unproved claim ("sauf erreur") — correct.** The group is T = T∞(Q̄₀), a plain T (the first pass's T̄ was a misreading). Its image is the closure of the cyclic group generated by a unipotent element, conjugate in Sl(2,Ẑ) to {(1 0; n 1) : n ∈ Ẑ} as stated; the struck inner hat on ℤ in (8) stays doubtful.

11. **Scan 25, (10) — unproved claim — correct.** "sera" and "caractère" are now read without doubt, and the group is T, not T̄. The claim that Γ → Aut(T) ≃ Ẑ* is the cyclotomic character amounts to T ≃ Ẑ(1) as a Galois module; the sign of the exponent depends on the generator convention, which the page does not fix.

12. **Scans 25–26, "on vérifie qu'" and margin "faire la vérification" — unproved claim — correct.** The two marks flag the existence and uniqueness of χ(u) as unchecked. The verification fails for uniqueness in the Sl(2,Ẑ) form (12): there χ(u) is unique only modulo Ẑ*² (own note E).

13. **Scans 25–26, D(a)U(n)D(a)⁻¹ = U(a⁻²n) — own idea (first pass) — correct.** The identity is right (checked by multiplication), and it settles the question the note left open: inside Sl(2,Ẑ) it shows that χ(u) is not unique in Ẑ*, only in Ẑ*/Ẑ*² (own note E). The note's T̄ should read T.

14. **Scan 26, "Ceci dit" — unpursued implication — keep.** Separating the outer automorphisms with the lacet property from those that also preserve the kernel of (7) and act on Sl(2,Ẑ) through the composite is the structure the text sets up but does not analyse.

15. **Scans 26–27, centre {±1} — unproved claim — correct.** Because of the centre, the outer action does not determine N₁,₁; after dividing by the centre, N′₁,₁ is obtained as the pull-back of (13), which is read with Aut_lac (ext struck) and Autext_lac. This uses that the centre of Sl(2,ℤ)^ is exactly {±1} and that Sl(2,ℤ)^/{±1}, the completion of ℤ/2 ∗ ℤ/3, has trivial centre; both are true but not argued on these pages.

16. **Scan 27, "Cette extension … a été aussi interprétée" — unproved claim — correct.** The identifications N′₁,₁ ≃ M₀,₃ ≃ N′₀,₄, 𝔖̂₀,₃ ≃ 𝒯̂′₀,₄ and the extension of 𝔖₃ ≃ Sl(2,ℤ/2ℤ) by Π̂₀,₃ are recalled, not proved here. The letter is now read 𝔖, the cartographic group of §38.

17. **Scan 27, "Nous savons déjà" — unproved claim (recall) — correct.** The faithfulness refers back to §35, titled in the author's table "L'injectivité de ΓQ → Autext_lac(π̂0,3)" (IUT witness l. 94). The commutation with the outer action of 𝔖₃ is reinterpreted on scan 28 as a case of the congruence conditions.

18. **Scans 26–27, central lifting data — own idea (first pass) — keep.** Keeping the quotient extension and the central data separate is sensible; no classification is done on these pages. Confidence medium, as given.

19. **Scan 28, triviality at modulus 2 — unproved claim — correct.** The factorisation through ((ℤ/nℤ)*)₂ is the finite-level form of (5) (own note C); triviality for n = 2 is (ℤ/2ℤ)* = 1, and non-triviality for n ≥ 3 is note 22. The ink reads "n ≠ 1,2 i.e. n ≥ 3", with a struck "2," before.

20. **Scan 28, "On peut essayer de les exprimer" — proposed investigation — keep.** Whether the reformulation through Π̂₀,₃ keeps all of the congruence conditions or only consequences is exactly the question of scan 30 (note 27).

21. **Scan 28, Π(2m) open, normal, invariant — unproved claim — correct.** The symbol is 𝔖₃. Openness and normality are immediate (kernels of maps to finite groups); invariance under 𝔖₃ holds because Π(2m) is the intersection of Π̂₀,₃ with a normal subgroup of the group mapping to Sl(2,2m). That group is 𝒯̂₁,₁ with superscript +′, but the ink adds a stroke and a hump (+′ or +/n), so the precise group is now doubtful.

22. **Scan 28, squares for n ≥ 3 — own idea (first pass) — keep, verified.** (ℤ/nℤ)* is a finite abelian group of order φ(n), even for n ≥ 3, and −1 ≠ 1 lies in the kernel of squaring, so squaring is not surjective and ((ℤ/nℤ)*)₂ ≠ 1. This checks only the unit-group part, as the note says.

23. **Scan 29, (15)–(16) — unproved claim — keep.** The maps φ₂ₘ, φ∞ are "pas bien difficile d'expliciter" by reduction mod 2m; no construction is given. (The ink writes "qu'il en doit", grammatically "ne".)

24. **Scan 29, (17)–(18) — unproved claim — correct.** Exactness at Sl(2,Ẑ)′ in (17) needs the image of Π̂₀,₃ to be the whole kernel of reduction mod 2: Π₀,₃ ≃ Γ(2)/{±1}, and Γ(2) is dense in the principal congruence subgroup of level 2 of Sl(2,Ẑ) because Sl(2,ℤ) → Sl(2,ℤ/N) is onto for every N. The page asserts this without argument.

25. **Scan 29, "de façon naturelle de définir une opération ext." — unproved claim — keep.** Independence of choices up to inner automorphisms and compatibility between the levels 2m and ∞ are not discussed.

26. **Scan 29, Π(∞) = ∩ₘ Π(2m) — own idea (first pass) — keep, verified.** Every n divides some 2m, and an element of Sl(2,Ẑ) congruent to ±1 modulo every 2m is ±1, so the kernel of Sl(2,Ẑ) → Sl(2,Ẑ)′ is the intersection of the kernels of the maps to Sl(2,ℤ/2mℤ)′. Hence Π(∞) = ∩ₘ Π(2m).

27. **Scan 30, "On devrait regarder si cette formulation est vraiment équivalente" — explicit question — keep.** Grothendieck asks whether the conditions on Π̂₀,₃ are equivalent to the congruence conditions in 𝒯̂⁺′₁,₁ "tout entier, et non seulement une conséquence", and leaves it as an exercise.

28. **Scan 30, restriction to the congruence kernel — own idea (first pass) — keep.** Comparing the outer action on the extension of 𝔖₃ by Π̂₀,₃ with its restriction to Π̂₀,₃ is the natural test; agreement on a subgroup need not determine the outer action on the whole group. Confidence medium, as given.

## Second-pass notes (own)

A. **Scan 21, (29) — own verification of an unproved claim.** The isomorphism is t = 1728/j(τ): j(ρ) = 0 at ρ = exp(2iπ/3) (the τ with Z[ρ], ramification 3) goes to t = ∞, j(i) = 1728 (ramification 2) goes to t = 1, and the cusp (j = ∞) goes to the removed point t = 0. This is exactly the normalisation written, so Y = ℙ¹∖{0}. Confidence high (standard values of j).

B. **Scan 21, (30) — own idea.** In Sl(2,ℤ) put S = (0 −1; 1 0), U = (0 −1; 1 1), T₀ = (1 1; 0 1); then S² = −1, U³ = −1 and SU = −T₀, so in Sl(2,ℤ)′ = GP(1,ℤ)⁺ the classes satisfy T₀⁻¹·S·U = 1, with orders ∞, 2, 3 and fixed points ∞ (cusp), i, ρ. Thus τ′₀ ↦ ±T₀⁻¹, τ′₁ ↦ ±S, τ′∞ ↦ ±U is one candidate for the identification Grothendieck wants, up to orientation and base-point conventions the page does not fix; the elements of determinant −1 (e.g. ±diag(1,−1)) are what π₁^{±} and Gl^{±} add. Confidence high for the identities, medium for the match with his τ′.

C. **Scans 23–24 — own proof of the centraliser step and of the NB.** For a commutative ring R and A = (a b; c d), AE = EA with E = (1 1; 0 1) forces c = 0 and a = d, and AF = FA with F = (1 0; 1 1) forces b = 0; so the centraliser of Sl(2,R) in Gl(2,R) is R*. Hence int(u) is inner on Sl(2,R) iff u ∈ Sl(2,R)·R*, and the kernel of R* → Autext Sl(2,R) is R*². R = Ẑ gives the injectivity of (5); R = ℤ/nℤ proves the NB of scan 24 with ((ℤ/nℤ)*)₂ = (ℤ/nℤ)*/squares. Confidence high.

D. **Scan 24, (6) — own verification.** Ẑ*/Ẑ*² = ∏ₚ ℤₚ*/ℤₚ*² ≅ (ℤ/2)² × ∏_{p odd} ℤ/2, and by Kronecker–Weber the kernel of (6) is the subgroup fixing ℚ(√−1, √2, √p : p odd prime), the compositum of all quadratic fields. So "(Γ_{ℚ ab})₂ — le quotient multiquadratique" is the Galois group of that field, identified with Ẑ*/Ẑ*² by (6). Confidence high.

E. **Scans 25–26, uniqueness of χ(u) — own correction of an unproved claim.** For every a ∈ Ẑ*, D(a) = diag(a, a⁻¹) lies in Sl(2,Ẑ) and D(a)U(n)D(a)⁻¹ = U(a⁻²n); so if χ(u) makes (12) commute up to conjugation, so does a²χ(u). In the forms "ou seulement de Sl(2,Ẑ)" and "seulement dans (12)" χ(u) is therefore unique only in Ẑ*/Ẑ*², not in Ẑ* as written. This is the precision the next composite uses (Γ → Ẑ* → Ẑ*/Ẑ*² ↪ Autext_{lac!}(Sl(2,Ẑ))), so the argument survives; uniqueness in (11) would need the normaliser of the cusp subgroup in Sl(2,ℤ)^ to be ± that subgroup, which these pages do not prove. Confidence high for the counterexample, medium for the remark on (11).

F. **Scans 27 and 29 — own note on notation (implication not pursued).** On scan 27 Sl(2,ℤ)^/{±1} is defined as "Sl(2,Ẑ)′" (hat over ℤ in the ink) and that group, the inner automorphism group of Sl(2,ℤ)^, is the kernel in (13); two lines later it is written Sl(2,ℤ)^′ with the inner hat struck. On scan 29 Sl(2,Ẑ)′ in (16)–(17) is Sl(2,Ẑ)/{±1}, a different group (they differ by the congruence kernel). The transcription keeps the ink; a reader must not identify the two uses. Confidence medium.

G. **Scan 30, (20) — own note on an inconsistency in the manuscript.** (20) is introduced as the composite of (19) with Ẑ*/Ẑ*² → (ℤ/2mℤ)*₂, but its target reads (ℤ/mℤ)*₂ with no 2 visible; likewise (15) writes Sl(2,ℤ/2m)′, (18) Sl(2,ℤ/2mℤ)′ and scan 28 Sl(2,2m). These look like slips or shorthand of the author and are kept as written. Confidence medium.

H. **Scans 28–30 — own note, implication not pursued.** By the condition of scan 30, Γℚ acts on the finite quotient Π̂₀,₃/Π(2m) through (ℤ/2mℤ)*₂, so the action is trivial on the subgroup fixing the largest multiquadratic subfield of ℚ(μ₂ₘ). For m = 1 this is the triviality on 𝔖₃ ≃ Sl(2,ℤ/2ℤ) used on scan 28; the text does not state the field-of-definition form. Confidence medium.

I. **Scans 23–24 — own note on two numerals.** In "u ∈ Gl(1,Ẑ)" (scan 23) and "sur Sl(1,Ẑ)" (scan 24) the numeral looks like a 1, which cannot be meant (Gl(1) is Ẑ* and Sl(1) is trivial; the argument needs 2).
