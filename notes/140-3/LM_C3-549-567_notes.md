## Editorial treatment and legibility

Repetition strokes in scan 549, (6) and (7), are retained as strokes rather than expanded into words. The last written term in scan 553, (28), is only `\varepsilon'_\gamma\circ\delta_G`; no left-hand `\varepsilon'_G=` has been supplied. Alternating prime/star forms, uncertain sign letters, and visibly revised counts have not been reconciled by mathematical inference. In scan 561, “dix carrés” and the revised adjoining count and list remain distinct pieces of ink. The question marks used as generic group placeholders in scan 562 are manuscript notation, not editorial doubt marks. Diagram (67), scan 563, has deliberately unwritten objects; names have not been inserted into those blank positions. Canceled diagram material includes its arrows as well as its objects and labels, including both canceled triangular diagrams on scan 556 (Grothendieck, 1981, supplied scans 549, 553, 556–557, 561–563).

The principal prose and many formulas are more legible than the revisions. The least secure regions are the canceled passage before (24) on scan 553; the small diagonal margins and layered cancellations on scans 554–558; the canceled upper block on scan 559; the rewritten lower third of scan 560; the blackened node and dotted routes in diagram (62); the abandoned starts around (65); and the canceled or overtraced material around diagram (67) and the parameter formulas on scans 565–567. Typographic alphabet choices, tiny primes and exponents, short arrowheads, and the order of superposed revisions often remain uncertain. Enlargement of the supplied 1540 × 2048 images helps separate surviving strokes but cannot recover concealed ink. Scan 567 ends within a sentence; no continuation has been supplied.

The final source was checked structurally for page markers, braces and environments, and arrow endpoints. **No render validation was possible** because `pdflatex.fmt` was unavailable; these checks and the inventory do not certify decipherments or rendered layout.
