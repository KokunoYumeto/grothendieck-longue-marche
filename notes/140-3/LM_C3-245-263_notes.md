## 4. Legibility and limits

The larger displays and much of the running prose are recoverable, but confidence varies sharply within individual pages. The least secure portions are the oblique notes and overwritten calculations on scans 245–246 and 250–252, the revised conjugacy wording on scan 255, and the crowded changes near the end of scan 261. Tiny group decorations, the ξ/ζ-like parameter, some dotted action symbols, and several exponents remain uncertain even where surrounding words are clear. The doubt table and inline marks identify these limitations; the text should not be treated as a fully settled critical transcription.

Every page was read and compared again with its transcription.
