## Places still unresolved

1. 181, display: π ⊗_ℤ ℂ or ℝ. The letters are superimposed; the insertion "ou π⊗_ℤℝ" suggests ℂ in the display. Kept `\unsure{\mathbb C|\mathbb R}`.
2. 181: "asso-/cié" is split at the line end ("associé" or "associée"). The overwritten "NB" also remains open.
3. 182: the circled insertion after "(g,ν+1)". The sign between ν and ℕ* is not Grothendieck's ∈, and no "+1" is visible.
4. 182: "card I' = ν". The ink shows ν, but a group of type (g,ν+1) has ν+1 loop classes (the witnesses print ν+1). Not changed.
5. 182: whether the group letter on this page is Π' (first-pass convention) or the π' used from scan 184 on. Also open: the subscript of "Π'_{s|α}", and the dark "d" after "[Π']".
6. 182: "d'origine | d'où une" and the first word of "\unsure{d'un} objt".
7. 183: λ or id in the couple; "=:" or "="; U or X before "−(Δ̊ᵢ−{sᵢ})"; "classe i" or "Π".
8. 184: "pt base | fixe". Also open: whether D_i^*(b_i) carries a tilde in its three occurrences (IUT reads bᵢ′ in the third).
9. 185: a glyph between π and "=" in the display "π = π(D̃ᵢ*)", probably an overwritten parenthesis or sign. Neither the first pass nor the witnesses have it; left untranscribed. Also open: the label "(*|5)", "l'|son" and "Facile.".
10. 187: the relation in "2g+ν … 4". It is overwritten, and the stroke shape leans towards ≤, while only ≥ agrees with the bracket and with both witnesses. Also open: the overwritten ν(+1) cluster, the subscript of π', the quotient Aut ext(π) or Aut ext(π'), "g≥1 | g=1", and "qu'on a".
11. 188: folio 94 or 99; 𝒯 or 𝔗; "00" or "∘∘"; the annotation after M_{g,ν} in π₂(M_{g,ν}, …); "standart | standard"; the struck "pointé".
12. 189: "&" before "les vérifications", and whether "Au" is written over a struck "Or".
13. 190: script ℰ or roman E for the normaliser and the extension.
