## Errors in the witnesses (scan, witness, reading, ink)

- 341, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "homorphisme surjectif". The ink has *hom*; this is their own typo.
- 344, CSG, IUT and Carmona: the central node reads "discrétifications orientées strictes (π)". The ink has "prédiscrétifications [orientées] strictes (π)": *pré-* was dropped, which gives the node a different meaning.
- 344, CSG and IUT: "[Bases de!(π) …]". The ink has "[Bases^!(π) …]" with no *de*; Carmona is correct.
- 344, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "…/M_{g,ν} 3", with a stray "3". The ink has no 3; the M is retraced.
- 347, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "il faut qu'il en soit ainsi pour leurs images". The ink has "il faut et s. qu'il en soit ainsi". Dropping *et s[uffit]* turns the equivalence, which the bijection on préarithmétisations needs, into a necessary condition only.
- 347, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "a fortiori avec T̂̂^!(π) → T̂̂^!(π′)". The ink has a short flat word, read as *un*; there is no v ascender for *avec*.
- 347, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "(et si on note π = π_{g,ν}, I′ réduit au dernier élément de I)". The ink has "(choisissant π = π_{g,ν}, I' … dernier élém. de I_ν)": the opening words and the index set differ.
- 348, IUT and Carmona: "1 → π′ → T̂̂(π, i) → Ŝ̂(π, i) → 1". The third term in the ink is Ĝ̂(π',i), with a prime. CSG loses primes throughout.
- 349, CSG, IUT and Carmona: "forage de trous". The ink has "forage des trous".
