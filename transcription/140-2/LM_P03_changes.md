## Places still unresolved

1. Scan 21, "sur \unsure{S|ξ}": an S-curve with a leftward lower swing, unlike the straight-descender ξ on scan 24. S is more likely (all witnesses read S).
2. Scan 21, "muni du ~~foncteur~~ hom. canonique": the layered word is unresolved. The witnesses' "d'un foncteur" ignores the ink *du*.
3. Scan 22, the wavy line under "au dessus": it carries loops and blots and is either the continued underline or a struck baseline word. The first pass's underline is kept.
4. Scan 23, left margin "\unsure{id.}" at "tout X ~~connexe~~": a slash and two small letters.
5. Scan 24: "\unsure{tels que}" (tels/tel), "(\unsure{ou|en} classe de chemins)" and the alphabet of P¹_K. *elles* is written over a darker first word, possibly *ils*.
6. Scan 25: "relatif \unsure{à|:} k(s)" (a bold dot with a faint mark above). The *S* of "Spec(O_s)" is written over an earlier letter with an *s* subscript, perhaps O_s.
7. Scan 26, subscript of the first T in "T_?(k̄_s)": a two-loop glyph (ε, *e* or &), visibly unlike the *s* subscripts of k̄_s and K̄_s next to it. The first pass's alternatives are kept.
8. Scans 26–27, p_s or ρ_s (rows 19, 20, 27): the hand gives no distinction.
9. Scan 26, "(i.e.|ou) sur Π₁(K)" (row 25).
10. Scan 26, the struck letter in "Π₁(~~?~~s)": K or k, perhaps the start of k(s).
11. Scan 27, the insertion "peut appeler le": no *qu'on* is visible, and the struck "on" of "Ceci dit, on a" may have been meant to stand.
12. Scan 27: each of the first four *Aut* is written over an earlier letter with a long top bar (Π or π?). The last *Aut* is not.
13. Scan 27: "\deleted{\unsure{où}}" after "Hom]," and the lower-right "\unsure{11|''}".
14. Scan 28, bottom note: faint and clipped by the page edge. The upper line ends "… des comp. conn. … qui com[…]", and the lower line has "… celles de X∖S, ou encore de X". The first pass's "\unsure{qui sont celles}" may conflate the two lines.
15. Scan 28: the target "X∖S" of ρ_S is written over an earlier "D_S".
16. Scan 29, cancelled 5°) diamond: both side nodes are overwritten (U or D), and the upper-right label is σ over ρ.
17. Scan 30: whether "6°) Un" is struck or underlined (a baseline stroke under an abandoned start).
18. Scan 30: *satisfont* (a short word: *satisf*, a wave, *t*) against the witnesses' *satisfaisant*. The ink favours *satisfont*.
