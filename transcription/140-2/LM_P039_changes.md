All three come from one group and agree on nearly every variant below, so their agreement counts as one reading, not three. ## Places still unresolved

- Whole package: the ornate letter of $\widehat{\widehat{\mathcal G}}(\pi)$, $\widehat{\mathcal G}_a^+$ and $\mathcal G(\pi_0)$ is kept as the first pass's $\mathcal G$. CSG/IUT/CK read it as S (Ŝ̂, Ŝ⁺, S(π₀)). The checker of LM_P032 identified the same glyph with Grothendieck's 𝔖 (as in 𝔖₃). The glyph alone does not separate a looped G from his fraktur S. This needs one decision for the whole edition.
