# LM_P033 (scans 321–330): second-pass changes

Method: each scan was read in its two halves at archive resolution. Every doubtful or disputed place was enlarged (crops in `second_pass\work_LM_P033`; margin notes on 321 and 322 rotated by −56°/−57°). The text was collated with the CSG draft (lines 6407–6620) and the IUT witness (lines 7325–7560). Witness readings are cited as evidence only.

## Places still unresolved

1. 321, margin NB: the two words after "se décrit" are hidden under the main text. CSG has "en termes de groupes extérieurs définis par π". Further words may be hidden between "ext" and "π". "une flèche" (CSG "une application") is not legible.
2. 321, line 7: "s'identifie à" or "s'identifiant" before the colon.
3. 321, lower half: "autom." between "des" and "à lacets" has a long horizontal stroke through it, either a deletion or a t-bar. It is kept as `\unsure{autom.}`.
4. 322: the verb before "à l'assertion" ("équivalent" in the first pass; CSG "ce qui équivaut") and the word after "(11) est" (injective/bijective/injection) are both under the margin note.
5. 322: "de" or "des" before "ss-groupes fermés". In this hand the two forms look alike.
6. 323: "ouvert" or "invariant" before "d'indice 2"; "grps" or "groupes"; whether "profinis" ends in -s.
7. 321–330: the identity of the extension-group letter (𝔖 or 𝒢) and of the symmetric-group target of (13); also their alphabet (fraktur or script).
8. 324, 325, 327: the abbreviation for *même* is drawn as "=" on 324 (enlarged), and apparently also on 325 and 327 (half-images). On 323 it is a barred *m*. The first pass's `$\overline{\mathrm m}$` is kept throughout.
9. 325: "aura" or "a" before "donc".
10. 327 and 328: "en dessus" or "au dessus". The compressed first word looks more like *en*; CSG has "au-dessus".
11. 328: upper folio 166 or 168 (the sequence 163, 164, 165, …, 167 favours 166); label (2̄1̄) or (2̄1̄′) (the stroke at the right end of the bar may be a prime); "isom" or "hom.".
12. 329: the reference (24) or (21) and its interlined mark; the interlined qualification ("… une fois choisi Q̄ ≃ Q̄₀" over a struck "l'iso…"); "2 homs" or "son hom."; "transcendante" or "transcendant"; whether the right vertical relation in (25) is "=" or an arrow.
13. 330: "du" or "des choix"; whether the last fundamental group is over Q or Q̄. Mathematically it should be Q (the arithmetic group), but the ink shows a bar-like stroke.
