2. 361, subscript of Norm: π_0 or π'_0. The preceding sentence ("normalisateurs de G … dans $\widehat{\widehat{\mathcal T}}(\pi'_0)$") expects π'_0.
3. 361, last line: lower-case π_{g,ν} or capital Π_{g,ν}.
