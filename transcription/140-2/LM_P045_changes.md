## Unresolved

- 441 l. 2: Ũ → U or Ũ – U; the stroke between Ũ and U has no head.
- 441 (7): "sss" or "ssi"; the third letter has no dot.
- 441 d): the hatched interlinear cluster before Z_i; S¹ or 𝕊¹ (a vertical stroke through the S).
- 442 Dém., l. 2: the struck word at the head of the line (first pass "prenant") begins with a p and has no replacement, so the ink reads "en, pour ∀x ∈ U^σ, l'opération …" without a verb.
- 442 and 443: the display labels. On 442 the darker strokes make 9 over 8, on 443 8 over 9; whether the author swapped the numbers or retraced them is not settled. The witnesses number them (8), (9).
- 443: the index i of Ũ_i in the interlinear "Ũ_i|Z_i"; "revêt^t" or "revêtement"; the struck word before π₀(U^σ).
- 444 l. 1: no "a" before "fortiori" is visible; 443 ends "donc c'est" with no "a", and the margin crowds the head of 444.
- 444 (12): the A of "Aut" is written over another letter (two heavy stems, perhaps π); the small mark under ≃ (an f-like stroke with a dot) is unidentified.
- 445: the exponent in "card π₀(X_g^{σ_i})" has a small stroke before σ and after i, possibly parentheses.
- 445–448: π and Π use one glyph. Π_g is kept for the surface group, as in the first pass and the earlier packages (Π_{g,ν}); `\unsure{\Pi|\pi}-conjugué` on 447 is kept.
- 446: the first index of Y_{g,i}, the h in c) and the exponent u_i in (14) are overwritten.
- 447: ḣ ∈ 𝒯_g^+ or 𝒢_g^+: the curl of a script T and the body of a G are drawn over each other. The following "un relèvement dans 𝒢_g^+" and the NB on 446 call for 𝒯.
- 447 (16): the hatched tails after 𝒯_g^{+σ_i} and 𝒯_g^{σ_i} (perhaps "→ 1").
- 448: the struck clause after "𝒢^+, 𝒯^+," (perhaps "qui se réduit … deux"); in the cancelled Cor. 3, the word before "0-conn.", "quand U … orientable" and "il f et s".
- 449: the exponent of 𝒯_g (♮ or ♯); the sign in (21), which the Euler characteristic fixes as g−i (see the notes) but the ink does not; "\unsure{g-i}" l. 13; "correspondants".
- 450: ε_j or ℰ_j; the number (22) or (21); "soit" or "sur" before (20); the cancelled clause "On construit/constate … de sorte que"; the cancelled words after "dedans,".
