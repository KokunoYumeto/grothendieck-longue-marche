# LM_P064 (cote 140-3, scans 51–60): second-pass changes

I read both halves of every scan and compared the first pass with them line by line. Enlarged crops are in `second_pass/work_LM_P064` (index at the end); a crop sheet stacks panels, cited as `#1`, `#2`, … from the top. Coordinates in the crop labels are pixels of the whole scan `scanNNN.jpg`.

Witnesses. None of the four transcriptions covers these pages. CSG and IUT stop inside §37 at equation (80); Carmona has only the headings of §38 and §39 (ll. 7193–7194). Distinctive words of scans 51–60 ("kyrielle", "gruppentheoretisch", "formules brutales", "biunivoquement", "trouage") occur in none of them; "forage de trous" occurs only in other sections. The only evidence is the author's table of contents: §41 begins at p. 315 (= scan 54), titled "Conditions de commutation de π̂0,3 →∼ Ŝ+0,3 aux opérations extérieurs de IΓQ; calcul des automorphismes extérieurs dans π̂0,3 et dans Ŝ+0,3" (Circle l. 130); §42 VII–VIII "cocycles sous ρ / sous σ∞", §43 "1-cocycles des groupes cycliques", §46 "Notations définitives (?)" (Circle ll. 134–160). Otherwise the ink alone decides.

Notation. As in P059–P063: matrix groups `\mathrm{Gl}`, `\mathrm{Sl}`, `\mathrm{GP}`; the cartographic and symmetric groups with one fraktur S `\mathfrak S`; the Teichmüller letter `\mathcal T`; the extension letter `\mathcal E`; `\pi_1` for fundamental groups. The alphabet is a typographic convention, not a reading, so the first pass's alphabet alternatives are dropped (rows 2, 3, 13, 26). Other doubts inside those symbols are kept.
