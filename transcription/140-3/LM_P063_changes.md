# LM_P063 (cote 140-3, scans 41–50): second-pass changes

I read both halves of every scan and compared the first pass with them line by line. Enlarged crops are in `second_pass/work_LM_P063` (index at the end); each crop stacks panels, cited as `#1`, `#2`, … from the top. Coordinates are pixels of the whole scan `scanNNN.jpg`.

Witnesses. None of the four transcriptions covers these pages. CSG, IUT and Carmona stop inside §37 at equation (80) (CSG ends at l. 11555), as found for P059–P062. Distinctive words of scans 41–50 ("bavure", "paraphrase topologique", "Legendre (55)", "sujet à caution", "choisi un noyau", "boucher le trou", "courbes elliptiques et courbes rationnelles") occur in none of them; the hits for "forage", "gerbe" and "bel et bien un iso" lie in other sections. The only evidence is the author's table of contents: §40 "Paraphrase topologico-isotopique de la théorie de Legendre … 307" (Circle l. 128, Carmona l. 71) and §41 beginning at p. 315 (Circle l. 130), so ms. 309–313 all belong to §40. Otherwise the ink alone decides.

Notation. As in P059–P062, matrix groups are written `\mathrm{Gl}`, `\mathrm{Sl}`, `\mathrm{GP}`, the Teichmüller groups with the script T `\mathcal T`, the cartographic and symmetric groups with one fraktur S `\mathfrak S`; the extension letter of scans 43–46 (one glyph, an open reversed 3) is written `\mathcal E`. The alphabet is a typographic convention, not a reading, so the first pass's alphabet alternatives are dropped (rows 7, 11, 22). Other doubts inside those symbols (indices, primes, superscripts, overwritten letters) are kept.
