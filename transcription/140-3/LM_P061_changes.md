# LM_P061 (cote 140-3, scans 21–30): second-pass changes

I read both halves of every scan and compared every line of the first pass with them. Enlarged crops are in `second_pass/work_LM_P061` (index at the end of this file); each crop stacks several panels, cited as `#1`, `#2`, … from the top. Coordinates are pixels of the whole scan `scanNNN.jpg`.

Witnesses. None of the four transcriptions covers these pages. CSG, IUT and Carmona stop inside §37 at equation (80) (CSG l. 11555, IUT l. 13215, Carmona l. 7188), as found for P059 and P060; distinctive words of scans 21–30 ("multiquadratique", "orbite critique", "sous-groupe-lacet", "réduction mod", "faire la vérification", "conditions de congruence", Q(j)) occur in none of them. The only evidence they give is the author's later table of contents: §38 "Détermination de π0,3 ≃ 𝔖+0,3" (p. 291) and §39 "Propriétés de congruence de l'opération de ΓQ sur π̂0,3, liées à l'homomorphisme N1,1 → GL(2,Ẑ)" (p. 299; Circle table ll. 123–126, Carmona l. 70 and ll. 7193–7197), and the title of §35 "L'injectivité de ΓQ → Autextlac(π̂0,3)" (IUT l. 94). The page number 299 agrees with `\mspage{299}` on scan 22. Otherwise the ink alone decides.

Notation. As in P059/P060, matrix groups are written `\mathrm{Sl}`, `\mathrm{Gl}` (the ink has a looped lower-case l), the Teichmüller groups with the script T `\mathcal T`, and the cartographic and symmetric groups with one fraktur S `\mathfrak S`. These are typographic conventions, so the alphabet alternatives of the first pass are dropped (rows 1–3). Other doubts inside those symbols (indices, hats, primes) are kept.
