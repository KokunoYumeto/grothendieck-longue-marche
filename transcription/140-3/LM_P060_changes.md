Witnesses. None of the four covers these pages. The ink alone decides. Two passages of the reading edition were used as context only: §37's Proposition with its N.B. and footnote 148 (CSG ll. 10508–10547), which state the same quotient theorem in a later redaction ("G opère sur X avec sous-groupe invariant G …: Rev(X, G) ≃ Rev((Y, d), H)"), and CSG ll. 2996–3002, where Grothendieck calls the group of matrices with det u = ±1 acting on the half-plane "le groupe des automorphismes conformes".

Notation. As in P059 (its notation decision), the cartographic group and the symmetric group are written with one script S, `\mathfrak S`; the alphabet is a typographic convention, not a reading, so the alternatives `\unsure{\mathfrak S|\mathcal S}` and `\unsure{\mathcal G_{0,3}|\mathcal C_{0,3}}` are dropped (rows 14, 27, 30). The script T of $\mathcal T_{0,3}$ stays. Looped path letters stay `\ell` (first-pass convention).
