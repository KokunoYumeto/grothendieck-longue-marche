# LM_P062 (cote 140-3, scans 31–40): second-pass changes

I read both halves of every scan and compared the first pass with them line by line. Enlarged crops are in `second_pass/work_LM_P062` (index at the end); each crop stacks panels, cited as `#1`, `#2`, … from the top. Coordinates are pixels of the whole scan `scanNNN.jpg`.

Witnesses. None of the four transcriptions covers these pages. CSG, IUT and Carmona stop inside §37 at equation (80) (CSG l. 11555, IUT l. 13215, Carmona l. 7188), as found for P059–P061. Distinctive words of scans 31–40 ("bel et bien une restriction", "c'est gros", "astucieux" in this sense, "Reprenons nos arguments", "scindage de cette", "torseurs sous le groupe", "initiale de Legendre", "le cas de le dire") occur in none of them. The only evidence is the author's later table of contents: §40 "Paraphrase topologico-isotopique de la théorie de Legendre … 307" (Circle l. 128, Carmona l. 71) and §41 "Conditions de commutation de π̂0,3 ≃ Ŝ+0,3 aux opérations extérieures de ΓQ" (Circle ll. 130–131). Otherwise the ink alone decides.

Notation. As in P059–P061, the Teichmüller groups are written with the hooked script T `\mathcal T`, and the symmetric and cartographic groups with one fraktur S `\mathfrak S`. The alphabet is a typographic convention, not a reading, so the first pass's alphabet alternatives are dropped (rows 2, 3, 18). Other doubts inside those symbols (hats, superscripts) are kept.
